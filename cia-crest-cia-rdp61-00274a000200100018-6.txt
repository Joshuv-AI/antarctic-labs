COORDINATION STAFF

I MISSION

Under the policy guidance of the DAD/RR the Chief, Coordination
Staff is charged with the responsibility for the coordination of for-
eign economic intelligence production activities? devel » ^ ple "
mentation and control of foreign economic intelligence
production for the fulfillment of ORR responsibilities in the Intel

ligence field*

II FUNCTIONS

The Chief, Coordination Staff shall:

A. Determine for planning purposes what foreign economic in-
telligence should be produced and presented in order to:

1. Reduce to the minimum the element of surprise in for-
eign affairs affecting the national security.

2. Provide the foreign economic intelligence basis for
the formulation, execution and appraisal of national policies,
strategic plans, operational decisions and courses of action.

3. Ensure that all vital foreign economic intelligence
fields are adequately covered, and provide for production of
that intelligence in fields of common concern which by a ^®®“
ment can be produced more efficiently as a centralized activity.

E. Develop the plans and the methods of implementation for
the foreign economic intelligence production responsibilities of

ORR.

C. Coordinate and control the assignment of foreign economic
intelligence productivity for all components of ORR and those
allocations to other governmental agencies wherein authority o
CIA has been delegated.

D. Receive all requests for economic intelligence production
support from other CIA offices for governmental agencies and make
final determination of the basic research program.

E. Determine suitability or acceptance of production respon-
sibility? frame of reference? order of priority; establishment
of deadlines? and project assignments.
