Summary

 By changing 2 surfaces (1 new lens) OSL1 can deliver a 2cm spot size and up to 28
 J/cm²

 This new lens does not have any deleterious first-order ghosts

 Further work could allow
 — Placement of a relay plane at the sample plane by adjusting an additional
 parameter (inter-lens spacing, changing a third lens etc.)
 — Changing between configurations by pure lens translation (no lens swapping)
