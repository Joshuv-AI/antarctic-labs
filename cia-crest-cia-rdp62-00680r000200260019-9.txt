B. Planning and Development

The city plan project at PIC will reach a point in June- July when
a review of the lists of cities submitted by DD/P will be necessary.

It is estimated that by the end of June approximately 31 cities, only
3 of which will be backlog, will have been completed or in preparation.

Progress on the maps has been accelerated by

the overtime help the Division has given St/l. This, however, results
in a feast-or-famine situation in D/GC since the work done on overtime
must subsequently be process ed by the same people in the GC/S Branch.
Dependence for producing the maps on overtime is not a long- term
solution to this problem. 25X1 C

The Special Support Branch has arranged with the Printing Services
Division to process special intelligence maps larger than x Ik for
insertion in ERA reports. In making this facility available to ORR
components, through GC/X, consideration must be given to the additional
security problems involved and all requests should be planned with this
in mind. However, when oversize maps are needed to show extra detail,
this facility is available.

A project to prepare two hemisphere maps in full color for use in
the Central Intelligence Bulletin was processed during the month. This
represents the first production request placed on the Terrain Section
for full color representation of the world. For some time the Division
has felt that the future development of terrain representation lies
in full color rendition with subsequent color separation reproduction and
this request represented a welcomed opportunity to start actual production
in this technique.

C . Significant Outside Contacts and Activities

Aside from completion of the blueline plates in support of the AGS
project, no outside contacts of significance were made during May.

D. Problems

No significant problems worthy of mention exist at this time.
