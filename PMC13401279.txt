The Editor would like to alert the readers that concerns have been raised about some image similarities across publications from the same authors. Specifically, there appear to be a high similarity between Fig. 3A, CHOP of this article and Fig. 5A, CHOP from [1].

Readers are therefore advised to interpret these results with caution.
