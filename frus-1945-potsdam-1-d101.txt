No. 101 The Commanding General, United States Military Mission in the Soviet Union (Deane) to the Commanding General, Twelfth Army Group (Bradley)

[Extract][1]

top secret urgent

Moscow, 26 June 1945.

For General Bradley and to General Marshall for his eyes only citing Shges [sic] from Deane top sec MX 24797.

. . . . . . .

We have already emphasized the importance of General Stoner’s proceeding to Berlin for the signal installations when we asked for authority to increase Parks’ group.

Upon receipt of your S 93051[2] last night, I again presented the matter to General Antonov and Harriman presented it to Vyshinski.[3] I shall see General Antonov or his representatives today and press the matter further.

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram
