3. Lesson Plans :

The question of lesson plans was discussed and the Director of
Training requested compliance with previous directives that
lesson plans be completed and forwarded to provide a reservoir
of materials in support of the overseas training effort of the
Agency, for the vital docitments program of the Agency, and for
the effective management of the Office of Training. A draft of
a proposed regulation on this subject was considered as the basis
for regularizing the system of filing and revising lesson plans.
The draft regulation will be revised to clarify more precisely
what the various elements of the lesson plan consist of and resolve
the question of guest lectures.
