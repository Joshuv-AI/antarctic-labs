Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 2918th meeting, on 17 April 1990 ([1])
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of Namibia for admission to the United Nations, ([2])
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Namibia be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Adopted without vote.
[2] Official Records of the Security Council, Forty-fifth Year, Supplement for April, May and June 1990, document S/21241.
