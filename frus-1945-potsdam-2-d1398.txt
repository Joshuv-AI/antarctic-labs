No. 1398 The Ambassador in France (Caffery) to the French Minister of Foreign Affairs (Bidault)

secret No. 675

Paris, August 1, 1945.

Excellency: Acting under instructions from my Government, I have the honor to transmit herewith the text of the document “Western Frontier of Poland” approved on July 31, 1945.[1]

I avail myself [etc.]

Jefferson Caffery

Source: 740.00119 (Potsdam)/8–145

Notes
[1] The enclosure consists of the text, without date or heading, of document No. 1152.
