Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 872nd meeting, on 7 July 1960

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Republic of the Congo <small>[1] [2]</small>,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of the Congo be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1]Republic of the Congo, capital Leopoldville.</small>

<small>[2]Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for July, August, and September 1960, document S/4361.</small>

1960
