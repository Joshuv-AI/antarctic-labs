Program was completed. A complete report of the program was
submitted in a report to the DD/A dated 26 May 1953.

periodical progress being made by offices in connection with
their Records Management Program. In connection with this
Program, a tour of the Printing and Reproduction Division was
arranged and to date 45 people have made the tour.

Vital Materials program to determine if additional records
should be declared vital or if any material presently con-
sidered vital could be eliminated.
