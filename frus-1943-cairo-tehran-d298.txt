The President to the Ambassador in Turkey (Steinhardt)[1] (24 November 1943, d298)

secret

[Cairo,] 24 November 1943.

From the President to Ambassador Steinhardt, Ankara, Turkey. Personal and secret.

Our next plans have been advanced. Hope to see you in Cairo in about a week,[2] and in regard to your number one[3] will advise you in a day or two.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington via military channels, forwarded by the White House Map Room to the Department of State, and relayed by the Department to the Embassy at Ankara (740.0011 EW 1939/32811b).
[2] Steinhardt arrived in Cairo at 5 o’clock on the afternoon of the same day. See the Log, ante, p. 297; see also ante, p. 100.
[3] President Inönü of Turkey. For subsequent correspondence regarding arrangements for Inönü to come to Cairo, see post, pp. 633, 662.
