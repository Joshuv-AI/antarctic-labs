Editorial Note (Potsdam 1945 Vol. II, Unnumbered document following Document 710 (#115))

—Document No. 739, post, mentions a meeting of July 25 which considered the question of officers of the Preparatory Commission of the United Nations and of the Executive Committee of that Commission. It is not clear whether the meeting referred to was a bilateral Anglo-American conversation or whether Soviet representatives participated as well. It appears, however, that there were later tripartite discussions of this subject at the Berlin Conference. See document No. 743, post.
