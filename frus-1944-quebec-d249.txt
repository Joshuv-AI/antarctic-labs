Editorial Note (Quebec 1944, Document 249)

On September 13, 1944, Hull sent to Roosevelt a memorandum on shipment of arms to Ethiopia so that the President would be prepared if Churchill raised the question at the forthcoming Quebec Conference. For the text of Hull’s memorandum, see Foreign Relations, 1944, vol. V, pp. 73–74. No indication has been found that the subject of the memorandum was actually discussed by Roosevelt and Churchill at Quebec.
