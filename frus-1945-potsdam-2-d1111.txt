No. 1111 The Ambassador in Italy (Kirk) to the Acting Secretary of State

plain

Rome, July 17, 1945.

2010. Most Rome papers this morning carry United Press item stating that Colonel James Henderson Douglas will arrive in Italy probably on July 24 to assume post of Chief Commissioner of Allied Commission replacing Admiral Stone (see my 1877, July 7th[1]).

Kirk
