ADDRESS REPLY TO
P. O. BOX DRAWER 1734
ATLANTA, GEORGIA 30301

Admiral Stansfield Turner
Director of Central Intelligence
Washington, D.C. 20505

Dear Admiral Turner:

The Rand Board of Trustees will meet next month in Washington, D.C.

As is our custom, we plan a working reception with a group of policy-
level government officials. Since Rand is a private, nonprofit
public service institution, the Board feels that such an informal
occasion for discussion is an important aid in discharging trustee-
ship responsibilities. Experience indicates that this meeting is
also useful to attending government officials.

I hope that you can join the Rand Board members and officers the
evening of Wednesday, April 9, at The Madison Hotel. The reception
will start at 6:00 p.m. in the Mount Vernon Room on the Mezzanine

Level. There will be no formal speeches or programs.

Enclosed is a list of our Board members and officials. If previous

years are a guide, we can expect a number of appointed officials

from throughout the government, senior military officers, and
representatives from the Congress to join us for the evening.

Realizing that you have a busy schedule, I would appreciate very
much hearing whether or not you anticipate being able to join us.
Please call Mrs. Lee Meyer at the Rand Washington Office, (202)
296-5000.

I look forward to seeing you on April 9.

Sincerely,

Enclosure
