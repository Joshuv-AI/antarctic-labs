b. Ch ange i n NIS Area Title

The Committee approved proposal of the State member
that NIS 99 (Philippine Islands) be titled "Philippines " It was
further agreed that in NIS contributions the term "Philippinee" be
used in the geographic context, and the term "The Republic of the
Philippines" be used in the political context.

The State member noted that "West Indies" had been
designated as the official name of the British Caribbean Federation,
which would affect the titles and general area descriptions of NIS 81,
81 A, and 8 IB. The Committee decided to take no action pending
further investigation and report by the State member.
