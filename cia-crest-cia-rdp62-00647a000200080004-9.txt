Attached are three statements in respect of the income and
expenditure for the Secretariat of the Coordinating Committee from
January 1st to December 31st 1959 •

Statement As Statement of Total Expenditure.

Statement B: Statement of Cash Income and Expenditure.

Statement C: Pattern of Contributions in Kind and Cash on the basis
of December 1959.

These three statements show the 1959 results. For the future pattern

of expenditure the following points should be noted:

(a) Possible staff changes may represent a saving in the expenditure
paid for out of cash contributions.

(b) The change of premises for the Coordinating Committee may very well
lead to an increase in the rent and other services. Nothing
definite can be said on this matter at the present time.
