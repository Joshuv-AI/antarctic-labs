1 Introduction
We investigate the joint distribution of the neutron number and cumulative fission-fragment kinetic energy
(FKE) deposition, with a specific focus on low-order statistical moments: the mean, variance, and correlation.
Starting from a point-kinetic framework, we derive a forward Master equation (FME) for the joint distribution
and develop the corresponding moment equations. This framework is subsequently generalized to account
for full neutron phase-space dependence (position, energy, and direction) and a general intrinsic random
source using a backward Master equation (BME) formulation. Previous work on the joint distribution of
the neutron number with related species or stochastic quantities has been done for the neutron number and
cumulative fission number [1, 2], the neutron number and the number of counts registered by a detector [3],
and the prompt and delayed neutron number [4, 5]. Previous work concerning the fission energy deposited
due to the FKE has been explored in [6, 7]. The work in this report is novel in the formulation of the joint
distribution of the neutron number and the cumulative FKE.

2 Point-Kinetic Model
We consider a lumped medium and derive a point-kinetic FME for the joint distribution and its statistical
moments. Loss due to leakage is assumed to be incorporated as an enhancement of the capture cross section.
