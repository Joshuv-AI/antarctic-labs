The Assistant Secretary of War (McCloy) to the Secretary of War (Stimson) (27 November 1943, d336)

secret priority

[Cairo,] 27 November 1943.

10065 (To Stimson from McCloy)

Have had talks with Joint Chiefs and British on civil affairs matters and discussions with others including President, Winant and Eden.[1] Feel that if British confirm agreement outlined in my 10064 we have accomplished all that we have set out to do on civil affairs matter. Though I have only been on fringe of strategical discussions, it is clear that all decisions of important matters are held up pending results Eastern conference[2] to which I did not go though I was requested to stay on here pending their return.

Source: Defense Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] See ante, pp. 351, 415, and telegram printed supra.
[2] Conference at Tehran.
