Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

This project is intended to model wind power plant based on monitored data at the wind power
plant. This project will promote the university research in Renewable Energy area and trains the
future highly qualified engineers.

The dynamic model will be based on neural net model with the input from the two met towers
(12 inputs), and the number of turbines in operation (one input). The overall input will be 13
inputs to drive the simulations. The output power at the point of interconnection will be used to
tune the neural net weight coefficients. Two neural net concepts will be investigated (the back
propagation neural net and the dynamic recurrent neural net with feedback).

CRADA benefit to DOE, Participant, and US Taxpayer:

This CRADA will investigate the use of Neural Net to model a wind power plant based on a set
of inputs monitored at the two meteorological towers (Met Tower). If this project is proven to be
successful, this concept can be expanded to other wind power plants (on-shore or off-shore).
With the available model, one can predict the output power of a wind plant more confidently.
This model is then used as a baseline predictor of wind plant output to improve the energy yield,
detect any performance degradation, thus, immediate action can be taken to do maintenance
(cleaning the blades, lubrication, etc.).

DOE, Participant, and US Taxpayer benefit from this CRADA on several aspects: technology
advancement in wind plant operation training high skilled labor (graduate students) to perform
R&D in wind plant operation potential innovation on wind plant power forecasting tools increase
collaboration among university, national laboratory, and utility company promote renewable
energy research in the university environment

Task Descriptions and Estimated Completion Dates Role and responsibilities:

NREL will participate in guiding and advising graduate students working in this project and to
provide data to the university team. University of Denver will perform the simulations and
analysis with collaboration from NREL team. Biweekly meetings will be conducted for the
duration of the project. The project will be summarized.in a final report and at least one
conference paper.
