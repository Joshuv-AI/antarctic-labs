Dear Dr. McDonald:

As you know, this Office is currently engaged in the preparation of
volumes on Vietnam 1955-1968 for inclusion in the Foreign Relations of
the United States series. In connection with this work we would appre-
ciate access to certain CIA classified histories, namely:

Allen Welsh Dulles as Director of Central Intelligence, 26

February 1953 - 29 November 1961. 5 vols.. History No. DCI-2.

July 1973. (ST"^

I enclose a copy of the list of histories upon which this request
is based.

I understand that your Office is presently working on the history
of McCone's directorate and other histories that pertain to Vietnam in
the early sixties. Would we be able to consult these histories as well?

The historians working on the Vietnam project, all of whom have
TOP SECRET clearances, include the following:

David M. Baehler
Suzanne E. Coffman
Edward C. Keefer
Ronald D. Landa
David W. Mabon
Charles S. Sampson
Louis J. Smith

Messrs. Keefer, Mabon, and Sampson also have special intelligence clear-
ance.

Any number of the above group, depending on what is convenient
for you, would be able to come to the Agency to consult the material.

This request is confined to permission to examine the studies.

Any use of information contained in them would be subject to separate
clearance and declassification review in accordance with established
procedures .

In the course of preparing the volumes on Vietnam for 1955-1960,

Mr. Keefer was given permission to read at the Agency some of the
other published histories and cables specifically identified in them.

This was a great help to him in preparing editorial notes as supplements
to the documents to be printed. With only slight modifications, these
notes were cleared for publication by CIA and the Department of State.

Should you wish to discuss this request, please contact my Deputy,
Neal Petersen (632-2033) or Ronald Landa (632-8979).
