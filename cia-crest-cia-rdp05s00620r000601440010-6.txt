** Herb advises that although the VFW Convention Monday morning

2. I asked the Air Force about the possibility of stopping in Charlottesville
to pick you up. They said it was possible; however, the short runway causes
problems. If the runway is "wet," they would not be able to land— breaking
distance restrictions. Additionally, they will have to take off with less than
a full load of fuel, necessitating a fuel stop in Knoxville, Tennessee.

Recommend departing from Andrews which would require your returning to Washington
on Sunday evening.
