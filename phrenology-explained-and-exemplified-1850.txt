THE  PRINCIPLES  OF  PHRENOLOGY.

The  principles  of  Phrenology  are,  that  the  brain  is  the
organ  of  the  mind  ;  that  the  mind  and  body  are  so  connected
tnat  certain  states  of  the  latter  affect  the  former  ;  that  we  are
interested  in  knowing  the  qualities  of  the  brain,  but  God  alone
can  know  the  essence  of  mind  or  matter.

The  cerebro-spinal  nerves  convey  impressions  to  the  brain,

feelings  are  placed.
