President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (30 August 1944, d38)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 30 August 1944.

Number 611, personal and top secret, from the President for the Prime Minister.[2]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

We can renew our Teheran talk about Trieste and Istria[3] at Octagon.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] For the paragraphs of this message omitted here, see post, pp. 222–223.
[3] See Foreign Relations, The Conferences at Cairo and Tehran, 1943, pp. 493, 503.
