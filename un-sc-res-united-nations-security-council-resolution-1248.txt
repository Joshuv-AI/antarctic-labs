Adopted by the Security Council at its 4016th meeting on 25 June 1999

<div style="text-indent:1.4em;">
The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Republic of Kiribati for admission
to the United Nations (S/1999/477),

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Kiribati be
admitted to membership in the United Nations.
</div>
