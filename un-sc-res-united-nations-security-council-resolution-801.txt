Adopted by the Security Council at its 3158th meeting, on 8 January 1993 <small>[1]</small>

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Czech Republic for admission to the United Nations, <small>[2]</small>

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Czech Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Adopted without vote</small>

<small>[2] S/25045.</small>
