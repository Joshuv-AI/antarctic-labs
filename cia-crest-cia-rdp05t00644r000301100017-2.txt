CONFERENCE REGISTRATION

The Conference Registration Desk, which is located at
the end of the Promenade on the Second Floor of the Annex,
will be open from 11:00 A.M. to 8:00 P.M. on Wednesday,
July 30, and daily throughout the Conference for registration
and information. All Conference members, whether pre-regis-
tered or not, should check in at the Conference Desk upon
arrival. A packet containing tickets, name plates and other
information may be picked up immediately upon checking in
at the desk by all pre-registrants. Do not hesitate to ask
personnel at the desk to assist you with any problems.
