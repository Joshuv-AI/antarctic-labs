VOLTAGE CONTOUR STUDY

 15 voltage violations introduced across 6 feeders in
 SFO, in three trials across two conditions: glyph and
 contour.

 Double-blind, within-subjects design measures
 conditions for all subjects with between-subjects
 counterbalancing.

 H1: Users will misinterpret outliers on voltage contour
 heatmaps with false-positive and false-negative outlier
 identifications.

 H2: Users will be more accurate in identifying outliers
 when bus voltages are represented by discrete glyphs
           rather than continuous contour heatmaps.        NREL | 15
