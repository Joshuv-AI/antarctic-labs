 Charging Controls and Limits

 Test cells a r e charged by the constant current method with

 the voltage limited with respect to temperature. These voltage

 limits a r e 1.45 volts a t 4OoC, 1.49 volts a t 25'C and 1.55 volts a t

 O°C. The charge current is limited to an amount equal to the

 average discharge current for the 90 minute test cycle. Longer

 test cycles have lower charge current limits since more time i e

 available for charging.

 Failure Analysis

 Cells a r e considered to have failed when the end discharge

 voltage is less than one-half of one volt. Failed cells are analyzed

 to determine the cause of failure and to note irregularities in strcc-

 ture. Problem a r e a s found in nickel-cadmium batteries, after one

 year's life cycle testing, include the following:

 a. Electrolyte leaking through seals.
 b. Shorting across seals caused by formation of silver base com-

 pounds over the ceramic portions.
 c. Shorting due to eeparator failure caused by grid screen wires

 that puncture the separator during testing. I
 d. Poorly welded electrical connections inside the cell.

 space cells a r e summarized in Tables 1 & 2 . The failures and

 An analysis of the data with respect to temperature indicates

 that most of the failures a r e occurring a t 4OoC. The cells on test

 at 0 OC a r e continuing without evidence of degradation.
