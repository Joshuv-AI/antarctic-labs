(Generalized) Spine Subalgebras of Fourier–Stieltjes algebras and their Homomorphisms
Nico Spronk, Ross Stokke, Aasaimani Thamizhazhagan

Abstract
For any upper semilattice 𝒟 {\cal D} of locally precompact topologies on a locally compact group G G , we define an associated generalized spine subalgebra A 𝒟 ∗ ​ ( G ) A^{*}_{\cal D}(G) of the Fourier–Stieltjes algebra B ⁡ ( G ) B(G) . We show that A 𝒟 ∗ ​ ( G ) A^{*}_{\cal D}(G) is a semilattice-graded ℓ 1 \ell^{1} -direct sum of maximal copies of Fourier algebras and we identify its spectrum as a semilattice of groups. We build a collection of examples of generalized spine algebras over whose spectra we exhibit fine control. We define notions of compatible fusions of homomorphisms and affine maps, and use these definitions to characterize all completely positive, completely contractive and, when G G is amenable, all completely bounded homomorphisms from a generalized spine algebra A 𝒟 ∗ ​ ( G ) A^{*}_{\cal D}(G) to a Fourier–Stieltjes algebra B ⁡ ( H ) B(H) . These results are new, even when A 𝒟 ∗ ​ ( G ) A^{*}_{\cal D}(G) is the full spine algebra A ∗ ​ ( G ) A^{*}(G) and even when G G and H H are abelian. We provide examples illustrating the scope of our theorems. Introduction

1.1
Background and plan

For a locally compact group,
G
, the primary objects in abelian and noncommutative harmonic analysis are its group and measure algebras

is a noncompact locally compact abelian group, according to common understanding, the spectrum of

[
16

is more accessible than

, though we note that Walter
[
29
]
and others have provided significant insights into the structure of

When

and J.L. Taylor, who introduced the terminology

. Through the Fourier-Stieltjes transform,

Lemma 4.1
.

Let
(G,\tau_{G})
and
(H,\tau_{H})
be locally compact groups.

1.

Let
\phi:(G,\tau_{G})\rightarrow(H,\tau_{H})
be a topological group isomorphism. Then there is a topological group isomorphism
\widetilde{\phi}:G^{ap}\rightarrow H^{ap}
such that
\widetilde{\phi}\circ\eta^{G}_{ap}=\eta^{H}_{ap}\circ\phi
. Moreover,
\phi:(G,\tau_{ap}^{G})\rightarrow(H,\tau_{ap}^{H})
is also a topological group isomorphism.

2.

The topological group compactifications
((G\times H)^{ap},\eta^{G\times H}_{ap})
and
(G^{ap}\times H^{ap},\eta^{G}_{ap}\times\eta^{H}_{ap})
of
G\times H
are equivalent. Thus,
(G^{ap}\times H^{ap},\eta^{G}_{ap}\times\eta^{H}_{ap})
is a representation of the topology
\tau^{G\times H}_{ap}
in
{\cal T}(G\times H)
and
(g_{\alpha},h_{\alpha})\rightarrow(g,h)
in
\tau^{G\times H}_{ap}
if and only if
\eta^{G}_{ap}(g_{\alpha})\rightarrow\eta^{G}_{ap}(g)
in
G^{ap}
and
\eta^{H}_{ap}(h_{\alpha})\rightarrow\eta^{H}_{ap}(h)
in
H^{ap}
.

Proof.

1. Since
(H^{ap},\eta^{H}_{ap}\circ\phi)
and
(G^{ap},\eta^{G}_{ap}\circ\phi^{-1})
are topological group compactifications of
G
and
H
respectively, the universal property of an almost periodic compactification yields continuous surjective homomorphisms
\widetilde{\phi}:G^{ap}\rightarrow H^{ap}
and
\widetilde{\psi}:H^{ap}\rightarrow G^{ap}
satisfying
\widetilde{\phi}\circ\eta^{G}_{ap}=\eta^{H}_{ap}\circ\phi
and
\widetilde{\psi}\circ\eta^{H}_{ap}=\eta^{G}_{ap}\circ\phi^{-1}
. For
g\in G
,
\widetilde{\psi}\circ\widetilde{\phi}(\eta^{G}_{ap}(g))=\widetilde{\psi}(\eta^{H}_{ap}(\phi(g)))=\eta^{G}_{ap}(\phi^{-1}(\phi(g)))=\eta^{G}_{ap}(g).
Similarly,
\widetilde{\phi}\circ\widetilde{\psi}
is the identity map on the dense subset
\eta^{H}_{ap}(H)
of
H^{ap}
, so
\widetilde{\phi}
is a topological group isomorphism (with inverse
\widetilde{\psi}
). From (
1
), the second statement in part 1 follows because
g_{\alpha}\rightarrow g
in
\tau^{G}_{ap}
if and only if
\eta^{G}_{ap}(g_{\alpha})\rightarrow\eta^{G}_{ap}(g)
in
G^{ap}
, which holds exactly when
\eta^{H}_{ap}(\phi(g_{\alpha}))=\widetilde{\phi}(\eta^{G}_{ap}(g_{\alpha}))\rightarrow\widetilde{\phi}(\eta^{G}_{ap}(g))=\eta^{H}_{ap}(\phi(g))
; equivalently,
\phi(g_{\alpha})\rightarrow\phi(g)
in
\tau^{H}_{ap}
.

2. We observe that the topological group compactification
(G^{ap}\times H^{ap},\eta^{G}_{ap}\times\eta^{H}_{ap})
of

is universal among all topological group compactifications of
G\times H
. (This must be known but we have no reference.) To this end, let
((G\times H)^{\alpha},\alpha)
be a group compactification of

. Then
(G^{\alpha_{G}},\alpha_{G})
and
(H^{\alpha_{H}},\alpha_{H})
are group compactifications of
G
and
H
respectively, where

are defined by
\alpha_{G}(g)=\alpha(g,e_{H})
,
\alpha_{H}(h)=\alpha(e_{G},h)
and

,

are, respectively, the closures in

of
\alpha_{G}(G)
and
\alpha_{H}(H)
. The universal properties of
G^{ap}
and
H^{ap}
yield continuous (group homomorphisms)
\phi_{G}:G^{ap}\rightarrow G^{\alpha_{G}}
and
\phi_{H}:H^{ap}\rightarrow H^{\alpha_{H}}
satisfying
\phi_{G}\circ\eta^{G}_{ap}=\alpha_{G}
and
\phi_{H}\circ\eta^{H}_{ap}=\alpha_{H}

\phi:G^{ap}\times H^{ap}\rightarrow(G\times H)^{\alpha}:(s,t)\mapsto\phi_{G}(s)\phi_{H}(t)

5

Let
S=\mathop{\vphantom{\bigcup}\mathchoice{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\displaystyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\textstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptscriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}}\displaylimits_{z\in Z}G_{z}

2.

where we have used property (c’). So, if it happens that

E_{\alpha}=\mathop{\vphantom{\bigcup}\mathchoice{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\displaystyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\textstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptscriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}}\displaylimits_{z\in Z}F_{z}

Definition 5.3

If

where

is a subset of a group

is a mapping of

into a type of semigroup, we will say that
\alpha:E_{\alpha}\subseteq H\rightarrow S=\mathop{\vphantom{\bigcup}\mathchoice{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\displaystyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\textstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}{\vtop{\halign{\hfil$\m@th\scriptscriptstyle#$\hfil\cr\bigcup\cr\cdot\crcr}}}}\displaylimits_{z\in Z}G_{z}

, if

has property

.
