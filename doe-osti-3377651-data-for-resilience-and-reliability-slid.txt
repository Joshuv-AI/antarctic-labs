 Proprietary Data:
 Utility Outage Data + Grid Data (Location)
• Outage data is associated with a given
  feeder
• A feeder or a circuit is a continuous
  component of infrastructure,
  conducting electricity from a
  substation (the power source) to
  customers served by that feeder.
• When we associate outage data with
  a feeder or a feeder section, we can
  see which feeders frequently have the
  most challenges.
• Utilities often know which feeders
  these are.

 Takeaways
• Publicly available data can reveal regional, state, or county level impacts of
  weather events on the power grid.
• EIA-861 is a great source for reliability metrics.
• Coordination with local utilities is key to understanding potential localized
  impacts.
• There may be opportunities for collaboration with distribution utilities to
  enhance community safety and comfort, and even grid performance.
