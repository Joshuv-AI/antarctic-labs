Loop Space Splittings for Codimensional Sphere Bundles
Wen Shen

Abstract
. In this paper, we establish two loop decomposition theorems for sphere bundles of real vector bundles under appropriate connectivity and characteristic class assumptions. Specifically, we treat two families of sphere bundles: those arising from rank- ( n − 1 ) (n-1) vector bundles and those from rank- ( n − 3 ) (n-3) vector bundles over closed smooth n n -manifolds.

Primary 55P15, 55P35

1.
Introduction

Loop decomposition equivalence is an increasingly prominent classification paradigm. Formally, two manifolds
X
and
Y
are equivalent under this relation if their loop spaces satisfy
\Omega X\simeq\Omega Y
, where
\Omega
denotes the loop functor that maps a topological space
X
to the space of all based loops in
X
, and a continuous map
f:X\to Y
to the induced map on loops.

Beben and Theriault
[
3
]
proved that two
(n-1)
-connected
2n
-dimensional manifolds are loop-equivalent if and only if they share the same
n
-th Betti number. Parallel results for the odd-dimensional counterpart, namely
(n-1)
-connected
(2n+1)
-manifolds, were established independently by Beben and Wu
[
4
]
, and Huang and Theriault
[
8
]
. More recently, Stanton and Theriault
[
12
]
investigated a loop space decomposition for simply-connected Poincaré duality complexes of dimension
n
whose
(n-1)
-skeleton is a co-H-space. A key advantage of these loop decomposition theorems is that they reduce the computation of homotopy groups of highly connected manifolds to that of homotopy groups of spheres and Moore spaces. Related calculations can also be found in the work of Samik Basu and Somnath Basu
[
1
,
2
]
.

Research on loop decompositions has lately been extended to sphere bundles over
4
-manifolds
[
6
,
7
]
. A notable result in
[
7
]
states that the loop spaces of sphere bundles associated to most real vector bundles over simply connected closed
4
-manifolds decompose into products of simpler homotopy types. Shen
[
10
,
11
]
further generalized this conclusion to sphere bundles over
(n-1)
-connected
2n
- and
(2n+1)
-dimensional Poincaré complexes.

This paper continues this line of investigation, focusing on loop space splittings for sphere bundles of real vector bundles over specific manifolds. Motivated by
[
13
]
, we observe that if a sphere bundle

S^{m}\stackrel{{\scriptstyle i}}{{\to}}E\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\pi}}{{\to}}N

admits a homotopy section
s:N\to E
(that is,
\pi\circ s
is a homotopy equivalence), then the composition

\Omega S^{m}\times\Omega N\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\Omega i\times\Omega s}}{{\rightarrow}}\Omega E\times\Omega E\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\mu}}{{\to}}\Omega E

induces isomorphisms on
\pi_{i}
for
i\geq 0
, thus
is a homotopy equivalence, where
\mu
is the product of loop space.
Suppose
N
is a
n
-manifold. Cell structure analysis shows that such a homotopy section always exists when
m\geq n
, but may fail for
m<n
.
In this work, we exploit the existence of homotopy sections to establish our main theorems.

We now state our first main theorem.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
n\geq 5
, and let
N
be a non-spin, simply connected closed smooth
n
-manifold. For any real spin vector bundle of rank
n-1
over
N
, the associated sphere bundle

S^{n-2}\to M\to N

splits after looping, i.e.,
\Omega M\simeq\Omega S^{n-2}\times\Omega N
.

Before presenting the second theorem, we recall the cohomology group of the classifying space
\mathrm{BSpin}
.
H^{4}(\mathrm{BSpin})\cong\mathbb{Z}
is generated by a class
X
such that
2X=p_{1}
, where
p_{1}
is the first Pontryagin class of the universal bundle over
\mathrm{BSpin}
, and
X\equiv w_{4}\pmod{2}
for the fourth Stiefel-Whitney class
w_{4}
. Accordingly, for any spin bundle
\xi
over a
3
-connected complex,
p_{1}(\xi)
is divisible by
2
, and the congruence
w_{4}(\xi)\equiv\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(\xi)\pmod{2}
holds.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
n\geq 8
, and let
N
be a
3
-connected closed smooth
n
-manifold satisfying:

(1)

either
\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(N)\equiv\text{1, 5}\pmod{6}
,

(2)

or

\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(N)\equiv 3\pmod{6}
and
n\equiv 0\pmod{2}
.

If a real vector bundle
\xi
of rank
n-3
over
N
satisfies
\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(\xi)=0
, then its associated sphere bundle

S^{n-4}\to M\to N

splits after looping, i.e.,

\Omega M\simeq\Omega S^{n-4}\times\Omega N
.

This paper is organized as follows. Section
2
discusses basic applications of Wu classes and the Wu formula. Sections
3
and
4
present the detailed proofs of Theorem
1.1
and Theorem
1.2
, respectively. Indeed, we will demonstrate that certain sphere bundles admit homotopy sections (see Lemma
3.6
and Lemma
4.6
).

2.
Preliminary

In this section, we study Steenrod operations acting on the cohomology groups of smooth manifolds.

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
N
be a closed simply connected non-spin smooth
n
-manifold. Then there exists a generator
x\in H^{n-2}(N)
such that

0\neq\mathrm{Sq}^{2}\rho_{2}(x)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})

where
\rho_{2}
denotes the mod
2
reduction.

Proof.

We prove this lemma in two steps.

(1) The mod
2
reduction
\rho_{2}:H^{n-2}(N)\to H^{n-2}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})
is surjective.

This follows from the following exact sequence

H^{n-2}(N)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\times 2}}{{\to}}H^{n-2}(N)\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\rho_{2}}}{{\to}}H^{n-2}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\delta}}{{\to}}H^{n-1}(N)

where
H^{n-1}(N)\cong H_{1}(N)\cong\pi_{1}(N)=0
.

(2) There exists a generator
x\in H^{n-2}(N)
such that
\mathrm{Sq}^{2}\rho_{2}(x)\neq 0
.

Since
N
is non-spin, we have
w_{2}(N)\neq 0
. By the Poincaré duality and Item (1), there exists a generator
x\in H^{n-2}(N)
such that

0\neq\rho_{2}(x)\cup w_{2}(N)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})

Recall from
[
9
, pp.132]
that the second Wu class satisfies
v_{2}(N)=w_{2}(N)
.
By the Wu formula,
\mathrm{Sq}^{2}\rho_{2}(x)=\rho_{2}(x)\cup v_{2}(N)\neq 0
. This completes the proof.
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

Let

be a closed smooth

-connected

-manifold.

(1) If

(2) If

0\neq\mathrm{Sq}^{4}\rho_{2}(x)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})

(3) If
\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(N)\equiv\text{1 or 5}\pmod{6}

such that

0\neq\mathrm{Sq}^{4}\rho_{2}(x)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2}),\quad 0\neq\mathcal{P}^{1}\rho_{3}(x)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{3})

where
\rho_{3}
denotes the mod

Proof.

By the argument for Lemma
2.1
, the
\rho_{2}
and
\rho_{3}
are surjective in degree

.

If
{p_{1}(N)}\equiv 0\pmod{3}

and hence vanishes
[
9
, pp.229]
. By the Wu formula, we finish the proof.

Let
\mathfrak{X}=\frac{1}{2}p_{1}(N)\in H^{4}(N)
.

It is well-known that
w_{4}(N)\equiv\mathfrak{X}\pmod{2}\in H^{4}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})
. Assume
w_{4}(N)\neq 0
. By Poincaré duality, there exists a generator
x\in H^{n-4}(N)
such that

0\neq\rho_{2}(x)\cup w_{4}(N)\in H^{n}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2}).

The mod-2 Wu class satisfies
v_{4}(N)=w_{4}(N)\in H^{4}(N;\mathbb{Z}_{2})

[
9
, pp.132]
.
By the Wu formula,
\mathrm{Sq}^{4}\rho_{2}(x)=\rho_{2}(x)\cup v_{4}(N)\neq 0
.

Assume
\mathfrak{X}\equiv\text{1 or 5}\pmod{6}
. Since
N
is
3
-connected,
H^{4}(N)\cong\mathbb{Z}^{d}
with
d\geq 1
. Let
\{a_{i}\}_{i=1}^{d}
be a basis for
H^{4}(N)
, and let
[N]\in H_{n}(N)
denote the fundamental class of
N
. By Poincaré duality, the torsion free part of
H^{n-4}(N)
admits a basis
\{b_{i}\}_{i=1}^{d}
satisfying
\langle a_{i}\cup b_{j},[N]\rangle=\delta_{ij}
where
\delta_{ij}
is the Kronecker delta.

Write
\mathfrak{X}=\sum_{i=1}^{d}k_{i}a_{i}
for
k_{i}\in\mathbb{Z}
. Since
\mathfrak{X}\equiv\text{1 or 5}\pmod{6}
, we may assume
k_{1}\equiv 1\pmod{2}
and
k_{2}\not\equiv 0\pmod{3}
.

If
k_{1}\not\equiv 0\pmod{3}
, set

. If
k_{2}\equiv 1\pmod{2}
, set

If

and

\mathrm{gcd}(g,6)=1
, and there exist

Set

. This element satisfies:

(1)

denotes a generator of
H^{n-4}(N)

(2)

and

In all cases above, combining the mod-

and mod-

Wu classes with the Wu formula yields
\mathrm{Sq}^{4}(\rho_{2}x)\neq 0
and
\mathcal{P}^{1}(\rho_{3}x)\neq 0
.
∎
