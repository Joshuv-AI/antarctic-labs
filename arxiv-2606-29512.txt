On uniqueness of solutions to stochastic Navier-Stokes Equations
Raymond Cotter, István Gyöngy

Abstract
. Theorems on uniqueness and continuous dependence on the initial condition of solutions to stochastic Navier-Stokes equations driven by Wiener processes and Poisson martingale measures are presented. These theorems generalise some results from [ 32 ] .

1.
Introduction

The Navier–Stokes equations

(1.1)

\partial_{t}u=\nu\Delta u-(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nabla p+D_{j}\mathfrak{f}^{j}+f,\quad\operatorname{div}u=0,

posed on a domain
\mathcal{R}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{d}
with boundary and
initial conditions

(1.2)

u|_{\partial\mathcal{R}}=0,\quad u|_{t=0}=u_{0},

describe the motion of incompressible viscous fluids (when
d=2,3)
).
Here
u_{t}(x)=(u^{1}_{t}(x),\dots,u^{d}_{t}(x))

and
p_{t}(x)
are the velocity of the fluid and the pressure at time
t\geq 0

at
x\in\mathcal{R}
, and
\nu>0
is the viscosity
coefficient.

Classical results of Leray
[
48
]
and Hopf
[
33
]
show that for
every given initial condition
u_{0}\in\mathcal{H}
, force fields

\mathfrak{f}^{j}\in L_{2}([0,\infty],L_{2}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d}))

(j=1,2,...,d)
and

f\in L_{1}([0,\infty),L_{2}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d}))
there exists at least one weak
solution
u
from the Hopf–Leray class

\mathcal{W}:=L_{\infty}([0,\infty),\mathcal{H})\cap L_{2}([0,\infty),\mathcal{V}),

where

\mathcal{V}
and
\mathcal{H}
denote the closure of the space

\mathcal{D}=\{\varphi\in C^{\infty}_{0}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d}):\,\text{div$\,\varphi=0$}\}

in
W^{1}_{2}=W^{1}_{2}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d})
and
L_{2}=L_{2}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d})
, respectively.
Here and throughout the paper
C^{\infty}_{0}(\mathcal{R},\mathbb{R}^{d})
denotes the space of
\mathbb{R}^{d}
-valued
smooth functions on
\mathcal{R}
with compact support.

By a theorem of Ladyzhenskaya
[
43
]
in 1958
and by Lions and Prodi
[
50
]
in 1959,
the weak solution in this class is unique when

d=2
, whereas the corresponding problem in dimensions

d\geq 3
remained open until a recent paper
[
2
]
by
Albritton, Brué and Colombo,
where the authors show the existence of more than one solution
to the 3D Navier-Stokes equations with an appropriate force field
and zero initial condition. In
[
34
]

the authors demonstrate by a computer-assisted
proof the non-uniqueness of a weak solution
for the unforced 3D Navier-Stokes equations.

These developments further motivate the search
for additional assumptions ensuring uniqueness.
There is an extensive literature
on such results, often called conditional uniqueness theorems.
The best-known conditional uniqueness criterion
is the Ladyzhenskaya-Prodi-Serrin condition, which assumes

(1.3)

u\in L_{q}([0,T],L_{p}(\mathcal{R})),\quad\frac{d}{p}+\frac{2}{q}\leq 1,\quad p\in(d,\infty),\quad q\in[2,\infty).

Under this condition, uniqueness of Hopf–Leray weak solutions was established
by Prodi
[
58
]
and Serrin
[
61
]
, uniqueness and regularity
results were obtained by Ladyzhenskaya
[
44
]
,
and Serrin
[
61
]
.

In

[
32
]
the authors generalised the conditional uniqueness
theorems in Prodi
[
58
]
and Serrin
[
61
]
, and extended them
to stochastic Navier–Stokes equations driven by
Wiener processes when
\mathcal{R}=\mathbb{R}^{d}

for
d\geq 3
.

Motivated by
[
32
]
, our purpose in this paper
is to extend these conditional uniqueness results in three directions:

•

to stochastic Navier–Stokes equations driven simultaneously
by Wiener processes and compensated Poisson random measures;

•

from the whole space to arbitrary Lipschitz domains;

•

from classical Ladyzhenskaya–Prodi–Serrin assumptions to a substantially
weaker Morrey-type condition.

To present a special case of our main result applied to deterministic Navier-Stokes,
we introduce the following restricted Hopf-Leray class.

For any
T>0
and
r\in(2,d]
(
d\geq 3)
a function
u:[0,T]\times\mathcal{R}\to\mathbb{R}^{d}

from
\mathcal{W}
is said to be in the restricted Hopf-Leray class if

can be represented
as
u^{M}+u^{B}
, such that for a constant
\hat{u}=\hat{u}(d,r)
we have

\left(\frac{1}{|B|}\int_{B}{\bf 1}_{\mathcal{R}}|u^{M}_{t}(x)|^{r}\,dx\right)^{1/r}\leq\hat{u}\rho_{B}^{-1}\quad\text{for $dt$-a.e. $t\in[0,T]$},

for any ball
B
of radius
\rho_{B}\leq 1
, and for
\bar{u}_{t}=\esssup_{x\in\mathcal{R}}|u^{B}_{t}(x)|

we have

\int_{0}^{T}|\bar{u}_{t}|^{2}\,dt<\infty.

for the deterministic problem (

)-(
1.2

.

There exists a constant

Assume now that

over the past five decades. The pioneering work
[
8

initiated the mathematical analysis of these equations,
and was followed by a substantial body of work on the existence

[

7

The two-dimensional case is now comparatively well understood.

see, for example,
[
4

52
,
5
,
26
,
56
,
55
]
.
More recently, stochastic Navier–Stokes equations driven by jump noise
have also received considerable attention. Local well-posedness
in
L_{p}
-spaces for equations with Lévy noise was obtained in
[
57
]
,
while existence and uniqueness in an
L_{p}
-framework for the two-dimensional equations
with jump noise were proved in
[
64
]
.
For the three-dimensional equations, local well-posedness in critical

L_{p}
-spaces with multiplicative cylindrical noise on the
whole space was established in
[
37
]
.

Several related directions have also been investigated.
Global existence and uniqueness of mild solutions to the
three-dimensional random vorticity equations for sufficiently
small initial vorticity were proved in
[
6
]
.
Ergodic properties of stochastic Navier–Stokes equations
have been studied extensively, with results in two dimensions obtained in
[
40
,
41
,
21
,
20
,
10
,
51
]

and in three dimensions in
[
18

for randomly forced Navier–Stokes equations on the two-dimensional

[

[

proved well-posedness, regularization,
and blow-up criteria for stochastic Navier–Stokes equations
on the

For a comprehensive account of stochastic fluid dynamics,
we refer the reader to the monograph
[
24

.

While the well-posedness theory for stochastic
Navier–Stokes equations is now well developed in many settings,
considerably less is known about conditional uniqueness results in three dimensions.
The present paper contributes to this direction
by establishing conditional uniqueness for equations
driven simultaneously by Wiener processes and compensated
Poisson martingale measures on general Lipschitz domains.

Proof of Theorem
