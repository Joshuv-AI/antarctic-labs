Agreement for Transfer and Operation
of the State Department Printing Plant
Located at 2430 E Street, Northwest

November 27, 1946

ILLEGIB

The Department of State, the Central Intelligence Group, and the
Government Printing Office hereby agree that the State Department printing
plant located at 2430 I Street, Northwest, shall be transferred to and
operated by the Government Printing Office, under the authority of
44 T7.S.C* 60, subject to the following stipulations*

1* A Policy Committee of three will be established under the
chairmanship of the State Department, and shall consist of
representatives of the State Department, the Central
Intelligence Group, and the Government Printing Office,

This Policy Committee will prescribe regulations dealing
with*

a. Priority of work to be produced

b. Physical and personnel security of the plant

c. Any other matters of substantial interest to the
Department of State, the Central Intelligence
Group, or the Government Printing Office in
Connection with tee operation of the plant.

2. The plant will be retained in its present location at
24K) E Street, Northwest, subject to such space adjust-
ments as may be agreed upon by the Policy Committee.

3. The Government Printing Office will review the findings
and recomsendations of the "Report on Survey of State
Department Printing and Reproduction Plant" dated
November 12, 1946, prepared jointly by representatives
of the State Department, the Bureau of the Budget and
the Government Printing Office. As a result of this
review, specific lists of personnel, material, and
equipment to be transferred will be prepared and
submitted to the Policy Committee for concurrence.

4. It is understood that the personnel, material, and
equipment referred to under item 3 will be transferred
from the State Department to the Government Printing
Office, and that any additional equipment and personnel
required for efficient operation of the plant will be
provided by the Government Printing Office.

5. The Government Printing Office, in operating the plant, will
conform to tho security requirements of either the State
Department or the Central Intelligence Group, whichever may
be the higher with respect to personnel, the operation of the
plant and physical security. Security investigations of
personnel will be conducted by either the State Department

or the Central Intelligence Group as mutually agreed upon
between these two agencies.

6. Except for matters of security and Job priority, the compxete
administrative responsibility for the operation of the plant
will rest with the Government Printing Office.

7. The plant is to be operated by the Government Printing Office
primarily for the accommodation of cap and classified work of
the State Department and the Central Intelligence Group. The
determination as to the production of unclassified work at
this plant or elsewhere shall be at the discretion of the
Government Printing Office. In order to utilise fully the
printing f-.cilities of this plant, the Government Printing
Office, at its discretion, will have the privilege of utilizing
equipment for the work of other Federal activities! provided,
however, that such work shall not interfere with the priority
production of classified or special map materials for the State
Department and the Central Intelligence Group.

8. The Government Printing Office will be reimbursed for work
performed for the State Department and the Central Intelligence
Group, respectively, under the provisions of Section 601 of
the Economy Act (31 U.S.C.686) for such portion of the fiscal
year of 1947 as this plant may be operated by the Government
Printing Office. Thereafter, effective July 1, 1947, the work
produced in this plant shall be charged to object classifica-
tion 06 - Printing and binding.

9. This transfer Bhall become effective on or before March 1,

1947, the specific date to be determined by the Policy Committee.
