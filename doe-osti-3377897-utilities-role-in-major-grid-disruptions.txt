 Outages - Utility Best Practices

Prior to an outage:
• Regular line, pole, and equipment inspections and replacements.
• Vegetation management.
• Blue sky exercises to prepare for emergency response and operations coordination
  scenarios.

During an outage:
• Live outage maps.
• Restoration information with estimated time.
• Multi-channel communication strategies including website, text alerts, social media,
  phone calls, radio, TV, etc.
• Dedicated public information and government affairs staffing.
