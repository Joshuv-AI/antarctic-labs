Structure of the 8 B and 8 Li nuclei and the astrophysical S 17 ​ ( 0 ) S_{17}(0) -factor of the 7 Be( p , γ p,\gamma ) 8 B direct capture process within a three-body model
E.M. Tursunov, D.S. Toshova, S.A. Turakulov

Abstract
The structure of the ground ( J π , T ) = ( 2 + , 1 ) (J^{\pi},T)=(2^{+},1) and excited ( 1 + , 1 ) (1^{+},1) bound states of the 8 B and 8 Li nuclei is studied within the framework of the α + 3 \alpha+^{3} He( 3 H)+ p ⁡ ( n ) p(n) three-body potential cluster model based on the hyperspherical Lagrange-mesh method. The two-body α − 3 \alpha-^{3} He( 3 H), α \alpha -nucleon, and 3 He( 3 H)-nucleon realistic potentials have been applied from the literature. Convergent theoretical estimates for the three-body binding energy and matter radius have been obtained with the maximal hypermomentum K m ​ a ​ x = 22 K_{max}=22 and K m ​ a ​ x = 28 K_{max}=28 for the ground 2 + 2^{+} and excited 1 + 1^{+} bound states, respectively. The ANC values of the virtual transition 8 B → 7 \rightarrow^{7} Be+ p p are estimated self-consistently by matching the overlap integral of the 8 B three-body and the 7 Be two-body wave functions with it’s asymptotics, which is expressed by the Whittaker function. The obtained values are C I = 1 = 0.211 C_{I=1}=0.211 fm -1/2 and C I = 2 = 0.739 C_{I=2}=0.739 fm -1/2 in the spin 1 and spin 2 channels, respectively. For the ANC values of the 8 Li → 7 \rightarrow^{7} Li+ n n virtual transition the estimates C I = 1 = 0.220 C_{I=1}=0.220 fm -1/2 and C I = 2 = 0.774 C_{I=2}=0.774 fm -1/2 are extracted. The ratio C 2 ( 8 B ) / C 2 ( 8 Li ) = 0.912 C^{2}(^{8}{\rm B})/C^{2}(^{8}{\rm Li})=0.912 satisfies an asymptotic relationship implying mirror symmetry of the strong nuclear forces [N.K. Timofeyuk et al., Phys.Rev.Lett. 91 , 232501 (2003)]. For the zero-energy astrophysical factor of the direct nuclear capture process 7 Be(p, OPEN γ ) 8 \gamma)^{8} B an estimate S 17 ​ ( 0 ) = 22.492 ± 0.014 S_{17}(0)=22.492\pm 0.014 eV b was obtained based on the asymptotic theory developed by D. Baye [Phys. Rev. C 62 , 065803 (2000)]. It was found that the most important contribution comes from the spin 2 channel with S 17 ( 2 ) ​ ( 0 ) = 20.838 ± 0.014 S^{(2)}_{17}(0)=20.838\pm 0.014 eV b, while the spin 1 channel yields S 17 ( 1 ) ​ ( 0 ) = 1.654 ± 0.003 S^{(1)}_{17}(0)=1.654\pm 0.003 eV b. The theoretical result for the astrophysical S 17 ​ ( 0 ) S_{17}(0) factor is in a good agreement with the estimate S 17 ​ ( 0 ) = 20.8 ± 0.7 ​ ( th ) ± 1.4 ​ ( exp ) S_{17}(0)=20.8\pm 0.7{\rm(th)}\pm 1.4{\rm(exp)} eV b of the Solar Fusion II, but larger than the recommended value S 17 ​ ( 0 ) = 20.5 ± 0.70 S_{17}(0)=20.5\pm 0.70 eV b of the Solar Fusion III. At the same time, this estimate is very close to the value 22.4 eV b used in the most successful Solar Model BAR2M [W. Yang and Z. Tian, AJ 970 , 38 (2024)].

and matter radius was demonstrated with the maximal hypermomentum

and
K_{max}=28
for the ground (
2^{+}
) and excited
(
1^{+}
) states, correspondingly. For the extraction of the ANC in a self-consistent way, the asymptotics of the overlap integral of
the three-body wave function and the
7

7

fm

The ratio of the squared ANC values of the mirror nuclei
{}^{8}{\rm B}
and
{}^{8}{\rm Li}
estimated within the three-body model,
satisfies an asymptotic relationship from Ref.
nat03
, implying the mirror symmetry of the strong nuclear forces.

A detailed three-body analysis of the
7
Be(p,
\gamma)^{8}
B direct nuclear capture reaction, a critical process for understanding
solar neutrino fluxes and the Solar Metallicity Problem has been carried out. The zero-energy astrophysical factor
