Public Law 118–121: To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 517 Seagaze Drive in Oceanside, California, as the “Charlesetta Reece Allen Post Office Building”.

An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 517 Seagaze Drive in Oceanside, California, as the “Charlesetta Reece Allen Post Office Building”.Nov. 25, 2024[H.R. 5985]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — CHARLESETTA REECE ALLEN POST OFFICE BUILDING.

SUBSECTION (a) — Designation

The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 517 Seagaze Drive in Oceanside, California, shall be known and designated as the “Charlesetta Reece Allen Post Office Building”.

SUBSECTION (b) — References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Charlesetta Reece Allen Post Office Building”.

Approved November 25, 2024.
