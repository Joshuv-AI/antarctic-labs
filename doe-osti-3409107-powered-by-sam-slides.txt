 History

Development began in 2004 by…
• U.S. Department of Energy (DOE)
• National Laboratory of the Rockies
• Sandia National Laboratories.

With the vision of…
• Allowing DOE to identify the strongest R&D opportunities based on analysis of
  the entire system, including costs
• Modeling different types of renewable energy projects in a single third-party,
  validated, and impartial platform
• Facilitating technology comparison by handling performance, costs, and
  financing consistently across technologies.
