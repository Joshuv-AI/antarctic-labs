Adopted by the Security Council at its 3157th meeting, on 8 January 1993 <small>[1]</small>

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Slovak Republic for admission to the United Nations, <small>[2]</small>

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Slovak Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Adopted without vote</small>

<small>[2] S/25046.</small>
