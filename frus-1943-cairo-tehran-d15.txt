Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (29 June 1943, d15)

secret

London, 29 June 1943.

To President from former Naval person personal and secret. Number 336.

I have now received your 297 and I repeat what I said in my 334 that if you and Uncle J. can fix a meeting together I should no longer deprecate it. On the contrary in view of his attitude I think it important that this contact should be established.

I should be very glad to arrange for a meeting between us and our staffs about the end of August in Quebec which I am sure MacKenzie King would welcome. Later on I will put the point to him.

Many thanks for your very full message.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

[1] Channel of transmission not indicated.
