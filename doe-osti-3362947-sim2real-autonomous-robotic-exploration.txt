 Autonomous robotics facilitate the exploration of low
 accessibility or hazardous environments. However, training
 robots in these physical environments is often infeasible due to
 safety concerns, cost and complexity. To overcome this,
 platforms like Nvidia Isaac help create high-fidelity virtual
 environments for robot simulation. This project demonstrates
 an end-to-end sim2real pipeline for a training a quadruped to
 autonomously explore a room. The proposed framework
 further paves the way for deployment in more complex A reinforcement learning policy was trained using IsaacLab to learn
 scenarios. the joint position targets necessary for a quadruped to walk in
 specified directions. IsaacLab enables the simultaneous training of

  Future Work
  Temperature detection and location: simulating heat sensors and training policies to walk towards “hot” or “cold” spots in a room
  Custom robot simulation: Extending the architecture to train crawler and wheeled robots for navigation tasks
  Dynamic trajectory: Extending the current navigation task to simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) of new environments
