YUGOSLAVIA: UNRRA supply distribution questioned--Embassy
Belgrade has received the following information from a high
UNRRA official in Yugoslavia: (a) the local UNRRA Mission has.
granted a Yugoslav Government request to transfer 800 tons of
wheat to the Yugoslav Army, contrary to UNRRA policy ‘which
forbids the distribution of food to armed forces; (b) stocks of
canned milk are accumulating in warehouses because of improper
distribution and prohibitively high retail prices; (c) all medical
and pharmaceutical supplies are distributed by a Jugoslav Govern-_
ment agency at a profit, and the prices of some of these supplies
are so high that they are, generally unobtainable by the public; and >
(d) supplies of cotton and needles for hand sewing apparently are
being used by the Yugoslavs to produce material for military
uniforms.
