The Presidents Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the Soviet Foreign Commissar (Molotov)[1]

[Cairo,] 2 December 1943.

I did so much enjoy our conferences together[2] and only hope that they may be continued. I am sure that the meeting between Marshal Stalin and the President has done infinite good in bringing our two countries closer together in War and in Peace. I do hope you have reached home safely. With my warmest regards.

Harry Hopkins

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House Map Room, via military channels, and forwarded by the Map Room to the Embassy at Moscow, via Navy channels.
[2] At Tehran.
