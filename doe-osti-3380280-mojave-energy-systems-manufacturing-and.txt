 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 This project aims to demonstrate all-electric liquid desiccant dehumidification product lines,
 developed by Mojave Energy Systems, Inc, at different building sites in the US. NLR will
 complement these field tests by leading a lab test of one unit, measuring detailed performance
 over several conditions.

 CRADA benefit to DOE, Participant, and US Taxpayer:

 • Assists laboratory in achieving programmatic scope
 • Uses the laboratory’s core competencies

 Task 2: Laboratory Test. The contractor will test one of the units from Task 1, over twenty
 operating points.

 NLR completed testing on the unit shown in Figure 1. The test procedure and results for the 26
 operating points are presented in a journal publication [1]. These results showed that the Mojave
 system delivered dehumidification with 25% less input electric power than a system not
 enhanced with liquid desiccant, and delivered air that was ~9 °C warmer. The warmer air
 eliminates the overcooling of commercial buildings in humid climates.

 Task 3: Update EnergyPlus models to match field test. The contractor will update existing
 EnergyPlus models based on lab and field test data.

 The model of the unit, for use in the EnergyPlus program, was updated based on the
 experiments. The model predicted the moisture removal efficiency of the unit with a mean
 absolute error of 6.9%. The full comparison of the unit’s performance with this model can be
 found in the same journal publication [1].
