Correction: Faunal exploitation at the elephant hunting site of Lehringen, Germany, 125,000 years ago
Authors: Ivo Verheijen, Gianpiero Di Maida, Gabriele Russo, Thomas Terberger
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-42538-4, published online 24 March 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in the Legend of Figure 1, where the name of author Gianpiero Di Maida was incorrectly given as Gianiero Di Maida.

Furthermore, the Legend’s description did not correspond to the correct colors of the Figure dots that represent the different chronological time periods.

As a result,

“Figure 1. Location of the site of Lehringen on a geographic map of Europe, showing all archaeological sites mentioned in this study. The colored dots represent the different chronological time periods: blue: MIS 9–11; red: MIS 7, green: MIS 5; yellow MIS 4−3. Map created by Gianiero Di Maida using QGIS, version 3.42.”

now reads:

“Figure 1. Location of the site of Lehringen on a geographic map of Europe, showing all archaeological sites mentioned in this study. The colored dots represent the different chronological time periods: orange: MIS 9–11; blue: MIS 7, purple: MIS 5; yellow MIS 4−3. Map created by Gianpiero Di Maida using QGIS, version 3.42.

The original Article has been corrected.
