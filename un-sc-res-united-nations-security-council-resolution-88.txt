Adopted by the Security Council at its 520th meeting, by 8 votes to 2 (China, Cuba) with 1 abstention (Egypt), on 8 November 1950

The Security Council,

Decides to invite, in accordance with rule 39 of the provisional rules of procedure, a representative of the Central People's Government of the People's Republic of China to be present during discussion by the Council of the special report of the United Nations Command in Korea.

1950

fr:Résolution 88 du Conseil de sécurité des Nations unies
