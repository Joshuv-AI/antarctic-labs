 IV. CONCLUSION
 Based on these benchmark simulations of the resistive wall
 instability in the absence of external magnetic focusing, one
 might expect measurable displacement of the last pulse in a
 train from the first pulse in a train at the exit of the MPTL. This
 displacement might approach 2 mm for closely spaced pulses
 in a 2-us train if the MPTL beam pipe is made from stainless
 steel. However, this can be reduced by about a factor of four (4)
 by using aluminum alloy beam pipe, as has been the practice
 for the DARHT-II DST, and has been recommended for the
 Scorpius DST.
