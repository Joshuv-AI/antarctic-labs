President Roosevelt to Marshal Stalin[1] (26 November 1943, d328)

secret operational priority

[Cairo,] 26 November 1943.

Thank you much for your message of November 23rd informing me of your intention to reach Teheran on the 28th or 29th.[2] I expect to reach there on the 27th. It will be good to see you.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by Harriman to Spalding at Moscow, via Navy channels, for delivery to Stalin.
[2] See ante, p. 385.
