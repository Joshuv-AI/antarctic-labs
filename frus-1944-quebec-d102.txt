The Secretary of State to the President (30 August 1944, d102)

[Washington,] August 30, 1944.

Memorandum for the President

Subject: Argentina

I attach hereto a draft of telegram which I would suggest that you send to the Prime Minister, if you agree.[1]

C[ordell] H[ull]
