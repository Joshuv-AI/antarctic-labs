The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President[1] (1 December 1943, d407)

[Tehran, December 1, 1943.]

Mr. President: It is 8.10.

Do you wish at dinner to introduce the method of our occupation of Germany after her collapse.

Harry

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] This message, which is handwritten, was presumably passed by Hopkins to Roosevelt near the end of the tripartite political meeting, December 1, 1943, ante, p. 596.
