No. 544 The Secretary of State to the Secretary of War (Stimson)[1]

[On Board the U. S. S. “Augusta”,] 12 July 1945.

I have received your No. 95[2] and discussed it with the President. As the question is a complicated one, I suggest we talk it over at Terminal.

Byrnes

Source: Truman Papers: Telegram
