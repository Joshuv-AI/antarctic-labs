The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the Secretary of State

[Washington,] September 5, 1944.

My Dear Mr. Secretary: With minor reservations about language which do not affect the intent of the document, “Suggested Recommendations on Treatment of Germany from the Cabinet Committee for the President”,[1] I approve of it.

If there be agreement on policies, then it becomes of the utmost importance for the proper Government Officials to indicate how the policies in this document are to be implemented.

Sincerely yours,

Harry L. Hopkins
