FROM: YPO Headquarters

REFERENCE: Taping of Education Programs

As you probably know, the Young Presidents' Organization, Inc.
is an educational association of presidents of corporations in
45 countries around the world. The purpose of the Organization
is to help its members become better presidents through education
and idea exchange. Our education program takes many forms-- chap ter
meetings, seminars, area conferences, universities- -in which our
members participate actively. Many of these education sessions
include the participation of an authoritative speaker.

To heighten the educational value of these sessions, a tape program,
exclusively for the use of current and past YPO members, has been
established.

Members who attend the sessions order the cassettes for the added
take-home value they provide. Members unable to attend may also
order cassettes. In addition, we maintain a complete tape library
at Headquarters. Those in charge of selecting resources for future
YPO education events often study the recordings, which may generate
further YPO engagements for the resource.

We hope that you will participate in this take-home facet of YPO
education. We would like to stress that taped material is never
released, under any circumstances, other than to members and ex-
members of the Organization. Also note that YPO produces "live"
classroom transcriptions, not studio recordings; therefore, the YPO
cassettes should not compete with any tapes produced commercially.

If your session is taped and released to the membership, you will
receive a complimentary copy.

Please initial and sign both copies of the attached tape release,
and return one copy to the address above. Keep the other for your
files. 1
