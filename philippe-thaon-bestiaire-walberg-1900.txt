LE BESTIAIRE DE PHILIPPE DE THAON
Philippe de Thaon (early 12th century)
========================================================================
PROVENANCE NOTE (Tower of Babel staging): Old French verse bestiary, after the
critical edition by Emmanuel Walberg (Paris/Lund, 1900). Public domain scan
(Internet Archive). The work is complete: the prologue (dedicated to Queen
Aaliz) and all the beast chapters through the lapidary section, ending at
verse 3190. The earliest bestiary in a vernacular language.
========================================================================
