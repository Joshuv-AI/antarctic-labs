Dear Stan;-

Thanks very much indeed for your warm and thoughtful
letter of 23 August, and for your earlier phone call when you
tracked me down at my vacation camping retreat. I am pleased
and flattered that you wanted to check with me before deciding
on the D/NFAC appointment. As I have said all along, the only
reward that I seek for my work with you is an assurance that
I am serving some useful purpose. You have always given me
this assurance, which means that I am always ready to serve
again .

On your appointment of Bruce Clarke to the D/NFAC
position, I feel quite comfortable with that — as I said to you
on the phone. More importantly, I feel certain that almost
everyone within NFAC will feel comfortable with Bruce's appoint-
ment, and this v/ill give a major shot in the arm to NFAC morale.
If everyone had been asked to name a perfect candidate for the
job, Bruce's name might not have been prominently mentioned.

But, in the real world in which we live, Bruce is a very good
illustration of Herb Simon's "satif icing" theory — a candidate
who in fact satisfies almost everybody. Well done!

On other items covered in your 23 August letter,

I strongly support all of your goals even if I retain a reserva-
