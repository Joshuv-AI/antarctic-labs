President Roosevelt to Marshal Stalin[1] (14 October 1943, d42)

secret operational priority

[Washington,] 14 October 1943.

Personal and secret from President Roosevelt to Marshal Stalin.

The Secretary of State and his Staff are well on their way to Moscow but it seems doubtful at this time that they will be able to get there before the seventeenth. I will let you know of their progress.

I am much disturbed in regard to the location of the other meeting but I will send you this problem in another message.[2]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attach, Moscow, via Navy channels.
[2] Infra.
