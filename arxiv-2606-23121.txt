Connecting vertex sets to walls
Henning Bruhn, Felix Joos, Arthur Ulmer

Abstract
Menger’s theorem on A A – B B -paths and Gallai’s theorem on A A -paths are among the most useful results in structural graph theory. Many variants and extensions are known. We add to this line of research and prove results that relate the maximal number of vertex-disjoint paths between vertex sets and a wall to the minimum number of vertices meeting all these paths. We also include types of paths that start and end in the wall.

P_{j}
contains all the endvertices in

of all the paths in
\mathcal{P}_{j}
.
(That is, the paths

,

Lemma 13
.

Let
W
be a wall.
Let
\mathcal{P}=\{\mathcal{P}_{1},\ldots,\mathcal{P}_{n}\}
be a set such that for every
i\in[n]
,
the set
\mathcal{P}_{i}
is either an in-series linkage of

or an

–

-connector for some vertex set
A_{i}
.
If
|\mathcal{P}_{i}|\geq 2ns
for every

,
then there are subsets
\mathcal{P}^{\prime}_{i}\subseteq\mathcal{P}_{i}

of size
s
for every
i\in[n]
such that
\{\mathcal{P}^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,\mathcal{P}^{\prime}_{n}\}

is clean.

Proof.

For each
i\in[n]
, let
P_{i}
be a subpath of the top row of
W
that contains all
the endvertices in
W
of the paths in
\mathcal{P}_{i}
. Split
P_{i}
into
2n
subpaths

such that each

contains the endvertices in

of exactly
s
of the paths in
\mathcal{P}_{i}
.
(If
\mathcal{P}_{i}
is a linkage, this is possible because it is then an in-series linkage.)
Note that we are done if we can pick a
j_{i}
for each
i\in[n]
such that all the paths

P_{1,j_{1}},\ldots,P_{n,j_{n}}

are pairwise disjoint; but this can be done inductively:
Pick

such that

does not properly contain some other path

;
then

is disjoint from at least

Delete all the paths that intersect

and iterate.
∎

Theorem 16
.

For all positive integers
n,s^{\prime},t^{\prime}
,
there are integers
t=t(n,s^{\prime},t^{\prime})
and
f=f(n,s^{\prime})
such that the following holds.
If
\mathcal{A}_{1},\mathcal{A}_{2}
are sets of at most
n
vertex sets,
and if
W
is a wall of size at least
t
, then there is a subwall
W^{\prime}
of size at least
t^{\prime}

with
B
as its set of branch vertices and subsets
\mathcal{A}_{1}^{\prime}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{1}
and

\mathcal{A}_{2}^{\prime}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{2}
such that

(i)

there is a vertex set
X
of size at most
f
such that
X
meets every
A
–
B
-path for
A\in\mathcal{A}_{1}^{\prime}

and every
B
–
A
–
B
-path for
A\in\mathcal{A}_{2}^{\prime}
, and such that

is disjoint from

;

(ii)

there is an
(\mathcal{A}_{1}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{1}^{\prime})
–
W^{\prime}
-connector
\mathcal{C}
of size at least
s^{\prime}
,
and an in-series
(\mathcal{A}_{2}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{2}^{\prime})
–linkage
\mathcal{L}
of
W^{\prime}
of size at least
s^{\prime}

such that
\mathcal{C}\cup\mathcal{L}
is clean and all paths in
\mathcal{C}\cup\mathcal{L}

are pairwise disjoint.

Note that we view the empty set as clean, so that (ii) also makes sense if
\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{L}=\emptyset
.
Moreover, observe that if
\mathcal{A}^{\prime}_{1}=\emptyset
and
\mathcal{A}^{\prime}_{2}=\emptyset
, then the empty set will do as

.

Proof.

Let

t=t(n,s^{\prime},t^{\prime})=t_{\ref{monsterlem}}(n,s^{\prime},t^{\prime})\cdot\left(3f_{\ref{monsterlem}}(n,s^{\prime})\right)^{n}

and

f=f(n,s^{\prime})=2n\cdot f_{\ref{monsterlem}}(n,s^{\prime}),

where
t_{\ref{monsterlem}}
and
f_{\ref{monsterlem}}
are the respective functions from Lemma
15
.

Let
G
be a graph with a wall
W
of size at least
t
. We start with
\mathcal{A}_{i}^{0}=\emptyset
for
i\in[2]
,
X_{0}=\emptyset
and
W_{0}=W
.
We will iteratively find subwalls
W_{j}
of
W
, vertex sets
X_{j}
and subsets
\mathcal{A}_{1}^{j}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{1}

and
\mathcal{A}_{2}^{j}\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{2}
, and
we will write
B_{j}
for the
set of branch vertices of
W_{j}
.
To generate these sets,
we iteratively apply Lemma
15
to
G-X_{j}
,
W_{j}
,
\mathcal{A}_{1}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{1}^{j}
and
\mathcal{A}_{2}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{2}^{j}

until outcome (iii) of Lemma
15
occurs, or until
\mathcal{A}_{1}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{1}^{j}=\mathcal{A}_{2}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{2}^{j}=\emptyset
.
In this process, we will guarantee that, in the
j
-th iteration, the following conditions are satisfied:

(a)

the set

has size

and meets every

–

-path for

and every

–

–

(b)

of

and is disjoint from

; and

(c)

.

As the process ends at the latest when
\mathcal{A}_{1}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{1}^{j}
and
\mathcal{A}_{2}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{2}^{j}
become empty,
the process will end after at most
2n

For

, the above conditions are true, so let

.
Applying Lemma
15
to

,
W_{j-1}
,

\mathcal{A}_{1}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{1}^{j-1}
,
\mathcal{A}_{2}\setminus\mathcal{A}_{2}^{j-1}
,

and

yields one of the three outcomes (i), (ii), and (iii) of the lemma.
Note that the size of

is large enough in any iteration.

First, assume that outcome (i) or (ii) of Lemma

That is, there is a set

–

,
or

from

(c)

and
(a)
