Public Law 119–72: To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the conditions under which the Secretary of Veterans Affairs is required to redevelop the individualized vocational rehabilitation plan for a veteran, and for other purposes.

An Act To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the conditions under which the Secretary of Veterans Affairs is required to redevelop the individualized vocational rehabilitation plan for a veteran, and for other purposes.Jan. 20, 2026[H.R. 4446]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Focused Assistance and Skills Training for Veterans’ Employment and Transition Success Act” or the “FAST VETS Act”.

SEC. 2. — CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH SECRETARY OF VETERANS AFFAIRS SHALL REDEVELOP INDIVIDUALIZED VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION PLAN.

PARAGRAPH (1)

by striking “The Secretary shall review” and inserting “(1) The Secretary shall review”;

PARAGRAPH (2)

by striking the second sentence; and

PARAGRAPH (3)

by adding at the end the following new paragraph:Determinations.On the basis of such review, the Secretary shall—redevelop such plan with such veteran if the Secretary determines the achievement of the long-range rehabilitation goals for such veteran is—no longer feasible due to changes in the employment handicap of the veteran; and likelier under a different plan formulated under subsection (a); or139 STAT. 2002 disapprove redevelopment of such plan if the Secretary determines such redevelopment is not appropriate.” .

Approved January 20, 2026.
