7 Numerical Time Integration

Now that all the time derivatives of the variables (s, C, V ) have been defined, any numerical
integration method such as 4th order Runge–Kutta may be used on the 21-element vector.
 This model is called “first order transport” because it is first order in space. The change in
shape of the ellipsoid is represented by linear transformations (matrices) and no higher-order
distortions are taken into account.
