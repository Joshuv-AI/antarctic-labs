8. Scheduling of Estimates

Approved a recommendation of the Board of National Estimates
that the present estimates schedule (IAC-D-1/23, 1 April 1958) be
changed to allow for the immediate initiation of a Polish estimate to
be completed in the Third Quarter of 1958, with the understanding
that the target date for the East German estimate would be postponed
until the Fourth Quarter.

9. Report by General Schow

25X1

General Schow reported to the members on impressions gained
^uringjhis recent trip to certain Central and South American countries.

General Schow also discussed with the members certain
questions related to a proposed reduction in the number of military
attaches, with special reference to the possible effect on reporting
from Latin American countries.
