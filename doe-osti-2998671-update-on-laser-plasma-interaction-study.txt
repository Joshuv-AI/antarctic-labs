Milestone report-out overview
Milestone:
• "Execute shot series probing LPI (laser-plasma interaction) risks in a scale 1.09 N221204-like
  design delivering up to 2 MJ laser energy and provide mitigation/design recommendations as
  appropriate. Compare measurements to simulations and/or existing data extrapolations."
• "Present peer-reviewed findings at an ICF seminar and to external community." (today)
Why:
• De-risk EYC/future facility target design
• Inform future facility laser architecture choices
What was done:
• Experiments
• Simulations (modeling, physics understanding)
• Experimental planning for the future (EYC, Next Gen)

Conclusions

• Physics of Inner-Cone SBS backscatter is determined by the expanding capsule ablator meeting the
  Au bubble with He in between.
• pF3D simulations based on ‘traditional’ single-species hydro (SSH) strongly over-estimate amplitude
  and duration of SBS backscatter.
• Multi-species hydro allows the Au bubble and He ions to interpenetrate causing Landau-damping of
  ion-acoustic waves – reducing SBS backscatter, similar to Au-Boron or Ta2O5
• Inner-Cone SBS backscatter drops when incoming beams are absorbed / refracted into Au bubble
• Observe similar conditions in various hohlraums with 0.3mg/cc He fill gas density
