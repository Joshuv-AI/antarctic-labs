Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt (14 September 1944, d69)

Quebec, September 14, 1944.

My Dear Friend, I understand that we are meeting at 11:30[1] about Stage 2. Dick Law arrived here late last night, and I wonder whether I might bring him at 11 o’clock with or without Anthony,[2] to discuss the application of U.N.R.R.A. to Italy. He tells me that a compromise proposal would get through whereby 50 million dollars of U.N.R.R.A. would be available for Italy. I consider this should be an essential part of our friendly gesture to Italy.

If agreeable then, I will turn up at 11.[3]

Yours always,

W[inston S. Churchill]

Source: Roosevelt Papers

Notes
[1] See post, p. 342.
[2] Eden.
[3] No record of the discussion at the 11 o’clock meeting between Roosevelt and Churchill on September 14 has been found.
