 Challenges – Project Specificity

  – Custom ZeroMQ "ESIF Relay" tool

Challenges – ZeroMQ

 Challenges – ZeroMQ

 • Issues
 – Single VM solution resulted in frequent downtimes for patching
 – Custom firewall setup to allow bridging between networks
                         – Hard to share data with other apps, especially apps on cloud     NREL | 5

Challenges – High Complexity

 Challenges – High Complexity

• Solution #2
  – Kafka brokers for streaming
 • Kafka is very powerful, but was overkill
 and struggled to get researcher buy-in
 • Complicated to use, resulting in the
 need for custom plugin development
 and expensive connector tools like
 StreamSets
 • Offsets, partitions, and replication were
 challenging to explain to researchers
 who were used to distinct streaming vs.
 recording workflows
 NREL ESIF Data Center Power Infrastructure

Challenges – Kafka

 Challenges – Kafka

 • Issues
 – Partitioning and consumer offsets created lots of confusion
 – Kafka hosting is complicated and expensive, especially with mirrored data
 – Duplicating Kafka messages reliably can be challenging

Convergence

 Convergence

• Stratus and DTSW teams both looking for
  solutions to this issue on behalf of CSC
• Identified MQTT as an enabling technology
 – MQTT had positive aspects of ZeroMQ and
 Kafka and few downsides
 – Natively supported by ICS equipment and
 meters, on-prem systems, and cloud IoT
 solutions
• Combined efforts to develop a hybrid
  approach leveraging on-premise systems and
  cloud IoT services NREL Energy Systems Integration Facility

 Real Time IoT Data Streaming

Where We Are Now

 Where We Are Now

 • Prototype phase
 • Two servers procured, configured, and installed
 – NVIDIA Ampere A2 GPU
 – AMD Epyc 24 core / 48 thread CPU
 – Fast SSD storage
 – Ubuntu
 • Collaborated closely with NREL ITS and ESIF Operations to verify
 that OS baseline and network security setup is sustainable and
 scalable to new installs
 • Migrated existing hydrogen IoT data streams as proof-of-concept
