Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 786th meeting, on 5 September 1957

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Federation of Malaya for membership in the United Nations, <small>[1]</small>

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Federation of Malaya be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Ibid., Supplement for July, August and September 1957, document S/3872.</small>
