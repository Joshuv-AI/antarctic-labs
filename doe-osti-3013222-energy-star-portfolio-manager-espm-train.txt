Using ESPM Data to Support NORTHH Retrofits
 How We Use ESPM in NORTHH:
 • We use ESPM to track how much
 energy NORTHH buildings use
 • ESPM allows us to measure
 building performance pre- and
 post-retrofit
 • Quantifies energy results to allow
 easy communication to funders
 How We Help Our Auditors:
 • ESPM allows buildings to be
 Energy Use Intensity Comparison Across NORTHH Cohort Buildings shared, allowing us to share
 building data and energy use with
 our auditor, streamlining this
 crucial step.

Property Creation in ESPM

Property Creation

Once logged in and on the
home page, select “Add a
Property” to begin.

                            NREL | 9

Property Creation

 Begin by selecting the
 Property Type that best fits
 the use of your property.

 Next simply record the
 number of buildings on the
 property and the
 construction status.

                                           NREL | 10

Property Creation

 Next, enter the following:

 - Property Name
 - Property Address
 - Year Built
 - Gross Floor Area
 - Occupancy Percentage

 All fields with red asterisks must
 be filled with accurate
 information.

 These values will inform the
 metrics that ESPM will produce
 after energy data is entered.

                                                   NREL | 11

Entering Energy Data into ESPM

Energy Type Selection

 Here, select the type of
 energy that you would like
 to report usage for.

 If the energy type that you
 would like to enter is not
 listed here, you may enter
 it at the bottom of the
 screen under “Other.”

                                              NREL | 13

Energy Meter Input

 To create an energy meter, the
 following information is needed:

 - The name of the meter
 - The units of the meter
 - The earliest bill available (date
 meter became active)
 - Custom meter ID

 Next, enter the start and end date
 of the bill, the usage, and the total
 cost.

                                                   NREL | 14

 Energy Meter Creation

Following the accurate entering of bills and cost data, ESPM automatically produces the graph shown above on the left, displaying the energy
use by month for each meter.

ESPM also offers users the opportunity to explore the entries for each meter, organized by year. Users can view one year of entries at a time
or all years at once.

Energy Metrics Summary

 After selecting your metrics
 (up to 7), ESPM produces a
 metrics summary on the home
 page of your building profile.

 This summary established a
 baseline and current time
 period (based on your earliest
 and most recent utility entry).

Other Features of ESPM

Property Customization

 ESPM allows for buildings to be
 customized based on the needs
 and preferences of each user.

 Users can add the following:
 ❖ Building Notes
 ❖ Pictures
 ❖ Building Files (deeds, etc.)
 ❖ Metric Goals (lower energy
 use)
 ❖ Project Tracking

                                                   NREL | 21

 Property Sharing

ESPM allows for buildings to be shared between users, as shown above. This feature allows for
collaboration within and beyond the user’s organization.

ESPM Reporting

ESPM can automatically
produce reports based on the
utility entries that the user
inputs into the system.

 ESPM Reporting

The reports that ESPM generates can include
multiple properties. Each report consists of
the metrics that the user selects (up to 50).
