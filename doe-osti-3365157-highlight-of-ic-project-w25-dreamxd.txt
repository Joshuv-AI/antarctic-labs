 Highlight of IC project: w25_dreamxd (PI: Xiangrong Fu)

 (left) The Earth’s radiation belts are donut-shaped regions containing MeV electrons (color contour for density) trapped
 by the magnetic field (white curves). We use DREAMxD code to model their dynamics. (right) We develop a new method
 to accelerate a key piece of the code – diffusion coefficient calculation [1]. Comparing the compute time in node hours
 required by the standard approach to the compute time required for the fast method, it shows that the new method can be
 100x faster for a large problem size.

 [1] Gregory S. Cunningham et al. “A fast algorithm to calculate quasilinear diffusion coefficients
LA-UR-26- for use in space physics”. submitted to Journal of Geophysical Research: Space Physics. 2026.
