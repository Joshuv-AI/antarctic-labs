NF) 1. THERE 1S A GROWING SUSPICION AMONG ARMED FORCES OF LIBERIA

(AFL) SERVICEMEN AND SENIOR GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS INCLUDING LIBERIAN
PRESIDENT SAMUEL K. ((DOE)) THAT EXECUTIVE MANSION GUARD (EMG) COMMANDER
MAJOR GENERAL CHARLES ((JULUE)) WAS BEHIND THE KILLING OF THE BUCHANAN
COMMANDER LIEUTENANT COLONEL SAMUEL ((APPLETON)). APPLETON WAS
REPORTEDLY KILLED ON 29 MAY 1990 WHEN REBELS ATTACKED THE PORT CITY OF
BUCHANAN AFTER THE AFL HAD RETAKEN IT DURING THE WEEKEND OF 26-27 MAY.

I NF) 2. APPLETON WAS HIGHLY RESPECTED BY THE SOLDIERS IN HIS UNIT
BECAUSE OF HIS WILLINGNESS TO LEAD HIS MEN INTO BATTLE AND HIS CONCERN FOR
THEIR WELFARE. APPLETON'S UNIT WAS COMPRISED OF MEMBERS OF THE KRAHN,
KRU, AND GREBO ETHNIC GROUPS. COMMENT: OTHER REPORTING INDICATES
THAT APPLETON WAS INDEED A CAPABLE MILITARY COMMANDER WHO EARNED THE
RESPECT OF HIS SOLDIERS. HOWEVER, APPLETON DID NOT MAINTAIN CLOSE CONTROL
OVER HIS SOLDIERS WHO WERE FREQUENTLY ACCUSED OF HARASSING AND ABUSING
THE LOCAL CITIZENS OF BUCHANAN. THESE ABUSES, IN PART, SUBSEQUENTLY LED TO
CIVILIAN SUPPORT FOR THE REBELS. APPLETON WAS ALSO REPORTED TO BE VERY
CORRUPT AND A HEAVY DRINKER.)

NF) 3. THE LOCAL NEWPAPERS GAVE MUCH OF THE CREDIT TO JULUE WHEN
REPORTING THE INITIAL AFL RECAPTURE OF BUCHANAN ON 26 MAY 1990. UPON
LEARNING OF THE PRESS REPORTS, APPLETON'S UNIT BECAME ANGRY AND LATER
CONFRONTED JULUE. JULUE DENIED ACCEPTING CREDIT FOR THE VICTORY AND TOLD
THE SOLDIERS THAT HE DID NOT HAVE ANY CONTROL OVER THE NEWS MEDIA. THE
SOLDIERS QUESTIONED JULUE'S SINCERITY AND HIS CONCERN FOR THEIR WELFARE.
THE SOLDIERS ARGUED THAT NEITHER JULUE NOR OTHER SENIOR MILITARY OFFICERS
FOUGHT IN THE BATTLES AGAINST THE REBELS AND FREQUENTLY RETURNED TO THE
SAFETY OF MONROVIA AT NIGHT. THE SOLDIERS REFUSED TO LISTEN TO JULUE WHO
THEN LEFT THE SCENE. SEVERAL OF JULUE'S EMG SOLDIERS, WHO HAD BEEN
ASSIGNED TO APPLETON'S UNIT, LATER TOLD HIM THAT APPLETON WAS UNDERMINING
HIS AUTHORITY AND SOUGHT TO REPLACE HIM AS THE TOP MILITARY LEADER. JULUE
CONFRONTED APPLETON AND ACCUSED HIM OF BECOMING TOO AMBITIOUS.
APPLETON WAS VISIBLY SHAKEN, BUT RESPONDED THAT HE WAS NOT INTERESTED IN
TAKING JULUE’S JOB AND ONLY WANTED TO DEFEAT THE REBELS. JULUE DID NOT
ARGUE THE MATTER AND DEPARTED.

| NF) 4. FOLLOWING THE INCIDENT JULUE AND COLONEL DAVID ((NIMELY))
RETURNED TO MONROVIA AND LATER MET WITH DOE WHO HAD INQUIRED ABOUT THE
CONFRONTATION BETWEEN APPLETON AND JULUE. JULUE TOLD DOE THEY HAD
RESOLVED THEIR DIFFERENCES AND WERE STILL ON GOOD TERMS. APPLETON WAS
REPORTEDLY KILLED FOLLOWING ANOTHER REBEL ATTACK ON BUCHANAN. SEVERAL
SOLDIERS REPORTED SEEING APPLETON ENTER A BUILDING WITH TWO BODYGUARDS
TO MAKE A TELEPHONE CALL. OTHER SOLDIERS CONTEND THAT THE TWO
BODYGUARDS WERE JULUE'S MEN WHO KILLED APPLETON AND BLAMED HIS DEATH ON
THE REBELS. AFTER LEARNING OF APPLETON'S DEATH, DOE BECAME UPSET AND
REMARKED OFFHANDEDLY THAT JULUE PROBABLY HAD MORE TO DO WITH
APPLETON'S DEATH THAN THE REBELS. (= ~~ COMMENT: THERE IS NO SOLID
EVIDENCE THAT JULUE ORDERED THE KILLING OF APPLETON; HOWEVER, BASED ON
JULUE'S PAST ACTIONS IT WOULD NOT BE SURPRISING FOR HIM TO ELIMINATE ANYONE
SUSPECTED OF BEING A THREAT TO HIM. IN ADDITION, MANY PEOPLE FEAR JULUE AND
SUSPECT THAT HE IS ALSO BEHIND THE RECENT RASH OF KILLINGS OF MEMBERS OF
THE GIO AND MANO ETHNIC GROUPS. IT IS ALSO POSSIBLE THAT IF DOE SHOULD
DECIDE TO STEP DOWN, JULUE MAY MOVE TO EITHER ELIMINATE OR REPLACE DOE AS
THE COUNTRY'S LEADER.)
