SIGNIFICANT ITEMS

Beginning on 22 June, and running for ten weeks, L ETS Trill have
the following three courses to be pro vided by Professor I
Director, Institute of Asian Affairs . [ I :

Basic Country Survey - Japan
Basic Country Survey - China
U. S. Foreigh Policy in the Far East

The Japan and China courses will meet for three two-hour sessions a
week. The Foreign Policy course will meet for one two-hour session
a week. The Japan and China courses are the first two offerings in
the new Basic Area Training Program.

Objectives of the course are to provide a broad general intro-
duction to assist the intelligence officer develop an ability to ap-
preciate, understand and interpret facts, events, attitudes and trends
in terms of the cultural totality that provides their environment, and
to anticipate and interpret responses from within the area to external
political, economic, psychological and other stimuli.

The course will provide an introduction to the culture, to the
political, economic and social institutions, to the geography, climate
and resources, to the origins and nature of the people, to the art,
religion, philosophy and religion, and to other aspects of the civili-
zation that need to be considered by any persons concerned with the
production, evaluation and use of intelligence. Course will also in-
clude a review of the significant U. S. policies and objectives in the
area, and of the policies of the area toward the U. S.
