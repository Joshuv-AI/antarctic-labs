Lord Lyons to Mr. Seward.

Washington, February 6, 1863.

Sir: I have this afternoon had the honor to receive your note of yesterday’s date respecting the blockade of the port of Galveston, in Texas.

In acknowledging the receipt of your communication, I take the opportunity of repeating to you the assurance of the high consideration with which I am, sir, your most obedient, humble servant,

LYONS.

Hon. William H. Seward, Secretary of State.
