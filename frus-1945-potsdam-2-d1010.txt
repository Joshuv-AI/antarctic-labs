No. 1010 The Commander in Chief, United States Fleet, and Chief of Naval Operations (King) to the Navy Department[1]

confidential routine

Babelsberg, 26 July 1945.

Victory 358. CominCh sends serial 25 to CominCh and CNO Admin for action. Action ad[dress]ee pass for info to ComNavEu and ComNavForGermany.

In consultation with Commodore Moran select officer qualified to sit on Capcraft Commission to select tugs suitable for United States purposes. Have selected officer report to ComNavEu for temporary duty on Capcraft Commission.

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] This message resulted from an agreement reached in Anglo-American conversations at Babelsberg. See ante, p. 299.
