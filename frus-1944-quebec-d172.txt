Editorial Note (Quebec 1944, Document 172)

According to Ehrman, p. 517, Churchill, on the day of his arrival at Quebec, spoke to Leahy about the participation of the British fleet in the Pacific war and “was told that its offer had been accepted”. For further discussion of this subject at the meeting of the Combined Chiefs of Staff with Roosevelt and Churchill on September 13, 1944, see post, p. 315.
