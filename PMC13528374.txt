Embracing global views to disseminate insights — A development vision for Eco-Environment & Health
Authors: Hua Jiang
Published: 2026

We are witnessing a pivotal moment in global environmental stewardship—one marked by deep change and systemic reshaping. As climate change, biodiversity loss, environmental pollution, and health risks converge, humanity has never been more aware that we share only one Earth and a common destiny with nature. Guided by the vision of a community with a shared future for humanity, advancing eco-civilization through green, low-carbon, and circular development not only meets the aspirations of people worldwide for a healthy and pleasant environment, but also fosters more equitable, resilient, and sustainable development-enhancing ecological integrity, human well-being, and planetary health for all. In keeping with this philosophy, Eco-Environment & Health (EEH) aligns itself with the mission of accelerating the development of world-class scientific and technological journals and building internationally influential platforms for scientific literature and data. Driven by the responsibility to serve global ecological sustainability and advance the interdisciplinary development of ecology, environment, and health, EEH is dedicated to presenting environmental governance experiences on the international academic stage, advancing the global discourse on green development, and contributing scientific insights and actionable solutions.

Building an internationally leading journal is by no means a simple accumulation of metrics. Its essence lies in continuously raising valuable academic questions, establishing robust methodological systems, and presenting research achievements in global scholarly dialogue. Looking to the future, EEH will take the enhancement of academic reputation and international influence as its central task. It will continue to optimize its thematic portfolio, strengthen the cultivation of high-level original research, deepen open science and international cooperation, and consolidate its academic quality and dissemination capacity. In doing so, the journal will align its development with the frontiers of global ecological and environmental governance and steadily advance toward the goal of becoming a world-class academic journal.

Building upon the ongoing work of its institutional partners in ecology and environment, alongside its broader engagement with the global research community, EEH will uphold the principle of addressing real-world problems and conducting rigorous research on environmental governance, and promote the transformation of research outcomes from theoretical exploration to engineering application and practical implementation, thereby enabling coordinated innovation across science, technology, and institutions. The journal attaches great importance to the role of scientific research in supporting the development of ecological and environmental governance systems, the improvement of standards, and the implementation of policies. It encourages original research on major theoretical questions and key technological bottlenecks, and is committed to building a high-level platform for academic exchange, bringing together multidisciplinary wisdom, and promoting the transition of research outcomes from “publication alone” to “decision-making support”, so as to serve public health and the broader cause of sustainable development.

Substantial progress in environmental governance—spanning pollution prevention and control, ecological conservation and restoration, and the development of governance systems—has been achieved worldwide, with China as a key representative of this trend. Sharing such experience with the international community goes beyond presenting achievements; it means summarizing practical experience through scientific research, distilling generally applicable principles, and building a knowledge system that bridges empirical insights with global scholarly discourse. As an international journal, EEH serves as a platform for such exchange. By examining China’s extensive practices alongside research from around the world, EEH interprets these approaches and their underlying theoretical logic in an international academic language, offers empirically grounded reference for developing countries, and contributes diverse perspectives to global environmental stewardship.

A great cause is never pursued alone; with shared endeavors, we can go far. Embarking on a new journey, EEH will remain committed to advancing environmental sustainability through scientific research and knowledge dissemination, resonating with the vision of a Beautiful China—the country’s overarching framework for balancing economic development with environmental protection and ecological restoration. It will build a stronger foundation of academic quality with higher standards, integrate more openly into the global academic system, publish world-class frontier research, and strive with determination to become a world-class scientific and technological journal. We look forward to working hand in hand with scholars around the world, deepening academic exchange and practical cooperation on the path toward harmonious coexistence between humanity and nature, and jointly advancing the global endeavor toward a new phase of global ecological civilization.
