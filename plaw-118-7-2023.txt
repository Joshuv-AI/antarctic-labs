Public Law 118–7: To modify the age requirement for the Student Incentive Payment Program of the State maritime academies.

An Act To modify the age requirement for the Student Incentive Payment Program of the State maritime academies.June 30, 2023[S. 467]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Changing Age-Determined Eligibility To Student Incentive Payments Act” or the “CADETS Act”.

SEC. 2. — AGE REQUIREMENT FOR THE STUDENT INCENTIVE PAYMENT PROGRAM OF THE STATE MARITIME ACADEMIES.

Section 51509 of title 46, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:The Secretary may make an agreement under this section only with a qualified student who will meet the age requirement for enlistment or commission in the Navy Reserve at the time of graduation from the academy.” .

Approved June 30, 2023.
