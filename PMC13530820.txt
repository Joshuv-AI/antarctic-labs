Poster 59. Autologous Minced Cartilage Implantation Versus Microfracture and Autologous Chondrocyte Implantation: A Systematic Review of Clinical Outcomes
Authors: Brian Caplan, Krish S. Sardesai, Jason E. Jesse, Samuel Alfonsi, Yusuf Mufti, Kyle Kunze, Adam B. Yanke, Brian J. Cole
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Objective:Focal chondral lesions of the knee are a common source of morbidity and are treated with a variety of surgical techniques. While microfracture (MFX) and standard and matrix-associated autologous chondrocyte implantation (ACI and MACI, respectively) are established approaches, autologous minced cartilage implantation (MCI) has emerged as a promising single-stage alternative. The purpose of this systematic review was to evaluate the safety and efficacy of MCI compared with MFX and ACI/MACI. We hypothesized that MCI would demonstrate noninferior or improved patient reported outcome measures (PROMs) at short- to mid-term follow-up.Methods:Following the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) guidelines, PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane databases were queried in May 2025. Inclusion criteria were Level I-IV human studies published between 2015-2025 with >=12-month follow-up and reporting >=2 validated PROMs. Non-human, cadaveric, biomechanical, radiologic-only studies, case reports, reviews, and studies without technique-specific clinical data were excluded. Two reviewers independently screened studies and extracted data, while a third reviewer resolved disagreements. Analysis on patient cohorts was completed utilizing forest plots to make qualitative assessments.Results:A total of 41 studies comprising 51 unique cohorts (2,116 patients) met inclusion criteria (Figure 1). Of these, 35 cohorts evaluated ACI/MACI (n=1590), 9 cohorts MFX (n=302), and 7 cohorts MCI (n=224). Risk of bias was assessed using the Methodological index for non-randomized studies (MINORS) criteria (Figure 2). Mean patient age was 34.4 years in ACI/MACI, 45.0 years in MFX, and 32.7 years in MCI. Mean follow-up was 61.1 months for ACI/MACI, 58.6 months for MFX, and 29.3 months for MCI. Reported PROMs included International Knee Documentation Committee (IKDC), Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score (KOOS), Lysholm, Tegner, and Visual Analog Scale (VAS) subscores. At large, there were no significant differences between PROMs at post-operative follow-up after ACI, MACI, MFX, and MCI. For example, there was no significant difference between IKDC for ACI (50.91-92.70), MACI (34.18-89.57), MFX (37.63-79.53), and MCI (56.97-87.29, Figure 3). Failure and reoperation rates were variably reported but were generally lowest in the MCI cohorts.Conclusions:Autologous minced cartilage implantation demonstrates short- to mid-term outcomes that are at least comparable and non-inferior to ACI, MACI, and MFX. However, available studies are heterogeneous, and long-term data for MCI remain limited. Further high-quality, prospective comparative studies are needed to clarify the role of MCI in cartilage restoration.
