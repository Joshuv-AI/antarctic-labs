Editorial Note (Potsdam 1945 Vol. II, Unnumbered document following Document 710 (#53))

—No official record of the substance of this conversation has been found. Stimson’s diary entry for July 21 contains the following summary: “I then left the ‘Little White House’, picked up Bundy, and went to the Prime Minister’s house where we conferred with him and Lord Cherwell. I turned over the paper[1] to Churchill and he began reading it but was interrupted a few minutes before five in order to hurry to the Big Three Conference at five o’clock. He asked me to return on the following morning to finish up the report.”

Notes
[1] Document No. 1305, post.
