What are Dual Fuel Heat Pumps?

 Dual fuel heat pumps are hybrid heating and cooling systems that pair
 an electric heat pump with a gas or propane furnace.

• Why is this a myth?
 • Several control options are available to balance cost, comfort, and impact

MYTH 2: Control Strategies Don’t Lead to Demand Flexibility

• Where did this myth come from?
 • HP RTUs with simple control configurations
 often use a fixed outdoor air temperature to
 switch between heat pump and auxiliary
 heating

• Why is this a myth?
 • There are several factors for optimal
 switchover setpoint
 • Electricity and natural gas prices
 • Building heating demands
 • Heat pump efficiency and capacity
 • Number and frequency of defrost cycles
