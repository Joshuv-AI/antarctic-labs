Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 770.]

Department of State, Washington, November 30, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 13th of November (No. 535) has been received. The President is gratified with the reception which Earl Russell has given to my suggestions in regard to obtaining a sanction, by the Mikado, of the treaties which have been made by the western powers with the Tycoon of Japan. The latest information received from Mr. Pruyn induces a hope that the efforts of the daimios, under the Mikado’s patronage, to expel foreigners from the country will not be continued.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

C. F. Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
