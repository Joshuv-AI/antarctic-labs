Adopted by the Security Council at its 259th meeting, by 8 votes to none, with 3 abstentions (Argentina, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, Union of Soviet Socialist Republics), on 28 February 1948

The Security Council,

Requests the Committee of Good Offices to pay particular attention to the political developments in western Java and Madura and to report to the Council thereon at frequent intervals.

1948

fr:Résolution 40 du Conseil de sécurité des Nations unies
