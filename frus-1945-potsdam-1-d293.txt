No. 293 The Acting Representative in Rumania (Melbourne) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret us urgent

Bucharest, June 23, 1945—noon.

430. The Brit political representative[1] and Mr. Berry discussed the question of a possible peace treaty with Rumania before the latter’s departure for the US (Dept’s 310, June 21[2]). Since he is scheduled to arrive in the US within a day or two of this telegram it is felt that Dept may wish to gain Mr. Berry’s personal views.[3] Rptd Moscow as 114 and London as 1.

Upon Genl Schuyler’s return from an inspection trip in three days I will suggest to him that he may wish to present his views by telegram reflecting his experience upon the ACC.[4]

Melbourne

Source: 740.00119 Control (Rumania)/6–2345: Telegram

Notes
[1] J. H. Le Rougetel.
[2] See document No. 290, footnote 1.
[3] No memorandum reflecting Berry’s views on this subject has been found.
