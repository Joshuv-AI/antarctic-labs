Coupling Hydrodynamics with AstroChem
 • Gas component concentration are treated as passive scalars and
 transported with gas dynamics
 • No momentum and energy equations
 • Has concentration diffusion
 • Ice components are treated as layers of dust particles and
 transported with dust dynamics
 • Each dust species has different velocity and can have many ice components:
 total passive dust variables= Nd x Nice
 • No momentum equations
 • Has concentration diffusion
 • Allow hydro and astro-chem to have different temperatures
 • Hydro (vertically isothermal) and astro-chem (Rosenfield’s two-layer model)

Computational Complexity
• Hydrodynamics : 2D (r-⍬) or 3D (r-⍬-⏀) spherical-polar grid
• Coagulation O(n3)→ O(n2) using fast algorithm (Rafikov+ ’20)
 • One coagulation call 15 times hydrodynamic step
• AstroChem solver
 • One astroChem call 100 times hydrodynamic steps
 • Periodically perform astro-chem solver
• Total number of variables in a grid cell

Test runs with 5 dust species, 45 gas species, 3 ices
