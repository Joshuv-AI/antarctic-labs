 SCOPE OF WORK
 • The Industrialized Construction Innovation (ICI) team at NLR and the Robotic
 Space Exploration (RoSE) Lab at Colorado School of Mines, are developing a
 synthetic digital environment framework to study multi-physics robot–
 environment interactions in unstructured settings using high-fidelity digital twins.

 sensor specifications as multi-physics ground-truth variables that can be
