Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 731st meeting, on 20 June 1956

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of Morocco <small>[1]</small>,

Recommends to the General Assembly that Morocco be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Ibid., Supplement for July, August, and September 1956, document S/3617.</small>
