 Thermal Fatigue

 • Plasma-facing tungsten surface heats while the coolant keeps the inner surface
 colder
 • The three materials have different temperatures, stiffnesses, and thermal-
 expansion behaviors
 • Because the bonded layers cannot expand independently, the mismatch
 produces cyclic stress and mechanical strain
 • Repeated cycling can initiate cracks at hot, highly strained locations

 Fatigue Life as a Distribution

 • A deterministic strain-life curve assigns one life to each applied strain amplitude
 • In strain-controlled tests, identical specimens can initiate cracks after different
 numbers of cycles
 • Strain range , is the controlled predictor; life, , is the observed random response
 • Research question: what are the conditional life distributions for W, Cu, and
 CuCrZr?
 • Required outputs: 5th-percentile life , median life , 95th-percentile life , and
 probability of crack initiation by a target life
 • denotes number of cycles to crack initiation, not final component fracture
 • Goal: survive 5000 cycles at heat flux = 10 MW/m² (Crescenzi, F. et al.)

 Why a Probabilistic Framework? Motivation

 • Deterministic codes use fixed safety factors, often calibrated on non-fusion, non-
 irradiated data
 • Critical components such as divertors are too design-, material-, and condition-
 dependent to rely on experience data alone
 • Probabilistic physics of failure links uncertainty, physics, and limited data to
 support risk-informed design decisions

 • Deterministic analysis predicts the
 monoblock will survive cycles at a
 heat flux of 10 MW/m²
 − for all materials
 • CuCrZr: lowest life variability and
 lowest crack initiation prob.
 • Pure Cu: highest life variability and
 highest crack initiation prob.
 • Monoblock crack initiation prob.
 = 0.427

27

 Important Limitations

 • The monoblock model is a two-dimensional generalized-plane-strain
 representation of a three-dimensional component
 • All three materials are currently modeled as linear elastic; plasticity and
 ratchetting are not represented
 • Fatigue datasets are sparse, particularly for tungsten at fusion-relevant
 temperatures and irradiation conditions
 • The current probabilistic model represents specimen-to-specimen life scatter but
 not every source of model-form or input uncertainty
 • Irradiation effects, tritium-assisted damage, and crack propagation are outside
 the present calculation
 • The predicted life is a crack-initiation life, not a final fracture or full component-
 replacement life

28

 Summary

 • The thermal-mechanical model converts the applied heat-flux cycle into a local
 cyclic mechanical strain
 • The strain-life relation is fitted in the life-given-strain direction and interpreted as
 the median crack-initiation life
 • A lognormal scatter model converts the median curve into percentile lives and a
 probability of crack initiation
 • The result is not one deterministic lifetime number, but a transparent connection
 between loading, material scatter, and reliability
