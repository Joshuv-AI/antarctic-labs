1. This month's Roundtable was held at Syracuse with the Air Force
and the University of Syracuse as co-hosts. In my opinion, this meeting
was by far and away the most outstanding and significant meeting we have
had in the year or so of our existence. Its superiority was due largely
to the facts that (a) we were captives together for 24 hours and (b) hos-
pitality conducive to discussions of problems of the profession created a
tempo of comraderie and consequent intellectual mingling relevent to our
individual missions and responsibilities. The session consisted of three
meals together, continuing casual discussions during and between meals,
the formal meeting on Friday, and a tour of the Air Force school at Syra-
cuse, On Thursday evening, the Vice Chancellor of the University was host
at dinner for the group at the Syracuse Hotel. In my opinion, no meeting
so far has accomplished so much for inter-agency relations for which this
Roundtable was created and is being supported by the participants and their
superiors.
