2 . Stude nt P erform ance

(a) A general statement concerning performance of the class

has been a troublesome factor during the course. Give the name,
grade, and office of origin of any student (s) who receives an
evaluation of poor or failure and comments or explanation thereon.

3 . Studen t Com ment

Include synopsis of student critiques and other significant
student comment. Attach typed individual reports if considered
pertinent.
