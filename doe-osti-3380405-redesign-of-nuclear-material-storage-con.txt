Conclusions

The new ergonomic lid design will make for easier
and safer use for handlers that reduces the time spent
in contact with the container. MCNP simulation data
and real data indicate that a nylon 6/66 inner sleeve is
the most effective dose shield that can be additively
manufactured, thus drastically improving upon the
current nuclear material container iteration that is
used at LANL.
