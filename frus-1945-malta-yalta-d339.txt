The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President [1] (5 February 1945, d339)

[Yalta, February 5, 1945.]

1. France is on the European Advisory committee now. That is only body considering German affairs now.

2. Promise a zone.

3. Postpone decision about Control Commission.

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] The relationship of this and the two following notes to the Second Plenary Meeting appears from internal evidence and from their association in the files with a paper bearing the following words in Hopkins’ handwriting: “Notes given to President by me during conference with Stalin and Churchill on Feb 5—45”. See ante, p. 618.
