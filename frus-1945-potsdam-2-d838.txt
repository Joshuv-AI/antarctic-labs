No. 838 The Chargé in the Soviet Union (Kennan) to the Acting Secretary of State[1]

secret

Moscow, July 19, 1945—midnight.

2634. Letter went forward to Foreign Office in conformity with Department’s 1621, July 16,[2] warning that exclusive Soviet Rumanian trade pact and monopolistic agreements on oil would cause serious concern in United States Government and might compel United States Government to reconsider present favorable oil policy toward USSR. (To Department, repeated Bucharest 94, London 372). Harriman has been informed.

Kennan

Source: 871.6363/7–1945: Telegram
