2b CONCEPT OF OPERATIONS

a. In general war the Office of Scientific Intelligence will
continue to perform the same primary missions as in peacetime j how-

(1) Scientific and technical intelligence research

3. SPECIFIC FUNCTIONS

a. Under each major wartime mission, specific functions will
be performed as follows:

(1) RESEARCH AM) PRODUCTION

(a) Scientific Intelligence Objectives, the
Long Range Intelligence Research Plan and the Fiscal
Year Production Programs will be revised to provide
increased research and production on the technologi-
cal applications of foreign science to the war-making
potential of hostile forces.

(b) Contributions to National Intelligence
Estimates and to Current Intelligence Publications
will be increased to reflect greater emphasis upon
the technological applications of science to warfare.

(c) Specific research and production will be
conducted as required to meet approved requests of
other offices and agencies of the Government for
scientific and technical intelligence support re-
lated to wartime operations.

(d) Surveillance and research on developments
in foreign science which may have breakthrough im-
plications or which may indicate significant trends,
will be intensified as an area of primary Office
responsibility.
