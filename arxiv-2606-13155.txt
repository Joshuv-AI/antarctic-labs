Snake Polyominoes of Maximal Area in a Rectangle
Alexandre Blondin Massé, Alain Goupil

Abstract
Given a discrete rectangle R R of dimensions h × w h\times w , let 𝐖 h × w snake \mathbf{W}^{\mathrm{snake}}_{h\times w} be the set of snake-like polyominoes contained in R R represented as binary matrices, i.e. polyominoes whose underlying simple graph is a chain with respect to the 4-adjacency relation. We present an algorithm that generates 𝐖 h × w snake \mathbf{W}^{\mathrm{snake}}_{h\times w} for any h h and w w . Also, let a max ​ ( h , w ) a_{\mathrm{max}}(h,w) the maximal area that can be realized by an element of 𝐖 h × w snake \mathbf{W}^{\mathrm{snake}}_{h\times w} . We provide exact formulas of a max ​ ( h , w ) a_{\mathrm{max}}(h,w) for h ∈ { 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 , 5 } h\in\{1,2,3,4,5\} and w ∈ ℕ ∗ w\in\mathbb{N}^{*} .

Due to space restriction, many proofs of the stated propositions and lemmas have been ommitted, but will be included in an upcoming extended version.

A

is an inscribed binary word

on
A

is a chain.
The set of all snake words of dimensions

Similarly, a

A binary word

is a factor of

The set of all snake factors of dimensions

is denoted by

The following two observations are immediate:

Proposition 1
.

(i)

A snake factor is a snake forest word.

(ii)

There exist snake forest words that are not snake factors.
