 The OTFB is based on two principles. Firstly, the beam
spectrum is made up of harmonics of the revolution fre-
quency ± the synchrotron sidebands. Therefore, we should
apply gain specifically at these frequencies, which can be
done using a digital comb filter. Secondly, we note that,
turn-by-turn, the beam spectrum does not vary significantly. Figure 2: BbyB Cavity Magnitude and Phase Measurement
Therefore, we don’t need to apply the correction right away,
instead we can wait exactly one revolution period before
applying the gain. By combining these two principles, we
get the OTFB.

 First, the DNA was used to measure the system response.

above. This was due to the delay through the direct analog
