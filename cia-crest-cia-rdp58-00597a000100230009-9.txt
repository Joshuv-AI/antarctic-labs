Section 3, necessary to effectuate the Act. Such contracts shall
include provisions that each school of medicine and school of
dentistry which provides training leading to an M.D. or D.D.S.
degree will receive an amount sufficient to cover tuition, fees
and laboratory expenses normally charged students by the school

Section 5 provides that the establishment, award, continuance
or discontinuance of scholarships provided for under this Act and
the effectuation of the purposes thereof shall be by regulations
prescribed by the Secretary of Defense. Subparagraphs (1), (2),

(3), (4) and. (5) contain provisions which are to be included in
regulations. These include qualifications of scholarship benefici-
aries j prior acceptance by an educational institution; requirement
for an agreement to accept an appointment upon graduation as an
officer in a reserve component of the Armed Forces concerned, and
to remain an officer therein until the eighth anniversary of his
original appointment and to serve on active duty, excluding any
time spent in internship or residency training, one year for each
year of training, with the exception that if less than three years
of payments are made on behalf of a beneficiary he may be required
to complete a minimum of three years of active duty; provision for
selection of qualified graduates for appointment as regular officers
by the Secretary concerned.

Section 6 provides for the voluntary relief of a graduate's
remaining active duty obligation after serving three years of active
duty exclusive of any time spent in internship or residency train-
ing, if the member refunds the total amount paid in His behalf by
the Government. This section is not intended to affect the total
8 year active duty-reserve obligation.

Section 7 provides for the repayment to the Government of the
total amount of tuition, fees and laboratory expenses, and the total
amount paid in the form of monetary allowances, plus interest, if a
scholarship beneficiary fails to complete a course of instruction.
The Secretary of Defense may remit in whole or in part, the amount
due in cases of hardship or for other good cause. Provision is
made for the reduction of the amount due if active duty is performed
as prescribed by the Secretary of Defense. Such reduction will not
be greater than the percentage between the number of full months of
active duty performed and the number of months for which the
beneficiary received a monetary allowance.
