Marshal Stalin to President Roosevelt [1] (22 July 1944, d4)

Secret and Personal from Premier J. V. Stalin to President F. D. Roosevelt.

I share your thought about the desirability of a meeting between you, Mr. Churchill and myself.

However, I must say, that now, when the Soviet Armies are involved in battles on such a wide front, it would be impossible for me to leave the country and depart for a certain period of time from the conducting of front matters. All my colleagues consider it absolutely impossible.

July 22, 1944.

Source: Roosevelt Papers

Notes
[1] Apparently transmitted by the Soviet Embassy, Washington.
