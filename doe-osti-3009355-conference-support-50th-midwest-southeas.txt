Technical Report for award number DE-SC0025676
Conference Support: 50th Midwest/Southeast Regional Photosynthesis Conference 2024
The DOE grant referenced above supported the 50th Anniversary Meeting of the
Midwest/Southeast Regional Photosynthesis Conference, held at Turkey Run State Park, Marshall,
Indiana, from October 25-27, 2024. The meeting featured four keynote speakers – Drs. Govindjee,
Wim Vermaas, David Vinyard, and Lauren Metskas – and 12 oral presentations by student and
postdoctoral trainees, selected from submitted abstracts. The meeting had a total attendance of 85
participants, including 62 students and research scientists. DOE funds were used to waive the
registration fees for all students and trainees (totaling $2,500) and to support seven travel awards
of $350 each, for a total of $2,450. Each travel award covered food and accommodation for the
recipient at Turkey Run State Park during the conference.

The following individuals received DOE-supported travel awards:

 1. Rajnandani Kashyap, Postdoctoral Researcher, Saint Louis University
 2. Chetna Sharma, Graduate Student, University of Florida
 3. Stavroula Nicolaou, Undergraduate Student, Loyola College
 4. Evan Smolen, Undergraduate Student, Loyola College
 5. Vidya Gundlapalli, Undergraduate Student, Loyola College
 6. Lydia Davies-Balogun, Undergraduate Student, Alabama State University
 7. Nidhi Kulkarni, Graduate Student, Louisiana State University

Travel awardees were selected based on the quality of submitted abstracts and demonstrated
financial need, with priority given to undergraduate students, followed by graduate students and
postdoctoral researchers.
