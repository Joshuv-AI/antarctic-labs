 Aggregation and Virtual Power Plants
 Demand-Side Management (DSM): electric
 utility activities designed to encourage
 consumers to modify their level and pattern
 of electricity usage.

 Demand Response (DR): DSM programs
 whereby utilities directly (or indirectly)
 control grid edge resource(s).

 Virtual Power Plant (VPP): An aggregation
 of generation, storage, and/or demand
 resources (often at the grid edge) managed
 to achieve a supply-side purpose.
