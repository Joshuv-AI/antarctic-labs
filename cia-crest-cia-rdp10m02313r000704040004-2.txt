SUBJECT: PD/NSC-55

That portion of PD/NSC-55, dated January 7, 1980, which
established the APEX system, is rescinded. The DCI , with
the other interested elements of the intelligence community,
is requested to conduct a review of the remaining portion
of the PD and provide recommendations for implementation.

SUBJECT: PD/NSC-55

As you know, APEX was a very controversial issue among
the agencies and. departments.. We therefore seek the
view of the community regarding revisions of the APEX
system.

The attached memo cancels the implementation of APEX
as you requested, requests that you solicit comments on
revisions to PD/NSC-55 that may be needed, and forward
your recommendations.
