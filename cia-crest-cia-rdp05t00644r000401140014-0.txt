YOU ARE INVITED TO VIEW THE GREAT DECISIONS 1980
TELEVISION SERIES OVER WETA, CHANNEL 26

Beginning Saturday, January 26, 1980 j and continuing each Saturday for eight
consecutive weeks, the GREAT DECISIONS 1980 television series on eight key foreign
policy issues facing the American people will be broadcast from 5:00 - 5:30 p.m.
over WETA, Channel 26, Washington, D.C.

The Foreign Policy Association invites you to watch this important series of
half-hour programs produced by the University of North Carolina TV Center in cooper-
ation with FPA and the Southern Educational Communications Association (SECA). The
series is being offered to ALL public television stations across the nation via
WESTAR Satellite and WETA will air the programs in the Washington area.

Martin Agronsky is the moderator and Dr. William C. Friday, President of the
University of North Carolina, is the host for the series. The list of participants
is attached and you will note the distinguished roster of experts who have contri-
buted their time and expertise to this significant media component of the nation-
wide GREAT DECISIONS 1980 discussion progreim,

FPA and UNC-TV would welcome your opinion of these programs for the purpose of
evaluating this year's efforts and in planning for 1981. Please write to: Depart-
ment of Community Programs, Foreign Policy Association, 205 Lexington Avenue, New
York, NY 10016.
