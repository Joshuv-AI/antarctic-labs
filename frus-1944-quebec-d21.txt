Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (8 August 1944, d21)

top secret

London, 8 August 1944.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt. Personal and top secret. Number 746.

I will certainly come to Bermuda at any time. We could make you very comfortable at Government House. I had preparations made some months ago for your personal convenience in every way. Whatever you wish in these matters will be agreeable to us.

By “climax” I did not mean “end”.

Thank you for your very kind personal thoughts.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels; forwarded by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt, who was then in Alaska, as telegram No. Red 265.
