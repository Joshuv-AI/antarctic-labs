Note a. EDAC D-136, June 26, 19g7

The Chairman reported that there is now in circulation EDAC D-I 36 which
is a memorandum from Mr. Staats, Executive Officer of the Operations Coordinating
Board, to Admiral DeLany, requesting that the possibility of securing a blanket
Presidential exception for Finland be explored through EDAC to permit lending
PL U80 proceeds to Finland for economic development. Admiral DeLany has responded
(June 2?) saying that the Department of State member of the Executive Committee
will bring before that Committee a proposal which will be given interagency co-
ordination and documentation for EDAC action as soon as possible.

The Chairman outlined the background which led up to this assignment from
the OCB and reported that the Department of State is going to prepare the above-
mentioned proposal which will suggest the basis for EDAC agreement on a Presidential
Determination that will make possible the granting of this kind of assistance to
Finland. This matter will be considered in the Executive Committee when the State
proposal is ready.

In response to a question from the Commerce member as to whether an opinion
on this matter had been obtained from the ICA General Counsel, -the Chairman replied
that the General Counsel had been present at a meeting held in State on this subject
and had stated that he felt there was discretion in the Act which would allow for
this under certain stipulated conditions, but he would have to see the nature of the
information on which EDAC proposed a Presidential Determination before making a ^
definite decision.

Note b. IL II China Quotas for 19i?7
Decision

The problem of IL II China quotas for 19^7 will be discussed at an
Executive Committee meeting to be held at 2.00 p.m. Friday, June 28.

Discussion

The Chairman stated that Working Group I had met in response to the
assignment given it in ED/EC M-2h£ to consider all aspects of the List II problem
which should eventually be raised in CHINCOM discussions. However, at its meeting
on June 26 no member had been in a position to reflect his agency's position on the
quota recommendations of the Ad Hoc Committee which had been established to study
this problem. Due to the deadline of July 1, the Chairman stated he requested the
Chairman of WG I to prepare a memorandum on this problem, giving his recommendations,
for the consideration of the Executive Committee.

The memorandum entitled "Report on IL II China Quotas for 19^7” (ED/EC
D-119), has attached to it a memorandum entitled "Report of Ad Hoc Committee on
China Quotas" (WG I D-72.3) and a memorandum from the Chairman of the Ad Hoc
Committee entitled "Background. Analysis of CHINCOM Quota Problem" (WG I D-72.2).

1. NSC/CFEP Assignment to Review Economic Defense Policy
(Ref. EDAC D-135, June 27, 1957)

Decisions

The Commerce and Defense members will prepare check lists of recent events
and other aspects of the program which will be applied to each paragraph of the
economic defense policy as it is reviewed.

The Executive Committee will meet on Monday , July 1 and regularly during
that week, to review the economic defense policy, paragraph by paragraph.

Working Group HI will assume responsibility for reviewing points (3) and
(i±) of the JCS memorandum attached to the CFEP assignment.

Discussion

The Chairman made reference to the several memoranda contained in
EDAC D-135 and noted that Point (5) of the JCS memo had not been assigned to EDAC
for consideration. He said his interpretation of para. 2 of the memorandum from
Mr. Randall is that paragraphs 8 and 20 of the economic defense policy have been
assigned to a Committee under the Chairmanship of the Under Secretaiy of Commerce
and, therefore, are not a problem for EDAC consideration under this assignment.

He felt that time prevents a broad general study such as was done in 1955.
He further felt that because of the review which was done earlier this year, it
would not be necessary to do a great deal of documentation, except with respect to
the changes that we propose, conditions which led us to make these changes and, if
necessary, to show how paragraphs are going to be implemented, though unchanged, in
order to get a full understanding or to bring out the differences among the agencies
He requested that the members be prepared on Monday, July 1, to discuss NSC 570U/1
paragraph by paragraph to determine the need for changes in the current language and
to offer alternative language if a split makes it necessary to do so. It will then
be necessary to prepare for EDAC an explanation of the changes and the reasons there
for. We would thereafter address ourselves to the questions raised by the JCS.

In response to a request from the Chairman, the State member said that
Working Group IH would consider points (3) and (U) of the JCS memo and report its
findings to the Executive Committee. /Para. 3b. (3) and (U) .7

3.b.(2) of the JCS memo -- to consider "The posture to be adopted by the United
States in the face of possible action on the part of our Allies further to weaken
the COCOM/CHINCOM controls".
