No. 999 The Secretary of State to the Chargé in the Soviet Union (Kennan)[1]

secret

Babelsberg, July 30, 1945.

Victory 419. Sent to Moscow repeated Department for information.

Re note delivered to Soviets on German assets in Spain,[2] we have received word from US Military Attaché, Stockholm,[3] that Russian Delegation has conducted a survey in Sweden of interned German planes (presumably including only those coming to Sweden from Soviet-German front), which are proposed to be flown out as war booty.[4] Newspapers have publicized Russian plans, together with statement that no request concerning remainder has been made by US or UK.

Byrnes
