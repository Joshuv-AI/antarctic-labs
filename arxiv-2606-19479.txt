Generating Function of single-centered Black Hole Index in CHL Models
Ranveer Kumar Singh

Abstract
We present the construction of the generating function of single-centered black hole index in general ℤ N \mathbb{Z}_{N} CHL models. This is done by subtracting from the index of quarter BPS dyons, described by a meromorphic Siegel modular form, the generating function for the index of two-centered black holes. We use black hole bound state metamorphosis in CHL models for the construction of the generating function of two-centered black hole index. We prove the convergence of the generating function for the cases N = 2 , 3 N=2,3 .

5
Conclusion and future directions

In this paper, we have generalized the results of
4
and constructed the generating function of single-centered black hole degeneracy for general CHL models. We also proved the convergence of the generating function for
N=2,3
. The results of this paper leave ample opportunity for future work. We list some possible future directions.

5.1
Generalizing the proof of convergence to
N>3

Our proof of convergence of the generating function is complete for
N=2,3
. We present some general steps in the proof of convergence for general
N
but some of the steps in the proof fail for
N>3
. The main technical bottleneck is the infinity of walls of the fundamental chamber
{\cal R}_{N}
for
N\geq 4
. The first place this will cause trouble is in the bound on
\tau_{2}^{\prime},\sigma_{2}^{\prime},z_{2}^{\prime}
derived in (
126
) and (
128
). One might be able to overcome this issue by writing infinitely many expression for
\tau_{2}^{\prime},\sigma_{2}^{\prime},z_{2}^{\prime}
as linear combinations of walls of the
{\cal R}_{N}
chamber as we did for
N=2,3
. But this will require a detailed knowledge of all the walls of the
{\cal R}_{N}
-chamber.

As mentioned in Remark
3.3
, Lemma
3.3
is an if and only if statement only for
N\leq 6
. For
N\geq 7
, Lemma
3.3
is only a necessary condition. We will need a more detailed knowledge of the walls of
{\cal R}_{N}
to get an equivalent condition for the nonvanishing of the Heaviside functions. This affects the proof of the convergence of
{\cal F}_{4}
. Thus, some novel methods are needed to establish the convergence of
\widetilde{F}_{k}
in full generality.

Finally, we prove the convergence of the sum in the
{\cal R}_{N}
-chamber and then use the formal
\Gamma_{1}(N)
-invariance to analytically continue the generating function to all of
\mathbb{H}_{2}
. This requires that
{\cal R}_{N}
be a fundamental chamber, in the sense that any other chamber can be reached from
{\cal R}_{N}
