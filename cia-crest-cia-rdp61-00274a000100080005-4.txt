TECHNICAL BRANCH

MISSION

The Technical Branch is responsible for the development,
establishment and maintenance of Agency- wide defensive audio sur-
veillance security programs in the United States and abroad. The
Branch also performs other functions in the audio field in support
of Agency Security Programs, The Branch develops security systems
utilizing technical methods.

FUNCTIONS

In carrying out the above mission, the Technical Branch will:

1. Conduct technical inspections to detect hostile
audio surveillance of Agency activities.

2. Develop and establish audio surveillance
counter-measures.

3. Develop and establish world-wide reporting
procedures in the defensive audio field.

4. Make analyses of technical inspection reports
received from world-wide sources.

5. Develop Training Programs in audio technical
fields in coordination with Administration and Training
Staff/ OS and Office of Training.

6. Review Security Operational Programs in order
to determine technical requirements.

7. Develop and establish equipment requirements
and convert such requirements to technical language and
schematics for procurement by the Agency.

8. Control and approve issuance of technical equip-
ment used in searching for hostile audio surveillance
plants.

ENGINEERING AND RESEARCH SECTION

1« Reviews Security Operational Requirements in order to
determine technical needs.

2. Develops and establishes equipment requirements and
converts such requirements to technical language and schematics
for procurement by the Agency.

3. Conducts research on existing and developmental equip-
ment and determines modifications necessary for adaptation of such
equipment to security purposes,

4. Conducts liaison within the Agency and with other Govern
ment and non-Government agencies on current and new technical
security equipment and methods,

5. Conducts tests and evaluations of technical equipment,
alarm systems, safes and other devices used in security programs.

6. Makes analyses of technical inspection reports received
on a world-wide basis and makes recommendations for and develops
countermeasures.

7. Develops programs for caching of equipment in foreign
countries preparatory for future technical inspections of Agency
activities.

PLANS, PROGRAMS AND TRAINING SECTION

1. Assures development of training programs for

a. U. S. Personnel with domestic and foreign
assignments

b. Technical personnel

c. Foreign nationals.

In such training programs the application of countermeasures without
compromising present or new offensive capabilities will be observed.

2. Prepares regulations and notices for use within the Agency
in the field of technical audio surveillance inspections and counter-
measures.

3. Conducts special briefings on offensive and defensive audio
surveillance activities from a security standpoint.

4. Renders guidance to Chief of Branch in recommending for
or against approval of revealing specific defensive audio surveillance
techniques to foreign nationals and/or governments.

5. Furnishes guidance and advice, conducts studies and reviews
and prepares progress reports for the CIA member of the NSC Committee
on Audio Surveillance Countermeasures.

6. Furnishes support to OS/Training Officer and Office of
Training in development of training programs in defensive audio
surveillance fields.

7. Assists the OS/Training Officer in providing training and
briefing of COMMO personnel on technical security threat to COMMO
facilities through technical audio surveillance devices and counter-
measures to offset such threats.

8. Responsible within the Office of Security for recommending
dissemination to appropriate levels of the Agency current developments
or discoveries in the defensive audio surveillance fields.

9. Performs other special assignments for the Chief of Branch.
