Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 653.]

Department of State, Washington, July 13, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of June 25 (No. 435) has been received, and your several proceedings therein related are approved.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
