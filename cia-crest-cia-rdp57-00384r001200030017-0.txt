Introduction

community have been impressed with the continuing high degree of
cooperation among the agencies represented here. Those of us who
work on the National Security Council Planning Board are benefiting

seen evidence that some of the major so-called intelligence gaps are
being identified and filled on an interagency basis, that duplication

throughout the comzmznity.
