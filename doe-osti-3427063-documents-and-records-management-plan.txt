1 Introduction
1.1 Purpose

This document defines the LANSCE Accelerator Modernization Project (LAMP) strategy for managing
documents and records generated by and for LAMP.

LAMP is required by Laboratory and DOE policies and orders to identify, maintain, and provide for the
long-term storage of project records. In addition, LAMP must manage documents to clearly identify and
make available approved project procedures and plans. These requirements are covered in the following
policies:

 • P1020-1, Laboratory Records Management

 • P1020-2, Laboratory Document Control

 • PD1020, Document Control and Records Management

1.2 Scope Description

This plan applies to all personnel working in support of LAMP and to all records generated by or in
support of LAMP.

Official Laboratory business must be memorialized in a formal record such as reports, email,
memorandums, project plans, or correspondence records, etc., as best suits the information
communicated. Official Laboratory business information includes discussion of substantive
issues, decisions, or direction regarding project scope, budget, schedule, milestones, and
deliverables as well as technical, management, or administrative issues.

Procurement-related documents are managed by Acquisition Services Management (ASM) personnel in
accordance with institutional ASM procedures and in ASM data systems; the documents/records they
manage include pre-award documents, such as scope of work definition and request for proposal,
contracts, purchase orders, modifications, and other related documents.

3 Implementation
3.1 Document Creation and Processing

The LAMP documents website is located within the internal (LANL Yellow Network) LAMP website:
https://int.lanl.gov/org/ddste/aldps/lamp/documents/index.shtml.

Project document creation process:
 1. Request a LAMP document number through an email to lamp-dcrm@lanl.gov.
 2. The document team will work with you to determine the category and type and then assign a
 document number for you to use.
 3. Select an appropriate document template, from templates located on the LAMP documents
 website.
 4. Proceed to work on the document, keeping drafts in the LAMP OneDrive shared folders.
 5. When the document is ready for release for use by LAMP, begin the review process. The author
 of the document is responsible for requesting feedback for peer review by emailing reviewers for
 the document. The author will collect feedback, incorporate the feedback, and prepare the
 document for signing and release. Before release, the author will ensure that there are no tracked
 changes or unresolved comments in the document.
 6. The author will designate who will sign the document for approval to release for project use. Co-
 authors are allowed, but only one primary author should be identified. See the signing matrix in
 the Appendix A for suggested signees.

Project document review and release process:
 1. When ready to release a document, notify the LAMP document team by sending an email to
 lamp-dcrm@lanl.gov. Please do not email the document. Rather, provide the path to the
 document in the LAMP OneDrive folders.
 2. The LAMP document team will create a pdf copy of the document in the OneDrive folder
 containing the draft.
 3. The LAMP document team will notify those required to sign of the need to do so. The document
 pdf will remain in place to electronically sign (the document itself is not copied and sent out).
 4. Once all signatures are complete, if appropriate for the sensitivity level of the information, the
 LAMP document team will submit the document for LA-UR designation if needed. A Derivative
 Classifier review is a required part of that process and will requested. If LA-UR designation is not
 needed, a DC review will be conducted if it has not already been done. The author may handle the
 LA-UR process after signatures on their own if desired.
 5. For conference and other publications, authors will obtain LA-UR numbers themselves. These
 documents will receive LAMP document numbers but will not require signatures. See the
 publications process below.
 6. When the LA-UR process is complete, the document repositories (EDRMS and the LAMP
 internal website) will be updated with the newly released version. Authors should visit the LAMP
 internal website periodically to confirm that correct revision of their document is uploaded.

Document process for publications such as journal articles, conference proceedings papers and
presentations:
 1. For conference and journal publications, authors will generate the journal, conference paper, or
 presentation, and obtain LA-UR numbers themselves before sending the document to the LAMP
 document team. See the LAMP Technical Report Approval Procedure in Appendix B for the
 approval process specified by the LAMP Technical Director.
 2. These documents will receive LAMP document numbers but will not require signatures or
 signature page. Publication author will email lamp-dcrm@lanl.gov and provide the LAMP
 document team with the document pdf and LA-UR number.
 3. The LAMP document team will add a LAMP document number cover page to each pdf
 document, so the doc number is available with the document when loaded to EDRMS and the
 LAMP documents website.
 4. Publications are then made available on the LAMP documents website.

3.2 Document and Records Numbering

Project documents and records are assigned unique identifiers by the project by the LAMP document
team. This number can be obtained at any point when starting a document. See Appendix C for the
document categories and types.

The numbering scheme assigned includes the project, the document category, the document or record
type, and a sequence number and revision number (e.g., “LAMP-PM-PLAN-123_R0 Name”).

Publications will have document numbers in the form LAMP-PUB-YY-123 where YY designates the
publication calendar year, and 123 designates the sequence number.

3.3 Records Processing

Records are processed by the LAMP RM-POC. Records generated, supplied, or maintained by and for the
project shall be electronic in an accepted format. The RM-POC will provide a list of acceptable formats if
needed. Hardcopy records must be scanned to create an electronic format file for storage. Records are
submitted via email to lamp-dcrm@lanl.gov.

Project released documents and records will be available on the LAMP internal website in the Document
Management section. Released documents may have an annual review conducted by the author to
determine if an update is needed.

3.4 Electronic Document and Records Management System (EDRMS)

EDRMS is the Laboratory's institutional records management system. It holds the records for the project
and will satisfy institutional long term records storage and retention requirements for LAMP. It is
maintained, backed up, and protected in accordance with institutional security and computing
requirements. Access to the project documents/records is controlled.

 • General users can view and export records; they cannot add or delete records.

 • Information Management personnel can add, delete, or modify records; changes are captured in
 the history of the document.

3.5 Records Inventory and Disposition Schedule

As the LAMP project progresses and new documents and records are identified, formal documentation of
the project's document/record holdings, file arrangement, and disposition schedule will be developed and
managed via a LAMP Records Inventory and Disposition Schedule (RIDS). The RIDS will be updated
periodically as needed. The project retains documents and records electronically; therefore, an inventory
of records can be generated whenever required or requested directly from the EDRMS. See LAMP-PM-
PLAN-010, Records Retention Plan.

The LAMP Records POC (RM-POC) is the Records Custodian for project documentation described in the
LAMP RIDS. The LAMP Project Manager is the Records Custodian for project controls financial and
schedule related records which are described in the ALDICP RIDS.

3.6 Records Turnover and Closeout

Documents and records required for operations after project completion will be provided to LANSCE
operations organizations. At project closeout a final verification will be made to ensure all documents and
records are submitted to EDRMS for transfer to LANSCE.
