A Bone-Resorption Surface-Targeting Nanoparticle to Deliver Anti-miR214 for Osteoporosis Therapy [Expression of Concern]
Published: 2026

Cai M, Yang L, Zhang S, Liu J, Sun Y, Wang X. Int J Nanomedicine. 2017;12:7469–7482. https://doi.org/10.2147/IJN.S139775

We, the Publisher and Editor of International Journal of Nanomedicine, are issuing an Expression of Concern for the published article.

Following publication of this article, the authors contacted the Journal in 2025 explaining that an incorrect image was inadvertently included in Figure 4C and 4D. The authors have requested the correction to Figure 4 of their article.

Further investigation by the Journal and Publisher confirmed unexpected similarities between Figure 4C and 4D; these are representative of different treatment conditions. As part of the investigation, the authors were asked for their original images and any information that would help to confirm the validity of the images. The authors have responded but have not been able to provide the original data that was acquired at the time the experiments were performed due to the time that has elapsed since these were conducted.

Therefore, we are unable to address the concerns regarding Figure 4; however, the Editor confirms that the concerns raised do not affect the overall validity or integrity of the reported findings. We advise readers to interpret the information presented in Figure 4 with due caution.

The authors have been notified about this Expression of Concern. Should further information come to light, the journal will reassess any concern raised.
