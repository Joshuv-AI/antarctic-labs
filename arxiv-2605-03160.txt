Pairwise matrices for sparse autoencoders: single-feature inspection mislabels causal axes
Michael A. Riegler, Birk Sebastian Frostelid Torpmann-Hagen

Abstract
The standard sparse-autoencoder (SAE) interpretability protocol labels each feature from its top-activating contexts and validates the label by single-feature steering. We propose the pairwise matrix protocol , co-varying steering coefficient with joint condition as a minimum probe of an SAE feature’s role, and report three findings that the standard one-corner protocol misses, demonstrated on Qwen3-1.7B-Instruct and replicated on Gemma-2-2B-it. First, a feature whose top-context label is AI self-disclaimer produces an inverted U-shape on disclaimer rate under a coefficient sweep: at c = + 500 c{=}{+}500 the model substitutes a fluent contemplative-philosopher voice for the disclaimer phrase. Two further Class-1 features anchor the criterion: #22082 is monotonic; #2932 has an apparent inverted U-shape that is pure breakdown. Second, three near-orthogonal cluster-specific features that individually steer a philosophy-of-mind register, jointly suppressed at c = − 500 c{=}{-}500 , damage grounded composition on recipes and engine explanations as well as on introspective prompts; single-feature suppression at the same magnitude leaves controls intact. Third, a matched-geometry comparison of single-feature, joint, and random-direction perturbations (norm ≈ 1.55 \approx\!1.55 , cosine ≈ 0.64 \approx\!0.64 at the matched coefficients) yields three distinct output regimes: single-feature substitutes strategy filler, random direction substitutes diverse content, joint suppression alone produces placeholder text. Coherence loss is direction-pattern-dependent, not magnitude-dependent. On Gemma all three matrix-level findings reproduce with model-specific damage signatures (Qwen’s (CCL) -style placeholder tokens vs Gemma’s human/I/AI -token injection into the recipe slot); the matched-geometry control on Gemma is CI-separated by ≈ 10 × \approx\!10\times . The cross-model register-collapse pipeline also locates a top causally responsible feature in Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.

3
Methods

The pipeline has four phases. Phase 1 generates samples under
matched introspective and control prompts. Phase 2 partitions
samples into pools by lexical cluster. Phase 3 ranks SAE features
by per-pool activation differences with bootstrap and permutation
controls. Phase 4 establishes causal status by decoder-direction
steering. The matrix protocol (§
3.5
)
co-varies the steering coefficient with the joint-feature
condition. All three models tested run the same pipeline with
model-specific SAE releases.

3.5
Pairwise matrix protocol

The same primitive (Eq.
2
) is varied along two axes.
The
coefficient axis
sweeps a single feature across
c

exposing mode-switches single-coefficient inspection cannot see.
The
joint-condition axis
sums multiple features at one
c

exposing structural roles single-feature steering cannot see
because near-orthogonal neighbours mask the role. Each axis
surfaces structure invisible to standard top-context labelling,
which inspects one corner of the matrix.

6
Discussion

The three findings sit on three axes of one matrix. The coefficient
axis (
Section

4.1
) carries information the local
top-context label cannot, on Qwen #26221 and Gemma #3997; the
joint-condition axis (
Section

4.2
) carries information
the single-feature dose-response cannot, on three features per
model on Qwen and Gemma; the matched-geometry random-direction
control (§
4.3
) rules out both the simplest
geometric reading and the matched-norm-fragility alternative. On
the cases reported, single-feature inspection produces a label that
is locally true and globally incomplete; we do not claim it fails
on every feature. The standard protocol inspects one corner of the
matrix. Our data most directly support (i) a coefficient
sweep with a coherence check at the inflection; (ii)
joint-suppression of near-orthogonal neighbours on target and
unrelated control prompts; and (iii) residual-stream norm, cosine
to baseline, and a matched-geometry random-direction control to
isolate the perturbation pattern from its magnitude. Where on the
coefficient axis the diagnostic edge sits is determined by baseline
saturation, not by the analyst: on Qwen the mode-switch surfaces
at amplification and joint damage at suppression; on the
more-saturated Gemma the signs invert. The protocol reads this
off the sweep automatically.

The label-from-top-contexts gave
AI self-disclaimer
; a
coefficient sweep produced a coherent contemplative-philosopher
voice at
c{=}{+}500
. The label captured one surface form, not
the direction’s full behavioural region. The
non-monotonic-with-coherence-preserved criterion was met on
1
of

3
Class-1 features sampled. Re-running the labelling protocol
blindly on the steered samples (§
M
), two
independent labellers produced
“introspective philosophical
contemplation framing applied indiscriminately to any topic”

verbatim — a cross-topic application phrase neither baseline
labeller produced. The label captures the regime, not the axis.

Each of #29108, #26221, #4405 is a content-bearing direction on
its own; populating fluent syntactic structure with semantically
coherent content depends on having at least some axes available.
The model-specific damage signature on Gemma sharpens the reading:
the structural role is
grounded composition
, not the
philosophy-of-mind register specifically. When the content-bearing
axes are perturbed off-range, what surfaces is whatever default
distribution the model falls back to: Qwen’s invented

(CCL)
-codes, Gemma’s
human / I / AI
-token injection
into the recipe slot. The fallback distribution is model-specific;
the structural mechanism is not.

All three post-training pipelines tested exhibit a sharp register
collapse with a single causally responsible SAE feature. Qwen and
Gemma reproduce all three matrix axes, with CI-separated
matched-geometry gaps (
\approx\!18\times
on Qwen,

\approx\!10\times
on Gemma). The Llama matrix-level tests are
not run; since Goodfire’s Llama SAE is the only instruct-trained
SAE in our set, running them would be the cleanest test of whether
matrix-level findings transfer across SAE training distributions.

7
Limitations

The headline coefficient-axis finding rests on Qwen #26221 and
Gemma #3997, with Qwen #22082 (monotonic) and #2932 (breakdown)
as falsifying anchors: two positives plus two controls earn the
non-monotonic-with-coherence-preserved criterion but do not
estimate its prevalence. The cross-model claim is similarly
N{=}3
,
limited to models with usable open SAE releases. The
K{=}50

random-direction control is run only at the matched coefficient

c{=}{-}1000
; the remainder of the sweep is at
K{=}5
.

Two of three SAEs (Qwen-Scope, Gemma-Scope) are trained on
base-model activations and applied to post-trained activations;
only Goodfire’s Llama SAE is matched, and the Llama matrix-level
tests are not run. The base-vs-instruct comparison on Qwen
additionally conflates post-training with chat-template handling
(the base cannot parse chat-template tokens, forcing raw prompts
on the base and chat-formatted prompts on the instruct), so the
conclusion that post-training amplifies an existing capability is
consistent with the Qwen data but not established across the three
models.

Phase 2 finds a model-specific lexical region by construction
(concentrated on intros vs. controls); whether introspection is
special or any open-ended prompt class yields a comparable narrow
distribution under the same filter is not addressed. The matrix
protocol itself is metric-agnostic, nothing in
§
4.1
–
4.3
is
register-specific, but the existence proof is on a single
phenomenon class; whether single-feature inspection systematically
mislabels causal axes for capability-tied features is open.
Coherence is measured by three automated signals (regex flags, NLL
under the unsteered model, residual-stream norm); a human-rated
pass would back the contemplative-philosopher claim with inter-rater
reliability rather than illustrative quotes.

8
Conclusion

The standard SAE interpretability protocol assigns each feature a
label from its top-activating contexts and validates it by
single-feature steering. We report three findings on Qwen3-1.7B
that this protocol misses: an inverted U-shape mode-switch on #26221 that
the top-context label does not name (§
4.1
),
a joint-suppression structural role for three near-orthogonal
features that single-feature steering cannot see
(§
4.2
), and a residual-stream geometric
control showing that matched geometric distortion does not predict
matched output coherence (§
4.3
). Each finding
is a probe of a different axis of the same matrix (steering
coefficient
\times
joint-feature condition); together they
motivate a protocol-level recommendation that practice should
probe both axes before assigning a feature its label. The
empirical regime in which the argument is run, register
collapse on introspective prompts in three small post-trained
LLMs, with model-specific content and a causally locatable top
SAE feature in each, supports the pipeline’s transferability.
The matrix-level findings reproduce on Gemma-2-2B-it
(§
L
, §
K
); the Llama
matrix-level tests are not run. Whether the mode-switch
generalises beyond the two reported features (one per model) is
open.

Appendix H
Coherence quantification: signals and thresholds

Regex degeneration flags.

We flag a completion as degenerate if any of: token-level word
loops (
\geq 5
consecutive repetitions of a word), character-level
loops (
\geq 20
consecutive identical characters), shorter than

20
characters of stripped text, or low word-diversity (
<\!45\%

unique words among
\geq 12
-word completions).

Per-token NLL under the unsteered model.

For each steered completion, we re-tokenise prompt
+
completion,
forward through the unsteered model with the steering hook
removed, and compute

\mathrm{NLL}=-\sum_{t}\log p(\mathrm{tok}_{t}\mid\mathrm{tok}_{<t})/|T_{\text{comp}}|

over completion positions. Lower NLL
\Rightarrow
steered output
is more predictable to the unsteered baseline. Joint suppression
at
c{=}{-}500
has mean NLL
1.41
versus baseline
0.32

(
4.4\times
), capturing the placeholder-text degradation invisible
to regex flags.

Residual-stream norm probe.

For a fixed prompt and sequence of coefficients, we forward
(without sampling) through the model with the steering hook
installed, capture the layer-
L
