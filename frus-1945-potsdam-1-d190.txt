No. 190 The Acting Secretary of State to the Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant)

[Extract][1]

secret

Washington, July 6, 1945—6 p.m.

5517. …

. . . . . . .

You may accordingly tell FonOff that … at forthcoming highest-level discussions we intend to press strongly proposals for implementation of Yalta Declaration[2] and reorganization of ACC’s in all three ex-satellites. We shall oppose proposals to establish diplomatic relations or conclude peace with present Govts there. …

Grew
