Dear Admiral Turner:

Walter N, Read of Haddonfield, New Jersey, the
President-Elect of the New Jersey State Bar Association,
and I had the privilege of hearing your address to the
National Conference of Bar Presidents of the American
Bar Association in Dallas this August. We were deeply
impressed by your remarks. The purpose of this letter is
to invite you to be the key speaker at the Annual Dinner
of the New Jersey State Bar Association scheduled for
Saturday evening, May 17, 1980 in Atlantic City, New Jersey.
The Annual Dinner is normally attended by 450 members of
the New Jersey State Bar Association and their spouses.

We hope that your busy schedule will permit you to
accept our invitation. President-Elect Read and I know
that the members of the New Jersey State Bar Association

would find your remarks most pertinent and interesting.
