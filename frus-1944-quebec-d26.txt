The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to Prime Minister Churchill[1]

top secret

[Washington,] August 19, 1944.

Personal and secret to the Prime Minister from Harry L. Hopkins.

The President wishes to discuss military requirements for Phase II with you at your next meeting. This means that this matter is in status quo here for the present.

I am looking forward to seeing you and hope you are well.

Harry

Source: Hopkins Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Channel of transmission not indicated. This message was sent in reply to Churchill’s telegram of August 10, 1944, ante, p. 16.
