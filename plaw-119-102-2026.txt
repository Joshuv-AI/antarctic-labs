Public Law 119–102: To amend the Agricultural Credit Act of 1978 to remove barriers to agricultural producers in accessing funds to carry out emergency measures under the emergency conservation program, and for other purposes.

An Act To amend the Agricultural Credit Act of 1978 to remove barriers to agricultural producers in accessing funds to carry out emergency measures under the emergency conservation program, and for other purposes.July 12, 2026[S. 629]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency Conservation Program Improvement Act of 2025”.

SEC. 2. — IMPROVING THE EMERGENCY CONSERVATION PROGRAM.

PARAGRAPH (1)

in subsection (b)—

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

in the subsection heading, by inserting “and Other Emergency Conservation Measures” after “Fencing”;

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

in paragraph (1)—

CLAUSE (i)

by inserting “or for other emergency measures to replace or restore farmland or conservation structures requiring an immediate response (as determined by the Secretary),” after “replacement of fencing,”; and

CLAUSE (ii)

by striking “option of receiving” and all that follows through the period at the end and inserting the following: “option of receiving, before the agricultural producer carries out the repair, replacement, or restoration—with respect to a payment to the agricultural producer for a replacement, 75 percent of the cost of the replacement, as determined by the Secretary; and with respect to a payment to the agricultural producer for a repair or restoration, 50 percent of the cost of the repair or restoration, as determined by the Secretary.” ; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (C)

in paragraph (2), by striking “60-day” and inserting “180-day”; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

by adding at the end the following:A wildfire that causes damage eligible for a payment under subsection (a) includes—a wildfire that is not caused naturally, if the damage is caused by the spread of the wildfire due to natural causes; and a wildfire that is caused by the Federal Government.” .140 STAT. 986

SEC. 3. — IMPROVING THE EMERGENCY FOREST RESTORATION PROGRAM.

PARAGRAPH (1)

in subsection (a)(2), by striking “wildfires,” and inserting “wildfires (including a wildfire that is not caused naturally, if the damage is caused by the spread of the wildfire due to natural causes, and a wildfire that is caused by the Federal Government),”;

PARAGRAPH (2)

by redesignating subsection (e) as subsection (f); and

PARAGRAPH (3)

by inserting after subsection (d) the following:The Secretary shall give an owner of nonindustrial private forest land the option of receiving, before the owner carries out emergency measures under this section, not more than 75 percent of the cost of the emergency measures, as determined by the Secretary. Time period.If the funds provided under paragraph (1) are not expended by the end of the 180-day period beginning on the date on which the owner of nonindustrial private forest land receives those funds, the funds shall be returned within a reasonable timeframe, as determined by the Secretary.” .
