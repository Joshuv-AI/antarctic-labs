g. The meeting will conclude with adjournment of the Saturday morn-
ing business session. The Tower Lounge, however, will be open all day
Saturday, incliKiing during the normal cocktail hour, and may be held open
on Sunday, dependent upon the number of people who are staying over.

h. There will be a men's golf tournament Friday afternoon on The
Homestead Course with an entry fee of $15.00 for prizes to be paid to the
Club Pro by Council members before teeing off. The Club Pro will assist
members and guests wishing to make up foursomes. It is important that
starting times be made well in advance. Arrangements for ladies' golf are
spelled out in the enclosed ladies' schedule.

i. Separate tennis tournaments for ladies and gentlemen will be held
on Friday, arranged with the help of the Tennis Pro, Mr. Pat Haynes. For
the men's play on Friday afternoon a $10.00 entry fee should be paid to the
Pro by Council members . Ladies are asked to refer to the enclosed schedule
for details on their play.

j . A hunter-style breakfast will be served in the Dominion Room on
Friday and Saturday mornings at 7:30 a.m. This is designed to provide fast
and convenient service to all who will be attending the morning business
sessions in the Commonwealth Room.

k. For those attending the morning business sessions, coffee service
will be available during the meeting breaks in the adjoining Dominion Room.
Telephones installed on the upper level of the Commonwealth Room can be used
during the breaks for outgoing calls only.

l. Dr. James Hamsberger will be in residence at The Homestead for the
duration of our meeting. His room number will be posted on the bulletin
board in the Tower Lounge. Anyone who has need of his medical services can
reach him in his room or through the hotel switchboard.

m. Attached is a separate schedule giving complete details on all
events for the ladies.

n. The Homestead telephone number is 703, 839-5500. Our office will be
in the Valley Room, extensions 2646 and 2647. The Business Council secre-
taries are Miss Jean Carter and Miss M ison Wells.

o. IT WOULD BE VERY MUCH APPRECIATED IF MEMBERS WOULD ADVISE MR. OR
MRS. BURKE OF ANY LAST-MINUTE CHANGES IN THEIR FRIDAY EVENING DINNER PLANS
OR DEPARTURE TIMES. MESSAGES MAY BE LEFT AT THE FROOT DESK, IN THE COUNCIL
OFFICE, OR IN THE BURKES' ROOM (1126).
