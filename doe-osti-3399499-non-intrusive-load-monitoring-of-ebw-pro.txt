 Objective

Main Study Objective: Determine if induced off-nominal
disturbances can be identified within EBW machine input power
data, aka Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM)
Preliminary Testing Objective: Gain familiarity with EBW
diagnostic signals and observe relationship between EBW
diagnostic signals and EBW machine input power at nominal
conditions

 Conclusions

 During electron beam profiling tests, and obvious correlation
 exists between input power and electron beam power

 EBW diagnostic signals have specific input impedance
 requirements and cover a large range of voltages. The
 PIPPES analog input channels must be more flexible to
 handle the various inputs

 15 of 16

 Future Work

 Modify PIPPES to accept all EBW diagnostic signals
 – Higher voltage signals

 – Signals with specific input impedance requirements


 Collect data while making welds under nominal conditions

 Collect data while making welds under off-nominal conditions

 Look for correlations between input power and EBW diagnostic
 signals for welds made under off-nominal conditions
