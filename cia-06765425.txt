(U) 1. ENDEAVORING TO DEVELOP LONG-RANGE MISSILES WITH A RANGE OF 1,000

(U) 2. OBTAINING MODERN SURVEY EQUIPMENT.

(U) 6. OBTAINING HIGHLY EFFICIENT TELECOMMUNICATION SYSTEM.

(U) 7. TACKLING THE PROBLEM OF OLD TRANSPORTATION VEHICLES, NOT SUITABLE
FOR MOVING LAUNCHERS.

(U) 8. MEETING THE NEED TO INCREASE THE RANGE OF OPERATIONAL MISSILES TO 150
KILOMETERS.

(U) 9. TACKLING TRAINING AND MANAGEMENT PROBLEMS, SUCH AS:
(U) 10. THE LACK OF TRAINING EQUIPMENT AND DECOYS FOR THE MISSILE SYSTEM.

(U) 11. THE LACK OF TRAINING MOVIES. WHAT WE HAD WERE A FEW VIDEOS WHICH
WERE NOT AS GOOD THEATER MOVIES.

(U) 12. THE PROBLEM OF PROVIDING HOUSING FOR THE MISSILE SECTORS AND THE
MISSILE SCHOOL.

(U) 13. THE PROBLEM OF SECURING THE NEEDS OF THE SPECIAL UNITS.

(U) 14. THE PROBLEM OF THE SPECIAL CADRES; THAT IS, VOLUNTEERS FOR
SPECIALIZED ASSIGNMENTS.
