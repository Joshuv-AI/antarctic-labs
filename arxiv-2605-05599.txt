2
Entropy on surface with boundary

In this section, we generalize some results established in
[
12
]
to pseudo-harmonic-Ricci flow. Specifically, we introduce the modified entropy

\begin{split}E_{\partial}(t)=\int_{M}S\log S\mathrm{d}v-\log(\bar{S})\int_{M}S\mathrm{d}v,\end{split}

(2.1)

provided that
S>0.

Theorem 1
.

Let
(M,g(t),\phi),t\in[0,T)
be a pseudo-harmonic-Ricci flow with
S>0
on compact surface with boundary
\partial M
,
R(g_{0})>0.
We further assume the Neumann type boundary condition
\frac{\partial R}{\partial\boldsymbol{n}}=0,\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial\boldsymbol{n}}=0
. Let f be a smooth function on
M
solving

\displaystyle\begin{cases}S+\Delta f=\bar{S}\\
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\boldsymbol{n}}=0,\end{cases}

then

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E_{\partial}(t)

\displaystyle=-\int_{M}\alpha\left\lVert\nabla\phi(\nabla f)\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v

\displaystyle-\int_{M}S\left\lVert\nabla S-\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}-2\int_{M}\left\lVert\nabla^{2}f-\frac{\Delta f}{2}g\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v

\displaystyle-\int_{\partial M}\Pi(\nabla f|_{\partial M},\nabla f|_{\partial M})\mathrm{d}A.

In particular, if the boundary is geodesic convex, then the entropy
E_{\partial}(t)
is non-decreasing.

Remark 1
.

Should the conditon of pseudo-harmonic-Ricci flow be replaced by harmonic Ricci flow, the Theorem may no longer hold.

Proof.

Adjusting the proof of
[
12
]
to our setting, we have

\begin{split}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E_{\partial}(t)&=\int_{M}\partial_{t}S(1+\log S)-S^{2}\log S\,\mathrm{d}v-\partial_{t}(\log\bar{S}\int_{M}S\,\mathrm{d}v)\\
&=\int_{M}(\Delta S+2\left\lVert Sc\right\rVert^{2}+2\alpha\left\lVert\tau(\phi)\right\rVert^{2})(1+\log S)-S^{2}\log S\mathrm{d}v-v(M)\bar{S}^{2}\,\\
&=\int_{M}(\Delta S\log S+2\left\lVert S\right\rVert^{2})\mathrm{d}v+\int_{M}2\alpha\left\lVert\tau(\phi)\right\rVert^{2}(1+\log S)\mathrm{d}v-v(M)\bar{S}^{2}\,\\
&=\int_{M}(-S\left\lVert\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}+2\left\lVert S\right\rVert^{2})\mathrm{d}v+\int_{M}2\alpha\left\lVert\tau(\phi)\right\rVert^{2}(1+\log S)\mathrm{d}v-v(M)\bar{S}^{2}.\end{split}

Observe that

\displaystyle\int_{M}\left\lVert S\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v=\int_{M}(\Delta f)^{2}\mathrm{d}v+v(M)\bar{S}^{2}.

Using this, we deduce that

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E_{\partial}(t)=-\int_{M}(S\left\lVert\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}-2\left\lVert\Delta f\right\rVert^{2})\mathrm{d}v+\int_{M}2\alpha\left\lVert\tau(\phi)\right\rVert^{2}(1+\log S)\mathrm{d}v.

By integration by parts and using
f_{\nu}=0
, we get

\begin{split}&\quad\,\,\int_{M}S\|\nabla f\|^{2}-2(\Delta f)^{2}+S\|\nabla\log S\|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}v\\
&=\int_{M}S\|\nabla f\|^{2}+2g(\nabla f,\nabla\Delta f)+S\|\nabla\log S\|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}v\\
&=\int_{M}\,S\|\nabla f-\nabla\log S\|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}v.\end{split}

(2.2)

Furthermore, using Reilly formula gives

\int_{M}\,2\left(\Delta f\right)^{2}-R\|\nabla f\|^{2}-2\left\|\nabla^{2}f\right\|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}v=2\int_{\partial M}\,\left(\nabla_{\partial M}(f|_{\partial M}),\nabla_{\partial M}(f|_{\partial M})\right)\,\mathrm{d}A.

(2.3)

Adding (
2.2
) and (
2.3
), we conclude that

\displaystyle\int_{M}(S\left\lVert\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}-2\left\lVert\nabla^{2}f\right\rVert^{2}-\alpha\left\lVert\nabla\phi\right\rVert^{2}\left\lVert\nabla f\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\int_{M}S\left\lVert\nabla S-\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v-\int_{\partial M}\Pi(\nabla f|_{\partial M},\nabla f|_{\partial M})\mathrm{d}A.

Combining these yields

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}E_{\partial}(t)=

\displaystyle\int_{M}2\left\lVert\Delta f\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v+\int_{M}2\alpha\left\lVert\tau(\phi)\right\rVert^{2}(1+\log S)\mathrm{d}v

\displaystyle\int_{M}-2\left\lVert\nabla^{2}f\right\rVert^{2}-\alpha\left\lVert\nabla\phi\right\rVert^{2}\left\lVert\nabla f\right\rVert^{2}\mathrm{d}v

\displaystyle-\int_{M}S\left\lVert\nabla S-\nabla\log S\right\rVert^{2}\,\mathrm{d}v-\int_{\partial M}\Pi(\nabla f|_{\partial M},\nabla f|_{\partial M})\,\mathrm{d}A.

It is now clearly that the claim of the Theorem follows immediately.
\hfill\Box

3
Perelman type
\mathcal{F}
-entropy

Perelman’s
F

\displaystyle F(g,f)=\int_{M}(R+\left\lVert\nabla f\right\rVert^{2})e^{-f}\mathrm{d}v,

Later,m

F

W

\begin{split}\mathcal{F}(g,\phi,f)=\int_{M}(R-\alpha\left\lVert\nabla\phi\right\rVert^{2}+\left\lVert\nabla f\right\rVert^{2})e^{-f}\mathrm{d}v,\end{split}

on
m

Moreover, they established monotonicity formulas of the functionals just mentioned. In this paper we try to extend these results to the case of surface with boundary.

In the case of Ricci flow on surface with boundary, there is already some progress on surface with boundary, such as
