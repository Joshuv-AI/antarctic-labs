SINGLE AXIS TRACKER OPTIMIZATION

Analysis

• On sunny days, the angle at which a SAT could achieve
 maximum irradiance (green line) is close to the ordinary
 tracking angle (blue line)

• On cloudy days, the angle at which the SAT achieves
 maximum irradiance fluctuates wildly

• We can devise a tracking algorithm (red line) which
 switches between “regular” tracking and horizontal
 depending on the presence of clouds as found in a sky
 image

Findings

• We can generate more energy with a SAT by
 modifying the tracking algorithm based on sky imagery

 • On the order of 0% to 0.4% annually

• Modifying tracking is most useful in locations where
 the sun is often behind a cloud and less useful in
 primarily sunny locations

 • Sandia-led, US DOE-funded research investigation to
 improve the reliability of the US solar sector
 • Rationale:
 § Connectors are ubiquitous and essential
 components for PV systems
 § Operate under demanding conditions and cost
 pressure
 § Intended to last the system lifetime, but…
 § Failures are widespread!
 § Consequences include Ground faults, downtime,
 and FIRES
 § Industry-wide data almost non-existent
 • What Sandia brings to the table:
 § Deep technical expertise, including materials
 characterization, forensics analysis, experimental
 and field inspection expertise
 § Non-biased center for data analysis and reporting
 § Proven track-record of data confidentiality
 § Close connection to standards bodies

• Many potential stressors (climate, age, model,
 installation errors, current, etc.

• Lots of data needed to deconvolute variables and for a
 principal variable analysis

• Plant-wide data at the national-scale is needed for
 accurate statistics, lifetime models, early alerts of
 potential issues and to support technological innovation,
 especially automated field monitoring

• Quantify the frequency & severity of PV connector
 failures across the US

• Collect and leverage PV plant data to:
 • Identify root causes of connector failure
 • Identify principal factors (climate, model, field
 assembly, cross-mating, system current)
 • Develop predictive failure models
 • Quantify the economic impact of connector
 failures, from lost generation to litigation
 • Inform best practices and new standards for
 connector safety and reliability

 • “MC4 compatible” is not standardized
 • Staubli MC4™ is the market leader
 • Cross-mating dissimilar connector models represents
 the greatest risk to PV modules according to TÜV
 Rheinland

 • Most connectors were “normal”
 • Tight wire bends were the most prevalent
 installation issue (2.3%, 1.3%)
 • Loose nuts were 24 times more likely in field-
 assembled connectors
