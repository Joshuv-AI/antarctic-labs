SECTION II

NATIONAL SECURITY 3 NATIONAL POLICY AND STRATEGIC PLANNING
1. ORGANIZATION

a. purpose : The purpose of this section is to provide an understanding
of national security, policy, planning, and strategy, the instruments
available to meet national security objectives, how they are
employed, the intelligence role in support of national security,

and a survey of national security objectives and measures.

b. Scope ; The section will include examination of the following:

(1) The meaning of national security.

(2) The instruments of national policy: h’hat they are and how
they are employed.

(3) The National Security organization of the Government and how
it works.

(U) The formulation and execution of national policy.

( 5 ) Strategic planning.

(6) The role of intelligence in support of national security,
national policy and strategic planning.

(7) A survey of established national security policies and
objectives.

(8) A survey of established national security measures: Their
objectives and effects.

c. Lectures : There will be a total of 30 lectures, each dealing with
basic topics relating to the intelligence process in support of
national security.
