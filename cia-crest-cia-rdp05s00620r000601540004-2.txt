Dear Mr. Baratz:

I appreciate your prompt response to my verbal undertaking
to participate in the panel discussion on the intelligence agencies
and the academic community in New Haven on 10 June 1978. Thank you
for the invitation to lunch with Professor Peter Steiner, which I
am pleased to accept, though with the following proviso.

In my reply to the informal invitation from your office I said
that there was a roughly twenty percent chance that I might not be
able to meet the engagement. I have also ascertained that Mr.

Anthony Lapham, our General Counsel, who was designated by Mr. Knight
of your office as the replacement you would prefer, will be unable to
serve. I regret having to say so, but I really do believe that if I
should. not be available at the time it is better that the Intelligence
Community not be represented on your panel. Having been concerned with
the scheduling of such events myself, I appreciate the awkward position
this leaves you in. I am afraid, however, that I cannot improve upon
it. Perhaps it would be better for you to withdraw thp invif-atinn tn —
me and resign yourself to an all-academic discussion

If that should be your choice I shall under-

stand and remain grateful to you for the invitation.

Sincerely,

STANSFIELD TURNER

Mr. Morton S. Baratz, General Secretary
American Association of University Professors
One Dupont Circle, Suite 500
Washington, D. C. 20036

Dear Admiral Turner:

I am delighted that you will he able to
join us in New Haven for our panel discussion on
the intelligence agencies and the academic community.
As you know, Morton Halperin has also agreed to
participate on the panel, and we hope that
President Bok of Harvard, or another eminent
university administrator, will complete the panel.

Your schedule, I realize, is quite crowded,
but there is no difficulty for us in arranging to
have the panel convene at 10:45 in the morning to
suit your schedule. If at all possible, could you
join Peter Steiner, Professor of Law and Economics
at the University of Michigan and current President
of the Association, and me for lunch after the panel
discussion?

I look forward to ^meeting you in Hew Haven.

incerely
