President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (11 August 1944, d25)

top secret priority

[U.S.S. “Cummings”,[2]] 11 August 1944.

Number 597 personal and top secret from the President for the Former Naval Person.

Your 750[3] is received at sea. I also deeply regret my inability to come to Scotland for this meeting.

It appears now that I should be able to arrive in Quebec on the 10th or 11th of September for a meeting of the Combined Staff on the Teheran scale.

My consultations with the military and naval commanders in the Pacific area have been most interesting and valuable.[4]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House Map Room as telegram No. Blue 106; forwarded to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Roosevelt was en route from Auk Bay, Alaska, to Bremerton, Washington. See ante, p. 10, fn. 1.
[3] Ante, p. 15.
[4] Concerning Roosevelt’s conferences with MacArthur and Nimitz, July 27–28, 1944, see Matloff, p. 482; Leahy, pp. 250–251.
