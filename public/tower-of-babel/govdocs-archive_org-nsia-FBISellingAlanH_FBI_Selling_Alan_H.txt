FBI Informant's Story of
Portland, Ore.
A onetime informant for the
Portland FBI office says he was
directed by his contact agents in
the bureau to enter the home of a
local attorney illegally in search of
information on the attorney’s son, a
fugitive member of the Weather
Underground.
Alan H. Selling, 23, told the Los
Angeles Times he first went to
work for the FBI to spy on the
Socialist Workers party but was
channeled into the other activity
after agents learned he was a
distant relative of the David M.
Spiegel family.
Michael Spiegel, 30, has been
sought by the FBI since 1970 on
various charges stemming from a
Chicago riot involving the Weather
Underground.
