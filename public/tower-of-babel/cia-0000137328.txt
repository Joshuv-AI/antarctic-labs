IV.

CUBA

US Embassy in Havana sees no hope that US will ever be able to
establish a Satisfactory relationship with any Cuban government
dominated by Fidel Castro and his associates.

A. Embassy believes that government charge of US responsibility
for ship explosion in Havana harbor, coupled with other
hostile acts and mounting anti-US frenzy in government con-
trolled mass media, indicate deliberate attempt to precipitate
a crisis and to force US to take drastic measures.

Anti-American feeling being whipped up by constant government propa-

ganda, having some effect ee Cubans previously cool to Castro

diatribes,

An incident could cause an outbreak of violence against

American personnel and interests in this highly tense Situation.

A. Embassy has invoked preparatory, limited Warning phases
of evacuation plan. Some classified material is being re-
moved from the Embassy and a blanket evacuation travel order
for Embassy personnel and dependents is being prepared for
possible use,

Castro is reliably reported to be Planning a demand for the evacua-

tion of the US Naval Base at Guantanamo Bay with a 30 6 45 day

deadline.

A. Demand would be presented as soon as legislation adversely
affecting US Sugar purchases is introduced into the uS Congress,

which Castro believes imminent.

B. If US refuses to evacuate, Cuba might then take issue to the

United Nations.

VI.

Abusive personal attacks on Pres Eisenhower and Secretary Herter by

government controlled radio and press are continuing,

A.

Radio Mambi, the most vicious of the radio Stations called
President Eisenhower "slavery's Spokesman, the champion of the
threatening atom bomb...who had the effrontery to talk of
democracy to exploited people and is now stained with the
blood /of Uruguayan students/...' Claimed the president's
trip was to form a political cordon in LA to isolate Cuba.
Chilean President Alessandri was recently called a US puppet
and Argentine President Frondizi “an unhappy comedian...ready
to sell his soul to the US devil."

Radio Mambi also said newsmen had to be beaten by police in the
countries the President visited to keep them from showing
photographs of how the people repudiated Eisenhower,
Government mouthpiece, the daily Revolucion ,editorially
charged after the 4 March explosion that a favorite tactic

of the US is to manufacture incidents in order to mobilize
public opinion for an attack on another country. "This was
the technique of the Alamo, the Maine, the Lusitania, and

Pearl Harbor,"

Castro and Guevara continue to condemn foreign and private invest-

ment. They will dominate the newly formed Central Planning Board,

which will supervise and control every phase of Cuba's econony,

particularly industry.

A.

Decision by US companies like Moa Bay Nickel and Bethlehem Steel

to close operations this month will probably be followed rapidly

by government intervention. Castro regime will increasingly

dominate basic industries as well as agriculture and turn to

USSR and European countries for technical advice and supplies.
