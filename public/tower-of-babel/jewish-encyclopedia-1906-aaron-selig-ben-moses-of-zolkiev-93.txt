Author; flourished in the seventeenth century. He wrote "'Amude Sheba'" (Seven Pillars) containing: (1) Commentaries and glosses on the old and the new Zohar, explaining the foreign words therein; (2) treatises not included in the old Zohar; (3) references where commentaries on the treatises of the old and the new Zohar can be found; (4) similar indications of the book "Tiḳḳunim"; (5) remarks on the style of the Zohar. The work was published at Cracow in 1637.

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 443; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 26; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. No. 4371. J. L. S.
