2. ACCORDING TO AN ARENA MEMBER
AS OF EARLY MAY 1984, THE ARENA
PARAMILITARY UNIT HEADED BY HECTOR ((REGALADO)), FORMER CHIEF OF
SECURITY FOR D'AUBUISSON, WAS INTACT AND FUNCTIONING. HE SAID
THAT MEMBERS OF REGALADO'S UNIT WERE MAINTAINING SMALL ARMS
PROFICIENCY AT A PRIVATE FIRING RANGE IN THE NATIONAL STADIUM IN SAN
SALVADOR AND CHECKING OUT COMMUNICATIONS EQUIPMENT. THE ARENA MEMBER
CLAIMED THAT REGALADO WAS ARRANGING THE PURCHASE IN THE UNITED
STATES OF APPROXIMATELY 30 INGRAM SUBMACHINE GUNS, IN 9MM AND 45
CALIBRE. REGALADO, ACCORDING TO THE ARENA MEMBER, PERSONALLY
SOLICITS FUNDS FOR HIS OPERATIONS FROM ARENA SYMPATHIZERS BUT DIVERTS
SOME OF THESE FUNDS FOR HIS PERSONAL USE, A PRACTICE OF WHICH
D'AUBUISSON IS ALLEGED TO BE AWARE. THE ARENA MEMBER ADDED THAT
ARTURO ((DUENAS)) REGULARLY SUPPLIES REGALADO AND OTHERS WITH A
VARIETY OF OFFICIAL SALVADORAN DOCUMENTS.

3. THE ARENA MEMBER STATED THAT ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT
MEMBERS IN REGALADO'S PARAMILITARY UNIT IS RICARDO ((ROGEL)) WHO
HANDLLS "ERNESTO," "LUIS" AND "MARIO," THREE SALVADORANS FORMERLY
INVOLVED WITH THE INSURGENTS. THE ARENA MEMBER SAID THAT A REGULAR
PARTICIPANT IN REGALADQ'S UNIT IS SALVATORI ((VENUTOLO)) , WHOM HE
DESCRIBED AS YOUNG, BLOND AND FAIR-COMPLEXIONED. WITHOUT ELABORATING
FURTHER, THE ARENA MEMBER SAID THAT TWO CLOSE ASSOCIATES OF REGALADO,
AMADEO ((MARTINEZ)) AND JORGE ((BAZBEK)), ARE PROBABLY MEMBERS OF
REGALADO'S PARAMILITARY UNIT AS WELL. THE ARENA MEMBER ADDED THAT
PATRICIA ((MARTINO)), WHO WAS RUMORED TO BE REGALADO'S GIRLFRIEND,
WAS A MEMBER OF THE PARAMILITARY UNIT WHO DISAPPEARED UNDER
MYSTERIOUS CIRCUMSTANCES AROUND APRIL/MAY 1983.

4- ACCORDING TO THE ARENA MEMBER, AMONG REGALADO'S CLOSEST
COLLABORATORS IN THE SALVADORAN MILITARY AND SECURITY SERVICES ARE
POLICE INSPECTOR ARNOLDO ((LINARES)) AND SALVADORAN ARMY LIEUTENANT
DAVID ((KOCH)). THE ARENA MEMBER STATED THAT LINARES IS A KEY MEMBER
OF THE SALVADORAN NATIONAL POLICE'S (PN) "DEATH SQUAD," THE
INTELLIGENCE ANALYSIS CENTER (CAIN) WHICH THE ARENA MEMBER CLAIMED TO
BE MUCH MORE ACTIVE THAN REGALADO'S UNIT. THE ARENA MEMBER SAID THAT
KOCH IS A MEMBER OF EITHER THE BELLOSO OR ATLACATL BATTALION OF THE
SALVADORAN ARMY. HE ADDED THAT LIEUTENANT COLONEL ARISTIDES
((MARQUEZ)), FORMER PN D-2 WHO WAS ASSIGNED ABROAD IN EARLY 1983, WAS
ALSO A CLOSE COLLABORATOR OF REGALADO'S UNIT.

5. THE ARENA MEMBER SAID THAT A GUATEMALAN CLOSELY INVOLVED WITH
REGALADO'S PARAMILITARY UNIT IS LUIS ((MENDIZABAL)), OWNER OF
BOUTIQUE EMILO ON FIFTH AVENUE IN GUATEMALA CITY. ACCORDING TO THE
ARENA MEMBER, MENDIZABAL'S ASSISTANCE TO REGALADO'S OPERATIONS
INCLUDES THE PROCUREMENT OF WEAPONS. THE ARENA MEMBER SAID THAT
ROLANDO ((MATAHU)), A GUATEMALAN PARAMILITARY SPECIALIST, HAS ALSO
WORKED CLOSELY WITH REGALADO. IN THE GUATEMALAN SECURITY SERVICES,
THE ARENA MEMBER SAID ONE OF REGALADO'S CLOSEST SUPPORTERS WAS
GENERAL GERMAN ((CHUPINA)) BARAHONA, FORMER POLICE CHIEF UNDER THE
LUCAS ADMINISTRATION. THE ARENA MEMBER ADDED THAT (FNU) ((GUIDEL)),
ALIAS: "EL GATO," WHO WAS KILLED WHILE IN PRISON, AND. AP
((AREDONDO)) WERE CLOSE REGALADO COLLABORATORS.

COMMENT: ACCORDING TO A RIGHTIST WITH EXCELLENT ACCESS TO OFFICIALS
OF ARENA AND WHOSE REPORTING IS GENERALLY RELIABLE, MENDIZABAL IS A
LONGTIME FRIEND OF REGALADO AND A GUN SUPPLIER FOR ARENA. HE HAS
WORKED CLOSELY WITH REGALADO IN THE PAST ON DEATH SQUAD ACTIVITIES
BOTH IN EL SAL VADOR AND GUATEMALA. HE IS MARRIED TO THE SISTER OF
LUIS FERNANDO ((PIVERAL)), THE OWNER OF A LARGE FARM CALLED "LA
SONRISA" (THE SMILE), A KNOWN HAVEN FOR RIGHTISTS. MENDIZABAL
REPORTEDLY ALSO HAS LONGTIME LINKS WITH D'AUBUISSON. )

WAS NOT A MEMBER OF REGALADO'S PARAMILITARY UNIT.
