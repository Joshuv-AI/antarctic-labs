Acoustic limit of Boltzmann equations for gas mixture Thanks:
Gaofeng Wang, Tianfang Wu and Linjie Xiong

Abstract
. In this paper, we study the hydrodynamic and acoustic limit from Boltzmann equations for two species gas mixture with potential γ ∈ ( − 3 , 1 ] \gamma\in\left(-3,1\right] . Here the particle masses are different which derives to the loss of symmetry to the linearized collision operator. We construct the hydrodynamic limit for two species based on the Hilbert expansion method when the Knudsen number is small. The key observation is the precise properties of the linearized collision operators, including the extra operators due to the different particle masses ( m A ≠ m B ) (m^{A}\neq m^{B}) . In additional, the acoustic limit of the Boltzmann equations for gas mixtures is rigorously justified by assuming the strength of the initial data depends on the Knudsen number. Keywords . Compressible Euler equations; Acoustic system; Boltzmann equations of gas mixture; Different particle masses. AMS subject classifications: 35B38, 35J47

We consider the Boltzmann equations for two species particles

Here

The collision kernels

is assumed to satisfy the following assumptions:

(1) The symmetry of collision kernels

B^{\alpha\beta}(|v-v_{*}|,\theta)=B^{\beta\alpha}(|v_{*}-v|,\theta)\,\quad\alpha,\beta\in\{A,B\}.

(2) The collision kernels are decomposed into

B^{\alpha\beta}(|v-v_{*}|,\theta)=\Phi^{\alpha\beta}(|v-v_{*}|)b^{\alpha\beta}(\cos\theta)\,\quad\alpha,\beta\in\{A,B\}.

Here the kinetic part takes

\Phi^{\alpha\beta}(|v-v_{*}|)=C_{\alpha\beta}^{\Phi}|v-v_{*}|^{\gamma},\,C_{\alpha\beta}^{\Phi}>0,\,\gamma\in(-3,1],

The properties of the collision operator.

For

,
we introduce the bilinear Boltzmann collision operator

\mathscr{C}\mathbf{F}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}Q^{AA}(F^{A},F^{A})+Q^{AB}(F^{A},F^{B})\\

The operator

has the following properties

Collision invariant
A function

is called a collision invariant of the operator

with respect to the inner product in

\langle\mathscr{C}\mathbf{F},\mathbf{\Psi}\rangle_{(L_{v}^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3}))^{2}}=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in{A,B}}\left\langle Q^{\alpha\beta}(F^{\alpha},F^{\beta}),\Psi^{\alpha}\right\rangle_{L_{v}^{2}}=0\quad\text{for any vector }\mathbf{F}.

(1.4)

It derives to

\mathbf{\Psi}\in\text{Span}\{\mathbf{e}_{1},\mathbf{e}_{2},v_{1}\mathbf{m},v_{2}\mathbf{m},v_{3}\mathbf{m},|v|^{2}\mathbf{m}\},

(1.5)

where
\mathbf{m}=(m^{A},m^{B})^{T}

and
\mathbf{e}_{j}

is the
j^{\text{th}}

unit vector in
\mathbb{R}^{2}
.

H-theorem
The entropy function of the two-species Boltzmann equations satisfies

\left\langle\mathscr{C}\mathbf{F},\log\mathbf{F}\right\rangle_{(L_{v}^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3}))^{2}}=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in\{A,B\}}\left\langle Q^{\alpha\beta}(F^{\alpha},F^{\beta}),~~\log F^{\alpha}\right\rangle_{L_{v}^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})}\leq 0,

(1.6)

and this equality holds if and only if

\mathbf{F}=\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}\frac{n^{A}(m^{A})^{3/2}}{(2\pi\theta)^{3/2}}e^{-\frac{m^{A}|v-\mathbf{u}|^{2}}{2\theta}}\\

3.
Acoustic Limit

The acoustic system is the linearization around the uniform equilibrium (1,1,0,1) of this compressible Euler system for mixed fluids. Its fluid fluctuations
(\sigma^{A},\sigma^{B},\mathbf{u},\theta)
satisfy the following linear system

\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}\partial_{t}\sigma^{A}+\nabla_{x}\cdot\mathbf{u}=0,\\[5.69054pt]

where
