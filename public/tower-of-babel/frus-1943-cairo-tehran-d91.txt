The President to the Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant)[1] (2 November 1943, d91)

secret priority

[Hyde Park?] 2 November 1943.

Personal and secret from the President to Winant.

You are absolutely right. I will let you know as soon as date can be arranged and you would meet us in North Africa.

It will be good to see you again.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attach, London, via the White House Map Room and Navy channels.
