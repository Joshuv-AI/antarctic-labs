Bohemian Talmudist; died at Prague in 1829. He wrote: "Dibre Meir," novellæ, on the Talmudic treatises Giṭṭin, Shabbat, Rosh ha-Shanah, and Baba Meẓi'a (Prague, 1810); "Sbemen ha-Ma'or," commentary on the Shulḥan 'Aruk, Oraḥ Ḥayyim, and more especially on its two commentaries, Magen Abraham and Ṭure Zahab ( ib. 1816). He also published his father's "Mar'eh Yeḥezḳel" ( ib. 1822). Glogauer died suddenly while delivering a eulogy on Mordecai Benet (see preface to responsum "Goren Dawid," Paks, 1885).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 336; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1700. D. M. Sel.
