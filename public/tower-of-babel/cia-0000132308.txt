According to TASS, Soviet Defense Minister Dmitriy Yazov
told the Soviet parliament that the entire Soviet air defense
system needed a review. He was responding to questions by
legislators Thursday about the failure of the largely Soviet-
trained and equipped Iraqi air defense force against coalition
air attacks. Other Soviet officials have attempted to deflect
doubts over Soviet air defense effectiveness by noting that
Irag’s equipment was old, poorly manned and included non-Soviet
weapons. They have stressed that coalition air forces
outnumbered those of Iraq and had the advantage of surprise. |
