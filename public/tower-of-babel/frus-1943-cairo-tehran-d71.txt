President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (29 October 1943, d71)

secret priority

[Washington,] 29 October 1943.

To the Former Naval Person from the President, Number 401. Secret and personal.

My immediately preceding message. Code word Sextant is accepted.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
