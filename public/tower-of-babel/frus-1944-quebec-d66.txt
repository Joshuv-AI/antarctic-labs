The President to the Secretary of the Treasury (Morgenthau)[1]

[Quebec,] 12 September 1944.

Please be in Quebec by Thursday, 14 September, Noon.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House Map Room at Washington. The message was delivered to Morgenthau at his farm at Fishkill, New York, by a Secret Service agent on the evening of September 12.
