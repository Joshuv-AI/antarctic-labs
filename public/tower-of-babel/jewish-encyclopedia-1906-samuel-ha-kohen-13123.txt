Rabbi of the sixteenth century. He was the author of the following works: "Derek Ḥayyim" (Constantinople, n.d.), on the 613 precepts; "Ner Miẓwah" (Venice, 1598), seventeen homilies on the thirteen articles of faith; and "Torah Or" ( ib. 1605), homilies on the Pentateuch.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 238. H. R. A. S. W.
