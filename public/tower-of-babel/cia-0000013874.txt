1. In late August 1965, the Company Commander of Company 1, Battalion 0,
Regiment 1, said that prior to the arrival of the 2nd NAM HAT Battalion 2
in SVN, Battalion lO, had captured a number of U.S, and SVN prisoners of war.
The prisoners were taken in a NVA counter-operation in QUANG NAM Province
in May 1965, and were sent tc regimental headquarters for interrogation.

; They then went on to an unknown destination.

2. The Company Commander also said that during the second battle of BA GIA,
QUANG NGAI Province, about early June 1965, Battalion 0 captured about
20 U.S. soldiers; all were sent to an unknown destination. The Company
Commander did not mention any other details.
