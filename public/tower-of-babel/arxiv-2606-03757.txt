Ulam stability for classes of nuclear C*-algebras
Vadim Alekseev, Ilijas Farah, Andreas Thom

Abstract
. We study Ulam stability for approximate ∗ * -homomorphisms of C ∗ C^{*} -algebras. We prove stability results for several classes of nuclear C ∗ C^{*} -algebras with respect to von Neumann algebra targets, including abelian C ∗ C^{*} -algebras and large classes arising in the Elliott classification program. We also discuss permanence properties, counterexamples, and related stability phenomena. As applications, we obtain rigidity and independence results for corona algebras.

Introduction

In
[
40
]
, S. Ulam defined an
\varepsilon
-homomorphism from a group
G
into a metric group
(H,d)
as a map
\varphi\colon G\to H
that satisfies

d(\varphi(gh),\varphi(g)\varphi(h))<\varepsilon

for all
g,h\in G
, and asked under what conditions such
\varepsilon
-homomorphism can be uniformly approximated by a true homomorphism.
This definition has been adapted to other algebraic structures equipped with a metric, giving rise to the very general and well-studied
concept of Ulam stability. Early on, Hyers observed that for an
\varepsilon
-homomorphism
\varphi\colon\mathfrak{X}\to\mathfrak{Y}
between Banach spaces the sequence
2^{-n}\varphi(2^{n}x)
converges to a homomorphism that
\varepsilon
-approximates
\varphi
(
[
23
]
, see also
[
22
]
).

In case of Banach space-based structures such as C*-algebras one could meaningfully weaken the definition of an
\varepsilon
-homomorphism in more than one way. B. Johnson studied Ulam stability of linear maps
\varphi
between Banach algebras that
are approximately multiplicative, in the sense that

\|\varphi(ab)-\varphi(a)\varphi(b)\|<\varepsilon\|a\|\|b\|

for all
a
and
b
in the domain
(
[
25
]
,
[
24
]
), apparently unaware of Ulam’s question.
Tangentially related is the well-studied notion of weakly stable relations on a C*-algebra (
[
30
]
); it could be considered as a local-to-local analog of Ulam stability of
*
-homomorphisms, or as the Ulam stability of equations. The study of
\varepsilon
-C*-algebras (
[

) is subtly different from Ulam stability.

Our notion of

-

\displaystyle\leq\varepsilon,

\displaystyle\|\varphi(a^{*})-\varphi(a)^{*}\|

2.
General results

The main result of this section, Corollary
2.5
, gives a convenient reformulation of Ulam stability. It is a consequence of Lemma
2.4
which shows that every
\varepsilon
-
*
-homomorphism can be uniformly approximated by a map with additional regularity properties.

Throughout, for
a,b\in A
and
\varepsilon>0
we write
a\approx_{\varepsilon}b
if
\|a-b\|\leq\varepsilon
. By
A_{1}
we denote the unit ball of the C*-algebra
A
.

Lemma 2.1
.

Suppose that
\varepsilon>0
and
\varphi\colon A\to B
is an
\varepsilon
-
*
-homomorphism, such that
\varphi(A_{\textrm{sa}})\subseteq B_{\textrm{sa}}
. Then the following holds.

(1)

Every
a\in A_{1}
satisfies
\|\varphi(a)\|\leq\|a\|+\varepsilon\|a\|
.

(2)

All
0\leq a\leq b\leq 1
satisfy
\varphi(a)\leq\varphi(b)+3\varepsilon
.

Proof.

(
1
) If
a\neq 0
then

\|\varphi(a/\|a\|)-\frac{1}{\|a\|}\varphi(a)\|\leq\varepsilon
. Since
\|\varphi(a/\|a\|)\|\leq 1
,
\|\varphi(a)\|\leq\|a\|(1+\varepsilon)
follows. Since

\|\varphi(0)-2\varphi(0)\|\leq\varepsilon
, the case when
a=0
follows.

(
2
) Let
c
be such that
a+c^{*}c=b
. Then
\varphi(b)-\varphi(a)\approx_{\varepsilon}\varphi(c^{*}c)\approx_{2\varepsilon}\varphi(c)^{*}\varphi(c)\geq 0
and the conclusion follows.
∎

Lemma 2.2
.

For all self-adjoint
a
and
b
with
\max(\|a\|,\|b\|)\leq 1
and sufficiently small
\varepsilon>0
we have that
\sup_{|t|\leq 1}\|\exp(ita)-\exp(itb)\|\leq\varepsilon
implies
\|a-b\|\leq 2\sqrt{\varepsilon}
.

Proof.

Set

g(t)=\exp(ita)-\exp(itb),\qquad t\in[-1,1].

Then
g(0)=0
, and by assumption

\sup_{|t|\leq 1}\|g(t)\|\leq\varepsilon.

Moreover,

g^{\prime}(t)=ia\exp(ita)-ib\exp(itb),

so that

g^{\prime}(0)=i(a-b).

Also

g^{\prime\prime}(t)=-a^{2}\exp(ita)+b^{2}\exp(itb).

Since
a
and
b
are self-adjoint contractions,
\|\exp(ita)\|=\|\exp(itb)\|=1
, hence

\|g^{\prime\prime}(t)\|\leq\|a^{2}\|+\|b^{2}\|\leq 2

for all
t\in[-1,1]
.
For
0<h\leq 1
, Taylor’s formula with integral remainder gives

g(h)=hg^{\prime}(0)+\int_{0}^{h}(h-s)g^{\prime\prime}(s)\,ds.

Therefore,

h\|g^{\prime}(0)\|\leq\|g(h)\|+\int_{0}^{h}(h-s)\|g^{\prime\prime}(s)\|\,ds\leq\varepsilon+h^{2}.

Thus

\|a-b\|=\|g^{\prime}(0)\|\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{h}+h.

Choosing
h=\sqrt{\varepsilon}
for
0<\varepsilon\leq 1
, we obtain

\|a-b\|\leq 2\sqrt{\varepsilon}.

This proves the claim.
∎

Lemma 2.3
.

There exists a function
g(\varepsilon)\to 0
as
\varepsilon\to 0
with the following property. For all C*-algebras
A
and
B
and every
\varepsilon
-
*
-homomorphism
\varphi\colon A\to B
such that

(1)

\exp(i\varphi(a))\approx_{g(\varepsilon)}\varphi(\exp(ia))
for all

.

(2)

\varphi(a)\approx_{g(\varepsilon)}-i\log\varphi(\exp(ia))
for all
a\in A_{\textrm{sa}}
with

. ∎

Proof.

We give a soft proof of (

).
Assume otherwise, that there is

and for every

1/n
-

-homomorphism

there is a

-homomorphism

Theorem 3.1
.

There exists a function
h(\varepsilon)\to 0
as
\varepsilon\to 0
with the following property.
For every unital C*-algebra

, unital C*-algebra
B

-

Lemma 3.2
.

Suppose that

and

are locally compact Hausdorff spaces,

be functions such that

defines a

-homomorphism from

to

such that

for all

Proof.

We claim that the function

is continuous.
Otherwise, there is a net

in

exists but
\lim_{\lambda}f(x_{\lambda})

of

-homomorphism

∎

Lemma 3.3
.

There exists a function

Suppose that

\sup_{\|a\|\leq 1}\|\Phi(a)-\varphi(a)\|\leq h(\varepsilon)

With

Lemma 3.4
.

There exists a function

As a consequence,

is continuous. Indeed, if

and

(2)

\exp(a+b)=\lim_{n\to\infty}(\exp(a/n)\exp(b/n))^{n},

By applying (

\displaystyle\exp(i\Phi(a)+i\Phi(b))

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\to\infty}(\exp(i\Phi(a)/n)\exp(i\Phi(b)/n))^{n}

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\to\infty}\pi((\exp(ia)/n)\exp(ib/n))^{n})

\displaystyle=\exp(i\Phi(a+b)).

, and

4.
Simple closure properties

In this section we focus on Ulam stability with respect to von Neumann algebras, which we call Ulam stability for the sake of brevity.

Theorem 4.1
.

The class of Ulam stable

-algebras is closed by taking:

norm-closed, two-sided, ideals,

(2)

quotients by norm-closed, two-sided ideals,

(3)

\mathsf{C}

obtained from

We are now going to prove these items one by one. The proof of Theorem

In each of the proofs we fix a class
\mathsf{C}
that is Ulam stable with respect to
\sfvN
.

Lemma 4.2
.

Let

be a C*-algebra, let

be a closed two-sided ideal of

be a von Neumann algebra, and let

We first record a useful continuity claim. Let

(

We next compare

to

Let

in norm, the claim gives

\limsup_{\lambda}\|\varphi(e_{\lambda}x)-\varphi(x)\|\leq 2\varepsilon.

Passing to the ultraweak limit and using ultraweak closedness of norm balls,

We verify that

is a

*
-homomorphism. Norm control, approximate additivity, approximate homogeneity, and approximate preservation of the involution are clear.
Approximate multiplicativity uses separate continuity of the multiplication on bounded sets: Let

. We use only separate ultraweak continuity of multiplication:

\displaystyle\widetilde{\varphi}(a)\widetilde{\varphi}(b)

\displaystyle=\left(\WOTlim_{\lambda\to\mathcal{U}}\varphi(e_{\lambda}a)\right)\left(\WOTlim_{\mu\to\mathcal{U}}\varphi(e_{\mu}b)\right)

\displaystyle=\WOTlim_{\lambda\to\mathcal{U}}\left(\varphi(e_{\lambda}a)\left(\WOTlim_{\mu\to\mathcal{U}}\varphi(e_{\mu}b)\right)\right)

\displaystyle=\WOTlim_{\lambda\to\mathcal{U}}\WOTlim_{\mu\to\mathcal{U}}\varphi(e_{\lambda}a)\varphi(e_{\mu}b).

For fixed

in norm as

. Therefore

\limsup_{\mu}\|\varphi(e_{\lambda}ae_{\mu}b)-\varphi(e_{\lambda}ab)\|\leq 2\varepsilon.

Combining the estimates above,
\widetilde{\varphi}
is a

*
-homomorphism, and

This finishes the proof.

5.
Ulam stability for Elliott classifiable C*-algebras

In this section we prove that the class that includes all Elliott classifiable C*-algebras and many nonclassifiable ones, such as all Villadsen algebras of the first type, is Ulam stable.

5.1.
Homogeneous C*-algebras and their limits

Corollary 5.1
.

The class

\mathsf{C}=\{A\otimes F:A\text{ abelian, $F$ finite-dimensional}\}

is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras and so is
the class
\sfAC
of all inductive limits of C*-algebras in
\mathsf{C}
.

Proof.

For
\mathsf{C}
this is a consequence of the fact that the class of finite-dimensional C*-algebras is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras (Theorem
1.4
) and the fact that tensoring with abelian C*-algebras preserves Ulam stability with respect to von Neumann algebras (Theorem
4.1
(
6
)).
Ulam stability of
\sfAC
follows from that of
\mathsf{C}
and the closure under inductive limits (Theorem
4.1
(
5
)).
∎

The class of C*-algebras in Corollary
5.1
includes all Villadsen algebras of the first type (see
[
38
, §3.1]
).

Now, we show that we do obtain uniform Ulam stability for all homogeneous C*-algebras when the base space has finite covering dimension.

Recall that for
n\in\mathbb{N}
a C*-algebra is called
n
-homogeneous
if each one of its irreducible representations is of dimension
n
(
[
4
, IV.1.4.1]
). The base space of an

-homogeneous C*-algebra

and

Theorem 5.2
.

For every

, the class
\mathsf{H}_{m}
of all C*-algebras that are
n
-homogeneous for some

and whose base space is a compact metric space of covering dimension

is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras.

Later on, we will prove that the conclusion of Theorem

holds without the assumption of separability (Corollary
5.12
).

The theorem will be proved after two lemmas.

Lemma 5.3
.

Suppose that

is a compact metric space of dimension

is an open cover of

By compactness we may assume

is finite and enumerate it as

for all

5.2.
Recursive subhomogeneous algebras

We now turn the study of recursively defined classes of C*-algebras and their inductive limits. This provides a class rich enough to include all Elliott classifiable C*-algebras.

Corollary 5.6
.

Suppose that
\mathsf{B}
is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras. Then the class
\mathsf{B}^{\prime}
of all pullbacks of the form

B\oplus_{C(Y,F)}C(X,F)

where

and

are in

,

is compact metrizable,

is closed,

is a

-homomorphism, and
\gamma\colon C(X,F)\to C(Y,F)
is the restriction map, is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras.

Proof.

By Corollary
5.1
and Theorem
4.1
(
4
), it suffices to notice that the map
\gamma
is surjective.
∎

We now iterate Theorem
5.6
. The class of recursive subhomogeneous C*-algebras was introduced in
[
35
]
and the class of NCCW complexes was introduced in
[
34
, §11]
. Let us say that the
length
of a NCCW complex
A_{n}
as defined in
[
34
, Definition 11.2]
is the number of iterations of the pullback construction used to construct it.

Corollary 5.7
.

For every
n\geq 1
,
the class
\mathcal{R}_{n}
of recursive subhomogeneous C*-algebras of length
\leq n
and the class
\mathsf{A}\mathcal{R}_{n}
of inductive limits of C*-algebras in
\mathcal{R}_{n}
are Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras.

Proof.

By
[
35
, Definition 1.2]
, these C*-algebras are obtained from C*-algebras of the form

for

and locally compact Hausdorff

by an
n
-fold application of the pullback construction. Therefore the conclusion follows by Theorem

and induction.
NCCW complexes of length

are a special case of recursive subhomogeneous algebras of length
n
(
[
35
, Example 1.5]
).
∎

Thus
\mathcal{R}_{1}
already contains the generalized dimension drop algebras, while the higher classes are obtained by iterating the same pullback construction.

Corollary
5.7
applies to every ASH (approximately subhomogeneous) algebra that admits an inductive limit decomposition by recursive pullback stages of uniformly bounded length. In particular, it applies to all finite-stage recursive subhomogeneous models built from homogeneous blocks by a bounded number of pullback steps.

By
[
37
, Theorem 6.2 (iii)]
, every separable simple unital nuclear stably finite
\mathcal{Z}
-stable C*-algebra satisfying the UCT is an inductive limit of subhomogeneous C*-algebras of topological dimension at most 2. More precisely, by
[

all building blocks in the inductive limit decomposition belong to the class
\mathcal{R}_{2}
described above. Therefore Corollary

applies to each building block, and since the control function is uniform along the inductive limit, we obtain the following.

Theorem 5.8
.

The class of all separable, simple, unital, nuclear, stably finite,
\mathcal{Z}
-stable C*-algebras satisfying the UCT, is Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras. ∎

8.
Open problems

We end with a list of open problems that seem natural in light of our results.

Question 8.1
.

Is the class of all stably finite, nuclear, separable C*-algebras Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras?

Question 8.2
.

Is the class of Ulam stable C*-algebras with respect to von Neumann algebras closed under passing to full hereditary subalgebras?

Question 8.3
.

What about Ulam stability with respect to von Neumann algebras for purely infinite nuclear C*-algebras?

Question 8.4
.

Is there a C*-algebra that is not Ulam stable with respect to von Neumann algebras?
