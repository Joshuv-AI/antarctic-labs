The Ambassador to the Soviet Union (Harriman) to the President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins)[1]

secret urgent

[Tehran,] December 3, 1943.

Molotov left here yesterday morning. I am therefore sending him message through Soviet Embassy here containing information in your cable to me[2] but as time is so short believe you should also have appropriate person in Algiers inform Vyshinsky and offer him air transportation to Cairo. Molotov stated in Tehran that he would designate Vyshinsky to represent the Soviet Government at the conference.[3] I have advised Molotov of the probability of your placing plane at Vyshinsky disposal in Algiers in the event he still desires him to attend conference.

Source: Hopkins Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Cairo via Army channels.
[2] Hopkins’ telegram of December 2, 1943, ante, p. 605.
[3] See ante, pp. 580–590.
