No. 110 The Representative on the Allied Commission on Reparations (Pauley) to the President [1]

top secret

Moscow, June 29, 1945—8 p.m.

2325. Top sec from Pauley to the President.

I am bending every effort to have a proposed reparation plan to submit to you and the heads of Great Brit and Russia at your forth-coming meeting. Maisky representing USSR and Monckton representing UK expect to be at such meeting and naturally I hope to be there as per my conversation with you before leaving US. Accordingly I would appreciate your notifying the Army and having the Army advise me of arrangements to attend this conference for myself, Dr. Lubin and four others. Best regards.

Source: 740.00119 EW/6–2945: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the Secretary of State ad interim over the signature of Harriman.
