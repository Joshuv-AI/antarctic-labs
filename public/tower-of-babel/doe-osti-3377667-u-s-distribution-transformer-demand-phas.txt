 contributing factor to much of
 the stock lasting well beyond

 and replacement of aging infrastructure

 accelerate transformer replacement in
 certain states – with larger impacts on loading
 for states with little existing electric load

Future Growth – Overall, the Total Demand for Increased
Transformer Capacity (MVA) Will Outpace Demand for a Number of
Transformers
  • Number of distribution transformers rises
 gradually out to 2050, driven by new
 customers, residential expansion, and
 transportation additions
  • An important implication for manufacturing is
 the growth in capacity relative to the growth
 in number of new transformers – the
 demand for larger transformers results in
 much greater demand for capacity than an
 increase in the number of transformers
