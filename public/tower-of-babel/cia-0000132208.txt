Algeria’s Ruling Party Urges Sanctions Breaking

The Central Committee of Algeria’s ruling National
Liberation Front Party (FLN) appealed today to Arab and
Islamic nations to break the international embargo of Iraq,
according to press. The FLN reportedly urged the Arab nations
taking part in the allied coalition to withdraw their troops
and "not to compromise themselves in military operation"
against Iraq "until the real objectives of the aggression has
been clarified." The FLN also called on political parties and
organizations in the Maghreb Arab nation to coordinate a
conference with "a view toward tackling the dangers of the
destructive war threatening Iraq."
