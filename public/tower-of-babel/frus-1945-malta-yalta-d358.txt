The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President [1] (6 February 1945, d358)

[Yalta, February 6, 1945.]

Mr. President

Why not let this wind up today when Stalin is thru—and say we will talk it over again tomorrow. It is 7.15

Harry

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] This note is undated, but the content indicates that it was passed to the President during Stalin’s extended statement on the Polish question during the Third Plenary Meeting on February 6, 1945. See ante, p. 670.
