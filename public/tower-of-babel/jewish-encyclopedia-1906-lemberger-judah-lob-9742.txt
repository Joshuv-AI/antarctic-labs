Judæo-German author of the seventeenth century. He was probably the son of the publisher Abraham Lemberger of Prague. Judah Löb Lemberger was the author of a work in Judæo-German, entitled "Ein Hübsch Göttlich Büchel" (Prague, n.d.), a selection of moral sayings from the Talmud and other rabbinical works, with an appendix, "Ḥibbuṭ ha-Ḳeber," on the sufferings of the dead in the grave.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1337; idem, in Serapeum, 1848, pp. 324, 333. G. A. S. W.
