Russian Talmudist and rabbi at Orlo, government of Grodno, at the beginning of the nineteenth century. He is the author of a collection of responsa, which he published in 1807 at Byelostok.

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 555; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books British Museum, p. 95. L. G. I. Ber.
