Memorandum by the British Chiefs of Staff[1] (15 September 1944, d278)

top secret C.C.S. 682

[Quebec,] 15 September 1944.

Operation “Highball”

The American Chiefs of Staff are asked to take note that Operation Highball will be available in the Eastern Theatre at the end of November, 1944.

Source: J.C.S. Files

Notes
[1] Noted by the Combined Chiefs of Staff at their 175th Meeting, September 15, 1944. See ante, p. 357.
