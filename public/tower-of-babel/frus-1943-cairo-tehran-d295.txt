The Secretary of State to the President[1] (23 November 1943, d295)

[Washington,] November 23, 1943.

For the President from Secretary Hull

Marshal Stalin, who is at the front, states that he will arrive not later than the 28th or 29th at the appointed place.[2]

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the White House Map Room, via military channels. The message from Stalin had been incorporated in a note dated November 22, 1943, from Molotov to Hamilton, who transmitted it the same day to the Department in telegram 2013, signed Harriman (811.001 Roosevelt, F. D./9551½).
[2] i. e., Tehran.
