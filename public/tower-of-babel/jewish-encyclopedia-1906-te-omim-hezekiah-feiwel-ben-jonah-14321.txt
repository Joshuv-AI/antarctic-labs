Rabbi at Przemysl; lived in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. He was the author of "Teḳa' be-Shofar" (Breslau, 1719), containing documents concerning the litigation between the author and the community of Przemysl, which, in spite of a formal contract, had nominated for rabbi Samuel of Lemberg, formerly rabbi of Slonim.
