Correction for De Anda et al., The archaeal roots of eukaryotic life
Published: 2026

Correction for “The archaeal roots of eukaryotic life,” by Valerie De Anda, Kathryn E. Appler, Emily Aguilar-Pine, Georgina L. Aitolo, Galen P. Halverson, and Brett J. Baker, which published March 13, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2516062123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2516062123).

The authors note that Fig. 4 appeared incorrectly. Specifically, the label “C” was incorrectly based at the base of the Njordaeales branch. Fig. 4 has now been updated so that the label “C” appears correctly on the base of the Hodarchaeales branch. The corrected figure and its legend appear below. The online version has been corrected.

Model of the transition from prokaryotes to eukaryotic metabolisms based on predicted ancestral metabolic capabilities of Asgard Archaea. The comprehensive genomic sampling, ancestral reconstructions, and detailed metabolic inferences of Asgards have indicated there was a transition from chemolithotrophy in the Asgard ancestor to heterotrophy in the lineages more closely related to eukaryotes. Adapted from ancestral reconstructions and characterization of modern genomes from Eme et al. (29) and Appler et al. (28). Ancestral reconstructions of translation elongation factor (EF-1a or EF-Tu) and experimental measurements of GDP-binding temperature indicate that there was a transition from thermophilic to mesophilic lifestyles in the Asgards (95) (Fig. 4). Phylogeny adapted from Appler et al. (28). ROS, reactive oxygen species; ETC, electron transport chain; LECA, last eukaryotic common ancestor; Hod, Hodarchaeales.
