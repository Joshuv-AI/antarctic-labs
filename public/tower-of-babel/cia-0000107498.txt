3. ONE REPORT INDICATES THAT A DELEGATION PLANS TO PRESENT/TO THE
OAS EMPHASIZING THE RIO TREATY SANCTIONS AND A DOCUMENT TO THE SOVIET
EMBASSY DEMANDING THE WITHDRAWAL OF SOVIET TROOPS FROM CUBA; EMILIO NUNEZ
PORTUONDO, FORMER CUBAN AMBASSADOR TO THE UNITED NATIONS, REPORTEDLY IS
THE AUTHOR OF THESE DOCUMENTS.

4. PRESS RELEASES BY THE MIAMI ORGANIZING COMMITTEE CLAIM THAT ABOUT
30,000 CUBAN EXILES WILL PARTICIPATE IN THE MARCH WHEN THE OAS BEGINS ITS
DISCUSSIONS. HOWEVER, DAVID CABEZA, EDITOR OF "LIBERACION," THE LEADER OF
THE MIAMI GROUP, HAS REMARKED FRIVATELY THAT HE EXPECTS 3,000 PERSONS FROM
MIAMI AND 15,000 FROM OTHER SITIES TO ATTEND, THE CONSENSUS OF KNOWLEDGEABLE
PERSONS IN THE MIAMI EXILE COMMUNITY IS THAT FROM 3C0 TO 500 PERSONS WILL
ATTEND FROM MIAMI AND 3,000 TO 5,000 FROM NEW YORK AND ELSEWHERE,

5, THE LEADERS OF THE MIAMI ORGANIZING COMMITTEE ARE NOT PROMINENT
LEADERS IN THE EXILE COMMUNITY, THE COMMITTEE HAS CLAIMED SUPPORT OF PROMINENT

MAGISTERIAL REVCLUCIONARIA (DMR, REVOLUTIONARY TEACHERS' DIRECTORATE), BUT
IN MOST CASES THE CLAIMS WERE MADE WITHOUT TEE APPROVAL OF SUCH PERSONS OR
