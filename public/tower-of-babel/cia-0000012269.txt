My dear President,

In my first letter of 19th July 1960, I have already
told you that I have reappraised my place in life and about
my decision and readiness to devote myself to the Cause of
a struggle for a true, just and free world for humanity.
For this Cause I will fight to the end. .

I ask you to consider me as your soldier. Hence-
forth the ranks of your Armed Forces are increased by one
man.

You can have no doubts about my devotion, stead-
fastness, selflessness ‘and resolution in the battle for your
Cause (which is also mine). You will always be satisfied
with me, you will always remember me with a good word,
