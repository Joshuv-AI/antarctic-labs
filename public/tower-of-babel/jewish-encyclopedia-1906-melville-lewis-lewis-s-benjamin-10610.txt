English author; born in 1874. He is the author of the following works: "Life of Thackeray" (1899); "Thackeray's Stray Papers" (1902); "In the World of Mimes" (1902); and "Introduction to Thackeray's Works" (1903-4). He is a contributor to the "Fortnightly Review," "The Bookman," "Temple Bar," and to other English periodicals.

Bibliography: Literary Year-Book, 1904. J. I. L. B.
