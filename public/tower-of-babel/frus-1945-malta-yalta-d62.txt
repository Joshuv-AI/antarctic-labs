Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt [1] (21 January 1945, d62)

top secret

London, January 21, 1945.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt Personal and Top Secret Number 891.

I suggest that the press should be entirely excluded from Argonaut, but that each of us should be free to bring not more than three or four uniformed service photographers to take “still” and cinematograph pictures to be released when we think fit. Please let me know if you agree.

There will of course be the usual agreement communiqué, or communiqués.

I am sending a similar telegram to U. J.

Prime

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels.
