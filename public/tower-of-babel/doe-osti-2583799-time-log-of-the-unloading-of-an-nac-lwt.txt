 1. INTRODUCTION AND ABSTRACT

On January 21, 2016, a NAC-LWT was unloaded at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL). This
document is a firsthand, moment-by-moment time record of the movements necessary for unloading
(NAC-LWT SARP). This is a legal weight truck cask conveyed within a shipping container that can be
disassembled. This is of interest to the NE-8 program because throughput is a key item to estimate for
various consolidated storage facility (CSF) components. Bottom-up time estimates are based upon the
individual steps performed. Bottom-up estimates benefit greatly from firsthand data.

This operation was an infrequent occurrence at ORNL, and although the unloading area is compatible
with a cask receipt, it is not optimized for daily usage. For optimized facilities (such as those potentially
at a CSF), faster operation could reasonably be expected. Inversely, rail casks are larger, and this
presumably slows down operations. Regardless, no baseline data have been identified by the authors in
this level of detail. The authors hope that this documentation can improve cask handling estimates and
inspire similar work.
