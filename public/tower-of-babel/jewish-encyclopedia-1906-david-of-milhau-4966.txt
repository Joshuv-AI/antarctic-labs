French liturgical poet; lived at L'Isle, France, about 1764. In Hebrew he was called (Zunz reads ). MS. No. 148 Montefiore Library (= Halberstamm. No. 266) contains a responsum of David of Milhau.

Bibliography: Gross, Gallia Judaica, p. 345, No. 7; Zunz, in Allg. Zeit. des Jud. 1839, p. 682; Jew. Quart. Rev. xiv. 384. G. M. Sel.
