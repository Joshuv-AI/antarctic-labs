Sec. 10. (a) Dependents of members of the Armed
Forces shall not be provided prosthetic devices, hearing aids,
orthopedic footwear, and spectacles, except that outside the

within the continental limits of the United States where ade-
quate civilian facilities are not available, those items, if avail-
able from military stocks, may be provided to dependents at
prices representing full cost to the Government.

(b) Dependents of members of the Armed Forces shall
not be provided —

and

(2) home calls except in special cases where it is

necessary.
