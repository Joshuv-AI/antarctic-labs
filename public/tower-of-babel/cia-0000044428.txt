((TEXT)) SAN SALVADOR, 126 NOV (ACAN-EFE) <= TRE OPPOSITION
SOCIAL CHRISTIAN PEOPLE'S MOVEMENT (NPSC) AND THE NATIONAL
REVOLUTIONARY MOVEMENT (MNR) TODAY CONDEMNED THE ASSASSINATION OF 81x
JESUITS IN SAN SALVADOR, AND HELD THE GOVERNMENT AND THE ARMED FORCES
RESPONSIBLE FOR THIS CRINE.

THE MPSC REQUESTED THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY AND THE DIPLOMATIC
CORPS ACCREDITED IN SAN SALVADOR TO MAKE "GOOD OFFICES BEFORE THE
SALVADORAN GOVERNMENT AND ARMED FORCES" SO THEY "ADOPT REALISTIC
POSITIONS AND AGREE TO AN INNEDIATE NEGOTIATION OF A CEASE-FIRE WITH
THE GUERRILLAS."

THE SOCIAL CHRISTIANS STRESSED THAT THE BLOODSHED OF CIVILIANS
MUST CEASE AND THAT IT 28 NECESSARY TO CREATE AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THE
NECESSARY CONDITIONS TO BRING PEACE TO EL SALVADOR.

THE MYR SAID THE ASSASSINATION OF THE JESUITS - FIVE SPANIARDS
AND A SALVADORAN -- "SEEKS TO SILENCE AND SUBDUE WITH TERROR THE WORK
OF THE CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY," WHOSE RECTOR WAS IGNACIO
ELLACURIA. “THIS I8 NOT THE FIRST TINE THAT THE CENTRAL ANERICAN
UNIVERSITY AND 178 MOST OUTSTANDING OFFICIALS ARE THE OBJECT or
ACCUSATIONS AND VERBAL AND PHYSICAL AGGRESSION BY VLTRA-RIGHTIST
SECTORS," THE SOCIAL DEXOCRATIC MNR NOTED.

THE MNR IS "CONCERNED AND SURPRISED BY THE IRRESPONSIBLE AND
SUSPICIOUS CONDUCT OF THE SALVADORAN GOVERNNENT AND THE ARKED FORCES'
HIGH COMMAND OF HOLDING THE GUERRILLAS RESPONSIBLE FOR TRE
ASSABSINATIONS, WHICH GIVES CARTE BLANCHE TO THE DEATH SQUADS AND TO
THE MASS TERROR OF 1980, 1981, AND 1962."

THE HIGHEST KNR AND XPSC LEADERS, GUILLERKO UNGO AND RUBEN ZANORA
RESPECTIVELY, cu ARK 2-2 EVECAR AND WEXTCAN
EMBASSIES ACCREDITED IN SAN SALVADOR.

THE GOVERNMENT AND THE ARMED FORCES CONDEMNED THE ASSASSINATION OF
THE JESUITS. MOREOVER, PRESIDENT ALREDO CRISTIANI SAID THAT IF IT
1S PROVEN THAT ARXED FORCES MEMBERS ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE CRIXE,
"THE FULL FORCE OF LAW WILL BE IMPOSED UPON PAN. “

SAN SALVADOR RESIDENTS ARE ENDURING THE 6TH CONSECUTIVE DAY OF
BLOODY CLASHES WHICH BEGAN WITH THE FARABUNDO MARTI OFFENSIVE ON 11
NOVEMBER. |

THE DEATH TOLL 18 SOKE 1,000 AND THE CONFLICT SEEMS TO INTENSIFY
EACH DAY, AS EVIDENCED BY THE CONTINUOUS ARMY ATTACKS ON TRE REBELS
REDOUBTS.
