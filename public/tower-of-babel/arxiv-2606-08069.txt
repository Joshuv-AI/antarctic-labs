Weak-split extensions of topological Abelian groups
María V. Ferrer, Salvador Hernández-Muñoz, Luis Javier Hernández-Paricio

Abstract
In the category of topological Abelian groups, we consider the usual notion of extension E = ( B → X → A ) E=(B\to X\to A) of B B by A A and the notion of weak-split extension (when X → A X\to A has a continuous split A → X A\to X ). Given a weak-split extension E E , the topological Abelian group X X is homeomorphic to B × A B\times A , but in general, X X need not be algebraic isomorphic to B × A B\times A . In this paper, for two topological Abelian groups A , B A,B , we study the Abelian group E 𝐓𝐀 w ​ s ​ ( A , B ) E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B) of weak-split extensions of B B by A A modulo extension isomorphisms. We prove that E 𝐓𝐀 w ​ s ​ ( A , B ) E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B) can be described as the Abelian group of all possible continuous sums given in the product topological space B × A B\times A (modulo topological isomorphism) having B B as a topological subgroup and A A as a topological quotient. We also give an alternative description of E 𝐓𝐀 w ​ s ​ ( A , B ) E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B) as a quotient 𝒵 c ​ ( A , B ) / ℬ c ​ ( A , B ) \mathcal{Z}_{c}(A,B)/\mathcal{B}_{c}(A,B) , where 𝒵 c ​ ( A , B ) \mathcal{Z}_{c}(A,B) are cocycles represented by certain continuous maps of the form A × A → B A\times A\to B , and similarly for the coboundaries ℬ c ​ ( A , B ) \mathcal{B}_{c}(A,B) . For two topological Abelian groups A , B A,B , we compare the Abelian group of w ​ s ws -extensions E 𝐓𝐀 w ​ s ​ ( A , B ) E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B) with the Abelian group of standard extensions E 𝐀 ​ ( A , B ) E_{\mathbf{A}}(A,B) where now A , B A,B also denote the subjacent Abelian groups. We relate these different types of extensions using an exact sequence with six terms. Although the Bohr topology of discrete Abelian groups has been investigated by many workers, there still remain many parts that are not well understood. Here, as an application of the methods developed in the paper, new examples of nontrivial w ​ s ws -extensions for discrete Abelian groups equipped with the Bohr topology are provided and some related open questions are also proposed.

5.1

-extensions

Given a topological Abelian group

Lemma 5.1

Let

be a topological Abelian group, and consider

Then,

is a subgroup of

(ii)

Note that if

is an open neighbourhood at

in

Remark 5.2
.

The subgroup

Theorem 5.3

As a consequence of Lemma

Then,

.

Suppose that
A,B\in\mathbf{TA}
. If

is a divisible free-torsion group and
\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}

\text{Hom}_{\mathbf{A}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\cong\text{Ext}_{{\bf A}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z})\cong\text{E}_{{\bf A}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}).

[
4

for the details of this construction):

Denoting by

the canonical quotient map, each

has the associated extension

0\longrightarrow\mathbb{Z}\overset{\iota}{\longrightarrow}E_{f}\overset{p}{\longrightarrow}\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}\longrightarrow 0,

with

E_{f}=\left\{(q,x)\in\mathbb{Q}\times(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\;\middle|\;\pi(q)=f(x)\right\},

such that the map

\Phi:\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext_{{\bf A}}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}),\qquad f\longmapsto[E_{f}],

is a group isomorphism.

.

by

.

\pi({{\bf s}}_{(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})}(f(x)))=(\pi\circ{{\bf s}}_{(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})})(f(x))=f(x)

\sharp
-continuous, which implies the

-continuity of

.

ws
-splits, which completes the proof since

6
Remarks, conclusions, and future work

For two topological Abelian groups
A,B\in\mathbf{TA}
we have analyzed the structure of the Abelian group
E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B)

of
ws
-extensions of
B
by
A
modulo topological isomorphism of
ws
-extensions, and we have given two different descriptions of this Abelian group:

\Sigma_{A,B}/\sim,

using all different structures of the topological Abelian group on the product space
B\times A
, and

\mathcal{Z}_{c}(A,B)/\mathcal{B}_{c}(A,B),

using cocycles of the form
f\colon A\times A\to B
where
f
is a suitable continuous pointed map.

We have also provided a six-term exact sequence to study the canonical map

E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B)\to E_{\mathbf{A}}(A,B)
.

We remark that for two Abelian groups
A,B\in\mathbf{A}
,
E(A,B)
can also be obtained as a derived functor using injective or projective resolutions; see
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
. If
A,B
are locally compact topological Abelian groups,

E_{\mathbf{TA}}(A,B)
can also be obtained as a derived functor with the homological algebra studied by Moskowitz and Hoffman for the subcategory locally compact topological Abelian groups; see
[
7
,
16
,
17
]
.

One of the pending objectives is to obtain
E_{\mathbf{TA}}^{ws}(A,B)
as a derived functor by taking suitable projective resolutions that can be constructed using free topological Abelian groups over pointed topological spaces. Note that the category of topological Abelian groups is isomorphic to the category of topological modules over the topological discrete ring
\mathbb{Z}
.
Nummela announced in the paper
[
18
]
a study of Ext functors for the category of modules over a topological ring using projective resolutions. However, this study appears to have not been published in the end.

We also remark that there are some interesting open problems related to Bohr topologies and
ws
-extensions, as seen in open Questions
5.22
and
5.24
.
