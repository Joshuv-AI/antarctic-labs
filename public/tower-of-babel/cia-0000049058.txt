ON 23 APRIL 1983, JUAN RAMON ((ROSALES)) Y ROSALES,
SP LAWYER FOR HANS ((CHRIST) ), A SALVADORAN RIGHTIST SUSPECTED

OF COMPLICITY IN THE 1980 SHERATON MURDERS CASE, SAID

THAT ALL LEGAL CHARGES AGAINST CHRIST WOULD BE DROPPED BY

THE END OF APRIL. ROSALES STATED THAT FACTUAL INCONSISTENCIES 1%

THE TESTIMONIES OF THE TWO MAIN WITNESSES AGAINST CHRIST WOULD

PROVIDE GROUNDS FOR THE DISMISSAL OF CHARGES. ROSALES ALSO SAID THAT,

FOR A VARIETY OF REASONS, SALVADORAN AUTHORITIES WERE WILLING TO LET

THE CASE DROP, IF A LEGAL PRETEXT COULD BE FOUND FOR DOING SO.

2. ACCORDING TO ROSALES, CHRIST 1S CURRENTLY RESIDING IN
MIAMI, FLORDIA AND PROBABLY WILL RETURN TO EL SALVADOR IN MAY OR
JUNE, AFTER THE DISMISSAL OF CHARGES. ROSALES SAID THAT HE JS
IN TOUCH WITH CHRIST THROUGH MEMBERS OF THE SALVADORAN COMMUNITY
IN MIAMI. COMMENT: ROSALES IMPLIED, BUT DID NOT STATE,
