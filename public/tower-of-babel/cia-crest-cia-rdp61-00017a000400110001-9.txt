GENERAL TBATNING DIVISION

Present (Fiscal 1952) Proposed .(Fiscal 1.253.1

Management Training Branch

This is an entirely new branch that was proposed on 23 May and tentatively approved by the office of the
Director of Training.

This branch will be responsible for developing the Human Resources Program along with other general
executive, administrative, and supervisory training throughout the Agency.

Position #1 will require an individual competent to develop supervisory training all the way from the
AD level to the first line supervisor.

Position #2 will require an individual who can give general assistance to the Chief of the Division and
the Chief of the Branch in conducting the Human Resources Program.

Position #3 will require an individual, a man or a woman, who would assist in the supervisory training
programs and could take over responsibility for developing administrative training programs. Administrative
training would be organized to assist the service offices of the Agency, - budget, personnel, property procurement,
space, etc.,- to meet more effectively the needs of the operating offices and reciprocally to assist the
operating offices to achieve closer coordination in working with the administrative service offices.

The Management Training Branch will be in the developmental stages during fiscal 1953# For all practical
purposes, it will be part of the office of the Chief of the Division and will secure secretarial support
from the GS-6, Secretary (Steno), in that office.

GENERAL TRAINING DIVISION

25X9

Proposed (Fiscal 1953)

Reading Improvement Branch

The proposed increase in use to be made of the Reading Improvement Laboratory accounts for the proposed
increase in the T/O for 1953. As a matter of fact, the proposed T/O for 1953 represents less than the
total number of positions allocated to reading improvement programs from TR(G) and TR(S). The positions
allocated to reading improvement by TR(S) have not been drawn on in 1952.

The proposed T/O for 1953 would provide reading improvement programs both for overt and for covert personnel.
The proposed T/O would provide a minimum staff to run a laboratory of the dimensions required by present plans
of the Director of Training to mate reading improvement part of the basic training for all CIA employees,
as well as providing program support for UTG/A, CIA Intelligence School (Professional Trainees), the needs
of miscellaneous groups of covert personnel, and the increasing demands from various offices within the
Agency.

Of the positions approved at present there is one vacancy,- position #3, GS-4> Clerk (Typist).
