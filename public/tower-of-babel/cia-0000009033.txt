2. As has been stated previously, due to the extremely poor
quality and lack of definitive features on POW 45 photo, a conclusive
comparison is impossible to make. To repeat the feeling of this
office, Ramsay and POW have caliper lines which appear to start just
above the nostril extending to the chin; each appears to have a
slightly jutting chin with a horizontal indentation under the lower
lip; liplines seem similar with a thin upper lip and a slightly
protruding lower lip. In addition to the above, it is also noted that
both individuals seem to have the same type of build. On the negative
side, Ramsay seems to have a fuller and rounder face than POW but
the poor quality of the POW photo precludes an accurate description
of POW's face. It is regretted that a more decisive conclusion cannot

be made. ,
