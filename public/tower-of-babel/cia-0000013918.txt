SUMMARY

On 27 December 1966, VC Regiment 22, SAO VANG Division shelled, attacked and.
dislodged two U.S. battalions garrisoned on XUAN SON strategic hill, HOAT AN
District, BINH DINH Province, SVN. Four U.S. soldiers were taken prisoner and
taken to LONG HOA Village, AN LOA District, BINH DINH Province, where they were
detained in people's houses. Later they were sent to the SAO VANG Division,
then to Military Region 5 and finally to NVN where they would be detained for
possible exchange after the,armistice. The VC policy regarding POWs stated
that one U.S. soldier could be exchanged for ten VC soldiers; and for one |
returned U.S. officer pilot, the U.S. had to compensate NVN with a factory.
