Rabbi of Prossnitz, Moravia, in the sixteenth century; born in Bohemia. He was the author of "Ḥiddushe " (Prossnitz, 1602 or 1618), novellæ and responsa on the marriage laws of the treatises Ketubot and Ḳiddushin of the Babylonian Talmud.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 49; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2424. E. C. A. Ki.
