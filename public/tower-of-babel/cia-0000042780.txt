FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:

1. [Text] San Salvador, 13 Dec (AFP)--The Society of Jesus
reaffirmed that the information available to them concerning the
assassination of six Jesuit priests and their two maids on 16
November in San Salvador *‘‘continues to point toward members of the ~-
Armed Forces.''

2. In a communique issued last night in San Salvador, the Jesuits
pointed out that information gathered over the past few weeks clearly
indicates that ‘‘the criminals acted like people under protection.’!

3. Almost a month ago, Ignacio Ellacuria, rector of the Central
American University (UCA), headquartered in San Salvador; five other
Jesuit priests; and two maids working for them at their residence
were killed by 30 men wearing camouflage military uniforms.

4. The sole witness, Lucia de Cerna, was recently taken to the
United States. Monsignor Arturo Rivera y Damas, archbishop of San
Salvador, denounced on 10 December that she had been harrassed and
pressured by her interrogators.

5. However, [words indistinct] that any Armed Forces member would
have killed innocent clergymen and their maids in cold blood.

6. The military man added that the death squads will be
investigated, although they ‘'do not really exist because they are a
creation of the FMLN [Farabundo Marti National Liberation Front]. In
any case, the squads would be made up of FMLN members.’'

7. To back up his claim concerning the perpetrators of the multiple
assassination, Montano pointed out that the Salvadoran insurgency has
organized special commando units to kill ‘‘personalities,
politicians, military men, and officials.’'’

8. However, in its communique the Society of Jesus stated that it
has received ‘‘excellent’’ information from the archbishopric’s Legal
Assistance Office, but it has ‘*‘received little information about the
investigation from those who are conducting it,'’ in reference to
official efforts.
