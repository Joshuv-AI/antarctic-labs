TEXT:

((TEXT)) SAN. SALVADOR, 16 JAN (ACAN-EFE) -- FOUR OFFICERS AND
FOUR SALVADORAN ARMY SOLDIERS WHO WERE ALLEGEDLY INVOLVED IN THE
ASSASSINATION OF SIX JESUIT PRIESTS AND TWO WOMEN, APPEARED THIS
AFTERNOON BEFORE.THE JUDGE.

THE EIGHT SOLDIERS WERE TRANSFERED, UNDER STRICT SECURITY,

FROM THE GARRISONS IN WHICH THEY ARE BEING HELD TO THE COURT.

PRESIDENT ALFREDO CRISTIANI NAMED ON 14 JANUARY COLONEL
GUILLERMO BENAVIDES OF THE MILITARY ACADEMY; TWO LIEUTENANTS, A
SUBLIEUTENANT, TWO NONCOMMISSIONED OFFICERS, A CORPORAL, AND TWO
SOLDIERS AS SUSPECTS IN THIS CRIME. HE ALSO SAID ONE OF THE SOLDIERS
IS AT LARGE.

THE CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION COMMISSION WAS IN CHARGE OF GATHERING
EVIDENCE, WHICH AFTERWARDS WAS SENT TO THE ARMY HONOR COMMISSION TO
ASSIGN RESPONSIBILITIES FOR THE CRIMES.

THIS IS THE FIRST TIME IN SALVADORAN HISTORY THAT ARMY MEMBERS
HAVE APPEARED BEFORE A JUDGE AS SUSPECTS IN A POLITICAL CRIME.
