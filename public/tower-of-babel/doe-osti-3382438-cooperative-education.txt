 1. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND OF THE PROJECT

Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) is a multidisciplinary national laboratory that conducts
research and development in national security, engineering, materials science, computational
modeling, and advanced manufacturing. The laboratory develops innovative technologies to
address complex scientific and engineering challenges.

This project focuses on the development and evaluation of high-performance absorbing
structures through computational design, simulation, and engineering analysis. Absorbing
structures are used in applications where damage mitigation, structural protection, and material
efficiency are critical performance requirements. The increasing demand for lightweight, high-
strength, and highly efficient structural systems has created a need for improved design
methodologies capable of maximizing absorption while minimizing weight and material usage.

The project utilizes advanced engineering software, including 3D CAD software and FEA, to
generate and optimize structural concepts. Computational simulations are performed to evaluate
structural behavior under loading conditions, while mathematical analyses are conducted using
Python-based tools as well as established analytical equations from material and structural
mechanics.

The project benefits LANL by supporting the development of advanced design methodologies
and improving the understanding of material and structural performance. During the internship
term, a significant portion of the design development, simulation, and data analysis activities will
be completed. Success of the project depends on collaboration among engineering mentors and
technical staff members. Work will be conducted at Los Alamos National Laboratory using
laboratory computing resources and engineering software.

 2. SCOPE OF WORK

The purpose of this project is to develop and evaluate these high-performance shock-absorbing
structures through computational design and engineering analysis. The project seeks to improve
structural performance by optimizing material selection and critical materialistic characteristics.

The project addresses the need for efficient structural systems capable of such tasks while
maintaining acceptable strength, durability, and manufacturability. Existing design approaches
may be limited by conventional manufacturing methodologies or inefficient material usage.
Advanced computational design tools allow engineers to generate and evaluate innovative
improvements with differing characteristics.

Project Features:

 A. Development of structural concepts using CAD and advanced design software.

 B. Creation of computational models for simulation and performance evaluation.

 C. Analysis of stress, strain, deformation, and design behavior.

 D. Processing and interpretation of simulation results using Python and analytical
 calculations.

 E. Documentation and testing of design methodologies, assumptions, and performance
outcomes.

 3. PERIOD OF CO-OP ASSIGNMENT

The period of performance for this internship assignment extends from May 25, 2026, through
July 31, 2026. During this time, project activities will focus on the design, simulation, testing,
analysis, and optimization of high-performance absorbing structures. Work will be performed
under a 9/80 alternate work schedule, averaging 80 hours over a two-week period.

Major project activities include computer-aided design (CAD) development using various
software, computational modeling and simulation using advanced FEA software, mathematical
and material analysis using Python tools, and technical documentation of results. A dedicated
project testing phase is scheduled for the week of July 6, 2026, during which developed designs
will be evaluated to assess performance and validate analytical results. These results will provide
further framework for FEA simulation development.

All project tasks, milestones, analyses, testing activities, and final documentation outlined in this
Statement of Work are expected to be completed within the duration of the Summer 2026
internship assignment.

 4. PLACE OF PERFORMANCE

 5. WORK REQUIREMENTS, SCHEDULE, AND MILESTONES

Work requirements include conducting background research, developing analytical and
computational solutions, creating CAD models, analyzing systematic finite element analyses
(FEA), generating advanced geometries using computational design software, remotely
supporting testing activities, and analyzing performance results. Python and Jupyter Notebook
tools will be utilized to process data and perform engineering calculations using established
material analysis methods. Additionally, safety training is also required to gain authorization to
operate necessary equipment and carry out other job tasks.

Major project milestones include completion of the Statement of Work, development of initial
design concepts, completion of computational modeling and simulation activities, participation
discussion after the project testing during the week of July 6, 2026, analysis of test and
simulation results, and the organization of results as well as the proposal of potential design
improvements. The final deliverables will include technical documentation of methodologies,
engineering analyses, design recommendations, a comprehensive presentation, and a final report
for this course summarizing the work completed during this internship without disclosing
controlled unclassified information (CUI).
