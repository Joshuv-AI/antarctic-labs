Intrinsic linking of simplicial n n -complexes in ℝ 2 ​ n \mathbb{R}^{2n} : An additional minimal n n -complex Thanks: The author was supported by JSPS KAKENHI Grant Number 25K06987.
Ryo Nikkuni

Abstract
. For any positive integer n n , the author previously constructed several minimal simplicial n n -complexes which necessarily contain a non-splittable two-component link, consisting of an ( n − 1 ) (n-1) -sphere and an n n -sphere, in any embedding into ℝ 2 ​ n \mathbb{R}^{2n} . In this paper, we present an additional simplicial n n -complex with the same property.

2.
The mod
2
van Kampen obstruction and proof of Theorem
1-2

Let
K
be a simplicial
n
-complex. It is well known that every such
K
admits a generic immersion into
\mathbb{R}^{2n}
. Here, an immersion
\varphi\colon K\to\mathbb{R}^{2n}
is said to be
generic
if all singularities of
\varphi(K)
are transversal double points occurring between the interiors of pairs of disjoint
n
-simplices. For a generic immersion
\varphi
of
K
into
\mathbb{R}^{2n}
and any two mutually disjoint
n
-simplices
s,s^{\prime}\in\varDelta^{n}(K)
, let
l(\varphi(s),\varphi(s^{\prime}))
denote the number of double points between
\varphi(s)
and
\varphi(s^{\prime})
. Denote by
K^{*}
the
deleted product
of
K
, that is, the subcomplex of
K\times K
consisting of all cells
s\times s^{\prime}
with
s\cap s^{\prime}=\emptyset
. Let
\widetilde{K}^{*}
be the quotient complex of
K^{*}
obtained by identifying
s\times s^{\prime}
with
s^{\prime}\times s
. Given a generic immersion
\varphi\colon K\to\mathbb{R}^{2n}
, the modulo-
2
reduction of
l(\varphi(s),\varphi(s^{\prime}))
defines a
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-cocycle
c_{\varphi}
on
\widetilde{K}^{*}
, and its cohomology class
o_{K}=[c_{\varphi}]\in H^{2n}(\widetilde{K}^{*};\mathbb{Z}_{2})
is called the
mod
2
van Kampen obstruction
of
K
.

Let
n
be a positive integer, and let
a
,
b
, and
c
be three vertices not belonging to
\sigma_{2n}^{n-1}
. In the following, we regard
\sigma_{2n}^{n-1}*\{a,b,c\}
as a simplicial
n
-complex
K
.

Lemma 2-1
.

Let
K=\sigma_{2n}^{n-1}*\{a,b,c\}
. Then

H^{2n}(\widetilde{K}^{*};\mathbb{Z}_{2})\cong\mathbb{Z}_{2}.

Moreover, this group is generated by the cohomology class
[c]
of the cocycle
c
defined as follows. Fix any two disjoint
(n-1)
-simplices
\tau,\tau^{\prime}\in\varDelta^{n-1}(\sigma_{2n}^{n-1})
, and let
\sigma=a*\tau
,
\sigma^{\prime}=b*\tau^{\prime}
. Define
c\in C^{2n}(\widetilde{K}^{*};\mathbb{Z}_{2})
to be the dual cochain of the
2n
-cell
\sigma\times\sigma^{\prime}
; that is,

c(s\times s^{\prime})=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }s\times s^{\prime}=\sigma\times\sigma^{\prime}\text{ in }\widetilde{K}^{*},\\
0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

Proof.

For a pair of disjoint
n
-simplices
s,s^{\prime}
in
K
, let
E^{s,s^{\prime}}
denote the dual cochain of the
2n
-cell
s\times s^{\prime}
in
\widetilde{K}^{*}
. Similarly, for an
(n-1)
-simplex
t
and an
n
-simplex
s^{\prime}
in
K
with
t\cap s^{\prime}=\emptyset
, let
V^{t,s^{\prime}}
denote the dual cochain of the
(2n-1)
-cell
t\times s^{\prime}
in
\widetilde{K}^{*}
.

First, fix an
(n-1)
-simplex
t=|aa_{0}\cdots a_{n-2}|
and an
n
-simplex
s^{\prime}=|ba_{n+1}\cdots a_{2n}|
in
K
. Note that
t
and
s^{\prime}
are disjoint. Let

s_{1}=|aa_{0}\cdots a_{n-2}a_{n-1}|,\quad s_{2}=|aa_{0}\cdots a_{n-2}a_{n}|

be two
n
-simplices containing
t
as a face. Then the
(2n-1)
-coboundary satisfies

\delta^{2n-1}(V^{t,s^{\prime}})=E^{s_{1},s^{\prime}}+E^{s_{2},s^{\prime}}.

Hence
E^{s_{1},s^{\prime}}
and
E^{s_{2},s^{\prime}}
are cohomologous in
H^{2n}(\widetilde{K}^{*};\mathbb{Z}_{2})
. By the symmetry of
K
, this relation implies that for any pair of disjoint
n
-simplices
\sigma=a*\tau
and
\sigma^{\prime}=b*\tau^{\prime}
(where
\tau,\tau^{\prime}\in\varDelta^{n-1}(\sigma_{2n}^{n-1})
are disjoint), all such
E^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}
are cohomologous to each other.

Next, fix an
(n-1)
-simplex
t^{\prime}=|a_{0}a_{1}\cdots a_{n-1}|
and an
n
-simplex
s^{\prime}=|ba_{n+1}\cdots a_{2n}|
in
K
. Let

s_{1}^{\prime}=|aa_{0}\cdots a_{n-1}|,\quad s_{2}^{\prime}=|ca_{0}\cdots a_{n-1}|

be two
n
-simplices containing
t^{\prime}
as a face. Then, similarly,

\delta^{2n-1}(V^{t^{\prime},s^{\prime}})=E^{s_{1}^{\prime},s^{\prime}}+E^{s_{2}^{\prime},s^{\prime}}.

By the symmetry of
K
, all
E^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}
with
\sigma=a*\tau
and
\sigma^{\prime}=c*\tau^{\prime}
(where
\tau,\tau^{\prime}\in\varDelta^{n-1}(\sigma_{2n}^{n-1})
are disjoint) are also cohomologous to each other. The same type of relation holds for pairs of the form
\sigma=b*\tau
and
\sigma^{\prime}=c*\tau^{\prime}
.

Combining these relations, we conclude that all dual cochains
E^{\sigma,\sigma^{\prime}}
corresponding to pairs of disjoint
n
-simplices in
K
are cohomologous to each other. Therefore,

H^{2n}(\widetilde{K}^{*};\mathbb{Z}_{2})\cong\mathbb{Z}_{2},

and this group is generated by the cohomology class
[c]
of the dual cochain of any single
2n
-cell
\sigma\times\sigma^{\prime}
, where
\sigma=a*\tau
and
\sigma^{\prime}=b*\tau^{\prime}
for disjoint
(n-1)
-simplices
\tau,\tau^{\prime}\in\varDelta^{n-1}(\sigma_{2n}^{n-1})
.
