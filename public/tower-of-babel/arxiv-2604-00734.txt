On sifted homotopy colimits of algebras over an N ∞ N_{\infty} -operad
Grégoire Marc

Abstract




Abstract

We prove that the forgetful functor from algebras over an
N_{\infty}
-operad to equivariant spaces preserves sifted homotopy colimits. In particular, we provide equivariant generalisations to results of Pavlov and Scholbach
[
17
]
and Berger and Moerdijk
[
2
,
3
]
.

1.
Introduction

Operads were introduced by May in
[
15
]
to study infinite loop spaces, and they play a central role in the study of homotopical algebraic structures. The theory of operads and their algebras admits two main formalisms, namely
\infty
-operads
[
13
]
and topological operads
[
15
]
. These two approaches, along with others such as dendroidal sets
[
8
]
, have been compared in the work of Barwick
[
1
]
, Cisinski and Moerdijk
[
10
,
9
]
, and Chu, Haugseng, and Heuts
[
7
]
.

The theories of
\infty
-operads and topological operads both offer different perspectives for homotopical algebra. The entire development of higher algebra by Lurie in
[
13
]
is based on
\infty
-operads, as they are more suited for categorical considerations. The importance of topological operads is more concrete and lies in their direct connection with topology and geometry. In particular, examples of such operads arise geometrically, such as with little discs operads which have a direct connection with configuration spaces
[
15
]
.

The homotopy theory of algebras over a (topological) operad was developed and studied by Berger and Moerdijk in
[
2
,
3
]
. An important player in this study is the notion of
\Sigma
-cofibrant operads
. The
\Sigma
-cofibrancy of a given operad
{\mathcal{O}}
is a technical condition that ensures the good behavior of the homotopy theory of its algebras. One important consequence of this is the rectification result of Pavlov and Scholbach
[
17
]
, which states that the homotopy theory of algebras over a
\Sigma
-cofibrant operad can be formally compared with the algebras over the associated
\infty
-operad. This applies in particular to
E_{\infty}
-operads, which are encoding infinite loop spaces.

Let
G
be a finite group. The theory of
N_{\infty}
-operads was introduced by Blumberg and Hill in
[
4
]
as an equivariant generalisation of the theory of
E_{\infty}
-operads. Unlike in the
E_{\infty}
case,

N_{\infty}
-operads do not form a contractible space; rather, they assemble into a poset, which can be described in terms of
G
-indexing systems
, by work of Blumberg and Hill
[
4
]
, Bonventre and Pereira
[
5
]
, and Rubin
[
18
]
. More generally, one can consider equivariant topological operads (and their algebras), which provides interesting examples, such as equivariant little discs operads
[
11
]
. As in the non-equivariant setting, there is also a notion of
G
-
\infty
-operads
[
16
]
along with nerve functors
[
6
]
relating equivariant topological operads and their algebras with their
\infty
-categorical analogues.

However, these comparison functors are not yet known to be equivalences, even under some assumptions on the operads. In particular,
N_{\infty}
-operads do not yet admit a rectification result for their algebras. A first problem that arises when one attempts to prove such a rectification for
N_{\infty}
-operads is that these operads are, in general, not
\Sigma
-cofibrant. This implies in particular that all the results of
[
2
]
and
[
17
]
cannot be applied in the proof of a potential rectification. The goal of this paper is to generalise certain results of
[
2
]
and
[
17
]
to a broader class of equivariant operads which contains
N_{\infty}
-operads. More precisely, we prove the following theorem.

Theorem A

(
3
)
.

Let
{\mathcal{O}}
be a
G
-simplicial operad. We denote by
{\mathscr{S}}^{G}
the
\infty
-category of equivariant spaces, by
\operatorname{sSet}^{G}
the category of
G
-simplicial sets, and by
W_{G}
the class of morphisms of
{\mathcal{O}}
-algebras which forget to
G
-weak equivalences.
If for every
n\geq 0
,
{\mathcal{O}}_{n}
has a free
\Sigma_{n}
-action, then the forgetful functor

U\colon\operatorname{Alg}_{{\mathcal{O}}}\left(\operatorname{sSet}^{G}\right)\left[W_{G}^{-1}\right]\to{\mathscr{S}}^{G}

preserves sifted colimits.

A
is a generalisation of
[
17
, Theorem 7.9]
. Note that
[
17
, Theorem 7.9]
can also be applied to equivariant operads but not
N_{\infty}
-operads, as they are not
\Sigma
-cofibrant (see
2
). In particular, the proof of
A
differs from the classical proof of
[
17
, Theorem 7.9]
and relies heavily on equivariant techniques. As we will see in
2
, some important results of
[
2
]
do not extend to
N_{\infty}
-operads, which complicates the proof of
A
.

The result of this paper is used in
[
14
]
to prove a rectification for algebras over
N_{\infty}
-operads, thus correcting the original version of this paper, which contained a mistake. Although
A
should be a first step towards a more general rectification for algebras over
G
-operads, the author does not know how to obtain such a rectification in full generality.

2.
Counter example

In this section, we enlighten the fact that the results of
[
2
]
cannot be genralised to
N_{\infty}
-operads. In particular, after proving that
N_{\infty}
-operads are not
\Sigma
-cofibrant in the sense of
[
2
]
, we show that
[
2
, Corollary 5.5]
is false for
N_{\infty}
-operads.
Let
I
be a small category and consider the equivariant model structure on
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet}^{G})
obtained from the projective model structure on
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
. Note that this model structure exists using
[
19
, Lemma 2.12]
.

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
{\mathcal{O}}
be an
N_{\infty}
-operad in
\operatorname{sSet}^{G}
. If the
G
-indexing system associated with
{\mathcal{O}}
is not trivial, then
{\mathcal{O}}
is not
\Sigma
-cofibrant.

Proof.

The projective model structure on
\left(\operatorname{sSet}^{G}\right)^{\Sigma_{n}}
corresponds to the model structure on
\operatorname{sSet}^{G\times\Sigma_{n}}
given by the family
{\mathcal{F}}
of
G\times\Sigma_{n}
spanned by the subgroups
H\times\{e\}
, with
H
a subgroup of
G
. If
{\mathcal{O}}
is not a trivial
N_{\infty}
-operads, there exists a graph subgroup
\Gamma\subseteq G\times\Sigma_{n}
for some
n
such that
\Gamma\notin{\mathcal{F}}
and such that
{\mathcal{O}}_{n}^{\Gamma}\neq\emptyset
. Using
[
19
, Proposition 2.16]
, we deduce that
{\mathcal{O}}_{n}
is not cofibrant in
\operatorname{sSet}^{G\times\Sigma_{n}}
for the model structure corresponding to
{\mathcal{F}}
. In particular, this implies that
{\mathcal{O}}
is not
\Sigma
-cofibrant.
∎

Proposition 2.2
.

For every cellular map
f
in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet}^{G})
, the map
f(n)\in\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{Set}^{G})
is of the form

(1)

X\to X\sqcup\left(\bigsqcupop\displaylimits_{j\in J}G/H_{j}\times\hom(A_{j},(-))\right).

In particular, cellular objects are of the form

\bigsqcupop\displaylimits_{j\in J}G/H_{j}\times\hom(A_{j},(-)).

Proof.

Sequential colimits of maps of the form
1
also have this form and it is enough to show that for every pushout square

(2)

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces G/H\times\hom(i,(-))\times\partial\Delta^{n}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces X}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces G/H\times\hom(i,(-))\times\Delta^{n}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces Y,}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f}

the map
f(n)
is of the form
1
. If we specify the square
2
at some simplicial degree
k
, we obtain a pushout square

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\bigsqcupop\displaylimits_{\partial\Delta^{n}(k)}G/H\times\hom(i,(-))}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces X(k)}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces\bigsqcupop\displaylimits_{\Delta^{n}(k)}G/H\times\hom(i,(-))}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces Y(k)}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f}

and because the map of sets
\partial\Delta^{n}(k)\hookrightarrow\Delta^{n}(k)
is an inclusion, we deduce that
Y(k)
is the coproduct

X(k)\sqcup\left(\bigsqcupop\displaylimits_{\Delta^{n}(k)\backslash\partial\Delta^{n}(k)}G/H\times\hom(i,(-))\right)

and that
f(k)
corresponds to the associated summand inclusion.
∎

Proposition 2.3
.

For every cofibrant
X
in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet}^{G})
, for every
f\colon i\to j
in
I
and every
n
,
X(f)(n)\colon X(i)(n)\to Y(i)(n)
preserves the stabilisers of elements.

Proof.

Note first that every retract of an object
X
in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet}^{G})
that satisfies the condition from the statement also satisfies this condition. The result now follows from the fact that cellular objects clearly satisfy the condition from the statement using
2
.
∎

Proposition 2.4
.

Let
{\mathcal{O}}
be an
N_{\infty}
-operad for
C_{2}
with a complete indexing system. The forgetful functor
U\colon\operatorname{Alg}_{{\mathcal{O}}}\left(\operatorname{Fun}(\operatorname{Fin},\operatorname{sSet})^{C_{2}}\right)\to\operatorname{Fun}(\operatorname{Fin},\operatorname{sSet})^{C_{2}}
does not preserve cofibrant objects.

Proof.

Consider the graph subgroup
\Gamma\subseteq C_{2}\times\Sigma_{2}
given by the diagonal. The simplicial set
{\mathcal{O}}_{2}^{\Gamma}
is contractible and, in particular, there is a
\Gamma
-fixed point in the set
{\mathcal{O}}_{2}(0)
. Because the
\Sigma_{2}
-action on
{\mathcal{O}}_{2}
is free, we obtain that the corresponding
C_{2}\times\Sigma_{2}
-equivariant map
(C_{2}\times\Sigma_{2})/\Gamma\to{\mathcal{O}}_{2}(0)
is injective. Define
X
as the free
{\mathcal{O}}
-algebra on
\hom(*,(-))
seen as a discrete simplicial presheaf. We obtain a
C_{2}
-monomorphism

\hom(\{1,2\},(-))\simeq(C_{2}\times\Sigma_{2})/\Gamma\times_{\Sigma_{2}}\hom(*,(-))^{2}\hookrightarrow{\mathcal{O}}_{2}(0)\times_{\Sigma_{2}}\hom(*,(-))^{2}

where the last term is a
C_{2}
-subset of
U(X)_{0}
and where the
C_{2}
-action on
\hom(\{1,2\},(-))
is induced by the free
C_{2}
-action on
\{1,2\}
. The identity map
\operatorname{id}_{\{1,2\}}
seen as an element of
\hom(\{1,2\},\{1,2\})
has the trivial group as stabiliser and the unique map
\{1,2\}\to*
seen as an element of
\hom(\{1,2\},*)
has
C_{2}
as stabiliser. This contradicts
2
and it follows, as
\hom(\{1,2\},(-))
is a
C_{2}
-subobject of
U(X)_{0}
, that
U(X)
is not cofibrant.
∎

3.
Cellular extensions of strong fixed cofibrant operads

Until the end of this paper,
I
is a small sifted category with finite coproducts. The category
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
will always be endowed with the projective model structure.

Definition 3.1
.

For every
n\geq 0
, the
graph model structure
on
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})^{G\times\Sigma_{n}}
is the model structure induced by the projective model structure on
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
and associated with the family of graph subgroups of
G\times\Sigma_{n}
, which exists using
[
19
, Lemma 2.12]
. We also call the corresponding model structure on collections in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})^{G}
the graph model structure. An operad
{\mathcal{O}}
in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet}^{G})
is
graph
\Sigma
-cofibrant
if its identity map
\eta_{{\mathcal{O}}}\colon 1\to{\mathcal{O}}
is a graph cofibration of collections.

Remark 3.2
.

Note that because
I
admits coproducts,
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
is a

monoidal model category.

Definition 3.3
.

A morphism
f\colon A\to B
in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})^{G}
is a
fixed cofibration
if, for every subgroup
H
of
G
, the following conditions are satisfied:

(1)

the morphism
f^{H}\colon A^{H}\to B^{H}
is a cofibration in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
;

(2)

the colimit of
f
, seen as a functor
I\to\operatorname{Arr}(\operatorname{sSet})^{G}
, commutes with
H
-fixed points.

Proposition 3.4
.

The class of fixed cofibrations is closed under pushouts and transfinite compositions.

Proof.

We prove first the result for transfinite compositions.
The point (1) is a direct consequence of the fact that fixed points commute with sequential colimits and of the fact that transfinite compositions of cofibrations are cofibrations. For the point (2), this follows from the fact that colimits commute with colimits and from the fact that fixed points commute with sequential colimits. For pushouts, the proof is similar and uses the fact that the underlying map (if we forget the
G
-action) of a fixed cofibration is a monomorphism and the fact that fixed points commute with pushouts along monomorphisms in any presheaf category.
∎

Proposition 3.5
.

Every cellular map in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})^{G}
(for the model structure of
3
) is a fixed cofibration.

Proof.

Using
3
, it is enough to prove the result for generating cofibrations. Consider
f\times G/H
with
f
a cofibration in
\operatorname{Fun}(I,\operatorname{sSet})
. We have
(G/H\times f)^{K}\simeq f\times(G/H)^{K}
which is a cofibration as
f
is a cofibration. Moreover,
\operatorname{colim}(G/H\times f)^{K}\simeq(G/H)^{K}\times\operatorname{colim}f\simeq\operatorname{colim}((G/H)^{K}\times f)
.
∎

Notation 3.6
.

For every partition
{\mathscr{P}}=(U_{1},\ldots,U_{k})
of
\{1,\ldots,n\}
, we denote by
\Sigma_{{\mathscr{P}}}
the subgroup of
\Sigma_{n}
spanned by the permutations that preserve
{\mathscr{P}}
.

Definition 3.7
.

A
marked partitioned tree
is a marked tree
T
(in the sense of
[
17
, Section 5]
) with a partition
{\mathscr{P}}
of the set of its leaves.

For a given marked partitioned tree
T
with partition
{\mathscr{P}}
, we denote by
\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathscr{P}}}(T)
the subgroup of
\operatorname{Aut}(T)
spanned by the automorphisms of

that preserve
{\mathscr{P}}
.

Remark 3.8
.

The subgroup
\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathscr{P}}}(T)
of
\operatorname{Aut}(T)
is the inverse image of
\Sigma_{{\mathscr{P}}}
by the homomorphism
\alpha\colon\operatorname{Aut}(T)\to\Sigma_{n}
that sends an automorphism of
T
to the associated permutation of its leaves.

Construction 3.9
.

Consider a partitioned marked tree
T
with partition
{\mathscr{P}}
and suppose we have the grafting operation
t_{m}(T_{1},\ldots,T_{m})
representing
T
. We can endow every
T_{i}
with a partition
{\mathscr{P}}_{i}
induced
{\mathscr{P}}
. As in
[
2
, Lemma 5.9]
, we can partition the set

into subsets of pairwise isomorphic partitioned marked trees

with

. We finally obtain that

\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathscr{P}}}(T)\simeq\left(\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathscr{P}}_{1}}(T_{1})^{m_{1}}\times\cdots\times\operatorname{Aut}_{{\mathscr{P}}_{k}}(T_{k})^{m_{k}}\right)\rtimes\left(\Sigma_{m_{1}}\times\cdots\times\Sigma_{m_{k}}\right).

Notation 3.10
.

Given a partition
{\mathscr{P}}=(U_{1},\ldots,U_{k})
of

and a tuple

the

-object

\mathop{\mathchoice{\vbox{\Huge\kern 2.56606pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 2.56606pt}}{\vbox{\Large\kern 1.23997pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 1.23997pt}}{\vbox{\normalsize\kern 0.86108pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 0.86108pt}}{\vbox{\small\kern 0.77498pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 0.77498pt}}}_{{\mathscr{P}}}g:=\mathop{\mathchoice{\vbox{\Huge\kern 2.56606pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 2.56606pt}}{\vbox{\Large\kern 1.23997pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 1.23997pt}}{\vbox{\normalsize\kern 0.86108pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 0.86108pt}}{\vbox{\small\kern 0.77498pt\hbox{$\mathchar 5123\relax$}\kern 0.77498pt}}}_{i=1}^{k}g_{i}^{\square^{n_{i}}},

.

.

The class of strong fixed cofibrations is closed under pushouts and transfinite compositions.

Proof.

Proposition 3.19
.

. Using

Proposition 3.20
.

For every operad
{\mathcal{O}}
in

-action on

, we can compute all sifted homotopy colimits by only considering homotopy sifted categories with finite coproducts. We can thus assume that
