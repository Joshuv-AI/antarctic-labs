UltraSep Acoustic Separation Platform
Resonance-Driven, Energy-Optimized Solid-Liquid Separation

Value Proposition
UltraSep is an intelligent ultrasonic separation platform that transforms solid–liquid separation through
real-time eigenfrequency resonance locking and ultra-low power energy optimization. By dynamically
matching ultrasonic output to system resonance while maximizing particulate removal per unit of applied
energy, UltraSep replaces centrifugation and fouling-prone filtration with precision-controlled acoustic
forces that significantly reduce power consumption, mechanical complexity, and operating cost while
improving recovery performance. This integrated platform unites patented resonance-based acoustic
control and energy-per-removal optimization with chemistry-enhanced separation and proprietary system
software into a scalable, high-impact commercial technology.

Overview
UltraSep continuously tracks system eigenfrequencies and locks ultrasonic output to resonance, ensuring
maximum acoustic energy coupling and eliminating wasted off-frequency power. Simultaneously, energy
optimization algorithms quantify particulate removal as a function of applied energy and automatically
select ultrasonic field conditions that deliver the highest separation efficiency per watt. Together,
resonance locking and energy-per-removal optimization create a closed-loop, adaptive separation system

that converts separation from a mechanically intensive process into a precision-engineered, low-energy
acoustic platform.
