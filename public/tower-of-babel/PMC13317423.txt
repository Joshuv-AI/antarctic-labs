The authors identified an inadvertent image duplication in Figure 7D (specifically, the immunofluorescence images for Ki67). This unintentional error occurred during the final figure assembly process. Due to the large volume of image files generated for this study, the incorrect representative image was mistakenly inserted into the panel.
