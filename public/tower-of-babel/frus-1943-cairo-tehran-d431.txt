President Roosevelt to President Inönü

Cairo, December 3, 1943.

My Dear President Inonu, I am sending one of my own planes to meet you and your party in Adana, together with my son-in-law, Major John Boettiger, who will hand this to you.

The plane is in command of Major Otis F. Bryan.

I hope you will have a smooth trip and I am looking forward with great pleasure to seeing you on Saturday afternoon.

Very sincerely yours,

F[ranklin] D R[oosevelt]

Source: Roosevelt Papers
