3 The Frontier Supercomputer
Developed for ORNL by Hewlett Packard Enterprise, Frontier is
the world’s first exascale computer. Frontier has 9,472 AMD Epyc
7713 2GHz CPUs and 37,888 AMD Instinct MI250X GPUs, capable
of performing 1.1 ExaFLOPs. Frontier has 74 compute cabinets each
containing 128 nodes connected with a HPE Slingshot-11 network.
Frontier’s 74 compute cabinets are each 400 kilowatt (kW) (rated)
making the system total ~30 (MW) (rated). Frontier was the world’s
first Exascale supercomputer as judged by the top500 ranking [8].
