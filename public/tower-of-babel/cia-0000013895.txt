In late June 1966 Source observed a U.S. POW walking along the road between
HOA BINH and HANOI at approximately 1400 hours. The POW had come from

the direction of NINH BINH Province and was being escorted by two NVN
guerrillas. The POW was observed inthe BA HANG DOL area about 20 kilometers
from CHI NE in HOA BINH Province. The prisoner wore dark blue pajamas, a
conical hat and sandals. He was about 1.75 meters tall, had an oval face,

a rosy complexion, a slender build and a paunch. He was not wearing glasses,
and he was not carrying anything. He appeared to be in good health, and

he had no visible wounds or scars.

The two guards were also dressed in pajamas. They walked on either side
of the POW and held, ropes which were secured to each of his arms; however,
the guards appeared to be treating him well. No one in the POW's group
was talking, and the guards did not speak to anyone in Source's unit.
