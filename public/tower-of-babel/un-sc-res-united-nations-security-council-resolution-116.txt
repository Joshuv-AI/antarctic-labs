Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 732nd meeting, on 26 July 1956

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of Tunisia <small>[1]</small>,

Recommends to the General Assembly that Tunisia be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Ibid., document S/3622</small>
