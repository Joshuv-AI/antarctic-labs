1. Father of one of the men deputed by Jehoiakim to capture Baruch, the scribe of Jeremiah (Jer. xxxvi. 26). 2. Chief of one of the families of Manasseh, living on the eastern side of the Jordan (I Chron. v. 24). 3. Father of Jerimoth, the leader of Naphtali at the time that David numbered the people (I Chron. xxvii. 19). J. Jr. G. B. L.
