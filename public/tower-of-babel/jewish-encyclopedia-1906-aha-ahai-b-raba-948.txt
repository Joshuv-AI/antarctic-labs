A Babylonian amora, son of Raba b. Joseph, and a contemporary of Amemar II. and of Ashi; died in 419. During the last five years of his life he filled the rectorate of the academy at Pumbedita (Shab. 93 b ; Yeb. 46 a ; B. B. 124 b ; Men. 3 b ; "Letter of Sherira"; Grätz, "Gesch. d. Juden," 2d ed., iv. 379).

S. M.
