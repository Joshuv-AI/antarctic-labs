  1. Project Identification and Scope
  The Participant in this project was to produce and supply mixed REOs prepared from a range of end-of-life NdFeB-
  bearing feedstocks, including those containing Tb.
  In the second project, they were to supply dilute streams/waste containing lanthanides as well as solutions directly
  leached from end-of-life magnets and other sources, both dilute and concentrated. Lastly, the Participant was to
  advise on process scale up and system/separation requirements for commercialization.

  2. Participant Contributions to the CMI Projects
  $5,460 was the reported cost-share over the course of the CRADA.

  Project 3.1.2 (Dan Park)
  -A mixed REO from an EOL wind turbine magnet (6% Dy) was provided by TdVib. The project used this feedstock to
  baseline the performance of new protein variants.
  -A mixed REO from terfenol-D scrap composed of Tb/Dy was provided by TdVib. The project’s milestone in Year 14 (Tb
  vs Dy separation) involves work with this feedstock. The Team is hoping that there will be a publication stemming
  from this work.
