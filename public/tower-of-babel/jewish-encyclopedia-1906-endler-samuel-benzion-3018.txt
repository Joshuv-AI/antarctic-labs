Talmudist; lived at Prague (?) in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. He was the author of "Emunat Yisrael," treating of morals and dogmas as dealt with by Maimonides, Judah ha-Levi, Naḥmanides, and others (Prague, 1832).

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 41, No. 773; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 109. K. M. Sel.
