Xingchao Deng, Beiyan Zou, Hong Zhai

Abstract
The precoloring extension problem involves initially assigning colors to certain vertices and determining whether such a partial coloring can be extended to a proper k-coloring of the entire graph for an integer k k . In a landmark result, Grötzsch demonstrated that every planar graph without triangle is 3-colorable. A significant generalization of this theorem was recently achieved by La et al. [ 6 ] . In this paper, we establish that any precoloring of three independent vertices (or two independent vertices) can be extended to a 3-coloring of the entire graph when it is a connected outerplane graph containing at most one triangle (or two triangles, respectively). Keywords: Grötzsch’s Theorem; precoloring extension; homomorphism; 3-coloring; triangle AMS subject classification 2020: 05C10; 05C15; 05C75.
