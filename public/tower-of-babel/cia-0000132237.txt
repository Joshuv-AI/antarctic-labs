Gorbachev Statenent

President Gorbachev issued a statement today expressing
concern that war in the Gulf is taking on an impetus of its
own and calling for a quick end to the "conflagration,"
according to TASS. He stated allied military operations
threaten to exceed their mandate and called Iraq’s attempts to
draw Israel into the conflict "extremely dangerous."

Gorbachev reiterated Soviet support for UN resolutions and
said he was sending a personal envoy to Baghdad to meet with

Saddam. Wa

Comments: Gorbachev’s comments reflect growing concern among
the leadership and Soviet society that the Gulf war will
escalate and spread. Moscow hopes to distance itself fron
allied military operations while seeking a political solution

to the Gulf crisis. er
