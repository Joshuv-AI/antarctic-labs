USsR/Persian Gulf: Goviets Fear Spread of Conflict

A Soviet Foreign Ministry spokesman expressed "profound
anxiety" over the third Iraqi Scud attack on Israel and the
sabotage of Kuwaiti oilfields by Iraqi troops, according to press
reports. Asked whether the US had gone beyond the mandate of the
US resolution, he affirmed the USSR’s "principled stand" but
repeated Gorbachev’s warning against further escalation of
military conflict. Soviet media have voiced similar concerns;
one Pravda article today called for efforts to localize the
conflict to prevent new forces--such as NATO--from establishing
dominance in the region.

Comment: Soviet statements reflect a genuine fear that military
conflict could reach the USSR’s borders, as well as Moscow’s
preoccupation with the changing balance of power in the Middle
Fast. Soviet officials probably realize that a wider Gulf war
would reduce their chances to lay the post-war role of
peacemaker in the region. ee]
