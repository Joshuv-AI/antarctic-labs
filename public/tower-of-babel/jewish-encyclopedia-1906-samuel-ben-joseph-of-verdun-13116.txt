French tosafist of the twelfth and thirteenth centuries. He was a disciple of Isaac ben Samuel the Elder of Dampierre, with whom he corresponded, and is mentioned in the Tosafot, in "Or Zarua'," and in "Haggạhot Maimuniyyot" as "ha-Baḥur" (the Younger). He is sometimes confounded with Samuel ben Ḥayyim, likewise cited as "Samuel of Verdun."

Bibliography: Gross, Gallia Judaica, pp. 206-207; Zunz, Z. G. p. 55. D. S. Man.
