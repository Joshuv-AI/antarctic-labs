Source said he had read the NVN newspapers NHAN DAN and QUAN DOI in January or 4,3(a)(4)
February 1966 that most of the pilots captured during 1965 were Majors and :
Lieutenant Colonels, while in 1966 mos¢ of them weke first. Lieutenants; most
of the pilots were shot dopn over THASH HDA Province, The newspaper also gave the
names of the U.S. pilots shekdewn and une places where they were shot down.

The newspapers further stated the NVN policy toward U.S. prisoners. They were
to receive double rations of food, which was the international standard, and
were not to be beaten. Most of the people in HANOI Source talked to wondered
why they had to be nice to the Americans and were angry because of the bonbings
and wanted to beat the U.S. prisoners. Source stated that before this policy
was instituted the militia used to beat the U.S. prisoners. If this policy
toward prisoners was disobeyed the person who committed the act would be
criticized; if he killed a prisoner he would be condemmed by the courts.

Source further stated that he had learned from a friend who worked at Hospital
9 in VINH YEN Village that there were POW camps and a special hospital to
treat U.S. prisoners, whose locations were kept secret because the populace
was angry and if they knew the location they might kill the prisoners. The
friend had heard about the hospital first at a medical assembly held in March
1966 at the Medical Corps Bureau, HANOI.
