Cabalist; lived at Zülz, Prussia, in the seventeenth century; corrector of the press in the printing-house of Shabbethai Bass at Dyhernfurth. He published (Amsterdam, 1688), with additions of his own, the cabalistic commentaries on the Pentateuch, "Pa'aneaḥ Raza" (The Revealer of the Secret), compiled in the thirteenth century by Isaac ha-Levi ben Judah.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1127; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 137. K. I. Br.
