Abstract
. A Cayley (di)graph Cay ⁡ ( G , S ) \mathrm{Cay}(G,S) of a finite group G G is called CI if, for every Cayley (di)graph Cay ⁡ ( G , T ) \mathrm{Cay}(G,T) of G G , Cay ⁡ ( G , S ) ≅ Cay ⁡ ( G , T ) \mathrm{Cay}(G,S)\cong\mathrm{Cay}(G,T) implies that S σ = T S^{\sigma}=T for some σ ∈ Aut ⁡ ( G ) \sigma\in\mathrm{Aut}(G) . The group G G is called an NDCI-group (resp. NCI-group) if every normal Cayley digraph (resp. graph) of G G is CI. It was shown that the generalized quaternion group Q 4 ​ n \mathrm{Q}_{4n} of order 4 ​ n 4n ( n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 ) is an NDCI-group if and only if either n = 2 n=2 or n n is odd, but its NCI-group classification has been left as an open question. In this paper, we solve the question and prove that Q 4 ​ n \mathrm{Q}_{4n} is an NCI-group for every n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 . A normal Cayley (di)graph of a group G G is called NNN if its automorphism group contains a non-normal regular subgroup isomorphic to G G , and G G is called an NNND-group (resp. NNN-group) if it admits an NNN Cayley digraph (resp. graph). In this paper, we show that Q 4 ​ n \mathrm{Q}_{4n} is not an NNN-group for every n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 , and is an NNND-group if and only if n ≥ 6 n\geq 6 and n n is even. \mathrm{\Gamma}

[

\mathrm{Cay}(G,S)
of

\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{Cay}(G,S))

[

is the Euler’s function. Finally, Muzychuk

proved that a cyclic group of order

and

, or

where

and

is odd square-free, which is one of the remarkable achievements in the study of DCI-groups and CI-groups. For other groups, there are also a lot of works on this line, and we do not intend to give a full account on their study in general. Interested readers are referred to

In recent years, CI have been considered for normal Cayley digraphs. Note that Li

provided examples of normal Cayley digraphs that are not CI, and proposed the following question: Characterize normal Cayley digraphs which are not CI-digraphs. More recently, Ryabov

A Cayley digraph

In 2018, Xu

Let

and let

be the generalized quaternion group of order

Theorem 1.1
.

Every generalized quaternion group
\mathrm{Q}_{4n}

(n\geq 2)
is an NCI-group.

By Babai
[

of a group

if all regular subgroups of

\mathrm{\Gamma}
is CI if and only if

is the unique regular subgroup
of
\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{\Gamma})
isomorphic to

Corollary 1.2
.

Every generalized quaternion group
\mathrm{Q}_{4n}

(n\geq 2)
is not an NNN-group.

For generalized quaternion NNND-group, we have the following result.

Theorem 1.3

A generalized quaternion group
\mathrm{Q}_{4n}

(n\geq 2)
is an NNND-group if and only if
n\geq 6
and

is even.
