No. 114 The President to the Ambassador in the Soviet Union (Harriman)[1]

top secret

[Washington,] 3 July 1945.

Pauley, Dr. Lubin and four assistants are authorized to report to President on proposed reparation plan during forthcoming meeting.

Army has been notified.

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent to the United States Naval Attaché Moscow, via Navy channels.
