 Geometrically Consistent High-Order Remapping for ALE Hydrodynamics
 Jan Nikl
 26-FS-014

Project Overview
Multi-physics hydrodynamic simulations using the Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian (ALE) method
are critical for LLNL’s mission. The overall idea of ALE hydrodynamics is Lagrangian modeling
capturing with high resolution shocks or other features of the flow, which is followed by steps
of remeshing and remapping to restore numerical quality of the computational mesh. However,
the existing methods of remapping of quantities discretized with high-order finite elements do
not typically conserve the fundamental invariants, like mass or energy. In contrast, the
geometrically consistent method conserves such integral quantities, while it preserves local
bounds of the solution. The central concept of the approach is exact consistency with the
exchange of volume between mesh cells, which achieves these properties while staying
compatible with the well-established advection-based remap of current ALE implementations.
We explored this approach in a modified version of Laghos miniapp and report results of typical
hydrodynamical test problems, where the overall quality is maintained or improved while
conservation is attained at the level of machine precision. This advancement benefits long-term
stability and reliability of ALE simulations and shows it can contribute to more accurate
hydrodynamic modeling at LLNL.

Mission Impact
The LDRD project shows potential of the novel remapping algorithm, which can improve ALE
hydrodynamic codes inside the lab and beyond. The main new feature of the researched
approach is conservation of the remapped quantities, increasing long-term stability and
accuracy of ALE simulations. Moreover, the method remains to be advection-based, which
allows its deployment in existing ALE hydrodynamic codes. Through that, the research can
contribute to more accurate mission-critical hydrodynamic simulations at LLNL and enhance
capabilities in advanced numerical modeling.
