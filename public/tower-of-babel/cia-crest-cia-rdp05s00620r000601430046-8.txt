activities

general luncheons

Nationally respected leaders discuss subjects of broad
interest at one or more of our 4 regional forums: down-
town Los Angeles, Orange County, Pasadena, and west-
ern Los Angeles.

section luncheons

The Sections are small discussion groups that deal with
specific areas of concern. Speakers are selected for their
distinction in specialized fields and the freshness and
vitality of their ideas. These meetings are held in down-
town Los Angeles and western Los Angeles.

publications and outreach

Town Hall Reporter

Give-and-take discussions held in the in-
formal atmosphere of the Sections are
reported in digest form in this monthly
magazine.

Town Hall Journal

A weekly publication that presents a con-
densation of the General Luncheon pro-
grams. It contains a 3-week Calendar of
Town Hall events for your future planning .
and keeps you abreast of what is happening in
your organization.

Town Hall On the Air

General Luncheon programs are broadcast
nationwide to an audience of approximately
one million listeners and by the Voice of
America in 36 languages.

Town Hall Reports

In-depth studies made by the Sections result
in informative and thought-provoking publi-
cations. Highly regarded in the community,
these reports have been used as guides for
public and business policies.
