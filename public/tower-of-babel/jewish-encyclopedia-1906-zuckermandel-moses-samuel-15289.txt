German rabbi and Talmudist; born at Ungarisch-Brod, Moravia, April 24, 1836. He became a rabbi in Pleschen, Prussia, and was appointed lecturer on the Mora-Leipziger foundation at Breslau April 1, 1898. He has published: "Die Erfurter Handschrift der Tosefta" (1876); "Die Tosefta nach den Erfurter und Wiener Handschriften" (1880-82); "Spruchbuch Enthaltend Biblische Sprüche aus dem Gebetbuche" (1889); and "Vokabularium und Grammatik zu den Hebräischen Versen des Spruchbuches I." (1890).

Bibliography: Kürschner, Literatur-Kalender, 1898, s.v.; Frankl-Grün, Gesch. der Juden in Ungarisch Brod, Vienna, 1905, pp. 56-57. S. N. D.
