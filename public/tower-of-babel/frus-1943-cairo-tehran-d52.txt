Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (22 October 1943, d52)

secret

London, 22 October 1943.

Most secret and personal from Former Naval Person to President. Number 469.

Your 391. I am instructing Eden to back you up to the full but I do not think Ankara would be possible; first, because of security, and secondly, because there are the Taurus Mountains to cross.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Whatever happens we have got to meet soon.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram
