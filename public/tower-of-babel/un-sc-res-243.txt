Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 1384th meeting, on 12 December 1967
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the People’s Republic of Southern Yemen for admission to the United Nations, [1]
Recommends to the General Assembly that the People’s Republic of Southern Yemen be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Twenty-second Year, Supplement for October, November and December 1967, document S/8284.
