SUBJECT: Military Activity Connected with the Hungarian
Crisis

Soviet Forces in Hungary

Major elements of at least four Soviet divisions
totaling approximately 40,000 men are now within the
borders of Hungary. In addition to the two mechanized
divisions normally stationed in Hungary, major elements
of the Soviet division at Timisoara, Rumania arrived
in the vicinity of Budapest by mid-day on 24 October,
Early on 27 October an unidentified element of a unit
normally stationed in the Carpathian Military District,
USSR, near Miskolc in Northeastern
Hungary. s element could represent units of either
or both of two mountian rifle divisions and their corps
headquarters stationed on the USSR-Hungarian border,

The 2d Gds Mech Div normally stationed in Kecskemet
and the Soviet division from Rumania have borne the brunt
of the fighting to date. In contrast to the heavy
fighting and casualties that these units have reported,

the 17th Gds Mech Div at
Szombathely reflects normal operating procedures
except for the dispatch of an element to the vicinity of
Budapest. Seriously wounded are being evacuated by air
from Tokol airfield in Budapest to Lvov in the USSR.
Fresh replacements from the USSR were reported arriving
in Szolnok on 26 October 7
Throughout the period of action heavy expenditure of
ammunition and difficulties of resupply have been
noted.

There has been no firm evidence that the USSR is
using military aircraft to provide other than logistic
support of ground units. On 26 October, altitute
restrictions and flight bans were imposed along the route
Moscow-Kiev-Lvov-Budapest, probably to facilitate high
priority flights.

Status of forces elsewhere

All East German military and security units are
on a high grade degree of alert. All Soviet units
in East Germany also appear to be on alert status.
Troop rotation continued through 22 October with 18
loaded trains going through Poland to the USSR and
18 loaded trains returning. Only two major fall
exercises were observed in the Soviet Zone of Germany
and since 25 September there have been no further major
exercises. Soviet Units in Poland have returned to

their garrison areas

Military units in the three western USSR military
districts were alerted during the 19-21 October period
and the Carpathian military district continues to
reflect this alert status.
