UFO SIGHTINGS NO.4: STATEMENTS BY MILITARY AND GOVERNMENT
OFFICIALS

HIGHLIGHTS OF RECENT INTENSIVE MEDIA COVERAGE OF THE UFO
PHENOMENON IN THE USSR INCLUDE A REPORT OF VISUAL AND RADAR TRACKING
OF UFOS BY A SOVIET AIR FORCE UNIT; A SIGHTING BY SOVIET COSMONAUTS;
AN ASSESSMENT BY THE DEPUTY CHAIRMAN, USSR COUNCIL OF MINISTERS; AN
ACCOUNT OF A SPACE LAUNCH MISTAKEN FOR A UFO; COMMENTARIES BY TwO
SOVIET GENERALS; AND REFERENCES TO STATEMENTS BY MIKHAIL GORBACHEV
ON UFOS.

THE 28 OCTOBER 1990 ISSUE OF RABOCHAYA TRLBUNA CITED THE 1
OCTOBER 1990 ISSUE OF AVIATION WEEK ON SIGHTINGS OF UFOS OVER NEVADA
THAT WERE REPORTEDLY MOVING AT INCREDIBLE SPEEDS. IT IS
HYPOTHESIZED IN THE SOVIET ARTICLE THAT SAUCER-LIKE AND
TRIANGULARSHAPED CRAFT WITH UNUSUAL CAPABILITIES ARE BEING DEVELOPED
AND TESTED IN NEVADA AND IN THE SOUTHWEST AS PART OF A U.S. MILITARY
PROJECT: ''BELGIUM WAS VISITED BY STRANGE FLYING MACHINES OF
TRIANGULAR SHAPE. THEN THE 'TRIANGLES' APPEARED HERE. (SOVIET)
AIR DEFENSE FORCES UNITS OBTAINED FIXES ON SIMILAR MACHINES....''
ACCORDING TO THE ARTICLE, THE SPEEDS OF THE UFOS SIGHTED OVER THE
MOJAVE DESERT WERE SIMILAR TO THOSE OF THE UFOS SIGHTED IN THE USSR
-- 10 TIMES THE SPEED OF SOUND, AND, AS IN THE SOVIET SIGHTINGS,
''THEY MOVE SILENTLY, CLIMB TO AN INCREDIBLE ALTITUDE, AND SOMETIMES
HOVER IN PLACE AND EMIT A PULSATING LIGHT.'' MOREOVER, ACCORDING TO
THE RABOCHAYA TRIBUNA ARTICLE, THE ''BELGIANS READ THE REPORT IN
AVIATION WEEK ‘WITH GREAT INTEREST' AND THE BELGIAN PRESS IS
HYPOTHESIZING THAT TOP SECRET AMERICAN AIRCRAFT, NOT UFOS, ARE
FLYING IN THE SKY OVER BELGIUM.''

THE 23 SEPTEMBER 1990 ISSUE OF SOVETSKAYA ROSSIYA INCLUDES AN
INTERVIEW WITH ACADEMICIAN L. I. “BALKI“. DEPUTY CHAIRMAN OF THE
USSR COUNCIL OF MINISTERS. IN REFERENC: TO GORBACHEV'S NEW ''500
DAYS PROGRAM,'' ABALKIN SAID THAT THE PROGRAM CONTAINED A ''KIND OF
MYSTERY EFFECT...SOME MIRACULOUS MEANS, AND OUR PEOPLE BELIEVE IN
MIRACLES....PERHAPS YOU NOTICED THAT IN SUCH CRISIS SITUATIONS A
MASS BELIEF IN ALIENS AND UFOS...DEVELOPS. THIS IS A SOCIAL
PHENOMENON, YOU KNOW. I DISCUSSED THIS MANY YEARS AGO. THIS
SITUATION BECAME CLEARLY EVIDENT IN THE LATE 70S AND EARLY
80S...SOCIA1 UPHEAVALS CAUSE SUCH A BELIEF.

THE 1 SEPTEMBER 1990 LENINGRADASKAYA PRAVDA CARRIED AN ARTICLE BY
VALENTIN GOLTS, ACTING MEMBER OF THE USSR GEOGRAPHICAL SOCIETY, WHO
DEVELOPS A STRONG CASE TO PROVE THAT THE 3 AUGUST SIGHTINGS OF A UFO
IN THE LENINGRAD OBLAST CAN BE EXPLAINED AS OBSERVATIONS OF THE

KOSMOS SPACE LAUNCH. HE NOTES THAT ALL OF THE OBSERVERS AGREE THAT

THE UFO APPEARED INITIALLY AS AN UNUSUALLY BRIGHT OBLONG CLOUD.

‘THEN, FROM THE RIGHT OF THE CLOUD, A SPHERE WITH A WELL-DEFINED

PROFILE SEPARATED, WITH ARCS OF BRIGHT ILLUMINATION EXTENDING OUT

FROM THE SPHERE. THE VELOCITY OF THE SPHERE SOMEWHAT EXCEEDED THE
VELOCITY OF A JET AIRCRAFT.'' GOLTS STATES THAT ALL OF THE SIGNS
DESCRIBED ABOVE COINCIDE WITH THE LIGHT EFFECTS ASSOCIATED WITH THE
LAUNCH OF KOSMOS, WHICH TOOK PLACE AT THIS TIME.

THE ARTICLE CONTAINS FOUR ILLUSTRATIONS OF THE ''3 AUGUST UFO''
SENT IN BY TWO OF THE OBSERVERS, AND GOLTS POINTS OUT THAT THE
ILLUSTRATIONS CLEARLY DEPICT THE PHASES OF THE LAUNCH. IN THE
FIRST PHASE OF THE LAUNCH (ILLUSTRATION L), A MOVING, BRIGHT,
SHINING SPOT IS OBSERVED, TOGETHER WITH A CONTRAIL RESEMBLING THE
CONTRAIL OF A JET AIRCRAFT. GRADUALLY THE CONTRAIL SPREADS OUT AND
BECOMES WIDER. WHEN OBSERVED FROM THE SIDE, IT RESEMBLES A FLATFISH
(ILLUSTRATION 2). THE SECOND PHASE OF THE LAUNCH BEGINS AT THE
MOMENT OF FIRSTSTAGE SEPARATION. A BRIEF FLASH OCCURS, AND IN THE
PLACE WHERE THE BRIGHT SPOT IS LOCATED, A RAPID EXPANSION OF GAS IN
THE FORM OF A SPHERE IS OBSERVED. THE LOWER FRINGES OF THE SPHERE
CAN REACH TO THE HORIZON. THIS CREATES THE IMPRESSION THAT THE

_BRIGHT SPOT HOVERS BRIEFLY. SOMETIMES, AT THE MOMENT OF THE FLASH
(ILLUSTRATION 3), CONCENTRIC CIRCLES MOVING OUTWARD, OR COMPLEX
VORTICES ARE OBSERVED. WHEN THERE IS A HEAD-ON OR END-VIEW
OBSERVATION OF THE LAUNCH, THE SHAPE OF THE GASES CAN RESEMBLE A
FLOWER OR A CROSS. THE SUBSEQUENT MOVEMENT OF THE SECOND STAGE IS
OBSERVED AS.A BRIGHT SPOT WITH A CONE OF GASEOUS JETS STREAMING OUT
BEHIND IT, RESEMBLING RAYS EXTENDING AT AN ANGLE OF 70-110 DEGREES.
THE SPEED OF THE ROCKET IS SIGNIFICANTLY GREATER THAN THE SPEED OF A
HIGH-ALTITUDE AIRPLANE. WHEN THE SECOND STAGE SEPARATES, THERE MAY
OCCUR ANOTHER FLASH, WITH RAYS STREAMING OUTWARD (ILLUSTRATION 4).
THE FIRST PHASES CAN LAST FROM 8 TO 25 MINUTES AND THE LAST, THE
TRAIL IN THE SKY, CAN LAST UP TO THREE HOURS.

THE JOURNAL AVIATSIYA I KOSMONAVTIKA, NO. 8, 1990, INCLUDES AN
INTERVIEW WITH MAJOR GENERAL OF AVIATION A. LEONOV. THE GENERAL,
WHO IS ALSO A COSMONAUT, SAID THAT A LOT OF BUNK IS PUBLISHED BY
UFOLOGICAL ORGANIZATIONS. UFOLOGICAL ORGANIZATIONS, ACCORDING TO
LEONOV, TOOK A PHRASE USED BY U.S. ASTRONAUTS OUT OF CONTEXT AND
FABRICATED THEIR FANTASIES AROUND IT. FOR EXAMPLE, AFTER THE
LANDING ON THE MOON THERE WERE TRANSMISSIONS BACK AND FORTH BETWEEN
THE ASTRONAUTS AND MISSION CONTROL IN HOUSTON. THE GENERAL SAID:
"'NEIL ARMSTRONG, LEAVING THE SHIP, STEPPED CAREFULLY ONTO THE SOIL,
AND THEN BECAME MORE BOLD; HIS STEPS BECAME MORE CONFIDENT AND HE
BEGAN TO JUMP AROUND. I SAW THESE VIDEO FRAMES. JAMES ALDRIN,
TURNING TO HIM, SAID 'WATCH OUT, THEY ARE WATCHING US.' 'THEY' IN
THE SENSE OF 'EARTH.' LATER HE ADVISED THAT THEY SHOULD NOT DISOBEY
INSTRUCTIONS AND THAT THEY SHOULD BE CAREFUL. AND, IN FACT,
ARMSTRONG BEGAN TO WALK AROUND AFTER THIS WITH NORMAL STEPS. I
HAVE SAID TO THE UFOLOGISTS ON MORE THAN ONE OCCASION: ‘WHAT ARE
YOU SPECULATING ABOUT? THERE IS NO BASIS FOR IT'.'' (THE

THE 12 MAY 1990 ISSUE OF KRASNAYA ZVEZDA INCLUDES A LENGTHY
INTERVIEW WITH COLONEL GENERAL ALEXANDER MAKSIHOV WHO WAS RESPONDING
TO THE PUBLIC'S DEMAND FROM THE MILITARY FOR INFORMATION ON THE
SURGE OF UFO REPORTS. MAKSIMOV, CHIEF SPECIALIST OF THE USSR
MINISTRY OF DEFENSE FOR REUSABLE SYSTEMS, SAID THAT, ALTHOUGH HE IS
NOT OPPOSED TO THE UFO HYPOTHESIS, AND IS ''ONLY CALLING FOR A SOBER
AND SCIENTIFIC ANALYSIS OF THE PHENOMENON,'' HE KNOWS FOR CERTAIN
THAT THE UFOS SIGHTED OVER BAYKONUR WERE ACTUALLY PHENOMENA
ASSOCIATED WITH ROCKET LAUNCHES. HE SAID THAT HE PERSONALLY
OBSERVED ONE OF THESE UFOS AND NOTICED THAT THE TRAJECTORY OF THE
FLY-OVER WAS FROM THE SOUTHWEST TO THE NORTHEAST. HE NOTED THAT
THAT COURSE ''IS OUR FAVORITE AND THE MAJORITY OF OUR SPACE VEHICLES
FLY RIGHT ALONG IT. BUT ALSO ALONG THIS COURSE FLY ALL OF THE
OBJECTS WHICH ACCOMPANY THE SPACE VEHICLE WHICH WENT INTO ORBIT WITH ,
IT...AS MANY AS 20 DIFFERENT OBJECTS CAN DROP OFF THE SPACE VEHICLE
AS IT MOVES INTO ORBIT--THIS TRASH ORBITS EARTH AT ALTITUDES VARYING
FROM 200 TO 800 KILOMETERS.'' MAKSIMOV SAYS THAT HE PERSONALLY
