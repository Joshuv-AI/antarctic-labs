Ambassador from the sultan Mohammed IV. to King Charles XI. of Sweden; died May 29, 1673, at Amsterdam, where he was buried with great honors. His son, Judah Berbi , succeeded him as ambassador, and returned from Amsterdam to Constantinople.

Bibliography: D. L. de Barrios, Historia Universal Judaica, Amsterdam, 1683; D. H. de Castro, Keur von Grafsteenen, pp. 95 et seq. D. M. K.
