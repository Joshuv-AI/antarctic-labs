SUBJECT: - MILITARY ATTACHE OF EL SALVADOR

IN PARAGUAY

i cae cf JOSE RICARDO ((POZO)) IS CURRENTLY
SALVADORAN ATTACHE IN ASUNCION. HIS RANK IS LIEUTENANT COLONEL.
FYI, THERE IS ANOTHER SALVADORAN MILITARY OFFICER IN ASUNCION,
MAJOR VICTOR HUGO ((VEGA)) VALENCIA. VEGA WAS SELECTED IN JAN 86
FOR STAFF COLLEGE TRAINING IN ASUNCION. WE UNDERSTAND VEGA IS
STILL IN PARAGUAY BUT DO NOT KNOW IF HIS STATUS HAS CHANGED AND
WHETHER HE MIGHT NOW BE ATTACHED TO THE ATTACHE OFFICE. WE
HAVE HEARD HIM REFERRED TO HERE, AT LEAST ONCE, AS "ATTACHE IN
ASUNCION." VEGA VALENCIA WAS ARRESTED IN MAY 1980 IN CONNECTION

WITH THE ASSASSINATION OF SALVADORAN ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARNULFO
((ROMERO)).
VEGA VALENCIA RETURN TO EL SALVADO INENTLY. UPON

HIS RETURN HE WILL PROBABLY BE ARRESTED AGAIN, STILL IN
CONNECTION WITH THE ROMERO CASE. HIS POSSIBLE ARREST IS IMPORTANT
AS HE WILL BE THE FIRST ACTIVE DUTY MILITARY OFFICER TO BE
ARRESTED IN THIS STAGE OF THE CASE AND HE WOULD BE EXPECTED

TO IMPLICATE OTHER ACTIVE DUTY OFFICERS IN THE ASSASSINATION.

Approved for Release
NOV 1993
