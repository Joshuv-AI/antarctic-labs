Dear Mr. Director:

The Gridiron Club will hold its 1980 Spring Dinner on Saturday,

March 15, at the Capital Hilton Hotel in Washington. There will be
a 6 p.m. reception followed by the dinner bell at 7 p.m.

I am writing now to alert you that you will receive a formal invitation
later from a Club member asking you to attend as his or her guest. I
hope you will reserve this date on your calendar.

The evening will follow the traditional format with satirical musical
and political skits built around a Republican and Democratic speaker.

We hope, of course, that the President and Mrs. Carter will attend.

the political climate, we are planning a lively and entertaining
evening, which would be greatly enhanced by your presence. I hope you
can be with us.

Sincerely,

Grant Dillman, President
The Gridiron Club
