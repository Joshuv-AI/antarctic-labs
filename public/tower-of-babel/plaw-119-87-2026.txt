Public Law 119–87: To amend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to extend the authorities of title VII of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978, and for other purposes.

An Act To amend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 to extend the authorities of title VII of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978, and for other purposes.Apr. 30, 2026[S. 4465]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — EXTENSION OF AUTHORITIES OF TITLE VII OF THE FOREIGN INTELLIGENCE SURVEILLANCE ACT OF 1978.

SUBSECTION (a) — Extension of Repeal Date of Title VII

PARAGRAPH (1)

in paragraph (1) (50 U.S.C. 1881 note), by striking “April 30, 2026” and inserting “June 12, 2026”; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

in paragraph (2) (18 U.S.C. 2511 note), in the matter preceding subparagraph (A), by striking “April 30, 2026” and inserting “June 12, 2026”.

SUBSECTION (b) — Effective Date

The amendments made by this section shall take effect on the earlier of the date of the enactment of this Act or April 29, 2026.

Approved April 30, 2026.
