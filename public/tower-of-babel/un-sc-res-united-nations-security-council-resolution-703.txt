Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 3002nd meeting, on 9 August 1991 <small>([1])</small>

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Federated States of Micronesia for admission to the United Nations,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Federated States of Micronesia be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Adopted without vote. </small>
