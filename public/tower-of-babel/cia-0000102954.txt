2. SOVIET PRISONS WERE USUALLY OVERCROWDED, BUT THERE WERE NO
KNOWN PLANS TO CONSTRUCT NEW FACILITIES. ON ONE OCCASION, THE
DIRECTOR OF THE DEPARTMENT OF ADMINISTRATION ORGANS OF THE CENTRAL
COMMITTEE OF THE ESTONIAN SSR COMMENTED THAT NO NEW PRISONS WOULD BE
BUILT FOR IDEOLOGICAL REASONS. TO DO SO WOULD BE TO ADMIT THAT THE
COMMUNIST SYSTEM HAD NOT DRAMATICALLY REDUCED THE AMOUNT OF CRIME IN
RUSSIA AS HAD BEEN PREDICTED BY THE FOUNDERS OF COMMUNISM.
