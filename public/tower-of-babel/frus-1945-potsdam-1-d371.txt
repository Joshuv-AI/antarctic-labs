No. 371 The Acting Secretary of State to the Representative on the Allied Commission on Reparations (Pauley)

top secret us urgent

[Washington,] July 8, 1945—2 p.m.

1557. For Pauley.

Instead of replying now to the questions raised in your 2418, July 5, 8 p.m.[1] Secretary suggests that these issues be discussed and decided when you join President at Berlin.

Grew
