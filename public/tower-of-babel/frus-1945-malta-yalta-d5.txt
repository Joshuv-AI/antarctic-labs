President Roosevelt to Marshal Stalin [1] (27 July 1944, d5)

top secret priority

[Pearl Harbor, Hawaii,] 27 July 1944.

Number 32. Top Secret and Personal. For Marshal Stalin from the President.

I can fully understand the difficulty of your coming to a conference with the Prime Minister and me in view of the rapid military progress now being made but I hope you can keep such a conference very much in mind and that we can meet as early as possible. Such a meeting would help me domestically and we are approaching the time for further strategical decisions.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, Moscow, via Navy channels.
