==============================================================================

Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 756th meeting, on 12 December 1956
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of Japan, [1]
Recommends to the General Assembly that Japan be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Ibid., Seventh Year, Supplement for April, May and June 1952, document S/2673; and ibid., Eleventh Year, Supplement for October, November and December 1956, document S/3752.
