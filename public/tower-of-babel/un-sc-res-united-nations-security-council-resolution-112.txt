Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 716th meeting, on 6 February 1956

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Sudan, <small>[1]</small>

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Sudan be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Supplement for January, February and March 1956, document S/3543.</small>
