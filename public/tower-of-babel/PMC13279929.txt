Correction: Epimedin C attenuates airway inflammation and remodeling in Asthma by intervening M2 macrophage polarization via modulating the PI3K/Akt/mTOR signaling pathway
Authors: Zewen Zhang, Shoubao Cao, Ziwen Qin, Tingting Liu, Qin He, Gen Li, Chuanjun Huang, Yujuan Chen, Yunshan Wang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-42160-4, published online 24 February 2026

The original version of this Article contained an error in the order of the Figures in which Fig. 2 was published as Fig. 3, Fig. 3 was published as Fig. 5, Fig. 5 was published as Fig. 6, Fig. 6 was published as Fig. 2.

The original Figs. 2, 3, 5 and 6 are added below.Fig. 2Epimedin C alleviated pathological changes in the lung tissue of asthmatic mice. (A) HE staining; (B) PAS staining; (C) Masson staining. HE, hematoxylin and eosin; PAS, periodic acid-schiff; Dex, dexamethasone.Fig. 3Epimedin C reduced the expression of M2 macrophage biomarkers of inflammation and EMT in asthma.
