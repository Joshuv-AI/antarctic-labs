Article title: Persisting predominant clinical CC87 Listeria monocytogenes in China: evolutionary dynamics and diversification

Authors: Huanga C., Qin X., Xu G., Xia R., Han H., Wang Z., Deng X., Jiao X., Li S., and Yin Y.

Journal:
Emerging Microbes & Infections

Bibliometrics: Volume 15, Issue 1

When this article was first published online, Figure 2A contained an error. During figure preparation, the data for pregnant women were inadvertently shifted by one row, resulting in incorrect alignment with the corresponding age groups (16–25, 26–35, and 36–45 years).

Importantly, this was a figure preparation error and does not affect the underlying data, statistical analysis, results, interpretations, or conclusions of the article.

The error has now been corrected. The corrected Figure 2 is displayed below.
