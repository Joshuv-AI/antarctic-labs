Father of Jacob Ḥagiz and grand-father of Moses Ḥagiz; according to an epitaph, died in 1634. He edited Solomon Duran's "Tif'eret Yisrael" about 1596, and wrote: "Debar Shemu'el," a haggadic commentary, especially on Deuteronomy Rabbah (Venice, 1596); and "Mebaḳḳesh ha-Shem," sermons on the Pentateuch ( ib. 1596).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2411; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 155. J. M. Sc.
