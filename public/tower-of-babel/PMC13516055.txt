Error in Figure

In the original publication [1], an accidental duplicate panel was found in Figure 9B during post-publication review. We have fully remade and replaced Figure 9B using raw data from independent repeated experiments. All revisions only involve image layout adjustment.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.

(A) The content of NP genes in each group, (B) the content of influenza virus NP protein in each group (Magnification is 10 × 10 (objective lens × eyepiece)), (C) the expression of NP protein in each group.
