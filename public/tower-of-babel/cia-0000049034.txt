1, @@BBPTRACES ON S. ((SAMAYOA)) REVEAL THAT A SALVADOR RICARDO
((SAMAYOA)) LEIVA (SUBJECT) DPOB 13 SEPT 1950, SAN SALVADOR, IS A
SELF-DECLARED MEMBER OF THE SALVADORAN DEMOCRATIC REVOLUTIONARY FRONT
(FDR), THE NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN), AND FARABUNDO MARTI
POPULAR LIBERATION FORCES (FPL), A SALVADORAN LEFTIST INSURGENT
GROUP. SUBJECT WAS A FORMER MINISTER OF EDUCATION IN THE SALVADORAN
REVOLUTIONARY JUNTA GOVERNMENT IN EXILE IN MEXICO CITY. IN 1979 AS A
MEMBER OF THE POPULAR REVOLUTIONARY BLOCK HE TOOK PART IN THE
OCCUPATION OF THE FRENCH AND VENEZUELAN EMBASSIES IN SAN SALVADOR.
SUBJECT ATTENDED COLLEGIO SAN JOSE EXTERNADO AND WAS A MEMBER OF THE
JESUIT HIGH SCHOOL FACULTY PRIOR TO BECOMING MINISTER OF EDUCATION.

<. INFORMATION ON ARMIN ((WERTZ)) COMES EXCLUSIVELY FROM WIRE
SERVICE STORIES, WHICH IDENTIFY HIM AS A FREE-LANCE WEST GERMAN
JOURNALIST IN EL SALVADOR WORKING FOR "STUTTGART ZEITUNG" AND RADIO
FREE BERLIN. HE WAS A FRIEND OF THE MURDERED DUTCH JOURNALISTS
((KUUYPER)) AND ((KOSTER)) AND WAS DESCRIBED BY ONE PRESS ACCOUNT AS
THE "LAST PERSON EXCEPT THEIR KILLERS TO SEE THEM ALIVE". AS A FAVOR
HE TOOK THE JOURNALISTS TQ MEET LEFTISTS GUERILLAS IN CHALATENAGO
PROVINCE. THE THREE MET FOUR ALLEGED LEFTIST GUERILLAS AT A PLACE
CLOSE TO A SALVADORAN MILITARY BASE LESS THAN ONE HOUR BEFORE THE
DEATHS OF THE JOURNALISTS. WERTZ FLEW FROM EL SALVADOR TO MANAGUA ON
20 MAR 82, WHERE HE CHALLENGED THE OFFICIAL GOES VERSION OF THE
KILLINGS OF THE JOURNALISTS AND ALLEGED THAT GOVERNMENT SECURITY
FORCES MAY HAVE BEEN INVOLVED IN THEIR DEATHS.
