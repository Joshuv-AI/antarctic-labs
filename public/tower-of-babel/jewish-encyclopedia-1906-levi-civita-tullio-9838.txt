Italian physicist; born at Padua March 29, 1873; educated at the university there (Ph. D). He was successively appointed assistant professor (1898) and professor (1902) of applied mechanics, and professor of higher mechanics. He is also (1904) instructor in applied mechanics in the Regia Scuola di Applicazione per gl' Ingegneri connected with the university.

Bibliography: Annuario della R. Università di Padova, 1898-99 et seq. S. U. C.
