This book argues that the Dogon people of Mali possessed detailed astronomical knowledge about
the star Sirius B (a white dwarf companion star) that could only have been obtained through
contact with an advanced civilization. Temple draws on the ethnographic work of Marcel Griaule
and Germaine Dieterlen to argue for ancient extraterrestrial contact.
