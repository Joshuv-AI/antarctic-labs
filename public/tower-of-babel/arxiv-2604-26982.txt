The quantity

A more precise combinatorial counting accounts for all the states with total occupation number

The theory does not account for the reason why the anomalous term

Transferred Fine-tuning

The naturalness problem of the cosmological constant is not solved, but is instead passed on to the problem of neutrino mass. This makes it less competitive than other neutrino mass models (e.g., the see-saw mechanism) which are better understood and more stable.

A Prerequisite for Neutrino Mass

No Genuine Unification

A central flaw in this gravitational anomaly proposal is its failure to genuinely unify dark energy and neutrino mass.
