The overconvergence of multivariable ( φ q , 𝒪 K × ) (\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}) -modules at the perfectoid level
Changjiang DU

Abstract
Let K K be a finite unramified extension of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} , and E E a finite extension of K K with ring of integers 𝒪 E \mathcal{O}_{E} . We define the overconvergence of multivariable ( φ q , 𝒪 K × ) (\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}) -modules over A mv , E A_{\mathrm{mv},E} and explore some basic properties. We prove the overconvergence at the perfectoid level using the geometry of relative Fargues-Fontaine curve.

Introduction

Let
K
be a finite unramified extension of
\mathbb{Q}_{p}
with ring of integers
\mathcal{O}_{K}
and residue field
\mathbb{F}_{q}
, and let

be the absolute Galois group. Let

be a finite extension of

-representations of

-modules for certain extension

(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules is equivalent to the category of

-representations of

(
[

). A common choice for

is the cyclotomic extension; the resulting
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules are then called

(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules. Cherbonnier and Colmez proved that cyclotomic
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules are always overconvergent; that is, every cyclotomic
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-module descends to a finite free module over the Robba ring
\mathcal{R}_{E}
, where
\mathcal{R}_{E}
is the ring of Laurent series in one variable with coefficients in
E
which converge in an open annulus of outer radius
1
and inner radius

for some
r<1
(
[
9
]
). This fundamental result allows us to relate cyclotomic
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules and
p
-adic differential equations, and to recover many invariants in
p
-adic Hodge theory using cyclotomic
(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules, see for example
[
12
]
,
[
4
]
, and
[
5
]
.

One can also choose

as the Lubin-Tate extension associated to some uniformizer of

(\varphi,\Gamma)
-modules (see for example
[

[

-analytic Galois representations and proved that every overconvergent Lubin-Tate

-module corresponds precisely to a quotient of the tensor product of a

-analytic Galois representation and a Galois representation which factors through the Lubin-Tate character (
[
6
]
,
[

]

Let

Definition 1.1

We say that a multivariable

-module

We establish the following result:

Theorem 1.2

(Theorem

The base change functor

(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module

Another possible approach is to consider locally analytic vectors of

for the action of

to prove the overconvergence of

is quite complicated. At present, the only overconvergent examples we know are unramified characters of

.

2.1
The coefficient ring

Recall that

A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}=\bigcup_{s\geq 1}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\left[\dfrac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}}\right],

hence there exists
s_{0},n\geq 1
,
x^{\prime}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s_{0}}
such that
x=\frac{x^{\prime}}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}}
.
Then
\pi x=\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}}x^{\prime}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
is topologically nilpotent for
s\geq\max\{s_{0},n\}+1
by Lemma
2.4
. Thus
(1+\pi x)^{-1}=1+\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}(-\pi x)^{i}
converges in
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
for
s\geq\max\{s_{0},n\}+1
, hence
1+\pi x
is invertible in
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
.
∎

Remark 2.11

Using Newton’s method, one can check that

is a henselian pair. We do not need this fact.

Corollary 2.12
.

If
M
is a finite projective
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
-module, then

is a free
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
-module.

Proof.

Since

is finite projective over

,

is finite projective over
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}/\pi=A
. By
[
27
, Satz 3, p. 131]
,
M/\pi M
is a finite free
A
-module. Let
e_{1},\dots,e_{r}\in M
such that the image of
e_{1},\dots,e_{r}
in
M/\pi M
forms an
A
-basis of
M/\pi M
. By Lemma

M=\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}e_{i}

\pi

Corollary 2.13

Let

\pi

2.4
Overconvergent multivariable
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-modules

The
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-equivariant inclusion
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
induces a functor

\displaystyle j^{\dagger,*}:\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})}(A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger})

\displaystyle\rightarrow\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})}(A_{\mathrm{mv},E})

(2.24)

\displaystyle M

\displaystyle\mapstochar\rightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}}M.

By Proposition
A.7
,
j^{\dagger,*}
is an exact functor. In this section, we will construct a right adjoint functor
j_{*}^{\dagger}
of
j^{\dagger,*}
and investigate some basic properties of
j^{\dagger,*}
. In particular,
j_{*}^{\dagger}
is left exact. The construction of
j_{*}^{\dagger}
follows Cherbonnier’s thesis (
[
10
]
).

Definition 2.16
.

Let
M
be an étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
,
s\geq 1
. We define

\displaystyle F_{\dagger}(M)

\displaystyle\coloneq\left\{N:N\ \text{is a finitely generated }A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\text{-submodule of}\ M,\varphi_{q}(N)\subseteq N\right\},

\displaystyle F_{\dagger,s}(M)

\displaystyle\coloneq\left\{N:N\ \text{is a finitely generated }A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\text{-submodule of}\ M,\varphi_{q}(N)\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N\right\},

\displaystyle F_{\dagger,\infty}(M)

\displaystyle\coloneq\left\{N:N\ \text{is a finitely generated }A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\text{-submodule of}\ M,\varphi_{q}(N)\subseteq N\right\}.

Then we define

\displaystyle j^{\dagger}_{*}(M)\coloneq\bigcup_{N\in F_{\dagger}(M)}N,\quad j^{\dagger,s}_{*}(M)\coloneq\bigcup_{N\in F_{\dagger,s}(M)}N,\quad j^{\dagger,\infty}_{*}(M)\coloneq\bigcup_{N\in F_{\dagger,\infty}(M)}N.

(2.25)

Lemma 2.17
.

Let
M
be an étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
. Then

\displaystyle j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)=j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)\left[\dfrac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}}\right],\quad j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)=\bigcup_{s\geq 1}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M).

(2.26)

Proof.

For every
N\in F_{\dagger}(M)
, assume that
N=\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}e_{i}
, and
\varphi_{q}(e_{i})=\sum_{j}a_{ij}e_{j}
for some
a_{ij}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
(these
a_{ij}
may not be unique). Then for
n\geq 1
,

\varphi_{q}\left(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}e_{i}\right)=\sum_{j=1}^{r}a_{ij}\dfrac{\varphi_{q}(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n})}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}}Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}e_{j}.

By (
2.11
),
\dfrac{\varphi_{q}(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n})}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}}\in Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{(q-1)n}+pA_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,1}
. Since
pA_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}
by (
2.5
), we have
a_{ij}\dfrac{\varphi_{q}(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n_{ij}})}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n_{ij}}}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}

for
n_{ij}\gg 0
. Thus replacing
e_{0},\dots,e_{r}
by
Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n_{0}}e_{0},\dots,Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n_{0}}e_{r}
with
n_{0}\coloneq\max_{i,j}n_{ij}
, we may assume that
\varphi_{q}(e_{i})=\sum_{j=1}^{r}a_{ij}e_{j}
for some
a_{ij}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}
. Then
N_{\infty}\coloneq\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}e_{i}
is an element of
F_{\dagger,\infty}(M)
and
N_{\infty}\left[\frac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}}\right]=N
.
Thus

j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)=j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)\left[\dfrac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}}\right].

For any
N\in F_{\dagger,\infty}(M)
, assume that
N=\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}e_{i}
, and
\varphi_{q}(e_{i})=\sum_{j}a_{ij}e_{j}
for some
a_{ij}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}
. Since
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}=\bigcup_{s\geq 1}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
, there exists
s(N)\geq 1
such that
a_{i,j}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s(N)}\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
for any
1\leq i,j\leq r
,
s\geq s(N)
. Then
\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}e_{i}\in F_{\dagger,s}(M)
and

N=\bigcup_{s\geq s(N)}\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}e_{i}.

This finishes the proof.
∎

Note that for
N\in F_{\dagger}(M)
and
a\in\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
, since
\varphi_{q}
commutes with the action of
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
,
a(N)
is also stable under
\varphi_{q}
. Hence
a(N)\in F_{\dagger}(M)
. This implies that
j^{\dagger}_{*}(M)
is stable under the
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
-action. For the same reason,
j^{\dagger,s}_{*}(M)
and
j^{\dagger,\infty}_{*}(M)
are also stable under the
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
-action. The following lemma is important in the proof of the finiteness of
j^{\dagger}_{*}(M)
:

Lemma 2.18
.

Let
M
be a finite free étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
,
s\geq 1
. Then

\left(\dfrac{1}{\pi}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\right)\cap M=\dfrac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M).

Proof.

Let
N\in F_{\dagger,s}(M)
(Definition
2.16
), and let
N_{1}\coloneq\left(\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}N\right)\cap M
. Note that every
x\in N
can be written as
\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}\cdot\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}x
with
\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}x\in\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}N
, hence
N\subseteq N_{1}
.
Since
N_{1}\subseteq\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}N
and
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
is Noetherian (Lemma
2.4
),
N_{1}
is a finite
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
-module.
By (
2.11
),

\dfrac{\varphi_{q}(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s})}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{qs}}\in\left(1+\dfrac{p\mathfrak{m}}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{q}}\right)^{s}\subset A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,q}\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}.

And note that

A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}\subseteq\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{qs}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}+A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
by Lemma
2.4
, thus we have

\displaystyle\varphi_{q}(Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s})A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}\subseteq Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{qs}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}\subseteq\pi A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}+Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}.

(2.27)

Therefore,

\displaystyle\varphi_{q}(N_{1})

\displaystyle\subseteq\left(\varphi_{q}\left(\dfrac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}\right)A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N\right)\cap M

\displaystyle\subseteq\left(A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N+\dfrac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}N\right)\cap M

\displaystyle=A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N+\left(\dfrac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}N\cap M\right)\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N_{1},

where the first inclusion follows from
N\in F_{\dagger,s}(M)
and
\varphi_{q}(M)\subseteq M
, the second inclusion follows from (
2.27
), and the last equality follows from
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,qs}N\subseteq M
, and the last inclusion is due to
N\subseteq N_{1}
. Therefore,
N_{1}\in F_{\dagger,s}(M)
. This implies that

\displaystyle\left(\dfrac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\right)\cap M\subseteq j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M).

Since every
x\in j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)
can be written as
\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}\cdot\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}x
where
\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}x\in\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)
, hence
j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\subseteq\left(\frac{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}{\pi}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\right)\cap M
, then the required equality follows.
∎

Corollary 2.19
.

Let
M
be a finite free étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
. Then

j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)\cap\pi M=\pi j_{*}^{\dagger}(M).

Proof.

The inclusion
\pi j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)\subseteq j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)\cap\pi M
is obvious. Conversely, let
x\in M
with
\pi x\in j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)
, we need to show that
x\in j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)
. By (

), there exists
n\geq 0
such that
Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}\pi x\in j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)=\bigcup_{s\geq 1}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)
. Thus there exists
s\geq 1
such that
\pi Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{n}x\in j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)
, hence

x\in j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\left[\frac{1}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}}\right]\subset j_{*}^{\dagger}(M)
.

Lemma 2.20
.

Let
M
be a finite free étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})

A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
,
s\geq 1
. If the image of

.

By Lemma

,

j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M)\cap\pi M=\dfrac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M).

Thus for

, there exists
y_{0}\in\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}e_{i}
such that

. By induction on
n\geq 0
, we can prove that there exists
y_{n}\in\sum_{i=1}^{r}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}e_{i}
such that

x-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}\right)^{j}y_{j}\in\left(\dfrac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}\right)^{n+1}j_{*}^{\dagger,s}(M),\quad n\geq 0.

Note that

.

Proposition 2.21

If

(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})

In particular,
j^{\dagger,\infty}_{*}(M)

Proof.

By Lemma
2.4

\displaystyle\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\cap\pi A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}=\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}}{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}\cap\pi A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}=A^{\circ}.

(2.28)

For

(Definition

\frac{N}{N\cap\pi M}

\dfrac{j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)}{j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)\cap\pi M}=\bigcup_{N\in F_{\dagger,\infty}(M)}\dfrac{N}{N\cap\pi M}\subseteq\left(M/\pi M\right)^{\circ}.

By Lemma

,
\left(M/\pi M\right)^{\circ}

A^{\circ}

is Noetherian, it follows that
\frac{j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)}{j_{*}^{\dagger,\infty}(M)\cap\pi M}
is also a finite

where the last equality uses the definition of
\Delta_{1}
. Hence,

\displaystyle W_{\mathcal{O}_{E}}(A_{\infty})\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}D_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\otimes}(\rho)\cong W_{\mathcal{O}_{E}}(A_{\infty})e_{0}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{f-1},

(2.32)

which implies that
D_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\otimes}(\rho)\cong A_{\mathrm{mv},E}e
with
\varphi(e)=e
,
a(e)=\chi(a)e
for
a\in\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
. Thus
D_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\otimes}(\rho)
is overconvergent.
∎

\varphi_{q}(e_{1}\wedge\dots\wedge e_{r})=\lambda e_{1}\wedge\dots\wedge e_{r}
. Let
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
act on
\wedge^{r}M
diagonally, for every
a\in\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
, there exists
c_{a}\in A^{\times}
such that
a(e_{1}\wedge\dots\wedge e_{r})=c_{a}e_{1}\wedge\dots\wedge e_{r}
.
Since
\varphi_{q}
commutes with the
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
-action, we have

\lambda\varphi_{q}(c_{a})=a(\lambda)c_{a},\quad c_{a}\in A^{\times},a\in\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}.

This implies that the ideal
\lambda A
is stable under the
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
-action, hence
\lambda A=A
(
[
7
, Corollary 3.1.1.7]
), hence
\lambda
is invertible.
∎

Corollary A.3
.

If
M
is an étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A
and
N\subseteq M
is a submodule stable under
\varphi_{q}
and the
\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times}
-action, then
N
is also an étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A
.

Proof.

Since
M
is finite and
A
is Noetherian,
N
is also a finite
A
-module. As
\varphi_{q}:A\rightarrow A
is flat, we see that
A\otimes_{\varphi_{q},A}N\subseteq A\otimes_{\varphi_{q},A}M
. Since
M
is étale, the map
A\otimes_{\varphi_{q},A}M\rightarrow M
is an isomorphism. This implies that
A\otimes_{\varphi_{q},A}N\rightarrow N
is injective, then we apply Lemma
A.2
to conclude.
∎

Lemma A.4
.

Let
M,N
be étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-modules over
A
with
N\subseteq M
. If
\mathrm{rank}_{A}N=\mathrm{rank}_{A}M
, then
M=N
.

Proof.

Since
\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}_{(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})}(A)
is an abelian category,
M/N
must be an étale
(\varphi_{q},\mathcal{O}_{K}^{\times})
-module over
A
, hence is a free
A
-module (
[
7
, Proposition 3.1.1.8]
), thus

\mathrm{rank}_{A}M/N=\mathrm{rank}_{A}M-\mathrm{rank}_{A}N=0,

thus
M/N=0
.
∎

A.3

Lemma A.5
.

Let
s\geq 1
. Then the inclusion
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is flat.

Proof.

Note that

\displaystyle\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\cong A^{\circ},\quad\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}=\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}{\pi A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}=A=\bigcup_{n=0}^{\infty}Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{-n}A^{\circ},

(A.3.1)

where the first isomorphism follows from Lemma
2.4
.
Thus
\frac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}
is a countable union of free
\frac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}
-modules, hence is a flat
\frac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}
-module. Consider the short exact sequence of
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
-modules

0\rightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\xrightarrow{\times\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\rightarrow\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\rightarrow 0,

applying
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}-
, we have a short exact sequence

\displaystyle\mathrm{Tor}_{1}^{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\left(A_{\mathrm{mv},E},A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}\right)\rightarrow\mathrm{Tor}_{1}^{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\left(A_{\mathrm{mv},E},\dfrac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\right)\rightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\xrightarrow{\times\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E},

and the first term of the exact sequence is zero since

-module. Thus
\mathrm{Tor}_{1}^{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\left(A_{\mathrm{mv},E},\frac{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}{\frac{\pi}{Y_{\sigma_{0}}^{s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}\right)=0

Proposition A.7
.

The inclusion
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is flat. As a consequence, the inclusion
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is faithfully flat.

Proof.

Let
I_{\infty}=\left(x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\right)\subseteq A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}
be a finitely generated ideal. Take
s_{0}\geq 1
such that
x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\in A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s_{0}}
, and let
I_{s}
be the ideal of
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}
generated by
x_{1},\dots,x_{n}
for
s\geq s_{0}
. Let
f_{s}
be the natural map
I_{s}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\rightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
for
s\geq s_{0}
or
s=\infty
. Let
t_{s}
be the natural map
I_{s}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\rightarrow I_{\infty}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
for
s\geq s_{0}
. The fact that
I_{\infty}=\bigcup_{s\geq s_{0}}I_{s}
implies that
I_{\infty}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}=\bigcup_{s\geq s_{0}}\mathrm{im}t_{s}
. Note that we have a commutative diagram of abelian groups

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces I_{s}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces I_{\infty}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces A_{\mathrm{mv},E}.}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f_{s}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces t_{s}}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f_{\infty}}

By Lemma
A.5
and Proposition
A.6
,
f_{s}
is always injective.
Thus for
x\in\ker f_{\infty}
, there exists
s\geq s_{0}
and
y\in I_{s}\otimes_{A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,s}}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
such that
x=t_{s}(y)
, hence
f_{s}(y)=f_{\infty}(t_{s}(y))=f_{\infty}(x)=0
, hence
y=0
, hence
x=0
, hence
f_{\infty}
is injective, hence the inclusion
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger,\infty}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is flat by Proposition
A.6
, hence
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}\hookrightarrow A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is flat. It remains to show that every closed point of
\mathrm{Spec}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
is contained in the image of
\mathrm{Spec}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}\rightarrow\mathrm{Spec}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
(
[
31
,
Tag 00HQ
]
). Note that
\pi
is contained in the Jacobson radical of
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}
(Lemma
2.10
) and the Jacobson radical of
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
(as
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}
is
\pi
-adically complete), thus it suffices to check that
\mathrm{Spec}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}/\pi\rightarrow\mathrm{Spec}A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}/\pi
is surjective. But this is obvious, since
A_{\mathrm{mv},E}/\pi\cong A\cong A_{\mathrm{mv},E}^{\dagger}/\pi
.
∎
