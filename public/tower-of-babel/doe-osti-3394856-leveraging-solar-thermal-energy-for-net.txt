 SECTION II: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Major Goals and Objectives
Firestone Walker Brewing Company (FWBC), Paso Robles, California, owns a 1-million-barrel
(BBL) brewery that requires up to 5 MW of process heat in form of saturated steam. Firestone
Walker Brewery has set a goal of reducing carbon emissions by 50% for our 1 million BBL
brewery. The major goals of the project, as defined in the Statement of Project Objectives
(SOPO), are:
 • Quantify the thermal and electrical energy demands of a 1 million BBL brewery to
 support deep decarbonization of process heat.
 • Evaluate opportunities to reduce overall heat demand through energy efficiency
 measures prior to renewable heat integration.
 • Develop an energy sourcing strategy leveraging concentrated solar thermal (CST),
 thermal energy storage (TES), and optional heat pump integration to replace natural gas
 consumption.
 • Establish a techno-economic and implementation roadmap to achieve a 50–90%
 reduction in carbon dioxide emissions from process heat generation.

Subtask 1.1: Baseline Assessment and Benchmarking
Subtask Summary: The Onsite measurement campaign has been completed.

Subtask 1.2: Energy Efficiency Study
Subtask Summary: The energy efficiency study is focused on quantifying available waste heat
streams and identifying opportunities to reduce overall thermal demand through direct heat
recovery. The objective is to minimize the net thermal energy (kWₜₕ) required for brewery
decarbonization by maximizing internal heat recuperation.

Findings are informed by both measured data and vendor-supported analysis conducted as part
of this project.

Subtask 1.3: Energy sourcing strategy
Subtask Summary: Multiple technology providers and consortiums submitted proposals
incorporating concentrated solar thermal (CST), thermal energy storage (TES), and hybrid
configurations (including cooling and power co-generation). To develop the most robust and
defensible financial model, Firestone Walker Brewing Company is optimizing its evaluation
framework to enable normalized comparison across all proposals and initiatives.
During the reporting period, FWBC initiated engagement with SLR to provide advanced logistics
and financial modeling.

The objective of this effort is to define a technically and economically viable energy sourcing
pathway to support Go/No-Go Decision Point 1

Subtask 1.4: Initial budgetary planning
Subtask Summary: Dependent on sourcing strategy: FWBC will need to wait for SLR analytics of
HaaS contracts and forecasting.

Subtask 1.5: Site Selection
Subtask Summary: Identify and evaluate candidate sites capable of supporting concentrated
solar thermal (CST) and thermal energy storage (TES) deployment, ensuring alignment with
technical, operational, and permitting requirements while minimizing project execution risk.

Approximately 9 acres of land just north of the wastewater Facility had been identified as the
ideal plot for a CSP development site.

Subtask 1.6: Permitting & Initiate NEPA process
Subtask Summary: Wallace Group / Kovesdi Consulting produced a permitting pathway report
identifying key regulatory requirements and approvals.
