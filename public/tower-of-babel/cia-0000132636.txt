LATIN AMERICA

Cuban President Using Latin American Tour to Attack US:
While he was attending Argentine independence celebrations
from 25 to 29 May, Cuban President Dorticos made a vitupera-
tive anti-US speech to a large and responsive audience at
the University of Buenos Aires. Argentine officials said
that they were shocked by the speech but that since it had
been staged primarily for a group already converted to Com-
munist attitudes it would not reach far beyond the university.
The Argentine ambassador to Cuba said that any attempt to

‘muzzle the universities would give the Cubans and Communists

an excuse to raise the issue of free speech and allow them
to call members of the Frondizi government "Yankee lovers."

Dorticos also went out of his way to meet with leaders
of an actively antigovernment, Peronista labor group in
Buenos Aires, inspiring at least one Argentine newspaper
to sharp criticism of him for meddling in Argentine political
affairs. The propensity of Cuban officials for stirring up
internal political tensions in their attempts to arouge sup-
port for the Castro regime in other Latin American countries
has irritated the governments of those countries, Uruguay
and Brazil are treating Dorticos' visit properly but coolly,
Venezuela is trying to put off his trip to Caracas, and
there has been outspoken criticism in Peru and Mexico of the
invitation extended to Dorticos' delegation-——after urging by Cuba.
