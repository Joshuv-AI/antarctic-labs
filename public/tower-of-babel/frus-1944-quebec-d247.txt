The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President’s Secretary (Tully)

secret

[Washington,] September 15, 1944.

MR-out-412. Personal and secret to Miss Grace Tully from Harry Hopkins.

I sent to the President memorandum entitled French Lend Lease Agreement and dated September 11th from the State Department.[1] FEA and State Department are anxious to know whether the President has initialed it.

Could you help me get an answer on this tomorrow?

Harry Hopkins

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Ante, p. 419.
