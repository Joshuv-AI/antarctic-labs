EC Ministers Back Peace Conference

At an emergency meeting in Paris, European Community
foreign ministers appealed to Iraq to withdraw immediately
from Kuwait to spare the Iragi people further destruction and
suffering. At the same time, the ministers renewed their
“total commitment" to the calling of an international
conference on peace in the Middle East. They pledged to work
for social justice and regional economic solidarity once
"international legality" was restored.

Italian Aircraft Missing in Gulf

An Italian Tornado fighter failed to return from a
Thursday evening mission over the Arabian Peninsula,
according to the Italian Air Force. The fate of the two-man
crew is unknown. |
