No. 1393 Diary Entry by the Polish Deputy Prime Minister (Mikołajczyk)[1]

[Translation]

[Babelsberg, July 31, 1945.]

Mr. Harriman called in the afternoon.[2] On behalf of President Truman, as chairman of the meeting of the Big Three which began at 4 o’clock, he invited the Polish Delegation to see President Truman the next day, when the latter would communicate the results of the meeting today.…

Source: Mikołajczyk Papers

Notes
[1] Cf. ante, p. 541.
[2] From the discussion which took place toward the end of the Eleventh Plenary Meeting on July 31, it would appear that Harriman probably paid his call on the Polish Delegation following the adjournment of that meeting at 7:15 p.m. Cf. ante, pp. 527–528, 538.
