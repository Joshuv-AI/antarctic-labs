The Ambassador to the Soviet Union (Harriman) to the Presidents Special Assistant (Hopkins)[1]

secret

[Tehran,] December 4, 1943.

Through Russian Embassy Teheran message from Molotov “Vishin-sky will represent USSR at Cairo”.[2] I will leave Teheran for Moscow morning fifth current month.

Source: Hopkins Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Cairo via Army channels.
[2] Vinogradov, not Vyshinsky, represented the Soviet Union at Cairo during the Second Cairo Conference, but he attended none of the formal meetings. See post, pp. 839, 858.
