A Levite, descendant of Kohath, and one of the ancestors of the prophet Samuel and of Heman, the singer. In Exodus vi. 24 and I Chronicles vi. 22 (37), ix. 19, Ebiasaph (Abiasaph) occurs as a son of Korah and brother of Assir and Elkanah; but in I Chronicles vi. 8 (23) he is stated to have been a son of Elkanah, son of Assir, son of Korah.
