Translator and historical writer of the nineteenth century, who lived in Salonica. He translated, from the Hebrew into Judæo-Spanish, Samson Bloch's geographical work, "Shebile 'Olam" (Salonica, 1853-57, 1860), with additions of his own, and wrote a short history of Napoleon.

Bibliography: Kayserling, Bibl. Esp.-Port. Jud. p. 12. M. K.
