German Talmudist of the beginning of the eighteenth century. He was the author of a work entitled "Ḳol Yehudah," a collection of notes on the Talmud by various rabbis of his time, with some remarks of his own. (Amsterdam, 1729, often reprinted).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 336; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1327. D. M. Sel.
