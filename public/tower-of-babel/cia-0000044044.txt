SUBJ: RIVERA Y DAMAS URGES CRISTIANI TO INVESTIGATE KILLINGS

SOURCE: PANAMA CITY ACAN IN SPANISH 1742 GMT 19 NOV 89
TEXT: °

((TEXT)) SAN SALVADOR, 19 NOV (ACAN - EFE) -- SAN SALVADOR
ARCHBISHOP ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS TODAY URGED PRESIDENT ALFREDO
CRISTIANI AND THE ARMED FORCES TO INVESTIGATE THE ASSASSINATION OF
THE SIX JESUITS, "BECAUSE THERE IS A STRONG PRESUMPTION THAT THE
ASSASSINS MAY. BE ARMED FORCES MEMBERS OR PEOPLE CLOSELY LINKED WITH
THE ARMY."

“PRESIDENT CRISTIANI AND THE ARMY'S HIGH COMMAND HAVE PROMISED TO
CONDUCT A THOROUGH INVESTIGATION. WE HAVE TAKEN THEIR WORD NOT JUST
BECAUSE THERE SHOULD BE JUSTICE, BUT ALSO BECAUSE THERE IS A STRONG
_PRESUMPTION THAT THE ASSASSINS MAY BE FROM THE ARMED FORCES OR PEOPLE
VERY CLOSELY RELATED TO THE ARMED FORCES," RIVERA Y DAMAS SAID DURING
THE SUNDAY HOMILY.

FOLLOWING THE RELIGIOUS CEREMONY, HELD AT THE “SACRED HEART"
BASILICA, AND IN THE PRESENCE OF SOME 50 NEWSMEN, RIVERA SAID: "I
STAND BY MY PREVIOUS STATEMENT. I HOPE THIS WILL MOTIVATE PRESIDENT
CRISTIANI AND THE ARMED FORCES."

ACCORDING TO WITNESSES, A GROUP OF PEOPLE WEARING MILITARY
UNIFORMS ASSASSINATED THE JESUITS ON 16 NOVEMBER -- FIVE SPANIARDS
