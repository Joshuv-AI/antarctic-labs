Correction: Optimization of enzyme-assisted extraction of polyphyllins from paris polyphylla var. yunnanensis rhizomes using response surface methodology
Authors: Linmei Dong, Peng Long, Yutian Jin, Lijia Chen, Xiahong He, Rui Sun
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-51333-0, published online 02 May 2026

The original online version of the Article contained errors.

First, the initials “Y. J.” and “L. C.” were inadvertently added to the author group as a 7th and 8th author. As a result, the author group now reads:

Linmei Dong, Peng Long, Yutian Jin, Lijia Chen, Xiahong He, Rui Sun

Furthermore, a Keyword was incorrectly written. As a result,

“pectinase-assisted extraction”

now reads:

“Pectinase-assisted extraction”

The original Article has been corrected.
