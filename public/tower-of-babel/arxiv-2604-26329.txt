Abstract
. In this paper, we study the rigidity properties of compact Kähler manifolds. Given a smooth family of compact Kähler manifolds 𝒳 {\cal X} over the unit disk Δ \Delta , we show that all the fibers are mutually isomorphic if the family is locally trivial at a point t 1 ∈ Δ t_{1}\in\Delta and the fiber 𝒳 t 1 {\cal X}_{t_{1}} is non-uniruled. This proves that the locally rigid implies the global rigid in Kähler. It also can be used to prove the so called global non-deformability for non-uniruled Kähler manifolds under Kähler morphisms.

Definition 2.2

A polarized family of compact Kähler manifolds (parametrized by a complex space

consisting of a proper smooth morphism

(1)

The value of the section

(2)

\eta(\omega_{{\cal X}/B})=0

of sheaves on
\mathcal{X}
.

When

is a smooth complex manifold, the above definition is related to the following definition of Kähler morphism.

Definition 2.3

A smooth proper morphism
