On the bottom-up construction of many-electron relativistic QED Hamiltonian
Wenjian Liu

Abstract
The charge-conjugated second quantization introduced for a bottom-up construction of the many-electron relativistic QED Hamiltonian [J. Chem. Phys. 139, 014108 (2013)] is elucidated in terms of the quantized electronic and positronic Dirac fields, so as to make the concept more transparent.



I
Introduction

Relativistic, correlation and quantum electrodynamics (QED) effects ought to be treated simultaneously and equally in high-precision spectroscopic calculations,
so as to make the left- and right-hand sides of the equation “Relativity + Correlation + QED = Experiment”
Liu 2023
as close as possible.
Since QED effect is generally smaller than relativistic and correlation effects in many-electron systems, the “first relativity and QED
then correlation” paradigm underlying relativistic QED is intrinsically inefficient. Instead, the “first relativity and correlation then QED”
paradigm
Liu and Lindgren 2013
should be adopted. To this end, a many-electron relativistic QED Hamiltonian
incorporating infinite-order relativistic effect and leading-order QED effect should be introduced. This can be done
in two ways: top-down reduction or bottom-up construction. The former tries to extract effect QED interaction operators
from the corresponding QED energy expressions, whereas the latter tries to build up a relativistic QED Hamiltonian based solely on the principles
of relativistic quantum mechanics. Following the former procedure, Shabaev
Shabaev 1993
proposed a relativistic QED Hamiltonian
under the no-pair approximation (NPA). Moreover, his Hamiltonian is defined only on an active space, which cannot be set up uniquely.
In contrast, the relativistic QED Hamiltonian constructed
Liu and Lindgren 2013
in a bottom-up manner is defined on the full Fock space without the NPA.
Interestingly, the same relativistic QED Hamiltonian can be obtained both algebraically
Liu and Lindgren 2013
and diagrammatically
Liu 2015
.
In particular, the latter clearly demonstrates why the previous formulation
Kutzelnigg 2012
is incomplete.
Although the bottom-up procedure for constructing a complete relativistic QED Hamiltonian
is fully scrutinized
Liu 2014a
;
Liu 2014b
, it is still often misunderstood
Inoue et al. 2023
;
Liu 2024a
;
Inoue et al. 2024
.
In essence, the underlying charge-conjugated contraction (CCC)
Liu and Lindgren 2013
of fermion operators, dictated by
the charge-conjugation symmetry of relativistic quantum mechanics, is not documented anywhere else in
the context of second quantization. Therefore, it appears necessary to reformulate the relativistic QED Hamiltonian
Liu and Lindgren 2013

in a different manner, so as to naturalize the emergence of the CCC
when normal-ordering fermion operators with respect to the filled Dirac sea of electrons.

The Einstein summation over repeated indices is to be employed throughout.
