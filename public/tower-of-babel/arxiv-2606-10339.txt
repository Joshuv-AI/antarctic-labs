A Constructive Version of Ekeland’s Variational Principle
Douglas S Bridges

Abstract
Building on preliminary results about lower sections of real-valued functions on a metric space, we provide a constructive counterpart of Ekeland’s theorem on the approximate optimisation of real-valued functions.

, we have either (
2
) or

f\left(x\right)+c\rho\left(x,v\right)<f\left(v\right)-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

(3)

To complete the proof, it will suffice to rule out the latter alternative.
Assuming (
3
), if
\lambda_{n}=1-\lambda_{n-1}
we have
\mathsf{L}(g_{n},f(x_{n})-\varepsilon_{n})=\varnothing
and
x_{m}=x_{n}
for all

m\geqslant n
. Hence
v=x_{n}
and therefore

f\left(x\right)+c\rho\left(x,v\right)=f\left(x\right)+c\rho\left(x,x_{n}\right)\geqslant f\left(x_{n}\right)-\varepsilon_{n}\geqslant f\left(v\right)-\frac{\varepsilon}{2},

contradicting (
3
). Thus, recalling that
\lambda_{0}=0
, for all
n

we must have
\lambda_{n}=0
and
K_{n}
equal to the compact set
\ \mathsf{L}(g_{n},f(x_{n})-\varepsilon_{n})
. By (
3
) and the continuity of
f

and
\rho
, for all sufficiently large
n
we have

f(x)+c\rho(x,x_{n})+\varepsilon_{n}<f(x)+c\rho(x,x_{n})+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}<f(x_{n});

whence
x\in K_{n}
and therefore

\rho(x_{n},x)\leqslant\mathsf{diam}(K_{n})<\frac{1}{2^{n-1}c}\rightarrow 0\text{ as }n\rightarrow\infty\,.

Thus
x=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}x_{n+1}=v
, a contradiction which ensures
that the alternative (
3
) is ruled out.

We now have a constructive version of a well-known classical consequence of
Theorem
7
that provides us with approximate minima.

Corollary 8

Let
f
be a uniformly continuous mapping on a locally compact
metric space
X
such that
\inf f
exists. Let
\varepsilon>0
and let

x_{0}
be a point of
X
such that
f(x_{0})<\inf_{x\in X}f+\varepsilon
.
Then for each
\lambda>0
there exists
v\in X
such that
f(v)\leqslant f(x_{0}),\ \rho\left(v,x_{0}\right)<\sqrt{\varepsilon}
, and

f\left(v\right)<f(x)+\sqrt{\varepsilon}\rho\left(v,x\right)+\varepsilon\

(4)

whenever
x\in X
and
x\neq v
.

Proof.
Taking
\alpha=\sqrt{\varepsilon}
in Theorem
7
, construct
v\in X
such that (
1
) and (
4
) hold. Then

f(v)+\sqrt{\varepsilon}\rho\left(v,x_{0}\right)\leqslant f\left(x_{0}\right)<\inf f+\varepsilon\leqslant f(v)+\varepsilon,

so
f(v)\leqslant f(x_{0})
and
\rho(v,x_{0})<\sqrt{\varepsilon}
.
