French Talmudist of the thirteenth century. Gross identifies him with Eliezer ben Aaron of Bourgogne, one of the six rabbis to whom Meïr Abulafia sent his letter on the doctrine of the resurrection. Eliezer was the author of a Talmudic work no longer extant, entitled "Sha'ar ha-Penim," mentioned by Aaron ha-Kohen of Lunel in his "Orḥot Ḥayyim."

Bibliography: Gross, Gallia Judaica, p. 109. L. G. A. Pe.
