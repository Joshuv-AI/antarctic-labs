English politician; born 1866. He was educated at Harrow School and University College, Oxford, and in 1888 was admitted to the bar at the Inner Temple, London. After unsuccessfully contesting the Isle of Wight in 1892, and Plymouth in 1895, he was elected member of Parliament for Plymouth in the Liberal interest in 1898, serving until 1900.
