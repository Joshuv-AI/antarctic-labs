President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (3 March 1944, d556)

[Washington,] 3 March 1944.

486. In reply to insistent questioning at a press conference today I stated that Italian merchant ships and war ships are now being used in our war effort by the Allied Mediterranean command and that some of the Italian ships or substitutes therefor from the British and American tonnage will be allocated to the Soviet Navy to assist in their requirements for their war effort.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent via military channels.
