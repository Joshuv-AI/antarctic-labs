2.1.
Yetter-Drinfeld modules and Nichols algebras

Let
H
be a Hopf algebra with bijective antipode. A left Yetter-Drinfeld module
V
over
H
is a left
H
-module
(V,\cdot)
and a left
H
-module
(V,\delta)
with
\delta(v)=v_{(-1)}\otimes v_{(0)}\in H\otimes V
for all
v\in V
, satisfying

\delta(h\cdot v)=h_{(1)}v_{(-1)}S(h_{(3)})\otimes h_{(2)}\cdot v_{(0)}
,
\forall\,v\in V,\ \forall\,h\in H.

We denote by
{}^{H}_{H}\mathcal{YD}
the category of finite-dimensional left Yetter-Drinfeld modules over
H
. It is a braided monoidal category: for
V,W\in^{H}_{H}\mathcal{YD}
, the braiding
c_{V,W}:V\otimes W\to W\otimes V
is given by

(2.1)

\displaystyle c_{V,W}(v\otimes w)=v_{(-1)}\cdot w\otimes v_{(0)},\quad\forall\,v\in V,\ w\in W.

In particular,
(V,c_{V,V})
is a braided vector space, that is,
c:=c_{V,V}
is a linear isomorphism satisfying the braid equation

(c\otimes\mathrm{id})(\mathrm{id}\otimes c)(c\otimes\mathrm{id})=(\mathrm{id}\otimes c)(c\otimes\mathrm{id})(\mathrm{id}\otimes c)
.

Definition 2.1
.

(
[
3
]
, Definition 2.1
)

Let
H
be a Hopf algebra and
V

,

is generated by

For any

It is well konwn that the Nichols algebra

2.2.
Bosonization and Hopf algebras with a projection

Let
