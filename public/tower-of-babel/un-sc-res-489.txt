Adopted unanimously at the 2291st meeting, on 8 July 1981.
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of Vanuatu[1] for admission to the United Nations,
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Vanuatu should be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

1. ↑ Official Records of the General Assembly, Thirty-sixth Session, Annexes, agenda item 20, document A/36/308-S/14506.
