## Summary

The novel follows the quest of Jason and the Argonauts aboard the ship Argo to retrieve the Golden Fleece from the kingdom of Colchis. The narrative weaves together:

- **Jason's backstory**: The rightful heir to Iolcos, robbed of his throne by his uncle Pelias
- **The Argo's construction**: Built by Argus with the help of Athena, containing prophetic wood from Dodona
- **The crew**: Including legendary heroes such as Orpheus, Heracles, Castor and Pollux, and Telamon
- **The journey**: Filled with adventures at the Symplegades, the island of Lemnos, the entrance to the Underworld
- **Medea**: The sorceress-priestess who aids Jason and falls in love with him
- **The retrieval**: Through magical means, Jason secures the Fleece and escapes with Medea
- **The aftermath**: Jason's eventual doom despite theFleece's recovery

## Key Themes

- The conflict between matriarchal and patriarchal religious systems
- The nature of poetic inspiration and artistic creation
- The transformation of consciousness through heroic quest
- The tragic consequences of cultural and religious collision
- Love and loyalty tested by impossible demands
