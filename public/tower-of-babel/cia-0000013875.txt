1. In late January 1966 (exact date unknown) an American pilot was seen
mid-way between THANH HOA City and HAM RONG Bridge, THANH HOA Province,
NVN. The FOW was a caucasian male (rank unknown) wearing civilian
clothes. He wore no glasses and had no visible wounds or dressings.
He was observed soon after being captured, in a dark green jeep being
driven north ih the direction of HAM RONG Bridge. It is unknown if

the prisoner was bound,

26 The driver and the one guard wore 5 light ¢ green ‘uniforms with ‘green
insignia on their lapels, The guard was holding a submachine gun,
The prisoner sat in a rear seat between the driver's seat and the
guard, There were no other vehicles in the party. No air strikes
were known to have taken place in the vicinity of THANH HOA City at
the time the prisoner was seen, No details were available on where
the pilot was downed or the fate of the aircraft,

(NIC COMMENT: Source observed the FOW from a truck while traveling south,
He was unable to talk with anyone in the group with the prisoner; however,
he did discover through hearsay from some workers five kilometers further
south of where the POW was seen that the prisoner was an American pilot
and that he had been captured recently).
