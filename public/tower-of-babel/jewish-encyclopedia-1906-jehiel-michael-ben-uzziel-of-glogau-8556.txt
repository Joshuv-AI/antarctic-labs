Rabbinical author; died in Vienna 1730. He was well versed in the Midrashim, and was the author of "Nezer ha-Ḳodesh," an extensive commentary on Midrash Rabbah, a part of which, namely, on the first section of Genesis, was published in Tessnitz 1718. Jehiel carried on a correspondence with Jacob Emden, as is mentioned in "She'elot Ya'beẓ," No. 2.

Bibliography: Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, i. 85; Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 521. S. S. N. T. L.
