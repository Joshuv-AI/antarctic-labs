CHILE: CHURCH, HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSION OFFICE BOMBED
PY21002B6 ;
PARIS AFP IN-SFPANISH 2159 GMT 19 JUL 85 -

C(TEXT)) -SANTT4GO, 18 JUL {AFP) -- a POWERFUL BOMBS HAS
DESTROYED THE WINDOWS AND WALLS OF A CATHOLIC CHURCH IN THE CITY
OF VILLA ALEMANA, 108 KM WEST OF SANTIAGO, IN AN ATTACK CLAIMED
BY THE CLANDESTINE CHILEAN ANTICOMMUNIST ACTION ({(ACCION
CHILENA ANTICOMUNISTA <= ACHA)), CHURCH SOURCES REPORTED TODAY,

ANOTHER BOMB BLAST DAMAGED THE CHILEAN HUMAN RIGHTS
COMMISSION HEADQUARTERS IN THE SAME CITY, BUT NO CASUALTIES WERE.
REPORTEO.

SOME ACHA LEAFLETS FCUND AT VILLA ALEMANA’ S SAN FELIPE
NERI PAPISH CHURCH, «HICK WAS HIT BY THE BOMB, READ, “AN EYE FOR
AN EYE AGAINST THE CASSOCKS. ”

AN ORGANIZATION OF NATIONALIST LEANING, THE ACHA HAS CLAIMED
NUMEROUS SIMILAR ATTACKS AGAINST CHILEAN CATHOLIC CHURCHES OVER
THE LAST FEW MONTHS.

DURING ONE OF THOSE ATTACKS,:° ON 6 OCTOBER 1964, AN ARMY
OFFICER DIED WHEN A FIPE BOMB HE WAS CARRYING DETONATED AND
DESTROYED A CHURCH IN PUNTA ARENAS, 2,508 KM SOUTH OF THIS
CAPITAL, ACCORDING TO AN INVESTIGATION CONDUCTED ‘BY THE COURTS,

TODAY'S BOMP ELASTS CCCURRED 1 WEEK AFTER THE GOVERNMENT OF
GENERAL AUGUSTO FINOCHET CONFIRMED THAT IT HAS BEGUN TO HO.D
TALKS WITH THE VATICAN IN AN EFFORT TO IMPROVE ITS DETERIORATED
RELATIONS WITH THE CHURCH.
