CYCLES IN THE UNIVERSAL MODULI STACK OF BUNDLES OF RANK TWO OVER GENUS TWO CURVES
Shubham Saha

Abstract
. We present a conjecture for the Chow ring of the universal moduli stack of bundles over hyperelliptic curves and prove it for rank and genus two. Consequently, we obtain explicit generators and relations to conclude that the Chow ring is tautological. In addition, we compute the Chow rings of products of universal Jacobians over genus two curves.

14C17, 14D20, 14H45

Introduction

The cohomology of the moduli space of semistable vector bundles over a curve has been the subject of extensive study
[
10
,
19
,
9
,
4
]
.
We have explicit sets of generators for all coprime ranks and degrees
[
1
]
, relations for the cohomology ring
[
4
]
, and formulas to compute Betti numbers
[
3
]
.
These results were later generalized to the moduli stack of vector bundles (including unstable) over a curve
C
, namely
Bun_{r,d}C
, in
[
7
]
.

Similar computations for universal moduli spaces of semistable bundles over curves were hypothesized to be accessible in the future,
when the moduli spaces
\overline{\mathcal{M}_{g,n}}(X,\beta)
of stable maps are better-understood and localisation techniques are used with greater effect,
by Kirwan in her ICM talk
[
11
, §8]
.

However, much remains unkown about the universal moduli spaces to date due to the technical difficulties outlined in
[
11
]
.
Recent computations of the Chow ring of the universal moduli space
[
12
,
13
,
17
]
have initiated progress in understanding their intersection theory.
Computations of the full Chow ring, in terms of explicit generators and relations, of the universal moduli stack of bundles over curves include bundles over genus zero curves
[
12
]
and universal Picard stacks over hyperelliptic curves
[
13
]
.

The twisted-kappa classes, defined in
[
13
,
17
]
, are tautological classes on the universal moduli stack of bundles. We show that they generate the Chow ring of the universal moduli stack of bundles of rank two over genus two curves and compute relations.
Our approach stands in contrast to the approach used by Heinloth
[
7
, §5]
where the Poincare polynomial for the moduli space of semistable bundles over a curve was computed using the moduli stack.
We hope to extend this approach to higher ranks and genera through the following conjecture.

Conjecture A
.

Let
\mathcal{V}ec^{\mathcal{H}}({r,d,g})
be the universal moduli stack of vector bundles of rank
r
, degree
d
over hyperelliptic curves of genus
g
.
For any
g\geq 2,d\in\mathbb{Z}
and a hyperelliptic curve
C
of genus
g
, the composition of restriction and cycle-class map

A^{*}(\mathcal{V}ec^{\mathcal{H}}({r,d,g}))\rightarrow H_{a}^{*}(Bun_{r,d}C)

is an isomorphism onto
H^{*}_{\kappa}
where
H^{*}_{\kappa}
is the cohomology subring generated by twisted-kappa classes and
H^{*}_{a}
is the algebraic cohomology subring.

We prove the conjecture for
\mathcal{V}ec(2,d,2)
.
Analogous versions of the conjecture have further been verified for universal moduli spaces of slope-stable and Higgs stable bundles of rank two in
[
6
]
.
For universal Jacobians over hyperelliptic curves, the conjecture is an immediate consequence of
[
13
, Theorem 1.1]
.

The conjecture is closely related to the
Franchetta Property
, which states that cycles in a family
\chi\rightarrow B
are homologically trivial when restricted to a very general fiber over
b\in B
if and only if they are trivial in Chow of the same fiber.
Effectively, the conjecture claims that the morphism
\mathcal{V}ec^{\mathcal{H}}(r,d,g)\rightarrow\mathcal{H}_{g}
satisfies the Franchetta Property,
and that the Gysin restriction map
A^{*}(\mathcal{V}ec^{\mathcal{H}}(r,d,g))\rightarrow A^{*}(Bun_{r,d}C)
is injective for all
[C]\in|\mathcal{H}_{g}|
.
For more details, see
[
14
]
.

We apply excision on
\mathcal{V}ec(2,d,2)
given by the Harder-Narasimhan(HN) stratification
[
7
, Proposition 5.3]
.
The unstable HN-strata have been extensively studied in the form of filtration stacks
[
8
]
.
We compute the Chow rings of products of universal Jacobians over genus two curves and relate them to cycles on filtration stacks using
[
8
, Corollary 3.2]
. The relevant notations are defined in §
0.1
.

1.
Computations on the universal moduli space

Theorem 1.1

The Chow ring

\displaystyle A^{*}(\mathscr{U}(2,3,2))=\mathbb{Q}[\kappa_{0,1,0},\kappa_{-1,2,0},\kappa_{-1,0,1},\kappa_{-1,1,1}]/(\Theta_{U}^{3},H_{U}^{2}\Theta_{U}^{2}-4B_{*}\Theta_{U}^{2},B_{*}H_{U}^{2}-B_{*}H_{U}\Theta_{U}+\dfrac{1}{2}B_{*}\Theta_{U}^{2},

\displaystyle H_{U}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(4\kappa_{-1,0,1}-\kappa_{-1,2,0}\right),\Theta_{U}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(3\kappa_{0,1,0}-\kappa_{-1,2,0}\right),\kappa_{0,0,1}=\dfrac{1}{4}\kappa_{0,2,0}+\dfrac{1}{8}(4\kappa_{-1,0,1}-\kappa_{-1,2,0})^{2},\kappa_{0,2,0}=\dfrac{1}{2}\kappa_{0,1,0}^{2},
