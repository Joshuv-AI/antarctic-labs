The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President [1] (5 February 1945, d338)

[Yalta, February 5, 1945.]

Mr. President:—

I would suggest that you say this is a very important and urgent matter and that the three foreign ministers present a proposal tomorrow as to the proceedure by which a determination as to to dismemberment can be arrived at an early date

Harry
