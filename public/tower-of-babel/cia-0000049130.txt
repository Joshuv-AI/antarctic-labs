SUMMARY: ON 9 MARCH 1989, THE ESAF SPECIAL HONOR COMMISSION
PRESENTED ITS FINDINGS IN THE SAN SEBASTIAN CASE. ACCORDING TO THE
COMMISSION'S REPORT, MAJOR BELTRAN, THE 5TH BRIGADE S-11, WAS
PRIMARILY RESPONSIBLE FOR THE 21 SEPTEMBER 1988 KILLING OF 10
CIVILIANS IN SAN SEBASTIAN. THE KILLING OF THE 10 CIVILIANS, WHO
WERE IN FACT INVOLVED IN FMLN SUBVERSIVE ACTIVITIES, WAS A CRIMINAL
ACT AND WAS OUTSIDE NORMAL OPERATING PROCEDURES. MAJOR BELTRAN
CONCOCTED A FALSE AMBUSH TO CAMOUFLAGE THE KILLINGS AND ORDERED THE
ESAF PERSONNEL INVOLVED, WHO OPPOSED HIS ORDERS, 10 CARRY OUT THE
FALSE AMBUSH AND KILL THE CIVILIANS. THE COMMISSION FOUND THE
COMMANDER OF THE 5TH BRIGADE INNOCENT OF ANY KNOWLEDGE REGARDING THE
KILLINGS AND RECOMMENDED THAT HE BE RETURNED TO HIS COMMAND. THE
COMMISSION PROVIDED A LIST OF PERSONS INVOLVED IN THE KILLINGS WHO
