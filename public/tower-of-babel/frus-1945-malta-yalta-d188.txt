The British Ambassador (Halifax) to the Secretary of State (9 December 1944, d188)

Washington, December 9, 1944.

My Dear Ed: I have just received a telegram asking me to convey to you the enclosed personal message from Anthony, in reply to yours of December 7th.

Yours ever,

Edward

[Enclosure]

Personal Message From Mr. Eden to Mr. Stettinius

Thank you so much for your helpful and understanding message which I received through Winant. It is my keen desire that we should work close together.[1]

Source: 865.01/12–944

Notes
[1] Copies of the Stettinius-Eden messages were sent to the President on December 10, 1944.
