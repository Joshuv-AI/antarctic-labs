Austrian painter; born in Vienna Dec. 10, 1856; daughter and pupil of Friedrich Friedländer. She has devoted herself to still-life subjects, producing many pictures of church and house interiors, etc. Her oil-painting "Orientalische Gegenstände," exhibited at the twentieth annual exposition of the Künstlerhaus in Vienna, was bought by the Emperor of Austria.

Bibliography: Singer, Künstler-Lexikon, i., s.v.; Eisenberg, Das Geistige Wien, i., s.v. S. N. D.
