A post differential is a prescribed percentage of base
salary payable to civilian officers and employees of the
Government - (at the present time excluding Foreign Service

a recruitment incentive and. an aid in retaining personnel while
assigned at the more undesirable posts.

The "official residence allotment 1 " is not an allowance, and

costs beyond those normal for a smaller or less elaborate
establishment.
