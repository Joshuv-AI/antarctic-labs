He expresses his own theory as to what happened on that November day in 1963, based on information he has uncovered

Oleg Maximovich Nechiporenko was a Colonel of Global Foreign Intelligence for the KGB (Soviet Committee for
State Security). Considered one of the KGB's most intrepid “Special Operations" officers. In 1961, Col. Nechiporenko
was posted to the KGB Station in Mexico City under diplomatic cover of the Soviet Embassy. Following his secret
meetings with Oswald, Nechiporenko remained in Mexico until 1965, returning again in 1967. He taught at the Soviet
Intelligence officer’s college and retired in 1991.
