Physician, astrologer, and mathematician of the ninth century ( c. 786-845 ?); father of the physician Ali ben Sahl. Sahl translated the "Almagest" of Ptolemy. Steinschneider identifies him with the celebrated Sahl ibn Bishr ("Zur Pseudepigraphischen Litteratur," p. 78).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Die Arabische Literatur der Juden, pp. 24, 31 et seq.; Grätz, Gesch. 3d ed., v. 187-188; Suter, Die Mathematiker und Astronomen der Araber und Ihre Werke, in Zeitschrift für Mathematik und Physik, ed. Mehmke and Cantor, supplement to the 45th year of publication, Leipsic, 1900. S. M. Sc.
