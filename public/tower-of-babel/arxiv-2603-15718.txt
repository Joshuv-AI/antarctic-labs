Fibonacci numbers along residue classes and convolutions
Helmut Prodinger

Abstract
. The sequence F d ​ n + h F_{dn+h} and its convolutions have (for h = 0 h=0 ) been studied in a recent paper at the arxiv. The instance with general h h is more involved and uses Chebyshev polynomials.

11B39

1.
A review of the Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind

These polynomials might be defined and studied in many ways
[
1
]
, but we consider
U_{n}(x)
via the generating function:

\frac{1}{1-2tx+t^{2}}=\sum_{n\geq 0}U_{n}(x)t^{n}.

The denominator
1-2tx+t^{2}
leads to the recursion
U_{n}(x)-2xU_{n-1}(x)+U_{n-2}(x)=0
, with
U_{0}(x)=1
and
U_{1}(x)=2x
.
From this one can obtain via induction the explicit formula

\begin{cases}U_{2n}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k}(-1)^{n-k}\binom{n+k}{2k}2^{2k},\\
U_{2n+1}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k+1}(-1)^{n-k}\binom{n+k+1}{2k+1}2^{2k+1}.\end{cases}

Since

\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\frac{1}{1-2tx+t^{2}}=\frac{2t}{(1-2tx+t^{2})^{2}}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\frac{1}{(1-2tx+t^{2})^{s}}=\frac{2st}{(1-2tx+t^{2})^{s+1}},

we may set

\frac{1}{(1-2tx+t^{2})^{s+1}}=\sum_{n\geq 0}U_{n}^{(s)}(x)t^{n}

and find again explicit formulæ

\begin{cases}U_{2n}^{(s)}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k}(-1)^{n-k}\binom{n+k+s}{2k+s}\binom{2k+s}{s}2^{2k},\\
U_{2n+1}^{(s)}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k+1}(-1)^{n-k}\binom{n+k+s+1}{2k+s+1}\binom{2k+s+1}{s}2^{2k+1}.\end{cases}

This may be derived via induction on the parameter
s
.

A small variation is via the introduction of a
\pm
sign; let
\epsilon\in\{-1,+1\}
and

\frac{1}{(1-2tx-\epsilon t^{2})^{s+1}}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\overline{U}_{n}^{(s)}(x)t^{n}

then

\begin{cases}\overline{U}_{2n}^{(s)}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k}\epsilon^{n-k}\binom{n+k+s}{2k+s}\binom{2k+s}{s}2^{2k},\\
\overline{U}_{2n+1}^{(s)}(x)=\sum_{k}x^{2k+1}\epsilon^{n-k}\binom{n+k+s+1}{2k+s+1}\binom{2k+s+1}{s}2^{2k+1}.\end{cases}

2.
The
d
-section of Fibonacci numbers

The recent paper
[
2
]
considers for Fibonacci numbers
F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_{n}
,
F_{0}=0
,
F_{1}=1

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{2n}z^{n}=\frac{z}{1-3z+z^{2}}\quad\text{and, more generally,}\quad\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{dn}z^{n}.

Here, we are interested in

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{dn+h}z^{n}

which makes the analysis more complete and also more challenging.

Proposition 1
.

\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{nd+h}z^{n}=\frac{F_{h}+(-1)^{h}zF_{d-h}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}.

Proof.
We will use the Binet formulæ

F_{n}=\frac{\alpha^{n}-\beta^{n}}{\alpha-\beta},\ L_{n}=\alpha^{n}+\beta^{n}\quad\text{with}\quad\alpha=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2},\ \beta=\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}.

Then

\displaystyle X

\displaystyle:=(1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2})\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{nd+h}z^{n}

\displaystyle=(1-z(\alpha^{d}+\beta^{d})+(-1)^{d}z^{2})\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{\alpha^{nd+h}-\beta^{nd+h}}{\alpha-\beta}z^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{1-z(\alpha^{d}+\beta^{d})+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}{\alpha-\beta}\sum_{n\geq 0}(\alpha^{nd+h}-\beta^{nd+h})z^{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{1-z(\alpha^{d}+\beta^{d})+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}{\alpha-\beta}\bigg[\frac{\alpha^{h}}{1-z\alpha^{d}}-\frac{\beta^{h}}{1-z\beta^{d}}\bigg]

\displaystyle=\frac{1-z(\alpha^{d}+\beta^{d})+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}{\alpha-\beta}\frac{\alpha^{h}(1-z\beta^{d})-\beta^{h}(1-z\alpha^{d})}{1-z(\alpha^{d}+\beta^{d})+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{\alpha^{h}(1-z\beta^{d})-\beta^{h}(1-z\alpha^{d})}{\alpha-\beta}

\displaystyle=F_{h}-z\frac{\alpha^{h}(\beta^{d})-\beta^{h}(\alpha^{d})}{\alpha-\beta}

\displaystyle=F_{h}-z(-1)^{h}\frac{\beta^{d-h}-\alpha^{d-h}}{\alpha-\beta}

\displaystyle=F_{h}+z(-1)^{h}F_{d-h}.\qed

The
s
-fold convolution of the sequence
\langle F_{nd+h}\rangle_{n}
is (in
[
2
]
for
h=0
only) defined via the generating function

\biggl(\sum_{n\geq 0}F_{nd+h}z^{n}\biggr)^{s+1}.

Indeed, for
h=0
, we have

\frac{F_{h}+(-1)^{h}zF_{d-h}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}\bigg|_{h=0}=\frac{zF_{d}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}},

and the simpler numerator makes life a bit easier. We have to deal with

(F_{h}+(-1)^{h}zF_{d-h})^{s+1}=\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}\binom{s+1}{j}F_{h}^{s+1-j}(-1)^{jh}z^{j}F_{d-h}^{j}

and eventually with

\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}\binom{s+1}{j}F_{h}^{s+1-j}(-1)^{jh}z^{j}F_{d-h}^{j}\frac{1}{(1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2})^{s+1}}

Fortunately, following the introduction, the expansion of

\frac{1}{(1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2})^{s+1}}=\sum_{n\geq 0}\overline{U}_{n}^{(s)}(L_{d})z^{n}

is known, and
\epsilon=(-1)^{d-1}
.

Therefore we have the fully explicit
s
-fold convolution of the sequence
\langle F_{nd+h}\rangle_{n}
via its generating function:

Theorem 1
.

\biggl(\frac{F_{h}+(-1)^{h}zF_{d-h}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}\biggr)^{s+1}=\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}\binom{s+1}{j}F_{h}^{s+1-j}(-1)^{jh}z^{j}F_{d-h}^{j}\sum_{n\geq 0}\overline{U}_{n}^{(s)}(L_{d})z^{n}.

Expanded,

\biggl(\frac{F_{h}+(-1)^{h}zF_{d-h}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}\biggr)^{s+1}=\sum_{n\geq 0}z^{n}\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}\binom{s+1}{j}F_{h}^{s+1-j}(-1)^{jh}F_{d-h}^{j}\overline{U}_{n-j}^{(s)}(L_{d}).\qed

The special case
h=0
leads to

\sum_{n\geq 0}z^{n}F_{d}^{s+1}\overline{U}_{n-j}^{(s)}(L_{d}).

Remark.
The
d
-section of Lucas numbers can also be considered:

\sum_{n\geq 0}L_{nd+h}z^{n}=\frac{L_{h}+(-1)^{h-1}zL_{d-h}}{1-zL_{d}+(-1)^{d}z^{2}}.

The details are left to the interested reader; the denominator is the same, so only the numerator needs to be changed.
