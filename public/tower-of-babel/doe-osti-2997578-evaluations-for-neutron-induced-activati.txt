 Activation cross sections are an important input for evaluating results of radiochemical analyses,
 both for basic science and programmatic applications. We present an update on cross sections
 for a select set of Y and Zr target isotopes, including several isomers. This effort will focus on a
 methodology to obtain uncertainty quantified systematic trends for reaction model parameters and
 propagate them to cross sections. We discuss our results in the context of previous calculations.

a scaling factor for the capture cross sections. The (n,2n) cross sections tend to show more complex
structure in their correlations as they are sensitive to the OMP and NLD of the target and product
nucleus, leading to non-trivial correlations.
