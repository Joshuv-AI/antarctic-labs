Semileptonic decay of Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2860 ) + / Λ c ​ ( 2625 ) + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}/\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}\ell^{-}\overline{\nu}_{\ell} within QCD light-cone sum rules
Hui-Hui Duan, Jia-Bao Feng, Feng-Mei Liu, Qin Chang

Abstract
In this work, we calculate the transition form factors for the weak decays Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2860 ) + \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+} and Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2625 ) + \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+} using QCD light-cone sum rules, and compute the branching fractions of the corresponding semileptonic decays Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2860 ) + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} and Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2625 ) + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} . Our predicted branching fraction for Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2625 ) + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} is consistent with experimental data and other theoretical predictions, validating the reliability of our method. On this basis, we also present the branching fraction of Λ b 0 → Λ c ​ ( 2860 ) + ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} . These results may serve as a theoretical reference for future experimental measurements of this decay channel.

Theoretical framework of
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}/\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}

Our starting point to calculate the weak decay form factors is the correlation function of hadron weak transition:

\displaystyle\Pi_{\mu\nu}(p,q)=i\int d^{4}xe^{ip^{\prime}\cdot x}\langle 0|\mathcal{T}\{\eta_{\mu}(x),j_{\nu}(0)\}|\Lambda_{b}(p)\rangle.

[
18
]

5
Conclusion

Within the framework of QCD light-cone sum rules, we calculate the form factors for the transitions
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}
and
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}
. The obtained form factors are then extrapolated to the physical region of the semileptonic decays
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}\ell^{-}\nu_{\ell}
and
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}\ell^{-}\nu_{\ell}
, and we compute their differential decay widths and branching fractions, as shown in Table
4
.

In the calculation of the form factors using QCD light-cone sum rules, we take into account both positive- and negative-parity states of the final-state spin-3/2 baryon, separate the Lorentz structures associated with the final-state spin-1/2 baryon, and consider only those of the spin-3/2 state. Our calculated branching fraction for the semileptonic decay
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2625)^{+}\ell^{-}\overline{\nu}_{\ell}
is found to be in good agreement with experimental measurements and other theoretical calculations. Furthermore, we present theoretical predictions for the branching fraction of
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda_{c}(2860)^{+}\ell^{-}\overline{\nu}_{\ell}
, which can serve as a theoretical reference for experimental searches and analyses of this decay channel.
