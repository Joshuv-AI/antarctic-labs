4. Programmed Publications- in .Process or Issued

(a) Collection Manual . Undertaken on a pilot-plant basis, this
oubli cation is intended primarily for use as a textbook or
reference book in basic courses. Its purpose is neither to
i»gvirt skills nor to explain all the details of collection
as conducted by any one organization. It aims, rather, to
develop understandings of collection as a major nh&se of
the intelligence process and of the interrelations, especially
as they affect collection, in the United States intelligence
community. Because of this broad approach, there is good
evidence that the manual can perform a secondary function
in various parts of this and other agencies, (For example,
several copies have been reouested for circulation among
00/C operational personnel.) The necessarily extensive
research and consultation in CIA ana other IAC agencies hae
made this a very protracted project. Writing is now in the

in Appendix VII Tteb B are a table of contents of the material
drafted thus far, v brief outline of the remainder of the
manual, and lists of illustrative material thusfar incor-
porated in the text or assigned to the appendix.

and Instructions, thorough coordination in the Office of
Training, and informal consultation elsewhere, a substantial
glossary has been produced and printed in a small edition.
