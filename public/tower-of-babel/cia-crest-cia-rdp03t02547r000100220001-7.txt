Chain of Command

Constitutionally, the new civilian President will com-
mand the armed forces. In practice, he will have to
defer to the Superior Defense Council on matters
relating to defense and national security. Even in
nonmilitary affairs, the President will need to consult
with the Council and operate within limits set by the
military.!

corps, the Superior Defense Council is the main

1975 and 1978 — it has removed officers from the
national presidency and chosen their successors. Es-

military government, the Council has approximately

er, the Defense Minister, the service chiefs, members

meets on an irregular basis to consider political as
