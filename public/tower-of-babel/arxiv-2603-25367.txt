Computing the local 2 2 -component of a non-selfdual automorphic representation of GL 3 \GL_{3}
YAMAMOTO Hirofumi

Abstract
In this paper, we explicitly determine the local 2 2 -adic component of a non-selfdual automorphic representation Π \Pi of GL 3 \GL_{3} constructed by van Geemen and Top. We prove that Π 2 \Pi_{2} is a parabolically induced representation of GL 3 ⁡ ( ℚ 2 ) \GL_{3}(\mathbbm{Q}_{2}) given by Π 2 = Ind P GL 3 ⁡ ( ℚ 2 ) ⁡ ( π ⊠ χ ) \Pi_{2}=\Ind_{P}^{\GL_{3}(\mathbbm{Q}_{2})}(\pi\boxtimes\chi) , where P P is the standard parabolic subgroup of GL 3 \GL_{3} with Levi subgroup GL 2 × GL 1 \GL_{2}\times\GL_{1} , χ \chi is an unramified character of ℚ 2 × \mathbbm{Q}_{2}^{\times} satisfying χ ⁡ ( 2 ) = − 2 ​ − 1 \chi(2)=-2\sqrt{-1} , and π \pi is a supercuspidal representation of GL 2 ⁡ ( ℚ 2 ) \GL_{2}(\mathbbm{Q}_{2}) . Furthermore, we describe π \pi explicitly as a compactly induced representation π = c-Ind J α GL 2 ⁡ ( ℚ 2 ) ​ Λ \pi=\cInd_{J_{\alpha}}^{\GL_{2}(\mathbbm{Q}_{2})}\Lambda and determine the representation Λ \Lambda explicitly. The proof relies on explicit computations of Hecke eigenvalues using computer calculations. The automorphic representation Π \Pi is realized in the cuspidal cohomology of the congruence subgroup Γ 0 ​ ( 128 ) ⊂ SL 3 ⁡ ( ℤ ) \Gamma_{0}(128)\subset\SL_{3}(\mathbbm{Z}) . By computing the Hecke eigenvalues of an associated Hecke eigenvector, we are able to uniquely identify the local structure of Π 2 \Pi_{2} . As an application, we obtain an explicit description of the 2 2 -adic local component of the Galois representation ρ vGT , ℓ \rho_{\mathrm{vGT},\ell} associated with Π \Pi .
