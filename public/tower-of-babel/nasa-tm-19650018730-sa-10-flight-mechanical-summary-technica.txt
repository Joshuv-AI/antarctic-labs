 ABSTRACT

 This report presents the Flight Mechanical Summary for Saturn I
vehicle SA-10 to be launched from Pad 3 7 B , Eastern Test Range on a
flight azimuth of 95.2 degrees East-of-North. A successful flight-will
insert the spent S-IV stage and payload consisting of an Apollo boiler-
plate (BP-9) and a Meteoroid Technology Satellite (Pegasus C) into a
535 km circular orbit. Included is a discussion of the operational
predicted trajectory with its orbital mission objectives and constraints,
guidance, sequencing, and insertion parameters. The three-sigma envelope
of thrust, flow rate, liftoff mass, and wind speed variations is given,
along with impact data, in the Range Safety part of this report. A
study of wind disturbances, three-sigma C1 and C2 variations, and + 10%
control gain deviations shows the vehicle to be structurally capabie of
withstanding the expected winds during the launch period. Pertinent
tracking and telemetry ground coverage data are presented for poweied
and orbital flight phases.
