 1.0 INTRODUCTION

The Abort Summary Document has been prepared to provide a single refer-
ence source of Crew Procedures and information to be used during Saturn V
nominal flight, abort, and abort entry training. The basic document re-
flects the control procedures to be used by the crew. Appendices A. B,
and C supplement the controlled procedures for training purposes and are
not under the control of this document.

Comments or changes should be direct to Mr. M. R. Wash, Launch and Entry
Procedures Section, CF24 9 utilizing Crew Procedures Change Request, For,
452, Revised.
