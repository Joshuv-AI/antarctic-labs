Rabbi in Constantinople in the first half of the nineteenth century and a contemporary of Ḥiyya Pontremoli. He was the author of "Mishnat de-Rabbi Eli'ezer," a collection of one hundred and thirty-four responsa on the civil laws of Ḥoshen Mishpaṭ (Salonica, 1853).

Bibliography: Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 124; Walden, Shem ha-Gedolim he-Ḥadash, i. 24. K. M. Sel.
