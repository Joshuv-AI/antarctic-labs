A Jew, perhaps a Marano, of Spanish descent; lived at Smyrna. His apology, written in Spanish and directed against a monk at Seville, and an interpretation by him of Daniel's "seventy weeks," entitled "Apoloxia Repuesta y Declaracion de las Setenta Semanas de Daniel, Contra lo Que Escrivio una Persona Residente en Ruan," appeared in a Latin translation at Basel, 1658.

Bibliography: Kayserling, Bibl. Esp.-Port.-Jud. p. 64; De Rossi, Bibliotheca Judaica Antichristiana, p. 58; De Rossi-Hamberger, Hist. Wörterb. p. 186. G. M. K.
