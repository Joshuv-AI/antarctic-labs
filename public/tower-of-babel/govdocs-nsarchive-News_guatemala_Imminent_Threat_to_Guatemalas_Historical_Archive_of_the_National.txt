<div class="node__content clearfix"><div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><strong>Washington, D.C., May 30, 2019</strong> –&nbsp;The National Security Archive joins our international and Guatemalan colleagues in calling for the protection of the Historical Archive of the National Police (AHPN) of Guatemala, which faces new threats to its independence and to public access to its holdings.</p><p>In a press conference on Monday, May 27,&nbsp;Interior Minister Enrique Degenhart signaled his intent to assert his agency’s control of the AHPN including the prospect of new restrictions on access to the archived police records and possible legal action against “foreign institutions” holding digitized copies of the documents.

Degenhart made his statements&nbsp;as a crucial deadline approached&nbsp;to renew an agreement that for a decade has kept the archive under the authority of the Ministry of Culture and Sports.
