French physician; lived in the fourteenth century. He was the author of a medical treatise entitled "Gabriel," still extant in manuscript (Günzburg, No. 316). Bonenfante translated also into Hebrew Arnold de Villeneuve's work, "Tabula Super Vita Brevis."

Bibliography: Rev. Et. Juives, ix. 215; xiii. 301, 303; Steinschneider, Hebr. Uebers. p. 843; Renan-Neubauer, Les Ecrivains Juifs Français, p. 416. G. I. Br.
