 Conclusions
● GBE Shielding Gas Disturbance Metric (GBESGDM)
 is sensitive to shielding gas disturbances caused
 by:
 – lack of shielding due to decreasing gas flow rate –
change in shielding gas composition – dirty/contaminated
 base plate – changes in welding parameters
