EL SALVADOR: VENCEREMOS ON RIFT IN GOVERNMENT OVER KIDNAPPING

.

PA2Z25464

(CLANDESTINE) RADIO VENCEREMOS IN SPANISH TO EL SALVADOR: 8896
GMT 22 OCT 85 :

(WEXT)) IN THE PAST FEW HOURS, THE CRISIS HAS DEEPENED IN
THE GOVERNMENT AND AMONG THE OLIGARCHIC AND PRO-IMPERIALIST GROUPS
OF POWER CONCERNING THE CASE OF THE CAPTURE OF INES GUADALUPE
DUARTE. :

THE NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE ((ARENA)}, WHICH ;
GROUPS THE NATION'S BUSINESSMEN, IN A RECENT PAID AD PUBLISHED A
COMMUNIQUE WARNING NAPOLEON DUARTE NOT TO NEGOTIATE AND
EXHORTING THE ARMED FORCES NOT TO SUPPORT DUARTE'S DECISIONS.’

AMONG OTHER THINGS, THE ARENA COMMUNIQUE ASKS IF THE SACRIFICE
IS ONLY FOR OUR PEOPLE WHEN THOSE IN POWER MUST FACE DIFFICULT
SITUATIONS. WE REALIZE THE GRIEF A PARENT MUST EXPERIENCE .
WHEN ONE OF HIS CHILDREN IS KIDNAPPED. BUT WE ALSO ASK IF THE
COUNTRY, AT THIS MOMENT, IS NOT ALSO SUFFERING.

THE ARENA PAID AD CONTINUES BY SAYING: WHAT ELSE ARE WE GOING
TO HAND OVER SHOULD OTHER SIMILAR CASES COME UP? WILL WE HAVE TO
CREATE A COLONY OF SALVADORANS OUTSIDE OUR COUNTRY AND SPEND
THE FEW DOLLARS LEFT? WHY WAS THE SITUATION OF THE MAYORS
RELEGATED TO A SECOND LEVEL?

THE ARENA COMMUNIQUE WARNING NAPOLEON DUARTE CONCLUDED BY
ASKING: WHY WAS THE SITUATION OF THE MAYORS “IN THE HANDS OF THE
FMLN RELEGATED TO A SECOND LEVEL? ((REPEATED QUESTION AS HEARD) )
LET US SAVE THE FATHERLAND, REGAROLESS OF THE SACRIFICE WE MUST
ENDURE. : : :

SUCH WAS THE PAID AD OF ARENA WITHIN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE
CRISIS UNLEASHED BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT, BUSINESS GROUPS, THE
ARMED FORCES, AND THE CHRISTIAN DEMOCRATIC PARTY, CONCERNING
THE CASE OF THE CAPTURE OF INES GUADALUPE DUARTE.
