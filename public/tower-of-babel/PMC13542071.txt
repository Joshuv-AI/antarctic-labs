Analysis of accuracy-influencing factors and data acquisition boundaries in crowdsourced road geomagnetic data mapping
Authors: Xiang Li, Bomu Zhu, Zifan Liu, Ting Zhao, Dongming Zhao, Songlin Liu
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
This study presents a systematic analysis framework for investigating the influencing factors of data acquisition accuracy in road geomagnetic mapping under a crowdsourcing paradigm, to solve the practical problems of uncontrollable data quality, inconsistent acquisition standards and high cost of mapping in crowdsourced geomagnetic mapping. Based on the trajectory data collected by a variety of smartphones according to the crowdsourcing mode, this study constructs a multi-dimensional crowdsourced data quality evaluation system from the perspective of positioning accuracy and geomagnetic accuracy. The impacts of key acquisition parameters—including equipment model, frequency of repeated sampling, acquisition period, and spatial environment—are comprehensively analyzed. To ensure high data quality, an acquisition boundary determination method is proposed, which provides an operational technical pathway and optimization strategy for constructing high-precision road geomagnetic maps in crowdsourced settings, thereby enhancing the reliability and usability of crowdsourced geomagnetic data. Key findings reveal that: (1) prioritizing high-performance mainstream devices (e.g., Huawei, Honor) significantly improves data quality; (2) when the frequency of repeated sampling is about 10 times, it can effectively improve the accuracy of the data, beyond which diminishing returns and saturation effects occur; (3) data acquisition during low-interference periods (e.g., nighttime or early morning) effectively reduces electromagnetic noise and improves data stability; (4) open areas exhibit superior signal conditions and measurement accuracy compared to challenging environments such as urban canyons with significant shading. These insights offer practical guidance for optimizing crowdsourced geomagnetic data acquisition and support the development of robust, low-cost, and wide-coverage data acquisition patterns. The proposed method holds promise for applications in intelligent transportation, underground navigation, and urban infrastructure monitoring, contributing to seamless indoor-outdoor positioning services.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-56369-w.

Introduction

With the rapid development of intelligent transportation system and automatic driving technology, the demand for high-precision maps is increasing. As an important map form, road geomagnetic map is widely used in vehicle positioning and navigation because it can provide stable geomagnetic characteristic information1, especially in complex environment with poor satellite signals such as urban canyon and indoor underground, which can effectively assist traditional navigation and positioning. However, the traditional construction method of geomagnetic map mainly depends on professional surveying and mapping equipment and manual operation, which is not only costly, but also has a long update cycle, which is difficult to meet the needs of modern urban dynamic changes2,3. In recent years, the rise of crowdsourcing mode provides a new way to solve this problem. Crowdsourcing mode significantly reduces the cost of data acquisition by using mobile devices (such as smart phones, vehicle sensors, etc.) in the hands of the public, and achieves wider space coverage and faster update frequency4,5. Especially in the field of geomagnetic map construction, crowdsourcing mode can realize dynamic capture and real-time update of road geomagnetic information with the help of spatiotemporal data generated by users during daily travel6. Although crowdsourcing mode has many advantages, its data quality is affected by many factors, including equipment performance, acquisition environment, acquisition period and so on7. Therefore, how to effectively analyze and control these influencing factors has become a key issue to improve the accuracy of crowdsourced road geomagnetic map.

Although crowdsourcing model has a broad application prospect in the construction of road geomagnetic map, there are still some problems to be solved urgently. First of all, most of the existing studies focus on the influence of single factor on the accuracy of geomagnetic map, and lack of systematic analysis of the comprehensive action of multiple factors8. For example, some studies only pay attention to the positioning accuracy of acquisition equipment, while ignoring the difference of equipment’s sensing ability to geomagnetic signals9; Other studies focus on the selection of acquisition time period, but do not fully consider the interference of external environment on geomagnetic signals in different time periods10. Secondly, in the boundary determination method of crowdsourced data, the existing literature seldom involves how to set reasonable collection scope and conditions according to the specific scenario requirements to ensure the efficient use and accuracy of data11. In addition, due to the diverse sources and uneven quality of crowdsourced data, how to identify and eliminate abnormal data in the data preprocessing stage is also a major challenge in current research12. Based on this, this paper aims to comprehensively analyze the influencing factors of crowdsourced data from the two core dimensions of positioning accuracy and geomagnetic accuracy, and propose a scientific boundary determination method to provide a reference basis of existing research.

The main goal of this study is to systematically analyze the data influencing factors of road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode, and reveal its action mechanism on positioning accuracy and geomagnetic accuracy, so as to provide theoretical basis and practical guidance for optimizing acquisition strategy and determining reasonable boundary13.

The main contributions of this paper include the following aspects: evaluate the influence of different brands and models of acquisition equipment on positioning accuracy and geomagnetic accuracy, and clarify the necessity of giving priority to high-quality equipment;the effect of frequency of repeated sampling on precision improvement is analyzed, and the best acquisition frequency is determined to avoid waste of resources;the time window suitable for geomagnetic data acquisition is put forward by studying the evasion effect of the choice of acquisition time period on external environment interference;the influence of the environmental complexity of the acquisition area on the accuracy is investigated, and the acquisition strategies for different scenarios are formulated14.

Related work

The construction of geomagnetic map depends on a series of basic theories, among which geomagnetic navigation principle and positioning technology principle constitute the core support. Geomagnetic navigation locates by using the characteristics of the earth’s magnetic field distribution. Its basic principle is to match the geomagnetic data collected in real time with the pre-constructed geomagnetic map, so as to determine the position of the carrier15. In geomagnetic navigation, magnetic field modulus is often used as matching data, because its calculation is simple and it is not affected by coordinate system transformation. However, the single-point magnetic field modulus is not unique, so the local geomagnetic modulus values are usually combined into fingerprint sequences to improve the uniqueness and accuracy of matching16. In addition, the principle of positioning technology provides the basis of data acquisition for the construction of geomagnetic map. Modern positioning technology relies heavily on the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) and other ancillary sensors, such as inertial measurement units (IMU) and cameras. The fusion of these sensors can provide high-precision position information, thus supporting the accurate modeling of geomagnetic map17. Classical theoretical literature shows that the combination of geomagnetic navigation and positioning technology not only improves the reliability of reference map, but also provides a solid theoretical framework for subsequent data analysis.

In the process of constructing geomagnetic map, the method of magnetic moments and Kriging interpolation algorithm are two important mathematical tools. By modeling and iteratively optimizing the magnetic field, the magnetic moment method can predict the magnetic field distribution in the uncollected area, thus generating a fine geomagnetic fingerprint database18. In contrast, Kriging interpolation algorithm interpolates geomagnetic data by considering spatial autocorrelation, which is suitable for quickly establishing geomagnetic digital reference map19. These two methods have their own advantages, but they should be selected and used according to the actual situation. For example, in crowdsourcing mode, due to the sparsity and uncertainty of data acquisition, the magnetic moment method may be more applicable because it can effectively deal with non-uniformly distributed data20. Generally, these theoretical foundations provide diversified technical support for the construction of geomagnetic map, and also lay a solid foundation for the follow-up research.

In recent years, scholars have made remarkable progress in the research of road geomagnetic map accuracy under crowdsourcing mode. Early research mainly focused on the improvement of traditional geomagnetic navigation methods, such as improving the positioning accuracy by optimizing the construction of geomagnetic fingerprint database21. With the development of mobile Internet and smart devices, crowdsourcing mode has gradually become an important direction of geomagnetic map construction. Some scholars took the lead in proposing a geographic information speculation method based on crowdsourced spatiotemporal data, emphasizing the advantages of crowdsourcing mode in reducing costs, expanding coverage and updating in real time22. Some studies paid more attention to practical application scenarios, especially in the field of autonomous driving, and the update technology of crowdsourced maps has been widely concerned23. For example, Qi et al.24 discussed the key technologies of multi-source updating of high-precision maps of smart cars, including data acquisition, processing and updating strategies, which provides an important reference for crowdsourcing mode of geomagnetic maps.

On the other hand, the research results of positioning accuracy and geomagnetic accuracy show a diversified trend. On the one hand, the research focus of positioning accuracy gradually shifted from single sensor to multi-sensor fusion. For example, Gu et al.25 proposed a crowdsourced high-precision map cloud fusion method based on factor map optimization, which significantly improved the positioning accuracy by fusing RTK-GPS and visual SLAM data. The method based on convolution neural network was widely used in super-resolution reconstruction of geomagnetic map26. Experimental results showed that this method can significantly improve the resolution and anti-noise ability of geomagnetic map. The research of geomagnetic accuracy paid more attention to the quality control method of data acquisition. Yuet al.27 put forward a method for evaluating the relative accuracy of lane lines based on point set registration and resampling, which provided a new idea for verifying the accuracy of geomagnetic map. In addition, aiming at the quality problem of crowdsourced data, the causes of positioning errors, data distribution inclination and their impacts on geographic information speculation were analyzed in detail, which provided an important theoretical basis for follow-up research28.

Although the existing research has made breakthroughs in many aspects, there are still some limitations. For example, most of the existing studied focus on the analysis of a single factor, and lack of comprehensive discussion on the influencing factors of crowdsourced data. In addition, there were relatively few researches on the method of determining the acquisition boundary of geomagnetic map, and a systematic theoretical framework had not yet been formed29. These problems provide a broad space for future research.

Through the comparative analysis of the existing literature, we can find that there are still some problems in the accuracy research of road geomagnetic map under the current crowdsourcing mode. First of all, in the comprehensive analysis of influencing factors of crowdsourced data, the existing research is mostly limited to the discussion of a single variable, such as the performance of acquisition equipment or the influence of frequency of repeated sampling, and lacks in-depth research on the interaction of various factors30. This single factor analysis method is difficult to fully reveal the internal mechanism of crowdsourced data quality, which limits the formulation of optimization strategies. Secondly, in the research of boundary determination method of geomagnetic map, the existing literature mostly pays attention to the technology of map generation and update, but there is no unified understanding on how to delimit the effective boundary of reference map reasonably. This problem is particularly important in practical application, because the determination of boundary directly affects the applicability and reliability of reference diagram.

In addition, the existing research also has some shortcomings in experimental design and data analysis methods. For example, many studies only chose simple road scenarios for experiments, and failed to fully consider the influence of complex environment on the accuracy of geomagnetic map31. At the same time, some studies lacked systematic measures in data quality control, which led to great influence of abnormal data on results32. The existence of these problems not only weakens the universality of the research conclusions, but also poses new challenges for the follow-up research. The purpose of this paper is to fill the deficiencies of the above research, through comprehensive analysis of the influencing factors in the process of crowdsourced data collection and propose boundary determination methods, to provide more perfect theoretical support and technical guidance for the construction of road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode.

Experimental scenario selection

In the process of constructing road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode, the choice of experimental scenarios directly affects the diversity and representativeness of data acquisition. In order to ensure the wide applicability of the research results, this study selects a variety of typical road scenarios as experimental areas, including open fields, urban canyons and complex traffic environments. Open site usually has low environmental interference and high positioning accuracy, which can reflect the data acquisition effect under ideal conditions; However, urban canyons are seriously blocked by high-rise buildings, which is an important scenario for evaluating positioning errors7. In addition, complex traffic environments, such as busy intersections and tunnels, not only pose challenges to geomagnetic signal acquisition, but also reveal the influence of dynamic traffic conditions on the accuracy of reference maps.

The experimental area is located in Zhengzhou High-tech Industrial Development Zone, Henan Province. This area contains diversified topographic features, such as roads, bridges, urban canyons, etc. It is suitable for the analysis of different environments and topographic conditions, which is helpful to extend the collected data to other similar urban environments and increase the universality of the study. Acquisition strategy and quality control

In order to ensure the standardization and systematicness of data acquisition, this study designed variable control covering multiple dimensions such as frequency of repeated sampling, acquisition time and acquisition area. Firstly, to eliminate the variability introduced by handheld usage (e.g., swinging arms, pocket placement, or random tilting), all smartphones were mounted on standardized vehicle phone holders. Secondly, in terms of frequency of repeated sampling, the experiment designed a comparative study from single acquisition to multiple acquisitions to analyze the improvement effect of repeated collection on data quality; In terms of acquisition time period, the experiment selected two time periods for data acquisition (daytime and nighttime), to evaluate the influence of external environment changes on positioning accuracy and geomagnetic signal stability. In addition, according to different acquisition areas, this study adopts the collection strategy of sub-areas and sub-scenarios. For example, in an open field, the acquisition path is designed as a straight line or a simple curve to reduce the influence of terrain on positioning errors; In the urban canyon, the acquisition path covers multiple street directions as much as possible to simulate the complex scenario in actual driving. Through this systematic collection scheme design, this study can comprehensively analyze the impact of various factors on crowdsourced data quality, and provide empirical support for optimizing collection strategies.

Among them, the acquired data mainly include two types: mobile phone original data and high-precision benchmark data. Mobile phone data are aquired synchronously by the above four smart phones of different brands and models in the same path and environment, covering GNSS position coordinates (such as longitude and latitude, altitude and speed) and geomagnetic field intensity measured by built-in magnetometer. These devices represent the sensor level of the current mainstream consumer-grade intelligent terminals, and the data has certain diversity and representativeness, which can reflect the data characteristics of multi-source heterogeneous devices in practical applications. In order to ensure the objectivity of the evaluation, all mobile phones are in the same acquisition· mode to avoid the interference caused by human operation differences. High-precision data are used as verification reference, and are collected synchronously by RTK (Real-time Dynamic) system and high-precision geomagnetic instrument. As a “true value” reference, this data set is used for spatiotemporal alignment and comparative analysis with mobile phone data, and then quantitatively evaluates the performance of mobile phone data in positioning accuracy and geomagnetic matching degree, providing reliable basis for subsequent index verification.

Overall technical methods

As shown in the Fig. 2, the accuracy factor analysis and boundary determination method of road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode can be divided into three stages: (1) crowdsourced collection stage; (2) Fusion mapping stage; (3) Quality evaluation stage.

First of all, various smart phones are used to collect crowdsourced data in different time periods and different scenarios, including geomagnetic field data and position coordinate data. Then, the location tag data is optimized by multi-source fusion location algorithm and position correction algorithm. Cleaning, intelligent denoising and gross error elimination of geomagnetic data to eliminate outliers and complete the consistency calibration of multi-source geomagnetic data. The geomagnetic data after geomagnetic optimization are filled into the grid corresponding to the position coordinates, and then the road geomagnetic map is constructed. Finally, by comparing the geomagnetic map constructed by crowdsourced data with the high-precision reference geomagnetic map, taking the position coordinate error RMS（Root Mean Square) and geomagnetic variation error RMS as the measurement standard, the influence of factors such as acquisition equipment model, crowdsourcing times, acquisition time period and acquisition area on the map quality is quantitatively analyzed, and the boundary conditions of crowdsourced acquisition are qualitatively summarized.

This study focuses on the third stage of the technical process, and studies the influencing factors and data analysis methods of road geomagnetic map accuracy under the crowdsourcing mode. It focuses on the quantitative analysis of the influence of different equipment models, scenarios, acquisition time period and frequency of repeated sampling on the map building quality during the crowdsourced collection process, and summarizes the mechanism of each influencing factor.

Crowdsourcing data processing and fusion methods

Optimized processing of crowdsourced data location coordinates

The originally collected crowdsourced trajectories are inevitably affected by multipath effects and sensor drift, resulting in significant deviations from the actual physical location of the acquisition points. In this study, cluster analysis is used to optimize the position labels of the trajectory, which provides a credible spatial anchor for the subsequent fusion and assignment of geomagnetic data.

The basic principle of this method is derived from the law of large numbers and the idea of density estimation in statistical learning: although there is a random error in the trajectory points of a single acquisition, the spatio-temporal distribution of a large number of user trajectories on the same physical path will show high-density aggregation characteristics, while the noise points will show low-density outliers. By constructing a multi-dimensional vector containing spatial coordinates, travel direction and geomagnetic features, and using density-based clustering algorithms such as DBSCAN or OPTICS, the “core cluster” representing the real traffic path can be automatically identified, and the anomaly points deviating from the core cluster can be judged as noise and eliminated. Subsequently, the weighted centroid of each core cluster (the weight is determined by signal quality, time freshness, etc.) is calculated, and the centroid coordinates are used as the optimal position estimation for the region to replace the original discrete point. This process essentially converges unstructured, noisy crowdsourced observations into structured, high-confidence geo-reference points.

Due to GNSS multipath effect and sensor noise, the original point Pi, j often deviates from the real physical path. Denoising method for geomagnetic variation of crowdsourced data

In outdoor road scenarios, the construction of geomagnetic positioning maps using crowdsourced trajectories faces more complex electromagnetic environment challenges than indoor ones. Although outdoor open spaces are less disturbed by building reinforcement, dynamic traffic flows (such as ferromagnetic interference from passing vehicles), fluctuations in high-voltage transmission lines, and high-speed motion vibrations from vehicle/handheld devices can introduce significant high-frequency noise into smartphone readings. Therefore, low-pass filtering is implemented for the incremental value of geomagnetic intensity at the crowdsourced trajectory points to eliminate transient interference and facilitate the extraction of stable geomagnetic features.

The basic principle of this method stems from the difference in the spatiotemporal characteristics of the geomagnetic field. In the outdoor road environment, the background geomagnetic field formed by the earth’s main magnetic field and underground fixed geological structures (such as buried pipelines and cable trenches) shows continuous and smooth low-frequency changes in spatial distribution. while interference caused by adjacent vehicles, engine ignition or high-frequency vibration of sensors is manifested as a violent jump in a short period of time, which is a typical high-frequency noise signal. In particular, the “geomagnetic strength increment value”, which reflects the rate of change of the geomagnetic field with displacement or time. Low-pass filtering of the incremental value only allows the low-frequency component reflecting the real road geomagnetic gradient to pass through, while the high-frequency component representing instantaneous interference is greatly attenuated. Compared with the absolute value of direct filtering, the incremental value filtering can more effectively suppress the long-term error caused by the accumulation of zero deviation drift of equipment, while retaining the relative morphology of road geomagnetic features. The specific steps are as follows:

Step 1:Preprocessing & spatio-temporal alignment.

Step 1:Preprocessing & spatio-temporal alignment.

Preprocessing & spatio-temporal alignment.

Since the movement speed v(t) of crowdsourced vehicles or pedestrians is variable, direct fixed-frequency filtering of the time series will lead to inconsistent spatial resolution, so it is necessary to convert the time series into a spatial series based on GNSS speed information and calculate the geomagnetic increment along the road direction.

Resampling: Using the velocity information provided by GNSS, the original geomagnetic sequence B(t) based on time t is resampled into an equispaced sequence B(s) based on distance s. The spatial sampling interval Δs = 0.5-1.0 m is usually set to ensure that the spatial cut-off frequency of the filter is constant.

Analysis of influencing factors on accuracy of road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode

Acquisition equipment model

In crowdsourcing mode, the positioning accuracy first depends on the performance parameters of acquisition equipment. Different types of acquisition equipment, such as GNSS positioning device, mobile phone positioning module, etc., have significant differences in positioning accuracy. For example, Liang et al.9 pointed out that the positioning error of mobile phone positioning module was usually between 5 and 15 m, especially in areas with obvious urban canyon effect, and the positioning error may further increase. This difference was mainly due to the different hardware performance of the equipment, mainly due to the differences in the built-in GNSS chip of the mobile phone, the quality of the geomagnetic sensor, the antenna design and software optimization. Some high-end mobile phone brands, such as Huawei and Apple, invest more R&D resources in positioning and geomagnetic technology, so their accuracy is often higher. However, some low-end mobile phones may have limited investment in the software and hardware of positioning and geomagnetic modules due to cost considerations, resulting in relatively low positioning accuracy. In addition, the sampling frequency of the equipment also has an important influence on the positioning accuracy. Low sampling frequency may lead to insufficient temporal resolution of trajectory data, thus affecting subsequent map matching and location discovery tasks.

From a theoretical point of view, the accuracy parameters of positioning equipment are closely related to their measurement error distribution. For example, the error of GNSS positioning device usually presents normal distribution characteristics, and its standard deviation directly reflects the dispersion degree of positioning results. However, because the collectors of crowdsourced data are mostly non-professionals, the equipment they use often lacks calibration and optimization, which further aggravates the uncertainty of positioning error. Therefore, when selecting acquisition equipment, it is necessary to comprehensively consider its accuracy parameters and the needs of practical application scenarios. For example, for urban road-level traffic information extraction, vehicle-mounted GNSS equipment is usually regarded as the first choice because of its high positioning accuracy and stability. For large-scale group behavior pattern mining, the low-cost mobile phone positioning module is more practical.

Frequency of repeated sampling

In addition to the equipment model, the frequency of repeated sampling is also an important factor affecting the positioning accuracy. Multiple acquisitions can effectively improve the stability and accuracy of crowdsourced data, which has been verified by a large number of theoretical and empirical studies5,6. Specifically, multiple acquisitions can reduce the influence of random errors through data fusion technology. For example, the cloud fusion method of crowdsourced high-precision map based on factor map optimization shows that the positioning error can be significantly reduced and the overall confidence of the map can be improved by repeated collection and fusion of multi-vehicle trajectory data1. In addition, multiple acquisitions can make up for the possible omissions or outliers in a single collection, thus improving the integrity of data.

In practical application, the influence of frequency of repeated sampling can be illustrated by specific cases. For example, Cuenca et al.8 proposed a location algorithm based on multi-sensor fusion, which realized high-precision vehicle location by preprocessing, feature matching and sliding window optimization of data collected many times on the same road section. Experimental results showed that with the increase of frequency of repeated sampling, the positioning error gradually converges to a stable value, and the positioning results after multiple acquisition had higher robustness than those of single acquisition. This phenomenon can be explained by the central limit theorem in statistics: the mean value of data collected independently for many times tends to the true value, thus reducing the influence of random errors. Therefore, in crowdsourcing mode, increasing the frequency of repeated sampling reasonably is an effective means to improve crowdsourcing accuracy.

Acquisition time period

The dynamic variation of geomagnetic field makes the acquisition time period become an important factor affecting the accuracy of geomagnetic data. The geomagnetic field is not constant, but is affected by many periodic changes, including diurnal, seasonal and long-term changes of geomagnetic field. For example, the diurnal variation of geomagnetism is mainly caused by the interaction between solar wind and earth’s magnetic field, and its amplitude usually fluctuates in the range of tens of nats. This diurnal variation will lead to systematic deviation of geomagnetic data collected in different time periods, thus affecting the construction accuracy of geomagnetic map. In addition, the seasonal variation of geomagnetic field cannot be ignored, especially in high latitudes, where the seasonal variation of geomagnetic field intensity can reach hundreds of nats.

In order to deal with the influence of acquisition time period on geomagnetic accuracy, researchers put forward a variety of compensation methods. For example, Guan et al.10 analyzed the interference characteristics of magnetic fields of soft and hard magnetic materials of navigation carrier on geomagnetic measurement, and proposed a combined denoising algorithm of fixed parameter compensation model and wavelet filtering. The experimental results showed that these methods can significantly weaken the influence of diurnal and seasonal geomagnetic variations on the measurement results, thus improving the stability and accuracy of geomagnetic data7. However, it should be noted that these compensation methods usually depend on a large amount of historical data, so in practical application, it is necessary to comprehensively consider the time distribution of data acquisition and the applicability of compensation models.

Acquisition scenario

The influence of acquisition scenario environment on crowdsourced data accuracy cannot be ignored. In open environment, such as suburbs or open areas, mobile phones can directly receive GNSS satellite signals, and the positioning accuracy is generally not interfered by external factors, which is relatively high. However, in the shielded environment, such as urban high-rise areas or indoors, obstacles such as buildings and trees will block or reflect GNSS signals, which will lead to the decline of positioning accuracy. In terms of geomagnetic accuracy, there are significant differences in geomagnetic field distribution characteristics in different regions, which are mainly determined by regional geological structure, surface buildings and man-made interference sources. For example, due to the existence of a large number of steel components and electrical equipment in urban areas, the geomagnetic field is often disturbed by strong magnetic disturbance noise. These interference sources include hard magnetic field, soft magnetic field of navigation carrier and scattered magnetic field of electrical equipment, which will have a complex impact on the measurement results of geomagnetic sensors7. In contrast, the geomagnetic environment in suburbs and mining areas is relatively stable, but its geomagnetic anomaly characteristics may be more significant, which puts forward higher requirements for the construction of geomagnetic map.

In view of the complexity of regional geomagnetic environment, researchers put forward a variety of modeling and interpolation methods to improve the accuracy of geomagnetic data. For example, Yan et al.15 proposed a fusion interpolation modeling algorithm based on threshold recognition of mutation parameter matrix, which can automatically distinguish the region with severe data changes from the region with gentle data changes, and intelligently select the appropriate modeling method. The experimental results showed that the algorithm not only keeps the local details of the regional geomagnetic map, but also realized the smooth transition of the whole data, thus significantly improving the resolution and accuracy of the geomagnetic map. These research results provide important technical support for the construction of road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode.

When the frequency of repeated sampling is from 9 times, the positioning error RMS decreases significantly. However, when the frequency of repeated sampling is further increased to 12 times and more, the improvement of positioning accuracy gradually decreases, and even fluctuates slightly. This phenomenon may be related to the low original accuracy of crowdsourced data and noise accumulation in the process of multiple acquisition, and it also indicates that excessive increase of frequency of repeated sampling may not continuously improve the positioning accuracy.

In terms of geomagnetic accuracy, the relationship between frequency of repeated sampling and geomagnetic variation error RMS shows a similar law. The experimental data show that the geomagnetic variation error RMS decreases gradually with the increase of sampling times, but tends to saturation after 10 times of sampling. This may be because too many frequency of repeated sampling will introduce new error sources, such as equipment drift or environmental changes, which limits the further improvement of geomagnetic accuracy. Therefore, considering the accuracy and efficiency, it is suggested that the frequency of repeated sampling should be controlled at about 10 times to achieve the best cost performance.

Impact of different acquisition scenarios

The positioning accuracy of mobile phone will be significantly restricted by the acquisition scenario. In open scenarios, such as suburbs or open areas, the GNSS satellite signal propagation path is smooth, and the mobile phone can stably receive multiple satellite signals, so the positioning accuracy is high and the external interference is less. However, in highly shielded scenarios, such as urban high-rise areas, avenues or indoor environments, building structures, metal components and vegetation have shading, reflection and multipath effects on GNSS signals, which lead to signal attenuation, time delay and even lock loss, and significantly reduce the positioning accuracy and reliability. In addition, the GNSS signal in indoor scenarios is extremely weak, so the positioning function is forced to rely on alternative means such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or cellular network, which is not considered as the scenario type in this study for the time being.

Normalized evaluation method of crowdsourced data accuracy based on multiple influencing factors

Based on the normalization method of influencing factors based on error RMS proposed in Chap. 3.5, the experiment systematically quantifies and decomposes the multi-source heterogeneous influencing factors in the process of crowdsourced data collection. Taking RMS as a unified measurement standard, the total error fluctuation is decomposed into key dimensions such as “scenario difference”, “mobile phone model difference” and “acquisition time period difference”. By constructing a variance analysis model, the “variance contribution rate” of each factor is calculated, that is, the proportion of error fluctuation caused by it to the total error variance, and the normalization evaluation of different influencing factors is realized.

In the experiment, multi-terminal, multi-time and multi-scenario data are collected synchronously, and the reference value is obtained by high-precision instruments. The geomagnetic variation error RMS of each sampling point is calculated, and the factor decomposition is carried out on this basis. The results show that this method can effectively identify the relative influence intensity of various factors, overcome the evaluation deviation caused by different dimensions and sample distribution differences, and has good comparability and interpretability. Especially in the complex urban environment, the contributions of different factors are significantly different, which provides theoretical support for the subsequent data weighted fusion and quality modeling. Conclusion and discussion

Boundary analysis of road geomagnetic map aquisition under crowdsourcing mode

Based on the previous systematic research on error characteristics, contribution rate of influencing factors and multi-source data fusion effect, this paper further refines the key aquisition boundaries for constructing high-precision road geomagnetic map under crowdsourcing mode. These boundaries do not exist in isolation, but are a multi-dimensional constraint system constructed by equipment performance, space environment, time window and data redundancy. By systematically evaluating the data performance of different intelligent terminals under the conditions of multi-scenario, multi-time period and multi-sampling aquisition, the core elements affecting the quality of geomagnetic map and their mechanism are clarified.

Based on this, this study puts forward the boundary conditions of crowdsourcing aquisition of road geomagnetic map, which provides operable, quantifiable and scalable reference suggestions for crowdsourced data aquisition, in order to improve the accuracy, consistency and practical efficiency of road geomagnetic map construction.

Crowdsourcing selection of equipment terminals

The results show that the performance differences of different brands and models of intelligent terminals in geomagnetic and positioning data acquisition are statistically significant. Taking mainstream new models such as Honor and Huawei as examples, their built-in high-precision magnetometer, multi-frequency GNSS receiving module and optimized sensor fusion algorithm significantly improve the stability and accuracy of original data. In contrast, low-end or old models are prone to introduce system-level errors due to high sensor noise level and defective calibration mechanism, which leads to significantly high RMS errors of geomagnetic data. Therefore, in crowdsourcing acquisition tasks, we should give priority to the new terminals of mainstream brands in the market, and introduce the weighted model of equipment performance in post-processing to optimize the quality of heterogeneous data fusion and ensure the reliability and consistency of the benchmark map.

Spatial boundary constraints of scenarios

In an open area, GNSS signal has excellent visibility conditions and relatively stable geomagnetic environment, and all kinds of terminals can achieve high positioning and geomagnetic accuracy. However, in complex spatial forms such as urban canyons, viaducts and avenues, the occlusion of buildings leads to satellite signal attenuation and multipath effect, while metal structures cause electromagnetic field distortion, which significantly deteriorates data quality. Therefore, priority should be given to open and low interference areas to carry out acquisition tasks. In view of the inevitable complex scenarios, it is suggested to adopt multi-source collaborative observation strategies (such as increasing sampling density, adopting multi-device synchronous aquisition, etc.), combined with scenario classification tags for differentiated processing, so as to improve data availability and robustness in complex environments.

Boundary optimization of acquisition time period

During the peak hours of daytime urban traffic flow, pedestrians’ intensive activities and dynamic electromagnetic noise sources such as vehicle motors and power lines are active, which lead to the aggravation of geomagnetic background field fluctuation and the significant increase of error RMS value. At night, the electromagnetic environment tends to be stable, the regularity of geomagnetic changes increases, and the noise level of measured data decreases significantly. Therefore, it is suggested that the low peak period at night should be taken as the priority collection window to avoid the interference of traffic and human activities. At the same time, an intelligent time window recommendation model can be constructed by combining meteorological data and traffic flow monitoring information to realize dynamic collection and scheduling and improve data collection efficiency and quality.

Redundant boundary setting of data refinement by frequency of repeated sampling

There is a nonlinear correlation between the frequency of repeated sampling and the data refinement effect, and there is an obvious law of diminishing marginal benefit of redundancy. It is proved that when the number of collections is small, the random error of data is large, which makes it difficult to effectively build a stable model. However, when the number of collections exceeds 10 times, the downward trend of error tends to converge, and new systematic deviations may be introduced due to factors such as equipment drift accumulation and environmental dynamic changes. Therefore, it is suggested that the frequency of repeated sampling of a single road section should be reasonably controlled at about 10 times as the redundant boundary criterion of crowdsourced data acqusition. At the same time, it is necessary to strengthen the application of post-processing technologies such as map matching, spatio-temporal filtering and error modeling, optimize the efficiency of data fusion, avoid over-reliance on repeated collection, and achieve the optimal balance between accuracy and efficiency.

Future research prospects

Deepen the modeling of multi-factor coupling relationship and build a comprehensive impact analysis framework

Based on the single factor analysis method, the influence boundaries of equipment, scenario, time period and frequency of repeated sampling on geomagnetic data quality are preliminarily defined. However, in the real acquisition environment, all kinds of factors often do not act independently, but have significant coupling effect. For example, the urban canyon scenario superimposes the traffic flow during peak hours, which may aggravate the superposition influence of geomagnetic interference and positioning drift; The performance differences of different devices in specific scenarios may also be enlarged with time. In the future, it is necessary to further construct a multi-factor coupling analysis model, and introduce ANOVA, structural equation model or machine learning method to quantify the main effects and interactive effects of various factors and reveal their synergistic mechanism.

Increase the influence analysis of equipment attitude to improve the experimental design of acquisition state classification

At present, the attitude changes of intelligent terminals in the acquisition process are not subdivided and controlled. In actual use, the holding mode (hand-held, pocket-placed, vehicle-mounted) and dynamic attitude (tilt, rotation, vibration) of mobile phones will significantly affect the measurement accuracy of magnetometers and inertial sensors. Different attitude may lead to the increase of conversion error between sensor coordinate system and geographic coordinate system, and then affect the accurate extraction of geomagnetic vector. In the future, it is planned to design a special attitude classification experiment, control the attitude of equipment through a fixed bracket or motion capture system, collect geomagnetic data under different pitch angles, roll angles and heading angles, and establish an attitude-error mapping model to improve the data reliability under complex use scenarios.

Extend the time dimension to seasonal and meteorological conditions, and evaluate the current situation and stability of geomagnetic map

This study mainly focuses on the influence of day and night time difference on crowdsourcing aquisition, but does not cover the environmental changes on a longer time scale. In the future, the collection period can be extended to quarters or even the whole year, and cross-seasonal repeated collection experiments can be carried out to evaluate the potential influence of seasonal factors such as surface temperature, soil moisture and vegetation coverage on geomagnetic field, and analyze its time stability and attenuation law, which can provide a basis for the update mechanism of geomagnetic map.

Expand to underground and indoor space, and design crowdsourced mapping strategy under special scenarios

At present, the research mainly focuses on open or semi-open spaces such as urban roads, while closed spaces such as underground passages, subway stations and large commercial bodies also have high-precision positioning requirements. In these areas, GNSS signals are missing, geomagnetic environment is complex, and people flow and metal structure are dense, which poses higher challenges to geomagnetic acquisition. Crowdsourcing incentive mechanism can be considered to encourage users to actively participate in collection in specific periods and areas. By establishing an “indoor and outdoor integration” geomagnetic map fusion framework, the high-precision geomagnetic reference service covering the whole urban space can be realized, and support can be provided for smart buildings, emergency rescue and other applications.

This study is limited by the research cycle and resource input, and has not fully covered the above complex scenarios. The above direction will be the key exploration content in the future, and will continue to promote the development of crowdsourcing geomagnetic mapping to a finer, smarter and more ubiquitous direction.
