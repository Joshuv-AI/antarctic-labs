THE PROBLEM

To examine the scale and nature of the Sino-Soviet Bloc air
defense system, and probable trends in its capabilities through

mid-1966.

SCOPE AND METHOD

1. This estimate will supersede NIE 11-3-60, "Sino-Soviet
Air Defense Capabilities through Mid-1965," published 29 March

1960. The new estimate is scheduled for completion in June 1961.

2. In general, the new estimate will follow the pattern of

the old, concentrating upon Soviet capabilities and considering
