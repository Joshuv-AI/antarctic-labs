No. 1346 Prime Minister Attlee to President Truman

Memorandum to the President From the Prime Minister

I have read your memorandum of July 24 about Palestine.[1] You will I am sure understand that I cannot give you any statement on policy until we have had time to consider the matter, and this is simply to inform you that we will give early and careful consideration to your memorandum.

C R Attlee

Berlin , 31st July, 1945.

Source: Truman Papers

[1] Document No. 1344.
