Differentiable plant hydrodynamic model training
 against global observations from sapfluxnet

 This is a visual that to be used as a highlights for LANL IC proposal report. It demonstrates that the differentiable version
 of HYDRO is able capture the observed sap flow observation around the globe to calibrate the main hydraulic traits. The
 differentiable HYDRO model combines neural networks with the plant hydrodynamic model in a hydrid differential
 framework. The model is trained against global observations from sapfluxnet and shows great performance against
 observations. The left panel shows the calibration sites and the right panels show the model performance with parameter
 calibration. This figure is from a paper in prep: Aboelyazeed, Xu, C., D., Lonzarich, L., Behroozi, A., & Shen, C. 2026.
 Increasing risk of tree mortality under future water stress. In Prep.
