Abstract
For the free complement theory with Gaussian-expanded complement functions, the energy convergence of n G = constant < ∞ , n → ∞ n_{\mathrm{G}}=\mathrm{constant}<\infty,n\rightarrow\infty is discussed, where n G n_{\mathrm{G}} is the number of the Gaussian functions in the STO- n n G expansion.

Additional remarks are:

(i)

The Gaussian-expanded free complement methods
