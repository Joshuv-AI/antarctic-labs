The authors have stated that eight of the nine retracted references appear exclusively in Table 2, which was constructed as a descriptive aggregation of reported microRNA–BDNF/TrkB associations identified through a broad literature search strategy. These references were not used as critical evidence supporting the central conclusions of the review. Only one retracted reference appeared in the main text, and the statement it supported is well-established and independently validated by multiple non-retracted sources. The authors also stated that this article was drafted in 2021, just a few months before submission, when none of the cited papers had yet been retracted.

Readers are advised to interpret the details of this article with caution.

The authors agree with this Editorial Expression of Concern.
