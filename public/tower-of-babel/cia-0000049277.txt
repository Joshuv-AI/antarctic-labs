@. El Salvador: In the opinion of C/LA/DDO, the
government of El Salvador is truly trying to do something
about the right-wing death squads. When Avila was picked up |
by the civilians for auspected death squad activities and was
released, the military re-arrested him. In addition to
taking action on the death squads, the military is now
appointing good people to key positions. We have now seen
the first instance of meritorious promotions, which is a
dramatic development. ‘There have also been instances where
commanders were fired for incompetence. Recently a Lieu-
tenant Colonel was given a brigade command with two Colonels
serving as subordinates. A lot more needs to be done in the
area of tactics, such as fighting at night. In order to
improve dramatically, however, the Salvadorean Army needs
assurances of constant military supplies. The on and off
again supply system form the U.S. has a debilitating affect.
