SUBJECT:

3. ON 22 JANUARY 1990, THE FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL
LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN) SENT AN EIGHT-PAGE, OPEN LETTER SIGNED
BY THE GENERAL COMMAND, TO RELIGIOUS GROUPS IN EL SALVADOR. THE
LETTER WAS ON FMLN LETTERHEAD. IN THE LETTER, THE FMLN STRESSED
THAT THE CHURCH AND THE FMLN PURSUED A COMMON GOAL, THAT 1S, TO
LOOK OUT FOR THE COMMON MAN AND TO FIGHT FOR PEACE. THE FMLN
EMPHASIZED THAT THE CHURCHES HAVE MADE GREAT CONTRIBUTIONS IN
THE AREAS OF HUMAN RIGHTS AND DEMOCRATIC FREEDOM, AND TO THE
SEARCH FOR A NEGOTIATED SETTLEMENT TO THE CIVIL WAR. THE GENERAL
COMMAND POINTED OUT THAT IT HAS PRESENTED SEVERAL PROPOSALS
TO THE GOVERNMENT OF EL SALVADOR, BUT THE GOVERNMENT HAS
RESPONDED WITH MORE REPRESSION, SUCH AS THE FENASTRAS BOMBING,
WHICH FORCED THE FMLN TO LAUNCH THE NOVEMBER 1989 OFFENSIVE

Jie,

Approved for Release

NOV 1993

IN MEMORY OF THOSE WHO WERE KILLED. THE GOVERNMENT RESPONDED

BY BOMBING CIVILIAN TARGETS AND KILILING HUNDREDS OF INNOCENT
PEOPLE, INCLUDING THE SIX JESUIT PRIESTS. THE GOVERNMENT'S
POLICIES ARE AN INVITATION TO WAR. THUS, THE FMLN MUST CONTINUE
ITS MILITARY OFFENSIVE, ALTHOUGH IT WANTS TO END THE WAR TO
AVOID GREATER SOCIAL, HUMAN, AND MATERIAL COST. GROUPS PURSUING
THE SAME CAUSE MUST UNITE THEIR RESOURCES. ALTHOUGH DIFFERENT
CHURCHES HAVE DIFFERENT INTERPRETATIONS OF THE SCRIPTURE, THEY
STILL WORK TO HELP THE POOR. THE FMLN GENERAL COMMAND ENCOURAGES
THE CHURCHES TO CONTINUE TO USE THEIR RESOURCES TO WORK TOWARD
PEACE, AND TO ENCOURAGE THE GOVERNMENT TO RESPECT HUMAN RIGHTS
AND TO NEGOTIATE WITH THE FMLN TO END THE WAR.

4. ON 5 FEBRUARY, IN A GENERAL NEWSLETTER TO PRIESTS AND
OTHER RELIGIOUS ELEMENTS IN EL SALVADOR, ARCHBISHOP OF EL SALVADOR
ARTURO ((RIVERA Y DAMAS)) RESPONDED TO THE FMLN MEMORANDUM
DIRECTED AT THE CHURCH. IN REFERENCE TO THE DOCUMENT, THE ARCH-
BISHOP SAID HE WANTED EVERYONE TO BE QUITE CLEAR ON WHAT HE, AS
THE PASTOR OF THE ARCHDIOCESE, THOUGHT ON THE SUBJECT. HE WROTE
THAT THE DOCUMENT WAS PROPAGANDA; THAT IT SOUGHT TO WIN SYMPATHY
FOR THE FMLN'S CAUSE IN THE WAKE OF THE KILLINGS OF THE JESUIT
PRIESTS; AND THAT THE METHOD THE FMLN USED WAS NOT CHRISTIAN.

THE ARCHBISHOP ADDED THAT THE MARXIST-LENINIST IDEOLOGY OF THE
FMLN GENERAL COMMAND IS IN CRISIS WORLDWIDE. IT HAS NOT BEEN
EFFECTIVE IN NICARAGUA, AND IN EASTERN EUROPE IT JS BEING
COMPLETELY RESTRUCTURED. THE ARCHBISHOP SAID THE CHURCH
BELIEVES THAT BUILDING INSTITUTIONS MORE HUMANE, MORE JUST, AND
MORE RESPECTFUL OF THE RIGHTS OF MAN IS IMPORTANT. HOWEVER,

HE SAID ONE MUST ALSO BE AWARE THAT EVEN THE BEST, MOST IDEAL
SYSTEMS CAN QUICKLY BECOME INHUMANE, IF THE INHUMANE INCLINATIONS
