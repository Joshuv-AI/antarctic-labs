SUBJECT: Proposed Amendments to the Annual and Sick Leave Act of 19Sl,

as Amended

1. The attached draft of proposed amendments to the Annual and Sick Leave
Act of 1951, as amended, has been reviewed by representatives of this Office.

2. It is noted that the proposal would effect four principal changes in
the Act*

(a) Provision of the same leave benefits for persons recruited or
transferi’ed from, or noimal]y resident in. United States Territories or
possessions outside the area of employment as the law now provides for
persons recruited f3x>m the continental United States*

(b) Removal of the i^5 day maximum accuDiulation now provided for
enployees serving overseas' tdiich has the effect of establishing a uni-
form limitation of 30 days for all employees under the Act*

(c) Provision of the same travel without-charge-to-leave benefits
to persons returning to United States Territories or persons outside the
area of enployment on leave as the law now provides for persons return-
ing to the continental United States on leave*

(d) Extension of the “home leave" benefits to all,anployeesj the
actu^ amount of "home leave" to be authorized is left to the discretion
of the President, provided he does not exceed a maximvjm rate of one week
for each fo^lr months of duty overseas*

3* It is recorauended that the Agency st^port these proposed amendments.

The extension of "home leave" benefits to Agency employees has been one of our
proposals for the past several years* The reduction of the hS day accumulation
is consistent with the extension of this benefit* The other two changes offer
no foreseeable problems for Agency employees with respect to leave.
