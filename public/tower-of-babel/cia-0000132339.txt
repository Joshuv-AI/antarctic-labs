ANNEX

Major Pedro Luis DIAZ Lanz

The 32-year old Diaz Lanz, who defected as chief of Cuban air
force on 29 June with bitter public blast against Communist
penetration Cuban armed forces, arrived in Florida 1 July.
A. Had been declared deserter and traitor by Castro and his
arrest ordered,
B. Was pilot for small Cuban airline, 1947-53,
C. From 1956-1958, was co-pilot employed by US-owned Moa Bay
Mining Company. |
D. Frommid-1958 to end of Castro's revolution, he ferried supplies
to rebels from “abroad,'' No previous military experience.
E, Castro named him head of air force in January.
