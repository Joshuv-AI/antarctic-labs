Headline: Ecumenical Council Condemns Killing of Jesuits

Source Line: FL1811014989 Havana Radio Rebelde Network in Spanish
0055 GMT 18 Nov 89

FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:
1. [Text] The Ecumenical Council of Cuba joins in with all
international institutions and figures who have voiced their

condemnation in repudiating the killing of Ignacio Ellacuria, rector -

of the Central American University based in El Salvador, Vice Recto
Amando Lopez, four Jesuit priests, and two of their employees. In a
Statement released in Havana today, the Ecumenical Council of Cuba
said that it is not difficult to see that the material authors of
this terrible crime were instigated by the same people who killed
Archbishop Monsignor Arnulfo Romero.

2. OAS Secretary General Joao Baena Soares will arrive in El
Salvador tomorrow to attempt to establish a dialogue between the
forces in conflict in the Central American nation. Baena Soares
stated to the press in Washington that his peace mission was a
mandate from the OAS General Assembly in light of the worsening of
the war between the ultra-rightist Salvadoran Government and the
Farabundo Marti National Liberation Front.
