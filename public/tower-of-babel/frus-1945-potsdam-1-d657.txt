No. 657 The Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret us urgent

London, June 25, 1945—8 p.m.

6403. French Govt being informed by British Embassy Paris that British Govt agreeable to Paris meeting re Tangier. Details of communication to USSR expected to be concerted Paris at once and we will be promptly informed when communications made.

Winant

Source: 881.00/6–2545: Telegram
