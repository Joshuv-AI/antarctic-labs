Retraction Note: MiR-337-3p confers protective effect on facet joint osteoarthritis by targeting SKP2 to inhibit DUSP1 ubiquitination and inactivate MAPK pathway
Authors: Shengsheng Jian, Dixin Luo, Yeyang Wang, Wangyang Xu, Hui Zhang, Li Zhang, Xiaozhong Zhou
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Cell Biol Toxicol (2023) 39:1099–1118

https://doi.org/10.1007/s10565-021-09665-2

The Editor in Chief has retracted this article because of concerns raised by the authors that the data in this article might have been fabricated. The authors have been unable to replicate the previous data, particularly the regulatory relationship where miR-337-3p targets SKP2. The Editor-in-Chief therefore no longer has confidence in the integrity of the data in this article.

The authors did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
