Correction to “Mechanistic Insights into Protein Corona Formation: The Surface Charge of Mesoporous Silica Nanoparticles Determines the Orientation and the Conformation of Adsorbed BSA Protein”
Authors: Alessandra Ballicu, Gaia M. Meloni, Matteo Farci, Davide Tocco, Marco Piludu, Drew F. Parsons, Cristina Carucci, Barbara Jachimska, Andrea Salis
Published: 2026

Due to a production error, an incorrect version of Figure was included in the published article, and the corrected graphic for Figure was instead erroneously placed in Figure . As a result, the correct graphics for both Figure and Figure were not shown as intended in the final published version.

The correct Figure and Figure together with their captions are shown below.

CD spectra of BSA at 50 μg/mL (A) and 100 μg/mL (B) after 1 h adsorption on MSN and MSN-NH2 50 μg/mL in Tris buffer 10 mM at pH 7.5.

CD spectra of BSA (50 μg/mL) with MSNs (A, B) and MSN-NH2 (C, D) at different concentrations (250, 500, and 750 μg/mL) immediately after mixing (A, C) and after 24 h adsorption at 4 °C (B, D). BSA in 10 mM NaCl, pH 7.5, is shown in green.
