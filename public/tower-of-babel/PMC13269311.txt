Correction to: Whole Genome Sequencing in 25 Families with Suspected Inborn Errors of Immunity: Diagnostic Yield and Clinical Relevance of Genome-wide Analysis
Authors: Jong Kwon Lee, Ja-Hyun Jang, Doo Ri Kim, Areum Shin, Jong-Ho Park, Young-Gon Kim, Jong-Won Kim, Yae-Jean Kim, Eun-Suk Kang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Journal of Clinical Immunology

10.1007/s10875-025-01947-2

In the original version of this article, the following errors were identified and have since been corrected:

1. Figure 1: The shapes and arrows were misaligned, causing the figure to appear distorted.

2. Table 2: The final column displayed incorrect numerical values instead of the originally submitted PMIDs.

3. Figs. 2 and 3: These figures have been updated to improve visual clarity.

The original version has been corrected.

The online version of the original article can be found at 10.1007/s10875-025-01947-2.
