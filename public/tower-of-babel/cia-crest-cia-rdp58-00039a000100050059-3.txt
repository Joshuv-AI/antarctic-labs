9. A survey is being made of all Agency components to determine
how many people would like to attend a lecture-discussion
series on The Formulation and Development of Foreign Policy
at the Foreign Service Institute. This series will include
ll+ sessions, twice a week, two hours a day, for seven weeks.
This series is designed to cover both the procedural aspects
in the formulation of U.S. foreign policy and also the current
problems of its conduct in the four geographic areas of the
world. Speakers will include leading authorities from the
Department of State's Policy Planning Staff and Geographic
Areas, the White House staff, the National War College and
other Government Agencies concerned with foreign policy.
