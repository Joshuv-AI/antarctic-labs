Mid-Year Report Summary
This year we will transfer the latest version of INCC6 with last year’s self-diagnostic
features to the IAEA. In FY25 we completed the development of three automated self-
diagnostic features into INNC6. The completion of these features marked the final
stable version of INCC 6.0 ready for release to the IAEA and other users. In the first half
of the Fiscal Year 2026 we have worked with LANL’s Feynman Center and Export
Control office to attain a license for exporting INCC6 to the IAEA. We have successfully
attained the export license for INCC6, and contacts at the IAEA have been granted
permission to receive INCC6. We are in the final stage of writing the version 6 user
manual. Once this is complete, we will deliver INCC6 to the IAEA for testing.

Task 1: Completion of e-IDR
The Electronic Intake and Disclosure Reporting form was the first step to attaining a
license for INCC6. This step was completed in November with little issue.

Task 3: Technology Transfer to IAEA
The transfer of INCC6 to the IAEA is underway. The licensing center has executed a
license to the IAEA to the users Davide Parise, Travis Trahan, and Rebecca Krentz-
Wee. Davide Parise at the IAEA will act as the contract administrator, meaning that

once he receives the INCC6 executable he is able to freely distribute INCC6 to other
members of the IAEA.
We are currently completing the INCC6 user manual. Since the IAEA plans to test
INCC6 and provide feedback and bug reports it is vital that they have a user manual so
testing can run smoothly. Once the user manual is complete we will send the approved
users the INCC6 executables for testing.

Other
We have approval to present INCC6 at the Non-Destructive Assay working group at
ESARDA/INMM/INMMJ joint workshop in May 2026. ESARDA is an association of
European organizations focused on nuclear safeguards that works with Institute of
Nuclear Material Management (INMM). Presenting INCC6 at ESARDA is excellent
timing. Since the licensing process is complete, we can send INCC6 to other
organizations once they get approval.
