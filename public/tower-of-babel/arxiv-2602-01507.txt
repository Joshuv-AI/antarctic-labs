Orthogonal 2-sphere basis of stable 4-sphere
Akio Kawauchi

Abstract
It is known by Wall that any two orthogonal bases of every stable 4-sphere are transformed into each other by an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of the stable 4-sphere. In this paper another proof of Wall’s result is presented, strengthened in the sense that the diffeomorphism is taken as the lift of an equivalence of a trivial surface-knot space to the double branched covering space identified with the stable 4-sphere. x Keywords: Stable 4-sphere, Trivial surface-knot, O2-handle basis, Branched covering, Orthogonal basis, O2-sphere basis. Mathematics Subject Classification 2010 : Primary 57N13; Secondary 57Q45

Conclusion.
For every orthogonal basis

\Sigma

-deduction
of the orthogonal basis

on a trivial surface-knot

of genus

in
S^{4}
.
By the proof of Lemma 1.2, it is shown that there is a replacement of
the O2-handle basis

and

\Sigma

\Sigma

[

To establish the same claim in smooth category, the unsolved problem
