Entropy and Holography through Adjunctions: A Bicategorical Perspective on Landauer’s Principle Journal: Nuclear Physics B
Petr Vlachopulos

Abstract
We develop a bicategorical framework for entropy and Landauer’s principle in which entropy-ordered state spaces are treated not merely through deterministic monotone maps, but through open many-to-many interfaces encoding feasible realizations between logical and thermodynamic descriptions. This leads to the bicategory of open entropy systems, whose objects are entropy posets, 1 1 -morphisms are profunctorial feasibility relations, and 2 2 -morphisms are refinements. In this setting, the classical order-theoretic Landauer connection is recovered as a representable special case, while the broader bicategorical language captures the openness, multiplicity, and environmental dependence of physical implementation, hence providing a more faithful language for many-to-many realizations between informational boundary states and thermodynamic bulk states. Additionally, the Landauer adjunction in this setting induces a boundary closure monad and a dual bulk interior operator, expressing categorically that bulk-mediated information processing is constrained by entropy and cannot increase recoverable information. We then show that this structure admits a holographic interpretation: the bulk accessible through a given interface is reconstructible from the boundary together with the induced idempotent monad. Via the Eilenberg–Moore construction, the visible bulk is identified, up to equivalence, with the closed sector of boundary data stable under the bulk-boundary round trip. The paper further formulates a quantitative enrichment in which interfaces carry dissipation costs and composition selects the least costly intermediate realization, thereby characterizing the optimal implementation of an information process as one that minimizes entropy production. In this way, this work tries to bring together entropy, Landauer’s principle, and holographic reconstruction within a common categorical framework.

Introduction

The operational content of the second law of thermodynamics can often be expressed in the form of a constraint on which transformations of thermodynamical states are feasible and on what must be dissipated when information is manipulated.

Landauer’s principle made this interconnection explicit within the setup of irreversible computation, i.e. the process of erasing information has inevitable consequences in the form of thermodynamic costs. Such costs are typically described as heat dissipation to the surrounding environment.

Main results

While the approach in
[
4
]
already captures a deep universality in the categorification of thermodynamic entropy as an adjunction between entropy-ordered phase spaces, it remains, by design, a thin categorification process. To be clear, a thin category (posetal category) is a category, in which, given a pair of objects
a,b
and any two morphisms
f,g:a\longrightarrow b
, the morphisms are equalities
f=g
. Within this thin-categorical setup, morphisms are merely order relations, and the implementing maps are deterministic monotone functions.

However, a logical macrostate may admit many physical realizations, since a physical state may support multiple compatible logical descriptions, and interaction with an environment is not an afterthought but part of the operational semantics. Hence, the notion of implementation is intrinsically
open
and
many-to-many
.

2.

Within this setup, the Landauer connections from

Second Conceptual Extension

The second conceptual extension consists of the holographic perspective in categorical form. In higher-categorical implementation of the holographic principle, the guiding theme is that the bulk structure is reconstructible from the boundary structure via a universal construction. In our setting, the corresponding universal object is not a geometric bulk spacetime but the bulk sector visible through a given coupling. Concretely, a bulk-boundary Landauer adjunction (on the boundary) induces a canonical monad/closure operator. This means that the holographic reconstruction problem transforms into the following question:

To what extent does the boundary, equipped with this induced monad, determine the bulk content accessible through the interface?

This can be formalized via the Eilenberg-Moore type constructions for the induced monad which, in the poset case, reduces to an explicit fixed-point (closed state) reconstruction that parallels the discussion about the closure operator in
[
4
]
.

Definition 7

(Composition (gluing of open couplings/interfaces))

Given two open couplings (morphisms)

\Psi:Q\nrightarrow R

\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,r\right)\iff\left(\exists q\in Q\right)\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\Psi\left(q,r\right)=\top\right).

(26)

Categorically, one can usually define (

\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,r\right):=\bigvee_{q\in Q}\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)\land\Psi\left(q,r\right)\right)

(27)

and since we are in
\operatorname{Bool}

).

In other words, the composition connects the output of
\Phi

\Psi

From a physical perspective, we connect an implementation
\Phi
(boundary-bulk feasibility) to another stage
\Psi
(bulk - deeper bulk feasibility) by hiding the intermediate degrees of freedom.

Definition 8

(

\operatorname{Id}_{P}\left(p,p\textquoteright\right)=\begin{cases}\top;\hskip 2.84544ptif\hskip 2.84544ptp\leq p\textquoteright\\
\perp;\hskip 2.84544ptotherwise.\end{cases}

Equivalently,

is the hom-relation in the thin category
P

, given by the order of
P
. Now, we proceed to the definition of the

Definition 9

(

2

Given two interfaces

\Phi\subseteq\Phi\textquoteright\iff\left(\forall p\in P,\forall q\in Q\right)\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\implies\Phi\textquoteright\left(q,p\right)=\top\right).

One can translate the inclusion

The pointwise implication can also be viewed as an inclusion of relations

. In a categorical language, the refinement

-cell.

Let us now define the composition of refinements (

Definition 10
.

Given two refinements

by the transitivity of implication

2.

if

and
\beta:\Psi\implies\Psi\textquoteright

[

At the beginning of this proof, it is necessary to show that the identity profunctor and composites are well defined.

We must show that

, so

Regarding the covariance in

r\leq r\textquoteright

Since our composition is well-defined, we can now show that

is a strict

First of all, let us focus on the associativity of the composition. For

\Phi:P\nrightarrow Q
,
\Psi:Q\nrightarrow R
and
\Sigma:R\nrightarrow S
, we must show that

\Sigma\circ\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)=\left(\Sigma\circ\Psi\right)\circ\Phi

as a
1

.

Fix
p\in P
and

\displaystyle\left(\Sigma\circ\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\right)\left(p,s\right)=\top\iff\left(\exists r\in R\right)\left(\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,r\right)=\top\land\Sigma\left(r,s\right)=\top\right)\iff

(33)

\displaystyle\iff\left(\exists r\in R\right)\left(\exists q\in Q:\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\Psi\left(q,r\right)=\top\right)\land\Sigma\left(r,s\right)=\top.

where the first equivalence stems from the definition of compositions, and the second equivalence is given by the expansion of
\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,r\right)
.

Now we can move the existential quantifiers outward in order to expand (

\left(\exists q\in Q\right)\left(\exists r\in R\right)\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\Psi\left(q,r\right)=\top\land\Sigma\left(r,s\right)=\top\right).

Regrouping using the definition of

\displaystyle\left(\exists q\in Q\right)\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\left(\exists r\in R:\hskip 2.84544pt\Psi\left(q,r\right)=\top\land\Sigma\left(r,s\right)=\top\right)\right)\iff

(35)

\displaystyle\iff\exists q\in Q:\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\left(\Sigma\circ\Psi\right)\left(q,s\right)=\top.

Hence by definition of composition, we obtain

\left(\left(\Sigma\circ\Psi\right)\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,s\right)=\top.

(36)

One can immediately see that the two predicates coincide pointwise, implying that the
1
-cells are identical.

Now, let us focus on the left and right unit laws. Specifically, we have to show that for any
1
-cell
\Phi:P\nrightarrow Q
,

\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\circ\Phi=\Phi,\hskip 5.69046ptand\hskip 5.69046pt\Phi\circ\operatorname{Id}_{Q}=\Phi.

Fix
p\in P
and
q\textquoteright\in Q
. Then

\displaystyle\left(\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,q\right)=\top\iff\exists q\textquoteright\in Q:\Phi\left(p,q\textquoteright\right)=\top\land\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\left(q\textquoteright,q\right)=\top\iff

(38)

\displaystyle\iff\exists q\textquoteright\in Q\left(\Phi\left(p,q\textquoteright\right)=\top\land\left(q\textquoteright\leq q\right)\right).

Now, if the implication
\implies
holds in (
36
), then pick such a q. Since
q\textquoteright\leq q
and
\Phi
is covariant in
Q
, we get
\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\implies\Phi\left(p,q\textquoteright\right)=\top
. Thus
\Phi\left(p,q\textquoteright\right)=\top
.

If the implication
\impliedby
holds, then
\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top
and we can choose
q\textquoteright=q
. Then
q\leq q
, so
\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\left(q,q\right)=\top
, and the existential condition holds. Therefore,
\left(\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,q\right)=\Phi\left(p,q\right)
for all

. This proves the left unit law
\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\circ\Phi=\Phi
.

Proceeding with the right unit law, we should fix

and
q\in Q
. Then

\displaystyle\left(\Phi\circ\operatorname{Id}_{P}\right)\left(p,q\right)=\top\iff\exists p\textquoteright\in P:\operatorname{Id}_{P}\left(p,p\textquoteright\right)=\top\land\Phi\left(p\textquoteright,q\right)=\top\iff

(39)

\displaystyle\iff\exists p\textquoteright\in P:\left(p\leq p\textquoteright\right)\land\Phi\left(p\textquoteright,q\right)=\top.

If the implication
\implies
holds, then pick such

. Since

and

is contravariant in

, then we obtain
\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top
.

Conversely, if the implication
\impliedby
holds, then we have
\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top
, so we can directly choose
p\textquoteright=p
. Then
p\leq p\textquoteright
, which gives us
\operatorname{Id}_{Q}\left(p,p\right)=\top
. So the existential condition holds. Therefore,
\left(\Phi\circ\operatorname{Id}_{P}\right)\left(p,q\right)=\Phi\left(p,q\right)
. This proves the right unit law
\Phi\circ\operatorname{Id}_{P}=\Phi
.

So far, we can conclude that
\operatorname{OEnt}
is a bicategory, which is indeed a strict
2
-category at the level of
1
-cells. Hence, it remains to show that
\operatorname{OEnt}
has a local posetal structure by proving that each hom is a poset.

At first, let us fix posets
P,Q
. Consider the class
\operatorname{OEnt}\left(P,Q\right)
of
1
-cells
P\nrightarrow Q
and define the order on
\operatorname{OEnt}\left(P,Q\right)
by

\Phi\leq\Phi\textquoteright\iff\forall\left(p,q\right):\Phi\left(p,q\right)\leq\Phi\textquoteright\left(p,q\right),

which holds pointwise in
\operatorname{Bool}

One can immediately see the reflexivity of (

\left(p,q\right)

\Phi\left(p,q\right)\leq\Phi\left(p,q\right)

Similarly, we can simply show the transitivity. For each pair

, we have

\Phi\left(p,q\right)\leq\Phi\textquoteright\left(p,q\right)\leq\Phi^{\prime\prime}\left(p,q\right)

\Phi\leq\Phi\textquoteright

, then

At last, it remains to prove the antisymmetry. Hence for each pair

2
-cell.

Furthermore, it is evident that the vertical composition in

is exactly the transitivity. So given

\alpha:\Phi\implies\Phi\textquoteright

\Phi\leq\Phi\textquoteright\leq\Phi{{}^{\prime\prime}}
and

-cells are just pointwise inequalities

.

Considering two pairs of profunctors
\Phi,\Phi\textquoteright:P\nrightarrow Q
and

and

with

and

. From

, we have
\Phi\left(p,q\right)\implies\Phi\textquoteright\left(p,q\right)=\top
and from
\Psi\leq\Psi\textquoteright
, we obtain
\Psi\left(q,r\right)=\top\implies\Psi\textquoteright\left(q,r\right)=\top
.

Similarly, we can prove that
\left(\Psi\textquoteright\circ\Phi\textquoteright\right)\left(p,r\right)=\top
. Thus
\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,r\right)\leq\left(\Psi\textquoteright\circ\Phi\textquoteright\right)\left(p,r\right)
in
\operatorname{Bool}
. This proves that horizontal composition preserves implication.

Finally, it is necessary to show the validity of the interchange law. In a strict 2-category, the interchange law says

\left(\beta\textquoteright\circ\beta\right)\star\left(\alpha\textquoteright\circ\alpha\right)=\left(\beta\textquoteright\star\alpha\textquoteright\right)\circ\left(\beta\star\alpha\right),

(41)

which holds whenever the composites make sense.

Now, because each hom is a poset, there is at most one
2
-cell between any two fixed
1
-cells. Therefore, to show the equality of two

-cells, it is adequate to show that they have the same source and target, and that they both exist.

Take
\alpha:\Phi\implies\Phi\textquoteright
,
\alpha\textquoteright:\Phi\textquoteright\implies\Phi^{\prime\prime}

\operatorname{OEnt}\left(P,Q\right)

\beta:\Psi\implies\Psi\textquoteright
,

\Phi\leq\Phi^{\prime\prime}
and

, i.e.
\Psi\leq\Psi^{\prime\prime}
.

Therefore,
\left(\beta\textquoteright\circ\beta\right)\star\left(\alpha\textquoteright\circ\alpha\right)
is the unique
2
-cell

\Psi\circ\Phi\implies\Psi^{\prime\prime}\circ\Phi^{\prime\prime},

(42)

and it exists.

On the other hand,
\beta\star\alpha
is the unique
2

\Psi\circ\Phi\implies\Psi\textquoteright\circ\Phi\textquoteright
and

\Psi\circ\Phi\implies\Psi^{\prime\prime}\circ\Phi^{\prime\prime}.

(43)

This has proven the locally posetal structure of

2.2
Landauer’s connection

Now we shall proceed to the formulation of Landauer’s connection.

Landauer’s connection can be clearly re-expressed as an adjunction once the entropy function is used to transform a state space into an order. In
[
4
]
, the author starts from an entropy function
S:\Gamma\longrightarrow\mathbb{R}
and defines an entropy order
x\preceq y\iff S\left(x\right)\leq S\left(y\right)
, thereby viewing
\left(\Gamma,\preceq\right)
as a thin posetal category. Within that setting, a Landauer connection between two entropy systems corresponds to the Galois connection
F\dashv G
between the corresponding posets, i.e. an adjunction between thin categories. Its core irreversibility content consists of the induced closure operator
GF
.

In our case, the locally posetal bicategory
\operatorname{OEnt}
retains precisely this order-theoretic background but replaces deterministic implementation maps by open interfaces, i.e.
1
-cells
P\nrightarrow Q
as Boolean profunctors, encoding which bulk states can realize which boundary states, and
2
-cells are refinements given by pointwise implication. Therefore, within
\operatorname{OEnt}
, a Landauer adjunction should be simply an adjunction
\Phi\dashv\Psi
between open interfaces, i.e. the pair of inequalities
\operatorname{Id}_{P}\leq\Psi\circ\Phi
and
\Phi\circ\Psi\leq\operatorname{Id}_{D}
in the hom-posets. This generalizes the Landauer connection from
[
4
]
in two decisive ways. First, it admits many-to-many, environment-mediated realizations, since a single boundary state may have a whole upward-closed family of feasible bulk realizations (and conversely), and second, it distinguishes the bulk-boundary coupling itself as a morphism, rather than collapsing it to a single monotone map. Furthermore, the original adjunction
F\dashv G
from
[
4
]
is then recovered as the representable special case where the open interface
\Phi
is generated by a monotone function F via
\Phi\left(p,q\right)\iff F\left(p\right)\leq q
. Generally speaking, the bicategory
\operatorname{OEnt}
allows implementations that cannot be summarized by a single canonical bulk state per boundary state. Therefore, the induced boundary endo-interface
T:=\Psi\Phi
is then the bicategorical analogue of the closure operator
GF
from
[
4
]
, capturing the Landauer principle as a structural constraint rather than a property of deterministic maps alone.

Considering a general bicategory
\mathscr{B}
, let
f:A\longrightarrow B
and
g:B\longrightarrow A
be
1
-cells. An adjunction
f\dashv g
consists of:

1.

a unit
2
-cell

\eta:\operatorname{id}_{A}\implies g\circ f,

(44)

2.

a counit
2
-cell

\epsilon:f\circ g\implies\operatorname{id}_{B},

(45)

such that the triangle identities hold

\left(\epsilon\circ f\right)\cdot\left(f\circ\eta\right)=\operatorname{id}_{f},\hskip 5.69046pt\left(g\circ\epsilon\right)\cdot\left(\eta\circ g\right)=\operatorname{id}_{g}.

(46)

Here, the symbol
\cdot
denotes the vertical composition of
2
-cells and
\circ
denotes the horizontal composition.

The following property of locally posetal bicategories was already used in the proof of Theorem
2
. Let
\mathscr{B}
be locally posetal bicategory with
1
-cells
f:A\longrightarrow B
and
g:B\longrightarrow A
, then the following data are equivalent

1.

f\dashv g
in
\mathscr{B}

2.

there exist 2-cells

\operatorname{id}_{A}\leq g\circ f\hskip 2.84544ptand\hskip 2.84544ptf\circ g\leq\operatorname{id}_{B}.

(47)

In other words, in a locally posetal bicategory, to specify the adjunction
f\dashv g
, it is sufficient to specify the two inequalities in (
47
).

This leads us to the following definition.

Definition 11

(
Adjunction in
\operatorname{OEnt}
)
.

Let P,Q be open entropy systems and
\Phi:P\nrightarrow Q
,
\Psi:\ Q\nrightarrow P
be
1
-cells. An adjunction
\Phi\dashv\Psi
in
\operatorname{OEnt}
precisely means that the following two

\operatorname{Id}_{P}\leq\Psi\circ\Phi,\hskip 5.69046pt\Phi\circ\Psi\leq\operatorname{Id}_{Q}.

(48)

Since the 2-cells are implications, the inequalities in (

Let
\Phi:P\nrightarrow Q
and
\Psi:\ Q\nrightarrow P
be
1

\operatorname{Id}_{P}\leq\Psi\circ\Phi

\forall p,p\textquoteright\in P,\left(p\leq p\textquoteright\right)\implies\left(\Psi\circ\Phi\right)\left(p,p\textquoteright\right)=\top.

\forall p,p\textquoteright\in P,\left(p\leq p\textquoteright\right)\implies\left(\exists q\in Q\right)\left(\Phi\left(p,q\right)=\top\land\Psi\left(q,p\textquoteright\right)=\top\right).

Next, the counit inequality

\forall q,q\textquoteright\in Q,\quad\left(\Phi\circ\Psi\right)\left(q,q\textquoteright\right)=\top\implies\left(q\leq q\textquoteright\right).

(51)

\forall q,q\textquoteright\in Q,\left(\exists p\in P\right)\left(\Psi\left(q,p\right)=\top\land\Phi\left(p,q\textquoteright\right)=\top\right)\implies\left(q\leq q\textquoteright\right).

(52)

Hence, the adjunction
\Phi\dashv\Psi
in
\operatorname{OEnt}
exactly corresponds to the pair of conditions (

) and (
52
).

Now, let us proceed to the following definition.

Definition 12

(
Landauer interfaces
)
.

Let

\Phi:B^{op}\times D\longrightarrow\operatorname{Bool}.

(54)

Similarly, we can define the so-called Landauer abstraction interface from the bulk

\Psi:D\nrightarrow B

.

Consequently, the Landauer (abstraction) interface induces the notion of the Landauer adjunction.

Definition 13

(
Landauer adjunction
)

A Landauer adjunction is an adjunction

\Phi\dashv\Psi\hskip 5.69046ptin\hskip 5.69046pt\operatorname{OEnt},

(57)

2.

Counit:

\forall d,d\textquoteright\in D:\left(\exists b\in B:\Psi\left(d,b\right)=\top\land\Phi\left(b,d\textquoteright\right)=\top\right)\implies d\leq_{D}d\textquoteright.

Technically, the relation (

\Phi

Since our considerations are situated in a locally posetal bicategory
\operatorname{OEnt}

Definition 15

2.3
Eilenberg-Moore object of an idempotent Landauer monad in
\operatorname{OEnt}

In category theory, a monad
T:C\longrightarrow C
packages a completion/closure process in the following way

1.

\eta:\operatorname{Id}\implies T
states that every object embeds into its completion

2.

\mu:T^{2}\implies T
states that completing twice serves us no better than completing once

The Eilenberg-Moore category
C^{T}
collects the objects that are already compatible with the completion, i.e. objects equipped with an action
T\left(X\right)\rightarrow X
satisfying the coherence condition.

This is where the idempotence of the monad
T
becomes important. When a monad is idempotent, then
\mu
is categorically an isomorphism. Hence, the completion turns into a reflection. In fact, applying
T
sends us into a full subcategory of
T
-complete objects, and those objects are precisely the fixed points of T. In that case, the Eilenberg-Moore category is equivalent to a reflective subcategory of
C
. This corresponds to the classical connection

idempotent\hskip 2.84544ptmonads\longleftrightarrow reflective\hskip 2.84544ptsubcategories\longleftrightarrow splittings\hskip 2.84544ptof\hskip 2.84544ptidempotents.

(121)

Within a bicategory, a monad on an object
P
is an endo-1-cell
T:P\longrightarrow P
with 2-cells
\mu:\operatorname{Id}_{P}\implies T
and
\nu:T\circ T\implies T
. The Eilenberg-Moore object
P^{T}
is the bicategorical substitute for the Eilenberg-Moore category, so it is essentially an object equipped with a universal
T
-algebra that represents all of the
T
-algebras.

As we have seen in all of our results above, in a locally posetal bicategory, the ideas of Eilenberg-Moore category and
T
-algebras become especially crisp, because every hom corresponds to a poset. Thus, all of the algebra axioms become inequalities, and idempotence becomes equality. Therefore, we shall write
f\leq g
for the unique
2
-cell
f\implies g
, if it exists.

Let us summarize these considerations in the following definitions.

Definition 17
.

[
5
]

\mathscr{B}
be a locally posetal bicategory. Fix an object

. A

-algebra with a domain

is

1.

1
-cell

,

2

, i.e.

3
Possible extension of current results

In this section, we elaborate on an extensive vision of a possible extension of the above results and paradigm, and how the concept of thermodynamic entropy could be generalized even further.

Generalization itself is focused on expanding the already used categorical structure in a way that would more closely correspond to the true physical nature of thermodynamic systems and their entropy.

The first natural step should be to replace our locally posetal bicategory
\operatorname{OEnt}
with a certain kind of "process" bicategory and also replace
\operatorname{Bool}
with a quantale
\left([0,\infty],+,\leq\right)
, so the hom sets carry some kind of entropy production costs. This would lead us to a bicategory of costed open interfaces, where the Landauer adjunction yields a quantitative boundary monad.

In other words, our original open interface would carry a minimal entropy (dissipation) cost, and the Landauer adjunction would then become a quantitative adjunction whose unit inequality shall recover precise numerical lower bounds rather than just order statements.

One can summarize the punchline of the possible extension as follows:

1.

\operatorname{OEnt}
can be viewed as the
\operatorname{Bool}
-enriched profunctor bicategory on thin categories (local posets)

2.

Replacing
\operatorname{Bool}
by a quantale
\mathscr{V}
turns the feasibility relation into a graded feasibility relation, i.e. relation with a cost function and the relation composition transforms into an infimal convolution

3.

A Landauer adjunction
\Psi\dashv\Phi
becomes an enriched bulk-boundary adjunction whose unit/counit inequalities correspond to quantitative Landauer constraints, i.e. the boundary monad
T=\Psi\circ\Phi
would become a costed closure operator

In the following paragraphs, we will attempt to rigorously formulate our considerations and outline the exact procedure for how we could implement the above-mentioned points.

Open problem 1 - Enriched Eilenberg-Moore theory

In the Boolean case, the boundary endo-interface

admits an Eilenberg-Moore object describing the boundary data stable under the bulk-mediated round trip. One may ask for an analogous structure in the quantale-valued setup.

The aim would be to develop an Eilenberg-Moore theory for suitable idempotent or split costed endo-interfaces
