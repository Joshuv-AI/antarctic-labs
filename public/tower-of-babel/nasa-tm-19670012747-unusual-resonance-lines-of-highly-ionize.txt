 ABSTRACT

 Resonance lines originating from energy levels far
above the ionization potential have been observed for
highly ionized atoms from Ti to Cu. Two of the lines
for each ion have been classified as the transitions
2s2 2p6 3s         - 2 s 2 2p5 3s2 ?Po3/2, 112   of the Na I
isoelectronic sequence. Wavelengths and energy tables
are given and a typical spectrogram is presented.
