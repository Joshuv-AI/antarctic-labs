Executive Summary of CRADA Work:
Employees of Sylvatex synthesized candidate cathode materials for lithium-ion batteries in PI’s
labs and at the Molecular Foundry using sustainable methods. They then benchmarked the
synthesized materials against available commercial cathode materials in lithium half-cells (coin
cell configurations) to compare performance and ensure that their materials met or exceeded
metrics of the commercial materials.

Summary of Research Results:
Work was initially focused on lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4 or LFP) . LFP does not contain
cobalt, unlike other cathode materials such as NMCs (lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxides
with a general formula of LiNixMnyCozO2; x+y+z»1). This is important not only due to cost but
also because of ethical concerns about the way that cobalt is mined. In addition, iron is benign,
widely available, and most precursors are low-cost. Further work was directed towards NMCs
with low cobalt content. Methods to synthesize materials using techniques that minimize
energy use, reduce carbon emissions, and minimize wastewater were evaluated. Materials
made by these techniques were evaluated in lithium half-cells using standard cycling and rate
determination protocols.
