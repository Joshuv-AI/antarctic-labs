Tax-collector for, and representative and traveling companion (about 1320) of, the infante Don Juan Manuel; the author of "Conde Lucanor." He was a very pious man, an admirer of R. Jehiel b. Asher of Toledo, and punished relentlessly all moral and religious offenses.

Bibliography: Asher b. Jehiel's Responsa, xvii. 8, xviii. 13; Grätz, Gesch. vii. 291 et seq. G. M. K.
