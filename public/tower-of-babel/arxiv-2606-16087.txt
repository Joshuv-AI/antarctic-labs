Problems related to strong connectivity and strong biconnectivity Journal: arXiv
Raed Jaberi

Abstract
Let G = ( V , E ) G=(V,E) be a strong biconnected graph and let B ⊆ V B\subseteq V such that for each vertex w ∈ B w\in B , the subgraph G ∖ { w } G\setminus\{w\} is strongly connected. In this paper we study the problem of computing a subset E β ⊆ E E_{\beta}\subseteq E of minimum size such that the subgraph G β = ( V , E β ) G_{\beta}=(V,E_{\beta}) is strongly biconnected and for each vertex w ∈ B w\in B , the subgraph G β ∖ { w } G_{\beta}\setminus\{w\} is strongly connected. We prove that there exists a polynomial time 7 7 -approximation algorithm for this problem.

Problem 4.1

Let

G_{\beta}=(V,E_{\beta})
is strongly biconnected and
|\{v\mid v\in B\text{ and }v\text{ not a strong articulation point of }G_{\beta}\}|\geq i

Problem
