Clifford Berens Found Murdered In Guatemala

Members of the Guerrilla Army of the Poor (EGP) murdered Clif-
ford Bevens, the kidnapped American manager of the Guatemalan. sub-
sidiary of the Goodyear Tire Company, on 13 August, when Government
of Guatemala security forces, unaware that Bevens was inside, raided a
wooden frame safehouse in Santo Domingo Xenacoj near Santa Maria
‘Caugue (14 38 N 90 42 W) In the firefight, five guerrillas, including two
women were killed. Bevens had been kidnapped on 7? December 1980.
(See the issue of 10 December 1980.

Bevens, who was clad in pajamas, was found in an underground
“people's jail"/cache with three bullet holes in his head. Guatemalan
authorities speculate that the Revolutionary Organization of the People
in Arms (ORPA), which originally abducted Bevens for US$10 million
ransom, recently turned Bevens over to the EGP because of the numer-
ous raids by Guatemalan forces on ORPA safehouses. They believe
ORPA ordered the EGP to kill Bevens rather than have him returned
alive. Four of the guerrillas were killed while attempting to defend the
site or escape. The fifth guerrilla apparently killed Bevens and then
committed suicide when be realized there was no escape; his body was
found near that of Bevens in the underground sit
