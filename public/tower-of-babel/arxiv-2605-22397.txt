On the Turán number of blow-ups of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5}
Xiamiao Zhao , Xin Cheng , Dániel Gerbner , Hilal Hama Karim ‡ , Shujing Miao , Yichen Wang , Junpeng Zhou

Abstract
Let ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} denote the 3 3 -uniform hypergraph on the vertex set { f 1 , f 2 , … , f 5 } \{f_{1},f_{2},\dots,f_{5}\} with hyperedges { f 1 ​ f 2 ​ f 3 , f 1 ​ f 2 ​ f 4 , f 3 ​ f 4 ​ f 5 } \{f_{1}f_{2}f_{3},f_{1}f_{2}f_{4},f_{3}f_{4}f_{5}\} . Recently, Balogh, Clemen and Luo determined the Turán number of a one-vertex blow-up of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} , more specifically, they blow up the vertex f 5 f_{5} to t t vertices, the resulting hypergraph is denoted by ℱ 5 ​ ( f 5 , t ) {\mathcal{F}}_{5}(f_{5};t) . They show that for infinitely many t t , ℱ 5 ​ ( f 5 , t ) {\mathcal{F}}_{5}(f_{5};t) has exponentially many extremal constructions and positive Turán density. In this paper, we determine the exact Turán number of the hypergraph obtained by blowing up f 3 f_{3} of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} to t t vertices and show that it also has exponentially many extremal constructions. We also give a general upper bound and lower bound of the Turán number of every blow-up of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} . For some special blow-ups of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} , for example, t t -disjoint copies of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} , we determine the exact Turán number. We construct a hypergraph ℱ s ​ i ​ m ​ ( t ) {\mathcal{F}}_{sim}(t) which is a subgraph of a blow-up of ℱ 5 {\mathcal{F}}_{5} , and is contained in the hypergraph obtained by adding any new hyperedge to the Turán hypergraph (the balanced complete 3 3 -partite hypergraph), but its extremal construction is not the Turán hypergraph. We also determine the exact Turán number of ℱ s ​ i ​ m ​ ( t ) {\mathcal{F}}_{sim}(t) .

|T_{A}|-|T_{C}|\leq 1
