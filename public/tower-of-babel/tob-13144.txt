Definition  of  Bhakti. 

BHAKTI-YOGA  is  a genuine,  real  search  after  the  Lord,  a 
search  heginning,  continuing  and  ending  in  Love.  One 
single  moment  of  the  madness  of  extreme  love  to  God 
brings  us  eternal  freedom.  “ Bhakti”  says  Narada  in  his  expla- 
nation of  the  Bhakti- Aphorisms,  “is  intense  love  to  God.”— 
“ When  a man  gets  it  he  loves  all,  hates  none ; he  becomes 
satisfied  for  ever.” — “ This  love  cannot  be  reduced  to  any 
earthly  benefit,”  because  as  long  as  worldly  desires  last  that  kind 
of  love  does  not  come.  “ Bhakti  is  greater  than  Karma,  greater 
than  yoga,  because  these  are  intended  for  an  object  in  view, 
while  Bhakti  is  its  own  fruition,  its  own  means  and  its  own 
end.”
