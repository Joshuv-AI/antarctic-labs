BV construction of SUSY vertex algebras from SUSY factorization algebras
Shintarou Yanagida

Abstract
. We construct N = 1 N=1 supersymmetric (SUSY) vertex algebras from supersymmetric enhancements of Costello–Gwilliam factorization algebras on super Riemann surfaces. Introducing SUSY factorization algebras defined on embedded SUSY disks together with natural symmetry conditions, we prove a SUSY analogue of the Costello–Gwilliam extraction theorem. As an application, we study the holomorphic sigma model in the BV formalism. For a linear target, we obtain the free b ​ c bc - β ​ γ \beta\gamma system and recover its structure as a SUSY vertex algebra. For general complex targets, we describe the descent of the theory under coordinate changes and identify the resulting SUSY vertex algebra with the chiral de Rham complex. We further show that Ricci-flat Kähler and hyperkähler targets give rise to N = 2 N=2 and N = 4 N=4 supersymmetric enhancements introduced by Ben-Zvi–Heluani–Szczesny.

Definition 1.1

(The category of SUSY disks)

Define a symmetric monoidal category

Objects are pairs

Definition 1.2

Let

\displaystyle\mu_{U}\colon\mathcal{F}(U_{1})\otimes\dotsb\otimes\mathcal{F}(U_{n})\longrightarrow\mathcal{F}\Bigl(\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{n}U_{i}\Bigr),

In addition, for an inclusion

Definition 1.4

Let

Definition 1.5

An

(

The essential issue is whether the chart-wise quantum theories glue on overlaps.

the corresponding local superfields in
[
BHS08
, §7]
.

To analyze their OPEs, choose holomorphic normal coordinates for
g
at a point.
At that point, the Christoffel symbols vanish,
so the leading singular terms are those of
the
bc
–
\beta\gamma
system of
section

2
.
The deviation from the free-field computation is governed by
the curvature of the Levi–Civita connection.
Because
(X,g,J_{0})
is Kähler and
\nabla J_{0}=0
,
all non-Ricci contractions cancel in the relevant

H
–
H
,
H
–
J
, and
J
–
J
singular terms,
and the remaining defect is the current associated to the Ricci form
\rho_{g}
,
namely
\mathcal{A}_{V}
from
eq.

4.19
.
Thus the singular OPEs agree with those of
the
N=2
superconformal algebra up to some multiple of
A_{V}
claimed above.
∎

4.3.
The Ricci-flat Kähler case

Assume that
(X,g,J_{0})
is Ricci-flat Kähler.
Then the Ricci form vanishes:
\rho_{g}=0
.
Hence, by
4.2
, the defect field
\mathcal{A}_{V}=\mathcal{J}_{\rho_{g},V}

in (
4.19
) vanishes on every holomorphic chart
V\subset X
.
Therefore the local extracted fields of
H_{V}
and
J_{V}

satisfy the
N=2
superconformal relations exactly.

We now globalize these local currents.
By
3.3
, the chart-wise quantum factorization algebras
glue to a sheaf
\mathop{\Obs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
on
X

Since the local expressions

\mathcal{H}_{V}(z,\overline{z},\zeta)=\mathopen{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}\mathrel{\Pi_{a}\,(D\Phi^{a})}\mathclose{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}(z,\overline{z},\zeta),\quad\mathcal{J}_{V}(z,\overline{z},\zeta)=\mathopen{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}\mathrel{J_{0}{}^{a}{}_{b}(\Phi)\,(D\Phi^{b})\,\Pi_{a}}\mathclose{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}(z,\overline{z},\zeta)

are defined invariantly from the metric
g
and
the parallel complex structure
J_{0}
,
they are compatible with the descent maps of
section

3
,
and hence glue to global local observables of
\mathop{\Obs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
.

Theorem 4.3
.

Let
X
be a complex manifold endowed with a Ricci-flat Kähler metric
g
.
Then:

(1)

The chart-wise local observables
H_{V}
and
J_{V}
from

sections

4.2
and
4.2
descend to global local observables

\mathcal{H},\,\mathcal{J}\in\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}(\bbD^{1|1}_{1})

for the SUSY disk
\bbD^{1|1}_{1}
.

(2)

The derivations of
\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
induced by
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{J}

satisfy the
N=2
superconformal relations up to homotopy.

(3)

Under the extraction functor of
1.10
, the cohomology

\mathcal{V}^{\textup{q}}\coloneqq\bbV(\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}})=H^{\bullet}\!\bigl(\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}(\bbD^{1|1}_{1})\bigr)

acquires a natural structure of a sheaf of
N_{K}=2
SUSY vertex algebras.

(4)

This
N_{K}=2
SUSY vertex algebra agrees locally, and therefore globally,
with the
N=2
structure on the chiral de Rham complex
in
[
BHS08
, Theorem 7.4 (ii)]
.

Proof.

The gluing statement in (1) follows from the same descent argument
as in
3.3
:
the currents are expressed invariantly in terms of
the basic superfields
\Phi,\Pi
and the parallel tensors
g
and
J_{0}
,
hence are preserved by the coordinate-change isomorphisms.
By
4.2
, the only local defect in the
N=2
OPE relations
is the Ricci-form current
\mathcal{A}_{V}=J_{\rho_{g},V}
.

Since
g
is Ricci-flat, we have
\rho_{g}=0
,
hence
\mathcal{A}_{V}=0
on every chart.
Therefore the extracted local fields satisfy
the
N=2
superconformal relations exactly.
This proves (2) and (3), using
1.10
to pass
from local factorization currents to the associated SUSY vertex algebra.
Finally, the local formulas coincide with the BHS superfields
attached to the metric and the complex structure,
so the resulting
N_{K}=2
structure is precisely
the one constructed in
[
BHS08
]
.
∎

4.4.
The hyperkähler currents

Assume now that
(X,g,I,J,K)
is hyperkähler.
Thus
g
is a Riemannian metric and
I,J,K
are parallel complex structures
satisfying the quaternionic relations

I^{2}=J^{2}=K^{2}=-1,\quad IJ=K=-JI.

For each
A\in\{I,J,K\}
, we define a local observable of
\mathop{\Obs^{\textup{q}}_{V}}

for local coordinate
V\subset X
by the same formula as in
section

4.2
:

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}_{A,V}(z,\overline{z},\zeta)\coloneqq\mathopen{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}\mathrel{A^{a}{}_{b}(\Phi)\,(D\Phi^{b})\,\Pi_{a}}\mathclose{\mathrel{\vbox{\hbox{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\raisebox{2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\cr\raisebox{-2.15277pt}{$\scriptstyle\circ$}\crcr}}}}}}(z,\overline{z},\zeta).

Since
A
is parallel, this expression is coordinate-covariant and hence defines
a local observable on every holomorphic chart
V\subset X
.

Observe that,
if we choose
A=I
and take holomorphic coordinates adapted to
I
,
then
section

4.4
reduces to
the Kähler current
section

4.2
of
section

4.3
.
The same applies to
J
and
K
after changing the holomorphic structure.
Thus
section

4.4
is the natural factorization-algebra lift of the currents
given in
[
BHS08
, §7]
attached to the three hyperkähler complex structures.
Hence, we have:

Proposition 4.4
.

Let
(X,g,I,J,K)
be hyperkähler.
On each holomorphic chart
V\subset X
, the local observables

\mathcal{H}_{V},\ \mathcal{J}_{I,V},\ \mathcal{J}_{J,V},\ \mathcal{J}_{K,V}

belong to
\mathop{\Obs^{\textup{q}}_{V}}
.
Under the extraction functor of
1.10
,
their extracted fields satisfy the local small
N=4
superconformal relations
(
[
K98
, §5.10, p.187]
,
[
HK07
, Example 5.11]
,
[
BHS08
, Example 2.6]
).

Now, we pass from local hyperkähler currents to
the global factorization algebra.

Theorem 4.5
.

Let
X
be a hyperkähler manifold. Then:

(1)

The local currents
\mathcal{H}_{V},\mathcal{J}_{I,V},\mathcal{J}_{J,V},\mathcal{J}_{K,V}

descend to global local observables

H,\ J_{I},\ J_{J},\ J_{K}

of the factorization algebra
\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
.

(2)

The corresponding derivations of
\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
satisfy the small

N=4

(3)

Under the extraction functor of
1.10
, the cohomology

\bbV(\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}})\coloneqq H^{\bullet}\!\bigl(\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}(\mathbb{D}^{1|1}_{1})\bigr)

becomes a sheaf of
N_{K}=4
SUSY vertex algebras.

(4)

This
N_{K}=4
SUSY vertex algebra is the factorization-algebra lift of
the hyperkähler structure on the chiral de Rham complex
in
[
BHS08
, Theorem 7.4 (iii)]
.

Proof.

Since
I,J,K
are parallel complex structures,
the local formula
section

4.4
is preserved by
the coordinate-change isomorphisms of
section

3
.
Hence the local currents descend to global local observables of
\mathop{\mathcal{O}bs^{\textup{q}}_{X}}
,
which proves
(1)
.
By
4.4
, on each chart,
their extracted fields satisfy the local small

Applying the extraction functor of

The comparison with
