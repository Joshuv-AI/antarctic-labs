# TOB-0088 — SWORN BOOK OF HONORIUS
## Pseudo-Honorius of Thebes | c.1250–1350 CE | Medieval Ceremonial Magic

---

## 📖 OVERVIEW

**Full Title:** Liber Juratus Honorii ("The Sworn Book of Honorius")
**Author:** Attributed to Honorius of Thebes; likely composed 13th–14th century
**Date:** c.1250–1350 CE (medieval)
**Pages:** ~300
**Language:** Latin / English
**Category:** Grimoire / Ceremonial Magic / Medieval Necromancy

---

## 📚 CONTENTS

### Part I — The Oath and Preparation

- The oath administered to the practitioner
- Qualifications required to practice
- Preparation of the operator
- Fasting, prayer, and purification

### Part II — The Magical Circle

- Construction of the sacred circle
- Consecration of circle and tools
- Names of God used in the rituals
- Psalms and prayers used during conjuration

### Part III — Conjuration Formulas

- Conjuring angels and spirits
- Infernal spirits and their bindings
- Pacts with spirits
- The different orders of spirits (angels, demons, elemental spirits)

### Part IV — Practical Applications

- Divination by spirits
- Magical operations for various purposes
- Obtaining hidden knowledge
- Binding spirits to service

---

## 📌 HISTORICAL NOTES

**Joseph Peterson Edition:** This copy is the Joseph Peterson edition, which is the scholarly standard. Peterson is known for his rigorous academic approach to occult texts.

**Significance:** The Sworn Book is one of the oldest grimoire-type texts in Western tradition. It represents the medieval bridge between earlier necromantic texts and later Renaissance ceremonial magic traditions.
