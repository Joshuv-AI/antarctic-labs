3.5

The energy constraint in (

\displaystyle\sum^{W}_{i=1}p_{i}^{q^{\prime}}E_{i}=\sum^{W}_{i=1}p_{i}p_{i}^{q^{\prime}-1}E_{i}=\sum^{W}_{i=1}p_{i}E_{i}^{\prime},

Stated differently, the relative energy level

of each microstate,

A remarkable fact is that, other than the linear case described in Subsection

), which has not been, to the best of our knowledge, previously studied in the literature. This case can be seen as a dual of the well-known escort case (i.e.,

We highlight that the linear constraint case described in Subsection

and Proposition

) that

is an adequate prediction, namely for

or

)

[

and Fig. 3 in
[

]
) as well as in the long-range-interacting Fermi-Pasta-Ulam anharmonic systems
[

]

Finally, the linear constraint case with

has been shown to correspond to confined overdamped many-body systems with repulsive two-body interactions

64
