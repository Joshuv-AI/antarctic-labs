Author Correction: Acoustic metamaterials-driven transdermal drug delivery for rapid and on-demand management of acute disease
Authors: Junhua Xu, Hongwei Cai, Zhuhao Wu, Xiang Li, Chunhui Tian, Zheng Ao, Vivian C. Niu, Xiao Xiao, Lei Jiang, Marat Khodoun, Marc Rothenberg, Ken Mackie, Jun Chen, Luke P. Lee, Feng Guo
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-023-36581-2, published online 16 February 2023

In the version of this article initially published, there was an error in Fig. 3d where the acoustics (+); 60 min mouse image was an inadvertent duplicate of the acoustics (+); 10 min mouse image to its left, while in the Fig. 3e acoustics (–); 10 min column, an extraneous data point appearing between 1 and 2 on the y-axis has been removed. The figure is amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
