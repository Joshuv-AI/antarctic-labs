Capital of a canton, arrondissement of Chatillon-sur-Seine, Côte d'Or, France. As the name indicates, there were Jewish inhabitants in this place during the Middle Ages. A secret inquiry was made between 1306 and 1308 into the debts due to the Jews. A century later Jews were still in the community.

Bibliography: Inventaire Sommaire des Archives Départementales: Côte d'Or, ii. 4032, iv. 414. D. I. L.
