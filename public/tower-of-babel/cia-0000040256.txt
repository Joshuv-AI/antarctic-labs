According to Chilean authorities, the last 3 years of leftist coalition rule were years

which all divisionist positions created by partisan political, economic or social

freedom, egalitarianism, fraternity and solidarity; all elements that will conform with

The proposed national objectives presuppose the establishment of an effective, modern
and representative social democracy, eliminating poverty and projecting to the whole
world a cultural, political and economic image of a new Chile. Regarding a general
policy, the tendency will be to create a renewed society and a new man through the
adequate exercise of authoritarian power but intimately linked with the invariable
application of flexible, apolitical andj regenerative national justice.

Tne action program ocutiines in general the policy to be implemented in a1] national
political, economic and social activities. In politics the government will, at an
unspecified date, reestablish the mational system by drafting a new constitution that
will provide adequate security for the new Chile, Until this happens, however, the
government considers it necessary to enforce this partisan political moratorium due to
present national conditims and to develop civic preparedness, especially among the
youth, According to this plan, Chileans must become aware of the levels and range that
partisan politics should have and of the damage caused when it interferes with national

interests in pursuit of partisan goals.

democratic system, such as the leftist parties which were outlawed when the military
junta took command. The system is meant to include only middle-6f-the-road and
conservative parties which nave been pecessed indefinitely by the authorities.

In economics, the general Yesective is to reach balanced developaent by increasing

changes to secure social development eclosely linked with economic development.
