To impose limitations with regard to Executive agreements.

or superseded by a subsequent Act of Congress; and

was recognized early by the Supreme Court ; and

Whereas Executive agreements entered into by the President
other than those limited specifically under certain circum-
stances to the exercise of his diplomatic powers also may
be vacated by inconsistent legislation enacted by the Con-
gress ; and

Whereas there is a present tendency to use Executive agree-
ments rather than submit the international undertaking to
the Senate in accordance with the constitutional require-
