Turkish rabbinical author; died at Smyrna in 1832; son of Benjamin Pontremoli. Ḥiyya Pontremoli wrote, among other works, the "Ẓappiḥit bi-Debash," a collection of responsa on Oraḥ Ḥayyim.

Bibliography: Hazan, Ha-Ma'alot li-Shelomoh, pp. 31, 85; Franco, Histoire des Israélites de l'Empire Ottoman, p. 268. S. M. Fr.
