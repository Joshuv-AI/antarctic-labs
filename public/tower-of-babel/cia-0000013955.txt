Between late January and May 1968 a regional Prisoner of War (POW) Camp

was located at approximately WS 875440 at MY LOI Hamlet, LONG HIEP Village,
KIEN VAN District, KIEN PHONG Province. This camp had 24 prisoners mostly
GVN Local Forces who had been captured in 1968 These prisoners were

treated well and received 2 meals with 1 kilo of rice a day. Sufficient

food was provided by the Viet Cong (VC) unit in charge of guarding the

place, In late May 1968, during an Allied sweeping operation the VC dispersed
the prisoners among the local people, later some were released «

In mid June 1968, following orders of the Military Region Headquarters another
POW Camp was constructed. This camp was situated at approximately WS
905530 in MY AN District, KIEN PHONG Province. It was constructed with

light materials of bamboo, wood and soil, measured approximately 6,00x/,,.00x:

1.50: meters, camouflaged on top by green grass, and designed to keep 6 prisoners,

36

There was 1 Platoon of 27 men fully armed with individual rifles to maintain
security under the guidance of UT QUE and his Assistant named SAU TRUNG,

According to UT LONG, Cadre of the Rear Service Section of KIEN PHONG
Province Committee, the construction of this new POW Camp was a part of the
preparation for the coming 1968-1969 Winter/Spring offensives. Two English
speaking instructors were assigned to the Camp to conduct the instruction/
indoctrination of any English speaking prisoners who might be sent there,
One instructor TU SON, a SVN national came, from the CAO LANH District, KIEN
PHONG Province, He was about 26 years old, single, and a High School
Graduate. He had been involved with VC activities since 1963. His father
was CHIN MAT a Regional Finance Cadre operating along the CAMRODIA/SVN
border. The other instructor, name unknown, was the son‘of BA TRUNG a
cadre of the THANH BINH District Committee in KIEN PHONG Province. He was
about 21 years old, single, a High School Graduate, and active in VC
affairg since 1963. Both men were sent in 1966-1967 by the Political
Staff of KIEN PHONG Province Committee to a school along the border to attend
a one~year English language training course.
