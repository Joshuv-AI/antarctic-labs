 PREFACE

 To achieve the late President John F. Kennedy's momentous
 goal of ":Landing a man on the moon and returning him safely
 to the earth" required not only technology of the highest
 r
 order but a sense of purpose rooted in deep personal moti-
 vation. The goal has been won. Now that time for analysis
 has become available, the Library has had many requests for
 information on motivation.

 This bibliography has been prepared to help meet these re-
 quests. It includes only materials available in the Kennedy
 Space Center Library. Books on the subject are listed re-
 gardless of publication date but only periodical articles
 published after 1960 are included. The bibliography is
 arranged by title, with a subject-author index.

 The following indexes were searched for relevant materials:
 Scientific and Technical Aerospace Reports, Business Periodicals
 Index, Readers' Guide to Periodical Literature and Applied
 Science and Technology Index. Entries not annotated were
 unavailable for examination while the bibliography was in
 preparation.
