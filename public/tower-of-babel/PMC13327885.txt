Moving forward as a global journal: a farewell reflection from the Editor-in-Chief
Authors: In Ja Park
Published: 2026

A very memorable day for me was the day when I became Editor-in-Chief of the Annals of Coloproctology (ACP). One of the goals during my term of work was to broaden the scope as well as to enhance the international standing of ACP. As Editor-in-Chief, the most meaningful work for me was reviewing the submitted manuscripts, the co-work with the reviewers as well as with the other editors. Moreover, I have had the pleasure of witnessing the development of ACP. I would like to express my deep gratitude to the editorial board members and reviewers as well as the authors from all over the world who submitted their valuable scientific papers to ACP.

THE PATH TO INTERNATIONALIZATION AND ACHIEVEMENTS

One of the goals which I set at the beginning of my term of Editor-in-Chief of ACP was to increase the international representation of ACP. The editorial team of ACP has continuously pursued this goal. In addition to an expansion of the ACP beyond the regional societies, the ACP editorial team has also developed an international online platform for scientists and colorectal surgeons.

ACP is no longer supported almost entirely by domestic submissions. Rather, the majority of submissions are from researchers worldwide: Asia, Europe, the Americas, and the Middle East. What started as an internationally recognized journal is becoming an increasingly major player in the global scene of published research.

ACP has been actively interacting with several international societies, starting with affiliation with Asia Pacific Federation of Coloproctology (APFCP). Then, ACP also affiliated with the Società Italiana Unitaria di Coloproctologia (Unitary Italian Society of Colon-Proctology)-ETS (SIUCP-ETS) which has resulted in the publication of clinical practice guidelines for anorectal and bowel functional diseases as well as many meaningful research results of SIUCP-ETS members (Fig. 1). In addition, ACP has also expanded collaboration with the Nigerian Society for Colorectal Disorder (NSCRD) and Society of Colorectal Surgeons, Singapore (SCRS). Through this collaboration, we were expected to expand the editorial scope and project ACP as a global forum for coloproctology research as well as its clinical application.

As for the number of submitted papers, the scientific quality of the published papers was increasing. The ways to peer review the papers were getting more refined, and the quality of publication has been more clarified, enhanced presentation and improved accuracy. All the indicators of citation impact and also the journal metrics are continuing to rise.

CHALLENGES

ACP is facing new challenges while continuously growing. ACP receives an increasing number of highly diverse submissions. The distribution of the workload to reviewers as well as the speed of the peer review process and the subsequent editorial process have to be appropriate. The editorial team is having difficulties to keep up with the fast development of medical publishing environment. The further development of continuously increasing ACP will need the same extent of improvement of the editorial process as was done so far. Also, the globally distributed editorial board has to be enlarged continuously.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY IN THE ERA OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

There has been rapid progress of artificial intelligence to a wide variety of areas of scientific research, publication and data analysis. A number of the very latest generative models as well as some of the large language models can provide substantial assistance with data analysis as well as with writing the resulting manuscript.

The advances in research technologies will change ways to conduct clinical research as well as ways to publish results of that research. But advances in research technologies do not change the advances in honest academic work. New methods to conduct research and to publish results of that research will still need to be conducted with the honest principles of full disclosure, full accountability and complete responsibility for the ethical material that is published. That material will still need to be published with the intention that results from published articles will be used by clinicians to care for patients.

The next generation of technological innovations will revolutionize the ways in which scientific investigation and dissemination of findings via research are conducted. For the immediate and distant future, ACP must continue to update its publication guidelines and collaborate with the latest technological advances to assist verify the scientific information record. ACP authors, reviewers, and editors must be the leaders of the publication guidelines for the scientific information record and become wise users of such tools to most effectively fulfill their responsibilities to verify the scientific information record in order to disseminate information that is put forth by ACP to be used by the worldwide clinical practitioners to provide the best patient care possible.

WELCOMING NEW LEADERSHIP AND FUTURE HORIZON

I would like to introduce and welcome support for the new Editor-in-Chief of ACP, Gyung Mo Son. With great confidence in his leadership capabilities and his vast academic publishing and international collaborative experience, I am sure that ACP will continue to grow from strength to strength to achieve all of its goals and maintain its leading position among the journals in the field. I trust that all the readers, authors, reviewers, and editors of the journal will give him the same support and trust as they have given me.

FAREWELL

As my term as Editor-in-Chief of ACP comes to an end, I would like to express my appreciation to all the readers as well as to all the editorial board members for their support during my term. I shall continue to read the journal, to write for it, and to be an honest reviewer as I am sure ACP will continue to progress and be successful. I am looking forward to the next chapter of excellence of ACP.
