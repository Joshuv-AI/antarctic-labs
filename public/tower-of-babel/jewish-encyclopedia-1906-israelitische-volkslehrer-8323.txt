Monthly, published at Frankfort-on-the-Main. It was founded in 1851, and continued to 1862. It was edited by Leopold Stein; in its last years S. Süss-kind, the publisher of the feuilletonistic family journal "Der Freitagabend," was associated with him in the editorship. The journal was popular in tone, and published sermons, rabbinical decisions, and discussions on religions matters.
