No. 724 The Secretary of State to the Acting Secretary of State

top secret

Babelsberg, July 16, 1945.

Victory 35. Top secret to Acting Secretary of State.

British Delegation informs us Department proposes issuing statement July 17th that United States will support Italy’s admission to international organization and has asked British Government to do likewise. Would prefer that you postpone action on this as I desire to give consideration to some statement regarding Italy into which would be incorporated if possible reference to this subject and to which I would like to consider inviting Soviet Government to join.

Byrnes

Source: 740.00119 (Potsdam)/7–1645: Telegram
