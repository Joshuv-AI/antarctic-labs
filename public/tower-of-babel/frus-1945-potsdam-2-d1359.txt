No. 1359 The Representative in the Conversations Concerning Tangier (Childs) to the Acting Secretary of State[1]

secret

Paris, July 26, 1945—1 p.m.

4505. From Childs.

Meyrier informed us today that British and French were concerting together on the form of invitation to the USSR to participate in the Tangier conversations to open in Paris on August 6 and that it was hoped to arrive at a definite text which would (sent Dept as 4505; repeated Tangier as 24; Madrid as 281 and London as 541) be communicated to the French and British Embassies in Moscow tomorrow.

Source: 881.00/7–2645: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent over the signature of Caffery.
