I . GENERAL INFORMATION

While in Williamsburg, Aerospace Industries
Association will be the Conference host. Upon
arrival please stop by the Office (Room 3127 of the
Inn) and pick up additional information and name tags.

HOUSING

Will be in Providence Hall, The Inn and The Lodge.

You need not and should not make your own reservations.
If using AIA arranged transportation, your room assign-
ments will be made known as you board the plane. If
providing your own transportation, please check at the
Front Desks of The Inn or Lodge upon your arrival in
Williamsburg .

ACTIVITIES

Will occur in various places at The Inn and Conference
Center. Details of the time, nature, and location of
these activities are set forth elsewhere in this
pamphlet. You are also reminded that Conference
activities are limited to industry representatives
and invited guests.

DRESS

Is informal. Most conferees wear sport clothes during
the day, but change to a business suit or sport jacket
with shirt and tie for the evening dinner sessions.

RECREATION

Those planning to play golf should arrange for starting
times by phoning the ProShop as early as possible. If
the AIA staff may assist you, they will be happy to do
so .

If you plan to play tennis, please be advised that the
courts will be open at 6 A.M. on Thursday and Friday,
May 25 and 26.

Should you have any questions, please contact the
office of:

III. TRANSPORTATION

(All times are Eastern Daylight Saving Time)

AIA ARRANGED TRANSPORTATION
WEDNESDAY - MAY 24

10:00 A.M. - Piedmont Charter from Washington
National.

At 10:00 A.M., plane doors will be closed for
taxi and takeoff.

Please arrange to be at the Commuter Terminal
(adjacent to the North Terminal in the same
building as the General Aviation Terminal)
Washington National Airport, no later than
9:30 A.M. so that baggage can be tagged and
loaded. Arrival at Williamsburg, including
ground transportation from Patrick Henry
Airport, will be at approximately 11:30 A.M.

Ground Transportation: At Patrick Henry Air-
port, there will be separate vehicles for each
of the three housing accommodations. These
limos will be clearly marked and you should use
the vehicle for the building in which your
assigned room is located. Your luggage will
also be loaded on this vehicle so that it will
arrive with you at your destination. (For
example: If you have been assigned to a room

in Providence Hall, your luggage will have a
green tag and the vehicle will have a green
sign with "AIA/Providence Hall" in the window.)

No other AIA transportation to Williamsburg has
been arranged for Wednesday, May 24. You are
advised that Williamsburg receives regular air-
line service (to Patrick Henry Airport/Newport
News) via Piedmont and United Airlines. If your
schedule precludes your using the 10:00 A.M.
flight, we will be pleased to assist you in mak-
ing alternate arrangements.

FRIDAY - MAY 26

Transportation from Williamsburg. All those
utilizing AIA transportation will be returned
to Washington National Commuter Terminal,
arriving at approximately 3:00 P.M. Departure
from The Inn will be immediately following the
luncheon promptly at 1:15 P.M.

FOR THOSE PROVIDING THEIR OWN TRANSPORTATION

Company Aircraft Arrivals

You may fly to either Patrick Henry Airport or
to the Williamsburg/Jamestown Airport. If we have
not been advised of your ETA, please do so. If
this will not be known until shortly before your
departure, please have your office transmit this
information to our Washington Office through May 22.
After that, please contact the AIA/Williamsburg
Office. Ground transportation will meet you.

Military and Government Aircraft Arrivals

Past experience indicates most government air-
craft will fly to Patrick Henry Airport.

If you plan to arrive at Patrick Henry Airport
and we have been advised of your ETA, ground trans-
portation will meet you. If your ETA has not been
made known to us, please have your office transmit
this information to the AIA Washington Office through
May 22. After that, please call us collect at our
Williamsburg Office.

If you plan to fly to one of the nearby military
airports (Fort Eustis or Langley Field) , we suggest
you call on the Motor Pools for transportation to
Williamsburg or let us know if we can assist you by
providing ground transportation.

Commercial Flights

If you plan to arrive by commercial aircraft,
please advise us of the flight number, airline and
arrival at Patrick Henry so that we can arrange for
ground transportation to meet your flight.

Arrivals by Car

If you have not advised us, information regard-
ing your expected arrival time would be greatly
appreciated. Upon arrival in Williamsburg, the
Front Desks of The Inn or Lodge will inform you
regarding your room assignment.

Ample parking space is available adjacent to
Providence Hall, The Inn and the Lodge.

Departures - Military or Company

Ground transportation to airports will be avail-
able at all times. If your departure will be at other
than the scheduled time for all, please be sure that
we have your departure time, either prior to or while
you are in Williamsburg.
