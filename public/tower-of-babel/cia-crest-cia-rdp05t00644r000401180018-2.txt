Dear Mr. Turner:

I wish to invite you to participate in the first of a special
series of briefings for Chief Executive Officers of major
American companies. Our offices have been in touch, and I
understand the time has been reserved on your calendar.

These briefings are designed to provide specific information
to bolster efforts by American businesses to compete in
foreign countries. They stem from an interagency Task Force
on Intelligence and the Business Community which recommended
that Commerce, closely supported by State, CIA and Treasury
establish a high level series of briefings on key foreign
countries. Although government participants in the briefing
will obviously have full access to intelligence, the briefings
are not to divulge classified information. (This letter is
classified because the existence of the Task Force, as well as
its work and recommendations are SECRET.)

For the first briefing, the Task Force selected high priority
major projects in two industrial sectors of China -- *

non-petroleum energy and mineral resource development. The
American companies bidding for projects in these industrial
sectors are facing stiff foreign competition. Enclosed is a
list of the major projects and industrial sectors and the
American companies that have been invited.

The briefing will be held Tuesday, December 9, at the
Department of Commerce, from 10:00 a.m. to about 2:30 p.m. An
agenda and list of speakers and topics are enclosed.

Questions about your participation and presentation should be
directed to Mr. David Laux, Director, People's Republic of
China Affairs Division, 377-3583.

I am informing the Chief Executive Officers or their
representatives that the briefing will be closed to all but
invited guests and senior government officials. The briefinq
will be strictly off-the-record.

Given the relatively small size of the group, I suggest that
your presentation be conversational, rather than speech form,
and as informative as possible given necessary constraints. I
also suggest leaving some time for questions.
