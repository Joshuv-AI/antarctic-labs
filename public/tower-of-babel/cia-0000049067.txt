GENERAL CHARACTERISTICS:

THE RIGHTIST PARAMILITARY GROUPS, OR "DEATH SQUADS", ARE
DIVERSE IN COMPOSITION AND PURPOSE BUT DO SHARE MANY
CHARACTERISTICS. THE GROUPS ARE NOT LARGE; THEY RARELY EXCEED 20
CONCURRENT MEMBERS AND COMMONLY OPERATE IN GROUPS OF 10 OR LESS.
ACTIVE MEMBERSHIP IN SUCH GROUPS IN EL SALVADOR PROBABLY OSCILATES
BETWEEN 100 AND 200. THE SQUADS ARE ESSENTIALLY IMPERMANENT. MOST
MEMBERS HAVE OTHER FULL TIME PROFESSIONS AND COALESCE TO PLAN AND
EXECUTE TERRORIST ACTS. RANK AND FILE MEMBERSHIP IS FLUID AND
SOMETIMES INTERCHANGEABLE, WHILE LEADERSHIP POSITIONS ARE MORE
PERMANENT. LEADERS OF ONE GROUP ARE LIKELY TO KNOW THE LEADERS OF
OTHERS OPERATING IN THE SAME AREA OR AGAINST COMMON TARGETS AND
AND DISTINCT GROUPS OCCASIONALLY CONDUCT JOINT OPERATIONS. INFORMA~
TION ABOUT THE MEMBERSHIP AND ACTIVITIES OF THE GROUPS IS TIGHTLY
HELD AND ANYONE BETRAYING THEIR TRUST JS SUBJECT TO EXECUTION.

THERE APPEAR TO BE TWO CATEGORIES OF DEATH SQUADS,
DIFFERENTIATED BY THE PUBLIC OR CLANDESTINE NATURE OF THEIR
OPERATIONS. THE FIRST CATEGORY IS PUBLICITY SEEKING. THEY EMPLOY
SELF-STYLED TITLES SUCH AS THE “WHITE WARRIORS UNION", THE "GENERAL
MAXIMILIANO HERNANDEZ MARTINEZ ANTI-COMMUNIST BRIGADE", OR THE
"SECRET ANTI-COMMUNIST ARMY" AND MAKE A PUBLIC EXAMPLE OF THEIR
VICTIMS, WHO ARE OFTEN TORTURED TO DEATH OR EXECUTED, AND DUMPED IN
PUBLIC PLACES. THE KILLERS PROCLAIM THEIR WORK WITH COMMUNIQUES OR
PLACARDS LEFT WITH THE BODY IDENTIFYING THE RESPONSIBLE SQUAD AND
CONTAINING A WARNING TO OTHERS, OR BY CARVING THE NON-SPECIFIC
LETTERS "E.M." (THE SPANISH ABBREVIATION OF "DEATH SQUAD") ON THE
BODY. THE SECOND TYPE OF DEATH SQUAD USES NO TITLE AND, APART FROM
THE EVIDENCE OF TORTURE, LEAVES NO INDICATION OF ITS RESPONSIBILITY.
MOST OF THOSE KILLED BY RIGHT-WING DEATH SQUADS APPEAR TO BE VICTIMS
OF THIS LATTER TYPE.
