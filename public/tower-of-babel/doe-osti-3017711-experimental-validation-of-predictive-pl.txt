  • Hermes-3 predicts plasma profiles and fluctuations using only
 the total power (120kW), particle flux (3e21/s) and magnetic
 equilibrium. No tuning parameters or coefficients are required.
  • We find better agreement with the TCV-X21 experimental
 reference dataset [D.S. Oliveira and T. Body et al. 2022] than any
 previously published.
  • Provides the basis for predictive simulations of future tokamak
 devices without ad-hoc transport coefficients.
  • Ongoing work to apply Hermes-3 to NSTX-U, DIII-D and SPARC.
