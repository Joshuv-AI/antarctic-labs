Editor's Note: “Enhanced performance of an eco-friendly and sustainable secalin-corn starch packaging film via citric acid cross-linking” [CRFS 11 (2025) 101231]
Published: 2026

This Editor's Note is related to the above-mentioned publication.

A reader has raised concerns regarding the similarity between Fig. 3b in this article and Fig. 2b in another LWT journal article, as well as between Fig. 3c in this article and Fig. 2c in another LWT journal article. For further details, please refer to the PubPeer post:

https://pubpeer.com/publications/F4AE9CDCE30C163C01A17A47146970#0.

As part of an investigation conducted on behalf of the journal by Elsevier's Research Integrity & Publication Ethics team, the authors were contacted to comment on the concern.

For the concerns regarding Fig. 3c in this article and Fig. 2c in the LWT journal article, LWT has published a corrigendum to address the issue: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0023643825016597.

For the concerns regarding Fig. 3b in this article and Fig. 2b in the LWT journal article, the Editor-in-Chief considered the authors’ explanation to be satisfactory.

Accordingly, the concerns have been duly acknowledged by means of this Editor's Note.
