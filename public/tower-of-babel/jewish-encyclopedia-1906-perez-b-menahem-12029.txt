Rabbi at Dreux; took part in his old age in the great synod held before 1160 under the presidency of Jacob b. Meïr (R. Tam). He was the father of Menahem Vardimas, author of an "'aḳedah" in forty-five verses. Perez was probably the author of a manuscript which is entitled "Sefer Pereẓ," and which contains notes on the Masorah.

Bibliography: Gross, Gallia Judaica, pp. 178, 572; Zunz, Literaturgesch. p. 328. D. M. K.
