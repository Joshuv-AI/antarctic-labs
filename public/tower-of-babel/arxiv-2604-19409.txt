The high order spectral extrema of 2 ​ K r 2K_{r} -free graphs
Changjiang Bu, Yifan Sun, Haotian Zeng

Abstract
In this paper, we determine the graphs with maximum value of the sum number from k k -clique spectral radius to ( 2 ​ r − 1 ) (2r-1) -clique spectral radius among all 2 ​ K r 2K_{r} -free graphs on n n vertices for r ≤ k r\leq k and large n n . We also determine the graphs with maximum 3 3 -clique spectral radius among all 2 ​ K 3 2K_{3} -free graphs on n n vertices. Our results are spectral versions of some results on generalized Turán numbers.

\displaystyle\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq\frac{r}{\omega}\tbinom{\omega}{r}^{\frac{1}{r}}|C_{r}(G)|^{\frac{r-1}{r}}=\frac{3}{4}\tbinom{4}{3}^{\frac{1}{3}}|C_{3}(G^{\prime})|^{\frac{2}{3}}\leq\frac{3}{4}\tbinom{4}{3}^{\frac{1}{3}}n^{\frac{2}{3}}=\frac{3}{4}(2n)^{\frac{2}{3}}.

We will show
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq\frac{3}{4}(2n)^{\frac{2}{3}}<\mu_{3}(K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1})
.
Let
g(n)=\mu_{3}(K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1})^{\frac{3}{2}}-2(\frac{3}{4})^{\frac{3}{2}}n.
By Lemma
2.23
, we have
\mu_{3}(K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1})^{\frac{3}{2}}>2\sqrt{3}(n-3)
. Then
g(n)>2\sqrt{3}(n-3)-2(\frac{3}{4})^{\frac{3}{2}}n.
We can easily get
g(n)
is monotonically increasing for
n>6
and
g(6)>0
.
Since for
n\geq 6
, we have
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})<\frac{3}{4}(2n)^{\frac{2}{3}}<\mu_{3}(K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1})
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
has maximum 3-clique spectral radius.

Case 3:
If
G^{\prime}
is
K_{4}
-free.

Since
G^{\prime}
is
2K_{3}
-free, for any
K\in C_{3}(G^{\prime})
,
G^{\prime}[V(G^{\prime})\backslash K]
is
K_{3}
-free. Without loss of generality, let
\{u,v,w\}
be a 3-clique in
G^{\prime}
,
G^{\prime\prime}=G^{\prime}[V(G^{\prime})\backslash\{u,v,w\}]
.

We claim that any
w_{1}\in V(G^{\prime\prime})
is adjacent to at most two vertices in
\{u,v,w\}
. Otherwise, if
w_{1}
is adjacent to all vertices in
\{u,v,w\}
, then
\{u,v,w,w_{1}\}
is a 4-clique in
G^{\prime}
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
is
K_{4}
-free.

If
|E(G^{\prime\prime})|=0
, we have
G^{\prime\prime}
is a union of some isolated vertices. Thus, we have
G^{\prime}
is a proper subgraph of
K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1}
and
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})<\mu_{3}(K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1})
by Corollary
2.19
, a contradiction.

Then
|E(G^{\prime\prime})|>0
. Let
k_{3}(e)
be the number of
K_{3}
in
G^{\prime}
containing
e\in E(G^{\prime\prime})
. We claim that
k_{3}(e)=1
for any
e\in E(G^{\prime})
. Otherwise, let
\{a,b\}
be an edge in
G^{\prime\prime}

with
k_{3}(\{a,b\})\geq 2
. Without loss of generality, let
u
and
v
be the two vertices in
\{u,v,w\}
such that
\{a,b,u\}
and
\{a,b,v\}
are two 3-cliques in
G^{\prime}
, then
\{a,b,u,v\}
is a 4-clique in
G^{\prime}
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
is
K_{4}
-free.

Thus, we have each edge in
G^{\prime\prime}
is contained in exactly one
3
-clique with the third vertex being one of
u,v,w
. Let

\displaystyle E_{u}

\displaystyle=\{ab\in E(G^{\prime\prime}):\{a,b,u\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\},

\displaystyle E_{v}

\displaystyle=\{ab\in E(G^{\prime\prime}):\{a,b,v\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\},

\displaystyle E_{w}

\displaystyle=\{ab\in E(G^{\prime\prime}):\{a,b,w\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\}.

and let

\displaystyle V_{uv}

\displaystyle=\{y\in V(G^{\prime\prime}):\{u,v,y\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\},

\displaystyle V_{uw}

\displaystyle=\{y\in V(G^{\prime\prime}):\{u,w,y\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\},

\displaystyle V_{vw}

\displaystyle=\{y\in V(G^{\prime\prime}):\{v,w,y\}\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\}.

Then
E(G^{\prime\prime})=E_{u}\cup E_{v}\cup E_{w}
. For each
e\in E_{u}
, we say that
e
has color
u
. Similarly for edges in
E_{v}
and
E_{w}
.

For
i=1,2
, define
S_{i}=\{T:T\text{~~is a $3$-clique in~~}G^{\prime}\text{~~and~~}|V(T)\cap\{u,v,w\}|=i\}
. Then we have

|C_{3}(G^{\prime})|=|S_{1}|+|S_{2}|+1.

Case 3.1:
There exist two edges of different colors.

Assume that
\{a,b\}\in E_{u}
and
\{c,d\}\in E_{v}
. If
\{a,b\}\cap\{c,d\}=\phi
, then
\{a,b,u\}
and
\{c,d,v\}
are two disjoint 3-clique in
G^{\prime}
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
is
2K_{3}
-free. Thus,
\{a,b\}\cap\{c,d\}=1
. Without
loss of generality, we suppose that
b=d
. Since
G^{\prime\prime}
is
K_{3}
-free,
\{a,c\}
is not an edge. For each
\{y_{1},y_{2}\}\in E(G^{\prime\prime})
, if
\{y_{1},y_{2}\}\cap\{a,b,c\}=\phi
, then the 3-clique containing
\{y_{1},y_{2}\}
is disjoint from one 3-clique of
\{a,b,u\}
and
\{c,d,v\}
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
is
2K_{3}
-free. Thus, each edge in
G^{\prime\prime}
has a vertex in
\{a,b,c\}
. Let
A=N_{G^{\prime\prime}}(a)\backslash\{b\}
,
B=N_{G^{\prime\prime}}(b)\backslash\{a,c\}
and
C=N_{G^{\prime\prime}}(c)\backslash\{b\}
. Since
G^{\prime\prime}
is
K_{3}
-free, we have
A\cap B=\phi
and
B\cap C=\phi
. Thus

|A|+|B|\leq n-6\text{~~and~~}|B|+|C|\leq n-6.

Then,

|S_{1}|=|A|+|B|+|C|+2\leq 2(n-6)+2-|B|\leq 2n-10.

Since
|V(G^{\prime\prime})|=n-3
and any
w_{1}\in V(G^{\prime\prime})
is adjacent to at most two vertices in
\{u,v,w\}
, we have

|C_{3}(G^{\prime})|=|S_{1}|+|S_{2}|+1\leq 2n-10+n-3+1=3n-12.

Since
r=3
and
\omega=3
, by Theorem
1.6
, we have

\displaystyle\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq\frac{r}{\omega}\tbinom{\omega}{r}^{\frac{1}{r}}|C_{r}(G^{\prime})|^{\frac{r-1}{r}}=|C_{3}(G^{\prime})|^{\frac{2}{3}}\leq(3n-12)^{\frac{2}{3}}.

We will show
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq(3n-12)^{\frac{2}{3}}<\mu_{3}(K_{1}\vee T_{2}(n-1))
.
Let
f(n)=(\frac{n-3}{2})^{2}-(3n-12)
, which is monotonically increasing for
n>14
and
f(14)>0
. Then for
n\geq 14
, we have
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq(3n-12)^{\frac{2}{3}}<(\frac{n-3}{2})^{\frac{4}{3}}<(\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\rfloor)^{\frac{2}{3}}=\mu_{3}(K_{1}\vee T_{2}(n-1))
. For
6\leq n\leq 13
, by direct calculation we can obtain
\mu_{3}(G^{\prime})\leq(3n-12)^{\frac{2}{3}}<(\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\rfloor)^{\frac{2}{3}}=\mu_{3}(K_{1}\vee T_{2}(n-1))
, which contradicts
G^{\prime}
has maximum 3-clique spectral radius.

Case 3.2:
Each edge in
G^{\prime\prime}
has the same color.

Without loss of generality, let

and
\{a,b\}
be an edge in

. We claim
V_{vw}=\emptyset
. Otherwise, let

. If

, which contradicts

-free. If

, which contradicts

is

-free.

, which means all the 3-cliques in

.

Similar to the proof of Claim
2
, we can obtain
G^{\prime}
is connected. Since all the 3-cliques in
G^{\prime}
share exactly one vertex
u
and

is connected, similar to the proof of Claim

, we can obtain

.

Through our discussion on Cases 1, 2 and 3, we have identified all possible extremal graphs that attain the maximum 3-clique spectral radius. Next, we compare the 3-clique spectral radius of these extremal graphs to determine which is largest.

Let
G_{1}=K_{1}\vee T_{2}(n-1)
,
G_{2}=K_{3}\vee(n-3)K_{1}
and
G_{3}=K_{5}\cup(n-5)K_{1}
.
\mu_{3}(G_{1})
and

have been given in Corollary

and Lemma

.
We can easily obtain
\mu_{3}(G_{3})=\mu_{3}(K_{5})=6
.

We claim that

(i)
For

,
\mu_{3}(G_{3})>\mu_{3}(G_{1})
and
\mu_{3}(G_{3})>\mu_{3}(G_{2})
.

(ii)
For

,
\mu_{3}(G_{2})>\mu_{3}(G_{1})
and
\mu_{3}(G_{2})>\mu_{3}(G_{3})
.

(iii)
For

,
\mu_{3}(G_{1})>\mu_{3}(G_{2})
and
\mu_{3}(G_{1})>\mu_{3}(G_{3})
.

By Lemma
2.20
,
\mu_{3}(G_{1})=(\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor\lfloor\frac{n}{2}\rfloor)^{\frac{2}{3}}
. Since
r=3
,
|C_{3}(G_{2})|=3n-8
and
\omega(G_{2})=4
, by Theorem

\displaystyle\mu_{3}(G_{2})\leq\frac{r}{\omega}\tbinom{\omega}{r}^{\frac{1}{r}}|C_{r}(G_{2})|^{\frac{r-1}{r}}=\frac{3}{4}\tbinom{4}{3}^{\frac{1}{3}}(3n-8)^{\frac{2}{3}}.

For
