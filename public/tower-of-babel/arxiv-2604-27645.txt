An Exact 56-Addition, Rank-23 Scheme for General 3 × 3 3\times 3 Matrix Multiplication
Yinqi Sun

Abstract
We present a rank- 23 23 algorithm for general 3 × 3 3\times 3 matrix multiplication that uses 56 56 additions/subtractions and 23 23 multiplications, for a total of 79 79 scalar operations in the standard bilinear straight-line model. This improves the recent sequence of 60 60 -, 59 59 -, and 58 58 -addition rank- 23 23 schemes. The algorithm works over arbitrary associative, possibly noncommutative, coefficient rings. Its tensor coefficients are ternary, meaning that every coefficient lies in { − 1 , 0 , 1 } \{-1,0,1\} . Correctness is certified by the 729 729 Brent equations over ℤ \mathbb{Z} , and the verifier also expands the straight-line program and performs additional finite-field and noncommutative implementation tests.



Keywords.
Fast matrix multiplication; bilinear algorithms; tensor rank; additive complexity.

2
Model and notation

We use the row-major indexing convention

C=AB=\begin{pmatrix}C_{0}&C_{1}&C_{2}\\
C_{3}&C_{4}&C_{5}\\
C_{6}&C_{7}&C_{8}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}A_{0}&A_{1}&A_{2}\\
A_{3}&A_{4}&A_{5}\\
A_{6}&A_{7}&A_{8}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}B_{0}&B_{1}&B_{2}\\
B_{3}&B_{4}&B_{5}\\
B_{6}&B_{7}&B_{8}\end{pmatrix}.

Thus
C_{0}=A_{0}B_{0}+A_{1}B_{3}+A_{2}B_{6}
, etc. Multiplication order is always left input form times right input form, so the construction is meaningful over noncommutative coefficient rings.

A rank-
R
bilinear algorithm is a decomposition

M_{\langle 3,3,3\rangle}=\sum_{r=0}^{R-1}U_{r}\otimes V_{r}\otimes W_{r},

where each
U_{r},V_{r},W_{r}
is a length-
9
coefficient vector. It computes

M_{r}=\Bigl(\sum_{i=0}^{8}U_{r,i}A_{i}\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_{j=0}^{8}V_{r,j}B_{j}\Bigr),\qquad C_{k}=\sum_{r=0}^{R-1}W_{r,k}M_{r}.

The displayed scheme has
R=23
and all entries of
U,V,W
are in
\{-1,0,1\}
.

The addition count is measured in the usual linear straight-line program model. Inputs are free. A gate computing
x+y
or
x-y
from previously available quantities costs one. Copies and sign changes cost zero. If a final output is assembled as a sum of
t
available atoms, it costs
t-1
additions. Products
M_{r}
are counted separately from additions.

Correctness of the factorization is equivalent to the Brent equations
[
2
]

\sum_{r=0}^{22}U_{r,(i,k)}V_{r,(k^{\prime},j)}W_{r,(i^{\prime},j^{\prime})}=\mathbf{1}[i=i^{\prime}]\mathbf{1}[j=j^{\prime}]\mathbf{1}[k=k^{\prime}]

(1)

for all
i,j,k,i^{\prime},j^{\prime},k^{\prime}\in\{0,1,2\}
. There are
3^{6}=729
equations.

3
Discovery Method

We obtained the displayed scheme from a verified ternary rank-
23
factorization of additive cost 58 due to Perminov
[
7
]
. We applied the cyclic tensor automorphism

(U,V,W)\longmapsto(V,\,W^{T},\,U^{T}),

where transposition is taken with respect to the
3\times 3
row-major indexing. This automorphism preserves the matrix-multiplication tensor while permuting the roles of the left input, right input, and output recombination factors.

After this reorientation, correctness of the bilinear decomposition is inherited from the source factorization. The remaining task is to realize the transformed linear maps with fewer additions. The transformed left factor admits a
13
-addition vector-chain schedule inherited from the original construction. We then found a
13
-addition schedule for the transformed right factor and a
30
-addition output recombination schedule, consisting of
7
shared product combinations and
23
final-assembly additions. Thus the total additive cost is
13+13+30=56
.

4
The 56-addition algorithm

The straight-line program has three stages: first form input linear forms, then multiply corresponding left and right forms, and finally assemble the output entries. All formulas below are in implementation order. The input stage forms the following
13
left-side intermediates and
13
right-side intermediates:

\begin{array}[]{rcl@{\qquad}rcl}u_{1}&=&A_{6}-A_{7}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{1}&=&B_{2}+B_{5}\\
u_{2}&=&A_{4}-u_{1}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{2}&=&B_{3}-B_{5}\\
u_{3}&=&A_{1}-u_{2}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{3}&=&B_{1}+B_{4}\\
u_{4}&=&u_{3}-A_{6}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{4}&=&B_{8}-v_{1}\\
u_{5}&=&u_{4}-A_{8}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{5}&=&B_{0}-v_{3}\\
u_{6}&=&A_{1}-u_{5}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{6}&=&B_{2}-v_{5}\\
u_{7}&=&A_{0}-u_{3}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{7}&=&B_{4}-v_{6}\\
u_{8}&=&u_{5}+A_{2}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{8}&=&B_{7}-v_{7}\\
u_{9}&=&u_{7}-A_{3}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{9}&=&B_{2}-v_{8}\\
u_{10}&=&u_{9}+A_{5}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{10}&=&B_{8}+v_{8}\\
u_{11}&=&u_{10}-A_{2}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{11}&=&v_{2}-v_{6}\\
u_{12}&=&A_{0}-A_{1}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{12}&=&B_{8}+v_{11}\\
u_{13}&=&u_{12}-u_{10}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&v_{13}&=&B_{6}-v_{12}.\end{array}

The product stage uses these intermediates together with raw input entries. A leading minus sign denotes a zero-cost sign change. Compute the
23
bilinear products:

\begin{array}[]{rcl@{\qquad}rcl}M_{0}&=&u_{6}(-v_{11})\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{12}&=&u_{7}B_{2}\\
M_{1}&=&u_{13}(-v_{7})\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{13}&=&A_{2}B_{6}\\
M_{2}&=&A_{5}v_{10}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{14}&=&A_{1}B_{3}\\
M_{3}&=&u_{8}B_{8}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{15}&=&A_{6}v_{3}\\
M_{4}&=&u_{5}(-v_{12})\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{16}&=&u_{9}v_{9}\\
M_{5}&=&A_{4}B_{5}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{17}&=&A_{0}B_{0}\\
M_{6}&=&A_{8}B_{7}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{18}&=&A_{5}B_{6}\\
M_{7}&=&(-u_{2})v_{6}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{19}&=&A_{3}B_{0}\\
M_{8}&=&A_{4}B_{3}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{20}&=&(-u_{11})B_{7}\\
M_{9}&=&u_{3}v_{5}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{21}&=&u_{1}B_{4}\\
M_{10}&=&(-u_{4})(-v_{4})\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&M_{22}&=&u_{10}(-v_{8})\\
M_{11}&=&A_{8}v_{13}.\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&&\end{array}

The output stage first extracts seven shared combinations of products:

\begin{array}[]{rcl@{\qquad}rcl}w_{1}&=&M_{4}+M_{9}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&w_{5}&=&M_{1}-M_{7}\\
w_{2}&=&M_{12}+M_{22}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&w_{6}&=&M_{10}+w_{3}\\
w_{3}&=&M_{14}+w_{1}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&w_{7}&=&M_{16}-w_{2}\\
w_{4}&=&M_{0}+M_{7}.\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&&\end{array}

Then assemble the nine output entries:

\begin{array}[]{rcl@{\qquad}rcl}C_{0}&=&M_{13}+M_{14}+M_{17}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&C_{5}&=&M_{2}+M_{5}-w_{7}\\
C_{1}&=&-M_{9}+M_{17}+M_{20}-w_{2}+w_{5}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&C_{6}&=&-M_{8}+M_{11}+M_{15}+w_{6}\\
C_{2}&=&M_{3}+M_{12}+w_{3}+w_{4}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&C_{7}&=&M_{6}+M_{15}-M_{21}\\
C_{3}&=&M_{8}+M_{18}+M_{19}\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&C_{8}&=&-M_{5}+w_{4}+w_{6}\\
C_{4}&=&M_{19}+M_{21}+w_{5}+w_{7}.\hfil\hskip 18.49988pt&&\end{array}

The final assembly costs are respectively
2,4,3,2,3,2,3,2,2
, summing to
23
. Together with the
7
shared output intermediates, the output side costs
30
additions.

7
Reproducibility

The following public GitHub repository contains our reference implementation of this algorithm and the verification code described in this paper:
https://github.com/sunyinqi0508/3by3r23-56a

The verifier reconstructs the tensor factors
U,V,W
from the printed straight-line program and checks that they match the compact ternary factor format in Appendix
6
. It then recomputes the straight-line costs
13
,
13
, and
30
, verifies all
729
Brent equations over
\mathbb{Z}
, and repeats the checks modulo
2
and
3
. The finite-field checks are redundant after the integer check, but they are useful for catching implementation errors such as sign mistakes or coordinate transpositions. The verifier also runs randomized end-to-end tests over bounded integer inputs, over
\mathbb{F}_{2}
and
\mathbb{F}_{3}
, and over a noncommutative test ring represented by random
2\times 2
integer matrices.
