1 8 MAY 1977

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD
SUBJECT: Business Council Thoughts

1. Joe Califano thinks that business can pick up a billion
dollars of the social security tab with higher payments for the
social security benefits for workers. In fact, added taxes to
business simply lead to an increase in price from these business-
men's points of view. They assume profits will stay constant -
and they probably will.

2. There is something called the Business Roundtable and
the National Association of Businessmen that also works with the
Government.

3. Russell Long talked eloquently about the "power" of the
press being such a vital determinant in many situations. Confirmed
Jim Shepley's commentary that you can't trust anybody on Capitol
Hill because they are usually beholding to the press and are scared
to death of the local press in their own area.

4. The next meeting is October 13, Thursday evening through
Saturday noon, October 15, at the Homestead. Program Chairman is
Robert Hatfield of the Continental Corporation, Inc., New York.

REMINDER MEMO

1 8 MAY 197?

1. Jerry Jasinowski, Assistant Secretary of Commerce for
Policy. At the Homestead Conference he indicated one of the four
principal goals in Commerce is to improve the use of science and
technology with specific reference to international trade.

2. This looks like a natural tie-in with some of our work,
e.g., what is foreign commerce doing in technology? Where are
we ahead or behind?
