Status of the Oil spill

The southern edge of the Mina’ al Ahmadi oil slick is within
50 kilometers of Jubayl and is expected to be south of there by
Sunday, according to the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric
Administration (NOAA). | The,US Embassy in Riyadh reports that the
slick had been slowed earlier this week by winds from the south.
There are heavy oil concentrations--with patches measuring a mile
or so long and several hundred yards wide--near As Saffaniyah,
according to NOAA.

Mitterrand Prepares France for Hard Fighting

_ In an interview broadcast today, French President Mitterrand
said that ground fighting is now inevitable and will be hard,
adding that France will neither resort to chemical or nuclear
weapons nor advocate an expansion of the war’s aims past goals
already approved by the UN Security Council. He also opined that
the region’s oil wealth should be more evenly distributed,
perhaps through some type of Middle East development bank. je |

Egyptian Paper Blasts King Hussein

An editorial in the State-owned Egyptian newspaper Al-Akhbar
condemned Jordan’s King Hussein today, saying he and other pro-
Iraq leaders share responsibility for the disaster Saddam
created.

Comment: There has been no official comment by the Egyptian
government on King Hussein’s speech yesterday.
