1. [Text] San Salvador, 6 Dec (AFP)--The intensification of the
civil war in the last month in El Salvador has led to a ‘‘dramatic
worsening’’ of human rights violations in this Central American
nation, that in the past 10 years has had one of the saddest records:
in this area, Catholic Church sources said in San Salvador today.

2. The Legal Affairs Office of the San Salvador Archbishopric said
that the situation ‘‘is so serious that the number of charges’’ filed
on serious human rights violations and reported daily to this church
organization cannot be counted.

3. The level of violence against Salvadoran citizens was clearly
demonstrated with the massacre of six Jesuit priests, including
Ignacio Ellacuria, the rector of the Central American University
(UCA), allegedly perpetrated by death squads.

4. ‘‘The situation has completely changed our possibilities for
exercising some control over the violations,’’ Lawyer Maria Julia
Hernandez, director of the Legal Affairs Office of the Archbishopric,
told AFP.

5. The official said that almost a month after the Farabundo Marti
National Liberation Front [FMLN] launched its guerrilla offensive in
the capital, ‘‘no one, not even the government, knows the number of
guerrillas and soldiers killed in the clashes.’’

6. ‘‘We have also confirmed the death of another youth, who was
beaten so much at the National Police headquarters he had to be taken
to a hospital where he died,’’ the official said.

7. ‘‘There have been more cases like these, but we have not been
able to keep up with all the cases reported,’’ said Lopez [as
received], who also said that ‘‘not everyone reports the arrest,
disappearance, or death of their relatives for fear of repression.’

8. ‘‘We are currently living in a climate of fear, the people are
afraid they will be arrested, reported, persecuted, or confused with
someone else. They only report a case when it is a life or death
