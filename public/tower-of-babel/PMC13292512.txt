Published: 2026

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors found out that Fig. 1 caption is incomplete. Some texts were missing and unintentionally not captured during production process.

Current Fig. 1 caption:

F at concentrations of 1.5 and 3 mL L− 1, Fo2 and Fo4: Foliar application at concentrations of 2 and 4 mL L− 1.

Correct Fig. 1 caption:

Treated and non-treated sage plants with VPHs under different NaCl concentrations. Fe1.5 and Fe3: Fertigation at concentrations of 1.5 and 3 mL L⁻¹, respectively; Fo2 and Fo4: Foliar application at concentrations of 2 and 4 mL L⁻¹, respectively; Control: plants not treated with VPHs.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
