UN Representative Says Thousands Missing

Only 8,000 Kuwaiti prisoners in Iraq have been registered
with the International Red Cross out of a total of 32,000
reported missing since the 2 August invasion, according to the
Kuwaiti Permanent Representative to the UN. Kuwait is demanding
that Baghdad furnish complete information about the missing
Kuwaitis before signing any permanent agreement on a ceasefire.
The UN Representative also indicated that it would be necessary
to station international forces in Kuwait for some time. ae |
