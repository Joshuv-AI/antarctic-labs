 Measurement of the Emi ance for the CeC Project
 I. Pinayev

Coherent Electron Cooling (CeC) experiment was carried out at RHIC [1]. It required small
emi ance of 10-15 MeV electron beam with high peak current. In most cases the emi ance was
measured by either quadrupole or solenoid scan. For a low-energy beam with high peak current
the measurements are aﬀected by space charge forces. To overcome these diﬃcul es the
pepper-pot [2,3] or slit scan [4] can be used.

Not only small emi ance is required for CeC based on the plasma-cascade instability but the
beam uniformity in the core. The slice emi ance can be measured with slit when the beam is
streaked with transverse deﬂec ng cavity [5, 6]. Quadrupole scan can be u lized for this
purpose as well [7].

Experimental Set Up

The general descrip on of the CeC diagnos cs can be found in [8]. The low energy beam from

to the ﬁnal energy. The transverse focusing in LEBT is provided by six solenoids which have

distance between the pepper pot and the proﬁle monitor is 0.34 meters. Between the gun and
LEBT1 solenoids the integra ng current transformer is placed. The cameras and other
diagnos cs equipment are triggered from the common ming system. The trigger delays can be
individually adjusted.

Slit Scan

To suppress the eﬀects of the space charge on the emi ance measurement we u lized the slits
installed in the diagnos cs line. The beam is moved across the slit, and image is observed on a
downstream proﬁle monitor. The angular distribu on is es mated from the spa al distribu on
observed on the screen, and the transverse beam size at the loca on of the slit is found from
the dependence of image intensity from the beam posi on on the slit.

In our experiment we u lize the ﬁxed slits, and we are scanning the beam with the help of the
calibrated trims. The calibra on is performed by observing the beam mo on on the screen
when slits are retracted.

Beamlet intensity 𝑤 is es mated from the image brightness. The angle 𝛼 is found from its
center of gravity on the proﬁle monitor, and uncorrelated divergence 𝜎 is calculated from the
beamlet width.

Operator can choose the slit, set the range of the trim current and number of spots. The
applica on displays found beam size and divergence and calculated emi ance. To reduce the
systema c errors the electron beam should be properly focused on the slit. Usually, the last two
quadrupoles Q3 and Q4 in the diagnos cs beamline are used. For the horizontal emi ance Q3 is
defocusing and Q4 is focusing to obtain small horizontal beam size. For the ver cal emi ance
the Q3 is focusing and Q4 is defocusing. Usually, one focuses beam on the screen with slits
being retracted and slightly increases the Q4 strength.

The lower limit for the size is deﬁned by the slit width of 100 microns. It should not be too large
either. The large beam size provides for small uncorrelated divergence, which should exceed the
slit width divided by the distance to the slit.

Slice Emi ance Measurement with Slit

The slice emi ance is measured with help of the deﬂec ng cavity. Since the beam is spread
ver cally, only horizontal slice emi ance can be measured. The measurement set-up includes
centering of the beam with the quadrupole Q4, phasing of the deﬂec ng cavity, and centering
of the beam on the proﬁle monitor. The centering is needed since selec on of the slice is
realized with change of the region of interest. It is modiﬁed to a narrow horizontal band. The
beam focusing is performed in the manner previously described for the slit scan. The scan of the
beam across the slit is performed with the calibrated horizontal trim.

Time scan is performed by changing the deﬂec ng cavity phase. In such approach. calibra on of
the deﬂec ng cavity is not required. Beamlet intensity 𝑤 is es mated from the image
brightness inside of the region of interest. The beamlet angle 𝛼 is found from its center of
gravity of the image spot on the proﬁle monitor and uncorrelated divergence 𝜎 from the
beamlet width. The measured beam size is corrected for the ﬁnal resolu on of the system
deﬁned by point spread func on of the op cal system and slit width. The result of the scan is
shown in Fig. 11.

 Figure 11: Snapshot of the applica on for the slice emi ance measurement.

The slice is deﬁned by region of interest on the proﬁle monitor. The loca on of the ROI is ﬁxed
during the measurement, and temporal scan is done by changing the phase of deﬂec ng cavity.
Such approach does not require knowledge of the cavity voltage and calibra on of deﬂec ng
angle. The calibra on, however, is used for se ng the ver cal size of ROI.

The emi ance of the whole bunch is calculated from the slices data. The weight of each slice is
sum of the beamlet intensi es. The posi on and angle of each slice is used with weighted
average. Uncorrelated angular divergence is used from the slice data. Emi ance calcula on is
based on equa ons (15-18). The emi ance of the whole beam is displayed upon comple on of
the last slice measurement.
