'''Adopted without a vote by the Security Council at its 1776th meeting, on 10 June 1974'''

''The Security Council''

''Having examined'' the application of the People's Republic of Bangladesh for admission to membership in the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the People's Republic of Bangladesh be admitted to membership in the United Nations
