({TEXT)) ...DETERMINED WITHIN THE ARMED FORCES, PEOPLE WHO SEE
WORK INTENDING TO HELP THE POOR AS A SUBVERSIVE ACTION AND THEREFORE,
AS SOMETHING AGAINST THE NATIONAL SECURITY, SAID SAN SALVADOR
AUXILIARY BISHOP MONSIGNOR GREGORIO ROSA CHAVEZ CONCERNING THE NEED
TO CONDUCT FURTHER INVESTIGATIONS INTO THE CASE OF THE ASSASSINATED
JESUIT PRIESTS. THE REST IS NOW IN THE HANDS OF THE JUSTICE
TRIBUNALS WHO WILL HAVE IN THEIR HANDS AN OPPORTINITY TO GIVE
CREDIBILITY TO THOSE STATE INSTITUTIONS, HE ADDED.

((ROSA CHAVEZ)) “WHEN CRISTIANI ANNOUNCED THAT THE MILITARY
WAS RESPONSIBLE, THE QUESTION WAS WHETHER OR NOT HE WOULD DISCLOSE
THE NAMES OF THE PRIMARY PEOPLE INVOLVED. THERE IS NOW A NEW
QUESTION BECAUSE WE DO NOT BELIEVE THESE ARE ISOLATED ACTIONS
COMMITTED BY PEOPLE WHO DEVIATE FROM THE PARAMETERS OF THE
INSTITUTION. RATHER, THIS RESPONDS TO A WAY OF THINKING, TO AN
UPBRINGING, WHICH VIEWS WORK DONE BY PEOPLE LIKE THOSE WHO WERE
ASSSASINATED, THE WORK DONE BEING DONE BY THE CHURCH, WHICH IS VERY

COMMITTED TO PEOPLE'S PROBLEMS, AND THE HUMAN RIGHTS WORKS AS
SOMETHING SUBVERSIVE, AND AGAINST THE STATE SECURITY. THEREFORE,

IN THE FORMATION PROCESS WE ARE SEEN AS ENEMIES. I BELIEVE THAT
PUNISHING A FEW IS NOT SUFFICIENT. IT IS ALSO NECESSARY TO
THOROUGHLY REVIEW THE CRITERIA, TO DETERMINE WHO THE TRUE ENEMIES OF
THE PEOPLE AND COUNTRY ARE. I BELIEVE THERE IS A LOT TO DISCUSS IN
THIS REGARD. IT IT VERY IMPORTANT TO REVIEW THE PATTERNS OF
FORMATION. THIS IS WHAT WE HAVE PROPOSED. NEXT WEEK WE WILL SEE THE
ATTITUDE OF THE MILITARY TOWARD THE CLERGY. THIS WILL BE A TEST FOR
OUR JUDICIAL SYSTEM. DURING THE HOMILY, I SAID THAT THERE HAS

BEEN MUCH TALK ABOUT THE NEED FOR RESPECTING LAWS. HOWEVER, EVIDENCE
SHOWS THAT IMPUNITY PREVAILS IN THE COUNTRY. THEREFORE, THERE ARE
TWO THINGS THAT WILL BE CLARIFIED DURING THE TRIAL THAT WILL BEGIN
AGAINST THE PEOPLE INVOLVED IN THE ASSASSINATION OF THE JESUIT
PRIESTS." :

THE DIALOGUE BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT AND THE FMLN GUERRILLAS
CONTINUES TO BE THE CHURCH'S MAIN EFFORT IN ATTEMPTING TO SOLVE THE
CONFLICT THROUGH POLITICAL MEANS. THE CHURCH WILL PLAY ITS ROLE IN
THIS RESPECT, EVEN THOUGH THERE ARE PLANS NOW TO HAVE UN SECRETARY
GENERAL JAVIER PEREZ DE CUELLAR JOIN EFFORTS IN RESUMING THE
DIALOGUE. THIS SITUATION POSES TWO ALTERNATIVES: DIALOGUE WITH
UNITED NATION'S MEDIATION OR THE MILITARY SOLUTION REACTIVATED WITH
POLITICAL MURDERS SUCH AS THE ASSASSINATION OF DR HECTOR OQUELI
COLINDRES, ACCORDING TO THE RELIGIOUS SECTOR IN THE COUNTRY.

((ROSA CHAVEZ)) "THERE ARE TWO ALTERNATIVES: THE PATH OF A
POLITICAL SOLUTION WHICH COULD BE RESUMED IF THE MEDIATION EFFORTS BY
DR PEREZ DE CUELLAR ARE SUCCESSFUL; AND THE PATH OF TOTAL MADNESS
WHICH IS PROMOTED WITH ASSASSINATIONS LIKE THE KILLING OF DR OQUELI
COLINDRES. THE ASSASSINATION OF OQUELI MAKES THE POLITICAL SOLUTION
MORE DIFFICULT. HOWEVER, SOLVING THE DIFFICULTIES WILL DEPEND ON THE
WISDOM OF ALL PEOPLE RESPONSIBLE IN OUR COUNTRY."

DURING THE CELEBRATION OF RELIGIOUS ACTIVITIES, AND DURING THE
TRADITIONAL PART OF THE HOMILY ON EVENTS THIS WEEK, ROSA CHAVEZ
MENTIONED THAT IT IS VERY POSSIBLE THAT THE VIOLENT EVENTS IN THE
PAST FEW DAYS COULD HELP TO ATTAIN PEACE IN EL SALVADOR.

((ROSA CHAVEZ)) "WE ASK PEOPLE TO HONOR AGAIN THE SACRED RIGHT TO
LIVE, TO ALL LIVES, REGARDLESS OF POLITICAL OR IDEOLOGICAL
DIFFERENCES. “IN FRONT OF THE CORPSE OF A MAN ((OQUELI -- FBIS)) WHO
PARTICIPATED IN SEVERAL ROUNDS OF DIALOGUE, WE SHOULD RENEW THE
UNYIELDING COMMITMENT TO SET ASIDE VIOLENCE AND PROMOTE A POLITICAL
SOLUTION TO THE COMPLEX AND DESPERATE ARMED CONFRONTATION DESTROYING
US EACH DAY. PARADOXICALLY, THE MOMENT WE ARE LIVING, SO FULL OF BAD
NEWS, COULD BE CRUCUIAL FOR PEACE."

NEXT WEEK, SALVADORAN BISHOPS WHO ARE MEMBERS OF THE SALVADORAN
EPISCOPAL CONFERENCE ({CEDES)) WILL HOLD A MEETING IN SAN SALVADOR,
EVEN THOUGH A LOT HAS BEEN SAID IN THE PAST FEW MONTHS RECARDING
DISAGREEMENTS EXISTING WITHIN THE CHURCH OVER THE SOCIAL-RELIGIOUS
WORK IT CARRIES OUT. FOR MSGR ROSA CHAVEZ, THIS MEETING WILL SERVE
TO DETERMINE, WITH THESE OBJECTIVES IN MIND, WHAT THE WORK OF CEDES
THROUGHOUT THE COUNTRY IN HELPING THE NEEDY SHOULD BE.
