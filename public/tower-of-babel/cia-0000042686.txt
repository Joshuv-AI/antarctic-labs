1. [Text] San Salvador, 16 Nov (ACAN-EFE) -- The opposition Social
Christian People’s Movement (MPSC) and the National Revolutionary
Movement (MNR) today condemned the assassination of six Jesuits in ~-
San Salvador, and held the government and the Armed Forces
responsible for this crime.

-

2. The MPSC requested the international community and the diplomatic
corps accredited in San Salvador to make ‘‘good offices before the
Salvadoran Government and Armed Forces’’ so they ‘‘adopt realistic
positions and agree to an immediate negotiation of a cease-fire with
the guerrillas.’’

3. The Social Christians stressed that the bloodshed of civilians
must cease and that it is necessary to create as soon as possible the
necessary conditions to bring peace to El Salvador.

4. The MNR said the assassination of the Jesuits--five Spaniards and
a Salvadoran--‘‘seeks to silence and subdue with terror the work of
the Central American University,’’ whose rector was Ignacio
Ellacuria. ‘‘This is not the first time that the Central American
University and its most outstanding officials are the object of
accusations and verbal and physical aggression by ultrarightist
sectors,’’ the social democratic MNR noted.

5. The MNR is ‘‘concerned and surprised by the irresponsible and
Suspicious conduct of the Salvadoran Government and the Armed Forces’
High Command of holding the guerrillas responsible for the
assassinations, which gives carte blanche to the death squads and to
the mass terror of 1980, 1981, and 1982.'"'

6. The highest MNR and MPSC leaders, Guillermo Ungo and Ruben Zamora
respectively, sought refuge and are at the Venezuelan and Mexican
Embassies accredited in San Salvador.

7. The government and the Armed Forces condemned the assassination
of the Jesuits. Moreover, President Alredo Cristiani said that if it
is proven that Armed Forces members are responsible for the crime,
