Polish physician; flourished at Kalisz in the second half of the seventeenth century. He wrote in Yiddish two medical works: (1) "Yerushat Mosheh" (Frankfort-on-the-Main, 1677); and (2) "Yarim Mosheh" ( ib. and Amsterdam, 1679), in continuation of the preceding work.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 6436; Serapeum, ix. 348; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. ii. 164; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 230, Wilna. 1880. H. R. A. S. W.
