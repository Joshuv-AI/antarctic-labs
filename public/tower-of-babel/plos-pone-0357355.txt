Correction: Characterization of an AGAMOUS-like MADS Box Protein, a Probable Constituent of Flowering and Fruit Ripening Regulatory System in Banana

Swarup Roy Choudhury, Sujit Roy, Anish Nag, Sanjay Kumar Singh, Dibyendu N Sengupta

After this article [ 1 ] was published, concerns were raised regarding Fig 6. Specifically, panels I-IV in Fig 6F appear similar to panels I-IV in Fig 6G. The corresponding author stated that panels I-IV in Fig 6F are incorrect and provided a corrected version of Figure 6 including replacement image panels for 6F that were acquired in the original experiment. The available underlying images for all panels of Figs 6F and 6G have been included with this notice as S1 File .
