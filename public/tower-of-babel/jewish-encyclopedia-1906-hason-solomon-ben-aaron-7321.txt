Turkish rabbi of the sixteenth century. Of his works the following are known: "Bet Shelomoh," responsa, at the end of which are added some responsa by R. Daniel Estorza (Salonica, 1720); and "Mishpaṭim Yesharim," containing responsa by him and by R. Samuel Gaon, in two parts ( ib. 1832).

Ḥason is mentioned in the responsa of Samuel de Modena (part i., No. 43) and in the "Torat Emet" of Aaron Sason.

Bibliography: Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, pt. i., p. 164; pt. ii., pp. 17, 88; Conforte, Ḳore ha-Dorot, pp. 37a, 39a, 43a, Berlin, 1846. K. N. T. L.
