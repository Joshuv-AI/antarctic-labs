— 1. THE POLICY AMONG HIGH-LEVEL CUBAN GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS, AT LEAST
UNTIL MID-DECEMBER 1963, WAS TO INSURE THAT NO ACTION BE TAKEN
AGAINST THE GUANTANAMO NAVAL BASE WHICH WOULD PROVOKE MILITARY
REACTION BY THE UNITED STATES. RAUL CASTRO, IN PARTICULAR,
GAVE EXPLICIT INSTRUCTION TO THE MILITARY TO AVOID ANY INCIDENTS.
2. FIDEL CASTRO'S RECENT PRECIPITANT ACTION IN CUTTING OFF
THE WATER SUPPLY TO THE BASE CAUSED SOME CONCERN AMONGST CUBAN
OFFICIALS ABROAD WHO FEARED THE ACTION MIGHT CAUSE U.S, MILITARY
RETALIATION. THIS CONCERN QUICKLY DISSOLVED WHEN IT BECAME EVIDENT 1.5(c)
