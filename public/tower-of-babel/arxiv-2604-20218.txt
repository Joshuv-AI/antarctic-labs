On the I ⁡ ( 1 ) I(1) -invariants: Non-abelian Hecke algebra case
Anand Chitrao, Arindam Jana, Asfak Soneji

Abstract
. Let F F be a finite extension of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} . The so-called supersingular representations are the basic building blocks in the theory of mod p p representations of GL 2 ​ ( F ) {\rm GL}_{2}(F) . The space of pro- p p -Iwahori invariants of a universal module played a crucial role in the construction of the supersingular representations of GL 2 ​ ( ℚ p ) {\rm GL}_{2}(\mathbb{Q}_{p}) . In this paper, we give an explicit description of the pro- p p -Iwahori invariants of the universal module π r \pi_{r} for r = 0 , q − 1 r=0,q-1 using the Iwahori-Hecke model. We also determine the action of the pro- p p -Iwahori-Hecke algebra on these newly found invariants. As an application, we recover π r \pi_{r} functorially from its space of I ⁡ ( 1 ) I(1) -invariants and extend a theorem of Ollivier for any totally ramified extension of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} other than itself.

Introduction

The study of mod
p
representations of
G
began with the pioneering works of Barthel and Livné in
[
4
,
5
]
. The authors classified the smooth irreducible representations of

with a central character over characteristic

where
\sigma
is a smooth irreducible representation of

with the action extended to

by declaring that

acts trivially.

The first three were realized as explicit quotients of
\mathrm{ind}_{KZ}^{G}\>\sigma_{r}/{\rm{Im}\>}(T-\lambda)
for some

and
\lambda\in\overline{\mathbb{F}}_{p}^{\times}
. Here

is the standard spherical Hecke operator defined in
[
4
, Section 3.1]
.

The supersingular representations, however, were only realized as non-explicit irreducible quotients of the universal module

\pi_{r}=\frac{\mathrm{ind}_{KZ}^{G}\>\sigma_{r}}{{\rm{Im}\>}T}.

The finding of an explicit supersingular quotient seems to be a much harder question, although some abstract constructions are available in
[

18

14

is two-dimensional. We note in passing that the subspace of

[

The space of

-invariants of

for

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}1+\varpi a&0\\

Lemma 5.2
.

For

, the map

\displaystyle a^{r-s}d^{s}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle\mathrm{ind}_{I(1)Z}^{IZ}\>\eta^{r}

\displaystyle v

\displaystyle\mapsto

\displaystyle-\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{\times}}\lambda^{-s}\left[\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\

Proof.

We only need to show that

acts via

on the element

Indeed, using the identity

\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\
\varpi c&d\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\

\frac{c\varpi}{[d\lambda/a]}&\frac{d}{[d/a]}\end{pmatrix},

we see that

The transformation

then yields

\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\
\varpi c&d\end{pmatrix}f=a^{r-s}d^{s}f.

For the final claim, we note that both sides are

dimensional and that the map is surjective since
