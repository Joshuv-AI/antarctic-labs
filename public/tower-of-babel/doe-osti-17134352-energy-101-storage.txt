 Storage: Physical Characterization
Technology Characterization (Different technologies are best suited for different applications & scales)
• Energy Density [volumetric, mass]: watts-hours/liter, watt-hours/kg
• Power Density [volumetric, mass]: watts/liter, watts/kg
• Round trip efficiency: Ratio of energy out to energy in
• Duration / Discharge rate
• Self-discharge rate
• Scale: At what size range(s) does this technology make sense?

System Characterization
• Energy capacity (kWh, MWh), Akin to the size of the fuel tank for a fossil-fuel generator
• Power capacity (kW, MW), Akin to the peak output of a fossil-fuel generator—the maximum power the system can provide.
  - Discharge duration = Energy capacity/power capacity:
  - For example, a 1 MW/2 MWh [1 MW/2-hr] BESS can provide 1 MW of power for 2 hours or 0.5 MW of power for 4 hours
• Overall size & mass
• State-of-charge limits
• Cycle life (Maximum cumulative energy throughput)

 Lithium-ion (Li-ion) Batteries

– Newer technology than lead-acid batteries
– Popular because they have high energy and power density,
 hold their charge, and can handle thousands of
 charge/discharge cycles
– Requires a ‘Battery Management System,’—battery package
 often includes the inverter & protection systems
– Most common types of Li-ion batteries:
 o Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) [primary for grid storage]

 o Lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide (NMC)
 o Primarily for EV’s but EV’s moving to LFP also

 BESS: Safety

Potential Risks
• Though rare, thermal runaway (fire and explosion) hazards can occur
 o ~1 failure per installed GW (~12 safety events worldwide during the period 2017 - 2022) (ACP 2023)
 o “Between 2018 and 2023, the global grid-scale BESS failure rate has dropped 97%.” (ERPI 2024)
 o LFP chemistry less likely to have fires than NMC

Risk Mitigation
• (EPA 2025) provides BESS installation and incident response considerations as well as links to various state level BESS
  safety resources.

Safety Measures for Installation (EPRI 2023)
• WHAT: Certified equipment, reputable manufacturers
• WHO: Licensed electricians, certified by the ESS manufacturer
• HOW: Follow manufacturer’s instructions, locally adopted building codes and standards
• WHERE: Outdoors, indoor in a detached garage, indoor in an attached garage, provide clearance

 BESS: Behind the Meter Use Cases

 Use Case Description Notes
“Whole House” BESS backs up all loads in the facility • Most costly solution
 • Most comprehensive solution.
 • Provides both resilience and potential electricity bill savings
 • Connected to facility wiring ➔ Installation requires an electrician
 • NOTE: Some EV models are configured for “whole house backup.
 Requires a bi-directional charger.
“Critical Circuits” BESS backs up selected critical circuits • Lower cost than “whole” house solution but also less comprehensive
 • Limitation is due to less energy storage capacity and/or limited
 number of circuits that can be served
 • Provides both resilience and potential electricity bill savings
 • Connected to facility wiring ➔ Installation requires an electrician
“Portable Power” BESS directly powers a very limited • Lowest cost
 number of appliances. • Mainly provides resilience and some load shifting
 • Appliances to be powered are plugged directly into the system ➔ No
 electrician needed
 • This is a fast-moving market.

 BESS: Recycling (Li-Ion)

Overview (IEA 2025)
• Recycling rates vary by geography, market segment, & battery sub-chemistry
• EV Batteries
 ◦ Tend to use NMC/NCA (nickel-manganese-cobalt / nickel-cobalt-aluminum) sub-chemistries
 ◦ Most profitable (and thus likely to be recycled) due to the value of cobalt and nickel.
 ◦ Potential for “second-life” use as stationary batteries
• Stationary Batteries
 ◦ Tend to use LFP (lithium-iron-phosphate) sub-chemistry
 ◦ Harder to (profitably) recycle due to lack of high-value materials
• Bulk of the world’s lithium-ion battery recycling capacity is outside of the U.S.
• Development of a lithium-ion battery recycling infrastructure in the U.S. remains a work in progress.

➔ Good News: Decent recycling infrastructure is likely by the late 2030s; when batteries purchased now will need it.

Storage Sizing

 [BTM] Storage System Sizing

Notes on Battery Lifetime.
• Calendar [float] Life: Maximum lifespan regardless of how little the battery is used.
• Energy throughput: Maximum cumulative energy that can be cycled through the battery. (This can be
  estimated from the manufacturer spec sheet)
  - Typically, with PV, the batteries aren’t cycled more than once per day

Power: Does the maximum discharge rate [power capacity] (kW) meet the power requirement?

Energy:
• For behind the meter (BTM) systems, a useful metric is the length of time the battery can meet the
  average load, “hours of autonomy”.
• Example: A battery with 10 kWh of storage capacity attached to a facility with an average load of 2
  kW, could provide 5 hours of autonomy.

                                                                                      National Laboratory of the Rockies   |   32

 [BTM] Storage System Sizing

General Rules of Thumb (to use as a starting point for further analysis)
• Additional increments of storage generally have decreasing marginal utility. [I.e., an
  additional increment of storage is typically less useful than the preceding increments.]
  - Batteries are typically best used for frequent, short outages
  - Infrequent, long outages may be more economically served with a generator
• Residential (off-grid): up to several days
• Residential (grid connected): up to a day or so
• Large commercial: up to a few hours

 Storage: Recap

Multiple Technology Types
• Mechanical: Pumped hydro
• Thermal: Ice
• Electrochemical: Li-Ion batteries, Lead-acid batteries
• Electrical: Capacitors

Characterization (Different technologies are best suited for different applications)
• Energy Density
• Power Density
• Duration / Discharge rate
• Round trip efficiency

Facility Size: Distributed to utility scale

• Rapidly decreasing Li-ion BESS costs are leading to rapidly accelerating deployment at all scales
• Development work continues on other technologies
