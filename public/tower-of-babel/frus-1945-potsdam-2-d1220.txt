No. 1220 The Ambassador in China (Hurley) to the Secretary of State

top secret

Chungking, 29 July 1945.

Cfb 2087. Reference to your message of 28 July for Doctor T. V. Soong[1] the following is Soong’s reply to you: “When I left Moscow I had distinct understanding with M. Molotov that he will keep me informed of Marshal Stalin’s movements so that I could reach Moscow soon after he returns.”

Hurley

Source: 761.93/7–2945: Telegram

Notes
[1] Document No. 1219.
