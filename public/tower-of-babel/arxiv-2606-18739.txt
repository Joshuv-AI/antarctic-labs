ASEP/DSSYK duality and strange correlator
Kazumi Okuyama

Abstract
We show that the moment of the transfer matrix of the double scaled SYK model is written as an overlap between the stationary state of ASEP (asymmetric simple exclusion process) and a product state. We argue that this overlap is an analogue of the strange correlator appearing in the correspondence between the Levin-Wen string-net model and the Turaev-Viro state sum.

ASEP and spin chain

4
Discussion

We have seen that there is an interesting similarity between
the ASEP/DSSYK duality in section
2

and the Turaev-Viro/string-net correspondence
in section
3
, as summarized in Table
1
.
There are many interesting open questions about this analogy,
which deserve further study.
We will discuss some of them below.
Also, based on this analogy, we expect that we can draw some lessons
for the holography of 3d gravity from the knowledge
of how the holography works in the lower dimensional case
of DSSYK.

Gapped bulk
H
and boundary CFT

Usually, the spectrum of the Levin-Wen Hamiltonian
H

is assumed to be gapped, which makes the ground space
H|\Psi\rangle=0

of
H
topologically protected.
This protected property allows us to use the string-net state in
a fault tolerant quantum computation, known as the
Turaev-Viro codes
Koenig:2010uua
;
Bonesteel:2012pkv
;
Schotte:2020lnz
.
It would be interesting to understand the relation between the Turaev-Viro codes
and the holographic error correcting codes

Almheiri:2014lwa
;
Pastawski:2015qua
, if any.

We should stress that the gapped spectrum of
H
does not contradict
the AdS/CFT correspondence, where the boundary theory is described by a gapless
CFT. As shown in Figure
1
,

H
and the transfer matrix
T
of the boundary theory
are different symmetry generators of the bulk geometry
\Sigma\times I
:

H
generates the translation along
I
while
T
generates the translation along
\Sigma
.
Since
T\neq H
,
it is completely consistent if the transfer matrix
T
of
the boundary theory is gapless while the
spectrum of the bulk
H
is gapped.

Off-critical holography

In the AdS/CFT correspondence, we usually assume that
the boundary theory is a critical theory described by a
conformal field theory.
However, the holographic duality is a more broader concept which
might work even in the off-critical regime.
In fact, the holography of DSSYK is an example of this type of
off-critical duality.
If we take the double-scaling limit near the spectral edge

E(\theta)=-E_{0}
of DSSYK, we find the near
\text{AdS}_{2}/\text{CFT}_{1}

holography between the JT gravity and the Schwarzian theory

Maldacena:2016upp
, or its
higher genus generalization of the double-scaled matrix model
of Saad-Shenker-Stanford
Saad:2019lba
.
It is argued in
Blommaert:2024ydx
;
Blommaert:2024whf

that the holographic dual of DSSYK is
given by the sine dilaton gravity, and this duality works even
away from the spectral edge

E(\theta)=-E_{0}
and the sine dilaton gravity is dual to DSSYK
including the whole spectrum
-E_{0}\leq E(\theta)\leq E_{0}
.
In other words, the holography between the sine dilaton gravity
and DSSYK works without taking a critical limit
(or the double-scaling limit of the matrix model).

This is similar to the situation of the holography between 2d lattice
statistical model and 3d Turaev-Viro state sum in
(
51
).
This duality (
51
) is an exact relation
between
Z_{\text{2d}}(\Sigma)
and
Z_{\text{TV}}(\Sigma\times I)

which is valid even away from the critical CFT limit of

Z_{\text{2d}}(\Sigma)
.
There are many example of 2d lattice models which flow to
CFT in a certain critical limit.
For instance, the Andrew-Baxter-Forester model

Andrews:1984af
corresponds to the
(p,p+1)
unitary minimal
model CFT in the critical limit.
However,
Z_{\text{2d}}(\Sigma)
and
Z_{\text{TV}}(\Sigma\times I)

in (
51
)
are written in terms of the data of fusion category which
make sense even away from the critical regime.
For instance, the partition function of the Ising model
was studied extensively in
Aasen:2016dop

from the viewpoint of fusion category without taking the critical
CFT limit. We expect that the relation
Z=\langle\Omega|\Psi\rangle

for the ASEP/DSSYK (
37
) and
the Turaev-Viro/string-net (
50
)
can be applied to a more general case of holography even
in the off-critical regime of the boundary theory.

Since we are not taking the continuum limit of boundary theory, the
bulk geometry has some built-in discreteness.
In the case of DSSYK, the chord number
n

in (
19
) corresponds to the
bulk geodesic length, which becomes quantized in units of
\lambda
in
(
4
)
Berkooz:2018jqr
;
Lin:2022rbf
.
Thus,
\lambda
can be thought of as the Planck length of the
bulk quantum gravity of DSSYK.
It would be interesting to understand the Planck scale structure
of the Turaev-Viro/string-net
correspondence and see how the continuous bulk geometry appears in the critical
limit of the 2d boundary theory on
\Sigma
.

Branes and defects

The stationary state
|\Psi\rangle
of the ASEP in (
28
)
is written as a MPS with the boundary conditions

\langle W|
and
|V\rangle
.
As discussed in
Okuyama:2023byh
, these boundary states
correspond to the end-of-the-world branes in the bulk dual
of DSSYK. In the case of Vuraev-Viro state sum, we can also introduce boundaries
on the two-dimensional surface
\Sigma
. Then the corresponding 3d bulk theory is
described by an extended TQFT
balsam2012
.
It would be interesting to understand the branes in the bulk 3d geometry

\Sigma\times I
in this case of Turaev-Viro/string-net correspondence
with boundaries.

We can also introduce defects in the Turaev-Viro/string-net correspondence.
Then the partition function becomes

\displaystyle Z_{\text{defect}}=\langle\mathcal{D}|\Psi\rangle,

(52)

where
|\mathcal{D}\rangle
is obtained by acting some defect creation operator

\mathcal{D}_{\varphi}
on the product state
|\Omega\rangle
in (
49
).
As discussed in
Aasen:2020jwb
, the
defect creation operator

\mathcal{D}_{\varphi}
commutes with the transfer matrix
T
of the 2d lattice model

\displaystyle=0.

(53)

This is
similar to the relation (
22
)
for the bi-local matter operator
\mathcal{D}_{\beta,\Delta}
in DSSYK.
It would be interesting to better understand the relation between
the defect creation operator in Turaev-Viro/string-net and the bi-local
matter operator in ASEP/DSSYK.

Sum over bulk topologies

In the holography of ASEP/DSSYK and
Turaev-Viro/string-net, the bulk geometries have a fixed topology:
the bulk dual of DSSYK is a disk while the bulk
geometry of Turaev-Viro/string-net is
\Sigma\times I
.
On general grounds, we expect that the sum over topologies in the bulk
arises if we consider some averaging on the boundary theory side.
In fact, in the case of DSSYK one can introduce the so-called ETH matrix model

Jafferis:2022wez
by replacing the transfer matrix
T
with a
random matrix
M
.
Then the moment
\langle 0|T^{k}|0\rangle
of the transfer matrix
T

is replaced by the average of
\Tr M^{k}
in the
N\times N
random matrix ensemble

\displaystyle\langle\Tr M^{k}\rangle=\frac{\int dM\Tr M^{k}e^{-N\Tr V(M)}}{\int dMe^{-N\Tr V(M)}},

(54)

where the potential is expanded in terms of the Chebyshev polynomials
