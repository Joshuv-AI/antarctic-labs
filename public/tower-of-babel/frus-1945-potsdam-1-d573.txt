No. 573 The Ambassador in Italy (Kirk) to the Acting Secretary of State

[Extract]

top secret

Caserta, June 27, 1945—midnight.

2794. …

. . . . . . .

When we asked SAC his view as to Yugo reservation on art 3 of Belg[rade] Agreement he expressed concurrence with our view that on this point [we] must stand. He then commented: “Please do not worry about civil admin in our area. As you know the Bel Agreement gives me final author in the matter and I shall see to it that the interpretation is such as to represent our views”.

. . . . . . .

Kirk
