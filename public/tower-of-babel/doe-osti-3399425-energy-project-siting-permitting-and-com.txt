 Ground-Mount Site General Considerations

Project design
• Site security and road access, circulation
• Lot coverage
• Soil conditions, topography, and grading
• Hazards & mitigation: brownfields, erosion,
  earthquake fault lines, stormwater and flooding
• Climatic conditions
• Location of proposed equipment including meters,
  AC/DC disconnects, inverters

Project impacts
• Environmental and cultural concerns (endangered
  species, habitat/open space/prime agricultural loss,
  viewsheds, etc.)

Community and Large-Scale Energy Projects Interconnection

  • Siting project close to grid interconnection point saves costs
  • Interconnection voltage increases with energy project capacity
 • Community scale PV 250 kW – 5 MW: distribution system
 • Utility-scale PV greater than 10 MW+: transmission lines (Solar@Scale, 2022)
 • Grid capacity at interconnection point determines project feasibility
