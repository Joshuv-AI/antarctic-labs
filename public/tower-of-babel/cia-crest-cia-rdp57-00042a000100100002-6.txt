INSTRUCTIONS FOR TRANSMISSION AND INVENTOR! OF TOP SECRET DOCUMENTS

I. OBJECTIVES:

A. To establish a simple, uniform, and effective system for the control
of Top Secret documents transmitted between Areas and/or to outside agen-
cies.

B. To provide to the Central Top Secret Control Office up-to-date infor-
mation on the location or disposition of all documents, as well as
accountability for all TS Control numbers.

C. To provide periodic inventories of all documents with minimum expen-
diture of time.

A. ’’Receipt for TOP SECRET Document", in quadruplicate. Form No. .

B. Filing equipment for receipts, 3i x 7|'. (IBM file drawers for those
areas handling a volume of TS material.)

B. Submission to Central Top Secret Control Office of "Top Secret Log",
Form 38-lU.

C. Central Top Secret Control Office only . — Preparation of "Top
Secret P.osting Record”, Form 60-73, except on documents initially dis-
seminated by CTSC.

A. TRANSMISSION :

ruplicate from supply of numbered receipt forms furnished to the
Area. (See attached sample.)

a. List all documents to one Addressee on one receipt (if more
space is needed to list documents, prepare additional receipts).
Do not enter titles of documents on receipts or other informa-
tion necessitating classification of the receipt.
