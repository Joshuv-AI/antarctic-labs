President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (6 September 1944, d58)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 6 September 1944.

Number 620, personal and top secret, from the President for the Former Naval Person.

Your 782.[2] We will arrive Octagon afternoon of September 11th. This time I want to have the privilege and great pleasure of meeting you and Clemmie when you arrive.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Supra.
