EL SALVADOR: BISHOP RIVERA Y DAMAS RETURNS FROM PANAMA
PA221996
SAN SALVADOR RADIO CADENA YSU IN SPANISH 1828 GMT 22 OCT 85

{((TEXT)) THERE HAS BEEN NO INFORMATION ON THE KIDNAPPING OF .
INES -GUADALUPE DUARTE DURAN AND HER CLASSMATE ANA CECILIA
VILLEDA IN THE PAST SEVERAL HOURS. .,

SAN SALVADOR ARCHBISHOP MSGR ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS
ARRIVED BEFORE DAWN TODAY FROM PANAMA, WHERE UNOFFICIAL REPORTS
INDICATE HE MET WITH TOP REBEL LEADERS. AT THIS TIME THE BISHOP
IS HOLOING MASS IN THE CHURCH OF DIVINE PROVIDENCE AND HE HAS YET
TO ISSUE A STATEMENT ON HIS TRIP TO PANAMA.

THERE IS ALSO NO INFORMATION ON THE RETURN TO EL SALVADOR
OF COMMUNICATIONS MINISTER JULIO ADOLFO REY PRENDES AND
PRESIDENTIAL ENVOY ABRAHAM RODRIGUEZ. A NEWS CONFERENCE SCHEDULED
FOR APPROXIMATELY 1138 WAS CANCELLED, SOURCES AT THE PRESIDENCY
REVEALED.

THERE IS THUS NO INFORMATION ON THE PROGRESS OF THE INES
GUADALUPE DUARTE DURAN CASE. THE PRESIDENT’ S DAUGHTER WAS
KIDNAPPED ON 10 SEPTEMBER IN SOUTHERN SAN SALVADOR. .
