Fodor space in generalized descriptive set theory
Ido Feldman, Miguel Moreno

Abstract
. We study the continuous reducibility of isomorphism relations in the space of regresive functions in κ κ \kappa^{\kappa} . We show for inaccessible κ \kappa , that if 𝒯 \mathcal{T} is a theory with less than κ \kappa non-isomorphic models of size κ \kappa and 𝒯 ′ \mathcal{T}^{\prime} is unstable or superstable non-classifiable, then the isomorphism of models of 𝒯 \mathcal{T} is continuous reducible to the isomorphism of models of 𝒯 ′ \mathcal{T}^{\prime} .

One of the main motivations to developed generalized descriptive set theory has been its connection with model theory.
This connection was studied by Friedman, Hyttinen, and Weinstein (former Kulikov) in

Let us recall the basic definitions, we refer the reader to

The generalized Baire space is the set
\kappa^{\kappa}
endowed with the bounded topology, in this topology the basic open sets are of the form

[\zeta]=\{\eta\in\kappa^{\kappa}\mathrel{|}\allowbreak\zeta\subseteq\eta\}

\zeta\in\kappa^{<\kappa}

Let

and

be
equivalence relations on
\kappa^{\kappa}
. We say that
E_{1}
is

Borel reducible to

if there is a Borel function

that satisfies

(\eta,\xi)\in E_{1}\iff(f(\eta),f(\xi))\in E_{2}.

We call
f
a reduction of
E_{1}
to

E_{2}
and we denote this by
E_{1}\hookrightarrow_{B}E_{2}
.
In the case

is continuous,
we say that
E_{1}
is continuously reducible to
E_{2}
and
we denote it by
E_{1}\hookrightarrow_{c}E_{2}
.

.

2.
Fodor space

Following the intuition of
[
6
]
, if
E
is a
\kappa
-Borel equivalence relation on
\kappa^{\kappa}
with at most
\kappa
equivalence classes, then the enumeration
\langle x_{i}\mathrel{|}\allowbreak x_{i}\text{ is an $E$- equivalence class}\rangle
induced the function
\mathcal{F}:\kappa^{\kappa}\rightarrow\kappa^{\kappa}
:

\mathcal{F}(\eta)(\alpha)=\begin{cases}i&\mbox{if }\alpha=0\\
0&\mbox{otherwise, }\end{cases}

where
\eta\in x_{i}
. Clearly
\mathcal{F}
is a
\kappa
-Borel reduction from
E
into
0_{\kappa}
, where
f\ 0_{\kappa}\ g
if and only if
f(0)=g(0)
. This kind of reduction obtained from an enumeration of the equivalence classes, may induce a reduction
\mathcal{F}
on “
evetually regresive functions
”, i.e. for all
\eta\in\kappa^{\kappa}
there is
\alpha<\kappa
such that
\mathcal{F}(\eta)\mathbin{\upharpoonright}(\kappa\backslash\alpha)
is regresive. Thus we are not interested on the whole Baire space but the one of the eventually regresive functions.

Definition 2.1

(Fodor space)
.

Let
\mathbb{F}=\{\eta\in\kappa^{\kappa}\mathrel{|}\allowbreak\exists\alpha<\kappa(\forall\beta>\alpha\ \eta(\beta)<\beta)\}
. The Fodor space is the set
\mathbb{F}
with the induced topology.

Notice that the subspaces
\beta^{\kappa}
are subpaces of
\mathbb{F}
, in particular the generalized Cantor space.

Let
S\subseteq\kappa
a stationary set, we define the equivalence relation
=_{S}^{\mathbb{F}}
as
=_{S}^{\kappa}\cap\ (\mathbb{F}\times\mathbb{F})
.
Clearly Fact
1.7
induced a result on
=_{S}^{\mathbb{F}}
, i.e. if
\diamondsuit_{\lambda}
holds, then
\cong_{\mathcal{T}}\ \hookrightarrow_{c}\ =^{\mathbb{F}}_{\lambda}
. In the case of theories
\mathcal{T}
with less than
\kappa
non-isomorphic models,
\diamondsuit_{\lambda}
is not needed. This follows from an observation of the proof of Theorem 2.8
[
4
]
.

Proposition 2.2
.

Let
S\subseteq\kappa
be a stationary set. Assume
\mathcal{T}
is a countable complete classifiable theory over a countable vocabulary. Then
\cong_{\mathcal{T}}\ \hookrightarrow_{c}\ =^{\kappa}_{S}
.

If
\cong_{\mathcal{T}}
has less than

\cong_{\mathcal{T}}\ \hookrightarrow_{c}\ =^{\mathbb{F}}_{S}

The idea behind the proof is to find an

and use the Approximation Lemma (Lemma 6.6
[

[

]
.

(The EF game)

For every

and

In the

chooses an

We will write

\neg(\eta\ \cong_{\mathcal{T}}\ \xi)\Longleftrightarrow\exists C\subseteq\kappa\text{ a club, such that }\ \neg(\eta\ E_{\alpha}\ \xi)

. For all

, let

be an enumeration of the

-equivalence classes (this can be done since

to
