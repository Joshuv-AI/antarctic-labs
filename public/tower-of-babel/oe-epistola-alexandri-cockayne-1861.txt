PROVENANCE NOTE (Tower of Babel staging): Old English text after Thomas
Oswald Cockayne, "Narratiunculae Anglice conscriptae" (London, 1861), from
Cotton MS Vitellius A.xv (the Beowulf manuscript), fols. 104-128. Public
domain scan (Internet Archive). The Old English version of the
marvels-of-India letter is complete as printed. The special Old English
characters (thorn, eth, ash) are roughly rendered by the OCR.
