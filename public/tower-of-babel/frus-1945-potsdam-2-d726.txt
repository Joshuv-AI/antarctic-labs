No. 726 Memorandum by the First Secretary of Embassy in Portugal (Cannon)[1]

Potsdam, July 20, 1945.

Memorandum

In presenting this paper[2] the Secretary may wish orally to restate the interest of the United States Government in the early admission of Italy to the United Nations Organization, and to observe that this matter is being handled separately by a Sub-Committee.

Cavendish W. Cannon
