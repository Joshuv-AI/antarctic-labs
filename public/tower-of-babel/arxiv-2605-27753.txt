Decoupled Delay-Doppler and Angle Estimation in BD-RIS Sensing via Nested Tucker Decomposition
Kenneth Benício, André L. F. de Almeida, Fazal-E-Asim, Bruno Sokal Behrooz Makki, Gabor Fodor, and A. Lee Swindlehurst

Abstract
We study single-target localization in a group-connected beyond-diagonal reconfigurable intelligent surface (BD-RIS)-assisted monostatic network with K K element groups. We propose a Nested Tensor Factorization and Estimation (NTFE) algorithm that models the received signal as a 3 3 -rd-order nested Tucker tensor, thereby decoupling the delay-Doppler and angle domains. The resulting two-stage procedure estimates the target-bearing tensor factors and then extracts the other physical parameters using subspace and closed-form steps. We also analyze identifiability and uniqueness conditions. Simulations show that NTFE exploits the group-connected BD-RIS structure and outperforms state-of-the-art sensing benchmarks.

\displaystyle\hat{\alpha}=\mathbb{E}\left\{[\boldsymbol{\mathcal{Y}}]_{(1)}\oslash[\boldsymbol{\mathcal{Y}^{\prime}}]_{(1)}\right\}.

We take the sample mean (denoted here by

) over all
LMQT
available elements in the unfolded tensor, effectively exploiting the full multidimensional dataset to mitigate noise.

IV
Identifiability and Uniqueness

Unlike a standard Tucker decomposition with an unknown dense core, the proposed model uses a deterministic known core
\boldsymbol{\mathcal{I}}
with the factor matrices corresponding to the first and third modes, which define the part of the system geometry assumed to be known at the receiver. Hence, for (
5
), arbitrary rotations of the estimates do not occur and the remaining ambiguities reduce to trivial scalings. Thus, the
BALS
estimates satisfy
[\hat{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{F}}}]_{(1)}=[\boldsymbol{\mathcal{F}}]_{(1)}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{1}
and
\hat{\boldsymbol{p}}^{\prime}=\boldsymbol{p}^{\prime}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{2}
, with
\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{1}\in\mathbb{C}^{MQ\times MQ}
and
\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{2}\in\mathbb{C}^{N^{2}\times N^{2}}
such that

\displaystyle\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{2}\otimes\boldsymbol{\Lambda}_{1}\otimes\boldsymbol{I}_{N}

\displaystyle=\boldsymbol{I}_{N^{4}}.

(31)

These scalings cancel each other in the reconstructed tensor and preserve the column space of
[\hat{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{F}}}]_{(1)}
, enabling delay-Doppler extraction. However, a non-uniform scaling may destroy the shift-invariance of
\hat{\boldsymbol{p}}^{\prime}
, so (
27
) is used to refine the angle subspace estimate. For (
10
), the ambiguities reduce to
\hat{\boldsymbol{d}}(\nu)=\lambda_{3}\boldsymbol{d}(\nu)
and
\hat{\boldsymbol{c}}(\tau)=\lambda_{4}\boldsymbol{c}(\tau)
, with
\lambda_{3}\lambda_{4}=1
.

Identifiability follows from the invertibility of the
LS
systems in Alg.
1
. For (
5
), define
\boldsymbol{P}_{1}=[\boldsymbol{\mathcal{I}}]_{(2)}(\boldsymbol{p}^{\prime}\otimes\boldsymbol{S}\otimes\boldsymbol{G})^{\text{T}}\in\mathbb{C}^{N\times LT}
and
\boldsymbol{P}_{2}=[\boldsymbol{\mathcal{I}}]_{(4)}([\hat{\boldsymbol{\mathcal{F}}}]_{(1)}\otimes\boldsymbol{G}\otimes\boldsymbol{S})^{\text{T}}\in\mathbb{C}^{N^{2}\times LMQT}
. Their right-invertibility requires
LT\geq N
and
LMQT\geq N^{2}
. For (
10
),
\boldsymbol{P}_{3}=([\boldsymbol{\mathcal{X}}]_{(2)}\text{D}(\boldsymbol{c}(\tau))\otimes\boldsymbol{G})^{\text{T}}\diamond\boldsymbol{I}_{M}
and
\boldsymbol{P}_{4}=([\boldsymbol{\mathcal{X}}]_{(3)}\text{D}(\boldsymbol{d}(\nu))\otimes\boldsymbol{G})^{\text{T}}\diamond\boldsymbol{I}_{Q}
require
NQ\geq 1
,
NM\geq 1
, and
MQ\geq K
(with
Q,M\geq 1
). Since the known core prevents arbitrary non-singular rotations
[
24
]
, these conditions ensure uniqueness up to trivial scaling ambiguities.

VI
Conclusion

We proposed the
NTFE
algorithm for single-target localization in a group-connected
BD-RIS
-assisted monostatic sensing scenario. Using a
3
rd order nested Tucker model, the method decouples the temporal and spatial parameters, supports identifiable estimation up to trivial ambiguities, and outperforms state-of-the-art benchmarks in terms of effective-channel
NMSE
and parameter
RMSE
.
