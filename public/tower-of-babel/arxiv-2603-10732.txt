Special alternating links of minimal unlinking number
Duncan McCoy, JungHwan Park

Abstract
. For any link in the 3-sphere, there is a natural lower bound for the unlinking number in terms of the classical signature. We prove that if this lower bound is sharp for a special alternating link L L , then the unlinking number of L L is necessarily realized by crossing changes in any alternating diagram for L L . As an application, we compute new values of the unknotting numbers for some special alternating knots with crossing number 11 and 12.

Introduction

Given a link
L

u(L)
is defined to be the minimal number of crossing changes required in any diagram of

Indeed results which predict exactly where the unknotting or unlinking numbers will be realized are rare. For example, among the few general results available, it is known that alternating knots with unknotting number one can always be unknotted by a single crossing change in any alternating diagram
