 Radiation shielding requirements for ANI-TXN’s downstream section
 R. S. Augusto,∗ G. Himanshu, M. Lucas, A. Broadbent, E. Stavitski, O. Chubar, R. Lee, and H. Xiaojing
 Brookhaven National Laboratory, Upton, NY 11973-5000.

 This work estimates the minimum shielding requirements for the downstream section of the 26-
 ID Advanced Nanoscale Imaging-Tender X-ray Nanoprobe beamline (ANI-TXN), which corresponds
 approximately to the end of the beam transport pipe connecting to the planned satellite building, as
 well as the whole building itself. The baseline shielding material for the transport pipe is considered
 to be steel, whereas the satellite building will be made entirely of concrete. The shielding thicknesses’
 requirements were assessed conservatively, for several maximum credible incidents using the Monte
 Carlo particle transport and interaction code FLUKA.

 APPENDIX: SOURCE TERM MODELLING

Given the difficulty to estimate a single worst case scenario for the radiological calculations, a conservative set of ANI source
  terms’ reflectivity was instead provided by the NSLS-II Experiment Development Program’s Simulations Group [10–12].

 Each of the source terms utilized in this work was constructed by combining a baseline spectrum with the harmonics. The
harmonics were not simply modeled as “lines”, but rather with realistic shape. Another conservative feature of this approach
has to do with the baseline model, which was considerably more conservative than the oscillation lines denoted in the spectra.
