 CONCLUDING REMARKS
 In view of the potential of cobalt-refractory-metal superalloys for
high-temperature aerospace applications, research is continuing at the NASA

with these alloys in order to f'urther delineate their properties. Although
these alloys were developed for space-power-system applications, their advan-

tageous properties suggest that they be considered for other high-temperature

aeronautical and industrial applications as well.
