Author Correction: Advancing dye–DES synergies in dye-sensitized solar cells for improved indoor efficiency and long-term stability under sustainable conditions
Authors: Giorgia Salerno, Chiara Liliana Boldrini, Norberto Manfredi, Vito Capriati, Ottavia Bettucci, Alessandro Abbotto
Published: 2026

Correction to: Communications Chemistry 10.1038/s42004-025-01821-7, published online 30 January 2026

In the version of this article initially published, chemical structures in Scheme 1 had presentation errors and have now been replaced, while the caption has been updated to read “Route for the synthesis of dyes CBZ-EG and CBZ-Gly” from the original “Synthetic route….” In the first paragraph of the Results, in the sentence now reading “Compound 3b was then brominated to obtain product 4b,” 3b was originally written “3a.” The errors do not affect the experimental results, data interpretation or the conclusions of the article. The scheme and text have been updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
