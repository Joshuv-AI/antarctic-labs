Introduction Results
• Modern communication networks increase bandwidth by enabling multiple signals to coexist in a
 single conductor
 – Signals can only be observed through active taps that introduce delay by
 intercepting and re-transmitting signals
 – Difficult to interpret individual signals
• Network signal disruption/delay can adversely affect sensitive control systems used in critical
 processes
• With passive techniques, cannot inject malicious behavior since no delays with signals

Full-Duplex Signals
• Used in Gigabit and Automative Ethernet communication protocols
• Automotive Ethernet: single twisted pair
• Signals sent simultaneously between 2 endpoints on a single conductor at the same
 frequency
 – Signals from each endpoint are merged

Research Question
 Can Machine Learning Algorithms be trained to interpret and decode
 passively observed Automative Ethernet full-duplex signals without
 access to the original signals transmitted by either endpoint?

Model Architecture
• Bidirectional LSTM (Bi-LSTM) with 3 layers: a Neural Network that handles long-term
 dependencies and processes the input sequence in both dimensions simultaneously
 – Can understand past and future context of the input sequence (full context)
 – Xi is an input token, Yi is an output token
• Utilize dropout to prevent overfitting, improves model generalization Figure 4. Performance Comparison after training on a single sample point “D”.

 Explainability

 generates a high-fidelity, easy-to-interpret decision tree and associated trust report as output
 – Helps identify model underspecification: shortcut learning, presence of spurious
 correlations, vulnerability to out-of-distribution samples
