Correction: Superficial temporal artery and branches in thai cadavers: anatomical variations, ethnic considerations, and filler injection safety
Authors: Anuch Durongphan, Warit Chongkolwatana, Rungravee Chokdee, Pavitra Sutantayawalee, Napatsorn Sripraditpong, Apinat Wattanaphichet, Kittitad Sovikul, Jate Lumyoungsatien
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-30644-8, published online 02 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors.

First, Table 3 contained an incorrect measurement site for OS Sahin et al. The correct and incorrect measurements are stated below:

Incorrect:

AuthorSite of measurementOS Sahin, et al.291 mm distal from the bifurcation

Correct:

AuthorSite of measurementOS Sahin, et al.293 mm distal from the bifurcation

Second, in the Results section under the subheading ‘Superficial temporal artery topographic measurements’ the abbreviation for the jugale-to-frontal branch distance is incorrect. Therefore,

“The frontal branch was located 35.69 ± 7.51 mm above the midlength of the upper border of the zygomatic arch (MF) and coursed superiorly, passing 43.89 ± 6.32 mm above the jugale (JZ) and 37.95 ± 15.71 mm from the orbital brim (OF).”

now reads:

“The frontal branch was located 35.69 ± 7.51 mm above the midlength of the upper border of the zygomatic arch (MF) and coursed superiorly, passing 43.89 ± 6.32 mm above the jugale (JF) and 37.95 ± 15.71 mm from the orbital brim (OF).”

In addition, in Table 4 an incorrect BV value was published which did not match the correct value published in both the Results and the Supplementary Material attached to this Article. The correct and incorrect values are stated below:

Incorrect:

mean (mm)SDBV (upper border of zygomatic arch to bifurcation)29.8610.64

Correct:

mean (mm)SDBV (upper border of zygomatic arch to bifurcation)30.1310.82

Furthermore, in the Discussion section, the same incorrect BV value was published, along with the incorrect abbreviation BH, which was swapped with the correct BH value in the same paragraph. Consequently,

“Using the same measurement reference, the bifurcation of the STA in our study was located an average of 29.86 ± 10.64 mm above the zygomatic arch (BH), a value close to that reported in a U.S. cadaveric study (31.7 mm)26. In contrast, an Egyptian study reported lower values, with the bifurcation located at 19.94 ± 12.09 mm in cadaveric specimens and 19.29 ± 18.29 mm on computed tomography angiography (CTA)29. The bifurcation in our study was 22.38 ± 6.64 mm medial to the tragus (BV), whereas the Korean study using the same reference line reported 17.2 ± 8.2 mm6.”

now reads:

“Using the same measurement reference, the bifurcation of the STA in our study was located an average of 30.13 ± 10.82 mm above the zygomatic arch (BV), a value close to that reported in a U.S. cadaveric study (31.7 mm)26. In contrast, an Egyptian study reported lower values, with the bifurcation located at 19.94 ± 12.09 mm in cadaveric specimens and 19.29 ± 18.29 mm on computed tomography angiography (CTA)29. The bifurcation in our study was 22.38 ± 6.64 mm medial to the tragus (BH), whereas the Korean study using the same reference line reported 17.2 ± 8.2 mm6.”

Finally, Figure 2 contained an error where an unnecessary label was inadvertently published on the hair portion of the Figure. The incorrect Figure 2 and its accompanying legend appear below:

Mean diameters in male cadavers and reference points for branch location relative to anatomical landmarks. J indicates the jugale, and M denotes the mid-upper border of the zygomatic arch.

The original Article has been corrected.
