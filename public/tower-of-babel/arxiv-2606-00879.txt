Weak almost contact structure with B-metric
Cornelia-Livia Bejan, Şemsi Eken Meriç

Abstract




Abstract
Corresponding to weak almost contact metric structures (defined recently by Rovenski and Wolak), we introduce and study here weak almost contact structures with B-metric, which generalize the classical almost contact structures with Norden metric (B-metric). Several geometric properties are obtained, some special classes are investigated and a lot of examples are constructed throughout this work.

53C15, 53C25, 53D15

Keywords.
Almost contact structures, Norden metric, Killing tensor field

Introduction

The geometry of almost contact metric manifolds, introduced in the 1960’s as odd dimensional counterparts of almost Hermitian manifolds, has undergone a great development (see
[
4
]
and references therein). Since then, contact geometry has been applied in physics (particularly mechanics and thermodynamics), in the energetic and dynamic structure of systems, optics and ray geometry, in engineering (motion constraints) and so on. For a new approach of almost contact metric geometry, see
[
1
]
. Correspondingly, the geometry of almost contact manifolds with Norden metric (B-metric) (e. g.
[
6
]
,
[
7
]
,
[
8
]
,
[
9
]
) stands for a natural extension to the odd dimensional case of the almost complex geometry with Norden metric (B-metric), (e. g.
[
5
]
,
[
10
]
,
[
11
]
). The letter B was chosen by Norden to highlight that complex manifolds with isometric metrics (i.e. Hermitian) were studied in the West, but his theory, where the metric was anti-isometric with respect to the complex structure, is a contribution of the Eastern school (i.e. Vostok, which is written Boctok).

Recently, as a generalization of the almost contact structure, Rovenski and Wolak introduced in
[
13
]
a new notion, namely weak almost contact structure
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta)
. They call a Riemannian metric
g
compatible with this structure, a weak almost contact metric structure
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
(see
[
13
]
-
[
18
]
) and we stress that
g
is Riemannian.

To fill a gap in literature, our natural task here is to introduce and study the other case, namely weak almost contact structures
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta)
admitting an anti-compatible metric
g
. We call this new notion a weak almost contact B-metric structure
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
, or a weak almost contact structure with B-metric. It generalizes almost contact structures with B-metric (Norden metric). Different from the above weak almost contact structure introduced by Rovenski and Wolak, the metric in our case is semi-Riemannian (not necessarily Riemannian) and we stress that the anti-compatibility of the metric in our case leads to different results.

We find here several geometric properties of weak almost contact B-metric structures, such as their splitting, their parallelism, their integrability, some Killing properties, and so on. We obtain the structural group acting on the underlying manifold. In the last section, we deal with some special classes of these structures. Throughout the paper, we construct a large number of illustrative examples of such manifolds, to support the motivation of this study.

4
Weak Almost Contact B-Metric Manifolds

We recall from
[
13
]
that a manifold
M^{2n+1}
carries a weak almost contact metric structure
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
, if it is endowed with a weak almost contact structure
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta)
and a Riemannian metric compatible with it, i.e.

\displaystyle g(\varphi X,\varphi Y)=g(X,QY)-\eta(X)\eta(Y),\ \ \ X,Y\in\chi(M).

This notion generalizes the classical almost contact metric structures.

We introduce here the main notion of our study as a dual counterpart of the above one:

Definition 12

Let
(M^{2n+1},\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta)
be a weak almost contact manifold, such that there exists a (semi-)Riemannian metric
g
, which is anti-compatible with it, i.e.,

\displaystyle g(\varphi X,\varphi Y)=-g(X,QY)+\eta(X)\eta(Y),\ \ \ X,Y\in\chi(M).

(11)

Then
(\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
is called a weak almost contact B-metric structure, or a weak almost contact structure with B-metric (Norden metric). In this case
(M^{2n+1},\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
is said to be a weak almost contact B-metric manifold, or a weak almost contact manifold with B-metric (Norden metric).

12
and the weak almost contact metric structure, given in
[

6
Weak Nearly Sasaki-Norden Manifolds

In this section, we define and study a class of a weak almost contact B-metric manifolds.

Definition 31

A weak almost contact B-metric manifold
(M^{2n+1},\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)
is called weak nearly Sasakian B-metric manifold or weak nearly Sasaki-Norden manifold if

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}\varphi)X

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-g(X,X)\xi+\eta(X)X,\ \ \ X\in\chi(M).

(27)

Remark 32

Equivalent to (
27

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}\varphi)Y+(\nabla_{Y}\varphi)X=-2g(X,Y)\xi+\eta(X)Y+\eta(Y)X,\ \ \ X,Y\in\chi(M).

(28)

Theorem 33

Let
(M^{2n+1},\varphi,Q,\xi,\eta,g)

(i)

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}Q)Y=0,\ \ X\in\chi(M),\ Y\in\Gamma(\mathcal{D}).

Remark 34

\displaystyle(\nabla_{X}\varphi)Y+(\nabla_{Y}\varphi)X=0,\ \ \ X,Y\in\chi(M).

\displaystyle(\nabla_{\xi}\varphi)X+(\nabla_{X}\varphi)\xi=\eta(X)\xi-X,\ \ X\in\chi(M),
