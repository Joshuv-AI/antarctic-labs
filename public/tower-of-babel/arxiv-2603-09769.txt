Abstract




Abstract

In the finite projective space PG
(2n,q)
we consider flags of type
(n-1,n)
, that is, pairs
(A,B)
consisting of an
(n-1)
-space
A
and an
n
-space
B
that are incident. Two such flags
(A_{1},B_{1})
and
(A_{2},B_{2})
are opposite if
A_{1}\cap B_{2}=A_{2}\cap B_{1}=\emptyset
.
Let
\Gamma_{2n}
be the graph whose vertices are the flags of type
(n-1,n)
of PG
(2n,q)
, with two vertices being adjacent if the corresponding flags are opposite.
Using the Erdős-Matching theorem for vector spaces shown by Ihringer, we determine, for
q
large enough, the largest cocliques of
\Gamma_{2n}
and obtain a stability result.
This EKR-type theorem
proves a conjecture of D’haeseleer, Metsch and Werner.

Keywords:
Erdős-Ko-Rado, generalized Kneser graph, coclique
