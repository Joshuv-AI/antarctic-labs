An NPDo Approach for Tensor Block-Diagonalization
Ren-Cang Li, Li Wang, Mei Yang

Abstract
This paper is concerned with Partial Tensor Block-Diagonalization of a multiway tensor by orthonormal matrices so that the extracted block-diagonal part optimally represents the tensor. The basic idea is to maximize the block-diagonal part via the tensor’s mode-multiplications by orthonormal matrices. For that reason, it will be referred to Principal Tensor Block-Diagonalization ( ptbd ), which contains the Tucker decomposition ( td ) of a tensor as a special case with just one block. Also as a special case is the approximate dominant tensor SVD in which each block-size is 1-by-1. An NPDo approach is proposed to optimize the block-diagonal part for computing ptbd . It is shown the NPDo approach combined with Gauss-Seidel-type updating is globally convergent to a stationary point while the objective increases monotonically. Numerical experiments are presented to illustrate the efficiency of the NPDo approach. Keywords: principal tensor Block-diagonalization, PTBD, principal tensor SVD, PTSVD, NPDo Mathematics Subject Classification 15A18, 15A69; 65F30; 65K05; 90C26

1\leq\ell\leq m

\displaystyle\frac{\big\|\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}-P_{\ell}^{(j)}\big([P_{\ell}^{(j)}]^{\HH}\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}\big)\big\|_{\F}^{2}}{\big\|\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}\big\|_{\F}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\big\|\sin\Theta\big({\cal R}(\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}),{\cal R}(P_{\ell}^{(j)})\big)\big\|_{\F}^{2}

(4.22a)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2\Big[\|\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}\|_{\tr}-\tr\big([P_{\ell}^{(j)}]^{\T}\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}\big)\Big]}{\sigma_{\min}(\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)})}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2\eta_{*j}}{\sigma_{\min}(\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)})}.

(4.22b)

Hence (
4.20b
) is a consequence of (
4.20a
).
To see (
4.20a
), we combine

c\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\eta_{*j}\leq\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\eta_{j}\leq\lim_{j\to\infty}f(P_{1}^{(j+1)},\ldots,P_{m}^{(j+1)})-f(P_{1}^{(0)},\ldots,P_{m}^{(0)})<\infty,

with (
4.22
).
The proof is completed.
∎

It is noted the difference between (
4.6a
) and (
4.20a
)
lies in the appearance of
{\cal R}(P_{\ell}^{(j+1)})
in the former but
{\cal R}(\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)})
in the latter. The reason is
no longer
P_{\ell}^{(j+1)}
is taken as the orthonormal polar factor of
\mathscr{H}_{\ell}^{(j)}

by
Algorithm

2
.

6
Conclusion

We are interested in the principal tensor block-diagonalization (
ptbd
) of a multiway tensor.
Specifically, given
B\equiv[b_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{m}}]\in\mathbb{C}^{n_{1}\times n_{2}\times\cdots\times n_{m}}
, we seek
m

orthonormal matrices
P_{\ell}\in\mathbb{C}^{n_{\ell}\times k_{\ell}}\,\,\forall\ell
such that

T=B\times_{1}P_{1}^{\HH}\times_{2}P_{2}^{\HH}\cdots\times_{m}P_{m}^{\HH}\in\mathbb{C}^{k_{1}\times k_{2}\times\cdots\times k_{m}}
is optimally approximately block-diagonal with given block-diagonal structure in a dominant way.
It becomes the principal tensor SVD (
ptsvd
) when each diagonal block of
T
is scalar and
the principal Tucker decomposition when
T
is considered as one block
[????]
.
We propose an NPDo (nonlinear polar decomposition with orthonormal polar factor dependency)
approach for that purpose. It includes an alternating SCF (self-consistent-field) iteration to
numerically solve the KKT condition equations and a relevant theory.
It is shown the SCF iteration combined with Gauss-Seidel-type updating is globally convergent to a stationary point while the objective increases monotonically.
An accelerated version of the approach via the locally optimal conjugate gradient (LOCG) technique is also established.
Numerical experiments are presented to illustrate the efficiency of the NPDo approach.
