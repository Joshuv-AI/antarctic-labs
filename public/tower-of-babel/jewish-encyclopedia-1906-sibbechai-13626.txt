Captain under David who came from the town of Shushan, near Ephrath-Bethlehem. He distinguished himself by overcoming a Philistine giant (II Sam. xxi. 18; I Chron. xx. 4). He is mentioned also in I Chron. xi. 29, xxvii. 11, as one of the thirty valiant followers of David. He may be the Mebunnai of II Sam. xxiii. 27.

E. G. H. E. I. N.
