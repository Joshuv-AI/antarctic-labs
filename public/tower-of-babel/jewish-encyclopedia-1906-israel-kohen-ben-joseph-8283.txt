Polish scholar; lived in the second half of the sixteenth century. He edited the anonymous philosophical work "She'elot 'Inyan ha-Neshamah," containing a dialogue between pupil and master on eighteen questions concerning the soul (Lublin, 1566). The work has been translated into Judæo-German by Isaac ben Ḥayyim.

Bibliography: Zunz, Z. G. p. 288; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 642. S. S. I. Br.
