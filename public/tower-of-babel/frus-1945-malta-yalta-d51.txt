The Ambassador in the Soviet Union (Harriman) to the President [1] (11 January 1945, d51)

Moscow, 11 January 1945.

Personal and Top Secret for the President from Harriman.

Molotov advises me that Marshal Stalin has accepted the code name Argonaut and has so advised Mr. Churchill. This is in reply to your White House 160 Jan 7.[2]

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Naval Attaché, Moscow, via Navy channels.
[2] See ante, p. 30, footnote 3.
