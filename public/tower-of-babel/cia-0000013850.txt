During an air attack which destroyed the HAM RONG Bridge, THANH HOA
Province, in 196 (date unknown) a US pilot was shot down and captured.

Source was informed about the capture. No other information is available.

In November/December 1966, Source was informed by his political cadre in

a meeting that a US soldier had been captured by another VC/NVA unit
(designation unknown) in THUA THIEN Province, SVN. Source was stationed

at this time in THUA THIEN Province. The political cadre called upon the
company soldiers to try to capture US soldiers, like other vVc/NVA units.

The cadre only mentioned that a US soldier had been captured after a battle.
He did not reveal any biographic information on the US prisoner.
