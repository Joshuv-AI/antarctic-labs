In the original publication of this article [1], In Fig. 3(E) the authors noticed an inconsistency between the method description and the statistical analysis.

In method section stated, “Immunohistochemical scoring was performed using product method”. percentage of positive cells method used.

To ensure scientific accuracy and consistency between the method and results, the authors have re performed the statistical analysis using the method described in the paper (the product of staining intensity × percentage of positive cells). The overall conclusions remain unchanged.
