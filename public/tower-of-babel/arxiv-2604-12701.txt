Supercurrent-induced phonon angular momentum
Takehito Yokoyama

Abstract
We propose a mechanism of supercurrent-induced phonon angular momentum in mixed parity superconductors and s s -wave superconductors with spin orbit coupling. We derive analytical expressions of phonon angular momentum induced by the supercurrent by perturbative calculation. The physical interpretation of this effect is also discussed.
