Reduced superblocks at next-to-next-to-extremality for half-maximally supersymmetric CFTs
Mitchell Woolley

Abstract
We consider mixed four-point correlators of 1/2-BPS operators ϕ k \phi_{k} in SCFTs with eight real Poincaré supercharges, namely the 3d 𝒩 = 4 \mathcal{N}=4 , 4d 𝒩 = 2 \mathcal{N}=2 , 5d 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 , and 6d 𝒩 = ( 1 , 0 ) \mathcal{N}=(1,0) theories. Using the basis of solutions to the superconformal Ward identity introduced in [ 20 ] , we demonstrate that the dynamical data in mixed correlators of extremality ℰ = 2 \mathcal{E}=2 is encoded in certain simpler “reduced correlator” functions that admit a block expansion, in close analogy to the recent result for maximally supersymmetric CFTs in [ 24 ] . These reduced blocks similarly involve ordinary blocks with shifted kinematics and reproduce what is known in 4d, generalize a known example in 6d, and offer novel results in 3d and 5d.

4
Outlook

The result of this paper was to compute reduced block decompositions for four-point functions
\langle\phi_{k_{1}}\phi_{k_{2}}\phi_{k_{3}}\phi_{k_{1}+k_{2}+k_{3}-4}\rangle
of 1/2-BPS operators

in terms of a single

block. The availability of reduced blocks should, in principle, present a drastic simplification of numerical and analytic superconformal bootstrap studies by allowing one examine correlators in terms of substantially simpler block expansions. In the following, we describe questions concerning implementation and comment on further directions.

)-(
2.6

Appendix B. Jack Polynomials

Jack polynomials
P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a,b}
form a useful basis for expanding symmetric 2-variable functions, such as conformal blocks and the reduced correlators in the main text. They can be defined in terms of Gegenbauer polynomials as

\displaystyle P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a,b}(z,\bar{z})=\frac{(a-b)!}{\left(2\varepsilon\right)_{a-b}}\left(z\bar{z}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}\left(a+b\right)}C^{(\varepsilon)}_{a-b}\left(\frac{z+\bar{z}}{2\left(z\bar{z}\right)^{1/2}}\right),

(B.1)

and satisfy an orthogonality condition given by

\displaystyle\int^{1}_{-1}C_{m}^{(\varepsilon)}(x)C_{n}^{(\varepsilon)}(x)\left(1-x^{2}\right)^{\varepsilon-\frac{1}{2}}dx=\delta_{m,n}\frac{2^{1-2\varepsilon}\pi\Gamma\left[m+2\varepsilon\right]}{m!(m+\varepsilon)\Gamma\left[\varepsilon\right]^{2}}.

(B.2)

For
d=4,6
, Jack polynomials can be written explicitly as

\displaystyle P^{\left(1\right)}_{a,b}(z,\bar{z})=

\displaystyle\;\frac{1}{\left(a-b+1\right)\left(z-\bar{z}\right)}\left(z^{a+1}\bar{z}^{b}-z^{b}\bar{z}^{a+1}\right),

(B.3)

\displaystyle P^{(2)}_{a,b}(z,\bar{z})=

\displaystyle\;\frac{6}{\left(a-b+2\right)\left(z-\bar{z}\right)^{3}}\left(\frac{z^{a+3}\bar{z}^{b}-z^{b}\bar{z}^{a+3}}{a-b+3}-\frac{z^{a+2}\bar{z}^{b+1}-z^{b+1}\bar{z}^{a+2}}{a-b+1}\right).

(B.4)

As discussed in the main text, conformal blocks in
d=2(1+\varepsilon)
dimensions can be expressed in the expansion

\displaystyle G^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}_{\Delta,\ell}(z,\bar{z})=(-1)^{\ell}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\;r_{m,n;\Delta,\ell}^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}\;P^{(\varepsilon)}_{\frac{\Delta+\ell}{2}+m,\frac{\Delta-\ell}{2}+n}(z,\bar{z}).

(B.5)

The action of the conformal quadratic Casimir operator
\hat{{\cal C}}_{\;\Delta,\ell}^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}
on Jack polynomials

\displaystyle\hat{{\cal C}}_{\Delta,\ell}^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}\;P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a,b}=

\displaystyle\;\left(a(a-1)+b(b-1-2\varepsilon)\right)P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a,b}

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\;\frac{a-b+2\varepsilon}{a-b+\varepsilon}\left(a-\frac{\Delta_{12}}{2}\right)\left(a+\frac{\Delta_{34}}{2}\right)P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a+1,b}

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\;\frac{a-b}{a-b+\varepsilon}\left(b-\frac{\Delta_{12}}{2}-\varepsilon\right)\left(b+\frac{\Delta_{34}}{2}-\varepsilon\right)P^{(\varepsilon)}_{a,b+1},

(B.6)

implies that the expansion coefficients

\displaystyle\left(m\left(\Delta+\ell+m-1\right)+n\left(\Delta-\ell+n-1-2\varepsilon\right)\right)r^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}_{m,n;\Delta,\ell}=

\displaystyle\;\;\;\;+\frac{\ell+m-n+1}{\ell+m-n+1+\varepsilon}\left(\frac{\Delta-\ell-\Delta_{12}}{2}+n-1-\varepsilon\right)\left(\frac{\Delta-\ell+\Delta_{34}}{2}+n-1-\varepsilon\right)r^{\Delta_{12},\Delta_{34}}_{m,n-1;\Delta,\ell}.

(B.7)
