‘1, A Viet Cong prison camp for U.S. and South Vietnamese
prisoners is located at Kilo Lek Hasep Buon (WU6209), about 3.5 kilo-
meters east of Cambodian national Route 15, in the Peam Cheang rubber
plantation, Kompong Cham Province, Cambodia. South Vietnamese and
U.S. prisoners are detained separately, ten men toa hut, The prison
detention huts are of simple construction with leaf or plastic cloth roofs.
The camp appeared to have been in its present location for a long time. *
The prisoners are not physically restrained because the location of the
camp and security measures are adequate to prevent their escape,
