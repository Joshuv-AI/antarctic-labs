Lithuanian rabbi; lived in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. His notes on Rashi and on Elijah Mizraḥi's commentaries on the Pentateuch were published, under the title "Ḥiddushe Maharsha" (Hanover, 1716), with Samuel Edel's novellæ on the Pentateuch, by his brother Ẓebi Hirsch b. Meïr.

Bibliography: Wolf, Bibl. Hebr. iii., No. 353d; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 745. S. S. M. Sel.
