3. Comparative Evaluation Studies
(IAC-D-126, 24 July; Limited
distribution)

After reviewing and discussing pertinent background information,
the members concurred in Mr. Dulles' memorandum on this subject
(IAC-D-126), including his designation of Lieutenant General
Earl W. Barnes, USAF (Ret.), as the intelligence representative on
the group undertaking the initial study. It was agreed that each IAC
agency would submit to the Secretary, IAC, the name of an individual
who would be a point of contact for General Barnes within that agency,
to facilitate the finding of experts in the field covered by this study.

General Breitweiser then circulated copies of changes in the draft
NSCID No. 7 which had been proposed by the Joint Chiefs of Staff
in a memorandum to the Secretary of Defense, dated 24 February
1958, and later circulated by the Executive Secretary, NSC to the
members of the NSC by a memorandum of 11 March. After dis-
cussion the members approved a revision of the new draft NSCID
No. 7 incorporating the changes proposed by the Joint Chiefs of
Staff. The Chairman stated that if NSA and General Erskine's office
approved the draft NSCID No. 7 as revised by the IAC members, it
would constitute final IAG-USCIB action on the draft NSCID No. 7.*
The members noted also that, following the receipt of these formal
concurrences on the draft NSCID No. 7, all of the necessary IAC-
USCIB actions with respect to the draft NSCIDs 1 through 7 would
have been completed and they would be ready for submission to the
NSC.

8. Budgetary Discussion

The Chairman read and commented on a letter to Mr. Dulles
from the Director, Bureau of the Budget, bearing on the foreign
positive strategic intelligence cost estimates for the intelligence
community. After discussion, the members agreed that copies of
the letter* and its enclosures should be circulated to each IAC member.
It was also agreed that a working group, under the Chairmanship of
the CIA Comptroller, should review these data and make recommenda-
tions to the IAC with respect to standardizing criteria for developing
and presenting intelligence cost estimates.

♦ Subsequent to the IAC meeting, NSA and General Erskine's office
concurred in the draft NSCID No. 7 as revised by the IAC members.
