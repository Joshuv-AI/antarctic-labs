Geographer of the eighteenth century. He wrote a geography of Palestine, in Hebrew, entitled "Ḳaẓwe Areẓ" (Zolkiev, 1772). In the second edition (Grodno, 1818) it bore the title "Ereẓ Yisrael li-Gebuloteha Sabib."

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Jewish Literature, p. 421; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 531. J. M. Sc.
