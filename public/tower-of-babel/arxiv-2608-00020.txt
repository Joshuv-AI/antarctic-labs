Let

\displaystyle\mathbb{H}\coloneqq\{a+ib+jc+kd:a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{R},i^{2}=j^{2}=k^{2}=ijk=-1\}

be the skew-field of quaternion numbers with the conjugation

\displaystyle\overline{a+ib+jc+kd}\coloneqq a-ib-jc-kd,\quad\forall a+ib+jc+kd\in\mathbb{H}

and the modulus

\displaystyle|a+ib+jc+kd|\coloneqq\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}+d^{2}},\quad\forall a+ib+jc+kd\in\mathbb{H}.

Let
\mathbb{H}^{n}
be the standard right quaternionic Hilbert space equipped with the inner product

\displaystyle\langle(z_{r})_{r=1}^{n},(w_{r})_{r=1}^{n}\rangle\coloneqq\sum_{r=1}^{n}\overline{z_{r}}w_{r},\quad\forall(z_{r})_{r=1}^{n},(w_{r})_{r=1}^{n}\in\mathbb{H}^{n}

and the norm

\displaystyle\|(z_{r})_{r=1}^{n}\|\coloneqq\left(\sum_{r=1}^{n}|z_{r}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}},\quad\forall(z_{r})_{r=1}^{n}\in\mathbb{H}^{n}.

Given a matrix
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})
, its operator norm is defined as

\displaystyle\|T\|\coloneqq\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}\|Th\|.

2.
Quaternionic Kittaneh Numerical Radius Inequalities

Similar to the Hilbert space case, the
quaternionic numerical range

[

,

,
28
,
25
,
21
,
27
,
29
,
7
,
6
,
26
,
8
,
3
]

of a quaternionic matrix
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})
is defined as

\displaystyle W_{\mathbb{H}}(T)\coloneqq\{\langle h,Th\rangle:h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1\}\subseteq\mathbb{H}

and the
quaternionic numerical radius
of
T
is defined as

\displaystyle w_{\mathbb{H}}(T)\coloneqq\sup_{\lambda\in W_{\mathbb{H}}(T)}|\lambda|=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|.

Unlike in the complex case, the quaternionic numerical range need not be convex (meaning the Toeplitz-Hausdorff theorem fails)
[
19
]
.
Let
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})
be self-adjoint. We note that

(3)

\displaystyle\|T\|=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|.

In fact, define
M\coloneqq\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|
. The we have

\displaystyle M=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|\leq\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}\|h\|\|Th\|=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}\|Th\|=\|T\|.

Now let
x,y\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
. Then

\displaystyle\langle x+y,T(x+y)\rangle-\langle x-y,T(x-y)\rangle=2(\langle x,Ty\rangle+\langle y,Tx\rangle).

Therefore

\displaystyle|(\langle x,Ty\rangle+\langle y,Tx\rangle|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}(|\langle x+y,T(x+y)\rangle|+|\langle x-y,T(x-y)\rangle|)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}(M\|x+y\|^{2}+M\|x-y\|^{2})

\displaystyle=M(\|x\|^{2}+\|y\|^{2}).

Since,
T
is self-adjoint,

(4)

\displaystyle|(\langle x,Ty\rangle+\langle Ty,x\rangle|\leq M(\|x\|^{2}+\|y\|^{2}),\quad\forall x,y\in\mathbb{H}^{n}.

For a given
h\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
with
\|h\|=1
and
\|Th\|\neq 0
, we take

\displaystyle x=\frac{Th}{\|Th\|},\quad y=h

in Inequality (
4
). Then we have

\displaystyle 2\|Th\|\leq 2M.

By varying
h
we get
\|T\|\leq M
. Therefore
\|T\|=M
.

We now derive our main theorem using Equation (
3
).
In the paper, the anti-commutator of
T,S\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})
is defined as
\{T,S\}\coloneqq TS+ST
.

Theorem 2.1
.

(
Quaternionic Kittaneh Numerical Radius Inequalities
)
Let
T\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})
. Assume that

\displaystyle T=A+iB+jC+kD,\text{ for some }A,B,C,D\in M_{n}(\mathbb{H})

satisfying the following conditions.

(i)

A^{*}=A
,
B^{*}=B
,
C^{*}=C
,
D^{*}=D
.

(ii)

iB=Bi
,
jC=Cj
,
Dk=kD
.

(iii)

\{iB,jC\}+\{iB,kD\}+\{jC,kD\}=0
.

Then

(5)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{\|T^{*}T+TT^{*}\|}\leq w_{\mathbb{H}}(T)\leq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{\|T^{*}T+TT^{*}\|}.

Proof.

We have

\displaystyle T=A+iB+jC+kD,\quad T^{*}=A-iB-jC-kD.

Therefore

\displaystyle T^{*}T+TT^{*}

\displaystyle=(A-iB-jC-kD)(A+iB+jC+kD)+(A+iB+jC+kD)(A-iB-jC-kD)

\displaystyle=2(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2})-2(\{iB,jC\}+\{iB,kD\}+\{jC,kD\})

\displaystyle=2(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2}).

For
h\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
with
\|h\|=1
, we have

\displaystyle|\langle h,Th\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,(A+iB+jC+kD)h\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,Ah\rangle+i\langle h,Bh\rangle+j\langle h,Ch\rangle+k\langle h,Dh\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,Ah\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Bh\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Ch\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Dh\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\|Ah\|^{2}+\|Bh\|^{2}+\|Ch\|^{2}+\|Dh\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\langle h,A^{2}h\rangle+\langle h,B^{2}h\rangle+\langle h,C^{2}h\rangle+\langle h,D^{2}h\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle h,(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2})h\rangle.

Since
A,B,C,D
are self-adjoint, we have

\displaystyle w_{\mathbb{H}}(T)^{2}

\displaystyle=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}\langle h,(A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2})h\rangle

\displaystyle=\|A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2}\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\|T^{*}T+TT^{*}\|.

On the other hand, for
h\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
with
\|h\|=1
, using the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, we also have

\displaystyle|\langle h,Th\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,(A+iB+jC+kD)h\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,Ah\rangle+i\langle h,Bh\rangle+j\langle h,Ch\rangle+k\langle h,Dh\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=|\langle h,Ah\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Bh\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Ch\rangle|^{2}+|\langle h,Dh\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}(|\langle h,Ah\rangle|+|\langle h,Bh\rangle|+|\langle h,Ch\rangle|+|\langle h,Dh\rangle|)^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}|\langle h,(\pm A\pm B\pm C\pm D)h\rangle|^{2}.

Since
A,B,C,D
are self-adjoint, we have

\displaystyle w_{\mathbb{H}}(T)^{2}

\displaystyle=\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,Th\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{4}\sup_{h\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\|h\|=1}|\langle h,(\pm A\pm B\pm C\pm D)h\rangle|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\|\pm A\pm B\pm C\pm D\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\|(\pm A\pm B\pm C\pm D)^{2}\|.

Therefore

\displaystyle w_{\mathbb{H}}(T)^{2}\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{16}(\|(-A+B+C+D)^{2}\|+\|(A-B+C+D)^{2}\|+\|(A+B-C+D)^{2}\|+\|(A+B+C-D)^{2}\|)

\displaystyle\geq\frac{1}{16}\|(-A+B+C+D)^{2}+(A-B+C+D)^{2}+(A+B-C+D)^{2}+(A+B+C-D)^{2}\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\|A^{2}+B^{2}+C^{2}+D^{2}\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{8}\|T^{*}T+TT^{*}\|.

∎

Conclusions

(i)

In 2005, Kittaneh derived a breakthrough generalization of classical numerical radius inequalities

(ii)

In this article, we showed that a constrained version of Kittaneh numerical radius inequalities can be obtained for certain classes of matrices over quaternions.

Quaternionic Crouzeix Problem

Breakthrough Crouzeix theorem says following.

Theorem 4.1
.

[

,

,

,

,

(
Crouzeix Theorem

For every

and for every matrix

\displaystyle(\text{{Crouzeix-Palencia Inequality}})\quad\quad\quad\|p(M)\|\leq(1+\sqrt{2})\sup\left\{|p(z)|:z\in W_{\mathbb{C}}(M)\right\},\quad\forall p\in\mathbb{C}[z].

Based on Theorem
4.1
, we formulate following problem.

Problem 4.2
.

(
Quaternionic Crouzeix Problem

Whether there is a universal constant

satisfying following: For every

and for every

\displaystyle\|p(A)\|\leq R_{\mathbb{H}}\sup\left\{|p(z)|:z\in W_{\mathbb{H}}(A)\right\},\quad\forall p(z)\coloneqq a_{0}+a_{1}z+a_{2}z^{2}+\cdots+a_{n}z^{n}\in\mathbb{H}[z],n\in\mathbb{N}

where
\mathbb{H}[z]

We conjecture that
