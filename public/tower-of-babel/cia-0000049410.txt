EL SALVADOR: Duarte's First 100 Days

President Duarte has markedly enhanced El Salvador's image at
home and abroad by asserting his authority over the armed
forces and working to improve the country’s human rights
situation. His first 100 days have been relatively calm, but have
seen no real progress In resolving economic problems or
developing a national war strategy.

Duarte's Achievements

The 58-year-old Christian Democrat has achieved several key
objectives:

— Establishment of his authority as the first popularly elected
civilian president in El Salvador in more than a half a century.

— Seizure of the political spotlight from the insurgents.
