THE PROBLEM

To assess the prospects for political and economic stability
in the European Satellites, especially in relation to the Berlin

crisis.

The last estimate done on the Satellites was NIE 12-59,
"Political Stability in the Evropean Satellites," dated 11 August
1959. Though there is thus some need for a more up-to-date esti-
mate, present interest in the Satellites largely focusses on the
effect of the Berlin crisis. We therefore propose, instead of
treating internal political and economic developments in great

detail, to discuss the chief trends, mainly in relation to the
