Enhanced left triangulated categories
Xiaofa Chen

Abstract
. In this short note, we study dg categories with homotopy kernels, whose homotopy categories are known to admit a natural left triangulated structure. Prototypical examples of such dg categories arise as dg quotients of exact dg categories. We demonstrate that the stablization of the homotopy category of such a dg category admits a canonical dg enhancement via its bounded derived dg category.

1.
Introduction

DG categories already appear in the work of Kelly
[
4
]
. They were applied to matrix problems in the representation theory of finite-dimensional algebras. In the 1990s, Bondal–Kapranov observed that the morphism spaces of the triangulated categories that appear naturally in algebra are the zeroth cohomology groups of certain complexes. The now basic principle (popularized by Grothendieck) in homological algebra is to consider the complex itself rather than its cohomology. Therefore, they proposed to enhance triangulated categories using dg categories, requiring that the triangulated structure should be compatible with the dg structure. Here, the sequences in the dg categories that enhance the distinguished triangles are essentially the graded-split short exact sequences.

Left triangulated categories are one-sided analogues to triangulated categories. A left triangulated category consists of an additive category
\mathcal{T}
with an endofunctor
\Sigma:\mathcal{T}\rightarrow\mathcal{T}
, and a collection of
exact left-triangles
, satisfying certain axioms. They enjoy about half of the usual properties of triangulated categories. Since an exact left-triangle will not induce long exact sequences of Hom spaces (as opposed to a distinguished triangle in a triangulated category), we could not expect using graded-split short exact sequences to enhance it.

On the other hand, based on the notion of
homotopy short exact sequence
, the author proposed in
[
3
]
the notion of
stable dg category
to enhance triangulated categories. A homotopy short exact sequence is at the same time homotopy left exact and homotopy right exact. One could therefore expect to enhance left triangulated categories based on the notion of homotopy left exact sequence. This was shown by Mochizuki in
[
6
]
.
A dg category
\mathcal{A}
is
left stable
(= finitely complete)
[
6
, Definition 2.1]
provided that it admits homotopy kernels, i.e. for any morphism
j:B\rightarrow C
in
Z^{0}(\mathcal{A})
, it has a homotopy kernel

Example 2.8
.

Let
\mathcal{A}
be an additive category with kernels, regarded as a dg category concentrated in degree zero.

3.
Exact dg categories with enough projectives

With the notion of left stable dg categories at hand, we could state the results in
[
1
, 6.37]
more precisely.
Let
(\mathcal{B},\mathcal{S})
be a connective exact dg cateogry with enough projectives. We denote by
\mathcal{P}
the full dg subcategory of
\mathcal{B}
consisting of the projectives. Our main aim in this subsection is to prove the following.

Theorem 3.1
.

The dg quotient
\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P}
is left stable.

Proof.

We first show that the dg quotient functor
\pi:\mathcal{B}\rightarrow\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P}
sends conflations to homotopy left exact sequences.
Let
X
be a conflation in
\mathcal{B}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces A}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces B}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces C.}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces f}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces h}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces j}

We denote by
N
its totalization. Put
V=\Sigma^{-1}\mathrm{Cone}(j)
.
We have
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{B})}(\mathcal{P},\Sigma^{i}N)=0
for
i\in\mathbb{Z}
.
Then for
A^{\prime}\in\mathcal{B}
and
i\in\mathbb{Z}
, we have
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{B})}(A^{\prime},\Sigma^{i}N)\xrightarrow{{}_{\sim}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{B})/\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{P})}(A^{\prime},\Sigma^{i}N)
.
Since
X
is a conflation in
\mathcal{B}
, we have
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{B})}(A^{\prime},\Sigma^{i}N)=0
for
A^{\prime}\in\mathcal{B}
and
i\leq-1
.
Hence we have
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{B})/\mathrm{tr}(\mathcal{P})}(A^{\prime},\Sigma^{i}N)=0
for
A^{\prime}\in\mathcal{B}
and

.
So we have

for
A^{\prime}\in\mathcal{B}
and

. It follows that the image of
X
in
\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P}
is homotopy left exact.

We have
H^{0}(\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P})=H^{0}(\mathcal{B})/[\mathcal{P}]
. Let

be a morphism in
Z^{0}(\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P})
. Then the homotopy equivalence class
\overline{g}
of

is a morphism in
H^{0}(\mathcal{B})/[\mathcal{P}]
represented by a morphism
\overline{h}
in
H^{0}(\mathcal{B})
with

a morphism in
Z^{0}(\mathcal{B})
.
So
\overline{\pi(h)}
is isomorphic to
\overline{g}
as objects in the morphism category
\mathrm{Mor}(H^{0}(\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P}))
. So
g
is isomorphic to
\pi(h)
in
H^{0}\mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{P})
.
Hence, we may assume that

is given by a morphism in
Z^{0}(\mathcal{B})
.
Since
\mathcal{B}
has enough projectives, we have the following diagram in
\mathcal{B}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces E}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces D}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces B}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces E}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces P}
{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces C.}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces g}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces r}
\scriptstyle{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces s}

We obtain a conflation
