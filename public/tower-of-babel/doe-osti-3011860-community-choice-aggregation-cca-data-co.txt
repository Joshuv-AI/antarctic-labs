Introduction to the
Annual Technical Report

  Voluntary Power Markets Report

• Voluntary renewable power refers to renewable electricity
  voluntarily purchased by retail electricity customers
• The voluntary power market refers to the suite of products
  that allow customers to procure renewable power:
• Utility renewable pricing • Unbundled RECs
  programs • Community choice aggregation
• Utility renewable contracts • Power purchase agreements
• Competitive suppliers

                  (2024 data)

- Word form available
- 4 sections: Program, Residential, Non-residential, Power Source above RPS
- Note: All sales (MWh) should represent renewable energy sales above and beyond
  Renewable Portfolio Standards (RPS), i.e., only those Renewable Energy Certificates
  (RECs) retired exclusively on behalf of program participants.

 Privacy Notices
Note:
All data collected in our annual Voluntary Energy Procurement survey is secure and
protected internally at NREL and is not used for any purposes outside of our project.
Data analyzed and published in our technical report is completely anonymized so no
personally identifiable data is shared in any way outside of our project team.
Awardees of our annual “Top 10” lists have the option to be acknowledged publicly or
decline. If you have questions, please reach out to dani.salyer@nrel.gov.

Privacy and Security Notices
We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you choose to provide it
to us. If you provide us with personally identifiable information—for example, by
sending an email or filling out a form and submitting it through our website or survey
platform —we use that personally identifiable information to respond to your message
and provide you with the information and services that you request. All uses of that
personally identifiable information will be described on the web page or platform
containing the form. Submitting voluntary information constitutes your consent to the
use of the personally identifiable information for the stated purpose.
