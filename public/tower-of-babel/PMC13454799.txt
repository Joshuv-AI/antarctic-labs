Text correction

In the Methods section, within the description of the PICO framework, the labels “Population:” and “Comparator:” were inadvertently removed.

A correction has been made to the section:

2 Materials and methods, 2.2 Systematic review

“Pediatric patients, including fetal, neonatal, infant, and childhood cases, diagnosed with PUV complicated by bladder rupture, with or without urinary ascites.”

It should read: “Population: Pediatric patients, including fetal, neonatal, infant, and childhood cases, diagnosed with PUV complicated by bladder rupture, with or without urinary ascites.”

“Not applicable, as the available evidence consists predominantly of descriptive case reports and no control group was expected.”

The original article has been updated.
