President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill [1] (12 January 1945, d53)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 12 January 1945.

Number 699, Personal and Top Secret, from the President to the Prime Minister.

Your 884.[2] It is regretted that projected business here for the Secretary of State will prevent Stettinius’ arrival Malta before January 31.

It is my present intention to send Harry Hopkins to England some days in advance of the Malta date to talk with you and Eden.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Ante, p. 33.
