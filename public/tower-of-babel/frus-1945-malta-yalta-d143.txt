President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill [1] (22 October 1944, d143)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 22 October 1944.

Number 632, Personal and Top Secret, from the President to the Prime Minister.

Your 795,[2] 796,[2] 797,[2] 799,[2] 800[2] and 801[3] received.

I am delighted to learn of your success at Moscow in making progress toward a compromise solution of the Polish problem.

When and if a solution is arrived at, I should like to be consulted as to the advisability from this point of view of delaying its publication for about two weeks. You will understand.

Everything is going well here at the present time.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .
