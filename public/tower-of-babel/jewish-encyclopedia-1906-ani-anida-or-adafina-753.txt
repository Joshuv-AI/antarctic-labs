Among Spanish Jews, a dish composed of beans, peas, fat meat, and eggs, placed in an oven over Friday night, and eaten at the Sabbath meal. The Ani , called by the German Jews Schalet , was regarded by the Inquisition as conclusive evidence of Jewish practises against Maranos.
