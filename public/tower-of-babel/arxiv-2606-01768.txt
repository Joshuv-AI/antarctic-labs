Semileptonic Decays of Λ → p ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ \Lambda\to p\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell} in the Light-Front Dynamics
Chong-Chung Lih and Chao-Qiang Geng

Abstract
We investigate the exclusive semileptonic decays of Λ → p ​ ℓ − ​ ν ¯ ℓ ​ ( ℓ = e , μ ) \Lambda\to p\ell^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\ell}~(\ell=e,\mu) within the Standard Model using the light-front quark model. The transition form factor behaviors of Λ → p \Lambda\to p are obtained from the effective treatment of nonvalence contributions in addition to the valence ones in the Drell-Yan-West frame due to the Bethe-Salpeter formalism. Based on these form factors, we obtain that the branching ratios of Λ → p ​ e − ​ ν ¯ e \Lambda\to pe^{-}\bar{\nu}_{e} and p ​ μ − ​ ν ¯ μ p\mu^{-}\bar{\nu}_{\mu} , including nonvalence contributions, are around 8.32 × 10 − 4 8.32\times 10^{-4} and 1.31 × 10 − 4 1.31\times 10^{-4} , which are consistent with the latest measurements from the BESIII Collaboration, respectively. Our results indicate that nonvalence contributions can play a non-negligible role in the semileptonic baryon decays within the light-front framework.

We have calculated all six form factors of the

LF coordinate system and extended them to the entire physical region.
These form factors are obtained based on the B-S formalism for the case

These form factors include nonvalence contributions and agree well

, which are close to the BESIII experimental results, respectively.
However, we have obtained that

R^{e\mu}=\frac{{\cal B}(\Lambda\to p\,\mu^{-}\,\bar{\nu}_{\mu})}{{\cal B}(\Lambda\to p\,e^{-}\,\bar{\nu}_{e})}

.
The present analysis indicates that nonvalence contributions play an important role
for a consistent light-front description of semileptonic baryon decays
in the physical timelike region.
Our framework may also be extended to
other semileptonic baryonic transitions
where nonvalence contributions become important in the physical timelike region.
