Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 850th meeting, on 31 May 1960

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Togolese Republic <small>[1]</small>,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Togolese Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Ibid., Supplement for April, May and June 1960, document S/4318.</small>

1960
