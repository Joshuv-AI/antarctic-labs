No. 1184 Prime Minister Churchill to President Truman

[Extract[1]]

most secret

Whitehall [Babelsberg], July 25, 1945.

My Dear Mr. President, I thank you for your letter of July 25,[2] and I await the Memorandum on Lend-Lease which you are kindly having prepared.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Yours very sincerely,

Winston S. Churchill
