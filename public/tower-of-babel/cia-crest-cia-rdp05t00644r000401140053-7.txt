Dear Admiral Turner:

The Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States has scheduled
its annual dinner honoring Members of Congress for Tuesday,

March 4, 1980. This dinner, which will be held at the Sheraton
Washington Hotel at 7 P.M., will be preceded by a reception at
6 P.M. Attending this function will be Members of Congress,
government and military leaders, as well as national and state
officers of the Veterans of Foreign Wars. The total attendance
will exceed 2,300 persons.

Highlighting the dinner this year will be the presentation of the
Veterans of Foreign Wars Congressional Award to the Honorable G. V.
(Sonny) Montgomery, U. S. House of Representatives, for his "out-
standing service to our nation." The influence of Congressman
Montgomery stems from his reputation for intelligence and expertise
He is, without question, one of the hardest working members of the
House of Representatives, who, as you know, has always conducted
himself with utmost dignity and, in our opinion, has added much
prestige to the deliberations of the U. S. House of Representatives

The purpose of this letter is to invite you to attend this annual
dinner as a HEAD TABLE GUEST . Your presence would add much to
this important occasion and I know that Congressman Montgomery
and the national and state officers of the Veterans of Foreign
Wars of the United States would be most pleased if you are able to
attend.

Sincerely ,
