On logarithmic Poisson cohomology of a degenerate Poisson bivector in affine plane. Abstract
. In this paper, we show that for a given degenerate bivector π = y n ∂ x ∧ ∂ y \pi=y^{n}\partial_{x}\wedge\partial_{y} with n > 1 n>1 , the classical Poisson cohomology group and the logarithmic Poisson cohomology group along the ideal ℐ = y n ​ 𝔽 ​ [ x , y ] \mathcal{I}=y^{n}\mathbb{F}[x,y] are isomorphics in every degree. This result follows from determination of the logarithmic Hamiltonian opreator and the logarithmic Poisson cochain complex in order to compute the cohomological invariants associated to π \pi . 𝔽 \mathbb{F} is the field of characteristic zero.
