What does ETIPP
Success Look Like?

 3

What Can You Expect From ETIPP?
• In depth analysis of various technologies and solutions your community wishes to pursue to
  achieve stated goals.
 • Analysis can include pros and cons, expected benefits and trade offs, best case system
 sizing, and reliability requirements.
• High level analysis of solution costs and when the community could expect to breakeven
• Overview of relevant policies that help or hinder potential projects and potential next steps to
  address these.
• Relationship building with your local utility to further determine what projects may be viable.
• Access to the national lab complex and part of the larger ETIPP world of like-minded
  communities.
• Data, data, and more data. ETIPP provides your community with the information necessary to
  make informed decisions and next steps.

 4

What Not To Expect From ETIPP?

• Fully realized projects. ETIPP analysis provides the information for a
  community to move forward in developing a project.
 • Sometimes ETIPP results may not support the project your
 community set out to do. This is OK! While you may not move
 in the direction you expected, you now know a direction NOT to
 move in.

 5

So, What Does ETIPP Success Look Like?
• An understanding of what is possible, what the community would be able to
  support, and direction on next steps to move forward and turn ETIPP analysis
  into real change for your community.
• Expanded relationships with your utility to make informed decisions on locally
  generated energy and what is physically possible for your community.
• Data and explored concepts to potentially develop an RFP, engage with local
  contractors, and know what to expect when project design begins.

What is Strategic Energy Planning?
 What does the process do?

 • Considers current reality and
 leverages local resources.

 • Clarifies community energy
 A strategic energy plan is a roadmap to achieving
 priorities
 community energy goals in both the near and long
 term. The goals are determined by interested parties’ • Identifies high impact potential

What Goes Into an Energy Plan?
• Plans are developed around the needs and goals of the community.
• Plans include common elements, such as energy focus areas, goals, defined projects or project ideas, and strategies for project
 implementation.

The following elements are critical to include in a
strategic energy plan:
• Energy objectives and energy vision
• Final energy goals
• Summary of Baseline Assessment
• Community energy Strength, Weakness, Opportunities,
 Threats (SWOT) analysis
• A prioritized list of programs and projects Identified
 funding and financing mechanisms for prioritized projects
• Recommendations and strategies for energy project
 implementation.

• Plan for tracking progress towards goals

Block Island Key Takeaways
1. Sustainability: All scenarios reflect meeting 100% of BI’s electricity needs with a portfolio of on-
 island and off-island energy resources.
2. Self-reliance: There is no scenario that will allow BI to be 100% self-reliant; instead, on-island
 resources could meet at most 45% of BI’s annual electricity needs.
3. Reliability: On-island resources can fully meet BI electricity demands in at most 30% of the hours
 of the year, even with battery storage devices.
4. Affordability: BI supply costs across all scenarios are lower than current supply costs and exhibit
 modest variability.
5. Roof-top vs. Community Solar: Community solar deployment is more controllable than roof-top
 solar but comes with an added cost if electricity must be curtailed.
6. Status Quo vs. Established Goals Demand Forecast: Achieving Established Goals will require
 deliberate and concerted action but will likely drive down BIUD rates, even more than Status Quo.

This presentation was funded by the U.S. Department of Energy’s Energy Technology Innovation Partnership
Project (ETIPP). ETIPP is a community-led technical support program for coastal, remote, and island communities
to access unique solutions and increase energy resilience. By uniting federal agencies, national laboratories,
regional organizations, and community stakeholders, ETIPP provides tailored technical support to help
communities achieve affordable reliable solutions to their energy system challenges. This collaborative model
leverages the combined expertise and resources of its partners to deliver comprehensive, practical solutions that
align with local needs.
