Global space correlations of polarization, charge density, and electric field in electrolytes under the fixed-potential condition
Akira Onuki

Abstract
We examine the thermal fluctuations of the polarization 𝒑 p , the charge density ρ \rho , and the electric field 𝑬 E in dilute electrolytes inserted between pararell metallic electrodes, where we fix the applied potential difference Φ a \Phi_{a} between the two electrodes. If the film thickness H H is shorter than the Debye screening length κ − 1 \kappa^{-1} , the space correlation of the polarization p z p_{z} and the electric field E z E_{z} along the surface normal (in the z z direction) acuire global components inversely proportional to the film volume V V , which vary slowly along the z z axis and are homogeneous in the x ​ y xy plane. The areal charge density on each electrode surface also has a component homogeneous on the surface, which produces the global electric fluctuations. On the other hand, if H H much exceeds κ − 1 \kappa^{-1} , the global correlations of p z p_{z} and ρ \rho become small in the bulk region outside the electric double layers, but that of E z E_{z} remains almost unchanged by ions in the whole cell at fixed Φ a \Phi_{a} . The dielectric constant ϵ eff \epsilon_{\rm eff} depends on H H and κ \kappa and is expressed in terms of the fluctuation variances of p z p_{z} and ρ \rho and that of the noblocal surface charge density at fixed Φ a \Phi_{a} . Keywords : Electrolytes, fixed potential condition, nonlocal correlations, polarization PACS: 05.20.Jj, 05.40.-a, 42.25.Ja, 82.45.-h
