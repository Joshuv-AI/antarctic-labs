Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 850th meeting, on 26 January 1960
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of Cameroun [1],
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Cameroun be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for January, February and March 1960, document S/4256.
