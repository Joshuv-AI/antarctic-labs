MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director(Support)

SUBJECT: Comments on the Survey by the Inspector General

of the Office of Communications

1. I forward herewith, my comments on the recommendations contained
in the Inspector General's report. You will note that I have made a statement
on each recommendation including those in which the Inspector General recom-
mends action by elements other than the Office of Communications.

2. I believe that in a few instances where I indicated non-concurrence
that the statements indicate a need for clarification of facts with the Inspector
General rather than disagreement. In one or two instances with this in mind,

I have specifically indicated a desire to have a discussion with the Inspector
General.

3. I wish to record my admiration for the members of the Inspector General's
staff who conducted the survey. They carried out what I am certain they found to
be a difficult task due to the complexities and technical facets of our work in an
extremely objective and thoroughly competent manner. I would appreciate your
bringing these remarks to the attention of the Inspector General.
