German scholar; lived at Posen at the end of the eighteenth century and at the beginning of the nineteenth. He was a shoḥeṭ at Posen, and made a careful study of Ibn Ezra's commentary on the Bible, on which he wrote a supercommentary entitled "Abi 'Ezer" (The Father of Help), published at Posen, 1802.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2311; Perles, Gesch. der Juden in Posen, reprinted from Monatsschrift, xiv. 262. S. I. Br.
