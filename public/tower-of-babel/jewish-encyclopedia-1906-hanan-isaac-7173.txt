Turkish rabbi; lived at Salonica about the middle of the eighteenth century. He was the author of a work called "Bene Yiẓḥaḳ," homilies and responsa (Salonica, 1757).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 407 (where Hanan occurs as "Honein"); Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 612; Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, p. 102. K. M. Sel.
