NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN) HAS ORDERED A STANDDOWN FROM
MILITARY ACTIVITY TO GIVE MARIO ((BAENA)) SOARES, SECRETARY GENERAL
OF THE ORGANIZATION OF THE AMERICAN STATES (OAS), THE OPPORTUNITY TO
ARRANGE AN END TO THE FIGHTING DURING HIS IMPENDING VISIT TO SAN
SALVADOR.
2. CES GENERAL SECRETARY JORGE

SHAFIK ((HANDAL)) TOLD HI THAT FMLN REPRESENTATIVES
HAVE SPOKEN WITH BAENA SOARES IN WASHINGTON, D.C., AND PROMISED HIM
THE FMLN TROOPS WILL NOT ENGAGE IN ANY OFFENSIVE MILITARY ACTIVITY
WHILE BAENA SOARES TRIES TO ARRANGE A CEASE-FIRE AND SUBSEQUENT
NEGOTIATIONS ACCEPTABLE TO THE FMLN. HANDAL SAID THE FMLN WANTS
BAENA SOARES TO TALK TO PRESIDENT ALFREDO ((CRISTIANI)) AND THE U.S.
AMBASSADOR TO EL SALVADOR DURING HIS STAY IN SAN SALVADOR. (FIELD
COMMENT: BAENA SOARES IS SCHEDULED TO ARRIVE IN SAN SALVADOR ON 20

NOVEMBER, ACCORDING TO REPORTING. )
