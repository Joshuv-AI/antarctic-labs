German rabbi; lived at Halberstadt in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. He was the author of "Nezer ha-Ḳodesh," glosses on "Bereshit Rabbah" (Jessnitz, 1719). A long responsum, addressed to Ẓebi Hirsch Ashkenazi and quoted in "She'elat Ya'beẓ" (§ 2), Altona, 1739, was written by him.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1281; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 336. D. M. Sel.
