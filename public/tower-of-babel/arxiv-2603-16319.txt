1.
Introduction

Let
\psi:(M,g)\rightarrow(N,h)
be a smooth map between Riemannian manifolds of dimensions
m
and
n
, respectively. It is well known that
\psi
is harmonic (see
[
2
,
7
]
) if its tension field
\tau(\psi)
vanishes; that is,

\tau(\psi)=\operatorname{Tr}_{g}(\nabla d\psi)=0.

A natural generalization of harmonic maps is the
H
-tensional map, introduced in
[
8
]
. A map
\psi
is said to be
H
-tensional if its tension field
\tau(\psi)
satisfies

\Delta^{\psi}\tau(\psi)=0,

where
\Delta^{\psi}
is the rough Laplacian. In
[
8
]
, several properties of
H
-tensional maps are investigated.
In particular, for an isometric immersion
\iota:M^{m}\rightarrow N^{n}
with mean curvature vector
H
, we introduce the notion of
H
-tensional submanifolds by requiring that
\Delta^{\iota}\tau(\iota)=\Delta^{\iota}(mH)=0
. We prove that an
H
-tensional submanifold
M
is minimal if it satisfies one of the following conditions:

(1)

M
is a curve;

(2)

M
has constant mean curvature;

(3)

M
is compact;

(4)

M
is a hypersurface with at most two distinct principal curvatures in a real space form;

(5)

M
is a pseudo-umbilical submanifold of dimension
m\neq 4
;

(6)

M
is a totally umbilical hypersurface in an Einstein space.

Motivated by these nonexistence results for nonminimal
H
-tensional submanifolds, the following conjectures were proposed in
[
8
]
:

Conjecture 1.
The only
H
-tensional submanifolds of real space forms are the minimal ones.

Conjecture 2.
The only complete
H
-tensional submanifolds of real space forms are the minimal ones.

These conjectures generalize, respectively, Chen’s biharmonic conjecture (see
[
4
]
) and its global version (see
[
1
]
). For recent developments related to these problems, we refer to
[
4
]
and the references therein.

In this paper, by adapting a method developed by Defever in
[
6
]
and by Balmuş, Montaldo, and Oniciuc

3

, we prove that any
H
-tensional hypersurface with at most three distinct principal curvatures in a

-dimensional space form is minimal (Theorem

). This result supports Conjecture 1.

We write the covariant derivatives as

From the Codazzi equations for

Conclusion

In this work, we investigated the geometric properties of
H
-tensional hypersurfaces
M
in a
4
-dimensional space form
N^{4}(c)
. By analyzing the characterization (
2.1
) and the resulting Codazzi and Gauss equations, we established a rigorous link between the mean curvature
\alpha
and the principal curvatures of the manifold.

The derivation of the key polynomial relation (
2.24
) and the subsequent elimination process showed that a non-constant mean curvature leads to an algebraic contradiction. Consequently, we have proved that such hypersurfaces must have constant mean curvature. Given the
H
-tensional condition, this constant must vanish, leading to the final classification: every
H
-tensional hypersurface in
N^{4}(c)
is minimal.
