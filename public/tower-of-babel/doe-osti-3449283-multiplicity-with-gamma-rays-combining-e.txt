 We built a NaI detector array with nanosecond timing resolution and have observed the correlations be-
 tween direct-from-fission gamma-rays and gamma-rays from (n, n′ γ) and (n, γ) processes. Direct-from-fission
 gamma-rays and (n, n′ γ) correlations happen on time scales of ≲ 10 ns consistent with the time resolution
 of the detector array; (n, γ) correlations typically show time scales of ≈ 100 µs as expected. Most exciting,
 we have shown that weak gamma-ray lines that are otherwise buried by the continuum can be identified by
 combining energy and timing filters.
