3« Discussion

a. Executive Order 10501 Section 7* (c), implies recognition of an exception
regarding the dissemination and reproduction of classified defense information
ori g i nat i n g In another department or agency by the Central Intelligence Agency.

In the past the Agency has followed the practice of obtaining the consent of the
originating Agency parlor to dissemination of that material to another department
or agency. Such material has been microfilmed in order to accomplish the
Initial supply and subsequent retrieval necessary for Intelligence production.
Recognition of the ability to reprieve rapidly is evidenced by the large somber
of requests for the Interchange pf copies of documents by members of the
community.

b. It is evident from the tenor of the Rational Security Act of 1947, the
Executive Order 10501, and the BSCXD's that there should be a free and continuing
Interchange of Intelligence and information within the Intelligence Conmunlty.

c. To accomplish this in & dexterous manner requires that all the IAC
Member Agencies be able to reproduce and disseminate the int ellig ence and
Information in their respective files as necessary in the interests of the
national defense.

d. It should be noted that Executive Order 10*501, Section 7, (c) requires
the consent of the originating aigency when reproducing Top Secret and Secret
material, thus implying that thetre is no prohibition of reproduction of Con-
fidential material, Adminstratly^ly Controlled material or Unclassified material.
