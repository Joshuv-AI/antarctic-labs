  Reducing Randomized Quantum Algorithm Cost
 Scientific Achievement
 • Importance Sampling (IS) is shown to reduce resources
 required to run randomized quantum algorithms.
 Significance and Impact
 Randomized protocols power many quantum algorithms,
 including Hamiltonian simulation, noise mitigation, and
 observable estimation. Our methods reduce cost in any
 such application, with no tradeoff.
