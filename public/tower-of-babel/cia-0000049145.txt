WAS D'AUBUISSON INVOLVED?

CITING A CIA REPORT, U.S. OFFICIALS TOLD NEWSWEEK LAST WEEK
THAT ON THE NIGHT BEFORE SIX JESUIT PREISTS WERE NURDERED IN EL
SALVADOR LAST MONTH, RIGHTIST POLITICAL LEADER ROBERTO D'AUBUTSSON
RAILED AGAINST SEVERAL OF THE VICTIMS TO HIS ADVISERS AND TOLD THEM
THAT SOMETHING HAD TO BE DONE ABOUT THE PRIESTS. ONE OF THE PRIESTS
SPECIFICALLY MENTIONED, ACCORDING TO THE REPORT, WAS FATHER IGNACIO
ELLACURIA, WHO WAS KILLED ALONG WITH SEVEN OTHERS. ASKED ABOUT
D'AUBUISSON'S REPORTED COMMENTS, CIA SPOKESMAN (AN OFFICER IN THE
PUBLIC AFFAIRS OFFICE) REPLIED, "I CAN'T HELP YOU ON THAT."

IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE KILLINGS, SUSPICION FOCUSED ON THE
SALVADORAN RIGHT. D'AUBUISSON HIMSELF IS WIDELY BELIEVED TO HAVE
BEEN INVOLVED IN BOTH THE RIGHT-WING DEATH SQUADS OF THE EARLY 1980S
AND THE 1980 ASSASSINATION OF ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARNULFO ROMERO. HE
DENIES THE CHARGES. EARLIER THIS YEAR VICE PRESIDENT DAN QUAYLE
TRAVELED TO SAN SALVADOR AND WARNED D'AUBUIJSSON ABOUT HUMAN-RIGHTS
ABUSES. STILL, D'AUBUISSON WAS PLACED BACK ON THE GUEST LIST AT THE
U.S. EMBASSY THIS SUMMER.

PROOF OF ANY D'AUBUISSON ROLE IN THE MURDERS COULD SERTOUSLY
COMPLICATE THE POLITICAL FORTUNES OF PRESIDENT ALFREDO CRISTIANI,
BECUASE D'AUBUISSON IS THE IDEOLOGICAL LEADER OF THE RULING ARENA
