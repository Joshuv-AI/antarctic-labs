Ab det din in Bunzlau, Bohemia; born 1764; died in Bunzlau May 6, 1842. He wrote "Mat'amme Yiẓḥaḳ," songs, melodies, and sayings, which was published by his son Yom-Ṭob in Prague in 1843.

Bibliography: Busch, Jahrbuch, i. 176; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 323; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2651. E. C. S. O.
