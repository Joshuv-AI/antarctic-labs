Headline: Communique Issued on Killings in El Salvador
Source Line: PA1611162789 Madrid EFE in Spanish 1536 GMT 16 Nov 89

FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:

1. [Text] Madrid, 16 Nov (EFE)--The Spanish Government, through the
Foreign Affairs Ministry, today expressed ‘‘consternation’’ over the
‘‘horrendous murder’’ of six Jesuit priests and two women in San
Salvador today. .
2. In an official communique ‘‘The Diplomatic Press Office expresses
the Spanish Government’s consternation over these horrendous murders,
which casts shame on those who instigated, encouraged, and committed
them.’ ’

3. The communique continues: ‘'The Spanish Government, through the
Foreign Affairs Ministry, has asked the Government of the Republic of
El Salvador to conduct a thorough investigation into the incident.’’

4. The communique adds: ‘‘This escalation of violence demonstrates
once again the need for an immediate cease-fire and the prompt
initiation of serious negotiations to end so much suffering in that
fraternal country.’/

5. The official Spanish Government communique was referring to the
Massacre perpetrated early this morning at the Central American
University in El Salvador. Acccording to the Salvadoran Curia, 30
men in uniform murdered six Jesuit priests and two women who were
asleep in a residence next to the University grounds.

Approved tor Reramse,
