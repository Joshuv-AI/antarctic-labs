Strong convergence rate of Euler-Maruyama approximations in temporal-spatial Hölder-norms for Lévy-driven stochastic differential equations
Vu Thi Hue, Ngoc Khue Tran, Hoang-Long Ngo

Abstract
This paper studies the error between the exact solution and it’s Euler-Maruyama approximation in temporal-spatial Hölder-norms for Lévy-driven stochastic differential equations.

Introduction

On a complete probability space
(\Omega,\mathbb{F},\mathbb{P})
, we consider the process
X=(X_{t})_{t\geq 0}
solution to the following one-dimensional stochastic differential equation (SDE) with jumps

dX_{t}=\mu(X_{t})dt+\sigma(X_{t})dW_{t}+\gamma\left(X_{t-}\right)dZ_{t},

(1)

where
W=(W_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is a standard Brownian motion, and
Z=(Z_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is a centered pure jump Lévy process whose Lévy measure
\nu
satisfies
\int_{\mathbb{R}}(1\wedge|z|^{2})\nu(dz)<+\infty
. Two processes
W
and
Z
are assumed to be independent. The natural filtration
(\mathbb{F}_{t})_{t\geq 0}
is generated by two processes
W
and
Z
and
X_{0}
is
\mathbb{F}_{0}
-measurable. Let
\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}_{0})
be the Borel
\sigma
-algebra on
\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}_{0}
, where
\mathbb{R}_{0}:=\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}
. The Lévy-Itô decomposition of
Z
is defined by

Z_{t}=\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}z(N(ds,dz)-\nu(dz)ds),

for any
t\geq 0
, where
N(dt,dz)
is a Poisson random measure on the measurable space
(\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}_{0},\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}_{+}\times\mathbb{R}_{0}))
associated with the intensity measure
\nu(dz)dt
. That is,

N([0,t]\times A):=\sum_{0\leq u\leq t}{\bf 1}_{\{\Delta Z_{u}\in A\}},\text{ for any }t>0,\text{ and }A\in\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}_{0}).

Here, the jump size of
Z
at instant
u
is defined as
\Delta Z_{u}:=Z_{u}-Z_{u-}:=Z_{u}-\lim_{r\uparrow u}Z_{r}
for any
u>0
,
\Delta Z_{0}:=0
.
The compensated Poisson random measure associated with
N(dt,dz)
is denoted by
\widetilde{N}(dt,dz):=N(dt,dz)-\nu(dz)dt
.

The aim of this paper is to extend the work in
[
8
]
to the SDEs driven by both Brownian motion and pure jump Lévy processes.
Throughout this paper, we suppose that
there exists a positive constant
L
such that

|(\xi(x)-\xi(y))-(\xi(\widetilde{x})-\xi(\widetilde{y}))|\leq L|(x-y)-(\widetilde{x}-\widetilde{y})|+L(|x-y|+|\widetilde{x}-\widetilde{y}|)|x-\widetilde{x}|,

for all
\xi\in\{\mu,\sigma,\gamma\}
and
x,y,\widetilde{x},\widetilde{y}\in\mathbb{R}
.
This condition implies the global Lipschitz continuity of the coefficients
\mu,\sigma
and
\gamma
, which ensures the existence of a unique càdlàg and adapted process
X=(X_{t})_{t\geq 0}
solution to equation (
1
) on the filtered probability space
(\Omega,\mathbb{F},(\mathbb{F}_{t})_{t\geq 0},\mathbb{P})
, see
[
1
]
(Theorem 6.2.3).

The integral equation (
1
) now can be written as

X_{t}=x_{0}+\int_{0}^{t}\mu(X_{s})ds+\int_{0}^{t}\sigma(X_{s})dW_{s}+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\gamma\left(X_{s-}\right)z\widetilde{N}(ds,dz),\quad t\geq 0.

The paper is organized as follows. We present preliminary results in Section
2
followed by the statements and the proofs of our main results in Section
3
.

For the sake of convenience, we denote
m_{p}:=\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}|z|^{p}\nu(dz)
for
p>0
. We denote by
\mathbb{E}
the expectation with respect to
\mathbb{P}
and by
C^{2}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})
the set of bounded first and second order derivatives. Let
C^{1,2}([0,T]\times\mathbb{R},[0,\infty))
be the class of functions which are continuously differentiable once with respect to the time variable and twice with respect to the spatial variable, and
\mathcal{L}([0,T],[0,\infty))
denotes the set of measurable functions from
[0,T]
to
[0,\infty)
. For all
x,y\in\mathbb{R}
we denote by
x\land y\in\mathbb{R}
the real number which satisfiles that
x\land y=\min\{x,y\}
.

Lemma 2.2
.

(A Lyapunov estimate)
Let
X
be the solution to the equation (
1
).
Let
V\in C^{1,2}([0,T]\times\mathbb{R},[0,\infty)),\alpha\in\mathcal{L}([0,T],[0,\infty))
with
\int_{0}^{T}\alpha(t)dt<\infty,
let
\tau:\Omega\to[0,T]
be a stopping time, the coefficients
\mu,\sigma,\gamma
be continuous functions and let
X:[0,T]\times\Omega\to\mathbb{R}
be a càdlàg and adapted stochastic process with sample paths satisfying

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})+\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})\mu(X_{t\land\tau})+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})\sigma^{2}(X_{t\land\tau})

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau}+c(X_{t\land\tau})z)-V(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})-\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})\gamma(X_{t\land\tau})z\right]\nu(dz)

\displaystyle\leq\alpha(t\land\tau)V(t\land\tau,X_{t\land\tau})\quad\mathbb{P}\text{-a.s. for all }t\in[0,T].

(2)

Then,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[V(\tau,X_{\tau})\right]\leq\exp\left(\int_{0}^{T}\alpha(s)ds\right)\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})]\in[0,\infty].

Proof.

First, w.l.o.g. we assume that
\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})]<\infty.
Since
X
is the càdlàg stochastic process and the coefficients
\mu,\sigma,\gamma
are continuous functions, we have

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{T}\left(|\mu(X_{s})|+\sigma^{2}(X_{s})+m_{2}\gamma^{2}(X_{s})\right)ds<\infty\quad\mathbb{P}\text{-a.s.},

where recall that
m_{2}=\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}z^{2}\nu(dz)
.
For all
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we define stopping times
\rho_{n}:\Omega\to[0,T]
by

\displaystyle\rho_{n}:

\displaystyle=\inf\Bigg(\{\tau\}\cup\Bigg\{t\in[0,T]:\sup_{s\in[0,t]}V(s,X_{s})+\int_{0}^{t}\Bigg|\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,X_{s})\sigma(X_{s})\Bigg|^{2}du

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left|V(s,X_{s-}+\gamma(X_{s-})z)-V(s,X_{s-})\right|^{2}\nu(dz)ds\geq n\Bigg\}\Bigg).

Then, using Itô’s formula, we have for all
(t,n)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle V(t\land\rho_{n},X_{t\land\rho_{n}})

\displaystyle=V(0,X_{0})+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\Bigg(\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}(s,X_{s})+\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,X_{s})\mu(X_{s})+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}(s,X_{s})\sigma^{2}(X_{s})

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(s,X_{s}+\gamma(X_{s})z)-V(s,X_{s})-\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,X_{s})\gamma(X_{s})z\right]\nu(dz)\Bigg)ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,X_{s})\sigma(X_{s})dW_{s}

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(s,X_{s-}+\gamma(X_{s-})z)-V(s,X_{s-})\right]\widetilde{N}(ds,dz).

Hence, using condition
(
2.2
)
, we get

\displaystyle V(t\land\rho_{n},X_{t\land\rho_{n}})

\displaystyle\leq V(0,X_{0})+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\alpha(s)V(s,X_{s})ds+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,X_{s})\sigma(X_{s})dW_{s}

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{0}^{t\land\rho_{n}}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(s,X_{s-}+\gamma(X_{s-})z)-V(s,X_{s-})\right]\widetilde{N}(ds,dz).

Next, using the definition of
\rho_{n}
and taking expectations of both sides, we obtain

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[V(t\land\rho_{n},X_{t\land\rho_{n}})\right]

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})]+\int_{0}^{t}\alpha(s)\mathbb{E}[{\bf 1}_{(s\leq\rho_{n})}V(s,X_{s})]ds

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})]+\int_{0}^{t}\alpha(s)\mathbb{E}[V(s\land\rho_{n},X_{s\land\rho_{n}})]ds,

for all
(t,n)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{N}
. Note that
\mathbb{E}\left[V(t\land\rho_{n},X_{t\land\rho_{n}})\right]\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})]+n\int_{0}^{t}\alpha(s)ds<\infty
for all
(t,n)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{N}
. Applying Gronwall’s lemma, we get

\mathbb{E}\left[V(t\land\rho_{n},X_{t\land\rho_{n}})\right]\leq\exp\left(\int_{0}^{t}\alpha(s)ds\right)\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})],

for all
(t,n)\in[0,T]\times\mathbb{N}
.
Consequently, taking
n\uparrow\infty
and using Fatou’s lemma, we obtain

\mathbb{E}\left[V(\tau,X_{\tau})\right]\leq\exp\left(\int_{0}^{T}\alpha(s)ds\right)\mathbb{E}[V(0,X_{0})].

Thus, the result follows.
∎

Lemma 2.4
.

(A Gronwall-Lyapunov inequality) Assume Setting
2.3
.
Let
\xi=(\xi_{t})_{t\geq 0}
be the solution to the SDE with jumps
d\xi_{t}=\widehat{\mu}(\xi_{t})dt+\widehat{\sigma}(\xi_{t})dW_{t}+\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{t-})dZ_{t}
with random initial condition
\xi_{0}
. Let
V=(V(s,x))_{s\in[0,T],x\in\mathbb{R}}\in C^{1,2}([0,T]\times\mathbb{R},[0,\infty))
. Let
\alpha,\beta:[0,T]\to[0,\infty)
are measurable functions which satisfy that
\int_{0}^{T}|\alpha_{r}|dr<\infty
and which satisfy that
\mathbb{P}\text{-a.s}
it holds for Lebesgue-almost all
s\in[0,u]
with
u\in[0,T]
that

\displaystyle\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial s}(s,\xi_{s})+\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\mu}(\xi_{s})+\dfrac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\sigma}^{2}(\xi_{s})

\displaystyle\quad+\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(s,\xi_{s}+\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{s})z)-V(s,\xi_{s})-\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{s})z\right]\nu(dz)

\displaystyle\leq\alpha(s)V(s,\xi_{s})+\beta(s).

(3)

Then for all
u\in[0,T]
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[V(u,\xi_{u})]\leq\exp\left(\int_{0}^{u}\alpha(r)dr\right)\left(\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{u}\dfrac{\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}ds\right).

Proof.

For
u\in[0,T]
, let
\tau_{n}:\Omega\to[0,T]
be the functions which satisfy for all
n\in\mathbb{N}
that

\displaystyle\tau_{n}=\inf\left(\{u\}\cup\left\{s\in[0,T]:V(s,\xi_{s})+\int_{0}^{s}\left|\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(r,\xi_{r})\widehat{\sigma}(\xi_{r})\right|^{2}dr\right.\right.

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad+\left.\left.\int_{0}^{s}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left|V(r,\xi_{r-}+\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{r-})z)-V(r,\xi_{r-})\right|^{2}\nu(dz)dr\geq n\right\}\right).

Applying Itô’s formula, we get for every
\epsilon\in(0,\infty)
and
t\in[0,T],

\displaystyle\dfrac{V(t,\xi_{t})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{t}\alpha(r)dr\right)}

\displaystyle=(V(0,\xi_{0}))+\int_{0}^{t}\dfrac{1}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\Bigg(\frac{\partial V}{\partial s}(s,\xi_{s})-(V(s,\xi_{s}))\alpha(s)

\displaystyle\qquad+\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\mu}(\xi_{s})+\frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\sigma}^{2}(\xi_{s})

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(s,\xi_{s}+\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{s})z)-V(s,\xi_{s})-\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{s})z\right]\nu(dz)\Bigg)ds

\displaystyle\qquad+\int_{0}^{t}\dfrac{\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}(s,\xi_{s})\widehat{\sigma}(\xi_{s})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}dW_{s}+\int_{0}^{t}\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\dfrac{V(s,\xi_{s-}+\widehat{\gamma}(\xi_{s-})z)-V(s,\xi_{s-})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\widetilde{N}(ds,dz).

Then replacing
t
with
\tau_{n}\land t
, taking expectations both sides and using (
2.4
), we get

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{V(\tau_{n}\land t,\xi_{\tau_{n}\land t})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{\tau_{n}\land t}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\mathbb{E}\left[\int_{0}^{\tau_{n}\land t}\dfrac{\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]ds

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{t}\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{{\bf 1}_{[0,\tau_{n}]}(s)\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]ds.

Hence, replacing
t
with
T
yields to

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{V(\tau_{n}\land T,\xi_{\tau_{n}\land T})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{\tau_{n}\land T}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]\leq\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{{\bf 1}_{[0,\tau_{n}]}(s)\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]ds.

Moreover,

\xi_{s}
has càdlàg sample path and the fact that
\mathbb{P}(\int_{0}^{T}\widehat{\sigma}^{2}(\xi_{s})ds<\infty)=1
imply that
\mathbb{P}(\lim_{n\to\infty}\tau_{n}=u)=1.

, we obtain

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{V(u,\xi_{u})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{u}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{V(u,\xi_{u-})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{u}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]=\mathbb{E}\left[\liminf_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{V(\tau_{n},\xi_{\tau_{n}})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{\tau_{n}}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{V(\tau_{n},\xi_{\tau_{n}})}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{\tau_{n}}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]

\displaystyle\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\left(\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{T}\mathbb{E}\left[\dfrac{{\bf 1}_{[0,\tau_{n}]}(s)\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}\right]ds\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{u}\dfrac{\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}ds.

Therefore, for all
u\in[0,T]
,

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[V(u,\xi_{u})]\leq\exp\left(\int_{0}^{u}\alpha(r)dr\right)\left(\mathbb{E}[V(0,\xi_{0})]+\int_{0}^{u}\dfrac{\beta(s)}{\exp\left(\int_{0}^{s}\alpha(r)dr\right)}ds\right).

This finishes the desired proof.

3
Strong convergence rate of Euler-Maruyama approximations in temporal-spatial Hölder norms

Throughout this paper, we always assume that the following assumptions hold. Assume that
p\geq 2
,
\mu,\sigma,\gamma\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})
and
V\in C^{2}(\mathbb{R},[1,\infty))

such that for all

A0.

\int_{\mathbb{R}_{0}}\left[V(y+\gamma(x)z)-V(y)-V^{\prime}(y)\gamma(x)z\right]\nu(dz)\leq\dfrac{1}{2}\overline{c}\left(V(x)+V(y)\right).

A1.

|\mu(0)|+|\sigma(0)|+c|x|+\left(|\gamma(0)|+c|x|\right)\sqrt{m_{2}}\leq\left(V(x)\right)^{1/p}.

A2.

|V^{\prime}(x)|\leq\overline{c}\left(V(x)\right)^{1-1/p}.

A3.

|V^{\prime\prime}(x)|\leq\overline{c}\left(V(x)\right)^{1-2/p}.

\max_{\xi\in\{\mu,\sigma,\gamma\}}\left|(\xi(x)-\xi(y))-(\xi(\widetilde{x})-\xi(\widetilde{y}))\right|\leq c|(x-y)-(\widetilde{x}-\widetilde{y})|+b\dfrac{|x-y|+|\widetilde{x}-\widetilde{y}|}{2}|x-\widetilde{x}|.

V(x)=2^{p}\left[1+\left(|\mu(0)|+|\sigma(0)|+|\gamma(0)|\sqrt{m_{2}}\right)^{2}+c^{2}x^{2}(1+\sqrt{m_{2}})^{2}\right]^{p/2}.

The proof of Theorem
3.7
is thus completed.
