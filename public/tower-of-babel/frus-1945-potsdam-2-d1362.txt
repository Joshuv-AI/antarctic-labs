No. 1362 The British Embassy to the Department of State

Aide-Mémoire

His Majesty’s Ambassador at Paris[1] has been instructed to inform the French Government that His Majesty’s Government are now able to agree to the participation of the Soviet Government at the Paris talks about Tangier and to suggest that the French and British Ambassadors in Moscow[2] should be instructed to make a joint formal communication to the Soviet Government, in reply to their representations in Paris and London of July 2nd,[3] inviting them to attend talks in Paris early in August.

Mr. Duff Cooper is further to inform the French Minister for Foreign Affairs[4] that, in line with the previous assurance of His Majesty’s Government, the general question of Tangier has not been discussed at Potsdam and that no reference will be made to Tangier in the communiqué to be issued at the end of the Conference.

G. H. M[iddleton]

Washington , July 28th, 1945.
