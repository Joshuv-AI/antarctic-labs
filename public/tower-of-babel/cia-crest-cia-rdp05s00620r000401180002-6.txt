Dear Admiral Turner:

You may have learned about The Business Council front
your predecessors at the Central Intelligence Agency, and
I feel sure that a quick glance through the enclosed mem-
bership list will bring to mind many friends and acquaint-
ances.

The Chairman of the Council, John deButts, has asked
that I extend his personal invitation to you and Mrs. Turner
to be guests of The Business Council when it meets in May
at The Homestead in Hot Springs, Virginia. Over the years,
it has developed into almost standard operating procedure
that the current Director of Central Intelligence be in-
vited to our Hot Springs meetings, and I must admit that
most of them have attended faithfully and have told me how
much they have enjoyed the sessions and how valuable the
discussions were. Dick Helms, one of our long-standing
regulars, could, I feel sure, fill you in further if you
wanted to touch base with him.
