The President to the Ambassador in Turkey (Steinhardt)[1] (20 November 1943, d145)

[Tunis] 20 November 1943.

From the President to Ambassador Steinhardt

Hope you can come to Cairo either immediately or later. I hope to be there until Saturday November 27 and to be back there about six days later.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House, apparently via military channels; forwarded to the Department of State; and forwarded further by the Department, in paraphrase, in telegram 902, November 20, 1943, 4 p.m., to the Legation at Ankara, marked “Most Secret and Personal” and “U. S. Urgent”.
