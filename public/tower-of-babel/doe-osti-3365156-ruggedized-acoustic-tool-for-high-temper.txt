 Topic Area 1 - High Temperature Tools for Well Integrity Evaluation

Development of a high-temperature well integrity evaluation tool that
(1) can operate in high-temperature EGS boreholes to provide consistent, high-resolution evaluation information,
(2) can operate without active cooling or substantial mitigation of borehole conditions, and
(3) can provide high-fidelity data to adequately characterize conditions that may present safety hazards, risk to the
 environment, and efficacy of wellbore construction for long-term operation.

 Targeting for a ruggedized tool that is able to operate
 for at least 24 hours
 at temperatures greater than 250°C and
 pressures of up to 15,000 psi
 in hostile corrosive EGS environments

 HR3D
 Principle of our approach
Tool based on two technologies developed in our Applied Acoustics Lab at Los Alamos National
Laboratory (LANL).

Both of these technologies were developed independently in the past, with funding from the
GTO and SubTER.

The first one was the Multipurpose Acoustic Sensor (MAS) for Downhole Fluid Monitoring,
while the second was the High-Resolution 3D (HR3D) Acoustic Borehole Integrity Monitoring.

• MAS is an acoustics sensor that was able to determine in real-time and in a single sensor
 package several parameters: temperature, pressure, fluid flow; and fluid properties, such as MAS
 sound speed and fluid composition. The unique design of the sensor allowed its operation at
 very high temperature and pressure.

• HR3D is an imaging system based on a unique acoustic source, ACCObeam (highly
 collimated, high power, and broad-band 10-250 kHz) and advanced image processing for
 borehole integrity monitoring.

  Direct Waveform Inversion (DWI)
 • Layer-by-layer inversion (no global initial model)
 • Recovers velocity + density
  Why it matters:
 − No initial model required
 − Handles multiples + noise
 − Avoids cycle skipping, a major challenge in FWI (Full-Waveform Inversion)

  Provides reliable background models for imaging

 Summary
• There are no commercial acoustic sources that provide a collimated beam over a
 frequency range of 10-250 kHz in a small package that works in different media
• Robust operation software, fast data acquisition
• Improved acoustic source, significantly more powerful than its predecessor (~ two
 orders of magnitude)
• Enhanced receivers sensitivity
• High azimuthal and longitudinal resolution (< 5mm)
• Extended investigation range beyond the wellbore casing
• Ruggedized tool for harsh conditions (high temperature, high pressure,
 corrosiveness, etc.)
 Target by end of FY28:
 ❑ operate for at least 24 hours
 ❑ at temperatures greater than 250°C and
 ❑ pressures of up to 15,000 psi
 ❑ in hostile corrosive EGS environments
