by an end-use certificate from the Yugoslav Chamber of Commerce in Belgrade
to the same effect.

British authorities were suspicious of the shipment for the following
reasons s

1. Purchase was direct from the supplier rather than through
the Exchange.

2. Payment was to be made in dollars.

3. Shipment was to be made via Antwerp.
