Sharp trace inequalities for conformally invariant fractional powers of the sublaplacian on the Heisenberg group and the CR sphere Thanks: The first author was partially supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China(No.12471056).
Qiaohua Yang, Leyuan Yu

Abstract
. We establish sharp Sobolev trace inequalities for conformally invariant fractional powers of the sublaplacian on the Heisenberg group and the CR sphere, extending the corresponding Euclidean results of Einav-Loss, Beckner, and Bez-Machihara-Sugimoto to these non-Euclidean settings. In the limiting case, sharp trace Beckner-Onofri inequalities are also established on the CR sphere. The proofs are based on a duality argument due to Bez-Machihara-Sugimoto, together with the Frank-Lieb sharp form of the Hardy-Littlewood-Sobolev inequalities on the Heisenberg group and the CR sphere. The same approach also yields trace Beckner-Onofri inequalities on the standard sphere.

1.
Introduction

Let
n\geq 3
and denote by
\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}=\{(x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^{n}:x\in\mathbb{R}^{n-1},t>0\}
the upper half-space in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
.
The Sobolev trace inequality on
\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}
, first established by Escobar
[
17
]
(see also Carlen–Loss
[
7
]
), states that for any real-valued function
f
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}
, sufficiently smooth up to the boundary and decaying rapidly enough at infinity, the following holds:

(1.1)

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-1}}|(\tau f)(x)|^{\frac{2(n-1)}{n-2}}dx\right)^{\frac{n-2}{n-1}}\leq C_{n}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}}|\nabla f(x,t)|^{2}dxdt,

where

C_{n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}(n-2)}\Bigl(\frac{\Gamma(n-1)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-1}{2}\bigr)}\Bigr)^{\frac{1}{n-1}},

and
(\tau f)(x)
denotes the trace of
f
on
\partial\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}
.
The equality in (
1.1
) holds if and only if
f
is a multiple of

\Bigl(\frac{1}{(\varepsilon+t)^{2}+|x-x_{0}|^{2}}\Bigr)^{(n-2)/2},\qquad\varepsilon>0,\;x_{0}\in\mathbb{R}^{n-1}.

We remark that, via the seminal extension problem of Caffarelli and Silvestre
[
6
]
, sharp Sobolev trace inequalities on
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}
can be established for
\gamma\in(0,1)
(see Carlen–Loss
[
7
]
for
\gamma=\frac{1}{2}
).
In
[
10
]
, Case extended the framework of Caffarelli and Silvestre by introducing a family of higher-order boundary operators, yielding a corresponding family of sharp Sobolev trace inequalities on
\mathbb{R}^{n+1}_{+}
.
Building on the work of Case et al. (who generalized these results to a conformally invariant class of manifolds and introduced the relevant conformally covariant boundary operators for the Paneitz and sixth-order GJMS cases
[
8
,
9
,
12
]
), the extension to Poincaré-Einstein manifolds was later established by Flynn, Lu, and the second author
[
19
]
.
Analogous results in the CR setting have been developed by Frank et al.
[
22
]
and by Flynn, Lu, and the second author
[
18
]
.
For more information on Sobolev trace inequalities, we refer to
[
1
,
3
,
11
,
14
,
25
,
33
]
and the references therein.

In their work
[
16
]
, Einav and Loss investigated sharp trace inequalities for fractional Sobolev spaces. We briefly summarize the results as follows.
Define the space
D_{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
to be the space of all functions
f\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
whose Fourier transform
\widehat{f}
satisfies

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\widehat{f}(y)|^{2}|y|^{s}dy<\infty.

For
f\in D_{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
, let

\mathbb{R}^{n-m}=\{(x_{1},\dots,x_{n-m},0,0,\dots,0):x_{j}\in\mathbb{R},1\leq j\leq n-m\}

and let
\tau_{m}
denote the restriction operator that maps functions on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
to
\mathbb{R}^{n-m}
.
Einav and Loss established the following sharp Sobolev trace inequality for fractional Sobolev spaces.

Theorem 1.1
.

(
[
16
, Theorem 1.1]
)
Let
0\leq m<n
and
\frac{m}{2}<s<\frac{n}{2}
. For any
f\in D_{2s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
we have

(1.2)

\|\tau_{m}f\|_{L^{\frac{2(n-m)}{n-2s}}}^{2}\leq C_{m,s,n}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|(-\Delta)^{s/2}f|^{2},

where

C_{m,s,n}=2^{-2s}\pi^{-s}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(n/2-s)\Gamma(s-m/2)}{\Gamma(s)\Gamma(n/2+s-m)}\left\{\frac{\Gamma(n-m)}{\Gamma((n-m)/2)}\right\}^{(2s-m)/(n-m)}.

There is equality only if
f(x)
is proportional to

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{m}}\frac{1}{\left(|x^{\prime}|^{2}+|x^{\prime\prime}-y^{\prime\prime}|^{2}\right)^{(n-2s)/2}}\frac{1}{\left(\gamma^{2}+|y^{\prime\prime}-a|^{2}\right)^{(n+2s-2m)/2}}dy^{\prime\prime}

for some
a\in\mathbb{R}^{n-m}
and
\gamma\neq 0
.

In particular, when
s=2
and
m=1
, the inequality (
1.2
) recovers the earlier result of Escobar.

Moreover, Beckner
[
3
]
and Bez-Machihara-Sugimoto
[
4
]
established another type of Sobolev trace inequality for fractional Sobolev spaces.
Unlike Beckner’s approach, Bez–Machihara-Sugimoto employed a dual argument. Specifically, they proved the following theorem:

Theorem 1.2
.

(
[
3
, Theorem 5]
,
[
4
, Theorem 4.1]
)
Let
n\geq 2
and
s\in\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{n}{2}\right)
. For
f\in D_{2s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,

(1.3)

\|\mathcal{R}f\|_{L^{\frac{2(n-1)}{n-2s}}(\mathbb{S}^{n-1})}^{2}\leq 2^{1-2s}\frac{\Gamma(2s-1)\Gamma\left(\frac{n}{2}-s\right)}{\Gamma(s)^{2}\Gamma\left(\frac{n}{2}-1+s\right)}\left(\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)}{2\pi^{\frac{n}{2}}}\right)^{\frac{2s-1}{n-1}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|(-\Delta)^{s/2}f|^{2}.

Here
\mathcal{R}
denotes the operator which restricts
f
on
\mathbb{R}^{n}
to the unit sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n-1}\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
.

By stereographic projection, inequality (
1.3
) is in fact equivalent to the case
m=1
of inequality (
1.2
). This equivalence can be observed from our subsequent proof in the case of Heisenberg group.

The purpose of this paper is to extend Theorems
1.1
and
1.2
to the settings of the Heisenberg group and the CR sphere.
To state our main results, we first introduce some necessary notations.
Recall that the Heisenberg group
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is
\mathbb{C}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}
with elements
u=(z,t)
and group law

uu^{\prime}=(z,t)(w,\mu)=\bigl(z+w,t+\mu+2\operatorname{Im}(z\cdot\overline{w})\bigr),

where

z\cdot\overline{w}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}z_{j}\overline{w}_{j}.

Here
\operatorname{Im}z
and
\operatorname{Re}z
denote, respectively, the imaginary and real parts of
z
.
The Haar measure on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is the usual Lebesgue measure
du=dzdt
.
We denote the convolution on the Heisenberg group by

f*g(u)=\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}f(v)g(v^{-1}u)dv.

The homogeneous norm on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is given by

|u|=|(z,t)|=\bigl(|z|^{4}+t^{2}\bigr)^{1/4},

and dilations of a point
u=(z,t)
are defined as

\delta_{r}(u)=(rz,r^{2}t),\qquad r>0.

Denote by
Q_{n}
the homogeneous dimension of
\mathbb{H}^{n}
. Then

Q_{n}=2n+2.

The sublaplacian on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
is the second-order differential operator

\mathcal{L}_{0}=-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\bigl(X_{j}^{2}+Y_{j}^{2}\bigr),

where

X_{j}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_{j}}+2y_{j}\frac{\partial}{\partial t},\qquad Y_{j}=\frac{\partial}{\partial y_{j}}-2x_{j}\frac{\partial}{\partial t},\quad j=1,\cdots,n.

The conformally invariant fractional powers of the sublaplacian on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
are defined by (see
[
15
,
29
]
)

\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}\;:=\;|2T|^{s/2}\frac{\Gamma\left(\mathcal{L}_{0}|2T|^{-1}+\frac{2+s}{4}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\mathcal{L}_{0}|2T|^{-1}+\frac{2-s}{4}\right)},

where
T=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}
.
The operator
\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}
is left-invariant and satisfies the homogeneity property

(1.4)

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}\bigl(f\circ\delta_{r}\bigr)=r^{s}\bigl(\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}f\bigr)\circ\delta_{r},\qquad f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{H}^{n}).

The Sobolev space
W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
is defined as the completion of
C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
with respect to the norm

\|f\|_{W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})}\;:=\;\Bigl(\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f\bigr|^{2}\Bigr)^{1/2}.

Let

\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}=\Bigl\{z=(\zeta_{1},\dots,\zeta_{n+1})\in\mathbb{C}^{n+1}\;:\;\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}=1\Bigr\}

be the CR sphere. The sublaplacian on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
is defined as

\mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}\bigl(T_{j}\overline{T}_{j}+\overline{T}_{j}T_{j}\bigr),

where

T_{j}=\frac{\partial}{\partial\zeta_{j}}-\overline{\zeta}_{j}\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}\zeta_{k}\frac{\partial}{\partial\zeta_{k}},\quad j=1,\cdots,n+1.

The transversal direction is given by the real vector field

\mathcal{T}=\frac{i}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{n+1}\Bigl(\zeta_{j}\frac{\partial}{\partial\zeta_{j}}-\overline{\zeta}_{j}\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline{\zeta}_{j}}\Bigr).

The conformal sublaplacian on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
is defined as

\mathcal{D}=\mathcal{L}+\frac{n^{2}}{4}.

The Sobolev space
W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
is defined as the completion of
C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
with respect to the norm

\|F\|_{W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})}=\|\mathcal{D}^{s/2}F\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})}.

It is well known that
L^{2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
admits a decomposition into
U(n+1)
-irreducible components

L^{2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})=\bigoplus_{j,k\geq 0}\mathcal{H}_{j,k},

where
\mathcal{H}_{j,k}
consists of the restrictions to
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
of harmonic polynomials
p(z,\overline{z})
on
\mathbb{C}^{n+1}
that are homogeneous of degree
j
in
z
and degree
k
in
\overline{z}
(see
[
21
,
31
]
and references therein).
We define the operator
\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
on
W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
for
-Q_{n}<s\leq Q_{n}
by its action on the spherical harmonics
Y_{jk}\in\mathcal{H}_{j,k}
:

(1.5)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}Y_{jk}=\lambda_{j}(s)\lambda_{k}(s)Y_{jk},

where

\lambda_{j}(s)=\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s}{4}+j\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}+j\bigr)}.

In terms of the conformal sublaplacian
\mathcal{D}
and the transversal vector field
\mathcal{T}
, the operator
\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
can be expressed as (see Section 2.2; for the case where
s
is an even integer, see
[
5
]
)

(1.6)

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}=\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\frac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\frac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\frac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\frac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)}.

Set

\mathbb{H}^{n-m}=\{(z,t)\in\mathbb{H}^{n}:z=(z_{1},\dots,z_{n-m},0,\dots,0)\},

considered as a subgroup of
\mathbb{H}^{n}
via the natural embedding.
For convenience, we write a point
z\in\mathbb{C}^{n}
as
z=(z^{\prime},z^{\prime\prime})
with

z^{\prime}=(z_{1},\dots,z_{n-m})\in\mathbb{C}^{n-m},\qquad z^{\prime\prime}=(z_{n-m+1},\dots,z_{n})\in\mathbb{C}^{m}.

Let
\mathcal{R}_{m}
denote the restriction operator that maps functions on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
to
\mathbb{H}^{n-m}
.
As shown in Lemma
3.2
,
\mathcal{R}_{m}
extends to a bounded operator from
W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
to
W^{s-2m,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n-m})
.

Correspondingly, we set

(1.7)

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}=\left\{\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}:\zeta_{k}=0,\;n-m+2\leq k\leq n+1\right\}.

Let
\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}
be the operator that restricts functions on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
to
\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
.
For brevity, and when no confusion is likely, we also identify

(1.8)

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}=

\displaystyle\Bigl\{(\zeta,t):\sum_{j=1}^{n-m+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}=1,\;\zeta_{k}=0,\;n-m+2\leq k\leq n,\;t=0\Bigr\}

\displaystyle\subset

\displaystyle\mathbb{C}^{n-m+1}\times\{0\}\times\cdots\times\{0\}\subset\mathbb{H}^{n}.

With this identification, the notation
\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}
will also be used for the restriction of functions on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
to
\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
.

Employing the method of Bez–Machihara–Sugimoto together with the sharp form of the Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality on the Heisenberg group due to Frank and Lieb, we obtain the following three types of Sobolev trace inequalities on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
and
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
1\leq m<n
,
2m<s<Q_{n}
, and
p=\frac{2(Q_{n}-2m)}{Q_{n}-s}
. For any
f\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
, it holds

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f|^{2}\geq

\displaystyle 2^{\frac{s-2m}{Q_{n}-2m}}\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{s-2m}{2}\right)}\left(\frac{2\pi^{n-m+1}}{(n-m)!}\right)^{\frac{s-2m}{Q_{n}-2m}}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}\|\mathcal{R}_{m}f\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{H}^{n-m})}^{2}.

Equality holds if and only if

\displaystyle f(u)=f(z^{\prime},z^{\prime\prime},t)=c|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|u^{-1}v|^{4m-Q_{n}-s}\Bigl\{J_{\mathscr{C}_{n-m}}\bigl(\delta_{r}(a^{-1}v)\bigr)\Bigr\}^{\frac{Q_{n}-s}{2(Q_{n}-2m)}}dv,

for some
c\in\mathbb{C}
,
r>0
, and
a\in\mathbb{H}^{n-m}
.

Theorem 1.4
.

Let
1\leq m<n
,
2m<s<Q_{n}
, and
p=\frac{2(Q_{n}-2m)}{Q_{n}-s}
.
Let
\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
be as defined in (
1.7
).
For any
F\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
, it holds

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}|\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}^{1/2}F|^{2}\geq

\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{s-2m}{2}\right)}\left(\frac{2\pi^{n-m+1}}{(n-m)!}\right)^{\frac{s-2m}{Q_{n}-2m}}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}\|\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}F\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1})}^{2}.

Equality holds if and only if

\displaystyle F(\zeta)=c\left(\sum_{j=n-m+2}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\right)^{\frac{s-2m}{2}}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}}\frac{1}{|1-\zeta\cdot\overline{\eta}|^{\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{2}}}\frac{1}{|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\eta|^{\frac{Q_{n-m}-s}{2}}}d\eta,

where
c\in\mathbb{R}
,
\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n-m+1}
with
|\xi|<1
, and
\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
.

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
1\leq m<n
,
2m<s<Q_{n}
, and
p=\frac{2(Q_{n}-2m)}{Q_{n}-s}
. Let
\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
be as defined in (
1.8
). For any
f\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
, it holds

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f|^{2}\geq

\displaystyle 2\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{s-2m}{2}\right)}\left(\frac{2\pi^{n-m+1}}{(n-m)!}\right)^{\frac{s-2m}{Q_{n}-2m}}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}\|\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}f\|_{L^{p}(\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1})}^{2}.

Equality holds if and only if

\displaystyle f(u)=f(z^{\prime},z^{\prime\prime},t)=

\displaystyle c|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}}|u^{-1}v|^{4m-Q_{n}-s}\frac{1}{|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot v|^{(Q_{n-m}-s)/2}}dv,

where
c\in\mathbb{R},\;\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n-m+1},\;|\xi|<1
.

We now consider the limiting case
s=Q_{n}
. At this endpoint, it becomes necessary to introduce the operator
\mathcal{A}^{\prime}_{Q_{n},\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
, which was originally defined by Branson, Fontana and Morpurgo
[
5
]
on the space of CR-pluriharmonic functions.

Let us denote

\mathcal{P}_{n}=\bigoplus_{j>0}\bigl(\mathcal{H}_{j,0}\oplus\mathcal{H}_{0,j}\bigr)\oplus\mathcal{H}_{0,0}=\bigl\{L^{2}\text{ CR-pluriharmonic functions}\bigr\};

and

\mathbb{R}\mathcal{P}_{n}=\bigl\{L^{2}\text{ real-valued CR-pluriharmonic functions}\bigr\}.

Define

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\prime}_{Q_{n},\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F=

\displaystyle-\frac{4}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}}{2}\bigr)}\frac{\partial}{\partial s}\Big|_{s=Q_{n}}\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F

(1.9)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{4}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}}{2}\bigr)}\lim_{s\to Q_{n}}\frac{1}{s-Q_{n}}\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F,\qquad F\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})\cap\mathcal{P}_{n}.

Branson, Fontana and Morpurgo proved the following Beckner–Onofri-type inequality for
\mathcal{A}^{\prime}_{Q_{n},\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
:

Theorem 1.6
.

[
5
]

For any
F\in W^{Q_{n}/2,2}\cap\mathbb{R}\mathcal{P}_{n}
, it holds

(1.10)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2(n+1)!|\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F\mathcal{A}_{Q_{n}}^{\prime}F+\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F\geq\log\Bigl(\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}e^{F}\Bigr).

Equality holds only for functions of the form

-Q_{n}\ln|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\zeta|+c,\qquad c\in\mathbb{R},\;\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n+1},\;|\xi|<1,\;\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}.

Our trace Beckner–Onofri inequality at the endpoint
s=Q_{n}
reads as follows:

Theorem 1.7
.

Let
1\leq m<n
. For any
F\in W^{Q_{n}/2,2}\cap\mathbb{R}\mathcal{P}_{n}
, it holds

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2(n-m+1)!|\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F\mathcal{A}_{Q_{n}}^{\prime}F+\frac{\pi^{m}}{|\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}}\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}F

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\pi^{m}\log\Bigl(\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}e^{\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}_{m}F}\Bigr)

Equality holds if and only if

\displaystyle F(\zeta)=\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-2m}{2}\bigr)}{2\pi^{n-m+1}}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}}\frac{\bigl(\sum\limits_{j=n-m+2}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\bigr)^{\frac{Q_{n}-2m}{2}}}{|1-\zeta\cdot\overline{\eta}|^{Q_{n}-2m}}\Bigl(-Q_{n-m}\ln|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\eta|+c\Bigr)d\eta,

where
c\in\mathbb{R}
,
\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n-m+1}
with
|\xi|<1
, and
\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{2(n-m)+1}
.

Our method further allows us to derive trace versions of the Beckner–Onofri inequalities on the standard sphere, a setting that has not been previously considered in the literature
[
16
,
3
,
4
]
.

Let
\mathbb{S}^{n}
denote the standard sphere

\mathbb{S}^{n}=\{x\in\mathbb{R}^{n+1}:|x|=1\}.

Denote by
H^{s}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
the completion of
C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
with respect to the norm

\|F\|_{H^{s}(\mathbb{S}^{n})}=\|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{n}})^{s/2}F\|_{L^{2}(\mathbb{S}^{n})},

where
\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}
is the Laplace–Beltrami operator on
\mathbb{S}^{n}
.
The classical Beckner–Onofri inequality on
\mathbb{S}^{n}
reads (see
[
28
,
2
]
):

Theorem 1.8
.

Define

P_{s,\mathbb{S}^{n}}=\frac{\Gamma\bigl(B+\frac{1+s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(B+\frac{1-s}{2}\bigr)},\qquad B=\sqrt{-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}+\frac{(n-1)^{2}}{4}}.

Then, for
s=n
and any
F\in H^{n}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
,

(1.11)

\ln\Bigl(\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{n}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}e^{F}\Bigr)\leq\frac{1}{2n!|\mathbb{S}^{n}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}FP_{n,\mathbb{S}^{n}}F+\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{n}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}F.

Equality holds only for functions of the form

-n\ln|1-\xi\cdot\zeta|+c,\qquad\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n+1},\;|\xi|<1,\;\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{n},\;c\in\mathbb{R}.

Define the lower-dimensional sphere

\mathbb{S}^{n-m}=\bigl\{(\xi_{1},\dots,\xi_{n+1})\in\mathbb{S}^{n}:\xi_{n-m+2}=\cdots=\xi_{n+1}=0\bigr\},

and denote by
\widetilde{\tau}_{m}
the restriction operator mapping functions
F\in H^{s}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
to
H^{s}(\mathbb{S}^{n-m})
.

Our trace Beckner–Onofri inequality on
\mathbb{S}^{n}
is as follows.

Theorem 1.9
.

Let
1\leq m<n
. For any
F\in H^{n}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2(n-m)!|\mathbb{S}^{n-m}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}FP_{n,\mathbb{S}^{n}}F+\pi^{m/2}2^{m}\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{n-m}{2}\right)|\mathbb{S}^{n-m}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F

(1.12)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\pi^{m/2}2^{m}\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{n-m}{2}\right)}\ln\Bigl(\frac{1}{|\mathbb{S}^{n-m}|}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}e^{\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F}\Bigr).

Equality holds only if

\displaystyle F(\zeta)=

\displaystyle\pi^{\frac{m-n}{2}}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(n-m\bigr)}{\Gamma(\frac{n-m}{2})}\Bigl(\sum_{j=n-m+2}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\Bigr)^{\frac{n-m}{2}}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}\frac{-(n-m)\ln|1-\xi\cdot\eta|+c}{|\zeta-\eta|^{2n-2m}}d\eta,

where
\xi\in\mathbb{R}^{n+1},\;|\xi|<1,\;\xi\in\mathbb{S}^{n},\;c\in\mathbb{R}.

This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we collect the necessary preliminary material, including the Cayley transform and the sharp Sobolev inequalities on both the Heisenberg group and the CR sphere. Section 3 is devoted to the proof of our main results: Theorems
1.3
,
1.4
,
1.5
and
1.7
. Finally, in Section 4 we establish the trace versions of the Beckner-Onofri inequalities on the standard sphere, i.e. Theorem
1.9
.

2.
Preliminaries

We begin by recalling some preliminary facts that will be used in the subsequent analysis.

2.1.
Cayley transform

The Cayley transform
\mathscr{C}_{n}:\mathbb{H}^{n}\to\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
and its inverse
\mathscr{C}_{n}^{-1}:\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}\to\mathbb{H}^{n}
are defined by

(2.1)

\displaystyle\mathscr{C}_{n}(z,t)=

\displaystyle\Bigl(\frac{2z}{1+|z|^{2}+it},\;\frac{1-|z|^{2}-it}{1+|z|^{2}+it}\Bigr),

(2.2)

\displaystyle\mathscr{C}_{n}^{-1}(\zeta)=

\displaystyle\Bigl(\frac{\zeta_{1}}{1+\zeta_{n+1}},\dots,\frac{\zeta_{n}}{1+\zeta_{n+1}},\;\operatorname{Im}\frac{1-\zeta_{n+1}}{1+\zeta_{n+1}}\Bigr).

The Jacobian of this transformation (see
[
5
]
) is

|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}(z,t)|=\frac{2^{2n+1}}{\bigl((1+|z|^{2})^{2}+t^{2}\bigr)^{n+1}}.

Observe that

(2.3)

\displaystyle 2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}(z,t)|=\left(\frac{4}{(1+|z|^{2})^{2}+t^{2}}\right)^{\frac{Q_{n}}{2}}.

Via the Cayley transform, the operators
\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}
and
\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
are related as follows (see
[
5
]
): for
0<s<Q_{n}
and
F\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})
,

(2.4)

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl((2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}|)^{\frac{Q_{n}-s}{2Q_{n}}}(F\circ\mathscr{C}_{n})\Bigr)=(2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}|)^{\frac{Q_{n}+s}{2Q_{n}}}(\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}F)\circ\mathscr{C}_{n}.

In particular, choosing
F\equiv 1
yields

(2.5)

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}(2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}|)^{\frac{Q_{n}-s}{2Q_{n}}}=\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}(2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}|)^{\frac{Q_{n}+s}{2Q_{n}}},\qquad 0<s<Q_{n}.

2.2.
Sobolev inequalities on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
and
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}

We first verify formula (
1.6
). For any spherical harmonic
Y_{j,k}\in\mathcal{H}_{j,k}
, we have (see
[
30
,
5
]
)

\displaystyle\mathcal{D}Y_{j,k}=\bigl(j+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr)\bigl(k+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr),\qquad\mathcal{T}Y_{j,k}=\tfrac{i}{2}(j-k)Y_{j,k}.

Consequently,

\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}Y_{j,k}=\sqrt{\bigl(j+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr)\bigl(k+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr)+\tfrac{(j-k)^{2}}{4}}Y_{j,k}=\tfrac{j+k+n}{2}Y_{j,k}.

Hence,

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\sqrt{\mathcal{D}-\mathcal{T}^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)}Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{j+k+n}{2}+\tfrac{j-k}{2}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{j+k+n}{2}-\tfrac{j-k}{2}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{j+k+n}{2}+\tfrac{j-k}{2}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{j+k+n}{2}-\tfrac{j-k}{2}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)}Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\tfrac{Q_{n}+s}{4}+j\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\tfrac{Q_{n}-s}{4}+j\bigr)}\;\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\tfrac{Q_{n}+s}{4}+k\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\tfrac{Q_{n}-s}{4}+k\bigr)}Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}Y_{j,k},

which establishes (
1.6
).

Observe also the relation

-\tfrac{1}{4}\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}=-\mathcal{L}-\mathcal{T}^{2},

where
\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
is the Laplace–Beltrami operator on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
. Indeed, for any
Y_{j,k}\in\mathcal{H}_{j,k}
,

\displaystyle\bigl(-\mathcal{L}-\mathcal{T}^{2}\bigr)Y_{j,k}=

\displaystyle\Bigl[\bigl(j+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr)\bigl(k+\tfrac{n}{2}\bigr)-\tfrac{n^{2}}{4}+\tfrac{(j-k)^{2}}{4}\Bigr]Y_{j,k}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\tfrac{1}{4}(j+k)(j+k+2n)=-\tfrac{1}{4}\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}Y_{j,k}.

Thus, an alternative representation of
\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}
is

\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}=\frac{\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}+n^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}+n^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2+s}{4}\Bigr)}{\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}+n^{2}}-i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)\Gamma\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{-\Delta_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}+n^{2}}+i\mathcal{T}+\tfrac{2-s}{4}\Bigr)}.

In a ground-breaking work
[
23
]
, Frank and Lieb proved the following sharp Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
:

Theorem 2.1
.

[
23
]

Let
0<\lambda<Q_{n}=2n+2
and
p=2Q_{n}/(2Q_{n}-\lambda)
. Then for any
f,g\in L^{p}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})

\left|\iint_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}\times\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}\frac{\overline{f(\zeta)}g(\eta)}{|1-\zeta\cdot\overline{\eta}|^{\lambda/2}}d\zeta d\eta\right|\leq\left(\frac{2\pi^{n+1}}{n!}\right)^{\lambda/Q_{n}}\frac{n!\Gamma\bigl((Q_{n}-\lambda)/2\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl((2Q_{n}-\lambda)/4\bigr)}\|f\|_{p}\|g\|_{p}

with equality if and only if

f(\zeta)=\frac{c}{|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\zeta|^{(2Q_{n}-\lambda)/2}},\quad g(\zeta)=\frac{c^{\prime}}{|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\zeta|^{(2Q_{n}-\lambda)/2}},

for some
c,c^{\prime}\in\mathbb{C}
and some
\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n+1}
with
|\xi|<1
.

Following an argument of Beckner (see
[
2
, Theorem 6]
), one can deduce the following sharp Sobolev inequality on
\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}
(see
[
23
]
, (5.13)):

(2.6)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}|\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}^{1/2}F|^{2}\geq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{2\pi^{n+1}}{n!}\right)^{s/Q_{n}}\frac{\Gamma^{2}(\frac{Q_{n}+s}{4})}{\Gamma^{2}(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4})}\|F\|_{L^{\frac{2Q_{n}}{Q_{n}-s}}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1})}^{2},

\displaystyle\quad 0<s<Q_{n},\;F\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}),

with equality if and only if

F(\zeta)=\frac{c}{|1-\overline{\xi}\cdot\zeta|^{(Q_{n}-s)/2}},\qquad c\in\mathbb{R},\;\xi\in\mathbb{C}^{n+1},\;|\xi|<1,\;\zeta\in\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}.

On the other hand, from (
2.4
) we obtain the identity

(2.7)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}\bigl((2|J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}|)^{\frac{Q_{n}-s}{2Q_{n}}}(F\circ\mathscr{C}_{n})\bigr)\Bigr|^{2}=2\int_{\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}|\mathcal{A}_{s,\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}}^{1/2}F|^{2},\;\;F\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{S}^{2n+1}).

Consequently, the sharp Sobolev inequality on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
reads (see
[
13
]
for
0<s\leq 1
)

(2.8)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f|^{2}\geq

\displaystyle 2^{s/Q_{n}}\left(\frac{2\pi^{n+1}}{n!}\right)^{s/Q_{n}}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}\|f\|_{L^{\frac{2Q_{n}}{Q_{n}-s}}(\mathbb{H}^{n})}^{2},

\displaystyle\qquad 0<s<Q_{n},\;f\in W^{s,2}(\mathbb{H}^{n}),

with equality if and only if

f(u)=c\Bigl\{J_{\mathscr{C}_{n}}\bigl(\delta_{r}(v^{-1}u)\bigr)\Bigr\}^{\frac{Q_{n}-s}{2Q_{n}}},\qquad c\in\mathbb{C},\;r>0,\;v\in\mathbb{H}^{n}.

For other fractional subelliptic Sobolev inequalities on
\mathbb{H}^{n}
, we refer to
[
24
]
and the references therein.

3.
Proof of Theorem
1.3
,
1.4
,
1.5
and
1.7

Define

k_{s}:=\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\delta_{0},\qquad 0<s<Q_{n},

where
\delta_{0}
denotes the Dirac delta at the origin.
Frank and Lieb (see
[
23
]
, Proposition 4.1) proved that
k_{s}
is real-valued, even (i.e.,
k_{s}(u^{-1})=k_{s}(u)
for all
u\in\mathbb{H}^{n}
), and homogeneous of degree
(s-2Q_{n})/2
in the sense that

k_{s}(\delta_{r}u)=r^{\frac{s-2Q_{n}}{2}}k_{s}(u),\qquad u\in\mathbb{H}^{n},\;r>0.

Moreover,
k_{s}
belongs to the weak Lebesgue space
L^{2Q_{n}/(2Q_{n}-s)}_{w}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
.
Note that (see
[
15
,
29
]
)

(3.1)

\displaystyle(\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1}\delta_{0})(u)=a_{n,s}|u|^{s-Q_{n}},\qquad a_{n,s}=\frac{2^{n-1-\frac{s}{2}}\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}{\pi^{n+1}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})},

from which one deduces the convolution identity

(3.2)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}k_{s}(u^{-1}w)k_{s}(v^{-1}w)dw=a_{n,s}|u^{-1}v|^{s-Q_{n}},\qquad u,v\in\mathbb{H}^{n}.

Following
[
4
]
, we introduce the linear operator

\mathcal{S}_{m}:C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{H}^{n})\subset L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})\longrightarrow L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n-m})

defined by

\mathcal{S}_{m}f=\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}f=\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}(f\ast k_{s}),\qquad f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{H}^{n}).

For simplicity, we restrict our attention to real-valued functions in what follows.
Let
\mathcal{S}_{m}^{*}:L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n-m})\to L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
be the adjoint operator of
\mathcal{S}_{m}
. Then

\mathcal{S}_{m}^{*}g(u)=\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}k_{s}(v^{-1}u)\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}g(v)dv,\qquad g\in L^{2}(\mathbb{H}^{n-m}).

Consequently,

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{m}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{*}g(u)=

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}(\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}k_{s})(v^{-1}u)\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}g(v)dv

(3.3)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{n,s}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|v^{-1}u|^{s-Q_{n}}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}g(v)dv.

Inserting (
3.1
) into (
3.3
) gives

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{m}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{*}g=

\displaystyle a_{n,s}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\Bigl(\frac{1}{a_{n-m,s-2m}}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{-1}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}g\Bigr)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{a_{n,s}}{a_{n-m,s-2m}}\;g=\frac{2^{n-1-\frac{s}{2}}\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}{\pi^{n+1}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\;\frac{\pi^{n-m+1}\Gamma(\frac{s-2m}{2})}{2^{n-m-1-\frac{s-2m}{2}}\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n-m}-(s-2m)}{4}\bigr)}\;g

(3.4)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}\;g.

Lemma 3.1
.

For any
f\in C_{0}^{\infty}(\mathbb{H}^{n})
and
0<s<Q_{n}
,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f\bigr|^{2}=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}\Bigl(f-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f\Bigr)\Bigr|^{2}+

\displaystyle\qquad\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}\bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f\bigr|^{2}.

Proof.

Set
h=\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f
; then
f=\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}h=h\ast k_{s}
. We compute

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}\Bigl(f-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f\Bigr)\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|h-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}h\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|h-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|h|^{2}+\Bigl(\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\Bigr)^{2}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2}-2\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}h\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|h|^{2}+\Bigl(\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\Bigr)^{2}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2}-2\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2}.

On the other hand, using (
3.4
),

\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}\mathcal{S}_{m}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{S}_{m}h\cdot\mathcal{S}_{m}h=\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2}.

Hence,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}\Bigl|\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{1/2}f-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n}}|h|^{2}-\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|\mathcal{S}_{m}h|^{2},

which is precisely the statement of the lemma.
∎

Now we derive an explicit expression for

\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f.

First,

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(u)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}k_{s}(v^{-1}u)\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(v)dv

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}(\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}k_{s})(v^{-1}u)\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(v)dv

(3.5)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{n,s}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|v^{-1}u|^{s-Q_{n}}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(v)dv.

Next, we compute
\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|v^{-1}u|^{s-Q_{n}}
.
For simplicity, write
u=(z^{\prime},z^{\prime\prime},t)
and
v=(w^{\prime},w^{\prime\prime},\mu)
with
w^{\prime\prime}=0
. Then

\displaystyle|u^{-1}v|^{s-Q_{n}}=

\displaystyle\bigl[(|z^{\prime\prime}|^{2}+|-z^{\prime}+w^{\prime}|^{2})^{2}+(-t+\mu-2\operatorname{Im}z^{\prime}\cdot\overline{w}^{\prime})^{2}\bigr]^{\frac{s-Q_{n}}{4}}.

If we set
\widetilde{u}=(z^{\prime},t)
and
\widetilde{v}=(w^{\prime},\mu)
, then

\displaystyle|u^{-1}v|^{s-Q_{n}}=

\displaystyle|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-Q_{n}}\Bigl[\Bigl(1+\frac{|-z^{\prime}+w^{\prime}|^{2}}{|z^{\prime\prime}|^{2}}\Bigr)^{2}+\frac{1}{|z^{\prime\prime}|^{4}}\bigl(-t+\mu-2\operatorname{Im}z^{\prime}\cdot\overline{w}^{\prime}\bigr)^{2}\Bigr]^{\frac{s-Q_{n}}{4}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-Q_{n}}\Bigl[2^{-1-2(n-m)}J_{\mathscr{C}_{n-m}}\bigl(\delta_{\frac{1}{|z^{\prime\prime}|}}(\widetilde{u}^{-1}\widetilde{v})\bigr)\Bigr]^{\frac{Q_{n-m}-(s-2m)}{2Q_{n-m}}}.

Using (
1.4
) and (
2.5

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|u^{-1}v|^{s-Q_{n}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n-m}+s-2m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n-m}-(s-2m)}{4}\bigr)}|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-Q_{n}}\frac{1}{|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}}\Bigl[2^{-2-2(n-m)}\Bigr]^{\frac{Q_{n-m}-(s-2m)}{2Q_{n-m}}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\bigl[2J_{\mathscr{C}_{n-m}}(\delta_{\frac{1}{|z^{\prime\prime}|}}(\widetilde{u}^{-1}\widetilde{v}))\bigr]^{\frac{Q_{n-m}+(s-2m)}{2Q_{n-m}}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}\frac{2^{s-2m}|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}}{\bigl[(|z^{\prime\prime}|^{2}+|-z^{\prime}+w^{\prime}|^{2})^{2}+(-t+\mu-2\operatorname{Im}z^{\prime}\cdot\overline{w}^{\prime})^{2}\bigr]^{\frac{Q_{n}-4m+s}{4}}}

(3.6)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{s-2m}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}|u^{-1}v|^{4m-Q_{n}-s}.

Substituting (
3.6
) into (
3.5
) yields

\displaystyle\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}\mathcal{L}_{s,\mathbb{H}^{n}}^{-1/2}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}\mathcal{L}_{s-2m,\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(u)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a_{n,s}\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}2^{s-2m}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|u^{-1}v|^{4m-Q_{n}-s}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(v)dv

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2^{n-1-\frac{s}{2}}\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}{\pi^{n+1}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\frac{\pi^{m}\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{s-2m}{2}\bigr)}2^{s-2m}\frac{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}+s-4m}{4}\bigr)}{\Gamma^{2}\bigl(\frac{Q_{n}-s}{4}\bigr)}|z^{\prime\prime}|^{s-2m}\int_{\mathbb{H}^{n-m}}|u^{-1}v|^{4m-Q_{n}-s}\mathcal{R}_{m}f(v)dv

(3.7)

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{-\frac{s}{4}}\widetilde{\mathcal{S}}_{m}^{\ast}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{4}}\tau_{m}f

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{-\frac{s}{4}}\frac{1}{\gamma_{n}(\frac{s}{2})}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n-\frac{s}{2}}}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}

(4.2)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\gamma_{n}(s)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n-s}}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}.

Write
x=(x^{\prime},x^{\prime\prime})
and
y=(y^{\prime},y^{\prime\prime})
with
y^{\prime\prime}=0
. Then

(4.3)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n-s}}=\bigl(|x^{\prime\prime}|^{2}+|x^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{2}\bigr)^{\frac{s-n}{2}}=|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-n}\Bigl(\frac{1}{\frac{|x^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{2}}{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{2}}+1}\Bigr)^{\frac{n-m-(s-m)}{2}}.

Consider the stereographic projection
\mathscr{S}_{n}:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to\mathbb{S}^{n}
and its inverse
\mathscr{S}_{n}^{-1}:\mathbb{S}^{n}\to\mathbb{R}^{n}
, given by

\mathscr{S}_{n}(x)=\Bigl(\frac{2x}{1+|x|^{2}},\frac{1-|x|^{2}}{1+|x|^{2}}\Bigr),\qquad\mathscr{S}_{n}^{-1}(\omega)=\Bigl(\frac{\omega_{1}}{1+\omega_{n+1}},\dots,\frac{\omega_{n}}{1+\omega_{n+1}}\Bigr).

Its Jacobian is

(4.4)

\displaystyle|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|(x)=\Bigl(\frac{2}{1+|x|^{2}}\Bigr)^{n}.

The operators
P_{s,\mathbb{S}^{n}}
and
\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}
are related by the following intertwining formula (see
[
27
]
): for
F\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
,

(4.5)

\displaystyle(P_{s,\mathbb{S}^{n}}F)\circ\mathscr{S}_{n}=|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{-(n+s)/(2n)}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{s/2}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}}|^{(n-s)/(2n)}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr).

Choosing
F\equiv 1
gives

(4.6)

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{s/2}|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{(n-s)/(2n)}=\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-s}{2}\bigr)}|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{(n+s)/(2n)}.

Combing (
4.3
) and (
4.6
) yields

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n-s}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{2}}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-n}\Bigl(\frac{1}{\frac{|x^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{2}}{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{2}}+1}\Bigr)^{\frac{n-m-(s-m)}{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{s-m}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-m+s-m}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-m-s+m}{2}\bigr)}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-n}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{m-s}\Bigl(\frac{1}{\frac{|x^{\prime}-y^{\prime}|^{2}}{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{2}}+1}\Bigr)^{\frac{n-m+(s-m)}{2}}

(4.7)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2^{s-m}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-s}{2}\bigr)}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}.

Inserting (
4.7
) into (
4.2
) yields

\displaystyle(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{-\frac{s}{4}}\mathcal{S}_{m}^{\ast}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{4}}\tau_{m}f

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\gamma_{n}(s)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n-s}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-s}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{s}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}2^{s-m}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n-s}{2}\bigr)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}

(4.8)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime}.

Therefore, if we define

\mathcal{Q}_{s,m}(\tau_{m}f)(x)=\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}\tau_{m}f(y^{\prime})dy^{\prime},

then by Lemma
4.1
, we obtain the following lemma:

Lemma 4.2
.

Let
m<s<n
. For any
f\in D_{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{\frac{s}{4}}f\bigr|^{2}=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{\frac{s}{4}}\bigl(f-\mathcal{Q}_{s,m}(\tau_{m}f)\bigr)\Bigr|^{2}+\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{4}}\tau_{m}f\bigr|^{2}.

We now extend Lemma
4.2
to the sphere
\mathbb{S}^{n}
.
For
\omega=\mathscr{S}_{n}(x)
and
\eta=\mathscr{S}_{n}(y)
, it holds (see
[
26
]
)

(4.9)

\displaystyle|\omega-\eta|^{2}=\left(\frac{2}{1+|x|^{2}}\right)|x-y|^{2}\left(\frac{2}{1+|y|^{2}}\right).

Consider

\widetilde{\mathbb{S}}^{n-m}=\bigl\{(\zeta_{1},\dots,\zeta_{n-m},0,\dots,0,\zeta_{n+1})\in\mathbb{S}^{n}\bigr\},

which satisfies

\mathscr{S}_{n}(\mathbb{R}^{n-m})=\widetilde{\mathbb{S}}^{n-m}\setminus\{(0,\dots,0,1)\}.

For simplicity, we continue to denote by
\widetilde{\tau}_{m}
the restriction operator that maps functions on
\mathbb{S}^{n}
to
\widetilde{\mathbb{S}}^{n-m}
.

Using Lemma
4.2
together with the intertwining relation (
4.5
), we obtain, for
F\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{\frac{s}{4}}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr)\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\Bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n}})^{\frac{s}{4}}\Bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})-\mathcal{Q}_{s,m}\bigl(\tau_{m}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr)\bigr)\Bigr)\Bigr|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\Bigl|(-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}})^{\frac{s-m}{4}}\tau_{m}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr)\Bigr|^{2}.

Observe that, by (
4.9
) and (
4.4
),

\displaystyle|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{-\frac{n-s}{2n}}\mathcal{Q}_{s,m}\bigl(\tau_{m}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr)\bigr)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{-\frac{n-s}{2n}}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}\tau_{m}\bigl(|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2n}}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})\bigr)dy^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n}}|^{-\frac{n-s}{2n}}|x^{\prime\prime}|^{s-m}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|x-y^{\prime}|^{n+s-2m}}|J_{\mathscr{S}_{n-m}}|^{\frac{n-s}{2(n-m)}}\tau_{m}(F\circ\mathscr{S}_{n})dy^{\prime}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\Bigl(\sum_{j=n-m+1}^{n}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\Bigr)^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\int_{\widetilde{\mathbb{S}}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|\zeta-\overline{\eta}|^{n+s-2m}}\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F(\eta)d\eta.

Hence, if we set

\displaystyle\widetilde{\mathcal{Q}}_{s,m}(\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F)=

\displaystyle\frac{\gamma_{n}(s)}{\gamma_{n-m}(s-m)}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\pi^{n/2}2^{m}\Gamma(\frac{s}{2})}\Bigl(\sum_{j=n-m+2}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\Bigr)^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|\zeta-\eta|^{n+s-2m}}\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F(\eta)d\eta

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\pi^{\frac{m-n}{2}}\frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{n+s-2m}{2}\bigr)}{\Gamma(\frac{s-m}{2})}\Bigl(\sum_{j=n-m+2}^{n+1}|\zeta_{j}|^{2}\Bigr)^{\frac{s-m}{2}}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}\frac{1}{|\zeta-\eta|^{n+s-2m}}\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F(\eta)d\eta,

then
we obtain the following lemma. (The case
s=n
is obtained by taking the limit
s\to n
.)

Lemma 4.3
.

Let
m<s\leq n
.
For any
F\in H^{s}(\mathbb{S}^{n})
,

\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}\bigl|P_{s,\mathbb{S}^{n}}^{1/2}F\bigr|^{2}=\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n}}\Bigl|P_{s,\mathbb{S}^{n}}^{1/2}\bigl(F-\widetilde{\mathcal{Q}}_{s,m}(\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F)\bigr)\Bigr|^{2}+\pi^{m/2}2^{m}\frac{\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}\right)}{\Gamma\left(\frac{s-m}{2}\right)}\int_{\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}\bigl|P_{s-m,\mathbb{S}^{n-m}}^{1/2}\widetilde{\tau}_{m}F\bigr|^{2}.

Proof of Theorem
1.9
.
The theorem follows directly by combining Theorem
1.8
with Lemma
4.3
.
