Italian rabbi; lived at the end of the sixteenth and beginning of the seventeenth century. He was rabbi in Florence, and author of homilies on the Pentateuch, entitled "Toledot Ya'aḲob" (Generations of Jacob), Venice, 1609.

Bibliography: Wolf, Bibl. Hebr. i. 580, 611; iii. 440, 519; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1179; Mortara, Indice Alfabetico, p. 2. W. M.
