CHAIRMAN, INTELLIGENCE ADVISOR! COMMITTEE

SUBJECT: Provision of Political -Economic Intelligence to NATO

1. In the capacity of executive agent for the Joint Intelligence
Committee of the JCS and under authorization by the Intelligence Adv
visory Committee, the Assistant Chief of Staff, G-2, is furnishing

by cable to SHAPE current intelligence in the form of a daily intelli-
gence briefing and a weekly Joint Intelligence Summary based upon the
conclusions of the Watch Committee Report. Information copies of these
intelligence items are furnished to the Standing Group.

2. The Director of Intelligence, SHAPE, has advised that the
intelligence being furnished to SHAPE fails to meet SHAPE requirements
for political-economic intelligence. Accordingly, the DI, SHAPE
requests that a separate weekly message reflecting a brief resume of
the significant political -economic developments be furnished.

3. The Standing Group of the Military Committee of the North
Atlantic Treaty Organization has authorized the National Staffs to
furnish factual political and economic intelligence items directly to
SHAPE. Inasiauch as the production of this type of intelligence is
beyond the scope of the intelligence agencies of the military services,
it is requested that the IAC approve the transmission of political

and economic intelligence to the major subordinate commands of NATO,
in this case, SHAPE.

J !-. It is . hoped that the Office of the Special Assistant for
Intelligence, Department of State, and the Central Intelligence Agency
will be able to furnish to the AC of S, G-2, Department of the Army,
significant political and economic intelligence items each week for
transmission to SHAPE s.nd to the other major commands of NATO, when
established, and to the Standing Group. If these items could be
transmitted directly to the A C of S, G-2, by secure teletype means
on each Thursday, they could then be forwarded to SHAPE -each Friday.
