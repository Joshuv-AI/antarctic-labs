FMLN POLITICAL COMMISSION MEMBERS
OF THE OFFENSIVE WAS BASED IN FART ON
NATIONAL UNITY OF SALVADORAN WORKERS (UNTS) DIRECTORATE MEMBER
JULIO CESAR ((PORTILLO))'S DECLARATION THAT HE COULD NOT GUARANTEE
RANK AND FILE MEMBERS WOULD BE PREPARED 70 SUPPORT A NATIONWIDE
ARMED OPERATION. MEMBERS WERE UNPREPARED BECAUSE THEY WERE
SHOCKED BY THE OFFENSIVE WHICH BEGAN ON I] NOVEMBER 1989, AND

UNTS MEMBERS AND OTHER FRONT GROUP MEMBERS WENT INTO HIDING AND
HAVE REFUSED TO EMERGE THE GOVERNMENT IMPOSED STATE OF SIEGE AND
SUSPENSION OF CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS HAVE ALSO HAMPERED THE GROUP'S
OPERATIONS.

3. HOWEVER, PORTILLO PROMISED TO SUPPORT THE NEXT NATIONAL
INSURRECTION ONCE THE STATE OF SIEGE WAS LIFTED AND CONSTITUTIONAL
RIGHTS ARE RESTORED. THE UNTS AND OTHER FRONT ORGANIZATIONS ALSO
PLANNED TO MOUNT CAMPAIGNS TO DENOUNCE “HUMAN RIGHTS ABUSES" BY

THE ARMED FORCES OF EL SALVADOR: THE 3} OCTOBER 1989 BOMBING OF
THE NATIONAL FEDERATION OF SALVADORAN WORKERS UNIONS (FENASTRAS) ,
WHICH CLAIMED THE LIVES OF UNION LEADERS, INCLUDING FENASTRAS
LEADER FEBE ELIZABETH VELAZQUEZ; AND THE 16 NOVEMBER ASSASSINATIONS
OF THE SIX CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY JESUIT PRIESTS.

4. PORTILLO CLAIMED THE MPSC BELIEVES THE JESUITS WERE NOT
KILLED BY LOW RANKING SOLDIERS, BUT THAT THE KILLINGS WERE
ORCHESTRATED BY COLONEL HECTOR HERIBERTO ((HERNANDEZ)), DIRECTOR
OF THE TREASURY POLICE, AND ROBERTO ((D'AUBUISSON)), HONORARY
PRESIDENT FOR LIFE OF THE NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE (ARENA)
PARTY. THE MPSC PLANS TO PUBLICLY CALL FOR AN INVESTIGATION OF
HERNANDEZ AND D'AUBUISSON, IF THEY ARE NOT IDENTIFIED BY THE
GOVERNMENT AS THE MASTERMINDS OF THE ASSASSINATIONS.

5. ARMY COLONEL GUILLERMO BENAVIDES AND

SEVERAL MEMBERS OF THE ATLACATL IMMEDIATE REACTION BATTALION HAVE
BEEN ARRESTED IN CONNECTION WITH THE MURDER OF THE JESUITS. THERE
IS NO EVIDENCE TO DATE IMPLICATING EITHER HERNANDEZ OR D'AUBUISSON. )
