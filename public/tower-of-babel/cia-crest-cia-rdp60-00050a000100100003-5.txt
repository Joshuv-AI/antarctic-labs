1. while on active duty at the Air University {Maxwell Air Force
Base, Alabama) I had several occasions to chat with Brigadier General
J.loyd Hopwcod , Commandant of the Command and Utaff College, as well as
other members of the Air University staff* I learned that the Air War
College, for example, has assigned to it representatives from the Arngr,
Navy, and Department of btate in advisory capacities $ I also learned
indirectly that the Air University of which the Air War College is a part,
wight have a need for and probably would look favorably upon a CIA advisor.

2. In an atmosphere of complete academic freedom, the Air University
seem a to be the recognized seat of United states Air Force doctrine and
strategy. It is also the one place outside highest military councils
where national objectives, WSC policy, and their means of implementation
are seriously examined, discussed, researched, and expounded without the
fetters of rank or intra-service politics#

3. I found a healthy respect for G1A and its responsibilities in
the pursuance of our nation's objectives, but a tinge of disappointment
In the one or two speakers who at one time or another addressed certain
courses on the subject of CIA's role in United States national security.
From a description of one such lecture, I guessed that it was the same
presentation we normally give to recruits and which rarely ventures beyond
Public Law 283* If It were not for the sober intent of the Air University
staff and a similar attitude among the students there I would hesitate
even to broach this suggestion. But because of this and the plain fact
that airpower presently plays s predominately vital part in the structure
of an entirely new war strategy (cold and hot) I would like to propose
that CIA carefully consider the possibility of assigning a first-drawer
officer to the Air University as an advisor similiar to those provided by
other governmental departments. If this proposal has any merit the first
move appears to be ours to determine officially whether a CIA advisor
would be welcomed.
