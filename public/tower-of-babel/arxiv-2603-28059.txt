Amerio’s Theorem for Remotely Almost Periodic Differential Equations
David Cheban

Abstract
. The aim of this paper is to study the remotely almost periodic motions of dynamical systems and solutions of nonlinear differential equations. We establish some properties of remotely almost periodic motions and generalize the well known Amerio’s theorem for abstract remotely almost periodic dynamical systems. Application of our general results for different classes of nonlinear differential/difference and algebraic equations is given.

6.5.
Algebraic equations

The aim of this subsection is to study the problem of existence of
remotely almost periodic solutions of algebraic equations

(40)

x^{n}+a_{1}(t)x^{n-1}+\ldots+a_{n-1}(t)x+a_{n}(t)=0

with the remotely almost periodic coefficients
a_{i}\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C})\ (i=1,\ldots,n)
, i.e., we study the problem
algebraic closeness of the space of remotely almost periodic
functions, where
\mathbb{C}
is the set of all complex numbers.

As was shown in the papers of Walter
[
61
]
and Bohr and
Flanders
[
9
]
, if the discriminant

D(t)=D[a_{1}(t),\ldots,a_{n}(t)]

of the equation (
40
) is separated from zero, i.e.,

\inf_{t\in\mathbb{R}}|D(t)|>0,

then the continuous solutions
X=X(t)
of the equation
(
40
) with the almost periodic coefficients
a_{i}\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C})\ (i=1,\ldots,n)
are almost-periodic
functions.

Zhikov (see, for example,
[
45
, Example 2, pp.111-112]
)
constructed an example of almost-periodic function
f
, given on
the real line, satisfying the conditions

|f(t)|>0,\ \inf\limits_{t\in\mathbb{R}}|f(t)|=0,

and such that the solutions
X=X(t)
to the equation

X^{2}-f(t)=0

are not almost-periodic. This means that in the
Walter–Bohr–Flanders Theorem, the condition of separation from
zero cannot be replaced by the condition

|D(t)|>0\ (\forall\ t\in\mathbb{R}).

Gorin and Lin, in their joint paper
[
37
]
, considered the
equation (
40
) with almost-periodic coefficients, given on
an arbitrary group, and obtained a deep generalization of the
Walter–Bohr– Flanders Theorem. Bronshtein
[
10
]

generalized Gorin-Lin results for algebraic equations with
recurrent coefficients.

Let

(41)

[

It is well known

that the discriminant

of the equation (

of the coefficients

.

.

Remark 6.61
.

Note that the condition
(
68
) in Theorem
6.58
is essential.

Below we will give an example which confirm this statement.

Example 6.62
.

Zhikov
[
62
, p.122]
constructed an example of an
almost periodic function
f\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})
satisfying
the conditions

(71)

|f(t)|>0\ (\forall\ \ t\in\mathbb{R}),\ \ \inf\limits_{t\in\mathbb{R}}|f(t)|=0

and such that the solutions of the equation
X^{2}-f(t)=0
are not
almost periodic.

Consider the function
p\in C(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})
defined by the
equality

p(t)\mathrel{\mathop{\ordinarycolon}}=f(t)+e^{-t}

for all
t\in\mathbb{R}
, where
f
is the almost periodic
function from Zhikov’s example
[
62
]
.

Lemma 6.63
.

Function
p
possesses the following properties:

(i)

the function
p
is asymptotically almost periodic and,
consequently, it is remotely almost periodic;

(ii)

\omega_{p}=H(f)
and, consequently, the set
\omega_{p}
is
minimal;

(iii)

\inf\limits_{t\in\mathbb{R}_{+}}|p(t)|=0.

Proof.

The first two statements of Lemma are evident.

Now we will show the third statement. If we assume that it is not
true, then there exists a positive number
\alpha
such that

|p(t)|\geq\alpha

or equivalently

(72)

|f(t)+e^{-t}|\geq\alpha

for all
t\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
. Since the function
f
is almost
periodic then there exists a sequence
\{t_{k}\}\subset\mathbb{R}

such that
t_{k}\to+\infty
and
f(t+t_{k})\to f(t)
(uniformly
w.r.t.
t\in\mathbb{R}
) as
k\to\infty
. Let now
s
be an
arbitrary number from
\mathbb{R}
then there exists a number

k_{0}=k(s)\in\mathbb{N}
such that

(73)

s+t_{k}\geq 0

for all
k\geq k_{0}
. From (
72
) and (
73
) we
receive

(74)

|f(s+t_{k})+e^{-s-t_{k}}|\geq\alpha

for every
k\geq k_{0}
. Passing to the limit in (
74
) as

k\to\infty
we obtain

(75)

|f(s)|\geq\alpha

for all
s\in\mathbb{R}
. The relations (
71
) and
(
75
) are contradictory. The obtained contradiction proves
our statement. Lemma is proved.
∎

Consider the algebraic equation

(76)

\lambda^{2}-p(t)=0.

It is easy to check that the condition (
70
) is not
fulfilled.

Lemma 6.64
.

The equation (
76
) has not
positively Lagrange stable remotely almost periodic solutions.

Proof.

Assume that this statement is false, i.e., there

\widetilde{\lambda}

2.21
the

\omega
-limits set
