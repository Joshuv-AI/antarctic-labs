London, July 31, 1945.

7709. Copre 4. From Gerig for Hiss and Ross.

Regarding officers for ExCom (Executive Committee) and PreCo (Preparatory Committee) agreement of Soviet awaited on proposal at Potsdam of rotation among five powers in ExCom with small or middle-sized powers as officers of PreCo.…
