On 2 September 196), Source saw two captured US pilots at-an exhibition
in HANOI, NVN. The exhibition was called "The 5 August Exhibition",
because the pilots had been shot down on that day.

The exhibition was held in a building in UNIFICATION Park, VAN HO Area
(near BAY MAU Lake), HANOI. The pilots, dressed in white, were seated

in one room; their uniforms, head gear, identification cards, and fragments
of their aircraft were displayed in another room. They both appeared to
be young, strong, and fat. Their hair was brown. Source could not

remember their names.
