Scholar of the seventeenth century; martyred, probably at Prague. He wrote the prayer "'Iyyun Tefillah," on the persecutions and the martyrdoms of Prague (2d ed., Amsterdam, 1671; 4th ed., Dessau, 1671; 5th ed., with German transl. by Ẓebi Hirsch Kaidanover, Frankfort-on-the-Main, 1709).

Bibliography: Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 183; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 437; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. cols. 832-833. S. S. O.
