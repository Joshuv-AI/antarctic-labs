No. 981 The Director of the Office of Financial and Development Policy (Collado) to the Acting Chief of the Division of Economic Security Controls (Oliver)

secret

Babelsberg, July 16, 1945.

Victory 23. For Oliver ES from Collado.

Please bring us up to date on progress in controlling German external assets especially in Spain since Department’s 5499 of July 6th to London[1] and your memorandum of July 7.[2]

Source: 800.515/7–1645: Telegram

Notes
[1] Document No. 383, printed in vol. i.
[2] Document No. 384, printed in vol. i.
