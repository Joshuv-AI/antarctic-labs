'''Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 3052nd meeting, on 14 February 1992''' ([1])

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of the Azerbaijani Republic for admission to the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Azerbaijani Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.


[1] Adopted without vote.
