Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt [1] (22 October 1944, d12)

[Excerpts]

top secret

London, 22 October 1944.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt Personal and Top Secret Number 801.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Para 8. I was delighted to hear from U. J. that you had suggested a triple meeting towards the end of November at a Black Sea port. I think this a very fine idea, and hope you will let me know about it in due course. I will come anywhere you two desire.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels. For other excerpts from this telegram, see post, pp. 159–160, 206, 328, 400.
