Questioning MAMI’s recent determination of B Λ B_{\Lambda} ( Λ 3 {}^{3}_{\Lambda} H) Journal: Physics Letters B
Avraham Gal

Abstract
A recent report on Li 7 ​ ( e , e ′ ​ K + ) {{}^{7}{\rm Li}}(e,e^{\prime}K^{+}) electroproduction runs by the A1 collaboration at the Mainz Microtron (MAMI) assigns a sharp pion-momentum line at p π − ≈ 113.8 ± 0.1 p_{\pi^{-}}\approx 113.8\pm 0.1 MeV/c to Λ 3 {}^{3}_{\Lambda} H → π − + He 3 \,\to\pi^{-}+{{}^{3}{\rm He}} weak decay, resulting in exceptionally large Λ 3 {}^{3}_{\Lambda} H binding-energy B Λ B_{\Lambda} ( Λ 3 {}^{3}_{\Lambda} H) = 0.523 ± 0.013 ± 0.075 =0.523\pm 0.013\pm 0.075 MeV. Here I suggest an alternative interpretation of the observed sharp line in terms of Λ 7 {}^{7}_{\Lambda} He → g . s . π − + Li 7 ( E x = 478 {}_{\rm g.s.}\to\pi^{-}+{{}^{7}{\rm Li}}(E_{\rm x}=478 keV) weak decay, discussing also the model dependence of B Λ B_{\Lambda} ( Λ 7 {}^{7}_{\Lambda} He).

4
Summary

In conclusion, we have presented arguments for associating
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He with
the sharp
p_{\pi^{-}}\approx\,
113.8 MeV/c line reported by MAMI in their
recent
{{}^{7}{\rm Li}}(e,e^{\prime}K^{+})
electroproduction experiment
[
6
]
.
This sharp line could have arisen from the
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He(1/2
{}^{+}_{\rm g.s.})\to\pi^{-}+{{}^{7}}
Li(1/2
{}^{-},E_{\rm x}=0.478
MeV) weak decay, provided the binding
energy of
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He is 5.84
\pm
0.07 MeV. We have shown how
B_{\Lambda}
(
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He) is related to
B_{\Lambda}
(
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
Be) and to
B_{\Lambda}
(
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
Li) which at present allows for two different experimental values,
one of which is consistent with
B_{\Lambda}
(
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He)=5.84
\pm
0.07 MeV.
Accepting our interpretation of the sharp
p_{\pi^{-}}\approx\,
113.8 MeV/c
line, future runs of
{{}^{7}{\rm Li}}(e,e^{\prime}K^{+})
electroproduction should look
for a
{}^{7}_{\Lambda}
He(1/2
{}^{+}_{\rm g.s.})\to\pi^{-}+{{}^{7}}
Li(3/2
{}^{-}_{\rm g.s.}
) weak
decay with
p_{\pi^{-}}\approx 114.5\pm 0.1
MeV/c and strength predicted to be
about twice that of the
p_{\pi^{-}}\approx 113.8\pm 0.1
MeV/c line.
