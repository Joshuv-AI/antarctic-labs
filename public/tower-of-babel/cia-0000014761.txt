From court testimony supplied by Soviet
espionage agents, this article describes in detail
the way in which the agents stole secret data on the
atom bomb from within Los Alamos, the most guarded
area in the US, where during World War II a project
for the manufacturing of atom bombs was being carried
out.

The Los Alamos area was physically hedged about
by the most careful secrecy. Only three categories
of persons, all workers on the project, could gain
admittance to the grounds. -In the first group were
the scientists who had access to the innermost
workings of the project, They wore white badges. -
In the second group were those who worked in the
machine shops and who wore red badges admitting them
only to the immediate areas in which they worked
and entitled them to be privy to only as much
