Correction for Wu et al., Paramyxovirus infection driven by heteromultivalent sialoglycotope binding
Published: 2026

Correction for “Paramyxovirus infection driven by heteromultivalent sialoglycotope binding,” by Xuesheng Wu, Mengying Liu, Laura Blekkenhorst, Mare H. L. Verbruggen, Nancy N. M. P. Schuurman, Lemeng Chao, Jun Yang Ong, Tom Wennekes, Laura L. A. van Dijk, Rory D. de Vries, Frank J. M. van Kuppeveld, Henrik Clausen, Yoshiki Narimatsu, Erik de Vries, and Cornelis A. M. de Haan, which published April 21, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2530862123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2530862123).

The authors note that Fig. 3 appeared incorrectly. “In the original figure, HEK-GSL cells were incorrectly annotated as harboring deletions of B4GALT5/6 (ΔB4GALT5/6) and C1GALT1 (ΔC1GALT1). The correct genotype comprises deletions of MGAT1 (ΔMGAT1) and C1GALT1 (ΔC1GALT1).” The corrected figure and its legend appear below.

The authors also note that the author contributions footnote appeared incorrectly. The authors’ contributions statement has been updated and now reads as follows: “X.W., M.L., E.d.V., and C.A.M.d.H. designed research; X.W., L.B., M.H.L.V., M.L., and N.N.M.P.S. performed research; M.L., L.C., J.Y.O., T.W., L.L.A.v.D., R.D.d.V., H.C., and Y.N. contributed new reagents/analytic tools; X.W. and C.A.M.d.H. analyzed data; C.A.M.d.H., E.d.V., and F.J.v.K. provided funding; C.A.M.d.H., E.d.V., and F.J.v.K. supervised the study; X.W., C.A.M.d.H., H.C., F.J.v.K., and E.d.V. edited the manuscript; and X.W., C.A.M.d.H., F.J.v.K., and E.d.V. wrote the paper.”

The published paper has been corrected.

Statistical significance of panel B–E was calculated by two-way ANOVA. (F) BLI analysis of HN-NPs binding to LAMP1 or glycophorin produced from in HEK-N/O/WT cells, or HEK-N∆Sia and HEK-O∆Sia cells cotransfected with ST3GAL1, ST3GAL4, ST6GAL1, or empty vector.
