Rabbi at Algiers; died at Blida, Algeria, January, 1794. At an early age he assisted his father in his duties as rabbi of Algiers; and at the death of the latter succeeded him. He published a work under the title "Yamim Aḥadim" (Some Days), containing sermons for all the feasts; preceded by a preface written by the bibliographer Azulai (Leghorn, 1790). Azubib signed one of the approbations attached to the work "Berit Abraham" of Abraham ben Raphael Jacob Bush'arah, Leghorn, 1791.
