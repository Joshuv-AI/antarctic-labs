Marshal Stalin to President Roosevelt and Prime Minister Churchill[1] (12 September 1943, d33)

[Moscow, September 12, 1943.]

Translation

Personal and secret message from Premier I. V. Stalin to President Franklin D. Roosevelt and Prime Minister Winston Churchill I have received your messages of September 10th.[2]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. As to the meeting of the heads of the three Governments, I do not have any objections regarding Tegeran [Tehran] as the place of the meeting, which (Tegeran) is more appropriate than Egypt where the Soviet Union does not have its representation.

September 12, 1943.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Apparently sent via the Soviet Embassy, Washington. The entire document is printed in Stalin’s Correspondence, vol. ii, p. 93.
[2] Roosevelt’s message referred to here is presumably the one sent from Washington September 9, 1943, ante, p. 24. Churchill’s is that of September 10, 1943, printed in Churchill, p. 281.
