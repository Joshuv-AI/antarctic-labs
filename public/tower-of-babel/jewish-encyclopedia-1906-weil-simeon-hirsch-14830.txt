German scholar; son of Nethaneel Weil ; lived in Carlsruhe in the eighteenth century. He published his father's "Netib Ḥayyim," "Torat Netan'el," and responsa, and wrote "Sefer Eldad ha-Dani" (with a German translation; 1769).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 501; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. pp. 217, 776. E. C. S. O.
