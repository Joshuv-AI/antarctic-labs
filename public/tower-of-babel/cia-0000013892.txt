Source assisted in the capture of one U.S. Pilot in QUANG NINH Province,
NVN, in early June 1967. The capturing wit observed the aircraft when
it was shot down somewhere in HAIPHONG Province and saw the wind carry
the pilot, who had bailed out, into QUANG NINH Province. Source's unit
was sent to capture the pilot. when he landed and to protect him from
maltreatment by the local populace. When Source's unit finally found
the pilot he was surrounded by a crowd of local people who \were~heating and’
kicking him, as well. as throwing stonés at him, The capturing unit
dispersed the crowd and took the pilot away from the immediate area.
Except for some light bruises received at the hands of the crowd, the
pilot was uninjured.

*The pilot wore a one~piece green flying suit; he had short light red

hair, copper colored eyes and appeared to be over six feet six inches
tall with a medium build, Although Source saw some letters on the
pilot's suit, he could not femember what they were and did not know what
they meant. He could not recall the insignia either.

The pilot was immediately searched for documents and weapons; however,
Source was busy holding back the crowd and did not observe what was

found on the pilot, The POW was not tied or blindfolded, He was taken

to the road connecting QUANG NINH and HAIPHONG Cities where he was turned
over to a civilian Vietnamese security agent wearing a yellow khaki uniform
and driving a jeep. The security agent was accompanied by three Westerners
who were identified as Soviets by the soldier who turned over the pilot

to the security agent. The three Soviets wore blue short-sleeved shirts
and yellow khaki trousers identical to those of the security agent.

All three Soviets carried briefcases and cameras, They took pictures of
the pilot and the capturing unit. From the fact that they took pictures,
Source believed they might be newsmen. Two of the Soviets wore glasses,
and the third had a full beard and a moustache. They appeared to be very
calm and did not speak to anyone. All of them were tall and stocky with
light red hair and generally resembled the captured pilot.
