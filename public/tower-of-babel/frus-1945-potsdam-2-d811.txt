No. 811 The Acting Representative in Rumania (Melbourne) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret

Bucharest, July 23, 1945—7 p.m.

495. See Telegram M–1285 July 23 [24][1] sent by Schuyler to War Dept outlining and asking for comment on his proposed course of action in view of Soviet proposed ACC procedure. General considers and I concur it is necessary to make clear American viewpoint on significant continuing unilateral actions taken by Soviet officials on ACC or risk assumption that now they are countenanced by Schuyler.

Melbourne
