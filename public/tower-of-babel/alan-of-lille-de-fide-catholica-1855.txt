De Fide Catholica contra Haereticos (Latin) (Alan of Lille, 1855)
======================================================================

Latin. The first systematic anti-heretical summa of the schools. Public domain. Sourced from Internet Archive: scan of the 1855 edition (https://archive.org/details/alanideinsulisdo00alan).
