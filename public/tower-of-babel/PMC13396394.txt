Author Correction: Integrative omics of the genetic basis for wheat WUE and drought resilience reveal the function of TaMYB7-A1
Authors: Yuxin Zhou, Dongzhi Wang, Hao Wang, Yunzhou Qiao, Peng Zhao, Yuan Cao, Xuemei Liu, Yiman Yang, Xuelei Lin, Shengbao Xu, Baodi Dong, Jun Xiao
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-63642-5, published online 29 September 2025

In the version of the article initially published, the left labels in Fig. 3a read (from top to bottom) “Root_WW (25,122), Root_DS1 (25,859), Root_DS2 (24,110), Shoot_WW (22,994), Shoot_DS1 (23,578), Shoot_DS2 (25,303)” but should have read “Root_WW (22,994), Root_DS1 (23,578), Root_DS2 (25,303), Shoot_WW (25,122), Shoot_DS1 (25,859), Shoot_DS2 (24,110)”. Fig. 3 has now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
