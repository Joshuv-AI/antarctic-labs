"EL SALVADOR - HINTON'S COMPLAINT

"AN AMERICAN DIPLOMAT 15 USUALLY SEEN BLT NOT HEARD IN
PUBLIC. IN EL SALVADOR, U.S. AMBASSADOR DEANE HINTON FOLLOWED THE
RULE FOR 17 MONTHS, DISCREETLY BOLSTERING THE SHAKY GOVERNMENT
AND ENCOURAGING REFORMS. BUT HIS RESERVE SEEMS TO BE RUNNING OUT.
IN RECENT WEEKS HE HAS WARNED THAT HUMAN-RIGHTS TRENDS "HAVE (TAKEN)
SOME HIGHLY UNFORTUNATE BACKWARD STEPS" IN EL SALVADOR, DENOUNCED
THE LETHAL TACTICS OF SOME BRANCHES OF ITS SECURITY FORCES AND
CONDEMNED A COURT THAT RELEASED A SUSPECT CHARGED IN THE DEATHS OF
TWO U.S. CITIZENS. "NORMALLY THE AMBASSADOR WOULDN'T TALK ABOUT
THESE THINGS, BUT THIS ISN'T & NORMAL SITUATION," HINTON SAID LAST
