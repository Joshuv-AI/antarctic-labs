Flourished probably in the fifteenth century. He was the author of notes on Rashi's commentary to the Pentateuch, which were published in "Perushim, le-Rashi," Constantinople, 1525 (?).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. No. 5575; Michael, Or ha-Ḥayyim, No. 281; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. s.v. Jacob Canizal. L. G. I. Ber.
