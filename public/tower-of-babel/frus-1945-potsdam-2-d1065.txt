No. 1065 The Ambassador in France (Caffery) to the United States Delegation[1]

top secret

Paris, July 21, 1945—4 p.m.

[46.] Bidault accepted at once on behalf of the French Govt the proposal regarding Greek elections as set out in your Victory 116 telegram.[2]

Sent Frankfurt for Berlin as 46, repeated Washington 4395 and Athens 9.

Caffery

Source: 868.00/7–2145: Telegram
