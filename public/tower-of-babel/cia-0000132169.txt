Iraqi Terrorist Killed by Own Bomb

One Iraqi was killed and another badly wounded in Manila
today by a bomb they were carrying, according to press. The bomb

exploded almost directly across the street from an American
cultural center. |

Renewed Diplomatic Efforts at UN

Representatives of Algeria, India, and perhaps the USSR are
seeking to push the Security Council to call for a ceasefire in
the Gulf:

o Algerian Foreign Minister Ghozali met with the ambassadors
of the Security Council’s permanent members to propose that
diplomacy be given another chance by having the Security
Council announce an end to hostilities.

o Indian Prime Minister Chandra Shekhar called for an
immediate Iraqi withdrawal, followed by a cessation of

hostilities and the resumption of efforts to find a peaceful
solution.

o The Soviet foreign ministry spokesman said that Gorbachev
will launch a Gulf initiative if Iraq announces its
willingness to leave Kuwait and comply with UN resolutions.

o Press reports indicate the PLO has urged the EC and the UN
Security Council to end "the catastrophe".

The Zairian President of the Security Council passed the Soviet
and Indian proposals to the Iraqi Ambassador earlier today,
according to press. |
