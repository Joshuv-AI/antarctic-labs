Polish translator; lived in Cracow; died 1575. He translated into Judæo-German the Book of Proverbs (Cracow, 1582) and the Book of Job (Prague, 1597), supplying them with commentaries in Hebrew.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1666; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. ii. 325; Zunz, Z. G. p. 280. E. C. S. J. L.
