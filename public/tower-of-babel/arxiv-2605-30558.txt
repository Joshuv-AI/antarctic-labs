BV pushforward as a quasi-isomorphism
Alberto S. Cattaneo, Pavel Mnev

Abstract
. Given a BV theory on a space of fields split into two subspaces (“infrared” and “ultraviolet”), one has the BV pushforward map P ∗ P_{*} , sending observables to observables of the effective theory on the infrared space. This note proves that P ∗ P_{*} is a quasi-isomorphism of BV complexes, by realizing it as a part of a strong deformation retraction constructed using the homological perturbation lemma. Two proofs are given: (i) comparing Feynman diagrams for P ∗ P_{*} with “cable diagrams” arising from homological perturbation theory and (ii) using topological quantum mechanics. This construction gives a formula for the quasi-inverse i int i_{\mathrm{int}} of P ∗ P_{*} —the map lifting observables of the effective theory to the full theory. The topological quantum mechanics perspective—and its realization as an AKSZ theory—allows one to write i int i_{\mathrm{int}} as a path integral (realizing cable diagrams for i int i_{\mathrm{int}} as Feynman diagrams) and to study its classical limit.

On the space of

, one has a canonical differential operator

(the BV Laplacian) of degree

Given a Lagrangian submanifold

\int_{\mathcal{L}}\colon\mathrm{Dens}^{\frac{1}{2}}(\mathcal{F})\rightarrow\mathbb{C},\quad\rho\mapsto\int_{\mathcal{L}}\rho|_{\mathcal{L}}

By default, we consider cochain complexes of finite-dimensional vector spaces over

Definition 3.1
.

A

(34)

and

Lemma 3.2

(Homological perturbation lemma)

with

\widetilde{i}=\sum_{k\geq 0}(-K\delta)^{k}i,\;\;\widetilde{p}=\sum_{k\geq 0}p(-\delta K)^{k},\;\;\widetilde{K}=\sum_{k\geq 0}K(-\delta K)^{k}.
