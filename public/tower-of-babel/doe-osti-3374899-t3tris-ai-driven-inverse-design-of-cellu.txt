Software Architecture and Model Design
 • Built in Keras/TensorFlow using a conditional variational autoencoder (cVAE) framework.
 • Inputs: Stress-strain curve → PCA encoding → generative model.
 • Outputs: Binary topology maps of 2D unit cells (50×50 resolution).
 • Includes decoder-only and encoder-decoder inference modes for exibility.

Next Steps and Development Goals
 • Scale from 2D to 3D mesostructures
 • Expand to multi-objective design (thermal, acoustic, biological)
 • Improve printability delity and resolution integration
 • Integrate with commercial AM platforms and topology optimization tools
