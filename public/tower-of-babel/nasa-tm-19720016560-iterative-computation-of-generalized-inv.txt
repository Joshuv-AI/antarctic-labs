1ft. ABSTRACT

 A cubically convergent iterative method for computing the generalized inverse of an
 arbitrary M x N matrix A is developed and a Fortran subroutine by which the method was
 implemented for real matrices on a CDC 3200 is given, with a numerical example to illustrate
 accuracy. Application to a redundant single-gimbal CMG assembly steering law is discussed.
