Accelerated Backward Forward Method for Convex Optimization
Zepeng Wang, Juan Peypouquet

Abstract
We analyze the convergence rate of an accelerated backward forward method for solving convex composite optimization problems. The method was developed by Taylor, Hendrickx and Glineur, and is different from the FISTA algorithm in its placement of the proximal operator. When the smooth part of the objective function is convex, we establish a fast convergence rate of 𝒪 ⁡ ( 1 k 2 ) \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}\right) for the function values, and prove the weak convergence of the iterates. When the smooth part is strongly convex, we propose a variant of the method, and establish an accelerated linear convergence rate for the function values.



keywords
Accelerated gradient method, Accelerated backward forward, Accelerated proximal gradient method, Convex composite optimization

1
Introduction

Let
H
be a real Hilbert space, and consider the convex composite optimization problem:

\min_{x\in H}F(x)=f(x)+g(x),

(1)

where
f:H\to\mathbb{R}
is convex and
L
-smooth;
g:H\to\mathbb{R}\cup\{\infty\}
is convex, proper and lower-semicontinuous. We assume that the solution set
\mathcal{S}=\argmin_{x\in H}F(x)
is nonempty and denote
F^{*}=\min_{x\in H}F(x)
. Throughout this paper, the proximal operator
\prox_{g}:H\to H
, associated with the function
g
, is defined by

\prox_{g}(z):=\argmin_{x\in H}\left\{g(x)+\frac{1}{2}\|x-z\|^{2}\right\},\quad\hbox{for}\quad z\in H.

To solve problem (
1
), one can employ the accelerated forward backward method, also known as FISTA
Beck and Teboulle 2009
:

\left\{\begin{aligned} y_{k+1}&=\prox_{sg}\left(x_{k}-s\nabla f(x_{k})\right),\\
x_{k+1}&=y_{k+1}+\frac{k}{k+\alpha}(y_{k+1}-y_{k}),\end{aligned}\right.

(FISTA)

where
k\geq 0
,
\alpha\geq 3
and
s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right]
. It was shown that
F(y_{k+1})-F^{*}\leq\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}\right)
when
\alpha\geq 3

Beck and Teboulle 2009
, and that
F(y_{k+1})-F^{*}\leq o\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}\right)
when
\alpha>3

Attouch and Peypouquet 2016
. A weak convergence of the iterates generated by (
FISTA
) can be guaranteed for all
\alpha\geq 3

Chambolle and Dossal 2015
;
Attouch et al. 2018
;
Jang and Ryu 2025
;
Boţ et al. 2025
;
Bauschke and Moursi 2026
. If
f
is
\mu
-strongly convex, (
FISTA
) can be modified as
Beck 2017

\left\{\begin{aligned} y_{k+1}&=\prox_{sg}\left(x_{k}-s\nabla f(x_{k})\right),\\
x_{k+1}&=y_{k+1}+\frac{1-\sqrt{\mu/L}}{1+\sqrt{\mu/L}}(y_{k+1}-y_{k}),\end{aligned}\right.

(FISTA-SC)

and provides an accelerated linear convergence rate of
\mathcal{O}\left((1-\sqrt{\mu/L})^{k}\right)
for the function values
Beck 2017
.

The algorithms (
FISTA
) and (
FISTA-SC
) generalize Nesterov’s accelerated gradient method
Nesterov 1983
;
Nesterov 2004
to the forward backward setting. Compared to forward backward algorithms, backward forward algorithms have received relatively less attention
Attouch et al. 2018
. An alternative method to solve (
1
) is the accelerated
backward forward
algorithms, one of which is
(
Taylor et al. 2017
, Algorithm FPGM2)
:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}y_{k+1}&=&x_{k}-s\nabla f(x_{k}),\\[3.0pt]
z_{k+1}&=&y_{k+1}+\lambda_{k+1}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}s}{\gamma_{k}}(z_{k}-x_{k}),\\[3.0pt]
x_{k+1}&=&\prox_{\gamma_{k+1}g}(z_{k+1}),\end{array}\right.

(ABF)

where
k\geq 0
,
s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right]
,
x_{0}=\prox_{\gamma_{0}g}(z_{0})
,
\gamma_{k+1}=(1+\lambda_{k+1})s
and
(\lambda_{k+1})_{k\geq 0}
is a positive sequence.

The performance estimation (see
Drori and Teboulle 2014
) analysis carried out in
Taylor et al. 2017
shows a slightly—but strictly!—better worst-case convergence rate for (
ABF
) with respect to (
FISTA
). However, no theoretical convergence proof has been established, and the strongly convex case has not been studied.

In this paper, we revisit (
ABF
) and carry out the corresponding convergence analysis. In the convex case, we establish a theoretical convergence rate guarantee for the function values, that exactly matches the one known for (
FISTA
), showing that (
ABF
) is at least as fast, and confirming the numerical findings in
Taylor et al. 2017
. We also prove the weak convergence of the iterates to a minimizer of the objective function. In the case where
f
is strongly convex, we propose an adaptation of (
ABF
) that achieves the accelerated linear convergence rate for the function values which is typical of (
FISTA-SC
).

The paper is structured as follows: In Section
2
, we briefly discuss the fixed-point properties of (
ABF
), especially in connection with the solutions of (
1
). Section
3
is technical, and contains estimations for the function value gap and its decrease along the iterations. Under convexity, the accelerated convergence rate result is given in Section
4
. The weak convergence of the sequences generated by (
ABF
) is shown in Section
5
. Section
6
is devoted to the study of a linearly convergent backward-forward method for strongly convex problems. We conclude in Section
7
.

2
Fixed-point and asymptotic properties

In (
FISTA
), the (constant) step sizes used in the proximal and the gradient descent operators are the same, namely
s
. As a consequence, the fixed points of (
FISTA
) are precisely the solutions of (
1
). On the other hand, (
ABF
) uses two different step sizes, namely
s
and
\gamma_{k+1}=(1+\lambda_{k+1})s
, one of which can be iteration-dependent. In the convex case, our choice of parameters implies that
\lambda_{k+1}\to 1
, whence
\gamma_{k+1}\to 2s
(not
s
) as
k\to\infty
.

Suppose
(x^{*},y^{*},z^{*})
is an
asymptotic
fixed point of (
ABF
), in the sense that the iteration-dependent parameters are replaced by their limits. This gives:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}y^{*}&=&x^{*}-s\nabla f(x^{*})\\
z^{*}&=&y^{*}+(y^{*}-y^{*})+\frac{1}{2}(z^{*}-x^{*})\\
x^{*}&=&\prox_{2sg}(z^{*}),\end{array}\right.

which is equivalent to

\left\{\begin{array}[]{rcl}2x^{*}-2y^{*}&=&2s\nabla f(x^{*})\\
2y^{*}-x^{*}-z^{*}&=&0\\
z^{*}-x^{*}&\in&2s\partial g(x^{*}).\end{array}\right.

This easily implies that

0\in\nabla f(x^{*})+\partial g(x^{*}),

and so
x^{*}
is a minimizer of
F
. It also follows that
y^{*}=x^{*}-s\nabla f(x^{*})
and
z^{*}=x^{*}-2s\nabla f(x^{*})
, respectively. These are the relationships between the limits of the sequences generated by (
ABF
), provided they exist (which they do, as we shall see later).

Remark 1
.

The set
\mathcal{S}
of solutions to problem (
1
) may have multiple points. However, the gradient of
f
is constant on
\mathcal{S}
, in view of the present hypotheses. Indeed, let
x_{1}^{*},x_{2}^{*}\in\mathcal{S}
, so that
-\nabla f(x_{i}^{*})\in\partial g(x_{i}^{*})
for
i=1,2
. Since
f
is
L
-smooth and convex, and
g
is convex, we have

\displaystyle f(x_{1}^{*})

\displaystyle\geq f(x_{2}^{*})+\langle\nabla f(x_{2}^{*}),x_{1}^{*}-x_{2}^{*}\rangle+\frac{1}{2L}\|\nabla f(x_{1}^{*})-\nabla f(x_{2}^{*})\|^{2}

\displaystyle g(x_{1}^{*})

\displaystyle\geq g(x_{2}^{*})-\langle\nabla f(x_{2}^{*}),x_{1}^{*}-x_{2}^{*}\rangle.

Adding the two inequalities, we deduce that

F(x_{1}^{*})\geq F(x_{2}^{*})+\frac{1}{2L}\|\nabla f(x_{1}^{*})-\nabla f(x_{2}^{*})\|^{2}.

Interchanging the roles of
x_{1}^{*}
and
x_{2}^{*}
, we deduce that
\nabla f(x_{1}^{*})=\nabla f(x_{2}^{*})
.

3
Estimations for the function value gap and its decrease

Proposition 1
.

Let
f:H\to\mathbb{R}
be
\mu
-strongly convex and
L
-smooth. Consider algorithm (
ABF
) with
s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right]
, and set
x^{*}\in\mathcal{S}
. Then, for every
k\geq 0
, we have

\displaystyle F(x_{k+1})-F(x_{k})

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{2s(1-\mu s)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle,

and

\displaystyle F(x_{k+1})-F^{*}+(1+\lambda_{k+1})\eta_{k+1}-\lambda_{k+1}\eta_{k}+\psi_{k+1}

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle,

where

\displaystyle\eta_{k}

\displaystyle:=\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k}-x^{*}\right\rangle-\left(g(x_{k})-g(x^{*})\right)\geq 0,

\displaystyle\psi_{k}

\displaystyle:=\langle\nabla f(x_{k}),x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle-\left(f(x_{k})-f(x^{*})\right)\geq 0.

Proof.

From the update rule for
x_{k+1}
and
z_{k+1}
in (
ABF
), we obtain

\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}}\in\partial g(x_{k+1}),

and

\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}}=\frac{-(x_{k+1}-y_{k+1})+\lambda_{k+1}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})}{(1+\lambda_{k+1})s}+\left(\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{1+\lambda_{k+1}}\right)\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}.

(2)

Multiplying both sides by
1+\lambda_{k+1}
gives

\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}}+\lambda_{k+1}\left(\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}}-\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}\right)=\frac{-(x_{k+1}-y_{k+1})+\lambda_{k+1}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})}{s}.

(3)

Taking inner product with
x_{k+1}-x_{k}
and using the convexity of
g
, we deduce that

g(x_{k+1})-g(x_{k})\leq-\frac{1}{s}\langle x_{k+1}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle.

Since
f
is
\mu
-strongly convex and
L
-smooth, we know from
(
Taylor et al. 2017
, Theorem 4)
that

\displaystyle\quad f(x_{k+1})-f(x_{k})

\displaystyle\leq\langle\nabla f(x_{k+1}),x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle-\frac{s}{2}\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})-\nabla f(x_{k})\|^{2}-\frac{\mu}{2(1-\mu s)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-x_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle-\frac{s}{2}\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})-\nabla f(x_{k})\|^{2}-\frac{\mu}{2(1-\mu s)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}.

Summing up the last two inequalities gives

\displaystyle F(x_{k+1})-F(x_{k})

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{s}{2}\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})-\nabla f(x_{k})\|^{2}-\frac{\mu}{2(1-\mu s)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}.

Using

\nabla f(x_{k+1})=-\frac{1}{s}(y_{k+2}-x_{k+1}),\quad\nabla f(x_{k})=-\frac{1}{s}(y_{k+1}-x_{k}),

we obtain

\displaystyle\quad\frac{s}{2}\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})-\nabla f(x_{k})\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2s}\|(y_{k+2}-y_{k+1})-(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2s}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}+\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle,

so that

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{s}{2}\|\nabla f(x_{k+1})-\nabla f(x_{k})\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2s}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}.

This results in

\displaystyle F(x_{k+1})-F(x_{k})

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{2s(1-\mu s)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle.

Likewise, we take inner product of (
3
) with
x_{k+1}-x^{*}
to obtain

\displaystyle\quad(1+\lambda_{k+1})\left\langle\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\right\rangle-\lambda_{k+1}\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{s}\langle x_{k+1}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle.

Since
\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}\in\partial g(x_{k})
and
g
is convex, we have

\displaystyle\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k}-x^{*}\right\rangle+\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k}-x^{*}\right\rangle+g(x_{k+1})-g(x_{k}),

which results in

\displaystyle\quad\left(g(x_{k+1})-g(x^{*})\right)+(1+\lambda_{k+1})\left[\left\langle\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\right\rangle-\left(g(x_{k+1})-g(x^{*})\right)\right]

\displaystyle\quad-\lambda_{k+1}\left[\left\langle\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}},x_{k}-x^{*}\right\rangle-\left(g(x_{k})-g(x^{*})\right)\right]

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1}{s}\langle x_{k+1}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{s}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle.

Combining this inequality with

\displaystyle\quad f(x_{k+1})-f(x^{*})

\displaystyle=\langle\nabla f(x_{k+1}),x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\left[\langle\nabla f(x_{k+1}),x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\left(f(x_{k+1})-f(x^{*})\right)\right]

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-x_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\left[\langle\nabla f(x_{k+1}),x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\left(f(x_{k+1})-f(x^{*})\right)\right],

we obtain the desired result.
∎

4
Convergence rate for convex problems

In this section, we establish a fast convergence rate
\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}\right)
for (
ABF
) when
f
is convex, which matches the one obtained for (
FISTA
). According to the PEP methodology used in
Taylor et al. 2017
, algorithm (
ABF
) has a strictly better worst-case convergence rate than (
FISTA
) in practice.

Define the sequence
(t_{k})_{k\geq 0}
by

t_{k}=\left\{\begin{array}[]{ccl}1,&&\text{if}\quad k=0,\\[3.0pt]
\frac{m+\sqrt{m^{2}+4t_{k-1}^{2}}}{2},&&\text{if}\quad k\geq 1,\end{array}\right.

(4)

with
m\in(0,1]
, which satisfies
t_{k}\geq 1
and

t_{k+1}(t_{k+1}-m)=t_{k}^{2},

and set

\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{t_{k}-1}{t_{k+1}}.

Remark 2
.

If
m=1
, the
(t_{k})
sequence reduces to the one defined by Nesterov’s method
Nesterov 1983
. In fact, a general
m\in(0,1]
gives

t_{k}^{2}\geq t_{k+1}(t_{k+1}-1),

which suffices to guarantee the convergence of algorithm (
ABF
). In view of this fact, setting
t_{k}=\frac{k+\alpha-1}{\alpha-1}
with
\alpha\geq 3
, we obtain

\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{k}{k+\alpha},\quad\hbox{with}\quad\alpha\geq 3,

using which a similar convergence rate for the function values can be derived.

The main convergence result of this section is:

Theorem 2
.

Let
F=f+g
, where
f:H\to\mathbb{R}
is convex and
L
-smooth, and
g:H\to\mathbb{R}\cup\{\infty\}
is convex, proper and lower-semicontinuous. Let
(x_{k})_{k\geq 0}
,
(y_{k})_{k\geq 0}
and
(z_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be generated by algorithm (
ABF
), where

s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right],\quad\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{t_{k}-1}{t_{k+1}},

with
(t_{k})_{k\geq 0}
defined by (
4
), and initial conditions

\gamma_{0}=s,\quad y_{0}\in H,\quad z_{0}=y_{0}-\gamma_{0}\nabla f(y_{0}),\quad x_{0}=\prox_{\gamma_{0}g}(z_{0}).

For every
k\geq 0
, we have

F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq\frac{\|y_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}}{2st_{k}^{2}}.

Besides, every weak subsequential limit point of
x_{k}
, as
k\to\infty
, minimizes
F
.

Our proof relies on the following energy sequence
(E_{k})_{k\geq 0}
:

E_{k}=E_{k}(x^{*}):=st_{k}^{2}\left(F(x_{k})-F^{*}\right)+s(t_{k}-1)\eta_{k}+s(t_{k}-1)\psi_{k}+R_{k},

(5)

where

R_{k}=\frac{t_{k}(t_{k}-1)}{2}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}+(t_{k}-1)\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}.

Remark 3
.

Observe that
R_{k}\geq 0
, since
t_{k}\geq 1
and

R_{k}=\frac{1}{2}\left\|(t_{k}-1)(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+(x_{k}-x^{*})\right\|^{2}+\frac{t_{k}-1}{2}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}.

The following result shall be useful in the forthcoming convergence proof.

Lemma 3

(Lemma 2.3 in
Beck and Teboulle 2009
)
.

Let
F:=f+g
, where
f:H\to\mathbb{R}
is convex and
L
-smooth;

F\left(y-sG_{s}(y)\right)\leq F(x)+\langle G_{s}(y),y-x\rangle-\frac{s}{2}\|G_{s}(y)\|^{2},

where
G_{s}(y)=\frac{y-\prox_{sg}\left(y-s\nabla f(y)\right)}{s}
.

\displaystyle\leq s[t_{k+1}(t_{k+1}-1)-t_{k}^{2}]\left(F(x_{k})-F^{*}\right)-st_{k}\eta_{k+1}-st_{k}\psi_{k}

(10)

\displaystyle-\frac{t_{k}(t_{k}-1)}{2}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}-\frac{t_{k+1}(t_{k+1}-1)}{2}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle+(t_{k}-1)t_{k+1}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq-st_{k}\eta_{k+1}-st_{k}\psi_{k}-\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{t_{k}}\right)\left\|t_{k}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-t_{k+1}(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\right\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq 0,

in view of
t_{k}^{2}\geq t_{k+1}(t_{k+1}-1)
. It follows that

F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq\frac{E_{k}}{st_{k}^{2}}\leq\frac{E_{0}}{st_{k}^{2}},

with

E_{0}=s\left(F(x_{0})-F^{*}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}.

Setting
x=x^{*}
,
y=y_{0}
and
s=\gamma_{0}
in Lemma
3
, we obtain

F(x_{0})\leq F^{*}+\frac{1}{s}\langle y_{0}-x_{0},y_{0}-x^{*}\rangle-\frac{1}{2s}\|y_{0}-x_{0}\|^{2}=F^{*}-\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}+\frac{1}{2s}\|y_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2},

so that

F(x_{0})-F^{*}+\frac{1}{2s}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{2s}\|y_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}.

As a result, we obtain

F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq\frac{\|y_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}}{2st_{k}^{2}}.

Since
t_{k}\to\infty
as
k\to\infty
, the weak lower-semicontinuity of
F
gives the minimizing property.
\square

Remark 4
.

From (
10
), we obtain

st_{k}\eta_{k+1}+st_{k}\psi_{k}+\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{t_{k}}\right)\left\|t_{k}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-t_{k+1}(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\right\|^{2}\leq E_{k}-E_{k+1},

so that

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}t_{k}\eta_{k+1}<\infty,\quad\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}t_{k}\psi_{k}<\infty,\quad\hbox{and}\quad\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left\|t_{k}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-t_{k+1}(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\right\|^{2}<\infty.

This implies that

\lim_{k\to\infty}t_{k}\eta_{k+1}=\lim_{k\to\infty}t_{k}\psi_{k}=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\|t_{k}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-t_{k+1}(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\right\|=0.

5
Boundedness and convergence of the iterates

We begin this section by establishing the following boundedness result:

Proposition 4
.

The sequences
(x_{k})_{k\geq 0}
,
(y_{k})_{k\geq 0}
and
(z_{k})_{k\geq 0}
, generated by algorithm (
ABF
), are bounded.

Proof.

Recalling Remark
3
and writing
\xi_{k}=(t_{k}-1)(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+x_{k}
, we obtain

R_{k}=\frac{1}{2}\|\xi_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}+\frac{t_{k}-1}{2}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}.

Since the sequence
(E_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is nonincreasing, we have

\frac{1}{2}\|\xi_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}\leq E_{0},

which implies that
\|\xi_{k}\|\leq\|x^{*}\|+\sqrt{2E_{0}}=:M
. Using
x_{k}=y_{k+1}+s\nabla f(x_{k})
, we obtain

\xi_{k}=t_{k}y_{k+1}-(t_{k}-1)y_{k}+s\nabla f(x_{k}),

so that

y_{k+1}=\left(1-\frac{1}{t_{k}}\right)y_{k}+\frac{1}{t_{k}}\xi_{k}+\frac{s}{t_{k}}\nabla f(x_{k}).

This gives

\|y_{k+1}\|\leq\left(1-\frac{1}{t_{k}}\right)\|y_{k}\|+\frac{M}{t_{k}}+\frac{1}{t_{k}}\|s\nabla f(x_{k})\|.

Since
f
is convex and
L
-smooth, we have

\frac{1}{2L}\|\nabla f(x_{k})-\nabla f(x^{*})\|^{2}\leq\psi_{k}\leq\frac{E_{k}}{s(t_{k}-1)}\leq\frac{E_{0}}{s(t_{k}-1)},\ \forall\,k\geq 1,

(11)

which implies that
\nabla f(x_{k})\to\nabla f(x^{*})
as
k\to\infty
, and that
\|\nabla f(x_{k})\|\leq B
for all
k\geq 0
and some
B>0
. As a result,

\|y_{k+1}\|\leq\max\left\{\|y_{k}\|,M,sB\right\}\leq\max\left\{\|y_{0}\|,M,sB\right\},

and so
(y_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is bounded. Since
x_{k}=y_{k+1}+s\nabla f(x_{k})
, we have

\|x_{k}\|\leq\|y_{k+1}\|+s\|\nabla f(x_{k})\|,

so that
(x_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is also bounded. Notice that

\|-(x_{k+1}-y_{k+1})+\lambda_{k+1}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})\|\leq\|x_{k+1}\|+\|y_{k+1}\|+\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|\leq D<\infty,

for some
D>0
. Using (
2
), we arrive at

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{z_{k+1}-x_{k+1}}{\gamma_{k+1}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{D}{(1+\lambda_{k+1})s}+\left(\frac{\lambda_{k+1}}{1+\lambda_{k+1}}\right)\left\|\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}\right\|,\frac{D}{s}\right\}.

This implies that
\left\|\frac{z_{k}-x_{k}}{\gamma_{k}}\right\|
is also bounded. Since
(x_{k})
is bounded and
\gamma_{k}\geq s
for all
k
,
(z_{k})
must be bounded as well.
∎

Remark 5
.

Using Remark
3
, and the fact that the sequence
(E_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is nonincreasing, we deduce that

\frac{t_{k}-1}{2}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}\leq R_{k}\leq E_{k}\leq E_{0}.

This shows that
\lim_{k\to\infty}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|=0
.

Remark 6
.

In view of (
11
), as
t\to\infty
,
\nabla f(x_{k})
converges (strongly) to
\nabla f(x^{*})
. This does not depend on the
x^{*}\in\mathcal{S}
chosen, as seen in Remark
1
.

5.1
Weak convergence of the iterates

The following results will be used to prove the convergence of the sequences generated by (
ABF
):

Lemma 5

(Lemma A.4 in
Boţ et al. 2025
)
.

Let
(u_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be a real sequence and
(\zeta_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be positive such that
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\zeta_{k}}=\infty
. If

\lim_{k\to\infty}[u_{k+1}+\zeta_{k}(u_{k+1}-u_{k})]=b\in\mathbb{R},

then,

\lim_{k\to\infty}u_{k}=b.

Lemma 6
.

Let
(W_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be a sequence in
H
, and let
(\zeta_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be a positive sequence such that
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\zeta_{k}}=\infty
. If

\lim_{k\to\infty}\big[W_{k+1}+\zeta_{k}(W_{k+1}-W_{k})\big]=W\in H
, then
\lim_{k\to\infty}W_{k}=W
.

Proof.

Write
u_{k}=\|W_{k}-W\|
. By the (reverse) triangle inequality, we know that

\displaystyle\big|u_{k+1}+\zeta_{k}(u_{k+1}-u_{k})\big|

\displaystyle=\Big|\big\|(1+\zeta_{k})(W_{k+1}-W)\|-\big\|\zeta_{k}(W_{k}-W)\big\|\Big|

\displaystyle\leq\Big\|W_{k+1}+\zeta_{k}(W_{k+1}-W_{k})-W\Big\|.

Since the right-hand side goes to zero, Lemma
5
shows that
\lim_{k\to\infty}\|W_{k}-W\|=0
.
∎

Now we are in a position to prove the weak convergence of the iterates.

Theorem 7
.

Consider algorithm (
ABF
), defined as in Theorem
2
. Then,
x_{k}
converges weakly, as
k\to\infty
, to a minimizer
x^{*}
of
F
. Moreover,
y_{k}
and
z_{k}
converge weakly, as
k\to\infty
, to
y^{*}:=x^{*}-s\nabla f(x^{*})
and
z^{*}:=x^{*}-2s\nabla f(x^{*})
, respectively.

Proof.

By Theorem
2
, every weak subsequential limit point of
x_{k}
, as
k\to\infty
, is a minimizer of
F
. Recalling that from Proposition
4
,
(x_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is bounded, we only need to prove that there cannot be two such limit points. To this end, suppose that
x_{m_{k}}\rightharpoonup\xi^{*}
and
x_{n_{k}}\rightharpoonup\widetilde{\xi}^{*}
, as
k\to\infty
. Then
\xi^{*},\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\in\argmin(F)
. Our objective is to show
\xi^{*}=\widetilde{\xi}^{*}
. Define

U_{k}=E_{k}(\xi^{*})-E_{k}(\widetilde{\xi}^{*}).

Since
(E_{k})_{k\geq 0}
is nonincreasing and bounded from below,
\lim_{k\to\infty}U_{k}
exists. From Remark
4
, we know that

\lim_{k\to\infty}t_{k}\eta_{k+1}=\lim_{k\to\infty}t_{k}\psi_{k}=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left\|t_{k}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-t_{k+1}(x_{k+1}-x_{k})\right\|=0.

From the definition of
E_{k}
in (
5
), we obtain

\displaystyle\lim_{k\to\infty}U_{k}

\displaystyle=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left[-(t_{k}-1)\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},\xi^{*}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\xi^{*}\right\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right]

\displaystyle=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left[-\left(t_{k+1}-\frac{t_{k+1}}{t_{k}}\right)\langle x_{k+1}-x_{k},\xi^{*}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\xi^{*}\right\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2}\right].

Defining

u_{k}=\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\xi^{*}\right\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left\|x_{k}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2}=-\left\langle x_{k},\xi^{*}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\|\xi^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left\|\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2},

we get

\displaystyle\lim_{k\to\infty}U_{k}

\displaystyle=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left[\left(t_{k+1}-\frac{t_{k+1}}{t_{k}}\right)(u_{k+1}-u_{k})+u_{k}\right]

\displaystyle=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left[u_{k+1}+\left(t_{k+1}-\frac{t_{k+1}}{t_{k}}-1\right)(u_{k+1}-u_{k})\right].

Invoking Lemma
5
, we deduce that
\lim_{k\to\infty}u_{k}
exists, and equals
\lim_{k\to\infty}U_{k}
. Replacing
k
by
m_{k}
, and then by
n_{k}
in the definition of
u_{k}
, and taking limits, we obtain

-\frac{1}{2}\left\|\xi^{*}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2}=\lim_{k\to\infty}u_{k}=\frac{1}{2}\left\|\xi^{*}-\widetilde{\xi}^{*}\right\|^{2},

which implies that
\xi^{*}=\widetilde{\xi}^{*}
. This means that
x_{k}
converges weakly to a minimizer of
F
, which we denote by
x^{*}
. Since
y_{k+1}=x_{k}-s\nabla f(x_{k})
, Remark
6
shows that
y_{k}
converges weakly to
y^{*}=x^{*}-s\nabla f(x^{*})
. For
z_{k}
, we rewrite the second line of (
ABF
) as

(1+\lambda_{k+1})(z_{k+1}-y_{k+1})=\lambda_{k+1}(1+\lambda_{k+1})(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+\lambda_{k+1}(z_{k}-x_{k}),

which is equivalent to

\displaystyle(1+\lambda_{k+1})(z_{k+1}-y_{k+1})-\lambda_{k+1}(z_{k}-y_{k})

\displaystyle=\lambda_{k+1}^{2}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+\lambda_{k+1}(y_{k+1}-x_{k})

\displaystyle=\lambda_{k+1}^{2}(y_{k+1}-y_{k})-\lambda_{k+1}s\nabla f(x_{k}),

where we have used
y_{k+1}-x_{k}=-s\nabla f(x_{k})
. As a consequence of Remarks
5
and
6
, the right-hand side converges to
W:=-s\nabla f(x^{*})
as
k\to\infty
. Using Lemma
6
with
W_{k}:=z_{k}-y_{k}
, we deduce that
z_{k}
converges weakly, as
k\to\infty
, to
z^{*}=y^{*}+W=x^{*}-2s\nabla f(x^{*})
.
∎

6
Convergence rate in the strongly convex case

In this section, we establish an accelerated linear convergence rate for (
ABF
) with

\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{1-\theta}{1+\theta},\quad\theta=\sqrt{\mu s},\quad s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right],

when
f
is
\mu
-strongly convex for some
\mu>0
. Algorithm (
ABF
) has not been studied in this context before. Our analysis centers around the following energy sequence
(E_{k})_{k\geq 0}
:

E_{k}=F(x_{k})-F^{*}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}+R_{k},

(12)

where

R_{k}=\frac{\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}+\frac{\theta}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\theta^{2}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}.

Remark 7
.

Notice that

R_{k}=\frac{1}{2s(1+\theta)}\|(y_{k+1}-y_{k})+\theta(x_{k}-x^{*})\|^{2}\geq 0.

Hence, we have
E_{k}\geq 0
and
F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq E_{k}
.

The following result shall be useful in the forthcoming convergence proof.

Lemma 8
.

Let
a,c\geq 0
and
b\in\mathbb{R}
satisfy
b^{2}\leq 4ac
. Then,
a\|\xi\|^{2}+b\langle\xi,\zeta\rangle+c\|\zeta\|^{2}\geq 0
for every
\xi,\zeta\in H
.

Proof.

Let
W(\xi,\zeta)=a\|\xi\|^{2}+b\langle\xi,\zeta\rangle+c\|\zeta\|^{2}
. If
a=0
, then
b=0
, and so
W(\xi,\zeta)=c\|\zeta\|^{2}\geq 0
. On the other hand, if
a>0
, we have
W(\xi,\zeta)=a\left\|\xi+\frac{b\zeta}{2a}\right\|^{2}+\frac{4ac-b^{2}}{4a}\|\zeta\|^{2}\geq 0
.
∎

The linear convergence result is as follows:

Theorem 9
.

Let
F=f+g
, where
f:H\to\mathbb{R}
is
\mu
-strongly convex and
L
-smooth, and
g:H\to\mathbb{R}\cup\{\infty\}
is convex, proper and lower-semicontinuous. Let
(x_{k})_{k\geq 0}
,
(y_{k})_{k\geq 0}
and
(z_{k})_{k\geq 0}
be generated by algorithm (
ABF
), where

s\in\left(0,\frac{1}{L}\right],\quad\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{1-\theta}{1+\theta},\quad\theta=\sqrt{\mu s},

and initial conditions

\gamma_{0}=s,\quad z_{0}\in H,\quad x_{0}=\prox_{\gamma_{0}g}(z_{0}),\quad y_{0}=x_{0}-s\nabla f(x_{0}).

For every
k\geq 0
, we have

F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq(1-\theta)^{k}\left[F(x_{0})-F^{*}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{0}+\frac{\theta}{2s}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}\right].

Proof.

Using (
12
), we have

\displaystyle E_{k+1}-(1-\theta)E_{k}

(13)

\displaystyle=(1-\theta)\left(F(x_{k+1})-F(x_{k})\right)+\theta\left(F(x_{k+1})-F^{*}\right)+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k+1}-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{1+\theta}\eta_{k}

\displaystyle+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k+1}-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}+\frac{1}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}-\frac{1-\theta}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle+\frac{\theta}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle

\displaystyle+\frac{\theta^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k+1}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{\theta^{2}(1-\theta)}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}.

Setting
\lambda_{k+1}=\frac{1-\theta}{1+\theta}
in Proposition
1
and keeping in mind that
\theta^{2}=\mu s
, we obtain

\displaystyle(1-\theta)\left(F(x_{k+1})-F(x_{k})\right)+\frac{1}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+2}-y_{k+1}\|^{2}-\frac{1-\theta}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}

(14)

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{1-\theta}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}-\frac{1-\theta}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}+\frac{(1-\theta)^{2}}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle,

and

\displaystyle\theta\left(F(x_{k+1})-F^{*}\right)+\frac{2\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k+1}-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{1+\theta}\eta_{k}+\theta\psi_{k+1}

(15)

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{\theta}{s}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle

\displaystyle=-\frac{\theta}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle

\displaystyle-\frac{\theta^{2}}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle+\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle.

For the second to last term in (
15
), we substitute (
9
), to obtain

\displaystyle\quad\theta\left(F(x_{k+1})-F^{*}\right)+\frac{2\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k+1}-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{1+\theta}\eta_{k}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k+1}-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\theta}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+2}-y_{k+1},x_{k+1}-x^{*}\rangle-\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k}-x^{*}\rangle

\displaystyle\quad+\frac{\theta^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k+1}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{\theta^{2}(1-\theta)}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{\theta(1-\theta)}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle+\frac{\theta^{3}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}.

Using this inequality and (
14
) in (
13
), we obtain

\displaystyle\quad E_{k+1}-(1-\theta)E_{k}

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k+1}+\frac{\theta^{3}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}-\frac{1-\theta}{2s(1+\theta)}\|y_{k+1}-y_{k}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\quad-\frac{1-\theta}{2s}\|x_{k+1}-x_{k}\|^{2}+\frac{1-\theta}{s(1+\theta)}\langle y_{k+1}-y_{k},x_{k+1}-x_{k}\rangle

\displaystyle\leq-\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{k+1}+\frac{\theta^{3}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{k}

\displaystyle\leq 0,

where the second inequality is due to
\theta\leq 1
and Lemma
8
, and the last inequality is due to strong convexity of
f
, which implies that
\psi_{k}\geq\frac{\mu}{2}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}=\frac{\theta^{2}}{2s}\|x_{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}
. Hence, we obtain
E_{k+1}\leq(1-\theta)E_{k}
for all
k\geq 0
. Iterating, we obtain

F(x_{k})-F^{*}\leq E_{k}\leq(1-\theta)^{k}E_{0}.

With
y_{0}=x_{0}-s\nabla f(x_{0})=y_{1}
in mind, we have

\displaystyle E_{0}

\displaystyle=F(x_{0})-F^{*}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{0}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\psi_{0}+\frac{\theta^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq F(x_{0})-F^{*}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{0}+\frac{\theta}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}+\frac{\theta^{2}}{2s(1+\theta)}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=F(x_{0})-F^{*}+\frac{\theta}{1+\theta}\eta_{0}+\frac{\theta}{2s}\|x_{0}-x^{*}\|^{2},

where the inequality is due to
L
-smoothness of
f
and
s\leq\frac{1}{L}
. Hence, we obtain the desired result.
∎

Remark 8
.

Setting
s=\frac{1}{L}
, the convergence rate
\mathcal{O}\left((1-\sqrt{\mu/L})^{k}\right)
matches the one for (
FISTA-SC
).

7
Conclusions

We provided a convergence proof for the
\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}\right)
rate of the accelerated backward forward algorithm (
ABF
) when
f
is convex, and established the weak convergence of the iterates. When
f
is strongly convex, we also developed a variant of (
ABF
), for which we proved an accelerated linear convergence rate for the function values.
