There was a mistake in Figure 4A as published.

By accident, the same image of the wild-type and complementing mutant strain was used at 50 μg/ml Congo Red. The experiment was repeated, and the corrected Figure 4A appears below.

PakA is important for the response to cell wall stress. (A) The wild-type, ΔpakA, and ΔpaKA:pakA+ and ΔcrzA ΔzipD mutant strains were grown on minimal media with increasing concentrations of congo red (A), calcofluor white (CFW) (B), and caspofungin (C) for 5 days at 37 °C. The results are expressed as the average of three repetitions ± standard deviation. Statistical analysis was performed using a one-way ANOVA test when compared to the wild-type condition (**p < 0.005; ***p < 0.001).
