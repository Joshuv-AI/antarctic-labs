Renormalization Group.

The tidal response
\mathcal{F}_{\ell,s}
is the ratio of the induced to the applied amplitude, and obeys a projective Riccati flow

\partial_{t}\,\mathcal{F}_{\ell,s}=\delta_{21}+(\delta_{22}-\delta_{11})\,\mathcal{F}_{\ell,s}-\delta_{12}\,\mathcal{F}_{\ell,s}^{2},

(2)

\delta_{ij}
are the entries of the
anomalous-dimension matrix whose eigenvalues are

\pm(\ell-\nu)

\nu=\ell-\nu^{(2)}_{\ell,s}\,\varepsilon^{2}+{\cal O}(\varepsilon^{4})
with
\varepsilon=\omega R_{\rm S}

ILPZu
;
CSZ

To solve the flow, we expand the response in frequency (
\eta=i\omega R_{\rm S}=i\varepsilon
) at fixed running
variable

\frac{\mathcal{F}_{\ell,s}}{4\pi R_{\rm S}^{2\ell+1}}=\sum_{k\geq 0}B^{(k)}_{\ell,s}(y)\,\eta^{k},

\eta

Derivation of the Lift.

It remains to explain why the running log is dressed by precisely

2H(-\eta)
, identically for every multipole and spin. Far from the
horizon the tortoise coordinate
r_{*}=r+R_{\rm S}\log(r/R_{\rm S}-1)
reduces the
radial equation to a Coulomb problem,

\Big[\partial_{r}^{2}+\omega^{2}+\frac{2\omega^{2}R_{\rm S}}{r}-\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{r^{2}}\Big]\psi=0,

(16)

where the Newtonian
1/r
acts as an attractive Coulomb potential with
Sommerfeld parameter
-\omega R_{\rm S}
, which in our variables enters as

i(-\omega R_{\rm S})=-\eta
. In the overlap region
R_{\rm S}\ll r\ll\omega^{-1}
the
outgoing wave is a superposition of the regular and irregular Coulomb
solutions carrying the renormalized angular momentum
\nu
,

\psi^{\rm out}\big|_{\omega r\to 0}\sim C_{\nu}\,(\omega r)^{\nu+1}+\frac{(\omega r)^{-\nu}}{(2\nu+1)\,C_{\nu}},

(17)

whose reciprocal normalizations (fixed by the Wronskian) are set by a single Gamow
factor
C_{\nu}\propto\Gamma(\nu{+}1{-}\eta)
.

The response is the ratio of
the induced to the applied terms evaluated at the renormalization scale,

Conclusions.

Our closed-form formula (
12
) isolates the scheme-independent content of the black hole tidal response to all orders and for every spin and multipole. It resums both logarithms and the zeta tower through the dressed running log, and serves as a check for future calculations in any scheme. Natural extensions include Kerr black holes, applications to waveforms and multipoles in gravitational-wave modeling, and a derivation of the factorization and its universal dressing directly within an EFT. The result also invites more open-ended questions. The conservative–dissipative pairing in (
12
) suggests a fluctuation–dissipation relation at the Hawking temperature. Separately, the vanishing of static Love numbers reflects a near-horizon
