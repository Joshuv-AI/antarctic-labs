No. 435 Briefing Book Paper

top secret

Suggested United States Policy Regarding Poland

summary

. . . . . . .

… We should facilitate insofar as our aid is requested the transfer of minority groups but we should not permit the forced repatriation of Poles now in the West or the uncontrolled deportation by unilateral Polish action of the 8–10,000,000 Germans formerly domiciled in the areas claimed by the Soviet-sponsored Polish Government.

. . . . . . .

[ Washington ,] June 29, 1945.
