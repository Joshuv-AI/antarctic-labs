# TOB-0085 — EGYPTIAN SECRETS
## Pseudo-Albertus Magnus | c.1840s | Folk Magic / Grimoire

---

## 📖 OVERVIEW

**Full Title:** Albertus Magnus — Being the Approved, Verified, Sympathetic and Natural Egyptian Secrets, White and Black Art for Man and Beast

**Author:** Traditionally attributed to Albertus Magnus (13th c. Saint and Scholar); actually an 18th–19th c. grimoire compilation
**Date:** Earliest German editions c.1722; this archive edition c.1840s–1850s
**Pages:** ~400
**Language:** English
**Category:** Grimoire / Folk Magic / Natural Magic

---

## 📚 CONTENTS

### White Art (Sympathetic Magic)

- Healing remedies for humans and animals
- Herbal medicine and treatments
- Protective charms and spells
- Agricultural magic (protecting livestock and crops)
- Love magic and binding spells

### Black Art (Demonic/Conjuring Magic)

- Formulas for summoning spirits
- Pacts with demons
- Fortune-telling and divination
- Necromantic practices
- Magical seals and sigils

### Practical Secrets

- Making and using magical amulets
- Conjuring treasures
- Witchcraft and sorcery techniques
- Various charms for specific purposes

---

## 📌 HISTORICAL NOTES

**On the name "Magnus":** This is likely the source of any "Magnus Grimoire" reference — Albertus Magnus (c.1206–1280) was a real Dominican friar and scholar, but this text was NOT written by him. It is a pseudepigraphal work from the 1700s–1800s.

**Folk Magic Tradition:** This book represents the " Petit Albert" tradition — a popular French and German folk magic compilation widely used in American colonial and frontier periods.
