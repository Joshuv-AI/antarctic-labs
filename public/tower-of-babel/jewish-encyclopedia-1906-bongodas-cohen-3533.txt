Provençal physician; flourished in 1353. No details of his life can be ascertained. He was the author of a Latin work on obstetrics, still extant in a Turin manuscript (Pasini, cod. 80, 3). If Steinschneider is right, and "Cohen" ( ) is a mistake for "Nathan" ( ) Bongodas may be identical with Judah Nathan , a Provençal writer on medicine and translator, between the years 1352 and 1358.
