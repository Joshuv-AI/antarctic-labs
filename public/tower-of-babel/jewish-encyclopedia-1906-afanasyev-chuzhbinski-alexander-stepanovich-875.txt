Christian Russian author and ethnographer (1817-75); he was an enlightened writer who did justice to the Jews. In his "Poyezdka v Yuzhnuyu Rossiyu" (2 vols., St. Petersburg, 1861-63) he gave a faithful picture of Jewish life in South Russia, and defended the Jews against the accusations of the prejudiced masses.

Bibliography: Sobranie Sochineni, ed. H. Hoppe, vols. viii. and ix., St. Petersburg, 1893; Entziklopedicheski Slovar, vol. ii., St. Petersburg, 1893. H. R.
