Retraction Note: Exploring the dermatological applications of human mesenchymal stem cell secretome: a comprehensive review
Authors: Sona Zare, Alireza Jafarzadeh, Solmaz Zare, Amir Shamloo
Published: 2026

Retraction: Stem Cell Research & Therapy (2025) 16:177

10.1186/s13287-025-04311-8

The Editor-in-Chief is retracting this article because it contains nonsense images. Figures 1 and 2 both contain nonstandard imagery and wording. As a result the Editor-in-Chief has lost confidence in this paper.

All of the authors disagree with this retraction.
