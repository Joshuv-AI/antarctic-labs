CHILE: FPMR ISSUES COMMUNIQUE ON HAEBERLE KIDNAPPING

PY26B3593

MOSCOW RADIO MAGALLANES IN SPANISH TO CHILE 8288 GMT
24 AUG 86

(EXCERPT)? THE MANUEL RODRIGUEZ PATRIOTIC FRONT
(ff PMR)) HAS REPORTED THAT IT HAS SEIZED IMPORTANT DOCUMENTS
FROM KIDNAPFED COLONEL MARIO HAEBERLE AND THAT THE DOCUMENTS
WILL BE RELEASED TO THE PUBLIC. im

' THE FPMR RELEASED A COMMUNIGUE STATING THAT THE COLONEL’ 5S
KIDNAPPING HAS MADE THE MEMBERS OF THE ARMED FORCES, ESPECIALLY
THE ARMY, SIT UP AND THINK, AND THAT IT HAS MADE THEM UNDERSTAND
THAT IF THEY CONTINUE WITH. THEIR REPRESSIVE ACTIONS THEY WILL BE
PUNISHED.

THE COMMUNIQUE ALSO SAYS THAT DECISIVE MOMENTS ARE APPROACHING,
WHEN THE MILITARY MEN MUST DECIDE WHETHER TO FOLLOW GENERAL
PINOCHET INTO THE ABYSS OR ABANCON HIM TO JOIN THE PEOPLE.

THE FPMR RELEASED COL HAEBERLE BECAUSE IT BELIEVED THAT THE
MAIN OBJECTIVE OF THE KIDNAPPING HAD BEEN FULFILLED. THE FPMR
COMMUNIQUE STATES THAT THE EXHORTATION MADE SY THE CHURCH ALSO

INFLUENCED THEIR DECISION TO RELEASE HIM. THE COMMUNIQUE
ADDED: THE CALLS OF THE CATHOLIC CHURCH SHOULD BE HEEDED
BY THE DICTATORSHIP, JUST AS.-WE WELCOME THEM, BECAUSE IN THIS WAY
MANY PATRIOTS COULD BE SAVED FROM THE DUNGEONS AND TORTURE,

THE FPMR HAS DELIVERED THE FIRST DOCUMENT ON THE COMMENTS
MADE BY GENERAL JOHN GALVIN. THE U.S. ARMED FORCES
SOUTHERN COMMAND CHIEF, DURING A SECRET MEETING WITH THE DEPUTY
CHIEFS OF THE CHILEAN ARMED FORCES HELD AT THE U.S.
