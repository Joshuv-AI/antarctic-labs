Nature of Work
Describe the research (summary of Scope of Work and principal objectives of the CRADA):
Fabrication and performance test data generated by Argonne showed the behavior and suitability of Advanced
Moderator Module (AMM) technology to contain metal hydrides (YH) in conditions relevant to the operation of
AlphaTech’s molten salt reactor technology.

Conclusions drawn from this CRADA; include any major accomplishments:
Argonne National Laboratory successfully designed, fabricated, and experimentally evaluated Advanced Moderator
Module (AMM) prototypes in conditions relevant to the operation of AlphaTech’s molten salt reactor under the
Department of Energy GAIN Voucher program. A moderator containment architecture capable of operating in high-
temperature and chemically aggressive environments associated with molten fluoride salts was fabricated and
tested. The AMM concept integrates a refractory-metal liner, multilayer barrier coatings, and a ceramic composite
shell to maintain hydride moderator stability while preventing hydrogen loss and environmental degradation.

Fabrication and assembly of complete AMM modules were performed by using established Argonne coating,
materials processing, and joining techniques. Experimental exposure studies under reactor-relevant thermal
conditions demonstrated that the module architecture preserves moderator integrity and retains hydrogen. Post-test
examinations confirmed the structural stability of the liner, coatings, and composite shell systems. Technical
feasibility of the AMM concept for hydride moderator encapsulation in molten salt reactor conditions was
demonstrated, providing a technical basis for continued technological maturation and validation.
