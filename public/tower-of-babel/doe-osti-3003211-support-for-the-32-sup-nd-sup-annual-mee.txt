DOE Award Number: SC0025920

Project Title: Support for the 32nd Annual Meeting of the Inter-American Photochemical Society

Project Director/Principal Investigator: Lisa A. Fredin (Lehigh University)

Summary. This award offset the registration and travel costs for researchers from U.S. institutions
to attend the 32nd Inter-American Photochemical Society meeting held on Jan 2-5, 2025, in
Miramar Beach, Florida. The Inter-American Photochemical Society (I-APS) is a non-profit
organization dedicated to promoting and disseminating knowledge and encouraging the
development of fundamental and allied photochemistry throughout the Americas. The I-APS
annual meeting is a particularly important tool for fulfilling this goal as it provides a welcoming
venue to highlight research progress in theoretical, experimental, and applied aspects of
photochemistry and molecular photophysics by researchers in all stages of their careers. The
participation of graduate students and postdocs at this conference is particularly important as it
aids in training the next generation of scientists in photochemistry. This award provided travel
awards to three Assistant Professors and fourteen graduate and two undergraduate students to
attend the I-APS Meeting in 2025. A majority of the science presented/discussed at the I-APS
meeting is in direct alignment with the mission of the DOE and the Solar Photochemistry program,
including studying fundamental phenomena, encompassing light absorption, photoinduced
energy/electron transfer, charge separation, and charge and energy transport across mesoscale
dimensions, as well as their application in generating electricity and chemical bonds using light.
