## Contents Overview

Atalanta Fugiens (Atalanta Flying) is an alchemical emblem book containing:
- 50 copperplate engravings with alchemical emblems
- Latin text inscriptions for each emblem
- Musical fugues (the first scientific work to combine music and alchemy)
- Greek and Latin mottoes
- Prose commentaries on each emblem

## Key Emblems

The work is structured around the transformation of base metals into gold, following the stages of the Great Work: nigredo (blackening), albedo (whitening), citrinitas (yellowing), and rubedo (reddening).
