Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 778.]

Department of State, Washington, December 5, 1863.

Sir: I have to acknowledge the receipt of your despatch Nos. 538 to 541 inclusive, and to express my approval of them.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq.,&c, &c, &c.
