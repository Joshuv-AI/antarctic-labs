A Gap in Stanfield’s Proof of Sachs’ Linear Linkless Embedding Conjecture
Ramin Naimi

Abstract




The aim of this note is to describe what I believe is a serious gap
in Stanfield’s
[
1
]
proof of Sachs’ conjecture
that
every linklessly embeddable graph
has a linear linkless embedding in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
.
In
[
1
]
, page 4594, lines 5-6, it says:

(*)

Because
x
is so close to the position of
v
,

\Gamma^{\prime}
is also disjoint from any edges incident
to
x
, since it was disjoint from the edges of
v
.

Below, I give an example that
contradicts statement
(*)
above.
Before doing so I will give a brief description of
the argument in
[
1
]

and what
(*)
is referring to.

Let
G
be a linklessly embeddable graph.
Let
\phi
be a paneled embedding of
G
in
\mathbb{R}^{3}

(this means every cycle in
\phi

bounds a disk in whose interior is disjoint from
\phi
).
We wish to show by induction on the size of
G

that
\phi
is ambient isotopic to a linear embedding.
Let
xy
be an edge of
\phi
.
Then, by the induction hypothesis,
there is an ambient isotopy
F:\mathbb{R}^{3}\times I\to\mathbb{R}^{3}
such that

F_{0}
is the identity map on
\mathbb{R}^{3}
and

F_{1}(\phi/xy)
is a linear embedding.

Let
B_{xy}
be a ball that is a closed
\epsilon
-neighborhood
of
xy\subset\phi
.
Let
v
be a point in the interior of
xy
.
Let
D_{xy}
be a disk in
B_{xy}
such that

D_{xy}\cap\phi=D_{xy}\cap xy=\{v\}
and

D_{xy}\cap\partial B_{xy}=\partial D_{xy}
.
We contract
xy
to
v
,
keeping
\mathbb{R}^{3}\setminus B_{xy}
pointwise fixed.
Let
D_{F}:=F_{1}(D_{xy})
.
(In
[
1
]

this disk is denoted as just
D_{xy}
.)

Now we try to modify
F_{1}(\phi/xy)
to construct a linkless linear embedding
\Psi
of
G
.
We place the vertices
x
and
y
very close to, and on opposite sides of,
D_{F}
.
For each neighbor
w
of
v
in
G/xy
,
the straight edge
vw
in
F_{1}(\phi/xy)

is replaced by the straight edge(s)
xw
or
yw
or both
according to whether

w
is adjacent to
x
,
y
, or both, in
G
.

To show the linear embedding
\Psi
is paneled,
part of the argument in
[
1
]
goes as follows.
Suppose
C
is a cycle in
\Psi

that contains
x
but not
y
.
Since
F_{1}(\phi/xy)
is paneled,
the cycle
C^{\prime}:=C/xy

bounds a disk
\Gamma^{\prime}
whose interior is disjoint from
F_{1}(\phi/xy)
.
It is also shown in
[
1
]

that the interior of
\Gamma^{\prime}
is disjoint from
D_{F}
;
thus
\Gamma^{\prime}\cap D_{F}=\{v\}

\subset\partial\Gamma^{\prime}
.

Now here is the gap.
Statement
(*)
above claims the interior of
\Gamma^{\prime}
is disjoint from
edges incident to
x
.
But the following construction and embedding of a graph
G

and disks
\Gamma^{\prime}
and
D_{F}
contradict
(*)
.

G

,

G

Lastly, we show the disk
D_{F}
can be embedded in a manner
compatible with the argument given in
[

]
.
Let
\beta\subset S^{2}
be the simple closed curve

Let
D_{F}
be the disk obtained by coning

\beta

Then

D_{F}
is disjoint from the interior of
\Gamma^{\prime}

since
\beta

\alpha^{\prime}
.
So this construction contradicts statement
(*)
.

Note that although we can assume

is a “flat” disk,
we cannot assume it is flat
after the ambient isotopy
F

Perhaps it was such an assumption that
led to
(*)
