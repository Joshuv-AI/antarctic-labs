SUBJ: PRESIDENT SAYS FMLN DID NOT KILL PRIESTS

SOURCE: MADRID EFE IN SPANISH 1416 GMT 18 NOV 89
TEXT: .

((TEXT)) CARACAS, 17 NOV (EFE) -- VENEZUELAN PRESIDENT CARLOS
ANDRES PEREZ SAID TODAY THT THE MURDER OF SIX JESUIT PRIESTS AND TWO
WOMEN IN SAN SALVADOR ON 16 NOVEMBER “IT NOT ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE
FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN)."

“PEREZ DESCRIBED THE GUNNING DOWN OF THE JESUITS, A DOMESTIC, AND
THE DOMESTIC'S DAUGHTER AS A “HORRIBLE CRIME," ADDING THAT THIS ACT
"DEMONSTRATES HOW FAR AN ABSOLUTELY UNJUSTIFIED WAR CAN GO."

THE VENEZUELAN PRESIDENT SAID THAT "IT IS INCONCEIVABLE THAT WITH
PEACE TALKS UNDERWAY, THERE SHOULD HAVE BEEN A RESUMPTION OF A WAR
THAT IS TAKING HUNDREDS OF LIVES EVERY DAY."

THE PRESIDENT ADDED THAT SO FAR THERE HAS BEEN NO RESPONSE TO HIS
"REPEATED OFFERS" TO HAVE VENEZUELA BE THE SEAT OF PEACE TALKS
