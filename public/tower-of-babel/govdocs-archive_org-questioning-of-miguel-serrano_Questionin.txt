1) Errors and confusion:
"The only representative of God on earth is the soul". At the beginning of the book, this sentence by 
Meister Eckhart already tells us everything about Miguel Serrano's thinking: The creator God and the 
soul created by Him. Are there not other things? Are there not other Gods, greater and more perfect 
than the God of the
