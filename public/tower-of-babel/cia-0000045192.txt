CUBA: PRIEST DENIES DUARTE REMARKS ON KIDNAPPING NEGOTIATIONS
PA181546
HAVANA INTERNATIONAL SERVICE IN SPANISH 1688 GMT 15 NOV 85

((TEXT)) JESUIT FATHER (IGNACIO AYACURIA), RECTOR OF THE
CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY IN SAN SALVADOR, HAS ACCUSED
SALVADORAN PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON DUARTE OF HAVING GONE TO THE \
UNITED STATES TO MAKE FALSE STATEMENTS ASOUT THE KIDNAPPING OF
HIS DAUGHTER, INES GUADALUPE, BY THE SALVADORAN GUERRILLAS.
(AYACURLA) LENIED THAT He RECEIVED GROERS FROM THE NICARAGUAN
GOVERNMENT IN THE NEGOTIATIONS FOR THE RELEASE OF INES GUADALUPE
DUARTE AND HER FRIEND AS THE SAL VADORAN PRESIDENT FALSELY TOLD
U.S, PRESIDENT RONALD REAGAN.

MAYACURLA) PARTICIPATED INTHE "NEGOTIATIONS WITH THE FMLN
FOR THE EXCHANGE OF INES GUADALUPE, ALONG WITH SAN SAL VADORAN
ARCHBISHOP MSGR ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS,

MEANWHILE, THE SALVADORAN HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSION ACCUSED
THE DUARTE REGIME OF VIOLATING THE GENEVA CONVENTIONS, THE
ADDITIONAL PROTOCOLS, AND THE UNIVERSAL DECLARATION OF HUMAN
RIGHTS. IT DESCRIBED AS TOTALLY FALSE THE OFFICIAL STATEMENTS THAT
THERE IS A PROCESS OF DEMOCRACY IN EL.SALVADOR, THE COMMISSION
STRESSED THAT FOUR OF LTS "WENBERS HAVE BEEN ASSASSINATED AND THREE
HAVE DISAPPEARED IN RECENT YEARS WHILE ITS PROPAGANDA SECRETARY,
JOAQUIN CACERES, IS IMPRISONED UNDER A SUBVERSION CHARGES
