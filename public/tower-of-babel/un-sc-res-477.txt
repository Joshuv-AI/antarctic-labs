'''Adopted unanimously at the 2244th meeting, on 30 July 1980.'''

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of Zimbabwe for admission to the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that Zimbabwe should be admitted to membership in the United Nations.


1. ''Ibid., Supplement for July, August and September 1980'', document S/14064.
