Editorial expression of concern: NMD-1198, a novel tubulin-binding agent reduces HIF-1alpha and STAT3 activity in human hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) cells, and inhibits growth and vascularization in vivo
Authors: Christian Moser, Sven A Lang, Akira Mori, Claus Hellerbrand, Hans J Schlitt, Edward K Geissler, William E Fogler, Oliver Stoeltzing
Published: 2026

Editorial expression of concern: Mol Cancer Ther 6, 2868 (2007)

https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2407-8-206

The Editor would like to alert the readers that concerns have been raised about a possible image similarity detected across two publications from the same authors. Specifically, Fig. 2A β-actin (HUH7) of this paper appears to be a vertical flip of the Fig. 1B, β-Actin bands (HCT116) in [1].

Readers are therefore advised to interpret these results with caution.

None of the Authors responded to correspondence from the Publisher about this Editorial Expression of Concern.
