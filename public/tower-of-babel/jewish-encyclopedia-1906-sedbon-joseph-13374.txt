Rabbinical and cabalistic author of Tunis in the second half of the eighteenth century. He composed a cabalistic treatise entitled "Ahabat Adonai," designed as a commentary on the "Idra Ẓuṭa." This work, although written in 1778, was not published until 1871, when, through the exertions of certain natives of Tunis, it was printed at Leghorn.

Bibliography: Cazès, Notes Bibliographiques, pp. 302-304. K. M. Fr.
