 ABSTRACT

 Our goal is to provide an economically and environmentally advantageous capture technology to
 clean the air at industrial manufacturing facilities, especially wood products manufacturing
 facilities. There are hundreds of wood products manufacturing sites at which this capture
 technology could be implemented and thousands more sites in the broader 104 industries subject
 to the clean air act. This work is critical because when implemented at just 50 wood products sites
 it could capture over 100 million pounds per year of pollutants (wastes) that could be converted
 into over 90 million pounds per year of valuable products rather than burning them as waste.
 These pollutants, primarily turpentine or terpenes, can be used to provide a wide variety of
 products including biofuels, flavors, fragrances, and more. Versus the baseline technology, the
 technology could reduce energy use including fossil fuels by more than 10 trillion BTUs per year
 and drive down operational costs for our customers by up to $150 million per year.

 1. STATEMENT OF OBJECTIVES

 The proposed project is aimed at developing a disruptive prototyping paradigm for an Advanced
 Air Pollution Control Technology. If successful, this project could drastically alter materials
 discovery and development with the potential for sweeping impacts on manufacturing
 capabilities, energy efficiency, and enabling materials and chemicals-based technologies.

 1

This project aims to provide blending components for sustainable aviation fuels that utilize
terpenes as a feedstock.

In this work, the CAIRE™ technology which stands for “Chemical Adsorption Innovation that
Reduces Emissions”, utilizes a Fluidized Bed Concentrator (FBC) with specialized Bead
Activated Carbon (BAC) to capture (adsorb) pollutants released in industrial wood drying
processes. The pollutants released are composed primarily of turpentine or terpenes that could be
utilized to provide biofuels including Sustainable Aviation Fuels (SAF). Versus the baseline
technology, the Regenerative Thermal Oxidizer (the Oxidizer) that controls pollutants by
oxidizing (burning) them, the energy efficient FBC controls pollutants by capturing them via
adsorption. The technology is both economically and environmentally advantageous as compared
to the baseline Oxidizer technology.

 3. TECHNICAL DISCUSSION OF WORK PERFORMED BY ALL PARTIES

The overall purpose of this project is to commercialize the CAIRE™ technology at our customers
sites to clean the air and capture valuable terpenes. The primary focus of this report is the use of
terpenes for providing renewable blending components for SAF. The primary research question
was whether blends prepared from CAIRE™ Terpenes SAF and other Sustainable Aviation Fuels
and/or Jet A (fossil jet fuel) would pass the jet fuel specifications per ASTM D4054 and D7566
and thus be capable of qualifying for use as commercial jet fuel.

To answer this question, multiple terpenes feedstocks and terpenes catalytically converted to
terpenes aromatics and/or saturated terpenes cyclics were obtained. Commercially available jet
fuel samples including Hydrotreated Esters and Fatty Acids (HEFA), a sustainable aviation fuel
(SAF), and Jet A fossil jet fuel were also obtained. A series of blending formulations were
strategically formulated to encompass the anticipated range of possible blending formulations.
Special care was taken to consider the widest possible range of blending formulations to test the
boundaries and maximize the possible use of the terpenes for SAF. The ternary plot shown in
Figure 2 illustrates the blending formulation strategy. Blends were prepared at the limits
identified in this figure. Blends between these points, within the designated regions, have either
been shown to be or are thought to perform similarly.

 5. COMMERCIALIZATION POSSIBILITIES

Work under this project led to a $2.5 Million award from DOE. This award enables the team to do three
things including 1) demonstrate the CAIRE™ technology at a commercial wood products manufacturing
site, 2) convert the terpenes feedstock to CAIRE™ Terpenes SAF, and 3) scale up the conversion of the
terpenes feedstock to CAIRE™ Terpenes SAF. This award furthers the ultimate objective of this work to
commercialize the technology. Multiple very large well recognized and well respected commercial wood
products manufacturers have indicated strong support for the technology as follows: “We would like to
replace all our Oxidizers” and “We are looking for game changing technology to help us meet our energy
efficiency and sustainability goals and are hoping that this technology could be one of those solutions.”

 6. PLANS FOR FUTURE COLLABORATION
