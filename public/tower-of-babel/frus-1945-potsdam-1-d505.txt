No. 505 The Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) to the Secretary of State

[Extract]

secret

London, July 5, 1945—noon.

6749. …

Same [British] official[1] said that FonOff had concluded yesterday with Polish Govt in London agreement whereby all leases, including lease on Polish Emb property, would be taken over by Brit Govt. Most leases are of short duration but Brit Govt will turn them over to new Polish Govt if it so desires or will continue them on own account.

. . . . . . .

Winant

Source: 860c.01/7–545: Telegram

Notes
[1] Not identified.
