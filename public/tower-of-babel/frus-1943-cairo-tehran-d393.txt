The President to the Secretary of State[1] (29 November 1943, d393)

[Tehran,] 29 November 1943.

Deliver to Secretary Hull from the President. Your message transmitted as White 55,[2] I think it more advisable to appoint Winant as our representative.[3]

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the White House Map Room, via military channels.
[2] Telegram printed ante, p. 616.
[3] On November 2, 1943, Winant at London had cabled Roosevelt at Washington that he “would like to have a chance to serve on the European Advisory Commission”. (Roosevelt Papers)
