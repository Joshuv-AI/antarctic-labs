 In the project period, PI’s work [1–39] mostly focuses on the lattice QCD
calculation to hadronic processes, in particular those involving QED interac-
tions. These include the two subjects CKM matrix elements (Vud and Vus ),
and muon g−2, which we plan to continue in this proposal. Most of the works
are performed with collaborators from the RBC-UKQCD collaborations.

1 CKM matrix elements (Vud and Vus)
 In 2021, we calculated the electroweak box diagrams for the kaon semi-
leptonic decays in the flavor SU(3) limit. [1]. This calculation provide the
necessary chiral perturbation theory (ChPT) low-energy constants for de-
termine the QED corrections to kaon semi-leptonic decay rate. This decay
process is one of the two main processes used to extract |Vus |.
 In 2023, we developed a new lattice calculation framework for calculat-
ing the QED corrections to kaon leptonic decay rate. [2] This decay process
is the other main process used to extract |Vus |. Compared with previous

 1

lattice calculations, the new framework combines infinite volume QED for-
mulation with finite volume lattice calculation, eliminating all the power-law
suppressed finite volume effects, which is the main source of uncertainty in
previous lattice calculations. In this framework, in addition to the analytical
infinite volume photon propagator, we also need to use the infinite volume
reconstruction (IVR) method, which we invented earlier, to control the fi-
nite volume effects in the region where the photon travels a long Euclidean
space-time distance, in which case the hadronic matrix elements should not
be directly read from lattice calculation. With this framework and reusing
the point source propagators generated for our HLbL calculation, we have
performed the lattice calculations for all the necessary matrix elements. The
results of the radiative decay is obtained. [3]
 In 2023, we calculated the electroweak box contributions to superallowed
nuclear and neutron beta decays to be [4]

                        □VγWA = 3.65(7)lat (1)PT × 10−3 .                  (1)

Superallowed nuclear beta decays are currently the most precise approach
to determine |Vud |. The electroweak box contribution is one of the main
systematic errors in determining the QED correction to these beta decays.
Our lattice calculation provide a cross-check to the previous determinations
based on dispersive analysis and slightly reduces the uncertainty.

2 QED correction to meson masses
 In 2021, we calculated the mass different between π + and π 0 to be

                     Mπ± − Mπ0 = 4.534(42)(43) MeV,                        (2)

which is mainly due to the QED correction. [5] This is the first lattice cal-
culation of this mass difference including the quark disconnected diagram.
We also used the infinite volume QED with infinite volume reconstruction
(IVR) method to control the finite volume error. The precision of the this
calculation is about 5 times smaller than previous lattice calculations.

3 Muon g − 2 HVP
 In 2023, we updated [6] iso-QCD value of the light quark connected dia-
gram for the short-distance part

 aHVP,SD,lqc
                      µ          = 48.7(0.5)(1.6) × 10−10 ,                (3)

and the window part

 aHVP,W,lqc
                      µ         = 206.36(44)(42) × 10−10 .                 (4)

The result is in good agreement with results from other lattice collaborations,
but is significantly higher than the previous dispersive results based on the
e+ e− → hadrons cross section experimental measurements (except the recent
CMD-3 experiment, which agrees with lattice calculations). This result, to-
gether with results from other collaborations, is one of the main reasons for
the new white paper [7] adopting lattice result average for its final value of
the HVP contribution.
 In 2024, we obtained updated [8] iso-QCD value of the light quark con-
nected diagram for the long-distance part

 aHVP,LD,lqc
                     µ          = 411.4(4.3)(2.4) × 10−10 ,                (5)

combined with short-distance and window contribution, the total iso-QCD
light quark connected diagram contribution is

 aHVP,lqc
                       µ       = 666.2(4.3)(2.5) × 10−10 .                 (6)

These results entered the recent muon g − 2 theory white paper [7] HVP
average.

4 Muon g − 2 HLbL
 In 2023, we calculated new result for the hadronic light-by-light (HLbL)
contribution. [9] It is the first time that HLbL be calculated using lattice
QCD with physical pion mass and infinite volume QED formulation. The
result is

 aHLbL
                       µ    = 12.47(1.15)(0.95) × 10−10 ,                  (7)

 3

with total uncertainty to be 12%, which is much better than our previous
results (total uncertainty reduced by a factor of three). The result shows no
significant discrepancy with previous lattice QCD calculations and dispersive
results, and enters the recent muon g−2 theory white paper [7] HLbL average.
At present, we have almost finished a new calculation of HLbL with a smaller
lattice spacing, which can help reducing the discretization error, which is main
systematic error in our previous result.
 In 2024, we calculated the pion-pole contribution to HLbL based on the
  0
π transition form factor. [10] This calculation is complementary to our direct
lattice calculation. In the calculation, with a lot of internal checks, we have
found in our new result, the contribution of the disconnected diagram has a
opposite sign to all previous lattice calculations (also include previous calcu-
lations from our collaborations). We are communicating with other lattice
collaborations to about this new findings and trying to reach consensus on
the sign.
