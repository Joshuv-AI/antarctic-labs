 Morgana Overview – NCERC Support for Dynamic Subcritical Experiments

The LANL Subcritical Experiments Program (SCE) recently executed the Morgana Subcritical
Experiment at the NNSS. This was the ﬁrst subcritical experiment executed by LANL for several
years (since 2021). It was also notably the ﬁrst that required SCE personnel to have FMH
qualiﬁcations for a portion of the work conducted under nuclear criticality safety limits.

The LANL SCE team approached NEN-2 around 2022 for assistance with developing and
standing up their Fissionable Material Handling program to ensure nuclear criticality safety in
their operations. After some discussion, they requested that the device assembly team (V-5) be
added to the NCERC Fissionable Material Handler (FMH) program. Over the next few years, the
V-5 team traveled to Nevada to participate in NCERC operations and were mentored toward
their FMH qualiﬁcation.

In the March-April 2025 timeframe, with the Morgana SCE ﬁrmly on the schedule, eﬀorts to
prepare for the FMH operations intensiﬁed. The NEN-2 NCERC Team developed FMH training
speciﬁcally for SCE-related duties. A focused week of training was provided to the V-5 team in
June 2025, in which NCERC FMH Subject Matter Experts (SME) instructed, evaluated, and tested
5 candidates. All passed and were qualiﬁed under the NCERC FMH program.

Without enough time to develop a Fissionable Material Person-In-Charge (FMPIC), the NEN-2
NCERC Team agreed to loan an experienced FM PIC to the Morgana project. This allowed them
to comply with the NCERC FMH program (which requires an FM PIC) and to have someone who
routinely performs operations in the DAF to assist with the operations.

 Morgana Receipt Team in NCERC highbay

In late October/early November 2025, the Morgana special nuclear material (SNM) arrived at
the DAF. NCERC staﬀ received the shipment and staged the item in the NCERC CSI vault until the
SCE project was ready to take the item. Attached photo shows the team involved in the receipt.

In early January, the SCE project was ready to begin the assembly process. NCERC and SCE
personnel moved the item to the SCE building. Attached photo shows the item after being
moved to a SCE building. From this point forward, the NCERC FM PIC assisted the various teams
in conducting operations on the east side of the DAF. This included unpacking the SNM,
downdraft table operations, radiography, metrology and diagnostics installation, high explosives
(HE) assembly, packing the assembly for transportation, and loading the packaged assembly.
DAF operations for Morgana concluded in April 2026.

The Subcritical Experiments Program has repeatedly expressed their appreciation of the NCERC
support in not only supporting the ﬁssionable material operations but also in navigating DAF
operations eﬃciently. They appreciate that the Nuclear Criticality Safety Program (NCSP)
maintains the NCERC capability. Eﬀorts continue to integrate SCE and NCERC operations for best
eﬃciency.
