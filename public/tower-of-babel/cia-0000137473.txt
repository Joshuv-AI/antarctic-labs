B. Influence Venezuelan political situation: Visit definitely
strengthened position of constitutional government headed by
Betancourt as well as his Accion Democratica (AD) political party.

It was serious defeat for Communist extremists and URD. Trip was
very helpful to AD party in interior of country which is party
stronghold and which was responsible for Betancourt's election as
President. Visit was also an endorsement of government alliance

with the United States and raised Venezuela to new high level as
influential leader Inter-American system. There is general agreement
that responsibility of Venezuelan government is now to expand on
success of visit and to give greater impetus to alliance for progress
program.

D. Suggestions for other Latin American visits: Venezuelan
visit showed importance of keeping down size Presidential parties :
thereby making it easier for host government and our diplomatic
missions to take care of them most efficiently. Timing of 24 to 36
hours is good and should not go beyond that. As condition to visit by
President to Latin American countries, as so well illustrated in case
of Venezuela, the trip should not be made unless there is very sincere
invitation and genuine interest shown on part of host government, and
the initiative for invitation should come from host government rather
than any suggestion by United States Government.

E. Other comments: The most important result of visit will
be measured in terms of respective governments in implementing
alliance for progress program in Venezuela which by reason of

President's visit is now showcase for this program in Latin America.
