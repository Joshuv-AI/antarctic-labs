Polish Talmudic scholar; lived at the end of the eighteenth century. Together with his two sons, YeḦiel Michel ha-Levi of Glogau and Moses ha-Levi of Glogau, he wrote homilies on the Pentateuch, published in 1820 under the title "Ḥuṭ ha-Meshullash" (The Threefold Cord).

Bibliography: Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 61; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 171. H. R. I. Br.
