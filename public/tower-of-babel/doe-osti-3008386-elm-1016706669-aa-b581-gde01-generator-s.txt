The generator’s available spare capacity must account for dynamically changing loads that can
increase the total power demand at any time. If the two online VFD’s are operated at full speed the
actual standby mode load is estimated to increase by 38kW, which would bring the total conﬁgured
load during standby power mode to 119kW, or 79%, still within 581GDE01’s acceptable capacity.

Per the LLNL Site 200 Generator Consolidation Final Study 2022, “Standby Emergency Generator
nameplate ratings are based upon operation with varying load averaging 70% of the nameplate for
200 hours per year. Continuous loading between 70% and 100% will reduce a generator’s expected
lifetime before a major overhaul. This is never a problem with Laboratory machines because of
conservative application of generators and the reliability of the normal power system combines to
keeps the load and hours down”. To achieve optimal performance and prolong generator life, the
recommended generator loading is between 40% and 70%, optimally at 70%, which 581GDE01
appropriately falls within.

Observed Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) Performance:

NIF UPS electronics equipment have experienced mis-operation issues when connected to
581GDE01 which is attributed to excessive input power voltage distortion (or harmonic voltages)
caused by harmonic currents produced by the nonlinear loads. The harmonic levels are believed to
have increased since the original system installation due to the introduction of more nonlinear
loads. For instance, during 581GDE01 testing on 12/12/24, SIS UPS equipment experienced
harmonics at levels that forced the equipment to rely on battery power. This causes a fault
condition where within tens of minutes, the SIS UPS's will shut down due to low battery power,
consequently shutting down SIS.

Harmonics become problematic only when they reach levels that start to adversely a ect power
distribution equipment or connected loads. IEEE Std. 519 2014, Recommended Practice and
Requirements for Harmonic Control in Electrical Power Systems, recommends a Total Harmonic
Distortion (THD) limit of 8.0% for <= 1kV electrical power systems.

Industry generator system design guidance recommends limiting nonlinear loads to no more than
25% of the generator alternator capacity to maintain harmonic distortion at an acceptable level.
The large motor VFDs alone account for 33%, not counting the other nonlinear loads such as the
UPS electronics equipment and LED lighting.

A Fluke 1750 power quality meter was installed to measure and analyze the electrical system
harmonic distortion when connected to the normal utility power, 581GDE01, and a LLNL temporary
230kW portable generator #02EGU46. The table below summarizes the measurements taken on
12/12/24:

 Energy Source THD Load Conﬁguration
 Normal Utility Power 3.8% Standby Power Mode
 Temporary 230kW generator 5.9% Standby Power Mode
 581GDE01 9.6% Standby Power Mode
 581GDE01 14.1% Standby Power Mode plus ACU39

The signiﬁcant di erence in THD levels between the normal utility power source and the generator
is related to the impedance of the energy source. The higher THD level experienced with GDE01 is
an e ect of its higher alternator impedance. The 230kW temporary generator’s relative lower
alternator impedance resulted in a THD nearer the utility THD, that did not cause mis-operation of
load equipment during testing on 12/12/24, consistent with the industry recommended limit.
