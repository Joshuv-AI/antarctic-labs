 Permitting/Licensing Considerations
BESS permitting and requirements differ, depending on the country and/or the jurisdiction in which BESS
will operate.
Nevertheless, the following broad categories are often included:
• Safety standards: National and/or local safety requirements may be enforced (e.g., fire safety standards
  and/or prevention).
• Siting of the project: General building/construction permitting requirements and codes may apply.
• Regulatory considerations: Permits and requirements may include environmental precautions (e.g., a
  BESS decommissioning plan, post-life recycling plan, and water runoff permitting).
• Interconnection requirements: Connecting the BESS to the grid may be at the utility or the grid
  operator level. This may be considered part of the grid code or a part of licensing.
 • May need specificity of BESS role possibilities in the grid/utility (e.g., transmission asset in addition
 to generator and consumer).

 Installation and Operation Path for BESS
Generally, BESS have a three-step process for installation and commercial operation of systems.
This includes:
  1. Application processes for BESS
 – This can include siting and environmental permitting as well as interconnection with the
 grid operator.
  2. Approval processes and authority/approval based on jurisdiction for BESS
 – Project purpose(s) may need to be defined—e.g., generator, consumer, transmission
 asset.
 – These vary based on the site jurisdiction and can include approval of specific applications
 and confirmation of longevity planning.
  3. Inspection processes for BESS
 – Safety and environmental inspections are typically required.

 Recycling and Reuse = Energy Efficiency
 Recycling and reusing batteries:
 • Alleviate possible material supply shortages, supply
 chain issues, and price spikes
 • Compensate for costs associated with end-of-life
 decommissioning
 • Reduce cost of future batteries using recovered
 batteries and materials
 • Reduce energy consumption and resource impacts
 versus making new batteries or extracting virgin
 materials
 • Show corporate responsibility and avoid negative
 perception of disposal as waste.
 In addition:
 • Recycling batteries recovers valuable materials.
