PIP3 antagonist as a molecular regulator in MSC-derived cardiomyocytes: Potential in vitro therapeutic implications for conotruncal heart defects
Authors: Alaaeldin Fayez, Nora N. Esmaiel, Haiam Abdel Raouf, Mohamad M. Aboelenin, Riham M. Aly, Ghada Nour Eldeen
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Conotruncal heart defects (CTDs) account for approximately one-third of all congenital heart defects. Elevated levels of phosphatidylinositol (3,4,5)-trisphosphate (PIP3) may contribute to CTD pathogenesis. PIP3 plays a pivotal role in mechanotransduction-based biological processes and remodeling of cardiac cytoskeletal proteins. Here, we aimed to evaluate the efficacy of the 322PESB derivative compound as a molecular regulator that antagonizes PIP3 binding pleckstrin homology (PH) domain of the Akt protein using mesenchymal stem cell-derived cardiomyocyte. Human adipose-derived MSCs (Ad-MSCs) were isolated. Immunophenotypic features of the hAd-MSCs were characterized according to minimal criteria of the international society for cellular therapy (ISCT) including immunophenotyping and trilineage differentiation potential. Subsequently, the differentiated hAd-MSCs were cultured in cardiomyogenesis-inducing medium. Successfully differentiated cardiomyocytes were assessed by measuring the expression levels of cardiomyocyte-specific genes using RT-qPCR. PIP3-primed cardiomyocytes were treated with 10 and 30 μmol/L of a 322PESB derivative molecule. The results showed a typical MSCs with high expression levels of CD73 (77.55%), CD90 (87.59%) and CD105 (91.88%) and that was accompanied by low expression levels of CD34 (0.59%) and CD45 (1.78%). After 21 days of MSC culture, cardiomyocyte-like cells with prominent striations were observed. Subsequent confirmation by RT-qPCR quantification of ADRB1 and MLC2a expression levels showed an average increase of 2.9-fold and 2.1-fold, respectively, in induced cardiomyocytes. Compared with the untreated control, PIP3 ELISA assay showed a significant increase in PIP3 levels in PIP3(10 nmol/L)-primed cardiomyocytes treated with 10 and 30 μmol/L of the 322PESB molecule derivative by 485.804 and 3564.164 ng/mL, respectively. In this study, we conducted the first promising molecular regulator with potential therapeutic implications for CTD patients. Further functional animal model and clinical phase studies are recommended.

Introduction

Despite advancements in medical and surgical interventions, but congenital heart disease (CHD) remains a significant lifelong burden. Recently, signaling pathways have become promising therapeutic targets, where many studies have found that nearly half of CHD variants are allocated to transcriptional regulators involved in Wnt/Notch and RAS/MAPK signaling pathways.1 These pathways are enriched by transcriptional regulators genes to regulate the developmental processes, homeostasis of cell-cell communication, cell differentiation, cell migration & survival and regeneration of tissue.2, 3, 4

The MAPK pathway interacts with Ras-Raf and PI3K-Akt signal pathways to add complexity in intracellular signaling networks,5 where the PI3K-Akt signaling pathway participates in the pathogenesis of heart diseases through regulation of the inflammatory responses and alteration of the cardiomyocyte phenotype.6

The PI3K/AKT pathway stimulates many downstream signal transductions and biological processes that maintain cardiomyocyte homeostasis, and hence, cellular transduction anomalies of mechanical forces, called mechanotransduction, in cardiomyocytes can drive aberrant cellular phenotypes.7 Dysregulation of this pathway is associated with heart failure (HF), cardiomyocyte apoptosis, myocardial infarction (MI), cardiac fibrosis (CF), and conotruncal heart diseases (CTD).5, 8, 9, 10, 11

Phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate (PIP3), produced by PI3K, orchestrates multiple downstream intracellular signaling events regulating cell growth, proliferation, and. Survival.12, 13 A newly formed PIP3 binds with target cytosolic PH containing proteins, like Akt protein, in which recruit these proteins in plasma membrane.6

Although diversity of phosphoinositide, like PIP2 and PIP3, in dominant regulation of cardiomyocyte functions by mechanotransduction, but both the molecular mechanistic and pathogenicity of phosphoinositides remain unclear.7 Our previous works showed that elevated plasma PIP3 levels were predicted and detected significantly in conotruncal heart patients carrying rs2517582 variant.9, 10 Therefore, this pathway considers promising targeted using drug development.11

Although majority of drugs used today were selected on toxicity-based animal model systems, but the recent reports pointed that in vivo animal models are insufficient analogs to human's conditions.14 Well-designed cell culture is most cost and time effective. Contrary to animal studies, a low drug and a short response time are counted as advantages for in vitro cell culture methods.15

Because that culture human cardiomyocytes (hCM) is limited due to the extreme difficulty in propagating these cells in vitro, generate induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs)-derived somatic cells is the majority.16

Despite Human cardiomyocyte cell lines from induced pluripotential stem cells (hiPSCs-CMs) have been employed in studies as a superb human-compatible model for drug screening and toxicity and for the development of novel drugs for heart diseases,11, 17 other cell types are being considered as well for early-stage studies. Human adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cells (hAd-MSCs) are multipotent mesenchymal progenitors that under suitable induction conditions can be differentiated toward the cardiomyogenic lineage and can be used as an in vitro model for initial drug screening as well as for mechanistic investigations.18

Here, we used derivative for our drug-like small molecule called 3-[2-(2-phosphonocyclohexyl)ethylsulfanyl]benzoic acid (322PESB), it was published previously,9 to screen its efficiency and toxicity using MSC-derived cardiomyocytes model.

This study aimed to investigate the role of 322PESB derivative as a molecular regulator in inhibiting the binding of PIP3 molecules to the PH domain of the Akt protein. This was achieved by applying a 322PESB molecule derivative to in vitro cardiomyocyte-like cells growth from human mesenchymal stem cells.

Previous reports suggested that cardiomyocytes derived from human stem cells exhibit the phenotype of cardiomyocytes at embryonic/fetal stage rather than mature adult cardiomyocytes. This makes stem cell–derived models applicable for the study of early cardiac development and congenital heart disease.19

In this study, we conducted assessment of a 322PESB molecule derivative on the human mesenchymal stem cell (MSC)-derived in vitro model of cardiomyocyte-like cell, where adipose tissue–derived mesenchymal stem cells (Ad-MSCs) were isolated, and then, the 322PESB molecule derivative was investigated for its effect on the interaction between phosphatidylinositol (3,4,5)-trisphosphate (PIP3) and Akt,

Since conotruncal heart defects arise during embryonic cardiac development, stem cell–derived cardiomyocyte-like model offers a more pertinent platform for studying developmental signaling pathways than adult cardiomyocyte cell lines. In line with this, Ad-MSCs offer a convenient and readily available source of human stem cells, which exhibit high proliferative potential and are capable of cardiomyogenic differentiation upon suitable induction, making it feasible to study intracellular signaling pathways related to cardiac development and diseases.18, 20

Methods

According to Fayez et al. (2023),9 the pharmacological and physicochemical properties of the PIP3 binding pocket on the Akt protein were investigated. Subsequently, An Akt-pharmacophore feature model was used to design and optimize the specificity of the 322PESB compound. 322PESB was selected from an internal database of 21 small molecules based on its superiority in binding affinity (ΔG), dissociation constant (Kd), inhibition constant (Ki), and ADME and Lipinski bases.

We isolated mesenchymal stem cells from normal adipose tissue, then we stimulated these cells to become cardiac muscle cells as a model for detecting and evaluating the effectiveness of the 322PESB-derived compound used. A 322PESB-derived compound was added at different concentrations (umol/L) as treated cardiomyocyte (CM) cells against untreated CM. The cardiomyocyte genes markers were detected using RT-PCR to identify the true differentiated CM. the functional CM phenotype was observed by using microscopical inspection. Antagonistic binding of a 322PESB-derived compound was assessed by quantification of intracellular free (unbound) PIP3. In details, the methods used were;

Isolation and propagation of human MSCs from adipose tissue

Ad-MSCs were extracted from recently removed human subcutaneous adipose tissue (donors aged 27–43 years) using the technique outlined by Cláudia et al. (2012).21 To put it briefly, excised fat was chopped and thoroughly cleaned using equivalent amounts of phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific). They were then treated in 0.1% type I collagenase (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific) at 37 °C for 2 h. To eliminate adipocytes, cells were spun at 1200 rpm for 10 min after enzyme activity was neutralized with Dulbecco's modified Eagle's medium-low glucose (DMEM-lg, Gibco,Thermo Fisher Scientific) containing 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS) (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific). In order to remove tissue debris, the cells were centrifuged for five minutes at 1200 rpm, filtered through 100 μm nylon mesh (Corning), and then cultivated on a culture plate for the entire night at 37 °C in a humidified atmosphere with 5% CO2 in the control medium (DMEM-lg, 10% FBS, 1% antibiotic/antimyocotic solution). To get rid of any remaining non-adherent cells, the plates were thoroughly cleaned 72 h later with PBS. When 80% cell confluence will be reached, the cells were sub-cultured up until passage 4, at a split ratio of 1:2 after achieving 80% confluency.22 Cellular morphology was assessed using an inverted microscope (Olympus CKX31, Tokyo, Japan).

Characterization of isolated hAd-MSCs

Immunophenotypically, to determine the typical MSC surface markers, MSCs were subjected to a flow cytometry study at passage 4. The following antibodies were applied as positive MSC surface markers: CD90 (0.06 μg/test, PE, eBioscience™, cat. no. 12–0900-81), CD73 (0.125 μg/test, PE, eBioscience™, cat. no. 12–0739-41) and CD105 (0.06 μg/test, PE, eBioscience™, cat. no. 12–1057-41). Also, CD34 (0.5 μg/1 × 106 cells, PE, cat. no. MA5–17379) and CD45 (0.125 μg/test, FITC, eBioscience™, cat. no. 12–0459-41) were the hematopoietic cell marker antibodies. Cells were adjusted to a concentration of 1 × 106 cells/mL and rinsed three times with PBS before to the flow cytometry analysis. Following that, cell suspensions were treated with the relevant antibodies at the manufacturer-recommended doses for 20 min at 4 °C in the dark. To get rid of any unbound antibodies, cells were rinsed with PBS after incubation. A Beckman Coulter flow cytometer (Brea, CA, USA) was used to perform a flow cytometry analysis in order to identify the cell surface antigens and ascertain the percentages of their expression. CytExpert Software (Beckman Coulter, Brea, CA, USA) version 2.3 was used to analyze the data.23

For Tri-Lineage Differentiation potential, Ad-MSCs at the passage 4 underwent differentiation using specific corresponding induction StemPro® Differentiation Kit (Gibco, Grand Island, NY) to evaluate their adipogenic, chondrogenic, and osteogenic potential. Undifferentiated Ad-MSCs cultured in a standard medium were used as the negative controls. Prior to the induction protocols, 3 × 103 /cm2 viable cells were seeded in 12-well plate in standard growth medium (DMEM+10% FBS). When 70% confluency reached, the medium was exchanged with corresponding differentiation medium for 14 days with medium replacement twice weekly. The differentiated cells were stained with oil red to find lipid droplets, Alcian blue (1%, SigmaAldrich, St. Louis, USA) for two hours to evaluate proteoglycan production, and Alzarin for mineralized bone matrix formation.24

Differentiation of hAd-MSCs to cardiomyocyte-like cells

Six-well plates were seeded with 1 × 104 third-passage cells per well. Two groups of cells were distinguished and cultured in various media. Basal growth medium was used to cultivate the control group, while cardiogenic induced medium (DMEM high glucose, 10% FBS, gentamycin, Pen Strep +10 μmol/l 5 aza; Sigma Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, USA) was used to cultivate the cardiogenic induced group. The cardiogenic induced medium was switched to basal growth medium after 24 h, and for the 21 days that the cells were cultured, the culture medium was changed every three days.25 hAd-MSC differentiation into cells resembling cardiomyocytes was assessed by the degree of expression cardiomyogenic specific genes (GATA4, cTnT, Nkx2.5, and Cx43) in relation to GAPDH which was utilized as the internal control gene for the normalization of the relative gene expression level using RT-qPCR and the 2 -ΔΔct formula.

Cytotoxicity assay of 322PESB molecule derivative

The Cell Counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) (Dojin Molecular Technologies, Inc., Japan) was used, according to the manufacturer's guidelines, to screen drug cytotoxicity effect on cell viability.26 To put it briefly, cells were planted (100 μl/well) in a 96-well plate after being suspended in complete medium at a density of 5 × 104 /ml. the 322PESB molecule derivative was administered at final concentrations of 10, 20, 30 or 40 μmol/L. The water-soluble tetrazolium salt (WST-8) reagent was added to each well after the cells had been grown for 24 h. The cells were then incubated for two hours in a CO2 incubator. A microplate reader was used to measure the optical density of the cells, which indicates the percentage of living cells in a sample, at a wavelength of 450 nm.

Preparation of cardiomyocyte- like cell treated with a 322PESB molecule derivative

Cardiomyocyte- like cells were divided into seven groups, including the control group of non- primed cardiomyocyte- like cells (Group1), PIP3-primed cardiomyocytes- like cells at concentrations 5 and 10 μmol/L (Groups 2 & 3). PIP3-primed cardiomyocytes- like cells at concentrations 5 μmol/L were treated with 2 different concentrations of the 322PESB molecule derivative 10 and 30 μmol/L (Groups 4 & 5), while PIP3-primed cardiomyocytes- like cells at concentrations 10 μmol/L were also treated with the 2 different concentrations of the 322PESB molecule derivative 10 and 30 μmol/L (Groups 6 & 7). Drug treatment was applied 24 h post priming and left for another 24 h before starting assessment.

Analysis of cardiac lineage specific markers at transcriptional level by relative quantitative PCR (qPCR) and ELISA for free PIP3

Total RNA was isolated with TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen, USA) from mesenchymal stem cells-derived cardiomyocytes control and treatment groups, according to manufacturer's instructions. cDNA was concocted using 5 μg of RNA by reverse transcriptase enzyme. The relative qPCR experiments were performed with SYBR Green I master Mix according to the manufacturer's instructions. Relative gene expression was evaluated by 2^−∆∆Ct formula. RT-PCR Reactions were carried out in a final volume of 10 μl with 0.3 μM of each primer of GAPDH (housekeeping gene) forward 5-′GAGTCAACGGATTTGGTCGT-3′, and reverse 5′-GACAAGCTTCCCGTTCTCAG-3′. Mlc-2v (marker for nodal cardiomyocyte) forward 5′-GGTGCTGAAGGCTGATTAGGTT-3′, and reverse 5′-TATTGGAACATGGCCTCTGGAT-3′. Mlc-2a (marker for ventricular-like cardiomyocytes) forward 5’-CAGACCTGAAGGAGACCTATTCC-3′, and reverse 5’-CTACCTCAGCAGGAGAGAACTTG-3′. ADRB1 (marker for mature cardiomyocyte) forward 5′-GACGCTCACCAACCTCTTCA-3′, and reverse 5′-ACTTGGGGTCGTTGTAGCAG-3′. All quantitative RT-PCR (qRT-PCR) was performed in triplicate.

Discussion

The inositol ring is a cyclohexane containing hydroxyl groups. Therefore, the inositol moiety is a water-soluble head group in phosphatidylinositol (PtdIns; phosphatidylinositides). The hydroxyl groups on the inositol ring are phosphorylated by phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) at positions 3, 4, and 5, yielding phosphatidylinositol(3,4,5)-triphosphate (PIP3) molecules.28, 29, 30

Several studies declared that Phosphoinositides are critical signaling molecules in diverse biological processes, where it occupies in critical cellular coordinator region within the interface between the cell membrane and cytoskeleton.7, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38 Therefore, PIP3 is participating in many serious cellular machines e.g. membrane biogenesis, cell wall formation, cell migration & differentiation, and signal transduction.7, 28

Serine/threonine protein kinase (Akt) is one of recruited proteins in a plasma membrane by PIP3, where Akt binds to PIP3 through the N-terminus of the pleckstrin homology (PH) domain.39 Specifically, PIP3 is a binding site PH of Akt1 and phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1 (PDK1). Then, Co-localization of recruited plasma membrane Akt1 with PDK1 by PIP3 mediates partial activation of Akt1 by PDK1 through phosphorylation of Thr308 in Akt1. Full activation of Akt1 is achieved by rapamycin complex 2 (mTORC2) through phosphorylation of Ser473 in Akt1 permitting. Therefore, PIP3 binding leads to a change in Akt conformation, allowing it to be phosphorylated by PDK1 and mTORC2.39, 40, 41

In the current study, to experimentally confirm the hypothesis that sustained Akt phosphorylation by excess amounts of PIP3 is associated with congenital heart defects, we used a 322PESB molecule derivative that has been shown to crowd out PIP3 binding to the Akt protein, thus confirming its function as a molecular regulator shield for this PH domain on Akt. This shield allows for the inhibition of persistent Akt activation.

CTDs are a group heart structure anomalies arising from faulty development of the heart's outflow tract, affecting how blood leaves the heart. Therefore, CTDs are heart developmental disease. The heart development is example of a process greatly influenced by mechanical forces (mechanotransduction), where dysregulation of a cellular transduction in heart development is causing cardiovascular disease due to aberrant cellular phenotypes.7, 42 Consequently, the phosphoinositides are crucial signal transduction force molecules inside PI3K/Akt signaling pathway in cardiovascular diseases and cardiomyocyte mechanotransduction.43

In line with the elevated PIP3 level may cause an increase in mechanotransduction load during cardiac development, we intended to use cardiomyocyte-like cells as an in vitro model to study the effect of the 322PESB derivative on modulating and reducing this mechanotransduction load by reducing the excess binding of PIP3 to the Akt protein.

PIP3 has critical role in cytoskeletal rearrangements and actin dynamic, so it is necessary for deserve of the integrity of a living cell.7, 44 Musfee et al. (2021) concluded that evaluation of multiple affected genes in 1413 patients with conotruncal heart defects and 509 patients with left ventricular outflow tract defects showed that cytoskeletal genes are significantly associated with CTDs.45 PIP3 mediates cytoskeletal remodeling through physical interaction of PIP3 with membrane-bound channels in cardiovascular cells.7

Results of Musfee et al. (2021)45 was confirmed by previous our results (Fayez et al. 2018; 2023),9, 10 where we indicated that elevated of PIP3 in Vars2-Pic3ca-Akt pathway leads to conotruncal heart defects, where we suggested a disease model in which elevated PIP3 led to sustained Akt activation increasing negative effect on down target proteins contributed in heart development such as RAF, MEK, ERK, and MAPK.9, 10 Perino et al. (2011) concluded that accumulation of PIP3 leads to prolonged or sustained Akt activation, and hence sustainable subsequent downstream signal relay.39

Upon these results, we designed drug-like small molecule, called 322PESB, with good pharmaceutical and antagonism properties.9 Here, we aimed to continue in explore activity of 322PESB derivative to abolish a binding site on PH domain of Akt protein. Our results indicated that the 322PESB molecule derivative is active antagonist for PIP3 binding, where in the cultured cardiomyocytes that treated by 30 μmol/L of it showed a high accumulation of free PIP3.

However, cardiomyocytes stimulated with 5 nmol/L of PIP3 showed almost no activity for 322PESB molecule derivative, as measured by quantification of unbound PIP3. This may be attributed to disequilibrium between the 322PESB molecule and the amount of Akt, where the amount of non-cellularly toxic 322PESB is insufficient to target all Akt targets. Consequently, the low concentration of 5 nmol/L PIP3 may target empty Akt binding sites, resulting in no change in the amount of unbound PIP3 during ELISA analysis of the treated cardiomyocytes.

On the other hand, the efficacy of 322PESB in displacing excess PIP3 at a concentration of 10 nmol/L without a low concentration at 5 nmol/L suggests that 322PESB is a potential targeted therapy that may not interfere with healthy cells with low PIP3 levels.

The current results are consisting with role PIP3 in remodeling of the heart cytoskeleton, where access of PIP3 has negative effect on MAPK1 (ERK2) protein through inhibition of RAF1. Cardiomyogenic targets of ERK including cytoskeletal and adherens junction proteins like integrins and motif-containing GTPase-activating protein 1 (IQGAP1).46, 47

Multiple studies concluded role of PI3K/Akt pathway in myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury, myocardial infarction (MI), and heart failure (HF), where activation or regulation of PI3K/Akt pathway can led to cardioprotective effects through enhancing anti-apoptotic, pro-survival signaling, and induction of activity of PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling.5, 48, 49, 50, 51

Another studies used drugs can modulate PI3K/Akt pathway to alleviate pathological effects of some cardiac diseases, where Tang et al. (2017) pointed that Urolithin A (UA) ameliorated myocardial injury by alleviation of hypoxia/reoxygenation injury via activation of PI3K/Akt pathway.52 Tong et al. (2018) and Zhang et al. (2019) showed that Celastrol and Nobiletin attenuates the elevated myocardial enzymes decreasing infarct size and number of apoptotic myocardium cells via upregulation of p-Akt and p-PI3K.53, 54

Li et al. (2018) referred that Tacrolimus is significantly alleviated the arrhythmias by increased phosphorylation of the PPARγ/PI3K/Akt pathway,55 and Li et al. (2019) found that Hydrogen-rich water increase PI3K, Akt alleviating ischemia-reperfusion injury and inhibiting cardiomyocyte apoptosis.56

Wang et al. (2017) found that Araloside C (AsC) ameliorates the cardiac functions and apoptotic rate in heart failure (HF) model via by inhibiting the overexpression of PI3K/Akt signaling pathway.57 Fayez et al. (2023) demonstrated that hyper-activation of PI3K/Akt signaling pathway leads to conotruncal heart diseases.9

Therefore, the key difference between 322PESB molecule and all the aforementioned drugs lies in the fact that these drugs act as a protective activator of the PI3K/Akt pathway in cardiovascular diseases occurring later in life, such as HF and MI. In contrast, 322PESB modulates the PI3K/Akt pathway as an inhibitor of persistent Akt activation in congenital structural heart defects.

From the above, it is clear that previous research is divided between findings that suggest a protective role for activated PI3K/Akt pathway in myocardial infarction patients and others that suggest a protective role for regulated PI3K/Akt pathway in heart failure and conotruncal heart defects. This can be explained by the fact that some heart diseases are closely related to the heart cytoskeleton proteins, like TTN (titin), LMNA (lamin), DES (desmin), ACTC (actin), and MYH7 (myosin), while others are weakly related to the heart cytoskeleton proteins. Cytoskeletal genes are significantly associated with CTDs.45

Using a derivative of 322PESB molecule, we concluded that this molecule shows its power to regulate of PI3K/Akt signaling pathway through its ability to abolish binding access of PIP3 on PH domain of Akt in cardiomyocytes. This molecule demonstrated tested cutoff efficacy for PIP3 displacement at a threshold of 10 nmol/L of PIP3, thus confirming that it may be clinically useful in disrupting PIP3 binding in CTD patients with significantly elevated PIP3.

Conclusion and recommendation

Modulation of PI3K/Akt signaling pathway has therapeutic protective role in CTD pathogenesis via decreased of PIP3 dosage in cardiomyocyte. Our results were strongly concluded regulation of cellular PIP3 using the 322PESB molecule derivative in cardiomyocyte-like cells. To expand upon these results, we recommend further functional studies, such as contractility and electrophysiology studies, as well as studies on animal models and clinical trials.

Also, we recommend widely exploring the role of PI3K/Akt pathway components to minimize effects of other cardiac diseases and screen for the most druggable protein in this pathway.

Based on the current study and our previous studies, the efficacy assessments of the 322PESB molecule indicate that it has potential therapeutic benefits for patients with CTD. Upon Fayez et al. (2023)9 study, we found that PIP3 levels were significantly higher in CTD patients carrying the rs2517582 mutant alleles. So, our treatment strategy after completion all the recommended further studies on 322PESB molecule, is to explore the possibility of applying this molecule to treat embryos carrying the rs2517582 variant who exhibit elevated PIP3 levels. This aligns with the etiology of CTDs, where the morphological outflow defects arise from the failure of specific populations of embryonic cells to migrate and properly form the septate the heart's outflow tract.
