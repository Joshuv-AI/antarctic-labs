Project Background

• Problem:
  • QC4.10 connectors are cracking within
 months after installation
  • Issue affects at least three sites across
 two developers
  • Root cause unknown

FTIR – Pristine vs. Aged QC4.10

 Pristine Aged and Cracked

• Aged and cracked connectors tend to have additional peaks near 3300 and
  1700, not seen in pristine connectors
• Peak at 3300 could be related to water uptake
• Peak at 1700 could be related to oxidation
• Baseline shift likely unrelated to chemical differences (penetration depth)

FTIR – Aged QC4.10 (Interior vs. Exterior)

Summary and Next Steps
Summary:
• Mechanical differences between aged and pristine QC4.10 samples indicates
  degradation
 • No loss of additives (plasticizers) were detected
• QC4.10s material is not polycarbonate like other connectors (MC4)
Next Steps:
• Oxidation
 • Oxidation increases brittleness
 • Oxidation induction time could indicate lack of anti-oxidants (ASTM D38-95)
• Check for water absorption
 • Oxidized polymers have polar groups and can hold more water
 • Humid environments cause swelling 15
