Russian rabbi; lived in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. He was the author of a work entitled "Mattenot 'Ani," or "Ḳiẓẓur Mizraḥi," a compendium of Elijah Mizraḥi's commentary on Rashi, with notes (Prague, 1604-09).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. ii. 143; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1130; Zunz, Z. G. p. 282. S. S. M. Sel.
