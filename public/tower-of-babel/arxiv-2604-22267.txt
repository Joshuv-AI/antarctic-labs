On a new modular equation of degree five and Eisenstein series identities of associated levels
Shruthi C. Bhat, B. R. Srivatsa Kumar

Abstract
. In this research article, we obtain few theta function identities of level ten employing Ramanujan’s ψ 1 1 {}_{1}\psi_{1} summation formula. Using these identities, we derive a new modular equation of degree five. Further, we establish Eisenstein series identities of level ten using Bailey’s very-well poised ψ 6 6 {}_{6}\psi_{6} summation formula.
