Arabo-Jewish poet of the eleventh century; great-grandfather of Samuel ibn Nazar and a contemporary of Moses ibn Ezra. A poem of his with the acrostic was edited from the fourth section of the Oran Maḥzor by Luzzatto in "Kerem Ḥemed," iv. 31-32; and an elegy on his death by Moses ibn Ezra was printed ib. p. 86.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 242. E. C. S. O.
