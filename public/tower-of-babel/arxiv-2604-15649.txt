Signless Laplacian index conditions for trebly chorded cycles in graphs with given order
Jin Cai, Bo Zhou

Abstract
It is proved that for a graph of order n n , where n ≥ 6 n\geq 6 , if the signless Laplacian index is larger than or equal to certain value depending on n n , then the graph contains a trebly chorded cycle, where the chords incident to a common vertex, unless it is one of two specified graphs. MSC: 05C50, 15A18 Keywords: cycle, trebly chorded cycle, signless Laplacian index

Introduction

Let

A chord of a cycle

A cycle

trebly chorded if there are three chords.
Answering Pósa’s
question
[
13
]
, Czipzer
[
4

proved
that any graph with minimum degree at least three contains a chorded cycle (see Lovász
[

Some researchers found conditions that imply the

[

3

,
18
,
7
]
. In the recent survey
[
8

,
Gould asked the question: What spectral conditions imply a graph contains a chorded cycle?
Answers are given for graphs with fixed order via index (spectral radius)
[
21
]
and signless Laplacian index (signless Laplacian spectral radius)
[
19
]
, respectively.
Wang and Zhai
[
20
]
found the signless Laplacian index conditions that imply the existence of
P_{1}\vee P_{2k}
, so they actually gave the signless Laplacian index conditions that imply the existence of cycles with even number of chords incident to a vertex.
As far as we know, there is no tight spectral condition that implies the existence of trebly chorded cycles.
A particular case of Jiang
[
10
]
states that for any
n
-vertex graph with

, if

, then

contains a trebly chorded cycle with three chords incident to a vertex.
Inspired by the above works,
we study the following question:

Question 1.1
.

What tight signless Laplacian index conditions imply a graph with fixed order contains a trebly chorded cycle (with chords incident to a vertex)?

For a graph
G
, we denote by

its signless Laplacian index, which is defined to be the largest eigenvalue of the signless Laplacian matrix of

.

Denote by

the complete graph on
n

the complete

Denote by

the graph obtained from

by adding one edge in the part with size

, where

.

For

does not contain a trebly chorded cycle with three chords incident to a vertex. The main result is listed as below.

Theorem 1.1
.

Suppose that
G
is a graph of order
n
without isolated vertices, where

.
If

and
