President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (31 August 1944, d42)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 31 August 1944.

Number 612, personal and top secret, from the President for the Prime Minister.

Your 773.[2] I am distressed at the news of your illness. Please guard your health first and above all other considerations.

We can postpone Octagon to some later date if it appears advisable to do so.

Where is landing spot? If New York you and I could go to Octagon together on my train.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Supra.
