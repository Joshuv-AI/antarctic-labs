Sec. 2. For the purpose of this Act, an hourly rate,
other than an hourly rate which is paid as a proportionate
part of an annual rate, shall be multiplied by two thousand
eighty to obtain an equivalent annual rate for the position;
and a daily rate, other than a daily rate which is paid as a
proportionate part of an annual rate, shall be multiplied
by two hundred sixty to obtain an equivalent annual rate
for the position.

Sec. 3. As used in this Act, the term “compensation
from any office or position” shall include —

(1) any basic salary, wages, or other emolument,
including permanent additions such as statutory pay
increases, but excluding any temporary additions such
as overtime pay or night differential, for or on account
of service as a civilian officer or employee of the United
States, or any department or agency thereof, including
Government-owned or controlled corporations, or of the
government of the District of Columbia; and
