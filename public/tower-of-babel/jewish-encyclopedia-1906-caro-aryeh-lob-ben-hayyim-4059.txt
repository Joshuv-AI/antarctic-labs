Preacher at Posen in the second half of the eighteenth century. He was the author of a work, "El ha-Millu'im" (Ram of Consecration), a homiletic commentary on the Pentateuch and the Song of Songs, published, with additions entitled "Abne Ẓedeḳ," by his grandson Abraham ben Isaac Zelig Caro, Krotoschin, 1845.

Bibliography: Perles, in Monatsschrift, xiv. 261; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 167. L. G. I. Br.
