Struggle for Control of Cuban Labor Movement Continues:
Communist sympathizers in the strong Cuban Labor Confedera-
tion (CTC) are apparently increasing their influence, al-
though Secretary General David Salvador is opposing some of
their moves to dominate the CTC executive committee. Sal-
vador, himself a former Communist whom Fidel Castro chose
to run the CTC, has made it an instrument of government
policy. Salvador is not opposed to working with the Com-
munists, but evidently fears that a rapid purge of anti-
Communist leaders would strengthen his rivals within the
executive committee, threaten his control of the CTC, and
end his usefulness to Castro.

The Communists have been well entrenched in the CTC
for years, but have not controlled it at the national level
since 1947. Their current strategy is one of "unity" with
Cuban revolutionary leaders, but union officials familiar

with their methods have resisted the government's acceptance
of Communist cooperation. Salvador is not a strong official
and may represent Fidel Castro's ambivalent attitude in this

struggle 1n which Raul Castro has actively supported the
unity approach.
