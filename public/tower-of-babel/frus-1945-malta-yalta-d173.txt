The Secretary of State to the President (1 December 1944, d173)

[Washingon,] December 1, 1944.

Special Information for the President

[Excerpts][1]

These international developments of the past two or three days will be of especial interest to you:

· · · · · · · · · · · · · ·

Tito-Subasic Agreement. Brigadier MacLean has told Ambassador Kirk that Mr. Churchill is expected to urge King Peter to accept the agreement recently formulated by Tito and the Yugoslav Premier and to send a British Ambassador to Belgrade as soon as the agreement becomes effective.

E. R. Stettinius, Jr.
