Talmudist; author of a work entitled "Sefer Bet ha-Melek," containing a commentary on Maimonides' Mishneh Torah; responsa on Oraḥ. Ḥayyim, Yoreh De'ah, and Ḥoshen Mishpaṭ, and novellæ on the Pentateuch (Salonica, 1804).

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 9; Jellinek, Ḳonṭres ha-Rambam, No. 26, Vienna, 1893. J. M. Sc.
