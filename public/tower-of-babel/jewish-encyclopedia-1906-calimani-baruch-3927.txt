Italian publisher; lived in the second half of the sixteenth and at the beginning of the seventeenth century at Venice. He issued the work of his teacher, Eliezer Ashkenazi, "Ma'ase Adonai" (Venice, 1583), and Moses Alshech's "Torat Mosheh" ( ib. 1601), adding rhetorical prefaces to both works.

Bibliography: Nepi-Ghirondi, Zeker Ẓaddiḳim, p. 54; Eliezer Ashkenazi, Ma'ase Adonai, ed. Cracow. L. G. I. Ber.
