 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 The Contractor will build a liquid hydrogen (LH2) fueling station model that is capable of
 simulating the amount of hydrogen transferred from the liquid storage tank to the vehicle,
 depending on the station’s design and control scheme. This modeling work will allow the Parties
 to seek the design and control scheme of an LH2 station that can fill a targeted number of
 vehicles while meeting a time-average flow rate of 10 kg/min for each fill. While evaluating a
 high dispensing performance station design and control scheme, the Contractor will analyze the
 design and control scheme are economically viable.

 Summary of Research Results:

 Task 1: System design

 The Contractor in collaboration with the Participant will develop a direct fill LH2 station system
 architecture design by leveraging the Contractor’s dynamic simulation (H2FillS) and
 technoeconomic analysis (TEA) modeling capabilities. The Participant and Contractor will
 develop at least three LH2 station system design concepts. One of the design concepts will be a
 conventional system while two additional design concepts should be direct fill systems. After the
 H2FillS and TEA modeling work conducted by the Contractor under Task 2, the direct fill
 system designs will be modified and refined to improve the performance and cost. This will be
 an iterative process. At the conclusion of this process, a final direct fill system architecture will
 be determined which also defines the performance requirements for each of the components.

 Task 1 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.

 Task 2: H2FillS and TEA modeling

 The Contractor will conduct a literature review on liquid hydrogen and then model an LH2 system
 consisting of a storage tank, vaporizer, and pump. The LH2 system model will have the capability
 to simulate the process where liquid hydrogen left from the storage tank is vaporized and then
 stored in a station buffer storage tank. The LH2 system model will be then integrated into the
 H2FillS model (LH2 H2FillS). The Contractor will leverage HDRSAM to conduct TEA for LH2
 stations. With the LH2 H2FillS and HDRSAM, the Contractor will simulate the entire operating
 process of a direct fill at LH2 stations along with estimates of capital, operational and maintenance
 costs. The Contractor will then provide the Participant with LH2 station system design concepts
 and access to H2FillS via its publicly available license. Any updates Contractor may make to the
 H2FillS model after the data comparison will be published to the publicly available repository.

 Task 3: Component design and build

 The Participant will design, manufacture, and assemble LH2 station subsystems and components.
 All construction will be completed at Participant’s facility.

 Task 3 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.

 Task 4: Design of component and system controls

 The Participant will develop and test all station subsystem control designs. This includes controls
 for the heating and thermal management system, the cryopump/direct Fill system, the boil-off
 capture and management system, the LH2 Offload, and the sub-cool LH2 fueling control.

 Task 4 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.

 Task 5: Evaluation of Sub-cool LH2 dispenser

 The Participant will test all sub-cool LH2 dispenser functionality to confirm valves actuate as
 expected, instrumentation is calibrated to the proper scale and mapped correctly, and dispenser is
 free of leaks.

 Task 5 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.

 Task 6: Site construction/installation/commissioning

 The Participant will permit, construct, and commission all station equipment for operation at its
 facility. Site construction will be conducted to prepare for equipment installation. The equipment
 will be delivered and installed. The station will go through a commissioning process which
 includes cleaning, pressure testing, I/O checkouts and equipment operation. In commissioning all
 equipment is operated together.

 Task 6 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.

 Task 7: Station operation

 The Participant will conduct fueling protocol validation tests and fueling performance tests to
 confirm that the station meets the fueling performance objectives. After this initial testing, the
 station will be operated commercially, and station and vehicle data will be collected and
 analyzed.

 Task 7 Results: No work was completed under this task because the project was canceled.
