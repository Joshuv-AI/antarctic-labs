PRESIDENT ALFREDO CRISTIANI KNOWS IT, HE KNOWS ABOUT THE EXISTENCE

CESAR VIELMAN JOYA MARTINEZ, A 27-YEAR-OLD SALVADORAN, WH
TWO MONTHS AGO BELONGED TO DEPARTMENT TWO (INTELLIGENCE) OF THE FIRST
INFANTRY BRIGADE (BASED IN THE SAN CARLOS GARRISON, IN SAN SALVADOR)
OF HIS COUNTRY'S ARMY, FROM WHICH HE DESERTED IN JULY LAST, IS
SPEAKING PUBLICLY FOR THE FIRST TIME, WITH 'LA JORNADA. '

THEY WERE GOING TO KILL ME, AS THEY DID WITH ALL THE OTHER AGENTS
AND INFORMANTS AT THE DEPARTMENT WHO CAME TO KNOW "TOO MUCH", THAT IS
WHY I DESERTED... AND ALSO BECAUSE, TRULY, I CANNOT STAND IT ANYMORE,
I FEEL ASHAMED, IT TURNS MY STOMACH.

EXILED IN THE U.S., IN WHOSE CAPITAL HE ARRIVED AFTER A HAZARDOUS
ESCAPE FROM HIS COUNTRY, AGENT 'ALEX' FROM THE ‘SPECIAL CORPS' ~- AS
WE CALLED HIM DURING THE CLANDESTINE OPERATIONS - TELLS THIS DAILY
ABOUT THE ACTIVITIES HE CARRIED OUT DURING HIS STAY IN THAT CORP:
SHADOWING (PERSECUTION), ABDUCTION, ASSAULT,.ARREST, IMPRISONMENT IN
SECRET JAILS BELONGING TO THE SALVADORAN ARMY, NATIONAL GUARD AND
POLICE, ASSASSINATION OF THOSE BEING SUSPECTED OF BELONGING TO OR
COLLABORATING WITH WORKERS', TEACHERS’ OR STUDENTS' ORGANISATIONS
AND, MAINLY, THE FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN) OF
EL SALVADOR, AND BURIAL OF THEIR CORPSES IN EQUALLY SECRET
CEMETERIES, OR THROWING THEM ON THE BEACH OF EL MAJAHUAL, AT THE PORT
"LA LIBERTAD', OR INTO THE ALCEHUATE RIVER.

DARK, SMALL, WITH AN ORIENTAL AIR IN THE SHAPE OF HIS FACE AND HIS
FEATURED, 'ALEX' RECOUNTS THE NUMBER OF ASSASSINATIONS IN WHICH HE
HAS PARTICIPATED OR WHICH HE HAS "SCRUPULOUSLY" COMMITTED BY HIMSELF
- BY CUTTING THE VICTIM'S THROAT WITH A 'DIATAGAN' COMMANDO KNIFE,
HANGING HIM OR INJECTING SULPHURIC ACID IN HIS VEINS - (A TOTAL OF
74) IS ALMOST THREE TIMES HIS AGE. HE SAYS HE NEVER USED FIRE ARMS
TO KILL THEM, TO AVOID THE ARMY BEING ACCUSED OF INVOLVEMENT IN THESE
CRIMES.

HIS TESTIMONY, WHICH HAS ALREADY BEEN RECORDED BY THE HUMAN
RIGHTS COMMISSION OF CENTRAL AMERICA (CODEHUCA) AND BELIZE, AS WELL
AS BY THE MEXICAN ACADEMY OF HUMAN RIGHTS, NOT ONLY PROVES THE
EXISTENCE OF DEATH SQUADRONS IN EL SALVADOR, BUT ALSO THE FACT THAT
THAT COUNTRY'S ARMY AND THE U.S. ADVISORS, PRESENT IN SALVADORAN
TERRITORY SINCE 1981, ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE RECRUITMENT,
ORGANISATION, TRAINING, MAINTENANCE AND MOBILISATION OF THESE GROUPS.

((TEXT)) DURING THE INTERVIEW WITH ‘LA JORNADA', HELD IN A MODEST
HOTEL ROOM IN WASHINGTON, THE YOUNG AGENT REVEALED THE EXACT LOCATION
OF THE CENTRAL AND PRIVATE OFFICES OF THE U.S. ADVISORS'S
HEADQUARTERS IN SAN SALVADOR: "THEY ARE BEHIND THE MILITARY SCHOOL",
HE SAYS. "NOT JUST ANYONE CAN GO THERE, ONLY THEY, THE U.S.
ADVISORS, THE 'CASE' OFFICERS, SUCH AS I, AND PEOPLE FROM THE GENERAL
STAFF, WHICH 1S WHERE THE CHEQUES CAME FROM TO PAY THE EXPENSES
INCURRED BY DEPARTMENT TWO."

"HOW AND WHY I GOT INVOLVED IN THIS? I DID IT UNDER THE PRESSURE
OF ECONOMIC PROBLEMS... IT ALL BEGAN IN 1982 WHEN I ENTERED THE
MARINE COMMANDO CORPS, BASED IN THE PORT OF 'LA UNION’. I WAS
IMMEDIATELY INCORPORATED INTO THE SPECIAL 'PIRANA' FORCES WHERE ]
BECAME THE CO-PILOT OF A SPEEDBOAT. MY WORK CONSISTED OF TEACHING
THE NEW MEMBERS TO HANDLE THE BOAT AND THE WEAPONS: A 50 AND A M-60",
HE SAYS. .

"THE 'PIRANA' GROUP DID VIGILANCE WORK IN EL ESPINO, THE GULF OF
FONSECA AND THE 'EL CUCO' BEACH. THE VIGILANCE CONSISTED OF
DETECTING CLANDESTINE BOATS COMING FROM NICARAGUA".

TWO YEARS LATER (IN 1984), HE ASKED IF HE COULD LEAVE AND WAS
ALLOWED TO DO SO. BACK IN CIVILIAN LIFE, HE WORKED IN A FISHING
FACTORY NAMED 'EMPRESA', WHERE HE WAS THE OPERATOR OF THE FLOUR
PLANT, A JOB WHICH HE STAYED ON FOR A YEAR. IN 1985 HE BECAME A
TRUCK DRIVER, UNTIL 1986. A YEAR LATER - JANUARY 1987 - HE ENTERED
THE SUPPORT AND COMBAT BATTALION OF THE FIRST INFANTRY BRIGADE.

“THERE I MADE CONTACT WITH UNDER-SARGENT BURGOS TORRES, WHOM I HAD
KNOWN A LITTLE EARLIER. HE TOLD ME THAT THE FIRST INFANTRY BRIGADE
HAD AN INTELLIGENCE DEPARTMENT AND THAT I MET THE ENTRY REQUIREMENTS.
THE REQUIREMENTS WERE THAT I WAS A COMMANDO AND HAD BELONGED 10 THE
SPECIAL 'PIRANA' GROUP FORCES. LATER ON I MET CORPORAL TORRES
ALFARO, WHO WAS A MEMBER OF INFANTRY DEPARTMENT TWO. THERE THEY PUT
ME IN CONTACT WITH A LIEUTENANT WHOSE LAST NAME IS MAJANO, AND WHO
WAS THE HEAD OF THE CASE OFFICERS' DEPARTMENT. I HAD AN INTERVIEW
WITH HIM AND I WAS ADMITTED THERE, WITH THE RANK OF A 'CASE' OFFICER.

WITH THAT RANK, I WAS TRANSFERRED TO THE ZONES OF APOPA, CHAPIN
AND PIEDRA TO RECRUIT CIVILIANS TO WORK AS "CONFIDENTIAL SOURCES" AND
THUS "HAVE UNDER CONTROL THE ZONES WHERE MEMBERS OF THE FMLN WOULD
MOVE”. LATER, 1 WAS SENT TO THE METROPOLITAN ZONE, TO THE CAPITAL,
SAN SALVADOR, TO HANDLE THE STAFF IN CHARGE OF CONTROLLING PEOPLE'S
DEMONSTRATIONS AND KEEPING SURVEILLANCE ON THE SAFE HOUSES OF THE
FMLN.

"ON THOSE OPERATIONS ONE COULD WORK NORMALLY, SINCE WE HAD TWO
U.S. ADVISORS AND A DEPARTMENT OF ANALYSIS. AT ANOTHER DEPARTMENT,
OF COUNTER-INTELLIGENCE, WE HAD SPECIAL VEHICLES WE COULD USE, A GREY
'HACI' WITH DARKENED WINDOWS AND A RED, FOUR-DOOR VAN WHOSE LICENCE
PLATES WERE CHANGED EVERY FIVE OR SIX DAYS TO AVOID THEIR BEING
REGISTERED. WE HAD SOME 40 LICENCE PLATES FOR PRIVATE VEHICLES,
WHICH WERE PERHAPS PROVIDED BY THE NATIONAL POLICE. THE VEHICLES
WERE GIVEN TO USE BY THE GENERAL STAFF FOR THE USE OF THE FIRST
INFANTRY BRIGADE'S DEPARTMENT TWO.

"THE VEHICLES WERE USED FOR SURVEILLANCE OR FOR CLANDESTINE
ARRESTS. IN THIS CASE, THE VEHICLE WAS MANNED WITH FIVE OR SIX
OFFICERS ARMED WITH M-16, 45 AND G-3, WHOSE BARRELS WERE CUT, AND
THIS IS HOW WE PROCEEDED TO CAPTURE PEOPLE UNDER SUSPICION".

"CLANDESTINE ABDUCTIONS IMPLIED DISLODGING THE PEOPLE FROM THEIR
HOMES AND TAKING THEM TO THE FIRST INFANTRY BRIGADE GARRISON, FROM
WHERE THEY WERE THEN SENT TO THE PRISONS OF THAT BRIGADE'S DEPARTMENT
TWO.
