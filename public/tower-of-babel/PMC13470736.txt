Retraction of “Exploring Microstructural, Interfacial, Mechanical, and Wear Properties of AlSi7Mg0.3 Composites with TiMOVWCr High-Entropy Alloy Powder”
Authors: Shashi Prakash Dwivedi, Shubham Sharma, Changhe Li, Yanbin Zhang, Rajesh Singh, Abhinav Kumar, Fuad A. Awwad, M. Ijaz Khan, Emad A. A. Ismail
Published: 2026

The Editor retracts “Exploring Microstructural, Interfacial, Mechanical, and Wear Properties of AlSi7Mg0.3 Composites with TiMOVWCr High-Entropy Alloy Powder” (DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.3c07837) due to a pattern of excessive and inappropriate citation manipulation and concerns relating to the accountability for the research across a group of related publications (DOIs: 10.1021/acsomega.3c08109 and 10.1021/acsomega.3c07837). These integrity concerns undermine the reliability of the work. As such, this article is being retracted.

The original article was published on April 17, 2024 and retracted on July 31, 2026.
