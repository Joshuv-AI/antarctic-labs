An approximate formula for the entropy of the negative binomial distribution
Sándor Lökös

Abstract
Recent theoretical developments revived the interest in charged particle multiplicities and their wide-spread parametrization, the negative binomial distribution (NBD). The central observable of the studies is the Shannon entropy of the NBD. A closed form is not known, however, there are representations with special series and integrals. In this note, we will investigate one of these and give an approximate formula for the entropy that is valid up to ∼ \sim 20% deviation from the exact value for extreme values of the NBD parameters.

II
An approximate formula for the entropy of NBD

Let’s consider the integral given in the second part of the formula in Eq. (
2
):

\displaystyle I(k,\langle n\rangle)=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-z)^{k-1}-1}{z\ln(1-z)}\left[\left(1+\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}z\right)^{-k}+\langle n\rangle z-1\right]dz,

(3)

with
k\geq 1
,
\langle n\rangle\geq 1
, that is expected in particle physics applications. The term that is proportional to
\langle n\rangle z
can be evaluated analytically by using the identity

\displaystyle\frac{(1-z)^{k-1}-1}{\ln(1-z)}=\int_{0}^{k-1}(1-z)^{t}\,dt,

(4)

so the integral becomes

\displaystyle I(k,\langle n\rangle)=\langle n\rangle\ln k+J(k,\langle n\rangle),

(5)

where

\displaystyle J(k,\langle n\rangle)=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1-z)^{k-1}-1}{z\ln(1-z)}\left[\left(1+\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}z\right)^{-k}-1\right]dz.

(6)

With this, we arrived to a new and a bit more simple formula than the one that is given in Ref.
12
, written as

\displaystyle S_{\rm NBD}=(\langle n\rangle{+}k)\ln(\langle n\rangle{+}k){-}\left(\langle n\rangle\ln\langle n\rangle{+}k\ln(k)\right){+}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1{-}z)^{k-1}{-}1}{z\ln(1{-}z)}\left[\left(1{+}z\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}\right)^{-k}{-}1\right],

(7)

or by rearranging the terms with logarithms

\displaystyle S_{\rm NBD}=-\langle n\rangle\ln\left(\frac{\langle n\rangle}{\langle n\rangle{+}k}\right){-}k\ln\left(\frac{k}{\langle n\rangle{+}k}\right){+}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{(1{-}z)^{k-1}{-}1}{z\ln(1{-}z)}\left[1{-}\left(1{+}z\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}\right)^{-k}\right].

(8)

Unfortunately, the integral, however, is finite, cannot be evaluated explicitly further. So let’s work out an approximation. A useful change of variables is

\displaystyle u=-\ln(1-z),\qquad z=1-e^{-u},\qquad dz=e^{-u}du.

(9)

Then

\displaystyle J(k,\langle n\rangle)=\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{1-e^{-(k-1)u}}{u(e^{u}-1)}\left[\left(1+\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}(1-e^{-u})\right)^{-k}-1\right]du.

(10)

Now, the analytic approximation is obtained by replacing the square bracket in this representation by a single saturating exponential,

\displaystyle\left(1+\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}(1-e^{-u})\right)^{-k}-1\approx-D\left(1-e^{-\lambda u}\right).

(11)

This gives the analytic, approximate contribution

\displaystyle J_{\rm approx}=-D\int_{0}^{\infty}\frac{(1-e^{-(k-1)u})(1-e^{-\lambda u})}{u(e^{u}-1)}\,du=-D\ln\left[\frac{\Gamma(k+\lambda)}{\Gamma(k)\Gamma(1+\lambda)}\right].

(12)

The corresponding approximation for the full integral is

\displaystyle I(k,\langle n\rangle)\approx\langle n\rangle\ln k-D\ln\left[\frac{\Gamma(k+\lambda)}{\Gamma(k)\Gamma(1+\lambda)}\right],

(13)

where

\displaystyle D=1-\left(1+\frac{\langle n\rangle}{k}\right)^{-k},\qquad\lambda=\frac{\langle n\rangle}{D}.

(14)

Finally, putting everything together, the approximate entropy for the NBD is given as

\displaystyle S_{\rm NBD}\approx(\langle n\rangle+k)\ln(\langle n\rangle+k)-\left(\langle n\rangle\ln\langle n\rangle+k\ln(k)\right)-D\ln\left[\frac{\Gamma(k+\lambda)}{\Gamma(k)\Gamma(1+\lambda)}\right]

(15)

or

\displaystyle S_{\rm NBD}\approx-\langle n\rangle\ln\left(\frac{\langle n\rangle}{\langle n\rangle{+}k}\right){-}k\ln\left(\frac{k}{\langle n\rangle{+}k}\right){-}D\ln\left[\frac{\Gamma(k+\lambda)}{\Gamma(k)\Gamma(1+\lambda)}\right].

(16)

This approximate formula still involves the special function
\Gamma
but that is the standard part of numerical packages like the ROOT framework

IV
Conclusion

There is no available, closed form for entropy of the negative binomial distribution in literature, not such that we are aware of. Special series involving the Pochhammer symbols and special series can be found. In this note, an approximate formula was given that is
\sim 10\%
accurate in the typical parameter domain of the NBD and
\sim 20\%
in the extreme, over dispersed cases. If one needs a precise determination of the entropy, the numerical implementation of the above integral cannot be avoided, but if the present precision is satisfactory, the closed form (that still involve the
\Gamma
function) can be utilized.
