On Ramanujan’s q q -Continued Fractions of Order Thirty-Four and Sixty-Eight
Dipika Sarkar, S. N. Fathima

Abstract
We derived q q -continued fractions X i ​ ( q ) X_{i}(q) of order thirty-four and continued fractions Y i ​ ( q ) Y_{i}(q) of order sixty-eight from a general continued fraction identity of Ramanujan, where i = 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 , 7 i=1,2,3,4,5,6,7 and 8 8 . We established some theta-function identities, and one has been proved for the continued fractions X i ​ ( q ) X_{i}(q) and Y i ​ ( q ) Y_{i}(q) . Furthermore, we obtained results on vanishing coefficients arising from these continued fractions and their reciprocals. As an application of the theta-function identities for Y i ​ ( q ) Y_{i}(q) , we derived certain color partition identities. Keywords: q q -continued fractions; theta-functions; vanishing coefficients; integer partitions; colored partitions Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 05A17, 11P83

Introduction

For any complex numbers
\lambda
and
q

q
-product
(\lambda;q)_{\infty}
as

(\lambda;q)_{\infty}:=\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(1-\lambda q^{n}\right),\quad|q|<1.

(1.1)

For brevity, we often write

(\lambda_{1};q)_{\infty}(\lambda_{2};q)_{\infty}(\lambda_{3};q)_{\infty}\cdots(\lambda_{l};q)_{\infty}=(\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},\lambda_{3},\cdots,\lambda_{l};q)_{\infty}.

The Ramanujan’s general theta-function
f(a,b)

[
1
, p. 34]
is defined as

f(a,b)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}a^{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}b^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}},\quad|ab|<1.

(1.2)

In terms of
f(a,b)
, Jacobi’s triple product identity
[
1
, p. 35, Entry 19]

can be stated as

f(a,b)=(-a;ab)_{\infty}(-b;ab)_{\infty}(ab;ab)_{\infty}=(-a,-b,ab;ab)_{\infty}.

(1.3)

Three useful special cases of
f(a,b)
are the theta-functions
\phi(q)
,
\psi(q)
and
f(-q)

[
1
, p. 36, Entry 22(i)–(iii)]
given by

\phi(q):=f(q,q)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}q^{n^{2}}=\frac{(-q;-q)_{\infty}}{(q;-q)_{\infty}},

(1.4)

\psi(q):=f(q,q^{3})=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}q^{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}=\frac{(q^{2};q^{2})_{\infty}}{(q;q^{2})_{\infty}},

(1.5)

f(-q):=f(-q,-q^{2})=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}q^{\frac{n(3n-1)}{2}}=(q;q)_{\infty}.

(1.6)

Ramanujan also defined the function
\chi(q)

[
1
, p. 36, Entry 22(iv)]

\chi(q)=(-q;q^{2})_{\infty}.

(1.7)

A partition of a positive integer

n

r

The generating function of
