EL SALVADOR: GOVERNMENT BREAKS SILENCE ON DUARTE KILLING
PA271812
CARACAS TELEVISION SERVICE IN SPANISH 1688 GMT 27 SEP 85

(CITEXT)) THE SALVADORAN GOVERNMENT HAS BROKEN ITS SILENCE
WITH THE MEDIA, AND HAS REPORTED THAT CONTACTS WITH INES GUADALUPE
DUARTE’ S KIONAPPERS CONTINUE ALTHOUGH NOT. MUCH PROGRESS HAS BEEN
MADE. THE GOVERNMENT DECISIGN HAS AN EXPLANATION. SPECULATION
MUST NOT BE PERMITTED, AS IN THE CASE OF THOSE WHO DESCRIBE THE
INCLOENTS AS A SELF-KIDNAPPING.

INES GUADALUPE DUARTE. PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON DUARTE’ S
DAUGHTER, HAS SEEN HELD 8Y THE KIDNAPPERS FOR 17 DAYS: DURING THE
FIRST DAYS, THEY WERE SILENT ABOUT THEIR DEMANDS SUT ARE NOW ASKING
FOR THE RELEASE OF 33 GUERRILLAS. IT ZS PRESUMED THAT THE
KIDNAPPERS ARE MEMBERS OF THE PEDRO PABLO CASTILLO FRONT, BUT
SO FAR THERE HAS ONLY BEEN SPECULATION, THE FACT IS THAT INES
GUADALUPE IS STILL SEING HELD, AND HER SITUATION IS A SOURCE OF
GREAT WORRY TO HER FaAMii’ AND FOR ALL SAL VADORANS. ‘
