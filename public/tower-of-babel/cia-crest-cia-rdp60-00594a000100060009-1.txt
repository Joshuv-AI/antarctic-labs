Point ; Publication of the list of lectures at the national
War College encouraged CS personnel (GS— 14' s and above) to absent
themselves from duty without permission.

the Editor of the Bulletin has been directed to add a line concerning

CSTC that supervisors in DD/P no longer consider such support

The Director of Training desires it to be
understood that should he, or other officers of OTB, be taxed with
complaint by any sensitive supervisor within DD/P who resents the
implied insult that he is incompetent, the supervisor will be
assured that this line is printed in the Bulletin over the protest
of the DTE and only at the express request of the CSTC.

GS l4*s are the equivalent of Colonels in the
military forces; absence without leave is a court martial offense.
This situation, in regard to military or civilian men of that rank,
is literally unheard of.
