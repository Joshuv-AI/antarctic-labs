 NSLS-II radioactive sample radiation field calculations using FLUKA
 R. S. Augusto,∗ J. P. Saraiva, K. Wehunt, L. Garcia, E. Fermin, A. Stavitski, and R. J. Lee
 Brookhaven National Laboratory, Upton, NY 11973‐5000.

                                                      (Dated: August 28, 2026)
 The demand for experiments involving radioactive samples at the National Synchrotron Light
 Source II (NSLS-II) has rapidly increased in recent years. Each sample brought to NSLS-II must
 be radiologically characterized to allow NSLS-II’s Radiation Protection Operations (RPO) to plan
 its safe handling, transport, and measurement at the beamline. Currently, residual dose rates to
 personnel and eventual shielding requirements are estimated analytically based on the the samples’s
 activity. However, for higher-activity samples, more complex shielding strategies may be required to
 minimize personnel exposure and maintain doses ALARA. In this work, we describe how Monte Carlo
 (MC) FLUKA calculations can be used to determine the radiation fields resulting from unshielded
 and shielded highly radioactive samples. Subsequently, the simulated values were compared against
 RPO estimates, and finally the possible limitations of this shielding validation strategy are discussed.
