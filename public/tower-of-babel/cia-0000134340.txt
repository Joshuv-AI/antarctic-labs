INTERNAT TONAL DEVELOPMENTS

Peking has handled Hanoi's release of three
American airmen in a gingerly way. The airmen and
their escort party flew to Peking on 26 September
aboard a regularly scheduled Chinese flight. There
was no official Chinese reception on their arrival
in Peking; they were met by a North Vietnamese .em-
bassy official and, according to western journal-
ists, hustled past newsmen into waiting buses by
Chinese officials. The Chinese press has not men~
tioned the stopover in Peking and, in fact, has
been silent on the entire prisoner-release matter
since the initial announcement early this month
that Peking repeated without comment.

By maintaining a low profile, Peking has tried
to make the best of a bad situation. The Chinese’
almost certainly would have preferred to stay clear
of the prisoner release operation entirely so. as
to avoid complicating their relationships with both
Hanoi and Washington.

27 September 1972
