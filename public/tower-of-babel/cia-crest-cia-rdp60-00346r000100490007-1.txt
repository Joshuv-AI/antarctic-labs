4 * Requirements for NIS on the Middle East

It was the consensus of the members that their agencies
did not have capabilities beyond scheduled commitments to fill by
rtli# production the existing gaps in basic intelligence on the Middle
East by next year as requested by ONI (refer to Item 5 NCM 240).

The Committee considers it imperative to sustain the present
capabilities devoted to maintenance in order to keep the published
NIS on the Middle East reasonably up to date. A full discussion of
this matter indicated that most of these gaps will be filled and
Chapters I on moat areas of the Middle East will b® produced within
the next two years under the forecast schedule. In view of these
considerations and the fact that the majority of unpublished NIS on
the Middle East are concerned with Sociological, Political, and
Economic Intelligence, the chairman requested the State member
to determine what current reports could be used as an interim
measure to satisfy this requirement until the necessary NIS are
produced. The State member said he would investigate this matter
and report on those studies which could support ONPs requirement.

In this respect, he observed that thare ware a number of intelligence
projects recently undertaken on the Middle East which ar© listed in
the latest issue of "Foreign Area and Psychological Warfare Research"
prepared by the External Research Staff of State.
