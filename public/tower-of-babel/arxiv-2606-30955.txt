Fractional integral and fractional maximal operators on generalized Fofana spaces
Pokou Nagacy, Bérenger Akon Kpata, Nouffou Diarra

Abstract
. Generalized Fofana spaces were recently introduced as generalizations of Fofana spaces and Nakai’s generalized Morrey spaces. In this paper, we establish the boundedness properties of the following operators in these spaces: fractional integral operators, fractional maximal operators and generalized fractional integral operators. As a consequence, we obtain generalized Olsen-type inequalities involving the Riesz potential and generalized fractional integral operators.

1.
Introduction

Let
1\leq q\leq p\leq\infty
. We denote by
\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
the set of all functions
\phi:(0,\,\infty)\rightarrow(0,\infty)
such that
t\mapsto\phi(t)\,t^{\frac{d}{p}}
is almost decreasing (
0<r\leq s<\infty\Rightarrow\exists\,C>0:\phi(s)\,s^{\frac{d}{p}}\leq C\phi(r)\,r^{\frac{d}{p}}
) and
t\mapsto\phi(t)\,t^{\frac{d}{q}}
is almost increasing (
0<r\leq s<\infty\Rightarrow\exists\,C>0:\phi(r)\,r^{\frac{d}{q}}\leq C\phi(s)\,s^{\frac{d}{q}}
).

For
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
, the generalized Fofana space
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
is defined as the set of all functions
f\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
such that

\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}=\sup_{r>0}\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}\>_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q,p}<\infty,

where
L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
stands for the complex vector space of equivalent classes (modulo equality Lebesgue almost everywhere) of Lebesgue measurable complex-valued functions on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
and

{}_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q,p}=\left\|\left[\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}|f\chi_{B(y,r)}|^{q}(x)dx\right]^{\frac{1}{q}}\right\|_{p},

with the
L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
-norm taken with respect to the variable
y
. Here,

B(y,\,r)
denotes the open ball centered at
y\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
with radius
r>0
. Generalized Fofana spaces
\left(L^{q},L^{p}\right)^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
were introduced in
[
18
]
, in which it is proved that
\left(\left(L^{q},L^{p}\right)^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d}),\,\left\|\cdot\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\right)
is a Banach space. Note that, if
q\leq\alpha\leq p
and
\phi(r)=r^{-\frac{d}{\alpha}}
then
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})=(L^{q},L^{p})^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, where
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\alpha}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
is the classical Fofana space. Moreover
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
coincides with the generalized Morrey space
\mathcal{M}_{q}^{\phi}
, defined by Nakai in
[
19
]
, when
p=\infty
.

In this paper, we are interested in the boundedness properties of the following operators in generalized Fofana spaces: Riesz potentials, fractional maximal operators and generalized fractional integral operators.

Recall that for a given function
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\longrightarrow\>(0,\,\infty)
, the generalized fractional integral operator
T_{\rho}
is defined by

\displaystyle T_{\rho}f(x)=\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\frac{\rho(\left|x-y\right|)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d}}f(y)dy,\quad x\in\mathbb{R}^{d},

for any suitable function
f
on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
.

Let
0<\gamma<d
. For
\rho=t^{\gamma}
,
T_{\rho}
is the classical fractional integral operator
I_{\gamma}
, also known as the Riesz potential of order
\gamma
. It is closely related to the fractional maximal operator
M_{\gamma}
, defined by

\displaystyle M_{\gamma}f(x)=\sup_{r>0}|B(x,r)|^{\frac{\gamma}{d}-1}\displaystyle\int_{B(x,r)}\left|f(y)\right|dy,\;\;\;\;\;x\in\mathbb{R}^{d},

for any locally integrable function
f
on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
. It follows from the definitions of
I_{\gamma}
and
M_{\gamma}
that

(1.1)

M_{\gamma}f(x)\leq C_{d}I_{\gamma}(|f|)(x),\;\;\;\;x\in\mathbb{R}^{d},

where
C_{d}
is the Lebesgue measure of the unit ball in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
.
Note that in the limiting case
\gamma=0
, the fractional maximal operator reduces to the Hardy-Littlewood maximal operator
M
.

It is proved in
[
3
]
that
M
is bounded on classical Fofana spaces. It is also bounded on generalized Morrey spaces (see
[
19
]
) and on generalized Fofana spaces (see
[
18
]
). The Riesz potential
I_{\gamma}
is an important tool in harmonic analysis and partial differential equations. It acts as the inverse of the fractional Laplacian
(-\Delta)^{-\gamma/2}
. Consequently it is useful for finding explicit solutions to wave and Schrödinger equations. It is also found applications in the theory of Sobolev embeddings (see, for example
[
16
]
). The boundedness property of the classical fractional integral operator and its corresponding fractional maximal operator on classical Fofana spaces have been investigated in
[
3
,
5
,
6
]
. In
[
7
]
, norm inequalities for the Riesz potential were examined in particular subspaces of Morrey spaces. For the study of the boundedness of fractional integral operators on generalized Morrey spaces the reader is referred to
[
13
,
19
,
25
]
. Recall that the boundedness of the operator
T_{\rho}
on the generalized Morrey spaces
\mathcal{M}_{q}^{\phi}
was first studied by Nakai
[
20
]
. Several papers were devoted to norm inequalities for
T_{\rho}
in the framework of generalized Morrey spaces, see for example
[
13
,
22
,
24
]
and the references therein.

In studying a Schrödinger equation with perturbed potentials
W
(functions on
\mathbb{R}^{d}
), Olsen
[
21
]
established
the boundedness of the multiplication operator
f\mapsto W\cdot I_{\gamma}f
on Morrey spaces. This estimate, later called Olsen’s inequality, was improved by Sawano et al. in
[
23
]
, where they showed its applications to partial differential equations and the potential theory. Olsen’s inequality was also extended not only to generalized Morrey spaces but also to the operator
T_{\rho}
, see
[
13
]
and
[
15
]
. In this note, we shall prove Olsen-type inequalities in generalized Fofana spaces involving the Riesz potential and generalized fractional integral operators.

This paper is organized as follows. In Section
2
, we give some properties of generalized Fofana spaces and we recall the boundedness result of the Hardy-Littlewwod maximal operator on these spaces. Section
3
is devoted to norm inequalities for the classical fractional integral operator and its corresponding fractional maximal operator in generalized Fofana spaces. In Section
4
, we investigate the boundedness of generalized fractional integral operators in generalized Fofana spaces. Finally, in Section
5
, we establish Olsen-type inequalities.

Throughout this note, the letter
C
will be used as a generic positive constant not depending on the relevant variables. Its value may change from one occurrence to another. We shall also use the abbreviation
A\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\mathrm{B}
for the inequalities
A\leq CB
. If
A\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}B
and
B\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}A
, then we write
A\approx B
. We adopt the usual convention
\frac{1}{\infty}=0
.

2.
Preliminaries

Let
1\leq q,\,p\leq\infty
and let
r>0
. Introduced by Wiener
[
26
]
and systematically studied by Holland
[
14
]
, the amalgam spaces
(L^{q},\,l^{p})_{r}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
are defined as the set of all functions
f\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
satisfying
\>{}_{r}\widetilde{\left\|f\right\|}_{q,p}<\infty
, where

{}_{r}\widetilde{\left\|f\right\|}_{q,p}=\left\|\left\{\left\|f\chi_{Q^{r}_{k}}\right\|_{q}\right\}_{k\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}}\right\|_{\ell^{p}},

with
k=(k_{1},\,...,\,k_{d})\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}
and

Q^{r}_{k}=\prod_{i=1}^{d}[rk_{i},r(k_{i}+1))
.

For more informations about amalgam spaces, we refer to
[
8
]
and the references therein. We recall the following properties.

Proposition 2.1
.

(See
[
14
]
or
[
8
]
.)
Let
r>0
.

(1)

Let
1\leq q,\,p\leq\infty
. Then, the following assertions
hold.

(a)

((L^{q},\,l^{p})_{r}(\mathbb{R}^{d}),\>_{r}\widetilde{\left\|\cdot\right\|}_{q,p})
is a complex Banach space.

(b)

There exists a constant
C>0
such that

C^{-1}\>r^{\frac{d}{p}}\>_{r}\widetilde{\left\|f\right\|}_{q,p}\leq\>_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q,p}\leq Cr^{\frac{d}{p}}\>_{r}\widetilde{\left\|f\right\|}_{q,p},\;\;\;\;\;f\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).

(2)

Let
1\leq q_{1},\,p_{1}\leq\infty
and Let
1\leq q_{2},\,p_{2}\leq\infty
such that

\frac{1}{q_{1}}+\frac{1}{q_{2}}=\frac{1}{q}\leq 1\text{ and }\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}=\frac{1}{p}\leq 1.

If
f\in(L^{q_{1}},\,l^{p_{1}})_{r}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
and
g\in(L^{q_{2}},\,l^{p_{2}})_{r}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, then we have
fg\in(L^{q},\,l^{p})_{r}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
. Moreover

{}_{r}\widetilde{\left\|fg\right\|}_{q,p}\leq\,_{r}\widetilde{\left\|f\right\|}_{q_{1},p_{1}}\,{}_{r}\widetilde{\left\|g\right\|}_{q_{2},p_{2}}.

Let
1\leq q\leq p\leq\infty
. The fact that the function
\phi
belongs to the set
\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
ensures that the generalized Fofana space is non-trivial. Furthermore, if
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
then there exist constants
C_{1}>0
and
C_{2}>0
such that:

(2.1)

\frac{1}{2}\leq\frac{r}{s}\leq 2\implies\frac{1}{C_{1}}\leq\frac{\phi(r)}{\phi(s)}\leq C_{1};

and

(2.2)

(2^{i}r)^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})}\phi(2^{i+1}r)\leq C_{2}\displaystyle\int_{2^{i}r}^{2^{i+1}r}\phi(t)t^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})-1}dt,

for any positive integer
i
and for any
r>0
.

Note that the doubling condition (
2.1
) is well-known and the proof of inequality (
2.2
) is similar to that of Lemma 3.3 in
[
18
]
.

The family of spaces
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
is increasing (in the sense of inclusion) with respect to the
p
power. More precisely, we have the following proposition.

Proposition 2.2
.

(
[
18
, Proposition 2.7 (2)]
)

Let
f\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).
If
1\leq q\leq p_{1}\leq p_{2}\leq\infty
and
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p_{1}}
, then

\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p_{2}})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p_{1}})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},

and consequently
(L^{q},L^{p_{1}})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\subset(L^{q},L^{p_{2}})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).

We now state the following Hölder-type inequality in generalized Fofana spaces.

Proposition 2.3
.

Assume
1\leq q_{1}\leq p_{1}\leq\infty
and
1\leq q_{2}\leq p_{2}\leq\infty
.
Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p},\phi_{1}\in\mathcal{G}_{q_{1},p_{1}}
and
\phi_{2}\in\mathcal{G}_{q_{2},p_{2}}
such that
\phi_{1}(r)\phi_{2}(r)\leq\phi(r)
for all
r>0
. If

\frac{1}{q_{1}}+\frac{1}{q_{2}}=\frac{1}{q}\leq 1\text{ and }\frac{1}{p_{1}}+\frac{1}{p_{2}}=\frac{1}{p}\leq 1,

then for all
(f,\,g)\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\times L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
we have

\|fg\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\lesssim\|f\|_{(L^{q_{1}},L^{p_{1}})^{\phi_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\|g\|_{(L^{q_{2}},L^{p_{2}})^{\phi_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

Proof.

Let
(f,\,g)\in L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\times L^{0}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
. Suppose that
\|fg\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\neq 0
,
\|f\|_{(L^{q_{1}},L^{p_{1}})^{\phi_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}<\infty
and
\|g\|_{(L^{q_{2}},L^{p_{2}})^{\phi_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}<\infty
, since otherwise the desired result is obvious.

Since
{}_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{1},p_{1}}<\infty
and
{}_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{2},p_{2}}<\infty
for all
r>0
, by applying Proposition
2.1
, we obtain

{}_{r}\left\|fg\right\|_{q,p}\lesssim\>_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{1},p_{1}}\>{}_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{2},p_{2}}

for all
r>0
. Multiplying both sides of the previous inequality by

\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}
, we get

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}\>_{r}\left\|fg\right\|_{q,p}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\phi_{1}(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q_{1}}-\frac{1}{p_{1}})}\>_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{1},p_{1}}\frac{1}{\phi_{2}(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q_{2}}-\frac{1}{p_{2}})}\>_{r}\left\|f\right\|_{q_{2},p_{2}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\|f\|_{(L^{q_{1}},L^{p_{1}})^{\phi_{1}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\|g\|_{(L^{q_{2}},L^{p_{2}})^{\phi_{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We conclude the proof by taking in the left-hand side, the supremum over all
r>0
.
∎

We end this section by recalling the boundedness property of the Hardy-Littlewood maximal operator on generalized Fofana spaces.

Theorem 2.4
.

(
[
18
, Theorem 3.1]
)

Let
1<q\leq p\leq\infty
and
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
. Assume that there is a constant
C>0
such that for any
r>0

(2.3)

\displaystyle\int_{r}^{\infty}\phi^{q}(t)t^{\frac{dq}{p}-1}dt\leq C\phi^{q}(r)r^{\frac{dq}{p}}.

Then

\left\|Mf\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},\quad f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d}).

3.
Norm inequalities for classical fractional integral and fractional maximal operators

Let us begin this section with the following notation used later in the work. For
1<q<\infty
, we shall denote by
q^{\prime}
its conjugate exponent:
\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{q^{\prime}}=1
. Our first result is the below theorem stating the boundedness property of the classical fractional integral operator in generalized Fofana spaces.

Theorem 3.1
.

Assume that
1<q\leq p\leq\infty
,
0<\gamma<\frac{d}{q}
and
-\frac{d}{q}\leq\beta<-\gamma
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
) and
\phi(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\beta}
for every
r>0
.

Put

\frac{1}{\bar{p}}=\frac{1}{p}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right)\quad\text{ and }\quad\frac{1}{\bar{q}}=\frac{1}{q}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right).

Then, for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, we have

\left\|I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},

where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
.

Remark 3.2
.

Observe that Theorem
3.1
in the case
p=\infty
was proved in
[
13
, Theorem 2.2]
. Moreover,
when
\phi(r)=r^{-\frac{d}{\alpha}}
, where
1<q\leq\alpha\leq p\leq\infty
and
0<\frac{\gamma}{d}<\frac{1}{\alpha}
, Theorem
3.1
reduces to Corollary 4.4 (1) in
[
3
]
.

Proof of Theorem
3.1
.

Let
1<q\leq p<\infty
and let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
). Suppose that
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\setminus\{0\}
since the case
f=0
is obvious. Let
r>0
and let
x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
. We have

I_{\gamma}f(x)=\displaystyle\int_{B(x,\,r)}\frac{f(y)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dy+\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(x,\,r)}\frac{f(y)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dy=A(x)+B(x).

Following the proof of Theorem 2.2 of
[
13
]
, we may write on the one hand,

(3.1)

\displaystyle|A(x)|=\left|\displaystyle\int_{B(x,\,r)}\frac{f(y)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dy\right|\leq Cr^{\gamma}Mf(x).

On the other hand, we have

\displaystyle|B(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i}r)^{-d+\gamma}|B(x,2^{i+1}r)|^{\frac{d}{q^{\prime}}}\left(\displaystyle\int_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}|f(y)|^{q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i}r)^{-d+\gamma}(2^{i+1}r)^{\frac{d}{q^{\prime}}}(2^{i+1}r)^{\frac{d}{q}}\phi(2^{i+1}r)

\displaystyle\times\frac{1}{\phi(2^{i+1}r)}(2^{i+1}r)^{-\frac{d}{q}}\left(\displaystyle\int_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}|f(y)|^{q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i}r)^{-d+\gamma}(2^{i+1}r)^{d}\phi(2^{i+1}r).

Since
\phi(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\beta}
for every
r>0
, we get

\displaystyle|B(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i}r)^{-d+\gamma}(2^{i+1}r)^{d}(2^{i+1}r)^{\beta}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle Cr^{\beta+\gamma}\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{\beta+\gamma}.

Hence,

(3.2)

\displaystyle|B(x)|\leq Cr^{\beta+\gamma}\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

From (
3.1
) and (
3.2
), we deduce that

\displaystyle|I_{\gamma}f(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left(r^{\gamma}Mf(x)+r^{\beta+\gamma}\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\right)

for all
r>0
. Thus, taking
r=\left(\frac{\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}}{Mf(x)}\right)^{\frac{1}{\beta}},
we get

\displaystyle|I_{\gamma}f(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C(Mf(x))^{\frac{\gamma+\beta}{\beta}}\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}

for all
x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
. It follows that

{}_{r}\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{\bar{q},\bar{p}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\>{}_{r}\|Mf\|^{1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{\bar{q}(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}),\bar{p}(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta})}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\>{}_{r}\|Mf\|^{1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{q,p}.

Then, multiplying both sides of the previous inequality by

\frac{1}{\psi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{\bar{q}}-\frac{1}{\bar{p}})}
, where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
, we get

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\psi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{\bar{q}}-\frac{1}{\bar{p}})}\>_{r}\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{\bar{q},\bar{p}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left(\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}\>_{r}\|Mf\|_{q,p}\right)^{1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}.

Hence,

\left\|I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|Mf\right\|^{1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

By Theorem
2.4
, we get

\displaystyle\left\|I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\|f\|^{-\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|f\right\|^{1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}}_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We end the proof by appying Proposition
2.2
.
∎

As an immediate consequence of Theorem
3.1
and inequality (
1.1
), we have the
following result.

Corollary 3.3
.

Assume that
1<q\leq p\leq\infty
,
0<\gamma<\frac{d}{q}
and
-\frac{d}{q}\leq\beta<-\gamma
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
) and
\phi(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\beta}
for every
r>0
.

Put

\frac{1}{\bar{p}}=\frac{1}{p}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right)\quad\text{ and }\quad\frac{1}{\bar{q}}=\frac{1}{q}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right).

Then, for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, we have

\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},

where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
.

The next theorem gives a norm equivalence of the Riesz potential and its corresponding fractional maximal operator when we deal with non-negative
measurable functions.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
1\leq q\leq p\leq\infty
,
0<\gamma<d
and
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
. Assume that there exists a constant
C>0
such that for any
r>0
,

(3.3)

\displaystyle\int_{r}^{\infty}\phi(t)t^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})-1}dt\leq C\phi(r)r^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})}.

Then, for all positive functions
f\in L_{loc}^{q}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, we have

\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\approx\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

Proof.

Suppose
1\leq q\leq p\leq\infty
and
0<\gamma<d
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying (
3.3
). In view of inequality (
1.1
) it suffices to prove that

\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\lesssim\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

Let
r>0
and let
y\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
. By Lemma 4.2 of
[
10
]
, we have

(3.4)

\|(I_{\gamma}f)\chi_{B(y,r)}\|_{q}\approx\|(M_{\gamma}f)\chi_{B(y,r)}\|_{q}+|B(y,r)|^{\frac{1}{q}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(y,r)}\frac{f(x)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dx.

Furthermore,

\displaystyle|B(y,r)|^{\frac{1}{q}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(y,r)}\frac{f(x)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dx

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}|B(y,r)|^{\frac{1}{q}}\displaystyle\int_{B(y,\,2^{i+1}r)\setminus B(y,\,2^{i}r)}\frac{f(x)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dx

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}|B(y,r)|^{\frac{\gamma}{d}-(1-\frac{1}{q})}\|f\chi_{B(y,2^{i+1}r)}\|_{1}.

Then, thanks to
[
10
, Theorem 5.2]
, we get

\displaystyle|B(y,r)|^{\frac{1}{q}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(y,r)}\frac{f(x)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d-\gamma}}dx\lesssim\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\|(M_{\gamma}f)\chi_{B(y,2^{i+1}r)}\|_{q}.

Taking into
account this inequality, the
L^{p}
-norm of both sides of (
3.4
), with respect to
y
, leads to

{}_{r}\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}\lesssim\>_{r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}+\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\>_{2^{i+1}r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}.

We have, on the one hand,

(3.5)

{}_{r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}\leq\phi(r)r^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

On the other hand,

\displaystyle\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\>_{2^{i+1}r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum^{\infty}_{i=0}\phi(2^{i+1}r)(2^{i+1}r)^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})}.

It follows from (
2.2
) that

\displaystyle\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\>_{2^{i+1}r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}

~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~

\displaystyle\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum^{\infty}_{i=0}\displaystyle\int_{2^{i}r}^{2^{i+1}r}\phi(t)t^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})-1}dt.

Hence,

\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\>_{2^{i+1}r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\displaystyle\int_{r}^{\infty}\phi(t)t^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})-1}dt.

Since
\phi
satisfies (
3.3
), we get

(3.6)

\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i})^{-\frac{d}{q}}\>_{2^{i+1}r}\|M_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\phi(r)r^{d(\frac{1}{q}+\frac{1}{p})}.

From (
3.5
) and (
3.6
), we deduce that

\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}\>_{r}\|I_{\gamma}f\|_{q,p}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|M_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We obtain the desired result by taking the supremum over all
r>0
in
the left-hand side of this last inequality.
∎

Note that Theorem
3.4
generalizes Theorem 4.7 in
[
3
]
and Theorem 7.1 in
[
10
]
.

4.
Boundedness of generalized fractional integral operators

In this section, under different appropriate conditions on
\phi
and
\rho
, we establish two results presenting the boundedness of the generalized fractional integral operator
T_{\rho}
from the generalized Fofana space
(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
to another one.

Theorem 4.1
.

Suppose that
1<q\leq p\leq\infty
,
0<\gamma<\frac{d}{q}
and
-\frac{d}{q}\leq\beta<-\gamma
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
) and let
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\to\>(0,\,\infty)\>
satisfying condition (
2.1
). Assume also that

\phi(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\beta}
and
\rho(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\gamma}
for every
r>0
.

Put

\frac{1}{\bar{p}}=\frac{1}{p}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right)\quad\text{ and }\quad\frac{1}{\bar{q}}=\frac{1}{q}\left(1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta}\right).

Then, for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
we have

\left\|T_{\rho}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},

where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
.

Remark 4.2
.

Note that Theorem
4.1
in the case
p=\infty
was proved in
[
13
, Theorem 2.3]
.

Proof of Theorem
4.1
.

Let
1<q\leq p<\infty
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
) and
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\to\>(0,\,\infty)\>
satisfying condition (
2.1
). We suppose that
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\setminus\{0\}
since the case
f=0
is obvious. Let
r>0
and let
x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
. We have

\displaystyle T_{\rho}f(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{B(x,\,r)}\frac{\rho(\left|x-y\right|)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d}}f(y)dy+\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(x,\,r)}\frac{\rho(\left|x-y\right|)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d}}f(y)dy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle I_{1}(x)+I_{2}(x).

Following the proof of Theorem B in
[
12
]
, we may write

\displaystyle|I_{1}(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle CMf(x)\sum\limits^{-1}_{i=-\infty}\rho(2^{i}r).

It follows that

|I_{1}(x)|\leq CMf(x)\sum\limits^{-1}_{i=-\infty}(2^{i}r)^{\gamma}.

Hence,

(4.1)

\displaystyle|I_{1}(x)|\leq CMf(x)r^{\gamma}.

Furthermore, we have

\displaystyle|I_{2}(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\frac{\rho(2^{i}r)}{(2^{i}r)^{d}}|B(x,2^{i+1}r)|^{\frac{d}{q^{\prime}}}\left(\displaystyle\int_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}|f(y)|^{q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\frac{\rho(2^{i}r)}{(2^{i}r)^{\frac{d}{q}}}(2^{i+1}r)^{\frac{d}{q}}\phi(2^{i+1}r)\frac{1}{\phi(2^{i+1}r)}(2^{i+1}r)^{-\frac{d}{q}}\|f\chi_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}\|_{q}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\rho(2^{i}r)\phi(2^{i}r)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}(2^{i}r)^{\gamma}(2^{i}r)^{\beta}.

Hence,

(4.2)

\displaystyle|I_{2}(x)|\leq Cr^{\beta+\gamma}\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

It follows from (
4.1
) and (
4.2
) that

\displaystyle|T_{\rho}f(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left(r^{\gamma}Mf(x)+r^{\beta+\gamma}\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\right)

for all
r>0
.

We end the proof as we did in that of Theorem
3.1
.
∎

A further statement of the boundedness of the generalized fractional integral operator
T_{\rho}
in generalized Fofana spaces reads as follows.

Theorem 4.3
.

Suppose that
1<q\leq p<\infty
. Let
\phi
be a surjective function belonging to
\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
and satisfying condition (
2.3
). Let
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\to\>(0,\,\infty)\>
satisfying condition (
2.1
).
Put
\frac{q}{\bar{q}}=\frac{p}{\bar{p}}<1
.
Assume that for any
r>0
, the following inequalities hold:

(4.3)

\displaystyle\int_{0}^{r}\frac{\rho(t)}{t}dt\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}-1}

and

(4.4)

\displaystyle\int_{r}^{\infty}\frac{\rho(t)\phi(t)}{t}dt\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}.

Then, for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
we have

\left\|T_{\rho}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})},

where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
.

Proof.

Suppose
1<q\leq p<\infty
. Let
\phi
be a surjective function belonging to
\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
and satisfying condition (
2.3
). Let
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\to\>(0,\,\infty)\>
satisfying condition (
2.1
). Let
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\setminus\{0\}
since the case
f=0
is obvious. Let
r>0
and
x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
. We have

\displaystyle T_{\rho}f(x)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\displaystyle\int_{B(x,\,r)}\frac{\rho(\left|x-y\right|)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d}}f(y)dy+\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\setminus B(x,\,r)}\frac{\rho(\left|x-y\right|)}{\left|x-y\right|^{d}}f(y)dy

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle I_{1}(x)+I_{2}(x).

Following the proof of Theorem B in
[
12
]
, we have

(4.5)

\displaystyle|I_{1}(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle CMf(x)\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}-1}.

Furthermore, we have

\displaystyle|I_{2}(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\frac{\rho(2^{i+1}r)}{(2^{i}r)^{d}}|B(x,2^{i+1}r)|^{\frac{d}{q^{{}^{\prime}}}}\left(\displaystyle\int_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}|f(y)|^{q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\frac{\rho(2^{i+1}r)}{(2^{i}r)^{\frac{d}{q}}}(2^{i+1}r)^{\frac{d}{q}}\phi(2^{i+1}r)\frac{1}{\phi(2^{i+1}r)}(2^{i+1}r)^{-\frac{d}{q}}\|f\chi_{B(x,2^{i+1}r)}\|_{q}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\rho(2^{i+1}r)\phi(2^{i+1}r)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\sum\limits^{\infty}_{i=0}\displaystyle\int_{2^{i}r}^{2^{i+1}r}\frac{\rho(t)\phi(t)}{t}dt

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\displaystyle\int_{r}^{+\infty}\frac{\rho(t)\phi(t)}{t}dt.

Hence,

(4.6)

\displaystyle|I_{2}(x)|\leq C\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}.

It follows from (
4.5
) and (
4.6
) that

\displaystyle|T_{\rho}f(x)|

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\left(Mf(x)\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}-1}+\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\phi(r)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}\right)

for all
r>0
.

Since
\phi
is surjective, we can choose
r>0
such that

\phi(r)=\frac{Mf(x)}{\|f\|_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}}.

Thus, for every
x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}
we have

\displaystyle|T_{\rho}f(x)|\leq C(Mf(x))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

It follows that

{}_{r}\|T_{\rho}f\|_{\bar{q},\bar{p}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\>{}_{r}\|Mf\|^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{\bar{q}\frac{q}{\bar{q}},\bar{p}\frac{p}{\bar{p}}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\>{}_{r}\|Mf\|^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{q,p}.

Multiplying both sides of the previous inequality by
\frac{1}{\psi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{\bar{q}}-\frac{1}{\bar{p}})}
, where
\psi(r)=(\phi(r))^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}
, we get

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\psi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{\bar{q}}-\frac{1}{\bar{p}})}\>_{r}\|T_{\rho}f\|_{\bar{q},\bar{p}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle C\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left(\frac{1}{\phi(r)}r^{d(-\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{p})}\>_{r}\|Mf\|_{q,p}\right)^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}.

Hence,

\left\|T_{\rho}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|Mf\right\|^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}

By Theorem
2.4
, we get

\displaystyle\left\|T_{\rho}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\psi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq C\|f\|^{1-\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{\infty})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|f\right\|^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We end the proof by applying Proposition
2.2
.
∎

The analogue of Theorem
4.3
within the framework of generalized Morrey spaces was proved in
[
12
, Theorem B]
.

5.
Olsen-type inequalities in generalized Fofana spaces

As a consequence of Proposition
2.3
and Theorem
3.1
, we have the following Olsen-type inequality.

Theorem 5.1
.

Assume that
1<q\leq p\leq\infty
,
0<\gamma<\frac{d}{q}
and
-\frac{d}{q}\leq\beta<-\gamma
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
) and
\phi(r)\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}r^{\beta}
for every
r>0
. Put
s=-\frac{\beta q}{\gamma}
and
l=-\frac{\beta p}{\gamma}
. If
W\in(L^{s},L^{l})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{s}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
,
then for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
we have

\left\|W\cdot I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|W\right\|_{(L^{s},L^{l})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{s}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

Remark 5.2
.

Note that Theorem
5.1
in the case
p=\infty
was proved in
[
13
, Theorem 3.2]
.

Proof of Theorem
5.1
.

Let
1<q\leq p<\infty
. Let
\phi\in\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
satisfying condition (
2.3
). Put
\frac{1}{\bar{p}}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{l}\>\text{ and }\>\frac{1}{\bar{q}}=\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{s}.

This implies that

\frac{1}{\frac{l}{p}}+\frac{1}{\frac{\bar{p}}{p}}=1\>\text{ and }\>\frac{1}{\frac{s}{q}}+\frac{1}{\frac{\bar{q}}{q}}=1.

According to Proposition
2.3
,

\left\|W\cdot I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\leq\left\|W\right\|_{(L^{s},L^{l})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{s}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|I_{\gamma}f\right\|_{(L^{\bar{q}},L^{\bar{p}})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{\bar{q}}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

We end the proof by applying Theorem
3.1
.
∎

The below estimate is also an Olsen-type inequality involving the generalized fractional integral operator
T_{\rho}
.

Theorem 5.3
.

Suppose that
1<q\leq p<\infty
. Let
\phi
be a surjective function belonging to
\mathcal{G}_{q,p}
and satisfying condition (
2.3
). Let
\rho:\>(0,\,\infty)\>\to\>(0,\,\infty)\>
satisfying condition (
2.1
) and the inequalities (
4.3
) and (
4.4
).
Put

\frac{q}{\bar{q}}=\frac{p}{\bar{p}}<1,\,\frac{1}{l}=\frac{1}{p}-\frac{1}{\bar{p}}\>\text{ and }\>\frac{1}{s}=\frac{1}{q}-\frac{1}{\bar{q}}.

If
W\in(L^{s},L^{l})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{s}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
, then for all
f\in(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})
we have

\left\|W\cdot T_{\rho}f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\raisebox{-4.30554pt}{$~\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\textstyle<}}{{\sim}}~$}\left\|W\right\|_{(L^{s},L^{l})^{\phi^{\frac{q}{s}}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\left\|f\right\|_{(L^{q},L^{p})^{\phi}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}.

Theorem
5.3
is a consequence of Proposition
2.3
and Theorem
4.3
. Its proof is similar to that of Theorem
5.1
.
