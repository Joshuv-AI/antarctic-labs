29 December 1955

Memorandum for: The Director
Subject: Congressional Contacts.

1. We are going ahead with arrangements to contact certain key
members of the Congress during the early stages of the forthcoming
session. We will attempt to spare the Director from as many routine
contacts as possible, but there are certain members of the House and
Senate whom the Director should see personally, both to receive their
own observations as a result of recent trips or other developments, and
to place before certain of the leaders the legislative problems which
directly affect CIA. It is assumed for these purposes that the Director
will want to continue the past procedure of having weekly luncheons on
Monday, to which key members of the Congress will be invited. It may
also be advisable on occasion to arrange more than one meeting a week,
at least in the early stages of the session.

2. The following list of Congressional members, broken down into
priorities, is submitted for the Director's approval:
