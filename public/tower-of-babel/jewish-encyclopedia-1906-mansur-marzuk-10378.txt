Egyptian rabbi and author; settled at Salonica toward the close of the eighteenth century. He was the author of several works: "Ẓur Todah" (Salonica, 1783), a commentary on the Yad ha-Ḥazaḳah; "Ben Pedahẓur" ( ib. 1786), sermons; "Ḳorban Eliẓur," a Talmudic commentary.

Bibliography: Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim; Franco, Histoire des Israélites de l'Empire Ottoman, p. 127. S. M. Fr.
