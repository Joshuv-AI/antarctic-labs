=== OVERVIEW ===
The Roswell incident refers to the recovery of an object that crashed near Roswell, New Mexico in July 1947. The U.S. Army Air Forces initially stated it recovered a "flying disc" but quickly retracted this, claiming it was a weather balloon. This later became attributed to Project Mogul - top-secret high-altitude balloons designed to detect Soviet nuclear tests.

The Majestic-12 documents purport to be classified materials about a special group established by President Truman in 1947 to investigate UFO crash retrievals. These documents remain controversial with disputed authenticity.
