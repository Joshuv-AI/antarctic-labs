On behalf of the officers, trustees, and members of the
World Affairs Council of Orange County, may I re-extend
to you our invitation to address our Council? Please
consider this an "open-ended" invitation to be used
anytime you are available. We have your reply of February]
5, 1979 to our original invitation, but realize these
invitations could easily get lost in the shuffle.

Our Council now has over 1500 members who are keenl;
interested in national and international affairs. We
try to present to our members 8 to 10 outstanding speakers
each year.

Enclosed you will find our brochure indicating some of
the speakers we have had recently and they have all re-
ceived warm responses by our membership.

We hope you can find time in your busy schedule to visit
with us in Orange County.

Cordially yours.

Executive Director
