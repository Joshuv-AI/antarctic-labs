Expression of Concern for “Highly Active Biphasic Anatase-Rutile Ni-Pd/TNPs Nanocatalyst for the Reforming and Cracking Reactions of Microplastic Waste Dissolved in Phenol”
Authors: Walid Nabgan, Bahador Nabgan, Tuan Amran Tuan Abdullah, Muhammad Ikram, Arvind H. Jadhav, Aishah Abdul Jalil, Mohamad Wijayanuddin Ali
Published: 2026

As of July 23, 2026, the Editor-in-Chief has issued an Expression of Concern for this article (DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.1c05488) due to unresolved questions about the accountability for the work. When viewed alongside a broader pattern of issues across related publications (DOIs: 10.1021/acsomega.3c08077, 10.1021/acsomega.3c05938, and 10.1021/acsomega.3c09890) these concerns raise doubts about the reliability of the findings. The authors provided an explanation but, at this time, it neither resolved nor confirmed the concerns.
