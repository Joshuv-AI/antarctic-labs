Correction: Copper-Based Targeted Nanocatalytic Therapeutics for Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
Authors: Yongfei Fan, Jiao Chang, Xichun Qin, Meng Li, Yan Li, Leilei Wu, Kun Li, Zhimin Chen, Yani Li, Zhongmin Tang, Dong Xie, Jianlin Shi
Published: 2026

Correction to : Nano-Micro Lett. (2026) 18:152. 10.1007/s40820-025-01998-5

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported the article needed to be updated. The required corrections are detailed below.

The full name of DMSA was incorrectly written at its first occurrence in the Highlights, Abstract, Introduction, Fig. 1 legend, and Supplementary Information (Section S1, Experimental Section). The correct expression is “copper (Cu) and dimercaptosuccinic acid (DMSA).” The chemical equations in the Table of Contents graphic (TOC) and Fig. 1 were incorrectly written. The correct expressions are “Cu⁺ + H₂O₂ → Cu2⁺ + OH⁻ + •OH.” Due to an error during image assembly involving copy-and-paste operations, the statistical plot for PC-9 in Fig. 6i was incorrectly duplicated from that of NCI-H1975.
