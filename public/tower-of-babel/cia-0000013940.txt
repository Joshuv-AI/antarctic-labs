1. On 30 March 1966, Company 1, Battalion 7, Regiment 18B ceptured’ four U.S.
prisoner's of war (POWs) during a battle near the YAMO River in Central
GIA LAI Province (PLEIKU Province), SVN, The company political officer said
that the POW's were members of the U.S. Army ist Airborne".

2. The POW's were uninjured and appeared to be in good health. They were light
complected, ranged in height from 1.8 to 2 meters and weighéd from 80 to 90
kilograms. They had brown hair. Two of the POW's had striped rank insignias
on the sleeves of their green uniforms and two wore insignias on the shoulders

of their shirts.
é
3 The POWs were being led southward in the direction of CAMBODIA by four guards.

Their hands were tied at the wrists. The political officer later said that the
POWs would probably be taken to CAMBODIA and, from there, would be flown to NVN

by helicopter.
