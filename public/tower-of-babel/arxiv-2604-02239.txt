On congruence conjectures of Andrews and Bachraoui
Koustav Banerjee, Kathrin Bringmann, Mohamed El Bachraoui

Abstract
. Andrews and the third author recently studied congruences for certain restricted two-color partitions. They made two conjectures for Ramanujan-type congruences and a vanishing identity for the limiting sequence. In this paper, we settle these conjectures by relating the corresponding generating function to modular forms and mock theta functions.

Proposition 7.1

We have

M(q)=\frac{4\left(q^{2};q^{2}\right)^{2}_{\infty}}{\left(q;q^{2}\right)^{6}_{\infty}}.

Proof.

We write
{\omega}(q)

Lemma

By

{\omega}(q)=\frac{1}{2}q^{-1}G(q)-\frac{1}{2}q^{-1}f\!\left(q^{8}\right)-\frac{1}{2}q^{2}{\omega}\!\left(-q^{4}\right).

Note that only the first term contributes to

Then, Lemma 2.7 yields

(7.2)

\displaystyle{\omega}_{0}(q)

\displaystyle=\frac{G_{1}(q)}{2}=\frac{\Theta^{2}(q)\psi\!\left(q^{2}\right)}{(q)^{2}_{\infty}},\quad{\omega}_{1}(q)=\frac{G_{2}(q)}{2}=\frac{2\Theta(q)\psi^{2}\!\left(q^{2}\right)}{(q)^{2}_{\infty}}.

By (

), we then note that

\displaystyle M\!\left(q\right)=\frac{4\Theta^{2}\!\left(q\right)\psi^{2}\!\left(q^{2}\right)}{\left(q\right)^{2}_{\infty}}=\frac{4\left(q^{2};q^{2}\right)^{2}_{\infty}}{\left(q;q^{2}\right)^{6}_{\infty}},

using (

Proof of

.

Next, by (
