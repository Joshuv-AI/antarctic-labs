CENOBITES (from Gr. κοινός , common, and βίος , life), monks who lived together in a convent or community under a rule and a superior,—in contrast to hermits or anchorets who live in isolation. The Basilians Basilian Monks y (q.v.) in the East and the Benedictines y (q.v.) in the West are the chief cenobitical orders (see
Monasticism).
