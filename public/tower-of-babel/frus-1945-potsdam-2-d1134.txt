No. 1134 The War Shipping Administrator (Land) and the Assistant to the War Shipping Administrator (Morse) to the Regional Director for the Combined United Kingdom and Continental Areas, War Shipping Administration (Monroe) and the Representative on the London Branch of the United Maritime Executive Board (Carnwath)

confidential priority

Babelsberg, 23 July 1945.

Victory 221. To Monroe and Carnwath, War Shipping Administration, London, England. Repeat to Conway, WSA, Washington, D. C. from Vice Admiral Land and Morse. Reference your Wsa 3844 July 21.[1]

Have discussed matter with State Department here and Weston. UMA papers should not be sent to officials of former Polish Government and they should not be invited to attend meetings or communicated with further on UMA matters.

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] Document No. 1132.
