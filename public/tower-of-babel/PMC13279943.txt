Correction: Exploitation of phage Carin-5’s own DNA polymerase to sequence its T-hypermodified genome
Authors: Rémi Sieskind, Sophia Missoury, Frédéric Bonhomme, Pauline Nogaret, Soizick Lucas-Staat, Marc Monot, Mikael Skurnik, Anne-Claire Baudoux, Marc Delarue
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-26930-0, published online 05 December 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in Fig. 5B, where the formula was incorrect in the glycosidic bond. The incorrect Figure 5 along with its captions is provided below.

Fig. 5Related phages and their DNA modifications. (A) Phylogenetic tree of DNA polymerases related to Carin-5 DNAP (PolA family), selected to have a length larger than 900 amino-acids and containing the N-terminal UDG-like domain. Phages known to have a hypermodified thymidine in their genome have a red star: RP13 is annotated as carrying a DNA modification on the thymine (Kawasaki et al.6), based on conservation of enzymes putatively involved in its biosynthesis (Kropinski et al.7). (B) DNA modification of SP15 on the thymine nucleoside, showing the deoxyribose, the base and its modifications: the linker, the phosphate, and the sugars R1 and R2 which can be glucose and glucuronic acid.
