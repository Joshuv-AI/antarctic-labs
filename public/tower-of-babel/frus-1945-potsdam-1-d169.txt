No. 169 Memorandum by the Representatives of the British Chiefs of Staff

top secret

[Washington,] 25 June 1945.

C. C. S. 880/1

Agenda for the Next United States–British Staff Conference

The British Chiefs of Staff have now one further item to add to the tentative outline agenda proposed in C. C. S. 880.[1] They suggest that the following item be added: (11) Combined Chiefs of Staff Machinery after the war with Japan.

The British Chiefs of Staff would submit a paper on this item as a basis for discussion.

Source: J. C. S. Files
