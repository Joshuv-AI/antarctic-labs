Polish rabbi and preacher; died at Altona in 1681. He was at first rabbi of Vladislav, government of Suwalki, Russian Poland, and then dayyan at Cracow. He wrote a work entitled "Neḥamot Ẓiyyon" (Frankfort-on-the-Main, 1677), a collection of twenty-two sermons.

Bibliography: Wolf, Bibl. Hebr. i., No. 1451; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1733. H. R. M. Sel.
