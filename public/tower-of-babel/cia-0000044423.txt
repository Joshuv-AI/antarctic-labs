//((INTERVIEW WITH SALVADORAN BISHOP ROBERTO JOAQUIN RAMOS BY
UNIDENTIFIED REPORTER AT RADIO STATION ~= LIVE) )

((TEXT)) ((REPORTER)) WELCOME, WR BISHOP.

((RANOS)) TRANK YOU. 1 WANT TO APPEAL TO THE SALVADORAN PEOPLE
FOR PEACE SO THAT WE MAY ALL STRUGGLE TO BUILD A COMMUNITY WHERE
((WORDS INDISTINCT)) VALUES.

TT 13 REGRETTABLE THAT THE DIALOGUE PROCESS HAS COKE TO A HALT
AND THAT AT A MOMENT WHEN THE GERMANS ARE CELEBRATING THEIR UNITY, WE
ARE INITIATING AN ARMED STRUGGLE.

T ESPECIALLY WANT TO EXPRESS MY REGRET AT THE ASSASSINATION OF THE
JESUIT PRIESTS. THIS WAS A SAVAGE CRIME WITHOUT PRECEDENT IN EL
SALVADOR'S HISTORY. ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) TO WORSEN THE COUNTRY'S
SITUATION AND TO EXACERBATE PASSIONS TO MAKE PEACE INCREASINGLY
DIFFICULT.

I HOPE THAT MY BROTHERS, ALL THE SALVADORANS WHO BELIEVE IN
CHRIST, MAY.LEARN TO VALUE LIFE AS A PRECIOUS GIFT FROM GOD, MAY
LEARN TO RESPECT LIFE, TO RESPECT OTHER PEOPLE'S OPINIONS, AND TO
LOVE OTHERS IN SPITE OF DIFFERENCES.

IT ALSO HOPE THAT THIS PAINFUL SITUATION WE HAVE BEEN ENDURING FOR
THE PAST 10 YEARS WILL MAKE US MORE AWARE OF OUR DUTIES AB CITIZENS
AND CHRISTIANS. WE HAVE THE OBLIGATION TO BUILD A JUSTER SOCIETY,
WORE DESERVING OF THE NAME THE NATION BRAS CARRIED SINCE THE DAYS OF
THE COLONY: EL SALVADOR.

I WANT THE SAVIOR TO ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) TO CALK DOWN THIS THIRST
OF BLOOD THAT 18 ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) AND DESTROYING USEFUL LIVES,
THE LIVES OF ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) AND GET OVER THIS STORM ( (WORDS
INDISTINCT)) AS MEMBERS OF A CIVILIZED SOCIETY, OF A CHRISTIAN
SOCIETY.

((REPORTER)) YOUR STATEMENT CONFORTS US AT THIS DIFFICULT
MOMENT. DO YOU HAVE ANY RECOMMENDATION FOR THE SALVADORAN PEOPLE AS
TO WHAT TO DO AT THIS TIXE?

((RAMOB)) I RECOXKEND TO THE SALVADORAN PEOPLE TO REMAIN CALM,
AND ESPECIALLY TO PRAY. ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) THE KEARTS OF PEACE
THROUGH LOVE FOR GOD AND THE FAITH WE KAY HAVE.

((REPORTER)) DO YOU HAVE AUT RECOMKENDATION FOR THE REBELS, FOR
TRE FXLW SPECIFICALLY?

((RAMOS)) I WOULD ASK THEM TO REESTABLISH THE DIALOGUE THAT HAS
BEEN BROKEN. IT HAS BEEN CONFIRMED, SINCE THE DAYS WHEY CAIN KILLED
HIS BROTHER ABEL, THAT VIOLENCE I8 NOT THE SOLUTION TO INJUSTICE.
VIOLENCE GENERATES MORE VIOLENCE, AND AS CIVILIZED PEOPLE WE SHOULD
RESOLVE OUR DIFFERENCES THROUGH NEGOTIATIONS AND DIALOGUE.
