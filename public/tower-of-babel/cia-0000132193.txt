Both Tehran and Baghdad radio services reported forced
landings by Iraqi aircraft in Tehran today:

Tehran radio reported at 10302 that Iraqi warplanes had
made “emergency landings" at an Iranian airport and that
the leadership had stressed its intention to “confront

any action that may violate (Iran’s] neutral stance."

Baghdad radio reported at 14202 that several Iraqi
Planes had been "forced to land" in Iran and that the
return of the planes and pilots was being pursued. pe]

A later broadcast from Tehran reported President Rafsanjani

had just chaired a Supreme National Security Council meeting that
discussed the emergency landings.

The Council reportedly warned
combatants--in view of Iran’s neutrality--not to use Iranian

airspace and declared that any aircraft from either side that
made emergency land

ings in Iran would be "confiscated until the
end of the war." —&

Australia Expels Iragi Diplomat

The Iraqi Charge d’Affaires in Australia, Sa’ad Omran, was
given 72 hours to leave the country, according to press reports
today. Australian Foreign Minister Gareth Evans said the
decision to expel the highest ranking Iraqi diplomat remaining in
Canberra was based on Australian security concerns, factoring in
| statements by Omran earlier this week linking Australia’s
membership in the coalition with the possibility of terrorisn.
The Iraqi Embassy in Canberra will be staffed by three remaining
diplomats and two administrative staff members.
