German scholar; lived at Fulda in the seventeenth century. He was the author of a Judæo-German work in two parts: the first, "Bet Yisrael," a history of the Jews up to the destruction of the Second Temple; and the second, "Bet ha-Beḥirah," in twenty-four chapters, a description of Jerusalem and of both temples (Offenbach, 1719).

Bibliography: Michael, Or ha-Ḥayyim, p. 225, No. 480; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 259; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 730. D. M. Sel.
