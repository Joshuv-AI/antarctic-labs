10. ON 8 JANUARY CRISTIAN] NAMED A MILITARY HONOR TRIBUNAL
TO INVESTIGATE THE CASE. THE TRIBUNAL IS COMPRISED OF ARMY
OFFICERS AND TWO CIVILIAN LAWYERS. THERE IS CONCERN IN THE
EMBASSY THAT THE TRIBUNAL WILL BECOME THE LEAD INVESTIGATING
BODY, REPLACING THE SIU, GIVING THE IMPRESSION THAT "THE FOX
IS INVESTIGATING THE CHICKENS" AND THAT THE SIU WILL LOSE
PRESTIGE AS AN INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATIVE BODY (THE SIU IS AN
EMBASSY CREATION). TO AVOID THE SITUATION THE EMBASSY IS ASKING
THAT THE HONOR TRIBUNAL ISSUE AN ORDER TO THE SIU TO THE EFFECT
THAT THE SIU INVESTIGATE THE MURDERS IN AN INDEPENDENT MANNER AND
REPORT THE RESULTS TO THE TRIBUNAL. IN EFFECT, THE TRIBUNAL'S
ORDER WOULD BE A FORM OF CHARTER FOR THE SIU AND WOULD PRESERVE
THE SIU'S IMAGE AS INDEPENDENT.
