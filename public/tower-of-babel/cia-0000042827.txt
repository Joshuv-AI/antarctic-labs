FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:

1. [Text] Salvadoran Archbishop Arturo Rivera y Damas has again
reported that he has received death threats through his secretaries
or the archbishopric’s cooks, ‘‘but, in this regard, I feel reassured
and I am in God’s hands,’’ the pastor said.
2. Nevertheless, he acknowledged that the persecution against the
church in general has decreased, although this does not mean it has
disappeared. He recalled that many priests have died in the last 10
years, including an archbishop, ‘‘but martyrs’ blood is Christians’
seed,’’ Rivera y Damas said.

3. The archbishop said that he received many complaints because of
the church’s stance on the Jesuits’ assassination, but today
**‘everyone knows that the archbishopric was right in pointing out
where it believed the assassins were located. This assumption was
confirmed by President Cristiani who, in keeping his promise to carry
out a thorough investigation, has earned the world’s recognition.’’

4. In another part of his homily, he stated: ‘‘Being a bishop of a
nation that has suffered the scourge of a fratricidal war for 10
years implies having mercy for human suffering above and beyond
political, economic, and ideological considerations; and that is a
commitment we cannot give up.’’

5. The archbishop said that one of the church’s most important
events this week is the episcopal conference of El Salvador, which
concluded with an appeal for peace; and the arraignment of the
military officers accused of the Jesuits’ death.

6. The meeting was held with special interest, particularly as it
was revealed that the Farabundo Marti National Liberation Front
(FMLN) sent a letter to the bishops asking for the church’s mediation
in its efforts to renew the dialogue.
