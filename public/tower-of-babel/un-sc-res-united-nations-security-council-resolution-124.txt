Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 775th meeting, on 7 March 1957

The Security Council,

Having considered the application of Ghana for membership in the United Nations, <small>[1]</small>

Recommends to the General Assembly that Ghana be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Twelfth Year, Supplement for January, February and March 1957, document S/3797.</small>
