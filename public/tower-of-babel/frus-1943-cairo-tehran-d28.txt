President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (26 August 1943, d28)

[Hyde Park,] August 26, 1943.

From: The President

To: Colonel Warden, Personal and Secret

Number 347. I hope you have seen Uncle Joe’s new message which is greatly improved in its tone. As you and he know, I am lost until Monday[2] and I would let a few days go by till we answer him.

However, my first feeling is that he has come around to our secondary meeting and that it should be held very soon.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent via the White House Map Room and the British Admiralty Delegation at Washington. Churchill was in Canada at this time.
[2] August 30, 1943.
