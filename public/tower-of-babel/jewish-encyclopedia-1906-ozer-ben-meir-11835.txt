Polish rabbi; died at Zolkiev May, 1710; great-grandson of Solomon Luria. 'Ozer was rabbi at Clementow. He wrote: "Eben 'Ozer 'al Yad" (with text, Amsterdam, 1742), on the Yoreh De'ah, on Oraḥ Ḥayyim, and on Eben ha-'Ezer; novellæ on some massektot (Zolkiev, 1753); and others of a similar character, published in "Maginne Ereẓ" (Amsterdam, 1753).

Bibliography: Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, i. 153; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. No. 6697; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 56. H. R. A. S. W.
