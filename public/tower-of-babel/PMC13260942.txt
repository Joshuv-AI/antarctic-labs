Correction: The prevalence of mental health disorders and stress coping strategies among forced migrants from Ukraine and Russia
Authors: Piotr Długosz, Liudmyla Yuzva
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-47201-6, published online 07 April 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in the Abstract.

“Participants were recruited through purposive sampling, with invitations disseminated via social media platforms (Telegram, Facebook) targeting Ukrainians residing in Russia. The sample comprised N = 200 Ukrainian and N = 164 Russian respondents.”

now reads:

“Participants were recruited through purposive sampling, with invitations disseminated via social media platforms (Telegram, Facebook). The sample comprised Ukrainian forced migrants residing in Poland (N = 200) and Russian forced migrants residing primarily in Serbia and other countries (N = 164).”

The original Article has been corrected.
