Some lower bounds for the maximal number of A-singularities in algebraic surfaces. II
Juan García Escudero

Abstract
. Algebraic surfaces in the complex projective space with a high number of A A -type singularities have been presented in a recent paper. We extend the construction in order to obtain lower bounds for the maximal number of A A singularities for certain additional cases.

1.
Introduction

A classical problem in algebraic geometry consists of determining the possible number of singularities of a given type for surfaces of degree
d
in the complex projective space
\mathbb{P}^{3}({\mathbb{C}})
. Upper bounds for the maximal number of
A
-type singularities on degree
d
complex algebraic surfaces have been given in
[
23
,
20
]
. Lower bounds for degree
d
have been obtained in
[
7
,
19
,
9
,
11
,
14
]
. For a number of low degree surfaces certain explicit constructions give better lower bounds
[
6
,
17
,
22
,
2
,
8
,
21
,
18
,
5
,
4
]
. The exact value of the upper bounds is known only for a few cases
[
3
,
16
]
.

Complex algebraic surfaces of degrees
d=3q
with a large number of type
A
singularities have been constructed in a recent paper
[
14
]
. We extend the construction to obtain lower bounds for the maximal number of singularities for a series of cases not considered in
[
14
]
.

The affine equations of the surfaces studied in
[
14
]
consist of a sum of a bivariate polynomial
{\mathcal{J}}
with three critical values and a univariate polynomial
{\mathcal{B}}
with two critical values. The surfaces constructed in this paper have similar equations with new polynomials
{\mathcal{G}}
replacing
{\mathcal{B}}
. The polynomials
{\mathcal{G}}
have also two critical values and can be described in terms of planar trees which are obtained by a series of transformations applied to the trees associated to
{\mathcal{B}}
. In this way the results about cusps and other
A_{\nu}
singularities obtained in
[
10
,
11
]
can be seen as special cases of this constructions.

2.
The families of polynomials with two critical values

A univariate polynomial with no more than two different critical values, also called a Belyi polynomial, can be represented by a plane tree with a bicoloring for the vertices. The polynomial critical points have the multiplicities given by the number of edges adjacent to the vertices minus one
[
1
,
19
]
. The degree of a vertex is the number of edges incident to it. A leaf vertex is a vertex with degree one. Black and white vertices represent critical points with critical value
\zeta=-1
and
\zeta=1
respectively, and their multiplicities are given by the degrees of the vertices minus one. We denote by
N_{\zeta}(\mathcal{P},\rho)
the number of critical points of a polynomial
\mathcal{P}
with critical value
\zeta
and multiplicity
\rho
.

(2.1)

{\mathcal{B}}^{(2)}_{3(m+lj+(n+l)(3l+j+1))+j(j+2),3(n+l)+j,3m+j-1}(w)

(2.2)

.

There exist polynomials

3.
The singular surfaces

We denote the number of singularities of type
A_{\nu}
in a surface
\mathcal{S}
by
\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{S},A_{\nu})
and the maximal number of
A_{\nu}

\hat{J}_{d,\tau}(x,y):=\lambda_{d,\tau}\prod_{\mu}L_{d,\tau,\mu}\left(x,y\right)

(3.1)

where
L_{d,\tau,\mu}(x,y):=y+({\rm cos}2\varphi-x){\rm tan}\varphi+{\rm sin}2\varphi=0,\varphi=\frac{(6\mu-1)\pi}{6d}-\frac{\tau}{d},\mu=-\lfloor\frac{d-2}{2}\rfloor,-\lfloor\frac{d-2}{2}\rfloor+1,\dots,\lfloor\frac{d+1}{2}\rfloor
with
(x,y)\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2}
and
\tau\in\mathbb{R}
. The parameters are
\lambda_{d,\tau}=(-1)^{m}2d
if
\tau=(6m-3d-1)\frac{\pi}{6}
(in this case the line

) and
