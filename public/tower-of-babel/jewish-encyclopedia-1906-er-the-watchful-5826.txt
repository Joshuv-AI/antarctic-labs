1. First son of Judah by Shuah the Canaanite (Gen. xxxviii. 3; Num. xxvi. 19). He died soon after marrying Tamar, because he "was wicked in the sight of the Lord" (Gen. xxxviii. 7). What his sin was is explained in Bereshit R. lxxxv. 4. 2. Son of Shelah, the son of Judah (I Chron. iv. 21). 3. Son of Jose, one of the ancestors of Jesus (Luke iii. 28). E. G. H. M. Sel.
