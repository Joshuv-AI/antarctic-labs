2. Work Program for Paragraph 9 of Economic Defense Policy
(See references below)

Decision

The Executive Committee will meet on Tuesday, November 12, to start its
discussions on recommendations to EDAC with respect to a work program under
Paragraph 9 of the Economic Defense Policy.

The first issues which will be discussed are (1) criteria, (2) adminis-
trative principles, and (3) changed concepts of List II and List III. A report
on controversies and alternative suggestions tiiich arise in these discussions
will be submitted to EDAC for resolution, preparatory to submitting a full
study.

Discussion

The Chairman said that the Ad Hoc Subcommittee on List Review had sub-
mitted a sufficient number of reports to allow the Executive Committee to
start discussions on recommendations to EDAC with respect to courses of action
under Paragraph 9 of the Economic Defense Policy. In this connection he said
that Defense had withdrawn its objection to starting these discussions in the
Executive Committee until the Ad Hoc Subcommittee had completed its review.

He reviewed the documents which have been prepared in connection with
this over-all review thus far and cited the following as those which the
Executive Committee must take into its consideration?

Annex A to EDAC D-135/la - work program for paragraph 9

ED/EC OM-23 - assignment to Working Group I to review criteria and admin-
istrative principles.

A summary of WG I comments on the Ad Hoc Subcommittee's reports
(to be prepared by the Chairman of WG I)

The Chairman felt that one of the major problems will be extracting from
all of the available material the most important elements for presentation to
EDAC, He asked for the views of the members on the extent of the material
which they felt should be forwarded to EDAC and in what form it should be done

He said it was his view that as the documents are discussed and reviewed
in the Executive Committee, only those important principles on which there are
disagreements or alternative ideas should be forwarded to EDAC. After EDAC
resolution of these issues, the Executive Committee would proceed with its
review to submit a full report to EDAC.

The Commerce member agreed that we should not put the whole package to
EDAC, but he thought we should move quickly to present the general problem
to that Committee. He felt the Executive Committee should prepare a progress
report (although not necessarily calling it by that name) to let EDAC know
the extent and status of activity to date under the work program. This
report should also seek EDAC advice on the controversial issues which have
come up. He felt the Executive Committee should also prepare a report on the
recent happenings which have an impact on this study, such as the British note
the NATO meeting, etc, and ask if, in the light of these developments, EDAC
wishes to change the assignment in any way. He also felt that EDAC should be
given a review of the direction, magnitude and character of the changes which
have cane out of the Ad Hoc Subcommittee's review.

Further discussion brought out that the major issues which need to be re-
solved in EDAC and which, therefore, should be discussed in the Executive Com-
mittee are as follows:

(1) Criteria

(2) Administrative principles

(3) Changed concepts of List II and List III

The Executive Committee will start its discussion on criteria on Tuesday,
November 12, and continue on these and subsequent matters in the above order
in an attempt to present controversial issues to EDAC as soon as possible.
