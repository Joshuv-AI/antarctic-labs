II. OTHER ITEMS ;

1. We have just received a letter, dated 11 July, from
the Armed Forces Staff College allocating two spaces to the
Agency for the Twenty-fifth Class, January - June 1959. We
will prepare a letter of acknowledgement for your signature.

2. We now have 44 applications for Operations No. 7,
only 12 of which are not for JOTs.

In the previous two runnings we had: (1) for Ops #5,
a total of 32 DD/P employees, other than JOTs, in a total of
50; and (2) for Ops #6, a total of 14 DD/P employees, other
than JOTs, in a total of 27. Based on the requirements for
the two previous courses, coupled with the fact that there
will be no other running until possibly August 1959, I feel
the 12 nominations to be low.

I anticipate that, after the Long Term Schedule is
disseminated early next week and it becomes generally known
that Ops #7 will be the last offering for the "general pub-
lic" until a year hence, we will be faced with the problem
of a number of additional applications.
