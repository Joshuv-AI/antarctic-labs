The President to the Ambassador in the Soviet Union (Harriman)[1] (8 November 1943, d196)

secret operational priority

[Washington,] 8 November 1943.

For Ambassador Harriman from the President.

My 041707,[2] your 06100.[3]

You are hereby authorized to attach our agreement regarding Turkey to the protocol with the following change.

Change the words “Commanders responsible” to the words “Combined Chiefs of Staff.”[4]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, Moscow, via Navy channels. A copy was sent to the Department of State for its information.
[2] Telegram of November 4, 1943; see ante, p. 151, footnote 1.
[3] Telegram of November 6, 1943, ante, p. 158. The date-time group was garbled.
[4] See Churchill’s telegram 493, November 6, 1943, to Roosevelt, ante, p. 156.
