Correction for Huang et al., A magnesium efflux transporter required for seed development and eating quality in rice
Published: 2026

Correction for “A magnesium efflux transporter required for seed development and eating quality in rice,” by Sheng Huang, Kiyosumi Hori, Naoki Yamaji, Yuma Yoshioka, Min Ning, Yu Nagaya, Takaaki Miyaji, Namiki Mitani-Ueno, Shin-ichiro Inoue, June-Sik Kim, Miho Kashino, and Jian Feng Ma, which published April 22, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2536813123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2536813123).

The authors note that the legends for SI Appendix, Figs. S3–S5 appeared incorrectly: “We misplaced the figure legends for SI Appendix, Figs. S3–S5. The legend for S5 should be moved to S3, S3 to S4, and S4 to S5.” The SI Appendix has been corrected online.
