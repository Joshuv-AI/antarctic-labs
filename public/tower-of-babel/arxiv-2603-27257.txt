Particle-antiparticle perturbation mode horizon crossing: baryogenesis, leptogenesis and magnetogenesis
She-Sheng Xue

Abstract
During the reheating epoch, gravitationally produced massive particle-antiparticle pairs undergo quantum oscillation. Perturbations in their relative densities cross out the horizon, leading to an asymmetry of particles and antiparticles inside the horizon. Massive particles decay into baryons and leptons, thereby explaining baryogenesis and leptogenesis, whose charged components must generate a nontrivial electric current, thereby producing a primordial magnetic field (magnetogenesis). As a result, the baryon (lepton) number-to-entropy ratio and the primordial magnetic field bounds are consistent with observational data. We also discuss the asymmetry of dark matter and anti-dark matter.

5
Summary and remarks

The article shows that, during the reheating epoch, massive particle-antiparticle perturbation modes that superhorizon-crossing lead to their asymmetry inside the horizon, and their subsequent decay into light particles produce baryogenesis and leptogenesis, thereby creating a non-vanishing electric current that accounts for magnetogenesis. These are initial asymmetric conditions persisting in subsequent standard cosmological evolution. If these initial asymmetric conditions
had been created during the inflation epoch, they would have
been erased by the inflation.

The massive particle
X
and antiparticle
\bar{X}
theory respects the fundamental CPT symmetry, and net
X
particle numbers are zero, so the baryon and lepton numbers are zero
B=L=0
. After the superhorizon crossing, the excess particles
X
decay to light particles, obeying the
B-L
symmetry, which results in the same amounts
n^{R}_{{}_{B}}=n^{R}_{{}_{L}}
(
3.10

The
\beta
-equilibrium in the reheating epoch
leads to comparable amounts
n^{R}_{{}_{B_{c}}}=n^{R}_{{}_{B_{0}}}
(
3.11
) of charged and neutral baryogenesis. The total charge neutrality
requires the same amounts
n^{R}_{{}_{B_{c}}}=n^{R}_{{}_{L_{c}}}
(
3.13
) of charged baryogenesis and leptogenesis. Therefore, neutral baryogenesis and leptogenesis are comparable (
3.14
), which is about half of the baryogenesis (leptogenesis).

In massive particles
X
decay to baryons
and leptons, their different kinematics and energy-momentum conservation establish a nontrivial electric current that creates a primordial magnetic field.
Thus, baryogenesis, leptogenesis and magnetogenesis during the reheating epoch are closely related.
Using the

[

and
[

Further studies are required to constrain the range (
