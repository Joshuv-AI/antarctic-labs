No. 1354 The Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret

London, July 20, 1945—noon.

7304. Embassy understands Foreign Office position unchanged by French proposals (re Paris 525, July 18 to London[1]) British still unwilling proceed prior conclusion Big Three Meeting. Should meeting end in time, and result in concurrence Soviet request as is expected, British will make counter-proposal Tangier Conference to open August 6 instead August 3.

Sent Dept as 7304; repeated Paris 467, Madrid 192, Tangier 45.

Winant

Source: 881.00/7–2045: Telegram

Notes
[1] Document No. 1350.
