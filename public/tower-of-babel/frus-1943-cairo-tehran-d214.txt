Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (12 November 1943, d214)

secret

London, 12 November 1943.

No. 503. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt personal and most secret.

Para 1. Reference your No. 417 of 11th November.[2]

Para 2. I have immediately telegraphed to General Auchinleck to render every possible assistance in the constriction [construction] of the four air bases.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington by the American Embassy, London, apparently via military channels, and forwarded by the White House Map Room by pouch on November 15, 1943; received by Roosevelt November 20 at Oran.
[2] Message sent from Washington, November 10, 1943; ante, p. 172.
