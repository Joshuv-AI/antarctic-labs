2. NO DISTINCTION BETWEEN POLITICAL AND CRIMINAL PRISONERS IN
FILLING OUT THE RESERVE UNITS WAS CONTEMPLATED. BOTH TYPES COULD
BE EMPLOYED. THE SEPARATION OF POLITICAL PRISONERS FROM CRIMINAL
TYPES WAS BECOMING LESS EXACT IN THE LATE 1970S. SOME POLITICAL
DISSIDENTS WERE CHARGED WITH CRIMES AND HANDLED LIKE COMMON PRISONERS.
THE RESERVE UNITS MERELY EXPECTED PRISONER SUPPORT NO MATTER HOW THE
SYSTEM CATEGORIZED THE INDIVIDUALS.

3. AN ESTIMATED 10 PERCENT OF THE APPROXIMATELY 4,300 WORKERS
EMPLOYED IN THE OFFSHORE CASPIAN SEA NEFTIANYE KAMNI OIL FIELD WERE
RELEASED PRISONERS RECLASSIFIED AS EXILES WHO WERE RESTRICTED
TO THE IMMEDIATE JURISDICTION AND LIMITED TO CERTAIN JOBS. THE
EXILES PLAYED AN ESPECIALLY IMPORTANT ROLE IN CONTINUITY BECAUSE
THE OTHER WORKERS TENDED TO ARRANGE FOR PROMOTION OR TRANSFERRED
TO OTHER LOCATIONS. THE EXILES PERFORMED SKILLEO AND UNSKILLED
WORK. MANY WERE WELDERS, THE HIGHEST POSITION AVAILABLE TO THEM.
