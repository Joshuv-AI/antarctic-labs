English and Hebrew author; born 1803; died in London, Dec. 17, 1890. He published: (1) "The Autobiography of a Jewish Gentleman" (1860); (2) "Autumn Gatherings," a collection of prose and poetry (London, 1866); (3) "Ha-Nistarot weha-Niglot" (The Secret and Revealed Things); (4) "Curiosities of Judaism: Facts, Opinions, Anecdotes, and Remarks Relative to the Hebrew Nation" (London, 1879).

Bibliography: Jew. Chron. Dec. 19, 1890, p. 9; Allibone, Dictionary of English Literature, supplement, vol. i., Philadelphia, 1891. B. B.
