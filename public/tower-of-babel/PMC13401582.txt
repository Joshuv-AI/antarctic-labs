In the original publication of this article [1], Fig. 3 has been published incorrectly. The incorrect and correct Fig. 3 are shown in this correction article.

Correct:

MSN knockout suppresses the proliferation, induces the apoptosis and improves the radiosensitivity ofGBM cells. a The expression level of MSN in radioresistant U87 and parental U87 cells detected by western blot. G1,G2 and G3 represent the first, second and third generations of cells, respectively. b The verification of MSN-KO-U87cells using western blot. c The effects of MSN knockout for 24 h (i), 48 h (ii) and 72 h (iii) on the proliferation of U87cells by CCK-8 assay. d The effect of MSN knockout on the apoptosis of U87 cells by flow cytometry (i) and thestatistical graph of apoptosis rates (ii). e The effect of MSN knockout on the radioresistance of U87 cells by colonyformation assay. f Cell survival curves were fitted by single-hit multitarget model (i) and linear quadratic model (ii) based on (e). *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01 and ***P < 0.001
