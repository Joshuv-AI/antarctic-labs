Quot spaces in tilted hearts and Hall algebra modules
Niccolò Giacomini

Abstract
We construct a two–sided categorical action of the Hall algebra of semistable coherent sheaves of fixed slope on a curve X X on the derived category of certain Quot–spaces in tilted hearts on X X . Following the philosophy in [ 9 ] , the action is induced by correspondence stacks that parameterize extensions of such quotients by semistable sheaves. In the process, we compare different moduli spaces on X X : Quot–spaces, Bradlow pairs, and stable pairs in the sense of [ 9 ] .
