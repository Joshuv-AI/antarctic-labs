1.0 Purpose

The Corrective Action Unit (CAU) 578 Closure Report (CR) was published in January 2024
(DOE/EMNV, 2024). The purpose of this addendum is to provide the waste disposal documentation
for waste generated during the corrective action investigation (CAI).

1.1 Waste Disposal Documentation

The CAU 578 CR was published before the disposal of the investigation-derived waste generated
during the CAI. As stated in Appendix E of the CAU 578 CR, the waste disposal documentation for
wastes generated during the CAI is provided in this addendum. The Uniform Hazardous Waste
Manifest is presented in Appendix A. The lead removed from CAU 578 is listed on Page 1,
Section 9b, line item #3; the certified receipt signature is on Page 1, Section 20.
