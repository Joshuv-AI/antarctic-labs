((TEXT)) SAN SALVADOR, 19 NOV (ACAN - EFE) -- THE FUNERAL SERVICE
OF THE SIX JESUITS ASSASSINATED ON 16 NOVEMBER BEGAN HERE THIS

CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY WHERE THE SLAIN JESUITS WORKED AND WHERE
THEY WILL BE BURIED.

THE CEREMONY IS BEING OFFICIATED BY ARCHBISHOP ARTURO RIVERA
DAMAS.

THE CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY IS SURROUNDED BY SALVADORAN ARMY
TROOPS.
