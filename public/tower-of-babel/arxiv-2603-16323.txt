Puzzles in charmed baryon semileptonic decays
Chao-Qiang Geng 1 , Chia-Wei Liu 1 , Sheng-Lin Liu 1,2,3

Abstract
Large apparent S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} breaking and a tension with lattice QCD have recently emerged in semileptonic decays of singly charmed baryons, hinting at either an unexpected hadronic mechanism or new physics. We formulate a framework that systematically matches lattice-QCD inputs onto an S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} analysis including first-order symmetry breaking. We predict ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Σ 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ν ℓ ) / ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Ξ 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ν ℓ ) = ( 2.6 ± 0.3 ) % , {\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Sigma^{0}\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})/{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{0}\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})=(2.6\pm 0.3)\%, and ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Λ 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ν ℓ ) / ℬ ⁡ ( Ξ c + → Ξ 0 ​ ℓ + ​ ν ℓ ) = ( 1.1 ± 0.1 ) % , {\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda^{0}\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})/{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{0}\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell})=(1.1\pm 0.1)\%, whose precise measurements would cleanly determine the origin of the anomalously large S ​ U ​ ( 3 ) F SU(3)_{F} breaking.

Formalism

The transitions of
{\bf B}_{c}\to{\bf B}
induced by a current are best described by form factors, where
{\bf B}_{c}
and
{\bf B}
stand for the charmed baryon and the octet baryon, respectively.
In this work, we adopt the parameterization of helicity form factors with definitions in

Bourrely:2008za

The

BCL

-expansion
Bourrely:2008za

\displaystyle f\left(q^{2}\right)=\frac{1}{1-q^{2}/\left(m_{\mathrm{pole}}^{f}\right)^{2}}\sum_{n=0}^{n_{\max}}a_{n}^{f}\left[z_{f}\left(q^{2}\right)\right]^{n},

\displaystyle z_{f}\left(q^{2}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{t_{+}^{f}-q^{2}}-\sqrt{t_{+}^{f}-t_{0}}}{\sqrt{t_{+}^{f}-q^{2}}+\sqrt{t_{+}^{f}-t_{0}}}.

(1)

\displaystyle f=f_{+},f_{\perp},f_{0},g_{+},g_{\perp},g_{0},

with

q=p_{{\bf B}_{c}}-p_{{\bf B}}
and

c\to d(s)

To

\displaystyle a_{2}^{f_{0}}\!=\!\frac{a_{0}^{f_{+}}}{z_{f_{0}}(0)^{2}}+\frac{a_{1}^{f_{+}}-a_{1}^{f_{0}}}{z_{f_{0}}(0)}+a_{2}^{f_{+}}+(a_{3}^{f_{+}}-a_{3}^{f_{0}})z_{f_{0}}(0),

(2)

\displaystyle a_{2}^{g_{0}}\!=\!\frac{a_{0}^{g_{+}}}{z_{g_{0}}(0)^{2}}+\frac{a_{1}^{g_{+}}-a_{1}^{g_{0}}}{z_{g_{0}}(0)}+a_{2}^{g_{+}}+(a_{3}^{g_{+}}-a_{3}^{g_{0}})z_{g_{0}}(0),

IV
Conclusions

We have developed an
SU(3)_{F}
analysis of singly charmed baryon semileptonic decays by matching the available lattice-QCD form factors onto the BCL
z
-expansion, including first-order symmetry-breaking effects. The resulting framework consistently reproduces the known lattice inputs and yields symmetry-breaking corrections of the expected size.

With these inputs, we have predicted the form factors and decay observables for
\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Lambda\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell}
and
\Xi_{c}\to\Sigma\ell^{+}\nu_{\ell}
. In particular, we identified the ratios

(R_{\Sigma^{-}},R_{\Sigma^{0}},R_{\Lambda})=(5.2\pm 0.6,\;2.6\pm 0.3,\;1.1\pm 0.1)\%\,.

These ratios are free from the normalization ambiguity associated with
{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{0}\to\Xi^{-}\pi^{+})
and
{\cal B}(\Xi_{c}^{+}\to\Xi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{+})
. They therefore provide clean probes of the first-order
SU(3)_{F}
breaking.

We have also shown that the corresponding signal yields should be experimentally measurable at Belle with the expected statistical precisions of

(\delta R_{\Sigma^{0}},\delta R_{\Lambda})\simeq(4.0,\;1.3)\times 10^{-3}
.
Precise determinations would offer a decisive test of the origin of the current puzzle.

in powers of
z_{f}
, we obtain formally

\tilde{z}_{f}=d_{0}+d_{1}z_{f}+d_{2}z_{f}^{2}+d_{3}z_{f}^{3}+{\cal O}(z_{f}^{4})\,,

(7)

where
d_{n}
is defined by

d_{n}=\frac{1}{n!}\frac{\partial^{n}}{\partial z_{f}^{n}}\tilde{z}_{f}=\frac{1}{n!}\left(\frac{\partial q^{2}}{\partial z_{f}}\frac{\partial}{\partial q^{2}}\right)^{n}\tilde{z}_{f}\,.

(8)

The explicit form is too lengthy to be informative and can be calculated by a modern computer program.

We denote the BCL coefficients in the expansion in
\tilde{z}_{f}
by
\tilde{a}_{n}^{f}
, and impose the matching condition

\sum_{n}a_{n}^{f}z_{f}^{n}=\sum_{n}\tilde{a}_{n}^{f}\tilde{z}_{f}^{n}\,.

(9)

This leads to the relations

\displaystyle a_{0}^{f}=

\displaystyle\tilde{a}_{0}^{f}+\tilde{a}_{1}^{f}d_{0}^{f}+\tilde{a}_{2}^{f}(d_{0}^{f})^{2}+\tilde{a}_{3}^{f}(d_{0}^{f})^{3}\,,

(10)

\displaystyle a_{1}^{f}=

\displaystyle\tilde{a}_{1}^{f}d_{1}^{f}+2\tilde{a}_{2}^{f}d_{0}^{f}d_{1}^{f}+3\tilde{a}_{3}^{f}(d_{0}^{f})^{2}d_{1}^{f}\,,

\displaystyle a_{2}^{f}=

\displaystyle\tilde{a}_{1}^{f}d_{2}^{f}+\tilde{a}_{2}^{f}\!\left[(d_{1}^{f})^{2}+2d_{0}^{f}d_{2}^{f}\right]

\displaystyle+\tilde{a}_{3}^{f}\!\left[3d_{0}^{f}(d_{1}^{f})^{2}+3(d_{0}^{f})^{2}d_{2}^{f}\right]\,,

\displaystyle a_{3}^{f}=

\displaystyle\tilde{a}_{1}^{f}d_{3}^{f}+2\tilde{a}_{2}^{f}\!\left(d_{0}^{f}d_{3}^{f}+d_{1}^{f}d_{2}^{f}\right)

\displaystyle+\tilde{a}_{3}^{f}\!\left[(d_{1}^{f})^{3}+6d_{0}^{f}d_{1}^{f}d_{2}^{f}+3(d_{0}^{f})^{2}d_{3}^{f}\right]\,,

up to the precision of
|z_{f}^{4}(q^{2})|<10^{-3}.

We note that
\tilde{a}_{n}^{f}
is denoted simply by
a_{n}^{f}
in Refs.
Meinel:2016dqj
;
Meinel:2017ggx
. The above relations therefore allow us to match their parametrization to the one in this work.
