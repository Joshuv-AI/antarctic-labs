No. 568 The Chargé in Yugoslavia (Shantz) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret

Belgrade, June 23, 1945—8 p.m.

197. Referring to Tito’s published remark that problem of civil govt of Venezia Giulia is still open (Embtel 109 [188], June 22[1]) I suggest that US and UK govts instruct Belgrade Embassies to remind Yugo Govt that question was settled by Article 3 of Belgrade Agreements and that attempts to stir up trouble in area under AMG is unlikely to benefit Yugos position.

Shantz

Source: 740.00119 Control (Italy)/6–2345: Telegram
