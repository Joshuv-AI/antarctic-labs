Chief rabbi and preacher of Pfersee, Bavaria; lived at the end of the seventeenth century. His sermons for the festivals of Passover, Pentecost, and Tabernacles were published with those of his father, Enoch ben Abraham , under the title of "Reshit Bikkurim" (Frankfort-on-the-Main, 1708). A collection of his responsa was published by his son Enoch ben Judah under the title "Ḥinnuk Bet Yehudah" ( ib. 1709), in which are included some responsa of Judah's father, as well as of Enoch ben Judah himself.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. ii. 38; Wolf, Bibl. Hebr. iii., No. 742c. S. S. M. Sel.
