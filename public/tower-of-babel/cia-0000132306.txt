Recent Indian Moves on the Gulf

New Delhi has set up a government committee to identify
reconstruction project opportunities for Indian firms in Iraq,
Saudi Arabia, and Kuwait, according to press. Many Indian
companias have long-standing connections in the Gulf that they
will use to bid for sub-contracting jobs. KeESse
