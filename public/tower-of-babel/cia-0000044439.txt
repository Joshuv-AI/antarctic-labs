((TEXT)) SAN SALVADOR, 19 NOV (EFE) == SALVADORAN PRESIDENT
ALFREDO CRISTIANI STATED TODAY THAT THE ARMED FORCES HIGH COMMAND
"DOES NOT DISMISS" THE POSSIBILITY THAT THOSE WHO INSTIGATED THE
ASSASSINATION OF THE SIX JESUITE ON 16 NOVEMBER COULD WAVE BEEN
MILITARY.

"THE HIGH COMMAND MEMBERS DO NOT DISSNISS THE POSSIBILITY THAT
THE PERPETRATORS COULD BEEN ARMY NENBERS, AND ARE READY TO GET TO THE
BOTTOM OF THINGS SO THAT THEY WILL BE PUNISHED," THE SALVADORAN
PRESIDENT SAID IN STATENENTS TO EFE AND OTHER NEDIA.

CRISTIANI, WHO INSISTED ON THE NECESSITY OF "NOT JUMPING TO HASTY
CONCLUSIONS," ADDED THAT "THE ARMED FORCES DO NOT WANT THE INSTITUTE,
PER SE, TO SUFFER THE CONSEQUENCES OF WHAT ITS MEMBERS DO AS
INDIVIDUALS."

ON 16 NOVENBER SIX JESUIT PRIESTS, FIVE OF THEN SPANIARDS, WERE
ABSASSINATED BY UNIFORMED NEN WHO NACHINEGUNNED THEM IN THEIR
RESIDENCE THAT NORNING, WHILE A RIGID CURFEW WAS IN EFFECT IN EL
SALVADOR.

THE JESUITS, WHO HAD LIVED IN EL SALVADOR FOR MANY YEARS, WERE
STANCHK CRITICS OF THE MILITARY REGINES AND OF THE EXTREXE RIGHTIST
SECTORS OF THE COUNTRY.

ACCORDING TO CRISTIANI, "THE ARNED FORCES HIGH COMMAND, OF WHICH
THE PRESIDENT IS A PART, ARE TOTALLY DETERMINED TO INVESTIGATE AND
CLEAR UP THE ABER

ais INTERVIEWED SHORTLY AFTER NEETING WITH INOCENCIO ARIAS, SPANISH
UNDER SECRETARY OF FOREIGN RELATIONS, CRISTIANI ASKED THAT SPAIN, THE
UNITED STATES, AND GREAT BRITAIN PARTICIPATE IN THE INVESTIGATION OF
THE ASSASSINATION "BY SENDING EXPERTS.

THE SALVADORAN PRESIDENT DESCRIBED THE ATTITUDE OF THOSE PERSONS
IN THE UNITED STATES WHO HAVE HINTED AT THE POSSIBILITY OF SUSPENDING
THE $1 MILLION DAILY AID GIVEN TO EL SALVADOR AS “VERY SIMPLISTIC."

“J TRUST THAT THIS WILL NOT BE SO AND I HAVE INFORMATION THAT THE
LARGE MAJORITY OF U.S. POLITICAL LEADERS DO NOT FEEL THAT WAY,” THE
PRESIDENT ADDED.

THUS FAR, THE AUTOPSIES PERFORNED ON THE JESUITS BY THREE U.S.
PATHOLOGISTS “ARE NOT CONCLUSIVE IN DETERMININING GUILT," THE
PRESIDENT ADDED.
