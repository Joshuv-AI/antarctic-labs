Retraction Note: A diagnostic model for sepsis using an integrated machine learning framework approach and its therapeutic drug discovery
Authors: Wuping Zhang, Hanping Shi, Jie Peng
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Infectious Diseases (2025) 25:219

10.1186/s12879-025-10616-z

The Editor has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the development and validation of the reported machine learning diagnostic model, including potential overfitting and possible data leakage that may have inflated estimates of model performance. Post-publication assessment concluded that these methodological issues substantially affect the reliability and generalisability of the reported diagnostic performance and the proposed diagnostic utility of the model. As a result, the Editor no longer has confidence in the article’s conclusions regarding the model’s ability to accurately diagnose sepsis.

All authors disagree with this retraction.
