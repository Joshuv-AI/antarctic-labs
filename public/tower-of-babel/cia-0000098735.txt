1. (Bernd Grabowski report: ‘'University Adopts New Name’ ’‘]

2. [Excerpt] Wilhelm-Pieck University will in the future simply be
called Rostock University. It was named after the first president of
the GDR on 8 January 1978 by a resolution of the then Council of
Ministers. At the beginning of this month, 79.2 percent of the
scientists, staff, and students who participated in the ballot, voted
in favor of the change. However, only 55.1 percent of those entitled
to vote actually participated in the ballot. [passage omitted]
