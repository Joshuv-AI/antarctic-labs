Editorial Expression of Concern: Estrogen receptor β activation impairs mitochondrial oxidative metabolism and affects malignant mesothelioma cell growth in vitro and in vivo
Authors: A. G. Manente, D. Valenti, G. Pinton, P. V. Jithesh, A. Daga, L. Rossi, S. G. Gray, K. J. O’Byrne, D. A. Fennell, R. A. Vacca, S. Nilsson, L. Mutti, L. Moro
Published: 2026

Editorial Expression of Concern to: Oncogenesis 10.1038/oncsis.2013.32, published online 23 September 2013

The Editor-in-Chief would like to alert readers that concerns were raised regarding potential splicing between lanes 2 and 3 of ERβ/High ERβ panel of Figure 1b and between lanes 3 and 4 of the ERβ/cytosol panel of Figure 3a. The authors have stated that the data are no longer available due to the age of the study. Therefore, readers are advised to interpret the data listed above with caution.

L Moro, S G Gray, R A Vacca, and G Pinton agree with this Editorial Expression of Concern.
