Quantitative asymptotic regularity and T T -asymptotic regularity for the inexact generalized Halpern iteration
Nicoleta Dumitru, Laurenţiu Leuştean, [2mm] LOS, Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Bucharest

Abstract
We apply proof mining techniques to obtain quantitative and qualitative results on asymptotic and T T -asymptotic regularity for the inexact generalized Halpern iteration, a viscosity-type extension of an iteration recently studied by Kanzow and Shehu. Specializing our results to the Kanzow–Shehu iteration and the sequential averaging method (SAM) yields analogous results for these iterations. Furthermore, we compute rates of ( T T -)asymptotic regularity for particular choices of the parameter sequences, and for one of them, we obtain linear rates as an application of a lemma due to Sabach and Shtern. Keywords: Rates of ( T T -)asymptotic regularity; Generalized Halpern iteration; Nonexpansive mappings; Viscosity-type extension; Proof mining. Mathematics Subject Classification 2010 : 47H05, 47H09, 47J25, 03F10.

Introduction

Let
X
be a Banach space, and let
T:X\to X
be a nonexpansive mapping with fixed points. Recently, Kanzow and Shehu
[
8
, Section 4]

introduced the following very general iteration:

x_{0}\in X,\qquad x_{n+1}=\delta_{n}u+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n},

(1)

where
u\in X
,
(r_{n})\subseteq X
is a sequence of residuals, and
(\alpha_{n})
,
(\beta_{n})
,
(\delta_{n})
are sequences in
[0,1]
satisfying

\alpha_{n}+\beta_{n}+\delta_{n}\leq 1
for all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
.

In this paper, we study the asymptotic behaviour of the iteration

x_{0}\in X,\qquad x_{n+1}=\delta_{n}f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n},

(2)

which we refer to as the
inexact generalized Halpern iteration
, where
f:X\to X
is a
\rho
-contraction for some
\rho\in[0,1)
.
If
f
is a constant mapping, then
(x_{n})
reduces to the iteration of Kanzow and Shehu (
1
). Hence
the inexact generalized Halpern iteration can be viewed as a viscosity-type extension (in the sense of
[
1
,
17
]
)
of the Kanzow–Shehu iteration.
The special case obtained by setting
r_{n}=0
for all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
in (
2
) will be referred to as the
generalized Halpern iteration
. Moreover, if we set, for all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
,
\alpha_{n}=0
,
\beta_{n}=1-\delta_{n}
, and

r_{n}=0
in (
2
), we obtain a viscosity version of the Halpern iteration studied by Xu
[
21
]
in Banach spaces and later by Sabach and Shtern
[
19
]
in Hilbert spaces,
who called it the sequential averaging method (SAM).

Let us recall that the sequence
(x_{n})
is said
to be asymptotically regular if

\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert=0,

and
T
-asymptotically regular
if

\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left\lVert x_{n}-Tx_{n}\right\rVert=0.

A rate of asymptotic regularity of
(x_{n})
is a rate of convergence to
0
of
\left(\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert\right)
, while a
rate of
T
-asymptotic regularity of
(x_{n})
is a rate of convergence to
0
of
\left(\left\lVert x_{n}-Tx_{n}\right\rVert\right)
.
If
\Phi:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}
is a rate of (
T
-)asymptotic regularity of
(x_{n})
, we say that
(x_{n})
is (
T
-)asymptotically regular with rate
\Phi
.

Our main results are Theorems
4.1
and
4.2
, which provide uniform rates of asymptotic regularity and
T
-asymptotic regularity for the inexact generalized Halpern iteration

(x_{n})
, under suitable assumptions on the parameter sequences
(\alpha_{n})
,
(\beta_{n})
,
(\delta_{n})
,
(r_{n})
. These theorems are obtained by extending to
this iteration the proof mining methods developed in
[
15
,
10
,
13
]
for the Halpern iteration. We refer, for example, to
[
9
,
11
]
for further details on
the program of proof mining. Recently, in
[
7
]
, the authors obtained rates of (
T
-)asymptotic regularity for the generalized Krasnoselskii-Mann-type iteration
also studied by Kanzow and Shehu in
[
8
, Section 3]
. However, the proof mining techniques and the quantitative hypotheses on the parameter sequences from

[
7
]
differ from those used in the present paper. As an immediate consequence of our quantitative results we obtain Theorem
4.3
,
a new asymptotic regularity and
T
-asymptotic regularity theorem for
(x_{n})
. By specializing our results to
the Kanzow–Shehu iteration and the sequential averaging method (SAM), we obtain new quantitative and qualitative (
T
-)asymptotic regularity results for these iterations as well.

Finally, in Section
5
, we compute rates of (
T
-)asymptotic regularity for two particular choices of
the parameter sequences satisfying the quantitative hypotheses of our main theorems.
Furthermore, we give a third example for which linear rates are obtained as an
application of a well-known lemma on sequences of real numbers due to Sabach and Shtern
[
19
]
.

Quantitative notions and lemmas

Let
(a_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}

\varphi:{\mathbb{N}}\to{\mathbb{N}}

If

\forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\,\forall n\geq\varphi(k)\left(\left|a_{n}-a\right|\leq\frac{1}{k+1}\right),

\forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\;\forall n\geq\varphi(k)\,\forall l\in{\mathbb{N}}\left(\left|a_{n+l}-a_{n}\right|\leq\frac{1}{k+1}\right).

Assume moreover that
(a_{n})\subseteq[0,\infty)
. Then
\varphi
is a Cauchy modulus of the series
\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}

\forall k\in{\mathbb{N}}\,\forall n\geq\varphi(k)\,\forall l\in{\mathbb{N}}^{*}\left(\sum\limits_{i=n+1}^{n+l}a_{i}\leq\frac{1}{k+1}\right).

If
\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}
is divergent, then
\varphi
is a rate of divergence of
\sum\limits_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{n}
if

\sum\limits_{i=0}^{\varphi(n)}a_{i}\geq n
for
all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
.

In the sequel,
X

\rho

(\alpha_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}

(\beta_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}

(\delta_{n})_{n\in{\mathbb{N}}}

[0,1]

\alpha_{n}+\beta_{n}+\delta_{n}\leq 1.

Let

be the inexact generalized Halpern iteration, defined by (

x_{0}\in X,\qquad x_{n+1}=\delta_{n}f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n}.

Lemma 3.1
.

For all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
and
p\in\operatorname{Fix}(T)
,

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n+1}-p\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert+(1-\delta_{n})\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert.

(4)

Proof.

Let
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
and
p\in\operatorname{Fix}(T)
. We get that

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n+1}-p\right\rVert

\displaystyle=\left\lVert\delta_{n}f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n}-p\right\rVert

\displaystyle=\left\lVert\delta_{n}(f(x_{n})-p)+\alpha_{n}(x_{n}-p)+\beta_{n}(Tx_{n}-p)+r_{n}+(\alpha_{n}+\beta_{n}+\delta_{n}-1)p\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert+\alpha_{n}\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert+\beta_{n}\left\lVert Tx_{n}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert+(\alpha_{n}+\beta_{n})\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert

since
\left\lVert Tx_{n}-p\right\rVert=\left\lVert Tx_{n}-Tp\right\rVert\leq\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert
, as
p\in\operatorname{Fix}(T)
and
T
is nonexpasive

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert+(1-\delta_{n})\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert.

∎

Let
p\in\operatorname{Fix}(T)
and define the sequence
(K_{p}^{n})
of non-negative real numbers similar to that in

[
5
, Section 4]
:

K_{p}^{0}=\max\left\{\left\lVert x_{0}-p\right\rVert,\frac{\left\lVert f(p)-p\right\rVert}{1-\rho},\left\lVert p\right\rVert\right\},\quad K_{p}^{n+1}=K_{p}^{n}+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert.

(5)

Lemma 3.2
.

For all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
,

\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert,\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert\leq K_{p}^{n}.

Proof.

The proof is by induction on
n
.

n=0
: We have that, by (
5
),
\left\lVert x_{0}-p\right\rVert\leq K_{p}^{0}
, and, since
f
is a

\rho-
contraction,

\left\lVert f(x_{0})-p\right\rVert\leq\left\lVert f(x_{0})-f(p)\right\rVert+\left\lVert f(p)-p\right\rVert\leq\rho\left\lVert x_{0}-p\right\rVert+\left\lVert f(p)-p\right\rVert\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\eqref{def-kzp-knp}}}{{\leq}}K_{p}^{0}.

n\Rightarrow n+1
: Use (
4
), the induction hypothesis, and (
5
) to get that

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n+1}-p\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})-p\right\rVert+(1-\delta_{n})\left\lVert x_{n}-p\right\rVert+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq K_{p}^{n}+(1-\alpha_{n}-\beta_{n}-\delta_{n})\left\lVert p\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert=K_{p}^{n+1}.

Then
\left\lVert f(x_{n+1})-p\right\rVert\leq K_{p}^{n+1}
follows easily.

3.1
Main technical lemma

Lemma 3.3
.

For all
n\in{\mathbb{N}}
,

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n+2}-x_{n+1}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq(1-(1-\rho)\delta_{n+1})\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert+|\delta_{n+1}-\delta_{n}|\left\lVert f(x_{n})\right\rVert+|\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n}|\left\lVert x_{n}\right\rVert

(6)

\displaystyle\quad+|\beta_{n+1}-\beta_{n}|\left\lVert Tx_{n}\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n+1}-r_{n}\right\rVert,

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n}-Tx_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert+(1-\beta_{n})\left\lVert Tx_{n}\right\rVert+\alpha_{n}\left\lVert x_{n}\right\rVert+\delta_{n}\left\lVert f(x_{n})\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n}\right\rVert.

(7)

Proof.

First note that

\displaystyle x_{n+2}-x_{n+1}

\displaystyle=(\delta_{n+1}f(x_{n+1})+\alpha_{n+1}x_{n+1}+\beta_{n+1}Tx_{n+1}+r_{n+1})

\displaystyle\quad-(\delta_{n}f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n})

\displaystyle=\delta_{n+1}(f(x_{n+1})-f(x_{n}))+(\delta_{n+1}-\delta_{n})f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n+1}(x_{n+1}-x_{n})+(\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n})x_{n}

\displaystyle\quad+\beta_{n+1}(Tx_{n+1}-Tx_{n})+(\beta_{n+1}-\beta_{n})Tx_{n}+(r_{n+1}-r_{n}).

It follows that

\displaystyle\left\lVert x_{n+2}-x_{n+1}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq\delta_{n+1}\left\lVert f(x_{n+1})-f(x_{n})\right\rVert+|\delta_{n+1}-\delta_{n}|\left\lVert f(x_{n})\right\rVert+|\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n}|\left\lVert x_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\quad+\alpha_{n+1}\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert+\beta_{n+1}\left\lVert Tx_{n+1}-Tx_{n}\right\rVert+|\beta_{n+1}-\beta_{n}|\left\lVert Tx_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\quad+\left\lVert r_{n+1}-r_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq(\delta_{n+1}\rho+\alpha_{n+1}+\beta_{n+1})\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert+|\delta_{n+1}-\delta_{n}|\left\lVert f(x_{n})\right\rVert+|\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n}|\left\lVert x_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\quad+|\beta_{n+1}-\beta_{n}|\left\lVert Tx_{n}\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n+1}-r_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\leq(\delta_{n+1}\rho+1-\delta_{n+1})\left\lVert x_{n+1}-x_{n}\right\rVert+|\delta_{n+1}-\delta_{n}|\left\lVert f(x_{n})\right\rVert+|\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n}|\left\lVert x_{n}\right\rVert

\displaystyle\quad+|\beta_{n+1}-\beta_{n}|\left\lVert Tx_{n}\right\rVert+\left\lVert r_{n+1}-r_{n}\right\rVert,

since
\alpha_{n+1}+\beta_{n+1}+\delta_{n+1}\leq 1
, so

\alpha_{n+1}+\beta_{n+1}\leq 1-\delta_{n+1}
.
Thus, (
6
) holds.

Furthermore, we have that

\displaystyle Tx_{n}-x_{n}

\displaystyle=(x_{n+1}-x_{n})+Tx_{n}-x_{n+1}=(x_{n+1}-x_{n})+Tx_{n}-(\delta_{n}f(x_{n})+\alpha_{n}x_{n}+\beta_{n}Tx_{n}+r_{n})

\displaystyle=(x_{n+1}-x_{n})+(1-\beta_{n})Tx_{n}-\alpha_{n}x_{n}-\delta_{n}f(x_{n})-r_{n},

hence (
7
) follows easily.
∎
