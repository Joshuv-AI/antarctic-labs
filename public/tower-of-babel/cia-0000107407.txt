SUBJ: | TELEVISION PROGRAM SUMMARY--SOL 90-212

SOURCE: SEOUL TELEVISION SERVICE IN KOREAN 1200 GMT 2 AUG 90
TEXT:

1. EXTENSIVE REPORT ON IRAQI INVASION OF KUWAIT TODAY. IRAQI ¢
TROOPS OCCUPY ALL TERRITORY OF KUWAIT, REPORT FROM DIRECTOR OF
BRANCH OFFICE OF S. KOREAN BUSINESS GROUP STATIONED IN KUWAIT
EXPLAINING BATTLES, ROK AMBASSADOR IN IRAQ SAYS THAT KOREANS IN IRAQ
ARE SAFE. (6 MIN) (VIDEO)

2. REPORT ON MOVES OF FOREIGN MINISTRY DISCUSSING EVACUATION OF
KOREANS FROM IRAQ AND KUWAIT. (VIDEO; TEXTING)

3. REPORTER REPORTS BACKGROUND OF IRAQI INVASION TO KUWAIT,
INCLUDING REASONS FOR FAILURE OF IRAQ-KUWAIT NEGOTIATIONS. (VIDEO)

4. REPORT FROM WASHINGTON ON U.S. GOVERNMENT REACTION TO IRAQI
INVASTION. (VIDEO)

5. REPORT ON REACTIONS OF BRITISH, FRENCH, JAPANESE, CHINESE
GOVERNMENTS TO IRAQI INVASION. (VIDEO)

6. REPORT FROM NEW YORK ON UN SECURITY COUNCIL SESSION ON IRAQI
INVASION. (VIDEO)

7. STATION REPORTER DETAILS BATTLES IN KUWAIT, INCLUDING
INVASION ROUTES, MILITARY STRENGTH OF TWO COUNTRIES, AIM OF IRAQI
INVASION, INFLUENCE OF IRAQI INVASION IN INTERNATIONAL OIL MARKETS
IN FUTURE. ALSO INTRODUCES PERSONAL BACKGROUND OF IRAQI PRESIDENT.
(VIDEO)

8. INTERVIEW WITH ENERGY AND RESOURCES MINISTER ON GOVRNMENT'S
STEPS TO GET OIL IN THE FUTURE AND GOVERNMENT'S POLICY TO COPE WITH
POSSIBLE DIFFICULTIES CAUSED BY IRAQ-KUWAIT WAR. SAYS THAT DETAILED
GOVERNMENT MEASURES TO BE DISCUSSED TOMORROW. (VIDEO)

9. REPORT ON PRESIDENT NO'S MEETING WITH DLP CADRES TO DISCUSS
MEASURES AT THE NEXT ASSEMBLY SESSION. (VIDEO)

10. REPORT ON UNIFICATION BOARD MINISTER'S PRESS CONFERENCE HELD
THIS MORNING. CITES GOVERNMENT'S CRITERIA FOR SELECTING THOSE WHO
CAN BE ALLOWED TO VISIT NORTH KOREA AND PROCEDURES FOR
APPLICATION. (VIDEO)

11. REPORT ON MOVES OF INFORMATION MINISTRY FOR FOUNDING PRIVATE
TV STATION. (VIDEO)
