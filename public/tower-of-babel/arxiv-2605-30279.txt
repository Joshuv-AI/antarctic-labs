A note on Azumaya algebras and one-forms
Siqing Zhang

Abstract
. The crystalline differential operators on a smooth variety X X give rise to a non-split Azumaya algebra over the cotangent bundle of the Frobenius twist X ′ X^{\prime} . In some cases, this Azumaya algebra splits when restricted to finite covers of X ′ X^{\prime} . In this short note, we show that, whenever X X has a non-closed global one-form, there is a degree one cover of X ′ X^{\prime} on which the Azumaya algebra does not split, answering a question of Sasha Petrov.



1.
The Question

Throughout
k
is an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
.
Let
X
be a smooth proper variety over
k
, and let
X^{\prime}
be its
Frobenius twist. By Bezrukavnikov–Mirkovic–Rumynin
[
1
, Theorem
2.2.3]
, the sheaf of crystalline differential operators on
X
defines
an Azumaya algebra
\mathcal{A}_{X}
on the cotangent bundle
T^{*}X^{\prime}
.

In many useful cases, especially in the characteristic-
p
geometric
Langlands and non-abelian Hodge Theory for curves

[
2
,
5
,
3
]
, and for abelian varieties

[
6
]
, this Azumaya algebra splits when
restricted to spectral varieties; compare also the
p
-adic analogue

[
7
]
.
Sasha Petrov asked the following question:

Question 1
.

[
10
, Question 7.3(1)]

Does there exist a smooth proper variety
X
together with a smooth
subvariety
Z\subset T^{*}X^{\prime}
, finite over
X^{\prime}
, such that

\mathcal{A}_{X}|_{Z}
does not split?

In this note, we answer this question affirmatively in every positive characteristic.

2.
The Answer

Let

X^{\prime}:=X\times_{k,F_{k}}k

be the Frobenius twist.
Write
F_{X}=F_{X/k}\colon X\to X^{\prime}
for the relative
Frobenius, and
\pi_{X}\colon X^{\prime}\to X
for the projection.
Milne’s exact sequence, in the form used by Ogus–Vologodsky

[
11
, (4.1.1)]
, is the following exact sequence of étale sheaves of
abelian groups on
X^{\prime}
:

(1)

0\longrightarrow\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}^{*}\longrightarrow F_{X,*}\mathcal{O}_{X}^{*}\xrightarrow{d\log}F_{X,*}Z^{1}_{X/k}\xrightarrow{\pi_{X}^{*}-C_{X}}\Omega^{1}_{X^{\prime}/k}\longrightarrow 0.

Here
Z^{1}_{X/k}\subset\Omega^{1}_{X/k}
is the sheaf of closed one-forms and

C_{X}
is the Cartier operator. Splice the exact sequence and the connecting morphisms define the obstruction homomorphism

(2)

\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}\colon H^{0}(X^{\prime},\Omega^{1}_{X^{\prime}/k})\to H^{2}_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X^{\prime},\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}^{*}).

Let
\mathcal{D}_{X/k}
be the sheaf of crystalline differential operators on
X
.
The center
of
F_{X,*}\mathcal{D}_{X/k}
is identified with

\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}}T_{X^{\prime}/k}
, and

F_{X,*}\mathcal{D}_{X/k}
is identified with an Azumaya algebra on
T^{*}X^{\prime}
. This is the algebra
denoted
\mathcal{A}_{X}
above.

Lemma 2
.

Let
X/k
be smooth, and let

\omega\in H^{0}(X^{\prime},\Omega^{1}_{X^{\prime}/k})
. Let

i_{\omega}\colon X^{\prime}\longrightarrow T^{*}X^{\prime}

be the section corresponding to
\omega
, with graph

\Gamma_{\omega}\subset T^{*}X^{\prime}
. Then

[i_{\omega}^{*}\mathcal{A}_{X}]=\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}(\omega)\hskip 10.00002pt\text{in }H^{2}_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(X^{\prime},\mathcal{O}_{X^{\prime}}^{*}).

In particular, if
\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}(\omega)\neq 0
, then

\mathcal{A}_{X}|_{\Gamma_{\omega}}
does not split.

Proof.

Ogus–Vologodsky identify
\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}(\omega)
with the class of
the
\mathbb{G}_{m}
-gerbe of line bundles on
X
with integrable connection and

p
-curvature
\omega

[
11
, Proposition 4.2]
. The same gerbe is the gerbe of splittings of the restriction of

\mathcal{A}_{X}
to the section
i_{\omega}
by
[
11
, Remark 4.3]
.
∎

The following proposition reduces Question
1
to finding a variety with non-closed global 1-forms.

Proposition 3
.

Let
X/k
be a smooth proper connected variety. If not every global
one-form on
X
is closed, i.e.

H^{0}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k})\subsetneq H^{0}(X,\Omega^{1}_{X/k}),

then the obstruction map

\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}
as in (
2
)
is nonzero.

Proof.

Let
G:=\Pic^{\nabla,\mathrm{rig}}_{X/k}

be the rigidified Picard scheme of line bundles with integrable
connection. Let
V:=H^{0}(X^{\prime},\Omega^{1}_{X^{\prime}/k})
, viewed as a vector group.
By
[
11
, Proposition 4.11 and the paragraph following it]
, taking
p
-curvature defines a morphism of group schemes

\psi\colon G\longrightarrow V.

By
[
11
, Proposition 4.2]
,
\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}(\omega)=0
if and only if
\omega\in\psi(G(k))
. Thus it is enough to show that

\psi(G(k))\neq V(k)
.

By Cartier descent,

\Pic_{X^{\prime}/k}\longrightarrow G,\hskip 20.00003ptM\longmapsto(F_{X}^{*}M,\nabla_{\mathrm{can}})

identifies
\Pic_{X^{\prime}/k}
with
\ker(\psi)
; see
[
8
, Theorem 5.1]
.
Forgetting the connection fits into the exact sequence of fppf sheaves

0\to H^{0}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k})\to G\xrightarrow{b}\Pic_{X/k}\to H^{1}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k}),

as in
[
11
, Proposition 4.11]
. In particular, if
G^{0}
is the neutral
component of
G
, then

\dim G^{0}\leq h^{0}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k})+\dim\Pic^{0}_{X/k}.

Let
\operatorname{Im}(\psi|_{G^{0}})
be the algebraic subgroup image.
The quotient morphism
G^{0}\to\operatorname{Im}(\psi|_{G^{0}})
is
faithfully flat. Therefore

\dim\operatorname{Im}(\psi|_{G^{0}})=\dim G^{0}-\dim\ker(\psi|_{G^{0}})\leq h^{0}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k})<\dim V.

Finally, passing from
G^{0}
to
G
only gives finitely many translates:
Let
G_{1}=b^{-1}(\Pic^{0}_{X/k})
. Since
G_{1}
is of finite type,

G_{1}/G^{0}
is finite. Moreover
G(k)/G_{1}(k)
injects into
\NS(X)
, so
its image under
\psi
is finite:
\NS(X)
is finitely generated, whereas

V(k)
is killed by
p
.

∎

Theorem 4
.

Let
X/k
be a smooth proper connected variety.
If not every global one-form on
X
is closed, then there is a smooth subvariety
Z\subset T^{*}X^{\prime}
, finite over
X^{\prime}
, such that
\mathcal{A}_{X}|_{Z}
does not split. Moreover, the finite morphism
Z\to X^{\prime}
is an isomorphism.

Proof.

Combine Lemma
2
and Proposition
3
, and take
Z
to be
\Gamma_{\omega}
for some
\omega
such that
\operatorname{ob}_{X^{\prime}}(\omega)\neq 0
.
∎

2.1.
The Examples

2.1.1.
Surface examples

The existence of smooth projective surfaces with non-closed global
one-forms goes back at least to Mumford
[
9
, Corollary on p.341]
.
The author would like to thank Sasha Petrov for pointing out this example.

In
[
12
, Corollary 3.4 and the paragraph following it]
, Takeda constructs some generalized Raynaud surfaces that also have non-closed global one-forms.

[
12
]
is written under the assumption that
p>2
, but one can check that these examples still hold when
p=2
, see e.g.
[
13
, §5]
for more details.

2.1.2.
Higher-dimensional examples

For higher-dimensional examples, we can take
X
to be any of the surfaces above and take

X\times\mathbf{P}^{n}_{k}
.
Indeed, if
\eta\in H^{0}(X,\Omega_{X/k}^{1})
is non-closed, then so is the pullback of
\eta
to
X\times\mathbf{P}^{n}_{k}
.

Remark 5
.

If the Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence degenerates at
E_{1}
, then we must have that
H^{0}(X,Z^{1}_{X/k})=H^{0}(X,\Omega^{1}_{X/k})
.
Therefore, by Deligne-Illusie
[
4
, Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.5]
, all examples above do not lift to the ring of second Witt vectors
W_{2}(k)
.
