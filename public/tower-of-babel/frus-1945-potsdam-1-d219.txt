No. 219 The Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) to the Acting Secretary of State

[Extract][1]

secret us urgent

London, July 15, 1945—6 p.m.

7146. We were called to FonOff this afternoon by Harvey who was considerably disturbed about Department’s proposed announcement July 17 re Italy and World Security Organization. …[2] British Embassy Washington is being instructed to approach Department and request announcement be postponed until the question of future status of Italy can be discussed at Big Three meeting.

. . . . . . .

Winant
