Very tangible progress was made by the Committee in 1957. Only the continued
interest and efforts evinced by both the permanent members and auditors could have
made the work of the Committee as successful as it has been. Attendance from
other divisions, many members of which have joined us as permanent guests, has

been large, consistent, and gratifvine.

Plans for 1958

The Committee plans no great departure from its current and recent past pro-
cedures. Background movies will continue to be shown on the first meeting of each
month. A Chinese language program, for which the Committee acted as inter-
mediary, was begun in 1957 but has since lapsed. Efforts will be made to give this
program new life. The minutes of the Committee will continue to reflect informa-
tive articles or books bearing on economic factors in the Far East.
