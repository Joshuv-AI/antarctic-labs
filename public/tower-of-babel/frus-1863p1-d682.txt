Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 779.]

Department of State, Washington, December 5, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 20th ultimo (No. 542) has been received. I give you my thanks for the report of the progress of the trial of the Alexandra, which accompanied it, and remain, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
