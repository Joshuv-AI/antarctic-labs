THE POWER OF COINCIDENCE

We know, we “feel” the coincidences which occur in our lives
cannot always be “blind.” A chance meeting, a puzzling
recurrence suggest to us that something is happening above
and beyond what we think of as the normal course of our
reassured, cause-and-effect existence. Now Alan Vaughan,
author of Incredible Coincidence, The Baffling World of
Synchronicity and a leading authority on synchronicity
(meaningful coincidences) offers an intriguing explanation of
why events or ideas occur at the same time or in the same way.
Vaughan offers a thoughtful, logical theory of this phenomena
and he tells how we can make coincidence — synchronicity —
work for us: by developing awareness of our life’s events,
perceiving what they tell us, and acting upon them in a way
that can purposefully fulfill the destiny our inner selves
already know.
