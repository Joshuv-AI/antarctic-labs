FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:

1. [Text] The Armed Forces are ‘‘more united than ever’’ and are
silently observing the proceedings against nine military men involved
in the assassination of the Jesuits, to rule out any public doubts by _
those who might want to manipulate the enforcement of justice,
Colonel Rene Emilio Ponce, chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff has
said.

2. During his first interview this year, the high-ranking military
officer said Marxist subversives have taken advantage of the killing
of the Jesuits and their two maids in an attempt to divide and
discredit the Army.

3. ‘*The Armed Forces are aware of this and are more united than
ever,’’ the Colonel said, adding that he was ‘‘very impressed’’ last
week when he confirmed this unity during a recent meeting with over
150 Army chiefs and officers.

4. A colonel, three other officers, two noncommissioned officers,
and two soldiers were arrested following orders by a judge conducting
an investigation on the assassination of the Jesuits perpetrated at
daybreak on 16 November at the Central American University (UCA).

5. The chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff said the Armed Forces
have maintained a silence in this regard so as not to interfere with
the investigations, because any statements could be interpreted as
pressure.

6. Ponce added that the Armed Forces have fulfilled their duty in
sending the military men involved to the judicial authorities.
However, he noted, ‘‘no one is guilty until it can be proven.’’

7. Reporters reminded the chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff
that, within the overall condemnation resulting from the killing of
the Jesuits, and Dr. Hector Oqueli Colindres’ assassination in
Guatemala, groups have been organized and are demanding
investigations and the enforcement of the law against those who turn
out to be involved. However, these same groups have forgotten crimes

such as the assassination of Dr. Francisco Peccorini, Dr. Roberto
Garcia Alvarado, Dr. Antonio Rodriguez Porth, Edgar Chacon, Gabriel
Payes, Dr. Jose Francisco Guerrero, and recently, the attack against
engineer Jose Hernandez, who is about to die.

8. The reporters added that in these cases, there have been no
socialist or religious groups demanding the execution of the
criminals, no homilies on the occasion of their death, and neither
the Federal Bureau of Investigation, Scotland Yard, nor the Spanish
police have been called in to investigate the crimes.

9. Col. Ponce said he agrees with the reporters. He reminded them
that hundreds of thousands of military men have been killed by the
left wing, yet no one has said anything. However, when others are
killed, be they from a religious or a political group, the first
thing people do is blame the Armed Forces or the so-called ‘‘death
squads,’’ or try to intentionally link these groups with the Army,
and forget the FMLN-FDR [Farabundo Marti National Liberation
Front-Democratic Revolutionary Front] terrorist death squads trained
in Cuba, Nicaragua, Vietnam, and Algeria.

10. In view of the reporters’ comments, Ponce explained that the
relations between Catholic leaders and the Armed Forces are ‘‘very
cordial.’’ He added that he has held several meetings with leaders
from the Salvadoran Episcopal Conference in which the two parties
have expressed their viewpoints regarding the crimes perpetrated by
FMLN-FDR criminals.

11. Ponce disclosed that terrorist groups are planning to attack
several sectors in southwest and northeast San Salvador between the
last week of January and 10 February.

12. He added that Military Intelligence has learned that the FMLN
intends to attack the zone of Finca El Espino, Santa Tecla, Zaragoza
Huizucar, Ayutuxtepeque, Soyapango, and other sectors where coffee
plantations are located.

13. During the criminal offensive unleashed by the FMLN-FDR on 11
November in 1989, bloody clashes occurred at the places mentioned.

14. According to Ponce, the FMLN leadership intends to carry out the
attacks at the same time the Nicaraguan presidential elections are
being held. ‘‘They want to be noticed and to show their sponsors
abroad that they are still alive and have strength, but we are
prepared,’’ Ponce emphasized.

16. Asked about the guilt of U.S. citizen Jennifer Casolo, who was
released by a judge ‘*because of lack of evidence,’’ the high-ranking
military officer said the Armed Forces simply follow orders issued by
civilian authorities.

17. ‘‘During the investigations conducted to determine the guilt or
innocence of Casolo, the National Police gathered all of the evidence
and sent it to the court, which afterwards ordered the release of
Casolo,’’ Ponce added.
