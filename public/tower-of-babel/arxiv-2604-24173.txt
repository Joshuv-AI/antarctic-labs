On 𝒟 𝘄𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗻 \bm{\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}} -Modules of Finite Length on Rigid Analytic Spaces
Julian Reichardt

Abstract
We show that for quasi-compact smooth rigid analytic spaces, the extension functor sends holonomic 𝒟 \mathcal{D} -modules to coadmissible 𝒟 wideparen \wideparen{\mathcal{D}} -modules which are of finite length as weakly holonomic 𝒟 wideparen \wideparen{\mathcal{D}} -modules. Using this, we show that the meromorphic connections considered by Bitoun–Bode and the local cohomology groups considered by Ardakov–Bode–Wadsley are of finite length as weakly holonomic 𝒟 wideparen \wideparen{\mathcal{D}} -modules for quasi-compact smooth rigid analytic spaces. As a central tool, we introduce and study Hilbert polynomials for finitely generated modules over completed Weyl algebras.

1
Introduction

The theory of algebraic
\mathcal{D}
-modules on smooth complex varieties is a classical topic, with numerous important applications to algebraic geometry and geometric representation theory. In
[
4
]
, K. Ardakov and S. Wadsley initiated the study of an analogous framework of so-called
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules for smooth rigid analytic varieties over a nonarchimedean base field
K
of mixed characteristic
(0,p)
. The starting point is the construction of a suitable completion of the sheaf of algebraic differential operators to account for the ubiquity of algebro-topological structures one encounters throughout rigid geometry.

Although the theory of
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules has been extensively developed further by various contributors, certain foundational aspects remain difficult to approach. Of particular notice is the development of a robust notion of holonomicity for
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. Recall that holonomic
\mathcal{D}
-modules on smooth complex varieties can be characterised as those coherent
\mathcal{D}
-modules whose associated characteristic variety is of minimal dimension (see
[
19
, Section 3]
). In contrast to arbitrary coherent
\mathcal{D}
-modules, holonomic
\mathcal{D}
-modules enjoy stability under the standard operations for
\mathcal{D}
-modules and are always of finite length as
\mathcal{D}
-modules. Note, however, that not every
\mathcal{D}
-module of finite length is holonomic.

In the absence of a suitable replacement for the characteristic variety, Ardakov–Wadsley, jointly with A. Bode, introduced weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules in
[
1
]
. These are defined in terms of a purely homological characterisation of holonomic
\mathcal{D}
-modules, which can be suitably translated to
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. However, there are pathological examples of weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules that are of infinite length or have infinite dimensional fibres (see
[
1
, Section 8.1]
). As an alternative, Bode proposed in
[
8
]
a definition of a (derived) category of holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules, taking stability under the standard operations essentially as a definition. This is inspired by the work of D. Caro
[
12
,
13
]
on arithmetic
\mathscr{D}^{\dagger}
-modules, where this definition provides an equivalent approach to holonomicity. This produces a functorially well-behaved category of holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules containing integrable connections. However, due to its abstract nature, both the precise relation to the previously considered weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules as well as questions of finite length are a priori unclear.

In this article, we develop a framework providing a criterion for weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules on the closed unit polydisc to be of finite length. More precisely, given a coadmissible presentation
M=\varprojlim_{n}M_{n}
of a weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-module
M
on the closed unit polydisc, we give a criterion for
M
to be of finite length by considering uniform bounds on the lengths of the
M_{n}
(see
Proposition 39
). Using Kashiwara’s equivalence, one can extend to general smooth affinoid
K
-spaces.

As an application, our first main result is concerned with the extension functor from coherent
\mathcal{D}
-modules to coadmissible
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. This functor has been considered before in
[
5
]
and
[
1
]
and is a valuable tool for studying
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. It is known that the extension functor sends holonomic
\mathcal{D}
-modules to weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. We obtain the following strengthening.

Theorem A

(
Theorem 44
)
.

Let
X=\Sp A
be a smooth affinoid
K
-space and let
\mathcal{M}
be a holonomic
\mathcal{D}_{X}
-module. Then the extended
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}_{X}
-module
E_{X}(\mathcal{M})=\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}_{X}\otimes_{\mathcal{D}_{X}}\mathcal{M}
is of finite length in the category of weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}_{X}
-modules.

Note that
Theorem A
immediately extends to quasi-compact smooth rigid analytic
K
-spaces by a simple covering argument (see
Corollary 46
). In some sense,
Theorem A
is a purely algebraic statement. However, the extension functor can be used to study questions of coadmissibility and weak holonomicity of
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules, as employed before in
[
5
]
and
[
1
]
. This way, we show that various weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules considered before are of finite length.

Theorem B

(

)
.

Let

be a quasi-compact smooth rigid analytic
K
-space. Let

Theorem C

.

Let

In the case where

Structure of the paper

Section 2
and
Section 3
constitute the technical foundation of this paper.

We begin in
Section 2
by studying deformations of modules over deformable
R
-algebras and doubly filtered
K
-algebras, as considered in
[
3
,
1
]
. Our main results are the stabilisation results
Proposition 6
and
Proposition 12
.

In
Section 3
we study a family of completions of the Weyl algebra over
K
as doubly filtered
K
-algebras. We introduce double filtrations based on the Bernstein filtration, which allow the definition of a Hilbert polynomial for finitely generated modules (see
Definition 26
). As an application, we show that holonomic modules over these completed Weyl algebras are of finite length, in analogy to holonomic modules over the Weyl algebra over
K
(see
Corollary 30
).

Moving towards geometry, we use
Section 4
to survey the theory of algebraic
\mathcal{D}
-modules and analytic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules on smooth rigid analytic
K
-spaces. Here we approach both theories uniformly using the language of Lie–Rinehart algebras, the algebraic incarnations of Lie algebroids. Of particular importance is
Section 4.4
, where we make the theory of
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules on the closed unit polydisc explicit in terms of the completed Weyl algebras we studied in
Section 3
. This also prepares the applications of our stabilisation results from
Section 2
.

We prove our main results in
Section 5
and
Section 6
.

Section 5
is devoted to the proof of
Theorem A
(see
Theorem 44
). We first recall the definition of the extension functor and establish some compatibility results. Using Kashiwara’s equivalence, we then reduce the general affinoid case to a simplified case on the closed unit polydisc. Here we can apply the machinery developed in
Section 2
and
Section 3
.

Finally, in
Section 6
we prove
Theorem B
and
Theorem C
(see
Theorem 51
and
Theorem 52
). As outlined before, this is done by reducing to the previously established theorems for extensions of holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules. We also obtain the more refined version
Theorem 48
of
Theorem 51
, based on
[
5
]
, and show that there are non-holonomic weakly holonomic
\wideparen{\mathcal{D}}
-modules of finite length in
Corollary 49
.

This paper has two appendices. In
Appendix A
we recall the construction and fundamental properties of Hilbert polynomials for polynomial rings over general base fields. In
Appendix B
we collect some generalities on coordinate systems for smooth affinoid
K
-spaces and go over some subtleties which arise when working with algebraic
\mathcal{D}
-modules on smooth rigid analytic
K
-spaces.

Notations and Conventions

We fix a complete discrete valuation field
K
of mixed characteristic with ring of integers
R
and residue field
k
. We also fix a uniformiser
\pi
. For an
R
-module
M
, we denote by

its
\pi

its extension of scalars from

. We also abbreviate

. Rings are assumed to be associative and unital. By noetherian we mean left and right noetherian and similar for other ring-theoretic adjectives. Modules will be left modules if not specified further. We work with rigid analytic

-spaces in the sense of Tate (see
[
10
]
). A rigid analytic
K
-space

will always assumed to be quasi-separated, quasi-paracompact and equidimensional.

3
On Completed Weyl Algebras

We show that completed Weyl algebras admit two structures as doubly filtered
K
-algebras, using either the order filtration or the Bernstein filtration. The latter gives rise to a notion of Hilbert polynomial for finitely generated modules over completed Weyl algebras.

3.1
Generalities on completed Weyl algebras

We start by collecting some generalities on the Weyl algebras over general commutative base rings, our cases of interests being the rings
R
and
k
.

16
.

Let
S
a commutative ring. The
\bm{d}
-th Weyl algebra over
\bm{S}
, denoted
\bm{A_{d}(S)}

S
-algebra with
2d

[x_{i},x_{j}]=x_{i}x_{j}-x_{j}x_{i}=0,\qquad[y_{i},y_{j}]=y_{i}y_{j}-y_{j}y_{i}=0,\qquad[y_{i},x_{j}]=y_{i}x_{j}-x_{j}y_{i}=\delta_{ij}\,.

For

-th Weyl algebra

[
16

and

[

\sum_{\alpha,\beta}s_{\alpha,\beta}x^{\alpha}y^{\beta}

-algebra filtrations of

[

with

. The

\bm{\mathcal{F}_{\bullet}}
on
\bm{A_{d}(S)}
is the positive
S
-algebra filtration with
\mathcal{F}_{i}A_{d}(S)
