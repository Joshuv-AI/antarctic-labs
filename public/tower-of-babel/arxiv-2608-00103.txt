The one-sided unit count of 𝔽 2 ​ [ 𝖯 ] \mathbb{F}_{2}[\mathsf{P}] at radius four, and an integral separation theorem
Moe Tabei

Abstract
. Let 𝖯 \mathsf{P} be the Hantzsche–Wendt (Promislow) group and B ⁡ ( 4 ) B(4) the radius-four ball in its standard word metric. Dietrich, Lee, Nies and Vinyals determined the two-sided count: exactly 36 36 nontrivial units u u of 𝔽 2 ​ [ 𝖯 ] \mathbb{F}_{2}[\mathsf{P}] with both supp ⁡ ( u ) \operatorname{supp}(u) and supp ⁡ ( u − 1 ) \operatorname{supp}(u^{-1}) in B ⁡ ( 4 ) B(4) . We determine the one-sided count: exactly 52 52 nontrivial units with supp ⁡ ( u ) ⊆ B ⁡ ( 4 ) \operatorname{supp}(u)\subseteq B(4) and no constraint on the inverse. The 16 16 new units have inverses supported at radius exactly 5 5 ; they form two orbits of size 8 8 under the symmetry group fixing the generating set, and all 52 52 units have support size 21 21 on both sides. Completeness is a single propositional unsatisfiability, certified by a drat proof checked with drat-trim . As an arithmetic consequence we prove: no unit of ℤ ⁡ [ 𝖯 ] \mathbb{Z}[\mathsf{P}] with support in B ⁡ ( 4 ) B(4) has nontrivial reduction modulo 2 2 — with no bound on the coefficients or on the support of the inverse. Since 𝔽 2 ​ [ 𝖯 ] \mathbb{F}_{2}[\mathsf{P}] has 52 52 nontrivial units on that ball, this separates, in the untwisted setting, the integral group ring from its characteristic-two quotient at the first radius where the unit conjecture fails over a field.

2.2.
The inverse box

That the inverse of a support-constrained unit is confined at all is not new,

The integral separation theorem

Let

By §

residues. Write

and

the
0/1

and

,

Proposition 4.1
.

For
48
of the
52
residues the depth-one system (equivalently,

) is infeasible. For the remaining
4
residues the
depth-one solution space is an affine
\mathbb{F}_{2}
-space of dimension

, and for
each of its
2048

\bmod\ 8

Proof.

Exact integer linear algebra over the supports
(B(4),\mathrm{Box})
. The
coefficient matrix at depth two is the same as at depth one — only the
right-hand side changes with the chosen depth-one solution — which is what
makes the

conclusion does not depend on any search heuristic. Full logs and the
verification scripts accompany the paper.
