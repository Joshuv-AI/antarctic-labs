Kuwaiti Amir’s National Day Message

Amir Jabir al-Sabah marked the thirtieth anniversary of
Kuwait’s independence with a message praising coalition forces
for working to liberate Kuwait and hailing the steadfastness of
Kuwaitis resisting the Iraqi occupation. The Amir emphasized the
need for Kuwaiti self-sacrifice and self-reliance after
liberation. Jabir also stressed that the upcoming "new phase" of
Kuwait’s history would require unity based on the "national

China ?xpresses Regret at Start of Land War

Beijing expressed its "deep regret" Sunday over the start of
the ground war and appealed to "all warring parties to exercise
restraint." As he departed Tehran Sunday, Vice Foreign Minister
Yang Fuchang offered the Chinese Government’s six-point peace
plan--including a general call for Iraqi withdrawal, a peaceful
settlement, and a regional approach to postwar security, without
foreign military forces. The plan also noted that an Arab-
Israeli settlement was essential to lasting peace in the region,
but stressed that it need not be linked to the Gulf crisis. The
Chinese media reported Monday that the US and France *claimed"
the ground war was going well but that the "international
community" continued to call for a halt to the war.
