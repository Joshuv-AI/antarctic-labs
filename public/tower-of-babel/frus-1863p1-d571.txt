Mr. Seward to Lord Lyons.

Department of State, Washington, June 17, 1863.

My Lord: I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt of your note of the 13th instant, and enclosure, relative to the case of the Hanover.

I have the honor to be, with high consideration, my lord, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Right Hon. Lord Lyons, &c., &c., &c.
