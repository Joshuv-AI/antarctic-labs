working together is not a one way street. For instance in the
field of archaeology, some parts of the world are inaccessible
to archaeologists due to either geography or political climate.

they can reveal features which are either invisible or distorted

villas, roads, and other structures which have been eroded by time,
destroyed by war, or in many cases vandalized can be revealed to
the aerial camera. The Intelligence Community, because of its
particular needs, has acquired a massive amount of photography
useful to the academic community.

It wouxd be a shame if this knowledge could not be shared
because universities chose to levy rigid prohibitions on their
faculty.
