Mr. Angel to Mr. Seward.

No. 76.]

Legation of the United States, Stockholm, May 22, 1861.

* * * * * * * * *

The Swedish law is very strict against the violation of the rights of neutrals, and I am assured that there is no apprehension that any of the ports of this kingdom or of Norway will he prostituted to the wicked purpose of fitting out privateers.

* * * * * * * * *

I have the honor to be, with much respect, your obedient servant,

B. F. ANGEL.
