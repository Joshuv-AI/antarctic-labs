French physician, municipal councilor, and member of the Academy of Nîmes. Son of David Carcassonne; died at Marseilles May 7, 1894. He was the author of the following works: (1) "Questions sur Diverses Branches des Sciences Médicales," Paris, 1842; (2) "Compte-Rendu des Travaux des Conseils d'Hygiène et de Salubrité Publique de Nîmes," a treatise on thework done by the health officers of Nîmes, Nîmes, 1866; (3) "Notice sur Philippe Boileau de Castelnau," ib. 1882.

Bibliography: S. Kahn, Notice sur les Israélites de Nîmes, pp. 35, 47. S. S. K.
