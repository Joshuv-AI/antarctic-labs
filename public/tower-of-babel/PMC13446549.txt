Retraction Note: Re: Clinical implications of heat shock protein 70 in oral carcinogenesis and prediction of progression and recurrence in OSCC patients
Authors: Zecra Yahia
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: European Journal of Medical Research (2025) 30:447 10.1186/s40001-025-02542-2

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article after concerns were raised regarding the suspected undeclared use of AI tools and the author’s affiliation. The author was unable to provide any satisfactory explanation and documentary evidence of affiliation with the University of Science and Technology, 15201, Sana’a, Yemen.

The Editor-in-Chief therefore has lost confidence in the integrity of this article.

Zecra Yahia disagrees with this retraction.
