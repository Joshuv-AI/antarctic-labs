INTRODUCTION AND SAMPLE RECEIPT
This report details the uranium age-dating of International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) bulk
environmental cotton swipe sample 27107-14-04 at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL).
The 230Th/234U radiochronometer was used to determine sample model age requiring the
following analyses: U assay, U isotope composition, Th assay and Th isotope composition.
Sample 27107-14-04 was received at LANL on November 3, 2025, along with cotton swipe
blank 30124-24-20.
SAMPLE PREPARATION
Sample preparation for routine bulk environmental cotton swipe samples typically includes the
high temperature ashing of swipes followed by acid digestion. However, characterization of in-
house swipe material in 2025 revealed that TexWipe® cotton swipes contain nanogram levels of
232
 Th and femtogram levels of 230Th. Therefore, a new method was developed to isolate the
sample material without releasing Th and U from the cotton swipe and potentially biasing the
sample 230Th/234U model ages.
The sample and blank swipe were each cut into 4 pieces, and the subsamples were treated
individually. A process blank (27107-LA-01) was also introduced to the set. Electronic grade
methanol was added to fully submerge the swipe pieces and then sonicated to dislodge sample
particulates from each swipe piece. Following sonication, the swipe pieces were removed. The
methanol was evaporated, and the subsamples were acid digested. The subsamples were then
recombined into a single, gravimetrically prepared 8 M HNO3 + 0.15 M HF solution.
To estimate the amount of U in each dissolved swipe, small fractions of the primary solutions
were analyzed using a Thermo Scientific Element XR single collector inductively coupled
plasma mass spectrometer. Sample 27107-14-04 was estimated to contain ~304 ng uranium.
URANIUM ISOTOPE COMPOSITION AND ASSAY
Uranium Aliquots and Chemical Purification
For U isotope composition and assay analyses, approximately 3 ng and 10 ng aliquots of the
sample were taken, respectively. Uranium assay was determined by isotope dilution mass
spectrometry (IDMS) and aliquots were spiked with a highly enriched 233U tracer (HE U-233,
Unit U002) solution (Essex et al. 2021). All aliquots were spiked with approximately 0.5 ng 233U.
Both U isotope composition and assay aliquots were purified using the standard LANL NWAL U
radiochemistry procedure (CSP-387).

 Uranium Measurements by Multi-Collector Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass
 Spectrometry (MC-ICP-MS)
 Purified U isotope composition and assay aliquots were analyzed by MC-ICP-MS using a
 Thermo Scientific Neptune Plus in accordance with CSP-388. For U isotope composition
 measurements, the limits of detection (Table 1) are calculated as three times the standard
 uncertainty of the measurement. The detection limits for U-IDMS measurements (Table 2) are
 based on a “well-known” blank measured during the analysis routine following
 recommendations from Currie (1968).
 Uranium Isotope Composition Results
 Uranium isotope compositions results are presented in Table 1. Sample 27107-14-04 has an
 enriched U isotope composition. The measured 233U/238U ratio is below the limit of detection.

THORIUM ISOTOPE COMPOSITION AND ASSAY
Thorium Aliquots and Chemical Purification
Thorium isotope composition and assay were determined by IDMS. Sample aliquots provided
approximately 0.013–2.0 ng total Th and all aliquots were spiked with approximately 3 pg 229Th
tracer (Essex et al. 2018).
Thorium was purified from the sample matrix using a two-column procedure based on ion
exchange with Eichrom 1-X8 (100–200 mesh) resin. The spiked aliquots were loaded onto the
first column in 8 M HNO3. The columns were rinsed with additional 8 M HNO3, then the Th was
eluted in 9 M HCl. This was loaded directly onto the second column to provide a second U-Th
separation. The effluent was collected as the purified Th fraction.
Thorium Measurements by Multi-Collector Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass
Spectrometry
Purified Th-IDMS aliquots were analyzed by MC-ICP-MS using a Thermo Scientific Neptune
Plus in accordance with CSP-386. The detection limits for Th-IDMS measurements (Table 3 and
4) were based on a “well-known” blank measured during the analysis routine following
recommendations from Currie (1968).
Thorium Isotope Composition and Assay Results
Thorium isotope composition and assay results are reported in Table 3 and 4. Note that sample
removal from the swipes may not have been complete, it is possible that some sample remained
on the swipes. Thorium contents are therefore reported as the total Th removed from the swipe.
Sample 27107-14-04 contained 508.5 ± 3.4 fg of 230Th and 2.278 ± 0.014 ng of 232Th. The
process and swipe blank 230Th contents were below the 0.45 fg detection limit but contained
12.80 ± 0.15 pg and 35.01 ± 0.39 pg 232Th, respectively. As such, any 230Th contributed from
sample preparation and purification is insignificant relative to the 230Th from the samples. The
232
 Th measured in the process blank represents ~73% of that measured in the 30124-24-20 swipe
blank IDMS aliquot, and 0.65% of the 232Th measured in the 27107-14-04 IDMS aliquots.
Accordingly, the 232Th contents and the 230Th/232Th ratios measured in the swipe blank and

samples are likely to be impacted by contribution from sample preparation and purification and
are reported here for completeness only.

 TH/234U RADIOCHRONOMETRY
The 230Th/234U model age for sample 27107-14-04 is provided in Table 5 and was calculated
with a reference date of December 8, 2025. Sample 27107-14-04 has a 230Th/234U model age of
64.66 ± 0.59 years which corresponds to a model purification date of April 12, 1961. Model age
uncertainties were calculated as the expanded uncertainties at the 95% confidence level and
include propagation of the measurement uncertainty and the uncertainties associated with the

half-lives of 230Th and 234U. The 230Th and 234U half-lives were taken from Nuclear Wallet Card
9th Edition (2023).

SUMMARY
Radiochonometry measurements to determine 230Th/234U model age of a bulk environmental
swipe sample with IAEA sample number 27107-14-04 was completed at Los Alamos National
Laboratory. The 230Th/234U model age of 27107-14-04 is 64.66 ± 0.59 years which corresponds
to a model purification date of April 12, 1961.
