The rationality problem for multinorm one tori, II
Sumito Hasegawa, Kazuki Kanai, Yasuhiro Oki

Abstract
. We investigate the stable and retract rationality of multinorm one tori associated to finite étale algebras. Our results are organized according to the greatest common divisor d d of the degrees of the factors. We show that these tori are stably rational for d = 1 d=1 , and obtain a criterion for retract rationality that can be attributed to our previous results. For d > 1 d>1 , we provide sufficient conditions for the failure of retract rationality. We further generalize results of Endo–Miyata (1975) and Endo (2011) by giving an equivalent condition for multinorm one tori to be stably rational under the assumption that they split over Galois extensions with Galois groups in which all Sylow subgroups are cyclic. A similar result also holds when they split over dihedral Galois extensions.

Introduction

Let
k
be a field, and fix a separable closure
k^{\sep}
of
k
. Consider an algebraic variety
X
over
k
.
We say that
X
is
rational
over
k
if it is birationally equivalent to a projective space over
k
.
It is also important to investigate weaker conditions than rationality, such as
stable rationality
,
retract rationality
, and
unirationality
.
These satisfy the following:

rational
\Rightarrow
stably rational
\Rightarrow

retract rational
\Rightarrow
unirational.

We remark that the direction of the implication cannot be reversed in general.
See Section
2
for more details.

This paper follows
[
HKO
]
, which gives a foundation for the study of stable rationality and the retract rationality of multinorm one tori. Let
\mathbf{K}
be a finite étale algebra over
k
, that is, a finite product of finite separable field extensions of
k
contained in
k^{\sep}
. Then, we set

T_{\mathbf{K}/k}:=\Ker(\N_{\mathbf{K}/k}\colon\Res_{\mathbf{K}/k}\mathbb{G}_{m}\rightarrow\mathbb{G}_{m}),

where
\Res_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is the Weil restriction. We call it the
multinorm one torus
associated to
\mathbf{K}/k
. It is an algebraic torus of dimension
[\mathbf{K}:k]-1
over
k
. Moreover, it splits over the Galois closure
\widetilde{K}
over
k
of the composite field of all the factors of
\mathbf{K}
, that is, there is an isomorphism of
L
-algebraic groups
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}\otimes_{k}L\cong\mathbb{G}_{m,L}^{[\mathbf{K}:k]-1}
. If
\mathbf{K}
is a field,
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is called a
norm one torus
, and the stable (resp. retract) rationality has been studied extensively in the literature. See the introduction in
[
HKO
]
for details.

For a finite étale algebra
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
over
k
, we define a positive integer
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
as follows:

d_{k}(\mathbf{K}):=\gcd([K_{1}:k],\ldots,[K_{r}:k]).

In this paper, we study the relation between the stable (resp. retract) rationality of
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
and
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
.
If
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})=1
, then we can completely resolve the problem as follows.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
be a finite étale algebra over
k
, where
r\geq 2
, which satisfies
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})=1
. Then the multinorm one torus
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is stably rational over
k
.

In the case
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})>1
, the situation becomes more complicated compared to the above. However, we can give some sufficient conditions for
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
to be not retract rational.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
a finite étale algebra over
k
, where
r\geq 2
, which satisfies
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})=2
. For each
i
, we denote by
L_{i}
the Galois closure of
K_{i}
over
k
.

(i)

If
r=2
, we further assume that

•

[K_{1}:k]\in 4\mathbb{Z}
or
[K_{2}:k]\in 4\mathbb{Z}
; and

•

L_{1}\cap K_{2}=k
or
L_{2}\cap K_{1}=k
.

Then the multinorm one torus
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is not retract rational over
k
.

(ii)

If
r\geq 3
, we further assume that there is a subset
I
of
\{1,\ldots,r\}
with
\#I=3
such that
L_{i}\cap K_{j}=k
or
L_{j}\cap K_{i}=k
for any
i,j\in I
with
i\neq j
. Then the multinorm one torus
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is not retract rational over
k
.

Theorem 1.3
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
a finite étale algebra over
k
, where
r\geq 2
, that satisfies
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\geq 3
. We further assume
L_{i}\cap K_{j}=k
for some
i\neq j
, where
L_{i}
is the Galois closure of
K_{i}
over
k
. Then the multinorm one torus
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is not retract rational over
k
.

Remark 1.4
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}
a finite étale algebra over
k
. Choose a compactification
X
of
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
over
k
, and put
\overline{X}:=X\otimes_{k}k^{\sep}
. Then, we have

(1.1)

H^{1}(k,\Pic(\overline{X}))=0

if
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is retract rational over
k
. On the other hand, (
1.1
) may hold even if
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is not retract rational. In
[
DW14
, Theorem 1]
, Demarche and Wei essentially gave the validity of (
1.1
) in the case
L_{I}\cap K_{J}=k
. Here
I
and
J
are subsets of
\{1,\ldots,r\}
with
I\sqcup J=\{1,\ldots,r\}
,
L_{I}
is the composite field of
L_{i}
for all
i\in I
and
K_{J}
is the composite field of
K_{j}
for all
j\in J
. Pick
i_{0}\in I
and
j_{0}\in J
. Then it is clear that
L_{i_{0}}\cap K_{j_{0}}=k
is true. In particular, if
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\geq 3
, then the assumption in Theorem
1.3
is much weaker than the one in
[
DW14
, Theorem 1]
.

We also discuss some cases where the assumption in Theorem
1.2
or Theorem
1.3
fails. We first consider the case that
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
splits over a finite Galois extension of which all Sylow subgroups of the Galois group are cyclic. In this case,
[
EM75
, Theorem 1.5]
implies that
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is always retract rational over

.

Theorem 1.5
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
a finite étale algebra over
k
satisfying
K_{i}\not\subset K_{j}
for any
i\neq j
. Denote by
L/k
the Galois closure of the composite of
K_{1},\ldots,K_{r}
over
k
, and put
G:=\Gal(L/k)
. We further assume that all Sylow subgroups of
G
are cyclic. Then the
k
-torus
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is retract rational. Moreover, it is stably rational over
k
if and only if

\mathscr{D}_{k}(\mathbf{K})\cap\mathscr{P}_{*}(G)=\emptyset.

Here
\mathscr{D}_{k}(\mathbf{K})
is the set of prime divisors of
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
, and
\mathscr{P}_{*}(G)
is the set of prime divisors
p
of
\#G
satisfying two conditions as follows:

•

a
p
-Sylow subgroup
G_{p}
of
G
is normal; and

•

the image of the homomorphism
G\rightarrow\Aut(G_{p})
induced by conjugation has order
\geq 3
.

If
\mathbf{K}=K
is a field, then Theorem
1.5
follows from
[
EM75
, Theorem 2.3]
and
[
End11
, Theorem 3.1]
.

Remark 1.6
.

The set
\mathscr{P}_{*}(G)
is empty if
G
is cyclic. In particular, we obtain that
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is stably rational over
k
if
L/k
is cyclic.

Second, we argue the case where
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
splits over finite dihedral Galois extensions. Denote by
D_{n}
the dihedral group of order
2n
, that is,

D_{n}:=\langle\sigma_{n},\tau_{n}\mid\sigma_{n}^{n}=\tau_{n}^{2}=1,\tau_{n}\sigma_{n}\tau_{n}^{-1}=\sigma_{n}^{-1}\rangle.

Note that there is an isomorphism
D_{2}\cong(C_{2})^{2}
.

Theorem 1.7
.

Let
k
be a field, and
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
a finite étale algebra over
k
that satisfies
K_{i}\not\subset K_{j}
for any
i\neq j
. Denote by
L/k
the Galois closure of the composite of
K_{1},\ldots,K_{r}
over
k
. We further assume that there is an isomorphism
\Gal(L/k)\cong D_{n}
for some
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}
.

(i)

If
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
is odd, then
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is stably rational over
k
.

(ii)

If
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
is even, then
T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is stably rational over
k
if and only if it is retract rational over
k
. Moreover, the above two equivalent conditions hold if and only if there exists a finite product
\mathbf{K}^{\prime}=\prod_{i=1}^{r^{\prime}}K^{\prime}_{i}
of intermediate fields
K^{\prime}_{1},\ldots,K^{\prime}_{r^{\prime}}
of
L/k
such that

T_{\mathbf{K}/k}\times_{k}S\cong T_{\mathbf{K}^{\prime}/k}\times_{k}S^{\prime}

for some quasi-trivial
k
-tori
S
and
S^{\prime}
. Here,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
and
\mathbf{K}^{\prime}
satisfy one of the following:

(a)

r^{\prime}=1
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\in 2\mathbb{Z}\setminus 4\mathbb{Z}
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\mid 2n
and
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{1})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})/2}\rangle
;

(b)

r^{\prime}=1
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\in 2\mathbb{Z}\setminus 4\mathbb{Z}
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\mid n
and
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{1})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})},\tau_{n}\rangle
;

(c)

r^{\prime}=2
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\in 2\mathbb{Z}\setminus 4\mathbb{Z}
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\mid 2n
,
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{1})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})/2}\rangle
and
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{2})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})},\tau_{n}\rangle
;

(d)

r^{\prime}=2
,
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})\mid n
,
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{1})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})},\tau_{n}\rangle
and
\Gal(L/K^{\prime}_{2})\cong\langle\sigma_{n}^{d_{k}(\mathbf{K})},\sigma_{n}\tau_{n}\rangle
.

If
\mathbf{K}
is a field, Theorem
1.7
follows from
[
HY24
, Theorem 1.1]
. Our contribution to Theorem
1.7
is the case that
\mathbf{K}
is not a field.

We now explain our proof of main theorems. It is based on the standard method, which rephrases the stable (resp. retract) rationality of algebraic tori by means of their character groups. For an algebraic torus
T
over
k
, let

X^{*}(T):=\Hom_{k^{\sep}\text{-groups}}(T\otimes_{k}k^{\sep},\mathbb{G}_{m,k^{\sep}}).

Let
L/k
be a finite Galois extension that contains
K_{1},\ldots,K_{r}
. Then, the Galois group
\Gal(L/k)
acts on
X^{*}(T_{\mathbf{K}/k})
. Moreover, it is known that
T
is stably (resp. retract) rational over
k
if and only if
X^{*}(T)
is quasi-permutation (resp. quasi-invertible).

For Theorem
1.1
, we prove that
X^{*}(T_{\mathbf{K}/k})\oplus\mathbb{Z}
is a permutation
\Gal(L/k)
-lattice. Moreover, we obtain the following as an application of Theorem
1.1
.

Theorem 1.8
.

Let
k
be a field,
\mathbf{K}=\prod_{i=1}^{r}K_{i}
a finite étale algebra over
k
. Take a finite Galois extension
L
of
k
containing
K_{1},\ldots,K_{r}
. For each prime divisor
p
of
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
, pick a maximal subextension
L^{(p)}
of
L/k
such that its extension degree is coprime to

. Then, the following are equivalent:

(i)

T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is retract rational over
k
;

(ii)

T_{\mathbf{K}/k}
is retract rational over
L^{(p)}
for any prime divisor
p
of
d_{k}(\mathbf{K})
.

Remark 1.9
.

The condition (ii) in Theorem
1.8
can be determined by the previous result (
[
HKO
, Theorems 1.2, 1.3]
). Therefore, we obtain a criterion for arbitrary multinorm one tori to be retract rational.

Theorems
1.2
and
1.3
follow from the criterion in Remark
1.9
. Next, we give an outline of the proof of Theorem

The field case

2.
Basic facts on the rationality of tori

Let
k
be a field. For a non-negative integer
n
, we denote by
\mathbb{P}_{k}^{n}
the projective space of dimension
n
over
k
. Consider an algebraic variety over
k
. We say that

is

•

rational
over
k
if it is birationally equivalent to a projective space over

;

•

over

for some

•

over

and

with

•

over

to

The notion of retract rationality was originally introduced by Saltman (
[
Sal84
]
) in the case where

is infinite (see also
[
Kan12
]
). It has been generalized for all varieties over arbitrary fields by Merkurjev (
[

]
). Note that one has implications

\Rightarrow

\Rightarrow

\Rightarrow

In this paper, we concentrate on the case where

is an algebraic torus over

. Fix a separable closure

of

-lattices, where

. We follow the same terminology as
[

]
,
[

]

Definition 2.1

(
[

, §1]
,
[

, Definition 2.1]
)
.

Let

(i)

(ii)

(iii)

(iv)

(
[
