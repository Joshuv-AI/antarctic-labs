Correction: A novel cell-free intrathecal approach with PRP for the treatment of spinal cord multiple sclerosis in cats
Authors: Mariam F Farid, Yara S Abouelela, Noha A E Yasin, Mohamed R Mousa, Marwa A Ibrahim, Abdelbary Prince, Hamdy Rizk
Published: 2026

Correction: Inflamm Regener 42, 45 (2022)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s41232-022-00230-w

A correction has been issued to address the undeclared reuse of controls in another study by the same authors, originally reported in a separate publication [1, 2]. The control images were reused to ensure consistency and comparability across studies and to reduce the number of animals in compliance with animal welfare and ethical guidelines. We followed the “3Rs alternatives” replacement, reduction, and refinement of animals to provide a framework for more humane animal research. The study design aimed not only to induce disease but also to apply therapeutic interventions, support recovery, and allow adoption, rather than euthanasia.
