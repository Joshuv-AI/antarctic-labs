No. 148 The British Minister (Balfour) to the Acting Secretary of State

top secret personal

Washington, 1st June, 1945.

My Dear Mr. Under Secretary, With reference to my letter of May 29th,[1] Mr. Eden has asked me to tell you that in view of Marshal Stalin’s message about surrendered German naval and merchant ships,[2] His Majesty’s Government propose the addition of the following item to Section 2 of the Agenda for the forthcoming meeting of Heads of Governments: “Disposal of German Fleet and Merchant Ships”.[3]

Yours very sincerely,

John Balfour

Source: 740.0011 E. W./6–145

Notes
[1] Document No. 144.
[2] See document No. 386, footnote 2.
[3] The substance of this message was forwarded by Grew to Truman on June 5.
