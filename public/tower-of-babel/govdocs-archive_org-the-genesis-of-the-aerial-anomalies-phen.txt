2. Precursors to the Boom: World War II
and the European Theater (1941–1946)
The chronological origin of the modern
UFO phenomenon predates the famous 1947
American wave, finding its roots in the con-
tested airspace of the Second World War. Long
before the public consciousness was intro-
duced to the concept of the "flying saucer,"
military aviators and naval personnel were re-
porting highly anomalous encounters with
unidentified aerial phenomena that actively
interacted with military assets.

3. The Catalyst: The Summer of the
Saucers (1947)
If the foo fighters and ghost rockets laid the
foundational precedent for military encoun-
ters with the unexplained, the summer of 1947
served as the absolute catalyst for global public
awareness and institutional panic. The year
1947 marked the turbulent intersection of Cold
War paranoia and a sudden, undeniable surge
in domestic aerial anomalies within the United
States.3
