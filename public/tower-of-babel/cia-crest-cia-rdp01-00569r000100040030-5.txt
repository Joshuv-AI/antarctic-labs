Memorandum for the DCI from Chief, History
Staff dtd 29 April 1982, Subj : DCI Histories:
William Colby et al .

1. As proposed in my referenced 29 April 1982 memorandum
to the DCI (copy attached) , last May I asked Richard Lehman,
retiring Vice-Chairman of the National Intelligence Council,

to undertake the History Staff's projected history of William E.
Colby's tenure as DCI. Mr. Lehman agreed and is now able to
concentrate on this work, his contract with the Office of the
DCI for post-retirement NIC work having expired 30 September 1982.

2. I would therefore like to request approval for
Mr. Lehman's employment as an independent contractor for a
period of twelve months, from 1 October 1982 to 30 September
1983, to research and write a study of Mr. Colby's period as
DCI. DCI studies are part of the History Staff's program
approved by the Executive Committee 30 October 1981.

3. I shall consult with Mr. Lehman on the scope and
focus of his work as he proceeds, and I shall remain responsible
for deciding how the completed work will be edited and reviewed.

I shall expect the Office of the DCI to provide office space and
secretarial support for Mr. Lehman. The completed draft history
should be a manuscript of around 200-300 double-spaced type-
script pages.

DCI Histories: William Colby et al.

1. This memorandum is for your information only, and reviews the
status of the DCI histories program.

2. As you suggested, I consulted William Colby about our projected
history of his tenure as Director of Central Intelligence. He had' not known
that Richard Lehman was on the verge of retiring , and agreed that he would be
an excellent choice to do this study. Moreover, Mr. Colby assured me of his
fullest cooperation in this work.

1 3. For his part, Mr. Lehman is willing to undertake this project, and
to begin work as he phases out of his remaining NIC commitments. He has pre-
pared the attached preliminary' outline, which he will revise as necessary as his
work progresses.

4. Unless you have other desires, I shall ask Mr. Lehman to research and
write a complete draft manuscript for this Colby study. I shall consult with
him on the scope and focus of the work as 'it proceeds, and remain responsible
for deciding how the work will be edited and reviewed.

5. I shall expect the Office of the DCI to arrange for compensation,
office space and secretarial support for Mr. Lehman. He and I will w r ork
out appropriate consultant arrangements with the DCI Administrative Officer.

From his estimates of the time he can give to this project, Mr. Lehman
will need until at least 1 July 1983, and possibly longer, to complete this
draft manuscript, which should be around 200-300 double-spaced typescript
pages .

6. Since the Colby project will be the third DCI study to get under
way, I should also report on the status of the other projected DCI histories:
