Subleading Chern-Simons soft factors in perturbative de Sitter


Abstract
Chern-Simons perturbations introduce corrections to soft theorems for gauge theories at subleading 𝒪 ⁡ ( ω 0 ) \mathcal{O}\left(\omega^{0}\right) order in soft momenta. We investigate these soft theorems in flat spacetime with perturbative 1 / ℓ 2 1/\ell^{2} de Sitter corrections. Following previous works, we define the perturbative scattering matrix in a compact region in the static patch of de Sitter. We show that Chern-Simons corrections do not mix with the 1 / ℓ 2 1/\ell^{2} de Sitter curvature corrections at subleading order 𝒪 ⁡ ( ω 0 ) \mathcal{O}\left(\omega^{0}\right) . Alternatively, one can say that the subleading Chern-Simons soft factors are insensitive to the de Sitter curvature at this order, indicating their topological nature at the level of amplitudes. This also suggests a universal behavior of these Chern-Simons soft factors.

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\left.+\frac{1}{\delta^{2}}\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\left\{\frac{1}{4}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon^{dh}(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\left(p_{1}\cdot\partial_{p_{1}}\right)+\frac{5}{8}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon^{dh}(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\right\}\right]\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{1}\mu_{1}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)

(20)

where,
\Gamma_{n-1}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)=\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{a_{1}h_{1}}(p_{1})\,\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{1}\mu_{1}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)
. Also, we’ve rewritten the structure constants in terms of the
\mathfrak{su}
(N) generators
T_{a}
in adjoint representation,
f^{abc}=i\left(\text{ad}_{T_{a}}\right)^{bc}
and identified the spin angular momentum operator term in spin-1 (vector) representation,

(S^{\mu\sigma})^{\nu}_{\rho}=\left(\eta^{\mu\nu}\delta_{\;\rho}^{\sigma}-\eta^{\sigma\nu}\delta_{\;\rho}^{\mu}\right).

(21)

Similar expression is there for the
\left(n-1\right)
leg with
p_{1}\rightarrow p_{n-1}
.
To this then we need to add the contribution from the diagram where the gluon attaches to the internal line. This can be obtained using the standard way of imposing gauge invariance on the amplitude
[
5
]
. The result is,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{{}^{\text{YM}}\left(\text{int}\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=

\displaystyle\,-g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,f^{ada_{1}}\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\epsilon^{dh}(k)\cdot\partial_{p_{1}}\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{1}\mu_{1}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle-g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,f^{ada_{n-1}}\epsilon_{\mu_{n-1}}^{ah_{n-1}}(p_{1})\epsilon^{dh}(k)\cdot\partial_{p_{n-1}}\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{n-1}\mu_{n-1}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right).

(22)

This term combines with the others to give us the soft gluon theorem with de Sitter corrections,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{{}^{\text{YM}}}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\,g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,\left(\text{ad}_{T_{d}}\right)^{aa_{1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\times\left[\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon^{dh}(k)}{\omega\,p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}+\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\frac{\epsilon_{\mu}^{dh}(k)\hat{k}_{\sigma}}{p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}L_{\left(1\right)}^{\mu\sigma}+\epsilon_{\nu}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\frac{\epsilon_{\mu}^{dh}(k)\hat{k}_{\sigma}}{p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}(S^{\mu\sigma})^{\nu}{}_{\mu_{1}}\right.

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad\qquad\left.+\frac{1}{\delta^{2}}\epsilon_{\mu_{1}}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\left\{\frac{1}{4}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon^{dh}(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\left(p_{1}\cdot\partial_{p_{1}}\right)+\frac{5}{8}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon^{dh}(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\right\}\right]\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{1}\mu_{1}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)

\displaystyle\qquad\qquad+\left\{1\rightarrow\left(n-1\right)\right\},

(23)

where
L^{\mu\nu}
is the orbital angular momentum operator,

L_{\left(i\right)}^{\mu\nu}=\left(p_{i}^{\mu}\partial_{p_{i}}^{\nu}-p_{i}^{\nu}\partial_{p_{i}}^{\mu}\right).

(24)

4
Chern-Simons correction to the perturbative soft gluon theorem in de Sitter

Now let us add a Chern-Simons term to the usual gauge invariant action for QCD or QED. For demonstration, let us again consider an
n
point colour ordered gluon amplitude
\Gamma_{n}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)
, where the
n
th gluon goes soft and hence
p_{n}=k
. But now we are working in a general Yang-Mills matter gauge theory with a Chern-Simons deformation. Then, as argued in
[
30
]
, the leading Chern-Simons contribution (subleading in soft momenta overall
\mathcal{O}\left(\omega^{0}\right)
) comes solely from the diagram where the soft gluon attaches to
p_{1}
or
p_{n-1}
(see the diagram in the left in fig.
2
) ,

\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}}=\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(1\right)}+\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(n-1\right)}

(25)

We calculate these two parts separately. This should then be added to the usual soft theorem with de Sitter corrections,

\displaystyle\Gamma_{n}(\{p_{i}\},\omega\hat{k})=

\displaystyle\,\mathcal{A}^{\text{gauge}}+\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[\frac{1}{\omega}\mathbb{S}^{\left(0\right)}+\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)}+\frac{1}{\delta^{2}}\mathbb{S}^{\prime\left(1\right)}\right]\Gamma_{n-1}(\{p_{i}\})+\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}}

(26)

where
\mathbb{S}^{\left(0\right)}
,
\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)}
are the usual leading and subleading gauge soft factors and
\mathbb{S}^{\prime\left(1\right)}
is the
1/\delta^{2}
de Sitter correction,

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}^{\left(0\right)}=

\displaystyle\,g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon(k)}{\,p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}+\left\{1\rightarrow\left(n-1\right)\right\}

(27)

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)}=

\displaystyle\,g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,\frac{\epsilon_{\mu}(k)\hat{k}_{\sigma}}{p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}J_{1}^{\mu\sigma}+\left\{1\rightarrow\left(n-1\right)\right\}

(28)

\displaystyle\mathbb{S}^{\prime\left(1\right)}=

\displaystyle\,g_{{}_{\text{YM}}}\,\left\{\frac{1}{4}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\left(p_{1}\cdot\partial_{p_{1}}\right)+\frac{5}{8}\frac{p_{1}\cdot\epsilon(k)}{\left(p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}\right)^{2}}\right\}+\left\{1\rightarrow\left(n-1\right)\right\}

(29)

where now we’ve defined
\epsilon\left(k\right)=T_{a}\epsilon_{a}
with the
\mathfrak{su}
(N) generators
T_{a}
in the appropriate representation. Also,
J^{\mu\nu}=L^{\mu\nu}+S^{\mu\nu}
. Now let’s calculate the Chern-Simons correction. The term in the action corresponding to the 3-point vertex is given by,

\mathcal{S}_{3}=\int d^{5}x\,\mathcal{L}_{3}.

(30)

with

\mathcal{L}_{3}=\mathcal{L}_{3}^{{}^{\text{gauge}}}+\mathcal{L}_{3}^{{}^{\text{CS}}}

(31)

where
\mathcal{L}_{3}^{{}^{\text{gauge}}}
is the usual gauge-invariant action for QED or QCD and,

\mathcal{L}_{3}^{{}^{\text{CS}}}=\kappa\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma\delta}A_{\mu}^{a}\partial_{\nu}A_{\rho}^{b}\partial_{\sigma}A_{\delta}^{c}\;\text{Tr}\left\{T_{a}T_{b}T_{c}\right\}

(32)

We again use the LSZ formula for
n
gluon amplitude
\Gamma_{n}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n})
to compute the Chern-Simons correction,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(1\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\int d^{5}x_{1}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{1})}\;f_{p_{1}\gamma}^{*\,a^{\prime}_{1},h_{1}}(x_{1})\eta^{\gamma\mu^{\prime}_{1}}\;(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{1}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\prod_{j=2}^{n-1}\int d^{5}x_{j}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{j})}\;f_{p_{j}\sigma}^{*\,a_{j},h_{j}}(x_{j})\delta_{\mu_{j}}^{\sigma}\;(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{j}}\int d^{5}y\sqrt{-g(y)}\ f_{k\delta}^{*\,bh}(y)\eta^{\delta\rho}\ (-i)\mathcal{D}_{y}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int d^{5}z^{\prime}\int d^{5}z\sqrt{-g\left(z\right)}\;\big\langle\mathcal{T}A_{\mu^{\prime}_{1}}^{a^{\prime}_{1}}\left(x_{1}\right)A_{\rho}^{b}\left(y\right)\mathcal{L}_{3}^{\text{CS}}\left(z^{\prime}\right)A_{\mu_{1}}^{a_{1}}\left(z\right)\big\rangle G_{n-1}^{a_{1}\ldots a_{n-1};\mu_{1}\ldots\mu_{n-1}}\left(z,2,\ldots,n-1\right).

(33)

Similarly for
\left(n-1\right)
vertex,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(n-1\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\int d^{5}x_{n-1}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{n-1})}\;f_{p_{n-1}\gamma}^{*\,a^{\prime}_{n-1},h_{n-1}}(x_{n-1})\eta^{\gamma\mu^{\prime}_{n-1}}\;(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{n-1}}

\displaystyle\times\prod_{j=1}^{n-2}\int d^{5}x_{j}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{j})}\;f_{p_{j}\sigma}^{*\,a_{j},h_{j}}(x_{j})\delta_{\mu_{j}}^{\sigma}\;(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{j}}\int d^{5}y\sqrt{-g(y)}\ f_{k\delta}^{*\,bh}(y)\eta^{\delta\rho}\ (-i)\mathcal{D}_{y}

\displaystyle\times\int d^{5}z^{\prime}\int d^{5}z\sqrt{-g\left(z\right)}\;\big\langle\mathcal{T}A_{\mu^{\prime}_{n-1}}^{a^{\prime}_{n-1}}\left(x_{n-1}\right)A_{\rho}^{b}\left(y\right)\mathcal{L}_{3}^{\text{CS}}\left(z^{\prime}\right)A_{\mu_{n-1}}^{a_{n-1}}\left(z\right)\big\rangle G_{n-1}^{a_{1}\ldots a_{n-1};\mu_{1}\ldots\mu_{n-1}}\left(1,\ldots,n-2,z\right).

(34)

Again evaluating the time ordered correlators using Wick’s theorem,

\displaystyle\big\langle\mathcal{T}A_{\mu^{\prime}_{1}}^{a^{\prime}_{1}}\left(x_{1}\right)A_{\rho}^{b}\left(y\right)\mathcal{L}_{3}^{\text{CS}}\left(z^{\prime}\right)A_{\mu_{1}}^{a_{1}}\left(z\right)\big\rangle=

\displaystyle\qquad\kappa\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\sigma\delta}\text{Tr}\left\{T_{a}T_{d}T_{c}\right\}\big\langle\mathcal{T}A_{\mu^{\prime}_{1}}^{a^{\prime}_{1}}\left(x_{1}\right)A_{\rho}^{b}\left(y\right)A_{\mu}^{a}\left(z^{\prime}\right)\left(\partial_{\nu}A_{\eta}^{d}\left(z^{\prime}\right)\right)\left(\partial_{\sigma}A_{\delta}^{c}\right)\left(z^{\prime}\right)A_{\mu_{1}}^{a_{1}}\left(z\right)\big\rangle

\displaystyle\qquad=\kappa\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\beta\alpha}\text{Tr}\left\{T_{a}T_{d}T_{c}\right\}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\left[D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\mu}^{a_{1}^{\prime}a}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\nu}D_{\rho\eta}^{bd}(y,z^{\prime})\partial_{\beta}D_{\alpha\mu_{1}}^{ca_{1}}(z^{\prime},z)+D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\mu}^{a_{1}^{\prime}a}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\beta}D_{\rho\alpha}^{bc}(y,z^{\prime})\partial_{\nu}D_{\eta\mu_{1}}^{da_{1}}(z^{\prime},z)\right.

\displaystyle\,\qquad\left.+D_{\rho\mu}^{ba}(y,z)\partial_{\nu}D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\eta}^{a_{1}^{\prime}d}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\beta}D_{\alpha\mu_{1}}^{ca_{1}}(z^{\prime},z)+D_{\rho\mu}^{ba}(y,z)\partial_{\beta}D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\alpha}^{a_{1}^{\prime}c}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\nu}D_{\eta\mu_{1}}^{da_{1}}(z^{\prime},z))\right.

\displaystyle\,\qquad\left.+D_{\mu\mu_{1}}^{aa_{1}}(z^{\prime},z)\partial_{\nu}D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\eta}^{a_{1}^{\prime}d}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\beta}D_{\rho\alpha}^{bc}(y,z^{\prime})+D_{\mu\mu_{1}}^{aa_{1}}(z^{\prime},z)\partial_{\beta}D_{\mu_{1}^{\prime}\alpha}^{a_{1}^{\prime}c}(x_{1},z^{\prime})\partial_{\nu}D_{\rho\eta}^{bd}(y,z^{\prime})\right].

(35)

Using this and again expanding the result till
\mathcal{O}\left(\ell^{-2}\right)
and simplifying we get,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(1\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\,\frac{\kappa}{2}\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\beta\alpha}\,d^{adc}\,\int d^{5}z\,\frac{e^{-iq\cdot z}}{q^{2}}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\Biggl\{\eta_{\alpha\mu_{1}}\delta^{ca_{1}}\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)\left[k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}-\frac{ik_{\nu}z_{\beta}}{2\ell^{2}}-\frac{3i\,k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}\,(q\cdot z)}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}-\frac{3z^{2}k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}}{4\ell^{2}}+\frac{5k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}+\frac{iq_{\beta}z_{\nu}}{4\ell^{2}}\right]

\displaystyle\qquad+\eta_{\alpha\mu_{1}}\delta^{ca_{1}}\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)\left[k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}-\frac{ip_{1\beta}z_{\nu}}{2\ell^{2}}-\frac{3i\,p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}\,(q\cdot z)}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}-\frac{3z^{2}p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}}{4\ell^{2}}+\frac{5p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}+\frac{iq_{\nu}z_{\beta}}{4\ell^{2}}\right]

\displaystyle\qquad+\eta_{\alpha\mu_{1}}\delta^{ca_{1}}\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)\left[k_{\nu}p_{1\beta}-\frac{ip_{1\beta}z_{\nu}}{2\ell^{2}}-\frac{3i\,p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}\,(q\cdot z)}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}-\frac{3z^{2}p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}}{4\ell^{2}}+\frac{5p_{1\beta}k_{\nu}}{2\ell^{2}q^{2}}-\frac{ik_{\nu}z_{\beta}}{4\ell^{2}}\right]\Biggr\}

\displaystyle\qquad\times\prod_{j=2}^{n-1}\int d^{5}x_{j}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{j})}\,f_{p_{j}\sigma}^{*\,a_{j},h_{j}}(x_{j})\,\delta_{\mu_{j}}^{\sigma}(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{j}}G_{n-1}^{a_{1}\cdots a_{n-1};\mu_{1}\cdots\mu_{n-1}}(z,2,\ldots,n-1)\,.

(36)

where again
q=-p_{1}-k
and we have simplified using
\frac{i}{2}d^{abc}=\text{Tr}\left[T^{a}\left\{T^{b},T^{c}\right\}\right]
. Further expanding around the soft limit
\omega\rightarrow 0
with
\delta=\omega\,\ell
held fixed we get,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(1\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\frac{3\kappa}{4}\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\beta\alpha}d^{adc}\,\frac{\hat{k}_{\nu}p_{1\beta}}{p_{1}\cdot\hat{k}}\,\eta_{\alpha\mu_{1}}\,\delta^{ca_{1}}\,\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{1}}(p_{1})\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int d^{5}z\,e^{i\,p_{1}\cdot z}\,\prod_{j=2}^{n-1}\int d^{5}x_{j}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{j})}\,f_{p_{j}\sigma}^{*\,a_{j},h_{j}}(x_{j})\,\delta_{\mu_{j}}^{\sigma}(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{j}}G_{n-1}^{a_{1}\cdots a_{n-1};\mu_{1}\cdots\mu_{n-1}}(z,2,\ldots,n-1)\,.

(37)

Similarly for the
\left(n-1\right)
leg,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}\left(n-1\right)}(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1},k)=\,\frac{3\kappa}{4}\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\beta\alpha}d^{adc}\,\frac{\hat{k}_{\nu}p_{n-1;\beta}}{p_{n-1}\cdot\hat{k}}\,\eta_{\alpha\mu_{n-1}}\,\delta^{ca_{n-1}}\,\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{n-1}}(p_{n-1})\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)

\displaystyle\qquad\times\int d^{5}z\,e^{i\,p_{n-1}\cdot z}\,\prod_{j=1}^{n-2}\int d^{5}x_{j}\,\sqrt{-g(x_{j})}\,f_{p_{j}\sigma}^{*\,a_{j},h_{j}}(x_{j})\,\delta_{\mu_{j}}^{\sigma}(-i)\mathcal{D}_{x_{j}}G_{n-1}^{a_{1}\cdots a_{n-1};\mu_{1}\cdots\mu_{n-1}}(1,\ldots,n-2,z).

(38)

Doing the final
z
integral we get,

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}}=\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)\text{CS}}\,\Gamma_{n-1}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)

(39)

where
\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)\text{CS}}
has the same form as in
[
30
]
,

\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)\text{CS}}\,\Gamma_{n-1}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right)=\widetilde{\sum_{j}}\frac{3\kappa}{4}\,\epsilon^{\mu\nu\eta\beta\alpha}d^{ada_{j}}\,\frac{\epsilon_{\eta}^{dh}(k)\hat{k}_{\nu}}{p_{j}\cdot\hat{k}}\,\eta_{\alpha\mu_{j}}\,\epsilon_{\mu}^{ah_{j}}(p_{j})\,p_{j\beta}\;\Gamma_{n-1}^{a_{j}\mu_{j}}\left(p_{1},\ldots,p_{n-1}\right).

(40)

\widetilde{\sum_{j}}
sums over all external gluon legs in accordance with colour ordering, here
j=1\ \text{or}\ n-1
.

5
Conclusion and future directions

We showed that in de Sitter, the subleading soft theorems of Chern-Simons gauge theories have the form,

\displaystyle\Gamma_{n}(\{p_{i}\},\omega\hat{k})=

\displaystyle\,\mathcal{A}^{\text{gauge}}+\mathcal{A}^{\text{CS}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left[\frac{1}{\omega}\mathbb{S}^{\left(0\right)}+\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)}+\frac{1}{\delta^{2}}\mathbb{S}^{\prime\left(1\right)}+\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)\text{CS}}\right]\Gamma_{n-1}(\{p_{i}\}).

(41)

where the
1/\delta^{2}
de Sitter correction
\mathbb{S}^{\prime\left(1\right)}
does not receive any contribution from the Chern-Simons term. Equivalently, the subleading Chern-Simons soft factor,
\mathbb{S}^{\left(1\right)\text{CS}}
does not receive any
1/\delta^{2}
correction.

It is apparent from the derivation in §
4
that even higher order de Sitter corrections (i.e.
\mathcal{O}\left(1/\ell^{4}\right)
or higher) will not correct the Chern-Simons soft factor at the subleading
\mathcal{O}\left(\omega^{0}\right)
order. This indicates that the Chern-Simons soft factor is completely independent of the perturbative deSitter corrections.

Therefore the topological nature of these terms in the action translates to a statement at the level of amplitudes– the subleading Chern-Simons soft factor is independent of the dS metric perturbation. In this sense, these Chern-Simons soft factors are more ”universal” than the subleading gauge soft factors, which receive
1/\ell
corrections in de Sitter.

The leading and subleading soft factors are understood as Ward identities of asymptotic symmetries of the underlying gauge theory. Given the results in
[
30
]
and here, one can speculate whether these universal Chern-Simons soft factors can also be understood as in terms of an asymptotic symmetry of the theory, in both flat and curved spacetime.

Appendix A
Gluon field in de Sitter spacetime

In this section, we find the mode expansion and propagator of the gluon field in de Sitter spacetime. We closely follow
[
6
]
. The equation of motion of this field in stereographic coordinates to
O\left(\frac{1}{\ell^{2}}\right)
is given by,

\displaystyle\left(1+\frac{x^{2}}{2\ell^{2}}\right)\left(\Box_{x}A^{a}_{\mu}-\partial_{\mu}(\partial^{\nu}A^{a}_{\nu})\right)-\frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}[x.\partial A^{a}_{\mu}-\partial_{\mu}(x^{\nu}A_{\nu})+A^{a}_{\mu}]=\ j^{a}_{\mu},

(42)

where
j^{a}_{\mu}
is some
\mathfrak{su}
(N) matter current and we have only kept
\mathcal{O}(\frac{1}{\ell^{2}})
corrections to flat space equation of motion. We can fix the gauge by setting

\displaystyle\partial^{\nu}A^{a}_{\nu}-\frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}\ (x^{\nu}A^{a}_{\nu})=0.

(43)

or equivalently in momentum space,

\displaystyle ik^{\nu}\varepsilon^{ah}_{\nu}\ a^{h}_{k}\ e^{ik.x}-\frac{1}{4\ell^{2}}\varepsilon^{ah}_{\mu}x^{\mu}\ a^{h}_{k}\,e^{ik.x}=0

(44)

Note that this gauge choice is not covariant. Using the gauge condition (
43
) in the equation of motion, we obtain

\displaystyle\Box_{x}A^{a}_{\mu}-\frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}[x.\partial+1]A^{a}_{\mu}=j^{a}_{\mu}\left(1-\frac{x^{2}}{2\ell^{2}}\right).

(45)

The equations of motion (
45
) admit homogeneous solutions (i.e.
j^{a}_{\mu}=0
) of the form

\displaystyle f^{ah}_{k\mu}(x)=\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2E_{k}}}\varepsilon_{\mu}^{ah}(k)\ \left(1+\frac{1}{8}\frac{x^{2}}{\ell^{2}}\right)\ e^{ik.x},\ \ k^{2}=\frac{3}{4\ell^{2}},

(46)

where
E_{k}
is the zeroth component of
k^{\mu}
. After fixing all redundant degrees of freedom of the gluon field, one is left with
3
physical degrees of freedom. Here we have used
h
to denote these physical helicity states with
h
taking values from 1 to
3
.

The inner product on the solution space is defined as

\displaystyle(g^{\mu\nu}\ f^{ah}_{k\mu},f^{a^{\prime}h^{\prime}}_{k^{\prime}\nu})=

\displaystyle-i\int d^{4}x\sqrt{-g}\ g^{\mu\nu}(x)\ e^{-\epsilon\frac{|\vec{x}|}{R}}\ [\ f^{*ah}_{k\mu}(t,\vec{x})\ \partial^{t}f^{a^{\prime}h^{\prime}}_{k^{\prime}\nu}(t,\vec{x})-f^{a^{\prime}h^{\prime}}_{k^{\prime}\mu}(t,\vec{x})\ \partial^{t}f^{*ah}_{k\nu}(t,\vec{x})\ ]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\ {(2\pi)^{4}}\delta_{h,h^{\prime}}\delta^{a^{\prime}a}\delta^{(4)}(\vec{k}-\vec{k^{\prime}}).

(47)

where we have again added exponential damping factor to get rid of the boundary terms. Hence, the gluon field can be expanded in terms of the above modes as follows

\displaystyle A^{a}_{\mu}(x)=\sum_{h=1}^{3}\int\frac{d^{4}\vec{k}}{(2\pi)^{4}}[a^{h}_{k}\ f_{k\mu}^{ah}(x)+a^{h\dagger}_{k}\ f_{k\mu}^{*ah}(x)].

Now let us look at the Feynman propagator of the gluon field. Again following the analysis for photons in
[
6
]
we obtain,

\Big[\Box_{x}-\ \frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}\ [x.\partial+1]\ \Big]D^{aa^{\prime}}_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}(x,x^{\prime})=i\eta_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}\delta^{aa^{\prime}}\delta^{5}(x-x^{\prime})\left[1+\frac{1}{4}\frac{x^{2}}{\ell^{2}}\right].

(48)

This can also be rewritten by defining the derivative operator,

\mathcal{D}_{x}\equiv\left(1+\frac{x^{2}}{\ell^{2}}\right)\Box_{x}-\frac{1}{2\ell^{2}}\left(x\cdot\partial+1\right)

(49)

such that now,

\mathcal{D}_{x}D^{aa^{\prime}}_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}(x,x^{\prime})=i\,\eta_{\mu\nu}\delta^{\left(5\right)}\left(x-x^{\prime}\right)\left(1+\frac{5}{4}\frac{x^{2}}{\ell^{2}}\right)

(50)

The solution to the propagator is given by

D^{aa^{\prime}}_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}(x,y)=-i\,\eta_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}\delta^{aa^{\prime}}\int\frac{d^{5}k}{(2\pi)^{5}}\frac{e^{ik.(x-y)}}{k^{2}}\Big[1+\frac{1}{\ell^{2}k^{2}}-\frac{1}{2}\frac{ik.y}{\ell^{2}}+\frac{1}{4}\frac{y^{2}}{\ell^{2}}\Big].

(51)

The propagator also admits an equivalent symmetric form in variables
x
and
y
given by,

D^{aa^{\prime}}_{\mu\nu^{\prime}}(x,y)=-i\eta_{\mu\nu}\ \delta^{aa^{\prime}}\int\frac{d^{5}k}{(2\pi)^{5}}\frac{e^{ik.(x-y)}}{k^{2}-\frac{3}{4\ell^{2}}}\Big[1+\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}}{8\ell^{2}}\Big].

(52)

One can use integration by parts to show that these two forms are equivalent.
