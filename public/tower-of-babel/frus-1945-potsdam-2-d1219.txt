No. 1219 The Secretary of State to the Ambassador in China (Hurley)

top secret

[Babelsberg,] July 28, 1945.

Please deliver as soon as possible the following message to Doctor Soong: I understand you were to communicate with Marshal Stalin by 30th or 31st July. I think it important that before that date you communicate with him here through Moscow requesting opportunity to return to Moscow and continue discussions in hope of reaching agreement.

Source: 761.93/7–2845: Telegram
