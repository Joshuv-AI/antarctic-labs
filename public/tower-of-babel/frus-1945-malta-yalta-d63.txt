President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill [1] (22 January 1945, d63)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 22 January 1945.

Number 704, Personal and Top Secret, for the Prime Minister from the President.

I am in full agreement with the suggestion regarding press representatives and photographers made in your 891.[2]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Churchill replied, in his telegram No. 892, “Please tell U. J.” (Roosevelt Papers).
