United Nations Security Council Resolution 29, adopted 1947.

The Security Council,
Having received and considered the report submitted by the Committee on the Admission of New Members regarding the re-examination of the applications for membership in the United Nations of the People’s Republic of Albania, the Mongolian People’s Republic, the Hashemite Kingdom of Transjordan, Ireland and Portugal, and the examination of the applications of Hungary, Italy, Roumania, Austria, Yemen and Bulgaria,
Having received and considered the application of Pakistan,
Having taken due notice of the statements of opinions of the members of the Security Council in regard to these applications,
Recommends to the General Assembly that it admit to membership in the United Nations the following applicants: Yemen and Pakistan.
