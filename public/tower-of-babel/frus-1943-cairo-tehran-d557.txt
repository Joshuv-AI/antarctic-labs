Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (4 March 1944, d557)

secret

London, 4 March 1944.

601. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt personal and most secret.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

About the Italian ships et cetera. I was much startled by the press accounts of your talk with them. The Russians have never asked for one-third of the Italian ships, but only for the specific vessels mentioned at Moscow and agreed to by us at Teheran. See list which follows. We have never agreed, as you know, to anything beyond this.…

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels.
