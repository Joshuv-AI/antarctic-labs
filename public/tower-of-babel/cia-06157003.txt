5. ON 8 DECEMBER APPROXIMATELY 350 LPA AND PAVN SOLDIERS
FROM VIENTIANE WERE OEPLOYED TO BAN KOK KRA (PC 61311, BAN HATXAT
(PC 6503) AND HOEUI THONG (PC 6622). EACH UNIT HAD ONE 82MM
MORTAR, THREE 60MM MORTARS AND A LARGE NUMBER OF B-40 ROCKET
LAUNCHERS AND AK-47 ASSAULT RIFLES. ON 11 DECEMBER THIS COMBINED
LPA-PAVN UNIT CONDUCTED SWEEP OPERATIONS AND CLASHED WITH LRF
TROOPS ON THREE OCCASIONS. SEVEN LRF TROOPS WERE KILLED AND AN
UNKNOWN NUMBER WERE WOUNDED. LPA-PAVN CASUALTIES WERE UNKNOWN.
THESE UNITS WITHDREW TO VIENTIANE ON 18 DECEMBER.

6. ON 12 DECEMBER A JOINT PAVN-LPA BATTALION-SIZED UNIT

FORCES OF- MAJOR ((CHANGSOUK)) KAEOQLA, THE ATTACK HAPPENED NEAR THE
NAM PHA STREAM IN THE VICINITY OF MUANG LONG (PD 8617). SEVEN

NO FURTHER CONTACT WAS REPORTED.

(PB 8866) IN SAYABOURI PROVINCE ORDERED THE LEADERS OF THE 13 MAJOR
