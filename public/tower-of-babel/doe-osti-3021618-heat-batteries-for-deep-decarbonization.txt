 1 Overall Progress Summary

 There has been significant progress towards the Go/No-Go Review Criteria for both sub-
 projects to prepare the project to enter Budget Period 2 in July:
 - Completion of the Pre-FEED deliverable for Shelbyville
 - Continued progress on further development of the design and engineering for
 Shelbyville and the initiation of design and engineering for Plainfield
 - Continued progress work for TEA and TPC deliverables for Shelbyville and Plainfield
 - Continued progress work towards the Permitting Plan and NEPA documentation for
 Shelbyville
 - Continued progress towards Financial Commitment deliverables for Phase 2 with Diageo
 Americas Supply and Rondo Energy
 - Completion of the updated Project Management Plan
 - Completion of the updated Risk Management Plan

 The Rondo team has finalized the Pre-FEED Deliverable. The Pre-FEED now provides a more
 updated Basis of Design in addition to supporting more detailed budgetary and equipment
 sizing information. The team is currently evaluating the design and financial implications to be
 further established by the end of April.

 For Plainfield, the Project Team is currently evaluating the solar requirements for the Plainfield
 facility. In addition, initial data analysis has commenced with Rondo.
