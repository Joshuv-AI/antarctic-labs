'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 3251st meeting, on 8 July 1993'''


''The Security Council'',

''Having examined'' the application of the Principality of Andorra for admission to
the United Nations (S/26039),

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Principality of Andorra be admitted
to membership in the United Nations.
