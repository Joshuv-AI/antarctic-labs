Targeting p130Cas- and microtubule-dependent MYC regulation sensitizes pancreatic cancer to ERK MAPK inhibition

There are errors in two figures of this paper. In Figure 6C, the p130Cas, total ERK, and phospho-p130Cas immunoblots inadvertently display alternative exposures from Figure 3D. In Figure S10B, images of four KX2-391-treated ex vivo tumors were inadvertently duplicated in the ERKi-treated ex vivo tumors. Although the paper itself cannot be updated due to the amount of time that has passed since its original publication, this correction notice presents the corrected figures for the scientific record.

KX2-391 + ERKi combinations drive robust synergistic apoptosis (corrected)

Neither issue affected the interpretation of the data or the conclusions of the study. The authors apologize for any confusion that the errors may have caused.
