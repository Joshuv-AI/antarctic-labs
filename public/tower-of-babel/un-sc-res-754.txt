'''Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 3077th meeting, on 18 May 1992''' ([1])

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of the Republic of Slovenia for admission to the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Republic of Slovenia be admitted to membership in the United Nations.


[1] Adopted without vote.
