Lemma 2.1
.

The identity automorphism of dihedral group
D_{l}
has exactly
l+2
involutions when
l
is even, and
l+1
involutions when
l
is odd.

Proof.

Since every element of
D_{l}
can be expressed uniquely in one of the forms
r^{k}
or
r^{k}s
, where
0\leq k<l
. We first determine when such elements are involutions. Suppose the rotation
r^{k}
for some
k
, is an involution, then
k
satisfies
2k\equiv 0\pmod{l}
. There are two possible cases:
l
is even or
l
is odd.

Case (i):

l
is even, then the congruence has two solutions, precisely
k=0,\frac{l}{2}
.

Case (ii):

l
is odd, then the congruence has a unique solution, i.e.,
k=0
.

Since, every reflection is an element of order
2
, so that, there are precisely
l
reflections in
D_{l}
. Therefore the total number of involutions of identity automorphism of
D_{l}
are

\displaystyle\begin{cases}l+2,&\text{if $l$ is even},\\[4.0pt]
l+1,&\text{if $l$ is odd}.\end{cases}

∎
