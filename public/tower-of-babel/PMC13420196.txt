Correction to: “Alpha-klotho, Insulin Resistance, and Adiposity Among Older Adults”
Published: 2026

In the above-named article by Ahmad A, Shardell M, Rastogi Kalyani R, Semba RD, Mitchell BD, Tanaka T, Taylor S, Shrestha A, Ferrucci L (J Endo Society. 2026; 10(7): bvag140; doi: 10.1210/jendso/bvag140), there was a typographical error in the abstract.

In the originally published article, in the abstract, there is a typographical error. In the first sentence under Main outcome measures, it reads, “The primary outcome was Homeostatic Model Assessment for Insulin Diabetes (HOMA-IR).” In the corrected article, this sentence reads, “The primary outcome was Homeostatic Model Assessment for Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR).” The authors regret the error.

The article has been corrected online.
