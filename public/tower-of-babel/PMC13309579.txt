In this article, the legends of figure 4 and 5 has been incorrectly published.

For completeness and transparency, the correct figure legends have been attached below with the respective figures.

Fig. 4. The PGE2-EP4 pathway is critical to mitochondrial dysfunction in human cardiomyocytes. (A) Effect of Nab treatment on prostaglandin (PGE2, PGD2, TBXA2, PGI2, PGF2α) production in hPCMs (n = 3 patients). (B-C) Pretreatment with PGE2 for 3 h attenuates the effects of Nab on mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP) and mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP) opening in hPCMs (n = 5 patients). (D-F) Pretreatment with EP3 and EP4 receptor agonists for 3 h modulates MMP, mPTP opening, and ROS production in hPCMs (MMP, mPTP: n = 3 patients; ROS: n = 4 patients). (G) Pretreatment with EP3 and EP4 receptor antagonists for 3 h reverses the effects of Nab on MMP, mPTP opening, and ROS production in hPCMs (MMP, mPTP: n = 3 patients; ROS: n = 4 patients). (H-I) Pretreatment with the EP4 agonist compound 31 for 3 h attenuates the effects of Nab on oxygen consumption rate (OCR) in hPCMs. (H) Representative OCR traces. (I) Quantification of OCR (n = 6 patients). (J-L) Pretreatment with the EP4 agonist for 3 h attenuates the effects of Nab on mitochondrial complex activity in hPCMs. (J) Representative OCR traces. (K, L) Quantification of mitochondrial complex activity (n = 4 patients). (M-O) Pretreatment with the EP4 agonist for 3 h attenuates the effects of Nab on electrophysiological action potentials in hPCMs. (M) Representative action potential (AP) traces. (N, O) Quantification of action potential parameters (n = 3 patients). Data are presented as mean ± SEM. Statistical significance was determined by Student’s t-test for two groups and repeated measures one-way ANOVA followed by Dunn’s post-hoc test for three or more groups. *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01
