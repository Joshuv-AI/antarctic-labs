Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

This TCF will focus on developing use-cases for scalable and optimal utilization of hydrogen systems
(combination of electrolyzers, hydrogen storage, and fuel cells) when integrated with the power grid. We
will utilize and mature the electrolyzer dispatch control developed in SWR-20-06.

Summary of Research Results:

Task 1 Collaboration on experimental design of the AWAKEN project

NREL and GE Renewable Energy will jointly model the AWAKEN experiment and optimize
sensor choice and placement to achieve the overall science goals. GE which has unique
knowledge of its turbines will advise and run existing models of different operating conditions to
determine their impacts on wind plant to atmospheric interactions. The range of conditions will
include variations in the atmosphere, and different turbine operating strategies such as those
needed for wind plant control. GE Renewable Energy and NREL will jointly define control
schemes to be deployed and tested during the AWAKEN project. GE will support NREL’s
modeling studies by providing sufficient turbine information required to model the experiment.
GE will not produce any Computer Software under this Task.

Task 2 Collaboration on instrumentation development

GE Renewable Energy and NREL have experience with wake observation campaigns and
instrumentation required to gather quantities of interest. GE Renewable Energy will advise
NREL on the critical quantities of interest for turbine manufacturers and model validation. GE
Renewable Energy will also share experience with different sensing devices and their ability to
measure different phenomena. GE Renewable Energy will advise on mounting and interface of
any wind-turbine-mounted sensors. GE will not produce any Computer Software under this Task.

Task 3 Collaboration on field measurement and analysis

GE Renewable Energy and NREL will jointly participate in the field measurement and analysis
of the AWAKEN project. GE will provide NREL with GE operational data of the turbines within
the wind farm. GE Renewable Energy and NREL will jointly analyze data and publish results at
conferences and in journals. GE will not produce any Computer Software under this task.

This statement of work is intended to facilitate research and development and dialogue on the
kinds of joint activity which could be undertaken in joint research between GE Renewable
Energy and NREL. It is not intended to exclude further research possibilities as they arise but as
a starting point for research into power losses due to wakes which will benefit both parties. Any
additional technical topics as mutually agreed will be developed through a modification to this
CRADA. Prior to such modifications being developed, NREL will convert this CRADA to a
multiproject CRADA in compliance with DOE procedures. If no sites with GE turbines are
selected for study under the AWAKEN project, then GE and NREL agree to modify all tasks that
pertain specifically to GE turbines accordingly.
