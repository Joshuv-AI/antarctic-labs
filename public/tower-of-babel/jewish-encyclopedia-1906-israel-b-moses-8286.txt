Polish cabalist; lived at the end of the sixteenth century. He is known only through his book "Tamim Yaḥdaw," in which all verses of the Psalms and the Proverbs found in the Zohar are collected, with the explanations there given. The collection is preceded by a cabalistic treatise on the soul and a "baḳḳashah" by the compiler.

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim; Zunz, Literaturgesch. p. 420. K. P. B.
