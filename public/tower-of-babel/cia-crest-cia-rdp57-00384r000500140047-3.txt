3. Military personnel who are found unfit by reason of physical disa-
bility for active duty may be placed on the temporary disability -retired
list for a period up to five years and receive compensation during this
period whether or not the disability was related directly to their duty.

"While on the temporary disability list they receive an amount equal to the
monthly basic pay of the rank held at the time they are put on the disabi-

If the officer is retired for physical disability in excess of 30$ and
contracted in the line of duty, he receives the disability percentage of
his base pay (not to exceed 73$). This is calculated in much the same manner
as the civilian employee's disability annuity, and carries the same top
limit.

d. If both employees are killed in line of duty (with the mili-
