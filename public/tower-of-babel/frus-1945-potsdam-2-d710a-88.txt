Editorial Note (Potsdam 1945 Vol. II, Unnumbered document following Document 710 (#88))

—No minutes of this conversation have been found. The conversation and the subject discussed are referred to, however, in the following memorandum of July 24 (in the Truman Papers) by Captain James K. Vardaman, Jr., Naval Aide to the President: “Remind the President Wednesday morning to write a letter to Mr. Churchill and send him a copy of telegram despatched to Generalissimo Chiang Kai Shek and also copy of revised Lend Lease document read to Churchill by Leahy on the porch this morning.”[1]

Notes
[1] For the letter which was sent to Churchill on July 25, see documents Nos. 1183 and 1248, post. The “revised Lend Lease document” referred to has not been identified. Cf. the enclosure to document No. 1185, post.
