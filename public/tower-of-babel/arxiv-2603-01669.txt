Overcolored Partition Restricted by Parity of the Parts
M. P. Thejitha, S. N. Fathima

Abstract
Very recently, Thejitha, Sellers, and Fathima defined the function a r , s ​ ( n ) a_{r,s}(n) , which enumerates the number of multicolored partitions of n n , wherein both even parts and odd parts may appear in one of r r -colors and s s -colors, respectively, for fixed r , s ≥ 1 r,s\geq 1 . In this paper, we extend the concept to overpartitions. Keywords: Partitions, Colored partitions, Overpartitions, Generating function, Congruences. Mathematics Subject Classification (2020): 05A17, 11P83.

Introduction

Let
n

\displaystyle(a;q)_{0}:=1,\;\;(a;q)_{n}:=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^{j}),\;\;\text{and}\;\;(a;q)_{\infty}=\prod_{n=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^{n}).

We denote by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_{k}(n)q^{n}=\dfrac{1}{f_{1}^{k}},

\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2},...,\lambda_{r}

Agarwal and Andrews

r
-colors and
s

and denoted by

. The generating function for
a_{r,s}(n)
is given by

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_{r,s}(n)q^{n}=\dfrac{f_{2}^{s-r}}{f_{1}^{s}}.

(1.1)

The case

are first studied by Hirschhorn and Sellers
[

An overpartition of

n

\displaystyle 3,\bar{3},2+1,\bar{2}+1,2+\bar{1},\bar{2}+\bar{1},1+1+1,\bar{1}+1+1.
