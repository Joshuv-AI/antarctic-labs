Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (16 October 1943, d45)

secret

London, October 16, 1943.

No. 459. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

I entirely agree with the telegram you have sent to Uncle Joe about Eureka. Let me know what he replies.

Please also see Saint Mark 9 verses 5 and 6 especially sixth verse.[2]

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Apparently sent via military channels.
[2] The following footnote was typed on the source text by the White House Map Room: “Saint Mark 9 “5. And Peter answered and said to Jesus, Master, it is good for us to be here: and let us make three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias. “6. For he wist not what to say; for they were sore afraid.”
