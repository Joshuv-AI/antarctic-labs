Director of Political-Military Affairs
National Security Council

SUBJECT: Execution of U.S. POW's in 1970s
REFERENCE: Your Memo dated 8 February 1985

In response to your query of 8 February 1985, a review of
our holdings did not reveal any specific Agency intelligence
reports concerning large scale execution of American POW's.
The only reference to large scale execution was sourced to a
contained in a 1974 debriefing report

[that a group of 1,5(c)
‘American pilots in Sam Neua prison camp in Laos were 4.6(d)(1)
executed by the Pathet Lao. did not have any

further details on the execution. Our search also revealed

two reports, one from DIA (dated August 1976) and one from

OJCS-C, Washington, D.C., (dated Oct 80) which refer to the

killing of American POW's. Copies of these two reports are

attached.

In view of the August 76 DIA report, it is possible that
DIA may have additional reporting concerning the execution of
American POW's. You may find it worthwhile to query them on
this matter.
