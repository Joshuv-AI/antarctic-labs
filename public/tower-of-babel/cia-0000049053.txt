COUNTRY: EL SALVADOR
SUBJ: EXISTENCE OF RIGHTIST DEATH SQUAD WITHIN THE SALVADORAN
NATIONAL POLICE; LOCATION OF CLANDESTINE PRISON USED
BY THE DEATH SQUAD.

DOT: DECEMBER 1979-18 MARCH 1983

Approved for Release
NOV 1493

TEXT: 1, A ING TO MEMBERS OF THE SALVADORAN NATIONAL
A RIGHTIST DEATH SQUAD HAS
EXISTED SINCE DECEMBER 1979 AND HAS
ENGAGED IN NUMEROUS POLITICAL ASSASSINATIONS, INCLUDING THE
MARCH 1981 MURDER OF ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARNULFO ((ROMERO)). ey
GROUP IS HEADED BY LIEUTENANT COLONEL ARISTEDES ((MARQUEZ)),
CHIEF OF THE INVESTIGATIONS DEPARTMENT OF THE NATIONAL POLICE.
IT MAINTAINS A CLANDESTINE PRISON IN THE CITY OF LA LIBERTAD,
LA LIBERTAD DEPARTMENT, THE PRISON IS USED FOR DETENTION,
INTERROGATION, AND EXECUTION OF SUSPECTED LEFTISTS AND LEFTIST
SYMPATHIZERS. IT IS SUPERVISED BY CAPTAIN RAFAEL ((LOPEZ))
DAVILA WHO IS CHIEF OF THE POLITICAL SECTION OF THE NATIONAL
POLICE AND MARQUEZ' DEPUTY. AMONG THOSE WHO HAVE BEEN HELD IN
THE PRISON WERE THE ALLEGED KIDNAPPERS OF WEALTHY SALVADORAN,
JEREZ DE ((GARCIA-PRIETO)). AT LEAST EIGHTEEN INDIVIDUALS

HAVE BEEN EXECUTED IN THE PRISON WHICH CONSISTS OF A SERIES

OF BUILDINGS IN A GUARDED COMPOUND LOCATED AT KILOMETER 38

ON THE PACIFIC HIGHWAY, LA LIBERTAD.

2. THE DEATH SQUAD HAS DRAWN MOST OF ITS MEMBERS FROM
THREE SECTIONS OF THE NATIONAL POLICE: THE CRIMINAL
INVESTIGATION SECTION (SIC), THE SPECIAL POLITICAL INVESTIGATION
SECTION (SIE), AND THE NARCOTICS CONTROL SECTION. WHEN THE
DEATH SQUAD WAS FORMED, IT WAS HEADED BY CAPTAIN AMILCAR

((MOLINA)) PANAMENO. MOLINA'S GROUP PLANNED AND CARRIED OUT

THE ASSASSINATION OF ARCHBISHOP ROMERO, IN WHICH A .223 CALIBRE
RIFLE WAS USED. THE ROMERO ASSASSINATION SQUAD CONSISTED OF
DETECTIVE SIGIFREDO ((PEREZ)) LINARES, INSPECTOR MISAEL ((RIVAS))
ROMERO, JOSE LUIS ((ALAS)) ROMERO, LUIS ((RODRIGUEZ)), AND FNU
((SANTIN)). MOLINA HIMSELF LATER WAS ASSASSINATED BY ANOTHER
DEATH SQUAD LED BY MAJOR MARIO DENIS ((MORAN)), A NATIONAL GUARD
OFFICER. MOLINA WAS SUSPECTED OF SYMPATHIZING WITH THE LEFT.
ALAS, ONE OF THE PARTICIPANTS IN THE ASSASSINATION, NOW RESIDES
ILLEGALLY IN HOUSTON, TEXAS.

3. FOLLOWING THE DEATH OF MOLINA, COMMAND OF THE DEATH
SQUAD PASSED TO INSPECTOR RIVAS WHO CONTINUED THE POLICY OF
ASSASSINATING SUSPECTED LEFTISTS, INCLUDING SALVADORAN JOURNALIST
ROBERTO ((SUAREZ)) SUAY, KILLED IN MID-1981. THE DEATH SQUAD
UNDER RIVAS WAS COMPRISED OF CARLOS ALBERTO ((ORELLANA) )
APARICIO, ISMAEL ((PARADA)) CACERES, JOSE ROBERTO ((RECINOS))
MARROQUIN, EFRAIN ((LARIOS)) MONROY, CARLOS ((ALVARADO)) GOMEZ,
MARTO SAUL ((MERINO)) ESCOBAR, EMMANUEL ((ALFARO)) VILLANUEVA,

IN MID-1982, APPROXIMATELY 25 OF THE DEATH SQUAD MEMBERS,

ALONG WITH OTHER MEMBERS OF THE NATIONAL POLICE, WERE SENT TO
VENEZUELA FOR SPECIAL TRAINING. DURING THEIR ABSENCE, DEATH
SQUAD ACTIVITIES WERE CONDUCTED BY A GROUP HEADED BY SECOND
LIEUTENANT SAUL TADEO ((SALAZAR)) ARDON, A CLOSE FRIEND OF
LIEUTENANT COLONEL MARQUEZ. SALARZAR'S GROUP INCLUDED BELARMIDES
((CASTILLO)) MEDRANO, SANTOS ((ULLOA)), FIDEL ((RAMOS)) RIVERA,
CONCEPCION ((AGUILAR)), INES ALFONSO ((ESCOBAR)), ROBERTO
((CHAVEZ)) SOAS, AND (FNU) ((FERNANDEZ)) HERNANDEZ. (Qi
COMMENT: IT IS NOT KNOWN WHETHER THE VENEZUELANS WERE AWARE

OF THE DEATH SQUAD ROLE OF THESE POLICE OFFICERS.)

4, FOLLOWING THE RETURN OF THE DEATH SQUAD MEMBERS FROM
VENEZUELA IN FEBRUARY 1983, INSPECTOR RIVAS RESUMED HIS ROLE
AS THE CHIEF DIRECTOR OF DEATH SQUAD ACTIVITIES. AS OF MID-MARCH,
DEATH SQUAD OPERATIONS WERE PLANNED BY LIEUTENANT COLONEL MARQUEZ
AND CARRIED OUT BY LIEUTENANT RIVAS AND CAPTAIN LOPEZ WHO ALSO
RUNS THE PRISON IN LA LIBERTAD. DETECTIVE PEREZ SERVED AS AN
INTERMEDIARY BETWEEN THE DEATH SQUAD LEADERSHIP AND ROBERTO

((D'AUBUISSON)), PRESIDENT OF THE SALVADORAN NATIONAL —
AND THE NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE (ARENA).

COMMENT: DEATH SQUAD MEMBERS BELIEVE D'AUBUISSON IS RESPONSIBLE
FOR SELECTING SOME OF THE LEFTISTS TO BE INTERROGATED OR EXECUTED
BY THE NATIONAL POLICE GROUP.)
