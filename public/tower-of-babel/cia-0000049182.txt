2. ON 12 DECEMBER 1989, MAJOR JUAN VICENTE ( (EGUIZABAL) )
FIGUEROA, DEPUTY CHIEF OF THE C-1} (INTELLIGENCE) OF THE SALVADORAN
GENERAL STAFF, ORDERED THAT A LIST OF FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL
LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN) INSURGENTS AND SYMPATHIZERS BE PREPARED.
THE LIST WAS TO BE PREPARED BY RESEARCHING INACTIVE ARCHIVES OF
KNOWN COMMUNIST SYMPATHIZERS AS WELL AS BY UTILIZING CURRENT
INFORMATION. EGUIZABAL'S INTENTION WAS TO USE THE LIST AS THE
BASIS FOR A CLANDESTINE CAMPAIGN TO PHYSICALLY ELIMINATE THE
LEADERSHIP AND SUPPORT APPARATUS OF THE FMLN.
