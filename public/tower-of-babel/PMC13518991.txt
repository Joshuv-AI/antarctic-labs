Diastereoselective and green synthesis of novel Hexahydro Imidazo [1,2-a] pyrimidine, from Azlactones, Brimonidine and Clonidine
Authors: Keikavoos Rahmati, Ali A Mohammadi, Vahid Amani, Mohammad Reza Halvgar
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractAn efficient direct domino approach has been developed for the diastereoselective synthesis of novel hexahydroimidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine-6-yl acetamides. This synthesis involves a domino Michael addition/cyclization reaction of azlactones with brimonidine (used as eye drops for treating open-angle glaucoma) and clonidine (used to treat high blood pressure and attention deficit hyperactivity disorder). The reaction is catalyzed by glacial acetic acid (GAA), which acts as a mild organocatalyst in a metal-free environment, adhering to green chemistry principles by utilizing green solvent (EtOH). Additionally, the transformation proceeds with an atom economy of 100%. The imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine moiety plays a crucial role as a benzodiazepine receptor agonist, antiviral, antibacterial, antifungal agent, and calcium channel blocker. This reaction has been performed for the first time, and all synthesized compounds are novel. Their structures were confirmed by single-crystal X-ray crystallography, as well as IR, 1H NMR, 13C NMR spectroscopy, and LCMS analysis. Surprisingly, this reaction was diastereoselective, yielding cis-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines 4b–g and trans-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines 6a-g in cyclocondensation reaction of brimonidine 1 or clonidine 2 with azlactones 3a-g, respectively.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1038/s41598-026-55668-6.Keywords: Imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine; Azlactone; Brimonidine; Clonidine; Diasteroselectivity; Green chemistrySubject terms: Chemistry, Drug discovery

Introduction

Heterocyclic compounds are an important class of organic compounds, especially nitrogen-containing heterocycles such as imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines. These compounds have significant biological and pharmacological activities. Imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines and dihydropyridines have been reported in structure of medicines with anti-inflammatory1, antibacterial2,3, antidiarrheal4, antitumor5, antitubercular6,7, antiprotozoal8, antioxidant9, antifungal10,11, antimicrobial12–14, antiviral15, and calcium channel blocker16 properties.

Numerous synthetic routes for imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine skeletons have been reported. These routes include 1,3-dipolar cyclization17, condensation reactions with β-ketoacetals18,19, Baylis-Hillman reaction20,21, pseudo-Michael reaction22–24, Click reaction25,26, and Biginelli multicomponent reactions27. However, these investigations remain in early stages.

Brimonidine 1 is used as an eye drop to treat open-angle glaucoma28,29 or ocular hypertension, as well as facial skin redness (erythema) in rosacea in gel form. It treats redness by vasoconstriction, acting as an α2-adrenergic agonist30. α2 Agonists inhibit adenylate cyclase activity via G protein-coupled receptor activation, causing vasoconstriction.

Clonidine 2 is used to treat hypertension, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, drug withdrawal symptoms, menopausal flushing, diarrhea, and certain pain conditions31. It lowers blood pressure by stimulating α2 receptors in the brain, relaxing arteries32. It also reduces pulse rate and serum concentrations of renin, aldosterone, and catecholamines (Fig. 1)33.

Methods

In our ongoing work on the preparation of bioactive nitrogen-containing heterocyclic compounds39,40, we designed a synthesis of novel imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines catalyzed by glacial acetic acid as an organocatalyst (Scheme 1).

Synthesis of cis-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine 4a.

A mixture of brimonidine 1 and azlactone 3a41–45 in ethanol was stirred at reflux in the presence of a catalytic amount of GAA, completing the reaction within 3.5 h. Workup afforded imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine 4a in 84% yield (Scheme 1). Notably, the reaction was diastereoselective, producing the cis-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine 4a.

The X-ray crystallographic analysis of compounds 4 g and 6d was performed to confirm their molecular structures, as detailed in the Supplementary Information (Tables S1, S2, Figs. S1–S4). Compound 4 g crystallizes in the triclinic Pī space group with a dimethyl sulfoxide solvate, while 6d, a twinned crystal, adopts the monoclinic P21/c space group. Due to instrumental limitations, data were collected at 293 K with a modest θ(max) ≈ 25°, and the twinned nature of 6d necessitated a higher I/σ(I) cutoff (threshold multiplier = 4.0) and the use of DELU/SIMU restraints (approximately 576 for 6d) to ensure reliable refinement, with a twin law [1 0 2] and BASF ≈ 0.46 applied for 6d. The observed/unique reflection ratios (4 g: 49%, Rint = 0.1069; 6d: 28%, Rint = 0.1850) and goodness-of-fit values (4 g: S = 0.769; 6d: S = 0.659) reflect the weak data quality, particularly for 6d, where twinning and low crystal quality limited precision (C-C bond precision ≈ 0.0082 Å for 4 g). Absorption corrections were applied using numerical integration methods, though full structure factors for 4 g were not included due to data constraints. A long H···A contact (H21···O2 = 2.65 Å) in 4 g was retained after verifying geometry, and the DMSO solvate in 4 g was modeled with appropriate restraints. These crystallographic data, while confirming the connectivity and stereochemistry of the synthesized compounds, are supplementary to the primary focus of this study on the diastereoselective synthesis of hexahydro imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines, with limitations in data quality attributed to equipment constraints and the absence of low-temperature data collection. These crystallographic data are of limited precision and used solely for confirmation of connectivity and stereochemistry, not for detailed conformational or metric analysis.

The observed divergent diastereoselectivity in the cyclization reactions cis products with brimonidine and trans products with clonidine can be attributed to steric and electronic effects during ring closure. The measured dihedral angles (H5–C5–C8–H9 ca. 169° for trans; H9–C8–C5–H10 ca. 49° for cis) and the corresponding J-coupling constants support the proposed stereochemical rationale, highlighting the influence of substituent interactions on the preferred cyclization pathway.

Therefore, these reactions are diastereoselective, yielding exclusively the cis or trans products depending on the starting compound.

Experimental and subject details

The progress of the reactions was monitored by thin layer chromatography (TLC) using Merck silica gel (70–230 mesh) plates. IR spectra were recorded in KBr pellets on a Shimadzu IR 470 spectrophotometer. 1H NMR (400 MHz) and 13C NMR (100 MHz) spectra were determined on a Q.One AS400 and 1H NMR (300 MHz) and 13C NMR (75 MHz) spectra were determined on a Bruker 300 DRX Avance instrument. Single crystal data were collected on diffractometer equipped with a STOE IPDS/2T imaging plate detector, T = 293(2) K, MoKa radiation (µMoKa = 0.71073 Å), graphite monochromator, Structure solution by direct methods, full-matrix-least-squares refinement against F. Mass of the pure isolated products were determined by LCMS-2010 A Shimadzu instrument.

General procedure for the preparation of compounds 4 (a-g) and 6 (a-g)

A mixture of brimonidine 1 or clonidine 2 (1mmol), azlactones 3a-g (1mmol), and glacial acetic acid (20 mol%) in EtOH (4 mL, 99–100% purity) in a 50 mL flask was stirred under reflux (78–80 °C) for the times indicated in Tables 2 and 4. Reaction progress was monitored by TLC (ethyl acetate/ n-hexane, 1:2, for 4a Rf = 0.15). After completion of the reaction, the solvent was evaporated at room temperature, and the resulting solid was filtered. The crude product was washed with EtOH: Water (1:1, 2 × 5 mL) to yield a white solid. The washed products were characterized as was obtained and no further purification or recrystallization was needed.

Conclusions

We have described a stereoselective strategy and convenient green synthesis for the preparation of cis-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine and trans-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidines in cyclocondensation reaction of brimonidine 1 or clonidine 2 (1mmol) and azlactones 3a-g (1mmol), respectively. The reactions are performed in EtOH, 20 mol% glacial acetic acid, mild, and one-pot conditions with simple work-up improving sustainablity. This method offers several advantages including high yield of products, stereoselective strategy, no need for metal catalysts, EtOH as a relatively benign green solvent, with atom 100% atom economy, and easy experimental workup procedure, which makes it a useful process for the synthesis of cis-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine and trans-imidazo[1,2-a]pyrimidine derivatives. Moreover, starting from biologically active medicines such as brimonidine and clonidine provides a basis for potential future bioevaluation of these novel derivatives.
