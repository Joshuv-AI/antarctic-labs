Spanish Talmudist of the fifteenth century. He was the author of a commentary (unpublished) to Ecclesiastes, Esther, and the Haggadah ("Cat. De Rossi," No. 177). He is quoted in the Responsa of Joseph di Trani (i., No. 33).

Bibliography: De Rossi, Dizionario, i. 79; Michael, Or ha-Ḥayyim, No. 111. K. M. Sel.
