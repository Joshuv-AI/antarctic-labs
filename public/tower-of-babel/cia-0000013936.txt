During the period 1100 - 1115 hours on 2 September 1966, a group of 30 U.S.
prisorérs described as pilots were observed along the north bank of the
HOAN KIEM Lake (WJ 890253) in the city of HANOI, These prisoners were
being, dscorted on a sight-seeing tour of the city by guards in military
uniform.some of whom spoke English.

A large group of people congregated around the prisoners and began to shout
at them and throw rocks. The armed guards protected the prisoners and
dispersed the crowd. Some of the prisoners appeared to be very frightened
by the crowd while others displayed no emotion. ‘Following this incident,
the prisoners were taken across the street to a small restaurant (in the
vicinity of WJ 890216) where they were given free beer. At approximately
1115 hours, they were loaded onto a truck which drove away to the south.

The prisoners looked well, healthy,well dressed and were not handcuffed
