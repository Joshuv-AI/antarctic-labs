Abstract
. We present a short proof, based on local character expansions, of the celebrated theorem of Harish-Chandra about local integrability of complex characters of p p -adic reductive groups. The proof gives an algebraic incarnation of the local integrability that works for some coefficients different from ℂ \mathbb{C} , verifies local integrability in cases that appear not covered in the literature, and shows that a character is locally- L α L^{\alpha} for some specified α > 1 \alpha>1 as in [ GGH23 ] .
