Saddam Talks to CNN’s Peter Arnett

CNN reporter Peter Arnett on Monday gave viewers a summary
of his 90 minute interview with Saddam Husayn conducted earlier.
in the day in Baghdad. CNN may broadcast the interview tomorrow. -

Saddam said he is prepared to fight a long, comprehensive,
and bloody war with the US and coalition forces. Saddam repeated
his now-familiar declaration that Kuwait is part of Iraq forever.
He also claims that Iraq has nuclear as well as chemical and

biological warheads for its missiles. Saddam’s major points
reportedly were: ; Se BG -

--The US is ultimately to blame for the huge oil spill in the
Gulf because: it first attacked Iraqi oil tankers and

facilities on land. He asserted that Iragi field commanders —

are fully justified in using the release of oil on land or
sea for detensive purposes. :

~-Iraq will use weapons that in terms of force "equate" with .
those used against Iraq. He also claimed that the Al-Husayn ..
missile--Iraq’s second largest Scud-type missile--had ,
nuclear, chemical, and biological capabilities. :

--Iraq’s treatment of coalition PoWs is justified because
detention of Iraqi students in Western countries amounts to
unjustified internment. Saddam reportedly expressed
frustration with “hypocritical Western politicians" who
convinced him to release Western civilian hostages last
December. ;

-~-The deployment of Iraqi aircraft to Iran, is appropriate
because Iran is a Muslim neighbor who also views the US
as the infidel. He said any decision to use those
aircraft later on in the war would depend on the
circumstances at the time, but that he would "respect"
Iran’s decision on the matter.

--The efforts of antiwar demonstrators in the West were
appreciated and any movement toward dialogue or negotiations
was completely up to President Bush. He said he is ready
for a land war he firmly believes Irag will win. 9

President Mubarak as a tool of the US and Israel and suggested

that he would be assassinated like former President Sadat.
