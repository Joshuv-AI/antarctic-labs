STATINTL

S, 294S, A Bill to Provide Preference in Government

Employment for Persons who Incur Permanent Disabilities
While Serving as Civilian Bnployees of the Government

1. Subject bill seeks to provide preferential consideration in the
Governmental employment of former employees who suffer from permanent physical
disabilities incurred in the performance of duty.

2. The bill apparently would not involve this Agency since it contem-
plates the granting of five points to the earned ratings of individuals with
permanent disabilities. We note, however, that the bill is not an amendment
to an existing act and the coverage provisions are very broad*

ILLEGIB

With regard to the objective sought by this proposal, the preferen-
tial employment of individuals who suffer permanent disabilities as a result
of an accident in the Government service would limit the Agency's flexibility
and efficiency of personnel utilization. Assuming all other requirements of
security, experience and qualifications were met, the employment of such
personnel would tend to reduce our reservoir of employees qualified and avail-
able for overseas service. Also not to be overlooked is our desire to place
in sedentary work permanently disabled CIA personnel who have incurred permanent
disability while in Agency service. Accordingly we do not believe the appli-
cation of a standard which would automatically give preference to handicapped
persons, without exception, is compatible with Agency requirements.

A BILL

To provide preference in Government employment for persons
who incur permanent disabilities while serving as civilian
employees of the Government.

1 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa-

2 tines of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

3 That in the employment of civilian officers and employees

4 in the executive branch of the Government, preference shall

5 be given to former civilian officers and employees of the

6 Government who are suffering from permanent disabilities

7 which (1) were incurred in the performance of service as

8 civilian officers or employees of the Government, and (2)

9 are of a degree compensable under the provisions of the

10 Federal Employees Compensation Act.

I

2

1 Sec. 2. In all examinations to determine the qualifica-

2 tions of applicants for employment as civilian officers and

3 employees in the executive branch of the Government, five

4 points shall be added to the earned ratings of former officers

5 and employees referred to in section 1.

6 Sec. 3. Nothing in this Act shall be construed to (1)

7 authorize or require the appointment of any person to a

8 position to which, but for the foregoing provisions of this

9 Act, a person entitled to preference under the Veterans’

10 Preference Act of 1944 would be appointed, (2) grant any

11 additional preference to persons entitled to preference under

12 the Veterans’ Preference Act of 1944, or (3) apply to any

13 appointment required by law to be made by the President

14 by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.

15 Sec. 4. The Civil Service Commission shall prescribe

16 such rules and regulations as may he necessary to carry out

17 the provisions of this Act.
