Italian deputy and lawyer; born at Venice Feb. 24, 1858. He is (1904) district attorney for the province of Como. In Jan., 1894, on the death of Genala, deputy for Soresina in the province of Cremona, Pavia was chosen as his successor, and was reelected at the general election of May 26, 1895, and again in Dec., 1904.

Bibliography: Pallestrini, I Nostri Deputati, p. 159, Palermo, 1896; Illustrazione Italiana, 1894, part i., p. 158. S. U. C.
