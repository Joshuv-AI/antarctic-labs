Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt [1] (16 December 1944, d154)

[Excerpt]

[London,] 16 December 1944.

853. I thank you cordially for your telegram Number 674 about Poland. I trust you will carry out your proposal to send a message to Stalin suggesting that he postpone any positive action on the Polish question until the three of us can get together. This suggestion is most valuable and also I feel extremely urgent. Would it be possible for you to do this today, as I apprehend Stalin may make some move recognizing the Lublin Committee as the government of Poland.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Prime
