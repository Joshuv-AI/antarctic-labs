President Roosevelt to Generalissimo Chiang[1] (30 October 1943, d78)

secret priority

[Hyde Park?] 30 October 1943.

From the President to Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek.

I have not heard definitely from Marshal Stalin but there is still a chance of Churchill and me meeting him near Persian Gulf.

Very confidentially, I hope you will make arrangements to meet with Churchill and me in general neighborhood of Cairo about November twenty-sixth.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Best regards

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the American Military Mission to China, Chungking, via the White House Map Room and Army channels, as telegram 3734, from the War Department to the Mission.
