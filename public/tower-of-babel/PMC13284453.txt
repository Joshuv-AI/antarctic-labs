Protocol for multiplexed transcription factor activity detection using optimized barcoded reporters and an automated computational pipeline

Published: 2026

Main text

(STAR Protocols 6, 104066; September 19, 2025)

Due to author oversight, an error was introduced under the “oligonucleotides” section of the key resources table in the originally published protocol. The sequence reported for the indexed Illumina barcode amplification primer forward (MT397) was published as “CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGATNNNNNNNNGTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCT,” whereas the correct sequence is “AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACNNNNNNNNACACTCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCT.” This has been corrected online and does not affect the experimental results, analyses, or conclusions, as the correct primers were used throughout. The authors sincerely apologize for this error.
