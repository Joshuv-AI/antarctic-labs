SUBJECT: Distribution of Particularly Sensitive
Intelligence Reports

In July 1985 the attached sensitive source reporting format
was established for highly sensitive CIA reporting concerning
the well-being and possible locations of U.S. hostages in
Lebanon. This format limits distribution within the CIA and
restricts dissemination to the above addressees in other
agencies. We request that recipients ensure this information
is shared only with those individuals who have a clearly evident

need to know.
