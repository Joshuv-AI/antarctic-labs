Shoḥeṭ at Cracow in the sixteenth century; a pupil of Moses Isserles. He was the author of "Haggahot le-Sefer Sheḥiṭah u-Bediḳah" (Cracow, 1631; n. p. 1723; Amsterdam, 1745; Zolkiev, 1793), annotations appended to Jacob Weil's work on the laws governing the slaughtering of animals and the examination of the lungs.

Bibliography: Nepi-Ghirondi, Toledot Gedole Yisrael, p. 298; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 396; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2753. J. I. Br.
