EL SALVADOR: PRISONER TIED TO DUARTE KIDNAPPING PARDONED
PAS18363

PARIS AFP IN SPANISH 8149 GMT 1 OCT 85

C(TEXT)? SAN SALVADOR, 308 SEP (AFP) -— THE SALVADORAN
LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY TONIGHT PARDONEO COSTA RICAN CITIZEN
JULIO ROMERO TALAVERA, WHO HAD BEEN CHARGED WITH TERRORISM AND
SENTENCED TO 25 YEARS IN PRISON BY A MILITARY TRIBUNAL. *_

ROMERO TALAVERA, CAPTURED 25 JANUARY. 1981 WHILE FLYING AN
AIRCRAFT THAT ALLEGEDLY SUPPLIED ARMS TO THE GUERRILLAS, IS ONE OF

2 THE 34 POLITICAL PRISONERS WHCSE RELEASE IS DEMANDED BY THE

KIDNAPPERS OF PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON OQUARTE'S ELDEST
DAUGHTER. |

THS DECISION WAS INTERPRETED IN POLITICAL CIRCLES AS A
SPECIFIC STEP BY THE GOVERNMENT TOWARD THE RELEASE OF INES
GUADALUPE DUARTE AND HER FRIEND ANA CECILIA VILLEDA, WHO
WERE KIONAPPED IN SAN SALVADOR ON 10 SEPTEMBER.
