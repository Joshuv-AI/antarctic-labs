Dear Admiral Turner,

Although I am sure that you already have innumerable demands upon your
time, I do hope you will respond favorably to this invitation. As as student
in the Strategy and Policy core of the Naval War College Graduate Study Sem-
inar Program, I have been attempting to assist my professor in exposing the
students in the course to the wide range of knowledge and experience avail-
able in the Washington area by having guest lecturers in various fields ad-
dress the seminars. We recently had former DCI William Colby with us to dis-
cuss the Cold War and Vietnam.

Because our very program is a result of your efforts while President of
the College, and since your name is so closely associated with the topic of
the changing missions of the navy and its effects on foreign policy, we hope
you will be able to lecture to the students in the program.

The usual format is for the lecturer to address his remarks to the class
approximately an hour, and then respond to their questions for another
hour. At various times we have added a luncheon or dinner to the program, or
shifted the site of the lecture to accomodate the speaker.

The time frame under consideration for this topic would stretch approx'
imately from the beginning of May into the first week of June. I realize
