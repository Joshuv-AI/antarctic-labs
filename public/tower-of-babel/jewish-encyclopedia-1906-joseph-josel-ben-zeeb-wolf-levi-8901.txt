Rabbi in Lesla during the first half of the eighteenth century. He was the author of a supercommentary on Rashi to the Pentateuch entitled "Tif'eret Yosef" (Prague, 1724); it deals with the first three books of the Pentateuch.

Bibliography: Zunz, Zeitschrift, p. 343; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. No. 6008. G.
