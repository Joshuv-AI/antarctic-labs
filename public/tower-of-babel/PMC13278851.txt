Trends in the Prevalence of Seat Belts Non-use among Adolescents, 2016–2025
Authors: Yangha Kim, 양하 김
Published: 2026

The prevalence of seat belts non-use among adolescents in 2025 was 13.0% for the front seat, 42.8% for the rear seat, and 27.0% for express buses. Although the prevalence of rear seat belts non-use has substantially declined over the past 10 years, it remained the highest (Figure 1).
