CIRCUMSTANCES OF CAPTURE

On 2h, July 1967, Source led a small group of soldiers to CHU ME Hamlet,

PHO CHAU Village, DUC PHO District, QUANG NGAI Province, to collect rice,
They left from DUC HOANH Montagnard Village, HOAI NHON District, BINH DINH,
and travelled through PHO CUONG Village, DUC PHO District, QUANG NGAI, The
group arrived at CHU ME Hamlet at 0400 hours on 27 July 1967 and hid in

a tunnel in the hamlet. At 1200 hours, the U.S. Ist Air Cavalry Division
conducted an operation in the hamlet and found the cover for the underground
tunnel. Two grenades were thrown into the tunnel, killing two VC and
wounding four others. Source and his companions were captured,
