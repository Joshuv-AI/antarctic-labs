Exact ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} electromagnetic duality of ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} toric code is non-Clifford
Ryohei Kobayashi

Abstract
The 2D ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} toric code admits a global symmetry exchanging electric and magnetic quasiparticles, known as electromagnetic duality. Known realizations include lattice translation symmetry, an exact ℤ 4 \mathbb{Z}_{4} symmetry generated by a Clifford circuit, and an exact ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} symmetry generated by a non-Clifford circuit. We show that a Clifford electromagnetic duality cannot realize an exact internal ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} symmetry. This is proved rigorously for symmetries with coarse translation invariance by l l lattice units for generic odd l l . Therefore an exact internal ℤ 2 \mathbb{Z}_{2} electromagnetic duality must be non-Clifford, whereas generic internal Clifford realization necessarily has ℤ 2 m \mathbb{Z}_{2^{m}} algebra with m ≥ 2 m\geq 2 . Our result suggests an unexpected connection between the algebra of exact electromagnetic duality and Clifford hierarchy of circuits.



Introduction.–

Global symmetry plays a central role in quantum many-body systems. It constrains dynamics, and organizes phases of matter. In topologically ordered systems, symmetry acts nontrivially on anyons and generates logical operators of error-correcting codes. Among solvable models of topological order, the
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
toric code occupies a distinguished place
Kitaev 2003

\mathbb{Z}_{2}
gauge theory, and its global symmetries provide a basic platform for both quantum error correction and fault-tolerant logical operations
Yoshida 2015
;
Yoshida 2016
;
Yoshida 2017
;
Barkeshli et al. 2023
;
Barkeshli et al. 2024a
;
Barkeshli et al. 2024b

At the level of topological order, (2+1)D
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
gauge theory admits an electromagnetic duality exchanging the electric and magnetic quasiparticles. In the square-lattice toric code this duality exchanges star and plaquette operators. A standard microscopic realization combines a transversal Hadamard transformation with a lattice translation, and therefore is not an exact
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry. More recently, an exact internal Clifford symmetry exchanging
e
and
m
was constructed as a finite-depth Clifford circuit generating a
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
action rather than a
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
action
Barkeshli et al. 2023
. On the other hand, exact non-Clifford
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry for electromagnetic duality are known to exist on the microscopic Hilbert space
Shirley et al. 2025
;
Tu et al. 2026
. Thus the obstruction to exact
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
electromagnetic duality is not absolute; rather, it is specific to Clifford operators.

In this Letter, we prove a no-go theorem for exact
Clifford

{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
internal symmetry for electromagnetic duality. More precisely, we show that there is no exact Clifford
\mathbb{Z}_{2}

l\hat{x}

While our proof is established for odd

The toric code is a commuting-projector Hamiltonian of qubits placed on the edges of a square lattice,

H_{\mathrm{TC}}=-\sum_{v}A_{v}-\sum_{p}B_{p},

(1)

where

A_{v}=\prod_{v\subset\partial e}X_{e}~,\qquad B_{p}=\prod_{e\subset\partial p}Z_{e}~.

(2)

The translation group is thus encoded by the Laurent polynomial ring

\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}=\begin{pmatrix}1+\bar{x}&0\\

\sigma^{\dagger}\Lambda\sigma=0.

(15)

Blocked polynomial formalism.–

To analyze Clifford symmetries that need only commute with translations by
l

R_{l}=\mathbb{F}_{2}[X^{\pm 1},Y^{\pm 1}],\qquad X=x^{l},\quad Y=y^{l},

(18)

(20)

(21)

(22)

(23)

With

(25)

The blocked toric-code stabilizer matrix takes the form

\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}^{(l)}=\begin{pmatrix}d&0\\
0&\tilde{d}\end{pmatrix},

(26)

where

1+T_{y}^{\dagger}\end{pmatrix},\qquad\tilde{d}=\begin{pmatrix}1+T_{y}\\
1+T_{x}\end{pmatrix}.

(27)

Here each displayed block is an

matrix over
R_{l}

Absence of exact Clifford
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
electromagnetic duality for odd supercells.–

We now state and prove the main result.

Theorem 1
.

Let
l
be odd. There exists no Clifford
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
symmetry of the square-lattice toric code Hamiltonian that

1.

is invariant under translations by
l\hat{x}
and
l\hat{y}
, and

2.

implements electromagnetic duality by exchanging star and plaquette stabilizers.

Equivalently, for odd

there is no matrix

(28)

such that

\displaystyle Q^{\dagger}\Lambda_{l}Q

\displaystyle=\Lambda_{l},

(29)

\displaystyle Q^{2}

\displaystyle=I,

(30)

\displaystyle Q\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}^{(l)}

\displaystyle=\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}^{(l)}S_{U},

(31)

where

S_{U}=\begin{pmatrix}0&U\\
U^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix}

(32)

and

Proof.

Write

Q=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\
C&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad A,B,C,D\in\mathrm{Mat}_{2l^{2}}(R_{l}),

(33)

with

defined in Eq. (

\displaystyle Ad

\displaystyle=0,

(34)

\displaystyle Cd

\displaystyle=\tilde{d}\,U^{-1},

(35)

\displaystyle B\tilde{d}

\displaystyle=d\,U,

(36)

\displaystyle D\tilde{d}

\displaystyle=0.

(37)

Since
Q
is symplectic, Eq. (
29
) implies

Q^{-1}=\Lambda_{l}Q^{\dagger}\Lambda_{l}.

(38)

Combining this with the involution condition (
30
), we obtain

(39)

Hence

(40)

In particular, any solution must contain a Hermitian matrix

satisfying Eq. (
35

exists when

is odd.

A particular solution of Eq. (

) is

(41)

since all multiplication operators commute and

Generic solution to Eq. (

∎

Theorem 1 shows that an exact Clifford implementation of electromagnetic duality in the square-lattice toric code cannot have order two whenever the symmetry is invariant under an odd
l\times l
enlarged translation group. Therefore any such Clifford electromagnetic duality must have order at least four, consistent with the exact
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
symmetry discussed in Refs.
Barkeshli et al. 2023
;
Shirley et al. 2025
. Concretely, one exact
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
Clifford symmetry is represented by

\displaystyle Q_{\mathbb{Z}_{4}}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1+x&1+\bar{x}+y\\
0&0&x\bar{y}&1+\bar{y}\\
1+y&\bar{x}y&0&0\\
1+x+\bar{y}&1+\bar{x}&0&0\end{pmatrix},

(71)

which satisfies

\displaystyle Q_{\mathbb{Z}_{4}}^{\dagger}\Lambda Q_{\mathbb{Z}_{4}}

\displaystyle=\Lambda,

\displaystyle(Q_{\mathbb{Z}_{4}})^{4}

\displaystyle=I,

\displaystyle Q_{\mathbb{Z}_{4}}\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}

\displaystyle=\sigma_{\mathrm{TC}}S_{U=1}.

l

35

when the Laurent-polynomial ansatz for each entry of
C
was truncated to sum of monomials

with
|m|,|n|\leq 5
, and

\mathbb{Z}_{2}
electromagnetic duality is absent more generally within Clifford setting.

Faithful
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
electromagnetic symmetry action in
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
gauge theory.–

We close with a brief comment on electromagnetic duality in
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
gauge theory in generic dimensions. Our lattice result isolates an obstruction to exact
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
symmetry specific to Clifford implementations, while admitting exact Clifford
{\mathbb{Z}}_{4}
symmetry for electromagnetic duality on the microscopic Hilbert space. The exact
{\mathbb{Z}}_{2}
electromagnetic duality symmetry is realized by a non-Clifford circuit.

On the other hand, in continuum
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
gauge theory on a generic closed spatial manifold, electromagnetic duality is generated by a condensation defect of fermionic Wilson surface operators
Roumpedakis et al. 2023
, whose action on the Hilbert space is generically faithful
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
rather than faithful
\mathbb{Z}_{2}

Chen et al. 2021
;
Kobayashi and Zhu 2024
; in particular, its square can differ from the identity by the fermionic topological operator
W_{\psi}
, depending on the topology of a spatial manifold (see Supplemental Materials for details). It would be very interesting to understand whether the
\mathbb{Z}_{4}
algebra of the Clifford circuit can be derived directly by identifying the Clifford circuits as condensation defects on lattices.
