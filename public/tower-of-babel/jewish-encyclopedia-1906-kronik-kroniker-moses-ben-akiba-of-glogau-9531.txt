Rabbi of Flatow (Zlotowo); lived in the first half of the nineteenth century. He was the author of "Tefillah we-Todah," hymns and prayers for the community of Glogau on the occasion of the raising of the siege on April 24, 1814, with a German translation by Mayer Neumark (Breslau, 1814); and "Yemin Mosheh," lectures and commentaries interspersed with Hebrew poems, with an appendix entitled "Ebel Yaḥid," containing an elegy on the death of Abraham Tiktin ( ib. 1824).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1846; Zeitlin, Bibl. Hebr. Post-Mendels. p. 187. G. I. Br.
