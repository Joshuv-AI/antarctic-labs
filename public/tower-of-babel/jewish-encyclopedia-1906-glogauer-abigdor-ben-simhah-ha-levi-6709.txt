German Hebrew scholar of the eighteenth century. He published "Dabar Ṭob," an elementary Hebrew grammar with paradigms, printed with Moses ibn Ḥabib's "Marpe Lashon" (Prague, 1783); "Iggerot," Mendelssohn's letters (Vienna, 1794); "Ḥotam Toknit," Hebrew poems, the appendix to which contains another edited series of Mendelssohn's letters ( ib. 1797).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 663; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 12. D. M. Sel.
