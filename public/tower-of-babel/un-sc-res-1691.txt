'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 5473rd meeting, on 22 June 2006'''

''The Security Council'',

''Having examined'' the application of the Republic of Montenegro for admission
to the United Nations (S/2006/409),

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Republic of Montenegro be
admitted to membership in the United Nations.
