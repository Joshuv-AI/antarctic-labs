FBI Dallas Teletype on the Roswell “Flying Disc” (SAC Percy Wyly, 8 July 1947, 6:17 PM)
Percy Wyly, Special Agent in Charge, FBI Dallas, 1947

US Government work — public domain. Same-day FBI wire relaying Eighth Air Force’s report of the Roswell recovery; notes Wright Field had not yet confirmed the weather-balloon explanation. Complete; “CURTAN” transmission artifact noted.
Transcription source: FBI file 62-HQ-83894, Section 1 (transcription)

================================================================

FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION
TELETYPE -- URGENT

FBI DALLAS 7-8-47 6:17 PM

DIRECTOR AND SAC, CINCINNATI

FLYING DISC, INFORMATION CONCERNING.

MAJOR CURTAN, HEADQUARTERS EIGHTH AIR FORCE, TELEPHONICALLY ADVISED THIS OFFICE THAT AN OBJECT PURPORTING TO BE A FLYING DISC WAS RECOVERED NEAR ROSWELL, NEW MEXICO, THIS DATE. THE DISC IS HEXAGONAL IN SHAPE AND WAS SUSPENDED FROM A BALLON BY CABLE, WHICH BALLON WAS APPROXIMATELY TWENTY FEET IN DIAMETER. MAJOR CURTAN FURTHER ADVISED THAT THE OBJECT FOUND RESEMBLES A HIGH ALTITUDE WEATHER BALLOON WITH A RADAR REFLECTOR, BUT THAT TELEPHONIC CONVERSATION BETWEEN THEIR OFFICE AND WRIGHT FIELD HAD NOT BORNE OUT THIS BELIEF. DISC AND BALLOON BEING TRANSPORTED TO WRIGHT FIELD BY SPECIAL PLANE FOR EXAMINATION. INFORMATION PROVIDED THIS OFFICE BECAUSE OF NATIONAL INTEREST IN CASE AND FACT THAT NATIONAL BROADCASTING COMPANY, ASSOCIATED PRESS, AND OTHERS ATTEMPTING TO BREAK STORY OF LOCATION OF DISC TODAY.

NO FURTHER INVESTIGATION BEING CONDUCTED.

WYLY

[Note: The teletype as transmitted names the caller "MAJOR CURTAN"; historians identify the Eighth Air Force officer as Major Edwin M. Kirton, and "CURTAN" is regarded as a teletype transmission artifact. The object described was the debris recovered near Roswell, New Mexico, on July 8, 1947.]
