SUBJECT: Killing of Dr. Ignacio Ellacuria

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

1. The assassination of Ellacuria and other priests 0300 hours 16
November 1989 could have been perpetrated by the extremists of the left or
the right.

Leftist Extremists

2. There are several occurrences which could indicate Ellacuria's death,
on the night of 15-16 November 1989, could have been perpetrated by the
Farabundo Marti National Liberation Front (FMLN):

a. Early morning 15 November 1989 an estimated 200 insurgents
harassed the Police Officer Academy near Ciudad Merlio, between San Salvador
and Santa Tecla. Insurgents set up roadblocks on the Santa Tecla highway, at
the intersection of the highway and the road to Ciudad Merliot. By
mid-morning the 15 November the insurgents withdrew from the area, leaving
mines and booby traps in the Ciudad Merliot area. Civilians reported the
presence of 100 insurgents eating and resting on the highway behind the
Hoechst factory. Ciudad Merliot is two kilometers southwest of the
University of Centra] America site where Ellacuria was killed.

b. One report received before the Ellacuria assassination indicated
eighty troops of the Forces of Popular Liberation (FPL) from the E] Volcan
and Colonia Cerro Monte, and staging areas at Finca El Espino, wil] try to
take over Santa Tecla 16 November 1989. They will be reinforced by five to
six groups of forces of the Revolutionary Workers Party of Central America
Workers (PRTC) from Zaragoza who will be wearing National Police uniforms.
They plan to attack the General Staff Headquarters and the National
Directorate of Intelligence (DNI), and small groups of FPL members with LAW's
and RPG-2's will attack Cherokee and other patrol] vehicles of the U.S.
Embassy. ‘They also will attack the homes of individuals, including Armando
Calderon Sol, the Mayor of San Salvador; Francisco Merino, Vice President of
El Salvador; Roberto D'Aubuisson, Honorary President for Life of the
Nationalist Republican Alliance (ARENA); Colonel Francisco Elena Fuentes,
Commander of the First Infantry Brigade; and possibly unidentified U.S.
Embassy advisors who work With and support the Salvadoran Armed Forces.

3. Public statements by Ellacuria, in September 1989, differed from his
earlier public statements in that they were increasingly favorable to the
Government of E] Salvador and to the detriment of the FMLN. The following
are public statements given recently by Ellacuria which favored the
Government of El Salvador and were unfavorable to the FMLN:

a. On 15 September 1989 Ellacuria said that Cristiani has proved to be

his own man and not just a puppet of the Nationalist Republican Alliance
(ARENA) party.
