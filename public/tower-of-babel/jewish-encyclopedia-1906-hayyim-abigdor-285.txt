Talmudist; lived in the eighteenth century. He was the author of "Peri 'Eẓ Ḥayyim" (Amsterdam, 1742), containing responsa, annotations to Maimonides' "Yad ha-Ḥazaḳah" and to the "Arba' Ṭurim," and sermons arranged in the order of the Sabbatical sections.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 819; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 156; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 181. S. S. I. Br.
