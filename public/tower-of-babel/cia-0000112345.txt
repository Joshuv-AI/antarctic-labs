((TEXT)) BEIJING, MAY 14 (XINHUA) -- HIGHLIGHTS OF TODAY'S MAJOR
BEIJING-BASED NEWSPAPERS:

~- CHINESE PRESIDENT YANG SHANGKUN HELD A GRAND CEREMONY TO
WELCOME KYRGYZSTAN PRESIDENT ASKAR ARAYEV-AT -THE-GREAT HALL OF THE
PEOPLE IN BEIJING MONDAY.

DIPLOMATIC RELATIONS BETWEEN CHINA AND SLOVENIA AND BETWEEN CHINA

AND CROATIA.
