The debate over whether or not Lt. Col. Philip J. Corso (author of Dawn of a
New Age, and co-author of The Day After Roswell) was “on the NSC” has
simmered for decades. It turns out the evidence was declassified way back in
2000, it’s also all over the microfilm, and it all corroborates his official
military record (DA Form 66). There are also some obscure published book
references, too (page 459).
The official cia.gov link proving his service on the OCB is here:
