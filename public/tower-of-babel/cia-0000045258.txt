PANAMA: UNGO DENIES ANY LINK WITH NEGOTIATIONS
PA222167
PANAMA CITY ACAN IN SPANISH 2615 GMT 22 OGT B85 _ |

((TEXT)} PANAMA CITY, 22 OCT (ACAN-EFE) -- THE CIVILIAN
LEADER OF THE SALVADORAN GUERRILLAS, GUILLERMO UNGO, DENIED ANY
INTERVENTION ON HIS PART IN THE NEGOTIATIONS FOR THE RELEASE OF THE
DAUGHTER OF PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON DUARTE.

“I NEITHER PARTICIPATED IN THE ACTION NOR AM I INVOLVED IN
THE NEGOTIATIONS," UNGO TOLD ACAN-~EFE, REFERRING TO THE
KIDNAPPING OF INES GUADALUPE DUARTE ON 12 SEPTEMBER AND TO THE
EFFORTS UNDERWAY TO EXCHANGE HER FOR SALVADORAN PRISONERS,

GUILLERMO UNGO IS THE PRESIDENT OF THE DEMOCRATIC
REVOLUTIONARY FRONT (FDR), A GROUP OF POLITICAL PARTIES ALLIED
TO THE FMLN, :

HE HAS BEEN RESIDING IN THIS CAPITAL FOR SEVERAL YEARS.

JOURNALISTIC SOURCES REPORTED THAT THE REBEL LEADER WAS TAKING
PART IN THE NEGOTIATIONS LAST SUNDAY AND MONDAY IN THIS CAPITAL
WITH THE PARTICIPATION OF THE ARCHBISHOP OF SAN SALVADOR, MSGR
ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS, AND DELEGATES OF, THE SAL VADORAN ;
GOVERNMENT.

HOWEVER, UNGO AFFIRMED THAT HE HAD ARRIVED IN PANAMA TODAY
FROM A POLITICAL TOUR OF EUROPE, AND THAT HE WAS LEAVING FOR
COSTA RICA TOMORROW TO ATTNED A SEMINAR ORGANIZED, BY THE SAN
JOSE GOVERNMENT.
