On the recommendation of the Military Attachd of the US Embassy I permit myself
to ask you a question which interestjme .

In 1945 I was a 2nd Lt with the French 94th Regiment of Infantry from which one
battalion was detached and put at the disposal of the American commander for
guarding German prisioners at Camp Musanne (Sarthe), In February or March five

general officers escaped from Camp Musanne.

A

On my own initiative I investigated the conditions of the escape of these five
generals and I was able to learn that they were transported from France to Switzerland
in order to cross the frontier. I have now retired and I plan to vaxite a novel about
this. However, from the beginning I have had doubts about the real character of this
escape and I have the problem of learning if it could have taken place with the
complicity of the American authorities. The Colonel commanding the camp was Colonel
Kauffman (I cannot guarantee the spelling). I mention this to facilitate your
research. Are you in a position to state if this escape was real or simulated?

I would think that with the passage of time you would no longer be constrained by
problems of military secrets.

Hoping that you can satisfy my curiosity.

With my best regards.

G. Fleury
