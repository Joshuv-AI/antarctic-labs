German author; lived at Dresden in the first quarter of the nineteenth century. He wrote, together with his brother Simon Bondi, the "Or Ester," a Hebrew dictionary of the Latin words occurring in the Talmud, Targumim and Midrashim. Some of his essays are also extant in the periodical "Jedidja" (i. 117-125; iii. 72, 196; iv. 35).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 125; Zunz, Die Monatstage des Kalenderjahres, p. 67; Karpeles, Gesch. der. Jüd. Lit. p. 1083. L. G. I. Ber.
