deemed necessary and desirable. It is believed that the language of section 7.

ability of assignment of graduates to the proposed U, S. Academy of Foreign Service,

the Departments of State, Commerce, Agriculture and Treasury. It is the view of

mended that the following be substituted at line 19 on page 5 for lines 19 through
23 on page^and lines 1 through 3 on page 61 "Second, to such other departments
and agencies of the government which have responsibilities for the conduct of
activities overseas, in accordance with a quota system to be negotiated with the
Secretary of State and the heads of the departments and agencies of the government
concerned."
