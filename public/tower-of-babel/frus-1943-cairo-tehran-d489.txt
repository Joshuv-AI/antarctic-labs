The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the British Secretary of State for Foreign Affairs (Eden), and Reply[1]

[Cairo, December 5 (?), 1943.]

Anthony:—It looks like Buccaneer is out & our military plans hence will be agreed to tomorrow

Harry

If so, you have been very generous, but our chances next year will surely benefit.

President has been grand about it all.

Note reinforcements (Scotch) just came in!

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] The exchange, which was handwritten on a single sheet of paper, presumably took place during the quadripartite dinner meeting, ante, p. 733.
