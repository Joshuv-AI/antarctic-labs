Biblical commentator; lived at Constantinople in the middle of the sixteenth century. He was the author of a commentary on Job, published, with the text, at Constantinople in 1545. He wrote also a commentary on Pirḳe Abot, still extant in manuscript.

Bibliography: Zunz, Z. G. p. 426; Steinschneider, Jewish Literature, p. 232; idem, Cat. Bodl. col. 1153; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. ii. 141. G. I. Br.
