Poster 79. Combined MPFL ReconstructionaAnd Anteromedialization Tibial Tubercle Osteotomy Does Not Decrease Patellar Height More Than Reconstruction Alone
Authors: Robert A. Magnussen, William A. Cantrell, Luke Comisford, Kyle Huntley, Eric M. Milliron, Parker A. Cavendish, David Flanigan, Christopher C. Kaeding, Tyler Barker, Nicholas Apseloff
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Objective:Recurrent patellar instability is often addressed with medial patellofemoral ligament reconstruction (MPFLR). In cases of severe patella alta or tibial tubercle lateralization, a tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO) may also be performed. While distalization TTO predictably lowers patellar height, the effect of anteromedialization (AMZ) TTO on patellar height has been less studied. The goal of this study was to compare patellar height changes after isolated MPFLR, MPFLR+AMZ TTO, and MPFLR+distalization TTO. We hypothesized that the isolated MPFLR group and the MPFLR+AMZ TTO group would demonstrate similar changes in patellar height, while the MPFLR+distalization TTO group would demonstrate a greater decrease in patellar height than the isolated MPFLR group.Methods:A cohort of consecutive patients undergoing MPFLR from a single institution were retrospectively identified. All patients had standardized lateral knee radiographs preoperatively and postoperatively. Patellar height was measured using the Caton-Deschamps Index (CDI), Insall-Salvati Ratio (ISR), and Blackburne-Peel Index (BPI). Patients were classified based on whether they underwent isolated MPFLR, MPFLR+AMZ TTO, or MPFLR+distalization TTO. Changes in patellar height indices were compared between groups using a one-way analysis of variance and followed by a post-hoc Tukey's tests.Results:A total of 169 patients were included in the study, including 129 who underwent isolated MPFLR, 23 who underwent MPFLR+AMZ TTO, and 17 who underwent MPFLR+distalization TTO. Mean changes in CDI were: (Isolated MPFLR: -0.082; MPFL+AMZ TTO: -0.070; MPFLR+distalization TTO: -0.33. No difference in CDI change was noted between the MPFLR+AMZ TTO group and the isolated MPFLR group (p = 0.94), but the MPFLR+distalization TTO group demonstrated a larger decrease in CDI than the isolated MPFLR group (p < 0.001). Mean changes in ISR were: (Isolated MPFLR: -0.076; MPFLR+AMZ TTO: -0.119; MPFLR+distalization TTO: -0.166. No difference in ISR change was noted between the MPFLR+AMZ TTO group and the isolated MPFLR group (p =0.40), but the MPFLR+distalization TTO group demonstrated a larger decrease in ISR than the isolated MPFLR group (p = 0.047). Mean changes in BPI were: (Isolated MPFLR: -0.131; MPFLR+AMZ TTO: -0.155; MPFLR+distalization TTO: -0.463. No difference in BPI changes were noted between the MPFLR+AMZ TTO group and the isolated MPFLR group (p = 0.87), but the MPFLR+distalization TTO group demonstrated a larger decrease in BPI than the isolated MPFLR group (p < 0.001).Conclusions:Across radiographic patellar height indices, similar changes in patellar height are noted between those who undergo isolated MPFLR and MPFLR+AMZ TTO. As expected, patients who undergo MPFLR+distalization TTO demonstrate a larger reduction in patellar height.
