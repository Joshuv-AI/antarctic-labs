These two types of interchange reactions, hydroxyl-ester and ester-ester,

Free ethylene glycol cannot be formed i f t h e r e a c t i n g ester linkage i s

i n an in-chain position. It can be s p l i t o f f from PET molecules only

refer t o those n e a r e s t t h e ends o f t h e Iplymer chains. I n certain

o p p s i t e side. The same i s t r u e i f t h e a t t a c k i n g group i s another

ester linkage.

I n t h e melt polymerization of PET, intramolecular r e a c t i o n has much
