Family-separated seesaw relations of Majorana neutrinos


Abstract
Given the canonical seesaw mechanism as a most natural extension of the standard model in its neutrino sector, we find out a special but brand new solution to the exact seesaw equation: m i / M i = − R 2 α ​ i / U 2 α ​ i m_{i}/M_{i}=-R^{2}_{\alpha i}/U^{2}_{\alpha i} for the masses and flavor mixing matrix elements of light and heavy Majorana neutrinos of the i i -th family (for i = 1 , 2 , 3 i=1,2,3 and α = e , μ , τ \alpha=e,\mu,\tau ). This family-separated seesaw scenario allows us to establish simple relations between the original seesaw parameters and the active degrees of freedom, and thus offers some testable predictions such as a direct correlation between the CP-violating effects in light neutrino flavor oscillations and heavy Majorana neutrino decays.

Motivation

The standard model (SM) of particle physics is widely recognized as the most
successful quantum field theory in describing the fundamental properties
of electroweak and strong
interactions, but it remains incomplete for several reasons
[
1
]
,
especially because it leaves no room for accommodating tiny neutrino masses
and significant lepton flavor mixing effects that have been discovered in a number
of milestone experiments of neutrino oscillations
[
2
]
.
But the unique dimension-five Weinberg operator of the SM effective field
theory can provide a natural and costless interpretation of why the three known
active neutrinos should have the Majorana nature and sufficiently small
masses
[
3
]
, with no need for invoking any details of
underlying new physics.

The simplest and well-motivated way of going beyond the SM to realize the Weinberg
operator is the canonical seesaw mechanism which
introduces three right-handed neutrino fields and their kinetic terms, the
Yukawa interactions of left- and right-handed neutrino fields with the SM Higgs
field, and the self-interaction term of right-handed neutrino fields and their
charge-conjugated counterparts — a lepton-number-violating Majorana mass
term as follows

The FSS scenario

The seesaw formula and the unitarity condition shown in Eq. (
4
)
can be more explicitly expressed as

\displaystyle\sum^{3}_{i=1}\left(m_{i}U_{\alpha i}U_{\beta i}+M_{i}R_{\alpha i}R_{\beta i}\right)=0\;,

\displaystyle\sum^{3}_{i=1}\left(U_{\alpha i}U^{*}_{\beta i}+R_{\alpha i}R^{*}_{\beta i}\right)=\delta_{\alpha\beta}\;,\hskip 45.52458pt

(5)

where the Greek subscripts
\alpha

\beta

lepton flavor indices
e
,
\mu
and
\tau

Motivated by the
well-known Occam’s Razor (simplicity) principle, we conjecture that the
three families of light and heavy Majorana neutrinos may have their own
seesaw formulas
. In other words, we expect that there may exist a
particular but instructive solution to the above seesaw equation:

m_{i}U_{\alpha i}U_{\beta i}+M_{i}R_{\alpha i}R_{\beta i}=0
itself instead of a summation over the family index

i

\alpha,\beta=e,\mu,\tau
and
i=1,2,3
), from which
the brand new FSS relations

\displaystyle\frac{R_{ei}}{U_{ei}}=\frac{R_{\mu i}}{U_{\mu i}}=\frac{R_{\tau i}}{U_{\tau i}}={\rm i}\sqrt{\frac{m_{i}}{M_{i}}}\;

(6)

can be easily achieved. It is obvious that the ratios

R_{\alpha i}/U_{\alpha i}
are insensitive to any redefinition
of the phases of three charged-lepton fields, but they impose some
strong constraints on the phases of the flavor mixing matrix elements

R_{\alpha i}
and
U_{\alpha i}
as follows:

\displaystyle\arg R_{\alpha i}=\arg U_{\alpha i}+\frac{4n+1}{2}\pi\;

(7)

with

m_{i}
and
M_{i}

or between
U_{\alpha i}
and
R_{\alpha i}

with almost maximal calculability and predictability.

4
Summary

Different from the traditional model building exercises based on the
canonical seesaw mechanism, which have been done in a chosen flavor basis of
Majorana neutrinos, the FSS scenario proposed in the present
work originates from a particular but brand new solution to the exact seesaw
equation in the mass basis of Majorana neutrinos. The most salient feature
of this idea is that each family of the light and heavy Majorana
neutrinos has its own seesaw formula of the form
m_{i}/M_{i}=-R^{2}_{\alpha i}/U^{2}_{\alpha i}
(for
i=1,2,3
and
\alpha=e,\mu,\tau
). We have shown that the FSS scenario allows us to establish
simple relations between the original seesaw flavor parameters and
those light and active degrees of freedom, and its predictability and
testability have been explicitly illustrated by a direct correlation
between the CP-violating effects in light neutrino flavor oscillations
and heavy Majorana neutrino decays.

Given the fact that the canonical seesaw mechanism is the most
natural and popular theoretical framework for the origin of tiny neutrino
masses beyond the SM but its predictability and testability are rather
limited due to the unknown flavor structures, we stress that the FSS
scenario as an especially novel benchmark example deserves a thorough
study in the era of precision neutrino physics.
