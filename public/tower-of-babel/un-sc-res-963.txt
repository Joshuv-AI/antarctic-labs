'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 3469th meeting, on 29 November 1994'''


''The Security Council'',

''Having examined'' the application of the Republic of Palau for admission to
the United Nations (S/1994/1315),

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Republic of Palau be admitted
to membership in the United Nations.
