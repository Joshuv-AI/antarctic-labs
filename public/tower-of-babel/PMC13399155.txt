Correction to: A machine learning model to detect falls mimicking cardiac arrest-related collapse based on wrist-derived accelerometry: the DETECT-2 study
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Roos Edgar, Kambiz Ebrahimkheil, Danny Meeuwsen, Maud C de Jong, Job J Herrmann, Alexander M Griffioen, Niels T B Scholte, Marc A Brouwer, Rypko J Beukema, Eelko Ronner, Eric Boersma, Aysun Cetinyurek-Yavuz, Peter C Stas, Claudine J C Lamoth, Niels van Royen, Judith L Bonnes, A machine learning model to detect falls mimicking cardiac arrest-related collapse based on wrist-derived accelerometry: the DETECT-2 study, European Heart Journal - Digital Health, Volume 7, Issue 3, April 2026, ztag043, https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjdh/ztag043

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the funding statement provided was incomplete.

This has been corrected to read:

“This research project is financed by public-private partnerships allowance (grant number R0007420) made available by Top Sector Life Science & Health to Radboudumc and Dutch Heart Foundation to stimulate public-private partnerships, and by Radboud Fonds (grant number SRF2231).”

instead of:

“This research project is financed by public-private partnerships allowance (grant number R0007420) made available by Top Sector Life Science & Health to Radboudumc to stimulate public-private partnerships, and by Radboud Fonds (grant number SRF2231)”.
