MEMORANDUM FOR: Chairman, DDS&T Historical Board

SUBJECT : Status Report on History Program as

of 15 January 1968

1. I have reviewed the subject report and noted the current
status of our history program.

2. I agree with you that every effort should be made to
wrap up the program as soon as possible and suggest that you
have all Offices work toward a deadline of 1 July 1968 for com-
pletion of their projects.

3. While it is true that a project of this kind frequently
has to give way to things having greater priority, I don’t think
we can expect our situation to get any better. I think, therefore,
we should exert what additional effort is required to complete the
program promptly.

4. Would you contact each Office and see if the above
deadline presents any unusual difficulties. If not, let's work
toward a completed program by 1 July of this year.
