BUSINESS JUSTIFICATION

Section I Procurement Description: As a result of RFQ 92-S004,
this is Contract written on behalf cfg MMos fulfill the
QED veguirements for Cushioning Material for Fiscal Year
1992. The period of performance for this agreement is 16 March
1992 to 30 September 1992.

The intent of this Contract is to enable the Contract Team
to issue Delivery Orders under this Contract when requested by
the COTR. Each Delivery Order will be separately funded. This
method of procurement will provide for expeditious contracting
in meeting customer requirements having critical schedule
requirements.

Section II Source Justification: Eleven contractors were
contacted to provide bids for different varieties and various
sizes of cushioning material for the Packing and Crating Section
at the Se Seven contractors responded with bids. R.S.
Willard, Inc. responded with the lowest bid overall on all
items, and therefore, was selected for the contract.
