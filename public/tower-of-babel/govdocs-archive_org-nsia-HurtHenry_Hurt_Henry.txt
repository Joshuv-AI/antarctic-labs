On New Year's Day a loud ex-
plosion was heard in North Chat-
ham. It seemed to come from the
vicinity of the Presbyterian
Church.

An investigation was launched
by two reporters from the News
& Gossip who discovered a crowd
that had gathered in front of
the John Evans home. The people
were all standing around talking
to each other. Now and then they
would look over at the carcass
of a gray squirrel lying nearby.

It turned out that the squir-
rel had been walking across a
power line when he took a short
cut to a short circuit.

The squirrel was sizzled on
the spot.

John Evans gave permission to
the News & Gossip reporters to
