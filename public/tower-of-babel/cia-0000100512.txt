March 24, 1995

Message From the ADCI on the New York Times Story on Guatemala

The suggestion that the Central Intelligence
Agency had any information about the deaths of
Michael Devine and Efrain Bamaca Velasquez at
the time the deaths occurred—and deliberately
corxealed such information—is a completely
false and utterly irresponsible charge. Credible
information about the deaths of these individuals
was acquired by the US Intelligence Community
well after they occurred. The Agency shared all
of this information with the appropriate US
Government authorities.

The CIA provided detailed briefings to
both Congressional intelligence oversight com-

mittees in early February 1995, providing our
most recent information on this subject. State-
ments that the CIA is “simply out of control” and
that there is a “criminal element” within the
Agency do a great disservice to the dedicated
men and women who work for this organization.
CIA officials briefed Representative Torricelli
on the evening of March 22, detailing our
knowledge of these events and the actions we
took.

Additional information will be provided
in subsequent Agency forums. (This is
UNCLASSIFIED.)
