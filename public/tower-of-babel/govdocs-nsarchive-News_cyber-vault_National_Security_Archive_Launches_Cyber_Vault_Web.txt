<div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><strong>Washington, D.C., March 30, 2016</strong>&nbsp;– The National Security Archive is pleased to announce the launch of its new&nbsp;<a href="/cybervault/" target="_blank">Cyber Vault project web site</a>.&nbsp;</p>

<p>&nbsp;The growing prominence of cyber activity as a global security concern with tangible effects on everyday lives has given rise to the production of a vast amount of documentation by governments and private industry.&nbsp; The Cyber Vault will serve as a centralized repository for key parts of the documentary record on this critical topic.</p>of a vast amount of documentation by governments and private industry.&nbsp; The Cyber Vault will serve as a centralized repository for key parts of the documentary record on this critical topic.</p>

<p>Directed by Jeffrey T. Richelson, an international expert and widely published author on intelligence and national security issues, the Cyber Vault project will add to its holdings on a regular basis.&nbsp;&nbsp;<a href="/cybervault/library/" target="_blank">All materials will be fully searchable</a>&nbsp;and permanently stored on the project site.&nbsp; The project aims to enhance the National Security Archive’s mission to broaden public understanding of the nature, history and conduct of U.S. foreign and national security policy.</p>

<p>Funding for The Cyber Vault has been provided by The William and Flora Hewlett Foundation.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>&nbsp;For more on the Cyber Vault and today’s launch, visit:</p>
