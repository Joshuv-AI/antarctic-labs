THE PROBLEM

To assess the situation in Syria with particular reference to possible moves by:
(a) the USSR; or (bd) one or a combination of the following powers: the UK, France,

Israel, Iraq, Turkey.

DISCUSSION

Syria’s Present Position

1. The highly unstable but strategically lo-
cated state of Syria has long been both a
major target and an active arena in the power
struggles in the Middle East. While previous-
ly Soviet influence in the area was exerted

chiefly through Egypt, the USSR is now focus- |

ing direct attention on Syria as well. Soviet
efforts are enjoying a large measure of suc-
cess, primarily because the USSR is able to
cater to the desire of key elements in the army
and government, backed by widespread popu-
lar opinion, for support against Israel, Iraq,
and the West. Sentiment in Syria in this re-
spect has become even more intense as @ result
of the attacks on Egypt by Israel, the UK, and
France.

2. The latter three powers, in turn, have long

reasons —~ over Syrian trends, and the frus-

the Middle Ecst, taken with the USSR’s cur-
