No. 1337 The Ambassador in Iran (Murray) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret priority

Tehran, July 30, 1945—1 p.m.

560. Booth has received further telegram[1] from Potsdam canceling War telegram reported mytel 554 July 28[2] and authorizing more gradual withdrawal troops from Iran. He is now instructed transfer HQ (headquarters) from Amirabad to Khorramshahr by October 1 but to leave custodial detachment at Amirabad. Orders state OANLC (Office Army Navy Liquidation Commissioner]) staff will remain here.

Murray

Source: 811.24591/7–3045: Telegram

[1] Not printed.
[2] Document No. 1335.
