FROM:

Assistant Director/Scientific Intelligence

The Office of Scientific Intelligence has always experienced difficulties
in attracting and holding scientific and technical personnel in view of
the more attractive opportunities often presented in private industry.
For this reason, we would favor congressional action along the lines of
the pending Bill. We would, however, recommend that limitations to
"the field of engineering" be omitted so that the legislation would
extend to technical and scientific personnel in general.
