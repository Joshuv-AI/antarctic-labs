Turkish Talmudist; lived at Constantinople during the sixteenth century. He was the author of "Gal shel Egozim," expositions on Genesis, published at Belvedere, near Constantinople. He also edited the responsa of the Geonim, comprising 400 numbers, Constantinople, 1575.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 224; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1726. K. B. P.
