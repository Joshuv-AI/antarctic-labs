In this article, Fig. 3 appeared incorrectly and has now been corrected in the original publication. For completeness and transparency, the correct and old incorrect versions are displayed below.

Incorrect Figure 3Fig. 3Effects of renalase knockdown on intracellular signaling pathways in satellite cells. (a) Representative immunoblots and quantitative analysis of STAT3 phosphorylation in satellite cells at day three. (c) Representative immunoblots and quantitative analysis of p38 MAPK, ERK1/2, and Akt phosphorylation in satellite cells at day three. Data are shown as mean ± SEM (n = 4). Statistical significance was determined using Student’s t-test. ***P < 0.001, **P < 0.01 vs. sh-NC; ns, not significant
