Retraction Note: Flavokawain C suppresses nephroblastoma growth by inducing autophagy-mediated downregulation of FABP4 via AMPK/mTOR pathway
Authors: Qi Liu, Yanjun Tian, Guijun Li, Sheng Zhang, Yangxu Gao, Xiaoyan Ma, Zeli Su, Weining Fan, Hongping Li
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-42414-1, published online 04 March 2026

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, it was brought to the Editors’ attention that the G401 cells used as a model for nephroblastoma in the study are known to be derived from a paediatric renal rhabdoid tumor1. Therefore the claims of this Article regarding the effect of Flavokawain C on nephroblastoma are unsupported.

All Authors agree with this retraction.

Footnotes
