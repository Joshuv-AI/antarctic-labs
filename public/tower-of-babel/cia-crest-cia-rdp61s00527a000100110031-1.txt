3. Present estimate of the results

Of the 36 people we interviewed, 9 were given Personal History
Statements to fill out} too others of immediate interest had FHS’s on file.
These eleven are interested, are available next summer, and appear to us to
be excellent prospects for the Agency in general and for ORR in particular.
Thirty-six additional people interviewed are of interest to ORR at a later
date (upon completion of additional graduate work or military service) or
appear to us to be good prospects for some other office. These people were
not given PHS's but we are filing interview reports on them because of their
potential value to the Agency. The balance of those interviewed are con*
