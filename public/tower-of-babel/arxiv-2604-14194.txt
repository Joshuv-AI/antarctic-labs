Finite Field Tarski-Maligranda Inequalities
K. Mahesh Krishna

Abstract




Abstract
: Let
\mathbb{F}
be a sub-modulus field such that
2\neq 0
. Let
\mathcal{X}
be a sub-normed linear space over
\mathbb{F}
. Then we show that

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}-(\|x\|+\|y\|)

and

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|-\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}.

Above inequalities are finite field versions of important Tarski-Maligranda inequalities obained by Maligranda [
Banach J. Math. Anal., 2008
].

Keywords
: Normed linear space, Finite field, sub-modulus field, sub-normed linear space.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 12E20, 46B20.

1.
Introduction

We all know that

(1)

\displaystyle\bigg||r|-|s|\bigg|\leq\min\{|r-s|,|r+s|\},\quad\forall r,s\in\mathbb{R}.

In 1930, Tarski observed a strengthening of Inequality (
1
) (Page 688,
[
2
]
):

(2)

\displaystyle\bigg||r|-|s|\bigg|=|r-s|+|r+s|-(|r|+|s|),\quad\forall r,s\in\mathbb{R}.

Note that Equality (
2
) fails for complex numbers
[
1
]
. In 2008, Maligranda showed that one can generalize Equality (
2
) by deriving inequalities that hold for any normed linear space (NLS)
[
1
]
.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
1
]
(
Tarski-Maligranda Inequalities
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NLS. Then for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
,

(3)

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|

\displaystyle\leq\|x+y\|+\|x-y\|-(\|x\|+\|y\|)

and

(4)

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|-\bigg|\|x+y\|-\|x-y\|\bigg|.

We ask for finite field versions of Inequalities (
3
) and (
4
).
We begin by introducing the notion of sub-modulus field.

Definition 1.2
.

Let
\mathbb{F}
be a (finite) field. A map
|\cdot|:\mathbb{F}\to[0,\infty)
is said to be sub-modulus or sub-valued if the following conditions hold.

(i)

If
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}
is such that
|\lambda|=0
, then
\lambda=0
.

(ii)

|\lambda\mu|\leq|\lambda||\mu|
for all
\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb{F}
.

(iii)

|\lambda+\mu|\leq|\lambda|+|\mu|
for all
\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb{F}
.

In this case, we say
\mathbb{F}
as sub-modulus or sub-valued field.

Example 1.3
.

Let
p
be a prime, let
\mathbb{Z}_{p}\coloneqq\{0,1,\dots,p-1\}
be the standard field of integers modulo
p
. Given
n\in\mathbb{Z}_{p}
, we define

\displaystyle|n|\coloneqq\text{unique }a\text{ such that }0\leq a\leq p-1\text{ and }n\equiv a(\text{mod }p).

We next introduce the notion of sub-normed linear space.

Definition 1.4
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a vector space over a sub-modulus field
\mathbb{F}
. A map
\|\cdot\|:\mathcal{X}\to[0,\infty)
is said to be sub-norm if the following conditions hold.

(i)

If
x\in\mathcal{X}
is such that
\|x\|=0
, then
x=0
.

(ii)

\|\lambda x\|\leq|\lambda|\|x\|
for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{F}
, for all
x\in\mathcal{X}
.

(iii)

\|x+y\|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|
for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
.

In this case, we say
\mathcal{X}
is a sub-normed linear space.

Example 1.5
.

Let
p\in[1,\infty)
. Let
\mathbb{F}
be a sub-modulus field. For
d\in\mathbb{N}
, let
\mathbb{F}^{d}
be the standard vector space. We define

\displaystyle\|(a_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\|_{p}\coloneqq\left(\sum_{j=1}^{d}|a_{j}|^{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},\quad\forall(a_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\in\mathbb{F}^{d}.

Then
\|\cdot\|_{p}
is a sub-norm on
\mathbb{F}^{d}
.

Example 1.6
.

Let
\mathbb{F}
be a sub-modulus field. Define

\displaystyle c_{00}(\mathbb{N},\mathbb{F})\coloneqq\left\{\{a_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}:a_{n}\in\mathbb{F},\forall n\in\mathbb{N},a_{n}\neq 0\text{ only for finitely many }n\right\}.

We define

\displaystyle\|\{a_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\|_{00}\coloneqq\max_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|a_{n}|,\quad\forall\{a_{n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\in c_{00}(\mathbb{N},\mathbb{F}).

Then
\|\cdot\|_{00}
is a sub-norm on
c_{00}(\mathbb{N},\mathbb{F}).

2.
Finite Field Tarski-Maligranda Inequalities

It is interesting to note that Inequality (
1
) may not hold for sub-modulus field. Fundamental reason for this is
|r|\neq|-r|
in general, in a sub-valued field
\mathbb{F}
.
For example, in
\mathbb{Z}_{3}
,
1=|1|\neq|-1|=|2|=2
. Hence

\displaystyle\bigg||0|-|1|\bigg|=\bigg|-|1|\bigg|=|-1|=|2|=2>\min\{|0-1|,|0+1|\}=\min\{|-1|,|1|\}=\min\{|2|,|1|\}=1.

We first derive finite field version of Inequality (
1
).

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a sub-normed linear space. Then

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}.

Proof.

Let
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
. Then

\displaystyle\|x\|=\|(x-y)+y\|\leq\|x-y\|+\|y\|

and

\displaystyle\|y\|=\|(y-x)+x\|\leq\|y-x\|+\|x\|.

Hence

\displaystyle\|x\|-\|y\|=\|(x-y)+y\|\leq\|x-y\|

and

\displaystyle\|y\|-\|x\|=\|(y-x)+x\|\leq\|y-x\|.

Therefore

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|=\max\{\|x\|-\|y\|,\|y\|-\|x\|\}\leq\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}.

∎

Following is finite field version of Theorem
1.1
.

Theorem 2.2
.

(
Finite Field Tarski-Maligranda Inequalities
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a sub-normed linear space over sub-modulus field
\mathbb{F}
. Assume
2\neq 0
. Then for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
,

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}-(\|x\|+\|y\|)

and

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|-\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}.

Proof.

Let
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
. Then

(5)

\displaystyle\|x\|+\|y\|+\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|=2\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}.

We also have

(6)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|+\|x-y\|\geq\|(x+y)+(x-y)\|=\|2x\|=|2|\|x\|

and

(7)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|+\|y-x\|\geq\|(x+y)+(y-x)\|=\|2y\|=|2|\|y\|.

Inequalities (
6
) and (
7
) give

(8)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|+\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}=\max\{\|x+y\|+\|x-y\|,\|x+y\|+\|y-x\|\}\geq|2|\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}.

Equality (
5
) and Inequality (
8
) give

\displaystyle\|x\|+\|y\|+\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|=2\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}\leq\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}.

Rearranging,

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}-(\|x\|+\|y\|).

We next see that

(9)

\displaystyle\|x\|+\|y\|-\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|=2\min\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}.

We note that

(10)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|\leq\|(x+y)-(y-x)+\|y-x\|=|2|\|x\|+\|y-x\|\implies\|x+y\|-\|y-x\|\leq|2|\|x\|

and

(11)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|\leq\|(x+y)-(x-y)+\|x-y\|=|2|\|y\|+\|x-y\|\implies\|x+y\|-\|x-y\|\leq|2|\|y\|.

Inequalities (
10
) and (
11
) give

(12)

\displaystyle\|x+y\|-\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}=\min\{\|x+y\|-\|y-x\|,\|x+y\|-\|x-y\|\}\leq|2|\min\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}.

Equality (
9
) and Inequality (
12
) give

\displaystyle\|x+y\|-\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}\leq\frac{|2|}{2}(\|x\|+\|y\|)-\frac{|2|}{2}\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|.

Rearranging,

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|-\frac{2}{|2|}\|x+y\|+\frac{2}{|2|}\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}.

∎

Corollary 2.3
.

Let
p>2
be a prime number. Let
\mathcal{X}
be a sub-normed linear space over sub-modulus field
\mathbb{Z}_{p}
. Then for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
,

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x+y\|+\max\{\|x-y\|,\|y-x\|\}-(\|x\|+\|y\|)

and

\displaystyle\bigg|\|x\|-\|y\|\bigg|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|-\|x+y\|+\max\{\|y-x\|,\|x-y\|\}.

3.
Conclusions

(1)

In 1930, Tarski observed an equality in the triangle inequality for real numbers
[
2
]
.

(2)

In 2008, Maligranda generalized Tarski equality to inequalities which are valid in every normed linear spaces
[
1
]
.

(3)

In this note, we derived finite field version of Tarski-Maligranda inequalities.
