President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (1 September 1944, d45)

top secret priority

[Hyde Park,] 1 September 1944.

Number 614, personal and top secret, from the President to the Prime Minister.

So happy your temperature is normal but this is the time to go slow. Remember I am entirely free and can go to Octagon literally any time if the doctors suggest a postponement for a week or several weeks. I suggest you make Clemmie[2] the arbiter. The armies keep rolling along and a few weeks of delay on our part will not slow them up.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House Map Room; forwarded to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Mrs. Churchill.
