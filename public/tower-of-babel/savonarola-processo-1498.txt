Processo di Fra Girolamo Savonarola (1498)
==============================================
Anonymous official pamphlet. The examination (examina) of Fra Girolamo Savonarola of Ferrara, of the Order of Preachers, conducted by the Florentine Signoria's examiners, with two letters of Pope Alexander VI appended. Printed 1498; the first printed record of a heresy/witchcraft-adjacent show trial's interrogation. Text: raw OCR of the 1498 incunabulum (Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze, Magl. Cust. G.6), via Internet Archive; ProQuest/Early European Books headers removed. Public Domain.
