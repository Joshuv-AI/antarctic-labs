Poetess of Spanish descent; lived in England in the early part of the eighteenth century, as did also Abraham Henriques Pimentel. She wrote "Espejo Fiel de Vidas" (London, 1720), laudatory Spanish verses on the Spanish metrical translation of the Psalms by the Marano poet Daniel Israel Lopez Laguna.

Bibliography: Kayserling, Sephardim Romanische Poesien der Juden in Spanien, pp. 251, 299. J. I. Co.
