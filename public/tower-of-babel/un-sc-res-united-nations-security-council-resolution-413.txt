Adopted at the 2025th meeting by consensus, on 20 July 1977.

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam for admission to the United Nations,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam be admitted to membership in the United Nations.
