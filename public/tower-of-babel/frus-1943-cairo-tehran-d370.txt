Editorial Note (Cairo-Tehran 1943, Document 370)

No official minutes of this meeting have been found. Apparently the principal subjects discussed were Iran’s economic problems and the desire of the United States to assist in their solution. The list of those present and this reference to the subjects discussed are based on (1) the Log, ante, p. 468; (2) a memorandum of January 29, 1954, by a Department of State historian of a conversation with Hurley (023.1/–2954); (3) a letter of February 19, 1954, from Dreyfus (640.0029/2–1954); and (4) Elliott Roosevelt, p. 192.
