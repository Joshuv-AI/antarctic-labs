Abstract
Huang and Li [arXiv:2603.00885] have raised the following two points regarding our previous work [arXiv:2502.18029]:(1) The real-value conditions associated with μ \mu - τ \tau reflection symmetry were overlooked. (2) Inverted Ordering (IO) remains viable when the latest experimental data are taken into account. As they have pointed out, we overlooked an important real-value condition. However, with regard to point (2), we believe that there may be a misunderstanding. In our original study, we excluded IO based on the constraint on the sum of neutrino masses, ∑ m ν \sum m_{\nu} . In contrast, they argue that IO remains viable when considering the effective neutrino mass, | M e ​ e | |M_{ee}| . While IO may indeed remain allowed in light of the latest | M e ​ e | |M_{ee}| data, it is still in tension with the experimental bounds on ∑ m ν \sum m_{\nu} under approximate μ − τ \mu-\tau symmetry within the discussed model parameter space.
