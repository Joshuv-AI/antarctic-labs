Polish-Hungarian rabbi at Paks, Hungary; born at Cracow. He was the author of "Maḳḳel No'am" (Vienna, 1799), in which he sharply criticized Aaron Chorin for declaring the sturgeon permissible food.

Bibliography: Löw, Gesammelte Schriften, ii. 263-267; Schreiber, Reformed Judaism, pp. 68-70; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 343; Zeitlin, Bibl. Post-Mendels. p. 57. D. S. Man.
