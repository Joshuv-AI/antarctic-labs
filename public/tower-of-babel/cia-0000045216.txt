EL SALVADOR: BISHOP PREDICTS WAVE OF REPRISALS :
PA®58335

PANAMA CITY ACAN IN SPANISH 2341 GMT 4 OCT 85

((TEXT)) PANAMA CITY, 4 OCT {(ACAN-EFE} +- A SALVADORAN
BISHOP ASSERTED TODAY THAT A "WAVE OF REPRISALS MIGHT BE UNLEASHE"
IN HIS COUNTRY IF THE TACIT AGREEMENT SETWEEN THE TWO SIDES "OF
NOT TOUCHING THE RELATIVES OF THE PERSONS DIRECTLY INVOLVED IN
THE STRUGGLE" IS NOT RESPECTED.

SAN SALVADOR AUXILIARY BISHOP MSGR GREGORIO ROSAS
CHAVEZ WAS REFERRING TO THE KIDNAPPING OF PRESIDENT JOSE
NAPOLEON DUARTE’ S DAUGHTER, PRESUMABLY PERPETRATED BY A GUERRILLA
GROUP.

ROSAS CHAVEZ PARTICIPATED IN A MEETING OF THE CENTRAL
AMERICAN EPISCOPAL SECRETARIAT, WHICH BEGAN LAST WEDNESDAY AND
CONCLUDED TODAY IN THIS CAPITAL, AND INCLUDED THE PARTICIRATION OF
BISHOPS FROM THE REGION. .

THIRTY-FIVE-YEAR-OLD INES GUADALUPE DUARTE WAS KIDNAPPED LAST
18 SEPTEMBER ALONG WITH A CLASSMATE, AND HER -KIDNAPPERS HAVE
OFFERED TO EXCHANGE HER FOR 34 GUERRILLA MEMBERS WHO ARE IN PRISON,
ACCORDING TO UNOFFICIAL SOURCES WHICH HAVE NOT BEEN REFUTED BY THE
GOVERNMENT.

EVEN THOUGH NO REBEL GROUP HAS PUBLICLY CLAIMED
RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE KIONAPPING, “IT IS AN OPEN SECRET" THAT THE
AUTHORS ARE MEMBERS OF A GUERRILLA ORGANIZATION, AS STATED BY .
BISHOP ROSAS CHAVEZ TO ACAN-EFE.

"IT £S OBVICUS THAT THE GUERRILLA GROUPS DID IT," THE SALVADORAN I
BISHOP ASSERTED.

ACCORDING TO THE OFFICIAL COMMUNIQUE, THE BISHOPS STUDIED
VARIOUS RELIGIOUS AND EDUCATIONAL MATTERS DURING THE MEETING,.

AND “ANALYZED THE SITUATION IN THEIR RESPECTIVE COUNTRIES CONCERNING
RELIGIOUS MATTERS AND OTHER AREAS AT NATIONAL LEVEL.”

IN ADDITION TO ROSAS CHAVEZ, THE MEETING WAS ALSO ATTENDED

BY THE ARCHBISHOPS OF SAN SALVADOR, GUATEMALA CITY, SAN JOSE,
AND PANAMA CITY,. ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS, PROSPERO PENADOS
ROMAN ARRIETA, ANO MARCOS MCGRATH, RESPECTIVELY
