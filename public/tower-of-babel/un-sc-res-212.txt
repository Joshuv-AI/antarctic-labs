'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 1243rd meeting, unanimously, on 20 September 1965'''

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of the Maldive Islands for admission to the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Maldive Islands be admitted to membership in the United Nations.
