{\cal M}_{\rm A}=\int d^{4}x\left\{\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta A}\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta\Gamma}+\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta\psi_{\alpha}}\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta Y^{\alpha}}+\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta F}\frac{\delta{\cal A}}{\delta\Lambda}\right\}
