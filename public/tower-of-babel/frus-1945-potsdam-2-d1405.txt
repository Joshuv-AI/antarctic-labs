No. 1405 The Ambassador in France (Caffery) to the Acting Secretary of State

restricted us urgent—niact

Paris, August 7, 1945—10 p.m.

4769. Bidault requested Bogomolov, Duff Cooper and myself to call this evening. He delivered to us six identical notes containing the views of the French Govt on the decisions made at Potsdam. The French Govt accepts in each case the decisions but formulates various “reservations”, “regrets” and “observations”. (My 4722, Aug 6, 4 p.m.[1])

The texts of these six notes are being cabled in my six immediately following telegrams.

Caffery
