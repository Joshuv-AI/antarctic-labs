 We report the experimental implementation of a Data-Driven Chaos Indicator (DDCI) [Y. Li et al.,
 Nucl. Instrum. Methods Phys. Res. A 1024 (2022) 166060] for online optimization of the National
 Synchrotron Light Source II (NSLS-II) storage ring. The DDCI quantifies the predictability of
 electron beam dynamics using turn-by-turn beam position monitor data. A surrogate model of the
 one-turn map is first trained, and its out-of-sample predictive uncertainty is then employed as a
 measurable indicator of chaos. By tuning sextupole magnets to mitigate nonlinear effects, a clear
 enlargement of the dynamic aperture is achieved, accompanied by a corresponding improvement in
 injection efficiency.
