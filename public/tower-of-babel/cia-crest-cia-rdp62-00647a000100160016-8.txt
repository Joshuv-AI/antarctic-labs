The main effect of this amendment is to generalise the
administrative exceptions which at present apply only to
equipment for aircraft on scheduled services to the free world*
In the U.Ks view it is unrealistic to maintain an embargo on
equipment whose export may be allowed under these administrative
exceptions. There is nothing to stop the Sino—Sovie t bloc
changing the operating mode of aircraft needed for internal
services to foreign service and vice versa. In this way the
whole of their civil aircraft could be fitted with imported
equipment.

The deletion of the existing (a) (3) and (a) ( 4 ) is
