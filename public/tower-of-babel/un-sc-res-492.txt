Adopted unanimously at the 2309nd meeting, on 10 November 1981.
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of Antigua and Barbuda[1] for admission to the United Nations,
Recommends to the General Assembly that Antigua and Barbuda should be admitted to membership in the United Nations.
