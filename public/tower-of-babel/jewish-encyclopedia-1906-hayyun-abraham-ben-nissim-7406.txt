Portuguese scholar; father of Don Joseph Ḥayyun, rabbi of Lisbon; lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. He was the author of an ethical work entitled "Amarot Ṭehorot" (Constantinople, 1516).

Bibliography: Kayserling, Gesch. der Juden in Portugal, p. 74; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 672; Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 27. J. I. Br.
