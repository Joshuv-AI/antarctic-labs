RNA-programmable cell-type monitoring and manipulation in the human cortex with CellREADR
Authors: Elizabeth A Matthews, Jeffrey B Russ, Yongjun Qian, Shengli Zhao, Peyton Thompson, Muhib Methani, Matthew Vestal, Z Josh Huang, Derek Southwell
Published: 2026

As this article was originally published, the calcium indicator used in the experiments by the authors was erroneously designated “GCaMP7f” when the indicator was in fact “GCaMP7s.” The manuscript has been updated to correctly refer to the calcium indicator used by the authors in their experiments, and Figure 5 has also been updated to reflect this. The errors do not affect the conclusions of the paper. The authors apologize for any confusion caused.
