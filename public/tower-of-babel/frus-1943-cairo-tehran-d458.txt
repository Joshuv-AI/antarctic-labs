Editorial Note (Cairo-Tehran 1943, Document 458)

The only account of this meeting that has been found is in Arnold, p. 473, which indicates that Marshall took a poll of the opinion of officers present as to when each of them thought the war with Germany would be ended. The place of the meeting is not given; the time of the meeting is inferred from Arnold’s account and from the Log, ante, p. 660.

TUESDAY, DECEMBER 7, 1943[1]

Notes
[1] For a post-Conference report of a Steinhardt-Vyshinsky conversation and an Eden–Vyshinsky conversation at Cairo after Roosevelt had departed, see post, pp. 858–859.
