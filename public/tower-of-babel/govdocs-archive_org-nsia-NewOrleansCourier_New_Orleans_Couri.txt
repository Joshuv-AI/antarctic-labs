Dear Mr. Weisberg: 
There seems to be numberous conflicts in the information contained 
in the Warren Commission transcript obtained by you in a suit filed 
under the Freedom of Information Act, and Mr. W. R. Morris's statement 
to the CIA Investigation Committee concerning CIA-Mafia assasination 
conspiracy. 
The recently declassified Warren Commission transcript you ob- 
tained under tl'.e Freedom of Information Act, definitely states that 
Lee Harvey Oswald had worked as an FBI undercover agent for more than 
one year before the assasination of President John F. Kennedy. 
Mr. Morris submitted a lengthy statement to the Rockefeller 
Commission which included information that Lee Harvey Oswald was em- 
ployed by the CIA at the time of President Kennedy's assasination. 
In your book Oswald in New Orleans, you mention rumors that the 
CIA offered a contract to the Mafia, the Mafia refusing and the Minute- 
men accepting - but there is no further elaboration on this•subject. 
I would appreciate it if you could assist me in obtaining a 
complete copy of the Warren Commission transcript as the Associated 
Press cannot give me any assistance. Additionally, I would like to 
have a definite address to correspond with you due to an interest in 
doing an interview with you and former District Attorney Jim Garrison. 
This will be for the purpose of continuing articles in the Courier 
until the assasination plot is completely revealed. 
Please address all correspondence to me at P. 0. Box 53411, New 
Orleans, Louisiana 70153. 
Sincerely, 
Russell Ellwood
