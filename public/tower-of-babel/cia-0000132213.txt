Political Revelopments

Baghdad Radio Calls US POWs "Killers"

Baghdad radio said coalition pilots captured by Iraq should
be treated as killers of women, children, and old people, not as
soldiers waging war against other soldiers. The editorial placed
the pilots’ actions in the context of past Western “colonialisn,
racism, arrogance, and aggression" and said coalition pilots
deliberately bombed residential districts and civilian
installations.

Comment. The editorial is intended to lower the morale of
coalition troops and foster antiwar sentiment on the allied home
fronts. The vehemence of the attack signals that the Iragis will
continue mistreating the POWs. Iraqi officials have repeatedly
stated that Iraq is free to violate the prisoner of war clauses
of the Geneva Convention because US aggression and the UN embargo
also violate the Convention. :

New Italian Gulf Naval Commander

Italy appointed Rear Admiral Enrico Martinotti today to
replace Vice Admiral Mario Buracchia, who resigned Tuesday after
expressing doubts about the coalition’s decision to go to war,
