Oil Blick Update

Winds from the southeast today are temporarily slowing the
southward movement of the oil slick, which is now in Al Dafi bay
about 30 kilometers north of Jubail according to press reports.
Abu Ali island is also blocking the progress of the slick toward
Jubail, but Saudi authorities are worried that wind and currents
may eventually push the slick around this natural oil boon,
according to press. US Embassy reporting indicates overflights
yesterday observed an oily film south of the island.
