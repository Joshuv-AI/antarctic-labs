Talmudic scholar of the sixteenth century. He carried on a learned correspondence with Samuel di Medina ( ), rabbi of Salonica, who refers to him as the "greatest of our teachers." He wrote also a preface to Moses Nagara's "Leḳaḥ Ṭob," Constantinople, 1574.

Bibliography: Michael, Or ha-Ḥayyim, No. 20. M. B.
