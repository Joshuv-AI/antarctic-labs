'''Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 3003rd meeting, on 9 August 1991''' ([1])

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of the Republic of the Marshall Islands for admission to the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Republic of the Marshall Islands be admitted to membership in the United Nations.


[1] Adopted without vote.
