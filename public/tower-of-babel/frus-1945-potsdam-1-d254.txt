No. 254 Note by the President’s Chief of Staff (Leahy)[1]

[Undated.]

Korea

Recommend a four power agreement that upon the defeat of Japan Korea be placed under a trusteeship composed of China, Great Britain, the Soviet Republic [sic], and the United States for so long a time as is necessary for it to demonstrate, and until it shall have demonstrated, its capacity to govern itself as a free and independent sovereign state.

Source: Truman Papers

Notes
[1] Printed from the ribbon copy, which is unsigned.
