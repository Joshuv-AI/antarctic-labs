Russian rabbi of the eighteenth century; author of "Or ha-Ne'erab," Talmudic commentary, with supplementary notes by his son Solomon Zalman Kahana (Ostrog, 1804). He also left in manuscript "Dibre Ḥemed," sermons, etc., and "Zer Zahab," a Biblical commentary.

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 27; Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 34. H. R. J. G. L.
