Convergence rate of the Halpern iterations with possibly distinct anchor and initial guess
Jianing He, Qiao-Li Dong

Abstract
All existing convergence rate estimates of Halpern iterations are established only for the case where the anchor coincides with the initial guess. This paper aims to investigate the convergence rate of general Halpern iterations, where the anchor and the initial guess may not necessarily be the same. We present tight convergence rate estimates for both predetermined and adaptive anchoring parameters. These results generalize existing related work.



Keywords:
Halpern iteration, fixed point, convergence rate, nonexpansive mapping.

2020 AMS Subject Classification:
Primary 47J26, 47J25; secondary 47H09, 65J15.

Introduction

The calculation of fixed points for nonlinear operators, especially nonexpansive mappings, has always been an interesting topic due to its theoretical significance and practical value. The Halpern iteration is one of the most commonly used algorithms for finding fixed points of nonexpansive mappings.

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a real Hilbert space with inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
and norm
\|\cdot\|
respectively and let
T:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
be a nonexpansive mapping. We use
\Fix(T)
to denote the set of fixed points of
T
, that is,
\Fix(T)=\{x\in\mathcal{H}\,|\,x=Tx\}
.
The Halpern iteration was first proposed by Halpern
[
4
]
in 1967 in a Hilbert space. It generates,
with an initial guess
x^{0}\in\mathcal{H}
arbitrarily chosen, a sequence
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
by the iteration process:

x^{k}=\lambda_{k}u+(1-\lambda_{k})Tx^{k-1},\qquad k=1,2,\ldots,

(1)

where
u
is a fixed point in
\mathcal{H}
, referred to as anchor, and the parameters
\{\lambda_{k}\}_{k=1}^{\infty}
are in
(0,1)
,
which will be referred to as anchoring parameters. Some researchers developed the sufficient conditions on anchoring parameters for guaranteeing the strong convergence of Halpern iteration
[
4
,
11
,
17
,
15
,
18
]
. He et al.
[
5
]
first studied the adaptive selections of optimal anchoring parameters for the Halpern iteration.

Estimating the convergence rate of an algorithm is a very important topic. To estimate the convergence rate of the Halpern iteration, several researchers have studied the properties of the displacement
\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|
, see
[
5
]
and
[
9
,
7
,
8
]
.
For more details, the reader is referred to the survey article
[
12
]
.

Recently, there have been two important progresses in estimating the convergence rate of the Halpern iteration. The first one is about the Halpern iteration with
u=x^{0}
and
\lambda_{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}
. Lieder
[
10
]
presented
the tight convergence rate estimate

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+1}\|x^{0}-x^{*}\|,\quad k\geq 1,

(2)

where
x^{*}
is an arbitrary fixed point of
T
.
The second one is about the Halpern iteration:

x^{k}=\frac{1}{\varphi_{k}+1}x^{0}+\frac{\varphi_{k}}{\varphi_{k}+1}Tx^{k-1},

(3)

with
u=x^{0}
and

\varphi_{k}:=\frac{2\langle x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1},x^{0}-x^{k-1}\rangle}{\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}}+1.

(4)

He et al.
[
6
]
provided the tight convergence rate estimate

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}+1}\|x^{0}-x^{*}\|,\quad k\geq 1,

(5)

where
x^{*}
is an arbitrary fixed point of
T
. In general, (
5
) is an improvement of
(
2
) owing to
\varphi_{k}\geq k
for all
k\geq 1
(see
[
6
, Lemma 3.1(i)]
).
These convergence rate estimates motivate the research on applying Halpern iteration to accelerate iterative methods in machine learning (generative adversarial networks (GANs), in particular)
[
1
,
19
]

and structural optimizations (see, e.g.,
[
13
,
14
,
16
,
20
]
).

We note that the existing convergence rate estimates of Halpern iterations are all restricted to the case where the anchor
u
and initial guess
x^{0}
are the same. In this paper, we aim to investigate the convergence rate of general Halpern iterations, where
u
and
x^{0}
may be distinct.
We provide the tight convergence rate estimates for both predetermined and adaptive anchoring parameters, which generalize (
2
) and (
5
), respectively.

The organization of the paper is as follows. In the next section we list several concepts and lemmas in a real Hilbert space that will be used in the convergence analysis of the algorithms. The main achievements of this paper are presented in Section 3.
A brief summary and commentary on the research results of this paper are provided in Section 4.

2
Preliminaries

Let
\mathcal{H}
be a real Hilbert space with inner product
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle
and norm
\|\cdot\|
. Some concepts and tools are listed in this section, which will be used in the proofs of our main results. The following notations will be used
throughout the rest of the paper.

(i)

I:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
denotes the identity operator.

(ii)

\rightarrow
and
\rightharpoonup
denote the strong convergence and the weak convergence, respectively.

(iii)

\omega_{w}(x^{k}):=\{x\mid\exists\,\,{\rm subsequence}\,\,\{x^{k_{i}}\}_{i=0}^{\infty}\subset\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}

such that
x^{k_{i}}\rightharpoonup x\}
denotes the
\omega
-weak limit point set of
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
.

Let
C
be a closed convex subset of
\mathcal{H}
. For an arbitrary point
u\in\mathcal{H}
,
the classical metric projection of

u
onto
C
, denoted by
P_{C}u
, is defined by

P_{C}u:=\argmin\{\|u-v\|\,|\,\,\forall v\in C\}.

Lemma 2.1
.

(
[
2
, Section 3]
)

Let
C
be a closed convex subset of
\mathcal{H}
. For
x\in\mathcal{H}
and
z\in C
,
z=P_{C}x
if and only if

\langle x-z,y-z\rangle\leq 0,\quad\forall\,y\in C.

Recall that a mapping
T:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
is called nonexpansive if

\|Tx-Ty\|\leq\|x-y\|

for all
x,y\in\mathcal{H}
.

Lemma 2.2
.

(The demiclosedness principle for nonexpansive mappings
[
3
]
)

Let
C
be a nonempty closed convex subset of a real Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
and let
T:C\rightarrow C
be a nonexpansive mapping such that
\Fix(T)\neq\emptyset
. If a sequence
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
in
C
is such that
x^{k}\rightharpoonup z
and
\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\rightarrow 0
, then
z=Tz
.

The following result is elementary and can be verified straightforwardly. We formalize it as a lemma as it will be useful in our analysis.

Lemma 2.3
.

The identity

\varphi\|a\|^{2}+2\langle a,b\rangle+\|c\|^{2}-\|a+c\|^{2}=\frac{\varphi+1}{2}\|a\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi+1}\|a+c-b\|^{2}+\frac{2}{\varphi+1}\|a+c-b-\frac{\varphi+1}{2}a\|^{2}

(6)

holds for any
a,b,c\in\mathcal{H}
and any number
\varphi>-1
.

Lemma 2.4
.

[
18
]

Assume that
\{a_{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
is a sequence of nonnegative real numbers such that

a_{k+1}\leq(1-\gamma_{k})a_{k}+\gamma_{k}\delta_{k},~k\geq 0,

where
\{\gamma_{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
is a sequence in
(0,1)
and
\{\delta_{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
is a real sequence such that

(i)

\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\gamma_{k}=\infty
;

(ii)

\limsup_{k\rightarrow\infty}\delta_{k}\leq 0~or~\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|\gamma_{k}\delta_{k}|<\infty.

Then
\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}a_{k}=0.

3
Main results

Both estimates (
2
) by Lieder and (
5
) by He et al. are established for nonexpansive mappings under the assumption that the anchor
u
coincides with the initial guess
x^{0}
, i.e.,
u=x^{0}
. To the best of our knowledge, no convergence rate estimates have been given for the general case where
u
and
x^{0}
are possibly distinct. In this section, we address this issue.

3.1
Convergence rates of general Halpern iterations with predetermined anchoring parameters

In this subsection, we estimate the convergence rate of the following Halpern iteration

x^{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}u+\frac{k}{k+1}Tx^{k-1},\quad k\geq 1,

(7)

where the anchor
u
and the initial guess
x^{0}
are chosen arbitrarily in
\mathcal{H}
.

Theorem 3.1
.

Assume that
T:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
is a nonexpansive mapping such that
\Fix(T)\neq\emptyset
.
Let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be a sequence generated by the scheme (
7
).
Then we have

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+1}\left(\|u-x^{0}\|+\|u-x^{*}\|\right),\quad k\geq 1,

(8)

where
x^{*}
is an arbitrary fixed point of
T
.
The convergence rate estimate (
8
) is tight.

Proof.

Let
y^{0}=u
and let
\{y^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be a sequence generated by the scheme:

y^{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}u+\frac{k}{k+1}Ty^{k-1},\quad k\geq 1.

(9)

According to (
2
), one has that for any
x^{*}\in\Fix(T)
,

\|y^{k}-Ty^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+1}\|y^{0}-x^{*}\|,\quad\forall k\geq 1.

(10)

On the other hand, we get from (
7
) and (
9
) that

\|x^{k}-y^{k}\|=\frac{k}{k+1}\|Tx^{k-1}-Ty^{k-1}\|\leq\frac{k}{k+1}\|x^{k-1}-y^{k-1}\|,\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

Consequently, it follows by induction that

\|x^{k}-y^{k}\|\leq\frac{1}{k+1}\|x^{0}-y^{0}\|,\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

(11)

Noting
y^{0}=u
, we obtain by combining (
10
) and (
11
) that

\displaystyle\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|

\displaystyle\leq\|x^{k}-y^{k}\|+\|y^{k}-Ty^{k}\|+\|Ty^{k}-Tx^{k}\|

(12)

\displaystyle\leq 2\|x^{k}-y^{k}\|+\|y^{k}-Ty^{k}\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{2}{k+1}\left(\|y^{0}-x^{0}\|+\|y^{0}-x^{*}\|\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{2}{k+1}\left(\|u-x^{0}\|+\|u-x^{*}\|\right).

Thus, (
8
) is established.
∎

The tightness of (
8
) will be confirmed by the following example.

Example 3.1
.

Let
\mathcal{H}=\mathbb{R}^{2}
and
T:\mathbb{R}^{2}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{2}
be defined by

T\left(\begin{aligned} &\xi\\
&\eta\end{aligned}\right)=\left(\begin{matrix}-1&0\\
0&-1\\
\end{matrix}\right)\left(\begin{aligned} &\xi\\
&\eta\end{aligned}\right),\,\,\forall\left(\begin{aligned} &\xi\\
&\eta\end{aligned}\right)\in\mathbb{R}^{2}.

(13)

Since
T
is a counterclockwise
\pi
-rotation centered at
(0,0)^{\top}
, it is obviously a nonexpansive mapping with the unique fixed point
x^{*}=(0,0)^{\top}
.

Let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be the sequence generated by the Halpern iteration (
7
) with
u=(1,0)^{\top}
and
x^{0}=(2,0)^{\top}
. It is easy to get from (
7
) and (
13
) that

x^{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}\{u+Tu+T^{2}u+\cdots+T^{k-1}u+T^{k}x^{0}\},\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

Consequently, we get

x^{k}-Tx^{k}=\frac{1}{k+1}(u-T^{k}u+T^{k}x^{0}-T^{k+1}x^{0}),\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

(14)

Note that
T
is norm-preserving as a rotation operator, therefore for all
k\geq 1
,

\displaystyle\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{k+1}(\|T^{k}(x^{0}-Tx^{0})\|-\|u-T^{k}u\|)

(15)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{k+1}(\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|-2\|u\|)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2(\|x^{0}\|-\|u\|)}{k+1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2}{k+1}.

Combining (
8
) and (
15
), we assert that

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|
and
\frac{1}{k+1}
are infinitesimals of the same order. Hence the Halpern iteration (
7
) has sublinear convergence rate. Moreover, using
T^{2}=I
, we also have from (
14
) that

\|x^{2k}-Tx^{2k}\|=\frac{1}{2k+1}\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|=\frac{4}{2k+1}=\frac{2}{2k+1}(\|u-x^{0}\|+\|u-x^{*}\|),\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

This implies that (
8
) is tight.

Remark 3.1
.

When
u=x^{0}
, (
8
) becomes (
2
), hence the latter is a special case of the former.

Next we investigate the convergence rate estimate for the Halpern iteration

x^{k}=\frac{1}{k+3}u+\frac{k+2}{k+3}Tx^{k-1},\quad k\geq 1,

(16)

where the initial guess
x^{0}
is chosen arbitrarily in
\mathcal{H}
and the anchor
u=2x^{0}-Tx^{0}
.

Theorem 3.2
.

Assume that
T:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
is a nonexpansive mapping such that
\Fix(T)\neq\emptyset
.
Let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be a sequence generated by the scheme (
16
).
Then we have

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+3}\|u-x^{*}\|,\quad k\geq 1,

(17)

where
x^{*}
is an arbitrary fixed point of
T
. The convergence rate estimate (
17
) is tight.

Proof.

We first show (
17
). It follows from (
16
) that

x^{k}-x^{k-1}=\frac{1}{k+3}(u-Tx^{k-1})+(Tx^{k-1}-x^{k-1}).

Consequently, we obtain

\displaystyle\|x^{k}-x^{k-1}\|^{2}

(18)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(k+3)^{2}}\|u-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}+\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+3}\langle u-Tx^{k-1},Tx^{k-1}-x^{k-1}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{(k+3)^{2}}\|u-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}+\frac{k+1}{k+3}\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+3}\langle u-x^{k-1},Tx^{k-1}-x^{k-1}\rangle.

Using (
16
) again, we also have

x^{k}-u=\frac{k+2}{k+3}(Tx^{k-1}-u),

and

Tx^{k-1}-Tx^{k}=\frac{1}{k+2}(x^{k}-u)+(x^{k}-Tx^{k}),

which imply

\frac{1}{(k+2)^{2}}\|x^{k}-u\|^{2}=\frac{1}{(k+3)^{2}}\|u-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2},

(19)

and

\|Tx^{k-1}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}=\frac{1}{(k+2)^{2}}\|x^{k}-u\|^{2}+\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+2}\langle x^{k}-u,x^{k}-Tx^{k}\rangle,

(20)

respectively. Using the nonexpansiveness of
T
and combining (
18
)-(
20
), we deduce

\displaystyle\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+2}\langle x^{k}-u,x^{k}-Tx^{k}\rangle

(21)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|x^{k-1}-x^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{1}{(k+2)^{2}}\|x^{k}-u\|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{k+1}{k+3}\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+3}\langle u-x^{k-1},Tx^{k-1}-x^{k-1}\rangle.

Letting
k=1
in (
21
) and substituting
u=2x^{0}-Tx^{0}
into its right-hand side yields

\|x^{1}-Tx^{1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{3}\langle x^{1}-u,x^{1}-Tx^{1}\rangle\leq\frac{1}{2}\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},Tx^{0}-x^{0}\rangle=0.

(22)

Based on (
21
) and (
22
), it can be easily proven by induction that

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+2}\langle x^{k}-u,x^{k}-Tx^{k}\rangle\leq 0,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1,

(23)

which yields

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+2}\|x^{k}-u\|,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

(24)

Let
a=x^{k}-Tx^{k}
,
b=x^{k}-u
,
c=Tx^{k}-x^{*}
and
\varphi=k+2
. Then
a+c=x^{k}-x^{*}
and
a+c-b=u-x^{*}
. Using (
6
), we have

\displaystyle(k+2)\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+2\langle x^{k}-Tx^{k},x^{k}-u\rangle+\|Tx^{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\|x^{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}

(25)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{k+3}{2}\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{k+3}\|u-x^{*}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{k+3}\|u-x^{*}-\frac{k+3}{2}(x^{k}-Tx^{k})\|^{2}.

Meanwhile, we get from (
23
) that

(k+2)\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+2\langle x^{k}-u,x^{k}-Tx^{k}\rangle+\|Tx^{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\|x^{k}-x^{*}\|\leq 0,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

(26)

Comparing (
25
) and (
26
), it is easy to see that

\frac{k+3}{2}\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{k+3}\|u-x^{*}\|^{2}\leq 0,

and consequently (
17
) holds true.

Next, we show that (
17
) is tight. To this end, we adopt the scheme (
16
) to solve Example
3.1
, where
x^{0}\neq(0,0)^{\top}
is chosen arbitrarily and
u=2x^{0}-Tx^{0}
.
It is easy to get from (
16
) and (
13
) that

x^{k}=\frac{1}{k+3}\left(u+Tu+T^{2}u+\cdots+T^{k-1}u+3T^{k}x^{0}\right),\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

Consequently, we get

x^{k}-Tx^{k}=\frac{1}{k+3}\left[u-T^{k}u+3(T^{k}x^{0}-T^{k+1}x^{0})\right],\,\,\,\,\forall k\geq 1.

(27)

Using
T^{2}=I
and (
27
), we have

\|x^{2k}-Tx^{2k}\|=\frac{3}{2k+3}\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|=\frac{6}{2k+3}\|x^{0}\|=\frac{2}{2k+3}\|u-x^{*}\|.

This implies that (
17
) is tight.
∎

Remark 3.2
.

It is obvious that the convergence rate (
17
) is better than (
8
). Furthermore, let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be generated by the scheme (
7
) with
u=2x^{0}-Tx^{0}
, then by (
8
) we obtain the following convergence rate estimate:

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{k+1}\{\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|+\|u-x^{*}\|\},\quad k\geq 1,

(28)

which is also worse than (
17
).

3.2
Convergence rate of general Halpern iterations with adaptive anchoring parameters

In this subsection, we consider the general Halpern iteration with adaptive anchoring parameters:

x^{k}=\frac{1}{\varphi_{k}+1}u+\frac{\varphi_{k}}{\varphi_{k}+1}Tx^{k-1},\quad k\geq 1,

(29)

where
\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k=1}^{\infty}
is given by

\varphi_{k}:=\frac{2\langle x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1},u-x^{k-1}\rangle}{\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}}+1,\quad k\geq 1,

(30)

and the anchor
u
and the initial guess
x^{0}
are chosen in
\mathcal{H}
such that
\varphi_{0}:=\frac{2\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-x^{0}\rangle}{\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}}>-1
.

Remark 3.3
.

Without loss of generality, we always assume that
x^{k}\neq Tx^{k}
for all
k\geq 0
in the algorithm (
29
)-(
30
).
Namely, it generates an infinite sequence of iterates
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
.

The following lemma provides the fundamental conclusions of the algorithm (
29
)-(
30
).

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be generated by the algorithm (
29
)-(
30
). Then the following properties hold for all
k\geq 1
,

(i)

\varphi_{k}\geq\varphi_{0}+k
,

(ii)

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}\leq\dfrac{2}{\varphi_{k}}\langle x^{k}-Tx^{k},u-x^{k}\rangle.

Proof.

We prove the conclusions (i) and (ii) by induction.
Noting
\varphi_{0}:=\frac{2\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-x^{0}\rangle}{\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}}>-1
, we get from (
30
) that
\varphi_{1}=\varphi_{0}+1>0
and this means that (i) holds for
k=1
.
From (
29
), we derive that

Tx^{0}=\frac{\varphi_{1}+1}{\varphi_{1}}x^{1}-\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}}u=x^{1}+\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}}(x^{1}-u).

(31)

By the nonexpansiveness of
T
and (
31
), we have

\displaystyle\|x^{0}-x^{1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\|Tx^{0}-Tx^{1}\|^{2}=\|(x^{1}-Tx^{1})+\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}}(x^{1}-u)\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|x^{1}-Tx^{1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}}\langle x^{1}-Tx^{1},x^{1}-u\rangle+\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}^{2}}\|x^{1}-u\|^{2}.

(32)

Using (
29
) again, we obtain

\displaystyle\|x^{0}-x^{1}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|(x^{0}-Tx^{0})-\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}+1}(u-Tx^{0})\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}+1}\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-Tx^{0}\rangle+\frac{1}{(\varphi_{1}+1)^{2}}\|u-Tx^{0}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}+1}\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-Tx^{0}\rangle+\frac{1}{\varphi_{1}^{2}}\|x^{1}-u\|^{2}.

(33)

Combining (
3.2
) and (
3.2
) implies

\displaystyle\|x^{1}-Tx^{1}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}}\langle x^{1}-Tx^{1},x^{1}-u\rangle

(34)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}+1}\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-Tx^{0}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{\varphi_{1}-1}{\varphi_{1}+1}\|x^{0}-Tx^{0}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}+1}\langle x^{0}-Tx^{0},u-x^{0}\rangle

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0,

where the second equality comes from (
30
).
Consequently,

\|x^{1}-Tx^{1}\|^{2}\leq\frac{2}{\varphi_{1}}\langle x^{1}-Tx^{1},u-x^{1}\rangle.

So (ii) also holds for
k=1
.

Suppose that (i) and (ii) hold for
k-1
(
k\geq 2
), that is,

\varphi_{k-1}\geq\varphi_{0}+k-1,

(35)

and

\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}\leq\frac{2}{\varphi_{k-1}}\langle x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1},u-x^{k-1}\rangle.

(36)

Then
\varphi_{k-1}\geq\varphi_{0}+k-1>k-2\geq 0
. By (
30
), (
35
) and (
36
), we get

\varphi_{k}=\frac{2\langle x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1},u-x^{k-1}\rangle}{\|x^{k-1}-Tx^{k-1}\|^{2}}+1\geq\varphi_{k-1}+1\geq\varphi_{0}+k,

that is, (i) holds for
k
.
Similar to the derivation of (
34
), we have

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}}\langle x^{k}-Tx^{k},x^{k}-u\rangle\leq 0,

which yields

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}\leq\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}}\langle x^{k}-Tx^{k},u-x^{k}\rangle.

Hence (ii) also holds for
k
and the proof is finished.
∎

Theorem 3.3
.

Assume that
T:\mathcal{H}\rightarrow\mathcal{H}
is a nonexpansive mapping such that
\Fix(T)\neq\emptyset
. Let
\{x^{k}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
be a sequence generated by the algorithm (
29
)-(
30
).
Then we have

\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|\leq\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}+1}\|u-x^{*}\|,\quad k\geq 1,

(37)

where
x^{*}
is an arbitrary fixed point of
T
. The convergence rate estimate (
37
) is tight.

Proof.

Set
\varphi:=\varphi_{k}
,

a:=x^{k}-Tx^{k}
,
b:=x^{k}-u
, and
c:=Tx^{k}-x^{*}
, then
a+c=x^{k}-x^{*}
,
a+c-b=u-x^{*}
. By (
6
), it follows that

\displaystyle\varphi_{k}\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}+2\langle x^{k}-Tx^{k},x^{k}-u\rangle+\|Tx^{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}-\|x^{k}-x^{*}\|^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{\varphi_{k}+1}{2}\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}+1}\|u-x^{*}\|^{2}+\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}+1}\|u-x^{*}-\frac{\varphi_{k}+1}{2}(x^{k}-Tx^{k})\|^{2}.

Combining the above equality, Lemma
3.1
(ii) and the nonexpansiveness of
T
, we have

\frac{\varphi_{k}+1}{2}\|x^{k}-Tx^{k}\|^{2}-\frac{2}{\varphi_{k}+1}\|u-x^{*}\|^{2}\leq 0.

It is immediately clear that the estimate (
37
) follows.

The estimate (
37
) is tight, which can be shown by Example 3.1 in
[
10
]
. In fact, by direct calculation,
it is easy to verify that for all
k\geq 1
,
\varphi_{k}=k
and (
37
) becomes an equality for this example.
∎

4
Some concluding remarks

In this paper, we provide the tight convergence rate estimates of general Halpern iterations for both predetermined and adaptive anchoring parameters for the case where the anchor may not coincide with the initial guess. These achievements generalize existing related results. How to further improve the convergence rate of the Halpern iteration deserves further in-depth exploration.
