2. THE STORY CURRENTLY MAKING THE ROUNDS BB : THAT
AN ANNOUNCEMENT WAS MADE AT A 16 NOVEMBER MORNING MEETING AT DNI
HEADQUARTERS THAT REVEREND ELLACURTA HAD BEEN KILLED "TRYING TO
ESCAPE" OR “RESISTING ARREST" (IMPLYING BY THOSE PHRASES THAT HE HAD
BEEN KILLED BY THE MILITARY); THAT THE ANNOUNCEMENT WAS GREETED BY
APPLAUSE, PROMPTING ONE OFFICER TO DECLARE THAT RESPONSE IN-
APPROPRIATE AND SHORT-SIGHTED IN TERMS OF WHAT THE EVENT WOULD
MEAN FOR THE FUTURE OF THE COUNTRY; AND THAT THOSE PRESENT OR
AWARE OF THE EVENTS OF THE MEETING WERE PART OF A COVER-UP
INASMUCH AS THEY DID NOT COME FORWARD WITH THIS INFORMATION.
THIS STORY, OR VARIATIONS ON THE THEME, HAVE GAINED ENOUGH
CURRENCY AMONG THE U.S. MEDIA REPS HERE THAT WE EXPECT WE MAY
WELL SEE SOME_REFERENCE 10 IT IN THE U.S. MEDIA.
