Note a; Norwegian Aluminum.

The Chairman informed the committee that he had recently approved a joint
agencies telegram to our Embassy in Norway (Emb/Oslo A-3). He explained that
some time ago we gave assistance to Norway and agreed to accept in repayment
aluminum for the stockpile,. Now the stockpile authorities have asked to be released
from this commitment. The message to the Embassy asked whether this will cause
problems for the Norwegian Government and, further,, what the U. S. should do. The
Chairman indicated that he had made the point that before a decision was made by the
U. S. the matter would have to go to EDAC. It was his judgment under the Mutual
Security Act that if they said anything to the Norwegian Government about this it
would force the Norwegians to insist on a big COCOM/CHINCOM quota. He said he
made sure that the language of the outgoing was very clear* that it was directed to
the Embassy, and no officials of the Norwegian Government were involved.
