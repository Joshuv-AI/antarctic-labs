French physician; lived at Narbonne in the second half of the fourteenth century. In 1387 he translated into Hebrew, under the title "Shoshan ha-Refu'ah," the "Lilium Medicinæ" of Bernard of Gordon. The translation is extant in manuscript.

Bibliography: Renan-Neubauer, Les Ecrivains Juifs Français, p. 386; Steinschneider, Hebr. Uebers. p. 785. G. I. Br.
