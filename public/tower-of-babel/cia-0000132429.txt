LATIN AMERICA

a Two High Cuban Officials Reportedly Defect From Castro
-- ‘Government: A reliable Source reports that Justo Carrillo

- and Jose Joaquin Sanjenis, who held important economic posts
in the Cuban revolutionary government, will remain in the
United States and seek asylum. The Castro regime is trying

to conceal their defection for the time being but, when it
becomes known, will charge Sanjenis with the theft of $500,-

000 from the Ministry of the Treasury.

These are apparently the first actual defections among
several experienced and moderate economic officials who have
resigned or been forced out of their posts since Che Guevara
became president of the National Bank. Most seem to have
been strong supporters of Castro's originally claimed re-
forms and did much to make them possible. However, as Castro's
actions became increasingly drastic and dictatorial, they op-
posed him and have steadily lost influence. They have re-
frained from making an open break with Castro and some have
accepted lesser government jobs, possibly in fear of denigra-
tion like that of Major Hubert Matos~-accused of treason
