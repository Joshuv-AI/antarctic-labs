recommendations of the Commission, as set forth in its Report, to
the Central Intelligence Agency and the National Security Agency.
Specific all y, we have been requested to advise whether "it was
the intent of the Commission not only to exempt employees of the
two agencies but also to exempt the agencies themselves from the
requirements of this program."

I am glad to clarify for the record that it was the intention
of the Commission to exempt not only the employees of the Central
Intelligence Agency and the National Security Agency but the agencies
themselves from all of the recommendations set forth in the Com-
mission’s Report. The only modification which the Commission felt
should be observed is set forth on page 51 of the Report with
reference to the program for civilian employees; namely, that the
standards and criteria of CIA and NSA for its employees should
not be less t han those applicable to other departments and agencies
in the Executive Branch.

I hope that this statement of intention is satisfactory for
your purposes.
