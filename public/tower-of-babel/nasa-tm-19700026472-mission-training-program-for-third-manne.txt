 Approximately 1800 hours of crew training is programmed to
achieve a highly skilled crew to fly the third manned Apollo
mission, (see table 51) . In addition to the programmed training,
each crewmember will spend many additional hours participating in
other training activities i.e., physical exercise, study, informal
briefings and reviews, and necessary mission support activities (A/C

mission development). Nonprogrammed activities will largely
have to be accomplished prior to or after the normal working day.

contained in this document.'
