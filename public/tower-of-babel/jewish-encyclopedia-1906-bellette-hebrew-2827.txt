Daughter of Menahem, and sister of Isaac ben Menahem called "the Great"; lived at Orleans in the middle of the eleventh century. She is cited by Rashi, in a responsum, as an authority on a ritual question (Pardes, 4 b ; Assufot, MS. Halberstamm, 48 b ).

Bibliography: Oẓar Neḥmad, ii. 10; Zunz, Zur Geschichte und Literatur, p. 172; Grätz, Gesch. der Juden, 3d ed., vi. 50, note 3; Gross, Gallia Judaica, p. 33. L. G. I. Br.
