Rabbi of the community of Leghorn Jews at Tunis, where he died in 1879. He was the author of "Ben Abraham," a work treating of difficult problems in religious law, and published at Leghorn, 1882, by his relative, Jacob Bocara. Several other writings of his are extant in manuscript form.

Bibliography: D. Cazès, Notes Bibliographiques sur la Littérature Juive-Tunisienne, pp. 29 et seq. L. G. M. K.
