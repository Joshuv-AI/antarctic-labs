Austrian archeologist; born at Vienna Sept. 1, 1857; educated at the gymnasium and university of his native city (Ph.D. 1882). He is now (1904) professor of archeology at the University of Rome.

Loewy is the author of: "Untersuchungen zur Griechischen Künstlergeschichte" (1883); "Inschriften Griechischer Bildhauer" (1885); "Lysipp und Seine Stellung in der Griechischen Plastik" (1891); "Die Naturwiedergabe in der Aeltesten Griechischen Kunst" (1900).

S. F. T. H.
