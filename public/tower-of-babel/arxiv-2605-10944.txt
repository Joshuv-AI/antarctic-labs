Eigenvalues of 𝑳 𝜶 − \boldsymbol{L_{\alpha}}- matrices under graph operations

Abstract
Let G G be a simple graph, A ⁡ ( G ) A(G) its adjacency matrix, and D ⁡ ( G ) D(G) its diagonal degree matrix. In 2022, 11 ( [ 11 ] ) defined the family of matrices L α L_{\alpha} as the convex linear combination: L α ​ ( G ) = α ​ D ​ ( G ) + ( α − 1 ) ​ A ​ ( G ) , L_{\alpha}(G)=\alpha D(G)+(\alpha-1)A(G), where α ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] \alpha\in[0,1] . The study of the spectrum of this family of matrices may provide a unified framework for analyzing the spectra of the adjacency, degree, and Laplacian matrices ( D ⁡ ( G ) − A ⁡ ( G ) D(G)-A(G) ). In this work, we investigate the spectrum of L α L_{\alpha} under graph operations and within specific families of graphs.

The next result presents an expression for the characteristic polynomial of

for graphs obtained by coalescence between the vertices of two graphs. We denote by

Theorem 3.9
.

Let

and

be disjoint graphs, and let

be the graph obtained by identifying a vertex

for
\alpha\in[0,1].

Proof.

Order the vertices of
G\cdot H
so that the first vertex is the identified one, followed by the vertices of
G\setminus\{u\}
and then those of

, we have

\lambda I-L_{\alpha}(G\cdot H)=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda-\alpha(d_{G}(u)+d_{H}(v))&\nu^{T}&\rho^{T}\\
\nu&\lambda I-L_{\alpha}(G)_{u}&0\\
\rho&0&\lambda I-L_{\alpha}(H)_{v}\end{bmatrix},

where
\nu
and
\rho
are column vectors whose nonzero entries, corresponding to the neighbors of

in
G
and

in

. Consider

Then

P_{L_{\alpha}(G\cdot H)}(\lambda)=\det\begin{bmatrix}a&\nu^{T}&\rho^{T}\\
\nu&X&0\\
\rho&0&Y\end{bmatrix}.

Applying Corollary
2.8
, we obtain

P_{L_{\alpha}(G\cdot H)}(\lambda)=\det(X)\det(Y)\Bigl(\lambda-\alpha(d_{G}(u)+d_{H}(v))-\nu^{T}X^{-1}\nu-\rho^{T}Y^{-1}\rho\Bigr).

(6)

Now consider the decomposition of
\lambda I-L_{\alpha}(G)
with respect to the vertex
u
:

\lambda I-L_{\alpha}(G)=\begin{bmatrix}\lambda-\alpha d_{G}(u)&\nu^{T}\\
\nu&X\end{bmatrix}.

For all
\lambda
such that

is invertible, Proposition
2.7
yields

P_{L_{\alpha}(G)}(\lambda)=\det(X)\Bigl(\lambda-\alpha d_{G}(u)-\nu^{T}X^{-1}\nu\Bigr).

Similarly,

Moreover,

Substituting these expressions, we obtain

\displaystyle P_{L_{\alpha}(G)}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)_{v}}(\lambda)+P_{L_{\alpha}(G)_{u}}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)}(\lambda)-\lambda\,P_{L_{\alpha}(G)_{u}}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)_{v}}(\lambda)

\displaystyle=\det(X)\det(Y)\Bigl(\lambda-\alpha(d_{G}(u)+d_{H}(v))-\nu^{T}X^{-1}\nu-\rho^{T}Y^{-1}\rho\Bigr).

Comparing the expression above with (
6

P_{L_{\alpha}(G\cdot H)}(\lambda)=P_{L_{\alpha}(G)}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)_{v}}(\lambda)+P_{L_{\alpha}(G)_{u}}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)}(\lambda)-\lambda\,P_{L_{\alpha}(G)_{u}}(\lambda)P_{L_{\alpha}(H)_{v}}(\lambda).

Finally, since both sides are polynomials in
\lambda
and coincide for all
\lambda
such that

and

are invertible, they are identical by Proposition
2.1
and the proof is concluded.

4
Conclusion

In this paper, we investigate the spectrum of the family
L_{\alpha}
under several graph operations, including union, join, products, and splitting. We provide explicit spectral descriptions of
L_{\alpha}
for each of these operations, highlighting the interplay between the structure of the graphs and their spectral properties. In addition, we establish a formula for the characteristic polynomial of
L_{\alpha}
under the coalescence operation. Furthermore, we derive explicit expressions for the spectra of
L_{\alpha}
for several important graph families, including pineapple graphs,
H_{n}^{l}
,
KK_{n}^{l}
, and core–satellite graphs, the latter naturally arising from combinations of union and join operations. As a direction for future research, we propose extending the spectral analysis of
L_{\alpha}
to broader classes of graphs and investigating the ordering of its eigenvalues and their connections to structural graph properties.
