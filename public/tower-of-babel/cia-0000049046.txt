1. AT APPROXIMATELY 0520 HOURS ON 22 FEBRUARY 19H,
THREE MILITARY TRUCKS CARRYING ABOUT 150 SOLDIERS FROM MILITARY
DETACHMENT SIX IN SONSONATE ENTERED THE VILLAGE OF LAS HOJAS.

A FOURTH TRUCK, CONTAINING ABOUT 50 SOLDIERS, WAS STATIONED
OUTSIDE THE VILLAGE. THE SOLDIERS IN LAS HOJAS, COMMANDED BY

A CAPTAIN ((FIGUEROA)) MORALES, STOPPED AND QUESTIONED A NUMBER
OF VILLAGERS WHO HAD JUST LEFT AN EARLY MORNING CATHOLIC
RELIGIOUS SERVICE. THE SOLDIERS STATED THAT THEY HAD INFORMATION
THAT THERE WERE SUBVERSIVES IN THE AREA WHOSE COMMANDER WAS A
FATHER ROBERTO LEONARDO ((ZACAPA)), A CATHOLIC PRIEST. THE
VILLAGERS REPLIED THAT THEY HAD NEVER HEARD OF ZACAPA.

(QMEQEED COMMENT: ONE OF THE VILLAGERS QUESTIONED BY THE SOLIDERS
WAS A CATHOLIC LAYWORKER NAMED ROBERTO LEONARDO ((PARRANGA)),

WHO IS NOT A LEFTIST. DUE TO THE SIMILARITY BETWEEN HIS NAME

AND ZACAPA'S, HOWEVER, HE IS FEARFUL OF POSSIBLE ACTION AGAINST
HIM BY THE ARMED FORCES).

2. THE DETACHMENT SIX SOLDIERS IN LAS HOJAS WERE
ACCOMPANIED BY A CIVILIAN WHO WORE A MASK OVER THE LOWER PART
OF HIS FACE. DESPITE THE MASK, A NUMBER OF INDIVIDUALS

RECOGNIZED HIM AS NAPOLEON ((GARCIA)) ARIAS, AGE 29. GARCIA
CARRIED A LIST AND INDICATED TO THE SOLDIERS THE LOCATIONS OF
VARIOUS RESIDENTS IN THE AREA. THE VILLAGERS WHO HAD ATTENDED
THE RELISIOUS SERVICE AND HAD BEEN QUEST [ONED SUBSEQUENTLY

BY THE SOLIDERS WERE THEN ORDERED TO LEAVE THE AREA. AS THEY
WERE LEAVING, THE VILLAGERS SAW THE SOLDIERS ROUNDING UP AND
ABUSING A NUMBER OF INDIVIDUALS ASSOCIATED WITH THE LOCAL
AGRICULTURAL COOPERATIVE. THE COOPERATIVE MEMBERS HAD THEIR
HANDS TIED BEHIND THEIR BACKS.

3. BY 2000 HOURS, ON 22 FEBRUARY, A GROUP OF COOPERATIVE
MEMBERS LED BY ADRIAN ((ESQUINO)) LIZCO, A MEMBER OF THE NATIONAL
[INDIAN ASSOCIATION (ANIS), DISCOVERED BETWEEN 19 AND 23 CORPSES
IN THE VICINITY OF LAS HOJAS. AMONG THE DEAD WERE THE INDIVIDUALS
WHO HAD BEEN ROUNDED UP THAT MORNING BY THE SALVADORAN MILITARY.
ALL OF THE VICTIMS HAD BEEN SHOT THROUGH THE HEAD AND ALL HAD
THEIR HANDS TIED BEHIND THEIR BACKS. SEVEN OF THE VICTIMS WERE
BURIED IN SEPERATE CEREMONIES AT 1100 HOURS ON 23 FEBRUARY.

THE REMAINDER WERE BURIED IN A COKMON GRAVE NEAR LAS HOJAS LATER
IN THE DAY AT A CEREMONY OFFICIATED OVER BY A LOCAL PRIEST,
FATHER (FNU) ((WALTERS)), SINCE THE FAMILIES WERE [00 FRIGHTENED
TO IDENTIFY OR CLAIM THE BODIES.
