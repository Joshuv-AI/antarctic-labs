Correction: Quality improvement: prevention of cardiac arrest in children with heart disease on the paediatric intensive care unit
Published: 2026

Russell MJ, Jones A, Burlton S, Morrison R, Ellis D, Jones T, et al. Quality improvement: prevention of cardiac arrest in children with heart disease on the paediatric intensive care unit. BMJ Paediatrics Open. 2026;10:e004840. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjpo-2026-004840

This article has been corrected since it was published online.

At the end matter, the Funding statement text currently reads:

"The project team gratefully acknowledge the funding received from the Hospital Cardiac Services Charitable fund allowing the appointment of a part-time cardiac arrest prevention quality improvement nurse on the PICU.”

This text contains an incorrect attribution of the funding source and should be replaced with:

“The project team gratefully acknowledge the funding received from the Birmingham Children’s Hospital Charity, Help A Heart Appeal, allowing the appointment of a part-time cardiac arrest prevention quality improvement nurse on the PICU.”
