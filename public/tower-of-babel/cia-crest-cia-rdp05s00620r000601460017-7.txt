November 15, 1977

On behalf of Judge John J. Sirica, General George S. Brown, General Alexander
M. Haig, General Louis H. Wilson and the entire Academy, we thank you for your
acceptance of the Academy's invitation to attend the 1978 Salute to Excellence
weekend "retreat" as a recipient of the Golden Plate Award.

We shall appreciate, at early convenience, your portrait for the 1978 Banquet
yearbook. . .plus a news story, magazine article, or other career summary from
which we may quote. We will adjust that data to our usual format.

Soon after we receive your Return Form #1 (enclosed), we will issue a news release
regarding your role in the 1978 "gathering of the greats."

Each of the Academy's Captains of Achievement will have two opportuniti es to
address the weekend c onference:

a) for approximately ten minutes or so, at one of the symposiums, on
career reminiscences — perhaps touching on unforgettable moments of challenge —
or on any appropriate subject. These informal remarks, to the combined array of
other guests of honor as well as the honor students, are followed by a brief period
for questions or comments, if any, from the audience.

b) for perhaps one minute or two, upon acceptance of the Golden Plate
Award at the Banquet of the Golden Plate, on a thought or emotion of that moment
. . .or possibly a brief message for the Young Leaders of Tomorrow.

Meantime, we are reserving a complimentary room — for you and your wife —
at the Executive Inn Rivermont in Owensboro, Kentucky for the Salute weekend,
June 22-24, which will get underway with the Reception and Dinner on Thursday
evening, June 22. Later, you may give us your arrival date.

The Awards Council of the Academy is honored by your acceptance, assures you
of a most memorable program. . .and eagerly awaits a personal meeting.

A non-profit, tax-exempt organization, dedicated to the Inspiration of Youth--to raise their sights high;
to excel in their endeavors--through the dramatic annual salute.. .at the Banquet of the Golden Plate.'.,
to Captains of Achievement from America's great walks of life and honor students from across the nation.

Dear Admiral Turner:

In tribute to your extraordinary accomplishment, we invite you as a guest of honor
to the 1978 Salute to Excellence "retreat" at Owensboro, Kentucky, June 22-24.

We shall especially appreciate your acceptance of the Golden Plate Award at the
black— tie Banquet of the Golden Plate — the culminating event of the weekend — —
in the luxurious new Executive Inn Rivermont on the evening of Saturday, June 24.

Forty eminent "exemplars of excellence" from the nation's many fields of endeavor
will meet one another in a truly unique "gathering of the greats". . .and will share
their wisdom and experience with 300 outstanding honor youths from across the USA.

The 1977 Banquet yearbook and other enclosures reflect the Academy's aims, history,
and significance — — particularly the tremendous inspirational impact upon the young
participants who exemplify America's Young Leaders of Tomorrow.

The 1978 weekend events — getting underway with the Reception and Dinner on the
evening of Thursday, June 22 -- include the symposium series (where each awardee
may reminisce on highlights of his career) and the Friday evening outing on June 23.

It will be the pleasure of the Academy to host each guest of honor (and spouse) at the
Executive Inn Rivermont, over-looking the Ohio River. The Academy will also provide
shuttle-jets between nearby airports and the Owensboro private terminal.

We look forward to your participation in our 17th annual "retreat" perhaps for the
full program, but at the convenience of your schedule as may be determined later .

In the meantime, your response within ten days will be appreciated. Thank you.

FOR THE GOLDEN PLATE AWARDS COUNCIL
