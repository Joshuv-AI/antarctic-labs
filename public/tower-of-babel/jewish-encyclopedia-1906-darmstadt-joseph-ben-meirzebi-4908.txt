German Talmudist; flourished in the second half of the eighteenth century. He was a pupil of Mordecai Halberstadt, author of "Ma'amar Mordecai." Darmstadt wrote "'Eẓ Yosef" (Joseph's Tree), novellæ to the tractates Berakot, Beẓah, and Megillah (Karlsruhe, 1763).

Bibliography: Walden, Shem ha-Gedolim he-Ḥadash, i. 56; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1454; Auerbach, Gesch. des Jüd. Gemeinde Halberstadt, p. 74. L. G. I. Ber.
