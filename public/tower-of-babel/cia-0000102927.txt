2. POLITICAL PRISONERS WORKING ON PROJECTS THROUGHOUT THE USSR IN

THE EARLY AND MID-1970S DID THEMOST MONOTONOUS, MENIAL, AND PHYSICALLY

DEMANDING WORK AT THE SITE. 1H OSE INDIVIDUALS LASSIFIO AS “COMMON”
CRIMINALS” HERE, FOR THE MOST PART, THE “OVERSEERS” ANO “CREW
LEADERS” WHO ON A HIERARCHICAL SCALE OF FORCED LABOR WERE SUPERIOR

TO POLITICAL CRIMINALS. THIS GROUP WOULD GENERALLY BE GRANTED THE
CREDIT FOR “SUPERIOR” WORK OR ACHIEVEMENT ON PARTICULAR PROJECTS AND
WERE FREE TO USE PHYSICAL FORCE, IF NECESSARY, ON OCCASION TO KEEP
POLITICAL PRISONERS WORKING. POLITICAL PRISONERS WERE THUS GENERALLY
REGARDED AS EXPENDABLE.

-3: ALTHOUGH THE POLITICAL AND COMMON PRISONERS USUALLY SHARED THE
SAME LIVING QUARTERS, COMMON CRIMINALS WERE GENERALLY PROVIDED WITH
BETTER CONDITIONS IN THE FORM OF SLIGHTLY LARGER ROOMS, SOMETIMES
ADDITIONAL FOOD ALLOTMENTS, AND OCCASIONALLY LONGER REST PERIODS ;
WHILE IN THE LIVING QUARTERS. AT LEAST ON THE BAM PROJECT, MURDERERS
AND RAPISTS HERE ROUTINELY AFFORDED BETTER LIVING CONDITIONS THAN WERE
POLITICAL PRISONERS, AS WELL AS THE OPPORTUNITY TO BECOME TEAM AND
CREW LEADERS. POLITICAL PRISONERS WERE GRANTEO ABSOLUTELY NO
COMPENSATION, MONETARY OR OTHERWISE, FOR THEIR SERVICES.
