Captain TAN of Regiment 95 said he saw the U.S. prisoners in November 1966
while riding his bicycle near the zoo in HANOI and followed the prisoners

as they ran to HANG CO Railroad Station. There he watched the U.S. prisoners
doing exercises; he also met a Captain (name unknown) who had graduated

from school in the USSR with him. This individual could speak English
fluently and had been assigned to the U.S. prisoners with the rank of
Lieutenant Colonel and Colonel.

This captain (name tinknown) stated that the U.S. prisoners wore the North
Vietnamese Air Force uniforms with their own U.S. ranks. Captain TAN had
seen only one Golonel. Also stated that although the NVN soldier only
received NVN$ 0.68 for food per day, the U.S. prisoners received NVN$ 7
because they drank so many soft drinks and beer, as well as coffee with
sugar. One U.S. Lieutenant Colonel (USAF) had stated that his family was
wealthy and that if he were allowed to return home he would see that all
the destroyed bridges were repaired.
