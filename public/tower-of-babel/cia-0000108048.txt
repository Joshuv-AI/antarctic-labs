TEXT: 1. THE ESCORT GUARD COMPANY V/CH 7461 WAS ONE OF THREE MVD:
GUARD COMPANIES STATIONED IN OR NEAR YOLGOGRAD. THE THREE COMPANIES
TAKEN TOGETHER DID NOT FORM A SINGLE ENTITY SUCH AS A BATTALION, BUT
WERE INDEPENDENT UNITS, EACH WITH A SEPARATE MISSION. TWO OF THE
COMPANIES, INCLUDING V/CH 7461, WERE STATIONED IN VOLGOGRAD, RSFSR,
AND THE THIRD COMPANY, V/CH 7461-B, WAS STATIONED AT A CORRECTIVE
LABOR CAMP IN THE NEARBY TOWN OF VOLZHSKIY, ABOUT 25 MILES NORTHEAST
OF VOLGOGRAD. THE SECOND COMPANY STATIONED IN VOLGOGRAD PROVIDED
HONOR GUARD SERVICE AT THE MAMAYEV KURGAN WAR MEMORIAL IN VOLGOGRAD,
WHICH COMMEMORATED A MAJOR SOVIET TANK VICTORY OVER NAZI INVADERS IN
1942.

2. THE TWO COMPANIES IN VOLGOGRAD, EACH OF ABOUT 120 MEN,

WERE HOUSED IN A SMALL, FENCED MILITARY COMPLEX OF FOUR ONE- AND TWO-
STORY BARRACKS NEAR THE CENTER OF TOWN. THE COMMANDING OFFICER (NAME
NOT RECALLED), A COLONEL, HAD A MAJOR AS HIS DEPUTY AND THERE WAS A
CAPTAIN IN CHARGE OF POLITICAL MATTERS. EACH COMPANY WAS HEADED BY A
CAPTAIN, AND EACH CONSISTED OF FOUR PLATOONS WITH ABOUT 25 OR 30 MEN
IN A PLATOON, COMMANDED BY A LIEUTENANT. THE MISSION OF THE FIRST
COMPANY, V/CH 7461, WAS TWO-FOLD: TO ESCORT (BAILIFFS) DEFENDANTS
BETWEEN DETENTION CENTERS (SIZO, SLEDSTVENNYY IZOLYATOR) AND COURTS
