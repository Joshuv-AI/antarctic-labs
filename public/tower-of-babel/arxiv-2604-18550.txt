Minimax optimal dual control — The single input case
Anders Rantzer

Abstract
An explicit solution is derived for the Bellman inequality corresponding to minimax optimal dual control. The minimizing player determines control action as a function of past state measurements and inputs. The maximizing player selects disturbances and model parameters for the underlying linear time-invariant dynamics. The optimal minimizing policy is a dual controller that optimizes the tradeoff between exploration and exploitation. Once sufficient data has been collected, the policy becomes a deterministic certainty equivalence controller. However, when data is insufficient, the policy introduces a randomized term to improve excitation.

Definition 1.

For
A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}
,
S\in\mathbb{S}_{+}^{n\times n}
and
\beta,R,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}_{+}
, let
\mathcal{B}
denote the set of all
B\in\mathbb{R}^{n}
such that

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\min_{u}\max_{w}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}-\gamma^{2}|w|^{2}+|Ax+Bu+w|^{2}\right)\leq|x|^{2}\\
1\leq|B|\leq\beta.\end{cases}

Moreover, for
Z\in\mathbb{S}_{+}^{(2n+1)\times(2n+1)}
, define

\displaystyle z_{B}

\displaystyle:=\gamma^{2}\big\|\begin{bmatrix}A&B&-I\end{bmatrix}^{\top}\big\|^{2}_{Z}

\displaystyle\tilde{z}_{B}

\displaystyle:=(z_{-B}-z_{B})/2

\displaystyle\bar{z}

\displaystyle:=\min_{B\in\mathcal{B}}(z_{B}+z_{-B})/2

Fig. 1:
We want a feedback controller that works for all
B
-vectors within given bounds. If
A
is unstable and
\mathcal{B}
includes sign uncertainty, even stabilization is impossible using linear time-invariant controllers. A randomized nonlinear dual controller can do much better by estimating
B
and use the estimate for control. A guaranteed bound is derived for the nonlinear gain from
|w|^{2}
to the expected value of
|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}

Theorem 1
(Main result)

Given
A,S,R,\beta,\gamma
and notation of Definition 1, suppose that

\displaystyle\gamma^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\left(1+\frac{2\|A\|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}\right)\max\{1+2R+\beta^{2},\|S^{-1}\|\}.

(1)

Then there exists a feedback law
u=\mu(x,Z)
generating (possibly random) inputs
u
such that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sum_{t=0}^{T}\left(|x_{t}|_{S}^{2}+|u_{t}|_{R}^{2}\right)\leq\gamma^{2}\sum_{t=0}^{T}|w_{t}|^{2}

whenever
B\in\mathcal{B}
and

\displaystyle x_{t+1}

\displaystyle=Ax_{t}+Bu_{t}+w_{t},\quad x_{0}=0,

\displaystyle u_{t}

\displaystyle=\mu\left(x_{t},\sum_{\tau=0}^{t-1}(x_{\tau},u_{\tau},x_{\tau+1})(x_{\tau},u_{\tau},x_{\tau+1})^{\top}\right).

In particular, the inequality holds for the feedback law

\displaystyle\;\;\mu(x,Z):=-\hat{K}x\qquad\qquad\quad\;\,\hbox{if $\tilde{z}_{\hat{B}}\geq\frac{2\hat{K}x\hat{B}^{\top}Ax}{1-\gamma^{-2}}$}

(2)

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\mathbb{E}\mu(x,Z):=\frac{\gamma^{-2}-1}{2\hat{B}^{\top}Ax}\,\tilde{z}_{\hat{B}}\\[5.69054pt]
\mathbb{E}[\mu(x,Z)^{2}]:=|\hat{K}x|^{2}\end{cases}\quad\text{otherwise, }

(3)

where

\displaystyle\hat{K}

\displaystyle:=\left((1-\gamma^{-2})R+|\hat{B}|^{2}\right)^{-1}\hat{B}^{\top}A

\displaystyle\hat{B}

\displaystyle:=\arg\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\max\bigg\{\min_{u}\left(|u|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax+Bu|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-z_{B}\right),

(4)

\displaystyle\hskip 18.49988pt\frac{|Ax|^{2}+\left[(1-\gamma^{-2})R+|B|^{2}\right]^{-1}(B^{\top}Ax)^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-\frac{z_{B}+z_{-B}}{2}\bigg\}

The proof of Theorem
1
will be based on two preliminary results, Lemma
2
and Theorem
3
. The first one shows that
\mathcal{B}
is the union of two (convex) second order cones:

Lemma 2

Given
R,S,\gamma
and
A
, let
\lambda_{1}\geq\ldots\geq\lambda_{n}
be the eigenvalues of
A^{\top}A-(1-\gamma^{-2})(I-S)
, with corresponding eigenvectors
U_{1},\ldots,U_{n}
. Then
\mathcal{B}
has the form

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}=\left\{B\in\mathcal{B}_{+}\cup(-\mathcal{B}_{+}):1\leq|B|\leq\beta\right\},

where

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\mathcal{B}_{+}=\emptyset\quad\;\;\;\text{if }\lambda_{2}>0\\
\mathcal{B}_{+}=\mathbb{R}^{n}\quad\text{if }\lambda_{1}\leq 0,\quad\text{else}\\
\mathcal{B}_{+}=\left\{B\;:\;B^{\top}AU_{1}\geq\sqrt{\lambda_{1}(B^{\top}B+(1-\gamma^{-2})R)}\right\}.\end{cases}

A proof of Lemma

Theorem 3
(Solution to the Bellman inequality)

Given Definition 1, let
\tau:=1+2\|A\|^{2}(1-\gamma^{-2})^{-1}
and

\displaystyle\hat{V}(x,Z)

\displaystyle:=\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\max\left\{\mathbb{E}(|x|^{2}-z_{B}),\mathbb{E}(\tau|x|^{2}-\bar{z})\right\}.

Assume that (
1
) holds. Then, for all
x,Z
,

\hat{V}(x,Z)\geq\max_{v}\left[\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|\bar{u}|^{2}_{R}\right)+\hat{V}\left(v,Z+\begin{bmatrix}x\\
\bar{u}\\
v\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x\\
\bar{u}\\
v\end{bmatrix}^{\top}\right)\right]

(5)

when
\bar{u}=\mu(x,Z)
and
\mu
is defined by (
2
)-(
3
).

Proof.

For random
x,Z
and
B\in\mathcal{B}
, let

\displaystyle\bar{V}(x,Z)

\displaystyle:=\mathbb{E}\left(\tau|x|^{2}-\bar{z}\right)

\displaystyle V_{B}(x,Z)

\displaystyle:=\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}-z_{B}\right).

Given a random
u\in\mathbb{R}
, define the operator
\mathcal{F}_{u}
by

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{u}V(x,Z)

\displaystyle:=\max_{v}\left[\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}\right)+V\left(v,Z+\begin{bmatrix}x\\
u\\
v\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x\\
u\\
v\end{bmatrix}^{\top}\right)\right].

Then

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{u}\bar{V}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\max_{v}\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+\tau|v|^{2}-\gamma^{2}|Ax-v|^{2}-\gamma^{2}|Bu|^{2}-\bar{z}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax|^{2}}{\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2}}-\gamma^{2}u^{2}-\bar{z}\right)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{B}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\max_{v}\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+|v|^{2}-\gamma^{2}|Ax+Bu-v|^{2}-z_{B}\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+(1-\gamma^{-2})^{-1}|Ax+Bu|^{2}-z_{B}\right).

To prove (
5
), it is sufficient to verify that
\bar{u}=\mu(x,Z)
gives

\displaystyle\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\max\left\{\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}\bar{V}(x,Z),\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{B}(x,Z)\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\hat{V}(x,Z).

(6)

We will first prove that
\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}\bar{V}(x,Z)\leq\tau|x|^{2}-\bar{z}
, or more specifically that

\displaystyle|x|^{2}_{S}+|\hat{K}x|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax|^{2}}{\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2}}-\gamma^{2}|\hat{K}x|^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\tau|x|^{2}

for all
x
. The condition
\hat{B}\in\mathcal{B}
gives

\displaystyle|x|^{2}

\displaystyle\geq\min_{u}\max_{w}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}-\gamma^{2}|w|^{2}+|Ax+\hat{B}u+w|^{2}\right)

\displaystyle=\min_{u}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+\frac{(1-\gamma^{-2})Ru^{2}+|Ax+\hat{B}u|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=|x|^{2}_{S}+\frac{|Ax|^{2}-((1-\gamma^{-2})R+|\hat{B}|^{2})|\hat{K}x|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}.

Hence, we need to prove that the desired inequality

\displaystyle(\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2})|x|_{(\tau I-S)}^{2}

\displaystyle\geq|Ax|^{2}-(\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2})(\gamma^{2}-R)|\hat{K}x|^{2}

follows from the known inequality

\displaystyle(1-\gamma^{-2})|x|_{(I-S)}^{2}

\displaystyle\geq|Ax|^{2}-((1-\gamma^{-2})R+|\hat{B}|^{2})|\hat{K}x|^{2}.

Comparing the left hand sides, we have

\displaystyle(\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2})(\tau I-S)-(1-\gamma^{-2})(I-S)

\displaystyle=\gamma^{-2}(1-\tau^{-1})(\gamma^{2}S-\tau I)\succeq 0,

since
\tau\geq 1
and
\gamma^{2}S\succeq\tau I
. Hence the left side of the desired inequality is bounded below by the left side of the known inequality. For the right hand sides, it remains to verify that

\displaystyle(\tau^{-1}-\gamma^{-2})(\gamma^{2}-R)

\displaystyle\geq(1-\gamma^{-2})R+\beta^{2}

or the slightly stronger condition

\displaystyle\gamma^{2}\tau^{-1}

\displaystyle\geq 1+2R+\beta^{2}.

This follows from (
1
), so the implication is proved and the inequality
\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}\bar{V}(x,Z)\leq\tau|x|^{2}-\bar{z}
holds.

It remains to prove that
\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{B}(x,Z)\leq\hat{V}(x,Z)
. For this, some notation will be needed. Put

\displaystyle\mathbf{M}(B)

\displaystyle:=\begin{bmatrix}S&0\\
0&R\end{bmatrix}+(1-\gamma^{-2})^{-1}\begin{bmatrix}A^{\top}A&A^{\top}B\\
B^{\top}A&|B|^{2}\end{bmatrix}

\displaystyle\mathbf{N}(B)

\displaystyle:=\begin{bmatrix}A&B&-I\end{bmatrix}^{\top}\begin{bmatrix}A&B&-I\end{bmatrix}

and let
\mathcal{M}
denote the set of all pairs
(M,N)
of the form

\displaystyle(M,N)

\displaystyle=\theta_{1}\left(\mathbf{M}(B),\mathbf{N}(B)\right)+\theta_{-1}\left(\mathbf{M}(-B),\mathbf{N}(-B)\right)

with
B\in\mathcal{B}_{+}
,
1\leq|B|\leq\beta
,
\theta_{\pm 1}\in[0,1]
and
\theta_{-1}+\theta_{1}=1
.
Then

\displaystyle\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{B}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{(M,N)\in\mathcal{M}}\mathbb{E}\left(|(x,u)|^{2}_{M}-\gamma^{2}\langle N,Z\rangle\right)

\displaystyle=\max_{(M,N)\in\mathcal{M}}\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\mathbb{E}\left(|(x,u)|^{2}_{M}-\gamma^{2}\langle N,Z\rangle\right)

(7)

\displaystyle=\max_{B,\theta}\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\left[\theta_{1}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{B}(x,Z)+\theta_{-1}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{-B}(x,Z)\right]

\displaystyle=\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max\left\{\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{B}(x,Z),\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{-B}(x,Z)\right\}

(8)

where
\mathcal{U}
is the set of random
u\in\mathbb{R}
with
\mathbb{E}u^{2}=\hat{K}x
.
The equality (
7
) is due to von Neumann’s minimax theorem, which can be applied since
\mathbb{E}\left(|(x,u)|^{2}_{M}-\gamma^{2}\langle N,Z\rangle\right)
is linear in the variables
\mathbb{E}u
and
(M,N)
, while their constraints defined by
\mathcal{U}
and
\mathcal{M}
are convex and compact.

Two cases need to be considered:

Case 1 (Exploitation):
(
2
) holds.

The assumption
(1-\gamma^{-2})\tilde{z}_{\hat{B}}\geq 2\hat{K}x\hat{B}^{\top}Ax
of (
2
) gives

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{-\hat{B}}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|\hat{K}x|^{2}_{R}+(1-\gamma^{-2})^{-1}|Ax+\hat{B}\hat{K}x|^{2}-z_{-\hat{B}}\right)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{\hat{B}}(x,Z)+\mathbb{E}\left(\frac{4(Ax)^{\top}\hat{B}\hat{K}x}{1-\gamma^{-2}}+z_{\hat{B}}-z_{-\hat{B}}\right)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{\hat{B}}(x,Z)+\mathbb{E}\left(\frac{4\hat{K}x\hat{B}^{\top}Ax}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-2\tilde{z}_{\hat{B}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{\hat{B}}(x,Z).

\displaystyle\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{B}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\min_{u}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax+iBu|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-z_{iB}\right)

\displaystyle=\min_{u}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax+i\hat{B}u|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-z_{i\hat{B}}\right)

\displaystyle=\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{i\hat{B}}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{\hat{B}}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|u|^{2}_{R}+(1-\gamma^{-2})^{-1}|Ax+{\hat{B}}u|^{2}-z_{\hat{B}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}-z_{\hat{B}}\right)

with
\bar{u}
as minimizer. The proof of (
5
) for Case 1 is complete.

Case 2 (Exploration):
(
2
) fails.

\displaystyle\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathcal{F}_{u}V_{iB}(x,Z)-\mathbb{E}\left(|x|^{2}_{S}+|\hat{K}x|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax|^{2}+|B\hat{K}x|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}\right)

\displaystyle=\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathbb{E}\left(\frac{2(Ax)^{\top}iBu}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-z_{iB}\right)

\displaystyle=\min_{u\in\mathcal{U}}\max_{i=\pm 1}\mathbb{E}\left(-\frac{z_{B}+z_{-B}}{2}+i\left[\frac{2(Ax)^{\top}Bu}{1-\gamma^{-2}}+\tilde{z}_{B}\right]\right)

\displaystyle=-\mathbb{E}(z_{B}+z_{-B})/2.

The last equality is due to the Case 2 assumption that (
2
) fails. This means that minimization over
\mathbb{E}u
subject to the constraint
|\mathbb{E}u|\leq|\hat{K}x|
cancels the term
i\left[\frac{2(Ax)^{\top}\hat{B}u}{1-\gamma^{-2}}+\tilde{z}_{\hat{B}}\right]
. Hence
\bar{u}
as defined by
\mu(x,Z)
is minimizing and

\displaystyle\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\mathcal{F}_{\bar{u}}V_{B}(x,Z)

\displaystyle=\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\mathbb{E}\bigg(|x|^{2}_{S}+|\hat{K}x|^{2}_{R}+\frac{|Ax|^{2}+|B\hat{K}x|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-\frac{z_{B}+z_{-B}}{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle=\max_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\mathbb{E}\bigg(|x|^{2}_{S}+\frac{2|Ax|^{2}}{1-\gamma^{-2}}-\frac{z_{B}+z_{-B}}{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle\leq\mathbb{E}\big(\tau|x|^{2}-\bar{z}\big).

The proof is for Case 2 also complete.

\Box

Proof of Theorem
1
.

Let
Z_{0}:=0
and

\displaystyle Z_{t}

\displaystyle:=\sum_{k=0}^{t-1}(x_{k},u_{k},x_{k+1})(x_{k},u_{k},x_{k+1})^{\top}

\displaystyle\text{for }t=1,\ldots T.

Then

\displaystyle\hat{V}(x_{t+1},Z_{t+1})+\mathbb{E}|u_{t}|_{R}^{2}

\displaystyle=\hat{V}(x_{t+1},Z_{t}+(x_{t},u_{t},x_{t+1})(x_{t},u_{t},x_{t+1})^{\top})+\mathbb{E}|u_{t}|_{R}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\max_{v}\hat{V}(v,Z_{t}+(x_{t},u_{t},v)(x_{t},u_{t},v)^{\top})+\mathbb{E}|u_{t}|_{R}^{2}

\displaystyle=\max_{v}\hat{V}(v,Z_{t}+(x_{t},\mu(x_{t},Z_{t}),v)(x_{t},\mu(x_{t},Z_{t}),v)^{\top})

\displaystyle\quad+\mathbb{E}|\mu(x_{t},Z_{t})|_{R}^{2}

\displaystyle\leq\left[\hat{V}(x_{t},Z_{t})-\mathbb{E}|x_{t}|_{S}^{2}\right].

This gives the telescope sum

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sum_{t=0}^{T}\left(|x_{t}|_{S}^{2}+|u_{t}|_{R}^{2}\right)

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{t=0}^{T}\left[\hat{V}(x_{t},Z_{t})-\hat{V}(x_{t+1},Z_{t+1})\right]

\displaystyle=\hat{V}(x_{0},Z_{0})-\hat{V}(x_{T+1},Z_{T+1})

\displaystyle=-\hat{V}(x_{T+1},Z_{T+1})

\displaystyle\leq\gamma^{2}\begin{bmatrix}A&B&-I\end{bmatrix}\left(\mathbb{E}Z_{T}\right)\begin{bmatrix}A&B&-I\end{bmatrix}^{\top}

\displaystyle=\gamma^{2}\mathbb{E}\sum_{t=0}^{T}|w_{t}|^{2}

for all
B\in\mathcal{B}
and the proof is complete.

\Box
