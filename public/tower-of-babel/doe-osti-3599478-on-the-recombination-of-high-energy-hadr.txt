 For a system with the ion beam co-propagating with the electron beam, such as a traditional electron cooler or a
Coherent electron Cooler (CeC), the recombination rate is an important observable for matching the energy of the
electrons with that of the ions [1, 2]. In this work, we have developed analytical expressions to calculate how the
recombination rate depends on the energy difference of the two beams, with the influences from the transverse motions
of the particles being considered. The analytical results are then applied to analyze the measured recombination data
collected during the CeC experiment.
