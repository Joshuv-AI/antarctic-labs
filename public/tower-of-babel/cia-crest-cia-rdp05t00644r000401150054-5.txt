Dear Admiral Turner:

The Women's Auxiliary of the Chamber of Commerce, New Orleans
Area and River Region, cordially invites you to be the key-
note speaker at our Twenty— Third United States Day Luncheon
and Flag Ceremony on October 23, 1980.

The Women's Auxiliary of the Chamber of Commerce of the New
Orleans Area, an organization of approximately 700 members,
works hard to make United States Day our most important and
impressive event. The celebration begins with an outdoor flag
ceremony at City Hall with 3,000 to 5,000 students, 6th
through 12th grades, present. At this time we would like you
to speak for about ten minutes on "America." The luncheon
follows at the Fairmont Hotel with an anticipated attendance
of 1,000 to 1,250. At this time we would like you to speak
for twenty-five to thirty minutes.

The Women's Auxiliary serves as primary sponsor, with the
Members Council and Americanism and Military Affairs Committees
of the Chamber of Commerce as co-sponsors. We receive fan-
tas tic support and cooperation for both events from many civic
and patriotic groups and military and veterans organizations.

In our continuing effort to understand and communicate with the
youth of our area, and to help them understand the real worth
and basic goodness of the American system, we hope to have as
our guests at the luncheon at least one student from each high
school, college and university in the Greater New Orleans Area.

Would it be possible for you to confirm the date of Thursday,
October 23, with an early reply in order that we may go for-
ward with our plans? The details involved in this program
are unbelieveable, so we need the maximum amount of time
possible for it to be well done. Before we make a final
commitment, however, we need to know our specific financial
responsibility because of our limited funds.

ORIGIN OF UNITED STATES DAY. OCTOBER 23rd

In 1953 a group of citizens who were concerned by what was happening to
patriotism among the people of the land concluded that some effective measures
should be taken to remind us of our precious heritage and the privilege of being
an American. A UNITED STATES DAY Committee was formed and is headed by Dr.
Marque 0. Nelson of Tulsa, Oklahoma, It was decided that October 23rd should
hereafter be proclaimed as UNITED STATES DAY and that the flag be flown by all
to renew their pledge of allegiance to it.

UNITED STATES DAY was started in New Orleans in 1958. A group of ladies
presented the idea to Mayor Chep Morrison who formally proclaimed October 23rd
as UNITED STATES DAY. The event grew larger each year and the original group
realized they could not handle it. The Women's Auxiliary of the Chamber of
Commerce of the New Orleans Area in 1964 was asked to take over the annual
program.

UNITED STATES DAY STANDS FOR:

- Respect for our Flag

- Love of God and our Country

- Appreciation of the advantages under our Constitution
offered by no other form of government

- Election of statesmen, not politicians to public office

- A better appreciation and understanding of the history,
traditions, and governmental principles which are the
foundation of our great republic
