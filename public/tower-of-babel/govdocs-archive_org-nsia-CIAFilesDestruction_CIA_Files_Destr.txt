Defendant, by its attorney, the United States Attorney, in 
opposition to plaintiff's motion for injunctive relief restraining 
the Director and employees of the CIA from destroying documents. 
1. it is clear that the CIA, its Director and employees are 
not defendants in the instant action and, accordingly, are not 
proper subjects of an injunctive order in this action. 
2. It is likewise clear that the documents plaintiff seeks 
in the instant action, i.e., three transcripts of the Warren Com- 
mission, are held by GSA in the National Archives, and are not 
held by the CIA. 
3. Defendant's counsel has been informed that the CIA is under 
a moratorium preventing destruction of documents which would likely 
Include any documents plaintiff might seek in another action. 
WHEREFORE, defendant respectfully requests the Court to deny 
plaintiff's motion for injunctive relief.
