Russo-Jewish weekly, established (1900) and edited by S. O. Gruzenberg. Like the "Voskhod," it gives valuableinformation concerning the history of the Jews and their social life and institutions; but while the former periodical is in favor of assimilation, the latter is of a Zionistic tendency. Most of the leading Zionist writers of Russia are among its contributors. The "Budushchnost" publishes a "sbornik," or literary annual, as a supplement.

H. R. M. R.
