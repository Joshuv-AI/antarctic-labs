MINISTER OF DEFENSE GENERAL EUGENIO CARLOS
LtVIDES!) CASANOVA. EXPRESSED HIS CONCERN ABOUT THE INSURGENT
SITUATION IN EL SALVADOR AND THE POSSIBILITY OF A REDUCTION IN
U.S. MILITARY AID. GENERAL WIDES SAID THAT THE ATTITUDE OF THE
U.S, CONGRESS TO THE U.S. PRESIDENT'S PROPOSAL FOR INCREASED AID
TC EL SALVADOR WAS EXTREMELY CRITICAL AND THAT HE WAS UNSURE

AT THIS TIME ABOUT THE OUTCOME OF THIS DELICATE SITUATION.

HE SAID THAT THE CONDITIONS SOUGHT BY THE U.S. CONGRESS FOR THE
CONTINUANCE OF MILITARY AID WERE TOO RIGID AND COULD BE A CATALYST
FOR FURTHER PROBLEMS WITHIN THE SALVADORAN ARMED FORCES.

2, GENERAL VIDES MENTIONED THE CASE OF LIEUTENANT RODOLFO
ISIDRO ((LOPEZ)) SIBRIAN, THE OFFICER JMPLICATED IN THE MURDER
OF TWO AMERICANS AND A SALVADORAN, AND THE INSISTENCE BY THE
U.S. CONGRESS THAT THE SALVADORAN ARMED FORCES PURSUE THE
PROSECUTION OF THIS OFFICER. GENERAL VIDES AID HE WAS CONSIDERING
A MEETING WITH THE JUDGES INVOLVED IN THE CASE TO EXPLAIN THE
SERIOUSNESS OF THE SITUATION AND TO TRY TO ACCELERATE THE PROSECUTION
OF THE CASE.

3. THE MINISTER ALSO NOTED THAT THE NEXT FEW MONTHS WOULD
BE CRUCIAL FOR THE COUNTRY MILITARILY AND THAT HE HOPED THAT THE

CONDITIONS IMPOSED By THE U.S. CONGRESS WOULD NOT AFFECT HIS PLANS
TO REORGANIZE THE ARMED FORCES.
