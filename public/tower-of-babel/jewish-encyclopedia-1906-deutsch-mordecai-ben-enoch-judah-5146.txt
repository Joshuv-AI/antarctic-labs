Rabbi of Kolin, Bohemia, and its subordinate communities; he flourished at the beginning of the eighteenth century. He was the author of a work called "Mor Deror" (Flowing Myrrh), novellæ on the following Talmudic treatises: Ketubot, Baba Ḳamma, Baba Meẓi'a, Ḥullin, Yoma, and Shebu'ot (Prague, 1738).

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1657; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 208; Walden, Shem ha-Gedolim he-Ḥadash, ii. 43. L. G. M. Sel.
