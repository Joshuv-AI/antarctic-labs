Moscow Denies Military Advisers in Iraq

A Soviet Defense Ministry spokesman yesterday denied Western
media reports that the USSR still has 100 to 150 military
advisers in Iraq. A Foreign Ministry official stated that the
last group of military advisers left Iraq on 9 January and only
about 40 Embassy personnel remain in country. Defense Ministry
officials also publicly denied press reports that the Soviets
were continuing to send arms to Iraq or that they were giving
Baghdad satellite photography. be |

Japanese Feuding over Gulf Aid

Press reports indicate Tokyo pledged $9 billion in
additional financial support for the coalition forces today but
postponed a decision on additional economic aid to frontline Arab
states because of political infighting. Finance Minister
Hashimoto reportedly opposes plans by his political rival Mutsuki
Kato--head of the ruling party’s policy planning staft--to pledge
additional economic assistance before Tokyo’s original $2 billion
commitment is disbursed.

Mufti Declares a Jihad

The Mufti of Jerusalem called for a holy war or "jihad"
against the United States and its allies in the Gulf yesterday,
according to the US Consulate in Jerusalem. In a statement
issued in Jerusalem, the Mufti termed the United States “enemy
number one for Islam and Muslims."
