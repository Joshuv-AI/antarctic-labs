SUBJ New FMLN Proposal May Be Offered by Ortega

Full Text Superzone of Message

1

2 [Text] We wish to report that the Farabundo Marti National Liberation
Front [FMLN] has put forth a new proposal in a communique that has been
released in Mexico and in the United States. :

3 The FMLN proposal regards as necessary an immediate cease-fire with on
siteinternational supervision and, simultaneously, an overall definitive
negotiation. It also calls for immediate guarantees of respect for human
rights in El Salvador, which includes the lifting of the state of siege,
repeal of the antiterrorist law, release of political prisoners, departure
of wounded fighters, and the immediate dismissal of the following
officers: Colonel Ponce, because of his responsibility in the bombings and
murder of the Jesuits; General Bustillo, General (Villa Mariona), chief
and deputy chief of the Salvadoran Air Force, respectively; Lieutenant
Colonel Martinez Varela; Colonel Orlando Cepeda, deputy defense minister,
for (?ordering) the murder of the Jesuit priests; Colonel Gilberto
Hernandez, who carried out the murder; and Captain Alfonso Chavez Garcia,
who was the perpetrator,

4 In addition, the FMLN has proposed an on site investigation of human
rights -- which must be carried out through the Inter-American Human
Rights Commission and the UN work team -- as well as of the forced:
disappearances, and reorganization of the negotiating table in order for
the political parties and social forces to participate.

5 This is what is known about the FMLN announcement, which has already been
released in Mexico and in the United States. Another version circulating
at the summit is that this would be a proposal to be revealed through a
letter President Ortega will deliver today to the Central American
presidents. (endall) 101611 Sa 012.14e 10/1920z dec BT
