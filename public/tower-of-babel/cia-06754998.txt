(U) The Agency's military reserve program provides trained military personnel to augment
active component forces with intelligence and operational support within the range of its
reservists’ military and unique skills. The program:

reserve program entered into by the D/CIA and the Secretary of Defense on 9 July
1998, which formalizes participation by employees of the CIA who are members of
the Ready Reserve or in the reserve components of the military departments of the
DoD.

are aligned to the war fighter's requirements.
