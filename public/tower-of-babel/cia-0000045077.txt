PHOTO COMPARISON - TOMMY E. GIST

Photos were compared between an unknown prisoner and missing prisoner of
war Capt. Tommy E. Gist in the following photographs:

Photo No. 1: Prisoner of War, identity and date unknown.
Photo Nos. 2 and 3: Personal data photographs.
Photo No. 4: Official Air Force photograph.

Ref Photo No. 1 includes no date, and therefore it cannot be determined
what the age difference fis between this photo and other earlier photos. The
quality is also very poor, hiding significant facial structure, chin, mouth,
and ear shape. Areas used for comparison were the general size, height and
age of individual, temple and-hairline, texture and style of hair, eyebrows
and nose. Reviewers used the Identi-kit system in determining a composite
based on knowledge of these factors:

Build - Tall (6 ft and over), medium build, age about 25.35 years old.
Hair - dark, wavy, parted on left side, combed back.

Eyebrows - slightly heavy.

Nose - long and narrow; light catches forehead and tip of nose.

Top of head from center hairline to back.

Shape of face is oval. wo ease ees

Based on this information a composite was created. Chin - CC19, Nose -
NNO], Eyes - EEZO, Lips - LLO5, Eyebrows - DDO7, Hair - HH56, Age lines -
AAOS. Enclosed is the composite. Since the actual chin and mouth area cannot
be determined from the photograph, these features have been substituted with
Identi-kit's standard formula for an age-type of that basic character.

SIMILARITIES

Photograph No. 3 shows a wide scalp similar to the top of the head in
Photo No. 1. The length and protrusion of the nose also indicates a
similarity in length and protrusion as compared to the light falling on the
forehead and nose in Photograph No. 1.

The hair in Photos 2, 3, and 4 indicate thick, dark, wavy hair parted on
the left side as also indicated in Photograph No. 1. However the hair in
Photo No. 1 has grown considerably longer and may concea} the actual hairline
due to the possibility of shadows over the forehead. The color and thickness
of the hair in Photo No. 1 suggests a young man.

CONCLUSION

Based on our analysis of these photographs and the absence of more
substantive information regarding the time factor of Photo No. 1, the lack of
Clarity, the degree of shadow that conceals the features, the angle of the
features which result in distortion, we cannot identify with any certainty
photograph No. 1 with the photographs of Tomy E. Gist. However, the
Identi-kit composite does suggest more of a similarity than is suggested in

the photograph.
