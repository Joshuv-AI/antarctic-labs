+ SC BRIEFING . | 8 June 1960

CUBA

In agreeing to visit Cuba Khrushchev is exploiting the Cuban
government's hostility to the US and its desire to strengthen
Cuban ties with Communist bloc.

A. Khrushchev invited on 3 June by Agrarian Reform Institute
Director Nunez Jimenez who heads commercial mission to USSR,
Czechoslovakia, East Germany, and Poland.

B, Visit may well come 26 July (anniversary date) but Khrushchev
probably will seek added invitations from other Latin American
governments before setting specific date.

Soviet trade specialists and technicians beginning to arrive in

Cuba in sizable numbers, in addition to Czechs, Bulgarians,

Fast Germans and Poles on similar missions.

A, Geologists, steel, petroleum, and power specialists in
Soviet groups feted by Che Guevara on 1 June,

B. Working sessions of eight Cuban-Soviet committees On industrial-
ization “and other matters" began 4 June in Moscow.

Two US (Texaco and Esso) and one British (Shell) oil companies--

which account for over 90% of Cuba's refined products-~-on 6 June

advised Cuban government they would not go along with recent
government order to begin processing Soviet crude oil.

A, Dependents of American employees have been evacuated,

B. Cuba will probably take over the refineries with propaganda

blast that oil companies acting under US government direction.
