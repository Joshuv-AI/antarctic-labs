3. “GROUP TWO,"

IS ASSISTING IN THE PROPOSED OPERATIONS AGAINST SUBVERSIVES
ABROAD BY PROVIDING INFORMATION AND OTHER ASSISTANCE.

BATTALION, FEDERAL POLICE, AND SIDE. THE ACTUAL TEAM TO IMPLEMENT

THE OPERATIONS IS COMPOSED OF PERSONNEL FROM ALL THESE ENTITIES BUT

6. FOLLOWING THE FORMATION OF THE TEAM, REQUESTS WERE MADE
TO FRENCH, ITALIAN AND SPANISH AUTHORITIES FOR APPROVAL TO CARRY OUT
THE OPERATIONS IN THEIR RESPECTIVE COUNTRIES. THE REQUESTS, HONEVER,
WERE FIRMLY TURNED DOWN BY OFFICIALS OF FRANCE AND ITALY WHERE MOST
OF THE KEY ARGENTINE SUBVERSIVES ARE BELIEVED TO BE RESIDING. SPANISH
AUTHORITIES, WHILE ONCE RECEPTIVE TO ARGENTINE OPERATIONS IN SPAIN,
HAVE RECENTLY BECOME LESS COOPERATIVE, DUE PRIMARILY TO SPAIN'S
INTERNAL POLITICAL PROBLEMS. AS A RESULT OF THE RESPONSE FROM THE
FRENCH, ITALIANS AND SPANISH, GENERAL CARLOS NARTINE2Z , CHIEF
OF G-2, ORDERED THAT THE PROPOSED OPERATIONS AGAINST ARGENTINE SUBVER-

SIVES IN EUROPE BE SUSPENDED. HOWEVER, THE MATTER WILL BE RAISED AGAIN
WITH EUROPEAN AUTHORITIES, IF CONSIDERED APPROPRIATE, AT A LATER DATE.
