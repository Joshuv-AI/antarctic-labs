Source saw and heard about U.S, prisoners of war on two occasions.
The first time occurred in mid-September 1965 when Source's unit
moved to QUANG TRACH District, QUANG BINH Province, and Source was
ordered to go with a number of comrades to CONG Market to buy

food. When they arrived, they found that the market had been
bombed three days previously. While they were purchasing supplies
a command-car carrying many men from the Armed People's Security
and a U.S, pilot stoppel in front of the market. A cadre led from
the car the pilot who was about 36 years old, wore black clothes
furnished by the NVN authorities, and who seemed afraid. The cadre
made a speech to the gathering crowd. to the effect that this man
was a U.S, Air Force Major who participated in the bombardment of the
market which had killed nearly 200 innocent people; that the

pilot had been shot down near DONG HOI by NVN anti-aircraft
artillery guns, and that the pilot had been captured by the NVN
people, The cadre continued that the pilot confessed to having
taken part in the bombing and so he had been led to the market

to explain his action to the people and to witness the atrocity
which he committed,

The cadre's statement angered the crowd which produced sticks

and knives in an effort to beat the pilot. One man in the crowd
seized a rake and stabbed at the pilot's face; fortunately, an alert
guard pushed the rake away, but injured his hand in the attempt

to deflect it, The cadre asked the people to remain calm so that
the pilot could offer an explanation and ordered the security
unit to construct am.enclosure to protect the pilot. Through an
interpreter, the major stated, "I did not intend to bomb the
market, but in flying over it, my supervisor and I saw fires, so
he throught that it was a convoy and ordered an attack", After
the pilot made his statement,the cadres, who feared that the people
would hurt him, led him away to an unknown destination.

Source heard about another prisoner of war in May 1966 after his
unit infiltrated SVN, arriving in KONTUM Province, A fellow
soldier returning from a rice-transportatiion mission told Source
that he had seen a U.S, prisoner of war whose hand had been injured
and who was bound, An armed VC group conducted the prisoner to a
nearby village where a VC soldier said that the prisoner held the
rank of sergeant and was a mechanic from a U.S, helicopter which
had been shot down one week earlier, The mechanic was the sole
survior and was being taken to a nearby camp when he took

advantage of the security group's going to transport rice, to beat
a guard,to seize a Klljrifle and to escape. However, because of his
unfamiliarity with the terrain and the weapon he was recaptured and
sent back to his security guard,
