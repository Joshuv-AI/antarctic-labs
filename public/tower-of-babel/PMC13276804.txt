Correction
Published: 2026

Article title: Trauma reflections: an interpretive phenomenological analysis of pellet-gun victims' experiences in Kashmir

Authors: Nazam, F., & Munawar, I.

Journal: Cogent Mental Health

Bibliometrics: Volume 04, Number 01, pages 1-16

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/28324765.2025.2561668

This article was first published with a reference that was later found to be erroneous.

Following publication, concerns were identified regarding the validity and accuracy of a reference in the article. After review by the Editor, the following reference was confirmed to be erroneous:

Sharkey, P., & Schwartz, G. L. (2022). Proximity to gun violence and its impact on emotional and behavioral regulation. PNAS Nexus, 118 (23). e2100846118. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2100846118

When asked for an explanation, the authors stated that this error was introduced due to an oversight during the final stages of manuscript preparation when revising the reference list. As a result, the authors are replacing the reference with the following:

Sharkey, P., & Shen, Y. (2021). The effect of mass shootings on daily emotions is limited by time, geographic proximity, and political affiliation. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 118 (23), Article e2100846118. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2100846118

This has been corrected in the online version of the article.
