No. 716 The Ambassador in China (Hurley) to the Secretary of State[1]

top secret urgent

Chungking, 28 July 1945.

Cfb 2015. For the Eyes alone of Byrnes, Secretary of State, Potsdam, Germany from Hurley, signed ComGenChina.

Received your Victory 375, July 27.[2] Will be prepared to obtain acceptance by Chiang Kai-Shek quickly and in the method suggested on receipt of authorization.

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the Commanding General, United States Forces, China, via Army channels.
[2] Document No. 715.
