  A 40+ YEAR QUANTUM LEGACY

 1982 No-cloning theorem
 Future LANL theorist Wojciech Zurek co-authors the landmark result that an unknown quantum state
 cannot be perfectly copied.

 1983 Feynman at Los Alamos
 Richard Feynman presents “Tiny Computers Obeying Quantum Mechanical Laws” at LANL’s 40th
 Anniversary symposium.

  1996– Foundational error-correction work
 1997 LANL researchers develop the perfect five-qubit code and the general Knill–Laflamme conditions for
 quantum error correction.

  2015– Commercial quantum system on site
 2016 LANL acquires and installs a D-Wave 2X quantum annealer, enabling hands-on application and
 algorithm research.

  2018– World-class workforce pipeline
  today The Quantum Computing Summer School launches in 2018; in 2024, 21 of nearly 700 applicants were
 accepted (≈3%), and students had produced 40+ papers over seven years.
