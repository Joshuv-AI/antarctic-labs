No. 770 Proposal by the British Delegation

[Babelsberg,] July 30, 1945.

Austrian Reparations

British Proposal

Reparations will not be exacted from Austria. Removal of plant and equipment and other goods from Austria as war booty or otherwise will, in future, be a matter to be settled by the Control Council.

Source: 740.00119 Potsdam/7–3045
