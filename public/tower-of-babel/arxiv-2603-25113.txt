Impact of local girth on the S S -packing coloring of k k -saturated subcubic graphs
Ayman El Zein, Maidoun Mortada

Abstract
For a non-decreasing sequence S = ( s 1 , s 2 , … , s k ) S=(s_{1},s_{2},\dots,s_{k}) , an S S -packing coloring of a graph G G is a vertex coloring using the colors s 1 , s 2 , … , s k s_{1},s_{2},\dots,s_{k} such that any two vertices assigned the same color s i s_{i} are at distance greater than s i s_{i} . A subcubic graph is said to be k k -saturated, for 0 ≤ k ≤ 3 0\leq k\leq 3 , if every vertex of degree 3 is adjacent to at most k k vertices of degree 3. The local girth of a vertex is the length of the smallest cycle containing it. Brešar, Kuenzel, and Rall [ Discrete Math. 348(8) (2025), 114477] proved that every claw-free cubic graph is ( 1 , 1 , 2 , 2 ) (1,1,2,2) -packing colorable, confirming the conjecture for this family. Equivalently, a claw-free cubic graph is one in which each 3 3 -vertex has local girth 3. Motivated by this observation and by recent progress on S S -packing colorings of k k -saturated subcubic graphs, we study the influence of local girth on their S S -packing colorability. We establish a series of results describing how the parameters of saturation and local girth jointly determine the admissible S S -packing sequences. Sharpness is verified through explicit constructions, and several open problems are posed to delineate the remaining cases.

-saturated

vertices of degree 3. Equivalently, a

neighbors of degree 2. In a 3-saturated subcubic graph, we call a 3-vertex

(3,k)
-saturated

15

Given a non-decreasing sequence of positive integers

The smallest integer

This parameter was introduced by Goddard

However, Balogh, Kostochka, and Liu

Several significant results have been obtained on the

Their result, obtained through a detailed structural decomposition, revealed the importance of local restrictions in achieving compact

The notion of

For a vertex
