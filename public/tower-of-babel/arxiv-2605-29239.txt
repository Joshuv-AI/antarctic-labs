Bistability of midpoint-fused arches with pinned-pinned boundary conditions
Rajat Goswami † , and Safvan Palathingal †

Abstract
Arranging multiple arches in a circular pattern and fusing them at their midpoint yields a three-dimensional configuration that we refer to as midpoint-fused arches (MFA). This study investigates the structural bistability of MFA, i.e., their ability to admit two distinct, force-free stable equilibrium states. Starting from an as-fabricated, stress-free configuration, MFA can invert into a stressed, toggled state reminiscent of an umbrella’s ribs. We develop an analytical model for the response of a pinned-pinned MFA subjected to a concentrated mid-span load by minimizing the total potential energy. Individual arches are treated as spatially-deforming, and kinematic compatibility relations are derived at the fusion point to couple their deformations. Various deformation symmetries are then exploited to simplify the problem. We demonstrate the model’s utility by characterizing the force-displacement response of a two-arch MFA, identifying distinct deformation pathways and discussing the pathway transitions that occur during toggling. In particular, we show how the structure switches between symmetric and asymmetric deformation modes as it moves between stable configurations. The generality of the framework is further established through analysis of a three-arch MFA, which exhibits richer coupled deformation behaviour. Nonlinear finite-element simulations and table-top experiments corroborate the analytical predictions, showing close agreement in both equilibrium states and the associated transition responses.

8
Summary

In this work, we presented the mechanics of midpoint-fused arches (MFA) as bistable elastic structures with potential for a range of engineering applications. An analytical model was developed for MFAs with pinned-pinned boundary conditions and a transverse point load applied at the midspan. The formulation accounts for the strain-energy contributions of each constituent arch and enforces kinematic compatibility at the fusion point to couple their deformations. Multiple deformation pathways are then identified by considering different deformation symmetries.

The model assumes shallow, slender arches, within which it provides accurate predictions of MFA mechanics. The fusion region is idealised through kinematic constraints, and the finite overlap of the arches at the fusion point is not explicitly considered. Consequently, for arches with relatively large widths, the model may under-predict the midspan stiffness because the enlarged fusion region alters the local deformation behaviour.

The generality of the framework is demonstrated through detailed analyses of 2-MFA and 3-MFA configurations. Using low-order modal truncations, closed-form expressions are obtained for the symmetric and asymmetric deformation branches, their intersections, and the associated switching behaviour. Higher-mode approximations are then shown to capture the effective hybrid deformation pathway and the force-displacement response, showing good agreement with finite-element simulations and experiments.

\overrightarrow{AC}
and
\overrightarrow{A^{\prime}C^{\prime}}
is then,

\displaystyle\Phi_{i}\big|_{X_{i}=1/2}=

\displaystyle\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{\overrightarrow{AC}\cdot\overrightarrow{A^{\prime}C^{\prime}}}{|\overrightarrow{AC}||\overrightarrow{A^{\prime}C^{\prime}}|}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\cos^{-1}\left(\sin^{2}\theta_{i}\cos\omega+\cos^{2}\theta_{i}\right)

(A3)

Now, by assuming that both
\Phi_{i}\big|_{X_{i}=1/2}
and
\omega
are small, we can simplify
Eq.

A3
as,

\displaystyle 1-\frac{\Phi_{i}\big|_{X_{i}=1/2}^{2}}{2}

\displaystyle=\sin^{2}\theta_{i}\left(1-\frac{\omega^{2}}{2}\right)+\cos^{2}\theta_{i},

\displaystyle\implies\Phi_{i}\big|_{X_{i}=1/2}

\displaystyle=\omega\sin\theta_{i}.

(A4)

Similarly, to compute
\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{dW_{i}}{dX_{i}}\big|_{X_{i}=1/2}
, we consider the vectors
\overrightarrow{OB}
and
\overrightarrow{O^{\prime}B^{\prime}}
and evaluate the angle between them:

\displaystyle\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{dW_{i}}{dX_{i}}\bigg|_{X_{i}=1/2}=

\displaystyle\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{\overrightarrow{OB}\cdot\overrightarrow{O^{\prime}B^{\prime}}}{|\overrightarrow{OB}||\overrightarrow{O^{\prime}B^{\prime}}|}\right)=\cos^{-1}\left(\cos^{2}\theta_{i}\cos\omega+\sin^{2}\theta_{i}\right),

(A5)

\displaystyle\implies\frac{1}{\lambda}\frac{dW_{i}}{dX_{i}}\bigg|_{X_{i}=1/2}

\displaystyle=\omega\cos\theta_{i},

(A6)

where,

\displaystyle\overrightarrow{OB}

\displaystyle=l\cos\theta_{i}\hat{i}+0\hat{j}+l\sin\theta_{i}\hat{k},

(A7)

\displaystyle\overrightarrow{O^{\prime}B^{\prime}}

\displaystyle=l\cos\theta_{i}\cos\omega\hat{i}+l\cos\theta_{i}\sin\omega\hat{j}+l\sin\theta_{i}\hat{k}.

(A8)

Appendix B
Strain Energy of the
i^{\mathrm{th}}
Arch

As discussed in
Section

2.2
, the non-dimensionalized strain energy due to bending for an individual arch is,

\displaystyle SE_{b_{i}}=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}W_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}+\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}.

(B1)

The as fabricated profile is
H_{i}=a_{1}\sin(\pi X_{i})
and the deformed in-plane profile is
W_{i}=\sum_{j=1,2,3}^{n}A_{ij}\sin(j\pi X_{i})
. The out-of-plane deformation of the
i\textsuperscript{th}~
arch is expressed as
U_{i}=\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}B_{ij}\bar{U}_{j}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}B_{ij}\tilde{U}_{j}
, with
\bar{U}_{j}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\cos[(j+1)\pi X_{i}]
and
\tilde{U}_{j}=\frac{1}{2}\left[1-2X_{i}-\cos\left(N_{j}X_{i}\right)+\frac{2}{N_{j}}\sin\left(N_{j}X_{i}\right)\right]
. The rotation about the longitudinal axis of the cross-section is
\Phi_{i}=\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}P_{j}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{l}C_{ij}\tilde{P}_{j}
, where
P_{j}
, and
\tilde{P_{j}}
are both defined as
\sin(j\pi X_{i})
for odd and even entries of
j
respectively.

To simplify
Eq.

B1
, we write
SE_{b_{i}}=A+B
, where

A=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}W_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}
and

B=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}
. Expanding
A
yields

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}W_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}W_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}^{2}+\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}^{2}-2\frac{d^{2}W_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\pi^{4}}{4}\left(a_{1}-A_{i1}\right)^{2}+4\pi^{4}A_{i2}^{2}+\frac{81}{4}\pi^{4}A_{i3}^{2}+\ldots.+\frac{n^{4}}{4}\pi^{4}A_{in}^{2}.

(B2)

Similarly, expanding
B
gives,

\displaystyle B

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}-\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}^{2}+\Phi_{i}^{2}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}^{2}-2\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)dX_{i}.

(B3)

For convenience, we denote the three contributions in
B
as
I_{1}=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}
,
I_{2}=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}
, and
I_{3}=-\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(2\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)dX_{i}
so that
B=I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}
. We evaluate each term below.
First,

\displaystyle I_{1}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}U_{i}^{\prime\prime 2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}B_{ij}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}B_{ij}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\right)^{2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}B_{ij}^{2}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime 2}dX_{i}+\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}B_{ij}^{2}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime^{2}}dX_{i}+\beta^{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m}B_{ij}B_{ik}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\tilde{U}_{k}^{\prime\prime}dX_{i}

\displaystyle+\beta^{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m}B_{ij}B_{ik}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\bar{U}_{k}^{\prime\prime}dX_{i}+\beta^{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m}B_{ij}B_{ik}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\tilde{U}_{k}^{\prime\prime}dX_{i}\quad\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\text{ where }\\
j\neq k\end{array}\right\}

\displaystyle=\beta^{2}\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}\frac{B_{ij}^{2}}{16}(j+1)^{4}\pi^{4}+\beta^{2}\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}\frac{B_{ij}^{2}}{16}N_{j}^{4}

(B6)

Next,
I_{2}
is

\displaystyle I_{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}a_{1}^{2}\left(\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\Phi_{i}^{2}H_{i}^{\prime\prime 2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}P_{j}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{l}C_{ij}\tilde{P}_{j}\bigg)^{2}\left[\sin(\pi X_{i})\right]^{\prime\prime 2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\bigg(\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}P_{j}^{2}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}\tilde{P}_{j}^{2}+2\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}P_{j}\tilde{P}_{k}+2\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}P_{j}P_{k}

\displaystyle+2\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}\tilde{P}_{j}\tilde{P}_{k}\bigg)\left[a_{1}^{2}\pi^{4}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})\right]dX_{i}

\displaystyle=\pi^{4}a_{1}^{2}\beta^{2}\biggr[\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}P_{j}^{2}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}\tilde{P}_{j}^{2}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}P_{j}\tilde{P}_{k}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}P_{j}P_{k}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}

\displaystyle+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}\tilde{P}_{j}\tilde{P}_{k}\sin^{2}(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}\biggr]

\displaystyle=a_{1}^{2}\beta^{2}\bigg(\frac{3\pi^{4}}{16}C_{i1}^{2}+\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{j=2,3,4}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}-\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}-\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}\bigg)

(B7)

Finally,
I_{3}
becomes

\displaystyle I_{3}

\displaystyle=-\frac{\beta^{2}}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\left(2\Phi_{i}\frac{d^{2}U_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\frac{d^{2}H_{i}}{dX_{i}^{2}}\right)dX_{i}=-\beta^{2}\int_{0}^{1}U_{i}^{\prime\prime}\Phi_{i}H_{i}^{\prime\prime}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=a_{1}\beta^{2}\pi^{2}{\bigg[\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}P_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\tilde{P}_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}\bigg.}

\displaystyle\bigg.+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}P_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\tilde{P}_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}\bigg]

\displaystyle=a_{1}\beta^{2}\pi^{2}\bigg[\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\bar{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}P_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}+\int_{0}^{1}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}\tilde{U}_{j}^{\prime\prime}\tilde{P}_{k}\sin(\pi X_{i})dX_{i}\bigg]

\displaystyle=a_{1}\beta^{2}\pi^{2}\Bigg(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}M_{jk}^{*}+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}M_{jk}^{*}\Bigg).

(B8)

Therefore, the bending strain energy
SE_{b_{i}}
is obtained by combining
A
and
B=I_{1}+I_{2}+I_{3}
, giving,

\displaystyle SE_{b}

\displaystyle=\frac{\pi^{4}}{4}\left(a_{1}-A_{i1}\right)^{2}+\sum_{j=2,3,4}^{n}\frac{j^{4}}{4}\pi^{4}A_{ij}^{2}+\beta^{2}\Bigg[\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}\frac{B_{ij}^{2}}{16}(j+1)^{4}\pi^{4}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}\frac{B_{ij}^{2}}{16}N_{j}^{4}+\Bigg.

\displaystyle a_{1}^{2}\Bigg(\frac{3\pi^{4}C_{i1}^{2}}{16}+\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{j=2,3,4}^{l}C_{ij}^{2}-\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}-\frac{\pi^{4}}{8}\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{l}C_{ij}C_{ik}\Bigg)+

\displaystyle\Bigg.a_{1}\pi^{2}\Bigg(\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=1,3,5\\
k=1,3,5\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}M_{jk}^{*}+\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}j=2,4,6\\
k=2,4,6\end{subarray}}^{m,l}B_{ij}C_{ik}M_{jk}^{*}\Bigg)\Bigg].

(B9)

The non-dimensionalized compression strain energy of the
i\textsuperscript{th}~
arch is

\displaystyle SE_{c_{i}}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2}Q^{2}\left\{\int_{0}^{1}\left[\left(\frac{dH_{i}}{dX_{i}}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{dW_{i}}{dX_{i}}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{dU_{i}}{dX_{i}}\right)^{2}\right]dX_{i}\right\}^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2}Q^{2}\left[\frac{\pi^{2}a_{1}^{2}}{2}-\pi^{2}\left(\frac{A_{i1}^{2}}{2}+2A_{i2}^{2}+\frac{9}{2}A_{i3}^{2}+\ldots.+\frac{n^{2}A_{in}^{2}}{2}\right)\right]

\displaystyle-\frac{3}{2}Q^{2}\pi^{2}\left(\frac{4B_{i1}^{2}}{8}+\frac{16B_{i3}^{2}}{8}+\frac{36B_{i5}^{2}}{8}+\ldots+\frac{(m+1)^{2}B_{im}^{2}}{8}\right)

\displaystyle-\frac{3}{2}Q^{2}\left(\frac{N_{2}^{2}B_{i2}^{2}}{8}+\frac{N_{4}^{2}B_{i4}^{2}}{8}+\ldots+\frac{N_{j}^{2}B_{im}^{2}}{8}\right)^{2}

\displaystyle=\frac{3}{2}Q^{2}\left(\frac{\pi^{2}a_{1}^{2}}{2}-\pi^{2}\sum_{j=1,2,3}^{n}\frac{j^{2}A_{ij}^{2}}{2}-\pi^{2}\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{m}\frac{(j+1)^{2}B_{ij}^{2}}{8}-\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{m}\frac{N_{j}^{2}B_{ij}^{2}}{8}\right)^{2}.

(B10)

And the non-dimensionalized torsional strain energy of the
i\textsuperscript{th}~
arch is

\displaystyle SE_{t_{i}}

\displaystyle=K\int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{d\Phi_{i}}{dX_{i}}\right)^{2}dX_{i}

\displaystyle=K\int_{0}^{1}\left(\sum_{j=1,3,5}^{l}C_{ij}P_{j}+\sum_{j=2,4,6}^{l}C_{ij}\tilde{P}_{j}\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{\pi^{2}K}{2}\sum_{j=1,2,3}^{l}j^{2}C_{ij}^{2}.

(B11)
