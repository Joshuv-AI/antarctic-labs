GENERAL RENE EMILIO ((PONCE)) NOR ARMED FORCES CHIEF
GILBERTO ((RUBIO)) HAVE RAISED THE ISSUE FOR DISCUSSION AMONG EMCFA
STAFF OFFICERS, AND THE TOPIC HAS DISAPPEARED FOR ALL PRACTICAL
PURPOSES. © POSSIBLE REASONS FOR THIS
SILENCE:

A. NEITHER PONCE NOR RUBIO BELIEVE THEY SHOULD TELL
THE JUDICIAL SYSTEM HOW TO OPERATE, AND APPROACHING PRESIDENT ALFREDO
((CRISTIANI)) TO SOLICIT ACTION IN THE CASE WOULD BE AN AFFRONT TO
CRISTIANI.

. THE ESAF IS AT A VERY VOLATILE STAGE IN ITS

HISTORY. IN LATE 1990 THE ESAF CONSIDERED REDUCING ITS FORCES BY
THIRTY PERCENT, AND MORALE SUFFERED. AFTER THE FARABUNDO MARTI
LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN) NOVEMBER 1990 OFFENSIVE, THE ESAF SHELVED
REDUCTION PLANS, AND BEGAN TO INCREASE ITS RANKS, AS WELL AS
EMPHASIZING COMBAT EFFICIENCY. THE ELECTION CAMPAIGN AND ATTENDANT
FMLN VIOLENCE, ALONG WITH THE POSSIBILITY OF ANOTHER FMLN NATIONAL
LEVEL OFFENSIVE, HAVE MADE SENIOR ESAF OFFICERS LOATHE TO UPSET THE
DELICATE SITUATION WHICH THE ESAF IS CURRENTLY EXPERIENCING IN TERMS
OF MORALE, MISSION EFFECTIVENESS AND TROOP STRENGTH. CONSEQUENTLY ,
THE EMCFA WILL LIKELY IGNORE THE JESUIT CASE UNTIL CIRCUMSTANCES
DICTATE OTHERWISE.
