'''Adopted unanimously at the 1978th meeting (private meeting)'''

''The Security Council'',

''Having examined'' the recommendation for the appointment of the Secretary-General of the United Nations,

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that Mr. Kurt Waldheim be appointed Secretary-General of the United Nations for a second term of office from 1 January 1977 to 31 December 1981.
