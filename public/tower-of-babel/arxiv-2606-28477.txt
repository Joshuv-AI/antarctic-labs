On the Dominions of Certain Semigroups of Transformations
Halima H. Assiri and Jehan A. Al-bar

Abstract




Abstract:
In the full transformation semigroup
T_{n}
on a finite chain
X_{n}
, let
D_{n}=\{\alpha\in T_{n}:(\forall x\in X_{n})\ x\alpha\leq x\}
be the subsemigroup of all order-decreasing maps of
T_{n}
, and let
O_{n}=\{\alpha\in T_{n}:(\forall x,y\in X_{n})\ x\leq y\Rightarrow x\alpha\leq y\alpha\}
be the subsemigroup of all order-preserving maps of
T_{n}
. The Catalan monoid
C_{n}
is a semigroup of all order-decreasing and order-preserving full transformations of
X_{n}
. In this paper, it is shown that
O_{n}
is closed in
T_{n}
. Also, the dominion of
D_{n}
and the dominion of
C_{n}
in
T_{n}
, denoted by
Dom_{T_{n}}(D_{n})
and
Dom_{T_{n}}(C_{n})
, are characterized, and it is shown that they are regular idempotent-generated subsemigroups of
T_{n}
. Moreover, a formula for the number of their elements and their idempotents is given.

Keywords:
full transformation semigroups, dominion, singular maps, oversemigroup, idempotent-generated, order-decreasing maps, order-preserving maps, Catalan monoid.

Let

in

Proposition 2.1
.

Let
\alpha

\in D_{n}
and
\alpha^{\prime}

\in T_{n}\setminus D_{n}
defined by

x\alpha^{\prime}=\begin{cases}\text{min}(x\alpha^{-1})&\text{if }x\in Im(\alpha)\\
y\in[(z\alpha)\alpha^{-1}]\ \ \text{such that}\ \ z\alpha<x&\text{if }x\notin Im(\alpha)\end{cases}

(1)

Then

1-
\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=\alpha

2-
\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in E(D_{n})

3-
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\in E(D_{n})

4-
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in Dom_{T_{n}}(D_{n})

Proof.

Let
x\in X_{n}
. First, we will show that
x\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=x\alpha
. Since
x\alpha\in Im(\alpha)
and
min((x\alpha)\alpha^{-1})\in(x\alpha)\alpha^{-1}
, then

x\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=(min((x\alpha)\alpha^{-1})\alpha=x\alpha,

so
\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=\alpha
.

For the second part, since
x\in(x\alpha)\alpha^{-1}
, then

x\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=min((x\alpha)\alpha^{-1})\leq x,

so
x\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\leq x
. Also

(\alpha\alpha^{\prime})^{2}=\alpha\alpha^{\prime}
, thus
\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in E(D_{n})
.

Next, for the third part,
if
x\in Im(\alpha)
, since
min(x\alpha^{-1})\in x\alpha^{-1}
, then

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=(min(x\alpha^{-1}))\alpha=x.

If
x\notin Im(\alpha)
, then

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=y\alpha=z\alpha<x,

so
x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\leq x
. Also
(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha)^{2}=\alpha^{\prime}\alpha
, thus
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\in E(D_{n})
.

And now for the last part,let
f,g:T_{n}\rightarrow T
be semigroup homomorphisms with
f|_{D_{n}}=g|_{D_{n}}
.

Then

(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime})f\vskip 5.69046pt=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha)f(\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha)g(\alpha^{\prime})f\hskip 42.0pt[\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\in D_{n}]\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha)g(\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha)f(\alpha^{\prime})f\hskip 30.0pt[\alpha\in D_{n}]\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha\alpha^{\prime})g\hskip 42.0pt[\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in D_{n}]\\
\vskip 5.69046pt\hskip 80.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime})g
.

Hence,
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in Dom_{T_{n}}(D_{n})
.

∎

Next we characterize

Lemma 2.1
.

Let
\alpha\in D_{n}
and
\alpha^{\prime}\in T_{n}\setminus D_{n}
defined by (1).

1- If
\alpha\in E(D_{n})
, then
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in D_{n}
.

2- If
\alpha\notin E(D_{n})
, then
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in Dom_{T_{n}}(D_{n})\setminus D_{n}
.

Proof.

1- Let
\alpha\in E(D_{n})
and
x\in X_{n}
. We will show that
x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\leq x
. If
x\in Im(\alpha)
, then

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=(min(x\alpha^{-1}))\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=x\alpha^{\prime}=min(x\alpha^{-1})=x.

If
x\notin Im(\alpha)
, then

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=y\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=z\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=(min((z\alpha)\alpha^{-1}))=z\alpha<x,

thus
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in D_{n}
.

2- Let
\alpha\notin E(D_{n})
. Then
\exists\ x\in Im(\alpha)
such that
x\notin x\alpha^{-1}
,
so

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=(min(x\alpha^{-1}))\alpha\alpha^{\prime}=x\alpha^{\prime}=min(x\alpha^{-1}).

Since
\forall\ y\in x\alpha^{-1},y>x
, then
x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}>x
,
and so
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in Dom_{T_{n}}(D_{n})\setminus D_{n}
.

∎

(3)

2-
\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in E(C_{n})

3-
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\in E(C_{n})

4-

Proof.

Let

Next, for the third part,

x\alpha^{\prime}\alpha=(min(x\alpha^{-1}))\alpha=x.

If

If

For the last part, let

(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime})f=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha)f(\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha)g(\alpha^{\prime})f\hskip 50.0pt[\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\in C_{n}]\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha)g(\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha)f(\alpha^{\prime})f\hskip 35.0pt[\alpha\in C_{n}]\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha\alpha^{\prime})f\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime})g(\alpha\alpha^{\prime})g\hskip 50.0pt[\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in C_{n}]\\
\vskip 2.84544pt\hskip 86.0pt=(\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime})g

Hence,
\alpha^{\prime}\alpha\alpha^{\prime}\in Dom_{T_{n}}(C_{n})
