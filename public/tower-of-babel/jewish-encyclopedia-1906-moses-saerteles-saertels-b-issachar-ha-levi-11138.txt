Exegete; lived at Prague in the first half of the seventeenth century. His name ( ) is a matronymic from "Sarah." He published the Pentateuch and the Five Rolls, with glosses and notes in Judæo-German, under the title "Be'er Mosheh" (Prague, 1605; frequently reprinted), which was highly esteemed by Ezekiel Landau. As a supplement to it he appended "Leḳaḥ Ṭob," glosses and notes, in German, to the Prophets and the Hagiographa.
