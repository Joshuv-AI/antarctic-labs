Staggered spin susceptibility at a two-dimensional antiferromagnetic quantum critical point
Yutaka Itoh

Abstract
We study the finite temperature staggered spin susceptibility χ ⁡ ( Q ) \chi(Q) as a function of the mode-mode coupling constant y 1 y_{1} in the self-consistent renormalization theory of two-dimensional antiferromagnetic spin fluctuations with zero-point quantum fluctuations just at the quantum critical point ( y 0 y_{0} = 0). We find that the value y 1 y_{1} = 0.1 is a criterion to classify the effect of the zero-point spin fluctuations on the temperature dependence of χ ⁡ ( Q ) \chi(Q) into a Curie law for weak y 1 < y_{1}< 0.1 and a Curie-Weiss type or a power law type for strong y 1 > y_{1}> 0.1.

V
Conclusion

In conclusion, the effect of the zero-point quantum fluctuations on the temperature dependence of the staggered spin susceptibility
\chi(Q)
depends on the magnitude of the mode-mode coupling constant
y_{1}
at the two-dimensional antiferromagnetic QCP.
In the weakly coupling regime (
y_{1}<
0.1), the predominant thermal spin fluctuations lead to a Curie law of
\chi(Q)
.
In the strongly coupling regime (
y_{1}>
0.1), the zero-point spin fluctuations have an influence comparable to that of the thermal fluctuations in determining
\chi(Q)
.
As to experimental studies, the absence of the Curie-Weiss temperature (
t_{\theta}
= 0) can serve as the identification of the QCP (
y_{0}
= 0) for the weak mode-mode coupling systems (
y_{1}<
0.1).
