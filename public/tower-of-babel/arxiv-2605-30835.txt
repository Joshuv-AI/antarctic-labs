Introduction

An important topic in topology and geometry is to find a topological invariant of a space, i.e., a quantity or property which is not changed by a homeomorphism. To be more precise, let
\mathscr{I}
be such an invariant, which means that if

\cong

f:X\sim Y
is a homotopy equivalence,
then
\mathscr{I}(X)=\mathscr{I}(Y)

(1.1)

\mathscr{E}(g)\copyright\mathscr{E}(f)\Longrightarrow\mathscr{E}(g\circ f)
