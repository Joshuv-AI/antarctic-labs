 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 We will develop structural mechanics models that capture the evolution of feedstock attributes
 during the Forest Concepts comminution process. Models will be validated by data provided by
 Forest Concepts and experiments performed by synergistic projects within the Feedstock
 Conversion Interface Consortium.

 Summary of Research Results:

 Task 1: Discrete Element Method (DEM) Modeling

 Develop DEM models with two primary objectives:

 1. Model fragmentation of woody biomass in response to shear and compression forces that
 are representative of those produced by the Participant’s comminution process, and
 2. Assessing the effects of particle shapes of milled biomass (distribution of size and aspect
 ratio) on bulk flowability and process costs in critical feeding operation units.

 Task 2: Finite Element Method (FEM) Modeling Development FEM models to:

 3. Understand the evolution of intraparticle porosity as a function of the mechanical stresses
 applied during pre-processing and
 4. Predict the impact of the altered microstructure on intraparticle transport phenomena and
 associated conversion reactions.
