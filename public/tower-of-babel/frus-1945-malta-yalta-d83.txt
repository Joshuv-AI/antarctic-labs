The Secretary of State to the Special Assistant to the Secretary of State (Pasvolsky)[1]

[Washington,] December 22, 1944.

Subject: Voting in the Security Council

I reported orally to the President on the latest developments on this matter and indicated to him that we were doing what we could to press the Russians and British for an early decision. I told him that if we received “yes” that we hoped to send out invitations for a March meeting. He felt that would be entirely appropriate.

He indicated some displeasure at the fact that things were moving so slowly in this field.

E. R. Stettinius, Jr.

cc: Mr. Edwin Wilson

Mr. Alger Hiss
