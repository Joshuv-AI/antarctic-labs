SUBJ: UFO SIGHTED OVER URUMQI EVENING OF 6 SEP

SOURCE: BEIJING XINHUA IN ENGLISH 0619 GMT 13 SEP 89
TEXT:

((TEXT)) URUMQI, SEPTEMBER 13 (XINHUA) -- AN

UNIDENTIFIED FLYING
OBJECT (UFO) WAS SIGHTED OVER URUMQI, CAPITAL OF THE XINJIANG UYGUR

AUTONOMOUS REGION, AT 11:13 PM ON SEPTEMBER 6.

THAT NIGRT TONG YUWEI, A WORKER IN THE CITY SAW A DARK CLOUD
SUDDENLY LIGHT UP. FOLLOWING A GOLDEN FLASH, A SAUCER SHAPED
OBJECT WITH A BLACK GAP ON ITS EDGE APPEARED. AFTER HOVERING IN
THE SKY FOR A MINUTE, THE UFO, BATHED IN RED AND YELLOW LIGHT,
ROTATING AT HIGH SPEED, DROPPED OUT OF SIGHT TOWARD THE SOUTHWEST.

DURING THE TIME OF THE OBJECT'S APPEARANCE, OBSERVERS REPORTED
HEARING SOUNDS LOUDER THAN A CAR ENGINE.
