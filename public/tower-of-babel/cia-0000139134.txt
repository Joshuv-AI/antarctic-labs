6. IRAN: SEEKING MOVEMENT ON THE HOSTAGE ISSUE

PRESIDENT RAFSANJANI IS TRYING TO CAPITALIZE ON, INTERNATIONAL
CONDEMNATION OF IRAQ BY ENCOURAGING MOVEMENT TOWARD RESOLVING THE
LEBANESE HOSTAGE SITUATION. ON SATURDAY, ACCORDING TO FRENCH PRESS
REPORTS, THE PRO-RAFSANJANI TEHRAN TIMES URGED THE WEST TO RESPOND
TO THE RELEASE OF IRISH HOSTAGE BRIAN KEENAN BY PRESSING ISRAEL TO
FREE SHAYKH OBEID AND OTHER ARAB PRISONERS BUT DID NOT REPEAT
TEHRAN'S DEMAND FOR RELEASE OF FOUR IRANIANS IT CLAIMS ARE PRISONERS

IN LEBANON.

; COMMENT: RAFSANJANI RECOGNIZES THAT THE GULF CRISIS HAS RAISED

i IRAN'S STOCK IN THE WEST AND PRESENTS AN OPPORTUNITY FOR HIM TO
IMPROVE RELATIONS WITH THE WEST. THE REPORTED OMISSION BY THE TEHRAN
TIMES OF DEMANDS REGARDING THE ALLEGED IRANIAN HOSTAGES SUGGESTS
RAFSANJANI IS PREPARED TO BE MORE FLEXIBLE, DESPITE CONTINUED CALLS
BY IRANIAN HARDLINERS FOR RELEASE OF THE IRANIANS. QT
