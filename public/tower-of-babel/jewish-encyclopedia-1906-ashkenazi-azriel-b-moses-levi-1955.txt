Preacher at Tarnogrod, government of Lublin, Poland, in the seventeenth century. He was the author of "NaḦalat' Azriel" (The Inheritance of Azriel), Frankfort-on-the-Oder, 1691, a work comprising homilies and comments upon parts of the Bible.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 754. H. R. I. Br.
