Parity of parts and excludant statistics in partitions
Gargi Mukherjee

Abstract
. In this paper, we study restricted excludant statistics depending on its parity in partitions where parts with same parity are distinct. Using q q -series transformations, we show that generating functions of these partition statistics are related to the quantum modular forms σ ⁡ ( q ) {\sigma}(q) , its companion σ ∗ ​ ( q ) \sigma^{*}(q) introduced by Ramanujan, and v 2 ​ ( q ) v_{2}(q) , a Nahm-type sum, introduced by Andrews. Utilizing Tauberian method, we obtain asymptotics of such sequences.

It is easy to see that

(3.4)

,

\displaystyle\mathsf{P}^{[1]}_{od}(n)

\displaystyle:=\left\{\lambda\in\mathsf{P}_{od}(n):\lambda_{o_{i}}-\lambda_{o_{i+1}}=\lambda_{e_{j}}-\lambda_{e_{j+1}}=2,\lambda_{o_{r}}=1,\lambda_{e_{s}}=2,\lambda_{o_{1}}<\lambda_{e_{1}}\right\},

(3.5)

\displaystyle\mathsf{P}^{[2]}_{od}(n)
