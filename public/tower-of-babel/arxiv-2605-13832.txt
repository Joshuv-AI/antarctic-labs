Combining moment matrices, symmetric extension, and Lovász theta: Φ E8 \Phi_{\text{E8}} is entangled
Jȩdrzej Stempin, Gerard Anglès Munné, Santiago Llorens, Felix Huber

Abstract
We solve an open problem in entanglement theory posed by Yu et al., Nature Communications 12, 1012 (2021) . The problem is to show, via an entanglement witness, that the 14 14 -qubit state Φ E8 \Phi_{\text{E8}} is entangled. Inspired by a method from quantum codes, we combine symmetric extension with moment matrices to prove that Φ E8 \Phi_{\textup{E8}} is entangled. The proof has the form of a rational infeasibility certificate for a semidefinite program, yielding an explicit entanglement witness. Our approach unifies and extends several earlier methods that involve the Lovász theta number of the Pauli anti-commutativity graph, promising scalability and flexibility in further applications.

is the number of its nontrivial tensor factors.
Therefore the entanglement of
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}

can be understood as the fact that there is no point in
the convex body
\text{TH}(G_{4+})

for which

1+\sum_{a\in G_{4+}}M_{aa}=2^{7}

holds.

Observation 5
.

If
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
is separable, then
there exists a point in
\textup{TH}(G_{4+})

with
1+\sum_{a\in G_{4+}}M_{aa}=2^{7}
.

Proof.

Suppose
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
is separable.
By Observation
2

the SDP in Eq. (
2
) is feasible.
The state
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
has vanishing expectation values
on all non-trivial Pauli operators of weight smaller or equal to three (c.f. Appendix
II.3
),

\tr\big((E_{a}\otimes E_{a}^{{\dagger}})\Phi_{\textup{E8}}\big)=0\,,\quad\text{if}\quad 0<\wt(E_{a})<4\,.

(28)

Thus
\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu_{aa}=0
must hold in

) for all

with

0<\wt(E_{a})<4
.
As
\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu
must be positive semidefinite,
so must all of its
2\times 2
minors.
As a consequence, if a diagonal entry

\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu_{aa}
vanishes,
then so does the whole corresponding row and column,
i.e.,
\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu_{ab}=\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu_{ba}=0
for all
b=0,\dots,N
.
As a consequence, we can truncate the matrix

\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu
without changing the objective value.
In terms of the anticommutativity graph of

\mathcal{E}_{7}
,
this corresponds to
removing all vertices
a

for which
0<\wt(E_{a})<4
,
leading to the graph
G_{4+}
.
Furthermore by Eqs. (
2
) and (

\sum_{a=0}^{N}\tr\big((E_{a}\otimes E_{a}^{\dagger})\Phi_{\textup{E8}}\big)=2^{7}\,.

(29)

We can thus relax the SDP in Eq. (
2
) to

find

\displaystyle M

subject to

\displaystyle M_{aa}=x_{a}\quad\quad\forall a\in G_{4+}\,,

\displaystyle M_{ab}=0\quad\quad\,\,\,\,\text{if}\quad a\sim b\,,

\displaystyle\begin{pmatrix}1&x^{T}\\
x&M\end{pmatrix}\succeq 0\,,

\displaystyle 1+\sum_{a\in G_{4+}}M_{aa}=2^{7}\,.

(30)

If the SDP (
2
) is feasible, then so is
the SDP (
Proof.
).
Let
M
be a feasible point of the latter.
Then
\diag(M)
is a point in
\textup{TH}(G_{4+})

satisfying

.

Similar to the SDP in Eq. (

Details and an exact certificate are given in Appendix

Entanglement witness. —

The open problem in Yu et al.
Yu et al. 2021

explicitly also asks for an operational entanglement criterion.
We derive an operator witness;
i.e., an observable that, if negative on a state
\varrho
,
shows that
\varrho
is entangled.
For this we relate our approach with that by Gois et al.
de Gois et al. 2023
.

Consider two sets of observables
\{A_{a}\}_{a=1}^{m}
,
\{B_{a}\}_{a=1}^{m}

with
anticommutativity graphs

G_{A}
,
G_{B}
.
Then the following is an entanglement witness
de Gois et al. 2023
,

W=\sqrt{\vartheta(G_{A})\vartheta(G_{B})}\,\mathds{1}\,-\sum_{a=1}^{m}A_{a}\otimes B_{a}\,.

(31)

That is,

\tr(W\varrho^{\text{sep}})\geq 0

for all states
\varrho^{\text{sep}}
that are separable across the bipartition
A|B
;
while

\tr(W\varrho^{\text{ent}})<0
holds
for at least one entangled state

\varrho^{\text{ent}}
.
From
Eq. (
31
)
follows that
entanglement in
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
is
detected by the witness,

\displaystyle W_{\Phi_{\textup{E8}}}

\displaystyle=\vartheta\big(G_{4+}\big)\mathds{1}\,-\sum_{a\in G_{4+}}\!\!E_{a}\otimes E_{a}^{\dagger}\,.

(32)

However, obtaining
\vartheta(G_{4+})

is computationally challenging due to adverse scaling.
In Appendix
IV
we compute a
symmetry-reduced relaxation
with value

\vartheta_{\text{sym}}\big(G_{4+}\big)\approx 126.8876\,.

(33)

Due to the fact that
\vartheta_{\text{sym}}
is a relaxation
of
\vartheta
,
one has
\vartheta_{\text{sym}}(G_{4+})\geq\vartheta(G_{4+})
.
Surprisingly,
this relaxation is already enough to obtain a witness.
Indeed the operator

W_{\Phi_{\textup{E8}}}^{\prime}=\vartheta_{\text{sym}}\big(G_{4+}\big)\mathds{1}\,-\sum_{a\in G_{4+}}E_{a}\otimes E_{a}^{\dagger}\,,

(34)

with

\vartheta_{\text{sym}}\big(G_{4+}\big)\approx 126.8876

also detects entanglement in
\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
,
as

\tr(W^{\prime}\Phi_{\textup{E8}})<0
due to Eq. (
2
)
and the expansion in Eq. (
15
).
In fact, one can show (Appendix
IV.2
)
that
\vartheta_{\text{sym}}\big(G_{4+}\big)=\vartheta\big(G_{4+}\big)
due to symmetries of the Pauli anti-commutativity graph, making the relaxation exact.

Conclusions. —

We have derived our main result,

\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
is entangled, via two semidefinite programming formulations.
Of these, the SDP (
2
)
is the strongest.
In turn,
the SDP (
Proof.
)
is a natural relaxation,
which has also already appeared in the
context of bounds on quantum codes
Munné et al. 2024
.
This last SDP then naturally links up to
a construction known from entanglement theory
de Gois et al. 2023
,
yielding a
computationally practical
witness [Eq. (
32
)].
In contrast to previous works,
our constructions clarifies
the connection
to the symmetric extension hierarchy,
a well known computational method
for outer approximations to the set of separable states.
The key insight is to combine symmetric extension with moment matrices,
yielding an object that can more easily be truncated
than density matrices.

Several natural constraints
could be added to the SDP in Eq. (
2
),
for example constraints arising from positive partial transpose or state inversion conditions
Moroder et al. 2013
;
Vourdas 2004
.
Also, purity type constraints from swap expectations,
e.g.,

\tr\big((123)\sum_{i}p_{i}\mu^{\otimes 3}\big)=2^{-7}\tr\big((12)\sum_{i}p_{i}\mu_{i}^{\otimes 2}\big)
,
and structural relations, e.g.,
\Gamma_{ab}\propto\Gamma_{\alpha\beta}
if

\langle E_{a}\rangle\langle E_{b}^{\dagger}\rangle\langle E_{a}^{\dagger}E_{b}\rangle\propto\langle E_{\alpha}\rangle\langle E_{\beta}^{\dagger}\rangle\langle E_{\alpha}^{\dagger}E_{\beta}\rangle
,
can be imposed on the moment matrix, c.f.
(
Munné et al. 2024
, Eq. (68))
.
While not needed to show entanglement in

\Phi_{\textup{E8}}
, these can strengthen our method.
Finally, one could generalize the approach to
detect entanglement in
non-symmetric states.

Our approach
establishes remarkable
links between
entanglement detection, graph theory, and coding bounds that revolve around the Lovász theta number.
One can hope that the Terwilliger methods develop here can be used to determine analytical bounds on the (anti-) commutation index (also known as
\beta
-number)
Xu et al. 2024
;
Morán and Huber 2024
,
relevant in the study of quantum Hamiltonians
Hastings and O’Donnell 2023
;
Anschuetz et al. 2025

and
stabilizer testing
Bao et al. 2025
.

As a consequence of this average, the entries of the symmetrized moment matrix depend only on the tuple
(i,j,t,p)
.
In particular, for every pair
E_{a},E_{b}

with parameters
(i,j,t,p)
one has

\displaystyle\tilde{\Gamma}_{ab}=x^{t,p}_{i,j}=\frac{1}{\gamma^{t,p}_{i,j}}\!\!\!\!\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}E_{a},E_{b}\in\mathcal{E}_{n}\\
|s(E^{\dagger}_{a})|\,=\,i\\
|s(E_{b})|\,=\,j\\
\lvert s(E^{\dagger}_{a})\cap s(E_{b})\rvert\,=\,t\\
\lvert s(E^{\dagger}_{a}E_{b})\rvert\,=\,i+j-t-p\\
\end{subarray}}\!\!\!\!\!\!{\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu}_{ab}\,.

(59)

Here
\gamma^{t,p}_{i,j}
is a normalization factor

\displaystyle\gamma_{i,j}^{t,p}

\displaystyle=3^{i+j-t}2^{t-p}\binom{n}{p,t-p,i-t,j-t}\,,

(60)

and the multinomial coefficient is defined as

\displaystyle\binom{n}{a_{1},\dots,a_{r}}=\frac{n!}{a_{1}!\dots a_{r}!(n-\sum^{r}_{\ell=1}a_{\ell})!}\,.

(61)

Since
\pi
acts on
\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu
by unitary adjoint action [Eq. (
56
)],
it follows that if

\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mu\Gamma\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu\succeq 0
,
then
\tilde{\Gamma}\succeq 0
.

III.2
Block diagonalization

Using the framework introduced in the previous section, we recall from Ref.
Gijswijt et al. 2006
that
\tilde{\Gamma}\succeq 0
if and only if

\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\begin{subarray}{c}a,k\in\mathds{N}_{0}\\
0\leq a\leq k\leq n+a-k\end{subarray}}\left(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}t,p\in\mathds{N}_{0}\\
0\leq p\leq t\leq i,j\\
i+j\leq t+n\end{subarray}}\alpha(i,j,t,p,a,k)x_{i,j}^{t,p}\right)_{i,j=k}^{n+a-k}\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\!\succeq 0\,.

(62)

Here
\alpha(i,j,t,p,a,k)

\displaystyle\alpha(i,j,t,p,a,k)

\displaystyle=3^{\frac{1}{2}\left(i+j\right)-t}\beta_{i-a,j-a,k-a}^{n-a,t-a}

\displaystyle\times\sum\limits_{g=0}^{p}2^{t-a-p+g}\left(-1\right)^{a-g}\binom{a}{g}\binom{t-a}{p-g}\,,

with

\displaystyle\beta_{i,j,k}^{m,t}

\displaystyle=\sum\limits_{u=0}^{m}\left(-1\right)^{t-u}\binom{u}{t}\binom{m-2k}{m-k-u}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\times\binom{m-k-u}{i-u}\binom{m-k-u}{j-u}\,.

(64)

Following Ref.
Munné et al. 2024

we relax the SDP of Eq. (

\displaystyle x^{t,p}_{i,j}

s.t.

\displaystyle x^{0,0}_{0,0}=1

\displaystyle x^{0,0}_{i,0}=x^{i,i}_{i,i},\quad\text{for}\quad i=1,\dots,n

\displaystyle x^{t,p}_{i,j}=0\quad\quad\text{if}\quad t-p\quad\text{is odd}\,,

\displaystyle\gamma^{i,i}_{i,i}x^{i,i}_{i,i}=\sum_{\begin{subarray}{c}E\in\mathcal{E}_{n}\\
\wt(E)=i\end{subarray}}\!\!\tr\big((E\otimes E^{\dagger})\Phi_{\textup{E8}}\big)\,,\quad i=1,\dots,n

\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\begin{subarray}{c}a,k\in\mathds{N}_{0}\\
0\leq a\leq k\leq n+a-k\end{subarray}}\left(\sum\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}t,p\in\mathds{N}_{0}\\
