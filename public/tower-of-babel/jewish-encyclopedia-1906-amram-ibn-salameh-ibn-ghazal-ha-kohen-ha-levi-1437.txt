Samaritan liturgical poet. A number of prayers by him are incorporated in a liturgy, a fragment of which is in the Bodleian Library at Oxford, England. They consist of hymns for the ten penitential days, for both the morning and the evening services, as well as liturgic poems for the seven days of the Feast of Tabernacles, morning and evening. Compare Neubauer, "Cat. Bodl. Hebr. MSS." No. 2537, p. 2.
