Topology of Plasma Wakefields Driven by Two-Color Laguerre–Gaussian Laser Pulses
Saumya Singh, Dinkar Mishra, Shivani Aggarwal, Bhupesh Kumar, Pallavi Jha

Abstract
Plasma wakefield excitation driven by two-color Laguerre–Gaussian laser pulses carrying orbital angular momentum is investigated analytically and through quasi-cylindrical particle-in-cell simulations. Using a perturbative framework together with the quasistatic approximation, the influence of the transverse laser mode structure on the longitudinal and transverse wakefields in an underdense plasma is examined in the weakly relativistic regime. The results show that drivers with finite azimuthal index produce reduced and less regular on-axis longitudinal wakefields compared to conventional Gaussian drivers. However, radial–longitudinal field distributions reveal that this reduction originates from a redistribution of the wakefield energy toward finite radii rather than a simple loss of wake excitation. Orbital-angular-momentum-carrying modes generate hollow and ring-shaped wake structures accompanied by strongly modified transverse electric fields and broader plasma density perturbations. Mixed Gaussian–Laguerre–Gaussian configurations exhibit intermediate behavior, combining weak on-axis acceleration with pronounced off-axis wake excitation. The study demonstrates that structured two-color laser drivers fundamentally modify the topology of plasma wakefields and provide an additional mechanism for controlling transverse plasma dynamics, off-axis acceleration, and angular-momentum-mediated wakefield structures in plasma-based accelerator schemes.

. The electromagnetic fields were expanded using

over transverse electron dynamics, structured acceleration, and angular-momentum-mediated
plasma interactions.

5  Conclusion

The present work combines analytical modeling and quasi-cylindrical particle-in-cell simulations to investigate plasma wakefield excitation driven by two-color Laguerre–Gaussian laser pulses carrying orbital angular momentum. Using a perturbative framework together with the quasistatic approximation, the longitudinal and transverse wakefields generated in an underdense homogeneous plasma are analyzed in the weakly relativistic regime. The results show that, unlike conventional Gaussian drivers, OAM-carrying laser pulses redistribute the wakefield energy from the propagation axis toward finite radii, producing hollow and ring-like wake structures accompanied by strongly modified transverse electric fields and structured plasma density perturbations.

The simulations further demonstrate that the wakefield topology depends strongly on the radial and azimuthal mode indices of the interacting laser pulses. Gaussian-dominated configurations generate the strongest and most coherent on-axis accelerating fields together with comparatively smooth density compression, whereas twisted LG modes produce weaker on-axis wakefields, broader density modulation, and pronounced off-axis accelerating regions. Mixed Gaussian–LG configurations exhibit intermediate behavior, combining residual on-axis acceleration with structured transverse wake excitation. These results confirm that orbital angular momentum modifies the spatial topology of plasma wake generation rather than simply reducing the coupling efficiency.

Minor differences between analytical and simulation results arise primarily from the approximations employed in the analytical treatment, including the quasistatic and weakly nonlinear assumptions, whereas the simulations self-consistently include kinetic effects, nonlinear plasma dynamics, diffraction, and mode coupling. Overall, the present study demonstrates that structured two-color LG laser pulses provide an additional degree of control over plasma wake excitation, offering possibilities for tailored wakefield topology, off-axis acceleration, transverse phase-space manipulation, and angular-momentum-mediated plasma dynamics relevant to advanced plasma-based accelerator concepts.
