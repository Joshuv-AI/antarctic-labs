FULL TEXT OF ARTICLE:

1. [Text] Maria Julio Hernandez, director of the Legal Assistance
Office of the Archbishopric, is the one who brainwashed the witness
of the murder of the six Jesuits and two women. .
2. The previous statement was made on 12 December by Colonel Orlando
Inocente Montano during a news conference at the Defense Ministry to
local and international reporters.

3. The witness of the massacre, Lucia Barrera de Cerna, was taken to
the United States where she underwent interrogations by the Federal
Bureau of Investigation [FBI].

4. The Salvadoran Government is very interested in the thorough
investigations that are being conducted by the Special Scientific
Investigation Forces of Spain, the FBI in the U.S., and Scotland Yard
of England.

5. According to a Washington dateline item transmitted on 11
December by the UPI news agency, San Salvador Archbishop Monsignor
Arturo Rivera y Damas accused the government of having brainwashed
Lucia Barrera de Cerna, the only witness in the murder of the six
Jesuits and two employees.

6. According to the FBI investigations, the witness has changed her
Story three times. This was determined by a lie detector test.
