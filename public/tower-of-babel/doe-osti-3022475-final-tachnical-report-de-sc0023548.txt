1. Key Research Accomplishments
 During this period, we investigated two
new qualitatively different magnetic
configurations of nonresonant stellarator divertor
(NRD). These two configurations are different
from the standard/proper magnetic configuration
of the nonresonant divertor which was published
in 2020 and 2022 [Punjabi 2020 and 2022]. We
have called these new configurations the hybrid
stellarator divertor, and the two-mode stellarator
divertor. We give a summary of key results for
these two configurations; see Sections A and B
below. During this period, some important new
properties of the NRD proper were discovered;
see Section C below.
