THE RECENT ASSASSINATIONS OF U.S.
CITIZENS IN EL SALVADOR HAVE NOT BEEN DONE BY THE RIGHT AS GENERALLY
ALLEGED IN THE U.S. PRESS, BUT RATHER WERE DONE BY LEFT WING
GUERRILLAS AS A PROVOCATION. @QMMMMMMB STATES THAT THE GUERRILLAS
WERE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE MURDERS OF BOTH THE U.S. MARYKNOLL NUNS

AND THE MORE RECENT MURDERS OF U.S. AGRARIAN ADVISORS IN HOPE

THAT THE KILLINGS WOULD BE BLAMED ON THE RIGHT AND WOULD UNDERMINE
U.S. SUPPORT FOR THE PRESENT SALVADORAN GOVERNMENT.

3. WHILE QQ STATEMENTS MAY CLEARLY BE SELF-SERVING FROM
THE RIGHTEST POINT OF VIEW, HE HAS NO HESITATION IN ADMITTING RIGHTEST
INVOLVEMENT IN OTHER TERRORIST ACTS. SPECIFICALLY, HE HAS STATED
THAT THE RIGHT WAS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE ASSASSINATION OF ARCHBISHOP
ROMERO, ROMERO'S ASSASSIN WAS RECRUITED
IN MIAMI, FLORIDA, AND FLOWN SALVADOR ON A SCHEDULED FLIGHT
OF TACA INTERNATIONAL AIRLINES. THE ASSASSIN'S WEAPON WAS SMUGGLED
ABOARD THE FLIGHT BY PWUK R I E T E, A SALVADORAN EXECUTIVE OF
TACA AND CLOSE RELATIVE OF TACA PRESIDENT PANCHOBALSAREDDE,
IN ADDITION, MONTALVO APPEARED TO HAVE PRIOR KNOWLEDGE OF THE
KIDNAPPING OF U.S. BUSINESSMAN DENNIS MC DONALD IN 1979.
