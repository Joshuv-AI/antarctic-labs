 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 NLR and Swift Solar, under a Technology Commercialization Fund award, will cooperate to
 incorporate NLR’s new recombination layer into current device architectures, optimize the new
 wide bandgap material and utilize it in tandem devices, and translate all lab scale work on rigid
 substrates to flexible substrates.

 Summary of Research Results:

 Task One. Incorporate new recombination layer into current device architectures

 The partners will combine their efforts to produce wide gap devices with lab scale, solution
    processing with (1) Voc≥1.20V, (2) PCE≥18% +/- 5% relative, (3) yeild≥75%, (4) T90 ≥ 1000
 hours of operation. Device metrics will be collected from a minimum of 5 device runs consisting
 of 10 or more substrates and 50 or more working devices; device runs will be conducted in at
 least 2 different labs and 2 different researchers to ensure reproducibility.

 The work completed under this CRADA has been documented in OSTI as “Flexible Perovskite-
 Perovskite PV for Mobile Power Applications: Final Technical Report”, OSTI ID: 2481051. A

 Task Two. Optimize the new wide bandgap material

 Together, the research staff from both Swift and NLR will produce recombination layer at lab
 scale <25nm thickness with no measurable halide migration through the barrier after 100 hours
 illumination at 85oC. Sheet resistance ≥1x108 Ω/▢ with through plane resistance that does not
 add measurable series resistance to the entire device stack as measured by the total series
 resistance of the tandem device.

 The work completed under this CRADA has been documented in OSTI as “Flexible Perovskite-
 Perovskite PV for Mobile Power Applications: Final Technical Report”, OSTI ID: 2481051. A

 Task Three. Utilize new wide bandgap material in tandem devices

 The goal of this task is to produce single junction wide gap devices 0.1 cm2 PCE ≥ 16% +/- 5%
 relative and yield ≥ 75%; 1 cm2 PCE ≥ 14.5% +/- 5% relative and yield ≥ 75% via vapor phase
 deposition

 The goal of this task is to produce single junction, wide gap, 1 cm2 devices on 5x5 cm substrates
 (4 to 6 devices per substrate), PCE ≥ 14.5% with <5% relative variation in PCE device to device
 and <10% relative variation substrate to substrate

 The parties will cooperate to produce recombination layer via spatial ALD while maintaining
 metrics from milestone #2

 The parties will produce single junction wide gap devices 0.1 cm2 PCE ≥ 16% +/- 5% relative
 and yield ≥ 75% via vapor phase deposition in Swift’s point source tool.

 The work completed under this CRADA has been documented in OSTI as “Flexible Perovskite-
 Perovskite PV for Mobile Power Applications: Final Technical Report”, OSTI ID: 2481051. A

 Task Four. Translate lab-scale work on rigid substrates to flexible substrates

 The parties will produce 3-cell tandem micromodule, >22% efficiency, >50% device yield, >T90
 after 1000 hours outdoor testing. Modules will be produced via vapor deposition using Swift’s
 deposition tools and fully encapsulated; encapsulation materials and architecture are being
 developed under separate funding streams and are not a part of this TCF.

 The parties will produce 5-cell tandem minimodule, 10x10cm, >21% efficiency, >50% device
 yield, >T90 after 1000 hours outdoor testing.

 The work completed under this CRADA has been documented in OSTI as “Flexible Perovskite-
 Perovskite PV for Mobile Power Applications: Final Technical Report”, OSTI ID: 2481051. A
