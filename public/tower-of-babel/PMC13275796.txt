Author Correction: Unexpected events and prosocial behavior: the Batman effect
Authors: Francesco Pagnini, Francesca Grosso, Cesare Cavalera, Valentina Poletti, Giacomo Andrea Minazzi, Anna Missoni, Laura Bogani, Mauro Bertolotti
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Mental Health Research 10.1038/s44184-025-00171-5, published online 03 November 2025

In the original manuscript, the percentages of prosocial behaviors detected in the two conditions were inconsistent between the text and Table 1, due to a material error in reporting the number of valid observations in the two conditions. Regarding Table 1, the original figure given for Total Observations in the Experimental condition was 68, this has now been corrected to 61. The original figure given for Prosocial Behavior was 60.29% and has now been corrected to 67.21%. Additionally in Table 1, the original figure given for Total Observations in the Control conditions was 70, this has now been corrected to 77. The original figure given for Prosocial Behavior was 41.42% and has now been corrected to 37.66%. Further, in the first line of the Results section, the original line incorrectly read “We conducted 138 observation sessions (70 control condition, 68 experimental)”. This has now been corrected to “We conducted 138 observation sessions (61 experimental condition, 77 control condition)”. This correction does not affect the results or the conclusions of the study.
