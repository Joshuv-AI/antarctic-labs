Dear Admiral Turner;

The Multinational Enterprise Club of the Wharton School would
be honored to have you address students of the school at a mutually con-
venient date during the forthcoming fall semester beginning in September.
Your presentation would be attended primarily by students pursuing their
Masters in Business Administration, with attendance ranging from app^oici-
mately 50 to 100 Individuals depending on the scheduling. You may wish
to speak on the role of intelligence in monitoring economic as well as
political trends around the world, although you are welcome to speak on a
topic of your choice.

Many influential corporate leaders visit the Wharton Scjiool
throughout the academic year. Last year's speakers included Reginald H.
Jones, Chairman of General Electric, and John R. Opel, President of IBM.
The present officers of the Multinational Enterprise Club also wish to
expose students at the Wharton School to heads of select government agencies
in view of the necessity for increased cooperation between the public and
private sectors.

The presentations normally start at 4:30 p.m. on Mondays through
Thursdays, and last 30 to 45 minutes, followed by a brief question and
answer session. Often our guests stay for cocktails or dinner at the
Faculty Club with the program generally ending by 8:30 p.m. We would be
delighted if you would also attend this reception, as it provides an excel-
lent opportunity for informal dialogue. A second alternative is a presen-
tation at 10:30 a.m. on a Tuesday or Thursday, followed by a luncheon if
your schedule permits.

I sincerely hope that you will decide to visit the Wharton School
this fall, and I look forward to hearing from you. I can be reached at
the following address: The World Bank, 1818 H Street N.W. , Room D-1302,
Washington, D.C. 20433. Thank you for your consideration.

Respectfully,
