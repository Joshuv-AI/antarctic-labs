SUBJ Venceremos Reports Rector’s Assassination

Full Text Superzone of Message. .

1 [Text] We have reports from the Salvadoran capital. Dr. Ignacio
Ellacuria, a priest and rector of the Jose Simeon Canas University [formal
name of the Central American university--fbis], has been assassinated by
Roberto D’Aubuisson’s deaths squads. We are trying to confirm this
report, which just arrived in Radio Venceremos. Father Ignacio Ellacuria,
rector of the Catholic university and a Jesuit, was assassinated by the
death squads led by ARENA [Nationalist Revolutionary Alliance -- FBIS] and
La Tandona [reference to the 1966 graduating class of the Captain Gerardo
Barrios Military Academy -- FBIS]. For years, Father Ellacuria has been
the target of a campaign of threats and false accusations by the top
military command, the newspaper EL DIARIO DE HOY, and ARENA.

2 We are trying to confirm this report. However, according to the
information we have received, he was machinegunned in his home. (endall)
