 This living document presents the Python package FETA. FETA computes observables resulting
 from the ssion process. This document provides the denition of these observables as well as the
 physics models that are implemented to compute them. Some of these models are used to determine
 the initial conditions of ssion fragments, e.g., the excitation energy E ∗ and spin distribution p(J, π)
 at scission for prompt decay, while others are related to the nuclear structure and decay properties
 of the fragments, e.g. the ground-state properties, level density and low-lying excitation spectrum,
 γ -strength functions and electromagnetic transitions, and neutron transmission coecients. The
 end goal for FETA is to enable users to substitute every one of these models by their own les
 providing these quantities.
