Characterization
Zachary Meehan

7/14/2026

Project Overview:

Over the duration of June 22nd to July 15th, I interned at Los Alamos National Laboratory within
ISR-2 on the Los Alamos Portable Pulser (LAPP) Program.

This program concerns a radar dish used for characterizing signal propagation through earth’s
ionosphere.

The bulk of my internship consisted of characterizing the delay of a Marx Bank Generator and
writing hardware-interface C/C++ code for the pulse-recording Digitizer System.

Experience:

 A. Marx Bank Generator:
 1. Characterized the trigger/timing chain of a 38-stage, ~30kV, 138.5 J Marx bank
 generator to nanosecond precision using an SRS DG535 pulse generator and
 oscilloscope. This included finding the delay of the coaxial cables, trigger-board, and
 trigger-transformer. This resulted in a fire-to-output delay of 830 ± 10 ns.
 2. Encountered RF Grounding issue preventing characterization of Marx Bank Delay
 with F250 resistor causing oscilloscope to shut off due to strong EM/Magnetic Fields,
 resulting in plans for further experimentation with a faraday cage.
 3. Instrumented with and fired the Marx Bank in 5-10 shot campaigns at ~8 kV under
 high-voltage IWD safety procedures.
 4. Designed a current-viewing-resistor mount and 52 dB attenuation chain to safely
 capture muti-kV discharge waveforms.
 5. Automated waveform analysis of firing-campaign oscilloscope data (rise-time
 detection, delay extraction, jitter statistics) in Python and C. Delivered these
 measured trigger-chain delays as a configuration file merged into the operations
 GitLab repository.

 6. Documented the system in engineering reports (architecture, clock integration,
 trigger validation, and bandwidth root-cause analysis) and contributed to project
 technical reports and weekly project reviews.

B. Digitizer System:
 1. Wrote embedded hardware C/C++ code for a multithreaded data-acquisition
 software (AlazarTech ATS-SDK) for two clock-synchronized 12-bit PCIe Gen3 digitizers.
 2. Designed applications within the AlazarTech software requiring high-speed
 continuous data capture beginning on a specific trigger from a pulse generator.
 3. Enabled the boards around a hardware abstraction layer with config-file-based
 sample rates, buffer counts, and trigger thresholds.
 4. Synchronized two 4-channel, 2 GS/s per-channel digitizer boards to absolute GPS
 time. This was done using a 10 MHz reference (SRS PRS10) and ATS SYNC 4X1G
 clock/trigger distribution, arming both boards simultaneously from a single sync
 pulse.
 5. Extensive debugging and code development utilizing agentic AI (Claude Opus).

C. Miscellaneous:
 1. Developed Python-based script to read default settings via a csv file to reset LAPP
 GUI after shots. Merged this script into the operations GitLab repository.
