Charmed hadronic decays provide a unique laboratory for studying weak and strong interactions in the non-perturbative regime of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD), which is inaccessible to perturbative methods. The copious decay products of charmed mesons also provide an ideal platform to investigate the nature of light mesons. The low-lying axial-vector meson
a_{1}(1260)
and the
scalar mesons below 1 GeV/
c^{2}
—
a_{0}(980),f_{0}(980)
, and
f_{0}(500)
—remain poorly understood. Their internal structures and even their basic properties continue to show significant experimental uncertainties
Navas et al. 2024
,
and all their decay channels are listed as
“seen” without quantitative information on their branching fractions (BFs).
The
a_{1}(1260)
and
a_{0}(980)/f_{0}(980)
masses are close to the strange meson pair thresholds:
K\bar{K}^{*}
for the former, and
K\bar{K}
the latter. Their widths suggest that both states may arise from a common underlying mechanism.
Theoretical studies
Hsiao et al. 2020
;
Yu et al. 2021
;
Hsiao et al. 2025
;
Drutskoy et al. 2002
;
Roca et al. 2005
;
Geng et al. 2009

;

;

have also shown that the
\bar{K}K^{*}
and
\bar{K}K
channels may
play essential roles in producing the
a_{1}(1260)
and
a_{0}(980)/f_{0}(980)
, respectively,
leading to their frequent interpretations as compact tetraquark or molecular states.

Understanding charmed meson decay dynamics has been significantly
improved through systematic amplitude analyses, which enable the extraction of
quasi-two-body decay parameters from multi-body final
states
Ablikim et al. 2024

Numerous

and

,

The intermediate process

An unbinned maximum likelihood method is adopted in the amplitude analysis. The fit probability density function is the sum of the signal amplitude and a background function, with their coefficients corresponding to their fractions. The signal amplitudes and background functions are described in the following.
The signal amplitude is parameterized with the isobar formulation in the covariant tensor formalism

The total signal amplitude
