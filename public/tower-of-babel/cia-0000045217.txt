EL SALVADOR: ANN CITED ON POSSIBILITY OF DUARTE-RESBEL TALKS
PA2@19@01

MANAGUA RADIO NOTICIAS IN SPANISH 1288 GMT 28 SEP 85

((EXCERPT)) AT THIS TIME WE ARE RECEIVING A REPORT FROM
SAN SALVADOR ISSUED BY THE AGENCIA NUEVA NICARAGUA NEWS
AGENCY.

EL SALVADOR, POSSIBLE NEGOTIATION BETWEEN PRESIDENT
DUARTE AND HIS DAUGHTER'S KIDNAPPERS.

SALVADORAN PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON DUARTE USED THE MEDIA
LAST NIGHT TO ASK THAT PUBLIC MESSAGES CONDEMNING THE KIDNAPPING
OF HIS DAUGHTER INES GUADALUPE 18 DAYS AGO STOP. THE REQUEST
WAS INTERPRETED HERE -AS INDICATING THE POSSIBILITY THAT
NEGOTIATIONS BETWEEN THE PRESIDENT AND HIS DAUGHTER’ S CAPTORS
MEMBERS OF THE PEDRO PABLO CASTILLO FRONT, HAVE BEGUN
EARLIER, THE FRONT ISSUED ITS FIRST PUBLIC MESSAGE, CLAIMING
RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE ACTION.

IN AN EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW WITH A U.S. NEWS AGENCY
CORRESPONDENT, THE REBELS SAID THAT ONE ({AS HEARD)) OF THE
CONDITIONS TO SEGIN NEGOTIATIONS FOR THE RELEASE OF INES GUADALUPE
DUARTE IS THAT THE ARMY SUSPEND ITS ACTIONS, AIR ANO GROUND
ACTIVITIES NATIONWIDE. THAT ARBITRARY ARRESTS CEASE, AND THAT
HUMAN RIGHTS BE RESPECTED.

PRESIDENT DUARTE’ 5 REQUEST CONSTITUTES A 180-DEGREE SHIFT LN
THE POLITICAL HANOSLING THE GOVERMENT INITIALLY MADE 8Y :
LAUNCHING MASSIVE MEDIA COVERAGE OF THE CASE.

HOWEVER, SEVERAL PRESS ANALYSTS HERE HAVE SPECULATED THAT
DUARTE MAY BE ENCOUNTERING DISAGREEMENTS WITH ,THE ARMY HIGH
COMMAND IN CONNECTION WITH THE PEDRO PABLO CASTILLO FRONT'S
DEMAND THAT POLITICAL PRISONERS BE FREED, IT APPEARS THAT THE
ORGANIZATION. ON WHICH NO MAJOR INFORMATION WAS AVAILABLE, IS
PRINCIPALLY STAFFED BY FORMER POLITICAL’ PRISONERS.

MEANWHILE, THE INTENSITY OF POLICE AND MILITARY SEARCH
OPERATIONS, HOUSE RAIDS, AND ARRESTS HAS DECREASED OVER THE FAST
SEVERAL HOURS, CONFIRMING THAT NEGOTIATIONS BETWEEN THE REBELS
AND GOVERNMENT MAY HAVE BEGUN. ( (PASSAGE ON BACKGROUND OF LNES
GUADALUPE DUARTE OMITTED) )
