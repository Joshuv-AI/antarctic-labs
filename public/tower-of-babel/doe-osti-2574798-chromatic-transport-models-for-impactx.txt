 Abstract

 This note describes the basic levels of Hamiltonian approximation (for straight-axis elements)
 that are used in the symplectic beam dynamics modeling code ImpactX. These include: purely
 linear models, models based on a paraxial (chromatic) expansion of the Hamiltonian, and models
 based on the exact nonlinear Hamiltonian. The focus here is on paraxial (chromatic) models.
