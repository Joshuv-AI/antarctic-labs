Spanish poet of Amsterdam. He translated from Hebrew into Spanish a Purim song, under the title "Cancio de Purim, Establecido Sobre su Historia, Echo por un Anonimo,y Ahora Nuevamente Sacado del Hebrayco al Espagnol,"Amsterdam, 1772. The poem contains 110 strophes of nine lines each.
