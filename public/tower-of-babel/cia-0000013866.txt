On 20 April 1965, while Source's unit made a temporary stop at LUU SON
Village, NGHE AN Province, Source went to the market at DO LUONG District
where he heard about the arrest of a U.S. Pilot with the rank of Senior
Lieutenant. The pilot had been captured at ANH SON District and was
being temporarily held in the DO LUONG District office (coordinates

WF 326986), :

Source was told that the main force troops and militia had difficulty
finding. the pilot in the forest at ANH SON District. He was captured

at a location far from the burning plane, The pilot was uninjured and
was carrying a pistol when taken prisoner by the militia, He was carried
by vwahicle to the DO LUONG District office and confined,

Source saw the pilot eitting on a chair,bareheaded and facing the crowd.

An armed sentry guarded the pilot from the people who could possibly become
angry and harm the prisoner. He was very young, about 23 years of age, tall,
blond short hair, square face and eyes "brass color". Since the time of

his capture the prisoner appeared calm and unafraid and kept chewing his
chewing Cum, The people believed he did not show any fear because he knew
that the NVN Government would not condemn him,

The local authorities treated the prisoner well, Evidently the prisoner
could not get used to the local rice food ration so he was given canned .
food, soup and beer. People would come to view the prisoner out of

' curiosity and anger. The people complained that the prisoner was treated
. too well and given special food while they did.not have enough rice.

Besides, the Americans had caused disaster to the NVN people and should
absolutely not receive any favors.
