2. THE SOVIET DIVISIONS FROM EAST GERMANY ARE. WITHDRAWING

FROM THE WESTERN BORDERS OF CZECHOSLOVAKIA AND POLISH UNITS WILL
HAVE WITHDRAWN FROM THE CSSR BY 12 OCTOBER. FIVE SOVIET DIVISIONS
WILL REMAIN NORTH OF PRAGUE. AT. PRESENT, THESE DIVISIONS FORM THE
38TH ARMY, WHICH IS NORMALLY. THE 28TH ARMY FROM GRODNO. THE
(ACTUAL) 38TH ARMY IS NOW IN SLOVAKIA. IT WILL BE REGROUPED AND
GARRISONED IN CZECHOSLOVAK .BARRACKS. THE CZECH FORCES WILL BE
REDUCED BY ABOUT 70,000 MEN, AND ELEMENTS WILL BE WITHDRAWN TO
SLOVAKIA. QD COMMENT: THE STATEMENT POSSIBLY MEANS THAT
CZECHOSLOVAK UNITS WILL BE WITHDRAWN FROM WESTERN CZECHOSLOVAKIA TO

SLOVAKIA.)
