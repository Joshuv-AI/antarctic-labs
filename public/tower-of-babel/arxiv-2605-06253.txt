Introduction

Let
G=(V,E)
be a graph. We introduce some standard notations used throughout this article. For a vertex

. We denote the subgraph induced by

as

. The length of the largest cycle in a graph

is called the

and the length of the smallest cycle is called the

and

is defined as the

where addition is taken modulo

and

is the minimum of the lengths of the arcs

and

Let

and

satisfies the property that either
\Gamma
contains

(
\Gamma\supseteq G
) or its complement contains
H
(
\overline{\Gamma}\supseteq H
) as subgraphs.
Let

be a connected graph and

be any graph satisfying

is the size of the smallest color class in a
\chi(H)
-coloring of

[
5

in 1981 provides a general lower bound on the Ramsey number of such pairs,

\displaystyle R(G,H)\geq(|G|-1)(\chi(H)-1)+\sigma(H).

We say

H
-good
if equality holds in the above inequality. The notion of

was introduced by Burr and Erdös
[
4
]
. Since then, there has been considerable study on the Ramsey goodness of different graph pairs. A

is the bipartite graph
K_{1,n}
. A
book
graph, denoted by
B_{n}
, is the graph consisting of
n
triangles sharing a common edge called the

. In graph Ramsey theory, star-cycle and book-cycle are two important and well-known graph pairs whose exact Ramsey value has been studied throughout the years. In 1973, Lawrence
[
10
]
obtained two exact values of the Ramsey number

Theorem 1

(Lawrence
[
10
]
)
.

\displaystyle R(K_{1,n},C_{m})

\displaystyle=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}2n+1&\textnormal{ if }&m\textnormal{ is odd and }m\leq 2n-1\\
m&\textnormal{ if }&m\geq 2n.\end{array}\right.

Note that the first result in the above theorem implies that
K_{1,n}
is
C_{m}
-good if

is odd and
m\leq 2n-1
. The later one establishes the

-goodness of

if
m\geq 2n
. Zhang et al.
[
14
]
and Allen et al.
[
2
]
respectively extended the study of star-cycle Ramsey number for the remaining parametric conditions. However, these results do not explicitly study the Ramsey goodness of the pair
(K_{1,n},C_{m})

A similar study has also been done on the Ramsey goodness of the pair

-good for odd

\displaystyle R(B_{n},C_{m})

\displaystyle=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lll}2n+3&\textnormal{ if }&m\geq 5\textnormal{ is odd and }n\geq 4m-13\\
2m-1&\textnormal{ if }&m\geq 2n+2.\end{array}\right.

The motivating observation is that by the deletion of the base edge from

Since

-good for odd

Theorem 3

(Gupta

If

In this article, we have explicitly studied the Ramsey goodness of the pair

Lemma 13

Theorem 0
.

If

Theorem 1

If

Proof.

The lower bound follows from Burr’s inequality. Therefore, it is enough to prove the upper bound. For that, let us consider a graph on
