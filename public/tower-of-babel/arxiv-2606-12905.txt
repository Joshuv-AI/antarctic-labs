The Belle detector was a cylindrical large-solid-angle magnetic spectrometer located at the interaction point (IP) of the KEKB
asymmetric-energy
e^{+}e^{-}

Belle:2000cnh

The Belle II detector, located at the SuperKEKB asymmetric-energy
e^{+}e^{-}
collider
Akai:2018mbz

version of the Belle detector. Belle II includes a SVD consisting of pixel sensors and double-sided strip detectors,
and a CDC. The CDC is surrounded by two types of Cherenkov light detector systems: time-of-propagation (TOP)
detector for the barrel region and an aerogel ring-imaging Cherenkov (ARICH) detector for the forward end-cap region.
The Belle ECL is reused in Belle II along with the solenoid and the iron flux-return yoke. However, the ECL readout system
has been upgraded. The solenoid flux return is instrumented with resistive-plate chambers
and plastic scintillator modules to detect muons and
K^{0}_{L}
mesons.
A detailed description of the Belle II detector can be found in Ref.
Belle-II:2010dht

It is estimated from simulation that after the BCS around 50% of the selected

\Delta E

M(\gamma\bar{\Xi}_{c}^{-,0})

The centers of the sideband regions are about
5\,\sigma

center of the signal region.
The sideband regions contain a significant SCF contribution,
with a shape in the
\Delta E
distribution similar to that observed in the signal region.
To better constrain the SCF component in the signal extraction,
we perform a simultaneous fit to the
\Delta E
distributions in the signal
and sideband regions of
M(\gamma\bar{\Xi}_{c}^{-,0})

B^{+}\to\Sigma_{c}(2455)^{++}\bar{\Xi}_{c}^{\prime-}

The likelihood functions used to model the
\Delta E

in the signal and sideband regions of

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}=\frac{e^{-(N_{\rm sig}+N_{\rm scf}+N_{\rm bkg})}}{N!}\prod_{i}^{N}

\displaystyle[N_{\rm sig}\mathcal{F}_{\rm sig}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle N_{\rm scf}\mathcal{F}_{\rm scf}+N_{\rm bkg}\mathcal{F}_{\rm bkg}]

and

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}^{\rm sb}=\frac{e^{-(N_{\rm sig}^{\rm sb}+N_{\rm scf}^{\rm sb}+N_{\rm bkg}^{\rm sb})}}{N^{\rm sb}!}\prod_{i}^{N^{\rm sb}}

\displaystyle[N_{\rm sig}^{\rm sb}\mathcal{F}_{\rm sig}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle N_{\rm scf}^{\rm sb}\mathcal{F}_{\rm scf}^{\rm sb}+N_{\rm bkg}^{\rm sb}\mathcal{F}_{\rm bkg}^{\rm sb}],

respectively. Here,
\mathcal{F}^{(\rm sb)}

N^{(\rm sb)}
denotes the number of observed events.
The signal PDF,

\mathcal{F}_{\rm bkg}^{(\rm sb)}

N_{\rm scf}^{(\rm sb)}
,
and
N_{\rm bkg}^{(\rm sb)}

The values of

and

and

SU(3)

\bar{\Xi}_{c}^{-}
and
\bar{\Xi}_{c}^{0}
