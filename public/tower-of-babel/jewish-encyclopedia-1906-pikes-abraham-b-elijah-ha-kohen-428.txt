German rabbi; mentioned in "Liḳḳuṭe Maharil," hilkots "Shabbat" and "Yom Kippur." He addressed two letters to the community of Halberstadt, in which he discussed the commandments and prohibitions. He requested that his epistles might be copied and read to others. These letters were printed at Basel in 1599.

Bibliography: Michael, Or ha-Ḥayyim, No. 42. E. C. S. O.
