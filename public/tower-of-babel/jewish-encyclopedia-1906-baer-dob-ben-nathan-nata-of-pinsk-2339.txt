Russian rabbi of the first half of the eighteenth century. He was a descendant of Rabbi Nathan Nata Shapira of Cracow (who was the author of "Megalleh 'Amukim"). Baer is the author of "Neṭa' Sha'ashuim," a commentary on some parts of the Talmud, Zolkiev, 1748.

Bibliography: Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 186, Warsaw, 1886. H. R.
