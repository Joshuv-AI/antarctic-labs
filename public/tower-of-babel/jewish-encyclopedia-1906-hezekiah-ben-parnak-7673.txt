Palestinian amora; lived at the end of the third century. The only mention of him is in Berakot 63a, in connection with the transmission of Johanan bar Nappaḥa's exegetical explanation of the fact that the section concerning the faithless wife (Num. v. 11-31) follows the section on the refusal of the priestly tithe ( ib. V. 5-11).
