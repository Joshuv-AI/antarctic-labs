LATIN AMERICA

Haiti Seeking Rapprochement With Cuba:

Haitian Ambassador Dorsinville, a member of Haiti's permanent
UN delegation who was in Geneva for meetings, had been in-
structed to approach his Cuban counterpart regarding Haiti's
desire to "normalize" relations between the two countries.
The Cuban reported to that Dorsin-
ville had assured him that "Haiti would make every effort to
convince us it will never adopt an anti-Cuban policy." When
chided by the Cuban for Haiti's lack of support in inter-
national meetings, Dorsinville -replied "“Everything.will be

better."

Comment: Haitian rapprochement with the Castro regime
would be consistent with the increasingly anti-US attitude of
the Duvalier regime, which appears to be coming under the
influence of a clique of extreme leftists close to the Presi-
dent. His current campaign to force the US to increase its
aid to Haiti has unleashed considerable anti-US feeling and
encouraged the expression of pro-Castro and pro-Communist
Sentiments. Haitian Foreign Ministry officials have implicit-
ly warned the US Embassy that Haiti's support for an OAS
foreign ministers' meeting on the Cuban problem should not
imply backing for any action against Cuba.

. &

Although diplomatic relations were not severed, each
country withdrew its missions from the other following the
abortive invasion of Haiti in August 1959 by a small group

from Cuba.
