Discussion - Attention was called to EDIC/AM-2 of 3 November
on this subject c This paper provided an outline which (it was proposed)
could serve as a basis for an EDIC study on the procedures by which
current economic defense intelligence (other than enforcement) is
furnished EDAC agencies 0 The need for such a study was first brought to
the attention of EDIC at its 30 September meeting and was prompted by
a G-2 inquiry concerning such procedures 0 The Acting Chairman reviewed
this outline and indicated that adequate procedures exist for transmittal
of enforcement intelligence through the DCN mechanisma He stated the
problem as that of determining how effective and timely is the distribution
of other current economic defense intelligence and of whether the needs of
action agencies have been adequately expressed to intelligence agencieso

Mrso Young and Mr„ Tilson, speaking for EDS and MDAC respectively ,
declared that their intelligence needs are adequately supplied 0 It
appears that Commerce and FOA also receive such information on a routine
basiSo Mr 0 Tilson added that it was assumed by MDAC that information of
this nature was brought to MDAC attention by the Economic Defense Division
of ORRo The Acting Chairman challenged this assumption, stating that
D/e/RR had no specific responsibility for providing such information to
action agencies 0

The Executive Secretary, recalling that earlier discussions of
the IWG Current Intelligence Subgroup had revealed that information
from the military services had not been regularly provided to action
agencies, inquired whether this situation now prevailed with regard to
other than enforcement intelligence 0 Lt„ Colo Brown replied that OSD
provides to its various representatives in the EDAC structure copies of
all intelligence reports provided by the services <, OSD assumes, further-
more, that the CIA reading panel distributes to action agencies intelligence

information to action agencies by OSD, therefore, is not considered
necessary o Cmdro Meyertholen qualified the remarks of the OSD member by
stating that ONI information is passed directly to action agencies 0

The Acting Chairman thought the OSD assumption fallacious since,

gence received from the services* He emphasized the responsibility of
the intelligence community to pass such material to action agencies but
cautioned that it was a mistake for action and policy agencies to leave
surveillance of incoming material entirely to the intelligence community*
