SUBJ

SAL VADORAN OFFICIALS REPORTEDLY NEGOTIATE KIDNAPPING IN PANAMA
PAZ12354

PARIS AFP IN SPANISH 2317 GMT 21° OCT 85

(CTEXT)} PANAMA CITY, 21 OCT (AFP) -- RELIABLE SOURCES
HAVE REPORTED HERE THAT ABRAHAM RODRIGUEZ AND ADOLFO REY
PRENOES, SALVADORAN FIRST PRESIDENTIAL DESIGNATE AND COMMUNICATIONS .
MINISTER, RESPECTIVELY, HAVE ARRIVED IN PANAMA CITY TO NEGOTIATE
THE RELEASE OF SALVADORAN PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON DUARTE’ S
DAUGHTER WITH GUERRILLA REPRESENTATIVES

IT HAS BEEN REPORTED THAT A SALVADORAN GOVERNMENT PLANE
WAS ABOUT TO ARRIVE AT ANY TIME TO PICK UP THE OFFICIALS.

MEANWHILE, MEMBERS OF THE PRESS ARE WAITING AT THE PANAMA CITY
AIRPORT FOR REY PRENDES, RODRIGUEZ, AND SAN SALVADOR ARCHBISHOP .
ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS TO DEPART. IT IS REPORTED THAT THE
ARCHBISHOP ALSO PARTICIPATED IN THE TALKS HELD TODAY AND YESTERDAY
IN A PANAMA CITY HOTEL.

IT HAS BEEN REPORTED THAT THE ARCHBISHOP ts ACCOMPANIED BY
IGNACIO ELLACURIA, RECTOR OF THE SAN SALVADOR CENTRAL AMERICAN
UNIVERSITY.

INES GUADALUPE DUARTE AND HER FRIEND ANA VILLEDA WERE
KIDNAPPED ON 18 SEPTEMBER. IN EXCHANGE FOR THEIR FREEDOM, THE
ABDUCTORS DEMAND THE RELEASE OF SEVERAL DOZEN POLITICAL PRISONERS,
