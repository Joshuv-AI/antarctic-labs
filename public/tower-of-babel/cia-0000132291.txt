Soviets Condemn Iraqi Intransigence

The Kremlin today said Baghdad has lost the chance to take
advantage of a political initiative and should "have the guts” to
withdraw from Kuwait, according to a spokesman for President
Gorbachev. The spokesman announced "regret" at the turn of
events but said his country would not condemn a ground war. He
concluded by responding to questions on US-USSR ties, saying he
aid not think the recent differences between Washington and
