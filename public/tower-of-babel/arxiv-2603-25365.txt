Localization of the clique spectral version of Zykov’s theorem
Changjiang Bu, Jueru Liu, Haotian Zeng

Abstract
Zykov’s theorem shows that r r -partite Turán graph uniquely has the maximum number of K t K_{t} among all n n -vertex K r + 1 K_{r+1} -free graphs for 2 ≤ t ≤ r 2\leq t\leq r . The clique tensor is a high-order extension of the adjacency matrix of a graph. Yu and Peng [ 26 ] gave a spectral version of the Zykov’s theorem via clique tensor. In this paper, we give some upper bounds on the spectral radius of the clique tensor of a graph, which can be viewed as the localizations of the spectral version of Zykov’s theorem.

For a positive integer

\mathcal{A}x^{k-1}

\left(\mathcal{A}x^{k-1}\right)_{i}=\sum\limits_{i_{2},\ldots,i_{k}=1}^{n}a_{ii_{2}\cdots i_{k}}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{k}}.

\mathcal{A}x^{k-1}=\lambda x^{[k-1]},

\mathcal{A}
associated with

A tensor

Let

[

Theorem 3.12
.

Let

be a graph with
t
-clique spectral radius
\rho_{t}(G)
and clique number
\omega

\left(\frac{\rho_{t}(G)}{t}\right)^{t}\leq\left(\sum_{I\in C_{t}(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{-t}}\right)^{t-1}.

Equality holds if and only if the graph obtained from

-cliques is a complete
w
-partite graph for
\omega=t
, or a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph for
\omega\geq t+1
(possibly with some isolated vertices).

Proof.

For the
t
-clique tensor
\mathcal{A}(G)
of the graph

, let

be an nonnegative eigenvector corresponding to
\rho_{t}(G)
with
x_{1}^{t}+\cdots+x_{n}^{t}=1
. Then

\rho_{t}(G)=\mathcal{A}(G)x^{t}=t\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)}x_{i_{1}}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{t}}.

Hőlder’s inequality shows that for two nonnegative vectors
x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})^{\mathsf{T}}
and
y=(y_{1},\ldots,y_{n})^{\mathsf{T}}
, if two positive number
p
and

\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1
, then
\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}y_{i}\leq\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}^{p}\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}y_{i}^{q}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}
,
the equality holds if and only if
x
and
y
are proportional.
Thus, we have

\displaystyle\rho_{t}(G)

\displaystyle=t\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}=I\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\frac{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t}}\left(\frac{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t}}x_{i_{1}}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{t}}

\displaystyle\leq t\left(\sum_{I\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\frac{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t-1}}\right)^{\frac{t-1}{t}}\left(\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}=I\in C_{t}(G)}\frac{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}x_{i_{1}}^{t}x_{i_{2}}^{t}\cdots x_{i_{t}}^{t}\right)^{\frac{1}{t}}.

Since

2.10

\rho_{t}(G)\leq t\left(\sum_{I\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\frac{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t-1}}\right)^{\frac{t-1}{t}}.

(3.1)

When

\rho_{t}(G)\leq t\left(\sum_{I\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\frac{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t-1}}\right)^{\frac{t-1}{t}}=|C_{t}(G)|^{\frac{t-1}{t}}.

By Lemma

\rho_{t}(G)=\rho_{t}(G^{\prime})=\left(\prod_{i=1}^{t}|V_{i}|\right)^{\frac{t-1}{t}}=|C_{t}(G)|^{\frac{t-1}{t}}

According to the proof above, by Hőlder’s inequality, equality in Eq. (

\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}=I\in C_{t}(G)}\frac{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}x_{i_{1}}^{t}x_{i_{2}}^{t}\cdots x_{i_{t}}^{t}=1

x_{i}>0

for

, we can get the following conclusion.

Corollary 3.14
.

Let
G
be a graph with
t
-clique spectral radius
\rho_{t}(G)
and clique number
\omega
and let
2\leq t\leq\omega
. Then

\left(\rho_{t}(G)\right)^{t}\leq t\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}c_{t}(v)\sqrt[t-1]{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{-t}}\right)^{t-1}.

Equality holds if and only if the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete
\omega
-partite graph for
\omega=t
, or a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph for
\omega\geq t+1
(possibly with some isolated vertices).

Proof.

Observe that for any
t
-clique
I=\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)
,
\alpha(I)\leq\alpha(i_{j})
for
j=1,\ldots,t
. Thus, by Theorem
3.12
, we have

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\rho_{t}(G)}{t}\right)^{t}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\sum_{I\in C_{t}(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(I)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(I)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{t^{t-1}}\left(\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}=I\in C_{t}(G)}\sum_{j=1}^{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{t^{t-1}}\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}c_{t}(v)\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}.

Next,we consider the equality. By Theorem
3.12
, the equality holds in the last inequality if and only if the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete
t
- partite graph for
\omega=t
, or a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph for
\omega\geq t+1
(possibly with some isolated vertices). In this condition, for any
t
-clique
I=\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)
,
\alpha(I)=\alpha(i_{j})=\omega
for
j=1,\ldots,t
, the equalities also hold in the further inequality, completing the proof.
∎

When
t=2
, Corollary
3.14
is a localized version of Wilf’s equality given in
[
14
]
.
The following result can provide a new upper bound on
t
-clique spectral radius based on
\alpha(v)
.

Theorem 3.15
.

Let
G
be an
n
-vertex graph with clique number
\omega
and let
2\leq t\leq\omega
. Then

\sum_{v\in V(G)}\frac{c_{t}(v)}{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{-t}}\leq\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{-t}}\right)^{t}.

Equality holds if and only if the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph (possibly with some isolated vertices).

Proof.

A specific form of Muirhead’s inequality states that for positive real number
z_{1},z_{1},\ldots,z_{m}
,

\sum_{k=1}^{m}\frac{1}{z_{k}}\leq\left(\sum_{k=1}^{m}z_{k}^{m-1}\right)\frac{1}{\prod_{j=1}^{m}z_{j}},

and equality holds if and only if
z_{1}=\cdots=z_{m}
.
Then, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{v\in V(G)}\frac{c_{t}(v)}{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t}\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\sum_{j=1}^{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}}\right)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{t}\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\left(\sum_{j=1}^{t}\frac{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}\right)\prod_{j=1}^{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle t\sum_{\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}\in C_{t}(G)}\left(\left(\frac{1}{t^{2}}\sum_{j=1}^{t}\frac{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}\right)\prod_{j=1}^{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}}\right).

Let
V(G)=\{v_{1},v_{2},\ldots,v_{n}\}
. We construct a vector

x=\left(\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v_{1})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v_{1})\right)^{t}}},\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v_{2})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v_{2})\right)^{t}}},\ldots,\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v_{n})}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v_{n})\right)^{t}}}\right)^{\mathsf{T}}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}.

And let
\mathcal{W}(G)=(w_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{t}})
be the weight
t
-clique tensor of
G
with
w_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots t_{t}}=\omega_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{t}}\frac{1}{(t-1)!}
if
\{i_{1},i_{2},\ldots,i_{t}\}
is a
t
-clique in
G
and
w_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots t_{t}}=0
otherwise, where
\omega_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{t}}=\frac{1}{t^{2}}\sum_{j=1}^{t}\frac{\left(\alpha(i_{j})\right)^{t}}{\binom{\alpha(i_{j})}{t}}
.
Then

\displaystyle\sum_{v\in V(G)}\frac{c_{t}(v)}{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\leq\mathcal{W}(G)x^{t}.

Let
y=\frac{x}{||x||_{1}}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}_{+}
. Then
||y||_{1}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}y_{i}=1
. By Lemma
2.11
, we have

\mathcal{W}(G)x^{t}=\mathcal{W}(G)y^{t}\cdot||x||_{1}^{t}\leq||x||_{1}^{t}.

Thus,

\sum_{v\in V(G)}\frac{c_{t}(v)}{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\leq\mathcal{W}(G)x^{t}\leq||x||_{1}^{t}=\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t}.

Next, we consider the equality in the above inequality.
If the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph, we will verify that equality holds.
Without loss of generality, assume that
\omega|n
and
G
is a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph.
Then
\alpha(v)=\omega
and
c_{t}(v)=\binom{\omega-1}{t-1}\left(\frac{n}{\omega}\right)^{t-1}
for any
v\in V(G)
.
Therefore, we can proof that the equality holds.

Conversely, if equality holds, then by Lemma
2.11
,
G[\mathrm{supp}(y)]
is a complete
\omega
-partite graph with partition
V_{1},V_{2},\ldots,V_{\omega}
satisfy
\sum_{v\in V_{i}}y_{v}=\frac{1}{\omega}
for all
i\in[\omega]
.
By Muirhead’s inequality, we know that
\alpha(v)=\omega
for each
v
contained in
t
-cliques.
Hence, for any
i\in[\omega]
,

\frac{1}{\omega}=\frac{1}{||x||_{1}}\sum_{v\in V_{i}}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}=\frac{|V_{i}|}{||x||_{1}}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\omega}{t}}{\omega^{t}}},

i.e.,
|V_{1}|=|V_{2}|=\cdots=|V_{\omega}|
.
Consequently, the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph.
∎

By the above theorem, a new upper bound for
\rho_{t}(G)
is given as follows. When
t=2
, this is the conclusion given in
[
14
]
, which serves as a local version of Wilf’s theorem.

Corollary 3.16
.

Let
G
be a graph with
t
-clique spectral radius
\rho_{t}(G)
and clique number
\omega
and let
2\leq t\leq\omega
. Then

\rho_{t}(G)\leq t\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{-t}}\right)^{t-1}.

Equality holds if and only if the graph obtained from
G
by deleting edges not contained in
t
-cliques is a complete regular
\omega
-partite graph (possibly with some isolated vertices).

Proof.

By Corollary
3.14
and Theorem
3.15
, we have

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\rho_{t}(G)}{t}\right)^{t}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{t^{t-1}}\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}c_{t}(v)\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\frac{c_{t}(v)}{t}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle\leq\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t(t-1)}.

By Corollary
3.14
and Theorem
3.15
, we can characterize all graphs attaining equalities in the above inequalities.
∎

2.7

Remark 3.19
.

From Corollary
3.18
, by Hőlder’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle|C_{t}(G)|

\displaystyle\leq n\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}\sqrt[t-1]{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle\leq n\left(\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}1^{\frac{t-1}{t-2}}\right)^{\frac{t-2}{t-1}}\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{t-1}}\right)^{t-1}

\displaystyle=n^{t-1}\left(\sum_{v\in V(G)}{\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}}\right),

which is a vertex-based localized Zykov’s inequality given in
[
1
]
.
By Hőlder’s inequality, equality holds in the second inequality if and only if
\frac{\binom{\alpha(v)}{t}}{\left(\alpha(v)\right)^{t}}=c
for each vertex
v
contained in
t
-cliques and some constant
c>0
, which is equivalent to the order of the largest clique containing each vertex in
G
is equal.
Thus, if there exist two vertices

]
