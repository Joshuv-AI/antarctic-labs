President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (1 September 1944, d47)

top secret priority

[Hyde Park,] 1 September 1944.

Number 615. Personal and Top Secret. From the President for the Prime Minister.

Perfectly delighted that Clemmie will be with you. Eleanor[2] will go with me to Octagon.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House Map Room; forwarded to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Mrs. Roosevelt.
