Hungarian journalist and politico-economic writer; born at Budapest Oct. 21, 1848. He studied in Budapest and Paris. Since 1870 he has worked with the "Pester Lloyd," the economic articles in which come from his pen. His most important writings are: "Fünfundzwanzig Jahre Ungarischer Volkswirtschaft und Finanzen" and "Magyar Vásárok Története" (History of the Hungarian Fairs).

Bibliography: Szinnyei; Magyar Irók Tard; Pallas Lexicon, iv. S. M. W.
