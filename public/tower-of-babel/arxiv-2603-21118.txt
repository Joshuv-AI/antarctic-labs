Buchstaber, Ochanine, Krichever, and Witten genera
Mikhail Kornev

Abstract
We introduce a new class of formal group laws whose modulus square construction yields Buchstaber’s family of polynomials. This class is related to, but does not coincide with, the family of formal group laws associated with the Krichever genus. We compute the values of the corresponding Hirzebruch genus on theta divisors and complex projective spaces, describe its relation to the Ochanine, Krichever, and Witten genera, and show how this construction gives examples not arising from Hirzebruch’s elliptic genera of level n n .

Introduction

In
[
8
]
, a family of polynomials

\Bb_{\bm{a}}(z;x,y)=(x+y+z-a_{2}xyz)^{2}-4(1+a_{3}xyz)(xy+yz+xz+a_{1}xyz),

(1)

where

, was introduced. It was shown that each polynomial defines a two-valued formal group
\G_{\bm{a}}
,
universal in the class of two-valued formal groups obtained by the modulus square construction (see
[
12
]
) from the class of formal groups

\Ff(u,v)=\frac{u^{2}\lambda_{1}(v)-v^{2}\lambda_{1}(u)}{u\lambda_{2}(v)-v\lambda_{2}(u)},

(2)

where
\lambda_{k}(u)\in A[[u]],\,k=1,2
; the ring
A
has a rather complicated structure (see
[
4
]
), but

with
\deg b_{i}=-2i
,
i=1,\ldots,4
, and
\wp(z)=\wp(z;g_{2},g_{3})
is the Weierstrass function satisfying the equation

(\wp^{\prime}(z))^{2}=4\wp(z)^{3}-g_{2}\wp(z)-g_{3}
.

The class of groups (
2
) contains the formal groups corresponding to the Todd genus, the signature,
the Ochanine (also elliptic) genus
[
22
]
, and the Krichever genus. In
[
8
]
, it was proved that the class
\Ff(u,v)
is a realization of the class of Krichever formal groups (see
[
17
]
) with exponential

f_{\Kr}(x)=\frac{e^{\alpha x}}{\Phi(x,z)},\quad\text{where}\quad\Phi(x,z)=\Phi(x,z;g_{2},g_{3})=\frac{\sigma(z-x)}{\sigma(z)\sigma(x)}e^{\zeta(z)x}

(3)

is the Baker–Akhiezer function, and
\sigma(z)=\sigma(z;g_{2},g_{3})
,
\zeta(z)=\frac{d}{dz}\log\sigma(z)
are Weierstrass functions (see, for example,
[
18
]
,
[
23
]
).

In the present paper, a new class of formal groups

F_{\Bc}(u,v)=\left[\left(\frac{u\sqrt{Q(v)}-v\sqrt{Q(u)}}{u^{2}-v^{2}}\right)^{2}+a_{3}u^{2}v^{2}\right]^{-1/2},

(4)

where
Q(t)=1-a_{1}t^{2}+a_{2}t^{4}-a_{3}t^{6}
is introduced, whose modulus square is given by the family of polynomials (
1
). The formal group law
F_{\Bc}(u,v)
belongs to the family of formal group laws (
2
) if and only if
a_{3}=0
. In the case of
a_{3}=0
, we get the Ochanine genus. Therefore, for
a_{3}\neq 0
, the corresponding genus is distinct from the elliptic genera of level
n
introduced by Hirzebruch (see
[
15
, Appendix III, Section 1]
).

The paper is organized as follows.

In Section
2
, we recall the basics of the theory of two-valued formal groups. Section
3
is devoted to the central result of our paper, Theorem
2
. Corollary
2.1
says that the formal group (
4
) defines a Hirzebruch genus called the
Buchstaber genus
. As applications, we compute its values on the smooth theta divisors and complex projective spaces. It turns out that the values
\Bc(\Theta_{n})
belong to the ring
\Z[a_{1},a_{2},a_{3}]
, see Proposition
4
. In Propositions
5
and
6
, we compare the Buchstaber, Ochanine, Krichever and Witten genera.

This paper develops ideas from the note “Formal Groups and Buchstaber Genus,” to appear in Uspekhi Matematicheskikh Nauk.

In

\textstyle{(\eta_{1}\oplus\overline{\eta}_{1})\otimes_{\Cc}(\eta_{2}\oplus\overline{\eta}_{2})\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}

\textstyle{\CP^{\infty}\times\CP^{\infty}\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces\ignorespaces}
\scriptstyle{\iota\times\iota}

There is an isomorphism of complex vector bundles:

Note that the bundles

\xi_{2}:=\eta_{1}\overline{\eta}_{2}\oplus\overline{\eta}_{1}\eta_{2}

Let
\Pic(M)

[\xi]

\sigma:[\xi]\mapsto[\overline{\xi}]

are identified with unordered pairs

. We get the 2-valued coset group (see

[\xi,\overline{\xi}]\ast[\eta,\overline{\eta}]=[[\xi\eta,\overline{\xi}\overline{\eta}],[\xi\overline{\eta},\overline{\xi}\eta]]

and neutral element
[\mathbf{1}_{\Cc},\mathbf{1}_{\Cc}]

\mathbf{1}_{\Cc}
denotes a class of a trivial complex line bundle.

Note that
\zeta_{1}\otimes_{\Cc}\zeta_{2}
cannot admit a quaternionic structure (an exercise in representation theory). But the bundle
\zeta_{1}\otimes_{\Cc}\zeta_{2}\otimes_{\Cc}\zeta_{3}
over

does admit one. A natural isomorphism

This two-valued group is a geometric realization of a more general construction:

Proposition 1
.

Let
G
be an abelian group and
\sigma:g\mapsto-g
an involution. Denote by
X
the orbit space

with points

. Then
X
carries an involutive commutative two-valued coset group structure with operation

[g,-g]\ast[h,-h]=[[g+h,-g-h],[g-h,-g+h]]

with neutral element
[e,e]
, and

is a unit.

This follows directly from the coset construction, see
[
9
, Section 6]
.
∎

The Buchstaber Genus

A central result of this paper is the following

Theorem 2
.

(i)

Let

Corollary 2.1
.

The formal group
F_{\Bc}(u,v)
corresponds to a Hirzebruch genus

\Bc:\Omega_{\Uu}\to\Z\!\left[\frac{1}{2},a_{1},a_{2},a_{3}\right],

which we call the Buchstaber genus, where
\deg a_{i}=-4i,\ i=1,\ldots,3
.

In
[
11
]
, it was established that the exponential of the universal formal group of complex cobordism is given by the series

f_{\Uu}(u)=u+\sum_{n\geq 1}[\Theta_{n}]\frac{u^{n+1}}{(n+1)!},
