CARTOGRAPHY DIVISION

Over the past few months , there has been a gradual increase in work
assigned to the Chart and Graph Section of the Division. This has necessi-
tated the temporary transfer of people in order to keep current with the
work. If this trend continues, it will be necessary to re-evaluate the
responsibilities and organization of this unit.

EUROPE-AFRICA COMPILATION BRANCH

Several NIS Chapter I maps have been completed recently and
it is suggested that the D/GC Staff conduct a critical review of
this "best seller" with the idea of improvement. A few suggestions
have arisen informally - none of which would basically alter the
standard specifications now in use but might contribute to the
refinement of this particular map series.

Chief, GC/E and Asst. Chief, GC/E, have discussed the possibility
of showing only one type of international boundary symbol particularly
on classified maps and perhaps on all maps carrying a boundary disclaimer.
A formal suggestion with arguments will be presented at some future
D/GC staff meeting.

It is suggested that the terrain disclaimer appearing on D/GC
maps be deleted on the basis that terrain rendering is not unlike other
items which also are subject to map scale limitations. It is felt that
the presence of a disclaimer tends to discourage the reader from "taking
a second look", - a second look which it justly deserves.

DEVELOPMENT AND CONSTRUCTION BRANCH

The pressure of work in the Chart and Graph Section is gradually
increasing each month. It Is hoped, however, that adequate support will
be supplied by the Drafting Section if work in that Section continues at
its present pace.

USSR-Satellites BRANCH

The branch has taken the initiative also in producing an index to
existing OCI base maps . There seems to be a good demand for this map series
but the absence of a map index has created considerable inconvenience,
particularly on rush projects suitable for this map format. This index
will be produced in limited quantity by photostat or photography.

GEOGRAPHY DIVISION

The USSR Branch received a memorandum from St/l listing allegedly
delinquent evaluations for the month of December. A check of their records
showed that of the four listed, three had been completed and forwarded on the dates
due and the reamining one was not due until 9 January. Discussion with St/l
revealed the fact that our evaluations were beginning to arrive at least two
weeks late, due to delays in transit. St/l will seek satisfactory solution to
this problem.

The Far East Branch reports as follows: The OCI article on "The Impact on
Southeast Asia of the Coalition Government in Laos" (OCI Weekly Summary of
21 November 1957) , in addition to being "timely", contined much that was zoX I Aaa

"geographic" in nature. The fact that it appeared in print while

The Coordination Staff reports that it is becoming increasingly obvious
to the CIA components that contribute to the NIS Program that there is need for
representation on the NIS Committee for the CIA producing units. The initiation
and development of the Chapter I Annuals has highlighted this need. The arguments
are twofold: 1. The viewpoints of the producing -units are not at present taken
into consideration in Committee discussions; and 2. Action taken by the
Committee is not relayed promptly to the CIA components (notification is through
distribution of the minutes, which are sometimes delayed as much as three
weeks). A cast in point, minutes of the 17 December meeting of the NIS Committee

GEOGRAPHY DIVISION (Cont'd)

EDITING AND REVIEW STAFF

The biggest problem now facing GG/R is the delay in issuing reports
after they have been edited. Typing has been held up, largely as a result
of Christmas leave; 4 reports are now waiting typing. Maps have been an
even greater cause of delay. MB- 53 and GR’s 162, 1 64, and 167 are being
held up in Reproduction because maps have not been completed (GR-162,
since 22 October); and GR-119 has been dittoed and assembled but cannot
be issued until the map is finished.

A number of dittoed reports that have been edited by GG/R are
being issued without notifying GG/R of the fact, which complicates record
keeping. Furthermore, changes have been made in the format and a note
was added to all UW reports without notifying GG/R. All of the changes
are legitimate, but the editors should know about them in advance in order
to avoid giving analysts instructions that conflict with those of the
Front Office. The editors also suggest that the approval sheet that
accompanies all reports be modified slightly. Another item should be
added at the end of the approval column — which might read "Reproduced
Copy Approved by Editors". In this way, the editors would automatically
see all dittoed reports immediately before they are released for distribution.

The editors would still like to have a clarification of the
definition of "memorandum", especially "intelligence memorandum".
