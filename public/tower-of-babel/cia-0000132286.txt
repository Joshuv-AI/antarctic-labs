IRAQ-KUWAIT: SITUATION REPORT #595

More Reactions to the Soviet Initiative

Overnight press has continued to feature international
reactions to the Soviet Union’s peace proposal and Iraq's
apparent acceptance of it.

o The European Community will take a cautious approach to
the Soviet peace initiative, according to Luxembourg Foreign
Minister Poos. The EC feels the proposal focuses solely on Iraqi
withdrawal and ignores the other UN resolutions.

o German Foreign Minister Genscher said Iraq’s acceptance
of the Soviet proposal increases the chance for peace in the
Gulf, but added that the plan needs clarification on several
important points.

o Italian Prime Minister Andreotti called the Soviet plan
generally positive but said several points in the proposal needed
to be "fine-tuned."

o Canadian External Affairs Minister Joe Clark called the
Soviet plan “promising"™ but said that more negotiations are
needed.

© Egypt’s Ambassador to the United Nations called Iraq’s
agreement to the Soviet initiative “an important first step."

© Japanese Foreign Minister Nakayama said the Japanese
government cannot immediately welcome the peace proposal and
expects that more talks will be held.

© Australian Prime Minister Hawke welcomed the possibility
ef an Iraqi withdrawal from Kuwait but acknowledged that the
Soviet proposal leaves significant uncertainties about the timing
and specifics of a cease-fire and the monitoring of the
withdrawal.

o Worwegian Foreign Minister Stoltenberg said Oslo is
positive Iraq seems prepared to withdraw from Kuwait and is ready

to help the United Nations carry out a peace plan. (ag
