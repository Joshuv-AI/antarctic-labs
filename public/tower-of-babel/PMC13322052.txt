Published: 2026

In this article the statement 'Yunnan Beisite (Yunnan Bestay) Biotechnology Co., Ltd. participated in this study as a collaborating partner of the Department of Blood Transfusion, The First People's Hospital of Yunnan Province' was missing in the Funding section. The correct Funding statement should have been 'All procedures conformed to the national Regulations on the Administration of Laboratory Animals and the Guidelines for Ethical Review of Laboratory Animal Welfare, and were approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (Approval No. BST-PZ-MICE-20251014-01). Yunnan Beisite (Yunnan Bestay) Biotechnology Co., Ltd. participated in this study as a collaborating partner of the Department of Blood Transfusion, The First People's Hospital of Yunnan Province'.

The original article has been corrected.
