Talmudist of the seventeenth century. He was the author of "Petaḥ 'Enayim," an index to Biblical passages found in the Zohar and Tiḳḳunim (Cracow, 1647); republished with the Zohar (Sulzbach, 1684). He also revised (1619) the "'En Ya'aḳob" of Jacob b. Ḥabib.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 962. L. G. M. Sel.
