1. Son of Jogli, prince of the tribe of Dan, who represented his tribe in the division of the land (Num. xxxiv. 22). 2. Son of Abishua' and father of Uzzi, a priest, the fourth in line from Aaron (I Chron. v. 31), and ancestor of Ezra (Ezra vii. 4). In the Apocrypha his name is given as Boccas (I Esd. viii. 2) and Borith (II Esd. i. 2).
