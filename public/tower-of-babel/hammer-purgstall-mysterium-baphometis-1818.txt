Mysterium Baphometis Revelatum (Joseph von Hammer-Purgstall, 1818)
======================================================================
Hammer-Purgstall's 1818 memoir in Fundgruben des Orients — the work that invented the modern Templar-idolatry/Baphomet thesis, arguing from trial records and artifacts that the Templars worshipped a Gnostic-Ophite idol. Latin with German notes. The single most consequential Templar-mythography text. Public domain. Sourced from Internet Archive: scan of the 1818 edition (https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_LrFEAAAAcAAJ).
