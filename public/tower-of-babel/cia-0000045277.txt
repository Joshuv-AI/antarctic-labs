EL SALVADOR: FMLN PROMISES NOT TO HARM OFFICIALS’ RELATIVES

PA262138
SAN SALVADOR LA PRENSA GRAFICA IN SPANISH 26 OCT 85
PP 4, 39
C(TEXT)) THE CONTENTS OF THE AGREEMENT SIGNED IN PANAMA 8Y

DELEGATES OF THE GOVERNMENT AND OF THE TERRORISTS COULD ‘HELP SOLVE
THE SITUATION OF VIOLENCE. : :

ONE OF THE POINTS IN THE AGREEMENT SAYS THE SUBVERSIVES HAVE
COMMITTED THEMSELVES NOT TO HARM THE RELATIVES OF OFFICIALS,
MILITARYMEN, OR FOLITICIANS INVOLVED IN THE CONFLICT.

FOLLOWING BASIC PRINCIPLES OF UNIVERSAL LEGAL NORMS, THE
RELATIVES OF PERSONS INVOLVED IN EVENTS, SITUATIONS, AND
CIRCUMSTANCES SHOULD NOT BE ATTACKED, KIDNAPPED, CAPTURED,
WOUNDED, OR THEIR DIGNITY OFFENDED., THE FMULN-FDOR
( REVOLUTIONARY DEMOCRATIC FRONT?) SUBVERSIVES, THROUGH THE
AGREEMENT SIGNED BY THEIR DELEGATES, WILL NO LONGER KIDNAP OR
ATTACK RELATIVES OF MILITARYMEN OR PUBLIC OFFICIALS.

IN THIS REGARD, MSGR ARTURO RIVERA Y DAMAS MADE COMMENTS
ON THE CASE TO THE COMMUNICATION MEDIA, INDICATING ‘THAT THE
AGREEMENT SIGNED IN PANAMA WILL SRING MAINT BENEFITS WITHIN THE
PACIFICATION PROCESS.

WE CONSULTED GOVERNMENT SOURCES CONCERNING THE GENERAL
CONTENTS OF THE AGREEMENT SIGNED IN PANAMA AND
SPECIFICALLY THE POINT CONCERNING THE COMMITMENT TO RESPECT .
DEFENSELESS PERSONS, AND THEY TOLD US THAT “THEY WILL BELIEVE
IT WHEN THE SEE IT."

THEY ALSO COMMENTED THAT THE FMLN-FOR TERRORISTS DO NOT
RESPECT AGREEMENTS OR ANYTHING, NOR DO THEY MAINTAIN ANY ETHICS
CONCERNING THE COUNTRY’S SITUATION.

CONCERNING THE KIDNAPPING OF MRS INES GUADALUPE DUARTE °
DURAN ANDO MISS ANA CECILIA VILLEDA SOSA, THE GOVERNMENT
SPOKESMEN SAID THAT IY WAS NOT UNTIL YESTERDAY THAT THE FMLN
CLAIMED FULL RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE KIDNAPPING. .
