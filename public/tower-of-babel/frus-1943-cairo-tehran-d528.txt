The President to the Secretary of State[1] (23 December 1943, d528)

Washington, December 23, 1943.

Memorandum for the Secretary of State

This[2] I think should be taken up by Winant with Prime Minister Churchill as soon as the latter gets back. It was not brought up in any way at Teheran in my presence.

F. D. R.

Source: 740.00119 EW 1939/2057½

Notes
[1] A paraphrase of this memorandum was cabled by Hull to Winant on December 24, 1943.
[2] The matter referred to in the preceding document.
