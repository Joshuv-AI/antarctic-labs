1. THIS IS A LAOS SITUATION REPORT PREPARED BY CAS AT 2188
HOURS LOCAL TIME ON 1 FEBRUARY. SOURCE: QUALIFIED AMERICAN OBSERVER.
2. THE AGREEMENT REACHED THIS MORNING BETWEEN THE LEADER
OF THE REVOLUTIONARY GROUP, COLONEL BOUNLEUT SAYCOCIE AND THE
GENERAL STAFF UNDER THE SUPERVISION OF PRIME MINISTER SOUVANNA
PHOUMA STARTED FALLING APART LATE IN THE AFTERNOON. COLONEL
BOUNLEUT DID WITHDRAW HIS TROOPS FROM SEVERAL POINTS IN VIENTIANE
BUT SENT THEM TO WATTAY AIRPORT OLD PASSENGER TERMINAL AREA
WITHOUT PRIOR COORDINATION. IT IS ESTIMATED THAT COLONEL
BOUNLEUT HAS APPROXIMATELY THREE COMPANIES THERE. HE ALSO
FAILED TO ORDER WITHDRAWAL OF HIS TROOPS FROM THE LAO NATIONAL
RADIO STATION WHICH HIS TROOPS CONTINUE TO CONTROL. WHEN FAR
LEADERSHIP ATTEMPTED TO CONTACT HIM FOR AN EXPLANATION OF THESE
MOVES THEY WERE UNABLE TO FIND HIM.

3. THE GENERAL STAFF IS INSISTENT THAT COLONEL BOUNLEUT'S
TROOPS, INCLUDING THOSE AT THE RADIO STATION, WITHDRAW COMPLETELY
FROM VIENTIANE BY TONIGHT BUT IT IS NOT CLEAR WHAT THEY INTEND
TO DO IF THEIR ULTIMATUM IS IGNORED. THE GENERAL STAFF OF THE
LAO ARMED FORCES DISPOSES OF AT LEAST FOUR BATTALIONS WITHIN
VIENTIANE; ONE IN THE VICINITY OF WATTAY AIRPORT, ONE AT THE g
ORDANCE DEPORT, ON IN THE VICINITY OF THE SIGNAL ENGINEER
DEPOT AND ONE AT THE PALACE GROUNDS, WHERE IT [5 PROVIDING
PROTECTION TO THE MEMBERS OF THE GENERAL STAFF. THE MAJOR IN
COMMAND OF BOUNLEUT’S TROOPS AT WATTAY AIRPORT HAS REQUESTED
FROM THE GENERAL STAFF PERMISSION TO BED HIS TROOPS THERE
DURING THE.NIGHT. THE GENERAL STAFF HAS NOT ANSWERED THE
REQUEST. ;
4. IN THE MEANTIME, DIRECIOR GENERAL OF NATIONAL POLICE
SIHO LANPHOUTHAKOUL BROUGHT INTO VIENTIANE BETWEEN ONE AND THREE
BORDER PATROL COMPANIES TO REINFORCE THE POLICE CAMP AT PHON KENG
ON THE OUTSKIRTS OF VIETIANE. THE GENRAL STAFF OF THE LAO
ARMED FORCES HAS NOT BEEN IN CONTACT WITH SIHO OR WITH GENERAL
PHOUMI NOSAVAN SINCE LATE AFTERNOON. THERE ARE NO INDICATIONS OF
POLICE INTENTIONS FOR THIS NIGHT. BOUNLEUT’S TROOPS AT WATTAY
AIRPORT HAVE STATED THAT THEY EXPECT REINFORCEMENTS FROM THE
SECOND MILITARY ZONE, BUT THE GNERAL STAFF HAS NO INFORMATION
INDICATING THAT ANY REINFORCEMENTS ARE MOVING IN FROM THE SECOND
ZONE. ;
5. THIS EVENING THERE IS A PLATOON OF ARMORED CARS AT THE
AIRPORT AND ARMORED CARS AT THE PALACE, ALL OF WHICH BELONG
TO THE FIFTH MILITARY ZONE BASED AT CAMP CHINAIMO. A CURFEW HAS BEEN
IMPOSED ON ALL UNAUTHORIZED MOVEMENT IN VIENTIANE FROM 2500L
TO 26d8L. -
