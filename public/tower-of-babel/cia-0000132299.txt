0600 EST

IRAQ-KUWAIT: GITUATION REPORT #608

Baddasw Announces Withdrawal

In a radio address early today, Saddam Husayn said his army
would "continue its withdrawal from Kuwait and complete it on
this day." Saddam lauded his forces for thelr "heroic combat"
against the coalition and declared, "you hava won ... you are
victorious." He avoided explicitly renouncing his annexation of
Kuwait, elusively stating that Kuwait was a part of Iraq from 2
August to 24 February, when the withdrawal began. Saddam made no
explicit reference to any UN resolution.

Soviet Bxegsasis of Saddanm’s Spsech

Some 20 minutes after the conclusion of Saddam’s speech,
Soviet Deputy Foreign Minister Belonogov told a Moscow press
conference he believed Saddam’s withdrawal announcement satisfied
"all interested parties" in the Gulf war and urged the UN to call
an immediate cease-fire. Belonogov did not address the other 11
UN resolutions that bear on the Gulf crisis, except to note that
Saddam had renounced his annexation of Kuwait by reciting the
inclusive date during which Kuwait had been part of Iraq.
