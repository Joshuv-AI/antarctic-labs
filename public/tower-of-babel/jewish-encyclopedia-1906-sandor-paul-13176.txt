Hungarian merchant and deputy; born in 1860 at Hodmezövásárhely; studied at the academies of commerce in Budapest and Dresden. He is a member of the municipal council and of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Budapest. In 1901 he was, returned to the Hungarian Parliament by the electoral district of Lipótváros, Budapest.
