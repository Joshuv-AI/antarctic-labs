On E 7 + 1 / 2 E_{7+1/2} gauge theory
Xin Wang 1,2, Yi-Nan Wang 3,4,2

Abstract
We study gauge theory based on the intermediate Lie algebra E 7 + 1 / 2 E_{7+1/2} , interpolating between E 7 E_{7} and E 8 E_{8} . We propose a concrete UV completion via a 6d SCFT whose tensor branch description contains a pure E 7 + 1 / 2 E_{7+1/2} gauge sector. The proposal is tested by 6d anomaly cancellation and by the 5d 𝒩 = 1 \mathcal{N}=1 Coulomb branch prepotential from the associated M-theory geometry. As a nonperturbative check, we determine the elliptic genus of the single-string worldsheet CFT using modular bootstrap. The result matches the vacuum character of the corresponding VOA for E 7 + 1 / 2 E_{7+1/2} , completing the elliptic genus/VOA correspondence along the Deligne–Cvitanović series.
