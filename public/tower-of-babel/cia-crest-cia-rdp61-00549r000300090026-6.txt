2. Situation Review

a. Watch Committee Report
No. 410

Noted.

b. Review of Sensitive Situations

Mr. Amory, who was then presiding, and the members
discussed recent information and intelligence pertinent to the Lebanese
situation. Reference was also made to the situation in Cyprus. In
this connection, Mr. Cumming commented on communications arrange-
ments for facilitating and expediting reporting from that general area.
In addition, Mr. Cumming noted and discussed with the members
recent military and political developments in Indonesia.
