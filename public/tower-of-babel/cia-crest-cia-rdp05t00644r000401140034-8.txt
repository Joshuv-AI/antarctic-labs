Dear Admiral Turner:

The Backstoppers is an organization of over
600 outstanding civic-minded members in the St. Louis
area whose sole purpose is to assist the widows and
families of policemen, firemen and law enforcement
officers who have lost their lives in the line of duty.
When such a death occurs, we immediately give the widow
$2000, see to it that the funeral expense is paid, take
care of all outstanding bills, assume the mortgage on
the home if there was one, or will pay ten years rent
not to exceed $12,000, assist the children in obtaining
a college education, provide Blue Cross and Blue Shield
protection for the family, give the family an annual
Christmas party with gifts for all and a $100 Savings
Bond to each child under 18 years and, if they have not
received the Federal allowance, $2000 to each child on
attaining the age of 21 years. Other benefits are also
provided.

Aside from the knowledge that we have assisted
the family of one who has sacrificed his life for the
benefit of the community, our only returns for our dues
are our Spring and Fall dinners. We were very fortunate
in 1978 and 1979 in having H. Stuart Knight and William
H. Webster, respectively, as speakers at our Fall meetings.

Our Board of Directors has asked that I write
you and extend to you an invitation to be the speaker
at our 1980 Fall meeting, which is usually held during
the month of November. We will, of course, take care of
the expenses of your trip here and of your stay in St.
Louis. Subject to our being able to obtaining reservations
at the Park Plaza Hotel, the date for the meeting would
be at your convenience, though we would recommend that
a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday evening would be
preferred.

This notice published without cost to the Backstoppers through the goodness of a benefactor.
