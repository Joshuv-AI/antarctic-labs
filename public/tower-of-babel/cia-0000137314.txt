“NSC BRIEFING - . 16 December 1959

CUBA

Cuba actively pursuing neutralist foreign policy and seeking

closer ties with Afro-Asian bloc.

A.

Shift toward neutralism began on return of pro~-Communist

"Che" Guevara in September from 3-month trip to nine

Afro-Asian countries and Yugoslavia.

Foreign Minister Raul Roa informed UK General Assembly

in September that Cuba regarded US and USSR systems as

twin evils and would ally itself with underdeveloped

nations of world.

1. Roa planning trip to Cairo in January, which may
extend to other Afro-Asian countries.

CQ indicate Roa's trip may be followed by

high-level economic mission and UAR ambassador to Cuba
advised that his suggestion that Raul
Castro be invited to UAR considered “great idea" by

Roa and Guevara,

UAR ambassador promoting sale to Cuba of Egyptian cotton
yarn which Guevara says Castro does not want to buy

from US. , 5

Cuba issued public statement Suggesting conference of
underdeveloped countries for mid-1960 in Havana. Attendance
of 28 Afro-Asian and some Latin American nations proposed,

but we have no evidence that invitations have been issued.

II. Cuba may also be drawing closer to Sino-Soviet bloc.
A. Soviet exhibition, which closed in Mexico 15 December,
to open in Havana in February.
B. Reliable embassy source said 11 December that Roa plans

visit to Communist China in January.
