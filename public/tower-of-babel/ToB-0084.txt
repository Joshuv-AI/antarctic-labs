# TOB-0084 — THE MAGUS
## Francis Barrett | 1801 | Ceremonial Magic / Occult Philosophy

---

## 📖 OVERVIEW

**Full Title:** The Magus, or Celestial Intelligencer; Being a Complete System of Occult Philosophy

**Author:** Francis Barrett, F.R.C. (Professor of Chemistry, Natural and Occult Philosophy, the Cabala)
**Published:** London, 1801
**Pages:** ~300
**Language:** English
**Category:** Grimoire / Ceremonial Magic / Occult Philosophy

---

## 📚 CONTENTS

### Book I — The CABALA

- The nature of the Cabala
- Sephiroth and the Tree of Life
- Divine names and their correspondences
- The ten Sephiroth
- Magical uses of Hebrew letters

### Book II — ELEMENTAL MAGIC

- The four elements and their qualities
- Elemental spirits and beings
- Correspondences of the elements
- Talismanic magic

### Book III — THEORETICAL PRACTICE

- The philosopher's stone
- Natural magic vs. ceremonial magic
- Astrological foundations
- The seven planets and their angels

### Book IV — CEREMONIAL MAGIC

- The art of invoking spirits
- Magical circles and their construction
- Ritual tools and implements
- The conjuration formulas
