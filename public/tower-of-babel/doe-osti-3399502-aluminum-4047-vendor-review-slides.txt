 OBJECTIVE
⚫ Follow-up vendor review (previous 10/2024) for Al 4047 filler metal

 2 of 6

 LANL Evaluation Criteria
• The selected filler wire manufacturer should have full control of the filler wire
 manufacturing process (i.e. ingot metal, green rod, filler wire manufacturing and
 packaging).

• LANL would like to purchase filler wire as an off-the-shelf product in small quantities
 over time to maintain a fresh stock and eliminate issues related to long-term storage
 of aluminum.

• The filler wire would be purchased in 0.047” dia. on small (ideally 5 lb.) spools, but no
 larger than 16 lbs.

 3 of 6

Vendor Summary

 4 of 6

 CONCLUSIONS
• Lincoln Electric Company can supply the filler metal product meeting all of the
 criteria. Filler metal would be 100% manufactured in North America.

• If direct control of the smelting process is not required, then two additional companies
 (Hobart and AlcoTec) can provide a 4047 filler metal product meeting the remaining
 criteria. Country of origin for all manufacturing content is not guaranteed.

• Alloy 4047 is more prone to porosity as compared to other 4000 series aluminum
 filler metals. All vendors recommend the used of heated, low humidity-controlled
 storage for the filler wire.

 5 of 6

 RECOMMENDATIONS
• Procure sample wire spools from multiple heats of 4047 filler wire from those vendors
 selected from this evaluation.

• Perform an evaluation of the wire spools to evaluate the filler wire surface quality,
 cleanliness and hydrogen content (surface and bulk).

• Perform weld tests to compare the consistency in performance and volumetric weld
 quality from spool to spool using an established welding procedure.

• Establish a procurement procedure that minimizes the time the filler wire has been in
 storage (especially outside a controlled storage environment) before purchase.

• Establish LANL procedure for storage, handling and rotating filler wire stock.
