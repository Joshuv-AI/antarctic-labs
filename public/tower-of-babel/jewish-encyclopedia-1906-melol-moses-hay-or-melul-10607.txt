Compositor and translator in Leghorn (1777-93); son of Jacob Raphael Melol and brother of David Ḥayyim Melol. He translated or edited the "Sefer Azharot ha-Ḳodesh" and the Book of Ruth (Leghorn, 1777), and translated into Ladino "Seder Hattarat Nedarim," the ritual for dissolving an oath, "como suelen practicar en la Yesiba de Gemiluth Hasadim" ( ib. 1792). Mordecai Melol, a relative of his, edited "Alegria de Purim" ( ib. 1792).

Bibliography: Ersch and Gruber, Encyc. section ii., part 28, p. 63; Kayserling, Bibl. Esp.-Port.-Jud. pp. 68, 92. G. M. K.
