The Spanish Congress yesterday overwhelmingly defeated a
motion sponsored by the United Left party calling for an end to
the use of Spanish air bases by US B-52s, according to press
accounts. The measure was rejected by a vote of 232 to 18.

Comment: The vote suggests that Madrid has been successful in
dampening increased opposition to Spanish support for Operation
Desert Storm in the wake of the Baghdad bunker bombing. Spanish
officials have called for an end to coalition pombing of cities
and have given public assurances that B-52s based in Spain are
not involved in such missions. Quy
