Talmudist; lived at Dessau at the beginning of the nineteenth century. He was the author of "Dibre Asher" (part i., "Miktab Ḥarbot Ẓurim"), treatise on circumcision (Dessau, 1804).

Bibliography: Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 156; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 50; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 752. H. R. A. S. W.
