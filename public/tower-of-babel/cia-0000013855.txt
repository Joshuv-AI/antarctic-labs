1. Source saw captured American and Vietnamese prisoners being conducted
through BINH DINH Province on five different occasions, The first of
these happened around December 1964, when he saw seven VC soldiers,
equipped with submachine guns and rifles, conducting about 40 GVN
prisoners. Although Source did not know where these prisoners were
captured, he saw them coming from the east of CAT SON Village, crossing
his own area of THACH BAN Hamlet (BR 799629), CAT SON Village, and
continuing in a northwest direction. The majority of prisoners were
wearing combat clothes, but had neither head coverings nor shoes, No
rank or insignia was visible on their uniforms. Each prisoner carried a
belt containing about five cans of rice and a cooking pot. The prisoners
were divided into three groups, consisting of 12 or 13 prisoners. The
first group was led by two VC; three VG were stationed between the first
and second groups; and two VC brought up the rear of the third group.
The prisoners! arms were tied and they walked in single file.

2, In April 1965, Source saw about nine VG soldiers equipped with submachine
guns and rifles conducting about 50 GVN prisoners from CaT KHANH Village
to the northwest of CAT SON Village. A VC cadre told Source that the
prisoners were captured during the battle of Nhong Pass by the QUYET
CHIEN Regiment, SAO VANG Division, in which GVN Infantry Division 22 en-
gaged the 20 QUYET CHIEN Regiment. The prisoners remained in CaT SON
Village for 1O minutes during which Source observed that each one carried
a belt containing five cans of rice. They. wore combat uniform, but had
no shoes or headgear. ‘Some of the prisoners wore a red unit insignia with
two black mountains on it; he did not see the insignia of their military
rank, The prisoners walked in single file, the wrist of the man in front
being tied to the man behind him. Guards were stationed at 10-prisoner
intervals along the chain of captives.

3, In May 1965, Source saw five VC, carrying rifles and machineguns,
conducting about 15 GVN prisoners from THUAN NINH Hamlet, BINH TaN
Village, BINH KHE District, to the northwest of CAT SON Village. They
passed by Source's station at 2000 hours and were wearing combat uniforms,
and were bareheaded and barefooted. No insignias were visible on their
uniforms, but it was said that the prisoners were GVN Main and Regional
Force soldiers.

L. In about August 1965, Source saw a VC sauad conducting about 60 GVN
prisoners from the direction of PY TAI Village, PHU MY District, to the
northwest of CAT SON’ Village, PHU CAT District. The prisoners were

’- wearing combat uniforms with neither shoes nor hats, and were carrying
rice and cooking pots. They had a red insignia with two black mountains
across it on their left arms. Source learned from a cadre that the
prisoners were captured during an engagement on National Route 1, BY
NIEP Village (BR 896627), PHU MY District.

5. Again in August 1965, Source saw five VC leading about 10 GVN prisoners
and one United States prisoner from BINH THUAN and BINH TAN Villages,
BINH KHE District, and heading northwest of CuT SON Village. The
prisoners passed Source's station at about 1700 hours.. Source observed
that they wore combat uniforms; the GVN prisoners were shoeless and
bareheaded, while the American wore a white plastic helmet and black

Source estimated that the BINH DINH Province unit's prisoner-of-war
camp might be located at BR 689699, in NCHIA DIEN and BINH SON Hamlets,
aN NGHIA Village, HOsI AN District. Source believed that his station
was lying right: on the VC commo-liaison line which is why the VC
conducted their prisoners through his hamlet end village, and sometimes
rested there. Source also overheard VC cadres telling each other that
if they went toward the northwest, they would have to travel only 12
kilometers to reach their destination, but if they went around the
mountain, their trip would be difficult and dangerous, and its Length
would be 20 to 25 kilometers.

The VC usually allowed the prisoners to eat, smoke and wash their hands
during their rest stops in TH:CH BAN Hamlet. VC gusrds and prisoners
did not talk to each other, but Source noticed that the prisoners
occasionally laughed and talked to each other.
