No. 462 The Chargé in Yugoslavia (Shantz) to the Acting Secretary of State

[Extract]

plain

Belgrade, July 11, 1945.

261. …

Borba sharply criticizes Reuters for attitude of “protectionism” and “inclination contrary to fact” in favor of Greeks during Yugoslav-Greek dispute on treatment of Macedonians in north Greece. Editorialist denies charge of Reuters editor that Yugoslavia wishes to make territorial claims on Greece and hence press campaign by pointing out alleged expansionist ambitions of Greek chauvinist officials. He admits that Yugoslavs and “all true democrats” are against Athens Government and that Yugoslavia hopes question of Greek misrule in Macedonia will be subject of “international forum” if not Big Three Conference.
