IN MAY OR JUNE 1970 AT ABOUT 1700 HOURS ONE AFTERNOON,
OBSERVED THREE U.S. PRISONERS OF WAR (POW'S)
IN BINN CO HAMLET, SIHN MY VILLAGE, TAN UYEN DISTRICT, BIEN KOA
PROVINCE, SOUTH VIETNAM (SVN), FOR ABOUT 30 MINUTES FROM A
DISTANCE “OF ABODT TEN METERS, ACCORDING TO THEIR SIX GUARDS,
WHO WERE MEMBERS OF. THE UNIT WHICH HAD CAPTURED THEM, THE POW'S
WERE AMBUSHED WiILE RIDING IN A JEEP BY SOLDIERS OF THE THE VIET
CONG (VC) CHAU THANH DISTRICT UNIT, BINH DUONG PROVINCE,
ON INTER-PROVINIE ROAD 16, NEAR X7T916292, ABOUT SEVEN KILOMETERS
NORTEWEST OF TAN UYEN DISTRICT TOWN,
2. ALL OF THE POW'S APPEAR TO BE IN GOOD HEALTH AND DID

NOT APPEAR TO BE WOUNDED. THEY WERE CAUCASIAN AND WORE CIVILIAN
CLOTHES, ONE POW WAS ABOUT 35 YEARS OLD, 1.90 METERS TALL, HAD
A MEDIUM BUILD AND A LONG FACE. THE SECOND WAS ABOUT 1.80 METERS
TALL AND KAD A HEAVY BUILD, THE THIRD POW WAS ABOUT 1,70 METERS
TALL AND ALSO KAD A HEAVY BUILD,

3. THE POW'S AND THEIR GUARDS REMAINED OVERNIGHT IN BIHN
CO HAMLET. THE POW'S WERE GIVEN BREAD TO EAT AND WERE PLACED IN
