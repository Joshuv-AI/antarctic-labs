Correction for Meng et al., Rethinking energy transition strategies for the European Union amid rising energy prices
Published: 2026

Correction for “Rethinking energy transition strategies for the European Union amid rising energy prices,” by Wenjun Meng, Jaime Nieto, Dabo Guan, Jing Meng, Robert Sander, Ulrich Pöschl, Klaus Hubacek, Hang Su, Shu Tao, and Yafang Cheng, which published June 1, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2609606123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2609606123).

The authors note that, due to a production error, the institutional unit in affiliation i was omitted. The affiliation should appear as “State Key Laboratory of Atmospheric Environment and Extreme Meteorology, Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100029, China.”

The published paper has been updated.
