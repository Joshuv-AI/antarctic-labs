The Secretary of State to the Ambassador in Turkey (Steinhardt)

u. s. urgent

Washington, November 20, 1943—5 p.m.

903. Secret and personal for the Ambassador.

By direction of the President[1] you are requested to report immediately in Cairo and there await further instructions.

Hull C[ecil] W. G[eay]
