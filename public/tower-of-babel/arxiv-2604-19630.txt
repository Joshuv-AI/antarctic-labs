An upper bound on the growth of minimal graphs
Allen Weitsman

Abstract
. We prove that solutions to the minimal surface equation over unbounded simply connected domains and having boundary values 0 have at most exponential growth. The classic horizontal catenoid shows that this is best possible. Keywords: minimal surface, harmonic mapping, asymptotics MSC: 49Q05



1.
Introduction

Let
D

In this paper we consider the boundary value problem
for the minimal surface equation

(1.1)

\left\{\begin{aligned} &\text{div}\frac{\nabla u}{\sqrt{1+|\nabla u|^{2}}}=0\quad\text{and }u>0\quad\text{in}\ D\\
&u=0\quad\text{on}\ \partial D\end{aligned}\right.

The positivity assumption on

|u(z)|

the sets where

and

[
10

We shall use complex notation and study the constraints on growth of nontrivial solutions to (
1.1
) as determined
by the maximum

M(r,u)={\max}\ u(z),

where the max is taken over the values
|z|=r
with

.

Theorem 1.

Let

With

as defined above, there exists a constant

(1.2)

M(r,u)\leq e^{Cr}.

Here and elsewhere, the constants C with or without subscripts may depend upon

We may simplify

there can be at most 3 disjoint domains corresponding to solutions to (
