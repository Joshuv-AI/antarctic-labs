A Separation Method of the Positivity of A Quartic Polynomial
Taehun Kim, Jung Chan Lee , and ByoungSeon Choi

Abstract
Although the positivity of a quartic polynomial is a well-researched topic, existing conditions are often highly complex. Some necessary and sufficient conditions for the positivity of a quartic polynomial are presented through a separation method based on Ferrari’s technique of solving a quartic equation. We apply the result to the problem of the projection of the coefficient space.



Keywords:
Quartic Polynomial, Positivity, Separation

The Followings Are Equivalent:

\displaystyle\mathbf{(C0)}\quad\text{The reduced quartic polynomial is positive, i.e.,}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\;f(x)=x^{4}+px^{2}+qx+r>0\quad\forall x\in\mathbb{R}.

\displaystyle\mathbf{(C1)}\quad\text{There exists a negative }m\text{ such that }D(m)<0,\text{ where }D(m)\text{ is defined as}

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\;D(m)\doteq-8m\left(m^{2}+pm+\frac{p^{2}}{4}-r\right)+q^{2},

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\;m_{-}\text{ and }m_{+}(>m_{-})\text{ satisfying }

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\;m_{-}<0,\ D(m_{-})<0,\ \text{and }D(m_{+})\geq 0.

\displaystyle\mathbf{(C4)}\quad\text{Let }

\displaystyle\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\,\Delta\;\;\doteq 16p^{4}r-4p^{3}q^{2}-128p^{2}r^{2}+144pq^{2}r-27q^{4}+256r^{3},
