President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (22 March 1944, d4)

secret priority

[Washington,] 22 March 1944.

Number 507. Personal and secret. From the President for the Former Naval Person.

Your 629[2] received and my Chiefs of Staff have been told.

I join you in hoping that we may arrange to have a meeting before the great event is launched.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Supra.
