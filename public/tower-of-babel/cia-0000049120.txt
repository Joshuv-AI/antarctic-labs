SUBJECT: BACKGROUND ON ASSASSINATION OF ARCHBISHOP ROMERO

ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARN © WAS MURDERED ON 24 MARCH
1980 IN THE CHAPEL OF THE DIVINE PROVIDENCE HOSPITAL IN
NORTHERN SAN SALVADOR COLONIA OF MIRAMONTE. HE WAS STRUCK IN
THE LEFT SIDE OF THE CHEST BY A SINGLE .22 CALIBER BULLET THAT
FRAGMENTED AND LODGED IN HIS LUNG (SOME BELIEVE AN "EXPLOSIVE"
BULLET WAS USED). THOUGH INITIAL OVERT REPORTING SAYS A GROUP
OF GUNMEN ENTERED THE CHURCH AND OPENED FIRE, LATER REPORTING
AND INVESTIGATIONS SHOWED THE SHOT TO HAVE BEEN FIRED THROUGH
THE OPEN DOORS OF THE CHAPEL BY A SINGLE GUNMAN, PROBABLY USING
A RIFLE. QM SOURCES WERE REPORTED BY THE PRESS TO SAY
THAT THE WEAPON WAS PROBABLY EQUIPPED WITH A TELESCOPIC SIGHT.
THE ASSASSIN IS ESTIMATED TO HAVE BEEN 90-150 FEET FROM THE
ARCHBISHOP WHEN THE SHOT WAS FIRED.

CHURCH OFFICIALS SAID THAT THE KILLER APPARENTLY GOT OUT
OF A CAR OPPOSITE THE DOORS TO THE CHAPEL, FIRED ONE SHOT AND
THEN WAS DRIVEN AWAY BEFORE ANYONE GOT A CLOSE LOOK AT HIM.
THE OFFICIALS SAID IT WAS BELIEVED THREE OTHER MEN WERE ALSO IN
THE CAR. THE PRESS REPORTED AT THE TIME THAT THE U.S.
AMBASSADOR SAID A GOVERNMENT SOURCE INFORMED HIM ABOUT A WEEK
BEFORE THE ASSASSINATION THAT “RIGHT-WING CUBAN TERRORISTS"
WERE OPERATING IN EL SALVADOR. THE SOURCE IMPLIED THAT THE
ASSASIN MAY HAVE BEEN ONE OF THESE CUBANS.

A VARIETY OF SOURCES HAVE ACCUSED ROBERTO ((D'AUBUISSON) )

OF COMPLICITY IN THE MURDER OF ROMERO. ALTHOUGH THE BULK OF

THE REPORTING IS CREDIBLE, THERE 1S NOTHING THAT COULD BE
CONSTRUED AS HARD PROOF. SEVERAL REPORTS ON THE ROMERO
ASSASSINATION CLAIM THAT D'AUBUISSON AND HIS COLLEAGUES HELD
MEETINGS IN EARLY 1980 TO PLAN THE KILLING. ACCORDING TO SOME

SOURCES, THOSE IN ATTENDANCE WERE ASKED TO DRAW LOTS TO
DETERMINE WHO WOULD CARRY OUT THE ACT. SOME SOURCES CLAIM THAT
MILITARY PERSONNEL COMPRISED THE HIT SQUAD AND THAT A FORMER
MEMBER OF THE NATIONAL GUARD FIRED THE FATAL SHOT.
