French tosafist; died about 1234. In his youth Eliezer was a tutor in the house of the wealthy scholar Hezekiah ben Reuben of Boppard. His tosafot on Beẓah are quoted by Zedekiah Anaw in his "Shibbole ha-Leḳeṭ,"and other quotations often made in his name may also have been taken therefrom.

Bibliography: Gross, Gallia Judaica, p. 211; Zunz, Z. G. p. 39. L. G. A. Pe.
