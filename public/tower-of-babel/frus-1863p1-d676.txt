Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 768.]

Department of State, Washington, November 24, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch No. 527, of the 30th ultimo, which shows the manner in which you have carried into effect the instruction of this department, (No. 730,) has been received, submitted to the President, and is approved.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
