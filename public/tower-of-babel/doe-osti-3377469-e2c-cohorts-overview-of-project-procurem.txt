 Scenario Discussion

A large school district wants to add rooftop solar PV systems to 15 different
school sites. The solar PV systems are part of important community
outreach efforts for each school, and the system designs, installation,
performance, and monitoring are integrated into district-wide STEM
curriculum.
The district decided to issue one RFP and award 3 contracts—each selected
contractor installs 5 rooftop systems each over the course of one summer
and the fall semester. Integration with STEM curriculum happens in time for
the spring equinox.
 ◦ How would you address this in the performance and monitoring requirements in
 an RFP? How would those requirements effect scoring?

 Scenario Discussion

Revisiting the school district that contracted with three different solar EPC
contractors. Each of those contractors offered a 2-year workmanship warranty
that includes a weekly system monitoring check-in and on-site inspections twice
per year. Within that period, any workmanship issue is resolved without cost. The
OEM warranty covers some but not all labor costs. Each system’s 2-year
warranty period starts with permission to operate from the utility and therefore
each expires at a slightly different time.
  ◦ How should the district be planning for O&M?
  ◦ How should they procure that O&M and what should the requirements be?
