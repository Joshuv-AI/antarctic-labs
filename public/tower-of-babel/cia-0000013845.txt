le Source saw two US pilots in LE THUY District, QUANG BINH Province, NVN,
on two separate occasions, The first Sncident occurred in May 1966 and
the second in mid-July 1966.

26 During May; while visiting his grandmother in MY DUC Hamlet, SON THUY
Village, Li THUY District, Source witnessed the bombings of “the CAM LY
public water and agricultural work areas (approximate coordinates 765033)
about five kilometers from MY DUC Hamlet. He heard that a jet was shot
down by antiaircraft artillery (AAA) and that a US pilot was. captured by
local SON THUY Village militia immediately after he parachuted safely to
a location about two. or three kilometers from MY DUC Hamlet (approximate
coordinates XE 808038). The pilot was kept temporarily in people's houses
in SON THUY Village. Source and hamlet residents observed him from a
long distance, The fouror five escort personnel wore regular clothes
and were equipped with rifles. Source did not know whether or not the
pilot was tied.

3. At about 1300 or 100 hours on a Sunday in mid-July, US jets bombed the
GAM LY public water and agricultural works area (approximate. coordinates
XE 865033) belonging to LE THUY District, QUANG BINH Province, NVN. One
plane was shot down by AAA fire and the pilot parachuted into a rice field
in XUAN HOA Hamlet (approximate coordinates XE 821090), HOA THUY Village.
The pilot was immediately captured by the farmers and local militia. A
university student spending his vacation in the area was asked, to interpret
for the local security office. According to the interpreter, the pilot —
was a Major. He had received a Leg injury when he landed and had diffi-
culty walking. The pilot was husky, about two meters tall, had short
hair, a long face, a high sharp nose, and wore green clothes and black
shoes. His arms were tied and he was led by a rope. When the US pilot
was led to XUAN TRANG Hamlet, the local authorities gave him some food
which he did not eat. The authorities there did not have any cigarettes
to give him. About 50 people gathered out of curiosity to look at the US
pilot. Many of the viewers were children who ran after the pilot shouting
Johnson} Johnson! Johnsons". The escort reprimanded the children.

le The escort group consisted of four local militia equipped with Communist
Chinese rifles and stick grenades. One soldier alwys held a rifle aimed
at the pilot's back while the other three carried rifles on their should-
erse The escort group was to lead the pilot to the XUAN HOA Hamlet ferry
Landing, HOA THUY Village. At 1800 hoursshe would be transported from LE
THUY District, then to DONG HOI, and finally to HANOI. Source said that
after the political indoctrination and ideological reformation in HANOI,
the US pilots would be sent to the "VIET Bac" forest and mountain areas
and assigned labor jobs such as operating equipment, bulldozers and trucks.
