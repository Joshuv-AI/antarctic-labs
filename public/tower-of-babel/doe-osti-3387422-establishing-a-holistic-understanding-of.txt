B. Provide a quick summary of results from each objec?ve.

ObjecEve 1: A\er many false starts, we were able to idenEfy low-frequency gravity wave
features associated with 4 MCSs (2 MC3E cases, 2 PECAN cases), largely using surface pressure
data. These results will be published in an arEcle led by PI Adams-Selin.

ObjecEve 2: Given our issues with observaEons in objecEve 1, we chose to complete this

impact of low-frequency gravity waves was signiﬁcantly smaller than the line-end vorEcal ﬂows,

will be published in an arEcle led by Co-I Blount.

ObjecEve 3: Given our diﬃculty in idenEfying potenEal waves in both objecEves 1 and 2,
