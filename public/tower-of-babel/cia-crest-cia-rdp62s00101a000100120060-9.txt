2, The suggestions are: (a) That since the review is to be under the
auspices of the DD/P, the letter be for my signature, (b) That the mechanism
of the ARC be used for this review, (c) That prior to the dispatch of the letter,
you informally sound out the other interested consumers to determine whether
the use of the ARC mechanism would be acceptable to them.
