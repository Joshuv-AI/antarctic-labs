 Particle Physics on the Plains 2025
1 Executive Summary

DE-SC0025946 supported Particle Physics on the Plains 2025 held at the University of Kansas on
November 15 and 16, 2025. Particle Physics on the Plains is a theoretical particle physics conference
series held at the University of Kansas. It has been run eight times since its beginning in 2017.
Particle Physics on the Plains 2025 took place over a weekend in November, 2025. The goal of
Particle Physics on the Plains is to support graduate students and postdocs. There was a lack
on conferences in the Great Plains region, making it difficult and expensive for faculty, students,
and postdocs to attend professional conferences. Particle Physics on the Plains helps fill this gap.
We expected Particle Physics on the Plains 2025 to have 70-80 registered participants with around
35-40 talks in 7-10 sessions. Around 90% of the talks were expected to be from graduate students
and postdocs. The funds were budgeted to support student and postdoc lodgings, as well as a high
profile invited speaker from outside the region.

A Accomplishments
We accomplished our goals for Particle Physics on the Plains:

 • There were 75 registered participants.
 • There were 35 total talks in 7 sessions. 33 were from students/postdocs.We had to introduce
 a parallel session for the first time to accommodate all of the talks.
 • We had a PRD editor come and tell the participants about the APS and PRD.
 • We were able to support two nights of lodging for a total of 30 students and postdocs.
 • Dr. Stefania Gori came to give the external talk. We supported her trip with DOE funds.
 • All talks are available on the conference website: https://indico.global/event/13864/
