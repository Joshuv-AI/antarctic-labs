NATIONAL SECURITY COUNCIL
WASHINGTON

The National Security Council hereby authorizes the Ex-
ecutive Secretary, National Security Council, to control, super-
vise and administer funds which have been or hereafter may be made
available to the National Security Council with full powers with
respect thereto, subject to policies established by the Council.
These powers shall include all authorities normally exercised by
ths head of a department or independent establishment in the ad-
ministration of necessary functions, including the right to dele-
gate such authorities to subordinate officials in the Office of
the Executive Secretary and to officials of the Central Intelli-
gence Agency to the extent that the Executive Secretary deems nec-
essary for the efficient provision of administrative and related
services while maintaining control over the expenditure of funds
and the performance of such services.

I certify that the National Security Council
approved the above resolution on 26 September

1947.
