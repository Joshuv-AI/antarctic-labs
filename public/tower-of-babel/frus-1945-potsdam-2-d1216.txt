No. 1216 The President to the Ambassador in China (Hurley)[1]

top secret

[Babelsberg,] 23 July 1945.

Number 310, personal and top secret, from the President for Ambassador Hurley.

Please deliver the following message to Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek: “I refer to your message of 19 [20] July[2] quoting your telegram to Generalissimo Stalin. I asked that you carry out the Yalta agreement but I had not asked that you make any concession in excess of that agreement. If you and Generalissimo Stalin differ as to the correct interpretation of the Yalta agreement, I hope you will arrange for Soong to return to Moscow and continue your efforts to reach complete understanding. Truman.”

Truman

Source: 740.00119 Potsdam/8–745: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the Commander, Naval Group, China, via Navy channels.
[2] Document No. 1214.
