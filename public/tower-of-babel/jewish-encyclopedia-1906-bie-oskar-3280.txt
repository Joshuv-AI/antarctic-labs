German archeologist and professor at the Technische Hochschule at Charlottenburg, near Berlin; born at Breslau Feb. 9, 1864. He studied at the gymnasium and university of his native town. Besides his position at the Technische Hochschule, he occupies at present (1902) thatof editor-in-chief of the "Neue Deutsche Rundschau." Bie is the author of the following works: "Die Musen in der Antiken Kunst," 1886; "Kampfgruppe und Kämpfertypen in der Antike," 1890; "Zwischen den Künsten," 1895; and "Das Klavier," 1898. He is a convert to Christianity.
