On 12 August 1965, at 1600 hours, U.S, planes flew over THANH HOA and
NINH BINH Provinces, Source did not know their target, but he saw one of
them (type unknoln) flying in a Nor*kweaterly to Southeasterly direction
over NGHIA DONG (THANH HOA) Province Hamlet area, It was flying at more
than 2000 meters altitude when a missile unit, stationed at approximate
coordinates WH, 889227, sent off four missiles. One of these four hit the
plane, From his position in the storehouse of the Division's Headquarters,
Source saw the missiles launched two by two, The first two missiles did.
not hit the target and exploded in the air, One of the latter-two hit the
plane but for some unknown reason the pilot had already ejected himself,

Source was informed that the pilot landed in HA THAT Village, HA TRUNG
District, THANH HOA Province, As soon as he reached the ground at appro~
ximate coordinates WH. 865185, he was captured by six militiamen who ar-~
med with rifles, conducted him to Division 30's Headquarters, There, the
pilot was handed over to the guard Company where he was secured by a chain
and had his shoes removed. The guard Company telephoned to an agency
(anknown to Source), About 2100 hours, a six-wheeled truck came and took
the pilot away to a destination unknown to Source,

Source did not know the pilot's name. He was about 36 years old, 1.90
meters tall and had blond hair, high nose, blond eyebrows,long face,
prominent cheekbones and a large build. He wore a white T-shirt, dark
green trousers, black ankle~boots, and gloves, He appeared to be in nor-
mal health had no wounds on his body was calm and remained silent.

The plane exploded in the air, Its wreckage fell down in a village unknown
to Source,
