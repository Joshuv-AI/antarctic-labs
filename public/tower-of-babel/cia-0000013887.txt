Sometime during the first two months of 1967, Source's entire battalion
was issued a leaflet by the battalion commander. They were told to leave
these papers in GVN controlled areas or on the bodies of enemy soldiers
after a battle. This leaflet had been handwritten by a captured American
soldier and had been duplicated or mimeographed at Division Headquarters
for distribution to all combatants» of subordinate units. One side of
the leaflet was handwritten in English with a translation written in Viet-
nam on the reverse side.

The message stated approximately the following "Since my capture, I have
been very well treated. I ask you my friends to return to the United States,
because this war in Vietnam is illogical." The American soldier's signature
was affixed to the'right-hand bottom of the paper. ‘Source said that he
could remember that the first letter was an "L" followed by a period; the
next word bagan with the letter "A", but he could not make out the rest

of the name,

The leaflet was issued to Source's unit just two weeks after the capture

of the American soldier. by an unknown unit in Source's division during a
battle on SIM SON Mountain, HOAI AN District, BINH DINH Province, sometime
during the first two months of 1967 (exact date unkonwn). From the
battalion political officer, Senior Captain NGUYEN VAN DY, Source learned
that the prisoner had been kept at Division Headquarters (location unknown;
for one week. There he was well treated and fed such foods as eggs and
milk, When asked to write the leaflet, he agreed and was only given the .
subject to write about. He was then escorted to Region Headquarters located
somewhere in the mountains on the border of QUANG NAM and KONTUM Province.

From his political officer Source also learned the following about the
prisoner! he was a Private First Class, a member of Company 2, Division 7,
UsSi Airbdrine, He was a caiicasian about 20 years old, tall and of medium
build, Sturce's recollection of the man's name was Limited to "L.A ----",
He could fecall no other data on the prisoner.

Source never did use this leaflet in the proposed manners He said that
since the paper was very thin, he utilized it to roll cigarettes.
