Strichartz and Spectral Projection Estimates on Asymptotically Conic Manifolds
Zhexing Zhang

Abstract
. We prove the lossless unit interval Strichartz theorem on asymptotically conic surfaces, assuming that a large enough neighborhood of its trapped set has negative curvature. We also prove the spectral projection theorem on surfaces with Euclidean ends and nonpositive curvature, assuming a large enough neighborhood of its trapped set has negative curvature. We also discuss the spectral projection theorem on asymptotically Euclidean manifolds with dimension greater than or equal to 3, assuming some local smoothing estimates.

2.1.
Construction of background manifold

Since curvature is continuous, without loss of generality, we may assume

has negative sectional curvature on
\{(r,\theta)\in[A,A+1]\times\partial M\}\subset M_{\infty}

\tilde{M}=\tilde{M}_{0}\cup\tilde{M}_{\infty}
, where the metric on
\tilde{M}_{0}
agrees with the metric on
M_{0}
.

Recall that the metric on
M_{\infty}
is

, we define the function

\displaystyle\tilde{f}(r,\theta)=(1-a(r))\sinh(c_{1}r)+a(r)f(r,\theta)

when
r\in[A,\infty)
, where
c_{1}>0
is a constant that will be chosen later. Meanwhile,

,
a(r)\equiv 1
when
r\leq A+1/2
, and
a(r)\equiv 0
when

.
Then, we let
\tilde{M}_{\infty}=[A,\infty)\times\partial M
and

\displaystyle\tilde{g}=dr^{2}+\tilde{f}(r,\theta)^{2}d\theta^{2}

on
\tilde{M}_{\infty}.

We also define

to be a compact cutoff function of

, such that

on

, and

.

2.1.1.

Now, we compute the Gaussian curvature of

Recall that for a 2-dimensional manifold with metric

\displaystyle dr^{2}+h(r,\theta)^{2}d\theta^{2},

its Gaussian curvature is

We assumed

\displaystyle\frac{\partial_{rr}f(r,\theta)}{f(r,\theta)}>0

We want to show

\displaystyle\frac{\partial_{rr}\tilde{f}(r,\theta)}{\tilde{f}(r,\theta)}=\frac{\partial_{rr}((1-a(r))\sinh(c_{1}r)+a(r)f(r,\theta))}{(1-a(r))\sinh(c_{1}r)+a(r)f(r,\theta)}>0.

Note that

\displaystyle\partial_{rr}((1-a(r))

\displaystyle\sinh(c_{1}r)+a(r)f(r,\theta))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle a(r)\partial_{rr}f(r,\theta)+(1-a(r))c_{1}^{2}\sinh(c_{1}r)

\displaystyle+2a^{\prime}(r)(\partial_{r}f(r,\theta)-c_{1}\cosh(c_{1}r))

\displaystyle+a^{\prime\prime}(r)(f(r,\theta)-\sinh(c_{1}r)).

We may choose

\partial_{r}f(r,\theta)-c_{1}\cosh(c_{1}r)\leq 0,

when

This gives us

has strictly negative curvature.

2.1.2.
\textnormal{supp}([\chi,\Delta_{\tilde{g}}])
is nontrapping in

Now, we aim to show that for any geodesic in

Let a geodesic on

be the geodesic on
\tilde{M}
, which agrees with

We first aim to show

when the metrics of

(15)

\displaystyle\partial_{r}\tilde{f}(r,\theta)=a(r)\partial_{r}f(r,\theta)+(1-a(r))c_{1}\cosh(c_{1}r)+a^{\prime}(r)(f(r,\theta)-\sinh(c_{1}r)).

We know

and
(1-a(r))c_{1}\cosh(c_{1}r)\geq 0

Finally, we have

Notice that

by the finite speed of propagation of Hadamard parametrix. By (2.8) of

. Thus,

(22)

\displaystyle\begin{split}B\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f&=\tilde{\beta}(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f+R\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f\\
&=\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f+R\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f+R^{\prime}f.\end{split}

Therefore,

\displaystyle\tilde{\beta}(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f

\displaystyle=\tilde{B}\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)f+\tilde{R}\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f

\displaystyle=B\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f+\tilde{R}\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)f

\displaystyle=\chi\beta(\sqrt{-\Delta_{g}}/\lambda)f+R_{1}f,

for
q\geq 2
and any

.

Thus, it suffices to estimate

\left\|e^{-it\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}\chi u_{0}+\int^{t}_{0}e^{-i(t-s)\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}\tilde{\beta}(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)[\Delta_{g},\chi]u(s)ds\right\|_{L^{p}_{t}L^{q}_{x}([0,1]\times\tilde{M})}.

For the first term above, by (
19

\displaystyle\left\|e^{-it\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}\chi u_{0}\right\|_{L^{p}_{t}L^{q}_{x}([0,1],\tilde{M})}\lesssim||\chi u_{0}||_{L^{2}(M)}.

For the second term, notice that

is supported on the nontrapping region of both
\tilde{M}
and

By (

17
) and (
18

\displaystyle\left\|\int_{0}^{1}e^{-i(t-s)\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}\tilde{\beta}(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)[\chi,\Delta_{g}]u(s,\cdot)ds\right\|_{L^{p}_{t}L^{q}_{x}([0,1],\tilde{M})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left\|\int_{0}^{1}e^{is\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}\tilde{\beta}(\sqrt{-\Delta_{\tilde{g}}}/\lambda)[\chi,\Delta_{g}]u(s,\cdot)ds\right\|_{L^{2}(\tilde{M})}

\displaystyle\lesssim\lambda^{-1/2}||[\chi,\Delta_{g}]u(s,\cdot)||_{L^{2}_{s,x}([0,1],\tilde{M})}

\displaystyle\lesssim||u_{0}||_{L^{2}(M)}.

The same estimate holds for

by the Christ–Kiselev Lemma.
Finally, we apply Lemma
