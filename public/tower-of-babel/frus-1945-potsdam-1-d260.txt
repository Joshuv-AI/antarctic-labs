No. 260 Briefing Book Paper

[Extracts][1]

top secret

Russian Participation in European Economic Organizations

recommendation

It is recommended that we endeavor to obtain agreement from the Soviet Government to participate fully in … the European Central Inland Transport Organization (ECITO), … including designation of representatives to cooperate in the day-to-day activities of these organizations.

discussion

. . . . . . .

… Principally due to disagreement on the issue of Polish representation, the Soviets have not participated in recent discussions concerning the ECITO. Their delegation in London, however, has informally indicated that the present agreement[2] has substantially met the objections which they had to earlier drafts. …

[ Washington ,] July 3, 1945.

Source: 740.00119 (Potsdam)/5–2446
