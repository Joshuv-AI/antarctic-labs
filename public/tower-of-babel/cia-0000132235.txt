Velayati to Hold Talks in Italy
. Iranian Foreign Minister Ali Velayati will hold talks with
Italian officials on 14 February, according to press reports.
Velayati reportedly will stop first in Belgrade to see Yugoslav
officials--Yugoslavia is chairman of the Nonaligned Movement--
before proceeding to Rome and other unspecified West European

papitais. ce}

Comment: Italy will be the first West European country Velayati
has visited since the onset of war. He probably hopes to} improve
ties with Western Europe, promote Iran’s efforts to broke a Guif
war settlement, and bolster Iran’s image as a major player in
Gulf diplomacy. Rome almost certainly does not regard Tehran’s
current effort as workable but will indulge the Iranians En an
effort to keep them from playing a counter-productive role. |
