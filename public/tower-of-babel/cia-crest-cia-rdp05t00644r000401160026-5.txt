Dear Mr. Turner:

As President-elect for the 1980/81 term of the South
Florida Chapter of the Federal Bar Association, I am inviting
you to address the South Florida Chapter of the Federal Bar
Association on a timely topic of national or local interest.

We have had a number of nationally prominent speakers,
including President Ford during his term in the Presidency,
who have given major addresses using our Chapter as a forum
in the past. Although we have set a tentative schedule for
our meetings, we will make changes in dates and times, if
necessary, to accommodate your schedule. The exact location
of the meeting will depend on the expected size of the
attendance at the meeting, which can vary from as few as 70 to
as many 1,500, depending on the speaker and the topic of his
talk.

Current dates open for addresses include the following:

September 12, 1980 - 7:00 P.M. dinner;

September 14, 1980 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

October 21, 1980 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

November 25, 1980 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

December 18, 1980 - 12:00 Noon luncheon.;

January 21, 1981,- 12:00 Noon luncheon;

February 24, 1981 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

March 26, 1981 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

April 22, 1981 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

May 26, 1981 - 12:00 Noon luncheon;

June 25, 1981 - 12:00 Noon luncheon.
