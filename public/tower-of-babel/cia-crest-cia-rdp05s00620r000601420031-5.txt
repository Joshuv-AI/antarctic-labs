Dear Admiral Turner:

This week I ran into a high school classmate of yours,

strongly recommended that we invite you to deliver the

commencement address at National University. When I told him we
had previously invited you, he was both surprised and delighted.

May I again extend to you an invitation to be our commencement
speaker on Sunday, June 14 at our new campus is Vista, Califor-
nia, 40 miles north of San Diego.

Please let me know if this is agreeable with you. I will then
recommend to the Board that you be awarded an Honorary Doctorate
Degree in recognition of your splendid accomplishments during the
past quarter century.

Most sincerely, STAT
