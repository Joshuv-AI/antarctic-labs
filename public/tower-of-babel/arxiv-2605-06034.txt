Evaluation of eight different families of cubic Euler sums
J. Braun, H. J. Bentz

Abstract
We present a study on cubic Euler sums of degree four, five and six, where three different types of denominators 1 / k n 1/k^{n} , 1 / ( ( 2 ​ k − 1 ) n ) 1/((2k-1)^{n}) and 1 / ( k ⁡ ( 2 ​ k − 1 ) ) 1/(k(2k-1)) will be considered We demonstrate that for all three orders the complete variety of corresponding nonlinear Euler sums belonging to the eight different families can be explicitly calculated in terms of zeta values and polylogarithmic values L ​ i 4 ​ ( 1 / 2 ) Li_{4}(1/2) , L ​ i 5 ​ ( 1 / 2 ) Li_{5}(1/2) , L ​ i 6 ​ ( 1 / 2 ) Li_{6}(1/2) , L i 6 ( − 1 / 2 ) Li_{6}(-1/2) and L i 6 ( − 1 / 8 ) Li_{6}(-1/8) .

II
The order four case for cubic Euler sums

In the first section we explicitly calculate all cubic Euler sums of degree four. In this case only cubic members of the third, fifth and sixth family exist.

II.1
Third family

We start with the following sum:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{3}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{37}{8}\zeta(4)-7ln(2)\zeta(3)+7\zeta(3)+6\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)-12ln(2)\zeta(2)+8\zeta(2)+16ln(2)-24\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}

(3)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 16\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}-4\left(ln(2)\right)^{4}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

The corresponding calculational procedure starts with the following expression:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i-1)}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(i+k)(2i-1)}\right).

(4)

Using the identity
ce16
:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k(i+k)}=\frac{1}{2i}H^{(2)}_{i}+\frac{1}{2i}H^{2}_{i}-\frac{H_{i}}{i^{2}}+\zeta(2)\frac{1}{i}

(5)

and the identity introduced as lemma 2a in
bra24

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k-1)(i+k)}=\frac{1}{2i+1}\left(\zeta(2)-2\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}+\zeta(2)-\frac{H_{i}}{i}+\frac{1}{2}H^{(2)}_{i}+\frac{1}{2}H^{2}_{i}\right)

(6)

the explicit computation results in Eq. (3).

II.2
Fifth family

The only member of the fifth family results to:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{3}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{57}{64}\zeta(4)+\frac{7}{8}ln(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{9}{4}\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)-\frac{1}{8}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(7)

Here the calculational procedure is very similar to that introduced before. We start with the following expression:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k-1)(2i+2k-1)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{2k-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{(2i-1)(2i+2k-1)}\right).

(8)

The two-valued help function on the left side is defined by lemma 4a from
bra24
and the two-valued help function on the right side is defined by Eq. 201 in
bra24
.
With this the explicit computation results in Eq. (7).

II.3
Sixth family

Finally, in the cubic case we have two members of the sixth family. It follows first:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\zeta(4)+\frac{11}{4}ln(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{3}{4}\zeta(3)-3\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)+3ln(2)\zeta(2)-\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(9)

The corresponding calculational procedure is based on the two-valued help function defined by Eq. (242) from
bra24
:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)=2ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h^{2}_{i}}{i(2i-1)}+\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}h^{2}_{i}}{i(2i-1)}-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)\penalty\ .

(10)

The last sum on the right side can be calculated as follows. We define:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(2k-1)}\right)+2\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{2k-1}\right)\penalty\ .

(11)

With

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(2k-1)}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{2}(2i-1)^{2}}\right)

(12)

it follows further:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{3}}{i(2i-1)}+\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i(2i-1)}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\right)^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{2}(2i-1)^{2}}\right)\penalty\ .

(13)

Using Eq. (7) the right side of Eq. (10) can be calculated explicitly. For the left side of Eq. (10) we can write

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{(2i-1)(i+k)}\right)\penalty\ .

(14)

Defining

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{(2i-1)(i+k)}=2ln(2)\frac{h_{k}}{2k+1}+\zeta(2)\frac{1}{2k+1}+\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{2k+1}-\frac{1}{2k+1}\sum^{k}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i}\penalty\ ,

(15)

where this identity results from the simple solution of a corresponding first order difference equation. This way we get:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{(2i-1)(i+k)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k(2k+1)}+\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(2k+1)}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k(2k+1)}

(16)

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(2k+1)}\left(\sum^{k}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i}\right)\penalty\ .

Inserting Eq. (13) and Eq. (16) into Eq. (10) and using the proportionality

\displaystyle-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k(2k+1)}\sim\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k(2k-1)}

(17)

Eq. (9) finally results.

The second cubic Euler sum of order four belonging to the sixth family is given by the following expression:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{2}h_{k}}{k(2k-1)}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\zeta(4)-\frac{17}{2}ln(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{17}{2}\zeta(3)+4\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)-8ln(2)\zeta(2)+4\zeta(2)+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(18)

Again the calculational procedure is similar to that introduced for Eq. (9). We start with

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)=\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}h_{i}}{i^{2}(2i-1)}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H^{(2)}_{i}h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H^{2}_{i}h_{i}}{i(2i-1)}\penalty\ .

(19)

Rearranging the summations on the left side of Eq. (19):

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{k}}{(2i-1)(i+k)}\right)\penalty\ ,

(20)

and inserting Eq. (15) in Eq. (20) an explicit calculation of the different Euler sums results in Eq. (18).
This way all cubic Euler sums of degree four are expressible in terms of zeta values and the polylogarithmic value
Li_{4}(1/2)
, where the odd-type linear Euler sum
\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}

can be expressed in terms of the polylogarithmic value
Li_{4}(1/2)

bra20
.

III
The order five case for cubic Euler sums

In the following section we demonstrate that all members of the eight families of cubic Euler sums of degree five can be calculated in terms of zeta values and polylogarithmic values

Li_{4}(1/2)
and
Li_{5}(1/2)
.

III.1
First family

In the order five case two members of the first family exist. We start with:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{155}{32}\zeta(5)+\frac{7}{8}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)\penalty\ .

(21)

For an explicit calculation it is helpful to introduce first a new identity that results as the solution of the corresponding first order difference equation:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k(i-k)}=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k(i+k)}-\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{2}}-4\frac{h_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i}\penalty\ .

(22)

This nonlinear two-valued identity can now be used as follows:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{1}{i}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k(i-k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{1}{i}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k(i+k)}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{3}}-4\frac{h_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i^{2}}\penalty\ .

(23)

Rearranging the summations on both sides of Eq. (23) we get:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{1}{i(i-k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{1}{i(k+i)}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{3}}-4\frac{h_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i^{2}}\penalty\ .

(24)

It follows further

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k}\left(\frac{2}{k^{2}}-\frac{H_{k}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k}\left(\frac{H_{k}}{k}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{i^{3}}-4\frac{h_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i^{2}}\penalty\ .

(25)

This way it results

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k^{2}}=\frac{3}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{2}}{k^{3}}+2\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}\penalty\ .

(26)

As both Euler sums on the right side are known explicitly Eq. (21) follows.

The next Euler sum is defined as:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{2}h_{k}}{k^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{341}{16}\zeta(5)-\frac{53}{4}ln(2)\zeta(4)+\frac{7}{4}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)+7\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(3)-\frac{4}{3}\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}\zeta(2)+\frac{2}{15}\left(ln(2)\right)^{5}

(27)

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle 2ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}-16Li_{5}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\penalty\ .

The calculational procedure for this sum starts with the following expression:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(i-k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(i+k)}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}h_{i}}{i^{3}}-2\frac{H_{i}h_{i}^{(2)}}{i^{2}}\penalty\ .

(28)

Here we used the identity defined as lemma 1 from
bra21
. Rearranging the summations on both sides of Eq. (28) we get:

\displaystyle-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i(k-i)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{k}}{i(k+i)}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{3}}-2\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}\penalty\ .

(29)

Using lemma 1 from
bra22
and Eq. (5) it follows further:

\displaystyle-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i(k+i)}-\zeta(2)\frac{1}{k}-2\frac{H_{k}^{(2)}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{k}}{i(k+i)}\right)-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{3}}-2\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}\penalty\ .

(30)

Rearranging the different sums we get:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i(k+i)}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{2}}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{(2)}h_{k}}{k^{2}}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(31)

This is equal to

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\left(\zeta(2)\frac{1}{k}-\frac{H_{k}}{k^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{H_{k}^{(2)}}{k}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{H_{k}^{2}}{k}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{2}}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{(2)}h_{k}}{k^{2}}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(32)

Finally we arrive at:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{2}h_{k}}{k^{2}}=-\zeta(2)\frac{h_{k}}{k^{2}}+\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{(2)}h_{k}}{k^{2}}+2\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}+3\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{3}}\penalty\ .

(33)

All Euler sums on the right side of Eq. (33) are known explicitly. Inserting the corresponding values Eq. (27) follows.

III.2
Fourth family

It is favorably to continue with the members of the forth and fifth family before we evaluate the members of the second family. First we introduce the only member of the fourth family.
It follows

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}^{3}}{(2k-1)^{2}}=-\frac{713}{256}\zeta(5)+\frac{249}{64}ln(2)\zeta(4)+\frac{3}{32}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)+\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}\zeta(2)-\frac{1}{40}\left(ln(2)\right)^{5}+3Li_{5}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\penalty\ .

(34)

This result is known from literature
zeh07
.

III.3
Fifth family

In the order five case two cubic sums exist in the fifth family. The first one is:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{2}h_{k}}{(2k-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{651}{32}\zeta(5)-\frac{257}{16}ln(2)\zeta(4)+\frac{45}{8}\zeta(4)-\frac{7}{16}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{7}{4}\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(3)+\frac{7}{2}ln(2)\zeta(3)-\frac{5}{2}\zeta(3)

(35)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{5}{3}\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}\zeta(2)-6\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)+7ln(2)\zeta(2)-4\zeta(2)+\frac{2}{15}\left(ln(2)\right)^{5}-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}-16Li_{5}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\penalty\ .

The above result can be obtained from:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{2i-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k+1)(i+k)}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{2k+1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{k}}{(2i-1)(i+k)}\right)\penalty\ .

(36)

The two-valued help functions on the left and right side of Eq. (36) had been defined before. This way we get:

\displaystyle\left(\zeta(2)-2\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\right)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{(2i-1)^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i-1}^{(2)}h_{i}}{(2i-1)^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i-1}^{2}h_{i}}{(2i-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}h_{i}}{i(2i+1)}-\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}

(37)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k-1}}{2k-1}\right)\penalty\ .

For the last sum on the right side of Eq. (37) we can write

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k-1}}{2k-1}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}^{2}h_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}-\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)\penalty\ .

(38)

This way it remains to calculate

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{2i+1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k(2i+2k-1)}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}

(39)

or

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k+1)(2i+2k-1)}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}

(40)

or

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{h_{i}}{i}\left(\frac{h_{i}^{(2)}}{2i}+\frac{h_{i}^{2}}{2i}-ln(2)\frac{h_{i}}{i}\right)-\frac{1}{2}\zeta(2)\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\penalty\ .

(41)

The result is:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i+1)^{2}}\left(\sum^{i}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k}\right)=\frac{31}{16}\zeta(5)-\frac{15}{16}ln(2)\zeta(4)\penalty\ .

(42)

With this an explicit computation of Eq. (37) is possible, and this way Eq. (35) results.

The second Euler sum belonging to this family is

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{155}{64}\zeta(5)+\frac{83}{16}ln(2)\zeta(4)+\frac{15}{16}\zeta(4)-\frac{7}{32}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)-\frac{7}{4}\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(3)+\frac{7}{4}ln(2)\zeta(3)-\frac{3}{8}\zeta(3)

(43)

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\frac{7}{6}\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}\zeta(2)+\frac{3}{2}\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\zeta(2)-\frac{3}{2}ln(2)\zeta(2)-\frac{1}{30}\left(ln(2)\right)^{5}+\frac{1}{4}ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}-\frac{1}{4}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{h_{k}}{k^{3}}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 4Li_{5}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\penalty\ .

The corresponding calculational scheme is based an the following expression:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{2k-1}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{i(2i+2k-1)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\left(ln(2)\right)^{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k-1)^{2}}-4\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{(2k-1)^{3}}-4ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{(2k-1)^{2}}

(44)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle 2\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{(2k-1)^{2}}+4ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{(2k-1)^{3}}+2\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{(2k-1)^{2}}

or

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k}\left(\sum^{\infty}_{i=1}\frac{H_{i}}{(2i-1)(2i+2k-1)}\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}}{k(2k-1)}-ln(2)\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{2}}-\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}}{k^{2}(2k-1)}+\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{(2)}}{k^{2}}

(45)

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}h_{k}^{2}}{k^{2}}\penalty\ .

All Euler sums are known explicitly. This way Eq. (43) results, where one member of the first family is needed.

III.4
Second family

The only member of the second family is:

\displaystyle\sum^{\infty}_{k=1}\frac{H_{k}^{3}}{(2k-1)^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-\frac{1271}{32}\zeta(5)+\frac{183}{8}ln(2)\zeta(4)+\frac{135}{8}\zeta(4)+\frac{7}{4}\zeta(2)\zeta(3)-42ln(2)\zeta(3)+\frac{35}{2}\zeta(3)-2\left(ln(2)\right)^{3}\zeta(2)

(46)

\displaystyle+
