Correction: Scalable flow synthesis of ultrasmall inorganic nanoparticles for biomedical applications via a confined impinging jet mixer
Authors: Andrea C. Kian, Mahima Gupta, Heeju Hong, Kálery La Luz Rivera, Nil Pandey, Katherine J. Mossburg, Derick N. Rosario-Berrios, Portia S. N. Maidment, Andrew R. Hanna, Priyash Singh, Zhenting Xiang, Jay Kikkawa, David Issadore, Andrew D. A. Maidment, Hyun Koo, David P. Cormode
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-41509-z, published online 26 February 2026

The original version of this Article contained an error in the quantities of the reagents used for a synthesis for silver telluride nanoparticles.

Consequently, in the Methods section, under the subheading ‘GSH-coated sub-5 nm silver telluride nanoparticles’,

“The aqueous solution (8 mL), containing 10 mg AgNO3 and 83 mg GSH was loaded into a syringe connected to inlet one. Another aqueous solution (8 mL), containing 15.3 mg Na2TeO3 and 1 mL of N2H4 was connected to inlet two.”

now reads:

"The aqueous solution (8 mL), containing 15.3 mg AgNO3 and 83 mg GSH was loaded into a syringe connected to inlet one. Another aqueous solution (8 mL), containing 10 mg Na2TeO3 and 1 mL of N2H4 was connected to inlet two."

The original Article has been corrected.
