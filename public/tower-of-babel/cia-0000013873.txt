Bach NVA regiment had a seven to ten-man interrogation team. There were
no interrogators below regiment level. An interrogation team was part

of the Political Section (Psywar) directly under regiment headquarters
and included political and military proselyting subsections. Regiment 21
had ten interrogators two of whom were English language qualified and

two of whom were Korean language qualified.

FOW's captured were immediately forwarded to the regiment for preliminary
interrogation and tacticel debriefing. Normally they were held only

one day at regitental level and were then evacuated to either division

or military region for classification and intensified interrogation.
Source did not know if FOW's-were finally evacuated to NVN.

Source heard that his regiment had captured a total of 15 TOW's, seven
U.S. and eight Vietnamese. The Vietnamese, local forcés prisoners were
captured during March 1967 at NGHIa NANH, QUANG NGAI Province, SVN, anc
the seven U.S. marines (one wounded) were captured on 11 May 1967 in
QUANG NAM Province, SVN. |

Source did not know the names of his regiments interrogators as he had

no contact with this section, All injured POW's were to be given
medical treatment and were not to be hurt or tortured.
