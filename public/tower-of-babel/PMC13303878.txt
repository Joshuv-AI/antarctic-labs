Author Correction: ATF7IP/SETDB1-mediated epigenetic programming regulates thymic homing and T lymphopoiesis of hematopoietic progenitors during embryogenesis
Authors: Wenqi Chen, Jiaxin Wu, Peilu She, Juan Li, Yuying Lan, Xueli Hu, Yangguo Huangfu, Chen Wu, Daqing Jin, Peng Xie, Guanglei Zhuang, Yuxuan Wu, Yiyue Zhang, Leonard I. Zon, Ping Zhu, Tao P. Zhong
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-61680-7, published online 16 July 2025

In the version of the article initially published, in Fig. 6f, the control IgG graphs for the “WT” group (first bar) and the “atf7ip−/− + setdb1b mRNA” group (fifth bar) were incorrect due to copy–paste errors in the Source Data file. In Supplementary Fig. 1g, the source data for the control expression of “tcrα” were inadvertently copied from the source data for the control “ikaros” expression. In Supplementary Fig. 1h, the control “rag2:DsRed” image was mistakenly replaced with the “WT, rag2:DsRed” image from Fig. 1f. In Supplementary Fig. 4g, the “WT” image was inadvertently replaced with the “setdb1b−/− + coro1a:irf4a” image from Supplementary Fig. 5e. In Supplementary Fig. 5k, although the embryo quantification graph in panel k was correct, the source data were inadvertently copied from Supplementary Fig. 5m. In the Source Data file, the titles for Supplementary Figs. 6 and 7 were incorrectly labelled as ‘Supplementary Fig. 7’ and ‘Supplementary Fig. 8’, respectively, and an unnecessary final data file was included; these have been corrected. These errors have been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
