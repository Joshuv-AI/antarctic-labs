No. 38 The President to the President’s Adviser and Assistant (Hopkins)[1]

top secret

[Washington,] 28 May 1945.

274. Your messages 262101[2] and 272211[3] received.

You may inform Stalin that I perceive no objection to meeting in the Berlin area and that about the 15th of July appears to be a practicable date for me.[4]

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent to the United States Naval Attaché, Moscow, via Navy channels.
[2] Not printed.
[3] document No. 36.
[4] See ante, p. 53.
