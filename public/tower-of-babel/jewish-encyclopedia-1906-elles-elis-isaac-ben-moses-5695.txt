Polish rabbi of the sixteenth century; author of "Yesod Emunah," a treatise on the dogmas of Judaism, Cracow, 1582. He also wrote "Yesod ha-Teshubah," on repentance, extracts from other works, and chiefly from the "Yoreh Ḥaṭṭa'im" of Eleazar b. Judah of Worms, ib. 1582.

Bibliography: Nepi-Ghirondi, Toledot Gedole Yisrael, p. 247; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1139; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 38. K. M. Sel.
