Higher Type ITTM-recursion and Determinacy of Infinite games
P.D. Welch

Abstract
We outline a theory of type 2 recursion for Infinite Time Turing Machines à la Kleene . We establish a connection between classical descriptive set theory and ittm theory, by calculating the complexity of its halting problem as exactly that of a complete ⅁ ​ Σ 3 0 \Game\Sigma^{0}_{3} (or G δ ​ σ G_{\delta\sigma} ) set. This mirrors exactly what Kleene, Moschovakis et al. achieved for Kleene’s type 2 recursion and Σ 1 0 \Sigma^{0}_{1} (or Open) Determinacy.



We ascertain the least ordinal which is not generalised recursive in this sense, and its characterisation
via
a concept of
infinite nestings
in Gödel’s constructible hierarchy. The results do not require large cardinal axioms, and are all provable within analysis.

Definition 2.7

(The rudimentary functions)

(v)f(y,x)=\bigcup_{z\in y}g(z,x).
