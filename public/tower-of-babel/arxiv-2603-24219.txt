Abstract
The STAR collaboration at RHIC (BNL) and the ALICE collaboration at LHC (CERN) published recently dielectron ( e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} pair) spectra in the intermediate mass region (IMR), M e + ​ e − = ( 1 − 3 ) ​ GeV M_{e^{+}e^{-}}=(1-3)~\textrm{GeV} , which show a constant, i.e. energy-independent, emission temperature T I ​ M ​ R ≃ 287 ± 27 ​ MeV T_{IMR}\simeq 287\pm 27~\textrm{MeV} , at all bombarding energies s N ​ N \sqrt{s_{NN}} from 27 to 200 GeV. Why the temperature is so small and constant, although the bombarding energy is increased by orders of magnitude, so the early temperatures of the created parton plasma ought to rise?
