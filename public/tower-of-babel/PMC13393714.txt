Correction: Impairment of delayed recall as a predictor of amnestic mild cognitive impairment development in normal older adults: a 7-year of longitudinal cohort study in Shanghai
Authors: Xiaoqian Bao, Wei Li, Yuanyuan Liu, Xia Li, Ling Yue, Shifu Xiao
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Psychiatry 23:892 (2023) 10.1186/s12888-023-05309-3

Following the publication of the original article [1], the author reported that the references were incorrect and that the instrument used in the article should be referred to as the ‘Chinese Mini-Mental Status (CMMS)’ instead of ‘Mini-Mental State Examination’ or ‘Chinese MMSE.’ The references are presented correctly, and the terminology has been corrected to respect the registered trademark and intellectual property rights.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
