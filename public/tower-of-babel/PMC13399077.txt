Response to: Methodological concerns on the predictive performance evaluation of anthropometric indices
Authors: Kiavash Semnani, Mostafa Qorbani
Published: 2026

Dear Editor,

We have read with great interest the comment by Zhang and Han 1 on our published manuscript 2 . We thank the authors for their interest in our work and rigorous evaluation of the methodology. We realize that the noted concerns seem to be stemming from ambiguity in the reporting of the employed methodology for our analysis. We extend our sincere apologies for this matter, and would like to offer clarifications on the points raised.

Regarding the comparison of AUCs, we would like to clarify that our analysis does, indeed, use the methodology established by DeLong et al. 3 rather than simple visual or magnitude comparisons. This was not clearly reported in the methods section, and we accordingly apologize for the confusion. Furthermore, we would like to clarify that the Bonferroni adjustment (to P‐values derived from the DeLong test) was used only for determining the superiority of different indices to BMI. The threshold for statistical significance in comparing each index's performance across different sexes was set at the default (p‐value <0.05), consistent with adjustment for family‐wise error rates.

Regarding collinearity and model performance, there seems to be a misinterpretation of the employed methodology. We did not construct a single multivariate model including all indices and adjustment covariates; each index was evaluated in a separate multivariate model adjusting for known risk factors. As such, multicollinearity between indices was not possible in our analysis, and the noted “independent performance” should accordingly be interpreted as performance independent from established risk factors (often used for risk stratification), not other indices.
