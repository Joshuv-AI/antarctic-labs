 Asset Utilization: Distribution Service Transformer
• Example results on lightly loaded Richmond suburban feeder
• Asset Utilization Rate (AUR) = Actual Output/Maximum Possible Output
 o Delivered electricity compared to maximum possible per nameplate capacity
• Base Case - AUR without EV loads
 o 13.8% average AUR across 497 distribution service transformers
