Hungarian painter; born at Budapest Feb. 27, 1854. He studied at the schools of drawing in Budapest, Munich, Vienna, and Rome. The best known of his paintings are: "On Flowery Mead," "Procession at Anacapri," "Young Tramps," and "Italian Evening Landscape."

Bibliography: Singer, Allg. Künstler-Lexikon, vol. iii. S. N. D.
