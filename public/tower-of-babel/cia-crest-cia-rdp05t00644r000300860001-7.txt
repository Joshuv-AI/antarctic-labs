HONORS PLAN

1. Sequence of Events - Prior to commencing the ceremony,

a. The official Party with Platform Guests will depart
SUPSIIIP Administrative Building in reverse order of seniority.

b. Upon arrival at the Commissioning Berth, platform
members of the Official Party will be directed to the foot of
the Forward Brow where the Prospective Commanding Officer will
greet them. They will proceed up the Forward Brow and be
greeted by the Prospective Executive Officer and Chief

Has ter "At- Arms , and Senior Enlisted Advisor. They will be
escorted to the weather break where they will remain until
individually introduced by the Propsective Executive Officer.

Chairs will be provided for the guests' comfort while awaiting
their introduction.

c. Upon his arrival, the Principal Speaker will be
greeted by the Prospective Commanding Officer and the Band
will sound "Attention" on the bugle. At this point, the
Ship's Crew and Honor Guard will come to attention.

d. The Honor Guard will then be reviewed. Upon completion
r vfthe review each platform guest will proceed to prearranged

seating as they are announced individually by the Prospective
Executive Officer.

e. Following the setting of the watch, the Admiral Flag
will be broken in honor of Admiral TURNER, appropriate musical
honors will be rendered.

2. Sequence of Events - After JOHN HANCOCK is Commissioned z

a. After reporting for duty to CAPT DALLA HIJRA, the Commanding
Officer will request permission to break the Admiral Flag in
honor of ADM TURNER. The Flag will then be hoisted and honors
subsequently rendered .

3. Honors will be rendered only to those guests in the Official
Party upon their departure from the ship. Side Honors will be
rendered. Personal Flag hauled down, and the Commissioning
Pennant broken.

4. Honors and Ceremonies Officer of the Reck will be LTJG WALLACE.
Ten side boys will be assigned by the Senior Hatch Officer with

Enclosure (7)

MANNING THE RAIL PROCEDURES

1. The crew will be divided into two groups, one on each side
of the general seating area. Weapons Department and Navigation
Department will form up at the northern end (stern) while
Engineering, Operations, and Supply will be at the southern end
(bow) . Personnel will be formed in ranks so as to present an
even height when positioned in their "Man the Rail" stations.

2. The ceremonial quarterdeck watch will be in the front of the
southern group and will board the ship via the forward brow when
"The Watch is Set" .

3. When the Boatswain pipes the crew to the rail the following
actions should take places

a. The northern group will file by column onto the ship via
the forward brow. The first man will proceed down the side until
he reaches the speaker's area, and others will follow until the
rail is manned up to the brow. At this time the next man will
proceed to the bullnose and those following will man the rail aft
to the quarterdeck.

• b. Simultaneously, the southern group will proceed up the
er brow manning the rail from the speaker's area aft, then up
the ladder to the flight deck. Both the 0-1 level and the
flight deck should be manned.

c. The after quarterdeck watchstanders will be the last of
their group to proceed up the brow, and will take their posts a--,
this time.

d. Seven (7) CPOs will roan the rail on the 0-3 level, irdusnips,
as the first person of the southern group reaches the after brow.

e. Nine (9) men will man the rail on the 0-4 level, wr-eu
the first man of the northern group reaches the forward brow ,

f. Four (4) men will man the rail on the 0-3 level, bridge
wing, when the first man of the northern group reaches the for-
ward brow.

g. Men manning positions d., e. , and f . will take position
inside the ship, during the ceremony, until ‘The Watch is Set".
