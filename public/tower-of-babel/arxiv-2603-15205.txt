\Omega
.
Starting from the Boltzmann equation,

\dot{n}_{\chi}+3H_{\Omega}n_{\chi}=-\langle\sigma v\rangle\left(n_{\chi}^{2}-n_{\rm eq}^{2}\right),

(31)

and defining the yield
Y=n_{\chi}/s
and
x=m_{\chi}/T
, one obtains

\frac{dY}{dx}=-\frac{s\langle\sigma v\rangle}{xH_{\Omega}}\left(Y^{2}-Y_{\rm eq}^{2}\right).

(32)

Freeze-out occurs when

Y(x_{f})\simeq(1+c)\,Y_{\rm eq}(x_{f}),\qquad c=\mathcal{O}(1).

(33)

Expanding the Hubble rate as

H_{\Omega}(a)=H_{r}(a)\,[1+\delta(a)],\qquad\delta(a)=\alpha_{\log}\log\!\log(a/a_{i}),

(34)

the shift in the freeze-out point is

\Delta x_{f}\simeq\frac{1}{2}x_{f}\,\delta(a_{f}),

(35)

which directly leads to

\frac{\Delta\Omega_{\chi}}{\Omega_{\chi}}\simeq\frac{1}{2}\,\alpha_{\log}\log\!\log(a_{f}/a_{i}),

(36)

in agreement with the analytic estimates presented in the main text.

Appendix C
Gamma-Ray Morphology Correction

The standard line-of-sight integrated
J
-factor is defined as

J(\theta)=\int_{\rm l.o.s.}ds\,\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}(s,\theta).

(37)

In CET
\Omega
, the annihilation rate receives the correction

\Gamma_{\gamma}(x)\propto\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}(x)\,\left[1+\beta_{\Omega}\Phi_{\Omega}(x)\right],

(38)

leading to the modified
J
-factor

J_{\Omega}(\theta)=\int_{\rm l.o.s.}ds\,\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}(s,\theta)\left[1+\beta_{\Omega}\Phi_{\Omega}(s,\theta)\right].

(39)

Writing
\Phi_{\Omega}=\Phi_{0}+\delta\Phi
and retaining only linear terms,
one finds

\frac{\Delta J}{J}=\beta_{\Omega}\frac{\int ds\,\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}\,\Phi_{\Omega}}{\int ds\,\rho_{\rm DM}^{2}}\equiv\beta_{\Omega}\,\langle\Phi_{\Omega}\rangle_{J}.

(40)

Since
\beta_{\Omega}=\mathcal{O}(1)\alpha_{\log}
and the primordial
normalization of
\Phi_{\Omega}
yields

\langle\Phi_{\Omega}\rangle_{J}\sim 10^{-3}
, the expected correction is

\left|\frac{\Delta J}{J}\right|\sim 10^{-3}\text{--}10^{-2},

(41)

within the reach of next-generation MeV–GeV gamma-ray observatories.
