 The Use of Machine Learning

Six different Machine learning approaches to predict ILs Capacity, Solubility, and Activity
coefficients.
  - SVR (Support Vector Regression) - ignores outliers, good for non-linear data, kernel
  - RF (Random Forest) - decision trees, avoids overfitting, literature
  - XGBoost - scalable tree boosting system, parallel processing, literature
  - LightGBM - incredibly efficient and fast, designed for large datasets
  - NN (Neural Network) - good for non-linear data, unstructured data, pattern recognition
  - Gradient Boost - decision trees, corrects errors from previous trees (iterative)
Over nine thousand data points used (7.6 thousand data points for training)

 Lignin

 One of the three components of

 p-hydroxyphenyl groups
 Divides into categories: hardwood,

 Molecular weights ranging from 1000
                                         to 50000 g/mol
 Can be a monomer, oligomer, or

 The lignin used in this research was
DMA-RT Sorghum First Fraction extracted from sorghum

Size-Exclusion Chromatography: the Column

 Columns contain pore-filled resin,
 which are able to trap the smaller
 molecules for the longest amounts
 of time while larger molecules are
 washed through faster. As a
 mixture passes through a column,
 it separates into “bands” of
 molecules of different sizes, as the
 largest molecules move the fastest
 through it and the smallest ones
 are retained the longest.
