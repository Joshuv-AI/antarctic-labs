Impact of N ∗ N^{*} and Λ ∗ \Lambda^{*} resonances on C ​ P CP violation in Λ b 0 \Lambda_{b}^{0} decays
Yu-Kuo Hsiao, Kai-Lei Wang, Juan Wang

Abstract
The four-body decay Λ b 0 → p ​ K − ​ π + ​ π − \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-} has led to the first observation of baryonic C ​ P CP violation. However, the underlying subprocesses Λ b 0 → N ∗ ​ M \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to N^{*}M and Λ b 0 → Λ ∗ ​ M \Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda^{*}M , as well as the roles of excited nucleon ( N ∗ N^{*} ) and hyperon ( Λ ∗ \Lambda^{*} ) resonances, remain largely unexplored. Within the constituent quark model, we identify the relevant resonant states contributing to these underlying two-body transitions, including N ⁡ ( 1535 ) N(1535) , N ⁡ ( 1520 ) N(1520) , Λ ⁡ ( 1670 ) \Lambda(1670) , Λ ⁡ ( 1690 ) \Lambda(1690) , together with the remaining 1 ​ P 1P -wave baryon states. We obtain the resonant branching fraction ℬ ⁡ ( Λ b 0 → p ​ K − ​ π + ​ π − ) = ( 30.0 − 1.3 − 3.4 + 2.8 + 4.0 ± 1.8 ) × 10 − 6 {\cal B}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(30.0^{+2.8+4.0}_{-1.3-3.4}\pm 1.8)\times 10^{-6} , while the resulting 𝒜 C ​ P ​ ( Λ b 0 → p ​ K − ​ π + ​ π − ) = ( 3.18 ± 0.11 ± 0.13 ± 0.11 ) % {\cal A}_{CP}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(3.18\pm 0.11\pm 0.13\pm 0.11)\% provides a natural interpretation of the first observed baryonic C ​ P CP asymmetry. Our analysis establishes the first comprehensive framework for quantifying the impact of excited baryon resonances in multi-body beauty-baryon decays, with the associated mechanism generally applicable to baryonic C ​ P CP asymmetries.

In contrast to the penguin-dominated modes studied here,
the corresponding tree-dominated channels are also of considerable interest.
Focusing on the dominant subprocess

\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\pi^{-}(N^{*+}_{\rm sum}\to)p\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
, we predict

{\cal B}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to p\pi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(8.3^{+1.3+1.7}_{-0.6-1.5}\pm 0.9)\times 10^{-6}

{\cal A}_{CP}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to p\pi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(-5.90\pm 0.14^{+0.01}_{-0.03}\pm 0.02)\%

which can be tested at LHCb.

In this work, we have investigated the resonant four-body decay

\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
and the underlying two-body transitions

\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to N^{*}M
and
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to\Lambda^{*}M
within the CQM framework.
The dominant contributions have been identified as the
1P
-wave baryon states

N^{*}=(N_{1535},N_{1520},N_{1650},N_{1700})
and

\Lambda^{*}=(\Lambda_{1670},\Lambda_{1690},\Lambda_{1520})
.
By combining the contributions from these excited baryons and the relevant meson resonances,
we have obtained
{\cal B}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(30.0^{+2.8+4.0}_{-1.3-3.4}\pm 1.8)\times 10^{-6}
and

{\cal A}_{CP}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(3.18\pm 0.11\pm 0.13\pm 0.11)\%
,
with the latter in remarkable agreement with experiment.
We have further predicted
{\cal B}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to p\pi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(8.3^{+1.3+1.7}_{-0.6-1.5}\pm 0.9)\times 10^{-6}
and
{\cal A}_{CP}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to p\pi^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(-5.90\pm 0.14^{+0.01}_{-0.03}\pm 0.02)\%
, which can be tested at LHCb.
We have thus established the first comprehensive framework
for systematically identifying the excited
N^{*}
and
\Lambda^{*}
resonances
relevant to multi-body beauty-baryon decays and for
quantitatively evaluating their impact on branching fractions and baryonic
CP
asymmetries.
