Adopted unanimously at the 2198th meeting, on 19 February 1980.
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines[1] for admission to the United Nations,
Recommends to the General Assembly that Saint Vincent and the Grenadines should be admitted to membership in the United Nations.
