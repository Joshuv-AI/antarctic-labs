No. 1099 Proposal by the Soviet Delegation[1]

[Translation]

[Babelsberg, July 24, 1945.[2]]

Regarding Reparations From Italy

To establish the total sums of reparations from Italy at $600,000,000.

Italy must bear the expense of the established reparations over a period of six years with deliveries of products of Italian industry and agriculture.

Reparations from Italy must serve as compensation of losses caused by the participation of Italy in the war to the Soviet Union, Great Britain, the United States of America, Yugoslavia, Greece and Albania.

Source: Truman Papers
