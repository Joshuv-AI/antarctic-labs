Public Law 119–66: To reauthorize the Congressional Award Act.

An Act To reauthorize the Congressional Award Act.Dec. 26, 2025[S. 284]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Congressional Award Program Reauthorization Act”.

SEC. 2. — TERMINATION.

SUBSECTION (a) — In General

Section 108 of the Congressional Award Act (2 U.S.C. 808) is amended by striking “October 1, 2023” and inserting “October 1, 2028”.

SUBSECTION (b) — 2 USC 808 note.Retroactive Effective Date

The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if enacted on October 1, 2023.

SEC. 3. — OTHER AMENDMENTS.

PARAGRAPH (1)

in subsection (a), by striking “Each medal shall consist of gold-plate over bronze, rhodium over bronze, or bronze and shall be struck in accordance with subsection (f).”; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

in subsection (f)(1), in the second sentence, by striking “Subject to subsection (a), the” and inserting “The”.

Approved December 26, 2025.
