A rabbi in Pruzhany, Rushony, Wilkowyszky, and Selva (Lithuania and Poland), from 1719 to 1768. Toward the close of his life he removed to Wilna, where his son Samuel was rabbi. He died there March 1, 1771. Responsa of his are found in the collection of Saul ben Moses of Lomzha.

Bibliography: Giba't Shaul, Zolkiev, 1774; Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 5, Warsaw, 1886. A. B. D.
