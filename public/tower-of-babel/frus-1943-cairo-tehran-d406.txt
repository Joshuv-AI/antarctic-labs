The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President, and the President’s Reply[1]

[Tehran, December 1, 1943.]

Mr. President: What do you think of letting the Russians give dinner tonight—your last chance at Russian food

Harry

OK but I have to leave early as we sleep at the camp.

FDR

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] According to Sherwood, p. 796, these messages were exchanged near the end of the tripartite luncheon meeting of December 1, 1943 (ante, p. 585). For a facsimile reproduction of the messages, which were handwritten, see Sherwood, p. 797.
