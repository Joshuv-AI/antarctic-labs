The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the Director of War Mobilization (Byrnes)[1]

[Tehran,] November 28, 1943.

Extensive studies of various problems required before decision can be reached.[2] In meantime you should proceed as before the question of increase in landing craft was raised.

Source: Hopkins Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington via military channels, and forwarded by the White House Map Room to Byrnes.
[2] See Byrnes’ telegrams of November 24 and 27, 1943, pp. 395 and 444, respectively.
