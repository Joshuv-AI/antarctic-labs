 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 NLR will perform experiments and simulations to validate catalyst layer coating processes.
 Catalyst inks will be formulated to tune rheology, surface tension, and other ink properties to
 achieve uniform coatings and appropriate thicknesses.

 Summary of Research Results:

 Task 1: Preparation of Catalyst Inks

 Ballard will supply catalyst inks or faux-catalyst inks (Pt free) with constitutions that are relevant
 for Ballard’s projected manufacturing processes to NLR. Faux ink will likely be used for the
 majority of the work and then final validation with intended ink. Inks will be characterized using
 available methodologies at Ballard. Supplied inks will include the higher solvent content and
 rheology of next generation inks. These inks will be prepared through the course of the project to
 support efforts in Tasks 2 – 4.

 Task 1 Results:

 No work was completed under this task because funding was not received and the project was
 subsequently cancelled.

 Task 2: Catalyst Ink Characterization

 NLR will perform measurements of catalyst inks to characterize critical fluid properties relevant
 for coating simulations and experiments. Characterization methods may include rheometric,
 force tensiometers, drop shape analysis, and force tensiometers. These methods will be used to
 measure ink properties such as viscosity, surface tension, and contact angle.

 Task 2 Results:

 No work was completed under this task because funding was not received and the project was
 subsequently cancelled.

 Task 3: Coating Process Modeling

 NLR will build coating process simulation models using GOMA software to simulate slot die
 coating and transfer-roll slot die coating processes. NLR will use these models to evaluate the
 stability of various coating process conditions and inks. Models will be built for NLR’s coating
 equipment and based on transfer-roll slot die coating equipment designs provided by the trial
 location entity.

 Task 3 Results:

 No work was completed under this task because funding was not received and the project was
 subsequently cancelled.

 Task 4: Coating Process Trials

 Subtask 4.1: NLR will perform coating trials to validate process modeling simulations. These
 experiments will be conducted validate coating process modeling from Task 3 to ensure ink
 characterization is correct and that models correctly predict the coating process. This work will
 utilize NLR’s roll-to-roll coater and slot die coating equipment.

 Subtask 4.2: Ballard, with assistance from NLR, will identify and select an equipment vendor(s)
 or contract manufacturer(s) to conduct standard slot-die and transfer-roll slot die-coating trials.
 Part of this selection process will include trying to work with suppliers that have the ability to
 include infrared drying as an option for coating process trials. Ideally 2-3 vendors should be
 screened as potential sites for executing the trials and suitability for Ballard’s production plant
 (compliance matrix). NLR and Ballard will travel to the selected vendors site to perform coating
 trials.

 Subtask 4.3: Following coating trials conducted in subtask 4.2, Ballard and NLR will evaluate
 the suitability of both coating methods for Ballard’s manufacturing process.
