8.0 Pre-Test Operational Procedures:
 8.1 Perform Pre-test briefing
 (a) Review testing requirments for daily test activities
 (b) Review test matrix
 (c) Review rigging and fixturing of device under test (DUT)
 (d) Ensure all workers are qualified for work taking place

 8.2 Inform surrounding workers that testing is taking place
 (a) Perform walk-through of 963/124 and inform surrounding workers that testing is
 commencing
 (b) Ensure safety stanchions are up surrounding the limited approach boundary (8’ based on
 maximum erected voltage of Marx bank – 40kV/stage)
 (c) Place stanchions on perimeter of testbed.
 (d) Turn on flashing red warning light indicator in test area

 8.3 Perform Visual inspection of test bed and attached grounds
 (a) Verify equipment rack is grounded
 (b) Verify test bed pulser external hard ground is connected
 (c) Verify pressure lines from controller and dry air supply are connected and in good
 working order.
 (d) Inspect that oil container is sealed, not leaking, and in good working order
 (e) Verify dry air bottle is mechanically secured/tied off
 (f) Verify all air fittings on pulser are connected

 8.4 (A) Perform Air Pressure System Loto and Zero Energy Verification on APELC Pulser
 Note: Use LOTO Procedure LOTO-093848-25
 (a) Close primary valve from dry air tank.
 (b) Install LOTO Valve cover and lock on primary air control valve of supply bottle.
 (c) Visually note that input pressure on regulator drops to 0 psi.
 (d) Open purge valve on pressure regulator and note that regulator output drops to 0 psi.
 (e) Close Purge valve on pressure regulator
 (f) On front panel of APELC control console open “Marx purge valve”
 (g) Visually verify on Marx pressure indication that pressure drops to 0 psi.
 (h) Close “Marx purge valve”
 (i) On front Panel of APELC control console open “Peaking purge valve”
 (j) Visually verify on Peaking Switch pressure indicator that pressure drops to 0 psi.
 (k) Close “Peaking purge valve”

 (B) Perform Electrical LOTO and Zero Energy Verification on APELC Pulser

9.0 Test Operational Procedures
 9.1 Power on and charge APELC Pulser (Figure 3)
 *Reference Page 22-23 APELC users manual Local Mode Operation
 (a) Perform area walkthrough ensuring no personnel are present in limited/restricted
 approach area
 (b) Plug in APELC Pulser to 120 VAC facility power
 (c) Install key to APELC pulser power supply
 (d) Open main air supply line and set regulator to ~600psi
 (e) Turn APELC pulser control key to “Local”
 (f) Set positive or negative polarity charge on front panel of pulser controller
 (g) Ensure that Marx charge line on back panel of power supply reflect desired polarity
 (h) Turn on main power key switch
 (i) Turn on Air controller box
 (j) Turn on Trigger box switch
 (k) Turn on HVPS main power
 (l) Using the Marx and Peaking regulators on the front panel of APELC Controller set
 pressure to read desired pressure for needed voltage level.
 *Note: Reference Table 1 on page 23 of APELC controller users manual for needed
 pressure levels.
 (l) Allow pressure to stabilize for a minimum of 30 seconds on both pressure vessels
 (m) Purge both the Marx and Peaking vessels for ~2-3sec using the purge valves on the
 front panel.
 (n) Set the Marx charge voltage using the voltage control knob on the APELC control rack.
 (o) Arm the O-scopes for data collection
 (p) Press the “ARM” button on the front panel controller

 9.2 Fire APELC Pulser and save data on DUT
 *Reference Page 22-23 APELC users manual Local Mode Operation
 (a) Verbally announce the firing sequence to the rest of the lab space
 (b) Press the red “FIRE” button to charge and trigger APELC pulser
 (c) Press the “ARM” button on the front panel controller to disarm pulser after firing
 (d) Visually verify that charge voltage on front panel reads 0 VDC
 (e) Visually verify secondary voltage meter reads 0 VDC
 (f) On DAQ save collected data and waveforms

 OVERVIEW
 Description
Both the Marx generator and the peaking section of the device require an insulation medium to provide
voltage hold-off at the higher operating voltages of the system. The pulsed-power used in the APELC RS-
105 system uses compressed, dry breathing air. The pneumatic control unit uses electronic pressure
regulators, allowing the user to make incremental increases in the system pressure with each increase in
charge voltage. This sufficiently insulates the system in a manner that does not add to system weight and is
easily purged every few shots ensuring an uncontaminated operating environment for the system with
each round of testing.

B. Brief Description of Use:
High voltage pulses will be fired into an array of wires to perform radiated electromagnetic field tests. The pulses will be produce
a field that is 50 kV/m at its maximum and decays with greater than 1/r in distance away from the maximum field point. The wire
array encompasses a volume with dimensions of 25' high, 40' wide, and 125' long. A series of less than 30 shots will be
performed from 7:30 a.m. at the earliest until 5:00 p.m. at the latest.
