 Exercise #1

LA-UR

 Exercise #1

 1. Understand the practical operation of a variety of TID types and their intended use.
 2. Determine the most effective TID type for a given closing mechanism through
 hands-on application.
 3. Identify additional considerations to determine the suitability of a specific TID type
 (i.e., the variables included in an engineering analysis).

You work at a facility that stores large amounts of nuclear material in varying
containerizations. Management has just approved the establishment of a tamper indicating
device program in order to cut down on the effort required to conduct inventories, and to
enhance overall control of nuclear materials within the site boundary. You have been
assigned, along with your colleagues, to conduct an initial assessment to determine the
ideal TID types to be used at your facility. You will apply TIDs to a variety of apparatuses
that are representative of the closure mechanisms on different containers and entry points
to rooms and vaults onsite.
While determining which TIDs to apply to each apparatus, note any important
considerations, concerns, or questions that you or your teammates identify. You will be
asked to provide a debrief on your findings following this initial assessment.

many successful activities within their MBA. Before they set out into the facility, they receive their
Work Authorizing Document, detailing today’s work.
You, as the Facility Operators, must perform work on a designated container housed within a vault. In
order to do this, you must do the following:
 1. Enter the vault
 2. Weigh your container
 3. Secure your container with a TID
 4. Exit and secure the vault
During the course of this activity, you will divide into groups and select the role of either Facility
Operator 1, Facility Operator 2 or Facility Operator 3. You will be divided into your groups now. Please
discuss with your fellow operator to determine these roles now. Your instructor(s) will be your TID
Custodian(s) and MBA Custodian(s), as necessary.
You are provided with three procedures and their relevant attachments to be used throughout the
activity. Also, you have been given a Work Authorizing Document to begin. Only once your material is
properly secured in its container with a TID, the vault is secured and the NMAC Accounting System is
updated can you complete the activity.
Today’s date is: June 9, 2425

You and your colleagues work at a facility that stores large amounts of nuclear material in varying
compositions. A notification and Work Authorizing Document just came in from your supervisor that
the facility will be receiving a shipment of material. You and your colleagues have been assigned to
receive the material. The material being received should be in labeled and TID’d item containers
approved for storage in the facility. The total mass limits on the shipment manifest have been
confirmed and the material should not result in the facility exceeding its regulatory limits.
You and your colleagues will perform an examination of the received material prior to placement into
facility storage to confirm that it matches the shipment manifest by following the Offsite Shipping
and Receiving Procedure.
Once the procedure has been completed, you and your team will submit an NMAC System Update
Request Form to the MBA Custodian (instructor) to add the material to the inventory.
