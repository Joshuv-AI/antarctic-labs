Generalized Turán problems for Berge hypergraphs
Xiamiao Zhao , Xin Cheng , Dániel Gerbner

Abstract
Let ℋ {\mathcal{H}} be a hypergraph and F F be a graph. If there exists a bijection between the hyperedges of ℋ {\mathcal{H}} and the edges of F F such that each hyperedge contains its image, then we say that ℋ {\mathcal{H}} is a Berge copy of F F , and the collection of Berge copies of F F is denoted by Berge- F F . Given r r -graphs ℱ {\mathcal{F}} and ℋ {\mathcal{H}} , the generalized hyper-Turán number ex r ​ ( n , ℋ , ℱ ) \mathrm{ex}_{r}(n,{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{F}}) is the maximum number of copies of ℋ {\mathcal{H}} in n n -vertex ℱ {\mathcal{F}} -free r r -graphs. We study ex r ​ ( n , ℋ , Berge- ​ F ) \mathrm{ex}_{r}(n,{\mathcal{H}},\textup{Berge-}F) . For general ℋ {\mathcal{H}} , we connect this problem to counting copies of the shadow graph of ℋ {\mathcal{H}} in F F -free graphs and obtain several exact results. In particular, we show that for any hypergraph ℋ {\mathcal{H}} , if k k is sufficiently large, then ex r ​ ( n , ℋ , Berge- ​ K k ) \mathrm{ex}_{r}(n,{\mathcal{H}},\textup{Berge-}K_{k}) is achieved by the balanced complete ( k − 1 ) (k-1) -partite r r -graph, generalizing a result of Morrison, Nir, Norin, Rzażewski and Wesolek [ Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B , 162 (2023) 231–243] to the case of hypergraphs. We show that ex r ​ ( n , K s r , Berge- ​ F ) ≤ ex s ​ ( n , Berge- ​ F ) \mathrm{ex}_{r}(n,K_{s}^{r},\textup{Berge-}F)\leq\mathrm{ex}_{s}(n,\textup{Berge-}F) and present sufficient conditions for equality. We also consider the connected generalized Turán number for Berge paths.



Keywords
: generalized Turán number, Berge hypergraph, Turán-good

In this paper, we use capital letters to denote graphs, and calligraphic letters to denote hypergraphs.
The only exception is that we use

We use

A fundamental result in extremal graph theory is

is the largest number of hyperedges in an

-uniform hypergraph that does not contain

Given graphs

n
-vertex

The systematic study of generalized Turán numbers was initiated by Alon and Shikhelman

[

It is a natural idea to combine the above generalizations.
Given

Generalized hypergraph Turán problems have attracted some attention, see e.g.

In this paper, we consider a particular class of hypergraphs.
Gerbner and Palmer

Let

Let us remark that the

For the upper bound in

Claim 3.2

If a component

Proof.

First we show that in

Let

We prove that
t\geq\left\lfloor k/2\right\rfloor

,

We consider the vertex

Thus, for every

Then we have that for every vertex
