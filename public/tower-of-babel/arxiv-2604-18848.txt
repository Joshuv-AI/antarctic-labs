Consensus and flocking with transmission and reaction delays
E. Continelli, J. Haskovec, C. Pignotti

Abstract
We investigate consensus formation and flocking behavior in multi-agent systems subject to two distinct types of delays: a transmission delay accounting for information exchange between agents, and a reaction delay representing the processing time before agents adjust their states. For a simplified linear two-agent system, we provide explicit insight into how these delays affect asymptotic stability. We then derive sufficient conditions for asymptotic consensus and flocking in the general multi-agent setting with a nonlinear, globally positive influence function. These conditions require the delays to be sufficiently small relatively to the initial data and the decay rate of the influence function. The analysis is based on a Lyapunov functional approach combined with a Halanay-type inequality. Our results establish rigorous conditions under which collective behavior emerges in delayed multi-agent systems where both communication and reaction lags are non-negligible, with applications to biological, social, and engineered systems.



Keywords
: Asymptotic consensus, flocking, long-time behavior, delay.

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)^{2}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}=\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),\dot{x}_{i}(t)-\dot{x}_{k}(t)\rangle}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 8.5359pt=\left\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)(x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\sigma))-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}a_{kj}(t)(x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{k}(t-\sigma))\right\rangle}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 8.5359pt=\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\rangle-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}a_{kj}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{k}(t-\sigma)\rangle}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 8.5359pt=:(\mbox{{I}})+(\mbox{{II}}).}\end{array}

Let us first estimate the term
(\mbox{{I}})
. We write

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{(\mbox{{I}})=\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rangle+\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 28.45274pt+\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{i}(t)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\rangle=:(\mbox{{I}}_{1})+(\mbox{{I}}_{2})+(\mbox{{I}}_{3}).}\end{array}

The first term is estimated with the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality,

(\mbox{{I}}_{1})\leq\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert\lvert x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rvert=d_{x}(t)\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)\lvert x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rvert.

Now, since
t-\tau\geq 0
, we write

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\lvert x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rvert\leq\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\lvert\dot{x}_{j}(s)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}s\leq\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(s)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}s.}\end{array}

Since
s\geq t-\tau\geq\tau
, we can apply estimate
(
3.1
)
to get

\lvert x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rvert\leq\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\left(d_{x}(s-\tau)+\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\right)\mathrm{d}s.

Hence, using
(
1.3
)
,

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{(\mbox{{I}}_{1})\leq\left(d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right)\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}a_{ij}(t)}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\leq d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s.}\end{array}

Similarly, we have

(\mbox{{I}}_{3})\leq d_{x}(t)\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\rvert\leq d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(s)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}s.

Then, since
s\geq t-\sigma\geq 2\tau-\sigma\geq\tau
,
(
3.1
)
yields

(\mbox{{I}}_{3})\leq d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s.

To estimate the term
(\mbox{{I}}_{2})
, we use the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and the identity
d_{x}(t)=\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert
, which gives

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle=-\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle+\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 8.5359pt\leq-d_{x}(t)^{2}+\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert\lvert x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert=d_{x}(t)(-d_{x}(t)+\lvert x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert)\leq 0,}\end{array}

where we used
\lvert x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert\leq d_{x}(t)
in the last inequality. Therefore,

(\mbox{{I}}_{2})\leq\underline{a}(t)\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle.

Combining the above estimates, we arrive at

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{(\mbox{{I}})\leq d_{x}(t)\left(\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right.}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\left.+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\sigma}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,dr\mathrm{d}s\right)+\underline{a}(t)\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle.}\end{array}

(3.7)

For the term
(\mbox{{II}})
we write

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{(\mbox{{II}})=-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}a_{kj}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{j}(t)\rangle-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}a_{kj}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 28.45274pt-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}a_{kj}(t)\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{k}(t)-x_{k}(t-\sigma)\rangle=:(\mbox{{II}}_{1})+(\mbox{{II}}_{2})+(\mbox{{II}}_{3}).}\end{array}

Applying similar steps as before, we obtain

(\mbox{{II}}_{1})\leq d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s,

(\mbox{{II}}_{3})\leq d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+d_{x}(t)\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s,

and

(\mbox{{II}}_{2})\leq-\underline{a}(t)\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle,

where in the last estimate above we used that
\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle\geq 0
.
Consequently,

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{(\mbox{{II}})\leq d_{x}(t)\left(\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right.}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\left.+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert drds\right)-\underline{a}(t)\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle.}\end{array}

(3.8)

Combining estimates
(
3.7
)
and
(
3.8
)
gives

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)^{2}\leq 2d_{x}(t)\left(\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s\right.}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 85.35826pt\left.+\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right)}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt+\,\underline{a}(t)\left(\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle\right).}\end{array}

Now,

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\underset{j:j\neq i}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle-\underset{j:j\neq k}{\sum}\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 28.45274pt=\underset{j:j\neq i,k}{\sum}(\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(t)\rangle-\langle x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t),x_{j}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rangle)}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt-\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}-\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 28.45274pt=-(N-2)\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}-2\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 28.45274pt=-N\lvert x_{i}(t)-x_{k}(t)\rvert^{2}=-Nd_{x}(t)^{2}.}\end{array}

Consequently,

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)^{2}\leq d_{x}(t)\left(2\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+2\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+2\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right.}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 85.35826pt\left.+2\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t)\right),}\end{array}

which directly implies that
(
3.4
)

holds for almost all
t\in I_{n}
. Repeating the above argument for every interval
I_{n}
,
n\in\mathbb{N}
,
we deduce that
(
3.4
)
holds for almost all
t\in(2\tau,\infty)
.
∎

Corollary 3.3
.

For almost all
t>2\tau
it holds

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+4\tau\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t),}\end{array}

(3.9)

where
\underline{a}=\underline{a}(t)
is defined in
(
3.5
)
.

Proof.

Due to the nonnegativity of the diameter
d_{x}=d_{x}(t)
and the assumption
\tau\geq\sigma
,
(
3.4
)
readily gives

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+2\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 85.35826pt+2\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t),}\end{array}

for almost all
t>2\tau
.
Moreover, for all
s\in[t-\tau,t]
we have
[s-\tau,s]\subset[t-2\tau,t]
, so that

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{2\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\leq 2\int_{t-\tau}^{t}\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 142.26378pt=2\tau\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r.}\end{array}

Similarly, since for all
s\in[t-\sigma,t]
we have
[s-\tau,s]\subset[t-\tau-\sigma,t]\subset[t-2\tau,t]
, we find

2\int_{t-\sigma}^{t}\int_{s-\tau}^{s}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\leq 2\sigma\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\leq 2\tau\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r,

and
(
3.9
)
follows.
∎

3.2
The Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional

For
\{x_{i}\}_{i\in[N]}
a global solution to
(
1.1
)
–
(
1.3
)

and a constant
\beta>0
we introduce the functional
F:\mathbb{R}^{+}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{+}
,

\displaystyle F(t):=d_{x}(t)+\beta\int_{[t-2\tau]^{+}}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\underset{i\in[N]}{\max}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\qquad\mbox{for }t\geq 0,

(3.10)

where we use the notation
[t-2\tau]^{+}:=\max\{0,t-2\tau\}
.
By definition,
F
is continuous and almost everywhere differentiable on
\mathbb{R}^{+}
.

Lemma 3.4
.

Assume that

\displaystyle 2\beta e^{-2\tau}-4\tau-\beta\geq 0.

(3.11)

Then, for almost all
t>2\tau
,

\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}F(s-\tau)\mathrm{d}s-(N-1)\underline{a}(t)F(t)+\beta(1-e^{-2\tau})F(t-\tau),

(3.12)

where
\underline{a}(\cdot)
is defined in
(
3.5
)
.

Proof.

For almost all
t>2\tau
we have

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)=\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)-\beta e^{-2\tau}\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r-\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt+\beta\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(t)\rvert\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\,\mathrm{d}s}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 39.83368pt=\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)-\beta e^{-2\tau}\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r-\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt+\beta(1-e^{-2\tau})\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(t)\rvert.}\end{array}

Using
(
3.9
)
to estimate the term
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}d_{x}(t)
gives

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\left(4\tau-\beta e^{-2\tau}\right)\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t)}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 85.35826pt-\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s+\beta\left(1-e^{-2\tau}\right)\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(t)\rvert.}\end{array}

Applying
(
3.1
)
to the last term of the right-hand side, we obtain

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\left(4\tau-2\beta e^{-2\tau}+\beta\right)\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t)}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 42.67912pt-\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s+\beta\left(1-e^{-2\tau}\right)d_{x}(t-\tau),}\end{array}

where we used the estimate
\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\leq\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r
.
Now, by definition
(
3.5
)
and assumption
(
1.3
)
, we have
(N-1)\underline{a}(t)\leq 1
. Therefore,

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{-N\underline{a}(t)d_{x}(t)-\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\leq-(N-1)\underline{a}(t)\left(d_{x}(t)+\beta\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}e^{-(t-s)}\int_{s}^{t}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s\right)}\\
\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt=-(N-1)\underline{a}(t)F(t).}\end{array}

Consequently,

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\left(4\tau-2\beta e^{-2\tau}+\beta\right)\int_{t-2\tau}^{t}\max_{l\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{l}(r)\rvert\,\mathrm{d}r

\displaystyle-(N-1)\underline{a}(t)F(t)+\beta(1-e^{-2\tau})d_{x}(t-\tau).

Recalling the assumption
4\tau-2\beta e^{-2\tau}+\beta\leq 0
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}F(t)\leq 4\int_{t-\tau}^{t}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s-(N-1)\underline{a}(t)F(t)+\beta(1-e^{-2\tau})d_{x}(t-\tau).

We conclude by observing that
d_{x}(t)\leq F(t)
for all
t\geq 0
.
∎

Lemma 3.5
.

For all
i,j\in[N]
and
t\geq 2\tau
we have

\displaystyle\left|x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\right|\leq d_{x}(t-\tau)+\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\beta^{-1}e^{2\tau}F(t-\sigma).

(3.13)

Proof.

For all
i,j\in[N]
, we estimate

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\left|x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\right|\leq\left|x_{j}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\tau)\right|+\left|x_{i}(t-\tau)-x_{i}(t-\sigma)\right|}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\leq d_{x}(t-\tau)+\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\left|\dot{x}_{i}(s)\right|\mathrm{d}s.}\end{array}

(3.14)

Estimate
(
3.1
)
gives

\displaystyle\left|\dot{x}_{i}(s)\right|\leq d_{x}(s-\tau)+\int_{s-\tau}^{s-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r,

so that

\displaystyle\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\left|\dot{x}_{i}(s)\right|\mathrm{d}s\leq\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\int_{s-\tau}^{s-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s

Using the change of variable
\tilde{s}:=s-\tau
in the second integral, we obtain

\begin{array}[]{l}\vskip 8.5359pt\displaystyle{\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\int_{s-\tau}^{s-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s=\int_{t-2\tau}^{t-\tau-\sigma}\int_{\tilde{s}}^{\tilde{s}+\tau-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\tilde{s}}\\
\displaystyle{\hskip 56.9055pt\leq\int_{t-2\tau}^{t-\tau-\sigma}\int_{\tilde{s}}^{t-2\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\tilde{s}\leq\int_{t-2\tau}^{t-\sigma}\int_{\tilde{s}}^{t-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\tilde{s}.}\end{array}

Therefore,

\displaystyle\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\left|\dot{x}_{i}(s)\right|\mathrm{d}s\leq\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\int_{t-2\tau}^{t-\sigma}\int_{s}^{t-\sigma}\max_{k\in[N]}\left|\dot{x}_{k}(r)\right|\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s.

Now, definition
(
3.10
)
gives

\displaystyle F(t-\sigma)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\beta\int_{[t-\sigma-2\tau]^{+}}^{t-\sigma}e^{-(t-\sigma-s)}\int_{s}^{t-\sigma}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\beta e^{-2\tau}\int_{t-2\tau}^{t-\sigma}\int_{s}^{t-\sigma}\max_{i\in[N]}\lvert\dot{x}_{i}(r)\rvert\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}s,

where we used the fact that for
t\geq 2\tau
we have
[t-\sigma-2\tau]^{+}\leq t-2\tau
.
Consequently,

\displaystyle\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}\left|\dot{x}_{i}(s)\right|\mathrm{d}s

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{t-\tau}^{t-\sigma}d_{x}(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\beta^{-1}e^{2\tau}F(t-\sigma).

Inserting the above estimate into
(
3.14
)
gives the desired claim
(
3.13
)
.
∎

Finally, we estimate the values of the Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional
(
3.10
)
in the time interval

[0,2\tau]
.
For this purpose we define, for
K\in\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle\Delta^{K}_{x}:=\max_{i,j\in[N]}\max_{t\in[-\tau,K\sigma]}\max_{s\in[(K-1)\sigma,K\sigma]}\lvert x_{j}(t)-x_{i}(s)\rvert.

(3.15)

Moreover, we recall the definition

. Consequently, repeating the above argument and applying the Halanay Lemma
A.1
, we get, using that
d_{v}(t)\leq G(t)
, for all
t\geq 0
,

d_{v}(t)\leq G(t)\leq\mathcal{Z}^{K}_{\sigma}\Delta^{0}_{v}\,e^{-C(t-2\tau)},\qquad\mbox{for all }t\geq 2\tau.

(4.14)

Hence, since one can easily show that

d_{x}(t)\leq d_{x}(0)+\int_{0}^{t}d_{v}(s)\mathrm{d}s,\quad\forall t\geq 0,

using
(
4.14
)
we conclude that

\displaystyle\sup_{t\geq 0}d_{x}(t)\leq d_{x}(0)+\int_{0}^{+\infty}d_{v}(s)\,\mathrm{d}s\leq d_{x}(0)+\frac{e^{2C\tau}}{C}\mathcal{Z}^{K}_{\sigma}\Delta^{0}_{v}<+\infty.

∎

Appendix A

We establish the following Grönwall–Halanay–type lemma
[
13
]
.
It is a slight modification of the results found in
[
1
,
16
,
18
]
.
We give its proof here for readers’ convenience.

Lemma A.1
.

Let
\tau>0
and the constants
\alpha,\gamma,\eta>0
be such that
\alpha+\gamma<\eta
.
Let
u:[0,+\infty)\rightarrow\mathbb{R}
be a nonnegative continuous function,
differentiable almost everywhere on
(2\tau,+\infty)
, that satisfies

\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}u(t)\leq\frac{\alpha}{\tau}\int_{t-\tau}^{t}u(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\gamma u(t-\tau)-\eta u(t),\qquad\text{for almost all }t>2\tau.

(A.1)

Then,

u(t)\leq\left(\max_{s\in[0,2\tau]}u(s)\right)e^{-\Gamma(t-2\tau)},\qquad\text{for all }t\geq 2\tau,

(A.2)

where
\Gamma\in(0,\eta)
is the unique solution of the equation

e^{\Gamma\tau}(e^{\Gamma\tau}\alpha+\gamma)+\Gamma=\eta.

(A.3)

Remark A.2
.

Let us note that as soon as the condition
\alpha+\gamma<\eta
is verified, equation
(
A.3
)
indeed has a unique solution
\Gamma\in(0,\eta)
.
Namely, for the continuous function

f(x):=e^{x\tau}(e^{x\tau}\alpha+\gamma)+x-\eta,\qquad x\geq 0,

we have
f(0)=\alpha+\gamma-\eta<0
and
f(\eta)=e^{\eta\tau}(e^{\eta\tau}\alpha+\gamma)>0
. Therefore, there exists
\Gamma\in(0,\eta)
such that
f(\Gamma)=0
.
Finally, since
f
is strictly monotonic,
the solution is unique.

Proof of Lemma
A.1
.

Let us define

\displaystyle M:=\max_{s\in[0,2\tau]}u(s),\qquad w(t):=Me^{-\Gamma(t-2\tau)},\quad\text{for }t\geq 2\tau,

with
\Gamma\in(0,\eta)
the unique solution of
(
A.3
)
. We either have
u(2\tau)<M=w(2\tau)
, then there exists
T>2\tau
such that

\displaystyle u(t)<w(t),\quad\text{for all }t\in(2\tau,T).

(A.4)

Or we have
u(2\tau)=M
, then from
(
A.1
)

\displaystyle D^{+}u(2\tau)\leq\frac{\alpha}{\tau}M\tau+\gamma M-\eta u(2\tau)=M(\alpha+\gamma-\eta),

where we used that
u(t)\leq M
for all
t\in[0,2\tau]
, and
D^{+}u(2\tau)
denotes the upper (right-hand) Dini derivative of
u=u(t)
at
t=2\tau
.
Then, since
\dot{w}(2\tau)=-M\Gamma
and
-\Gamma=e^{\Gamma\tau}(e^{\Gamma\tau}\alpha+\gamma)-\eta>\alpha+\gamma-\eta
,

\displaystyle D^{+}u(2\tau)<-\Gamma M=\dot{w}(2\tau),

and again there exists
T>2\tau
such that
(
A.4
)
holds.

Now, let us denote with

S:=\{t>2\tau:u(s)<w(s),\text{ for all }s\in(2\tau,t)\},

and
T:=\sup S
. Then, from
(
A.4
)

S\neq\emptyset
and
T>2\tau
. We are going to prove that
T=+\infty
.

For contradiction, let us assume that there exists
T<+\infty
such that
(
A.4
)
holds and

\displaystyle u(T)=w(T).

(A.5)

Then, necessarily
D_{-}u(T)\geq\dot{w}(T)
, where
D_{-}u(T)
denotes the lower (left-hand) Dini derivative of
u=u(t)
at
t=T
, since otherwise we would contradict the maximality of
T
. So, since
\dot{w}(T)=-\Gamma w(T)
, we have

\displaystyle D_{-}u(T)\geq-\Gamma w(T).

(A.6)

On the other hand,
(
A.1
)
combined with
(
A.5
)
and with the fact that
u(t)\leq w(t)
, for all
t\in[0,T]
, gives

\displaystyle D_{-}u(T)\leq\frac{\alpha}{\tau}\int_{T-\tau}^{T}w(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s+\gamma w(T-\tau)-\eta w(T).

Using the identities

\displaystyle\int_{T-\tau}^{T}w(s-\tau)\,\mathrm{d}s=\frac{w(T)}{\Gamma}e^{\Gamma\tau}\left(e^{\Gamma\tau}-1\right),\qquad w(T-\tau)=e^{\Gamma\tau}w(T),

we arrive at

\displaystyle D_{-}u(T)\leq\left[\frac{\alpha}{\Gamma\tau}e^{\Gamma\tau}\left(e^{\Gamma\tau}-1\right)+\gamma e^{\Gamma\tau}-\eta\right]w(T).

Using the inequality
\frac{e^{\Gamma\tau}-1}{\Gamma\tau}<e^{\Gamma\tau}
and
(
A.3
)
, we have

\displaystyle\frac{\alpha}{\Gamma\tau}e^{\Gamma\tau}\left(e^{\Gamma\tau}-1\right)+\gamma e^{\Gamma\tau}-\eta<\alpha e^{2\Gamma\tau}+\gamma e^{\Gamma\tau}-\eta=-\Gamma,

so that

\displaystyle D_{-}u(T)<-\Gamma w(T),

which is a contradiction to
(
A.6
)
.
We therefore conclude that
T=+\infty
, namely
u(t)\leq w(t)
, for all
t\geq 2\tau
, which is
(
A.2
)
.
∎
