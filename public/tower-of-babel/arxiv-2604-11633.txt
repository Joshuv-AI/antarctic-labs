Edge disjoint Hamilton cycles in random digraphs of constant minimum degree
Colin Cooper, Alan Frieze

Abstract
We study the existence of directed Hamilton cycles in random digraphs with m m edges where we condition on minimum in- and out-degree δ ≥ k + 1 \delta\geq k+1 , where k ≥ 1 k\geq 1 . Denote such a random graph by D n , m ( δ ≥ k + 1 ) D_{n,m}^{(\delta\geq k+1)} . Let m = c ​ n m=cn and c ≥ c k c\geq c_{k} , where c k c_{k} is a sufficiently large constant. We prove that w.h.p. D n , m ( δ ≥ k + 1 ) D_{n,m}^{(\delta\geq k+1)} contains k k edge disjoint Hamilton cycles.
