Hebrew scholar of the eighteenth century. He translated into Hebrew, under the title "Leshon Zahab" (A Tongue of Gold), the second Targum to Esther. The translation was published at Constantinople in 1732.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Jewish Literature, p. 234; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 146. L. G. I. Br.
