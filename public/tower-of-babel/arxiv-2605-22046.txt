Alberto Merici, Kay Rülling, Shuji Saito

Abstract
. We show that the cohomology of the structure sheaf of smooth and proper schemes over a complete non-archimedean field K K of characteristic zero, can be refined to an 𝐀 1 \mathbf{A}^{1} -invariant cohomology theory of smooth (not necessarily proper) schemes over K K with values in 𝒪 K \mathcal{O}_{K} -lattices, and the same holds for K K of positive characteristic in dimensions at most 3 3 . As one application, we obtain that the automorphism group of the function field of a proper smooth variety X X of dimension at most 3 over a field of positive characteristic acts quasi-unipotently on the cohomology of the structure sheaf of X X . The construction of the lattices relies on a variant of the tame cohomology of Hübner–Schmidt with coefficients in a twisted version of the tame structure sheaf and uses results from rigid analytic geometry on the cohomology of twisted integral rigid structure sheaves due to Bartenwerfer and van der Put.

4.1.
An extension to singular schemes

The content of this subsection is a result of a private correspondence with B. Bhatt.
In the above proof of Theorem
1
, the smoothness is used only in
Theorem
4.18
, which depends on Bartenwerfer’s theorem
[

(see Theorem
3.2
).
Having a generalization to affinoids with certain prescribed singularities, we can relax the smoothness condition to such singularities, see Theorem
4.19
below.

Let
\mathcal{P}
be a class of singularities for normal schemes separated of finite type over
K
(e.g. rational, log canonical, Du Bois) and
\mathrm{NSch}_{K}^{\mathcal{P}}
be the category of normal schemes
X
separated of finite type over
K
such that singularities of
X
are at most
\mathcal{P}
and that
X_{\mathrm{sing}}
is proper over
K
.
Consider the functor (cf. (
0.0.1
)):

\mathrm{NSch}_{K}^{\mathcal{P}}\ni X\mapsto\mathcal{F}^{\mathcal{P}}(X):=R\Gamma_{t}(X/R,\mathbf{G}_{a}(0)).

Consider the following condition:

(\dagger)

For any proper normal scheme
X
over
K
whose singularities are at most
\mathcal{P}
and for any affinoid subdomain

V\subset X^{\mathrm{rig}}
and for any
i\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}

c\in K^{\times}
with
|c|<1
such that
c\cdot H^{i}(V,\mathcal{O}(1))=0

If
(\dagger)

4.18
hold true assuming that the singularities of

is at most
\mathcal{P}
.
Hence, by Remark
4.10
, we get the following.

Theorem 4.19
.

Assume that
(\dagger)
holds and
K
is either a discrete valuation field or algebraically closed. Then, the following hold:

(1)

We have
\mathcal{F}^{\mathcal{P}}(X)\in\mathcal{D}^{\geq 0}(R)^{f}
, for every
X\in\mathrm{NSch}_{K}^{\mathcal{P}}
.

(2)

For a morphism

such that
X_{\mathrm{sing}}\subset U
and
Y_{\mathrm{sing}}\subset f^{-1}(U)

\mathcal{F}(X)\simeq\mathcal{F}(Y)

(3)

Let

{\operatorname{ch}}(K)=0
