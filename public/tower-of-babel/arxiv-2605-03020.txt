Exact Quantum Many-Body Scars by a generalized Matrix-Product Ansatz
Sascha Gehrmann and Fabian H.L. Essler

Abstract
We construct exact eigenstates of quantum many-body systems with Hamiltonians that are not frustration-free in matrix product form, based on a local error cancellation ansatz motivated by the Derrida-Evans-Hakim-Pasquier method for finding the stationary state of the asymmetric simple exclusion process. We demonstrate the approach with explicit examples in both one and two spatial dimensions.

Introduction

Scars have also been constructed in Floquet systems

The scar eigenstates constructed in the literature are mostly simple by design. In particular they can take the form of matrix product states (MPS). One well-established way of obtaining exact eigenstates

H=\sum_{\langle j,k\rangle}h_{j,k}\ ,

(1)

condition

h_{j,k}|\Psi\rangle=0\quad\forall\text{ links }\langle{j,k}\rangle.

This has been employed to great effect to obtain exact MPS ground states of spin models

It is then very natural to ask whether it is possible to construct an algorithm for obtaining exact MPS eigenstates that relaxes the frustration-free condition (

II
Generalized DEHP Ansatz

Let
\mathscr{G}=(\mathscr{V},\mathscr{E})
be general oriented graph, where
\mathscr{V}
and
\mathscr{E}\subset\mathscr{V}\times\mathscr{V}
denote respectively the sets of
N_{v}

\mathscr{E}_{v}

d

\displaystyle H=\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathscr{E}}\,h_{i,j}\,.

(3)

Our objective is to construct exact eigenstates
\ket{\Psi}

H

\displaystyle\ket{\Psi}=\sum_{\{s_{v}|v\in\mathscr{V}\}}\sum_{\{\alpha_{e}|e\in\mathscr{E}\}}\raisebox{-3.00003pt}{\Big(}\prod_{v\in\mathscr{V}}[A_{v}]^{s_{v}}_{\{\alpha_{k}|k\in\mathscr{E}_{v}\}}\raisebox{-3.00003pt}{\Big) }|\{s_{v}\}\rangle\ .

(4)

Here
\{|\{s_{v}\}\rangle|v\in\mathscr{V}\}
is a basis of product states, where
s_{v}\in\{1,2,\dots,d\}
labels the
d
eigenstates of a single qudit and

\displaystyle[A_{v}]^{s_{v}}_{\{\alpha_{e}|e\in\mathscr{E}_{v}\}}

(5)

are tensors with one physical index
s_{v}
of dimension
d
, and a set of auxiliary indices
\alpha_{e}\in\{1,\dots,\chi\}
.
To ease notations, we will henceforth suppress free indices and sum over repeated indices. The
A

A_{v}

We propose the following generalization of a frustration-free condition for constructing eigenstates of
H
: for each pair of nodes
k

\ell

k

\ell

\displaystyle h_{k,\ell}\,[A_{k}]^{s_{k}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}[A_{\ell}]^{s_{\ell}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}\ket{s_{k},s_{\ell}}

(6)

\displaystyle=\big([E^{(k,l)}_{k}]^{s_{k}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}[A_{\ell}]^{s_{\ell}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}+[A_{k}]^{s_{k}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}[E^{(k,\ell)}_{\ell}]^{s_{\ell}}_{\alpha_{(k,\ell)}}\big)\ket{s_{k},s_{\ell}}

E^{(k,\ell)}_{k}

E^{(k,\ell)}_{\ell}

A

\displaystyle\sum_{e_{v}\in\mathscr{E}_{v}}E^{e_{v}}_{v}=0\qquad\forall\,v\in\mathscr{V}

(7)

|\Psi\rangle
is an exact energy eigenstate with zero energy

\displaystyle H|\Psi\rangle=0.

When
H
acts on
\ket{\Psi}
the error terms cancel by translational invariance and one obtains

\displaystyle H\ket{\Psi}=0\ .

(11)

Our ansatz by construction gives a vanishing energy eigenvalue and reduces the problem of constructing exact MPS eigenstates to finding representations of the quadratic algebra (
10
). For a given Hamiltonian the tensors
A
and
E
provide us with
2d\chi^{2}
free parameters, which are subject to
d^{2}\chi^{2}
relations, so finding solutions is a non-trivial problem. We have obtained a number of solutions and now present representative cases.

III.1
Model I: degenerate multiplet of scars

Our first example is a spin-S chain with a Hamiltonian that combines a generalized Rydberg term with a Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya (DM) interaction

H=\sum_{j=1}^{N}\bigg(1-\frac{S^{z}_{j}}{S}\bigg)\bigg(1-\frac{S^{z}_{j+1}}{S}\bigg)+4\vec{D}\cdot(\boldsymbol{S}_{j}\times\boldsymbol{S}_{j+1})\ ,

(12)

where
\vec{D}=(D_{x},D_{y},D_{z})
,
\boldsymbol{S}=(S^{x},S^{y},S^{z})
denotes the vector of spin-S operators and we take
N
to be even.
We note that the Hamiltonian (
12
) has a continuous spin-rotational symmetry only in the special case when
\vec{D}
points along the z-direction.
We start by considering the simplest case
S=1/2
. A solution to eqn (
10
) for the Hamiltonian density of (
12
) is given by

\displaystyle A=\begin{pmatrix}\ket{\uparrow}+\frac{{\rm i}}{a}\ket{\downarrow}&\frac{1}{a}\ket{\downarrow}\\
\frac{1}{a}\ket{\downarrow}&\frac{b}{a}\ket{\uparrow}-\frac{{\rm i}}{a}\ket{\downarrow}\end{pmatrix}\,,\

(13)

where
a
is a free complex parameter and

b=\frac{a}{1-a\Delta}
,
\Delta=\frac{D_{y}-{\rm i}D_{x}}{D_{z}}
.
The elements of the tensor
E
are given by

\displaystyle E^{1}_{11}

\displaystyle=\frac{{\rm i}\alpha}{a}+a\beta+\frac{2{\rm i}D_{z}b}{a},\ E_{11}^{2}={\rm i}\beta+\alpha^{*}+\frac{2b\alpha^{*}}{a}\ ,

\displaystyle E_{22}^{1}

\displaystyle=-\frac{{\rm i}\alpha}{a}+b\beta+2{\rm i}D_{z},\ E_{22}^{2}=-{\rm i}\beta+2\alpha^{*}+\frac{b\alpha^{*}}{a},

\displaystyle E_{12}^{1}

\displaystyle=E_{21}^{1}=\frac{\alpha}{a}\ ,\ E_{12}^{2}=E_{21}^{2}=\beta\ ,

(14)

where

\alpha=-(D_{y}+{\rm i}D_{x})\ ,\qquad\beta=\frac{D_{x}^{2}+D_{y}^{2}}{D_{x}}.

(15)

The state
|\Psi\rangle
exhibits a finite correlation length. In particular connected two-point functions decay exponentially at large separations
\langle\Psi|\sigma^{\alpha}_{1}\sigma^{\alpha}_{r+1}|\Psi\rangle_{c}\propto e^{-r/\xi}
. An explicit expression for
\xi
can be readily obtained by standard MPS methods.

III.1.1
Degenerate multiplet

We observe numerically that the zero energy eigenvalue of (
12
) is
N/2+2
-fold degenerate. Out of these states
N/2+1
have zero momentum and interestingly all can be obtained from our MPS as follows. We first note that the parameter
b
can be expanded in a power series in
a^{-1}
around
a=\infty
as

b=-\frac{1}{\Delta}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(a\Delta)^{-n}\,.

This in turn provides us with a series expansion of the state
|\Psi\rangle

|\Psi\rangle=\sum_{n\geq 0}a^{-n}|v_{n}\rangle.

(16)

As
a
is a free parameter that does not enter the expression of
H
the
|v_{n}\rangle
’s must be zero energy eigenstates and lie within the Rydberg subspace, which forbids neighbouring up spins. Writing

\displaystyle A

\displaystyle=A_{1}+\frac{b}{a}A_{2}+\frac{1}{a}A_{0}\ ,

\displaystyle A_{1}

\displaystyle=e^{11}\ket{\uparrow}\ ,\quad A_{2}=e^{22}\ket{\uparrow}\ ,\quad A_{0}=\sigma\ket{\downarrow}

(17)

with
\sigma=\sigma^{x}+{\rm i}\sigma^{z}

we see that
A_{1}A_{2}=0=A_{2}A_{1}=A_{0}A_{0}
. These restrict the non-vanishing terms in the expansion (
16
) to those where
A_{2}
is never adjacent to
A_{1}
and
A_{0}
is never followed by
A_{0}
. We conclude that

1.

The leading term in the expansion is the fully polarized ferromagnetic state in the z-direction
\ket{v_{0}}=\ket{\Uparrow}
.

2.

For
1\leq n\leq N/2

\ket{v_{n}}
contains contributions with at most
n
overturned spins. This implies that
\ket{v_{n}}
is linearly independent from
\{\ket{v_{0}},\dots,\ket{v_{n-1}}\}
, and the expansion (
16
) generates the entire degenerate multiplet of
N/2+1
zero momentum, zero energy eigenstates of
H
.

The structure of the degenerate multiplet is somewhat reminiscent of the one found in spin-1/2 XYZ chain
3
;
17
;
16
;
6
. Explicit expressions for
|v_{n\leq 5}\rangle
can be worked out in a straightforward fashion. It is useful to define generalized spin-lowering operators

\displaystyle{\cal S}^{-}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j}e^{21}_{j+1}\ ,

\displaystyle{\cal S}_{2,n}^{-}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j}e^{21}_{j}e^{21}_{j+n+1}\ ,

\displaystyle{\cal S}_{3,1}^{-}

\displaystyle=\sum_{j}e^{21}_{j-2}e^{21}_{j}e^{21}_{j+2}\ .

(18)

A straightforward calculation then gives

\displaystyle|v_{1}\rangle

\displaystyle\propto{\cal S}^{-}\ket{\Uparrow}\ ,

\displaystyle|v_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle\propto\mathscr{P}_{R}({\cal S}^{-})^{2}\ket{\Uparrow}\ ,

\displaystyle|v_{3}\rangle

\displaystyle\propto\mathscr{P}_{R}\Big[\frac{{\rm i}}{3!}({\cal S}^{-})^{3}\ket{\Uparrow}+\frac{1}{\Delta}{\cal S}^{-}_{2,1}\ket{\Uparrow}\Big],

\displaystyle|v_{4}\rangle

\displaystyle\propto\mathscr{P}_{R}\Big[\frac{1}{4!}({\cal S}^{-})^{4}\ket{\Uparrow}-\frac{{\rm i}}{\Delta}\mathscr{S}^{-}\mathscr{S}_{2,1}\ket{\Uparrow}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+\frac{1}{\Delta^{2}}({\cal S}^{-}_{2,2}-{\cal S}^{-}_{2,1})\ket{\Uparrow}\Big],

\displaystyle|v_{5}\rangle

\displaystyle\propto\mathscr{P}_{R}\Big[\frac{{\rm i}}{5!}({\cal S}^{-})^{5}\ket{\Uparrow}+\frac{1}{2\Delta}(\mathscr{S}^{-})^{2}\mathscr{S}_{2,1}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad+\frac{{\rm i}}{\Delta^{2}}(\mathscr{S}^{-}(\mathscr{S}_{2,2}-\mathscr{S}_{2,1})-{\cal S}^{-}_{3,1})\ket{\Uparrow}

\displaystyle\qquad\quad-\frac{1}{\Delta^{3}}({\cal S}^{-}_{2,1}-2{\cal S}^{-}_{2,2}+{\cal S}^{-}_{2,3})\ket{\Uparrow}\Big],

(19)

where
\mathscr{P}_{R}
is the projection operator on the Rydberg subspace.

III.1.3
Higher spins
S\geq 1

We now turn to higher spins
S\geq 1
. Numerical results for short chains suggest that the corresponding Hamiltonian again has an extensive number
N/2+2S
of translation-invariant zero-energy eigenstates that only involve spin configurations that fulfil the generalized Rydberg constraint, i.e. neighbouring spin excitations are suppressed due to the interaction penalty. We have verified for
S\leq 2
that our construction provides an exact MPS eigenstate in this subspace if we choose

\displaystyle A=\begin{pmatrix}\ket{S}+\frac{{\rm i}}{a}\ket{S-1}&\frac{1}{a}\ket{S-1}\\
\frac{1}{a}\ket{S-1}&\frac{b}{a}\ket{S}-\frac{{\rm i}}{a}\ket{S-1}\end{pmatrix}\ ,

(23)

where
|m\rangle
denotes the
S^{z}
eigenstate with eigenvalue
m
and impose
b=\frac{a}{1-a\Delta_{S}}
with
\Delta_{S}=\frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{2S}}
.
We omit the explicit expressions for the
E
tensors, as they become increasingly cumbersome with increasing
S
.
Expanding the MPS in a power series in
1/a
around zero provides us with
N/2+1
linearly independent states in the degenerate multiplet in a way analogous to the
S=1/2
case. It would be interesting to prove that our results extend to
S>2

V
Discussion

We presented a method for constructing exact matrix product eigenstates of quantum many-body Hamiltonians.
Our approach makes an ansatz for the action of the Hamiltonian density that goes beyond the frustration-free setting by allowing for the generation a local ”error” that telescopes to zero globally. Our framework can be viewed as a generalization of both the DHEP matrix product ansatz
12
, and the method of Klümper and Zittartz
24
;
35
;
37
, which are recovered by particular, simple choices of the error terms. Specifically, they corresponds to choosing the identity operator in the bond space and vanishing error terms respectively.
We have applied our framework to both one and two-dimensional systems and constructed illustrative examples of models that harbour exact quantum many-body scars of exact MPS form. Given their low bond-dimension these scar states could be seen in the dynamics of local observables on present-day quantum computers.

During the writing of this manuscript we became aware of a recent preprint
48
, which investigates a more general version of our Ansatz in the context of injective MPS.
