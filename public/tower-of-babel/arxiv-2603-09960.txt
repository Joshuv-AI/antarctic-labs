Back-to-back dijet production in DIS with finite-energy corrections and twist-3 gluon TMDs
Cyrille Marquet

Abstract
This work presents the summary of calculation of the cross section of the dijet production in deep inelastic scattering at small x at next-to-eikonal accuracy. The cross section is calculated in the back-to-back limit of the produced jets using results obtained in our previous works. The cross section is expressed via the transverse-momentum-dependent (TMD) parton distributions. Specifically, we show how the next-to-eikonal corrections are related to the x x dependent phase of twist-2 gluon TMD and to twist-3 unpolarized gluon TMDs.



1
Introduction

Deep inelastic scattering (DIS) on a dense gluon target is one of the processes used to study gluon saturation effects of the target. It provides a clean environment for such studies and therefore will be at the core of experimental focus in the Electron Ion Collider (EIC). In that process an incoming lepton emits a virtual photon, which then splits into a pair of quark and antiquark. The pair interacts perturbatively multiple times when traversing the target and the scattering is treated within the Color Glass Condensate (CGC) approach. For reviews of CGC see, for example,
[
1
,
2
]
. The observables produced in DIS processes are studied using the dipole factorization framework
[
3
,
4
]
.

While the calculations of DIS related observables have been mostly focused on including NLO corrections, the newly-built EIC has stimulated the interest in effects occurring at relatively low energies where next-to-eikonal corrections become important. Studies on next-to-eikonal corrections involve relaxation of shockwave approximation, which is a typical approximation of a target at high energies. Therefore, by going beyond the shockwave approximation one relaxes the Lorentz contraction in the longitudinal direction, relaxes the Lorentz time dilation to include dynamics of the target and includes the subleading (to "+" component) transverse component of the gauge field. Over last years, there have been significant improvements in calculation of subeikonal corrections, see for example
[

,

,

,

,

,

]
.

In Ref.
[
20
]
it was observed that in a specific kinematic limit, which is known as correlation limit, results on dihadron or dijet production obtained within CGC can be connected to the high energy limit of the leading twist gluon Transverse Momentum Dependent distribution functions (TMDs). These findings triggered multiple efforts to explore the connection between CGC and TMD factorization frameworks, see for example
[
21

In this work we present a summary of DIS dijet production calculation with next-to-eikonal corrections, which is done within CGC approach in the correlation limit, and then study its connection to TMD factorization framework beyond leading twist. We focus only on the case when the photon is longitudinally polarized. All details of the calculation, the case of the transverse photon and more comprehensive discussions are shown in our original paper
[
25

.

2
Definition and notation

The cross section at next-to-eikonal accuracy for DIS dijet for longitudinal photon is defined as
[
15
]

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}=\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{\rm Gen.\,Eik}+\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{\rm NEik\,corr.}+O({\rm NNEik})

(1)

with the Lorentz invariant phase space measure for the produced dijets given by

\displaystyle d{\rm P.S.}=\frac{d^{2}{\mathbf{k}}_{1}}{(2\pi)^{2}}\frac{dk_{1}^{+}}{(2\pi)2k_{1}^{+}}\frac{d^{2}{\mathbf{k}}_{2}}{(2\pi)^{2}}\frac{dk_{2}^{+}}{(2\pi)2k_{2}^{+}}\,.

(2)

The momenta of produced jets (quark and antiquark) are denoted by
k_{1}
and
k_{2}
. The first contribution to the cross section in Eq. (
1
) is the generalized eikonal cross section given by

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{\rm Gen.\,Eik}\!\!\!=2q^{+}\!\!\int\!\!d(\Delta b^{-})e^{i\Delta b^{-}(k_{1}^{+}+k_{2}^{+}-q^{+})}\!\!\!\sum_{\rm hel.\,,\,col.}\!\!\Big\langle\Big(\mathbf{M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm Gen.\,Eik}\Big(-\frac{\Delta b^{-}}{2}\Big)\Big)^{\dagger}\mathbf{M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm Gen.\,Eik}\Big(\frac{\Delta b^{-}}{2}\Big)\Big\rangle\,,

(3)

where
q^{+}
is the momentum of the photon and
\mathbf{M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm Gen.\,Eik}
is the generalized eikonal scattering amplitude. The sum is taken over colors and helicities of the produced jets, and
\langle\dots\rangle
denotes target averaging. The generalized eikonal term goes beyond the strict eikonal result due to weak dependence on the light-cone coordinate
z^{-}
of the background gluon field, which represents relaxing infinite Lorentz time dilation. The strict eikonal contribution can be obtained by neglecting
b^{-}
dependence in the formula (
3
).

The second term in Eq. (
1
) contains the explicit next-to-eikonal corrections to the cross section and is given by

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{\rm NEik\,corr.}

\displaystyle=(2q^{+})\,2\pi\delta(k_{1}^{+}\!+\!k_{2}^{+}\!-\!q^{+})\sum_{\rm hel.\,,\,col.}2{\rm Re}\Big\langle\Big({\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm strict~Eik}\Big)^{\dagger}{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm NEik\,corr.}\Big\rangle\,,

(4)

where the next-to-eikonal correction to the amplitude is the sum of the following three different contributions:

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm NEik\,corr.}=i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\textrm{dec. on }q}+i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\textrm{dec. on }\bar{q}}+i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\textrm{dyn. target}}\,,

(5)

where the first and the second term stand for amplitudes calculated with decorated quark and antiquark, respectively, and the last term corresponds to the contribution with
z^{-}
dependence included.

Our goal is to study the DIS dijet production in the correlation limit, where it is possible to track the interplay of CGC and TMD factorization approaches. In this limit, it is useful to change variables for the generic quark and antiquark momenta
({\mathbf{k}}_{1},{\mathbf{k}}_{2})\to({\mathbf{P}},{\mathbf{k}})
so that

\displaystyle{\mathbf{k}}_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\mathbf{P}}+z_{1}{\mathbf{k}},

(6)

\displaystyle{\mathbf{k}}_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle-{\mathbf{P}}+z_{2}{\mathbf{k}}\,,

(7)

where we introduced

\displaystyle z_{1,2}

\displaystyle\equiv

\displaystyle\frac{k^{+}_{1,2}}{k_{1}^{+}\!+\!k_{2}^{+}}.

(8)

Similarly, the transverse positions are also changed
({\mathbf{v}},{\mathbf{w}})\to({\mathbf{r}},{\mathbf{b}})
as follows

\displaystyle{\mathbf{v}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\mathbf{b}}+z_{2}{\mathbf{r}}

(9)

\displaystyle{\mathbf{w}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\mathbf{b}}-z_{1}{\mathbf{r}}\,.

(10)

In the new variables
{\mathbf{k}}
is the total momentum of the jets,
{\mathbf{P}}
is the relative transverse momentum between the jets,
{\mathbf{r}}
is the dipole size and
{\mathbf{b}}
is the dipole impact parameter. The back-to-back jets kinematics shall be identified with taking the limits:
|{\mathbf{k}}|\ll|{\mathbf{P}}|
and
|{\mathbf{r}}|\ll|{\mathbf{b}}|
. This regime is particularly convenient as it allows to expand color operators as powers of
{\mathbf{r}}
and systematically study the corrections of the type
(|{\mathbf{k}}|/|{\mathbf{P}}|)^{n}
.

In the correlation limit the formula (
5
) can be reorganized to the form

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm NEik\,corr.}=i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{(1)+(2)}+i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{(3)}+i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\textrm{dyn. target}}\,,

(11)

where the first term comes from contributions involving derivatives of the Wilson lines and it contains only
\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\,-}
components of the background field, the second term involves the component
\mathcal{F}^{ij}_{a}
, and the third term is given by
\mathcal{F}^{+-}
.

In these proceedings we do not present explicit forms of the contributions to the scattering amplitude in general kinematics and we do not discuss all possible structures emerging at subeikonal order. All these issues are comprehensively discussed in our previous papers
[
25
,
15
,
12
]
.

3
The amplitude of the back-to-back dijet production in DIS

The scattering amplitude of the longitudinal photon splitting into quark-antiquark pair in the correlation limit in the strict eikonal approximation is given by

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\rm Eik}=\,-\frac{Q\,ee_{f}}{q^{+}}\,\bar{u}(1)\gamma^{+}v(2)\,z_{1}z_{2}\,\int_{{\mathbf{b}}}\,e^{-i{\mathbf{b}}\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,

\displaystyle\times\Bigg\{\bigg[-\frac{2{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}+(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\bigg(\frac{{\mathbf{k}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}-\frac{4({\mathbf{k}}\!\cdot\!{\mathbf{P}}){\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{3}}\bigg)\bigg]\,t^{a^{\prime}}\!\!\!\int dv^{+}\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\;

\displaystyle+\bigg[-\frac{\delta^{ij}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}+\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{3}}\bigg]\,t^{a^{\prime}}t^{b^{\prime}}\int dv^{+}\int dw^{+}\,\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\;

\displaystyle\times\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,w^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{b^{\prime}b}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}(w^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Bigg\}+O\left(\frac{Q|{\mathbf{k}}|}{|{\mathbf{P}}|^{5}}\right)\,.

(12)

and this contribution was found from the generalized eikonal amplitude after neglecting
z^{-}
dependence in the color structures. In the formula (
12
),
\mathcal{U}_{A}
stands for the Wilson line in the adjoint representation,
\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\,-}
is the leading component of the strength tensor of the background gluon field and
{\bar{Q}}=\sqrt{m^{2}+Q^{2}k_{1}^{+}k_{2}^{+}/(q^{+})^{2}}
with
Q
being the photon virtuality.

In Eq. (
12
), the term proportional to
-2{\mathbf{P}}^{j}
is the leading-power contribution, which is of order
Q/(|{\mathbf{k}}|\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{3})
at amplitude level. The other explicitly written terms are next-to-leading-power corrections, of order
Q/|{\mathbf{P}}|^{4}
.

The first next-to-eikonal correction that comes from decorated Wilson lines involving one or two covariant derivatives,
\mathcal{U}^{(1)}_{F;j}
and
\mathcal{U}^{(2)}_{F}
, is found to be of the form

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{(1)+(2)}=-\frac{Q\,ee_{f}}{q^{+}}\,\bar{u}(1)\gamma^{+}v(2)\int_{{\mathbf{b}}}\,e^{-i{\mathbf{b}}\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,

\displaystyle\times\Bigg\{\frac{i}{2q^{+}}\bigg[\frac{-2{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}+(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\bigg(\frac{{\mathbf{k}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}-\frac{4({\mathbf{k}}\!\cdot\!{\mathbf{P}}){\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}\bigg)\bigg]\,t^{a^{\prime}}\!\!\!\int\!dv^{+}\,v^{+}\,\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\;

\displaystyle+\frac{i}{2q^{+}}\,t^{a^{\prime}}t^{b^{\prime}}\int dv^{+}\int dw^{+}\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\;\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,w^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{b^{\prime}b}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}(w^{+},{\mathbf{b}})

\displaystyle\times\Bigg[-\frac{\delta^{ij}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}\,\min(v^{+},w^{+})+\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}\,(z_{2}v^{+}\!+\!z_{1}w^{+})\Bigg]\Bigg\}+O\left(\frac{Q|{\mathbf{k}}|}{W^{2}\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{3}}\right)\,.

(13)

The next subeikonal contribution, which involves the decorated Wilson line
\mathcal{U}^{(3)}_{F;ij}
with
\mathcal{F}^{ij}
dependence, is obtained as

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{(3)}=-\frac{Q\,ee_{f}}{2(q^{+})^{2}}\,\bar{u}(1)\gamma^{+}\frac{[\gamma^{i},\gamma^{j}]}{4}v(2)\,\frac{1}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}\int_{{\mathbf{b}}}\ e^{-i{\mathbf{b}}\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}

\displaystyle\,\times\,\int dz^{+}\,t^{a^{\prime}}\mathcal{U}_{A}(+\infty,z^{+};{\mathbf{b}})_{a^{\prime}a}\,g\mathcal{F}_{ij}^{a}(z^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\>+O\left(\frac{Q|{\mathbf{k}}|}{W^{2}\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{3}}\right)\,.

(14)

Finally, the dynamics of the target results in the following form of the subeikonal correction to the amplitude

\displaystyle i{\cal M}_{q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}\leftarrow\gamma^{*}_{L}}^{\textrm{dyn. tar.}}=Q\,ee_{f}\,\frac{(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})}{(q^{+})^{2}}\,\bar{u}(1)\gamma^{+}v(2)\,\frac{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}+m^{2}]}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{2}}\int_{{\mathbf{b}}}\ e^{-i{\mathbf{b}}\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}

\displaystyle\,\times\,\int dz^{+}\,t^{a^{\prime}}\mathcal{U}_{A}(+\infty,z^{+};{\mathbf{b}})_{a^{\prime}a}\,g\mathcal{F}^{+-}_{a}(z^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\>+O\left(\frac{Q|{\mathbf{k}}|}{W^{2}\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{3}}\right)\,.

(15)

The three contributions given in Eqs. (
13
), (
14
) and (
15
) obtained in the back-to-back jets regime are the explicit NEik corrections defined in the amplitude (
11
).

4
The cross section of the back-to-back dijet production in DIS

The eikonal contribution to the cross section for the dijet production via longitudinal photon in the back-to-back regime is found by squaring the strict eikonal amplitude (
12
) and it gets the form

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{\rm Eik}=2q^{+}\,2\pi\,\delta(q^{+}-k^{+})\,4\,Q^{2}\,e^{2}e_{f}^{2}\,z_{1}^{3}z_{2}^{3}\int_{{\mathbf{b}},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}}\,e^{i({\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}-{\mathbf{b}})\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,

\displaystyle\times\Bigg\{\Bigg[\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}-2(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\frac{({\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{k}}^{j}+{\mathbf{k}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j})}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}+16(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\frac{({\mathbf{k}}\cdot{\mathbf{P}}){\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{5}}\Bigg]

\displaystyle\times\int dv^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}\Big\langle g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})\Big[\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)^{{\dagger}}\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)\Big]_{ba}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Big\rangle

\displaystyle+\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{l}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}\bigg[\delta^{ij}-\frac{4\,{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}\bigg]\,{\rm tr}_{F}\big(t^{a^{\prime}}t^{b^{\prime}}t^{c^{\prime}}\big)\int dv^{+}dw^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}\Big\langle\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)_{c^{\prime}c}g{\mathcal{F}_{l}^{\;-}}_{c}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})

\displaystyle\times\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}({v}^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{w}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{b^{\prime}b}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({w}^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Big\rangle

\displaystyle+\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{l}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}\bigg[\delta^{ij}-\frac{4\,{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}\bigg]\,{\rm tr}_{F}\big(t^{a^{\prime}}t^{b^{\prime}}t^{c^{\prime}}\big)\int dv^{+}d{w^{\prime}}^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}\Big\langle\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{c^{\prime}c}g{\mathcal{F}_{l}^{\;-}}_{c}({v}^{+},{\mathbf{b}})

\displaystyle\times\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{w^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)_{b^{\prime}b}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({w^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})\Big\rangle\Bigg\}+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{P}}^{8}}\right)\,.

(16)

The first term in the formula (
16
) is the leading-power contribution and it is of order
Q^{2}/({\mathbf{k}}^{2}\,{\mathbf{P}}^{6})
, while all other terms written explicitly are next-to-leading-power corrections, of order
Q^{2}/(|{\mathbf{k}}|\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{7})
.

Note that if
z^{-}
dependence was kept one would get the generalized eikonal cross section of a similar form to the strict eikonal cross section (
16
). These two cross sections would differ by contributions at next-to-next-to-leading-power order in the back-to-back dijet limit, which is beyond the accuracy of the present study. Thus we do not study the generalized eikonal contribution here.

The explicit next-to-eikonal corrections to the cross section, as defined in (
4
), are computed as the interference terms between the eikonal amplitude (
12
) and next-to-eikonal amplitudes (
11
). The final result, where we also include eikonal contribution and then group terms according to types of field strength correlators, can be written in the form

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\rm Eik}+{\rm NEik}}=\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}+\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}+\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{+-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}\,.

(17)

The first two terms appear as the combination of strict eikonal cross section and the next-to-eikonal interference between the amplitude (
13
) and the strict eikonal amplitude (
12
). The third term in Eq. (
17
) is obtained as the interference between the amplitude (
15
) and the strict eikonal amplitude (
12
). The three consecutive contributions in the expression (
17
) are given by

\displaystyle\,\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}=2q^{+}2\pi\,\delta(q^{+}\!-\!k^{+})\,\,4\,Q^{2}\,e^{2}e_{f}^{2}\,z_{1}^{3}z_{2}^{3}\Bigg[\frac{4{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}-2(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\frac{({\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{k}}^{j}+{\mathbf{k}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j})}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}

\displaystyle+16(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})\frac{({\mathbf{k}}\!\cdot\!{\mathbf{P}}){\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{5}}\Bigg]\int_{{\mathbf{b}},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}}\,e^{i({\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}-{\mathbf{b}})\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,\int dv^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}\,\bigg[1-i\frac{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}{2q^{+}z_{1}z_{2}}\,({v^{\prime}}^{+}\!-\!v^{+})\bigg]

\displaystyle\times\,\Big\langle g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})\Big[\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)^{{\dagger}}\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)\Big]_{ba}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Big\rangle+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{P}}^{8}}\right)+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{k}}^{2}{\mathbf{P}}^{2}W^{4}}\right)

(18)

and

\displaystyle\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}=2q^{+}2\pi\,\delta(q^{+}-k^{+})\,16\,Q^{2}\,e^{2}e_{f}^{2}\,z_{1}^{3}z_{2}^{3}\,\frac{{\mathbf{P}}^{l}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}\,

\displaystyle 2\,{\textrm{Re}}\;\,\int_{{\mathbf{b}},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}}\,e^{i({\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}-{\mathbf{b}})\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,\int dv^{+}dw^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}{\rm tr}_{F}\big(t^{a^{\prime}}t^{b^{\prime}}t^{c^{\prime}}\big)\;\Big\langle\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)_{c^{\prime}c}g{\mathcal{F}_{l}^{\;-}}_{c}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})

\displaystyle\times\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{a^{\prime}a}g{\mathcal{F}_{i}^{\;-}}_{a}({v}^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{w}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)_{b^{\prime}b}g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({w}^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Big\rangle

\displaystyle\times\Bigg\{\delta^{ij}\bigg[1-i\frac{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}{2q^{+}z_{1}z_{2}}\,\left({v^{\prime}}^{+}\!-\!\min(v^{+},w^{+})\right)\bigg]-\frac{4\,{\mathbf{P}}^{i}{\mathbf{P}}^{j}}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}\bigg[1-i\frac{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]}{2q^{+}z_{1}z_{2}}\,\left({v^{\prime}}^{+}\!-\!z_{2}v^{+}\!-\!z_{1}w^{+})\right)\bigg]\Bigg\}

\displaystyle+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{P}}^{8}}\right)+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{k}}^{2}{\mathbf{P}}^{2}W^{4}}\right)\,,

(19)

and

\displaystyle\,\frac{d\sigma_{\gamma^{*}_{L}\rightarrow q_{1}\bar{q}_{2}}}{d{\rm P.S.}}\Bigg|_{{\mathcal{F}^{+-}}{\mathcal{F}^{\perp-}}}=2q^{+}\,2\pi\,\delta(q^{+}-k^{+})\,8\,Q^{2}\,e^{2}e_{f}^{2}\,\frac{z_{1}^{2}z_{2}^{2}(z_{2}\!-\!z_{1})}{q^{+}}\;\frac{{\mathbf{P}}^{j}[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}+m^{2}]}{[{\mathbf{P}}^{2}\!+\!\bar{Q}^{2}]^{4}}\,2\,{\textrm{Re}}\,\int_{{\mathbf{b}},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}}\,e^{i({\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}-{\mathbf{b}})\cdot{\mathbf{k}}}\,

\displaystyle\times\int dv^{+}d{v^{\prime}}^{+}\Big\langle g{\mathcal{F}_{j}^{\;-}}_{b}({v^{\prime}}^{+},{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime})\Big[\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,{v^{\prime}}^{+};{\mathbf{b}}^{\prime}\right)^{{\dagger}}\mathcal{U}_{A}\left(+\infty,v^{+};{\mathbf{b}}\right)\Big]_{ba}g\mathcal{F}^{\,+-}_{a}(v^{+},{\mathbf{b}})\Big\rangle

\displaystyle+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{{\mathbf{P}}^{6}W^{2}}\right)+O\left(\frac{Q^{2}}{|{\mathbf{k}}|\,|{\mathbf{P}}|^{3}W^{4}}\right)\,.

(20)

Let us point out that the term (
14
) in the amplitude does not contribute to the cross section at next-to-eikonal accuracy because its Dirac structure vanishes

\displaystyle\sum_{h_{1},h_{2}}\,\bar{v}(2)\gamma^{+}u(1)\,\bar{u}(1)\gamma^{+}[\gamma^{i},\gamma^{j}]v(2)=0\,.

(21)

An essential observation in our results is that the next-to-leading-power and the next-to-eikonal power corrections factorize from each other in Eq. (
18
). In that expression, the next-to-leading-power corrections are purely kinematical corrections to the hard factor, while the next-to-eikonal correction (involving the light-cone time interval
{v^{\prime}}^{+}\!-\!v^{+}
between the two field strength insertions) is a correction to the operator.

To connect the cross section given by the expression (
17
) with the TMD results we introduce the following notation for the gluon field strength correlator in an unpolarized target
