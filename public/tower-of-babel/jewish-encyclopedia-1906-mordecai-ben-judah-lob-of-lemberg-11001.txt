Commentator; lived in the latter part of the seventeenth century. He was rabbi of Dobri, Bohemia. His commentary to the Pentateuch, "Ma'amar Mordekai" (Dyhernfurth, 1719), was edited by his son Judah Löb, "shammash" of Breslau.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1667; Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 405; Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, ii. 75; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 282, No. 211. W. B. S. J. L.
