MEMORANDUM FOR THE DIRECTOR OF CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE
SUBJECT: The Egyptian-Soviet Threat in the Yemen*

1. While world attention has been focused on Jordan and the
Suez Canal, Egypt and the USSR have been steadily pushing ahead with
their efforts to increase their influence in the Yemen, In view of
their setback in Jordan and the hardening attitude of King Saud,

Egypt and the USSR have a special incentive to consolidate their
position in the Yemen before it is too late. The Imam’s present
coolness toward the US, as well as the UK, presents a favorable
opportunity to do so. If the present trend continues unchecked,

Cairo and Moscow are likely to end up with a beachhead on the Arabian
Peninsula which not only threatens the British position in Aden but
also provides a flanking position against Saud,
