United Nations General Assembly Resolution 26, adopted 1946.

==============================================================================

The General Assembly decides to establish two ad hoc committees as follows:

(a) League of Nations Committee to consider the possible transfer of certain functions, activities, and assets of the League of Nations;
(b) Permanent Headquarters Committee to consider the site of the permanent headquarters of the United Nations.
Each Member will have the right to be represented on each of these two Committees.
