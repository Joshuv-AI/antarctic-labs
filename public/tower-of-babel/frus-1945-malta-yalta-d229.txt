The Ambassador in Iran (Morris) to the Secretary of State

secret

Tehran, October 10, 1944—6 p. m. [Received October 11—9:40 a. m.]

749. Prime Minister confirmed to me last night that oil concession negotiations had been postponed until after war (ReEmbstel No. 744, October 9[1]). He further stated he was immediately informing Soviet representatives here as well as representatives of American and British Oil Companies now in Tehran of this postponement.

Morris

Source: 891.6363/10–1044: Telegram

[1] Not printed.
