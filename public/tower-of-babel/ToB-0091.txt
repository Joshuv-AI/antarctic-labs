# TOB-0091 — SIXTH AND SEVENTH BOOKS OF MOSES
## Johann Scheibel | 1849 | Folk Magic / Biblical Pseudepigrapha

---

## 📖 OVERVIEW

**Full Title:** The Sixth and Seventh Books of Moses (Die sechsten und siebten Bücher Mosis)
**Author:** Compiled by Johann Friedrich Wilhelm Schreiber / compiled posthumously by Johann Scheibel (1736–1809)
**Date:** First published 1849 (Stuttgart)
**Pages:** ~200
**Language:** German / English
**Category:** Grimoire / Folk Magic / Biblical Pseudepigrapha

---

## 📚 CONTENTS

### Moses Seal and Conjurations

- The famous "Moses Seal" — a magical sigil used for numerous purposes
- Various conjurations in the name of Moses
- Psalms and biblical passages used magically
- Instructions for making magical amulets

### Practical Folk Magic

- Healing charms
- Love magic
- Curse-breaking and protection
- Finding lost objects
- Ensuring success in business
- Summoning spirits

### Magical Formulas

- "The Great and Powerful Moses" conjurations
- Spirit bindings
- Oaths and agreements
- Various secrets passed down in folk traditions

---

## 📌 HISTORICAL NOTES

**Not Biblical:** The title is misleading — Moses did not write these books. They are an 1849 German compilation of folk magic traditions, magical psalms, and mystical texts attributed to biblical figures.

**American Folk Magic Influence:** This text was enormously influential in:
- Appalachian "Granny Magic"
- Hoodoo and Conjure traditions
- Southern folk practices
- Early American spiritualist movements

**Grimories Connection:** Represents the "grimoire tradition" that crossed from Europe to America, blending with African, Native American, and other folk traditions to create uniquely American magical practices.
