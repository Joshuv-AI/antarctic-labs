Hausdorff measure of the free boundary for the p p -obstacle problem with subcritical exponents
Jing Yu, Jun Zheng

Abstract
This paper investigates a class of p p -obstacle problems with subcritical exponents having the form div ( a ( x ) | ∇ u | p − 2 ∇ u ) = m 1 χ { u > 0 } − m 2 u λ − 1 χ { u > 0 } in Ω , \displaystyle\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)=m_{1}\chi_{\{u>0\}}-m_{2}u^{\lambda-1}\chi_{\{u>0\}}\ \text{in}\ \Omega, where Ω \Omega is a smooth bounded domain in ℝ N ​ ( N ≥ 2 ) \mathbb{R}^{N}(N\geq 2) , m 1 , m 2 m_{1},m_{2} are positive constants, the coefficient function a ∈ C 2 ​ ( Ω ) a\in C^{2}(\Omega) has a positive lower bound, and 2 ≤ p < λ < p ∗ := N ​ p N − p 2\leq p<\lambda<p^{*}:=\frac{Np}{N-p} when p < N p<N and N ≥ 3 N\geq 3 , or 2 ≤ p < λ < + ∞ 2\leq p<\lambda<+\infty when N = 2 N=2 . By using the mountain-pass lemma, combined with the penalty method, we first establish the existence of non-negative weak solutions. Then, using the De Giorgi-Nash iteration, we prove the L ∞ L^{\infty} bound and local C 1 , α C^{1,\alpha} continuity for the solutions. In addition, we prove local porosity of the free boundary based on the optimal growth and non-degeneracy of solutions near the free boundary. Furthermore, by means of Lebesgue measure estimates for gradient level sets, we show that at least one solution corresponds the free boundary having locally finite ( N − 1 ) (N-1) -dimensional Hausdorff measure.

Introduction

Free boundary problems described by partial differential equations (PDEs) of elliptic type arise naturally in a wide range of physical models and variational settings, and constitute a central subject in the theory of PDEs. Typical examples include the obstacle problem, Bernoulli-type free boundary problems, chemical reaction equations, among others. A crucial theme of studying the free boundary problems is proving the existence and regularity of solutions and analyzing the geometric properties of the free boundary such as its porosity and Hausdorff measure.

Recently, the authors of
[
18
]
studied a superlinear obstacle-type free boundary problem of the form

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\Delta u=\left(1-u^{\lambda-1}\right)\chi_{\{u>0\}}\text{ in }\Omega,\\
u\geq 0\text{ in }\Omega,\\
u\in W_{0}^{1,2}(\Omega),\end{cases}

where
\Omega
is a smooth bounded domain in
\mathbb{R}^{N}(N\geq 2)
,
\chi_{\{u>0\}}(\cdot)
is the standard characteristic function,
and the exponent
\lambda
satisfies

\displaystyle 2<\lambda<\begin{cases}2^{*}:=\frac{2N}{N-2},&N\geq 3,\\
+\infty,&N=2.\end{cases}

The main challenge in studying regularity theory for such a problem
comes from the coupling of the superlinear term
u^{\lambda-1}
and the characteristic function
\chi_{\{u>0\}}
. This coupling disrupts the coercivity of the associated energy functional (thus ruling out standard minimization arguments for existence). As a consequence, classical variational methods cannot be applied straightforwardly. To overcome this obstacle, the authors of
[
18
]
employed a penalty method, combined with the mountain pass lemma, to establish the existence of nontrivial solutions, and further demonstrated that these solutions possess locally optimal regularity by means of a suitable monotonicity formula combined with a bootstrap iteration argument. Moreover, non-degeneracy of solutions near the free boundary and local porosity of the free boundary were established. Furthermore, it was shown that the free boundary possesses locally finite
(N-1)
-dimensional Hausdorff measure and a decomposition property of the free boundary was provided; see
[
18
]
.

Motivated by the superlinear obstacle-type free boundary problem studied in
[
18
]
, the aim of the present paper is to investigate a class of obstacle problems with more involved operators and nonlinear structures. In particular, in this paper, quasilinear operators of the
p
-Laplace type with variable coefficients are considered and the nonlinear term on the right-hand side of the equation allows for subcritical exponents, both of which bring more complexities in the establishment of solutions and analysis of free boundaries. More precisely, we study the following problem:

\displaystyle\begin{cases}\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)=m_{1}\chi_{\{u>0\}}-m_{2}u^{\lambda-1}\chi_{\{u>0\}}\text{ in }\Omega,\\
u\geq 0\text{ in }\Omega,\\
u\in W_{0}^{1,p}(\Omega),\end{cases}

(1)

where
\Omega
is a smooth bounded domain in
\mathbb{R}^{N}(N\geq 2)
, the coefficient function
a\in C^{2}(\Omega)
satisfies

\displaystyle 0<a_{0}\leq a\ \text{in}\ \Omega\ \ \text{and}\ \ \|a\|_{C^{2}(\Omega)}\leq a_{1}

with positive constants
a_{0}
and
a_{1}
,
m_{1},m_{2}
are positive constants, and the exponent
\lambda
satisfies

\displaystyle 2\leq p<\lambda<\begin{cases}p^{*}:=\frac{Np}{N-p},&N\geq 3,p<N,\\
+\infty,&N=2.\end{cases}

(2)

Formally, equation (
1
) corresponds to the following energy functional

\displaystyle\mathcal{J}(u)=\int_{\Omega}\frac{a(x)}{p}|\nabla u|^{p}+m_{1}u^{+}-\frac{m_{2}}{\lambda}(u^{+})^{\lambda}\mathrm{d}x,

where
u^{+}=\max\{u,0\}.

It is worth mentioning that the authors of
[
11
,
14
,
19
,
20
]
and
[
4
,
5
]
investigated the porosity and Hausdorff measure of the free boundary for the
p
-Laplace type obstacle problem with
m_{2}=0
in Sobolev spaces, and for the
A
-Laplace type obstacle problem with
m_{2}=0
in Orlicz spaces, respectively. The porosity and Hausdorff measure of the free boundary for a
p
-Laplace obstacle problem associated with the Ginzburg-Landau equation, for which the coefficient of the superlinear term is non-negative, were studied in
[
10
]
. It is also worth mentioning that the porosity of the free boundary for the
p
-Laplace non-zero obstacle problems was established in
[
2
]
. Nevertheless, the regularity, in particular, the Hausdorff measure of the free boundary, for the
p
-Laplace obstacle problem with variable coefficients and general subcritical terms remain unexplored.

In this paper, we first prove the existence and regularity of weak solutions of the
p
-Laplace type obstacle problem (
1
), and then study the local porosity and finite
(N-1)
-dimensional Hausdorff measure of the free boundary
\Upsilon^{+}:=\partial\{u>0\}\cap\Omega
. The main contribution of this paper is twofold:

•

By using a regularization and penalty method, we prove the existence and regularity of weak solutions for a wider class of
p
-Laplace type obstacle problems, which extends relevant results obtained in
[
18
]
for the obstacle problem governed by the Laplacian.

•

We overcome the technical difficulties arising from the general
p
, the variable coefficients, and the nonlinearity of subcritical terms and show that for each solution the free boundary is locally porous and that at least one solution corresponds the free boundary having locally finite
(N-1)
-dimensional Hausdorff measure, extending the work in the literature
[
18
,
11
,
14
,
19
,
10
,
2
,
20
]
.

The paper is organized as follows. In Section
2

The first result is concerned with the optima growth of solutions near the free boundary.

Proposition 5.8
.

Let

and

Proof 5.9
.

The following result gives the non-degeneracy of weak solutions of equation (

) near the free boundary

Proposition 5.10
.

and let

with

where

and

are positive constants the same as in Proposition

.

Proof 5.11
.

Let
y\in\{u>0\}
. For any

satisfying

, we have

with

. Define
C_{2}:=\frac{p-1}{p}\left(\frac{m_{1}}{2N(a_{1}+1)}\right)^{\frac{1}{p-1}}
and

in
B_{r}(y)
. By direct computations, we have

\displaystyle\nabla v

\displaystyle=C_{2}\frac{p}{p-1}|x-y|^{\frac{2-p}{p-1}}(x-y),

\displaystyle|\nabla v|

\displaystyle=C_{2}\frac{p}{p-1}|x-y|^{\frac{1}{p-1}},

\displaystyle|\nabla v|^{p-2}

\displaystyle=C_{2}^{p-2}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-2}|x-y|^{\frac{p-2}{p-1}}.

Noting that
r_{2}<\frac{1}{a_{1}}
and using the definition of
C_{2}
, we obtain

\displaystyle\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)|\nabla v|^{p-2}\nabla v\right)=

\displaystyle\mathrm{div}\left(C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}a(x)(x-y)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)(x-y)\right)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle NC_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}a(x)+C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}(x-y)\nabla a(x)

\displaystyle Na_{1}C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}+Na_{1}r_{2}C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle Na_{1}C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}+NC_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle N(a_{1}+1)C_{2}^{p-1}\left(\frac{p}{p-1}\right)^{p-1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m_{1}}{2}\ \text{in}\ B_{r}(y).

and Proposition

, we deduce that

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle m_{1}-m_{2}C_{1}^{\lambda-1}r_{2}^{\frac{p(\lambda-1)}{p-1}}

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{m_{1}}{2}\ \text{in}\ B_{r}(y)\cap\{u>0\},

\displaystyle-\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)|\nabla u|^{p-2}\nabla u\right)\leq-\mathrm{div}\left(a(x)|\nabla v|^{p-2}\nabla v\right)\ \text{in}\ B_{r}(y)\cap\{u>0\}.

which, along with the continuity of
