Rabbi of Byelostok, Russia, in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries; author of "Sha'agat Aryeh" (Byelostok, 1805), novellæ on the treatise Makkot. The author quotes frequently his father's "Ner Tamid" (Grodno, 1789); and in the preface he states that he has written novellæ on the whole Talmud.

Bibliography: Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 553; Walden, Shem ha-Gedolim he-Ḥadash, p. 79. H. R. M. Sel.
