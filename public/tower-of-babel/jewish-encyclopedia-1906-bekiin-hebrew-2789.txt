A small town in Palestine, between Jabneh and Lydda. It is mentioned as the seat of a Talmudical school founded by R. Joshua ben Hananiah during the reign of Gamaliel II. (Bab. Sanh. 32 b ; Yer. Ḥag. i. 75 d ; Tosef., Soṭah, vii. 9).

Bibliography: Neubauer, La Géographie du Talmud, p. 81. G. I. Br.
