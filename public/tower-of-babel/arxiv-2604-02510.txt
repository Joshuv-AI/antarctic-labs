Abstract
We present a broadly applicable structurally flat triangular form for x x -flat control-affine systems with three inputs. Building on recent results for the derivative structure of flat outputs, we define the triangular form together with regularity conditions that guarantee structural flatness, and derive necessary and sufficient conditions for a system with a given x x -flat output to be static feedback equivalent to this form. Further, we present sufficient conditions under which general x x -flat three-input systems can be rendered static feedback equivalent to the proposed triangular form after a finite number of input prolongations.
