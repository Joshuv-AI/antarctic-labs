Introduction

The kernel of the universal central extension of
\mathscr{G}\otimes\mathscr{A}
where
\mathscr{G}
is a finite dimensional simple Lie algebra and
\mathscr{A}
is a unital commutative associative algebra, is the Kähler differentials
\Omega(\mathscr{A})
of
\mathscr{A}
modulo the exact Kähler differentials
d(\mathscr{A})
; see
[
9
,
15
]
. A toroidal Lie algebra is the universal central extension
(\mathscr{G}\otimes\dot{\mathscr{A}})\oplus\frac{\Omega(\dot{\mathscr{A}})}{d({\dot{\mathscr{A}}})}
of
\mathscr{G}\otimes\dot{\mathscr{A}}
where
\dot{\mathscr{A}}
is the set of Laurent polynomials in
\ell
variables
t_{1},\ldots,t_{\ell}
.
The split central extension
{\rm Der}(\dot{\mathscr{A}})\oplus\frac{\Omega(\dot{\mathscr{A}})}{d({\dot{\mathscr{A}}})}
of
\dot{\mathscr{D}}:={\rm Der}(\dot{\mathscr{A}})
appears in generalizing the vertex operator construction on Fock spaces
[
6
]
.
Then, one can construct

\tau:=(\mathfrak{g}\otimes\dot{\mathscr{A}})\oplus\dot{\mathscr{D}}\oplus\frac{\Omega(\dot{\mathscr{A}})}{d({\dot{\mathscr{A}}})}

which is known in the literature as a full toroidal Lie algebra. In
[
8
]
, the authors prove that the classification of simple modules over
\tau
is reduced to the classification of simple so-called
\dot{\mathscr{A}}\dot{\mathscr{D}}
-modules. In
[
13
]
, the author constructs a class of simple
\dot{\mathscr{A}}\dot{\mathscr{D}}
-modules referred to as Shen-Larsson modules and in
[
5
]
, the author shows that Shen-Larsson modules exhaust all simple
\dot{\mathscr{A}}\dot{\mathscr{D}}
-modules having finite weight space decomposition with respect to
\{{\tt d}_{i}:=t_{i}\frac{d}{dt_{i}}\mid 1\leq i\leq\ell\}
with bounded weight multiplicities.
The pair
(\dot{\mathscr{A}},\dot{\mathscr{D}})
is an example of a Lie-Rinehart pair
(A,\mathfrak{k})

which contains a unital supercommutative associative superalgebra
(A,\cdot)
as well as a Lie superalgebra
(\mathfrak{k},[\cdot,\cdot])
satisfying the following:

•

\mathfrak{k}
is an
A
-module; we denote the action of
a\in A
on
\delta\in\mathfrak{k}
by
a\delta,

•

\mathfrak{k}
acts on
A
as derivations; we denote the action of
\delta\in\mathfrak{k}
on
a\in A
by
\delta(a),

•

for
a\in A
and
\delta,\gamma\in\mathfrak{k},
we have
[a\delta,b\gamma]=a\delta(b)\gamma-(-1)^{|a\delta||b\gamma|}b\gamma(a)\delta+(-1)^{|b||\delta|}ab[\delta,\gamma].

For a Lie-Rienhart pair
(A,\mathfrak{k})
, we say that a superspace
\Omega
is an
A\mathfrak{k}
-module if
\Omega
is both an
A
-module and a
\mathfrak{k}
-module satisfying the Leibniz rule

\delta(a\omega)=\delta(a)\omega+(-1)^{|\delta||a|}a(\delta\omega)\hskip 10.00002pt(a\in A,~\delta\in\mathfrak{k},~\omega\in\Omega).

If
\Phi:U(\mathfrak{k})\longrightarrow U(\mathfrak{k})\otimes U(\mathfrak{k})
is the natural co-product of the Hopf algebra
U(\mathfrak{k})
with
\Phi(u)=\displaystyle{\sum_{i}u_{i}^{1}\otimes u_{i}^{2}},
one can construct the smash product
A\raisebox{0.86108pt}{\scalebox{.6}{$\displaystyle~\#\;$}}U(\mathfrak{k})
whose underlying vector space is

with the product

(a\otimes u)\raisebox{0.86108pt}{\scalebox{.6}{$\displaystyle~\#\;$}}(b\otimes v)=\sum_{i}(-1)^{|b||u_{i}^{2}|}au_{i}^{1}(b)\otimes u_{i}^{2}v;

here

is the induced action of

on
A
.
A\mathfrak{k}
-modules are in fact modules over the associative superalgebra

and they are recovered by the action of
A\raisebox{0.86108pt}{\scalebox{.6}{$\displaystyle~\#\;$}}\mathfrak{k}.

Assume

\zeta_{1},\ldots,\zeta_{n}

A

-module

is called a (strong) Harish-Chandra module (resp. cuspidal (strong) Harish-Chandra module) if

\mathfrak{k}_{A}

We introduce the concept of a (cuspidal) QP-module over a Lie-Rinehart pair and for

-modules and simple cuspidal QP-modules over

We classify simple cuspidal QP-modules over
(\dot{A},\dot{\mathfrak{k}})
and as a byproduct, we obtain the classification of simple cuspidal Harish-Chandra
A\mathfrak{k}_{A}
-modules.

-module. A simple cuspidal QP-module over

The subalgebra

In fact, the classification of simple cuspidal QP-modules of
