A, Overall functions and responsibilities

The functions and responsibilities of the Graphics
Register as stated in the overall CIA Organization
and Function Charts are bel(|&ved, after examination,
to be generally defined and sound in concept. The
personnel assigned have clearly indicated a sincerity
of purpose and a desire to perform the best possible
service from an Agency standpoint.

B. Operational Procedures

Certain inherent procedural difficulties were disclosed,
most of these were stated to be mandatory either for
records of individual attainment or for utilization by
higher authority. The routine administrative procedures
were found to be decentralized and as a result profes-
sional personnel were utilizing ■ approximately 40$ of
their available time for these functions.
