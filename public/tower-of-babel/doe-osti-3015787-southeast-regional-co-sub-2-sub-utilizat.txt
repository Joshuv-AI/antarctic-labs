SECARB-USA: Activities
• One of four DOE Regional Initiatives tasked with identifying and
  removing barriers to the commercial deployment of CCUS
• Developed a needs assessment framework for prospective storage
  complexes in the region based on Class VI requirements
• Preliminary evaluation of regional storage potential and costs using
  NRAP and SCO2T
• Identified prospective sub-basins in the region for CO2 storage
• Developed an inventory of non-technical challenges to the
  commercial deployment of CCS
• Identified need for further evaluation of CO 2 storage potential in
  under explored basins in the region (~ half of the region’s point
  source emissions) SECARB-USA 2022 Fact Sheet

• Drilled stratigraphic test wells in Alabama, Georgia, and Virginia

Source-Sink Matching
• Evaluated the storage capacity and associated injection & storage cost for
  the 69 potential saline storage targets in the SECARB region using SCO2T
  tool
• Determined point sources, emission amount, and corresponding costs
• About 70% CO2 emission are eligible for 45Q tax credit
• Electricity (gas), chemical manufacturing, and Ag/food manufacturing are
  responsible for the majority of emission

 69 saline formations are
 eligible to store CO2
 (NATCARB Database)

Source-Sink Matching
• Built pipeline scenarios in five phases for a
  total of 20 years
• Pipeline length in phase-based mechanism
  varies incrementally in each phase - the length
  is defined by the CO2 transportation amount
• The volume of CO2 assumed captured and
  stored in each is 200, 400, 600, 800 and 956
  million tons per year
• The captured volume at each phase, except
  the last one, is 200 million tons/year
• The pipeline length in each phase includes
  7200, 11400, 13800, 16000 and 17400 miles
• Scaled capture, storage, and transport costs
  are 91.3%, 6.6%, and 2.3%, respectively

Concluding Remarks
SECARB-USA created a flexible platform to expand CCUS in the Southeast

projects (TXLA-CMC, ACS:DSE), and numerous projects that have not pursued federal funds

decarbonization challenges
