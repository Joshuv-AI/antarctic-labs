Abstract
A sign pattern is a matrix whose entries belong to { + , − , 0 } \{+,-,0\} . Let 𝒫 \mathcal{P} be a symmetric sign pattern and A A a real symmetric matrix in its qualitative class. A vertex v j v_{j} of the underlying graph of 𝒫 \mathcal{P} is called a P P -vertex if m A ⁡ ( j ) ​ ( 0 ) − m A ​ ( 0 ) = 1 , m_{A(j)}(0)-m_{A}(0)=1, where A ⁡ ( j ) A(j) is the principal submatrix obtained by deleting the j j -th row and column of A A , and m A ​ ( 0 ) , m A ⁡ ( j ) ​ ( 0 ) m_{A}(0),\penalty\ m_{A(j)}(0) denotes the algebraic multiplicity of the eigenvalue 0 0 of A , A ⁡ ( j ) A,\penalty\ A(j) , respectively. We say that 𝒫 \mathcal{P} requires full P P -vertices if every symmetric matrix in its qualitative class has all vertices as P P -vertices. In this paper, we investigate structural conditions under which symmetric sign patterns require full P P -vertices. We also derive necessary and sufficient conditions for sign patterns whose underlying graphs contain cycles but no loops to require full P P -vertices.

The structure of this paper is as follows. In Section

4
Conclusions

We have studied the full
P
-vertex problem for symmetric sign patterns and established structural criteria for requiring full
P
-vertices. In particular, we obtained necessary and sufficient conditions for important classes of graphs, including trees and unicyclic graphs. We showed that a tree sign pattern with a zero diagonal requires full
P
-vertices if and only if its underlying graph admits a perfect matching. We also derived several conditions for sign patterns whose underlying graph contain cycles and characterized such pattern requiring full
P
-vertices.

Although Theorem
3.8
addresses sign patterns whose underlying graph contain more than one cycle, the general problem remains open. Further research may focus on broader classes of graphs, including bicyclic and more general cyclic structures, to determine conditions under which they require full
P
-vertices.
