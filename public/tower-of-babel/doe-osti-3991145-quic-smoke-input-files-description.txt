1 Introduction
QUIC-SMOKE is a fast-running Lagrangian particle dispersion model that simulates the atmospheric transport, dispersion, and deposition of gasses
and particulates. The model tracks individual particle trajectories through three-dimensional wind fields, accounting for turbulent mixing,
gravitational settling, and surface deposition processes. QUIC-SMOKE is run coupled to QUIC-Fire to simulate smoke transport from wildland fires.

In the following, x-direction refers to the east-west direction in UTM or CONUS Albers coordinates, y-direction refers to the north-south direction,
z-direction refers to the direction perpendicular to the (x, y) plane. The indexes (i, j, k) are used to identify domain cells in the three directions
respectively.

2.6 QP_observations.inp

or time-varying paths. The file begins with the number of sensors and an averaging period, followed by one block per sensor containing its
type/flag, number of path points, and the corresponding time-speed-position entries.
