Russian scholar; lived at Shklov in the nineteenth century. He was the author of "Oẓar ha-Shemot," a concordance of the proper names found in the Bible, forming a supplement to the general Biblical concordance published at Wilna in 1878; and of "Massoret ha-Ḳeri'ah" (Warsaw, 1888), a Masoretic and grammatical manual for the public reader of the Law.

Bibliography: Zeitlin, Bibl. Post-Mendels. p. 356. H. R. I. Br.
