Abstract
. Let Γ \Gamma be a cancellation monoid and R = ⨁ α ∈ Γ R α R=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma}R_{\alpha} be a Γ \Gamma -graded ring. It is shown that R R is graded left semihereditary if and only if R R is graded left coherent and every graded submodule of a flat left R R -module is flat. Hence it gives a new characterization of graded-Prüfer domains.



1.
Introduction and preliminaries

Let
\Gamma
be a cancelation monoid, and
R=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma}R_{\alpha}
be a
\Gamma
-graded ring with identity. The authors in
[
1

defined and studied the notion of graded left (right) semihereditary rings which is a generalization of graded-Prüfer domains. Recall that
R

if each finitely generated homogeneous left (right) ideal of

R

Assume that

Recall that a graded ring

is a
graded left (right) coherent ring
if every finitely generated graded left (right) ideal is finitely presented
[

. In

Theorem 2.10
.

Assume that
R=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma}R_{\alpha}
is a
\Gamma
-graded ring. Then
R
is graded left semihereditary if and only if
R
is graded left coherent and every graded submodule of a gr-flat left
R
-module is gr-flat.

Proof.

Assume first that
R
is graded left semihereditary. Then every finitely generated homogeneous left ideal

is (gr-)projective; hence,

is finitely presented, by
[

, Proposition 3.11]
. Therefore
R
is graded left coherent. Since every finitely generated homogeneous left ideal is (gr-)projective, it is (gr-)flat. It follows from
[
1
, Remark 3.14(1)]
that every graded left ideal is gr-flat. Hence every graded submodule of a gr-flat left
R
-module is gr-flat by Corollary
2.9
.

Conversely, if
I
is a finitely generated homogeneous left ideal, then
I
is a graded submodule of the gr-flat module
R
, and so
I
is gr-flat; since
R
is left coherent,
I
is also finitely presented. Hence,
I
is projective, by
[
1

, and so
R
is graded left semihereditary.
∎

Similarly one can prove the above statement by replacing “right” instead of “left”.

Corollary 2.11
.

Assume that
R=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Gamma}R_{\alpha}
is a
\Gamma
-graded ring. Then
R
is graded left (right) semihereditary if and only if
R
is graded left (right) coherent and
\text{gr-wD}(R)\leq 1
.

Recall that a
\Gamma
-graded commutative integral domain
R
is a
graded-Prüfer domain
if each nonzero finitely generated homogeneous ideal of

is invertible, equivalently projective.

Corollary 2.12
.

Assume that

\Gamma
-graded integral domain. Then
R
is graded-Prüfer if and only if
R
is graded left coherent and
\text{gr-wD}(R)\leq 1

Remark 2.13
.

Assume

\Gamma
