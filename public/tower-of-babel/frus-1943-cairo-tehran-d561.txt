Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (9 March 1944, d561)

secret

London, 9 March 1944.

613. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt. Personal and secret.…[2]

You will remember that we discussed the dollar balances in Cairo on December 8th and that I gave a memorandum to Harry.[3] I certainly understood that you felt we ought not to be treated worse than France or Russia in these matters.…

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent via military channels.
[2] For the full text of this telegram, see Churchill, p. 697.
[3] Roosevelt left Cairo on the morning of December 7, 1943. For what is believed to be the memorandum given to Harry Hopkins, see ante, p. 822.
