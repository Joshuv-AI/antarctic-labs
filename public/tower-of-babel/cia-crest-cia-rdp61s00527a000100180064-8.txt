Note a. Progress Report of Ad Hoc Subcommittee on List Review

The Chairman of the Ad Hoc Subcommittee reported that he hopes that by
about November the basic job of the Subcommittee should be completed except
for ,! tag ends”.

He reported that to date, except for the one recommendation that was made
with respect to criteria, there have been no differences of view on the inter-
pretative problem, and he doubts that there will be any differences in the

categories to come.

In response to the State members Inquiry regarding the British request
for a redefinition of electronics items, the Commerce member said the Sub-
committee is taking it into account, but there does not seem to be any justi-
fiable rationale for modifying the current coverage of those items, and it
does not appear to make good technical or strategic sense. The State member
pointed out that the British have said they have experts in this field who are
