Radiative decays of the 1 P P , 1 D D , 2 S S , and 2 P P Λ c \Lambda_{c} and 1 D D , 2 S S , and 2 P P Ξ c \Xi_{c} charmed baryons
R. Gamboa-Goni, Ailier Rivero-Acosta, H. García-Tecocoatzi, A. Gutierrez-Rodriguez, A. Ramirez-Morales, E. Santopinto, Carlos Alberto Vaquera-Araujo

Abstract
We analyze the radiative decays of the the 1 P P , 1 D D , 2 S S , and 2 P P Λ c \Lambda_{c} and 1 D D , 2 S S , and 2 P P Ξ c \Xi_{c} charmed baryons, which belong to the flavor anti-triplet ( 𝟑 ¯ F \bf{\bar{3}}_{\rm F} ), using the constituent quark model. We compute electromagnetic transitions from ground and P P -wave states to ground states, as well as from second-shell states to both ground and P P -wave final states. Electromagnetic decay widths are especially valuable for identifying resonances when multiple states share the same mass and total decay width. We give branching ratios which can confirm the assignment of the Ξ c ​ ( 3055 ) \Xi_{c}(3055) reported by LHCb. We also give branching ratios that can support the assignment of the Ξ c ​ ( 3080 ) \Xi_{c}(3080) , and discuss the possibilities for the Ξ c ​ ( 3080 ) \Xi_{c}(3080) to be the 1 D D state with J P = 5 / 2 + J^{P}=5/2^{+} or the 2 S S with J P = 1 / 2 + J^{P}=1/2^{+} . For the first time, this work provides calculations of electromagnetic decays for D ρ D_{\rho} -wave states, ρ − λ \rho-\lambda mixed configurations, and ρ \rho -mode radially excited states in singly charmed baryons of the flavor anti-triplet. Both experimental and model-dependent uncertainties are taken into account throughout our analysis.

Introduction

The recent observation of numerous excited states in the heavy-baryon sector has considerably improved our understanding of singly heavy baryons. Charmed baryons, in particular, provide a prominent example of this progress. In addition to the determination of their masses and decay widths, increasing attention has been devoted to measuring their quantum numbers, which play a crucial role in establishing a consistent connection between theoretical predictions and experimental observations. \displaystyle 2\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{k}}\sum^{3}_{j=1}\mu_{j}\left[{\rm k}\,\mathbf{s}_{j,-}e^{-i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r}_{j}}\right.

\displaystyle-

\displaystyle\left.\frac{1}{2}\left(\mathbf{p}_{j,-}e^{-i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r}_{j}}+e^{-i\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r}_{j}}\mathbf{p}_{j,-}\right)\right],

II.3
Uncertainties

Uncertainties are reported for each electromagnetic decay width and arise from the masses of the charmed baryons involved in the transition
A\rightarrow A^{\prime}\gamma
, taken from PDG measurements
Navas et al. 2024
, together with a model uncertainty associated with the mass description in Eq. (
). Error propagation is performed through a Monte Carlo procedure in which baryon masses are sampled from Gaussian distributions centered on either the PDG values or the theoretical predictions of Ref.
Garcia-Tecocoatzi et al. 2023
, with widths given by the combined experimental and model uncertainties. For each sampled set, the decay width is computed and the procedure is repeated
10^{3}
times. The mean value defines the central result, while the 68% confidence interval is obtained from quantiles. Fits and uncertainty propagation are performed using
MINUIT

James and Roos 1975
and
NUMPY

Harris et al. 2020
.

III.2
Discussion

Electromagnetic decay widths are generally smaller than the dominant strong and weak decay widths. Nevertheless, they can serve as powerful tools since they allow the estimation of observable branching ratios in collider experiments, particularly when resonances exhibit similar masses and strong decay widths.
In particular, we focus on the electromagnetic decay widths of the
\Xi_{c}(3055)^{+,0}
and
\Xi_{c}(3080)^{+,0}
states. In Ref.
Garcia-Tecocoatzi et al. 2023
, these resonances were interpreted as members of the flavor sextet. These assignments were proposed in a context where angular-distribution measurements were unavailable and several nearby resonances had been reported in the literature, making their identification especially challenging.
Fortunately, recent experimental measurements by the LHCb collaboration
Aaij et al. 2025a
have established the quantum numbers of the charmed
\Xi_{c}(3055)^{+,0}
states. Motivated by these results, we revisit their assignment and interpret them instead as members of the flavor anti-triplet.
In the same spirit, we also discuss the
\Xi_{c}(3080)^{+,0}
states, whose quantum numbers have not yet been experimentally determined, and likewise consider them as anti-triplet states.
These states are displayed in the mass spectra shown in Fig.
2
. In the following discussion, we provide information that may help establish their quantum number assignments based on the electromagnetic decay widths calculated in this work.

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{+}\gamma]}{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{*+}\gamma]}=0.06^{+0.01}_{-0.01}\,.

(19)

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{0}\gamma]}{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{{}^{\prime}0}\gamma]}=0.7^{+0.2}_{-0.2}\,,

(20)

\displaystyle\frac{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{0}\gamma]}{\Gamma_{em}[\Xi_{c}(3080)\to\Xi_{c}^{*0}\gamma]}=3^{+0.5}_{-0.5}\,.

(21)

We observe that the electromagnetic branching ratios for the decays into
\Xi_{c}^{+/0}\gamma
,
\Xi_{c}^{\prime+/0}\gamma
, and
\Xi_{c}^{*+/0}\gamma
differ significantly between the
\mathbf{J^{P}}=1/2^{+}
and
\mathbf{J^{P}}=5/2^{+}
assignments. Therefore, these branching ratios may serve as useful observables for distinguishing the correct quantum-number assignment of the
\Xi_{c}(3080)
states.

IV
Conclusions

We calculate the electromagnetic decay widths for the
\Lambda_{c}
and
\Xi_{c}
charmed baryons belonging to the flavor
{\mathbf{\bar{3}}}_{\rm F}
-plet.
We consider transitions from ground and
P
-wave states to ground states for the
\Lambda_{c}
baryons, since transitions involving the
\Xi_{c}
baryons at this level were already addressed in our previous work
García-Tecocoatzi et al. 2025
. We also analyze transitions from all second-shell
\Lambda_{c}
and
\Xi_{c}
states to both ground and
P
-wave final states.

The EMDs of
P
-wave states have been investigated in Refs.
Chow 1996
,
Gamermann et al. 2011
,
Zhu 2000
,
Luo et al. 2025b
,
Bijker et al. 2022
,
Ortiz-Pacheco and Bijker 2023
,
Wang et al. 2017b
,
Peng et al. 2024
,
Ivanov et al. 1999a
,
Ivanov et al. 1999b
,
Tawfiq et al. 2001
. The EMDs of second shell singly charmed baryons were studied in Refs.
Yao et al. 2018
,
Peng et al. 2024
; however, in both cases only a subset of second shell states was considered.

It is worth mentioning that this is the first calculation of the electromagnetic decays of
D_{\rho}
-wave states,
\rho\lambda
mixed states, and radially excited
\rho
-mode states for singly charmed baryons belonging to the flavor antitriplet.

This is particularly relevant, as electromagnetic decay widths can assist in the assignment procedure, when different states have similar masses and strong decay widths, as illustrated in Sec.
III
. Our interpretation of the
\Xi_{c}(3055)^{+,-}
baryons as
D_{\lambda}
-wave excitations with
J^{P}=3/2^{+}
and
S=1/2
, belonging to the flavor antitriplet, is consistent with the recent LHCb determination
Aaij et al. 2025a
. The predicted mass and decay width are also in agreement with the values reported by the PDG
Navas et al. 2024
.

Finally, for the present work, we have accounted for the propagation of parameter uncertainties using a Monte Carlo bootstrap method. The inclusion of uncertainties, often missed in similar and related research studies, is essential for a statistical significant comparison between theory and experiment to take place, driving future discussion.
