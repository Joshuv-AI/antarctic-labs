'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 3219th meeting on 26 May 1993'''


''The Security Council'',

''Having examined'' the application of the Principality of Monaco for admission to
the United Nations (S/25796),

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Principality of Monaco be admitted
to membership in the United Nations.
