Open Gromov–Witten invariants in genus zero and Lagrangian cobordisms
Amanda Hirschi, Kai Hugtenburg

Abstract
. We construct open Gromov–Witten invariants in genus zero for arbitrary closed symplectic manifolds and embedded relatively spin Lagrangians which are weakly unobstructed by a bounding cochain. This uses the foundational work of [ 16 , 15 ] and the algebraic framework of [ 27 ] . We prove the open WDVV relations and show that these invariants are independent of the choice of almost complex structure and under Hamiltonian isotopy. We also prove a relation between open Gromov–Witten invariants of cobordant Lagrangians.

1.1.
Context

Let

In

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k,\ell}(\beta)
.
∎

2.2.
Recursive structure of the boundary

We restate
[

, Theorem 3.5]
for the special case discs. Thus, there are two types of boundary degeneration: disc bubbling and boundary collapse. The latter happens exactly if the boundary carries no marked points. In either case, we can obtain a global Kuranishi chart for the (normalised) boundary stratum by pulling back the global Kuranishi chart
\mathcal{K}_{\alpha}
constructed in the previous subsection. To make this concrete, suppose

and

(2.6)

\vartheta_{L,i}\colon\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k_{0}+1,I}(\beta_{0})\times_{L}\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k_{1}+1,J}(\beta_{0})\to\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k+1,\ell}(\beta)

, §3]
we denote this map by

We recall the convention that the boundary marked points are ordered anti-clockwise. Thus, we have to reorder marked points as shown in

, Assumption 3.3]

Similarly, by

2.3.
Cubical cobordisms

From the proof of the equivalence statement in
[
16
, Theorem 3.5]
, we obtain in particular that (a stabilisation) of the fibre product global Kuranishi chart of
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k_{0}+1,I}(\beta_{0})\times_{L}\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{k_{1}+1,I}(\beta_{1})

3.1
. The details can be found in
[
16
, §4]
, especially Theorem 4.1; we just describe the idea behind the construction as it might be more enlightening.
Define the sets

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{o}

\displaystyle\coloneqq\left\{(\beta,k,\ell)\mid\beta\in H_{2}(X,L;\mathbb{Z}),\,\omega(\beta),k,\ell\geq 0\right\}

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}_{c}

\displaystyle\coloneqq\left\{(\beta,\ell)\mid\beta\in H_{2}(X;\mathbb{Z}),\,\omega(\beta),\ell\geq 0\right\},

and set
\mathcal{A}:=\mathcal{A}_{o}\sqcup\mathcal{A}_{c}
. For each
\mathsf{a}\in\mathcal{A}
let
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathsf{a}}^{\,J}(X,L)
denote the moduli space of
J
holomorphic stable maps (from either a sphere or disc) of type
\mathsf{a}
. By a
stable map tree

\Gamma
we mean the combinatorial data underlying the domain of a (possibly open) stable map; see
[
16
, Definition 4.16]
for the precise definition. In contrast with
[
16
]
, we do not explicitly divide marked points into incoming or outgoing ones; the (boundary or interior) marked point labelled by
0
should be thought of as outgoing. Each vertex

\mathsf{a}_{v}\in\mathcal{A}

L

(2.10)

\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\Gamma}^{\,J}(X,L)/\text{Aut}(\Gamma)\to\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathsf{a}}^{\,J}(X,L)

onto a (virtual) boundary or divisor stratum
\partial_{\Gamma}\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathsf{a}}^{\,J}(X,L)
of
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathsf{a}}^{\,J}(X,L)
. We consider only the morphisms
\Gamma\to\Gamma^{\prime}
given by contraction of edges (and corresponding merging of decorations) and the contraction of the boundary of a disc to a point. Such a morphism is

if it contracts a single edge or only turns a single interior marked point into a boundary node.

If we choose an unobstructed auxiliary datum
\alpha_{\mathsf{a}}
for each
\mathsf{a}\in\mathcal{A}
with

and the trivial auxiliary datum for

2.1
yields a global Kuranishi chart

\mathsf{a}\in\mathcal{A}

and
(\beta,0,0)

and
(\beta,0)

\mathcal{K}_{\Gamma}

\Gamma
.

.

\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}|_{\{t_{e}=0\}}\;\stackrel{{\scriptstyle\sim\,}}{{\longrightarrow}}\;(\pm 1)\,\partial_{\Gamma}\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma_{e}}

3.
Open Gromov–Witten invariants in genus zero

For any smooth manifold
Y
, let
\Omega^{*}(Y;\Lambda)\coloneqq\Omega^{*}(Y)\otimes\Lambda
denote the completed tensor product. Throughout this section,
(X,\omega)
is a closed symplectic manifold of real dimension
2n
and

is a nonempty embedded Lagrangian equipped with a relative spin structure
(V,\mathfrak{s})
.

Consider the Novikov rings

\displaystyle\Lambda\,

\displaystyle\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}Q^{\beta_{i}}\,\big|\,a_{i}\in\mathbb{R},\;\beta_{i}\in H_{2}(X,L),\;\lim_{i\to\infty}\omega(\beta_{i})=\infty\right\}

\displaystyle\Lambda_{c}

\displaystyle\coloneqq\left\{\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}a_{i}Q^{\beta_{i}}\,\big|\,a_{i}\in\mathbb{R},\;\beta_{i}\in H_{2}(X),\;\lim_{i\to\infty}\omega(\beta_{i})=\infty\right\}

These are graded rings with
|Q^{\beta}|=\mu(\beta)
and
|Q^{\beta}|=2c_{1}(\beta)
respectively.
We write
|\alpha|
for the degree of a differential form
\alpha
and set
|\alpha|^{\prime}:=|\alpha|-1
.

3.1.
Construction of the
\mathfrak{q}
-operations

Suppose now that we have the data of a system
\{\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}\}_{\Gamma}
of cubical cobordisms as defined in the previous subsection and an associated Thom system
\{\eta_{\Gamma}\}
. We assume that if
\Gamma
has energy zero, then
\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}
is the trivial global Kuranishi chart
I^{E(\Gamma)}\times\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\Gamma}
.
In what follows, it might be helpful to think of the ‘total chart’ for
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathsf{a}}(X,L)
as the
spiky chart

\mathcal{K}_{\mathsf{a}}^{+}
given by

\mathcal{K}_{\mathsf{a}}^{+}\coloneqq\bigsqcup\limits_{\begin{subarray}{c}\Gamma\rightarrow\mathsf{a}\end{subarray}}\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}.

For any
\mathsf{a}
, let
\partial(\mathsf{a})
denote the set of stable map trees
\Gamma
which contract to
\mathsf{a}
and are such that
\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}
has the same virtual dimension as
\mathcal{K}_{\mathsf{a}}
. Then
\Gamma\in\partial(\mathsf{a})
if and only if all edges of
\Gamma
correspond to codimension
1
phenomena, that is, disc bubbling, or boundary contracting.
For
k,\ell\geq 0
and
\beta\in H_{2}(X,L)
with
(k,\ell,\beta)\notin\{(1,0,\beta_{0}),(0,0,\beta_{0})\}
,
define the operations

\mathfrak{q}_{k,\ell}^{\beta}\colon\Omega^{*}(L)^{\otimes k}\otimes\Omega^{*}(X)^{\otimes\ell}\rightarrow\Omega^{*}(L)

as follows. Let
\mathsf{a}=(k+1,\ell,\beta)
and set

(3.1)

\mathfrak{q}_{k,\ell}^{\beta}(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k};\gamma_{1},\dots,\gamma_{\ell})\coloneqq(-1)^{\epsilon(\alpha)}\sum_{\Gamma\in\partial\mathsf{a}}(evb_{0}^{\Gamma})_{*}\bigg(\bigwedge_{j=1}^{\ell}(evi^{\Gamma}_{j})^{*}\gamma_{j}\wedge\bigwedge_{i=1}^{k}(evb_{i}^{\Gamma})^{*}\alpha_{j}\wedge s_{\Gamma}^{*}\eta_{\Gamma}\bigg),

where
\epsilon(\alpha)\coloneqq 1+\sum_{j=1}^{k}j|\alpha_{j}|^{\prime}
. The special (unstable) cases are

\mathfrak{q}_{1,0}^{\beta_{0}}\coloneqq d\alpha\qquad\quad\text{and}\quad\qquad\mathfrak{q}_{0,0}^{\beta_{0}}\coloneqq 0.

Similarly, we define disc counts with only interior marked points

\mathfrak{q}_{-1,\ell}^{\beta}\colon\Omega^{*}(X)^{\otimes\ell}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}.

by setting

(3.2)

\mathfrak{q}_{-1,\ell}^{\beta}(\gamma_{1},\dots,\gamma_{\ell})\coloneqq\sum_{\Gamma\in\partial(0,\ell,\beta)}c_{\Gamma}\int_{\mathcal{T}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}}\bigwedge_{j=1}^{\ell}(evi^{\Gamma}_{j})^{*}\gamma_{j}\wedge s_{\Gamma}^{*}\eta_{\Gamma}

for
(\ell,\beta)\notin\{(0,\beta_{0}),(0,\beta_{0})\}
,
c_{\Gamma}=\frac{|\text{Aut}(\Gamma)|}{|\text{Aut}(\Gamma^{ps})|}
with
\Gamma^{ps}
being the prestable graph obtained from
\Gamma
by forgetting the degrees. This coefficient is
1
for the graphs appearing in the definition of
\mathfrak{q}_{k,\ell}
with
k\geq 0
. We have the special cases

\mathfrak{q}_{-1,1}^{\beta_{0}}\coloneqq 0\qquad\quad\text{and}\quad\qquad\mathfrak{q}_{-1,0}^{\beta_{0}}\coloneqq 0.

For
\beta\in H_{2}(X)
, the operations associated to closed curves are

\mathfrak{q}_{\emptyset,\ell}^{\beta}\colon\Omega^{*}(X)^{\otimes\ell}\rightarrow\Omega^{*}(X)

defined by

\mathfrak{q}_{\emptyset,\ell}^{\beta}(\gamma_{1},\dots,\gamma_{\ell})\coloneqq(-1)^{w_{\mathfrak{s}}(\beta)}\sum_{\Gamma\in\partial(\ell,\beta)}(ev_{0}^{\Gamma})_{*}\Bigl(\bigwedge_{j=1}^{\ell}(ev_{j}^{\Gamma})^{*}\gamma_{j}\wedge s_{\Gamma}^{*}\eta_{\Gamma}\Bigr),

with the two special special cases

\mathfrak{q}_{\emptyset,1}^{0}\coloneqq 0\qquad\quad\text{and}\quad\qquad\mathfrak{q}_{\emptyset,0}^{0}\coloneqq 0.

For all of the above
\mathfrak{q}_{*}^{\beta}
operations, set
\mathfrak{q}_{*}=\sum_{\beta}T^{\beta}\mathfrak{q}^{\beta}_{*}
.

The extra coefficient in (

. Let

and

Proposition 3.2

(Unit)
.

For

As our

∎

Proposition 3.3

(Cyclic symmetry)
.

For any
\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k+1}\in\Omega^{*}(L;R)
and

\langle\mathfrak{q}_{k,\ell}(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k};\gamma),\alpha_{k+1}\rangle_{L}=(-1)^{|\alpha_{k+1}|^{\prime}(|\alpha_{1}|^{\prime}+\dots|\alpha_{k}|^{\prime})}\langle\mathfrak{q}_{k,\ell}(\alpha_{k+1},\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{k-1};\gamma),\alpha_{k}\rangle_{L}

Proof.

The proof is analogous to the proof of

applied to each
\mathcal{K}^{\mathchoice{\scalebox{0.6}{$\displaystyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\textstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptstyle\square$}}{\scalebox{0.6}{$\scriptscriptstyle\square$}}}_{\Gamma}

Proposition 3.4

(Degree)
.

For any

and

\displaystyle|\mathfrak{q}^{\beta}_{k,\ell}(\alpha;\gamma)|

\displaystyle=2+|\alpha|^{\prime}-\mu(\beta)+\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}(|\gamma_{j}|-2)

Proof.

This follows from the same proof as
[

, Proposition 3.5]
, noting that the Thom form has the same rank as the obstruction bundle.
∎

Proposition 3.5

(Symmetry)
.

If
k\geq-1
or

, then for any permutation
