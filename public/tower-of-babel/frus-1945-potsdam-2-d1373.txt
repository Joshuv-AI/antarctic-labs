No. 1373 Proposal by the United States Delegation[1]

[Extract[2]]

Berlin Conference, July 25, 1945.

Draft Proposal by American Delegation

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

The three states will support the policy of free and unrestricted navigation in any revision of the Montreux Convention of 1936.
