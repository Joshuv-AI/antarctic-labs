Engage Overview
Summary of Engage, including what the
modeling tool does and how it can be used

 What is Engage? Introduction to Capacity Expansion

• Engage is an open-access capacity expansion
  and operational modeling web application, hosted
  by the U.S. Department of Energy’s National
  Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR)
• Engage empowers stakeholders to participate in
  energy decisions by enabling multi-system
  optimization and simulation via an accessible,
  collaborative, and communicative modeling tool
• Engage can co-optimize electrical, fuel, water,
  transport, and other energy infrastructure with
  various technologies at any scale—from district to
  continental systems

                                                       National Laboratory of the Rockies   |   9

 What Questions Can Engage Answer?

1. What role should nuclear or geothermal energy
 have in our future generation portfolio?
2. What operational and capital cost savings can
 be anticipated by integrating new technologies into
 our energy system?
3. How does the cost optimal energy system vary
 with constraints on land use or economic
 development goals?
4. How can distributed energy resources be
 effectively integrated with bulk power systems?
5. What is the most cost-competitive pathway for
 achieving our energy targets by 2050?

                                                         National Laboratory of the Rockies   |   10

 How Do I Use Engage?

First, collect performance and cost data
on existing and/or future generation and
transmission assets in addition to timeseries
data on available resources and demand.

Second, develop capacity expansion
models that optimize investment and
retirement decisions on assets by
minimizing total system costs.

Finally, visualize scenarios and compare
their costs and asset buildouts across
planning years to understand energy system
implications.
And reiterate as needed!

(e.g., 0-1). This allows the same profile to be used for different production
capacities (critical for capacity expansion).

 Summary

• Capacity expansion models are models that simulate
  investment into new technologies in order to
  model different system pathways
• Engage has a five-step process for building and
  running models, which users can follow using the
  tool’s user interface
• Engage enables flexible and multi-sectoral
  technology configuration via its use of technology
  archetypes and carriers
• Carriers represent any energy, resource, or
  commodity flow users are interested in tracking
• Technology archetypes represent system
  relationships (e.g., supply, demand, transmission)
