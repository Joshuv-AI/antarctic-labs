No. 113 Prime Minister Churchill to President Truman [1]

top secret

London, 1 July 1945.

Prime Minister to President Truman. Personal, private and top secret. No. 99.

Your Number 76.[2]

The King has now decided he must visit Ulster in the period during the Berlin conference and therefore your kind telegram on this subject would have no application. You will no doubt have received His Majesty’s invitation to visit this island.[3]

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels. Document No. 71.
[2] Document No. 71.
[3] Not printed.
