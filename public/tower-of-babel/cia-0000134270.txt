3 Increased Soviet Press Attention to Cuba: An Izvestia
article recently characterized the Castro movement not aS a
usual Latin American revolution but a "national liberation
movement" in which the working class and laboring peasantry
‘took the most active role." The application of this termi-
nology specifically to Cuba implies that Moscow has put its
ideological stamp of approval on the Castro regime. The Oc-—
tober issue of the Soviet journal International Affairs had
called Perez Jimenez' overthrow in Venezuela, as well as that
of Batista in Cuba, a “turning point" marking the emergence

of a "new stage in the national liberation struggle in Latin
America."
