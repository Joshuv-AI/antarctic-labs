Minimal Weierstrass models and regular models of hyperelliptic curves
Qing Liu

Abstract
. Let C C be a hyperelliptic curve of genus g ≥ 2 g\geq 2 over a discrete valuation field K K with perfect residue field. We study the minimal Weierstrass models of C C . When there is more than one such model, we find interesting properties on the minimal regular model and the canonical model of C C . For curves of genus 2 2 , we characterize the existence of the stable reduction in terms of the minimal Weierstrass models. When there is more than one such model, we can compute the Euler factor of Jac ⁡ ( C ) \mathrm{Jac}(C) and a volume form of the Néron model of Jac ⁡ ( C ) \mathrm{Jac}(C) , using two specific minimal Weierstrass models.

Next we show that

We have

As

Let

Suppose that

Lemma 2.19
.

Keep the above notation and assume that

is odd.
Let

(1)

Let

\mathcal{D}
, then

2p_{a}(\Gamma)+1\geq\deg\left(R_{C/Z_{K}}|_{|x|\geq 1}\right).

(27)

(2)

Write

where

.
Then Inequality (

or,
|c|\geq|2|
and

(1) As
p_{0}
is a cusp, there is only one point
in
\mathcal{D}_{k}
lying over

. So
\mathcal{D}\to Z
is ramified
above

\begin{matrix}\deg R_{D/Z_{K}}&=&\sum_{p\in D,|x(p)|<1}[k(p):k]\deg_{p}R_{D/Z_{K}}+\deg(R_{D/Z_{K}}|_{|x|\geq 1})\\

The condition

(2) As

(28)

Proposition 2.20
.

Let
C
be a hyperelliptic curve
over a complete discrete valuation field
K
. Let
W
be a Weierstrass
model of
C
with integral closed fiber and let

be a

\widehat{W}\setminus\{p_{0}\}
has a hyperelliptic compactification

) is satisfied.

The proof will be done in several steps. First, Lemma
2.19
(1)
implies the “only if” part.
To prove the “if” part, we will use the formal
patching technique. Let

\mathcal{O}_{K}\{x\}=\left\{\sum_{i\geq 0}a_{i}x^{i}\ |\ a_{i}\in\mathcal{O}_{K},\ \lim_{i\to+\infty}|a_{i}|=0\right\}\subset\mathcal{O}_{K}[[x]]

(29)

the ring of restricted power series. Then

.
Consider the
\mathcal{O}_{K}
-algebras

,

,
A_{2}=\mathcal{O}_{K}[[x]]
,
and

2.6.
Stable reduction

We refer to

When

See

Recall that for any smooth projective geometrically connected curve

Proposition 2.27
.

Let

be a hyperelliptic curve of genus

over

.
Then

has geometrically irreducible stable reduction (e.g.
good reduction) if and only if

has
a unique minimal Weierstrass model and if the latter has geometrically
integral and stable closed fiber.

Proof.

Suppose that
C
has a stable model
\mathcal{X}
over
\mathcal{O}_{K}
. Then the
hyperelliptic involution
\sigma
of

acts on
\mathcal{X}
. Let

\mathcal{Z}=\mathcal{X}/\langle\sigma\rangle
. As
\mathcal{X}_{k}\to\mathcal{Z}_{k}
is dominant,
\mathcal{Z}_{k}
is also

and

\mathcal{Z}_{k}
is a

is reduced
(because stable), and all

By

It follows from

The converse is immediate.
∎

3.2.

We characterize in this subsection

The stable curves over an algebraic closure

A stable curve
