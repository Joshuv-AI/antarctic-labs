Twin Phases: Phase Transitions Without Hidden Symmetry Breaking
Alison Warman, Yuhan Gai, Sakura Schäfer-Nameki

Abstract
We introduce the concept of twin phases for a symmetry 𝒮 \mathcal{S} , defined as inequivalent phases, whose order parameters are part of the same generalized charge under 𝒮 \mathcal{S} . Stable, direct transitions between such twin phases are never spontaneous-symmetry-breaking transitions, even after (partially) gauging the initial symmetry 𝒮 \mathcal{S} : they are phase transitions without hidden symmetry breaking. We illustrate this with an (anomalous) finite group symmetry in 1+1d, which exhibits such intrinsically beyond Landau transitions.

The twin phases are distinct gapped phases (since they preserve non-conjugate symmetries). To describe the transiton between them, we consider the linear interpolation between the Hamiltonians (

\displaystyle(1-\lambda)H_{S_{3}^{(1)}}+\lambda H_{S_{3}^{(2)}}

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{j}(1-\lambda)\left[L_{j}^{h,\omega}+\left(Z^{\rho_{4}}_{j}\cdot Z^{\rho_{4}\dagger}_{j+1}\right)_{1,1}\right]

\displaystyle-\frac{1}{4}\sum_{j}\lambda\left[L_{j}^{hc,\omega}+\left(Z^{\rho_{4}}_{j}\cdot Z^{\rho_{4}\dagger}_{j+1}\right)_{2,2}\right]
