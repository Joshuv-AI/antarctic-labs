to Aristotle (the marvels-of-India letter, a cornerstone of the medieval
monster-race tradition), after the Venice 1555 edition of Francesco Storella
("Secretum secretorum Aristotelis... Alexandri Magni Ad Aristotelem epistola de
admirabilibus Indiae"). Public domain scan (Google/Internet Archive). The
letter is complete as printed in that edition, ending before the appended
Hippocratic prognostic tract. Period typography preserved (OCR is rough in
places but the full text is present).
