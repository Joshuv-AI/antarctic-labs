 1. PURPOSE

In support of the Certified Reference Material (CRM) program managed by the Nuclear Reference
Material Program (NRMP), the Material Signatures and Isotopic Standards (MSIS) group of Oak Ridge
National Laboratory (ORNL) was asked to prepare a set of CRM U030A units for use as standards for
isotopic analysis using multicollector thermal ionization mass spectrometry (TIMS) and inductively
coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS) instruments. This report documents the results of the
verification measurements performed on three randomly selected units by the MSIS group’s ISO/IEC
17025:2017 accredited operating procedure CSD-AM-CIMS-IN20, Determination of Uranium and
Plutonium Isotopic Composition using Thermal Ionization Mass Spectrometry [1], and in accordance with
the quality assurance plan as described in QAP-X-96-CSD/RML-001, Nuclear Analytical Chemistry
Laboratory Section Quality Assurance Plan [2].

Figure 1 shows a picture of the repackaged NRMP CRM U030A selected for verification measurements,
and Figure 2 shows the prepared units of CRM U03A in the final configuration for shipment.

 2. PREPARATION OF THE U030A UNITS

The glass vials used to pack/store the repackaged CRM U030A material as well as the fluorinated
ethylene propylene (FEP) bottles and pipette tips used to prepare master solutions and aliquots of the
verification units were purchased new for this project from Fisher Scientific, I-Chem, or Eppendorf.

All labware was leached consecutively in 6 M HCl, 8 M HNO3, and finally in 18.2 MΩ∙cm−1 water for
24 h according to CIMS-WA-003, Acid Leaching of New Laboratory Supplies [3]. After rinsing, the
labware was dried in an ISO 5 laminar flow hood and stored in new, clean plastic bags until used for
project activities.

Starting with a roughly 1 g unit of the CRM U030A U3O8 powder, 100 units of NRMP CRM U030A
were produced by weighing approximately 10 mg of the U3O8 powder into 20 mL glass vials with
polypropylene caps and PTFE-faced silicone liners. Verification measurements are required for these
CRM U030A units to demonstrate that the isotopic composition of the repackaged material had not been
altered by contamination from natural or other uranium materials in the laboratory environment during the
preparation of these units. Three units (shown in Figure 1) were taken for dissolution in 8 M HNO3 and
diluted to a concentration of approximately 5 mg/mL using 1 M HNO3. Five units were kept aside for
storage at ORNL, and 92 units were shipped to the Y-12 National Security Complex.

 3. VERIFICATION MEASUREMENTS

Isotopic verification used the modified total evaporation (MTE) method, with NRMP CRM 112A as the
comparator standard and NRMP CRM C129A as the quality control (QC) standard. Table 1 shows the
arrangement of the comparator standard and of the QC standard as well as the verification aliquots on the
analyzed turret.

MTE analytical methodology followed the double filament configuration described in ASTM C1832-23,
Determination of Uranium Isotopic Composition by Modified Total Evaporation (MTE) Method Using
Thermal Ionization Mass Spectrometer [4], and used a 24 V summed intensity (sum of the 235U + 238U
signal intensities).

 4. CONCLUSIONS

The %RSD data shown in Table 2 for the verification measurements show that the isotopic composition
of the prepared CRM U030A units matched the certified isotopic ratios of the starting material. The
preparation of the units was not affected by contamination from natural uranium or from other possible
sources within the laboratory. Figure 6 shows that QC data associated with the verification measurements
are within the performance limits established for the 235U/238U major isotope ratio measurements by the
MTE analytical methodology. The prepared NRMP CRM U030A units are suitable for use as isotopic
standards for calibration/QC in uranium isotope ratio measurements using TIMS/ICP-MS instruments.
