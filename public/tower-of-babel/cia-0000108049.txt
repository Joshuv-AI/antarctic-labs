3. Prisoner barracks were one-story structures, with dormitory-type
rooms furnished with two-level bunk beds and providing accommodation for from
40 to 60 inmates per room. Praporshchiki accompanied prisoners for all
activities within the prisoner compound. This included movement in groups of
10 or 12 to the club and library, the dining hall, the bath house, and the
work area. Prisoners were allowed up to two visitors a month, but there were
no facilities for overnight visits with spouses. Prisoners included criminals
sentenced to from a few months to up to 20 or more years, and included
sentences under Criminal Code articles concerning tape, murder, embezzlement —s
of state property, monetary speculation, and bribery.
