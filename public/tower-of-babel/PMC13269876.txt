Author Correction: PCM1 orchestrates centrosomal and flagellar protein transport to promote sperm maturation
Authors: Zicong Huang, Runduan Yi, Xixian Cen, Hanbin Zhang, Minyu Xie, Ke Ma, Shipeng Ruan, Yuge Zhuang, Xiaoyuan Zhang, Wenyuan Liu, Hongrui Feng, Deyu Chen, Mohamed Morsi M Ahmed, Hong Liu, Chuyu Huang, Xiangjin Kang, Zhenguo Chen
Published: 2026

Correction to: Communications Biology 10.1038/s42003-025-08319-x, published online 07 June 2025

In the version of the article initially published, the column headings “NOA” and “OA” were switched in Table 2 and have now been amended so that the column headings read, from left to right, “Expression intensity, OA, NOA, p value”. In the first paragraph of the Results, the text “…containing 30 NOA and 25 OA samples” has been amended to “…containing 30 OA samples and 25 NOA samples”. These corrections have been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
