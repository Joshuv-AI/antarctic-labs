Retraction Note: AI-optimized GRU-based self-attention model for predictive diabetes staging in IoT healthcare 5.0
Authors: Liang Zhou, Brij B Gupta, Akshat Gaurav, Razaz Waheeb Attar, Ahmed Alhomoud, Varsha Arya, Ching-Hsien Hsu
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-29674-z, published online 09 December 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the Diabetes Prediction Dataset, used in this study1. The Authors relied on a publicly available dataset, whose provenance and validity cannot be confirmed. As this dataset was used for the training and validation of the proposed model in this Article, the Editors no longer have confidence in the reliability of the results and conclusions of this Article.

Brij B. Gupta disagrees with this retraction. Akshat Gaurav and Varsha Arya did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. Liang Zhou, Razaz Waheeb Attar, Ahmed Alhomoud, and Ching-Hsien Hsu did not respond to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction.
