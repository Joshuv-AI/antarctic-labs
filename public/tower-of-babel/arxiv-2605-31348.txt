Abstract
. We show that the cohomology of the finite element Stokes complex consisting of piecewise polynomials spaces on an Alfeld split mesh from Fu, Guzmán, & Neilan (2020, Math. Comp. , 89 , 1059–1091) is isomorphic to the cohomologies of the continuous Stokes and de Rham complexes. We also construct novel “minimal” conforming finite element complexes where the H 1 H^{1} -conforming space is the lowest-order space from Guzmán & Neilan (2018, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. , 56 , 2826–2844) and the L 2 L^{2} -conforming space is piecewise constants. These minimal complexes also have cohomologies isomorphic to the continuous Stokes and de Rham complexes. We further construct local, bounded, cochain projections for the minimal complexes. All the results hold for strongly Lipschitz domains with nontrivial topologies and in the presence of mixed boundary conditions.

10

11

We mostly follow the framework in
[
26
]
, originally developed
for determining the cohomology of discrete complexes spaces without boundary

conditions in the Stokes complex. We first require some additional
notation. Give a collection of
d

, let

\Delta(\mathcal{S}):=\bigcup_{\ell=0}^{d}\Delta_{\ell}(\mathcal{S})

denote the collection of all subsimplices of
\mathcal{S}
. Moreover,
given a simplex
\tau
, we say
\eta\subsimplex\tau
if

\eta
is a subsimplex of
\tau
and
\eta\strictsubsimplex\tau

if
\eta\subsimplex\tau
and
\eta\neq\tau
.

3.1.
Trace structure

We begin by defining ambient spaces for the various trace operators we
will define.
For
K\in\mathcal{T}
, we take

.
For
\tau\in\Delta_{1}(\mathcal{T})\cup\Delta_{2}(\mathcal{T})

and

, we define

\displaystyle A^{0}(\tau)

\displaystyle:=\mathcal{P}_{p+2}(\tau)\oplus\mathcal{P}_{p+1}(\tau)^{3}\oplus\bigoplus_{z\in\Delta_{0}(\tau)}\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3}_{\sym},\qquad

\displaystyle A^{0}(z)

\displaystyle:=\mathbb{R}\oplus\mathbb{R}^{3}\oplus\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3},

\displaystyle A^{1}(\tau)

\displaystyle:=\mathcal{P}_{p+1}(\tau)^{3}\oplus\mathcal{P}_{p}(\tau)^{3}\oplus\bigoplus_{z\in\Delta_{0}(\tau)}\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3},\qquad

\displaystyle A^{1}(z)

\displaystyle:=\mathbb{R}^{3}\oplus\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3},

\displaystyle A^{2}(\tau)

\displaystyle:=\mathcal{P}_{p}(\tau)^{3},\qquad

\displaystyle A^{2}(z)

\displaystyle:=\mathbb{R}^{3},

where
\mathbb{R}^{3\times 3}_{\sym}
denotes the set of
3\times 3
symmetric
matrices with real entries.

For each
k\in 0:3
, we
show that
A^{k}:=\{A^{k}(\tau):\tau\in\Delta(\mathcal{T})\}

may be equipped with a trace structure in the sense of

[
26
, Definition 2.1]
.
That is, for
\tau\in\Delta(\mathcal{T})
and
\eta\subsimplex\tau
,
we define linear operators

\Tr^{k}_{\eta\from\tau}:A^{k}(\tau)\to A^{k}(\eta)
satisfying the
following properties:

(i)

\Tr^{k}_{\tau\from\tau}
is the identity map for all

\tau\in\Delta(\mathcal{T})
.

(ii)

For all
\sigma\in\mathcal{T}
and

\eta\subsimplex\tau\subsimplex\sigma
, there holds

(3.1)

\displaystyle\Tr^{k}_{\eta\from\sigma}u=0\implies\Tr^{k}_{\eta\from\tau}\circ\Tr^{k}_{\tau\from\sigma}u=0\qquad\forall u\in A^{k}(\sigma).

(iii)

\Tr^{k}:=\{\Tr^{k}_{\eta\from\tau}:\eta\subsimplex\tau,\ \tau\in\Delta(\mathcal{T})\}

characterizes
V^{k,h}
:

(3.2)

V^{k,h}=\{u\in L^{2}(\Omega)\otimes\mathbb{X}^{k}:u|_{K}\in A^{k}(K)\text{ and }\\
\Tr^{k}_{\tau\from K}u|_{K}=\Tr^{k}_{\tau\from K^{\prime}}u|_{K^{\prime}}\ \forall\tau\subsimplex K,K^{\prime},\ \forall K,K^{\prime}\in\mathcal{T}\}.

Recall that
\mathbb{X}^{k}
are the global ambient spaces
defined in
eq.

2.8
.
If
(A^{k},\Tr^{k})
satisfy (i-ii), then

is a trace structure
in the sense of
[
26
, Definition 2.1]
, while (iii)
ensures that

is the “global space” with respect to
the trace structure
[
26
, Definition 2.2]
. In the following
subsections, we construct the trace operators
\Tr^{k}_{\eta\from\tau}
for

\eta\neq\tau
, tacitly assuming that (i) always holds.

For

Recall that

\displaystyle\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U}):=\{z\in\Delta_{0}(\mathcal{U}):\text{ all faces meeting at $z$ are coplanar}\}.

Then, the following result shows that not all Hessian degrees
of freedom for functions in

or gradient

Lemma 3.3
.

For
v\in V_{\Gamma_{0}}^{0,h}
and
w\in V_{\Gamma_{0}}^{1,h}
, there holds

(3.14a)

\displaystyle\Tr_{z}^{0}v

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}0\oplus 0\oplus\partial_{\hat{n}_{\Gamma}}^{2}v(z)\hat{n}_{\Gamma}\otimes\hat{n}_{\Gamma}&\text{if }z\in\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U}),\\
0&\text{if }z\in\Delta_{0}(\mathcal{U})\setminus\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U}),\end{cases}

(3.14b)

\displaystyle\Tr_{z}^{1}w

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}0\oplus\partial_{\hat{n}_{\Gamma}}(w\cdot\hat{n}_{\Gamma})(z)\hat{n}_{\Gamma}\otimes\hat{n}_{\Gamma}&\text{if }z\in\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U}),\\
0&\text{if }z\in\Delta_{0}(\mathcal{U})\setminus\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U}),\end{cases}

where
\hat{n}_{\Gamma}
is the outward unit normal vector on

\partial\Omega
.

Proof.

Assume first that
z\in\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U})
and let

f\in\mathcal{U}
be such that
z\in\Delta_{0}(f)
.
Then,
v|_{f}=0
and
\partial_{n}v|_{f}=0
, and so
v(z)=0

and
\grad v(z)=0
. Moreover, differentiating in the tangent plane
of

then shows that
D_{f}^{\alpha}\grad v(z)=0
for all

|\alpha|\geq 0
, where
D_{f}
denotes the surface differential.
In particular,

(I-\hat{n}_{f}\otimes\hat{n}_{f})\hess v(z)=0
.
Similarly,
w|_{f}=0
and
\curl w|_{f}=0
and so

w(z)=0
,
\grad_{f}w(z)=0
, and
\curl w(z)=0
. Consequently,

(I-\hat{n}_{f}\otimes\hat{n}_{f})\grad w(z)=0
.
The first case of
eq.

3.14a
and

3.14b
now follows since

\hat{n}_{f}=\hat{n}_{\Gamma}
.

Now suppose that
z\in\Delta_{0}(\mathcal{U})\setminus\Delta_{0}^{\flat}(\mathcal{U})
. Then, there exist

not coplanar such that

z\in\Delta_{0}(f)
and
z\in\Delta_{0}(f^{\prime})
. The same arguments
as above show that
v(z)=\grad v(z)=0
,
w(z)=0
, and

\displaystyle(I-\hat{\mu}\otimes\hat{\mu})\hess v(z)=(I-\hat{\mu}\otimes\hat{\mu})\grad w(z)

\displaystyle=0,\qquad\forall\mu\in\{\hat{n}_{f},\hat{n}_{f^{\prime}}\}.

Thus,
\hess v(z)=\grad w(z)=0
.
