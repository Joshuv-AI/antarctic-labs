Rabbi of Tunis; son of Nathan Burgel. He is the author of "Migdanot Natan," a work in two parts. The first part, printed with a literary production by his father (Leghorn, 1778), contains a commentary on the tractate Baba Meẓi'a and notes on several tracts of the Babylonian and Jerusalem Talmuds and on the Yad ha-Ḥazaḳah. The second part (Leghorn, 1785) contains novellæ on various treatises and a funeral oration.

L. G. M. K.
