 Addendum to February 2026 Environmental Baseline Survey Report for Tract C-2

This addendum to the February 2026 environmental baseline survey report (EBSR) supports
the conveyance of ownership of the C-2 White Rock Y-1 Tract parcel (hereinafter referred to as
“White Rock Y-1 Tract”) at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL or Laboratory) from the U.S.
Department of Energy (DOE)/National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) to Los Alamos
County (LAC) pursuant to Public Law (PL) 105-119, Section 632. It also documents DOE’s
findings that, despite the potential presence of trace levels of contamination (from sources
external to this tract), White Rock Y-1 Tract is in such condition that DOE/NNSA may issue
deeds on the basis that human health and the environment will be protected based on LAC’s
intended continued use of the property as a right-of-way for existing New Mexico State Road
(NM) 502 and utilities, along with occasional recreational use. The land will be maintained in
similar condition to what it is now.

The February 2026 EBSR of the White Rock Y-1 Tract was prepared consistent with the
American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM) Standard Practice for Environmental Site
Assessments: Phase I Environmental Site Assessment Process (ASTM E1527-21), and the
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency’s final rule: Standards and Practices for All Appropriate
Inquiries (the “AAI Rule”), as required under the Comprehensive Environmental Response,
Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) at 40 Code of Federal Regulations Part 312. The
assessment included the following:

• Reviews of historical and current information, documents, and government records pertinent to
White Rock Y-1 Tract;

• Visual inspections of White Rock Y-1 Tract and adjoining properties;

• Consultations with Triad National Security, LLC (Triad) and DOE/NNSA staff to confirm
existing information or develop additional information related to the identification of recognized
environmental conditions and any potential impacts on the property conveyance; and

• Interviews with subject matter experts on the history of White Rock Y-1 Tract.

The assessors visited the tract on August 28, 2000, subsequently on July 30, 2002, and on
March 28, 2003, to gather first-hand information. Assessors conducted visual inspections to
confirm other information regarding potential liabilities and historical environmental releases and
to assess the tract’s status. A site visit was conducted on November 2, 2015, to take
photographs to document current conditions. Mr. Christopher Catechis conducted an updated
visual site assessment on August 12, 2025, and again on August 27, 2026. The current site
condition photographs from the August 27, 2026, assessment, are presented in this addendum.
Additionally, available environmental databases were searched to determine whether hazardous
sites or serious local environmental problems exist on or near the tract. A database search by
Environmental Data Resources, Inc., a commercial search vendor, was conducted January 21,
2026, and updated August 13, 2026. The updated EDR report is attached to this addendum.

Based on this information, Triad and DOE/NNSA conclude that no outstanding environmental
issues on White Rock Y-4 Tract would prevent its conveyance to LAC.

I declare that, to the best of my professional knowledge and belief, I meet the definition of
Environmental Professional as defined in § 312.10 of this part.
I have the specific qualifications based on education, training, and experience to assess a
property of the nature, history, and setting of the subject property. I have developed and
performed the all appropriate inquiries in conformance with the standards and practices set forth
in 40 CFR Part 312.
