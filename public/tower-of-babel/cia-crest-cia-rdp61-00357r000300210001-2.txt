3 February 1959

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

SUBJECT: Briefing by DC1 before Defense Appropriations Subcommittee
of House Appropriations Committee - 2 and 3 February
1959

1. The Director accompanied by Mr. Amory and Mr. Warner
appeared before the Mahon Defense Subcommittee of House Appropriations
for two days. On February 2 they met at 10:30 and concluded the morning

at 12:00. They returned for two hours in the afternoon at 2:00. Subsequently
on the 3rd of February the Director met with this group for two hours in
the morning and two hours in the afternoon. Present from the Subcommittee
were: Mahon, Sheppard, Sikes, Norrell, Andrews, Riley, Flood, Boyle,

Ford, Ostertag, Laird, Lipscomb, Weaver, Minshall and Taber. Present
from the staff were: Sam Crosby, Robert Moyer, Aubrey (Tex) Gunnels,
and Kelly Campbell.

2. Mr. Mahon made a brief opening statement commenting that he
had received many views from members of Congress and the public
generally to the effect that CIA was not doing a good job since we were
obviously continually caught by surprise. A transcript was taken and a

copy will be retained in the Agency (without the knowledge of the Subcommittee).

3. Mr. Sikes would very much like to receive a briefing to explain
the comparison of Russian v. U. S. defense values. This was offered to
him by the Director and Mr. Sikes stated he would "like to very much. "

4. Mr. Flood asked for information on seizures of arms and caches
in Panama and also riots by various groups including students in Panama.

Flood also asked for information on Communist penetration in San Paulo,

Brazil. The recent statement by Mikoyan on his return to Russia was
discussed and 20 copies were made available to the Subcommittee members.

5. Mr. Ostertag inquired about a recent report that 2400 people
had been killed in Russia as the result of some type of nuclear explosion.

We promised to supply information,

6. Mr. Laird inquired about recent reports of magnetic devices
being found near U. S. shores. We also offered to provide to Mr. Minshall
the CIA reports concerning sputniks prior to launching. We also agreed to
supply Mr. Sheppard with the numbers of CIA agents who had lost their

lives.

7. The Director invited the members of the Subcommittee to come
down to the Agency for further briefing on the Agency if they desired. Mr.
Lipscomb and Mr. Minshall expressed their desire to do this. The Acting
Chairman, Mr. Sheppard, commented on this proposal indicating the
Agency should not do this with too many of the Congress.
