# The Vision and the Voice

**Author:** Aleister Crowley
**Year:** 1929
**Source:** Archive.org (scanned edition)

## Overview

The Vision and the Voice is Aleister Crowley's account of his spiritual visions and experiences in the desert at Algeria in 1909 CE, accompanied by his wife Rose and their associate Charles Stansfeld Jones (Frater Achad). It is considered one of the most important works of Thelemic mysticism, documenting the receiving of the Angelic Holy Guardian Angel and the initiation into the Second Order.

## Contents

The work is divided into four parts:
- The Cairo Working (1909)
- The Ouija board communications
- The visions of the Averse Serpent
- The Voice of the Spearbearer

## Significance

This text represents one of Crowley's most profound mystical experiences and contains the core Thelemic doctrine of the True Will and the achievement of the Holy Guardian Angel. It is a foundational text of Thelemic philosophy and ceremonial magic.
