Spanish scholar and author; died at Venice in 1629. He wrote a work in Spanish entitled "Tratado de la Oracion y Meditacion y Conocimento Proprio y del Dio" (Venice, 1654), a treatise on prayer and on the knowledge of oneself and of God.

Bibliography: Wolf, Bibl. Hebr. iii., No. 505b; Kayserling, Bibl. Esp.-Port.-Jud. p. 75. E. C. M. Sel.
