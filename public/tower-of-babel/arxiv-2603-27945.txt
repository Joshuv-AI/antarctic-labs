Pumping of spin supercurrent in unitary triplet superconductors
Ping Li, Tao Yu

Abstract
One efficient mechanism for generating a charge supercurrent is Andreev reflection, in which the electric current injected from a normal metal into a conventional superconductor is converted into a supercurrent, thereby preserving charge conservation. We here propose a general principle for generating spin supercurrents in triplet superconductors by analogy with such charge transport, i.e., assuming spin conservation. We find a spin torque that is proportional to the triplet superconducting order parameter and, in the spin-conservation scenario, converts the particle spin to that of Cooper pairs. Based on this general principle, we propose an implementation to efficiently generate a spin supercurrent in unitary triplet superconductors, even though Cooper pairs carry no spin polarization at equilibrium, by the magnetization dynamics 𝐌 ⁡ ( t ) {\bf M}(t) of a proximity magnetic nanostructure. The efficiency of this spin pumping is not solely limited to the d ​ 𝐌 / d ​ t × 𝐌 d{\bf M}/dt\times{\bf M} due to the emergent particle-hole symmetry, thereby going beyond the conventional spin pumping of electrons. This general principle provides an efficient approach to generating and manipulating dissipationless spin currents in many unconventional superconductors.

III
Exchange interaction with magnetization

Generally, we consider here the coupling Hamiltonian between the ferromagnetic nanostructures and the electron spins due to the interfacial exchange interaction

\displaystyle\hat{V}

\displaystyle=J_{\rm ex}\int\hat{\mathbf{s}}(\boldsymbol{\rho},t)\cdot{\mathbf{M}}(x=0,\boldsymbol{\rho},t)d\boldsymbol{\rho}

\displaystyle=\frac{J_{\rm ex}\hbar}{4}\int d{\bm{\rho}}\hat{\Psi}^{\dagger}({\bm{\rho}}){\boldsymbol{\cal S}}\cdot{\mathbf{M}}(x=0,{\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\Psi}(\boldsymbol{\rho}),

(47)

where
J_{\rm ex}
is the exchange coupling constant that induces the local exchange splitting
\Delta=J_{\rm ex}\hbar M_{s}
of electrons by the ferromagnet with a saturation magnetization
M_{s}
.
Here, the energy splitting is local to the
O(100)
nm region. This region is much shorter than the coherent length of the superconductor; the associated Zeeman splitting is much smaller than the condensation energy of electrons. Thereby, we assume the local exchange field does not affect the overall superconductivity.
We therefore focus on the dynamical response of the superconductor to the coherent magnetization dynamics.

In the ferromagnetic nanostructures, the coherent magnetization
\mathbf{M}({\bf r},t)
precesses around the saturation magnetization. The AC exchange magnetic field due to the exchange coupling (
47
)

\displaystyle{\bf H}_{\rm ex}(x=0,{\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)

\displaystyle\equiv({J_{\rm ex}\hbar}/{4}){\bf M}(x=0,{\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{A}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\bf k}{\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{\xi}({\bf k})e^{i{\bf k}\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}

(48)

oscillates by the frequency
\omega
. For a two-dimensional superconductor with an area
A
, the matrix
{U}({\bf k})=(\phi_{1}({\bf k}),\phi_{2}({\bf k}),\phi_{3}({\bf k}),\phi_{4}({\bf k}))
and annihilation operators of quasiparticles
\hat{\textbf{\emph{a}}}_{\bf k}=(\hat{a}_{{\bf k}1},\hat{a}_{{\bf k}2},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{-{\bf k}1},\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{-{\bf k}2})^{T}

\displaystyle\hat{\Psi}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})=\frac{1}{\sqrt{A}}\sum_{\bf k}{U}({\bf k})e^{i{\bf k}\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}}\hat{\textbf{\emph{a}}}_{\bf k}.

(49)

We note that by particle-hole symmetry,
\hat{a}_{{\bf k}3}=\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{-{\bf k}1}
and
\hat{a}_{{\bf k}4}=\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{-{\bf k}2}

In terms of the field operator (
49

47

\displaystyle\hat{V}(t)=\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{4}{\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})}\hat{a}^{\dagger}_{{\bf k}\alpha}\hat{a}_{{\bf k}^{\prime}\beta}e^{-i\xi\omega t},

(50)

where the

50

\displaystyle\mathcal{G}^{\xi}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})

\displaystyle=(1/A){\bf H}^{\xi}_{\rm ex}({\bf k}-{\bf k}^{\prime})\cdot U^{\dagger}({\bf k})\boldsymbol{\cal S}U({\bf k}^{\prime})

(51)

are the coupling constants between quasiparticles of wave vector
{\bf k}
and
{\bf k}^{\prime}
mediated by the external fields.

IV
Scattering theory

We formulate the nonlinear dynamics of triplet superconductors in terms of the scattering theory.
When a local AC exchange field is applied to the superconductor, the external driven field (
50
) acts as the local time-dependent scattering potential that scatters the incident quasiparticles.
To this end, we develop the scattering theory to calculate the pumped spin-current density (
6
) and spin-torque density (
II
).

We derive the wave function of quasiparticles in the presence of the time-dependent scattering potential using the time-dependent perturbation theory.
The time evolution operator
\hat{U}_{I}(t,t_{0})=T\exp{-(i/\hbar)\int^t_{t_0}{\cal V}_I({\pmb\rho},t^\prime)dt^\prime}
in the interaction picture “
I
”,
in which

stands for the time-ordering operator and

When the interaction

is introduced adiabatically from

According to the time evolution operator, the wave function at time
t
in the interaction representation reads

\displaystyle\Phi_{I}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)=\hat{U}_{I}(t,t_{0}\rightarrow-\infty)\phi_{n,I}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t_{0}\rightarrow-\infty).

(52)

In the Schrödinger picture, retaining up to the second order of

\displaystyle\Phi({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)

\displaystyle=e^{-i{\cal H}_{0}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})t/\hbar}\hat{U}_{I}(t,t_{0}\rightarrow-\infty)\phi_{n}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=e^{-i{\cal H}_{0}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})t/\hbar}\Big[1-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int^{t}_{t_{0}}{\cal V}_{I}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t^{\prime})dt^{\prime}+\left(-\frac{i}{\hbar}\right)^{2}

We can, in principle, find the wave function of quasiparticles at time

\displaystyle\Phi_{\alpha}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)=\sum_{{\bf q}^{\prime}}\sum_{\alpha^{\prime}=1}^{4}T_{\alpha^{\prime}\alpha}({\bf q}^{\prime},{\bf k},t)\phi_{\alpha^{\prime}}({\bf q}^{\prime})e^{i({\bf q}^{\prime}\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\omega_{\alpha^{\prime}}(q^{\prime})t)},

VI
Discussion and conclusion

In conclusion, we develop a general theory of spin pumping of DC
spin supercurrent
by coherent magnetization dynamics in prototypical triplet superconductors. We find many features that go beyond the conventional spin pumping that focus on the spin current carried by normal electrons or quasiparticles
[
58
]
. In the existence of the triplet superconducting order parameter, the spin pumping does not simply transfer the FMR magnon spin
d{\bf M}/dt\times{\bf M}
into the Cooper pairs, but all the other combinations of magnetization components are allowed (Table
1
), thereby going beyond the conventional spin pumping
[
58
]
. Our theory is based on the robust conservation law, in which we find that the triplet superconducting order parameter provides a sink to the spin density carried by quasiparticles, which shares exactly the same structure as the charge dynamics in a conventional superconductor, in which the singlet superconducting order parameter provides a sink to the charge density carried by quasiparticles. The latter effect has been exploited to understand the formation of a charge supercurrent during Andreev reflection between a normal metal and conventional superconductors. Our formalism thereby establishes a solid basis for analogous spin dynamics in triplet superconductors. It is also crucial that our proposed effects work in the unitary triplet superconductors, in which the Cooper pairs carry no spin polarization at equilibrium, a system that is not easy to excite spin supercurrent by temperature gradient or charge supercurrent. The proposed spin pumping of spin supercurrent holds a high efficiency: The spin current is of the same order as the spin Hall current generated by an electric field of
1~{\rm kV/cm}
and a spin Hall conductivity
\sigma_{x}^{y}=10^{7}~(\Omega\cdot{\rm m})^{-1}
, which should be easily measurable
[
65
;
66
;
67
]
. Our results open new pathways to
rapidly and energy-efficiently generate and control spin supercurrent in intrinsic triplet superconductors
and related triplet superconductivity in heterostructures.

A.2
Spin-current density in linear-response regime

In the linear-response regime, the excited spin-current density by the AC magnetic field according to Eq. (
6
) reads

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{i\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}}\sum_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}\left\langle\hat{\textbf{\emph{a}}}^{\dagger}_{\bf k}U^{\dagger}_{\bf k}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)(\overleftarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}}-\overrightarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}})\tau_{3}\boldsymbol{\cal S}U_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\textbf{\emph{b}}}^{(1)}_{\bf k^{\prime}}\right\rangle+{\rm H.c.}

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}}\sum_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum^{4}_{\alpha,\beta=1}\left({\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}f(E_{\beta}(k))+{\rm H.c.}

\displaystyle\approx\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}}\sum_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\Bigg[\sum_{\alpha,\beta=\{1,2\}}\left({\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}f(E_{\beta}(k))

\displaystyle+\sum_{\bar{\alpha},\bar{\beta}=\{3,4\}}\left({\bf Q}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}f(E_{\bar{\beta}}(k))\Bigg]+{\rm H.c.},

(83)

in which, when the magnon frequency
\omega\ll 2\Delta_{p}/\hbar
, the optical transition between the particle
\alpha=\{1,2\}
and hole
\bar{\alpha}=\{3,4\}
bands is disregarded.
Thereby, the contributions to
{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})
arise exclusively from optical transitions among the particles and among the holes.
By the particle-hole symmetry, Eq. (
83
) can be calculated only in the particle space by

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=\{1,2\}}\Big({\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\Big)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t)\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}\big[2f(E_{\beta}(k))-1\big]+{\rm H.c.}.

(84)

We now show that
{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})
is solely carried by quasiparticles, which is absent when
T\rightarrow 0
with
f(E_{\beta}(k))=0
and
f(E_{\bar{\beta}}(k))=1
, i.e., no quasiparticles exist. Equation (
83
) then becomes

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}(T\rightarrow 0)

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\left({\bf Q}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t)\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}

\displaystyle+\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\left({\bf Q}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}-{\bf k}^{\prime})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}+\xi\omega t)\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)-i\delta}

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\left({\bf Q}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t)\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}

\displaystyle+\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\left({\bf Q}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t)\right)}}{-\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})-i\delta}=0,

(85)

where we use
\mathcal{G}^{\xi\dagger}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})=\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})
. In the second step, we change the variables
{\bf k}\leftrightarrow{\bf k}^{\prime}
,
{\bar{\alpha}}\leftrightarrow{\bar{\beta}}
, and
\xi\rightarrow-\xi
for the second term.
With this relation, the spin-current density (
84
) is simplified as

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=\{1,2\}}\left({\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i\left(({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-\xi\omega t)\right)}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}f(E_{\beta}(k))+{\rm H.c.}.

(86)

We then perform the summation over the wave vectors
\{{\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime}\}
in Eq. (
86
) by the contour integral. In polar coordinates,
{\boldsymbol{\rho}}=\rho\cos\varphi\hat{\bf y}+\rho\sin\varphi\hat{\bf z}
,
{\bf k}=k\cos\theta_{k}\hat{\bf y}+k\sin\theta_{k}\hat{\bf z}
, and
e^{i{\bf k}\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}}=e^{ik\rho\cos(\varphi-\theta_k)}
. For the integral over
k^{\prime}
, when
(\theta_{k^{\prime}}-\varphi)\in(-\pi/2,\pi/2)
,
\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)>0
, so we close the integral contour in the upper complex plane, where the singularities are located at
(1/\hbar)\sqrt{{2m}(\mu+\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx k_{\omega}^{+}(1+i\delta)
and
-(1/\hbar)\sqrt{{2m}(\mu-\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx-k_{\omega}^{-}(1-i\delta)
. Here,
k_{\omega}^{\pm}=({1}/{\hbar})\sqrt{{2m}(\mu\pm\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}
.
When
(\theta_{k^{\prime}}-\varphi)\in(\pi/2,3\pi/2)
,
\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)<0
, so we close the integral contour in the lower complex plane, where the singularities are located at
(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu-\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx k_{\omega}^{-}(1-i\delta)
and
-(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu+\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx-k^{+}_{\omega}(1+i\delta)
.
When
\varphi\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2)
, we divide the integral region
\theta_{k^{\prime}}\in[0,\pi]
into two parts
[0,\pi/2+\varphi]
and
(\pi/2+\varphi,\pi]
and calculate Eq. (
86
) as

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(1)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{8\pi}\sum_{{\bf k}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta}\frac{-i(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)}{\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta^{2}_{p}}}f(E_{\beta}(k))e^{i[-k\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)-\xi\omega t]}

\displaystyle\times\bigg[\int^{\varphi+\pi/2}_{\varphi-\pi/2}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{+}_{\omega})\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},{\bf k}^{+}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{+}_{\omega},{\bf k})e^{i[k^{+}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)-k\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)-\xi\omega t]}

\displaystyle+\int^{\varphi+\pi/2}_{\varphi-\pi/2}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}({\bf k}-{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega})\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},-{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}(-{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega},{\bf k})e^{i[-k^{-}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)-k\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)-\xi\omega t]}\bigg],

(87)

in which
{\bf k}^{+}_{\omega}=k_{\omega}^{+}(\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}},\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}})
and
{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega}=k_{\omega}^{-}(\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}},\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}})
.

For the one-dimensional case, e.g., the exchange magnetic field is uniform along the
\hat{\bf y}
-direction with the Fourier components

{\bf H}^{\xi}_{\rm ex}({\bf k})\rightarrow 2\pi\delta(k_{z}){\bf H}^{\xi}_{\rm ex}(k_{y})
, the coupling matrix

\displaystyle\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\rightarrow\frac{2\pi}{A}\delta\left(k^{\prime}\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}}-k_{z}\right)\left[{\bf H}^{\xi}_{\rm ex}(k^{\prime}\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\right].

(88)

In this case, when away from the source,

\displaystyle{\bf J}^{(1)}(y)=\frac{i}{4A}\sum_{k_{y},k_{z}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1,2}\frac{-(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)}{\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta^{2}_{p}}}f(E_{\beta}(k))

\displaystyle\times\bigg[\frac{1}{k^{+}_{\xi}}\left((k_{y}+k^{+}_{\xi})\hat{\bf y}+2k_{z}\hat{\bf z}\right)\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}[(k_{y},k_{z}),(k^{+}_{\xi},k_{z})]{\bf H}^{\xi}_{\rm ex}(k^{+}_{\xi}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\beta}[(k^{+}_{\xi},k_{z}),(k_{y},k_{z})]e^{i[(k^{+}_{\xi}-k_{y})y-\xi\omega t]}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{k^{-}_{\xi}}\left((k_{y}-k^{-}_{\xi})\hat{\bf y}+2k_{z}\hat{\bf z}\right)\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}[(k_{y},k_{z}),(-k^{-}_{\xi},k_{z})]{\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{\xi}(-k^{-}_{\xi}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\beta}[(-k^{-}_{\xi},k_{z}),(k_{y},k_{z})]e^{i[-(k^{-}_{\xi}+k_{y})y-\xi\omega t]}\bigg]

\displaystyle+{\rm H.c.},

(89)

where
k_{\xi}^{\pm}=\sqrt{(2m/\hbar^{2})\left(\mu\pm\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}}\right)-k^{2}_{z}}
.

A.3
DC spin-current density

In the nonlinear-response regime, the spin-current density (
6
) reads

\displaystyle{\bf J}_{s}^{(2)}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{i\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\bigg[\left\langle\hat{\textbf{\emph{a}}}^{\dagger}_{\bf k}U^{\dagger}_{\bf k}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)(\overleftarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}}-\overrightarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}})\tau_{3}\boldsymbol{\cal S}U_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\textbf{\emph{b}}}^{(2)}_{\bf k^{\prime}}\right\rangle

\displaystyle+\left\langle\hat{\textbf{\emph{b}}}^{(2)\dagger}_{\bf k}U^{\dagger}_{\bf k}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)(\overleftarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}}-\overrightarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}})\tau_{3}\boldsymbol{\cal S}U_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\textbf{\emph{a}}}_{\bf k^{\prime}}\right\rangle+\left\langle\hat{\textbf{\emph{b}}}^{(1)\dagger}_{\bf k}U^{\dagger}_{\bf k}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)(\overleftarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}}-\overrightarrow{\boldsymbol{\nabla}})\tau_{3}\boldsymbol{\cal S}U_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\textbf{\emph{b}}}^{(1)}_{\bf k^{\prime}}\right\rangle\bigg]

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}{\bf q}}\sum^{4}_{\alpha\beta\gamma=1}\sum_{\xi_{1}\xi_{2}}\Bigg[{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})\otimes({\bf k}+{\bf k}^{\prime})\frac{\mathcal{G}^{\xi_{1}}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi_{2}}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{i[({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-(\xi_{1}+\xi_{2})\omega t]}f(E_{\beta}(k))}{\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\beta}(k)-\xi_{2}\hbar\omega-i\delta\right)\left(E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-E_{\beta}(k)+(\xi_{1}+\xi_{2})\hbar\omega-i\delta\right)}

\displaystyle+{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})\otimes{\bf k}\frac{\mathcal{G}^{\xi_{2}}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{-\xi_{1}}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{i[({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}+(\xi_{1}-\xi_{2})\omega t]}f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{\left(\xi_{2}\hbar\omega-E_{\beta}(k)+E_{\gamma}(q)-i\delta\right)\left(\xi_{1}\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\gamma}(q)+i\delta\right)}\Bigg]+{\rm H.c.}.

(90)

We focus on the pumped DC spin-current density, i.e., taking
\xi_{1}+\xi_{2}=0
in the first term and
\xi_{1}=\xi_{2}
in the second term of Eq. (
90
). We also disregard the optical transition between the particle and hole bands when
\hbar\omega<2\Delta_{p}
.
By the particle-hole symmetry, we thereby express the DC spin-current density
within the particle space
as

\displaystyle{\bf J}^{\rm DC}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{8mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma=\{1,2\}}{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})\otimes{\bf k}^{\prime}\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}}

\displaystyle\times\Bigg[\frac{1}{\left(E_{\beta}(k)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+i\delta\right)}\left(\frac{2f(E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime}))-1}{E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta}+\frac{2f(E_{\beta}(k))-1}{E_{\beta}(k)-E_{\gamma}(q)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta}\right)

\displaystyle+\frac{2f(E_{\gamma}(q))-1}{\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\beta}(k)-\xi\hbar\omega-i\delta\right)}\Bigg]+{\rm H.c.}.

(91)

With the on-shell approximation, the DC spin-current density (
91
) becomes

\displaystyle{\bf J}^{\rm DC}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{\hbar^{2}}{4mA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma=\{1,2\}}\big({\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})\otimes{\bf k}^{\prime}\big)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}}

\displaystyle\times\frac{f(E_{\beta}(k))-f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)\left(E_{\beta}(k)-E_{\gamma}(q)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)}+{\rm H.c.}.

(92)

We then calculate the DC spin current (
92
) by using the contour integral.
We first perform the integral over
k^{\prime}
. When
(\theta_{k^{\prime}}-\varphi)\in(-\pi/2,\pi/2)
,
\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)>0
, so we close the integral contour in the upper complex plane for
k^{\prime}
, where the singularities are located at
(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu+\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx q_{\omega}^{+}(1+i\delta)
and
-(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu-\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx-q_{\omega}^{-}(1-i\delta)
, where

q_{\omega}^{\pm}=(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu\pm\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}
.
When
(\theta_{k^{\prime}}-\varphi)\in(\pi/2,3\pi/2)
,
\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)<0
, so we close the integral contour in the lower complex plane, where the singularities are located at
(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu-\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx q_{\omega}^{-}(1-i\delta)
and
-(1/\hbar)\sqrt{2m(\mu+\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta)^{2}-\Delta_{p}^{2}})}\approx-q^{+}_{\omega}(1+i\delta)
. When
\varphi\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2)
, we divide the integral region
\theta_{k^{\prime}}\in[0,\pi]
into
[0,\pi/2+\varphi]
and
(\pi/2+\varphi,\pi]
and calculate Eq. (
92
) as

\displaystyle{\bf J}^{\rm DC}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{8\pi}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\frac{-i(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega)}{\sqrt{(E_{\gamma}(q)-\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta^{2}_{p}}}\frac{f(E_{\beta}(k))-f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{E_{\beta}(k)-E_{\gamma}(q)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta}e^{-ik\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)}

\displaystyle\times\bigg[\int^{\pi/2+\varphi}_{0}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega}}\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf q}^{+}_{\omega},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{iq^{+}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)}

\displaystyle-\int^{\pi/2+\varphi}_{0}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{{\bf q}^{-}_{\omega}}\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},-{\bf q}^{-}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}(-{\bf q}^{-}_{\omega},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{-iq^{-}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)}

\displaystyle+\int_{\pi/2+\varphi}^{\pi}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{{\bf q}^{-}_{\omega}}\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},{\bf q}^{-}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf q}^{-}_{\omega},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{iq^{-}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)}

\displaystyle-\int_{\pi/2+\varphi}^{\pi}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega}}\otimes{\bf Q}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},-{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}(-{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{-iq^{+}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)}\bigg],

(93)

in which
{\bf q}^{+}_{\omega}=q_{\omega}^{+}(\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}}\hat{\bf y}+\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}}\hat{\bf z})
and

.
We then perform the integral over
k
.

\varphi\in[\pi/2,3\pi/2)

\displaystyle{\bf J}^{\rm DC}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle+{\rm H.c.}.

(94)

B.2
Spin-torque density in the linear-response regime

The spin-torque density in the linear-response regime reads

\displaystyle{\bf T}^{(1)}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\left\langle\hat{\boldsymbol{a}}^{\dagger}_{{\bf k}}U^{\dagger}_{\bf k}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t){\bf Q}^{p}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})U_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}({\boldsymbol{\rho}},t)\hat{\boldsymbol{b}}^{(1)}_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}\right\rangle+{\rm H.c.}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum^{4}_{\beta\alpha=1}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}f(E_{\beta}(k))}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}+{\rm H.c.}

\displaystyle\approx\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\Bigg(\sum_{\beta\alpha=\{1,2\}}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}f(E_{\beta}(k))}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}

\displaystyle+\sum_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}=\{3,4\}}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}f(E_{\bar{\beta}}(k))}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}\Bigg)+{\rm H.c.}.

(108)

When
T\rightarrow 0
K, the spin-torque density in the linear-response regime vanishes:

\displaystyle{\mathbf{T}}^{(1)}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle\rightarrow\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}=\{3,4\}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}+{\rm H.c.}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}=\{3,4\}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)+i\delta}

\displaystyle+\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}=\{3,4\}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}-{\bf k}^{\prime})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}+i\xi\omega t}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\bar{\alpha}}(k^{\prime})+E_{\bar{\beta}}(k)-i\delta}=0,

(109)

in which
{\bf Q}^{p\dagger}_{\bar{\beta}\bar{\alpha}}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})=-{\bf Q}^{p}_{\bar{\alpha}\bar{\beta}}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})
is used.
With this property, Eq. (
108
) is reduced to

\displaystyle{\mathbf{T}}^{(1)}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\beta\alpha=\{1,2\}}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf k})\frac{e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i\xi\omega t}}{\xi\hbar\omega-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+E_{\beta}(k)+i\delta}f(E_{\beta}(k))+{\rm H.c.}.

(110)

By the contour integral, Eq. (
110
) becomes

\displaystyle{\mathbf{T}}^{(1)}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4\pi}\sum_{{\bf k}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta}\frac{-m(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)}{\hbar^{2}\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta^{2}_{p}}}f(E_{\beta}(k))

\displaystyle\times\bigg[\int^{\varphi+\pi/2}_{\varphi-\pi/2}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},{\bf k}^{+}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}({\bf k}^{+}_{\omega},{\bf k})e^{i[k^{+}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)-k\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)-\xi\omega t]}

\displaystyle+\int^{\varphi+\pi/2}_{\varphi-\pi/2}d\theta_{k^{\prime}}{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}\left({\bf k},-{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega}\right)\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\alpha\beta}(-{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega},{\bf k})e^{i[-k^{-}_{\omega}\rho\cos(\theta_{k^\prime}-\varphi)-k\rho\cos(\theta_{k}-\varphi)-\xi\omega t]}\bigg]+{\rm H.c.},

(111)

in which
{\bf k}^{+}_{\omega}=k_{\omega}^{+}(\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}},\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}})
and
{\bf k}^{-}_{\omega}=k_{\omega}^{-}(\cos\theta_{k^{\prime}},\sin\theta_{k^{\prime}})
.
Driven by the one-dimensional exchange field, with the coupling constant Eq. (
88
), the spin-torque density when away from the source

\displaystyle{\mathbf{T}}^{(1)}(y)=\frac{1}{2A}\sum_{k_{y},k_{z}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta=1}^{2}\frac{-m(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)}{\hbar^{2}\sqrt{(E_{\beta}(k)+\xi\hbar\omega)^{2}-\Delta^{2}_{p}}}f(E_{\beta}(k))

\displaystyle\times\bigg[{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}[(k_{y},k_{z}),(k^{+}_{\xi},k_{z})]{\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{\xi}(k^{+}_{\xi}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\beta}[(k^{+}_{\xi},k_{z}),(k_{y},k_{z})]e^{i(k^{+}_{\xi}y-k_{y}y-\xi\omega t)}

\displaystyle+{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}[(k_{y},k_{z}),(-k^{-}_{\xi},k_{z})]{\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{\xi}(-k^{-}_{\xi}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\beta}[(-k^{-}_{\xi},k_{z}),(k_{y},k_{z})]e^{i(-k^{-}_{\xi}y-k_{y}y-\xi\omega t)}\bigg]+{\rm H.c.}.

(112)

B.3
DC spin-torque density

In the nonlinear-response regime, the spin-torque density

\displaystyle\mathbf{T}^{(2)}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi_{1}\xi_{2}=\pm}\sum^{4}_{\alpha\beta\gamma=1}\bigg({\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\frac{{\mathcal{G}}^{\xi_{1}}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf q}){\mathcal{G}}^{\xi_{2}}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q}-{\bf k})e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}-i(\xi_{1}+\xi_{2})\omega t}f(E_{\beta}(k))}{(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\beta}(k)-\xi_{2}\hbar\omega-i\delta)(E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-E_{\beta}(k)-(\xi_{1}+\xi_{2})\hbar\omega-i\delta)}

\displaystyle+

\displaystyle\tilde{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})\frac{{\mathcal{G}}^{\xi_{2}}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf q}){\mathcal{G}}^{-\xi_{1}}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q}-{\bf k})e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}+i(\xi_{1}-\xi_{2})\omega t}f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})+\xi_{2}\hbar\omega+i\delta)(\xi_{1}\hbar\omega-E_{\beta}(k)+E_{\gamma}(q)-i\delta)}\bigg)+{\rm H.c.},

(113)

in which the matrix elements

\displaystyle\tilde{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{\bf k}^{\prime})=\Delta_{p}\phi^{\dagger}_{\beta}({\bf k})\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}0&0&-{e^{i\theta_{\bf k}}}&0\\
0&0&0&e^{-i\theta_{\bf k}}\\
-e^{-i\theta_{\bf k}}&0&0&0\\
0&e^{i\theta_{\bf k}}&0&0\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}&\\
&-{\boldsymbol{\sigma}^{\ast}}\end{array}\right)\phi_{\alpha}({\bf k}^{\prime}).

The DC component

\displaystyle\mathbf{T}^{\rm DC}_{s}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2iA}\sum_{{\bf k}{\bf k}^{\prime}{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma=1}^{2}\overline{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})\mathcal{G}^{-\xi}_{\alpha\gamma}({\bf k}^{\prime},{\bf q})\mathcal{G}^{\xi}_{\gamma\beta}({\bf q},{\bf k})e^{i({\bf k}^{\prime}-{\bf k})\cdot{\boldsymbol{\rho}}}

\displaystyle\times\frac{f(E_{\beta}(k))-f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k^{\prime})-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)\left(E_{\beta}(k)-E_{\gamma}(q)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)}+{\rm H.c.},

(120)

in which the matrix elements

\displaystyle\overline{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}({\bf k},{{\bf k}^{\prime}})=\Delta_{p}\phi^{\dagger}_{\beta}({\bf k})\left(\begin{array}[]{cc}{\boldsymbol{\sigma}}&\\
&-{\boldsymbol{\sigma}^{\ast}}\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}[]{cccc}0&0&{e^{i\theta_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}}}&0\\
0&0&0&-e^{-i\theta_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}}\\
e^{-i\theta_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}}&0&0&0\\
0&-e^{i\theta_{{\bf k}^{\prime}}}&0&0\end{array}\right)\phi_{\alpha}({\bf k}^{\prime}).

In the absence of the quasiparticle population at zero temperature, the DC spin-torque density
\mathbf{T}_{s}^{\rm DC}({\boldsymbol{\rho}})
vanishes.

For the one-dimensional exchange field, with the coupling constant Eq. (
88
), the DC spin-torque density

\displaystyle\mathbf{T}^{\rm DC}_{s}(y)=\frac{1}{8\pi^{2}iA}\sum_{{\bf q}}\sum_{\xi=\pm}\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma=1}^{2}\int dk^{\prime}_{y}dk_{y}\overline{\bf Q}^{p}_{\beta\alpha}[(k_{y},q_{z})(k^{\prime}_{y},q_{z})]e^{i(k^{\prime}_{y}-k_{y})y}

\displaystyle\times\left({\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{-\xi}(k_{y}^{\prime}-q_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\alpha\gamma}[(k^{\prime}_{y},q_{z}),(q_{y},q_{z})]\right)\left({\bf H}_{\rm ex}^{\xi}(q_{y}-k_{y})\cdot\boldsymbol{\cal S}_{\gamma\beta}[(q_{y},q_{z}),(k_{y},q_{z})]\right)

\displaystyle\times\frac{f(E_{\beta}(k_{y},q_{z}))-f(E_{\gamma}(q))}{\left(E_{\gamma}(q)-E_{\alpha}(k_{y}^{\prime},q_{z})-\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)\left(E_{\beta}(k_{y},q_{z})-E_{\gamma}(q)+\xi\hbar\omega+i\delta\right)}+{\rm H.c.}.

(127)

By the contour integral over the associated expression and numerical calculation, we can obtain the DC spin-torque density induced by the exchange interaction.
