MEMORANDUM FOR THE PRESIDENT

SUBJECT: President's Trip to Venezuela and Colombia

Forwarded herewith is a memorandum which contains
a brief assessment of public reaction in Venezuela and
Colombia to the President's visit. The memorandum also
summarizes some of the activities undertaken by this
Agency in connection with the visit.

The trip was undoubtedly an outstanding success and
may very well mark a turning point in United States relations
with Latin America. Current intelligence coverage of
Venezuela indicates, however, that the leftist opposition to
Betancourt continues to be active and aggressive and reports
