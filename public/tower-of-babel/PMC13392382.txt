Retraction Note: Study on novel neutron irradiation without beam shaping assembly in Boron Neutron Capture Therapy
Authors: Antònia Verdera, Javier Praena
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-73458-w, published online 28 September 2024

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, concerns were raised about the analyses and some of the claims of this Article. A post-publication peer review confirmed that the neutron fluence reported in the paper was overestimated because in Equation 7 an angular conversion factor was missing, which led to significantly different yield. Moreover, in Equation 7 the energy and the angular differentials are missing. The significantly different yield calculated in the Article affects the dose achievable with the beam geometry described, invalidating the key conclusions of this Article. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the claims of this Article.

Javier Praena agrees with the retraction and its wording. Antònia Verdera did not reply to the correspondence from Editors about this retraction.
