Correction: Protective effects of liraglutide on hypercholesterolemia-associated atherosclerosis involve attenuation of endothelial-monocyte adhesion through down-regulating the LOX-1/NF-κB signaling pathway
Authors: Aiping Wu, Ying Wu, Meiyan Song, Wen Chen, Kaizu Xu, Meifang Wu, Liming Lin
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-13014-2, published online 28 July 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors.

As a result of errors during figure assembly panels 6D, 6E and 6F were incorrectly labeled as “-/-/-/-/+” for the corresponding conditions, where instead they should read ox-LDL “-/+/+/+/+”. The figure was updated, and the original incorrect Figure 6 appears below for the record.

Effect of liraglutide on P-NF-κB p65 protein expression in ox-LDL-cultured TAECs cells. A-C represents the temporal correlation between the impact of ox-LDL on NF-κB p65 phosphorylation in TAECs cells. D-F represents the concentration-effect relationship of liraglutide on NF-κB p65 phosphorylation induced by ox-LDL in TAEC cells. Original uncropped blot images with visible membrane edges are provided in the Supplementary material. The ratio of target protein to β-actin was used to indicate the level of target protein. P-NF-κB p65, phosphorylated nuclear factor-κB p65 subunit; T-NF-κB p65, total nuclear factor-κBp65 subunit. Values are expressed as mean ± SD, n = 4 independent experiments; aP < 0.05 vs. Control group; bP < 0.05 vs. ox-LDL (50 mg/L).

Additionally, Figure 8 included incorrect data. The figure was updated with the data that is correct and consistent with the main text. The original Figure 8 appears below for the record.

Effect of liraglutide or IKK-16 on ox-LDL-induced monocyte adhesion to endothelial. Values are expressed as mean ± SD, n = 4 independent experiments; aP < 0.05 vs. vehicle-treated TAECs; bP < 0.05 vs. ox-LDL-treated TAECs.

The original Article has been corrected.
