No. 928 The Delegation to the Allied Commission on Reparations to the Representative on the Allied Commission on Reparations (Pauley)[1]

secret urgent

Moscow, 25 July 1945.

To Deane’s party Berlin for Ambassador Pauley from Sproul, Abramovitz and Bergson signed Deane M 25102.

Following correction of paragraphs (4) and (5) of Confmess 14 of 24 July.[2]

Value of livestock and agricultural equipment: A equals 1,250 million, B equals 435 million.

Based on gross value of production, output of agriculture in A equals 1,200 million, or about 9 per cent of total for old Reich; B equals 425 million.

New subject. Confmess 14 re Alexander should be 15.[3] This Confmess 16.

Source: Pauley Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Mission, Moscow, via Army channels.
[2] document No. 919.
[3] Not printed.
