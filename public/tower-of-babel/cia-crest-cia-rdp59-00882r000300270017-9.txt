STATEMENT REGARDING DAMAGE TO THE HOUSEHOLD EFFECTS

25X1 A9a

MY household effects were received in a battered and shattered
condition. The heavy damage resulted from negligent and careless
packing which is nearly indescribable. Fragile items were thrown
loosely into boxes with no packing whatsoever. In one such box a Toast-
aster toaster was smashed and a l6m movie projector was broken into
several pieces. In another box two children’s tricycles were thrown
in on top of two new mahogany lamp tables. As a result the tables
were removed from the box in splinters.

There was absolutely no attempt made to prevent furniture from
being Jarred to pieces, gouged, and splintered. A mahogany desk
was taken from an u nda maged crate in same thirty-five pieces. A
radio-phonograph console was cracked in the center and shattered on
the corners. A dining room table had all four legs broken off.

Book-cases were gouged and broken apart. A mahogany bed looked
as though it had been hacked with an axe. Chests of drawers were
packed in a manner which permitted the drawers to move back and forth,
resulting in the fronts of the drawers being knocked off. A relatively
heavy typewriter was placed cm the shelf of a large bookcase with
lattice work sides, and as a result the typewriter slid back and
forth shattering the sides of the bookcase and ruining the case of the
typewriter.

Overstuffed furniture arrived will all the legs broken. A new
Philco refrigerator had been removed from the factory crate p ec k ed
in an unstable box which permitted the front and sides to the refrigerator
to be cur shed. Phonograph records were stacked in a box wit out protective
packing; dozens of them were broken.

Item, which ordinarily are not considered fragile, were ruined by
nails being driven into the contents. Thus several encyclopedias had nails
driven through them and a mattress was ripped.

Most of the damage can never be repaired, item which have been
repaired have nothing near their former appearance or value, while indirect
expense resulting from the damage have not been taken into consideration.
