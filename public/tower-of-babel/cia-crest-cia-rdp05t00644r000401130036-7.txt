Dear Stan:

)«/

One of the nice things about being in touch with you again after a stretch of time
is that it may lead to our actually seeing one another again. I hope that happens,
which is another way of saying that I hope you will accept the invitation which I'm
delighted to extend to you in this letter.

I'm writing on behalf of the Forum Committee of the City Club of Cleveland, and of
the Club's 1,200 members, to invite you to address the City Club Forum.

The Forum is held each Friday - luncheon at Noon, the address at 12:30 p.m. The
address, generally 30 minutes in length, is followed by a question-and-answer period
to 1:30 p.m. The Forum is carried by 17 area radio stations and has national wire-
service coverage. The Forum is the lonqest uninterrupted weekly forum series in the
U.S.A.

In addition to the Forum address there are options for a Press Conference and for
interviews before local TV cameras and in local radio stations.

One of the V. I. P.'s this year to have stood before the City Club audience and the
podium microphones is another son of Sabrina, our friend Bill Webster. Having had
the experience here. Bill could fill in any missing bits of information for you.

Forum speakers have been local, regional, national and international persons: senators,
cabinet members, national leaders of various movements and causes. Among '78-' 79 Forum
speakers besides Bill have been: Joseph Califiano, John Glenn, Jack Anderson, Dr.
Benjamin Spock, George Steinbrenner, Adalai Stevenson, Jr., William F. Buckley, Eleanor
Smeal, Patricia Deri an, Maynard Jackson, and Mary Rose Oakar.

We propose the following Fridays: January 18th or 25th; any Friday in February except
the 8th and any Friday in March, April, or May. If none of these times are suitable
and you're intrigued by this invitation, please suggest another Friday date which fits
your calendar.

14 December 1979

Dear Perry,

How nice to hear from you. It has certainly been some time and
I wish our paths could cross again soon. I was. in Cleveland just a
few weeks ago both for an off-the-record interview with Tom VaiS and
his editorial board and to have dinner with a group of fifteen leading
business executives. Had I realized that you were in Cleveland, I
would most certainly have asked to have you included with the dinner
group so that we could have said hello.

Unfortunately, I am going to have to decline your very kind invi-
tation to address the Forum in the first half of 1980. It is just the
kind of thing I like to do and enjoy. The demands for such appearances,
however, are never ending. At this point I do not feel I can justify
a return trip to Cleveland so soon. I am conscientiously trying to
get around the country and speak to and with various types of groups
in many areas. While I realize that this last trip to Cleveland did
not cover anywhere near the size of audience you would have at the Forum,

I still feel that I should touch some other geographical bases before
returning. On top of that, the calendar is already mercifully full
through the spring.

I apologize for not being able to be more responsive. Perhaps we
can work something out at a more distant time. Alternatively, if anything
should bring ire anywhere near Cleveland on a Friday, I will give you as
much notice as I can and see if we can work something out.

STANS FI ELD TURNER

Again, thanks for thinking of me find very warmest wishes.
