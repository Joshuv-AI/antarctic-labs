Memorandum for: Chief, Graphics Register/OCD

1. The staff of the Senate Internal Security and Immi-
gration Subcommittees, have long been interested in Com-
munist penetration in Cuba and relationships which they feel
exist between General Batista on the one hand and the Com-
munists on the other. They feel that this relationship is
closer than General Batista would desire the U.S. to believe.

2. The staff has just made available to me the attached
photographs which they wish to have returned ta as soon as
possible. Will you, therefore, please return the pictures

to me as soon as you have copied them.

3. You will note that figure "1” in the larger photograph
is alleged to be President Batista, and figure "2" Bias Roca,
an alleged Latin American Communist Party organizer.

4. The second picture allegedly is of President Batista
with some communists in Cuba.

5. These pictures were supposed to have been taken at
about the time that Cuba broke off relations with the USSR.

6. I am sending a copy of this memorandum to Chief,
Staff C/DDP and the Chief, WH Division/DDP, with the sug-
gestion that they may wish to have copies of these prints as
