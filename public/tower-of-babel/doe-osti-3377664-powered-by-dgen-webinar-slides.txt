 Spatial Data Matters in Forecasting Load and
 Technology Adoption

• dGenTM uses household-level
  data to instantiate consumers
  (agents).
• Attributes can include building
  suitability or location on the
  distribution system.
• Agents can be individual or
  statistical.
• This level of spatial resolution is
  crucial for distribution system
  modeling.

 Create Statistically Representative Agents

To reconcile datasets of disparate resolutions, we represent customers
through a statistical framework:
1. Sample eligible spatial points, assigning customers a
representative location. These are used to associate
with other spatial attributes. 3. Associate customer segments with
 national distributions, such as customer
 load patterns.

 2. Associate with exclusions,
 resource, rates, and so on.

 PySAM Integration
PySAM: Python implementation of NLR’s System Advisor Model, a free software tool that
enables detailed performance and financial analysis for distributed PV, batteries, and other
energy technologies (https://sam.nlr.gov).
• Detailed battery module: Allows users to specify a variety of technical parameters (e.g.,
 chemistry type, current/capacity values, degradation) and, importantly, dispatch strategies.
• Financial modules: Enable complex tariff simulation and detailed cash flow analysis.
