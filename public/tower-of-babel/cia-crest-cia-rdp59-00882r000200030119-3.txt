DEFINITIONS

Sec. 6. As used in this Act.— —

(a) The tens "foreign intelligence" shall be construed tc sear, the product

of the timely evaluation , correlation, and interpretation of foreign intelligence
information.

(b) The term "foreign intelligence information" consists of all data pertaining
to foreign governments or areas, will oh may affect the foreign policy or tlie
national defense and security of the United States.

(c) The terra "research" is a process of evaluation (selection), correlation
(synthesis), and interpretation (analysis) of intelligence information for
the production of intelligence.

(d) The term "evaluation" is the process of systematic and critical examination of
information for the purpose of determining its usefulness, credibility anc!
accuracy*

(e) The term "synthesis"ls the correlation of information with all available
related material.

(f) The terra "interpretation" is the determination of the probable significance of
evaluated information.
