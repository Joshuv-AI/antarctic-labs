Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

The purpose of this project is to identify local zoning ordinances, state permitting requirements,
federal regulatory bottlenecks throughout the development process, and the potential to use Large
Language Models (LLM) and aggregate permitting and interconnection data for commercial and
industrial (C&I), community, and utility-scale solar photovoltaic (PV) and battery storage
projects.

Summary of Research Results:

The intent of this project was to identify state and municipal zoning ordinances and permitting
processes for C&I, community, and utility-scale solar and battery storage projects; validate and
improve the accuracy of LLMs to retrieve this data in the future iterations of the 1Climates
application and provide an overall roadmap of federal statutes that can have a significant impact
on development timelines. Due to the CRADA partner, 1Climate, selling its intellectual property
during the execution of this CRADA, these tasks were either partially completed or not
undertaken.

Task 1:

1.2 Contractor will compile data on solar and battery storage permitting and siting
requirements for C&I, community, and utility-scale projects into a query-able database.

1.1 Contractor will research zoning ordinances and land use restrictions for solar and battery
storage across federal, state, municipal, and utility levels.

1.2 Contractor will detail pathways to permitting for discretionary permits and other special
permit requirements for solar and battery storage.

State and Municipal Permitting and Zoning Ordinances for Solar and Battery Storage

The research team began its collection of zoning ordinances and land use restrictions for solar and battery
storage projects at the state and municipal levels for the following states (See Figure 1):

 • Massachusetts
 • Michigan
 • Illinois
 • Pennsylvania

This research entailed collecting state-level permitting requirements for solar and battery storage
projects, identifying municipal zoning ordinances and permitting requirements for approximately
15-20 municipalities, and identifying any interactions between state-level permitting
requirements and local zoning ordinances and permitting requirements. Initially, data collection
was planned in at least six states; however, early termination of the CRADA agreement limited
the collection to the four states identified above.

Task 2:

Contractor will support the Participant in validating and improving the accuracy of solar
regulatory data retrieved through Large Language Models through techniques like prompt
engineering.

LLM Validation of Additional State and Municipal Permitting and Zoning Ordinances for
Solar and Battery Storage

The research team planned to assist 1Climate in validating additional state and municipal
permitting and zoning ordinances for solar and battery storage based on the terminology and
types of permits and zoning ordinances identified during the data collection in Task 1.

The CRADA was terminated before this task could be started.

Task 3:

Contractor will create a solar project development roadmap with detail on federal statutes that
can have a significant impact on development timelines (e.g., NEPA, Endangered Species Act,
National Historic Preservation Act) and the alternative pathways for avoiding lengthy reviews
(e.g., categorical exclusions)

Solar Project Development Roadmap on Federal States Impacting Development Solar and
Battery Storage Timelines

The research team planned to assist 1Climate by creating a solar project development roadmap
with detail on federal statutes that can have a significant impact on development timelines (e.g.,
National Environmental Policy Act, Endangered Species Act, National Historic Preservation
Act) and the alternative pathways for avoiding lengthy reviews (e.g., categorical exclusions).
