Linkage problem on optimal 1 1 -planar graphs
Shohei Koizumi, Ryosuke Osaka, Yusuke Suzuki

Abstract
Enami and Maezawa [ 3 ] give a complete characterization of ( s 1 , s 2 , … , s k ) (s_{1},s_{2},\ldots,s_{k}) -linked planar graphs for any k k -tuple of positive integers. In this paper, we investigate linkage problems for optimal 1-planar graphs. In particular, we show that every optimal 1-planar graph with connectivity 6 6 is ( 5 , 5 ) (5,5) -linked. Moreover, for an optimal 1 1 -planar graph G G that is not ( 2 , 2 , 1 ) (2,2,1) -linked, we characterize disjoint vertex subsets S 1 , S 2 , S 3 S_{1},S_{2},S_{3} in G G with | S 1 | = | S 2 | = 2 |S_{1}|=|S_{2}|=2 and | S 3 | = 1 |S_{3}|=1 such that G G is not { S 1 , S 2 , S 3 } \{S_{1},S_{2},S_{3}\} -linked.
