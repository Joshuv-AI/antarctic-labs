Y_{r}\xrightarrow{\text{$\phi_{r}$}}Y_{r-1}\xrightarrow{\text{$\phi_{r-1}$}}\cdots Y_{2}\xrightarrow{\text{$\phi_{2}$}}Y_{1}\xrightarrow{\text{$\phi_{1}$}}Y_{0}=\{pt\}

is a generalized Bott tower with stage
r
.

Proof.

We have
w_{1,n_{1}}=s_{\alpha_{q(1)}}s_{\alpha_{q-1(1)}}\cdots s_{\alpha_{1(1)}}
. Thus from
[
3
, Lemma 5.8]
,
X(w_{1,n_{1}})
is the unique minimal dimensional Schubert variety in
G_{1}/Q_{1}
admitting semistable points for the
\lambda_{q(1)}(\mathbb{G}_{m})
-linearized line bundle
\mathcal{L}(n_{1}\omega_{1(1)})
. Therefore, from
[
3
, Theorem 7.1]
, we have
Y_{1}
is isomorphic to
\mathbb{P}^{q-1}
.

Fix
2\leq k\leq r
. Then using
Theorem

6.2
, we have
Y_{k}
is isomorphic to the projective space bundle
\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E}_{k})
over
Y_{k-1}
, where
\mathcal{E}_{k}=\mathcal{L}_{k,0}^{\oplus q}\oplus(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{k-1}\mathcal{L}^{\oplus q-1}_{k,j})
and for all
0\leq j\leq k-1
,
\mathcal{L}_{k,j}
is a line bundle on
Y_{k-1}
.
Now the proof follows from the definition of generalized Bott tower in
Section

2.2
.
∎
