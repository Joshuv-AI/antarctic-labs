Occurrence of Drowning Accident, 2020–2024
Authors: Sebin Han, 세빈 한
Published: 2026

1. Occurrence of Drowning Accident by Sex and Age Group

From 2020 to 2024, the number of patients who visited the emergency room of 23 participating hospitals that joined the Emergency Department In-depth Injury Survey due to drowning accident was 523, with 385 men, and 138 women (Figure 1). By age group, the occurrence of children under the age of 9 was more frequent (29.6% of total cases). The proportion of death was 28.7%, and that of group of 70 years old and over was 40.3%.

Occurrences of drowning accident by age group in Emergency Department In-depth Injury Survey, 2020–2024

*Source: Emergency Department In-depth Injury Survey, from 2020 to 2024.

*Subjects: Patients induced by drowning accident excepted self harm, suicide, violence, homicide, etc.

*Attention: Emergency Department In-depth Injury Survey has been conducted in the emergency departments of 23 institutions, of which data could not represent the nation-wide occurrences.

2. Occurrence of Drowning Accident by Place and Time

The drowning accident occurred mainly outdoors, including seas, rivers, etc. (46.1%), while the case mostly occurred between June and August (Figure 2).

1. 성‧연령별 발생 현황

응급실손상환자심층조사(23개 참여병원에서 실시)에 의하면 2020년부터 2024년까지 불의의 익수사고로 인해 응급실에 내원한 환자 수는 523명이었으며, 남자가 385명(73.6%), 여자가 138명(26.4%)이었다(그림 1). 연령별로는 9세 이하에서 발생빈도가 가장 높았고(전체 환자의 29.6%), 50대 이후 증가하였다. 익수사고 환자 중 28.7%가 사망하며, 70세 이상 사망분율이 40.3%로 가장 높았다.

2. 발생 장소 및 시기
