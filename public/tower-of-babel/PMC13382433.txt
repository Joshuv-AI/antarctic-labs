Expression of concern—Identification of critical brain regions for autism diagnosis from fMRI data using explainable AI: an observational analysis of the ABIDE dataset
Authors: The Editors for eClinicalMedicine
Published: 2026

On Sept 18, 2025, eClinicalMedicine published the paper Identification of critical brain regions for autism diagnosis from fMRI data using explainable AI: an observational analysis of the ABIDE dataset.1

In March 2026, we received a Letter2 from a reader pointing out some issues regarding the analysis. In response, the authors of the paper started to investigate the issue. In June 2026, the authors confirmed a drop in performance related to the unintentional methodological leakage identified in the original evaluation pipeline. The authors are now completing a thorough check of all the data and are working on the re-analysis.

The Editors wish to alert readers to these concerns while we work to resolve this matter; we will provide an update once available.
