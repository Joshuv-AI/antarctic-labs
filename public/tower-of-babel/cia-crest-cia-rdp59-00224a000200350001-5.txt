1. In response to your request, subject bills H.R. 7353,

S.2488 and S. 2335 have been reviewed by this Office to determine
from the viewpoint of personnel administration whether any action
or the establishment of an Agency position through your Office is
indicated or recommended.

2. The effect of the basic measure, as presented by these
bills, is to establish a definite limitation on a veteran's right
of appeal under the Veterans' Preference Act thus rendering
legislative recognition to a recommendation of the Hoover Commission.

It is felt that the subject is one which may prove to be highly
controversial in nature, and that if the limitation is approved, it
would eliminate the only effective appeal light available to large
numbers of Federal employees. However, evaluating the measure in
the light of a desire and effort to improve personnel administration,
it is recommended that, if called upon. Agency support be accorded
the legislation. Such a position is well supported on the basis that
veteran's rights sure reasonably protected by the five-year period,
from which military service is excluded, and that it will assist in
an eventual equalization of rights among all Federal employees. It
is requested that this Office be kept informed in regards to any
reports, discussions and as to the progress of the measure during
its Congressional consideration.
