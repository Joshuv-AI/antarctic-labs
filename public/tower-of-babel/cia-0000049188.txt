ON 15 NOVEMBER 19 AT APPROXIMATELY 1800 HOURS, MILITA SCHOOL
DEPUTY COMMANDER CARLOS CAMILO ((HERNANDEZ)) ORDERED, BY RADIO,
ATLACATL BATTALION LIEUTENANTS YUSSHY RENE ((MENDOZA)) AND
GOKZALO ((GUEVARA)) TO REPORT TO THE MII.ITARY SCHOOL. UPON

THEIR ARRIVAL, MAJOR HERNANDEZ ADVISED THEM THAT MILITARY

SCHOOL COMMANDER COLONEL GUILLERMO ((BENAVIDES)) WISHED TO SPEAK
WITH THEM, AND THE TWO LIEUTENANTS WENT IMMEDIATELY TO BENAVIDES'
OFFICE. AFTER THE MEETING WITH BENAVIDES, THE TWO LIEUTENANTS
ASSEMBLED THE ATLACATL BATTALION TROOPS PRESENT AT THE MILITARY
SCHOOL AND BEGAN TO CALL OUT SEVERAL NAMES FROM EACH PLATOON,

USING ONLY NICKNAMES.

3. AT APPROXIMATELY 1900 HOURS, LIEUTENANT GUEVARA ORDERED
SEVERAL SOLDIERS ON DUTY AT THE MILITARY ACADEMY TO ACCOMPANY
HIM TO THE PUBLIC SECURITY ACADEMY (CETIPOL) IN SANTA TECLA
TO PICK UP SOME UNIFORMS. THE GROUP, LEAD BY LIEUTENANT GUEVARA,
ARRIVED AT CETIPOL AND WAS MET BY CETIPOL DIRECTOR COLONEL
RIGOBERTO ((SOTO)) MARTINEZ, WHO GAVE THE GROUP APPROXIMATELY 15
BOXES OF NEW CAMOUFLAGE UTILITY UNIFORMS WITHOUT INSIGNIA OR
PATCHES OF ANY KIND. THE GROUP RETURNED TO THE MILITARY ACADEMY
AND THE NEW UNIFORMS WERE DISTRIBUTED TO THE BIRI ATLACATL TROOPS
SELECTED BY LIEUTENANTS MENDOZA AND GUEVARA. AS THE SOLDIERS
DRESSED IN THE NEW UNIFORMS AND PUT ON CAMOUFLAGE PAINT, MAJOR
HERNANDEZ CIRCULATED AMONG THE SOLDIERS OFFERING THEM ENCOURAGE-
MENT AND SUPPORT.

4. ETWEEN 2000 HOURS AND 2100
HOURS THE SELECTED GROUPS, ACCO IED BY LIEUTENANTS GUEVARA

AND MENDOZA, DEPARTED THE MILITARY SCHOOL IN A BEIGE MILITARY
PICK-UP TRUCK. SHORTLY AFTERWARD, A SECOND BEIGE MILITARY PICK-UP
TRUCK WITH SEVERAL SOLDIERS DEPARTED THE MILITARY SCHOOL AS WELL.

5. AT APPROXIMATELY 0400 HOURS ON THE MORNING OF

16 NOVEMBER 1989 THE PICK-UP TRUCKS RETURNED TO THE MIIITTARY
SCHOOL. COLONEL BENAVIDES MET THE TRUCKS AT THE FRONT GATE

OF THE ACADEMY. AS THE TROOPS DISPERSED, ONE SOLDIER WAS
OVERHEARD TO SAY THAT THE SOLDIERS HAD JUST KILLED "THE HEADS

OF THE FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT (FMLN)." THE
SOLDIER EXPRESSED HIS BELIEF THAT, AS A RESULT OF THESE KILLINGS,
THE WAR WOULD END SOON BECAUSE COLONEI BENAVIDES HAD SAID
KILLING THE "FMLN HEADS" WOULD END 11.

6. ON 16 NOVEMBER 1989 AT APPROXIMATELY 0900 HOURS COLONEL
BENAVIDES GATHERED ALL OF THE SOLDIERS WHO HAD BEEN ON GUARD
DUTY AT THE MILITARY SCHOOL ON 15 ANI) 16 NOVEMBER. COLONEL
BENAVIDES ORDERED THE GROUP TO MAINTAIN TOTAL SILENCE ABOUT
THE MURDERS OF THE JESUIT PRIESTS ANID TWO OTHERS. HE INFORMED
THEM THAT THE OFFICIAL STORY THEY WOULD ALL ADHERE TO WAS THAT
THE FMLN HAD INFILTRATED THE UNIVERSITY OF CENTRAL AMERICA CAMPUS
AND MURDERED THE JESUITS AND TWO LAY PERSONS. COLONEL BENAVIDES

REITERATED THAT THE SOLDIERS WERE NOT TO SPEAK TO ANYONE ABOUT
THE MURDERS OR "WE WILL ALL BE DEAD."

7. ON APPROXIMATELY 18 NOVEMBER 1989 LIEUTENANT MENDOZA
PRIVATELY EXPRESSED REMORSE AT HAVING KILLED THE JESUIT PRIESTS
AND EXPRESSED HIS HOPE THAT COLONEL BENAVIDES WOULD NOT "TURN
US IN." LIEUTENANT MENDOZA SAID THAT HE HAD NOT WANTED TO KILL
THE JESUIT PRIESTS BUT HAD RECEIVED A DIRECT ORDER TO DO SO.

HE ADDED THAT THE KILLINGS WERE PARTICULARLY DIFFICULT FOR HIM
BECAUSE ONE OF THE MURDERED PRIESTS, IGNACIO MARTIN ((BARO)),
HAD BEEN HIS PROFESSOR AT ONE TIME. LIEUTENANT MENDOZA ALSO SAID
THAT THEN AIR FORCE COMMANDER GENERAL JUAN RAFAEL ((BUSTILLO) )
HAD OFFERED HIM PILOT TRAINING AS A REWARD FOR HIS (MENDOZA'S)
PARTICIPATION IN THE KILLINGS.
