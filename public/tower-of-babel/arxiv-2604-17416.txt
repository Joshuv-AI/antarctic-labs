Abstract
We report 59 Co nuclear quadrupole resonance (NQR) measurements on the Nd-based compound NdCo 2 Zn 18 Ga 2 , which undergoes an antiferromagnetic transition at T N = 1.5 T_{\rm N}=1.5 K. Although the NQR spectra show no detectable change across T N T_{\rm N} , the nuclear spin-lattice relaxation rate, 1 / T 1 1/T_{1} , exhibits a clear anomaly at T N T_{\rm N} . An analysis based on the alignment of the Nd moments demonstrates that the internal magnetic fields generated by these moments cancel each other at the Co sites. If the nearest-neighbor Nd moments align antiferromagnetically, this finding suggests that Ga substitution removes magnetic frustration, thereby increasing T N T_{\rm N} .
