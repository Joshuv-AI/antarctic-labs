Cabalist of Galicia in the eighteenth century; author of two cabalistic commentaries: one on the Psalms, "Migdal Dawid," with a general introduction under the title "Ṭal Orot," published together with the text, Vienna, 1792: and one on the Proverbs, "Bet Shelomoh," with an introduction entitled "Petaḥ ha-Bayit," Zolkiev, 1788.

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. i. 133: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 959; Walden, Shem ha-Gedolim he-Ḥadash, ii. 13. K. I. Br.
