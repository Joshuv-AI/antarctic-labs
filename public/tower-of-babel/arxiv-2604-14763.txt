Tight spectral conditions for the Hamiltonicity of K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free split graphs
Yiting Cai, Haiyan Guo, Hong-Jian Lai, Bo Zhou

Abstract
The Hamiltonicity and related subjects of split graphs, and in particular K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free split graphs with r ≥ 3 r\geq 3 received much attention. Dai et al. [Discrete Math. 345 (2022) 112826] conjectured that every ( r − 1 ) (r-1) -connected K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free split graph is Hamiltonian. They proved the case when r = 4 r=4 , and earlier Renjith and Sadagopan [Int. J. Found. Comput. Sci. 33 (2022) 1–32] proved the case when r = 3 r=3 . Recently, Liu, Song, Zhang and Lai [Discrete Math. 346 (2023) 113402] proved that a split graph is Hamiltonian if and only if it is fully cycle extendable. So for r = 3 , 4 r=3,4 every ( r − 1 ) (r-1) -connected K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free split graph is fully cycle extendable. We give tight spectral sufficient conditions for a K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free split graph to be Hamiltonian for r = 3 , 4 r=3,4 . Keywords: K 1 , r K_{1,r} -free graphs, split graphs, Hamiltonian graphs, Hamiltonian problem, spectral radius AMS Classification: 05C45, 05C40, 05C50
