EL SALVADOR

INSTRUCTIONS FROM PRESIDENT DUARTE TO THE

MINISTER OF JUSTICE TO INVESTIGATE NEW INFORMATION
ABOUT THE MURDER OF ARCHBISHOP ROMERO

LATE AUGUST 1985

— Soo

approved for Release
NOV 1993

TEXT: 1. IN LATE AUGUST 1985, PRESIDENT JOSE NAPOLEON
((DUARTE)) ORDERED THE MINISTER OF JUSTICE JUI.10 ALFREDO
((SAMAYOA)) TO LAUNCH AN IMMEDIATE INVESTIGATION OF NEW
INFORMATION OBTAINED BY DUARTE ABOUT THE MURDER OF
ARCHBISHOP OF SAN SALVADOR OSCAR ARNULFO ((ROMERO)). DUARTE
INSTRUCTED SAMAYOA TO EMPLOY THE SPECIAL INVESTIGATIVE

UNIT (SIU) AND ITS OVERSIGHT COMMISSION TO COMPLETE

THE INVESTIGATION BY THE END OF OCTOBER 1985 AND TO ARREST
ANY SUSPECTS IMPLICATED BY THE NEW EVIDENCE. COMMENT:
NO INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE ON THE CONTENT OF T
EVIDENCE. THE SIU WAS ORIGINALLY ESTABLISHED TO
INVESTIGATE THE 19 JUNE ZONA ROSA MASSACRE BUT WAS
EVENTUALLY TO ASSUME OTHER RESPONSIBILITIES.)

2. BASED ON THE NEW INFORMATION OBTAINED BY DUARTE,
SEVERAL INFLUENTIAL SALVADORAN CITIZENS ARE IMPLICATED
IN THE ROMERO MURDER CASE. THE NEW EVIDENCE DOES NOT
APPEAR TO INCRIMINATE NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE
(ARENA) PRESIDENT ROBERTO ((D'AUBUISSON)) ARRIETA,

BUT SEVERAL OF HIS ASSOCIATES ARE POSSIBLE SUSPECTS.
SAMAYOA, WHO HEADS THE SIU OVERSIGHT COMMISSION WHICH
CONSISTS OF PRIVATE SECRETARY TO THE PRESIDENT ALVARO
ERNESTO ((SALAZAR)) BRENES AND VICE-MINISTER OF INTERIOR
CARMEN AMALIA BARAHONA PANTOJA DE ((MORALES)), EXPRESSED
CONCERN FOR HIS PERSONAL SAFETY AND THAT OF THE OTHER
COMMISSION MEMBERS. SAMAYOA INDICATED THAT "POWERFUL
PEOPLE" IN THE SALVADORAN SOCIETY WOULD BE INVESTIGATED

AND THAT THIS COULD LEAD TO THREATS AGAINST THE LIVES
OF COMMISSION MEMBERS.

3. DUARTE'S ENTHUSIASM FOLLOWING THE ARREST OF
SEVERAL MEMBERS OF THE TERRORIST GROUP WHICH CONDUCTED
THE ZONA ROSA MASSACRE LED TO HIS DECISION TO PROCEED
WITH A SIMILAR INVESTIGATION OF ROMERO'S MURDER.

DUARTE IS CONFIDENT THAT THE INFORMATION WHICH HE
PROVIDED TO SAMAYOA WILL LEAD TO THE ARREST OF ONE OR
MORE SUSPECTS IN THE ROMERO MURDER CASE. HIS GOAL IS
TO MAINTAIN THE MOMENTUM OF PUBLIC SUPPORT GENERATED BY
THE ZONA ROSA INVESTIGATION BY MAKING A SIMILAR PUBLIC
DECLARATION OF THE SIU'S FINDINGS IN THE ROMERO CASE.
