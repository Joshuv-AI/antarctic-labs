WDH Dataset AI-Readiness Curator Intake Form v1.0
 (Machine-Actionability & Documentation)
Use this form when reviewing a dataset submission for an AI-readiness designation. Record a
verdict for every criterion using one of five values:
 • Met (fully satisfies the criterion),
 • Partial (substantially but not fully satisfies it; note what is missing in the evidence
 column),
 • Not met (does not satisfy it),
 • N/A (not applicable to this dataset), and
 • Not assessed (evidence not yet gathered, or insufficient evidence to decide).
N/A requires a rationale in the evidence column, and N/A on an Essential item additionally
requires curator approval. “Not assessed” is distinct from N/A: it marks an item as unresolved,
never as passing. Any Essential item that is Partial, Not met, or Not assessed blocks the AI-
ready designation until it is resolved.
Scope. This form produces two independent readings for a dataset. Section A (Machine-
Actionability) assesses whether an automated pipeline can find, load, parse, interpret, quality-
filter, and legally reuse the data, from the data file and provider-supplied file metadata; it yields a
readiness tier. Section B (Documentation) assesses whether the landing page gives a human
the content needed to find, understand, and judge the dataset for reuse; it yields a
documentation-completeness reading. The two readings are reported side by side and neither
gates the other. This form applies to WDH datasets at processing levels c0 and above (b1 by
exception, with rationale recorded). Raw and near-raw levels are out of scope. The form does
not assess suitability for any specific machine-learning task (representativeness, sampling bias,
or train/test leakage), which depends on the task and is characterized separately.

Scoring summary
Tally the verdicts and assign a readiness tier. Tiers are additive and gated: a dataset earns a tier only
when it satisfies that tier and every tier below it. The gates are:
 • Not Yet AI-Ready: at least one applicable Essential item is Partial, Not met, or unresolved.
 • Baseline: all applicable Essential items are Met.
 • Mature: all applicable Essential and all applicable Recommended items are Met.
 • Reference: all applicable items, including Advanced, are Met.

Item tiers and dataset tiers are distinct scales: Essential, Recommended, and Advanced describe how
much a criterion matters; Baseline, Mature, and Reference describe what a dataset has achieved. All
Essential items are required for Baseline; Recommended items are additionally required for Mature;
Advanced items for Reference. “Applicable” excludes items validly marked N/A (see verdict policy above);
an item marked Not assessed is treated as unresolved and blocks any tier that would require it.

Validator policy
Automated conformance checks use the IOOS compliance-checker (record the exact version in the
assessment record above), run against CF 1.10 or later and ACDD 1.3. Findings map to verdicts as
follows: errors are blocking; findings on required attributes are blocking for Essential criteria; informational
findings are non-blocking. A curated allowlist of known, benign warnings (e.g., a standard_name warning
on a variable that legitimately has no CF entry) is maintained so that conformant files are not forced to a
Partial verdict. Exceptions require a rationale and curator approval, recorded in the assessment record.
For compound criteria (e.g., WDH Data Standards conformance, or CF and ACDD conformance), itemize
each sub-condition in the evidence column; the verdict reflects the weakest sub-condition.
