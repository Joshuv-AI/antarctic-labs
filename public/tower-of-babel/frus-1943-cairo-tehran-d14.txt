Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (28 June 1943, d14)

secret

London, 28 June 1943.

Former Naval Person to President. Personal and secret. No. 334.

I send you first of all the very unpleasant reply I have received from U. J. and my rejoinder.[2] This certainly has its bearing on your proposal to meet him alone and I shall not seek to deter you if you can get him to come.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Channel of transmission not indicated.
[2] Neither printed herein. The exchange concerned the second front.
