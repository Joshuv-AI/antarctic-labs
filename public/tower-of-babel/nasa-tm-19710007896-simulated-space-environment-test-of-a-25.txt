 16. Abstract

 A 25-cell cadmium sulfide thin-film solar-cell module was tested in a simulated space

 electrical connections and l e a r n something of module electrical characteristics.
 During the test, the module w a s mechanically and electrically loaded to simulate

 cells.

 CONCLUDING REMARKS

 A 25-cell CdS solar cell module mechanically loaded at 32. 5 newtons per meter
(2.25 lb/ft) shows no joint deterioration after simulated space environment testing. The
electrical performance of the module during cycling was in all respects similar to that
of single cells manufactured in the same period and tested under similar cyclic condi-
tions. Based on the limited data obtained from this test, no additional problems a r e
evident when CdS cells a r e operated in a matrix in a module.
