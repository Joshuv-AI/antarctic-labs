Initial Political Reactions to Operation Desert Storm

Iraq: Baghdad’s Voice of the Masses radio at approximately
2200 EST was broadcasting patriotic songs. There has been no
official Iraqi reaction to the initiation of hostilities yet.

Israel: At approximately 1940 EST, CNN announced that the
Israeli military broadcast a statement advising citizens to
stay home and open the kits that had been distributed to
them, which contain gas masks. According to press reports,
Israeli Defense Forces imposed a curfew on the occupied
territories in response to Desert Storm.

Iran: Iranian radio at 2030 EST and later at 2125 EST
broadcast information concerning Operation Desert Storm,
quoting US sources and CNN sources in Baghdad. he broadcast
contained only factual data and no commentary. om

Japan: Prime Minister Kaifu announced that Japan resolutely
supports military action taken by the US based on the UN
Security Council resolutions, according to press. The
Cabinet endorsed Kaifu’s statement. r

U88R: According to Yugoslavian press reporting, Soviet Armed
‘Forces in the southern USSR were put on alert shortly before
the start of Desert Storm. The Soviet news agency TASS has
reported the onset of hostilities and repeated details from
Western reporting, but the Soviet government has not yet
issued any formal statements. a
