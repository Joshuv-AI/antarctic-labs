# TOB-0086 — GRIMORIUM VERUM
## Pseudo-Solomon | 1817 | Black Magic / Demonology / Solomonic

---

## 📖 OVERVIEW

**Full Title:** Grimorium Verum, vel probatissima Salomonis Claviculae Rabini Hebraïci ("The True Grimoire")
**Author:** Traditionally attributed to King Solomon; first printed 1817 (Chez Alibeck, Paris)
**Date:** First printed edition: 1817; claims much older origins
**Pages:** ~150
**Language:** French (this edition)
**Category:** Grimoire / Black Magic / Demonology / Solomonic

---

## 📚 CONTENTS

### Part I — The Art of Invoking Demons

- The Grand Grimoire's methods for summoning spirits
- Lucifer — the supreme demon
- The demon hierarchy
- Consecration of the magical book
- The "Ointment of Invocation"

### Part II — Pacts with Demons

- Methods for making pacts
- The "Pact of Blood"
- Promises and obligations
- Breaking or fulfilling pacts

### Part III — Magical Seals

- Various sigils and seals of demons
- Uses of each seal
- Methods of binding spirits to seals

### Demon Listings

- Lucifer
- Beelzebub
- Astaroth
- Mammon
- Belial
- And others

---

## 📌 HISTORICAL NOTES

**Authenticity:** The Grimorium Verum claims to be ancient but was first printed in 1817 in Paris. It represents a post-medieval grimoire tradition that combined older Solomonic elements with newer demonic conjuration practices.

**Solomonic Tradition:** Part of the "Solomonic grimoire" family, texts attributed to King Solomon that use his legendary wisdom and power over spirits as their authority structure.
