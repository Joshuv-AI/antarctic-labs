17 March 1978 .

Dear Bob,

I've just received an invitation to your retire-
ment ceremony on the 20th of April and wanted to get
back to you now because I will be out of town for the
next two weeks. Both Pat and I very much hope to be
able to make the ceremony and I have enclosed the
accept RSVP card. I say we hope to make it because
I am scheduled to testify on the Hill on the morning
of the 20th, but if that changes we will most certainly
be there. I'll have my staff get in touch with yours
just as soon as I know one way or the other.

If we are unable to attend, please know we will
be with both you and Kelly in thought and spirit as
you close out a most distinguished career. Since you
will temporarily hang your hat down the road in Falls
Church, please do get in touch.

Again, hope we make it on the 20th; in the
meantime, all the best.

STANSFIELD TURNER

General Robert J. Dixon, USAF
Commanding General
Tactical Air Command
Langley Air Force Base
Virginia 23665
