((OPPES)) THE FARABUNDO MARTI NATIONAL LIBERATION FRONT ((FNLN))
HAS DESCRIBED THE ARREST OF FIVE SOLDIERS ACCUSED OF COMKITTING THE
MASSACRE ((or SIX JESUIT PRIESTS -- FBIS)) AS "A NANIPULATED COVER
OPERATION." BASICALLY CRISTIAN! IS ALLEGED TO HAVE DECIDED TO
PUNISH A NUMBER OF LOW-RANKING OFFICERS IN ORDER TO CLOSE THE CASE
AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE AND TO END INTERNATIONAL PRESSURES. WHAT IS
YOUR OPINION IN THIS REGARD?

((ZAMORA)) THE FACT THAT PRESIDENT CRISTIAN] ACKNOWLEDGES THAT
NENBERS OF THE ARMY WERE RESPONSIBLE FOR THE MASSACRE IS A FIRST
STEP AND DEFINITELY A GOOD THING, BUT IT IS ENTIRELY INSUFFICIENT
UNLESS THE SUBSEQUENT STEPS ARE ALSO TAKEN. FIRST, TO IDENTIFY WHO
THE RESPONSIBLE PARTIES WERE: THIS 18 THE FIRST INPORTANT POINT,
BECAUSE NOBODY CAN FEEL SATISFIED IF ONLY Lo- RANKING OFFICERS ARE
IDENTIFIED. SUCH AN ACT CAN ONLY HAVE BEEN ORDERED BY HIGH-RANKING
OFFICERS. WE MUST NOT FORGET THE MURDER OF MONSIGNOR ROXERO, FOR
WHICH THERE WERE NO SENTENCES AND NO TRIALS.

((OPPES)) HOWEVER, THE ARKED FORCES HIGH COMMAND IKMEDIATELY
DENIED INVOLVEMENT IN THE OPERATION, STATING THAT IT WAS AN
ISOLATED ACTION "DIVORCED FRON ANY INSTITUTIONAL POLICY."

((ZAMORA)) 1 BELIEVE THIS IS ENTIRELY WRONGs THE MURDER OF THE
JESUIT FATHERS CANNOT BE CONSIDERED AN ISOLATED ACTION. OVER 7,000
PEOPLE HAVE DIED IN EL SALVADOR DURING 10 YEARS OF CIVIL WAR, AND
90 PERCENT OF THEM WERE CIVILIANS. POLITICAL ASSASSINATION BY THE
GOVERNMENT FORCES ITS A CONSTANT PRACTICE. FURTHERNORE TORTURE IS
REGULARLY PRACTISED IN EL SALVADOR. THE ARMY HIGH COMMAND CANNOT
FAIL TO KNOW THIS. THEY CANNOT CLAIM TO BE INNOCENT, BECAUSE THEY
HAVE THE GREATEST RESPONSIBILITIES FOR HUMAN RIGHTS VIOLATIONS.

((OPPES)) YET LAST MONTH THE SUMMIT OF CENTRAL AMERICAN HEADS
OF STATE IN SAN JOSE ENDED WITH A SUCCESS FOR PRESIDENT CRISTIANI,
WHO SECURED AN EXPLICIT CONDEMNATION OF THE OFFENSIVE LAUNCHED BY
THE GUERRILLAS IN NOVEMBER, WHEREAS NO SPECIFIC CHARGES WERE
LEVELED AT THE GOVERNMENT FOR HUNAN RIGHTS VIOLATIONS.

((ZAWORA)) THERE 18 A POSITIVE FACTOR THAT SHOULD RE BORNE IN
MIND, NAMELY, THE CALL FROM THE FIVE PRESIDENTS FOR A RESUNPTION OF

“THE DIALOGUE IN EL SALVADOR AND THE APPEAL TO THE UN SECRETARY
GENERAL TO CARRY OUT MEDIATION TO THIS END. BUT IN THE SUMKIT'S
FINAL DOCUNENT IT 18 THE FMLN AND THE NICARAGUAN CONTRAS WHO PAY
THE PRICE FOR THE AGREEMENT AMONG THE PRESIDENTS! THESE ARE
DIPLOMATIC GAMES THAT DO WOT FACILITATE THE RESTORATION OF PEACE IN
CENTRAL AMERICA.
