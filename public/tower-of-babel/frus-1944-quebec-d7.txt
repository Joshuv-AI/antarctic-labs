Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (28 May 1944, d7)

top secret

London, May 28, 1944.

685. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt top secret and personal.

Cabinet and I are most grateful to you for your number 543[2] about my 680.[3]

I still think that a short visit is, above all things, desirable for the Staffs and above all for you, but any time after D + 14 might be convenient to you, and would be received with rejoicing by us. I am delighted at what you say and that you are keeping this in view. Doctor Churchill informs you that a sea voyage in one of your great new battleships would do you no end of good.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Channel of transmission to Washington not indicated; forwarded by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt, who was then at Shangri La (a mountain camp in Maryland), as telegram No. Red 40.
[2] Supra.
[3] Ante, p. 5.
