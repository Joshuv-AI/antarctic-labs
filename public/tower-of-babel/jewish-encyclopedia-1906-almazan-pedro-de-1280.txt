One of the conspirators against the inquisitor Pedro d'Arbuez. He escaped death by flight, but his wife Isabella, together with his brothers, Pedro junior and Manuel, were burned at the stake at Saragossa, January 25, 1487, while he himself was burned in effigy.

Bibliography: Libro Verde, in J. Amador de los Rios, Historia de los Judios, iii. 628. M. K.
