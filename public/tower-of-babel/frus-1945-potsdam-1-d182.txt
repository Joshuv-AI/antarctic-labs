No. 182 Minutes of a Meeting of the Combined Policy Committee[1]

Washington, July 4, 1945, 9:30 a.m.

[Extract][2]

top secret

Minutes of Combined Policy Committee Meeting Held at the Pentagon on July 4th, 1945—9:30 a.m.

. . . . . . .

3. use of weapon against third parties

Field Marshal Wilson stated that the British Government concurred in the use of the T. A. weapon against Japan. He added that the Prime Minister might wish to discuss this matter with the President at the forthcoming meeting in Berlin.[3]

. . . . . . .

Harvey H Bundy

Roger Makins

Joint Secretaries
