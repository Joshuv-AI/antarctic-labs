212. Letter From Secretary of State Marshall to Senator Chan Gurney

Washington, May 5, 1947.

Dear Senator: This is to confirm the message I asked General Carter[1] to pass on to you Friday afternoon.[2]

The legislation regarding the CIG reflects the unchanged status of the agreement reached in February by the Secretaries of War and Navy and myself.[3]

Faithfully yours,[4]

Source: Source: National Archives and Records Administration, RG 59, Records of the Department of State, Decimal File 1945–49, 101.5/5–547. No classification marking. Drafted by Marshall. A note on the source text reads: “Delivered by hand 5/6/47.”

Notes
[1] Brigadier General Marshall Carter, Secretary Marshall’s Special Assistant.
[2] Presumably Friday, May 2.
[3] No documents concerning this agreement, and no other references to it, have been found.
[4] Printed from an unsigned copy.
