Abstract
. We consider a free smooth action Φ : G × M → M \Phi\colon G\times M\to M of a connected compact Lie group G G on a manifold M M . We examine the Cartan filtration of the complex of differential forms of M M . The associated spectral sequence E p , q r {E}^{{}^{p,q}}_{{}_{r}} converges to the cohomology of M M . It is well known that the second page E p , q 2 {E}^{{}^{p,q}}_{{}_{2}} of this spectral sequence is given by H p ( M / G ) ⊗ H q ( 𝔤 ) {H}^{{}^{p}}{\left(M/G\right)}\otimes{H}^{{}^{q}}{\left(\noindent{\mathfrak{g}}\right)} , where 𝔤 \noindent{\mathfrak{g}} denotes the Lie algebra of G G . In this note, we provide a straightforward proof of this fact without using Mayer-Vietoris, harmonic operators, or other such methods found in existing proofs. In fact, we extend this result to the case where the action is locally free and G G is not compact, under the hypothesis that Φ \Phi extends to a smooth action of a compact Lie group K K . The compactness of K K is a crucial aspect of our proof. When G G is not compact, the cohomology H p ( M / G ) {H}^{{}^{p}}{\left(M/G\right)} is not the cohomology of the orbit space M / G M/G , which may be a topologically wild space, but rather the basic cohomology of the foliation determined by the action of G G .

We consider in this note a locally free smooth action

This result has already been proven by other authors

7

using different techniques such as Mayer-Vietoris on

The compactness of

).
In other words, we begin with

The computation of

3.
Tame actions

We introduce the specific class of actions we are interested in. These actions are obtained by restricting smooth actions of compact Lie groups on manifolds. The goal of this section is to present a canonical decomposition of a differential form on the ambient manifold
M
in terms of its characteristic forms and horizontal forms.

Definition 3.1
.

A smooth action
\Phi\colon G\times M\to M
of a connected Lie group on a manifold is called a
tame action
if the following condition is satisfied:

(T)

There exists a smooth action
\Psi\colon K\times M\to M
, where
K\supset G
is a compact Lie group, extending the action of
G
.

Notice that a smooth action
\Phi\colon G\times M\to M
of a connected Lie group on a manifold
M
is tame if either
G
is compact or
M
is compact and the action is by isometries (see
[
5
, Theorem 1.2 of Chap. II]
).
The group
K
of the definition is not unique, but it may be chosen with richer properties.

Definition 3.2
.

Let
\Phi\colon G\times M\to M
be a tame action. An action
\Psi
satisfying condition
(T)
is a
tamer
of
\Phi
if
G
is dense in
K
.

Any tame action
admits a tamer. Note that the Lie group
K
is connected because
G
is connected.
Furthermore,
G
is necessarily a normal subgroup of
K
(see
[
8
, Proposition 1.4.1]

The

(on
M

.
These vector fields are preserved by the action of

in the following way:

(6)

g_{*}X_{u}=X_{\mathop{\rm Ad\,}\nolimits(g)\cdot u}

for each
g\in K
.
This makes sense since
\mathop{\rm Ad\,}\nolimits(g)\cdot u\in\noindent{\mathfrak{g}}
because
G
is normal in
K
. For the sake of simplicity, we write

, where
\{u_{1},\ldots,u_{n}\}
is the orthogonal basis of
\noindent{\mathfrak{g}}
we have chosen in the previous section.

Given a Riemannian metric

\chi_{u}=i_{X_{u}}\mu\in{\Omega}^{{}^{1}}{\left(M\right)}

4.
Cartan filtration

We define the Cartan filtration and the induced Leray-Serre-deRham spectral sequence, whose second term is the focus of our work.

The
Cartan filtration
of
{\Omega}^{{}^{*}}{\left(M\right)}
associated with the action we work with is defined by

{F}^{{}^{p}}{\Omega}^{{}^{p+q}}{\left(M\right)}=\left\{\omega\in{\Omega}^{{}^{p+q}}{\left(M\right)}\ \big|\ i_{X_{u_{0}}}\cdots i_{X_{u_{q}}}\omega=0\hbox{ for each family }\{u_{0},\ldots,u_{q}\}\subset\noindent{\mathfrak{g}}\right\}.

\displaystyle{F}^{{}^{p}}{\Omega}^{{}^{p+q}}{\left(M\right)}=

\displaystyle\left\{\omega\in{\Omega}^{{}^{p+q}}{\left(M\right)}\ \big|\ \llbracket\omega\rrbracket\leq q\right\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\left\{\sum_{1\leq i_{1}<\cdots<i_{q}\leq n}\omega_{i_{1},\ldots,i_{q}}\wedge\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\chi$}_{i_{1}}\wedge\cdots\wedge\raise 2.0pt\hbox{$\chi$}_{i_{q}}\ \big|\ \hbox{ $\omega_{\bullet}$ horizontal forms}\right\}.
