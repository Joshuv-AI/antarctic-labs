Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

The Participant is developing a high temperature, high pressure generator to power downhole
instrumentation. The generator harvests vibrational energy from the drill string and converts it to
stored electrical potential energy. NREL has two principle obligations 1) is modeling for the
overall potential downhole effectiveness of the potential drill. Second is the packaging for
downhole extreme environments based on prior NREL IP this was discussed with the PI at length
but the generator was never provided so direct packaging experiments were not conducted.

Summary of Research Results:

The CRADA was terminated before the tasks below could be completed.

Task 1: NREL will Develop simulations of the generator (5K). 3D files describing the generator
geometry will be imported into the simulation software. Finite element analysis of the eddy
currents and electromagnetic fields will be performed.

Task 1.1: NREL will provide initial quantification for frequency shifts of the generator

Task 2: NREL will build and test electrical connections made with epoxy (5K). The generator
will be subject to extreme temperatures, large shocks, and thousands of cycles of vibration.

Task 3: NREL will provide the CRADA Final Report – Preparation and submission in
accordance with Article X
