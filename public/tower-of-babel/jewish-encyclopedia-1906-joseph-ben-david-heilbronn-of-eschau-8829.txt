German Masorite; lived at The Hague in the eighteenth century. He was the author of "Sefer Mebin Ḥidot" (Amsterdam, 1765), an important work on the Masorah, with an appendix containing a reply to a criticism of Asher Worms. Joseph edited, under the title "Seder Tefillot li-Yeme Simḥat Torah" ( ib. 1769), prayers for the feast of Simḥat Torah and other occasional prayers.

Bibliography: Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1455; Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 283; Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 491. D. I. Br.
