Talmudist of the seventeenth century; lived probably in northern Italy. Under the title "Shemen ha-Mor" he wrote responsato the four ritual codices, with an appendix consisting of "exhortations" to his son (Leghorn, 1793).

Bibliography: Zedner, Cat. Hebr. Books Brit. Mus. p. 658; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 595. E. C. S. O.
