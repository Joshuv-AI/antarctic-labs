Preacher and homiletic exegete in Karlin at the beginning of the eighteenth century. He wrote: "Siaḥ Sefunim" (Zolkiev, 1751-52), a commentary on the Five Rolls, each of them having its special subtitle; and "Ṭa'ame Torah" ( ib. 1752-65), on the accents, the Masorah, and the recitation of the Pentateuch.

Bibliography: Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, i. 125; Benjacob, Oẓar ha-Sefarim, pp. 211, 572. S. S. M. Sc.
