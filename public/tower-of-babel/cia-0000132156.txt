Political Developments

British Parliament Supports Government’s Policy

On Tuesday both houses of Parliament overwhelmingly
backed the government’s policy on the Gulf crisis, according
to press. The House of Lords gave its full backing ina
debate that did not conclude with a vote, and the House of
Commons voted 534 to 57 in favor of allowing Prime Minister
Major to commit British forces to combat. [3

King Fahd Replies to Saddam

Saudi media yesterday publicized a letter from King Fahd
which responded to Saddam’s message of 14 January by refuting
Saddam’s accusations, emphasizing Iraq’s transgressions, and
reiterating Arab efforts to resolve the crisis. Perhaps
stung by Saddam’s complaint about paltry financial support,
Fahd cited specific dollar amounts of aid given to Baghdad,
totalling over $25 billion. Fahd also accused Saddam of
inflicting a deep wound on the Arab nation which will not
heal quickly. Fahd challenged Saddam to prove that he is a
respons ibis world leader by withdrawing immediately from
Kuwait.
