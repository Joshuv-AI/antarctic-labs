Karaite scholar and author; flourished during the latter part of the sixteenth century; brother of Joseph b. Mordecai Troki. He was the author of "Ḳiddush ha-Ḥodesh we-Sod ha-'Ibbur," a work on the calendar, and of responsa on the laws governing Sheḥiṭah . Both works are mentioned by Simḥah Luzki in his "Oraḥ Ẓaddiḳim."

Bibliography: Fürst, Gesch. des Karäerthums, iii. 39; idem, Bibl. Jud. iii. 448; Gottlober, Biḳkoret le-Toledot ha-Ḳara'im, p. 209. J. M. Sel.
