 Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

 The proposed project activities include the design, development, fabrication, and testing of a
 multilayer membrane-electrode-sub-gasket-PTL assembly (MESPA).

 Summary of Research Results:

 Task 1 (SOPO Task #4): Manufacture MESPA Parts and Perform Defect Detection and
 Qualification

 1.1 Cummins will manufacture, at own premises, MESPA (small scale) parts and will send
 them to NLR.

 1.2 NLR will perform, at own premises, defect detection and qualification of MESPA (small
 scale) parts. NLR will then provide report of results to Cummins as given in Task 8.

 1.3 Utilizing its cell testing and spatial diagnostics capabilities, NLR will study the impact of
 material irregularities encountered on the parts (small scale) on the ultimate performance and
 durability of electrolyzer cell. NLR will then provide report of results to Cummins as given in
 Task 8.

 Using a small-scale fabrication process, Cummins manufactured a set of MESA parts. The set
 consisted of 12 samples and was selected to illustrate typical manufacturing defect types, as
 shown in the table below. After fabrication, the samples were provided to NLR for initial defect
 detection and qualification analysis.

 Activities under tasks 1.2 and 1.3 commenced in June 2025 and were ongoing until the
 termination of the project in October 2025. For task 1.2, defect detection for MESPA parts was
 done by NLR using optical scanning and optical profilometry techniques (Figure 1a). For task
 1.3, parts provided contained irregularities that were categorized by type to develop an
 experiment plan for defect classification (Figure 1b). Sub gasket frame wrinkles were identified
 as a critical feature, resulting in a change of the experimental plan to include the assessment of
 cell sealing integrity. To assess sealing impact, a custom compression and leak check system was
 developed and fabricated by NLR (Figure 1c). Planned cell testing, spatial diagnostics
 measurements, and leak check investigations were not completed prior to project termination.

 Task 2: (SOPO Task #6): Manufacture and Inspect MESPA Parts

 2.1. Cummins will manufacture, at its own premises, MESPA (full scale) parts and will send the
 parts to NLR.

 2.2 NLR will inspect, at their own premises, MESPA (full scale) parts. NLR will then provide a
 report of results to Cummins as given in Task 8.

 2.3 Utilizing its cell testing and spatial diagnostics capabilities, NLR will study the impact of
 material irregularities encountered on the parts (full scale) on the ultimate performance and
 durability of electrolyze cell. NLR will then provide a report of results to Cummins as given in
 Task 8.

 No work was completed under this task. Project was terminated prior to start of task activities.

 Task 3: (SOPO Task # 7): Provide Recommendations on Inspection Systems

 3.1. Cummins to provide NLR the required information to support task 3.2

 3.2 NLR will provide recommendation to down select inspection systems for manufacturing
 starting materials, intermediate materials, and final (full scale) parts to Cummins.

 No work was completed under this task. Project was terminated prior to start of task activities.

 Task 4: (SOPO Task #9): Validate Inspection Systems

 4.1 Cummins to provide the required information and or materials to NLR to support task

 4.2 NLR will validate, at own premises, the inspection system ability to detect and mark critical
 irregularities of produced full scale MESPA parts and provide report of results to Cummins

 No work was completed under this task. Project was terminated prior to start of task activities.

 Task 5: (SOPO Task #10): Build, Test and Inspect Short Stack

 5.1. Cummins will build and test a short stack with parts produced during the Factory
 Acceptance Test (FAT)

 5.2 NLR will inspect, at own premises, batch variability of MESPA production from different
 batches fabricated during FAT and provide a report of results to Cummins

 No work was completed under this task. Project was terminated prior to start of task activities.
