CITIZEN, ADELBERTO ((SALAZAR)) (DPOB: CA 1945, EL SALVADOR) WAS
ARRESTED FOR STEALING A MOTORSCOOTER IN SAN SALVADOR CA 15 APRIL.
THE MOTORSCOOTER BELONGED TO A MINOR CHRISTIAN DEMOCRATIC PARTY
(PDC) OFFICIAL WHO RECOGNIZED THE THIEF AS THE MAN WHO HAD
APPEARED IN AN ARENA PARTY CAMPAIGN TELEVISION ADVERTISEMENT IN
MARCH 1984, CLAIMING TO BE THE ASSASSIN OF MONSIGNOR OSCAR
ARNULFO ((ROMERO)). (FYI: ROBERTO ((D'AUBUJSSON)) INTRODUCED
THE TAPED TV INTERVIEW STATING THAT THIS WOULD CLEAR HIM OF

ANY SUSPICION OF PARTICIPATION IN THE ASSASSINATION.) WHEN

TOLD HE WOULD BE CHARGED AS ROMERO'S ASSASSIN INSTEAD OF AS A
MOTORSCOOTER THIEF, SALAZAR ADMITTED THE VIDEONXTAPE HAD BEEN A
HOAX, WHICH HE HAD BEEN HIRED TO PERPETRATE BY A HONDURAN
MILITARY OFFICER ON BEHALF OF THE ARENA PARTY.

2. SALAZAR IS NOW UNDER PROTECTION OF THE SALVADORAN NATIONAL
POLICE (HE CLAIMS HE WILL BE KILLED IF HE IS ALLOWED TO GO FREE)
WHO ARE INVESTIGATING HIS STORY, AT THE BEHEST OF THE PDC. HE
HAS SOMEHOW MANAGED TO INVOLVE THE C-2 OF BOTH THE HONDURAN
AND SALVADORAN ARMED FORCES IN THE LATE 1983-EARLY 1984 PERIOD
IN HIS PARTICIPATION THE INTERVIEW, IMPLYING THAT THEY ARRANGED
THE INTERVIEW FOR ARENA. THE DETAILS SALAZAR PROVIDED IN THE TAPED

WAS BRIEFED BY EITHER A PARTICIPANT IN THE ACTION OR AN
