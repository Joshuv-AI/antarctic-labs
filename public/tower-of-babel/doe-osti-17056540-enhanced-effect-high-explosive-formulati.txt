 Enhanced-Effect High Explosive Formulations
 Alan DeHope and Sorin Bastea
                                        24-ERD-013

Project Overview
This project explored a formulation-based approach to discover higher performing energetic
materials, which are needed to meet current and future national security challenges. Two
general families of high explosive (HE) formulations were explored and developed. Specific
formulations were tuned using the Cheetah thermochemical code, scaled up in Livermore’s
High Explosive Applications Facility (HEAF), interrogated using a variety of performance and
safety test platforms, and compared to institutional benchmarks.

Mission Impact
One of LLNL’s missions is to develop science and technology tools and capabilities to meet
future national security challenges. The emergence of near-peer adversaries has highlighted the
need for higher performing energetic materials. This LDRD project has contributed to our
scientific understanding of the behavior of HE formulations and has resulted in the discovery of
new materials.
