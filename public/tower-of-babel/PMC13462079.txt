Author Correction: Sustainability in medical retina: the environmental impact of using aflibercept 8 mg instead of aflibercept 2 mg in treatment-naïve patients with nAMD
Authors: Neil Bowley, Peter Thomas, Sobha Sivaprasad, Robert M. J. Purbrick, Emily Beardmore, Rose Gilbert, Andy Clarke, Thomas J. G. Chase
Published: 2026

Correction to: Eye

10.1038/s41433-025-04020-9, published 06 October 2025

In this article, the current text has been published erroneously: ‘The reduction in packet size of aflibercept 8 mg PFS (70 × 137 × 31 mm) relative to aflibercept 2 mg PFS (94 × 135 × 31 mm) allowed approximately 67% more units to fit on a single pallet, decreasing transport emissions by one-third.’

It should read:

'The reduction in packet size of aflibercept 8 mg PFS (70 × 137 × 31 mm) relative to aflibercept 2 mg PFS (94 × 165 × 31 mm) allowed approximately 67% more units to fit on a single pallet, decreasing transport emissions by one-third.’

The original article has been corrected.
