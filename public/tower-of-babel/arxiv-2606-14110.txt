Abstract
. Let G G be a connected affine algebraic group defined over a field of positive characteristic. We prove that the action of G G on a smooth projective variety can be lifted to its associated Artin-Schreier root stacks, whenever G G has no non-trivial characters. The existence of a G G -linearization on a certain tautological invertible sheaf on such Artin-Schreier root stacks is also shown.



2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
14A20, 14D23, 14L30.

1.
Introduction

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0, any smooth orbifold curve is isomorphic to a root stack over its coarse moduli space. This is essentially due to the fact that, the stabilizer groups of the stacky points are always cyclic. Due to this nice property, a smooth orbifold curve is uniquely determined upto isomorphism by its coarse moduli curve and a finite list of integers corresponding to the orders of the (cyclic) stabilizer groups of the stacky points. In positive characteristic, such nice description fails due to the possibility of non-cyclic – or even non-abelian – stabilizer groups. This necessitates the study of finer invariants like ramification jumps in positive characteristic. Ramified covers of curves in characteristic
p>0
can be studied using various ramification filtrations of their Galois groups. For example, given a
\mathbb{Z}/p{\mathbb{Z}}
-Galois cover of curves
Y\rightarrow X
over an algebraically closed field
K
of characteristic
p
, taking the completions of the function fields
K(Y)
and
K(X)
, one gets a Galois extension of degree
p
of the form
K((y))/K((x))
, where
y
satisfies an equation of the form
y^{p}-y=f(x)
. In this case, the ramification jump is
m=-v(f)
, where
v
is the usual discrete valuation associated to
K((x))
.
The notion of Artin-Schreier root stacks was introduced by Kobin in
[
K
]
to study such
\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
-covers of curves. Using Artin-Schreier root stacks, a classification of stacky curves with wild ramification of order
p
can be achieved, which parallels the case of characteristic zero.

It is a natural question to study group actions on these stacks, which can be useful in the study of coverings from an equivariant perspective. It can also be potentially useful in the study of sheaves on these stacks. For example, over
\mathbb{C}
, vector bundles on root stacks can be understood through the data of parabolic vector bundles on the coarse moduli variety in many situations
[
B
]
. Group actions on root stacks over
\mathbb{C}
were studied in
[
CP
]
, and as an application, an equivalence between equivariant connections on the root stack and equivariant parabolic connections on its coarse moduli variety was established. In this article, we aim to study a similar question in the positive characteristic setup. Given an action of a connected affine algebraic group on a smooth projective variety, we aim to provide a criterion for lifting the action on the Artin-Schreier root stacks of Kobin
[
K
]
. Let
X
be a nonsingular projective variety over an algebraically closed field of characteristic
p>0
with an action of a connected affine algebraic group
G
. Suppose
D
is an effective Cartier divisor on
X
invariant under the action of
G
; see Section
3
for more details. The invertible sheaf
L:=\mathcal{O}(D)
admits a tautological section
s\in H^{0}(X,L)
whose divisor of zeros is
D
. To introduce the data of ramification jump, fix a positive integer
m
coprime to
p
, and another section
f\in H^{0}(X,L^{\otimes m})
which has no common zeros with
s
. Consider the Artin-Schreier root stack
\wp_{m}^{-1}((L,s,f)/X)
associated to this data; see Section
2
for more details. Our first main result is the following.

Theorem 1.1

(
Theorem
3.10
)
.

Suppose
G
has no nontrivial characters. Assume that the restriction of
f
to the divisor
D
is invariant for the induced
G
-linearization on
L^{\otimes m}
. Then the
G
-action on
X
admits a lift to a
G
-action on the Artin-Schreier root stack
\mathcal{X}^{\nu}_{m}:=\wp_{m}^{-1}((L,s,f)/X)
.

In the final section, we discuss the existence of a tautological invertible sheaf
\mathcal{N}
on the Artin-Schreier root stack
\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
which serves as a
p
-th root of the pullback to
\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
of the line bundle
L
on
X
. We prove that, the
G
-action induced on
\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
lifts to a
G
-linearization on this tautological invertible sheaf
\mathcal{N}
.

2.
Preliminaries

2.1.
Artin-Schreier root stacks

Let
m\geq 1
be a positive integer coprime to a given prime number
p
. The
weighted projective line

\mathbb{P}(1,m)
is a stacky curve with coarse moduli space
\mathbb{P}^{1}
whose only stacky point is
\infty:=[0:1]
with stabilizer group
\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}
. Its
T
-valued points for a
K
-scheme
T
is given by tuples
(\mathcal{L},s,f)
, where
\mathcal{L}\in\Pic(T)
, and
s
and
f
are sections of
\mathcal{L}
and
\mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}
, respectively, such that they do not share any common zeros (see
[
K
, Corollary 6.7]
). The coarse moduli map is given by
\mathbb{P}(1,m)\longrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{1}
,
[x:y]\mapsto[x:y^{m}]
.

Consider the isogeny
\wp:\mathbb{G}_{a}\longrightarrow\mathbb{G}_{a}
,
x\mapsto x^{p}-x
. This extends to a cyclic
p
-cover
\Psi:\mathbb{P}(1,m)\longrightarrow\mathbb{P}(1,m)
,
[u:v]\mapsto[u^{p}:v^{p}-vu^{m(p-1)}]
. On the other hand,
\mathbb{G}_{a}
acts on
\mathbb{P}(1,m)
by the rule
x\cdot[u:v]:=[u:v+xu^{m}]
. It is easy to see that this action commutes with
\Psi
, and thus we get an induced morphism on the quotient stack
[\mathbb{P}(1,m)/\mathbb{G}_{a}]
. This is known as the
universal Artin-Schreier cover with ramification jump m
, denoted by

\wp_{m}:[\mathbb{P}(1,m)/\mathbb{G}_{a}]\longrightarrow[\mathbb{P}(1,m)/\mathbb{G}_{a}]\,.

According to
[
AB
, Definition A.3]
, a locally Noetherian algebraic stack
\mathcal{X}
is said to be
normal
if there is a smooth surjection
U\longrightarrow\mathcal{X}
where
U
is a normal scheme. A representable morphism
\nu:\mathcal{X}^{\nu}\longrightarrow\mathcal{X}
from an algebraic stack
\mathcal{X}^{\nu}
is said to be a
normalization
of
\mathcal{X}
if for any scheme
U
and any smooth morphism
U\rightarrow\mathcal{X}
, the
pullback
\mathcal{X}^{\nu}\times_{\mathcal{X}}U\rightarrow U
is the normalization of
U
. By
[
AB
, Lemma A.4]
, the normalization of a Noetherian algebraic stack always exists, and it is unique upto unique isomorphism.

Remark 2.9
.

Let us mention a special case when the valued points of the Artin-Schreier root stack can be described explicitly. Let
u:U\longrightarrow\mathcal{X}_{m}
be a smooth atlas for the unnormalized stack, where
U
is a scheme. Consider the fiber product
U^{\nu}:=U\times_{\mathcal{X}_{m}}\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
. Since
\mathcal{X}^{\nu}_{m}\rightarrow\mathcal{X}_{m}
is representable,
U^{\nu}
is again a scheme; moreover, it is the normalization of
U
. Also, the pullback morphism
u^{\nu}:U^{\nu}\longrightarrow\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
is again a smooth atlas for
\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
. Clearly, the map
u^{\nu}
is essentially uniquely determined by the composition
U^{\nu}\xrightarrow{\,\,h\,\,}U\xrightarrow{\,\,u\,\,}\mathcal{X}_{m}
due to properties of normalizations. Now, as in Remark
2.3
, if
u
is given by the tuple

\bigl(\varphi^{\prime}:U\longrightarrow X,\,M^{\prime},\,t^{\prime},\,g^{\prime},\,\psi^{\prime}:M^{\prime^{\otimes p}}\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow}\varphi^{\prime*}L\bigr),

then the composition
U^{\nu}\xrightarrow{\,\,h\,\,}U\xrightarrow{\,\,u\,\,}\mathcal{X}_{m}
is given by the tuple

\bigl(h^{*}\varphi^{\prime}:U^{\nu}\longrightarrow X,\,h^{*}M^{\prime},\,h^{*}t^{\prime},\,h^{*}g^{\prime},\,h^{*}\psi^{\prime}:M^{\prime^{\otimes p}}\overset{\simeq}{\longrightarrow}(h\circ\varphi^{\prime})^{*}L\bigr)\,.

This tuple uniquely determines the map
u^{\nu}:U^{\nu}\longrightarrow\mathcal{X}^{\nu}_{m}
above.

It was observed in Remark
2.9
that
u^{\prime}:U^{\nu}\rightarrow\mathcal{X}^{\nu}_{m}
is an atlas for the Artin-Schreier root stack
\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}
. Consider the following invertible sheaf on
\mathcal{X}^{\nu}_{m}
:

\mathcal{N}:=w^{*}\mathcal{M}\,.

Clearly, if
\mathcal{M}
is given on
U
by the invertible sheaf
M_{u}
, then
\mathcal{N}
is given by the invertible sheaf
w^{\prime*}M_{u}
on
U^{\nu}
. Also, the coarse moduli map
\pi:\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}\longrightarrow X
is equal to the composition
\pi=p\circ w
. The invertible sheaf
\mathcal{N}
can therefore be thought of as the tautological sheaf on the Artin-Schreier root stack, which provides a
p
-th root for the line bundle
L
on
X
, namely
\mathcal{N}^{\otimes p}\xrightarrow{\,\,\simeq\,\,}\pi^{*}L\,.
Now, since the map
w:\mathcal{X}_{m}^{\nu}\rightarrow\mathcal{X}_{m}
is
G
-equivariant by construction, it follows that the
G
-linearization on
\mathcal{M}
will induce a
G
-linearization on
\mathcal{N}
as well. This proves our claim.
