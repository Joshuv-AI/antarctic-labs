Comment: The Castro government evidently considers
El Salvador a particularly receptive target for efforts to
establish Cuba as a leader among Latin American countries.
Ambassador Rayneri's activities among student and other
groups have irritated the Salvadoran Government to the point
that it suggested he be transferred, a move expected soon.

The recent withdrawal of the Cuban Workers’ Confederatior
(CTC) from the anti-Communist Inter-American Workers' Confeder-—
ation (ORIT) and its reconmendatton that a revolutionary Latin
American labor organization be formed are considered victories
for the CTC's determined and influential Communist Minority.
These moves are consistent with apparent Communist plans to
renew attempts to gain power in labor groups outside’ the
largely discredited Communist-front Latin American Workers'

Confederation (CTAL).

Raul Castro is known to be particularly active in labor
matters and “may believe that a successful Central American
nucleus under Cuban direction would hasten the development
of the new organization and ensure Cuba's dominant role in

it.
