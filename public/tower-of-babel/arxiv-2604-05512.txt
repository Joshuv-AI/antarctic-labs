Measurement of charged-particle production in s NN = 9.62 \sqrt{s_{\text{NN}}}=9.62 TeV proton-oxygen collisions as a probe of cosmic-ray air showers with the ATLAS detector


Abstract
This Letter presents a measurement of prompt charged-particle production in proton-oxygen interactions at s NN = 9.62 \sqrt{s_{\text{NN}}}=9.62 TeV center-of-mass energy with the ATLAS detector, corresponding to 634 μ \mu b -1 of integrated luminosity. A total of 246 million selected events have at least one track with transverse momentum p T > 500 p_{\text{T}}>500 MeV and pseudorapidity | η | < 2.5 |\eta|<2.5 . The measured fiducial proton-oxygen cross section is σ fid. p ​ O = 396 ± 6 ​ ( exp. ) ± 9 ​ ( lumi. ) ​ mb \sigma_{\text{fid.}}^{p\text{O}}=396\pm 6\penalty\ (\text{exp.})\pm 9\penalty\ (\text{lumi.})\penalty\ \text{mb} and the extrapolated inelastic proton-air cross section is σ inel. p + air = 406 ± 6 ​ ( exp. ) ± 9 ​ ( lumi. ) ± 28 ​ ( th. ) ​ mb \sigma^{p+\text{air}}_{\text{inel.}}=406\pm 6\penalty\ (\text{exp.})\pm 9\penalty\ (\text{lumi.})\pm 28\penalty\ (\text{th.})\penalty\ \text{mb} . Measurements of charged-particle multiplicity, p T p_{\text{T}} , and η \eta distributions are an order-of-magnitude more precise than differences between hadronic-interaction models. These results enable improved modeling of cosmic-ray air showers, which is important for astroparticle physics.

Events are not removed if a second vertex has fewer than five tracks, which are dominated by secondary interactions reconstructed as another PV or one PV split into two vertices, where one has few tracks.
The fraction of events rejected by this veto due to these reconstruction effects is estimated in the simulation to be
0.02% and deemed negligible.

Tracks must have an innermost-pixel-layer hit if expected

The dominant background is secondary-particle tracks due to hadronic interactions, photon conversions, and decays of long-lived particles.
This is estimated by fitting simulated track

4<|d_{0}^{\text{BL}}|<9.5

[

The fit scales the secondaries’ normalization by

Uncertainties in

are estimated by repeating this procedure using
Pythia

Additional subdominant uncertainties arise from varying the sideband fit range and reweighting the simulated
d_{0}^{\text{BL}}
distributions to match data in the
|d_{0}^{\text{BL}}|<1.5

Charged particles satisfying
30<\tau<300

The fraction of reconstructed tracks due to strange baryons,

for

GeV

Pythia

Random combinations of silicon hits producing fake-track backgrounds are estimated by using simulation to have a negligible rate

.
Beam-induced backgrounds

140

141

The number of events is corrected for the efficiencies of the trigger
\epsilon_{\text{trig}}
and vertex
\epsilon_{\text{vtx}}

w_{\text{evt}}=1/(\epsilon_{\text{vtx}}\cdot\epsilon_{\text{trig}})

These are determined as a function of the number of selected tracks with the

The trigger efficiency is measured in data relative to a ZDC-based reference trigger, and is found to be 97.8% (99.4%) for
n_{\text{sel}}^{\text{no-z}}=1(2)
, plateauing at 99.8% for
n_{\text{sel}}^{\text{no-z}}\geq 3
, with negligible dependence on track
p_{\text{T}}
and
\eta

Repeating this procedure using a reference trigger based on random L1 hits results in differences of at most 0.3% and is taken as the uncertainty.
The vertex efficiency is measured from data as the ratio of the number of selected events with a reconstructed vertex to that with the PV requirement removed, whose uncertainties arising from beam-induced backgrounds are negligible
[
52
]
.
This is found to be 91.7% for
n_{\text{sel}}^{\text{no-z}}=1
and rises to 100% for
n_{\text{sel}}^{\text{no-z}}\geq 2

For events with
n_{\text{sel}}^{\text{no-z}}=1

is also determined differentially in the selected track
\eta

|\eta|=2.5
to 93% at
|\eta|=0

99.6\%
(
99.5\%

Track reconstruction efficiencies are defined via simulation by
\epsilon_{\text{trk}}(p_{\text{T}},\eta)=N^{\text{match}}_{\text{reco}}/N_{\text{gen}}

N_{\text{gen}}
is the number of generated primary particles in a
(p_{\text{T}},\eta)

This accounts for resolution effects that cause primary particles to migrate outside the kinematic range.
The passive material between the Pixel and SCT in the

\epsilon_{\text{trk}}
are derived by comparing the efficiency to extend a track reconstructed in the Pixel into the SCT for data and simulation

The simulation imperfectly describes the

\langle N_{\text{IBL}}\rangle

\langle N_{\text{IBL}}^{\text{MC}}\rangle/\langle N_{\text{IBL}}^{\text{data}}\rangle

The corrected efficiencies range from 86% for
|\eta|<1

|\eta|>2.4

Track distributions are corrected as a function of
