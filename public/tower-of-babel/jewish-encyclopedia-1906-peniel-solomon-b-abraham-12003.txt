Scholar of unknown date and place. He was the author of a work entitled "Or 'Enayim," on the influence of the constellations upon mankind, the days of Creation, and the wisdom of the Patriarchs (Constantinople, 1519; Cremona, 1557; Breslau, 1807).

Bibliography: Fürst, Bibl. Jud. iii. 71; Benjacob. Oẓar ha-Sefarim, p. 27. No. 543; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2383. D. S. O.
