German poet; died at Breslau April 12, 1836. He was the author of the following works: "Yosef we-Asenat," a drama (1817); "Ḥayye Shimshon," an epic poem (1824); and "Ṭal Yaldut," poems and proverbs (1835).

Bibliography: Zunz, Monatstage, Berlin, 1872; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 2664; Winter and Wünsche, Die Jüdische Litteratur, vol. iii., s.v., Treves, 1896. H. R. I. War.
