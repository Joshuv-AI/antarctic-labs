In late October 1966 one U.S. prisoner of war was observed near the
border of the VINH LINH area and QUANG BINH Province in NVN. Source
spoke for a few minutes with the three NVA soldiers who were escorting
the prisoner; the conversation took place at a rice storage area where
Source had been sent to obtain rice for his unit.

Taya

The U.S. POW was about six feet tall, weighed about 180 lbs., had blond
hair, was clean shaven, had no visible scars and was not wounded. The
POW was wearing an NVA uniform and VC rubber sandals; he was not tied

up or blindfolded, and he appeared to be in good healthe Source observed
that he was able to walk at a fast pace. :

The guards stated that the POW had been captured near route 9. At first
he was not able to eat rice, but he had been a VO prisoner for such a
Long period of time that he had grown accustomed to it and actually
Liked ite The POW could not speak Vietnamese and appeared to be afraid
of the Vietnamese people, since he jumped aside when Source passed him.

The three guards and the POW were headed north when Source met them,
but he did not know their destination or to what unit the guards belonged.
The guards were unfriendly and reluctant to answer questions.
