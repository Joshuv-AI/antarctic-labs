EL SALVADOR: ARENA'S CALDERON, D’' AUBUISSON CONDEMN KINAPPING

PAL22235

SAN SALVADOR EL DIRARIO DE HOY IN SPANISH 12 SEP 85

PP 3, 18s °
((EXCERPT)) YESTERDAY, THE POLITICAL PARTIES REPRESENTED AT

THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY REPUDIATED THE KIDNAPPING OF INES
GUADALUPE DUARTE DURAN, DAUGHTER OF PRESIDENT NAPOLEON DUARTE.

ARENA ( (NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE)) DEPUTY ARMANDO
CALDERON SOL SAID THAT THE KIDNAPPING IS A LOATHSOME, ENTIRELY
UNREASONABLE ACTION THAT HAS AFFECTED THE COUNTRY’S POLITICAL LIFE
AND THAT IT DESERVES CONDEMNATION HOWEVER ONE SEES IT, CALDERON
SAID THAT ARENA HAD IMMEDIATELY REPUDIATED THE ABDUCTION AND
THAT THE PARTY’ S SECRETARY GENERAL, MAJOR ROBERTO D' AUBUISSON,
PUBLICLY ANO VIGOROUSLY CONDEMNED IT.

CALDERON SAID THAT ARENA MORALLY SUPPORTS THE GOVERNMENT AND
PRESTIOENT OUARTE IN THIS REGRETTABLE SITUATION

HE ALSO SAIO: “WE PRAY TO GOD TO GIVE HIM THE STRENGTH
NECESSARY TO OVERCOME THIS SITUATION, AS BEFITS A HEAD OF STATE."

HE POINTED OUT: THE ABDUCTION IS THE RESULT OF THE TERRORISTS’
DESPERATION SINCE THEY FEEL THEY ARE REPUDIATED BY THE PEOPLE,

HE SAID: “WE SALVADORANS ARE FED UP WITH THESE ACTS OF VIOLENCE .
AND SEEK HARMONY, PEACE, AND CONCORD TO DEAL WITH OUR CURRENT
ECONOMIC CRISIS. SUCH ACTIONS ARE UNREASONABLE AND LOATHSOME,
EVEN MORE SO IN THIS CASE BECAUSE THE VICTIM IS A LADY, A MTHER
A SAL VADORAN WOMAN, “*

ASKED WHETHER THE GUERRILLAS ABDUCTED DUARTE'S DAUGHTER TO
PRESSURE THE GOVERNMENT INTO RESUMING THE DIALOGUE OR TO EXCHANGE
HER FOR CAPTURED TERORISTS, CALDERON SAID: “WE HAVE NO REPORTS
ON THE GUERRILLAS’ CONTACT OR DEMANDS, PERHAPS THE TERRORISTS
ARE USING THE ASDUCTION AS A FORM OF BLACKMAIL’ SINCE WE HAVE'SEEN

THAT THEY WILL GO TO ANY EXTREMES. “ (PASSAGE OMITTED) )
