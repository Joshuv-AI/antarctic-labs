Lower bound on the radii of black-hole shadows
Shahar Hod

Abstract
The non-linearly coupled Einstein-matter field equations predict the existence of shadows with well-defined boundaries around black holes. We prove that, in spherically symmetric hairy black-hole spacetimes whose matter fields satisfy the weak energy condition, the radii of these shadows are bounded from below by the dimensionless relation r sh / r H ≥ 3 ​ 3 / 2 r_{\text{sh}}/r_{\text{H}}\geq 3\sqrt{3}/2 , where r H r_{\text{H}} is the horizon radius of the central hairy black hole. The characteristic shadow of the (bald) Schwarzschild black-hole spacetime saturates the analytically derived lower bound.

How close can the outer edge of a black-hole shadow be to the outermost horizon of the central black hole?
