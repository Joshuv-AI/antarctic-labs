1.0 Introduction

1.1 Scope and Objectives

This report includes visual inspection results, descriptions of maintenance and repair activities, and
recommendations for calendar year (CY) 2025 for use restrictions (URs) at corrective action sites
(CASs) located on the Nevada National Security Site (NNSS) and on the Nevada Test and Training
Range (NTTR) that are accessed through the NNSS Main Gate. This document is arranged by project
activity (i.e., Industrial Sites, Soils, and Defense Threat Reduction Agency [DTRA]); then by
corrective action unit (CAU); and then by CAS. Post-closure UR inspections are performed on an
annual basis, unless as noted in text.

Post-closure UR inspections verify the condition of physical controls required by each UR, which
may include the following activities:

 • Visual verification of the legibility of signs
 • Visual inspection of fencing, signs, monuments, and/or markers for indications of wear
 • Visual inspection of soil covers for indications of subsidence, erosion, or unauthorized use

Deficiencies in UR requirements that were identified during CY 2024 inspections and not addressed
in the CY 2024 report are addressed in this CY 2025 report.

Post-Closure Inspection (PCI) Checklists are included in Appendix A. Applicable site figures,
photographs, other reports, and/or notes are included in Appendix B.

2.1.1 CAU 5, Landfills

A UR inspection is required every five years from the previous inspection year at CASs associated
with CAU 5. The last inspections for the eight CAU 5 CASs in 2021 are as follows:

      •   CAS 05-15-01 – October 4, 2021
      •   CAS 05-16-01 – September 27, 2021
      •   CAS 06-08-01 – October 20, 2021
      •   CASs 06-15-02 and 06-15-03 – October 20, 2021
      •   CAS 12-15-01 – August 9 and 10, 2021
      •   CAS 20-15-01 – August 24, 2021
      •   CAS 23-15-03 – October 21, 2021

The next UR inspection for all eight CAU 5 CASs will be performed by the end of CY 2026.

 Note: In early 2023, unauthorized ground-penetration activities were performed within
 the CAU 5, Landfills, CAS 23-15-03, Disposal Site, UR boundary. Support structures
 (magazines for explosives) were relocated in the waste trench area, and protective
 grounding rods were installed to a depth of 10 feet (ft) below ground surface (bgs).
 On September 21, 2023, the Nevada Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP)
 was informed of the incursion into CAU 5, Landfills, CAS 23-15-03, Disposal Site, in
 Area 23 on the NNSS.

 On February 21, 2024, NDEP requested the following corrective actions:

 1. The structures (explosive magazines) be relocated; the grounding rods remain in place;
 and the upper elevations of the rods be removed at shallow depths and covered with
 inert material.

 2. NDEP be notified on the completion of the corrective actions and be updated during
 an upcoming NDEP/U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Environmental Management
 (EM) Nevada Program Federal Facility Agreement and Consent Order (FFACO)
 monthly meeting.

 Page 3 of 18

 The status of the corrective actions is as follows:

 • Corrective action 1: Completed in August 2024.

 • Corrective action 2: The corrective actions were documented in a letter to EM Nevada
 Program dated March 6, 2025; all actions were noted as completed except for the installation
 of one UR sign.

 On May 20, 2025, NDEP representatives visited the site to observe the completion of
 the corrective actions (including the repaired UR sign noted in “Corrective action 2”).
 Subsequently, in a June 24, 2025, letter, NDEP affirmed the completion of all
 agreed-upon corrective actions and closed the matter.
