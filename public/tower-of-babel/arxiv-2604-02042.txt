A Fenchel Theorem for Gauss maps and uniqueness of minimizers of nonlocal curvature energies
Elias Döhrer, Alexander Dohmen

Abstract




In this paper, we prove a Fenchel theorem for Gauss maps by providing sharp lower bounds for the path length of Gauss maps of an embedding.
By combining the Fenchel-type theorem with various techniques from the field of geometric analysis, we show that circles minimize most generalized tangent-point energies. Furthermore, we prove that disks minimize all fractional Willmore energies among the class of convex planar sets.
