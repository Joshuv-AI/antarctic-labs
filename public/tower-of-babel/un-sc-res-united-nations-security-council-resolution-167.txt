Adopted by the Security Council at its 971th meeting, by 9 votes to 1, with 1 abstention (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics), on 25 October 1961

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Islami Republic of Mauritania be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

1961

fr:Résolution 167 du Conseil de sécurité des Nations unies
