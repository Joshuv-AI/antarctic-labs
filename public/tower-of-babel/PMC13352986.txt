A correction has been made to the section Discussion, paragraph 2:

“ Although our findings support a potentially universal sedentary threshold, its implications for falls may vary with cultural and environmental contexts. Routine floor-sitting or squatting is common in some non-Western cultures (20). Sedentary patterns also differ across ethnic groups due to distinct lifestyle and household routines (21). These differences suggest that this threshold may have varying relevance depending on culturally specific physical activities or living environments. Future research should consider such factors to refine fall-prevention guidelines and design strategies suited to diverse populations (22).”
