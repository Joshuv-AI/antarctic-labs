Russian Hebraist; born at Ivenitz 1800; died at Minsk 1847. Under the title "Marpe le-'Am," with a supplement entitled "Ḳonṭres Reshit Da'at," he translated from the Polish into Hebrew the medical work of Friedrich Pauliczki (2 vols., Wilna, 1834; 2d ed., Jitomir, 1868).

Bibliography: Fuenn, Keneset Yisrael, p. 190; Zeitlin, Bibl. Post-Mendels. p. 77. H. R.
