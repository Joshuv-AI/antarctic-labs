Same-Sign Tetralepton Signature at μ \mu TRISTAN
Lin-Kun Yan 1, Zhi-Long Han 1, Feng-Lan Shao 2, Fa-Xin Yang 2

Abstract
Naturally tiny neutrino masses can be explained by the low scale seesaw with heavy neutral lepton N N coupling to the neutrinophilic Higgs doublet Φ ν \Phi_{\nu} , which obtains a much smaller vacuum expectation value than the standard Higgs doublet Φ \Phi . Within this model, the neutrino masses originate from the new Yukawa interaction y ​ L ¯ ​ Φ ~ ν ​ N y\overline{L}\tilde{\Phi}_{\nu}N . In this paper, we propose the novel same-sign tetralepton signature at the 2 TeV same-sign muon mode μ + ​ μ + \mu^{+}\mu^{+} of μ \mu TRISTAN. We investigate two distinct channels of this signature, which are both generated by the Yukawa interaction y ​ L ¯ ​ Φ ~ ν ​ N y\overline{L}\tilde{\Phi}_{\nu}N . One is from the pair production of charged Higgs μ + ​ μ + → H + ​ H + → μ + ​ N + μ + ​ N → μ + ​ μ + ​ j ​ j + μ + ​ μ + ​ j ​ j → 4 ​ μ + + 4 ​ j \mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to H^{+}H^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N\to\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj+\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj\to 4\mu^{+}+4j , and the other one is from the single production of charged Higgs μ + ​ μ + → μ + ​ N ​ H + → μ + ​ N + μ + ​ N → μ + ​ μ + ​ j ​ j + μ + ​ μ + ​ j ​ j → 4 ​ μ + + 4 ​ j \mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to\mu^{+}NH^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N\to\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj+\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj\to 4\mu^{+}+4j . We then perform a detailed simulation of this same-sign tetralepton signature, and obtain the promising region at μ \mu TRISTAN.

II
The Model

In this model, one new Higgs doublet
\Phi_{\nu}
with lepton number
L_{\Phi_{\nu}}=-1
and three heavy neutral leptons
N
with
L_{N}=0
are introduced. Under the
U(1)_{L}
symmetry, the most general scalar potential is

\displaystyle V

\displaystyle=-

\displaystyle m_{\Phi}^{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)+m_{\Phi_{\nu}}^{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}_{\nu}\Phi_{\nu})+\frac{1}{2}\lambda_{1}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\lambda_{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}_{\nu}\Phi_{\nu})^{2}

(1)

\displaystyle+\lambda_{3}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)(\Phi^{\dagger}_{\nu}\Phi_{\nu})+\lambda_{4}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi_{\nu})(\Phi^{\dagger}_{\nu}\Phi)-\mu^{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi_{\nu}+\text{h.c.}),

where the
\mu^{2}
-term breaks the
U(1)_{L}
symmetry explicitly. The boundedness condition of
the potential requires
Gunion:2002zf

\lambda_{1},\lambda_{2}>0,~\lambda_{3}+\sqrt{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}>0,~\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{4}+\sqrt{\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}}>0

(2)

Because the
\mu^{2}
-term is the only source of lepton number violation, it is naturally small. Such small
\mu^{2}
-term generates a tiny VEV of neutrinophilic Higgs doublet
\Phi_{\nu}
as

v_{\nu}\simeq\frac{\mu^{2}v}{m_{\Phi_{\nu}}^{2}+(\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{4})v^{2}/2}.

(3)

For instance,
v_{\nu}\sim\mathcal{O}(1)
MeV can be obtained with
\mu\sim 1
GeV and
m_{\Phi_{\nu}}\sim 500
GeV.

After the spontaneous symmetry breaking, the mass eigenstates of scalars are two CP-even Higgs
h
and
H
, a CP-odd Higgs
A
, and a charged Higgs
H^{\pm}
.
Masses of these physical Higgs bosons are

\displaystyle m_{h}^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\lambda_{1}v^{2},

(4)

\displaystyle m_{H}^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle m_{\Phi_{\nu}}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{4})v^{2},

(5)

\displaystyle m_{A}^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle m_{\Phi_{\nu}}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}(\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{4})v^{2},

(6)

\displaystyle m_{H^{\pm}}^{2}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle m_{\Phi_{\nu}}^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\lambda_{3}v^{2},

(7)

where the terms of
\mathcal{O}(v_{\nu}^{2})
and
\mathcal{O}(\mu^{2})
are neglected. Here,
h
is regarded as the discovered 125 GeV Higgs at LHC
ATLAS:2015yey
.

In this model, the neutrino sector couples to the new Higgs doublet
\Phi_{\nu}
as

-\mathcal{L}_{N}=y\overline{L}\widetilde{\Phi}_{\nu}{N},

(8)

with
\widetilde{\Phi}_{\nu}=i\sigma_{2}{\Phi}_{\nu}^{*}
, which generates the light neutrino mass through the seesaw mechanism

m_{\nu}=-\frac{1}{2}v_{\nu}^{2}{y}m_{N}^{-1}y^{T}.

(9)

By adapting the Casas-Ibarra parametrization
Casas:2001sr
, the Yukawa coupling matrix can be expressed through the neutrino oscillation parameters

y=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{v_{\nu}}U_{\text{PMNS}}\hat{m}_{\nu}^{1/2}Rm_{N}^{1/2},

(10)

where
U_{\text{PMNS}}
is the Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) matrix that describing three-neutrino oscillation
Esteban:2024eli
,
\hat{m}_{\nu}=\text{diag}(m_{\nu 1},m_{\nu 2},m_{\nu 3})
, and
R
is a generalized orthogonal matrix.

With a small value of VEV
v_{\nu}
, the relatively large Yukawa coupling
y
not only naturally generates the tiny neutrino mass, but also induces observable charged lepton flavor violation processes
Ma:2001mr
;
Toma:2013zsa
;
Bertuzzo:2015ada
. The theoretical branching ratio of
\mu\to e\gamma
is calculated as
Ding:2014nga

\text{BR}(\mu\to e\gamma)=\frac{3\alpha}{64\pi G_{F}^{2}}\left|\sum_{i}\frac{y_{\mu i}y_{ei}^{*}}{m_{H^{+}}^{2}}F\left(\frac{m_{N_{i}}^{2}}{m_{H^{+}}^{2}}\right)\right|^{2},

(11)

where
G_{F}
is the Fermi constant, and the loop function
F(x)
is

F(x)=\frac{1}{6(1-x)^{4}}(1-6x+3x^{2}+2x^{3}-6x^{2}\ln x).

(12)

Under the current most stringent MEG II limit BR
(\mu\to e\gamma)<1.5\times 10^{-13}
(90% C.L.)
MEGII:2025gzr
, the Yukawa coupling should satisfy

\left|\sum_{i}y_{\mu i}y_{ei}^{*}\right|\lesssim 2.6\times 10^{-5}\left(\frac{m_{H^{+}}}{100~\text{GeV}}\right)^{2}.

(13)

As we consider the muon collider in this paper, we assume
|y_{ei}|\ll|y_{\mu i}|
to satisfy the lepton flavor violation constraint, which can be obtained by modifying the matrix
R
via Equation (
10
)
Vicente:2014wga
;
Liu:2022byu
.

IV
Decay Property and Various Signatures

Decay properties of the new particles heavily depend on the mass spectrum, which then leads to various interesting signatures via cascade decays. First, we consider the
scenario with
m_{H^{+}}<m_{N}
, where the Yukawa coupling
y
mediates the decays of heavy neutral lepton as
N\to\ell^{\pm}H^{\pm},\nu H,\nu A
. Due to mixing between the light and heavy neutrinos, the neutrinophilic scalars could decay as
H^{\pm}\to\ell^{\pm}\nu,H/A\to\nu\nu
when
v/v_{\nu}\gtrsim\mathcal{O}(10^{5})

Haba:2011nb
. Then, pair production of charged Higgs at LHC could generate the dilepton signature
pp\to H^{+}H^{-}\to\ell^{+}\nu+\ell^{-}\nu\to\ell^{+}\ell^{-}+\cancel{E}_{T}
. Direct searches of this dilepton signature have excluded the region with
m_{H^{+}}\lesssim 700
GeV
ATLAS:2019lff
;
CMS:2020bfa
. On the other hand, when
v/v_{\nu}\lesssim\mathcal{O}(10^{5})
, the neutrinophilic scalars will decay into SM final states as
H^{+}\to t\bar{b},H\to W^{+}W^{-},A\to Zh

Batra:2023mds
. Although the LHC has performed the search of charged Higgs via the channel
pp\to tbH^{+}

ATLAS:2018ntn
, the corresponding constraint can be easily satisfied as long as
v_{\chi}\ll v

Batra:2023mds
.

Focus on the muon flavor final states, the pair production of charged Higgs at
\mu
TRISTAN would lead to the same-sign dilepton signature when
v/v_{\nu}\gtrsim\mathcal{O}(10^{5})

\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to H^{+}H^{+}\to\mu^{+}\nu+\mu^{+}\nu.

(16)

This signal is similar as
\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to W^{+}W^{+}\to\mu^{+}\nu+\mu^{+}\nu

Meanwhile, the single production of charged Higgs at
\mu

\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to\mu^{+}H^{+}N\to\mu^{+}H^{+}+\mu^{\pm}H^{\mp}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\nu+\mu^{\pm}\mu^{\mp}\nu.

(17)

When
v/v_{\nu}\lesssim\mathcal{O}(10^{5})
, the pair and single production of charged Higgs then generate the signature as

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle H^{+}H^{+}\to t\bar{b}+t\bar{b},

(18)

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to\mu^{+}H^{+}N

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle\mu^{+}H^{+}+\mu^{\pm}H^{\mp}\to\mu^{+}t\bar{b}+\mu^{\pm}tb.

(19)

Obviously, the pair and single production channels lead to different signatures in this scenario with

For the opposite scenario with
m_{H^{+}}>m_{N}
, the decay

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle H^{+}H^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N,

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle\mu^{+}H^{+}N\to\mu^{+}+\mu^{+}N+N.

\displaystyle\to

V
Same-Sign Tetralepton Signature

Both the pair and single production of charged Higgs could lead to the same-sign tetralepton signature at
\mu
TRISTAN. The explicit signal processes are

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle H^{+}H^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N\to\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj+\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj

(25)

\displaystyle\mu^{+}\mu^{+}

\displaystyle\to

\displaystyle\mu^{+}H^{+}N\to\mu^{+}+\mu^{+}N+N\to\mu^{+}+\mu^{+}\mu^{+}jj+\mu^{+}jj

(26)

There is no irreducible SM background of this signature. Certain contributions might originate from the lepton charge mis-identification. Provided the charge mis-identification rate of 0.1%

10^{-3}
fb when additional jets are required in the final states. In the following study, we assume one background event

In principle, we can distinguish the origins of the same-sign tetralepton signature by fully reconstructing the charged Higgs. However, as already shown in Figure

VI
Conclusion

The neutrinophilic two Higgs doublet model can naturally generate tiny neutrino mass at the TeV scale. This model introduces a new Higgs doublet
\Phi_{\nu}
with
L_{\Phi_{\nu}}=-1
and three heavy neutral leptons with
L_{N}=0
. The neutrinophilic scalar obtains a small VEV
v_{\nu}
through the lepton number violation term
\mu^{2}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi_{\nu}+\text{h.c.})
. Then the Yukawa interaction
y\overline{L}\tilde{\Phi}_{\nu}N
generates the light neutrino via the seesaw mechanism. With small VEV
v_{\nu}
, the Yukawa coupling
y
could be relatively large even with
m_{N}\sim\mathcal{O}
(TeV).

In this paper, we propose the novel same-sign tetralepton signature
4\mu^{+}+4j
at
\mu
TRISTAN in the neutrinophilic two Higgs doublet model. This signature is mediated by the muon-flavor Yukawa coupling
y_{\mu N}
. One contribution is from the pair production of charged Higgs as
\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to H^{+}H^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N\to 4\mu^{+}+4j
, and the other one is from the single production of charged Higgs as
\mu^{+}\mu^{+}\to\mu^{+}NH^{+}\to\mu^{+}N+\mu^{+}N\to 4\mu^{+}+4j
. The former one is typically dominant when pair production is kinematically allowed
2m_{H^{+}}<\sqrt{s}
and
y_{\mu N}\gtrsim 0.1
. Above the threshold, the single production channel becomes the only viable contribution.

With quite a clean SM background, we study the inclusive same-sign tetralepton signal
4\mu^{+}+\geq 2j
at
\mu
TRISTAN. For 100 (1000) fb
-1
luminosity, we could discover most parameter space within
y_{\mu N}\gtrsim 0.14(0.08)
and
m_{H^{+}}\lesssim 1130(1250)
GeV when
m_{H^{+}}=2m_{N}
. By fixing
y_{\mu N}=1
, we report that almost all the kinematically allowed region of the pair production channel could be discovered with 100 fb
-1
data. The single production channel could further discover
m_{H^{+}}\lesssim 1170
GeV. Meanwhile, increasing the luminosity to 1000 fb
-1
could enlarge the discover region to
m_{H^{+}}\lesssim 1480
GeV. Therefore, the same-sign tetralepton signature is promising at
\mu
TRISTAN, which provides an appealing pathway to test the TeV-scale origin of neutrino masses.
