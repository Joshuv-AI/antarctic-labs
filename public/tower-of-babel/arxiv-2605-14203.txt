Numerical characterizations for integral dependence of graded modules
Suprajo Das, Sudeshna Roy, Vijaylaxmi Trivedi

Abstract
. In this paper we construct adic , saturated and ε \varepsilon -density functions for a torsion free module in a graded setup. Then we give some simple criteria for checking the integral dependence of two graded modules N ⊆ M N\subseteq M in terms of various well-studied invariants.



1.
Introduction

The main objective of this article is to
construct density functions and
provide a new (necessary and sufficient) numerical criterion for detecting integral dependence of arbitrary graded modules defined over a standard graded Noetherian domain over a field.
This extends the earlier results
in
[
5
]
and
[
6
]
, which dealt with graded ideals.

The idea of characterizing integral dependence through numerical invariants, was initiated in the pioneering work of Rees
[
17
]
. Since then finding such criteria became an important task in both commutative algebra and singularity theory. For a nice detailed survey, one may refer to
[
10
, Chapter 11]
.
There are also extensions of Rees’ theorem to the case of modules and algebras, see
[
9
,
18
,

2

{\mathbb{N}}
-graded torsion-free
A

The Rees algebra of

Theorem
(D) (see Theorem

)
Let

as above, where

and

. Further let

Then the following statements are equivalent.

(1)

.

(2)

(4)

\varepsilon({\mathsf{M}}_{c}S)=\varepsilon({\mathsf{N}}_{c}S)

(5)
