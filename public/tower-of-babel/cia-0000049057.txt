SUBJ:

EL SALVADOR

ALLEGATION BY CHIEF OF THE NATIONALIST REPUBLICAN
ALLIANCE (ARENA) PARAMILITARY UNIT THAT A NATIONAL
GUARD OFFICER ASSASSINATED ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARNULFO
ROMERO IN 1980,

MID-APRIL 1983

Approved for Release
NOV 1993

NATIONAL GUARD OFFICER, CAPTAIN (FNU) ((VEGA)) VALENCIA, HAD
BEEN RESPONSIBLE FOR THE 24 MARCH 1980 ASSASSINATION OF
ARCHBISHOP OSCAR ARNULFO ((ROMERO)). ACCORDING TO REGALADO,
VEGA IS A RIGHTIST AND AN EXPERT MARKSMAN WHO KILLED ROMERO WITH
ONE EXPLOSIVE BULLET FROM A SMALL CALIBER WEAPON.

2. REGALADO FURTHER SAID THAT VEGA WAS ASSIGNED TO MEXICO CITY
AS MILITARY ATTACHE FOLLOWING THE ASSASSINATION. HE STATED THAT

SALVADORAN ARMED FORCES, REGALADO DESCRIBED VEGA AS A FORMER
NATIONAL GUARD INTELLIGENCE SECTION COLLEAGUE AND PERSONAL

FRIEND OF ROBERTO ((D'AUBUISSON) ) , PRESIDENT OF THE CONSTITUENT
ASSEMBLY AND PRESIDENT OF ARENA. REGALADO ADDED THAT D'AUBUISSON
HAD BEEN KNOWLEDGEABLE OF THE PLAN TO ASSASSINATE ROMERO AND
APPROVED THE PLAN AS A MEANS OF LIMITING LEFTIST INFLUENCE WITHIN
THE CATHOLIC CLERGY. ( COMMENT: SOME PARAMILITARY MEMBERS
SUSPECT THAT DOCTOR REGALADO WAS AN ACTIVE CONSPIRATOR IN THE
ASSASSINATION AND BELIEVE THAT HE MAY HAVE MANUFACTURED THE
EXPLOSIVE BULLET USED TO KILL ROMERO. THEIR BELIEVE IS BASED

ON THE FACT THAT REGALADO HAS USED HIS DENTIST'S TOOLS TO FABRICATE
SPECIAL BULLETS FOR OTHER ASSASSINATIONS CARRIED OUT BY THE
ARENA PARAMILITARY UNIT.)
