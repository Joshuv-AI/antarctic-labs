Correction: Interpretable machine learning-based real-time sepsis diagnosis
Authors: Fahim Mahmud, Muhammad Quamruzzaman, Abdurrashid Ibrahim Sanka, Ray C C Cheung, Mehdi Hasan Chowdhury
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-36945-w, published online 17 February 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in the References 10, 19 and 21, where the in-text citations in the Introduction section were stated incorrectly. As a result,

“Tomas et al. proposed a specialized loss function in their work10 to deal with the class imbalance problem.”

now reads:

“Vicar et al. proposed a specialized loss function in their work10 to deal with the class imbalance problem.”

And,

“Petr et al. used AUPRC in their work19 on sepsis detection in ICU settings, which penalized false positives more than the AUROC to deal with the uneven class distribution.”

now reads:

“Nejedly et al. used AUPRC in their work19 on sepsis detection in ICU settings, which penalized false positives more than the AUROC to deal with the uneven class distribution.”

And,

“Simon et al.21 first labelled 6 h of prior data to actual sepsis onset as positive to facilitate proper learning process.”

now reads:

“Lyra et al.21 first labelled 6 h of prior data to actual sepsis onset as positive to facilitate proper learning process.”

Furthermore, the following References were omitted and are now listed as References 22, 23, 24, 25 and 26. These References are now cited in the Introduction, and in the Results and analysis section, under the subheading ‘Computational rationale for real-time deployment’:

22. Rathi, S. et al. Real-Time Multimodal Anaemia Detection Using Deep Learning. 2026 4th Int. Conf. Intell. Data Commun. Technol. Internet of Things (IDCIoT). 1718–1725, DOI: 10.1109/IDCIoT67589.2026.11455887 (2026).

23. Yakimov, B. et al. Dataset of human skin and fingernails images for non-invasive haemoglobin level assessment. Sci. Data. 11, 1070, DOI: 10.1038/s41597-024-03895-9 (2024).

24. Kumar, K. et al. Non-Invasive Hemoglobin prediction: A machine learning approach. 2025 5th Int. Conf. Adv. Electron. Commun. Eng. (AECE). 620–625, DOI: 10.1109/AECE67531.2025.11386530 (2025).

25. Das, S., Kesarwani, A., Kisku, D. R. & Dalui, M. Attention enabled precise haemoglobin prediction: A GNN based non-invasive approach. Biomed. Signal Process. Control. 105, 107604, DOI: 10.1016/j.bspc.2025.107604 (2025).

26. Kesarwani, A., Das, S., Kisku, D. R. & Dalui, M. Multi-scale vision transformer toward improved non-invasive anaemia detection using palm video. Multimed. Tools Appl. 83, 85825–85848, DOI: 10.1007/s11042-024-20118-w (2024).

As a result of these changes, the References have been renumbered.

The original Article has been corrected.
