Rumpa, et al., “Assessment of Toxicity and Beneficiary Effects of Garcinia pedunculata on the Hematological, Biochemical, and Histological Homeostasis in Rats,” Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2017, 4686104, https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/4686104.

The above article, published online on 22 January 2017 in Wiley Online Library (https://wileyonlinelibrary.com), has been retracted by agreement by John Wiley & Sons Ltd. The retraction has been agreed due to concerns identified in Figure 6, some of which have been raised on PubPeer [1].
