TEXT: 1. IN MID-DECEMBER a
SAID THE NATIONAL POLICE RECEIVED ORDERS FROM THE PRESIDENTIAL

HOUSEHOLD TO CONDUCT A COMPLETE AND HONEST INVESTIGATION OF THE
MURDERS OF SIX JESUIT PRIESTS AND TWO OTHER PERSONS WHICH OC ED ON
16 NO R 1989 AT THE CENTRAL AMERICAN UNIVERSITY (UCA).
SAID SENIOR POLICE OFFICIALS IN TURN ORDERED THE NATIONAL

TECTIVE DIVISION TO CONDUCT A PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION OF
THE MURDERS AND PROVIDE ALL NECESSARY SUPPORT TO THE SPEC
INVESTIGATIONS UNIT (SIU) INVESTIGATION.
SENIOR POLICE OFFICIALS ARE COMMITTED TO SOLVING THE MURDERS AND

BRINGING THE GUILTY PARTIES TO JUSTICE, WHETHER THEY ARE FROM THE
POLITICAL RIGHT OR LEFT, OR THE MILITARY.

2. THE NATIONAL POLICE ROLE HAS CONSISTED OF PROVIDING

TECHNICAL SUPPORT TO THE SIU, INCLUDING BALLISTIC COMPARISONS,
F INGERPR WORK AND ROUTINE INVESTIGATIVE PROCEDURES.

THE NATIONAL POLICE ALSO CONDUCTED A PRELIMINARY
INVESTIGATION, WHICH FAILED TO TURN UP ANY PHYSICAL EVIDENCE THE
INFORMATION GATHERED BY THE NATIONAL POLICE INVESTIGATION
CORRESPONDED WITH THAT OBTAINED BY THE SIU: THE AREA SURROUNDING THE
UCA CAMPUS WAS UNDER THE CONTROL OF SALVADORAN ARMED FORCES (ESAF)
AND SECURITY FORCES PERSONNEL; THERE WAS NO GUERRILLA ACTIVITY
REPORTED IN THE IMMEDIATE AREA AROUND THE UCA ON THE NIGHT OF THE
MURDERS; AND THERE WERE NO ABANDONED OR STOLEN VEHICLES LEFT IN THE
AREA TO PROVIDE CLUES AS TO THE AUTHORS OF THE CRIME.

3. Ss ATTN RS ETT ae eA TY HIS OPINION THAT THE
ESAF WAS LVED IN THE MURDERS. HE SAID HE BASED THIS OPINION ON

THE FACT THERE WAS APPARENTLY NO GUERRILLA ACTIVITY IN THE JMMEDIATE
AREA AROUND THE PRIESTS' RESIDENCE PRIOR TO THE MURDERS, AND THAT

THERE WAS A HEAVY MILITARY PRESENCE IN THE AREA. HE ADDED, HOWEVER,
THAT WHILE MANY IN THE NATIONAL POLICE SUSPECT ESAF PARTICIPATION IN
THE MURDERS, NO PROOF EXISTS TO IMPLICATE THE MILITARY IN THE CRIME.
