Reply
Authors: Jacqueline L Chen, Amanda J Lu, Rohan Verma, Li Wang, Douglas D Koch, Allison J Chen
Published: 2026

We thank Dr Vassallo for his thoughtful comments regarding our study evaluating large language model responses to ophthalmology continuing medical education questions. We agree that artificial intelligence (AI) models are evolving extraordinarily rapidly and that newer-generation systems are already demonstrating meaningful improvements in reasoning ability and diagnostic performance.

We also agree that continued benchmarking and more dynamic evaluation frameworks will be important as the technology advances. However, the primary purpose of our study was not simply to assess whether a large language model could select the correct multiple-choice answer but rather to evaluate the quality and safety of the prose explanations accompanying those answers.

Although ChatGPT-4 demonstrated strong multiple-choice accuracy, our analysis found that the associated free-text responses frequently contained incorrect reasoning, omitted clinically important information, or included recommendations with the potential to cause patient harm. Importantly, these issues were observed even when the model selected the correct answer. In clinical medicine, explanatory reasoning is often just as important as the final conclusion itself, particularly when patients and clinicians may rely on these responses to guide decision-making.

We share Dr Vassallo’s view that the ophthalmic community should proactively help shape the integration of AI into clinical practice. We believe ophthalmologists should play a central role in developing appropriate validation standards, ethical safeguards, and physician-guided oversight mechanisms to ensure these technologies are implemented safely and responsibly.

Artificial intelligence has tremendous potential to augment ophthalmic education, workflow efficiency, and patient care. At the same time, rapid technological advancement should not diminish the need for rigorous evaluation of model outputs in high-stakes medical settings. We appreciate Dr Vassallo’s engagement with our work and share his optimism regarding the future role of AI in ophthalmology when paired with appropriate clinical oversight.
