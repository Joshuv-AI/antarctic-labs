Note: In years past we have had a round robin doubles
tournament open to invitees of both sexes. Since that
arrangement has proven to be so successful, we are
using that format again this year. Again, the only exception
to the “Invitees only” rule will be that you and your
spouse may play together if you believe you are a
competitive team; in that case please let us know by May 1.
All other participants will be paired by the tournament
director. Of course, non-playing members of your family are
encouraged to attend.
