Reduction mod p p of semi-stable representations of some super-Breuil weights
Anand Chitrao, Eknath Ghate

Abstract
We determine the mod p p reductions of the semi-stable representations V k , ℒ V_{k,\mathcal{L}} of weight k ∈ [ p + 5 , 2 ​ p ] ∪ [ 2 ​ p + 6 , 3 ​ p + 1 ] k\in[p+5,2p]\cup[2p+6,3p+1] and v p ​ ( ℒ ) < 1 − k / 2 v_{p}(\mathcal{L})<1-k/2 for primes p ≥ 5 p\geq 5 . In particular, this shows that the techniques introduced in [ 5 ] involving the p p -adic and mod p p local Langlands correspondences can be used to compute the reduction of V k , ℒ V_{k,\mathcal{L}} outside the range k ∈ [ 3 , p + 1 ] k\in[3,p+1] . Moreover, this shows that the bound on v p ​ ( ℒ ) v_{p}(\mathcal{L}) given by Bergdall-Levin-Liu [ 1 ] can be improved, at least for weights k ∈ [ 2 ​ p + 6 , 3 ​ p + 1 ] k\in[2p+6,3p+1] .

Preliminaries

First, we state some combinatorial lemmas.
We begin with the mod
p^{2}
version of Lucas’ theorem, proved by Rowland.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
10
, Theorem 4]
)
.

If
0\leq s\leq r\leq p-1
,

, and

{pa+r\choose pb+s}\equiv{a\choose b}{r\choose s}\left(1+pa(H_{r}-H_{r-s})+pb(H_{r-s}-H_{s})\right)\mod p^{2}.

This recovers classical Lucas after going mod
p

for

consists of the sum of a complete set of non-zero residues mod

We also need Stirling numbers of the second kind, which we recall are defined by (see
[
11
]
)

{t\brace s}=\frac{1}{s!}\sum_{j=0}^{s}(-1)^{j}{s\choose j}(s-j)^{t}\text{ for integers $t,s\geq 0$}.

It can be shown by applying the operator

if

While manipulating the congruences obtained using Proposition

{y+p^{i}\brace x}\equiv{y+1\brace x}+\sum_{j=1}^{i}{y\brace x-p^{j}}\mod p.
