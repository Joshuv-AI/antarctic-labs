Spring Quarter Outline

 Scintillator Manufacture

This paper will be a literature review of composite scintillators, and their applications in neutron detection. Due to

the shortage of 3He, the traditional medium for neutron detection, new materials need to be explored for this

purpose. Composite scintillating particle materials can serve as a solution to replace 3He, offering faster response

times, and all-solid-state form factors for diversified applications in the field. This project will include

manufacturing Europium (Eu) and Sodium (Na) doped LiCaAlF 6 (LiCAF) scintillating particles for neutron

detection.

Scintillator paper components:

 • Literature review of the scintillating process for composite neutron detectors, from “slowing” of fast

 neutrons to thermal neutrons via moderation, to the ionizing interactions within scintillators, and

 subsequent light output from luminescent centers in the material.

 • General overview of luminescent materials, specifically rare earth ions and the crystal field effect.

 • Literature review of the LiCAF scintillator with Eu and Na doping. Outline reasons for doping LiCAF with

 europium ions, along with sodium ions.

 • Outline the process and findings from our own LiCAF:Eu2+ and: LiCAF:Eu2+;Na1+ study. Use quantum

 yield measures to determine light yield dependence on Na ion content, and radioluminescence

 measurements to determine the radio-luminescence spectra as a function of temperature.
