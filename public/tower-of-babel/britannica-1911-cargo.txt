CARGO (Span. for “loading,” from Lat. carrus, car), a shipload,
or the goods (or even, less technically, persons) carried on
board a ship; and so, by analogy, a term used for any large
amount. The maritime law affecting the cargo of a ship is dealt
with in the articles Average, Affreightment, Insurance,
Salvage, Bottomry, Lien; and the specialities of cargo-ships under Ship.
