Member of the rabbinical college of Lemberg, and appears in that capacity among the rabbis who had to decide a case in matrimonial law with regard to the marriage of the widow of a man who had been killed by the bands of Chmielnicki . Aaron died at Lemberg, June 20, 1651.

Bibliography: Abraham Rapoport, Etan ha-Ezraḥi, responsum No. 21, Ostrog, 1796; Buber, Anshe Shem, p. 22, Cracow, 1895. A. B. D.
