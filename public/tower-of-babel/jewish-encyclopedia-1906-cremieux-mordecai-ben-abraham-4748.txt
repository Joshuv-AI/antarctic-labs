Rabbi at Aix, Provence; born at Carpentras in 1749; died May 22, 1825. He was the author of "Ma'amar Mordekai" (Treatise of Mordecai), a commentary on the Shulḥan 'Aruk, Oraḥ Ḥayyim, in two parts, Leghorn, 1784.

Bibliography: J. Milhand, in Archives Israélites, 1849, pp. 11, 136; and 1840, p. 374; Nepi-Ghirondi, Toledot Gedole Yisrael, p. 241; Allg. Zeit. des Jud. 1840, p. 411; Azulai, Shem ha-Gedolim, ii. 42; Steinschneider, Cat. Bodl. col. 1657. S. A. R.
