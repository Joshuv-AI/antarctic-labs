MEMORANDUM FOR

FROM { |

Administrative Officer, DCI

SUBJECT : Travel Allowances When Accompanying

the Director to San Francisco

1. As you requested we have checked JTRs applicable to military
personnel assigned to CIA which we are compelled to adhere to and they
are as follows:

a) It is the policy of the DDA that aides travelling
with the Director and required to reside in the same hotel

as the Director (per DCI’s instructions) will not be required
to assume the lodging costs from within the per diem allowance.
The Agency will assume the cost of the lodging in total as
"government furnished quarters." Therefore, while staying at
the Stanford Court in San Francisco, we will pick up the entire
cost of a single room without charge to your per diem allowance.

b) JTRs require a reduction in the authorized per diem
allowance of 50 percent when government quarters are furnished
(the p er diem in San Francisco is $41.00 and in Los Angeles
$40.00) unless there are "unusual circumstances* 1 in which case
we can reimburse for actual expenses u£ jto the maximum amount
of actual expenses authorized. This means that if you are
required to incur meal costs in excess of $20.00 (or $20.40

in San Francisco), please be prepared to explain the unusual
circumstances and to provide receipts wherever possible.

c) With respect to travel by rental automobile between San
Francisco and Los Angeles for personal reasons in lieu of travel
by commercial aircraft, you are limited under the JTRs to an
allowance of .07 per mile or the cost of commercial aircraft,
whichever is the lesser. According to my information, it is 424
miles between Los Angeles and San Francisco and at .07 per mile
this would be $29.68. The airfare is $26.00, therefore, the
latter figure will be the reimbursement authorized.

STAT

2. In any event we will go over your accountings with you
item by item upon your return and try to give you the best legal
break. Hope this clarifies your situation.
