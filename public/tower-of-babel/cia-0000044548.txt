//((NOTE ENTITLED "CRISTIANI AND THE VIDEO REPORT") )
((TEXT)) BECAUSE OF ITS IMPORTANCE, WE REFER ONCE MORE TO THE

TESTIMONY BY FORMER DEATH SQUAD MEMBER CESAR VIELMAN JOYA MARTINEZ.
THE NONGOVERNMENTAL HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSION SHOWED THE LOCAL AND

INTERNATIONAL PRESS THE VIDEO RECORDING SHOWING THE DESERTER MAKING

HIS STATEMENTS, WHICH HAD BEEN PREVIOUSLY PUBLISHED BY THE MEXICAN

NEWSPAPER LA JORNADA. ANSWERING QUESTIONS FROM THE PRESS, THE

NONGOVERNMENTAL HUMAN RIGHTS COMMISSION EXPLAINED WHY FORMER AGENT
ALEX DESERTED FROM THE DEATH SQUADS. HE DESERTED BECAUSE THE NEJAPA

OPERATION FAILED AND, BECAUSE OF THIS, HE WAS IN DANGER OF BEING

TRIED.

THE OPERATION FAILED BECAUSE IT BECAME KNOWN THAT TWO PEASANTS

WHO HAD BEEN TAKEN PRISONER DIED FROM BEING TORTURED BY MEMBERS OF
THE 1ST INFANTRY BRIGADE. THE HIGH COMMAND FOUND ITSELF COMPELLED TO
INVESTIGATE THE DEATH OF THE PEASANTS AND THE MILITARY MEN WHO
DIRECTED THE OPERATION HAD NO OPTION BUT TO MURDER THE OFFICER WHO
WAS IN COMMAND -- CESAR VIELMAN JOYA MARTINEZ -- WHO THEN FLED THE
COUNTRY. oo ee
WE CAN ALL RECALL THE GRUESOME REPORT IN JULY -- ON 25 JULY, TO BE
PRECISE -- THAT THE 1ST BRIGADE HAD CAPTURED SEVEN PEASANTS IN THE
VICINITY OF APOPA AND THAT TWO OF THEM HAD DIED FROM THE TORTURE AND
BEATINGS THEY SUFFERED. THIS WAS THE NEJAPA OPERATION WHICH WAS
CARRIED OUT BY THE ATLACATL BATTALION AND MEMBERS OF SECTION 2 OF THE
1ST BRIGADE AND IN WHICH JOYA MARTINEZ PARTICIPATED. WE RECALL ALSO
THAT THE DESERTER SAID IN HIS TESTIMONY THAT PRESIDENT CRISTIANI
PERSONALLY ORDERED OPERATION ATLACATL IJ, WHICH WAS CARRIED OUT 2
DAYS AFTER THE PRESIDENT'S LATEST VISIT TO THE GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
OF THE DEATH SQUADS, LOCATED BEHIND THE MILITARY SCHOOL. THERE IS NO
QUESTION THAT THIS WAS THE SAME OPERATION.
THE DESERTER'S TESTIMONY MAKES IT CLEARER THAN EVER THAT A DEATH
SQUAD MEMBER IS THE PRESIDENT OF OUR NATION AND THAT HE ORDERS
THE CAPTURE, TORTURE, AND MASSACRES OF PEASANTS. PRESIDENT CRISTIANI
IS NOT A MODERATE ADMINISTRATOR. HE IS LIKE MURDERER ROBERTO
D'AUBUISSON -- A DEATH SQUAD MEMBER.
