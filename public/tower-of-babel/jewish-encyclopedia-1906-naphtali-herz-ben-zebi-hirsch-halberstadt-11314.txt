Rabbi at Dubno, Russia, in the eighteenth century. Responsa of his in regard to the Cleve divorce case are found in Israel Lipschütz's collection "Or Israel" (1770). In the same collection are some responsa by his brother Solomon Dob Baer, rabbi of Glogau; another brother, Simḥah, was rabbi of Dessau.
