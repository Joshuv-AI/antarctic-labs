Political Developments
Iraqi Response to Bush Address

In a six-minute commentary on President Bush’s State of the
Union address, Baghdad Radio called the President a war criminal
and a liar. The commentator asked how Bush could claim to be
defending humanity when "he is ordering his mercenaries to hit
women and children and destroy residential areas and cultural and
social establishments." The commentator also called for a "deep
and balanced dialogue" to resolve the crisis. ee |

Syria Unhappy With Western Aid to Israel

' The government-controlled Syrian media today accused the
West of undermining the Arabs and future Arab-Israeli peace
efforts by increasing aid to Tel Aviv. The media claims that,
although Saddam is responsible for the war, the West should not
use this as an excuse to rush aid to Israel.

_Iraq will Receive Red Cross

Ibrahim al-Nuri, president of the Iraqi Red Crescent
Society, announced today that Iraq is ready to receive an
International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) delegation to
examine the treatment of coalition POWs, according to press. In
return, Al-Nuri set the condition that the ICRC also report on
the conditions of the Iraqi people.
