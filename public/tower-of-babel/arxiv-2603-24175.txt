Nowhere-zero 3 3 -flows in Cayley graphs on solvable groups of twice square-free order
Milad Ahanjideh, István Kovács

Abstract
We verify Tutte’s 3 3 -flow conjecture in the class of Cayley graphs on solvable groups of order 2 ​ n 2n , where n n is square-free. The proof relies on a new necessary and sufficient condition for a simple 5 5 -valent graph to admit a nowhere-zero 3 3 -flow in terms of a pseudoforest decomposition. Keywords: nowhere-zero 3 3 -flow, pseudoforest, Cayley graph, solvable group. Math. Subj. Class.: 05C21, 05C25.

Our goal is to construct a nowhere-zero

.

To achieve this goal, we define first a

According to case (b) in (iv), there is a common edge

.
In view of (iii), we may assume without loss of generality that

\Sigma

\Lambda

\displaystyle\mathrm{Null}(\varphi_{i})=\{e_{i}\}

\displaystyle~~(i\in[1,m]),

\displaystyle D_{i}(e_{i})=D(e_{i}),~D_{j}(e^{\prime}_{j})=D(e^{\prime}_{j})

3
Nowhere-zero 3-flows and a pseudoforest decomposition

Let
\Gamma
be a graph and
D
be an orientation of
\Gamma
. For a vertex
v\in\mathrm{V}(\Gamma)
,
the
out-degree

d^{+}_{D}(v)
of

in
D
is equal to
the cardinality
|D^{+}(v)|
and
the
in-degree

d^{-}_{D}(v)
is equal to the cardinality

|D^{-}(v)|
. In the case where every vertex has out-degree
0
or
1
, we say that
D
is a
(0,1)
-orientation
. In the next lemma we describe the graphs that admit at least one
(0,1)
-orientation. For this purpose, we need to introduce two more definitions.
A graph is called
unicyclic
if it contains exactly one cycle and it is called a

pseudoforest
if each of its components is a tree or unicyclic.

Lemma 3.1
.

(i)

Let
D
be a
(0,1)
-orientation of a graph
\Gamma
. Then
\Gamma
is a pseudoforest.

(ii)

Let
\Gamma
be a pseudoforest and fix a subset
V^{\prime}\subseteq\mathrm{V}(\Gamma)
.
Then there exists a
(0,1)
-orientation
D
of
\Gamma
such that

V^{\prime}=\{v\in\mathrm{V}(\Gamma)\mid d^{+}_{D}(v)=0\}

if and only if
V^{\prime}
has exactly one vertex from each tree component of
\Gamma
and no vertex
from the unicyclic components.

Proof.

(i): Let
U=\{v\in\mathrm{V}(\Gamma)\mid d^{+}_{D}(v)=0\}
and
\Gamma^{\prime}
be a component of
\Gamma
.
Then the following inequality holds for
\Gamma^{\prime}
:

|\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|-1\leq|\mathrm{E}(\Gamma^{\prime})|=\sum_{v\in\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})}d^{+}_{D}(v)=|\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|-|U\cap\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|.

Thus
|U\cap\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|\leq 1
. Furthermore, if
|U\cap\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|=1
, then

|\mathrm{E}(\Gamma^{\prime})|=|\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|-1
, whence
\Gamma^{\prime}
is a tree; whereas if
|U\cap\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|=0
, then
|\mathrm{E}(\Gamma^{\prime})|=|\mathrm{V}(\Gamma^{\prime})|
, whence
\Gamma^{\prime}
is unicyclic.

(ii): The necessity of the statement can be red off from the proof of (i). For the sufficiency
assume that
V^{\prime}
has exactly one vertex from each tree component and none from the
unicyclic components. A required
(0,1)
-orientation can be constructed as follows.
If
\Gamma^{\prime}
is a tree component of
\Gamma
, then orient each edge of
\Gamma^{\prime}
toward its unique
vertex in
V^{\prime}
; whereas if
\Gamma^{\prime}
is a unicyclic component, then orient the edges in
the cycle of
\Gamma^{\prime}
cyclically, and then
the remaining edges toward the cycle.
∎

In what follows, we say that a set
U
of vertices of a pseudoforest
\Gamma
is a
tranversal
of
\Gamma
if
U
has exactly one vertex from each tree component of
\Gamma
and no vertex from the unicyclic components.

Lemma 3.2
.

Let
\Gamma
be a graph of valency
5

\Gamma
admits a nowhere-zero
\mathbb{Z}_{3}
-flow if and only if

\mathrm{V}(\Gamma)
can be partitioned into non-empty subsets

(i)

The induced subgraphs
\Gamma[U]
and
\Gamma[W]
are pseudoforests.

(ii)

Either all components of
\Gamma[U]
and
\Gamma[W]
are unicyclic, or there
exist a transversal
U^{\prime}
of
\Gamma[U]
and a transversal

of
\Gamma[W]
such that

\Gamma[U^{\prime},W^{\prime}]
has a perfect matching.

Proof.

Part “
\Rightarrow
”: Assume that
(D,\varphi)
is a nowhere-zero
\mathbb{Z}_{3}
-flow of
