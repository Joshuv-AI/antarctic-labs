PROPOSED INTERAGENCY OPERATING PROCEDURE

SUBJECT: Permanent Committee on Soviet Capabilities and Intentions

AGENCIES PARTICIPATING IN THIS AGREEMENT: Central Intelligence Agency

Department of State
Department of the Army
Department of the Navy
Department of the Air Force

1, A permanent interagency intelligence committee (hereinafter
referred to as the JACKPOT Committee) is hereby established for the
purpose of facilitating the production and maintenance by the Central
Intelligence Agency of comprehensive coordinated estimates of Soviet
capabilities and intentions. To this end, the JACKPOT Committee is
expected:

a. to provide a Mechanism whereby the most capable analysts
of the Departments of State, Army, Navy and Air Force collaborate
with those of the Central Intelligence Agency in consideration of
all factors bearing on Soviet capabilities and intentions, against
the broadest possible regional and global background, and

b, to ensure that the fullest intelligence resources of the
participating agencies are effectively marshalled in support of

, the production and 'maintenance of such estimates.

2. The Central Intelligence Agency, through the mechanism of the
JACKPOT Committee, shall undertake the production of comprehensive
coordinated national intelligence estimates of Soviet capabilities and

3. Each of the agencies represented on the JACKPOT Committee

Member, Department of the Army
Member, Department of the Navy
Member, Department of the Air Force

5. The JACKPOT Committee shall, in the first instance, establish
its methods of procedure.

6. The JACKPOT Committee shall conduct regularly scheduled meetings
Attendance at JACKPOT Committee meetings shall be regarded as having
priority over any other duty assigned to JACKPOT Committee members. In
addition to the regularly scheduled meetings, special meetings and
emergency meetings shall be called by the Chairman on his own initiative,
or on the request of any member, whenever the situation warrants such
action.
