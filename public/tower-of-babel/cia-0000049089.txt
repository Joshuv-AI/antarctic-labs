SHB FOLLOWING 1S BRIEF BACKGROUNDER ON THE NATIONAL
REPUBLICAN ALLIANCE PARTY (ARENA) AND ITS ASSOCIATION WITH
RIGHT-WING DEATH SQUAD ACTIVITY. MAJOR ROBERTO ((D'AUBUISSON)),
ARENA'S PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDATE AND PARTY LEADER, IS A FORMER
SALVADORAN ARMY MAJOR. PRIOR TO THE PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS,
D'AUBUISSON HEADED THE CONSTITUTIONAL ASSEMBLY WHICH WAS FORMED
TO DRAFT A CONSTITUTION FOR EL SALVADOR. WORKING FOR D' AUBUISSON
AS HEAD OF THE CONSTITUTENT ASSEMBLY'S SECURITY WAS DR. HECTOR
((REGALADO)), WHO CONCURRENTLY SERVED AS HEAD OF ARENA'S
PARAMILITARY UNIT (THE GROUP FROM WHICH DEATH SQUAD MEMBERS
ARE PRINCIPALLY DRAWN). THIS GROUP OF PARAMILITARY MEN COMPRISED
THE SECURITY FORCE FOR THE CONSTITUTENT ASSEMBLY AND PROVIDED
SECURITY FOR INDIVIDUAL ARENA FUNCTIONARIES INCLUDING D'AUBUISSON,
AND ARENA PARTY HEADQUARTERS. DR. HECTOR REGALADO IS CHARGED
WITH THE IMMEDIATE CONTROL OF THIS GROUP OF MEN. WHILE THE
MAJORITY OF ARENA PARTY SUPPORTERS KNOW NOTHING OF ARENA'S
INVOLVEMENT WITH DEATH SQUAD ACTIVUTY, MAJOR D'AUBUISSON HAS

approved for Release

NOV 1993

REEN IMPLICATED WITH DIRECT INVOLVEMENT THROUGH THE ACTIVITIES

OF DR. REGALADO. ALL DEATH SQUAD ACTIVITY IN EL SALVADOR is

NOT DIRECTED BY THE RIGHT-WING POLITICAL FACTION. HISTORICALLY,
THE SALVADORAN PEOPLE HAVE BEEN VIOLENCE-PRONE WITH NASTY TEMPERS
WHICH FLARE TO APPEASE PERSONAL AFFRONTS EITHER REAL OR IMAGINED
WHICH HAVE NOTHING TO DO WITH POLITICS. THE LEFT AS WELL HAS
BEEN INVOLVED IN KILLINGS FOR WHICH THE RIGHT HAS BEEN BLAMED

AND VICE VERSA.
