Mr. F. W. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 748.]

Department of State, Washington, November 2, 1863.

Sir: I have to acknowledge the receipt of your despatch of the 16th ultimo, (No. 518.) Your note of the 12th of October to Earl Russell, as well as that of the 24th of September, is approved.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

F. W. SEWARD, Acting Secretary.

Charles F. Adams, Esq., &c, &c., &c.
