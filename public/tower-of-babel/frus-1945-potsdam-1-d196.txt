No. 196 The British Embassy to the Department of State [1]

[Extract][2]

secret

Memorandum

. . . . . . .

Sir A. Clark Kerr has accordingly been instructed to point out to the Soviet Government that, as they are aware, the Turkish Government have consulted His Majesty’s Government about the recent Turco-Soviet conversations. … They wish the Soviet Government to be aware of their views on these recent developments as they consider the whole question will have to be discussed at Terminal.

Washington , 7th July, 1945.
