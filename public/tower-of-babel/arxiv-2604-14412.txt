Abstract
. We consider the Cauchy problem for the Korteweg–de Vries equation with real initial data q ∈ L 1 ∩ L 2 q\in L^{1}\cap L^{2} supported on ( 0 , ∞ ) (0,\infty) . Using the left reflection coefficient and Hankel operators on the Hardy space H 2 ​ ( ℂ + ) H^{2}(\mathbb{C}^{+}) , we derive a trace-type representation for the corresponding solution. The proof is based on approximation by compactly supported potentials, uniform convergence of the associated reflection coefficients away from the origin, and continuity properties of the resulting Hankel operators. This yields a rigorous inverse scattering construction for a class of summable half-line supported initial data beyond the standard short-range setting.

(3.2)

Define the Hankel operator on

Clearly

\varphi\in L^{2}\cap L^{\infty}

(3.4)

We will also need the Nehari theorem saying that

\left\|\mathbb{H}(\varphi)\right\|\lesssim\left\|\mathbb{P}_{-}\varphi\right\|_{BMO},

The following lemma is likely well-known and valid in much more generality.
Since we cannot find an exact reference we give a simple proof specific to our situation.

Lemma 6.1
.

Let
f\in L^{\infty}\left(S_{a}\right)

where
S_{a}=\left[-a,-a+\mathrm{i}a,a+\mathrm{i}a,a\right]
,
a>0
, is a
polygonal contour (rectangle). If

F\left(k\right)=\int_{S_{a}}\frac{f\left(\lambda\right)\mathrm{d}\lambda}{\lambda-k},\ \ \ k\in\mathbb{R}\text{,}

then
F\in BMO\left(\mathbb{R}\right)

\left\|F\right\|_{BMO}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{a}\right)}.

Moreover,
F\in L^{2}
and
\left\|F\right\|_{2}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{a}\right)}.

Proof.

By the scaling it suffices to prove the estimate for
a=1
. Write

F=F_{\mathrm{top}}+F_{+}+F_{-},

where
F_{\mathrm{top}}
is the contribution of the horizontal side
\left[-1+\mathrm{i},1+\mathrm{i}\right]
and
F_{\pm}
are the contributions of
the vertical sides
\left[\pm 1,\pm 1+\mathrm{i}\right]
. Since
\left[-1+\mathrm{i},1+\mathrm{i}\right]
stays away from
\mathbb{R}
one
immediately conclude
F_{\mathrm{top}}\in L^{\infty}\subset BMO
and,
moreover,
\left|F_{\mathrm{top}}\left(k\right)\right|\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{\infty}/\left(1+\left|k\right|\right)
for
\left|k\right|\geq 2
, so
F_{\mathrm{top}}\in L^{2}

with

\left\|F_{\mathrm{top}}\right\|_{BMO}+\left\|F_{\mathrm{top}}\right\|_{2}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}.

Next, for the right side,

F_{+}\left(k\right)=-\int_{0}^{1}\frac{f\left(1+\mathrm{i}y\right)\,\mathrm{d}y}{y-\mathrm{i}\left(1-k\right)},

which is the Cauchy transform of the density
g_{+}\left(y\right)=f\left(1+\mathrm{i}y\right)\mathrm{1}_{\left(0,1\right)}\left(y\right)
. By
the Calderón–Zygmund theorem,

\left\|F_{+}\right\|_{BMO}\lesssim\left\|g_{+}\right\|_{\infty}\leq\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}.

Also,

\left|F_{+}\left(k\right)\right|\leq\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\sqrt{\left(1-k\right)^{2}+y^{2}}}=\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}\operatorname{arsinh}\frac{1}{\left|1-k\right|}.

The function
\operatorname{arsinh}\left(1/\left|x\right|\right)
is square integrable on
\mathbb{R}
: near
x=0
it behaves like

\log\left(2/\left|x\right|\right)
, while for
\left|x\right|\geq 1
it is
\lesssim 1/\left|x\right|
. Hence

F_{+}\in L^{2}
and

\left\|F_{+}\right\|_{2}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}.

The left side
F_{-}\left(k\right)
is treated in exactly the same way and
we finally conclude

\left\|F\right\|_{BMO}+\left\|F\right\|_{2}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\infty}\left(S_{1}\right)}.

∎

Lemma 6.2
.

Let

and
Q=\max\left(\left\|q\right\|_{1},\left\|\widetilde{q}\right\|_{1}\right)

\sup_{\left|k\right|\geq a}\left|k\left[L\left(k\right)-\widetilde{L}\left(k\right)\right]\right|\lesssim_{a,Q}\left\|q-\widetilde{q}\right\|_{1}.

\left(Q/2a\right)\exp\left(Q/a\right)<1.

Proof.

Recall that by (

L\left(k\right)=b\left(k\right)T\left(k\right)

where

Moreover

[
17
]
(see also
[

]
),

\left\|\widetilde{m}\left(k,\cdot\right)\right\|_{\infty},\left\|m\left(k,\cdot\right)\right\|_{\infty}\leq\exp\left(Q/\left|k\right|\right)

\left\|m\left(\cdot,k\right)-\widetilde{m}\left(\cdot,k\right)\right\|_{\infty}\leq\exp\left\{2Q/\left|k\right|\right\}\left\|q-\widetilde{q}\right\|_{1}/\left|k\right|.

It follows that

\displaystyle 2\left|k\right|\left|b\left(k\right)-\widetilde{b}\left(k\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|q\left(x\right)-\widetilde{q}\left(x\right)\right|\left|m\left(k,x\right)\right|\mathrm{d}x+\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|\widetilde{q}\left(x\right)\right|\left|m\left(k,x\right)-\widetilde{m}\left(k,x\right)\right|\mathrm{d}x

\displaystyle\leq\left\{\exp\left(Q/\left|k\right|\right)+\exp\left\{2Q/\left|k\right|\right\}\right\}\left\|q-\widetilde{q}\right\|_{1}

and thus

Similarly

Next,

and hence if we choose

Lemma 6.3
.

If
\varphi\in H^{\infty}+C
,
\left|\varphi\right|<1
a.e. and
\mathbb{H}\left(\varphi\right)

\|\mathbb{H}(\varphi)\|<1

Proof.

(By contradiction) Assume that

\mathbb{H}\left(\varphi\right)

\left\langle\mathbb{H}(\varphi)f,f\right\rangle=\left\langle\varphi f,\mathbb{P}_{-}\mathbb{J}f\right\rangle=\left\langle\varphi f,\mathbb{J}f\right\rangle

and hence by the Cauchy inequality

\displaystyle 1

\displaystyle=\left|\lambda\right|=\left|\left\langle\mathbb{H}(\varphi)f,f\right\rangle\right|\leq\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|\varphi\right|\left|f\right|\left|\mathbb{J}f\right|\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|\varphi\right|\left|f\right|^{2}\right)^{1/2}\ \|f\|_{2}

\displaystyle=\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}}\left|\varphi\right|\left|f\right|^{2}\right)^{1/2}<\|f\|_{2}=1\text{ (since }\left|\varphi\right|<1\text{ a.e.).}

\left|\lambda\right|<1
