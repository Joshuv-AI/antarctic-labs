On Csányi’s and Arias’ Functional for Ground States Energy of Multi-Particle Fermion Systems: Asymptotics Thanks: This work was partially supported by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft via the TRR 352 – Project-ID 470903074 and EXC-2111-390814868
Heinz Siedentop

Abstract
. We show that Csányi’s and Arias’ energy functional of the one-particle reduced density matrix is bounded from below by the Müller functional and bounded from above by the Hartree-Fock functional. We use this fact to derive an asymptotic expansion of the ground state energy of this functional which agrees with the quantum energy to third order.

Using this notation the

(2)

\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{CA}}(\gamma):=\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{HF}}(\gamma)-X[\sqrt{\gamma(1-\gamma)}].

Csányi and Arias found the functional

(3)

\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{M}}(\gamma):={\rm tr\,}((T({\mathbb{p}})-V({\mathbb{x}}))\gamma)+D[\rho_{\gamma}]-X[\sqrt{\gamma}].

We consider these functionals on certain subsets of the set

We start with some preparatory facts.

Lemma 1
.

Assume
\mu,\lambda\in[0,1]
. Then

(6)

\lambda\mu+\sqrt{\lambda(1-\lambda)\mu(1-\mu)}\leq\sqrt{\lambda\mu}.

Proof.

Applying the Schwarz inequality to the sum on the left side of
(
6

(7)

\lambda\mu+\sqrt{\lambda(1-\lambda)\mu(1-\mu)}\leq\sqrt{\lambda^{2}+\lambda(1-\lambda)}\sqrt{\mu^{2}+\mu(1-\mu)}=\sqrt{\lambda\mu}

which is identical with the right side of (
6

∎

We also remind of the formula (Fefferman and de la Llave

[
5

{1\over|{\mathbb{x}}-{\mathbb{y}}|}={1\over\pi}\int_{0}^{\infty}{\mathrm{d}r\over r^{5}}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}\mathrm{d}\mathbf{z}\;B_{r,\mathbf{z}}({\mathbb{x}})B_{r,\mathbf{z}}({\mathbb{x}})

(9)

B_{r,\mathbf{z}}({\mathbb{x}}):=\begin{cases}1&|{\mathbb{x}}-\mathbf{z}|<r\\
0&|{\mathbb{x}}-\mathbf{z}|\geq r\end{cases}.

Writing

Theorem 1

Assume

\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{M}}(\gamma)\leq\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{CA}}(\gamma)\leq\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{HF}}(\gamma).

Proof.

The upper bound on
\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{CA}}(\gamma)

\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{CA}}

(12)

X[\gamma]+X[\sqrt{\gamma(1-\gamma)}]\leq X[\sqrt{\gamma}].

First note that

0\leq\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}x\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}yB_{r,\mathbf{z}}(x)\xi_{m}(x)\overline{\xi_{n}(x)}\overline{\xi_{m}(y)\overline{\xi_{n}(y)}}B_{r,\mathbf{z}}({\mathbb{y}})

and therefore, choosing
\lambda=\lambda_{m}
and
\mu=\lambda_{n}

(

0\leq\left(\sqrt{\lambda_{m}\lambda_{n}}-\lambda_{m}\lambda_{n}-\sqrt{\lambda_{m}(1-\lambda_{m})\lambda_{n}(1-\lambda_{n})}\right)\\
\times\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}x\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}yB_{r,\mathbf{z}}(x)\xi_{m}(x)\overline{\xi_{n}(x)}\overline{\xi_{m}(y)\overline{\xi_{n}(y)}}B_{r,\mathbf{z}}({\mathbb{y}}).

We divide the inequality by

r

\tfrac{1}{2}\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}x\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}y{|\sum_{m}\lambda_{m}\xi_{m}(x)\overline{\xi_{m}(y)}|^{2}+|\sum_{m}\sqrt{\lambda_{m}(1-\lambda_{m})}\xi_{m}(x)\overline{\xi_{m}(y)}|^{2}\over|{\mathbb{x}}-{\mathbb{y}}|}\\
\leq\tfrac{1}{2}\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}x\int_{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}y{|\sum_{m}\sqrt{\lambda_{m}}\xi_{m}(x)\overline{\xi_{m}(y)}|^{2}\over|{\mathbb{x}}-{\mathbb{y}}|}
