Introduction
This is a guide to reviewing and releasing a set of records in Granta’s Materials Database where
Dynamic Properties Team is responsible.

Background
A set of records is comprised of one or more groups of testing records (Uniaxial, SHPB, Gun,
RUS, etc.) and appropriate support records (Testing Series, Pedigree, Project, Documents, etc.).

Overview
The process for completing a review requires multiple individuals; each reviewer has a specific
role(s). Appropriate corrections and/or additions are made to records throughout each review
to comply with conventions described here or referenced. Once a comprehensive review has
been fulfilled by a team of individuals, the set of records may be released.

General Guidelines
 • A set of records up for review consists of at least 1 Pedigree and 1 testing record of any
 type.

 • A set of records must be reviewed by at least 2 separate individuals, meaning an
 individual can take up only 2 out of the 3 review roles.

 • Corrections and/or additions made to a set of records under review should be
 documented.

 • A set of records must be reviewed and released within a year testing was performed.

Guide to Reviewing Records
Review Roles

A review is to be performed by a Data Modifier, a Technical SME, and a Granta SME, each with
their own checklist of responsibilities (see Figure 1).

  Data Modifier – individual that creates new records and edits or adds data into existing
 records.

  Technical SME – individual that has technical background of material and linked testing
 data including material pedigree, testing purpose and matrix, project, etc.

  Granta SME – individual that understands Granta conventions specific to attributes and
 how linking of testing records and support records ought to be.

Responsibilities of Roles

Each role is responsible for checking specific aspects of records and attributes. Any edits or
additions deemed necessary are made and communicated.

  Data Modifier
 • Follows role-specific checklists documented here or referenced according to
 record type
 • Communicates with Technical SME and Granta SME on missing information and
 irregularities of meta data, functional data or database network
 • Makes corrections or additions to records deemed necessary by each role and
 records changes
 • Tracks Granta status of sets of records

  Technical SME
 • Follows role-specific checklists documented here or referenced according to
 record type
 • Communicates with Data Modifier and Granta SME on missing information and
 irregularities of meta data, functional data or database network
 • Ensures meta data is technically correct, especially within support records
 (pedigrees, projects, testing series, documents, etc.)
 • Checks functional data behavior is within reason (CMPs, testing records)
 • Recovers missing meta data from affiliated groups and forwards to Data Modifier

  Granta SME
 • Follows role-specific checklists documented here or reference according to
 record type
 • Communicates with Data Modifier and Technical SME on missing information
 and irregularities of meta data, functional data or database network
 • Ensures a set of records has the appropriate support records and all records are
 correctly linked
 • Ensures data entries are in proper attributes and comply with Granta
 conventions
 • Brainstorms solutions to overcome weak points in database’s design or
 conventions as they arise (the Granta SME may need to reach out to Granta
 concierges)
 • Typically releases the reviewed set of records
