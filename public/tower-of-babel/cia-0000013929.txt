1. As Source was travelling to join Training Group 32 in RONG TAM Hamlet,
LAM SON Village, ‘IUONG SON District, HOA BINH Province, NVN, in December
1965, he saw a U.S. prisoner being transported by truck passing RONG
VONG Hamlet on National Route 6 in the direction of HANOL. The prisoner
was about 30 years old, had a large build and red hair, He wore a Light
green uniform and his arms were tied in front of him, The prisoner sat
in the front seat of a command-car, next to the driver, while two soldiers
sat in the rear. Another car followed the prisoner's vehicle in which
sat four soldiers armed with AK rifles, According to the villagers, this
prisoner was a pilot who had been captured in the forest at IUONG SON
District, HOA BINH Province. He had hiddon for two days before he was
found and had probably parachuted out of his plane and landed in the woods,

2. In mid-November 1966, as Source and two comrades were hunting in the
area of DONG CHUON, PHU LOC District, THUA THIEN Province, SVN, they saw
two U.S. prisoners of war (POWs) accompanied by three Viet Cong (VC)
soldiers travelling to an wunknowm destination, One prisoner was about
30 years old and had a large build and curly red hair, His trousers were
wet to the knees which indicated that he had waded across a stream,

His arms were bound behind him with nylon string, and he looked worried.
The second prisoner was approximately of the same age and build, The
three VC soldiers were dressed in short, khaki uniforms and were armed
with one K50 machine pistol and 2 Kl rifles. Each VG soldier carried
a woman's belt which contained enough rice to last for three days .
Replying to Source'!s inquiry, one of the VC guards said that one of the
prisoners was 4 lieutenant, and the other a Master Sergeant; they were
in charge of a radio and were captured at PHU VANG District, THUA THIEN
