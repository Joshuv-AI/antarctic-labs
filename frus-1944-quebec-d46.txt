Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (1 September 1944, d46)

top secret

London, 1 September 1944.

Number 777. Prime Minister to President Roosevelt. Personal and top secret.

I hope you will pardon a further transgression of the Teheran scale. I am going to bring Mrs. Churchill with me.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels; forwarded by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt, who was at Hyde Park, as telegram No. Red 348.
