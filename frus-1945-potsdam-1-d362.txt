No. 362 The Representative on the Allied Commission on Reparations (Pauley) to the President [1]

[Extract][2]

top secret

Moscow, June 29, 1945—8 p.m.

2325. Top sec from Pauley to the President.

I am bending every effort to have a proposed reparation plan to submit to you and the heads of Great Brit and Russia at your forthcoming meeting. …
