New Haven, CT, Yale University, Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, Mellon MS 28

Description: ff. 56 - Original, North Italian (Bolognese?). Blind-stamped white leather over blue-green pasteboards, the
sides paneled with repeated stamps of conventionalized foliage within double heart-shaped outlines, the whole enclosed in
blind fillets, the central compartment on each cover with a large, circular floral stamp and two impressions of a lozenge-
shaped stamp, the back with three bands, fillets in the compartments; four linen ties on each cover (two at fore-edge, one
each top and bottom, all lacking except for traces of seven inside covers); modern leather title label on backstrip. Portion of
a leaf from a 13th-century legal manuscript used for sewing-in and visible inside both covers. - Denis Duveen; Mellon MS
52, acquired with the Duveen collection. Gift of Paul and Mary Mellon, 1965. - Witten, Laurence C. and Pachella, Richard.
Alchemy and the Occult: A Catalogue of Books and Manuscripts from the Collection of Paul and Mary Mellon. Volume Three:
Manuscripts, Mellon MS 28.

Notes:-Manuscript on paper of 1) John Dastin, Rosarius philosophorum, with the prologue. 2) Elementa sunt quattuor...
(with a diagram and directions for mixing pigments). 3) Hermes or Merlin or Rasis, Laudabile sanctum, or Gemma salutaris.
