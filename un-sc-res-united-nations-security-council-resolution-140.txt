Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 870th meeting, on 29 June 1960

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Malagasy Republic <small>[1]</small>,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Malagasy Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

<small>[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for April, May, and June 1960, document S/4352/Rev.1.</small>
