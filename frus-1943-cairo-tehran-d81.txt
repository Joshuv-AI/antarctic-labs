Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (31 October 1943, d81)

secret

London, 31 October 1943.

Prime Minister to President. Number 482. Most secret and personal.

My immediately preceding telegram, para 1: Your humble servant, yourself and the Generalissimo respectively.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Apparently sent to Washington via military channels, and forwarded by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt at Hyde Park.
