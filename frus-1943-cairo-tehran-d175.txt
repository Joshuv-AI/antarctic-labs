The President to the Secretary of State[1] (30 October 1943, d175)

secret urgent

[Hyde Park,] 30 October 1943.

Personal and secret for the Secretary of State only from the President. My 302232.[2]

Final disposition of all Italian ships should apply to those used by British and Americans as well as to those used by Russians.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, Moscow, via the White House Map Room and Navy channels.
[2] Telegram of October 30, 1943, supra.
