Jezebel Background

• The Jezebel assembly was operated from
  1954-1977
• Jezebel is the primary experiment for fast
  239Pu nuclear data validation for the last 70

  years (PMF-001)
• Validation included keff, spectral indices,
  Rossi-α, reactivity coefficients, and neutron
  leakage spectra

                                                  10/16/2025   3

Jezebel Background

• Jezebel stopped operation in 1977, nearly
  50 years ago
• Complete reliance on historical
  experiments has several issues:
  − Documentation on many of these
 experiments is lacking
  − Operators no longer available
  − Components no longer available
  − No way to continually test new R&D
 advances
  − Limited opportunities to observe system
 changes over time

                                              10/16/2025   4

An enduring Pu assembly would help advance Pu
research in multiple focus areas
• A "Jezebel replacement“ used for a single experiment
  campaign would not be worth the effort ND
 validation

• New enduring (100 year target) Pu assembly would be
  extremely valuable AM
 validation
  − Requires simple geometry and low uncertainties
• Need has been in the DOE Nuclear Criticality Safety
  Program (NCSP) Mission and Vision for over 10 years Lilith Dosimetry

• Need also identified as the top priority established at the
  2022 National Criticality Experiments Research Center Reactor
 kinetics

New Assembly Design Requirements

• Easily manufacturable
• Maximally sensitive to 239Pu (n,f) cross section
• Neutron spectrum as hard as possible
• Monotonic worth curve of control element
• Glory hole to accommodate sample irradiation and reactivity worth studies

                                                                        10/16/2025   7

Lilith Design

• 23.5 kg Ga stabilized δ-phase WG-Pu

• Fuel rings canned in Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5)

• Reactivity controlled by lower moveable
  safety block

                                             10/16/2025   8

 Conclusions
• A design of an enduring Pu critical assembly has been produced
• This is very important to DOE/NNSA missions
• This assembly would result in advances/improvements in
  − Nuclear data validation
  − Analytical methods validation
  − Dosimetry
  − Reactor Kinetics
  − Materials
  − And more
