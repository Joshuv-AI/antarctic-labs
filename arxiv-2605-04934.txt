On λ \lambda -existence over a predicate
Alexander Usvyatsov

Abstract
We prove that in a countable theory T T fully stable over a predicate P P , any λ \lambda -complete set A A has the λ \lambda -existence property. This means that A A can be extended to a λ \lambda -saturated model of T T without changing the P P -part. The notion of λ \lambda -completeness, introduced in this paper, captures some obvious necessary conditions for such an extension to be possible (for example, the P P -part of A A has to be a λ \lambda -saturated model of the appropriate theory). So in a fully stable theory T T , λ \lambda -existence can only fail for trivial reasons. This generalizes results of Chatzidakis in the context of difference fields of characteristic 0. 1 Introduction Let T T be a complete first order theory, P P a distinguished unary predicate. Let T P T^{P} be the complete first order theory of P P . More precisely, we will work in a monster model 𝒞 ⊧ T \mathcal{C}\models T , and T P T^{P} will be the theory of 𝒞 P \mathcal{C}^{P} . In addition, assume that P P is embedded and stably embedded; i.e., there is no additional structure definable on P P , which is not definable already in T P T^{P} with parameters from P P . One can ask the following natural question: does every model of T P T^{P} “occur” as the P P -part of some model of T T ? We know that, in general, the answer to this question is “no”. So the question becomes: under what conditions on T T is the above the case for all models of T P T^{P} ? Alternatively, one can ask: under what conditions on the model of T P T^{P} is the above the case for an arbitrary T T ? These and related questions are addressed in several papers, e.g., [ 6 , 4 , 5 , 7 ] . For example, in a recent paper [ 7 ] , the author has showed that if T T is fully stable over P P (see Definition ), then the answer is “yes” for any model of T P T^{P} . In fact, more is true: any complete set A A (Definition ) can be extended to a model of T T without changing its P P -part (completeness is an obvious necessary condition on A A for this to be possible). A similar result has been recently proven in a preprint by Bayes, Kaplan, and Simon assuming a different notion of stability over P P (in particular, it holds for any simple theory T T ). One motivating example for this study is the theory of difference fields. Afshordel [ 1 ] has shown that any difference field ( K , σ ) (K,\sigma) with a pseudo-finite fixed field k = F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) ​ ( K ) k=Fix(\sigma)(K) , can be extended to a model M M of A ​ C ​ F ​ A ACFA without changing the fixed field, i.e., k = F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) ​ ( M ) k=Fix(\sigma)(M) . The author’s result mentioned above generalizes this for the case of characteristic 0 0 : it is shown in [ 7 ] that A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} is indeed fully stable over P = F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) P=Fix(\sigma) . The results of Bayes, Kaplan, and Simon, generalizes this for all characteristics, since A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} is simple for any value of p p . At the same time, the results in [ 7 ] give more: specifically, if T T is fully stable, and A A is a complete set, the model M ⊧ T M\models T extending A A with P A = P M P^{A}=P^{M} can be chosen to be locally constructible over A A . In particular, in case of A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} , this shows that every difference field k k of characteristic 0 0 with a pseudo-finite fixed field can be extended to a model K K of A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} with F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) ​ ( K ) = F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) ​ ( k ) Fix(\sigma)(K)=Fix(\sigma)(k) such that K K is locally constructible over k k . It would be interesting to ask whether the same is true for A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} . This paper addresses a different (but related) question. Suppose N ⊧ T P N\models T^{P} is λ \lambda -saturated. Is there a λ \lambda -saturated model M M of T T with P M = N P^{M}=N ? If the answer is “yes”, we say that T T has the λ \lambda -existence property over N N . One can ask for more, e.g., M M is λ \lambda -prime, or even λ \lambda -primary over N N . If e.g. λ = λ < λ \lambda=\lambda^{<\lambda} and N N is a saturated model of T P T^{P} of cardinality λ \lambda , then the answer to the above questions in “yes” [ 4 ] . If, however, N N is a λ \lambda -saturated model of T P T^{P} of a larger cardinality, things become more complicated. The original motivation comes, again, from the study of difference fields. In [ 2 ] , Chatzidakis has shown that A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} has λ \lambda -existence (over the fixed field) for all uncountable λ \lambda (and even λ = ℵ ε \lambda=\aleph_{\varepsilon} ). Moreover, every λ \lambda -saturated pseudo-finite field k k of characteristic 0 0 can be extended to a λ \lambda -saturated model K K of A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} with F ​ i ​ x ​ ( σ ) ​ ( K ) = k Fix(\sigma)(K)=k such that K K is λ \lambda -prime over k k . Motivated by Chatizidakis’ work, the author has shown in [ 8 ] that, given a theory T T fully stable over P P and λ > | T | \lambda>|T| , the class 𝒦 λ ​ ( T , N ) \mathcal{K}_{\lambda}(T,N) of λ \lambda -saturated models of T T with a fixed P P -part N N , admits prime models. This, however, relies on the class 𝒦 λ ​ ( T , N ) \mathcal{K}_{\lambda}(T,N) being non-empty. It was left open whether, given a prescribed model of T P T^{P} which is λ \lambda -saturated, it always “occurs” as the P P -part of some λ \lambda -saturated model of T T . In the case of A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} , for example, this is not hard, and is one of the first steps in the proof of the main theorem in [ 2 ] . In the general context of model theory over P P , it is often the existence problems that prove more difficult, and require more sophisticated techniques (in our case, stability theory). In this paper we address this question and prove that if T T is fully stable over P P , then T T has λ \lambda -existence over P P . Moreover, every complete set A A whose P P -part is a λ \lambda -saturated model N N of T P T^{P} can be extended to a λ \lambda -saturated model M M of T T with P M = N P^{M}=N such that M M is λ \lambda -constructible over N N . This generalizes the results in [ 2 ] , since A ​ C ​ F ​ A 0 ACFA_{0} is fully stable. It is interesting to point out that, although we do not know whether a similar result holds for A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} for p > 0 p>0 , Chatzidakis has conjectured that the answer is “no”. This would immediately imply that A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} is not fully stable, even though it has full existence. It may also turn out to be that case that A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} has full existence, but the models of A ​ C ​ F ​ A p ACFA_{p} that are constructed in [ 1 ] are not locally atomic over the underline difference fields. These would all be interesting questions to investigate. 2 Preliminaries In this section we set up the framework and recall basic notions and facts.
