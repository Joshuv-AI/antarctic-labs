Upon the basis of the information submitted on this case, it appears
that the contribution of the Government to the making of the invention is
insufficient equitably to Justify requirement of assignment to the Govern-
ment cf the entire right, title and interest in and to the invention, but
is sufficient to Justify a license thereunder*

Therefore, the C h a irm a n of the Governaant patents Board concurs in

exclusive, irrevocable and royalty-free license in the invention with
power to grant licenses for all governmental purposes, such reservation,
in the terms thereof, to appear, where practicable, in any patent,
domestic or foreign, which may issue on the invention.
