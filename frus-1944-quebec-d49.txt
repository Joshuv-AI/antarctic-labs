The President to the War Shipping Administrator (Land)

secret

[Hyde Park,] September 2, 1944.

Memorandum for Admiral Land

I have not heard anything about the proposed personnel if the Prime Minister comes over but if he brings Lord Leathers you must, of course, come to Quebec. I will let you know shortly. Meantime, will you keep yourself in readiness to come to Quebec, leaving the ninth or tenth?

F[ranklin] D. R[oosevelt]

Source: Roosevelt Papers
