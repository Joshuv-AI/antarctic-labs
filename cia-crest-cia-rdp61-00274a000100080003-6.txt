DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF SECURITY

MISSION AND FUNCTIONS

Makes decisions as the full deputy in important or sensitive
matters within established policy of the Director of Security; follows
intimately the progress of the counter-intelligence operations of the
office; conducts specific liaison, internally and externally, on certain
highly sensitive matters delegated to him; assists the Director of
Security in the performance of his mission and the exercise of his
functions; and acts for the Director of Security in his absence. Serves
as Chairman, Office of Security Career Service Board.

EXECUTIVE OFFICER

MISSION AND FUNCTIONS

Provides staff coordination for the Director of Security in the
policy planning, administration and operations of the Office of Secur-
ity; guides and gives executive direction to the various components
of the Office on policy planning and procedural matters; exercises
administrative control over the Office of Security Staffs, the CIA
Emergency Security Patrol, and the Office of Security segment of the
CIA Emergency Force; and represents the Director of Security in co-
ordinating with other offices of CIA and other agencies of the govern-
ment, and by membership on various Agency and Office of Security
boards, panels and committees, and otherwise, as directed.

SECURITY RESEARCH STAFF

A. MISSION

Acting immediately under the supervision of the Director of
Security and the Deputy Director of Security, plans and implements
all domestic counter intelligence programs of CIA.

B. FUNCTIONS

1. Collects, receives, evaluates, develops and maintains infor-
mation for the purpose of preventing, detecting and arresting penetration,
of the Agency by foreign or domestic organizations or individuals
whose activities maybe inimical to the United States.

2. Coordinates with DD/'P concerning domestic counter intelligence
activities having overseas implication, or overseas counter intelligence
activities in which domestic investigation maybe involved.

3. Coordinates with certain other government agencies in con-
nection with counter intelligence activities, as appropriate.

4. On a continuing basis, reviews and evaluates the security records
of CIA employees to guard against penetration or defection or any assoc-
iation or activity contrary to the interest of the Agency.

5. Coordinates the counter intelligence effort within the Office of
Security and. keeps the Director of Security currently advised on all new
developments.
