President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (10 November 1943, d112)

secret priority

[Washington,] 10 November 1943.

Personal and secret from the President for the Former Naval Person. Number 416.

Your 497.[2] As we cannot arrive Oboe before forenoon November 20th it seems to me impracticable to have a meeting at Malta enroute to Sextant. It is best to go straight through with one stop at Tunis for the night.

Perhaps we can visit Malta on the return journey.

I am glad you are bringing Winant.[3]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Ante, p. 76.
