President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (30 August 1944, d37)

top secret priority

[Washington,] 30 August 1944.

Number 610, personal and top secret, from the President for the Prime Minister.

Your 771.[2] I am pleased that our final arrangements for Octagon will keep the number who will participate in the conference to the Teheran level insofar as practicable.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché London, via Navy channels.
[2] Ante, p. 23.
