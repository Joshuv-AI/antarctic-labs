Primary Goal: To systematically review research and toolsets for federated learning
applied in semiconductor manufacturing, discover gaps, and identify opportunities.

 Background and Importance
The CHIPS and Science Act of 2022 refocused
national attention on domestic semiconductor
manufacturing by incentivizing domestic
semiconductor R&D, manufacturing, and workforce
development, and called for the creation of the
Semiconductor Manufacturing and Advanced
Research with Twins (SMART) USA Institute. The
semiconductor supply chain and manufacturing
processes are complex and highly interdependent.
Regulations and intellectual property concerns
restrict sharing of semiconductor manufacturing data
to train and create deep learning analytical models. Gaps and Opportunities
