Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 2084th meeting, on 17 August 1978
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of Solomon Islands [1] for admission to the United Nations,
Recommends to the General Assembly that Solomon Islands be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Thirty-third Year, Supplement for July, August and September 1978, document S/12801.
