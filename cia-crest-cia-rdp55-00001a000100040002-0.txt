4. As the first of a series of measures designed to correlate
ORE production more closely with the recurring needs of the using
agencies, it has been thought advisable for ORB to draw up a tentative
over-all list of national intelligence production requirements. 'Yttien
completed, this list will be transmitted to the NSC and other policy
and operational planning bodies for recommendations, additions, and
designation of priorities. It is hoped that such a list, by bringing
together not only the ideas of intelligence producers but also the needs
of intelligence users, will for the first time furnish an agreed frame-
work upon which ORE can plan its production.

5. Some explanation of the term "intelligence production require-
ment", as used here and in ORE Operating Procedure No. 29, would perhaps
be helpful. An intelligence production requirement, is the statement of
a need for intelligence production for a specifio purpose, expressed in
definite, as opposed to general, terms. An intelligence production
requirement oan be the expression of need for an estimate on a single
topic or situation. It oan be an expression of need for several estimates
treating of varied aspects of one problem. It oan also be the expression
of need for either an irregular or recurrent series of papers on a con-
tinuing problem. In all oases, however, an intelligence production
requirement shoild be specific as to subject, and unequivocal as to
terms of reference. Any aonfusion of an intelligence production require-
ment with a naticnal intelligence objective, as ooitmonly known, should

be avoided. National intelligence objectives are already familiar, and
are too broad to be used other than as general guides for deduolng
intelligence production requirements.

6. In the light of the foregoing discussion, it is requested that
you prepare, with respect to your field of responsibility, a list of
intelligence production requirements, for transmittal to the Flans and
Polioy Staff of ORE by 29 March 1949.
