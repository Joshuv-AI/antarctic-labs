1.0 Summary
 This activity was to enable us to install air quality and meteorological instrumentation such as an
 automated Air Quality Drone, PurpleAir Sensor, and Sensit Ramp Air Quality Sensor. This installation
 formed part of the U.S. Department of Energy Atmospheric Radiation Measurement (ARM) User
 Facility’s Coast-Urban-Rural Atmospheric Gradient Experiment (CoURAGE) at the Baltimore site.
 Figure 1 shows the aerial image of the CoURAGE Baltimore site with all the installed instruments.

 Figure 1. CoURAGE main field site.

 The Air Quality Drone employs DJI Dock including Matrice 30T with a Sniffer4d Sensor from
 Soarability. It samples air pollutants such as NOx, CO, O3, SO2 and particulate matter (PM). The drone is
 fully automated and programed to perform one mission daily (4 pm). Figure 2 shows the image of the Air
 Quality Drone (left) including the flight plan (right) for the field campaign.

 Figure 2. DJI Matrice 30T Air Quality Drone with Sniffer 4d and the flight plan.

 The Sensit Ramp Air Quality Sensor is to monitor carbon monoxide (CO), nitrogen dioxide (NO2), sulfur
 dioxide (SO2), ozone (O3), PM, and carbon dioxide (CO2) in addition to meteorological parameters.
 Figure 3 shows the image of the Sensit Ramp Air Quality instrument.

 Figure 3. Sensit Ramp Air Quality Drone Sensor.

 1.1 Scientific Focus
 The aim of this research was to install air quality monitoring instruments at the COURAGE field site at
 Lake Clifton to study the influence of a low-level jet on pollution in the Baltimore region.

 1.2 Relevance to ARM
 The data from this study is needed in understanding the atmospheric dynamics relevant to ARM’s mission
 of significantly improving the representation of challenging atmospheric processes in earth system
 models.

2.0 Results
 The various instruments collected useful scientific data needed to understand atmospheric dynamics
 relevant to pollution transport in the Baltimore region. Figure 4 shows the diurnal ozone variation for July
 17, 2025 sampled by the Sensit Ramp Air Quality Sensor. The ozone concentration ranges from 0 ppb to
 more than 50 ppb. The Air Quality Drone on the other hand shows a spatial distribution of PM.25
 exceeding 81 µg/m3 on November 21, 2025.
