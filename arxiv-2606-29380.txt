Xin-Ru Liu, Qi Liu, Jiao-Kai Chen

Abstract
We propose Regge trajectory relations for the hexaquark ( u ¯ ​ ( c ​ c ) ) ​ ( b ​ ( b ¯ ​ b ¯ ) ) {(\bar{u}(cc))(b(\bar{b}\bar{b}))} by using the Regge trajectory relations for diquarks and triquarks. With these newly derived relations, we investigate five series of hexaquark Regge trajectories: the λ \lambda -, ρ 1 \rho_{1} -, ρ 2 \rho_{2} -, σ 1 \sigma_{1} -, and σ 2 \sigma_{2} -trajectories. We demonstrate that, apart from the simplest λ 1 \lambda_{1} -trajectories, the ρ 1 \rho_{1} -, ρ 2 \rho_{2} -, σ 1 \sigma_{1} -, and σ 2 \sigma_{2} -trajectories cannot be constructed by merely mimicking the meson Regge trajectories, since mesons possess no internal substructures. To derive these trajectories, one must account for the structure and internal substructure of hexaquark. Without this structural information, the ρ 1 \rho_{1} -, ρ 2 \rho_{2} -, σ 1 \sigma_{1} -, and σ 2 \sigma_{2} -trajectories could only be obtained through direct fits to available theoretical predictions or future experimental data. We demonstrate that the ρ 1 \rho_{1} -, ρ 2 \rho_{2} -, σ 1 \sigma_{1} -, and σ 2 \sigma_{2} -trajectories for the hexaquark do not correspond respectively to the Regge trajectories for the triquark, antitriquark, diquark, and antidiquark. Nevertheless, their behaviors match those of the Regge trajectories for the triquark ( u ¯ ​ ( c ​ c ) ) (\bar{u}(cc)) , the antitriquark ( b ⁡ ( b ¯ ​ b ¯ ) ) (b(\bar{b}\bar{b})) , the diquark ( c ​ c ) (cc) , and the antidiquark ( b ¯ ​ b ¯ ) (\bar{b}\bar{b}) , in that respective order. Furthermore, we present rough mass estimates for the excited states corresponding to the λ \lambda -, ρ 1 \rho_{1} -, ρ 2 \rho_{2} -, σ 1 \sigma_{1} -, and σ 2 \sigma_{2} -trajectories.

IV
Conclusion

In this work, we propose Regge trajectory relations for the quintuply heavy hexaquark
{(\bar{u}(cc))(b(\bar{b}\bar{b}))}
by employing the diquark and triquark Regge trajectories. Using these newly derived relations, we systematically investigate five series of hexaquark Regge trajectories, namely the
\lambda
-,
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories. The masses of the
\lambda
-,
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-excited states are roughly estimated.

The complete forms of the Regge trajectories for hexaquark
{(\bar{u}(cc))(b(\bar{b}\bar{b}))}
are lengthy and cumbersome. Except for the
\lambda
-trajectories, the
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{2}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories cannot be constructed by simply mimicking the meson Regge trajectories; instead, they should be constructed based on hexaquark’s structures and substructures.
Otherwise, the
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories can only be determined by fitting either theoretical predictions or experimental measurements. The fundamental relationship between the slopes of the obtained trajectories, constituents’ masses, and string tension would become obscure.

We demonstrate that the
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories are not the triquark, antitriquark, diquark, and antidiquark Regge trajectories, respectively. However, they share identical trajectory behaviors, respectively.
We show that the lengthy complete forms of the
\rho_{1}
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories can be well approximated by the simple fitted formulas.
The
\rho_{1}
-trajectories behave as
M{\sim}\sqrt{x_{\rho_{1}}}

(x_{\rho_{1}}=n_{r_{1}},\,l_{1})
, while the
\lambda
-,
\rho_{2}
-,
\sigma_{1}
-, and
\sigma_{2}
-trajectories all exhibit behavior of
M{\sim}x^{2/3}
, where
x=N_{r},\,L,\,,n_{r_{2}},\,l_{2},\,n_{r_{3}},\,l_{3},\,n_{r_{4}},\,l_{4}
.

Ref.
Pan:2025gqn
discusses hexaquarks with various quark contents, including
qQQQQQ
. Both
{(\bar{u}(cc))(b(\bar{b}\bar{b}))}
and
qQQQQQ
are quintuply heavy hexaquarks. Although there is no sufficient experimental information on these hexaquarks, the results still have a certain guiding significance for future research.
