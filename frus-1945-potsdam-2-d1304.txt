No. 1304 The Acting Chairman of the Interim Committee (Harrison) to the Secretary of War (Stimson)

top secret priority

Washington, 17 July [19]45.

War–33556. TopSec Secretary of War from Harrison.

Doctor has just returned most enthusiastic and confident that the Little Boy is as husky as his big brother.[1] The light in his[1] eyes discernible from here to Highhold[1a] and I could have heard his screams from here to my farm.[2]

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] i. e., Fat Man.
[1] i. e., Fat Man.
[2] At Upperville, Virginia. Stimson’s diary entry for July 18 includes the following: “Harrison’s second message came, giving a few of the far reaching details of the test. I at once took it to the President who was highly delighted. … The President was evidently very greatly reenforced over the message from Harrison and said he was very glad I had come to the meeting. …”
[1a] Stimson’s home on Long Island.
