Non-Archimedean Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic Inequality
K. Mahesh Krishna

Abstract




Abstract
: Massera and Schaffer [
Ann. Math. (2), 1958
] derived a breakthrough upper bound for the Clarkson angle between two nonzero vectors in a normed linear space, which was later improved by Maligranda [
Am. Math. Mon., 2006
]. Pecaric and Rajic [
Math. Inequal. Appl., 2007
] extended Maligranda’s inequality to finitely many nonzero vectors. We derive a non-Archimedean version of Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic inequality.

Keywords
: Normed linear space, Triangle inequality, Non-Archimedean linear space, Ultra-norm.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 12J25, 46S10.

1.
Introduction

Let
\mathcal{X}
be a normed linear space (NLS). Recall that for
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
, the
Clarkson angle

[
1
]
between
x
and
y
is defined as

\displaystyle\alpha[x,y]\coloneqq\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|.

Important Massera-Schaffer inequality
[
4
]
says that

(1)

\displaystyle\alpha[x,y]=\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\frac{2\|x-y\|}{\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}},\quad\forall x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}.

Maligranda improved Inequality (
1
) in 2006
[
3
]
.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
3
,
5
]
(
Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda Inequality
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NLS. Then for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
,

(2)

\displaystyle\alpha[x,y]=\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\frac{\|x-y\|+|\|x\|-\|y\||}{\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}}\leq\frac{2\|x-y\|}{\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}}.

We note that, in 1964 Dunkl and Williams
[
2
]
independently showed that

(3)

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\frac{4\|x-y\|}{\|x\|+\|y\|},\quad\forall x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}.

Note that

\displaystyle\frac{2\|x-y\|}{\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}}\leq\frac{4\|x-y\|}{\|x\|+\|y\|},\quad\forall x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}.

Therefore Inequality (
1
) improves Inequality (
3
).
Inequality (
3
) is famously known as Dunkl-Williams inequality in the literature. In 2007, Pecaric and Rajic extended Inequality (
2
) to finitely many nonzero elements
[
6
]
.

Theorem 1.2
.

[
6
]
(
Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic Inequality
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NLS and
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Then for all
x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
,

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|\leq\min_{1\leq k\leq n}\left\{\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}\left(\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|+\sum_{j=1}^{n}\big|\|x_{j}\|-\|x_{k}\|\big|\right)\right\}.

It is important to ask what are non-Archimedean versions of Theorems
1.1
and
1.2
? We answer the question by deriving non-Archimedean Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic Inequality (Theorem
2.2
).

2.
Non-Archimedean Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic Inequality

Let
\mathbb{K}
be a field. A map
|\cdot|:\mathbb{K}\to[0,\infty)
is said to be a non-Archimedean valuation if following conditions holds.

(i)

If
\lambda\in\mathbb{K}
is such that
|\lambda|=0
, then
\lambda=0
.

(ii)

|\lambda\mu|=|\lambda||\mu|
for all
\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb{K}
.

(iii)

(Ultra-triangle inequality)
|\lambda+\mu|\leq\max\{|\lambda|,|\mu|\}
for all
\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb{K}
.

In this case,
\mathbb{K}
is called as non-Archimedean valued field
[
8
]
. Let
\mathcal{X}
be a vector space over a non-Archimedean valued field
\mathbb{K}
with valuation
|\cdot|
. A map
\|\cdot\|:\mathcal{X}\to[0,\infty)
is said to be a non-Archimedean norm if following conditions holds.

(i)

If
x\in\mathcal{X}
is such that
\|x\|=0
, then
x=0
.

(ii)

\|\lambda x\|=|\lambda|\|x\|
for all
\lambda\in\mathbb{K}
, for all
x\in\mathcal{X}
.

(iii)

(Ultra-norm inequality)
\|x+y\|\leq\max\{\|x\|,\|y\|\}
for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
.

In this case,
\mathcal{X}
is called as non-Archimedean linear space (NALS)
[
7
]
. Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NALS over
\mathbb{K}
. Let
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
with
\|x\|,\|y\|\in\mathbb{K}
. We define the
non-Archimedean Clarkson angle
between
x
and
y
as

\displaystyle\alpha[x,y]\coloneqq\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|.

Non-Archimedean version of Theorem
1.1
now reads as follows.

Theorem 2.1
.

(
Non-Archimedean Massera-Schaffer Inequality
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NALS over
\mathbb{K}
. Then for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
with
\|x\|,\|y\|\in\mathbb{K}
it holds

\displaystyle\alpha[x,y]=\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\min\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|x\|}{\|y\|}\right|\|y\|\right\},\frac{1}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|y\|}{\|x\|}\right|\|x\|\right\}\right\}.

Proof.

Let
x,y\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
with
\|x\|,\|y\|\in\mathbb{K}
. Then

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\frac{x-y}{\|x\|}+\left(\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right)y\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\frac{x-y}{\|x\|}\right\|,\left\|\left(\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right)y\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\frac{\|x-y\|}{\big|\|x\|\big|},\left|\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right|\|y\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\frac{\|x-y\|}{\big|\|x\|\big|},\frac{1}{\left|\|x\|\right|}\left|1-\frac{\|x\|}{\|y\|}\right|\|y\|\right\}

and

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right)x+\frac{x-y}{\|y\|}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\left(\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right)x\right\|,\left\|\frac{x-y}{\|y\|}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left|\frac{1}{\|x\|}-\frac{1}{\|y\|}\right|\|x\|,\frac{\|x-y\|}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\left|1-\frac{\|y\|}{\|x\|}\right|\|x\|,\frac{\|x-y\|}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\right\}.

Therefore

(4)

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\frac{1}{\big|\|x\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|x\|}{\|y\|}\right|\|y\|\right\}

and

(5)

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|\leq\frac{1}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|y\|}{\|x\|}\right|\|x\|\right\}.

Inequalities (
4
) and (
5
) give

\displaystyle\left\|\frac{x}{\|x\|}-\frac{y}{\|y\|}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\min\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|x\|}{\|y\|}\right|\|y\|\right\},\frac{1}{\big|\|y\|\big|}\max\left\{\|x-y\|,\left|1-\frac{\|y\|}{\|x\|}\right|\|x\|\right\}\right\}.

∎

Note the additional assumption
\|x\|,\|y\|\in\mathbb{K}
in the previous theorem. This is necessary because, since the norm is a real number, we generally cannot guarantee that it belongs to the given non-Archimedean field. We now derive the non-Archimedean version of Theorem
1.2
.

Theorem 2.2
.

(
Non-Archimedean Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic Inequality
)
Let
\mathcal{X}
be a NALS over
\mathbb{K}
and
n\in\mathbb{N}
. Then for all
x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
with
\|x_{1}\|,\dots,\|x_{n}\|\in\mathbb{K}
it holds

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|\leq\min_{1\leq k\leq n}\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\max\left\{\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\max_{1\leq j\leq n}\left|1-\frac{\|x_{k}\|}{\|x_{j}\|}\right|\|x_{j}\|\right\}\right\}.

Proof.

Let
x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\in\mathcal{X}\setminus\{0\}
with
\|x_{1}\|,\dots,\|x_{n}\|\in\mathbb{K}
. Let
1\leq k\leq n
be fixed. Then

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\frac{x_{k}}{\|x_{k}\|}+\sum_{j=1,j\neq k}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{k}\|}-\sum_{j=1,j\neq k}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{k}\|}+\sum_{j=1,j\neq k}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{k}\|}-\sum_{j=1,j\neq k}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}-\frac{1}{\|x_{j}\|}\right)x_{j}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{k}\|}-\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}-\frac{1}{\|x_{j}\|}\right)x_{j}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{k}\|}\right\|,\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}-\frac{1}{\|x_{j}\|}\right)x_{j}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}-\frac{1}{\|x_{j}\|}\right)x_{j}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\max_{1\leq j\leq n}\left\|\left(\frac{1}{\|x_{k}\|}-\frac{1}{\|x_{j}\|}\right)x_{j}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\max_{1\leq j\leq n}\left|1-\frac{\|x_{k}\|}{\|x_{j}\|}\right|\|x_{j}\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\max\left\{\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\max_{1\leq j\leq n}\left|1-\frac{\|x_{k}\|}{\|x_{j}\|}\right|\|x_{j}\|\right\}.

By varying
k
and taking minimum in the right side of previous inequality gives

\displaystyle\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{x_{j}}{\|x_{j}\|}\right\|\leq\min_{1\leq k\leq n}\left\{\frac{1}{\big|\|x_{k}\|\big|}\max\left\{\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_{j}\right\|,\max_{1\leq j\leq n}\left|1-\frac{\|x_{k}\|}{\|x_{j}\|}\right|\|x_{j}\|\right\}\right\}.

∎

3.
Conclusions

(1)

In 1958, Massera and Schaffer derived a surprising upper bound for the Clarkson angle between two nonzero elements in a normed linear space
[
4
]
.

(2)

In 2006, Maligranda improved Massera-Schaffer inequality
[
3
]
.

(3)

In 2007, Pecaric and Rajic extended Maligranda inequality for finitely many nonzero elements
[
6
]
.

(4)

In this article, we introduced the notion of non-Archimedean Clarkson angle and derived non-Archimedean version of Massera-Schaffer-Maligranda-Pecaric-Rajic inequality.
