President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (8 November 1943, d197)

secret priority

[Washington,] 8 November 1943.

Number 412 from the President to the Former Naval Person secret and personal.

Your 493.[2]

I accept your suggestion that “Commanders responsible” in Paragraph 3 be changed to “Combined Chiefs of Staff” and have so informed Harriman and have authorized him to attach agreement to the protocol.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] Ante, p. 156.
