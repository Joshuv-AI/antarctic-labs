2 . Re vie w of Chapter I on Fin l and

The Committee reviewed the Chapter I on NIS 12 (Finland)
and approved the draft with certain changes. The Chapter I Task Group
was requested to review the detailed comments submitted by the member
agencies. The Committee directed that this Chapter I be published
without control and be released only to the United Kingdom.

The Army member reported that the geographic staffs in
Army were impressed with the excellence of the terrain base maps
in Chapter I prepared by CIA’s Cartographic Division. The chairman
said he continues to be gratified by the thorough review of Chapters I
by the member agencies, which does so much to improve the quality
of the intelligence in theBe chapters.
