G. Paragraph VI . Paragraph VI, as written, should become
paragraph VII. Paragraph VI should be as follows:

''VI, The Implementation of this agreement will be by
the establishment of appropriate directives and procedures
as determined by the Director NRO and the Deputy Director
NStO. In the event of disagreement as to the mechanics of
implementing this Charter, the area of disagreement will
be referred to the Secretary of Defense and the Director of
Central Intelligence for decision. "

COMMENT: This agreement does not and should not attempt
t© resolve many of the disagreements between the Department of
Defense and the Central Intelligence Agency which have come up in
the past. Such disagreements have been over how the National
Reconnaissance Program Would be implemented. A paragraph
such as recommended above provides both parties with some
recourse in the event these problems cannot be resolved within
the NR.O management levels established by this agreement. In
addition, a charter agreement of this type should provide general
guidance on the mechanics of its implementation.
