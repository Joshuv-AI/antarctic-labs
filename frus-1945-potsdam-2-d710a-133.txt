Editorial Note (Potsdam 1945 Vol. II, Unnumbered document following Document 710 (#133))

—No official record of the substance of the conversation at this meeting has been found. Truman states in Year of Decisions, page 402, that at this meeting the British participants were informed of the suggestion made by Molotov earlier in the day (see ante, page 476) concerning a formal request to the Soviet Government to enter the war against Japan.
