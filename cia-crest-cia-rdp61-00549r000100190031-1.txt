The Scientific Estimates Committee (SEC) was established on 14 August
1952 by Director of Central Intelligence Directive (DCID) 3/4, pursuant to
the provisions of National Security Council Intelligence Directive (NSCID
No, 3 and the recommendation of the IAC AjJ Hoc Committee to Survey Existing
