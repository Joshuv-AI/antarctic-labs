On a counterexample to a conjecture of J. Harris for octic surfaces


Abstract
We take a sum C 1 + r ​ C 2 , r ∈ ℚ C_{1}+rC_{2},\ r\in\mathbb{Q} of a line C 1 C_{1} and a complete intersection curve C 2 C_{2} of type ( 3 , 3 ) (3,3) inside the octic Fermat surface and with no intersection points. We gather strong evidences to the fact that for all except a finite number of r r , the Noether-Lefschetz loci attached to the cohomology classes of C 1 + r ​ C 2 C_{1}+rC_{2} are set theoretically distinct 31 31 codimensional subvarieties intersecting each other in a 32 32 codimensional subvariety of the ambient space. The maximum codimension for components of the Noether-Lefschetz locus in this case is 35 35 , and hence, we provide a possible counterexample to a conjecture of J. Harris.

Introduction

In the parameter space
{\sf T}
of smooth surfaces of degree
d\geq 4
in
\mathbb{P}^{3}
the Noether-Lefschetz locus
{\rm NL}_{d}
is a union of enumerable subvarieties of
{\sf T}
and its points parameterize surfaces with Picard number
\geq 2
. A component of
{\rm NL}_{d}
of codimension equal to (resp. strictly less than)
h^{20}=\binom{d-1}{3}
is called general (resp. special). It is known that general components are
dense in
{\sf T}
in both usual and Zariski topology, see
[
CHM88
]
,
[
Voi03
, §5.3.4]
and
[
CL91
]
, and special components of codimension
d-3
and
2d-7,\ d\geq 5
are unique and parameterize respectively surfaces with a line and conic, see
[
Gre88
,
Gre89
,
Voi88
,
Voi89
]
. This implies that for
d=5
we have only two special components. J. Harris in 1980’s conjectured that the number of special components must be
finite. C. Voisin in
[
Voi91
]
found counterexamples to this for a large
d
, however, the conjecture in lower degrees remains open. In
[
Voi90
]
it is proved that for
d=6,7
the number of reduced special components
is finite, and so, it is expected that Harris’ conjecture is true in these cases. In
[
Mov21b
]
we have described a conjectural description of an infinite number of components of
{\rm NL}_{8}
of codimension
31
, whereas the general components have codimension
35
. In the present article we gather more evidences to this conjecture.

Let
C_{1}
and
C_{2}
be respectively a line (complete intersection of type
(1,1)
) and a complete intersection of type
(3,3)
inside the Fermat octic
X_{0}:x_{0}^{8}+x_{1}^{8}+x_{2}^{8}+x_{3}^{8}=0
obtained by canonical factorization of
x_{0}^{8}+x_{1}^{8}
and
x_{2}^{8}+x_{3}^{8}
. Further assume that
C_{1}
does not intersect
C_{2}
. For a rational number
r\in\mathbb{Q},\ r\not=0
we consider
the cohomology class of

\delta_{0}:=[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]\in H^{2}(X_{0},\mathbb{Q})

and its parallel transport
\delta_{t}\in H^{2}(X_{t},\mathbb{Q}),\ t\in({\sf T},0)
, that is,
\delta_{t}
is a flat section of the Gauss-Manin connection. Here,
{\sf T}
is the full parameters space of smooth octic surfaces. The local Noether-Lefschetz locus
V_{[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]}\subset{\sf T}
parameterizes those
X_{t}
such that
\delta_{t}
is still of type
(1,1)
.
It has a natural analytic space structure (ring of functions might have zero divisors and nilpotent elements).
Let
V_{C_{i}}
be the local branch of the subspace of
{\sf T}
parameterizing a surface together with a line for
i=1
and a complete intersection of type
(3,3)
for
i=2

(X_{0},C_{i})
. In the present article we gather strong evidences to the following conjecture.

Conjecture 1
.

The local Noether-Lefschetz loci
V_{[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]},\ r\in\mathbb{Q},\ r\not=0
are smooth, distinct analytic spaces of codimension
31

32
-codimensional subvariety

<35
.

The parameter space
{\sf T}
is of dimension
165
and it is hard to perform computations on this.
By
{\rm GL}(4,\mathbb{C})
action on
{\sf T}
, we can reduce the above conjecture to
149

{\sf T}
which is still big.
For a natural number

X_{t}:\ \ \mathbb{P}\{f_{t}=0\},\ \ \ f_{t}:=x_{0}^{d}+x_{1}^{d}+x_{2}^{d}+x_{3}^{d}-\sum t_{\alpha}x^{\alpha},\ \ t=(t_{\alpha})\in{\sf T},\ \

{\mathbb{P}}^{1}:\left\{\begin{array}[]{l}x_{0}-\zeta_{1}x_{1}=0,\\

0&\hbox{otherwise}\end{array}\right.

[
MV18

.
We have the residue map

\delta_{0}
in cohomology.

\check{\sf T}
of
{\sf T}
such that

runs through monomials of the form

x_{0}^{\alpha_{0}}x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}x_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}x_{3}^{\alpha_{3}},\ \ \alpha_{0}+\alpha_{1}=\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}=\frac{d}{2}.

Proposition 1
.

For a monomial

x^{\beta}=x_{0}^{\beta_{0}}x_{1}^{\beta_{1}}x_{2}^{\beta_{2}}x_{3}^{\beta_{3}},\ \beta_{0}+\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}+\beta_{3}=kd-4,\ \ \beta_{0}+\beta_{1}\not=k\frac{d}{2}-2

(7)

{{\int}}_{\delta_{t}}\omega_{\beta}=0,\ \ t\in\check{\sf T}.

Proof.

Let

be the coefficient of the monomial

3

\frac{\partial}{\partial t_{\alpha}}\int_{\delta_{t}}\omega_{\beta}=k\int_{\delta_{t}}\omega_{\beta+\alpha}.

Therefore, we only need to prove the proposition for

Definition 1

We say that
V_{\delta_{0}}^{{\check{\sf T}}}
(resp.
V_{\delta_{0}}^{{\sf T}}

{\rm codim}({{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{0}}^{{\check{\sf T}}})=\hat{h}^{20}
(resp.

In this article we treat a cycle
\delta_{0}
such that

is not very general, however,

is general. For this reason it is useful to know the following proposition.

Proposition 2
.

Let
\delta_{1},\delta_{2}\in H_{2}(X_{0},\mathbb{Q})
be two cycles with non-zero primitive part.
If the locus
V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{{\check{\sf T}}}
is very general for at least one

and
{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}}^{\check{\sf T}}\not\subset{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}

then the underlying analytic varieties of

V_{[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]}^{\sf T},\ r\in\mathbb{Q}
have infinite number of irreducible components
V_{i},i\in\mathbb{N}
with no inclusion between them.

Proof.

For a pencil of local Noether-Lefschetz loci
V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{{\check{\sf T}}}
, since
the function
r\mapsto{\rm codim}({{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}})
is lower semi-continuous, we know that if
for one
r
it reaches its maximum
\hat{h}^{20}
, then
for all except a finite number of
r
we have
{\rm codim}({{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}})=\hat{h}^{20}
, and hence,

V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}
is smooth, and in particular it is reduced.
Moreover, for two rational such numbers
r_{1},r_{2}
with
r_{1}\not=r_{2}
we have

{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r_{1}\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}\cap{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r_{2}\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}={{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}}^{\check{\sf T}}\cap{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}

and the codimension of this vector space is bigger than
\hat{h}^{20}
. This follows from our hypothesis

{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}}^{\check{\sf T}}\not\subset{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}
and
{\rm codim}{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}}^{\check{\sf T}}=\hat{h}^{20}
.
Therefore, there cannot be inclusion between
{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}
for all
r
except a finite number.
This implies that the vector spaces
{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}+r\delta_{2}}^{\check{\sf T}}
form a pencil with the axis
{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{1}}\cap{{\bf T}}_{0}V_{\delta_{2}}
. We have proved that intersecting the underlying analytic varieties of
V_{\delta_{1}+r_{1}\delta_{2}}^{\sf T}
with
{\check{\sf T}}
, we get infinite number of analytic
varieties with no inclusion between them. This implies the desired statement.

Usage of LLM’s

Recently LLM’s have become powerful tools which have learned almost all algorithms and reasoning available in the web. They also learn very fast content of new mathematics, as far as such a new mathematics relies on classical concept such as Taylor series. For this reason May 16, 2026, I asked the following question from an LLM.
What is the largest
N
such that you can prove
[
Mov21b
, Theorem 2]
for
N

N=5

N\geq 6

[
Mov21b
]
well, and in particular, it found out that the
32
-dimensional parameter space
[
Mov21b
, (9) ]
contains the
10
-dimensional parameter space in (
2
).
This parameter space is the intersection of the set of monomials in
[
Mov21b
, (9)]
and those in (
3
). For this parameter space, let us call it
\check{\sf S}
, we can verify easily that
{\rm codim}({{\bf T}}_{0}V^{\check{\sf S}}_{[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]})=9
, and hence,
V^{\check{\sf S}}_{[C_{1}]+r[C_{2}]}
is a general pencil of curves. Therefore, the answer
the above question is
N=\infty
.
This was the beginning of my excitement with LLM’s, however, the rest of my questions from the LLM only produced hallucination both for LLM and myself. Therefore, the author stopped asking questions from LLM’s and started to write this article and the computer code in the next section without any external assistance.
