No. 241 The Secretary of State to the Acting Secretary of State

[On Board the U. S. S. “ Augusta ”,] 12 July 1945.

MR–in–35. Reference your MR-out-99 (State Department No. 16, 11 July).[1] I concur with your recommendation and approve your suggested approach to the British Government.

Source: Truman Papers: Telegram
