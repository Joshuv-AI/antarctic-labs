MEMORANDUM FOR: Legislative Counsel

SUBJECT: H.R. 2639, To amend the Classification Act of

1949, as amended, so as to authorize longevity
step-increases for offifeers and employees in
grades above grade 15 of the General Schedule

1. In accordance vdth your request, we have reviewed the
bill described above as a basis for coranent as to whether it is of
interest to Agency activities and whether further action by your
office is necessary or desirable* The bill proposes an amendment
to section 703 of the Classification Act of 1949, from which this
Agency is specifically exempted.

2. We do not believe further action by your office is re-
quired. However, if this proposal should be enacted, we would
recommend adoption of the amended provision by this Agency to en-
sure that our employees receive at least comparable benefits to
those enjqyed by employees in the classified service.

6 Schedule shall be entitled to a longevity step-increase) , is

7 hereby repealed.

9 as amended, is hereby amended to read :
