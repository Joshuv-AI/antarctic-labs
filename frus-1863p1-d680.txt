Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 777.]

Department of State, Washington, December 5, 1863.

Sir: Your very interesting despatch of the 19th of November (No. 537) has been received, together with the pamphlet on the foreign enlistment act of Great Britain by Mr. Gibbs, which is highly appreciated.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c, &c, &c.
