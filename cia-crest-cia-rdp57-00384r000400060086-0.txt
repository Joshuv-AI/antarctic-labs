10. Parachute accident. — a. Definition. — For the purposes of these regulations,
a parachute accident is defined as an accident occurring during the regular tours
of duty of a qualified parachutist (see par. 2) when the parachutist is injured
while an occupant of an aircraft or as the result of jumping from, being thrown
from, or being struck by an aircraft or any part or auxiliary thereof.

6. Nonrequirement of parachute duty. — Any officer or enlisted person who
regularly performs parachute duty and who becomes incapacitated for such duty
by reason of a parachute accident as defined in a above, shall not be required to
perform such duty for a period not to exceed 3 months following such accident.

11. (Added) Members of the Organized Reserve Corps. — a. Active duty . —
Members of the Organized Reserve Corps who are not already qualified as para-
chutists may qualify as such by completing the basic airborne course at the In-
fantry School or in the course of a tour of active-duty training of not less than
60 days with a Regular Array airborne unit and subject to the approval of the
commanding officer of that unit. Under the provisions of paragraph 7, personnel
who are undergoing parachute training or who are on parachute duty will be
required to perform one or more parachute jumps from an airplane in flight during
any three consecutive calendar months In order to be eligible to receive parachut-
ist’s pay.

6. Inactive duty training . — Members of the Organized Reserve Corps who are
qualified parachutists and who are on an Inactive duty training status shall be
required to perform only one parachute jump from an airplane In flight In 1 year
In order to be eligible to receive parachutist’s pay. A jump In any calendar month
shall qualify the parachutist to receive parachutist’s pay for that month and for
the 11 succeeding months, provided that he qualifies for Inactive duty training
pay In accordance with current regulations as they apply to his unit. When It Is
impracticable for the reservist to perform one parachute jump from an airplane
in flight during such year, a jump In any calendar month, not more than 2 months
prior to nor more than 2 months subsequent to the anniversary date of such year,
may be used as his date of qualification for the purpose of maintaining continuity
of parachutist’s pay.
