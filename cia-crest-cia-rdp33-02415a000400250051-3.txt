5. Authorities of OSA/DDS&T Contracting Officer:

a. The Contracting Officer, OSA/DDS&T in consultation
with other appropriate members of the OSA/DDS&T, and under
the general supervision of the DDS&T, has authority to sign all
contracting documents which obligate Agency funds, regardless
of amount, provided:

(1) That each such contract is authorized in a duly
approved Activity Program or budget, and

(2) That such contract, contract amendment, or other
procurement document does not raise any significant question
of procurement policy. If such contract or other document
does, in the judgment of the Contracting Officer, raise a
policy question, it shall be submitted, together with a brief
written statement of points on which policy guidance is re-
quired, to the DDS&T for approval, or for reference by him
to the DCI or DDCI for approval.
