No. 1166 The Assistant Secretary of State (Dunn) to the Secretary of the Soviet Delegation (Novikov)

Berlin, July 27, 1945.

Dear Mr. Novikov: I refer to your note of July 23, 1945[1] with which you enclosed an aide-mémoire in regard to the British camp for Soviet citizens in the region of Cesenatico (Italy).

Since this is entirely a British matter I assume that you will approach the British separately and that your aide-mémoire was transmitted for information purposes only.

Sincerely yours,

James Clement Dunn
