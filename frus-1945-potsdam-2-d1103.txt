No. 1103 United States Delegation Working Paper[1]

[Babelsberg,] July 25 [1945.]

Reparations From Italy

Greece, Yugoslavia, Ethiopia and any other United Nation which claims to be entitled to reparations from Italy shall file a statement of the nature and amount of such claim with the Allied Commission for Italy prior to November 1, 1945.

The Governments of the Soviet Union, the United Kingdom and the United States will consider such claims, in consultation with the other governments concerned, for the purpose of determining the extent to which and the manner in which Italy shall be required to make compensation for the losses suffered by other countries as a result of Italy’s participation with Germany in the war.

Source: Pauley Files

Notes
