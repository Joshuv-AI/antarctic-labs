 Motivations for option products
ISO New England introduced option products in day ahead to address three root causes of
market design shortcomings:*
 • “Root Cause 1: Uncertainty over whether the generating unit will be in demand, or
 not.”
 • “Root Cause 2: Irrevocable (i.e., upfront) costs of making arrangements for fuel, which
 must be incurred in advance of learning whether the generator will be in demand
 (asked to operate) or not.”
 • “Root Cause 3: Materiality of energy supply arrangements, in the precise sense that if
 the generator does make arrangements for fuel in advance, then with some probability
 (i.e., when the generating unit is in demand), the real-time price for energy will be
 lower, or reliability will be better, than if it does not.”

* ISO New England white paper: Energy Security Improvements: Creating Energy Options for New England. April 2020. Available online: https://www.iso-
ne.com/static-assets/documents/2020/04/esi-white-paper-final-with-cover-page-04152020.pdf.

Energy option design

A call option is characterized by three components:
  • Option premium 𝑉. The clearing price paid for the option, intended to
 hedge against uncertainty up to, for example, the 95th percentile.

  • Strike price 𝐾. Defined as the forecasted real-time price at the 95th
 percentile uncertainty level, assuming the option is physically available.

  • Real-time settlement. The option pay back in real time is
 Max{0, RTLMP−K}, where RTLMP is real-time locational marginal price.

 enhanced based on larger historical dataset).

 distribution over the 7-day horizon in the absence of the option. With better data, we will solve

 Summary

• Initial study of SPP cases shows with proper design, multi-day and intra-
  day option product may provide benefits of reducing energy and reserve
  shortages as well as total cost.
• Resources with inflexibility may introduce complexity. Further studies include:
 ◦ Market rules on resources cleared for option but not committed at their last
 commitment period
 ◦ Option requirement and strike price settings
 ◦ IDRUC look-ahead configuration.
