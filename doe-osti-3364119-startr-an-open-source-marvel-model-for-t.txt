 Introduction Geometry
The National Reactor Innovation Center (NRIC) seeks to improve the
understanding of microreactor physics in industry and academia
through the development of a Microreactor Applications Research
Validation and Evaluation (MARVEL) reactor-based model, published
on the Virtual Test Bed (VTB) database. This poster summarizes the
development and initial reactor physics analysis of the Sodium-cooled
Thermal-spectrum Advanced Research Test Reactor (STARTR) in
OpenMC.

 Flux Spectrum
 STARTR utilizes uranium-zirconium
 hydride (UZrH) fuel meat, which has a
 negative temperature reactivity coefficient
 and acts as a moderator. As the fuel meat
 heats up, the neutron spectrum hardens
 which can be seen in the plot comparing
 the cold zero power (CZP) and hot full
 power (HFP) reactor conditions. In isolated
 single effects material analysis, heating the
 beryllium reflectors resulted in greater
 hardening compared to only UZrH.

 Axial PPF:
 The axial PPF was determined by
 tallying fissions within ten equal-
 length sections that span the fuel CZP
 meat, then dividing that tally
 value by the fissions for each
 section, assuming all sections
 contribute equally to the reactor
 power. The resultant plot exhibits
 the expected shape, given that the
 center of a fuel rod experiences
 the highest flux, it also generates
 the most fissions and thus has a
 higher PPF.
