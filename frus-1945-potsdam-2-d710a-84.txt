The Secretary of State to the Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) (23 July 1945, 84)

secret

Babelsberg, July 23, 1945.

Victory 241. To AmEmbassy London for Winant. Info [Acting] SecState Washington.

(Message has been sent to MA, London.)

Gousev informed Mosely tonight instructions sent Saksin last night or this morning to sign EAC agreement on additional requirements.[1]

Byrnes
