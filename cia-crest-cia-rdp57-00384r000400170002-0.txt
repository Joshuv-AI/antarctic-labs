military policies affecting national security. Members :

President (Chairman), Vice-President, Secretary of State,
Secretary of Defense, Chairman NSRB. Executive Secretary :
James S. Lay.

President). Overall military andeconomic planning. Advises
President on coordination of military, industrial and civilian
mobilization. Members: Chairman (Stuart Symington),

Secretaries of State, Treasury, Defense, Interior, Agriculture,
Commerce, and Labor.

Council of Economic A dvisers (in the Executive Office of the
