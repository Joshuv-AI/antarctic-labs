Nature of Teleph one Recordlng._Deyices

Devices to record telephone conversations have been the subject of
experimentation for over thirty years, directed toward the . achievement
of more efficiency and accuracy in recording than was possible through
written recordings by the parties to' the conversation, or a stenographer
listening in for the purpose. The workability of such devices for
commercial use was largely the result of the development of. the vacuum,
tuba, which made it possible. to amplify the conversation, and to equalize
its strong and weak .portions, so that a clear recording. could bo obtained.
Modern telephone recorders are electronic devices for picking up electric
signals from the telephone lino, amplifying them through vacuum- tubes,
and recording then as cylinders, discs, bolts, or wires. -The power to
operate the recorder is obtained from, the electric power or 1 light circuit
on the user’s premises. .By such devices, an entire telephone conversation

can be recorded.

In order to record a talcphoho conversation the recorder must, of
course, be usecT in some manner ■ in connection with the telephone circuit.
There are three methods for’ accomplishing this, namely, the aceustic, tho
inductive, and tho direct physical connection.
