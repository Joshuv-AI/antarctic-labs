b. Defense Department Appeal re Machine Tools and Electric Melting Furnaces.

(Ref. EDAC D-127/ 6k, u/17/ 57)

Decisions

1. The Executive Committee decided that preparation for high policy review after
an EDAC meeting will be accomplished as rapidly as possible but preparation for

that possibility should not be attempted at this time.

2. The Committee examined the possibility of preparing a compromise position be-
tween the positions of the recommendations contained in EDAC D- 127/6 and the Defense
proposal in their appeal papers. It was the judgment of the Executive Committee that
no constructive compromise without the existing disadvantage of the Defense Depart-
ment proposal can be recommended at this time.

3. The Executive Corrmittee will transmit to EDAC the report of the Chairman,

EDAC D-127/6k, on the Defense appeal, as well as EDAC D-127/6j, the list of proposed
decontrolled items.

EDAC Chairman is prepared to call a meeting based on the State Department estimate
of requirement.
