Dear Senator Kennedy:


Thank you for your note of 1 July 1978 on behalf of your constituent,
Mrs. John J. Greenler, who requested "a copy of the CIA report on drug
experiments in the 1950's."


There is no official Agency report on this subject as such, however,
under the Freedom of Information Act the Agency has released 15,259
pages of documents concerning drug/behavior control. If Mrs. Greenler
is interested in obtaining copies, she can write to Mr. Gene If. Wilson,
Information and Privacy Coordinator, Central Intelligence Agency,
Washington, D.C. 20505. A copy of our fee schedule is enclosed.


Mrs. Greenler can also obtain the following Congressional publications
which contain information on this topic by writing to the Superintendent
of Documents, United States Government Printing Office, Washington,
D.C. 20402:


Project MKULTRA, The CIA's Program of Research in


Behavioral Modification, Joint Hearing Before the Select
Committee on Intelligence and the Subcommittee on Health

and Scientific Research of the Committee on Human Resources,
United States Senate, 95th Congress, August 3, 1977,

Stock No. 52-070-04357-1.


Human Drug Testing by the CIA, 1977, Hearings Before the
Subcommittee on Health and Scientific Research of the


Committee on Human Resources, United States Senate,
95th Congress, September 20 and 21, 1977.


Foreign and Military Intelligence, Book I, Final Report of


the Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations
with Respect to Intelligence Activities, United States Senate,
94th Congress, April 26, 1976, Report No. 94-755.
