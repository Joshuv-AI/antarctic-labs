Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (26 October 1943, d58)

secret

London, 26 October 1943.

Former Naval Person to President personal and most secret number 475 your number 396.[2]

1. I shall be delighted to meet you anywhere in North Africa, but I hope we need not wait till the twentieth. Strongly recommend sea voyage to Anfa which we know, moving on to Pyramids if and when convenient. By all means ask Chiang. If UJ refuses, then Molotov with a Russian military mission would do. It seems to me fine progress has been made by Hull and Eden at Moscow and still more on the Russian southern front.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Prime

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Apparently sent via military channels.
[2] Ante, p. 39.
