Publisher Correction: Sibling cooperation in boreal owls during the post-fledging dependence period
Authors: Marek Kouba, Simona Stehlíková Sovadinová, Filip Tulis, Michal Ševčík, Jitka Bartošová, Erkki Korpimäki, Luděk Bartoš
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Biology 24, 158 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-026-02626-3

In this article, the words “Ural owls]” were incorrectly removed from the third paragraph of the ‘Study areas’ section, due to a typesetting mistake.

The sentence “Predator communities are also very different, composed mainly of birds of prey [primarily northern goshawks (Accipiter gentilis) and in Finland but of pine martens in Czechia [43].” was therefore corrected to “Predator communities are also very different, composed mainly of birds of prey [primarily northern goshawks (Accipiter gentilis) and Ural owls] in Finland but of pine martens in Czechia [43].”

The original article has been corrected. The publisher apologizes to the authors and readers for the inconvenience caused by this error.
