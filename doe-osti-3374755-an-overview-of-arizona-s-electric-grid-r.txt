 An Overview of Arizona’s Grid Resilience Challenges
In 2020, the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE),
gave Arizona’s critical infrastructure a grade of C,
across all infrastructure types (i.e. energy,
transportation, water systems, etc.), highlighting a need
for statewide coordination across counties, agencies,
and jurisdictions.

While outage data are recorded across Arizona’s largest
population centers, little is known about customers in
the most rural and remote counties. TASTI-GRID’s
findings reinforce ASCE’s recommendation for
collaboration across the state – particularly given the
large areas of public and Tribal lands.

ASCE’s recommendation for community-based
infrastructure strategizing further highlights the need for
electric grid investment planning to consider local
needs, manage load growth, and maintain the quality of
life and values of populations across Arizona.
