No. 791 The Information Officer in the Office of the Secretary of State (Reams) to the Assistant Secretary of State (Dunn)[1]

[Babelsberg,] July 27, 1945.

Mr. Dunn: General Hull prefers that this matter be presented to the War Department in Washington. However, if you feel that there is some special need for haste, he is prepared to take it up with General Eisenhower and the War Department from Potsdam.

R. Borden Reams
