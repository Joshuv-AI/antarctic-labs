3 . Operat i onal Summary :

a. The OXCART project has maintained a Quick Reaction
Concept (QRC) for A-12 aerial reconnaissance of Cuba
(SKYLARK) and A-12 aerial reconnaissance of Far East
targets (BLACK SHIELD) .

b. On 15 May 1937 the OXCART detachment was ordered
to deploy to Kadena Air Base, Okinawa and conduct recon-
naissance operations over North Vietnam. Deployment was
executed successfully and the first operational mission
was conducted on 31 May 1967 . Three more missions were
flown in the period through 30 June 1937 .
