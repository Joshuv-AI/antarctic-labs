Public Law 119–62: To provide for the standardization, consolidation, and publication of data relating to public outdoor recreational use of Federal waterways among Federal land and water management agencies, and for other purposes.

An Act To provide for the standardization, consolidation, and publication of data relating to public outdoor recreational use of Federal waterways among Federal land and water management agencies, and for other purposes.Dec. 26, 2025[H.R. 187]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Modernizing Access to our Public Waters Act of 2025” or the “MAPWaters Act of 2025”.

SEC. 2. — DEFINITIONS.

In this Act:

PARAGRAPH (1) — Federal fishing restriction

The term “Federal fishing restriction” means a defined area in which all or certain fishing activities are temporarily or permanently prohibited or restricted by a Federal land or water management agency.

PARAGRAPH (2) — Federal land or water management agency

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

the Bureau of Reclamation;

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

the National Park Service;

SUBPARAGRAPH (C)

the Bureau of Land Management;

SUBPARAGRAPH (D)

the United States Fish and Wildlife Service; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (E)

the Forest Service.

PARAGRAPH (3) — Federal waterway

The term “Federal waterway” means waters managed by 1 or more of the relevant Secretaries.

PARAGRAPH (4) — Federal waterway restriction

The term “Federal waterway restriction” means a restriction on the access or use of a Federal waterway applied under applicable law by 1 or more of the Secretaries.

PARAGRAPH (5) — Secretaries

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

the Secretary of the Interior.

PARAGRAPH (6) — State

The term “State” means each of the several States, the District of Columbia, and each territory of the United States.

SEC. 3. — INTERAGENCY DATA STANDARDIZATION.

Not later than 30 months after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretaries, in coordination with the Federal Geographic Data Committee established by section 753(a) of the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018 (43 U.S.C. 2802(a)), shall jointly develop and adopt interagency standards to ensure compatibility and interoperability among applicable Federal databases with respect to the 139 STAT. 1980 collection and dissemination of geospatial data relating to public outdoor recreational access of Federal waterways and Federal fishing restrictions.

SEC. 4. — DATA CONSOLIDATION AND PUBLICATION.

SUBSECTION (a) — Deadline.Public information.Web posting.Federal Waterway Restrictions

Not later than 5 years after the date of enactment of this Act, each of the Secretaries, to the maximum extent practicable, shall digitize and make publicly available online, as applicable, geographic information system data that includes, with respect to Federal waterway restrictions—

PARAGRAPH (1)

status information with respect to the conditions under which Federal waterways are open or closed to entry or watercraft, including watercraft inspection, decontamination requirements, low-elevation aircraft, or diving;

PARAGRAPH (2)

the dates on which Federal waterways are seasonally closed to entry or watercraft;

PARAGRAPH (3)

the areas of Federal waterways with restrictions on motorized propulsion, horsepower, or fuel type;

PARAGRAPH (4)

the areas of Federal waterways with anchoring restrictions, no wake zones, exclusion zones, danger areas, or vessel speed restrictions;

PARAGRAPH (5)

Federal waterway restrictions on the direction of travel, including upstream or downstream travel; and

PARAGRAPH (6)

the uses, including by watercraft, that are restricted on each area of a Federal waterway, including the permissibility of—

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

canoes and other paddlecraft;

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

rafts and driftboats;

SUBPARAGRAPH (C)

motorboats;

SUBPARAGRAPH (D)

personal watercraft;

SUBPARAGRAPH (E)

airboats;

SUBPARAGRAPH (F)

amphibious aircraft;

SUBPARAGRAPH (G)

hovercraft;

SUBPARAGRAPH (H)

oversnow vehicles and other motorized vehicles on frozen bodies of water;

SUBPARAGRAPH (I)

oceangoing ships;

SUBPARAGRAPH (J)

swimming; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (K)

other applicable recreational activities, as determined to be appropriate by the Secretaries.

SUBSECTION (b) — Federal Waterway Access and Navigation Information

PARAGRAPH (1)

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

the location of boat ramps, portages, and fishing access sites under the authority of the Federal land or water management agency; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

the identification of the dates on which the facilities and sites identified under subparagraph (A) are open or closed, as applicable; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

bathymetric information and depth charts, as feasible.

SUBSECTION (c) — Deadline.Public information.Web posting.Federal Fishing Restrictions

Not later than 5 years after the date of enactment of this Act, each of the Secretaries, to the maximum extent practicable, shall digitize and make publicly available online geographic information system data that describes, with respect to Federal fishing restrictions—

139 STAT. 1981

PARAGRAPH (1)

the location and geographic boundaries of Federal fishing restrictions on recreational and commercial fishing, including—

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

full or partial closures;

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

no-take zones; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (C)

Federal fishing restrictions within or surrounding marine protected areas;

PARAGRAPH (2)

Federal fishing restrictions on the use of specific types of equipment or bait; and

PARAGRAPH (3)

Federal requirements with respect to catch and release.

SUBSECTION (d) — Public Comment

The Secretaries shall develop a process to allow members of the public to submit questions or comments regarding the information described in subsections (a) and (b).

SUBSECTION (e) — Updates

The Secretaries, to the maximum extent practicable, shall update—

PARAGRAPH (1)

Time period.the data described in subsections (a) and (b) not less frequently than 2 times per year; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

the data described in subsection (c) in real time as changes go into effect.

SUBSECTION (f) — Exclusion

This section shall not apply to irrigation canals and flowage easements.

SUBSECTION (g) — Disclosure

Any geographic information system data made publicly available under this section shall not disclose information regarding the nature, location, character, or ownership of historic, paleontological, or archaeological resources, consistent with applicable law.

The Secretaries may work with the Director of the United States Geological Survey to collect, aggregate, digitize, standardize, and publish data on behalf of the Secretaries to meet the requirements of this Act.

SEC. 7. — EFFECT.

Nothing in this Act—

PARAGRAPH (1)

modifies or alters the definition of the term “navigable waters” under Federal law;

PARAGRAPH (2)

affects the jurisdiction or authority of State or Federal agencies to regulate navigable waters;

PARAGRAPH (3)

modifies or alters the authority or jurisdiction of Federal or State agencies to manage fisheries; or

PARAGRAPH (4)

authorizes or is intended to result in a change in the accessibility of waters open to hunting, fishing, or other forms of outdoor recreation as of the date of the enactment of this Act.

Approved December 26, 2025.
