Major Goals and Objectives
As part of our multi-phased sustainability initiative, Goucher College is implementing a series of
necessary changes to become a model “green” campus. This comprehensive approach includes
implementing systems designed to reduce energy consumption, water usage, and waste
production by 2030. The first phase of this holistic endeavor is to develop two independent solar
projects. One will be on the Facility Management Services building (FMS) and the other will be
on the Central Utility Plan (CUP). The anticipated duration of this project is expected to be 24
months. When completed, the new photovoltaic panels (PVPs) and resulting solar array
system will help offset campus energy consumption. The projects will also help the
College achieve its goal of working towards operating primarily on renewable
energy. During initial review of the project, it was revealed replacing the roofs prior to installing
new PVPs would not only provide a sound structural foundation for the projects, but the new
roofing systems will also aid de-carbonization.

The project’s scope is divided between two buildings. Building A: FMS is the first building and
Building B: CUP is the second building.

First, we will replace the roof on Building A: FMS. This work is anticipated to have a duration of
approximately six months and will be followed by the installation of a solar array system, which
we anticipate will take an additional six months to complete. The individual tasks related to
both installations may have overlapping durations for some activities but will consist of the
following: design and permitting activities, demolition and installation of new roofing,
installation of PVPs, and activation of the solar array system.

We will capture energy consumption data for each building at the completion of each of the
above tasks. We will then compare that data to baseline information to understand the energy
offset of each task. Once we are able to capture and confirm the energy consumption data and
ensure that proper function of the solar array on building A, we will begin work on Building B.

Building B: CUP is expected to follow the same tasks and order as Building A. Information
gathered from the work on Building A will be used to inform any needed decisions or
modifications to the tasks, components, or processes associated with successfully completing
Building B.

The PVPs for Building A & B will be built to a nameplate capacity of 110 kW. The newly
completed PVPs will serve
as a source of electricity for both buildings. All required governmental approvals, drawings, and

Technical Achievements
Goucher has engaged the services of The Efficiency Network (TEN), to assist with development
of the solar project, and project management. TEN is in the process of hiring a solar engineer to
assist with the development of the PV systems. Concurrently, TEN is investigating potential
solar installers to include in a bid process once the project scope is defined. TEN is also engaged
in conversations with MCEC about financing the non-grant portion of the solar PV projects.

Impact:
Solar PV systems should help Goucher College towards a goal of becoming a sustainable self-
sufficient energy generator, reducing demand on local power grids.

Project Schedule Status:

Task Number and Title:
Building A: Facilities Management Services Building (FMS)

Task 1: FMS Roof Design, Permitting, Demolition and Replacement (M1-M6)
Task Summary: Occurring during task 1, the College will contract with an appropriately licensed
and insured roofing contractor who will complete all necessary roof replacement and PV panel
design work. This will include compiling all necessary information for permit
applications. Once permitted, the college will engage with the vendor to successfully demolish,
remove, and rebuild the roof using a TPO roofing system.

Subtask 1.1: FMS Roof Design & Permitting (M1 to M3)
Subtask Summary: Occurring during this subtask and after the FMS roof replacement project
is initiated, the College will confirm completion of a project-specific roof replacement design has
been completed. A completed design will include an evaluation of existing conditions. The
completed design will also
include securing all permits and local authorizations required to start and complete all roof
replacement activities.

Subtask 1.2 FMS Roof Demolition (M3-M4)
Subtask Summary: Remove the existing roof system down to the steel deck and
remove all debris from the project site. Inspect the existing deck and drainage system
to identify and correct deficiencies that may interfere with the proper installation of the new
roof system.

Subtask 1.3 Install a new thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO) roof on FMS (M5-M6)
Subtask Summary: The college will contract with an appropriate licensed and insured roofing
contractor to install a new TPO roof using the previously developed design documents.

Milestone 1.1.1: The project team has successfully designed, permitted, and completed all work
associated with Task #1. A new roof has now been installed on the facilities building (FMS
building). All project data will be recorded and stored for use as needed. Inspections and any
