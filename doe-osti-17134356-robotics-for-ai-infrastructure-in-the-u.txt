 Overview

• Case Study: NLR led case study involving site visits to hyperscale datacenters
  totaling 13GW in collaboration with industry partners including Mortenson, Google,
  Meta, and NVIDIA. Documentation of construction tasks, identification of tasks
  amenable for robotics and calculating productivity benefits using simulations.

• Pilot Demo: NLR led pilot demo evaluating feasibility and failures in simulation-to-
  real deployment of robotic solutions for one task in datacenter construction process –
  robotic assembly of datacenter server rack

 Summary + Next Steps

• Visited datacenters and decomposed construction/commissioning into 64 tasks and developed simulations of
 potential robotic solutions.

• Identified 21 tasks where robotics could deliver meaningful productivity benefits using simulations and
 productivity calculations.

• Grouped 21 tasks into 7 buckets of automation based on similarities in task and robotic equipment necessary.

• Estimated productivity for 7 automation buckets, amounting to approximately 3-10x productivity improvement
 with robotics.

• Demonstrated feasibility for one of those 21 tasks: robotic server-rack assembly and installation and evaluated
 failure points in real-world handling of rigid/non-rigid components and identified future mitigation strategies.

• Evaluate 20 more steps through more field demonstrations to identify sim-2-real deployment gaps and refine it
 for commercial industry deployment.
