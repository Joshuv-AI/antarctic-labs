No. 37 President Truman to Prime Minister Churchill [1]

[Extract][2]

top secret

[Washington,] 28 May 1945.

48. …

. . . . . . .

Stalin has informed me through Mr. Hopkins that he would like to have our three party meeting in the Berlin area and I will reply that I have no objection to the Berlin area.

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] For the first two paragraphs of this message, omitted here, see document No. 142.
