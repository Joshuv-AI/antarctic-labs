No. 718 The Ambassador in China (Hurley) to the Secretary of State[1]

top secret urgent

Chungking, 31 July 1945.

TopSec and Urgent Personal for Byrnes, Secretary of State, Potsdam, Germany Cfb 2223 from Hurley signed ComGenChina.

Received your unnumbered message July 30,[2] 1040 hours,[3] Chungking time, July 31.

Proceeding immediately on instructions contained in your Victory number 375.[4]

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the Commanding General, United States Forces, China, via Army channels.
[2] Document No. 717.
[3] i. e., 10:40 a.m.
[4] Document No. 715.
