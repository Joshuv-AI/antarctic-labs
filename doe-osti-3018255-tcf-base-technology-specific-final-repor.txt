PROJECT SCOPE AND OBJECTIVE
 The primary objective of this project is to develop a new polyethylene terephthalate (PET)
hydrolase enzyme to depolymerize relevant PET substrates for Birch Biosciences, using high-
throughput protein expression, purification, and assaying systems and machine learning-guided
enzyme design.
 As a secondary project objective, we also aim to develop a more energy-efficient ethylene glycol
(EG) recovery strategy relative to distillation.
 Scope includes:
Prime Laboratory:
National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR) (formerly NREL)
 • Updates to the process model, TEA, and LCA throughout the project
 • Robotics-based protein expression, purification, and testing experiments
 • Computational enzyme design
 • EG enrichment experiments

Industry Partner:
Birch Biosciences
 • Iterative protein engineering
 • in vitro enzyme characterization and benchmarking
 • DNA construction
 • Microfluidics
 • Optimization and validation of EG recovery and purification efforts

 BENEFIT TO DOE MISSION
 This work delivered new, innovative processes that enable realistic, cost-effective, and energy-
 efficient biological recycling of polyester plastics. This work is directly aligned with the DOE
 mission of greater supply chain resiliency and enabling new, impactful manufacturing processes
 in the United States, using waste generated domestically.
