Adopted by the Security Council at its 1161st meeting, unanimously, on 30 October 1964

The Security Council,

Having examined the application of the Republic of Zambia for admission to the United Nations,

Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Zambia be admitted to Membership in the United Nations.

1964

fr:Résolution 197 du Conseil de sécurité des Nations unies
