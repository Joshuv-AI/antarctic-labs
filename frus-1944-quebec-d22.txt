Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (8 August 1944, d22)

top secret

London, 8 August 1944.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt. Personal and top secret. Number 748.

My telegram number 746.[2]

I am sending you an amended version of my reply about Octagon on account of later information. Please therefore regard my telegram as cancelled.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to Washington by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels; forwarded by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt, who was then in Alaska, as telegram No. Red 274.
[2] Supra.
