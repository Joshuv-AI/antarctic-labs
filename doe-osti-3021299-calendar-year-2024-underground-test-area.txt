1.0 Introduction

The Underground Test Area (UGTA) Sampling Plan for Corrective Action Units (CAUs) 101 and
102: Central and Western Pahute Mesa, Nevada National Security Site (NNSS), Nevada (referred to
herein as “the Plan”) (DOE/EMNV, 2025) describes the approach for collecting and analyzing
groundwater samples to meet the objectives of the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), Environmental
Management (EM) Nevada Program’s UGTA Activity. The Plan is designed to ensure compliance
with the UGTA Quality Assurance Plan (QAP) (DOE/EMNV, 2024), and the Federal Facility
Agreement and Consent Order (FFACO) (1996, as amended).

Although not a regulatory document, the Plan states that results of activities associated with the Plan
(laboratory analyses and any investigations that take place as a result of Plan implementation) will be
presented in annual and/or special reports. The Plan’s scope comprises sample collection and analysis
requirements for assessing the extent of groundwater contamination from underground nuclear
testing. Sample location types are defined in the Plan (DOE/EMNV, 2025). The Plan location types
are defined and the objectives for each type identified in Table 1-1.

This calendar year (CY) 2024 annual sampling letter report presents Plan implementation
(Section 2.0); and a summary of field activities, sampling methods, well completion diagrams, and
analytical laboratory results from sampling of wells on Pahute Mesa (Section 3.0).

2.0 CY 2024 Sampling Results

Sampling in support of the Plan (DOE/EMNV, 2025) took place at the Pahute Mesa CAUs and on the
Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR) during CY 2024 (Figure 2-1). A summary of field
activities, sampling methods, well completion diagrams, and analytical laboratory results are
described in this section. Field water-quality parameters, purge volumes, and flow rates (if applicable)
at the time of sample collection are provided in Table 2-1. The CY 2024 results reported in this
section are maintained in the UGTA Chemistry Database (Navarro, 2025b).

Sample collection methods are based, in part, on the physical attributes and configurations of the
well. A total of 7 wells with 11 completions were sampled during CY 2024. Three wells were
sampled with a depth-discrete bailer, and four wells were sampled with an electric submersible
pump (ESP).

While it is generally recognized that pumped samples provide a better measure of depth-averaged
concentration than depth-discrete bailed samples, data suggest bailed sample values are sufficiently
representative in many cases. Hershey (2018) presented 3H data from wells with both bailed and
pumped samples that span a range of 3H values from less than 10 pCi/L to about 150,000 pCi/L.
In most cases, the 3H value from bailed and pumped samples differed by less than a factor of 2.
The largest difference was a factor of 5. Thus, for many wells, bailed sample results will provide
sufficiently accurate results to track the trends in 3H concentrations over time. To ensure the
representativeness of the bailed sample, the discrete sample depth must be carefully selected, and
subsequent samples should be sampled from the same depth to provide trend comparison.
Documentation, sampling, water-quality monitoring, chain of custody, and quality control (QC)
requirements associated with sample collection are performed in accordance with the UGTA QAP
(DOE/EMNV, 2024) and Navarro Requirements-Based Management System (RBMS) desktop
instructions (Navarro, 2025a). The UGTA QAP provides a systematic approach to evaluate analytical
data essential to sustaining data quality.

2.1 Characterization Well

2.1.1 ER-EC-13

ER-EC-13 has two completions listed in the Plan. ER-EC-13_p1 and ER-EC-13_p2 are categorized
as a characterization location per the Plan; however, it was determined that sufficient characterization
data has been collected (2010, 2012, 2013, and 2019). Figure 2-2 provides the ER-EC-13 well
completion diagram.

A sample was collected from ER-EC-13_p1 from a depth of 2,350 feet (ft) below ground surface
(bgs) using a depth-discrete bailer on September 27, 2024. Although this zone was not purged,
water-quality parameters were measured and are provided in Table 2-1. A regular field sample was
collected from ER-EC-13_p1 for low-level 3H and analyzed by ARS International, LLC (ARS).
No field duplicate (FD) samples were collected. The results for the sample are reported in Table 2-2.

A sample was collected from ER-EC-13_p2 from a depth of 2,050 ft bgs using a depth-discrete bailer
on September 27, 2024. Although this zone was not purged, water-quality parameters were measured

ARS. No FD samples were collected. The results for the sample are reported in Table 2-2.

3.0 Summary

The Plan (DOE/EMNV, 2025) describes the approach for collecting and analyzing
groundwater samples to meet the objectives of EM Nevada Program’s UGTA Activity.
Analytical data are generated in compliance with the UGTA QAP (DOE/EMNV, 2024) and the
FFACO (1996, as amended).

A total of 7 wells with 11 completions were sampled in CY 2024 from the Pahute Mesa CAUs and on
the NTTR in support of the Plan. The analytical results for these samples are presented in Section 2.0.
During CY 2024 sampling, four characterization locations, two source/plume locations, and one early
detection location were sampled using depth-discrete bailers and ESPs. Figure 3-1 shows locations
