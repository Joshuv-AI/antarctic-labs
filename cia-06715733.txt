Dear | (b)(6)

This letter is in further response to your March 6, 2006 appeal to the Interagency Security
Classification Appeals Panel (ISCAP). As we recently communicated to you, Executive Order
13526, “Classified National Security Information” (the Order), states that the mandatory
declassification review (MDR) provisions of the Order shall not apply to MDR requests
submitted to elements of the Intelligence Community by “a person other than an individual as
that term is defined by 5 U.S.C. 522a(a)(2).” According to 5 U.S.C. 522a(a)(2), “the term
‘individual’ means a citizen of the United States or an alien lawfully admitted for permanent
residence.”

In our letter of November 22, 2010, we asked that you provide proof that you meet one of these
criteria. _In response, you communicated that you are currently seeking permanent resident
status. Therefore, as Executive Secretary, I will extend the deadline previously discussed for
furnishing residency status by an additional 90 days. Please provide the ISCAP with proof of
your permanent resident status by May 18, 2011 so that the ISCAP can continue to consider your
appeal.

If you have any questions regarding this matter, please contact me at (202) 357-5250 or e-mail
iscap@nara.gov.

Sincerely,

Executive Secretary
