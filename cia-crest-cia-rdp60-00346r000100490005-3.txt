4 . "Comments on Principal Sources" - Supplements I and VI

The Committee approved the following changes in the outline
guides for Supplement I (Ports and Naval Facilities) and Supplement VI
(Communism):

Supplement I -- Delete Section 7 "Comments on Principal
Sources" and add same topic as title of a new subsection at the
end of each Section 2 through 6; Supplement VI ~ delete
Section 10 "Comments on Principal Source*" and add same
topic as title of a new subsec tion at the end of each Section 1
through 8 (refer to Item 4 NCM 238).

In a discussion of this matter, the chairman observed that
it is the intent to have the Comments tailored to the specific topic of
each section, except where the Comments require a common treatment
for the Supplement as a whole, in which event such treatment will be
repeated in each section. He also noted that, while it is necessary
to restrict the list of sources to the principal ones, the commentary
should fully meet the requirements of the NIS Standard Instructions
as supplemented by NIS Memo No. 4 of 9 May 1949.
