Overview
At Sandia National Laboratories, I developed a C++
program that converts printed circuit boards(PCB) and
integrated circuits(IC) design files to be compatible for
computed tomography (CT) simulations through
Monte Carlo methods.

 Figure 1. Visualization of a copper and substrate layer.
