Twenty-third Conference on Flavor Physics and CP Violation (FPCP
2025) at the University of Cincinnati - Final Report

The High Energy Physics (HEP) group at the University of Cincinnati (UC) hosted the ``Twenty-third
Conference on Flavor Physics and CP Violation'' (FPCP 2025), on June 2-6, 2025. The FPCP
conference is one of the highest visibility conferences in flavor physics, drawing both theory and
experimental participants from all over the world. The conference focused on the latest experimental
and theoretical results in flavor physics and CP violation, and new ideas and future projects in the field
were discussed. For the 2025 conference in Cincinnati, we had 56 regular national and international
attendants, and 40 graduate student attendats (15 of those were local students), with participation
from all leading experiments (Belle II, BABAR, BES III, LHCb, CMS, ATLAS, Fermilab (g-2), DUNE
etc.) as well as from leading theory groups working in flavor/CP phenomenology and lattice QCD.

The conference consisted of four days of plenary talks and one day of parallel talks. All plenary talks
will be held in downtown Cincinnati at the Queen City Club, while the parallel talks were held at the
Univeristy of Cincinnati campus. We had 37 invited plenary talks and 30 parallel talks. The speakers
came from China, Europe, India, Japan, the US, and Canada.

The funds provided by DoE were used to cover in part the rental cost for the conference facility, and in
particular allowed us to offer a reduced conference fee for graduate student ($80 reduced vs. $400
regular). We were very happy to have a large number of graduate student atted the conference.

In all, the conference was a big success, with high-quality presentations and summaries of current
research on CP violation and flavor physics, and countless fruitful physics discussions.
