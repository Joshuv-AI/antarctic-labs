Resolution 2774 (2025)
Adopted by the Security Council at its 9866th meeting, on 24 February 2025
The Security Council,
Mourning the tragic loss of life throughout the Russian Federation-Ukraine
conflict,
Reiterating that the principal purpose of the United Nations, as expressed in the
Charter of the United Nations, is to maintain international peace and security and to
peacefully settle disputes,
1. Implores a swift end to the conflict and further urges a lasting peace
between Ukraine and the Russian Federation
