No. 989 The Acting Secretary of State to the Assistant Secretary of State (Clayton)[1]

secret

[Washington,] July 24, 1945.

115. (For Clayton.)

For your information and with reference Department’s 56 of July 18,[2] Bucharest reports Soviets now demand German bank stocks in Rumania.

[Grew]

Source: 740.00119 Potsdam/7–1845: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the Secretary of State at Babelsberg.
[2] Document No. 984.
