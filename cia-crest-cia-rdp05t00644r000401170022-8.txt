Dear Andy,

It was great to hear from you and to get caught up. What an
exciting summer you and Anna have had. Your going down to La Rochelle
reminded me of our aborted canal trip in Southern France. We still have
that in mind for some day in the future!

I appreciate Dick Bullock and Joe Carter's enthusiasm for my talk
in Phoenix. I am unable to accept their nice invitation for the Worcester
Economic Club, however. On the one hand, I really find it difficult to
look ahead to next March, at least until the election is over! Beyond
that, March is a difficult time to get away here because the Congress
is just beginning to get into full swing. I’m up on the Hill several
times a week for half-day briefing sessions. Each one of those takes
a lot of preparation in addition. Let me say that if I see an opening
that is taking me to New England at that time of the year. I'll certainly
let you know. I am afraid the odds are not high. Incidentally, when I
was attending the Advanced Management Program at the Harvard Business
School in 1966, my roommate was an EnglishmanJ ^ who had some
kind of a connection with Wyman-Gordon. The two ot us drove out and had
an afternoon|s tour of their forging operations plus dinner with one of
their executives. I was most impressed.

We talked with the other day. He just could not get

enough freedom from the Board of Trustees at The Citadel. He decided it
was better to quit early than to keep struggling with it. You may know
he is returning, or .perhaps has returned, to Newport. He and Sybil have
an apartment right near the foot of the bridge to .lamps town (address is

They are going to

settle there until they make up their minds what comes next.

We're counting on seeing you again this year as you drive to or
from Florida. Let us know. In the meantime, all the best.

Yours,

Mr. Andrew B. Sides. Jr.
