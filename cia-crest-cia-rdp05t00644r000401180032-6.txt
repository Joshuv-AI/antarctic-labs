The Hon. James E. Johnson

Chairman

Dr. Orv Owens

National Coordinator

For the first time in our history, the citizens of the United States of America will be invited to the
Nation’s Capital to celebrate the Inauguration of our Fortieth President in a unique
‘‘PRESIDENTIAL INAUGURAL CELEBRATION.. .WITH LOVE.” We are contacting
dignitaries from around the world, inviting them to join us in this historic Celebration featuring
nationally and internationally known speakers and singers, as well as political figures.

Traditionally, the Inauguration of our President has been celebrated in the form of an Inaugural
Ball- and so it will be on January 20th, 1981. But this year something new has been added-two
exciting days and nights of celebrating the President’s Inauguration for people of all faiths and
political ideals.

This Celebration with Love is nonsectarian-nonpartisan-nonpolitical. Doctrinal and political
beliefs will not be a factor. It is a Celebration of common grounds and interests and support
from the religious communities of the world.

History is being written! This will be the first “PRESIDENTIAL INAUGURAL CELEBRA-
TION ..WITH LOVE.” A pictorial history book will be published following the Celebration,
creating a pattern for future Presidential Inaugural celebrations with love. This is an oppor-
tunity to express the common bond of love of mankind and our thanksgiving for this bounteous
Nation under God.

We have invited leaders of the religious community, government, and the private sector to serve
on the Advisory Board for the Celebration to support our Nation.

We are spreading the word across the Nation for those interested in coming to the Nation s
Capital to join in the Inaugural Parade, and to convene on Monday and Tuesday, January 19-20,
1981 at the STAR PLEX (the Armory) to begin at 1:00 p.m. Monday and 8:00 p.m. both
evenings.

It is a tradition for the elected president to visit all Balls and it is our plan for him to visit with us
during our Celebration on Tuesday evening, January 20, 1981.

We would like to personally invite you to join with 10,000 others from the religious community
who will be supporting and praying for our Nation and national leaders at this Celebration as
well as leaders of Nations from around the world.

We are enclosing your invitation, an Informational sheet concerning hotels, the program, how to
obtain tickets and order forms for the historical book and souvenir program. Please include with
your order names, addresses and security information for all additional tickets purchased.
