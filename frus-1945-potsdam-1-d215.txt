No. 215 Mr. V. N. Pavlov, of the Soviet Foreign Commissariat, to the First Secretary of Embassy in the Soviet Union (Page)

[Translation]

Moscow, July 13, 1945.

Dear Mr. Page: At the instruction of the People’s Commissar I request you to inform Mr. W. A. Harriman that V. M. Molotov has received his letter of July 12[1] regarding consideration of the reparations problem at the forthcoming meeting of the heads of the three governments.

Respectfully yours,

Pavlov

Source: Moscow Embassy Files—500 Berlin Conference

[1] For the full text of this message, see document No. 312.
