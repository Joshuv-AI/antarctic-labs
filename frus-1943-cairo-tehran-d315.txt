Marshal Stalin to President Roosevelt[1] (25 November 1943, d315)

secret urgent

Moscow, November 25, 1943.

Personal and strictly secret from Premier Stalin to President Roosevelt. Your message from Cairo[2] received. I will be at your service in Teheran the evening of November twenty-eighth.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by Spalding at Moscow to Harriman at Cairo, via Navy channels.
[2] Telegram of November 22, 1943, ante, p. 373.
