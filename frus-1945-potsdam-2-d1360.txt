No. 1360 The Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant) to the Acting Secretary of State

secret

London, July 26, 1945—5 p.m.

7529. FonOff now states French Govt will be informed immediately British concurrence in Soviet participation Tangier discussions and that French and British Govts will communicate with Moscow upon concerting their action. (Sent Dept as 7529; repeated Paris as 484; repeated Tangier as 47; repeated Madrid as 200).

Winant

Source: 881.00/7–2645: Telegram
