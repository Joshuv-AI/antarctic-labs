 CHAPTER V. JUDICIAL POWER

 Article 96
 The judicial power in Turkmenistan shall belong solely to the courts.

 The judicial power shall be aimed to guard the citizens’ rights and freedoms, state and
 public interests protected under the laws.

 courts, stipulated by law.

 The establishment of emergency courts and other entities, empowered as court, shall
 not be allowed.

 Judicial independence Article 98
 Judges shall be independent; they shall be subject only to the Constitution and law.
 Interference in the work of judges, by whichever side, shall be unacceptable and
 punishable by law.

 Article 99
 The immunity of judges shall be guaranteed by law.
