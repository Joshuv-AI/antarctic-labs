Item 1088 part (b) :

"Capable of the production of gears of a module finer than 0.5 mm."

Remarks

Gear making machines of a module finer than 0.5 ram are
machines of very small capacity for precision mechanics and watch-
making, whereas the same machines for modules larger than 0.5 mm
are machines of medium or very great capacity.

It should be noted moreover that old Item 1088 authorised the export
of bobbing machines up to 48 inches (1.22 m) maximum work diameter.

ELECTRONICS AND PRECISION INSTRUMENTS
Item 1520

Item 1520 as defined at present gives rise to divergencies
of interpretation.

In any television network, however complex, there are one or
several cameras at one extremity and one or several transmitters
at the other. Moreover, a television network constitutes a non-
strategic whole all of whose parts have been removed from the
Coordinating Committee's lists except for Hertsian links of high
power and long range, which ?/ere only excepted because of the fear
that these links would he utilised by the Eastern countries in order
to constitute a large number of base telephone channels.

The French Delegation consider that a limit of 5 watts on the
unitary power of each relay practically excludes this possibility,
as to obtain stable telephonic links having a large number of
channels and operating over long distances it is necessary to have
a much greater power.

The French Delegation reserve the right to propose to the
Committee a formula which would avoid any misunderstanding.

Item 1658(b)

Add to the present definition;

" .... This sub-item does not cover clean wire of a diameter not
exceeding 800 microns and which, after having been fully annealed,
has an elongation factor not exceeding 1 2$".

Remarks

This new wording is justified by the following considerations;

(1) The electronic uses of molybdenum wires require an
elongation factor of about 20$;

(2) The higher diameter limit of 800 microns may be admitted
owing to the almost insurmountable difficulties in
cleaning a wire whose diameter does not exceed that
figure, with a view to later re-drawing;

(3) A wire which has undergone the necessary operations to
give it the characteristics indicated is by that very
fact rendered unfit for electronic uses, as no further
treatment can improve its qualities (elongation factor).
