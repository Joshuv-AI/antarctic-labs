The annual Oxford-Cambridge Dinner in Washington

will be held Wednesday, April 2, 1980

at the International Club, 18th and K Streets, N.W., Washington, D.C,
(entrance to the building and interior parking on 18th Street) .

Cocktails at 7 p.m. (cash bar) in the Tavern and Club Rooms on the second floor.

Dinner at 8 p.m. in the Wadsworth Room

Dress: black tie, college blazer, evening kilt, or equivalent.

The Hon. Potter Stewart (Trinity, Cambridge), U.S. Supreme Court, will preside.

The Rev. Harold T. Lewis (St. John's, Cambridge), Rector, St. Monica's Episcopal
Church, will say The Grace.

H.E. Mr. Zain Azraai (Exeter, Oxford), Ambassador of Malaysia, will give the Toast
to the President.

The Hon. George McGhee (Queen's, Oxford), former Undersecretary of State and
Ambassador to Germany, will give the Toast to the Queen.

H.E. Sir Nicholas Henderson (Hertford, Oxford), Her Majesty's Ambassador to the
United States, will give the Toast to the Universities.

Dr. A.J.P. Kenny, The Master of Balliol College, Oxford, will give the Response for
the Universities.

Mr. Robin Fletcher (Trinity, Oxford), Warden-elect of Rhodes House, will also attend.

The price of the dinner will be $23, wine with dinner and port afterward included.

Attendance is open only to those who have attended Oxford or Cambridge. There are
new arrivals in the greater Washington area who might be interested, and we would
be grateful for your additions to our mailing list. Extra copies of this notice
are available for bulletin board use.
