Convolution algebras associated to representations
Drago s , Cri s , an

Abstract
Given a complex reductive group G G , a representation V V of G G and a Borel-stable subspace M ⊆ V M\subseteq V , we consider the associated Steinberg-type variety Z Z . We prove that, under a certain condition on ( V , M ) (V,M) , called gluability, the equivariant Borel-Moore homology or K K -theory of Z Z , equipped with the convolution product, is obtained as the intersection of two copies of the nil-Hecke algebra inside its localization. We also provide a description of these new algebras in terms of poles and residues. Similar results are obtained when G G is replaced by its loop group. This generalizes results of Ginzburg, Kapranov and Vasserot describing the affine Hecke algebra and DAHA, as well as a result of Teleman and Gannon–Webster that realizes certain Coulomb branches by gluing two copies of the universal centralizer.

Let
G

Let

)

If the pair

[

When

The current paper is organized as follows. In Section

\textbf{H}^{\text{sph}}_{M}\coloneqq\textbf{H}^{\text{sph}}\cap\Pi_{M}^{-1}\textbf{H}^{\text{sph}}\Pi_{M}\subseteq eQ[\mathbb{W}]e
, where

e\coloneqq\frac{1}{|W|}\sum_{w\in W}[w]\in Q[\mathbb{W}]

Gluability for the pair
(V,M)
is equivalent to the condition that
\frac{\Pi_{M}}{\Pi_{M\cap e^{\lambda}M}}
and
\frac{\Pi_{e^{\lambda}M}}{\Pi_{M\cap e^{\lambda}M}}
are coprime in
S
, for all
e^{\lambda}\in X_{*}(T)\subseteq\mathbb{W}
.

Corollary 4.5
.

If the pair
(V,M)
is gluable, then there exists an isomorphism of algebras
H_{\bullet}^{\mathbb{P}}(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbb{P}})\cong\textbf{H}^{\text{sph}}_{M}
.

Proof.

Following the proof of Corollary 6.3 in
[
11
]
, we obtain an isomorphism of algebras
H_{\bullet}^{\mathbb{P}}(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbb{P}})\cong eH_{\bullet}^{\mathbb{B}}(\mathcal{R})e
. Using Theorem
4.3
and Lemma
2.10
, we obtain
H_{\bullet}^{\mathbb{P}}(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbb{P}})\cong\textbf{H}^{\text{sph}}_{M}
.
∎

In particular, given a finite dimensional
G
-representation
N
, one can take
V=N(\mathcal{K})
and
M=N(\mathcal{O})
. Then
H_{\bullet}^{\mathbb{P}}(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbb{P}})
is the quantized Coulomb branch corresponding to
(G,N)
, and Corollary
4.5
recovers a quantized version of Theorem 2.8 in
[
8
]
. A version of this result was originally proved in
[
21
, Theorem 1]
. Also, this corollary provides another description of the algebra described in
[
11
, Corollary 6.3]
.

Flavor symmetry

It is possible to extend our results in the case of flavor symmetry, as follows. Let
G\subseteq\widetilde{G}
be finite dimensional reductive groups, such that
G
is normal in
\widetilde{G}
. Choose maximal tori
T\subseteq\widetilde{T}
. Let
\widetilde{\mathbb{T}}\coloneqq\widetilde{T}\times\mathbb{C}^{*}_{\text{rot}}
,
\widetilde{\mathbb{h}}\coloneqq\text{Lie}(\widetilde{\mathbb{T}})
,
S=\mathbb{C}[\widetilde{\mathbb{h}}]
,
Q=\text{Frac}(S)
and
\mathbb{W}
be the extended affine Weyl group of
G
. As before, we define the (extended) nil-Hecke algebra
H
as the subalgebra of
Q[\mathbb{W}]
generated by
S
and the Demazure operators corresponding to elements of
\mathbb{W}
. For a collection
\Sigma\subseteq X^{*}(\widetilde{\mathbb{T}})
satisfying assumption (
A
), we define
\Pi_{\Sigma}
and
\textbf{H}_{\Sigma}\subseteq Q[\mathbb{W}]
as in section
2
. The results of section
2
hold with the same proofs.

Let
\mathbb{B}\subseteq\widetilde{\mathbb{B}}
be Iwahori subgroups of
G(\mathcal{O})\rtimes\mathbb{C}^{*}_{\text{rot}}\subseteq\widetilde{G}(\mathcal{O})\rtimes\mathbb{C}^{*}_{\text{rot}}
. Assume that the action of
G(\mathcal{K})
on
V
extends to an action of
\widetilde{G}(\mathcal{K})
, and that
M
is stable under the action of
\widetilde{\mathbb{B}}
. Define the variety of triples
\mathcal{R}
as before. As in
[
4
, 3(viii)]
, the action of
\mathbb{B}
on
\mathcal{R}
extends to an action of
\widetilde{\mathbb{B}}
. We define
H_{\bullet}^{\widetilde{\mathbb{B}}}(\mathcal{R})
and equip it with an algebra structure using the same constructions as for
H_{\bullet}^{{\mathbb{B}}}(\mathcal{R})
. In this context, Definition
4.1
is the same, but
S=\mathbb{C}[\widetilde{\mathbb{h}}]
, and
\Pi_{M}
is a (possibly infinite) product of characters of

. Then, imitating the proof of Theorem
4.3
, it follows that if the pair
(V,M)
is gluable, then there exists an isomorphism
H_{\bullet}^{\widetilde{\mathbb{B}}}(\mathcal{R})\cong\textbf{H}_{M}
, intertwining the two actions on
S
.

Using Theorem 4.2 in
[
3
]
, it is likely that Theorem
4.3
, and thus the results in Section
2
, apply to cyclotomic double affine Hecke algebras, recovering Theorem 2.10 and Remark 3.12 in
[
3
]
.
