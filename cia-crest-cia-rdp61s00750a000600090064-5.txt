1. Assumption

That a basic CIA policy decision will be made to the effect that
all military reservists will be called to active duty on M-day and will
report to the DCI for duty.

2. Discussion

a. It appears that there are possible alternatives for the
utilisation of military reservists called to active duty and assigned
to CIA:

(1) to be assigned to headquarters DD/X units

(2) to be assigned to BD/P (overseas and/or headquarters)

(3) to be released to their respective arm and service.

b. In order to properly staff a headquarters DD/l complement

of military personnel it will be necessary to establish which positions
at the DD/l area would best be filled by military types. The responsi-
bility fear this determination must be placed on the respective Assistant
Directors.

c. Before release of any military reservists called to active
duty to the DD/p or the military establishment it will be necessary to
provide alternates (civilians) to the presently assigned positions so
released.
