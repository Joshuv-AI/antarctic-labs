Oxygen deficiency and valency reconstruction in multiferroic V-doped HfO 2
Vincenzo Fiorentini

Abstract
The interplay of oxygen deficiency and vanadium multiple valency in the candidate multiferroic V-doped P ​ c ​ a ​ 2 1 Pca2_{1} hafnia HfO 2 is studied by first-principles calculations. Low-lying V majority gap states accept electrons from oxygen-vacancy donors, reducing their formation energy, and converting nominal V 4+ centers into V 3+ . The resulting local magnetization and screening changes are reflected in the calculated V core-level shifts, which are consistent with the experimentally observed XPS signatures. The calculated V 3+ /V 4+ population ratio determined by oxygen vacancies only matches experiment in reducing conditions, suggesting that additional electron reservoirs may contribute under ALD growth conditions. A similar scenario also seems to apply to the recently observed multiferroicity in Cr-doped hafnia, where oxygen deficiency is intrinsic to the growth technique.
