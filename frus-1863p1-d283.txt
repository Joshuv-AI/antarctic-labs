Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 696.]

Department of State, Washington,, September 5, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 21st of August (No. 479) has been received. You have proceeded very properly in communicating to Mr. Dudley Earl Russell’s note, correcting what is represented as an erroneous statement of the former in regard to Mr. Welsman, of the firm of Fraser, Trenholm & Company. Earl Russell’s note will be placed on the files of this department.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
