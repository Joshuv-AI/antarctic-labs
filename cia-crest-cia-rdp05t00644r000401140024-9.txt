SUBJECT: All Federal - Agency Conference on the Equal

Employment Opportunity Coordination Function -
Executive Order 12067

The President's Reorganization Plan No. 1, passed over-
whelmingly by the Congress in the Spring of 1978, and
j E.O. 12067 give the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission

responsibility for eliminating duplication and maximizing
effort in Federal equal employment opportunity programs
and activities. The first year of this important work,
which must be done in consultation with affected agencies,
is described in the First Annual Report to the President ,
recently received by your agency. As the Report shows,
during the first year the Commission consulted on over 60
issuances, initiated a survey of all Federal equal employ-
ment programs and engaged in many other activities required
by the Order.

As a part of our effort to review the first year's Federal
equal employment coordination experience with other
agencies, and to discuss new initiatives for the next year,
3 ?ou are invited to a half-day Federal Agency Conference on
Equal Employment Coordination Function on Wednesday ,

January 16 , 1980 at the Department of Commerce Auditorium ,
14th Street and Constitution Ave . , N.W. Washington, D.C.

The Director, Office of Management and Budget, James McIntyre
will give the Conference keynote address. The agenda is
enclosed .

The goals of E.O. 12067 require sharing, collaboration and
coordination on a scale unprecedented in the Federal govern-
ment. We are, therefore, most interested in your comments
and suggestions as we seek to shape a vigorous, efficient
and consistent Federal equal employment opportunity effort.
