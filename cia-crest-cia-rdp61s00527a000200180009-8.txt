Proposal to Revise the Methodology for the Computation of Railroad Capability

13* Information : Mr. Staats stated that he felt that the transportation,
intelligence community had now had sufficient experience in using the
methodology prepared by a working group of the Subcommittee on the computation
of railroad capability, so that some refinements in this methodology could
be suggested and put into effect. Mr. Farrell suggested that a number of
agencies bad comments on the use of the methodology, and that it would be
desirable to meet to determine to what extent the methodology should be
revised.

14. Action : The Chairman asked Mr. Farrell to head a group which would
meet to discuss this problem at an early date.

Discussion of the Status of the Proposed Exchange Visits with the USSR in
the Field of Transportation

15. Information : The Secretary and Mr. Perry briefed the Subcommittee
on the status of an exchange of visits in the field of highway transportation
with the USSR. It is contemplated that the Bureau of Public Roads and
Highway Research Beard will sponsor the exchange for the US and that the
USSR will be invited to be present at the January 1959 meeting of the
Highway Research Board. Following this meeting, a tour on the highway
system in the Utalted States would be made, visiting installations of interest.
The US highway experts propose to visit the USSR during the summer of 1959.
