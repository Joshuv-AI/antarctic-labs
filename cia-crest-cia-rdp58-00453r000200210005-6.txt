The BUSINESS SERVICE CHECKLIST is issued weekly. Annual subscription $1.25 domestic, $2.25
foreign. It lists material published by the U. S. Department of Commerce and by other agencies. When a
price is not shown, the material is free.

Department of Commerce publications are usually available also in the Department field offices. For
convenience in ordering publications, including subscriptions to the BUSINESS SERVICE CHECKLIST,
an order form is provided on page 4.

Publications of other agencies, including news releases and other published material, must be requested
from the Director of Information of the publishing agency. They are not included on the order form and
are not available from the Department of Commerce.
