The reV Model

 The reV Model
reV is an open-source geospatial platform for
examining the barriers and opportunities to deploy
energy assets at regional to continental scales.
 Land Availability &
 Siting Constraints

 Key Research Questions
 • How do social, ecological, and technical
 considerations impact energy siting?
 • How do spatial dynamics of system performance, System
 Grid
 Performance &
 costs, and interconnection drive deployment? Costs
 Interconnection

 • To what extent can these constraining factors be
 mitigated through cost reductions, technology

                                                                               National Laboratory of the Rockies | 4

 Why is reV uniquely suited to answer these questions?
Multi-Resolution and Detail at Scale: Native data resolution (e.g., building footprints), spatiotemporal resource

Technology and Cost Insights: Site-level parameterization, evaluation of technology innovation and R&D investments

Seamless Model Integration: Capacity expansion (e.g., NEMS, ReEDS ), production cost/stability (e.g., Plexos, Sienna)

 What are the specific outputs of the reV model?
Technical Potential: The total available land and generation potential given a set of land exclusion assumptions

Transmission Routing and Costs: Estimated distance and interconnection costs for new spur line (gen-tie line)

Supply Curves: Potential capacity, site-based + interconnection costs, time series generation profiles

 Who are the users and stakeholders of the reV model?
Energy Planners and Developers: ISOs/RTOs, utilities, state and local governments, project developers

Energy Analysts: Universities, national labs and other FFRDCs, consultants, R&D nonprofits

Federal Agencies: DOE, BLM, USFS, DoD, EPA, BOEM, USFWS, USGS, EIA
