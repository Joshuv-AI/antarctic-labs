 What Is the Microgrid Design Toolkit?

• The Microgrid Design Toolkit (MDT), developed by Sandia National
  Laboratories, is decision support software that can provide microgrid
  designers the information they need to identify optimal designs for:
 ➢ Performance
 ➢ Reliability
 ➢ Cost.
• The MDT is a free resource available from the U.S. Department of Energy.
Source: DOE, 2025

 What Are the Benefits of the MDT?

❑ Perform topology (physical arrangement and connections of the components)
  optimization.
❑ Create a trade space where designers can explore different design options and evaluate
  trade-offs..
❑ Review a variety of performance, reliability, and cost-related insights for potential
  microgrid configurations.
❑ Microgrid designers can get a comprehensive understanding of the trade-offs linked to
  design decisions.

 How Can MDT Help Designers?
 Best for use in the early stages of the design process, MDT uses powerful
 search algorithms to identify and characterize alternative microgrid designs
 using user-defined inputs and objectives such as cost, performance, and
 reliability.
Using the MDT, a designer can:
• Tailor inputs and site-specific factors for each project.
• Search through extensive design spaces for efficient alternatives.
• Explore the simultaneous impacts of multiple design options.
• Understand the relationships between design objectives and the
 trade-offs associated with alternate technologies. Physical power microgrid setup for wireless 5G cellular virtual

 What Kinds of Questions Can MDT Answer?
• Should an existing generator be replaced? If so, what type should the new one be and
  how big should it be?
• Should we build a new building to house additional generation assets, and if so, what
  generation assets should be used and where should the building be connected to the
  grid?
• Should we install a new redundant backup feeder on our microgrid?
• Should we add a photovoltaic installation, and if so, how big? If we add one, should we
  also add a battery to support it, and if so, how big should the battery be?
• What are the characteristics of solutions that perform well along a particular trade-off
  dimension?
• What are the technology decisions that have been ruled out as undesirable?
• Given a baseline solution, how much money will be required to realize a 10%
  improvement in performance?

 Input Features
• MDT has several input features:
 o An extensive set of input validation checks that include navigation support
 and detailed error descriptions.
 o A separate asset specification database that can be reused from
 multiple analyses.
 o User selectable metrics that can be grouped into trade-off dimensions.
 o Fixed and variable topology input that allow users to ask specific questions.

 Results
• The MDT performs a Mult objective search to produce a Pareto frontier of
 efficient microgrid solutions.
• That frontier is the expression of the efficient trade-offs that can be made
 amongst the user-defined dimensions (commonly cost, performance, reliability,
 etc.).
 In plain terms, instead of just one best solution, there is a group of
 equally good solutions. These solutions represent different trade-offs
 between goals, where you cannot improve one goal without making
 another worse.

 The MDT provides a wide range of interactive displays and charts to
 help a designer understand the results and the impacts of different
 choices on the resulting quality of the microgrid.

Case Studies

 Backup Power System for Hoboken, New Jersey
Description: After Hurricane Sandy, Sandia
National Laboratories used MDT to design a
steady supply of power for critical infrastructure
in in preparation for future natural or manmade
or natural disasters.
Technology: Microgrid
Impact: 10 sections of the city were identified as
potential independent microgrids using a graph-
based clustering technique that classified
Hoboken buildings into sets of distinct spatial
clusters.
Initial microgrids designs were entered into MDT
software to support microgrid design capability
planning and preparation for future installment.
