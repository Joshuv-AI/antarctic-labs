The President to the Ambassador in Turkey (Steinhardt)[1] (20 November 1943, d147)

[Tunis,] 20 November 1943.

From the President to Ambassador Steinhardt.

Please tell President Inonu I have landed safely in North Africa and that I would greatly welcome him. I cannot make my plans until Monday morning[2] but will advise you at that time.[3]

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the White House, apparently via military channels; forwarded to the Department of State; and forwarded further by the Department, in telegram 904, November 20, 1943, 7 p.m., to the Legation at Ankara, marked “Secret” and “U. S. Urgent”.
[2] November 22, 1943.
[3] For Roosevelt’s next message to Steinhardt, see post, p. 386.
