B. STATEMENT OF FUNCTIONS RELATING TO THE USE OF MISSION 9009 MATERIALS AND
INFORMATION BY THE U.S. NAVY

At both the Departmental and Operational levels, because of the functions
of the commands and organizational elements listed In Section A, direct
utilization of Mission 9009 Collection is made from two broad aspects:

(1) the exploitation of the primary materials and (2) the utilization of
the intelligence derived from this exploitation. Departmental and Theatre
functions are listed below under each of these broad aspects. In addition,
complete detail is furnished on the two attached charts which have been
drawn up on these two separate bases of utilization.

1. U.S, Navy Exploitation of T-KH Primary Materials

a. Departmental Level

(1) Through the U.S. Navy Photographic Interpretation Center and the
Office of Naval Intelligence Liaison Officers, the Navy provides support to
the Joint Exploitation effort at CIA; prepares intelligence products to
support the Navy Department and the Theatre Commanders in their planning and
estimating functions, and in coordination with the Fleet Intelligence Centers
supports and supplements the Fleet production effort.

b. Theatre Level: Fleet Intelligence Centers :

(1) Prepare intelligence materials to support Fleet and Unified
Command Staff functions in planning and estimates.

(2) Prepare specific strike target materials for emergency operational
use and update other target materials for general use.

(3) Supply intelligence data for order of battle, amphibious opera-
tions and Navy Intelligence studies for Theatre use.

Detailed breakdown of the Commands and functions are shown on Chart I attached.

2 . U.S. Navy Utilization of T-KH Intelligence

a. Departmental Level

(1) The Bureaus and Offices of the Navy Department specified in
Section A directly utilize the intelligence information provided to prepare the
Navy position in national estimates and planning; determine Navy posture,
offensive and defensive, and to prepare Naval Intelligence estimates and
War plans,

b. Theatre Commands

(1) The Operational Units and Theatre Commanders specified in Section
A utilize this material to plan theatre operations and organization, and to
contribute to overall Navy planning.

Details on Chart II attached.
