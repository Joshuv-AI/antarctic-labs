Psychological Intelligence Support (o/RR, o/o, O/PC)

Problem: To recommend the necessary arrangements for inter-
agency support of psychological warfare.

Status: The problem has been discussed with all agencies

concerned both from the planning or operating
viewpoint and from the intelligence side. As a
result of these conversations a tentative break-
down has been made of the kinds of intelligence
support that are required.

The immediate need is: (a) to find out if this
is an accurate, complete, and meaningful breakdownj
(b) to . determine who will provide each type of
intelligence, or what portion of each type, and
the extent to which each requires interagency
coordination; (c) to clarify agency responsibilities
and to recommend appropriate ways of achieving
necessary coordination. CIA has the responsibility
and initiative to see that appropriate intelligence
support is provided for psychological warfare and
that the intelligence activities of government
departments and agencies in this field are properly
coordinated.

Economic Interpretation of Photographic Materials (o/RR, O/CD)

Problem: To develop the most effective arrangements by which

economic intelligence can be derived from photographic
materials within the government.

Status: Exploitation of Defense Department resources and

those of Graphic Register of o/CD has been explored
by 0/RR and 0/lC. It now appears that while much
of value can be produced as a by-product of Air
Target Analysis, the results are likely to prove
disappointing as contributions to current economic
intelligence. Further investigation of photographic
sources is required.
