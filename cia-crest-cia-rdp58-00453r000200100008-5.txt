I. Form of Government

The Vietnamese nation, independent, united and Indivisible, forms a
democratic state with republican government.

Sovereignty resides in the people end Is exercised by the President of
the Republic together with the Parliament.

Yellow flag with three red stripes.

National anthem.

By 2/3 majority, the House of Representatives can vote a motion of

The House of Representatives can ask questions in writing to
individual Ministers, to which the Ministers are obliged to reply
either verbally or in writing, unless the question jeopardizes the
interests of the country or the national defense. If the Chamber
is not satisfied with the written reply of a given minister, it can
request that he appear personally in the Chamber to reply verbally to
questions of the representatives of the people.

VII* Process for Revision of th.e Constitution

A. All modifications of the present constitution must be
requested by 2/3 of the members of the House of Representatives.

B. A text amended and voted by the Chamber with an absolute
majority shall be submitted to a popular referendum.

The present constitution shall go into effect the same day that
the people of Vietnam, consulted by means of referendum, «hnn
