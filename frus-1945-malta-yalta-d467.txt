The President’s Secretary (Early) to the President’s Administrative Assistant (Daniels)[1]

[Yalta, February 11, 1945.]

Source: UNA Files

Notes
[1] Authorship not indicated. A copy of this document in the Hiss Collection, which embodies the alterations written on the copy reproduced above, bears the following handwritten notations: “Mr. Early took original 2/11 10.30 a. m. & said he would send it.” “Map room informed us about 1.30 p. m. that it had been sent.” A copy of the same telegram obtained from the Roosevelt Papers indicates that the time of release was to be twenty-four, rather than forty-eight, hours after publication of the communiqué.
