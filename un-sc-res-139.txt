Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 869th meeting, on 28 June 1960
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Federation of Mali [1],
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Federation of Mali be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for April, May, and June 1960, document S/4347.
