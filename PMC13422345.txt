Correction: Serum and cerebral folate are normal in Down Syndrome Regression Disorder
Authors: Jonathan D Santoro, Mackenzie Silverman, Lilia Kazerooni, Samuel T Otey, Maeve C Lucas, Mariam M Yousuf, Mellad M Koshnood, Noemi A Spinazzi, Eileen A Quinn, Catherine Franklin, Taryn R Liu, Shafali S Jeste, Michael S Rafii
Published: 2026

Correction to: Molecular Autism (2026) 17:9

10.1186/s13229-026-00703-6

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors were alerted to a discrepancy between the written results and Fig. 1. The article stated that all cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) 5-methyltetrahydrofolate (5-MTHF) values in the final analytic cohort were within the reference interval, whereas the published version of Fig. 1 displayed two data points below the stated reference interval.

The authors completed a detailed review of the source laboratory data, cohort eligibility file, figure-generation files, and final analytic dataset. This review confirmed that the published Fig. 1 was inadvertently generated from the secondary review dataset (n = 73), rather than from the final locked analytic cohort (n = 67). The secondary review dataset included six participants who were subsequently removed during final cohort confirmation but were inadvertently retained in the figure-generation file. These six participants were not included in the final analytic cohort used for the manuscript’s primary results.

The six participants removed after secondary review were excluded for prespecified eligibility reasons. Four were excluded because Down Syndrome Regression Disorder (DSRD) diagnostic inclusion could not be confirmed due to incomplete or exclusionary clinical information, and two were excluded because CSF collection timing was outside the prespecified window. The two participants with CSF 5-MTHF values below the reference interval were among those excluded because final DSRD diagnostic inclusion could not be confirmed. Specifically, participant F21 had a CSF 5-MTHF value of 17 nmol/L and was excluded because DSRD diagnostic inclusion could not be confirmed in the setting of prior methotrexate exposure for inflammatory arthropathy, an exclusionary/confounding condition for the purposes of this cohort. Participant F56 had a CSF 5-MTHF value of 27 nmol/L and was excluded because the available clinical information did not permit the regression syndrome to be distinguished from manifestations of epilepsy; therefore, DSRD diagnostic inclusion could not be confirmed. These exclusions were based on cohort eligibility adjudication and were not based on CSF 5-MTHF values.

A corrected Fig. 1 has therefore been provided using the final locked analytic cohort (n = 67). In this corrected figure, all CSF 5-MTHF values are within the reference interval, with a range of 41–108 nmol/L. The corrected Fig. 1 excludes the six participants who were not part of the final analytic cohort. The primary conclusion of the article remains unchanged: among participants meeting final inclusion criteria for possible or probable DSRD, CSF 5-MTHF values were within the reference interval.

During the same review, the authors also identified a presentation error in Fig. 2: the X-axis labels for developmental regression (Yes, No) were reversed. A corrected Fig. 2 has been provided with the X-axis labels corrected. The plotted data and statistical result are otherwise unchanged.

The authors regret these figure-preparation errors and appreciate the opportunity to correct the published record.

The correct Figs. 1 and 2 have been provided in this Correction.
