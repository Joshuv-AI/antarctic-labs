SHIRLEY ROSE FRANK
On September 28, 1964, Robert L. Poole,
Immigration and Naturalization Service, Savannah, Georgia,
advised that Mrs. Frank contacted his office and stated
that she wanted to go to Russia because she is a Communist.
She stated that it was her understanding that the "Yankees"
kicked all the commies out of the country.
On September 28, 1964, Mrs. Frank advised that
she was a Communist and wanted to go to Russia. When asked
why she considered herself a Communist, Mrs. Frank simply
laughed and restated that she was a Communist and that
was all. She advised that her son was living in Leningrad,
Russia, and that is where she wanted to go. She stated that
site was born in Leningrad, Russia, and that the birth
certificate in Madison, Wisconsin, reflecting that she was
born in that state was erroneous. When queried as to how
she entered this country, Mrs. Frank stated that she came
to the United States when she was two years old and does not
recall any of the details.
Mi-s. Frank advised that her husband, Harold Lorin
Frank, Jr., died in England after he was "poisoned by the
crazy Yankees because he was a Jew." When asked who the
"crazy Yankees" wore, Mrs. Frank stated that they arc all
"fairies who take dope." Mrs. Frank advised that these
"crazy Yankees" follow her around all the time and continually
dope her and keep her covered with guns.
Mrs. Frank stated that she was in jail in Madison,
Wisconsin, and she said that this jail was also called the
Mongoda State Hospital. Mrs. Frank stated that during her
stay at the Mongoda State Hospital, the "crazy Yankees doped
her and threatened her with guns.
