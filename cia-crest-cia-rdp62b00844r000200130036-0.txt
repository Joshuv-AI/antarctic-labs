6. In summary, the preceding paragraphs are intended to express
cautious optimism that it may be possible both to avoid unauthorised damaging
disclosures concerning the true purpose of CORONA and that it will be
possible to provide plausible explanations of an extended DISCOVERY series
which characterise it as a continuing developmental activity. Care has been
taken up to this point not to announce any firm schedule for the DISCOVERY
series or any definitely scheduled number of flights. Hence, there is no need
to announce a change of plana (which always invites attention) nor, indeed, to
make at this time any announcement about the duration of the DISCOVERY
series. If an affirmative decision is made on the proposal in the referenced
memorandum for a I960 series, it would probably be beet either to say nothing
at this time about toe duration of toe activity or to say frankly that the number
of DISCOVERY flights has not yet been finally determined. This cautious
optimism. It must be emphasised, is contingent upon continued more strenuous
efforts by all concerned both to avoid unauthorised disclosure and to support
the cover stories. In particular the maintenance of cover for CORONA (even
through 1959) will require toe stern exercise of authority at all appropriate
points to discourage toe kind of technical discussion in advisory committees
and in Military and civilian components that quickly reveals the fact of a highly
classified project, toe purpose and character of which can toon be readily
surmised.

7. Finally, it must be repeated (from toe last paragraph of toe referenced
memorandum) that toe degree to which the cover of CORONA will be eroded

in forthcoming months, and thereby the judgments expressed above, is inevitably
subject to a high degree of uncertainty. Accordingly, if it is decided to conduct
a series of flights with the CORONA vehicle in I960 and if concealment of the
true purpose of this activity is believed desirable, toe best course of action
might well he to embark on this program with every intention of conducting it
under toe security with which CORONA has been surrounded but to review the
security status of toe program in six months. U its cover has then begun to
wear thin . It would probably be better in a carefully contrived manner to surface
its true purpose. Alternatively, if cover is holding up well, the original intent
can be adhered to. No attempt has been made here to comment on the importance
of concealment of the reconnaissance mission. If this is of low importance,
it will be well to decide at this point to surface the purpose of toe I960 program
at a time and in a manner which will best preserve the security of CORONA in
1959. If, at the other extreme, toe importance of concealing toe mission is so
great that the program could be carried out only if such concealment is effectively
maintained, then it must be said in all honesty tost there is a substantial risk
that the objective cannot be achieved.
