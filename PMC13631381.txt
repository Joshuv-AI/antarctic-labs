In the originally published article, the values in Table were derived from an earlier analysis that was not restricted to complete cases. As described in the Methods, effectiveness outcomes were to be summarized only for patients with recorded values at baseline and at both prespecified follow‐up intervals of 3 to 6 months and 6 to 12 months. As a result, the medians and interquartile ranges published in Table were incorrect. Separately, the interquartile range published for the baseline Patient Global value, 6.0 (6.9–8.1), contained a typographical error in its lower bound, which as printed exceeds the median.

The summary statistics have been recalculated using complete cases. Physician Global Assessment and PGA‐skin have been omitted from the corrected table because complete‐case data at all three time points were available for too few patients to permit meaningful summary, and body surface area and the patient skin visual analog scale, which were not reported in the original table, have been added because complete‐case data were available for these measures. In addition, in the Results, under the subheading “Treatment patterns and duration”, the text “By three to six months, TJC and SJC showed reductions, accompanied by decreases in DAPSA and PASI. Improvements in PROs were more apparent at later follow‐up.” was incorrect. This should have read: “By three to six months, TJC, DAPSA and PASI showed reductions, whereas SJC was unchanged and declined only by six to twelve months. Improvements in PROs were also more apparent at later follow‐up.”

These corrections do not affect the abstract, the baseline characteristics reported in Table 1, the safety analyses reported in Tables 3 and 4, Figure 1, or the conclusions of the article.

We apologize for this error.
