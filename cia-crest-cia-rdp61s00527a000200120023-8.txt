The appearance of civil defense radio networks is a logical
development. The United States, for example, has included in its
civil defense plans the recruitment of amateur radio operators and
the use of their equipment for emergency communications. 6/

In view of their generally parallel development in civil
defense and paramilitary organization, it is believed that other
Sovbloc nations have evolved, or will develop, radio networks
similar to the one found in Czechoslovakia. The obvious nucleus
for such operations is to be found in the various paramilitary
societies which sponsor radio amateur activity and civil defense
training.
