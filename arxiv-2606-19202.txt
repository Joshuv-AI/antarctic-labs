Renormalization of the SMEFT to dimension eight: Fermionic interactions II


Abstract
We compute the one-loop mixing of bosonic and two-fermion interactions into two-fermion operators in the dimension-eight Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT). Together with the results in Refs. [ 33 , 40 , 17 , 94 ] , this leaves only the mixing of four-fermion operators into two-fermion ones as the remaining piece to complete the SMEFT renormalization program at this order.

Discussion

This calculation almost completes the one-loop renormalization of the SMEFT to dimension eight. The only missing piece is the mixing of four-fermion operators into two-fermion ones. (Certain
\mathscr{O}(v^{2}/\Lambda^{2})
corrections are also missing in the dimension-six sector; e.g., the running of dimension-six two-fermion terms due to loops of dimension-eight two-fermion interactions proportional to the Higgs mass
\mu^{2}
.)

Some comments on cross-checks are in order:

1.

Our results fulfill amplitude-based non-renormalization theorems
[
38
,
84
]
. For example,
\psi^{2}\phi^{5}
operators can not mix into
\psi^{2}\phi^{4}D
ones on-shell (see Tab.
4
), even though diagrammatically allowed; see Tab.
1
.

2.

Our results fulfill the constraints implied by positivity restrictions
[
37
,
34
]
. For example, considering
\phi^{4}D^{4}
and

e^{2}\phi^{2}D^{3}

\displaystyle\begin{aligned} -\dot{c}_{e^{2}\phi^{2}D^{3}}^{(1)}-\dot{c}_{e^{2}\phi^{2}D^{3}}^{(2)}=&-\frac{1}{3}|y^{e}|^{2}c^{(1)}_{\phi^{4}}-\frac{1}{2}|y^{e}|^{2}c^{(2)}_{\phi^{4}}-\frac{1}{6}|y^{e}|^{2}c^{(3)}_{\phi^{4}}\\
&+\frac{2}{3}|y^{e}|^{2}c^{(1)}_{l^{2}\phi^{2}D^{3}}+\frac{2}{3}|y^{e}|^{2}c^{(2)}_{l^{2}\phi^{2}D^{3}}\,,\\
