No. 717 The Secretary of State to the Ambassador in China (Hurley)

secret urgent

[Babelsberg,] July 30, 1945.

Victory 439. Please proceed at once in accordance with the instruction in our telegram Number 375 of July 27[1] inasmuch as we wish to have President Chiang’s reply within the next 48 hours.

Byrnes

Source: Department of the Army Files: Telegram

[1] Document No. 715.
