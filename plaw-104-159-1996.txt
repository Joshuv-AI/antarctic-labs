[104th Congress Public Law 159]

Public Law 104-159
104th Congress

 An Act


  To provide that the United States Post Office building that is to be
located at 7436 South Exchange Avenue, Chicago, Illinois, shall be known
 and designated as the ``Charles A. Hayes Post Office
           Building''. <<NOTE: July 9, 1996 -  [H.R. 2704]>>

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. DESIGNATION.

 The United States Post Office building that is to be located at 7436
South Exchange Avenue, Chicago, Illinois, shall be known and designated
as the ``Charles A. Hayes Post Office Building''.

SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

 Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other
record of the United States to the United States Post Office building
referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the
``Charles A. Hayes Post Office Building''.

 Approved July 9, 1996.
