Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 765.]

Department of State, Washington, November 23, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 4th of November (No. 529) is received, and the note which you addressed to Earl Russell on the 3d of November, a copy of which you have annexed, is approved.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

C. F. Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
