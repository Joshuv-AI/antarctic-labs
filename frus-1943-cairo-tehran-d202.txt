The Acting Secretary of State (Stettinius) to the Ambassador in the Soviet Union (Harriman) (9 November 1943, d202)

secret u. s. urgent

Washington, November 9, 1943.

1180. Secret and personal for the Ambassador from the Acting Secretary.

The President has handed to me your message no. 6100 sent via Navy.[1] You are authorized to comply with Molotov’s request that your letter containing the message concerning Turkey be made a part of the secret military record of the Conference.

Stettinius

Acting

Source: 740.0011 Moscow/256a: Telegram

Notes
[1] This is the telegram of November 6, ante, p. 158.
