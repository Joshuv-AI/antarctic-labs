The importance of contributing to twist-4 at small- x x is visible in the linear region where ξ A ′ ≤ 1 \xi^{\prime}_{A}{\leq}1 . We perform that twist-4 corrections of the saturation model are shown the successful behavior of F L F 2 ​ ( A = 2 , x , Q 2 ) \frac{F_{L}}{F_{2}}(A=2,x,Q^{2}) in comparison with the JLab data at ξ A ′ ≤ 1 \xi^{\prime}_{A}{\leq}1 .
