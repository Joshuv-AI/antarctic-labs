Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 715.]

Department of State, Washington, September 19, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 3d of September (No. 487) has been received. The President is satisfied with the note of Earl Russell, a copy of which you have transmitted.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
