No. 17 President Truman to Prime Minister Churchill [1]

[Extract][2]

top secret

[Washington,] 21 May 1945.

45. Your number 53. …[3]

I may, within the next two weeks, have more information bearing on a date and location for the proposed tripartite meeting if Stalin agrees to participate.

I hope he will agree to come west into Germany or further west, but I am advised that he is not likely to go beyond Soviet controlled, territory.

. . . . . . .

Source: Leahy Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Presumably sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
[2] The portion of this message not printed here related to Venezia Giulia. See Truman, Year of Decisions, pp. 250–251.
[3] document No. 16.
