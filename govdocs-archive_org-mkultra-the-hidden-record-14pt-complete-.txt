MKULTRA is documented history. The missing files are also part of that history.
In 1973, the main MKULTRA files were destroyed. Later searches found
financial records, internal reports, and other surviving documents. Those
fragments showed a program far larger than the public had been told.
The hearing in this book returns to the questions left behind: Who
approved the work? Who knew? How many people were exposed? Which
records still exist? And which popular theories go beyond what the
documents can prove?
The complete official hearing is presented in order. Between parts of the
record, dossier inserts open the related files: destruction orders,
subproject counts, institutional networks, safe houses, CIA funding
routes, exact quotations, and unresolved claims.
Some statements in the hearing are documented by surviving records.
Others are sworn testimony, interpretation, or open speculation. This
edition keeps those differences visible without telling the reader what
conclusion to reach.
