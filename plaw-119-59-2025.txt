Public Law 119–59: To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 West Oklahoma Avenue in Guthrie, Oklahoma, as the “Oscar J. Upham Post Office”.

An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 West Oklahoma Avenue in Guthrie, Oklahoma, as the “Oscar J. Upham Post Office”.Dec. 18, 2025[S. 2283]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — OSCAR J. UPHAM POST OFFICE.

SUBSECTION (a) — Designation

The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 201 West Oklahoma Avenue in Guthrie, Oklahoma, shall be known and designated as the “Oscar J. Upham Post Office”.

SUBSECTION (b) — References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Oscar J. Upham Post Office”.

Approved December 18, 2025.
