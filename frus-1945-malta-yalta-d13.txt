President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill [1] (22 October 1944, d13)

[Excerpts]

top secret priority

[Washington,] 22 October 1944.

Number 632, Personal and Top Secret, from the President to the Prime Minister.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

The selection of a Black Sea port for our next meeting seems to be dependent upon our ability to get through the Dardanelles safely as I wish to proceed by ship. Do you think it is possible to get U. J. to come to Athens or Cyprus?

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to the United States Naval Attaché, London, via Navy channels.
