No. 123 The Secretary of State to the Ambassador in the United Kingdom (Winant)

top secret

Washington, July 5, 1945—10 a.m.

5446. You may inform the Foreign Office (your 6566, June 29[l]) that the State Department Delegation to the Big Three meeting will include Secretary Byrnes, Assistant Secretaries Dunn and Clayton, Ambassadors Murphy and Harriman, H. F. Matthews, Director of the Office of European Affairs, and several specialists on European and Far Eastern political and economic matters.

Byrnes

Source: 740.0011 EW/6–2945: Telegram

[l] Document No. 108.
