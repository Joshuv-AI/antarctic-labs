Correction for Zhang et al., Host genetic regulation of rumen 6-hydroxymelatonin reduces methane emissions in dairy cattle
Published: 2026

Correction for “Host genetic regulation of rumen 6-hydroxymelatonin reduces methane emissions in dairy cattle,” by Chenguang Zhang, Ye Liu, Guoyan Wang, Mengqi Huang, Jun Zhang, Zongjun Li, Linhao Zhou, Guangfu Tang, Shengru Wu, Lu Deng, and Junhu Yao, which published June 8, 2026; 10.1073/pnas.2604454123 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 123, e2604454123).

The authors note that an error occurred in the second paragraph of subsection 1.14 in Materials and Methods, “Methane Emission Measurement,” and in the first paragraph of subsection 2.7 in Results, “To Identify the Genetic Variants and Elucidate the Molecular Mechanisms of Host’s Synthesis of 6-hydroxymelatonin Based on GWAS.” “The system used to measure methane emissions from the 31 lactating cows was incorrectly described as the GreenFeed system. The correct system is an automated head-chamber (AHC) system. While both platforms operate on similar principles of methane quantification, they differ significantly in operational parameters and engineering design. To ensure methodological precision and reproducibility, we have updated the description to align with the protocol established by Wang et al. (31), the developers of the AHC system. This attribution properly credits the original methodology and adheres to best scholarly practice. This correction does not affect any of the results, conclusions, or scientific interpretations reported in the study.” Reference 31 has been updated accordingly. The published paper has been updated.

31. R. Wang et al., Relationship between daily variations of methane emissions and eructation peaks in dairy cows measured with an automated head-chamber system. Anim. Feed Sci. Technol. 303, 115714 (2023).
