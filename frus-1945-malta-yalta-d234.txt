The President to the Secretary of State (8 December 1944, d234)

[Warm Springs, Georgia,] December 8, 1944.

Memorandum for the Secretary of State

I think this Soviet-Iranian matter should be taken up by Harriman with Stalin in person. The Teheran agreement was pretty definite and my contribution was to suggest to Stalin and Churchill that three or four Trustees build a new port in Iran at the head of the Persian Gulf (free port), take over the whole railroad from there into Russia, and run the thing for the good of all. Stalin’s comment was merely that it was an interesting idea and he offered no objection.

F[ranklin] D. R[oosevelt]
