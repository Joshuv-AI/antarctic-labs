Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 872nd meeting, on 7 July 1960
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of the Congo [1] [2],
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of the Congo be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1]Republic of the Congo, capital Leopoldville.
[2]Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for July, August, and September 1960, document S/4361.
