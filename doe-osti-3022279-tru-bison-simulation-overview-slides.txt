Conclusions

• G591 simulations developed assuming that the addition of MA components
  provides a negligible contribution to thermomechanical behaviors predict fuel
  performance parameters associated with failure criteria with good agreement
  • Profilometry, plenum pressure, and plenum volume are simulated within experimental error
  • Temperature and burnup are simulated accurately compared to the FIPD database
  • Fission gas release is underpredicted by ~11%
• U-TRU-Zr can be modeled as U-Pu-Zr while ignoring small amounts of MA
  content
• More data for varying irradiation time, burnup, and temperatures is needed to
  validate this modeling methodology.
