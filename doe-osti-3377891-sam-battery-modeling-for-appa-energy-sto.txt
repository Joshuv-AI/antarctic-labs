 ✓ Currently integrated with PV, Marine, “Generic System” model, Standalone battery

     ✓   Behind-the-meter or front-of-the-meter operation                              National Laboratory of the Rockies | 15

 Hybrid System Models
Combine co-located generation sources such as PV and wind with

  ✓ Currently integrated with PV, Wind, Generic System, Fuel Cell, and
 Battery technologies
  ✓ Available for FOM and BTM analyses
  ✓   Separate cost definitions for each system subcomponent               National Laboratory of the Rockies | 16

 Why batteries in SAM?
• Exploding interest in modeling hybrid and battery systems generally
• Existing SAM users want to know about resiliency of the system
 • Ability to implement a power outage in SAM enables ability to
 answer “Will this system adequately provide backup during an
 outage?”
 • Of greater interest to users modeling systems on fragile grids.
• Storage modeling for future R&D
 • Robust hour by hour calculation of the LCOS (levelized cost of
 storage) not just using average lifetime values for usage
 • SAM is also being used to model long-duration thermal storage
 and potentially other options as well.

Component Models

Cell Capacity
 • Sizing for power and capacity of the battery system

Lifetime Fade
 • How capacity decreases over time due to calendar (pure storage)
 and cycling fade
 • Calculating needed replacements as a function of performance

Voltage Curve
 • Voltage discharge curve as a function of the state-of-charge (SOC)

Thermal
 • Heat transfer with environment for instantaneous thermal effects
 on capacity, resistance, and lifetime

 Financial Models Overview
• Single Owner
 • Revenue by long term (15 – 25 year) power purchase agreement
 • Prices repeat year to year with escalation term
 • All tax benefits go to project owner and operator
• Merchant Plant
 • Time series prices and cleared capacity for the entire analysis
 period
 • Same tax assumptions as Single Owner
• Sale Leaseback and Partnership Flip
 • Other US-centric tax and investment structures
• Residential and Commercial
    • Savings on utility bills for building owners      National Laboratory of the Rockies | 32
