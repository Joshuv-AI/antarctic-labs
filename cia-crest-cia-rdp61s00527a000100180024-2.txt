Note a* Contemplated Change in PL It- 80 .

The Chairman told the group that he had been informed by the General
Counsel that the Bureau of the Budget has requested responses from Executive
Branch agencies regarding legislative changes in PL U80. One of the provisos in
the Senate proposed revision states that barter should be allowed with countries
other than the USSR, Communist China, and North Korea. The State Department has
recommended that North Vietnam and Outer Mongolia be added. DD/MDAC has been
asked merely to concur or non-concur. If there is any feeling among the agencies
that this would be detrimental to economic defense policy, the Chairman asked to
be so informed. The members agreed to check with their principals and report to
the Chairman within the next 2U hours. ^/jDD/MDAC subsequently concurred^/

Discussion

The Chairman suggested a paragraph by paragraph review, 9 through 22, of the
Courses of Action in the proposed policy statement with the intent of drafting a
work program to be ready for EDAC consideration as soon as the policy paper has
been approved* He called attention to EDAC D-55, 55a, 55b, 55c, which had been
prepared as a work program under NSC 152/3. EDAC D-121/WG I, 121/WG IT, and
121/WG III were mentioned as sources of reference for the -drafters. This paper,
as distinct from U. S« position papers for CG, should cover all the points nec-
essary in broad and general terms. EDAC will be asked to adopt a work program
which fixes responsibility for actions on each Course of Action on Working Groups,
agencies, or elements of the Government whose terras of reference appropriately in-
clude these Courses of Action.
