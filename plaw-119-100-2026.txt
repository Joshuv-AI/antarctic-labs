Public Law 119–100: To require the Federal Communications Commission to issue an order providing that a shark attack is an event for which a wireless emergency alert may be transmitted, and for other purposes.

An Act To require the Federal Communications Commission to issue an order providing that a shark attack is an event for which a wireless emergency alert may be transmitted, and for other purposes.June 26, 2026[S. 1003]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as “Lulu’s Law”.

SEC. 2. — WIRELESS EMERGENCY ALERTS.

SUBSECTION (a) — Definition

In this section, the term “Alert Message” has the meaning given the term in section 10.10(a) of title 47, Code of Federal Regulations, or any successor regulation.

SUBSECTION (b) — Requirement

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Federal Communications Commission shall issue an order to provide that a shark attack is an event for which an Alert Message may be transmitted.

Approved June 26, 2026.
