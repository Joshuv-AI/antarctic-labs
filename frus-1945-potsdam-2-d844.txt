No. 844 The Acting Secretary of State to the Acting Representative in Rumania (Melbourne)

[Extract[1]]

secret

Washington, July 28, 1945—1 p.m.

378. …

. . . . . . . .

… On the six articles mentioned in his[2] M–1285[3] his views are in general concurred in by Dept. We would not, however, regard any specific protest on Article 7 as called for at the present time since Soviet Govt has several times been apprised of this Govt’s position and matter is under discussion at governmental level as part of question of defining war booty in all ex-enemy countries. …

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Grew S[amuel] R[eber]
