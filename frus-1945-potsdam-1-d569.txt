No. 569 The Ambassador in Italy (Kirk) to the Acting Secretary of State

[Extract]

secret us urgent

Caserta, June 24, 1945—7 p. m

2749. Re our immediately preceding tel[1] and your 607, June 22.[2] When Gen Morgan reported to AFHQ signature of agreement with Yugos he made no ref to exception re Art. 3 and Belgrade learned of it before ourselves because Col Clarke Brit Mil Attaché Belgrade was present during negotiations and kept Brit Ambass[ador] Belgrade[3] fully informed. On Morgan’s return to AFHQ when we asked for text as set forth in our 2725, June 22[4] he withheld copy until he could “explain” text. He did not mention reservation re Art 3 of Belgrade agreement until he held meeting referred to in our 2725. Text of Yugo reservation has already been sent to Dept[5] …

. . . . . . .

Kirk
