 SOFIA instrument specifications
 1. Summary of requirements:
 a. 1.5m x 2.5m lab space
 b. 220V, 20A single phase receptacle for compressor
 c. 120V, 15A receptacle or 220V, 10A receptacle for electronics
 2. STAR Cryoelectronics DRC-100 cryostat
 a. https://starcryo.com/adr-cryostats/drc-100/
 b. Side-looking detector window
 c. Cryostat body is 102 cm tall, 33 cm wide (width of face with detector window), 22.1
 cm deep. Weight is approximately 45 kg.
 3. Cryomech PT403 pulse tube compressor
 i. Air Cooled Weight: 176lb (80kg) Air Cooled Dimensions: 19 X 22 X 22in
 (48 X 56 X 56cm) Power Consumption 60Hz (Input Power): 208/230 VAC
 1PH / 3.0kW, 20A minimum circuit
 ii. Easy to change plug type to match facility
 iii. Connects to cryostat with two 10 ft. flexible hoses
 iv. Compressor has wheels
 4. Electronics rack
 a. Half rack of electronics for cryostat control, detector readout, and data acquisition
 b. Power requirement: single 115V, 15A receptacle with modules switchable to 220V
 c. Rack has wheels
 5. Control computers
 a. Windows for cryostat control
 b. Linux for data acquisition
 c. Set up as standalone systems, no network access required
 6. Vacuum pump (not yet purchased) *

* Equipment could be shared between SOFIA and DES instruments

 DES instrument specifications
 1. Summary of requirements:
 a. 3m x 1m lab space
 b. 200V 3-phases 38A, Delta phase receptacle for compressor
 c. Compressor cooling water: 6.8L – 9L/min, 5C – 25C, less than 8 bar
 d. 120V, 15A receptacle or 220V, 10A receptacle for electronics
 e. Recommended wall cutout size for He flex lines:
 2. STAR Cryoelectronics DRC-100 cryostat
 a. https://starcryo.com/adr-cryostats/drc-100/
 b. Side-looking removable vacuum shell panel
 c. Cryostat body is 107 cm tall, 33 cm wide (width of face with removable vacuum
 shell panel), 22.1 cm deep. Weight is approximately 50 kg.
 3. Sumitomo F-70LP pulse tube compressor
 a. Water Cooled Weight: ~100kg; Dimensions: 45 X 45 X 52cm; Power Consumption
                60Hz (Input Power): 8.0-8.5kW, startup: < 9.8kW
 b. Easy to change plug type to match facility
 c. Connects to cryostat with two 20 ft. flexible hoses
 d. Compressor has wheels
 4. Electronics rack (cryostat)
 a. Half rack of electronics for cryostat control and TES SQUID electronics
 b. Power requirement: single 115V, 15A receptacle with modules switchable to 220V
 c. UPS will be changed to 220V version
 d. Rack has wheels
 5. Electronics rack (detector readout)
 a. Preamplifier – SRS SIM910 (might be shared with TES)
 b. MMC SQUID Power Supply
 c. TES bias voltage supplies (SRS DC205)
 d. All modules switchable to 220V/ 60Hz
 6. Control computers
 a. Windows for cryostat control
 b. Linux for data acquisition
 c. Set up as standalone systems, no network access required
 7. Vacuum Pump (not yet purchased) *
 8. Service Stand (footprint: 2m (height) X 0.9m X 0.6m) *

* Equipment could be shared between SOFIA and DES instruments
