The Honorable Stansfield Turner
Director, Central Intelligence Agencv
Washington, D.C. 20505

Dear Admiral Turner:

John deButts has asked that I extend his per-
sonal invitation to you and Mrs. Turner to join with
The Business Council when it meets at The Homestead
in Hot Springs, Virginia on May 11th to the 13th.

The discussions during ..the .morning .business
sessions will center on : .the. value of the dollar and
on unemployment and we are in great hopes that the
Vice President will be our Friday evening dinner
speaker .

It would add so much to our meeting if we could
include you both among our guests. I shall look for-
ward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
