To explain the original Perky data, we had assumed that the signal level, S, and the noise level, N, were uncoupled. The variance of the noise, σNo2, then remains constant when only the signal level is varied, as in Eq. 1. However, this cannot explain the cross-over from reverse to regular Perky.
