The minimum genus of Galois covers of curves
Manish Kumar, Poulami Mandal

Abstract
. Let V ⟶ X V\longrightarrow X be a G G -Galois connected cover of smooth projective connected curves over an algebraically closed field k k of positive characteristic p p with the branch locus contained in a finite subset of closed points S X S_{X} in X X , where G G is a finite cyclic p p -group. Let l l be a prime number other than p p . Let Γ \Gamma be an extension of an elementary abelian l l -group H H by G G . We find G G -stable submodules of the l l -torsion of the Picard group of V V and its generalisation. This is used to describe a method for finding the minimum genus of Γ \Gamma -covers of X X , étale over X ∖ S X X\setminus S_{X} , dominating V V ; and also the minimum of the genera of Γ \Gamma -covers of ℙ 1 \mathbb{P}^{1} étale over 𝔸 1 \mathbb{A}^{1} .



1.
Introduction

Let
X
be a smooth connected projective curve over an algebraically closed field
k
of characteristic
p>0
, and
S_{X}
be a finite subset of closed points in
X
. Since the étale fundamental group
\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})
is a profinite group, understanding its finite quotients is helpful in understanding its structure. Abhyankar’s conjecture on affine curves, proved by Raynaud and Harbater (
[
21
, Théorème 2.2.1 ]
,
[
6
, Theorem 6.2]
and
[
7
, Corollary 4.7]
) states that any finite group is a quotient of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})
if and only if the maximal prime-to-
p
quotient of this group has a generating set of cardinality less than
2g_{X}+r_{X}-1
, where
g_{X}
is the genus of
X
and
r_{X}>0
is the cardinality of
S_{X}
. However, this does not completely describe the profinite group structure of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})
. Given two finite quotient groups
\Gamma
and
G
of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})
where
G
is a quotient of
\Gamma
, we want to know how they fit in the inverse system of finite quotients of
\pi_{1}^{\e}(X\setminus S_{X})
. In other words, given a
G
-cover
\psi:V\longrightarrow X
étale away from
S_{X}
, we are interested in
\Gamma
-covers
W\longrightarrow X
étale away from
S_{X}
that dominate
V\longrightarrow X
. Note that
S_{X}
contains the branch locus of
\psi
, but it may also contain some non-branch points.

The existence and non-existence of such
\Gamma
-covers have been studied in
[
18
]
,
[
9
]
,
[
12
]
. Also see survey papers
[
20
]
and
[
8
]
for more details.

A finite group is called quasi-
p
if it is generated by its Sylow-
p
subgroups. Let
H
be the subgroup of
\Gamma
such that
\Gamma/H\cong G
. When
S_{X}
is non-empty and
H
is quasi-
p
, such
\Gamma
-covers always exist (see
[
18
, Theorem B]
,
[
7
, Corollary 4.6]
). If
S_{X}
is empty and
H
is a
p
-group, the existence of these
\Gamma
-covers was discussed by Pacheco, Stevenson (
[
17
, Theorem 1.3]
) and also Borne (
[
1
, Theorem 1.1]
). Hence, we investigate the situation when
H
is prime-to-
p
(abelian).

Let
l
be a prime number different from
p
. Let
G
be the cyclic group
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
and
H\cong(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
, for (fixed) natural numbers
a
and
n
. Then
\Gamma\cong H\rtimes_{\theta}G
(by the Schur-Zassenhaus Theorem) for some
G
-action
\theta:G\to\Aut(H)
.
It was shown in
[
13
]
that an
l
-torsion finite abelian group
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
, where
S_{V}
is the inverse image of
S_{X}
in
V
, can be useful for constructing an
H
-cover
W\longrightarrow V
, which is a
\Gamma
-cover of
X
and dominates
V
. This group
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
is a generalisation of the
l
-torsion part of the degree zero Picard group of
V
,
\P 0(V)[l]
, and the Galois group
\Aut(V|X)\cong G
acts on it from the right. Its linearly independent subsets of cardinality
n
are in bijection with
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
-covers of
V
étale over
V\setminus S_{V}
(Lemma
2.1
). It was shown in
[
13
]
that the
\Gamma
-covers of
X
, dominating
V
, for various group actions
\theta
are in bijection with the
G
-submodules of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
. Here we study the
G
-module structure of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
(see Lemma
3.3
, Theorem
3.14
and Corollary
3.17
). It is not clear how
\P 0(V)[l]
(a direct summand of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
) decomposes as a direct sum of irreducible
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
-representations.

Another way to understand the étale fundamental group is to examine the ramification groups at the branch points of Galois étale covers. One interesting question is to find Galois étale covers with prescribed “small” degree of the ramification divisor. Since the genus of this cover is related to the ramification filtration, finding a Galois cover with the least genus is of interest. In
[
16
, Theorem 4.6]
and
[
2
, Corollary 2.2]
, some Galois étale covers of
\mathbb{A}^{1}
with Galois group isomorphic to alternating groups were constructed, and it was shown that each of these covers was of minimum genus. In
[
5
]
, for a prime number
l
coprime to
p
,
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
-Galois covers of
\mathbb{A}^{1}_{k}
with minimal genus were described. Note that the group
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
depends on the action of
\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
on
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
. And in
[
5
]
, the minimal genus is computed among all such Galois covers with all possible actions of
\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
on
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
(with even
n
allowed to vary).

Main results

Given a smooth projective connected curve
X
of genus
g_{X}
over an algebraically closed field
k
of characteristic
p
, a finite set of closed points
S_{X}
in
X
, a group action
\theta:\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}\longrightarrow\Aut(\mathbb{Z}/l^{n}\mathbb{Z})
, and a
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-cover
V\longrightarrow X
with the branch locus contained in
S_{X}
, we discuss the minimum genus for an
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
-cover
W\longrightarrow V
such that
W\longrightarrow X
is a
((\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes_{\theta}(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}))
-Galois cover, étale away from
S_{X}
. More precisely, when
a
,
n
and
\theta
are fixed, we give a formula for the minimum genus possible for
W
when (1) every branch point of
V\longrightarrow X
is totally ramified or (2) the fibre of
V\longrightarrow X
over each branch point has exactly
p
points (see Theorem
4.2
). The general case is described below:

Let
d_{s}
be the order of
l
in
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{s}\mathbb{Z})^{*}
for
1\leq s\leq a
. We label the
d_{s}
dimensional irreducible
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-modules over
\mathbb{F}_{l}
by
M_{s,t}
, for
1\leq t\leq p^{s-1}(p-1)/d_{s}
(see Notation
3.1
). Let
a
,
n
and
\theta
be fixed. Let there be a
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-module monomorphism from
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
into
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
. Then, by
[
13
, Theorem 3.5]
, there exists at least one
((\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes_{\theta}(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}))
-cover
W\longrightarrow X
, dominating
V\longrightarrow X
.
Let
H^{\prime}
be a maximal
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-submodule of
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
that can be embedded in
\P 0(V)[l]
(a direct summand of
P_{l}(V\setminus S_{V})
). Let the multiplicity of the
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-module
\mathbb{F}_{l}
with trivial
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-action, (resp.
M_{s,t}
) in
H/H^{\prime}
be
\alpha_{0}
(resp.
\alpha_{s,t}
). Let
r_{s}
be the number of points in
S_{X}
for which each point has exactly
p^{s}
pre-images in
V
.

Theorem 1.1
.

A method of finding the genus
\mathfrak{g}
of a
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes_{\theta}(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-cover of
X
, minimum among the genera of all
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes_{\theta}(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-covers of
X
, étale over
X\setminus S_{X}
, dominating
V\longrightarrow X
, is given by the following integer linear programming problem with bounded variables.

Minimize

\sum_{s=0}^{a}p^{s}y_{s}

subject to :
\sum_{i=s}^{a}y_{i}\geq\max_{t}\{\alpha_{s,t}\},
for
s>0
,
\sum_{s=0}^{a}y_{s}\geq\alpha_{0}+1
and
0\leq y_{s}\leq r_{s}
and
y_{s}\in\mathbb{Z}
,
0\leq s\leq a
.

If
c_{\min}
is the minimum value of
\sum_{s=0}^{a}p^{s}y_{s}
, then the minimum genus is

\mathfrak{g}=\frac{2+l^{n}(2g_{V}-2)+l^{n-1}(l-1)c_{\min}}{2}.

Finally, in the Section
5
, we denote by
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
the class of groups consisting of extensions of
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
by
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
, where the
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-action
\theta
on
\mathbb{Z}/l^{n}\mathbb{Z}
is allowed to vary. For
a=1
, we find the minimum genus of
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
-covers of
\mathbb{P}^{1}
étale over
\mathbb{A}^{1}
in Proposition
5.4
. This recovers
[
5
, Theorem 4.2 and 4.3]
(see Remark
5.5
). Moreover, Theorem
5.6
, Corollary
5.7
and Theorem
1.2
extend the earlier results to obtain the minimum genus of
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
-covers. The minimum genus is computed among all Galois covers with all possible groups
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
as the action of
\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}
on
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}
varies.

Theorem 1.2
.

Let
a=2
and
n
be a positive integer. Let the order of
l
in
(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^{*}
and
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z})^{*}
be
d_{1}
and
d_{2}
respectively. The minimum genus of all
((\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z}))
-Galois covers of
\mathbb{P}^{1}
, étale over
\mathbb{A}^{1}
, for all positive integer
n
and all group homomorphisms
\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z}\longrightarrow\Aut((\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n})
is

1+l^{d_{1}}\left(\frac{-1-p^{2}+(p-1)c}{2}\right)

where

c=\begin{cases}p^{2}+1,&\text{ if }d_{1}=d_{2};\\
15,&\text{ if }d_{1}<d_{2}\text{ and }p=2;\\

Here the positive integer

and the group action
\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z}\longrightarrow\Aut((\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n})
are varying. Hence, the minimum is over a class of groups of the type
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z})
. Also note that in view of Lemma
5.1
and Abhyankar’s conjecture (
[
21
]
), there exists an étale
(\mathbb{Z}/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/p^{2}\mathbb{Z})
-cover of the affine line iff

divides
n
.

We will adopt the following convention.

(1)

Unless otherwise mentioned, a cover will mean a connected cover.

(2)

A finite subset
B
of a free
\mathbb{Z}
-module will be called linearly independent if the intersection of submodules generated by any two disjoint subsets of
B
is zero.

(3)

We say that the covers
W_{1}\longrightarrow Y
,
\ldots
,
W_{n}\longrightarrow Y
are mutually linearly disjoint if the fibre product
W_{1}\times_{Y}\ldots\times_{Y}W_{n}
is an irreducible and reduced scheme.

2.
Cyclic covers of a curve and formulae of genus

Let
Y
be a smooth connected projective curve of genus
g
over an algebraically closed field
k
of characteristic
p>0
, and
S
be a finite subset of closed points in
Y
, with
|S|=r
. Let
m>1
be an integer coprime to
p
,
\mathbf{\mu_{m}}=\Spec\mathbb{Z}[T]/(T^{m}-1)
. Let
\Pic(Y)
denote the Picard group of
Y
and
\mathbb{Z}[S]
denote the Cartier divisors of
Y
with support in
S
. Note that
\mathbb{Z}[S]
is a free abelian group with rank
r
. Let
(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})[S]=\mathbb{Z}[S]\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})
be the free module over
\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}
with basis
S
.
Let us define an
m
-torsion abelian group

P_{m}(Y\setminus S):=\frac{\{([L],D)\in\Pic(Y)\oplus\mathbb{Z}[S]\mid L^{\otimes m}\cong\mathcal{O}(-D)\}}{\{(\mathcal{O}(-D),mD)\mid D\in\mathbb{Z}[S]\}}.

(2.1)

Then we have the following isomorphism of groups (see
[
23
, Proposition 3.5]
)

P_{m}(Y\setminus S)\cong H^{1}_{\e}(Y\setminus S,\mathbf{\mu_{m}})\cong\Hom(\pi_{1}^{\e}(Y\setminus S),\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})\cong(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{2g+r-1+b^{(2)}},

where
b^{(2)}=1
if
r=0
, else
b^{(2)}=0
. This gives a bijection between
m
-cyclic étale covers of
Y\setminus S
and the elements
([L],D)\in P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
(for simplicity, we also denote the equivalence class of
([L],D)
by
([L],D)
).

2.1.
Construction of
m
-cyclic covers

Choose
([L],D)\in P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
. We may assume
D
is an effective Cartier divisor with support in
S
, since the multiplicity at each point in
S
can be chosen from
\{0,1,\ldots,m-1\}
. For any
([L],D)\in P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
, we have
L^{\otimes m}\otimes\mathcal{O}(D)=\dv(f)\sim 0
, for a rational function
f\in k(Y)
. The corresponding
m
-cyclic cover (possibly disconnected) is the normalization of
Y
in
k(Y)[t]/(t^{m}-f)
.
If the cover is connected and
D=\sum_{i=1}^{r}a_{i}y_{i}
for
a_{i}\in\{0,1,\ldots,m-1\}
and
y_{i}\in S
,
1\leq i\leq r
, then this cover is tamely ramified at
y_{i}
if and only if
\gcd(m,a_{i})<m
. In this situation, the
ramification index
is
\frac{m}{\gcd(m,a_{i})}
(see
[
3
, 3.15. Lemma]
). Note that
[L]\mapsto([L],0)
gives an injective homomorphism from
\P 0(Y)[m]
to
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
and the corresponding cover will be étale over
Y
. Conversely an
m
-cyclic cover of
Y
, étale over
Y\setminus S
, corresponds to an element
([L],D)
in
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
(see the discussion above
[
23
, Proposition 3.5]

The following well-known result describes important properties of
(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^{n}
-covers of
Y
, étale away from
S
.

Lemma 2.1

(
[
13
, Proposition 3.3]
)
.

For the curve
Y
and the
\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}
-module
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
as above, let
W_{\mathfrak{L}}\longrightarrow Y
be the
m
-cyclic cover corresponding to
\mathfrak{L}\in P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
. Then
\mathfrak{L}
has order
m
in
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
if and only if
W_{\mathfrak{L}}
is a connected
m
-cyclic cover. For a finite subset
B
of
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
,
B
is a
\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}
-basis of a free submodule of
P_{m}(Y\setminus S)
if and only if the set of covers
\{W_{\mathfrak{L}}\longrightarrow Y:\mathfrak{L}\in B\}
are mutually linearly disjoint, i.e., the fibre product
\times_{\mathfrak{L}\in B}W_{\mathfrak{L}}\longrightarrow Y
is an integral scheme. In this case, the normalisation of this fibre product is a
\langle B\rangle
-Galois cover of

that dominates each connected component of

Note that when

.

2.2.
Equation of Artin-Schreier-Witt curves of degree
p^{2}

Let
V\longrightarrow X
be a Galois cover of degree
p^{2}
. Then it is given by a reduced Witt vector
(f_{1},f_{2})
. Then
k(V)=k(X)[x_{1},x_{2}]/I
. When
p
is odd, the ideal
I
is generated by

x_{1}^{p}-x_{1}-f_{1}\text{ and}

x_{2}^{p}-x_{2}+x_{1}^{p(p-1)+1}-\frac{\binom{p}{2}}{p}x_{1}^{p(p-2)+2}+\frac{\binom{p}{3}}{p}x_{1}^{p(p-3)+3}-\ldots-(-1)^{p-1}x_{1}^{p+(p-1)}-f_{2}.

When
p=2
,
I=\langle x_{1}^{2}-x_{1}-f_{1},x_{2}^{2}-x_{2}-x_{1}^{2}+x_{1}^{3}-f_{2}\rangle
(see
[
11
, Chapters 8.10 and 8.11]
or
[
24
]
).

2.3.
Genus formulas in various cases

Let us recall the Riemann-Hurwitz formula and Hilbert’s different formula (
[
10
, Theorem 11.72]
) for calculating the genus of a cover in various cases:

2.3.1.
Genus of tamely ramified covers

Let
W\longrightarrow V
be an
H
-Galois cover, where the order of
H
is coprime to
p
. Let the genera of
W
and
V
be
g_{W}
and
g_{V}
respectively. Since each ramified point in
W
is tamely ramified,
2g_{W}-2=(2g_{V}-2)|H|+\sum_{w\in W}(e_{w}-1)
, where
e_{w}
is the ramification index of
w
over its image in
V
.

2.3.2.
Genus of Artin-Schreier covers

Let
X_{2}\longrightarrow X_{1}
be a
\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
-Galois cover given by
z^{p}-z=F
for a rational function
F
in
k(X_{1})
such that
F\neq f^{p}-f
for any
f\in k(X_{1})
. Then
k(X_{2})=k(X_{1})[z]/\langle z^{p}-z-F\rangle
. Let us assume that all the poles of
F
are of order coprime to
p
. Let
g_{1}
and
g_{2}
be the genera of
X_{1}
and
X_{2}
, respectively. For any ramified point
y\in X_{2}
, let
\mathfrak{n}_{y}
denote the order of the pole of
F
at the image of
y
in
X_{1}
. By the assumption,
\gcd(\mathfrak{n}_{y},p)=1
. Note that the ramified points in
X_{2}
are totally ramified and at each such
y
, the
j
-th lower ramification group is
\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
for
0\leq j\leq\mathfrak{n}_{y}
and others are
\{0\}
. Then

g_{2}=\frac{2+p(2g_{1}-2)+(p-1)\sum_{y\in X_{2}}(\mathfrak{n}_{y}+1)}{2}.

2.3.3.
Genus of Artin-Schreier-Witt covers using upper jumps

Let
\psi:V\longrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{1}
be a
p^{a}
-cyclic cover, étale over
\mathbb{A}^{1}
, and
X_{i}\longrightarrow\mathbb{P}^{1}
be the
p^{i}
-cyclic quotient cover of
\psi
, with genus
g_{i}
,
0\leq i\leq a
. Here
X_{a}=V
,
g_{0}=0
, and
g_{a}=g_{V}
.Then by
[
4
, Theorem 1]

V
is given by a reduced Witt vector of length
a
consisting of polynomials (poles only at
\infty
) with degrees
\mathfrak{d}_{1},\ldots,\mathfrak{d}_{a}
which are coprime-to-
p
or zero. The upper jumps (i.e. breaks in the upper numbering ramification filtration) above
\infty
are given by:

u_{1}:=\mathfrak{d}_{1},u_{2}:=\max\{\mathfrak{d}_{1}p,\mathfrak{d}_{2}\},u_{3}:=\max\{\mathfrak{d}_{1}p^{2},\mathfrak{d}_{2}p,\mathfrak{d}_{3}\},\ldots,u_{a}:=\max\{\mathfrak{d}_{i}p^{a-i}:1\leq i\leq a\}.

It follows immediately that
(u_{1},\ldots,u_{a})
satisfies the following conditions:

Condition 2.2
.

(1)

u_{i}\geq 0
;

(2)

u_{1}\geq 1
and
\gcd(u_{1},p)=1
;

(3)

u_{i}\geq pu_{i-1}
, and
u_{i}=pu_{i-1}
or
\gcd(u_{i},p)=1
for
i>1
.

Conversely, it follows from Artin-Schreier-Witt theory (
[
11
, Theorem 8.31]
) that given any
(u_{1},\ldots,u_{a})
satisfying the above conditions, there are
p^{a}
-cyclic covers of
\mathbb{P}^{1}
étale over
\mathbb{A}^{1}
whose
i
-th upper jump at
\infty
is
u_{i}
,
1\leq i\leq a
.

The genus of
X_{i}
(
[
19
, Lemma 1]
) is given by

\displaystyle g_{i}

\displaystyle=1+p^{i}(0-1)+\frac{p^{i}\left[p^{i}-1+(p-1)\sum_{t=1}^{i}p^{t-1}u_{t}\right]}{2p^{i}}

(2.2)

\displaystyle=\frac{1-p^{i}+(p-1)\sum_{t=1}^{i}p^{t-1}u_{t}}{2}.

(2.3)

Clearly, when
G\cong\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
, the genus of
X_{1}
is
g_{1}=(p-1)(\mathfrak{d}_{1}-1)/2
.

•

•

•

Lemma 5.1
.

Let

over

. But

.

Conversely, if

(see Notation

). For this

be its quasi-

then

for some nonzero

. Let

. Since

and

are normal subgroups of
((Z/l\mathbb{Z})^{n}\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z}))

. Hence,

is a
(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-subrepresentation of

of dimension

(\mathbb{Z}/p^{a}\mathbb{Z})
-subrepresentation

of

. For
