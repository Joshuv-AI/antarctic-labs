President Roosevelt to Prime Minister Churchill[1] (21 November 1943, d152)

secret

[Tunis,] 21 November 1943.

Personal and Secret for the Prime Minister from the President.

Thank you very much for your messages Numbers 502,[2] 503,[3] 504[4] and 505[5] which were delivered to me upon my arrival early yesterday. I expect to reach Sextant early morning November twenty-second, and it will be grand to see you again. I hope your cold is better.

Roosevelt

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] The source text bears the following typed notation: “Above message sent by the President from Tunis to the Prime Minister at Cairo thru local channels.”
[2] Ante, p. 81.
[3] Post, p. 188.
[4] Post, p. 189.
[5] Ante, p. 85.
