 An IDEA Is Born
 Inspired by this unmet need, Ye evaluated several other existing Device
 options for microcapsule production including coating, freeze-
 drying, emulsification, extrusion, and spray drying. None could
 produce the encapsulated material in the quantity, quality, and
 cost required for commercialization, so he turned to a different Fluid
 option: in-air curing. This process does not involve capsules jet
 in solution; rather, droplets are formed and solidified almost
 instantly in air using ultraviolet (UV) light. “In this case,
 the droplets are created in midair and fully cross-linked into
