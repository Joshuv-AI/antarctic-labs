2, NIS Outline Guides

The Army member distributed copiss of revised outline
guides for Sections 31, 32, and 33, and reported that the proposed
changes represented inter-agency working level agreement (refer to
Item 3 NCM 235). The Committee deferred action on the outline
guides until the next meeting in order to afford opportunity for the
members to examine them.

The Navy member proposed by memorandum dated
5 February 195? that a working group be appointed to review the
requirements for Section 56 (Intelligence and Security) and to submit
a revised outline guide covering all important aspects affecting the
internal security of foreign countries (refer to Item 5 NCM 236).

The chairman observed that Sections 56 include specific detailed
information on internal security of foreign countries when such is
available to the contributing office. Be then read portions of
Section 56 on Venesuela to illustrate this point. The Navy member
requested that his proposal be tabled until he could investigate this
matter further. __

3 . Proposed Supplement for Coasts and Landing Beaches

The Navy member reported that the proposed outline guide
on a new supplement for Coasts and La ndi ng Beaches was still being
developed in coordination with the Beach Erosion Board and would be
submitted to the NIS Committee at an early date (refer to Item 8 NCM 236).

The question of assigning an NIS number for this supplement
was left open. The chairman suggested the possibility of using Sup-
plement n "Air Facilities" for this purpose since it is presently
inactive and the published Supplements II should probably be retired
since there are no plans for their revision under the maintenance
program. The chairman requested the Air Force member to determine
any future requirement for Supplements II in view of the reliance on
AFOIN's current periodical "Air Facilities", and the more complete
reference publication "Airfields and Seaplane Stations of the World."
