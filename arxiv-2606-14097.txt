4

Let

be the associated digraph. Then
\delta^{+}(D_{H})=\delta^{c}(H)>n/5
. Given

and

to obtain the reduced digraph

\delta^{+}(\mathcal{R}_{D})\geq(1/5-O(d))m

\mathcal{R}_{D}
contains a
C_{4}
or it contains an
(m/5,\mu)
-blow up of a directed cycle of length five for some

, and we have the following fact.

Fact 4.1
.

Let
\eta>0
. There exist
0<{\varepsilon}\ll d\ll\eta
and

such that either

\eta
-extremal.

Proof.

We will only outline the proof. Given

2.5

and suppose the reduced digraph of

,
\mathcal{R}_{D}

In this section, we will address the case when

(37)

The rest of this section is organized as follows. In Section
