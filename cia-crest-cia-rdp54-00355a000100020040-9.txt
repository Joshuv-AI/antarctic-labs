Outline of Course

1. ORGANIZATION

a* Purpose ; To acquaint the student with the government ' s intelligence
structure through examination of the organizations, missions, and
relationships of its intelligence departments, with detailed dis-
cussion of the Central Intelligence Agency.

b. Scope : This course covers five major areas:

(1) Lines of command, liaison and working level contact among the
intelligence agencies.

(2) Intelligence Advisory Committee

(3) Central Intelligence Agency

(L) Intelligence Organizations and Missions of State, Joint Chiefs
of Staff, and the three Services.

(5) Intelligence activities of other Government Departments and
Agencies.

c. Lectures : Seven lectures will be delivered during this course.

Each will, be followed by a discussion period. This course will also
include four field trips.
