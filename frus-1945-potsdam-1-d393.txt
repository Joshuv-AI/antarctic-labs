Draft Agreement on Treatment of Germany in the Initial Control Period

. . . . . . .

4. War criminals and those who have participated in planning or carrying out Nazi enterprises involving or resulting in atrocities or war crimes shall be arrested, with a view to their ultimate disposal.[3] Nazi leaders and influential Nazi supporters and any other persons dangerous to the occupation or its objectives shall be arrested and interned.

. . . . . . .

Notes

[3] A manuscript change by Byrnes substitutes “and prosecuted to final judgment” for the last seven words of this sentence. In Matthews’ copy of the Briefing Book the words “and brought to judgment” are substituted for the same seven words.
