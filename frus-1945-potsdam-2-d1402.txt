No. 1402 The Ambassador in France (Caffery) to the Acting Secretary of State

top secret

Paris, August 6, 1945—4 p.m.

4722. Bidault tells me confidentially that replies to the various notes I handed to him having to do with the Potsdam Conference[1] will be handed to me within the next day or two. He remarked that his government is very much upset over the statements regarding reparations which they read in the Potsdam communiqué.[2] He said that they are also much concerned about the article referring to the possibility of setting up a central government for Germany[3] for they take it to mean that it is the intention of the Soviet Government to Sovietize all Germany as rapidly as they can do so.

Caffery

Source: 740.00119 Potsdam/8–645: Telegram

Notes
[1] Documents Nos. 1395–1400.
[2] See document No. 1384, sections iii and iv.
[3] The reference is probably to document No. 1384, section ii, paragraph (3)(i).
