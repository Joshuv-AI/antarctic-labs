Dear Stan,

As I noted in a recent letter the time is
rapidly approaching when I will be turning over
the reins of NETC to Captain Ed Whelan and,
coincidentally, retiring from active duty. In
view of the significance of NETC to the Southern
New England area, the Change of Command ceremonies
will have to be done on an appropriate scale. With
that in mind and recognizing the strong links be-
tween you and Newport, it would be awfully nice to
have you as principal speaker. You were respon-
sible for my coming to Newport, you presided at
the Change of Command which saw me take over the
Officer Training Center from and you

were a participant during the Consolidation cere-
monies in April of 1974 which midwifed birth of
NETC. Thus, your speaking in June would complete
the loop. I recognize that this may very well be
an impossible request to fulfill in view of a
schedule which I am sur e presents enormous chal lenges.

Dear Stan,

I have a feeling that you are not looking for
additional reading matter but I forward the en-
closed as a matter of possible interest. Inexorably
30 June is now sweeping down on us and with it my
migration into the pinstripe world. I guess people
tend to become philosophic at a time like this but
I find it difficult to accept the fact that 35 years
have sped by so quickly. Certainly the last five
have moved at Mach 2. What a magnificent 5 years
they have been! I still remember that serendipitous
meeting we had in the Pentagon when you first sug-
gested my coming to Newport.

My regret at leaving with much yet to be done
is tempered only by the fact that I have personally
selected my relief, Ed Whelan. He is currently on
board serving as our Chief Staff and will fleet up
when I leave. He is exactly the kind of person I
would want in this job: perceptive, articulate, calm,
able to sort out the issues. During his previous
tour as Commander Destroyer Squadron TWENTY-EIGHT
here in Newport, he gained real respect among both
the Navy and civilian communities. I am certain
that he is going to do a super job.
