Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

This TCF will focus on developing use-cases for scalable and optimal utilization of hydrogen
systems (combination of electrolyzers, hydrogen storage, and fuel cells) when integrated with the
power grid. We will utilize and mature the electrolyzer dispatch control developed in SWR-20-06.

Summary of Research Results:

Task 1: Cycle the SolarSCADA equipment in one of their chambers through as many cycles as
reasonable. SolarSCADA will return after completion of cycles as needed to photograph and
document any failures occurring during cycle testing to improve the product. This should start by
end of July, with completion before December.

Task 2: Measure the performance of SolarSCADA’s analog input chain through test article
provided, to provide verification of analog input accuracy and long-term drift. Original test
article to be tested whenever equipment available, with a follow-up within 6 months and possibly
12 months from original date.

Task 3: Prepare report with SolarSCADA to help inform our further product development. To be
complete when testing is complete.

Task 4: Other work agreed by both Parties, consistent with the scope and subject to the
availability of funding.

Task 5: NREL will prepare a CRADA Final Report: Preparation and submission in accordance
