B. Conclusio ns

On the basis of this preliminary survey, it is concluded that
CIA cannot concur in an across-the-board downgrading of the documents
in question. Since this downgrading project has as its ultimate purpose
H economy, greater speed transmission and greater ease and convenience
storage and handling...** (TOPOL 812), it is further concluded that this
purpose would be served and intelligence sources and methods protected
by a system such as follows* Documents issued prior to a cut-off date
to be established would be re-classified either Secret or Confidential
by categories on the basis of content. Henceforth, only certain kinds
of information would be included in Secret and Confidential categories
on the basis of criteria to be established by COCCM.

C. Recommendation

It is recommended that a panel consisting of the CIA
Classification Control Officer, and the Assistant Classification Control
Officers of 0SR, OCR, FI and 30 be created to work with a D/E representa-
tive to* study the results of this survey, establish Agency responsibility
for determining the classification of documents involved, and make
appropriate recommendations to FQA/MDAC, the Department of State and the
03DEL Paris.
