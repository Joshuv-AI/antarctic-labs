 Interconnection on the Distribution System
 • Depends on how much power the local distribution network
 can manage (“hosting capacity”)
 • Also depends on local utility’s interconnection policies
 • Hosting capacity information is readily available for some
 utilities and not for others
 • Might not be able to fully interconnect a MW-scale system

 Takeaways

• A wide range of business models and partnership
 agreements exist and can be tuned to
 accommodate specific stakeholder groups
• Best practice is for key stakeholders to start
 collaborating early on project design, leading to
 long-term contracts
• Agrivoltaics might not clearly fit in local zoning
 code, requiring a special use permit and
 engagement with county officials and the public
• Interconnection can be a months- to years-long
 process, and available interconnection capacity
 might impact system design and economics
