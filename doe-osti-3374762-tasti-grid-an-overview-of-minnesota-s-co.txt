EAGLE-I collects utility power outage data every 15 minutes and aggregates them to the county level
(2012 – Present). The data provides accurate information where utilities maintain outage management
  systems or maps and is used to calculate Cost of Outage, SAIDI/SAIFI, and resilience & restoration
 metrics. 79.7% of all Minnesota utility customers’ outage data are captured by EAGLE-I .

ORNL’s novel approach using NASA’s Nighttime Lights Data (NLD) 24-hour satellite images,
calculates daily county-level power outage estimates at 500-meter resolution. NLD fills in gaps where
EAGLE-I is not available and can be used to calculate Cost of Outages and restoration and resilience
metrics. Cook, Cottonwood, Jackson, Lake of the Woods, & Martin counties use NLD (2018-present).
In Tribal communities lacking outage data, NLD can facilitate resilient energy sovereignty discussions.

 Restoration Metrics in TASTI-GRID
Of all US states, DC, and Puerto Rico, Minnesota’s grid resilience ranks A
above the midpoint relative to other states:

• 21st in impact level, or the maximum number of customers
 impacted during extreme events (≈ 4,241.68 customers),

• 12th in recovery duration, (in the top performers) or the amount of
 time it takes to restore power for the maximum number of
 customers (≈ 414.61 minutes – or 6.91 hours), meaning Minnesota
 is adept at recovering from outages, and

• 19th in the per minute recovery rate of customer outages (≈ 10.23
 meters/min).
