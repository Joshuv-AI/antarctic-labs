No. 398 Briefing Book Paper[1]

[Extract][2]

top secret

European Territorial Settlements

i. germany

. . . . . . .

The United States Government is opposed to the separation from Germany of the Ruhr[3] or the left bank of the Rhine either by internationalization or by other means. …

. . . . . . .

[ Washington ,] June 29, 1945.
