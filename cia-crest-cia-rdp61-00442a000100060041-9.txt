2. DESERT SURVIVAL TRAINING MATERIALS t

At C/OS f request, I called Lt. Col. William Adams,

Subsidiary Plans Division, USAF, concerning Air Force materials
for use in the preparation of a survival course covering some
UO hours of instruction and targetted at a Near East area.

C/OS had been requested by DPD/DDP to obtain these materials
from the Air Force. Before calling Col. Adams, we checked
our files to see what we might already have on the subject
and found the following Air Force items:

a. SAC Land Survival Guide Book, July 1952; copy in
Training Branch Library, OCR;

b. Survival, USAF Manual 6U-5, December 1952; copy
in our vault;

c. Sun, Sand and Survival: An Analysis of Survival
Experiences in Desert Areas, USAF ADTIC, January 1953;
copy in Training Branch Library, OCR:

d. Survival, USAF Manual 6U-3, 1956; copy in our vault.

I then called Col, Adams and explained our problem. He said he
was not familiar with these items, but said that the request had
been discussed with him previously by someone in the Agency whose
name he could not remember. He had suggested a memorandum be
forwarded to the Subsidiary Plans Division setting forth, in
general, the objectives to be covered by the course. He said
he had pointed out to the requestor that it would probably
take from six weeks to two months to fill the request. We
are preparing the memorandum for the DTR's signature. Col. Adams
assured me it would receive his prompt attention and that he
would keep me advised as to the progress made.
