Economic Defense Program

1. The IWG Secretariat is continuing to make every effort to insure
that intelligence material produced in connection with the activities of
the Intelligence Working Group (IWG) is distributed only to security-
cleared persons within the Government. As a follow^-up to our recent
conversation on the subject, I am forwarding the following list of those
persons who, to my present knowledge, are now or have in the past been
engaged in some asi«:t of the economic defense program.

2. These persons may be direitly involved in the program through
membership on one or more of the several interagency committees in this
field - the Economic Defense Advisory Committee (EDAC), the Executive
Committee of EDAC and its working groups, the Joint Operating Committee
(JOC), the Advisory Committee on Expat Policy (ACEP), its technical task
groups, and the IWG. If not designated members on one of the foregoing,
these persons may presently be employed by an agency having responsibility
in the economic defense field,

3. This list is furnished alphabetically with no listing by committee
with the exception of current IWG membership, indicated by double asterisk.
A single asterisk is indicated for persons other than IWG members on Whom

the IWG Secretariat holds a statement of security clearance from an agency
other than CIA.
