4 Other Approaches

It is also possible to express ∇×(∇×E0 ) = ∇(∇·E0 )−∇2 E0 where ∇2 E0 is the vector Laplacian
that must be chosen appropriately for the coordinate system being used. This has a simpler
form than the double curl, so assuming that ∇ · E0 = 0 by one of Maxwell’s equations, it gets
 2
to the second-order recurrence relations more quickly. The expression ∇2 E0 = − ωc2 E0 is known
as the Helmholtz equation. The downside of this approach is that the first-order recurrence
relation is not recovered, because it comes from the assumption that ∇ · E0 = 0, so it must be
added manually by substituting the series for E0 into that equation.
