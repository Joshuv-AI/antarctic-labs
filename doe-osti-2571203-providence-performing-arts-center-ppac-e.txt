 II. Executive Summary

The Energy Efficient Upgrades project at the Providence Performing Arts Center
(“PPAC” or the “Theatre”) represents a significant investment in modernizing
infrastructure, enhancing performance capabilities and improving visitor experience, all
while promoting sustainability. The project’s primary goals included the installation of
building-wide WIFI coverage, replacement of the outdated theatrical lighting system with
energy-efficient alternatives, upgrades to the telephone systems and enhancements to
the internal sound systems, both backstage and in the lobby.

This initiative deepens understanding of how historic performance venues can integrate
modern technologies without compromising architectural integrity or artistic function. It
serves as a model for how cultural institutions can adapt to 21st-century technological
standards while preserving their unique character. Research and implementation in this
project demonstrated how energy-efficient lighting and digital communication systems
can reduce long-term operating costs and environmental impact, offering both technical
effectiveness and economic feasibility. For example, LED theatrical lighting not only
reduces electricity consumption and heat output but also lowers maintenance costs due
to longer fixture lifespans.

The project is particularly beneficial to the public by ensuring a more inclusive,
connected and immersive experience for patrons, artists and staff. Enhanced WIFI
supports accessibility features and improves operational efficiency, while upgraded
sound systems and lighting enrich the quality of the performances. By maintaining its
status as a top-tier venue, PPAC continues to drive cultural tourism and economic
vitality in downtown Providence and for the entire state of Rhode Island. Ultimately, this
project supports sustainability, accessibility and artistic excellence, reinforcing PPAC’s
role as a dynamic and forward-thinking community asset.

 III. Accomplishments, Goals & Objectives Met

The Energy Efficient Upgrades project at PPAC was conceived as a strategic initiative
to modernize critical infrastructure, enhance energy efficiency and improve the overall
experience for patrons, staff and performers alike. At its core, the project aimed to
reduce the venue’s environmental footprint, replace outdated systems and position the
Theatre as a leader in sustainable and technologically advanced performing arts
venues. Each objective was carefully aligned with PPAC’s broader mission to provide
world-class performances in a space that is operationally efficient, accessible and
future-ready.

Among the most transformative achievements of this project was the installation of
building-wide WIFI. For the first time in its history, PPAC now has seamless wireless
internet coverage across the entire facility, from the auditorium and lobbies to backstage
corridors, administrative offices and dressing rooms. This advancement has unlocked
new levels of digital readiness and accessibility. Visitors can now benefit from mobile
ticketing and real-time program updates, while staff and performers enjoy uninterrupted
connectivity essential for daily operations, production management and emergency
preparedness. Additionally, this upgrade lays the groundwork for future enhancements
such as real-time captioning, assistive listening apps and other digital accessibility tools
that will make performances more inclusive for all audiences.

The replacement of the theatrical lighting system has also been fully realized. Outdated,
high-energy lighting fixtures were removed and replaced with modern LED theatrical
lighting. This transition dramatically reduces energy consumption, lowers heat output
and extends the lifespan of each fixture, resulting in lower operating and maintenance
costs. Beyond these practical benefits, the new system offers creative advantages.
Programmable LED fixtures expand the range of visual possibilities available to lighting
designers, enriching the storytelling potential of every production staged at PPAC.
These artistic gains were achieved alongside measurable reductions in electricity
usage, directly supporting environmental sustainability goals.

The project also included the complete overhaul of PPAC’s internal telephone system.
Replacing the aging analog infrastructure with a Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP)
solution has streamlined internal communications and improved operational agility. Staff
can now access voicemail remotely, coordinate across departments more efficiently and
respond more quickly in urgent situations. The new system has proven both cost-
effective and reliable, with the added benefit of integrating seamlessly with the building’s
upgraded digital infrastructure.

 Upgraded Phone Administrative Console

In addition to lighting and communications, the internal sound system was significantly
improved. Focused upgrades were made to the backstage and lobby areas, which are
essential zones for both audience experience and behind-the-scenes coordination.
Backstage, the new audio system supports clearer communication among technical
staff and performers, enhancing performance quality and safety. In the lobby, visitors
are now greeted with clear, evenly distributed sound, improving the delivery of pre-show
announcements, ambient music and emergency notifications. These enhancements not
only elevate the visitor experience but also reinforce PPAC’s commitment to high
standards of hospitality and accessibility.

The benefits of the Energy Efficient Upgrades project extend well beyond the walls of
the Theater. By reducing energy consumption, embracing sustainable technologies and
expanding digital infrastructure, PPAC is setting an example for how historic
performance venues can adapt to modern expectations while preserving their legacy.
These upgrades reaffirm PPAC’s role not only as a venue for entertainment but as a
steward of community development, innovation and environmental responsibility.

The Energy Efficient Upgrades project at PPAC is more than a facilities improvement
effort - it is a meaningful extension of the Theatre’s broader commitment to community
enrichment and engagement. As a cornerstone of downtown Providence’s cultural
landscape, PPAC welcomes thousands of community members each year - not only as
patrons of the performing arts but also as students, educators, artists and neighbors
who participate in a wide range of outreach and educational programs. Ensuring that
this historic space remains safe, comfortable and environmentally responsible is vital to
the continued success and impact of these efforts.

In essence, the Energy Efficient Upgrades project is not only a reflection of PPAC’s
dedication to environmental stewardship but also a strategic investment in the future of
its community programs. By fostering a modern, welcoming and sustainable space,
PPAC reaffirms its role as a cultural and civic anchor, ensuring that the transformative
power of live performance remains accessible, inclusive and impactful for generations to
come.

Collectively, the Energy Efficient Upgrade accomplishments fulfilled and exceeded the
original goals of the project. PPAC now operates more efficiently, with lower
environmental impact, greater technical capacity and improved service to the public.
Importantly, these upgrades position the Theater to remain competitive in the
performing arts landscape, capable of attracting top-tier touring productions and serving
as a cornerstone of Rhode Island’s cultural and economic ecosystem.

“This grant has allowed PPAC to bring our infrastructure into the 21st century,
integrating state-of-the-art technologies in lighting, WIFI, telephone and
video/communications systems,” said PPAC’s General Manager Alan Chille. “These
improvements not only deliver significant energy savings but also enhance the patron
experience, all made possible through strategic investment in essential capital
upgrades.”

Conclusion:

The completion of the Energy Efficient Upgrades project marks a transformative
moment in the Providence Performing Arts Center’s long-standing legacy as a cultural
and civic leader in Rhode Island. By integrating sustainable technologies, upgrading
critical infrastructure and enhancing the experience for performers, staff and audiences,
the Theatre has not only modernized its operations but reaffirmed its commitment to
innovation, accessibility and environmental stewardship.

This project demonstrates how historic venues can successfully evolve to meet
contemporary needs without compromising their architectural heritage or artistic
mission. Through thoughtful planning and strategic investment, PPAC now operates
with greater energy efficiency, technical reliability and digital readiness. These
improvements will generate lasting economic, environmental and community benefits
while lowering operating costs, reducing environmental impact and improving access to
world-class performing arts experiences.

Looking ahead, these upgrades position PPAC to serve as a model for other historic
theaters seeking to modernize responsibly and sustainably. With this project complete,
PPAC is better equipped than ever to fulfill its mission: to serve the entertainment needs
of Rhode Island, connect the community to educational opportunities, preserve the
historic integrity of the building and maintain fiscal responsibility while engaging and
enriching the lives of all who enter its doors.
