Adopted unanimously by the Security Council at its 871st meeting, on 5 July 1960
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of Somalia [1],
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Somalia be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

[1] Official Records of the Security Council, Fifteenth Year, Supplement for July, August, and September 1960, document S/4360.
