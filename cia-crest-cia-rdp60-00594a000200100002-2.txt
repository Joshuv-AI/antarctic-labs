1. The Committee on Language Development met on 10 October
to discuss:

a. Submittals of Form 444c as of 30 September

b. Status of proficiency test development and the plans

for administering the tests ^

c. Applications for awards for foreign language achieve-
ment.

2. The first group of applications for Language Achievement _
Awards was adjudicated at a special meeting of the Committee on
Thursday, 31 October. A total of $725.00 was awarded to ten of the
thirteen applicants; two were ineligible; and the payment to a third
was deferred until a more accurate check can be made as to whether
or not the training was directed.

The Committee based its decisions on the following poli-

a. The awardable level for comprehensive proficiency
in a language will be the lowest level achieved in tests in
speaking, writing, and reading the language.

b. The total amount for a series of achievement awards

will not exceed the amount specified in | to

be awarded for attainment of the highest levels of proficiency
in the language .

c . The amount of an achievement award will be the
difference between the sum payable for the initial level of
proficiency and the amount authorized for the next level
achieved.
