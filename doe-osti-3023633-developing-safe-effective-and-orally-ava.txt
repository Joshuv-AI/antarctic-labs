Executive Summary of CRADA Work:

As part of the Cyclotron Road program, HOPO Therapeutics investigated the technical
capabilities of heavy metal chelating agents for the sequestration, (re)distribution, delivery,
and removal of metal ions, and their applications in the health, environment, and energy
sectors. The project team researched chelating agents for use in the removal of heavy

metals, and evaluated their potential benefits for a range of therapeutic, environmental, and
energy applications.

Summary of Research Results:

The project team explored the complete range of heavy metal binding properties for
relevant ions, and the development of formulation, dosing, and application strategies that
can optimize an intended therapeutic benefit or other desired outcome. The research
assessed the decorporation efficacy of chelating agents for removal of bismuth, lead,
gadolinium, and other heavy metals and determined the decorporation efficacy of chelating
agents for removal of a mixture of heavy metals. The team explored methods to optimize
efficacy of chelation and sequestration for chelating agents delivered via a range of
formulation and dosing methods and evaluated additional applications of chelation agents
in areas beyond therapeutics, and in combination with other therapeutic products.
