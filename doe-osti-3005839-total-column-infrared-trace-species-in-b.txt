1.0 Summary
 The U.S. Department of Energy’s Atmospheric Radiation Measurement (ARM) User Facility
 Coast-Urban-Rural Atmospheric Gradient Experiment (CoURAGE), including the first ARM Mobile
 Facility (AMF1), occurred during 2025 at a site in Baltimore, Maryland owned by Morgan State
 University. Our goal was to supplement the measurements taken by locating a Bruker EM27/SUN
 spectrometer at the site to make total column measurements of trace gases in the near-infrared. This
 EM27 location was one of six locations in the Washington, D.C./Maryland region during the summer of
 2025, and the network of observations was intended to help us better understand emissions in the region,
 as well as how emissions interact with meteorology to affect air quality and other atmospheric attributes.

 We deployed an EM27/UN to the AMF1 location in Baltimore for the summer months of 2025. The
 instrument was intended for use by Morgan State principal investigators of a funded NASA proposal call
 and their chosen deployment location was the AMF1. The original plan for the summer was to hire a
 Morgan State undergraduate student to operate the instrument but when that fell through, there was no
 urgency to get an instrument operational at the site because it would not be routinely operated. Deploying
 the instrument still gave some valuable lessons learned for the Goddard Space Flight Center EM27 team
 as we move towards deploying instruments at 10 university locations in the next year.
