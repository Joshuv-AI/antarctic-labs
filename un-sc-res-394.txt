Adopted unanimously at the 1952th meeting, on 16 August 1976.
The Security Council,
Having examined the application of the Republic of Seychelles[1] for admission to the United Nations,
Recommends to the General Assembly that the Republic of Seychelles be admitted to membership in the United Nations.

1. ↑ Official Records of the Security Council, Thirty-first Year. Supplement for July, August and September 1976, document S/12164.
