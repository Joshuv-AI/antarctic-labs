2 Review ofjChapter 3 on Bulgaria

The Committee reviewed the Chapter I on NTS 23 (Bulgaria)
and approved the draft with certain changes. The Chapter I Task
Group was requested to review the detailed comments submitted by
the member agencies. In particular, the Task Group was requested
to substitute the name "Varna” for "Stalin" if the former has been
accepted by BGN; otherwise both names will be used with "Varna"
in parenthesis following "Stalin".

NIS Contributor Statements

The Committee approved with a few minor changes, the
revised NIS Contributor Statements which were passed out for review
at the last meeting (refer to Item 5 NCM 248).

4 NIS^Area Name Change

The State member reported in memorandum dated
5 July 1957 that the British Caribbean Federation had been formalized
(refer to Item 7 NCM 238) and proposed that the areas concerned be
designated as follows:

NIS 81 - British Western Atlantic Possessions

NIS 81A - British Caribbean Federation

NIS 8 IB - Bermuda and Bahama Islands

The Committee approved State's proposed change in
NIS area designation. Since British Honduras and British Guiana
have not joined the Federation, the Committee agreed that these
areas should remain as NIS 72 and 95A, respectively, and that
they be absorbed into NIS 81A should they join the Federation
later on.

The Air Force member reported that Section 23 on
NIS 81 was in an advanced stage of preparation and a change at
this time to conform to the new area breakdown would require
complete reworking of the section, which would create a major
production problem. It was agreed that this section would be
completed in its present form, but all new production and all
sections not far advanced in preparation should be prepared
according to the new area designations.

5 N IS Prod uction Forecast

The Vice Chairman asked for a report on the status of
the agencies proposed production forecast for FY 1959-60-61,
deadline for which had been set for 15 July 1957 (refer to Item 2
NCM 248). He referred to the OBI comments and suggestions
relative to the forecast which had been submitted to the members
on 9 July for consideration by their respective agencies, and noted
that these were prepared following the close of Fiscal Year 1957,
so they reflect the production situation as of the start of this fiscal
year.

State reported that the many changes which they had to
make in the FY 1958 production schedule because of defections in
FY 1957 will necessitate extensive changes in the three-year
forecast on which they are now working.

Army reported that they are well along on the forecast,
and expect to have a consolidated statement of comments by the end
of the week. Tentative comments were submitted from coordinators
of Chapters II, III, and Section 80..

Navy reported that they had a rough forecast, but
wished to coordinate with Army before submitting it to OBI.

Air Force submitted tentative forecast schedule for
Sections 23, 37, and 83.

The Navy member distributed copies of a memorandum
from CINCPAC dated 9 July 1957 listing certain NIS areas on which
early coverage is desired for Pacific Theater planning purposes
{refer to Item 6c NCM 245). Navy considered these requirements
in the preparation of its forecast, and felt that it would be helpful
if the other agencies would do likewise The Vice Chairman re-
quested the members to examine this request with a view to
determining what adjustments could be made in their forecast
schedules to meet it.

Following a general discusnion on various aspects of
the forecast scheduling, the Committee agreed to a tentative target
date of 30 July for all agencies to submit forecast proposals to OBI.

6 NIS Reference Guide

Revised page proofs of the NIS Reference Guide were
distributed to the members The Vice Chairman stated that this
page proof contained all changes approved at the last meeting (refer
to Item 6 NCM 248), and that it would go to the printer shortly.

A.ny comment on the page proofs by the members should, therefore,
be forthcoming without delay.

The Vice Chairman informed the Committee that in the
initial dissemination of the NIS Reference Guide a copy of the
current NIS Quarterly Report will be contained in the pocket on the
hack cover. It was pointed out that State and the Defense Agencies
now have standing requirements for the NIS Quarterly Report and
that many of the present recipients of these reports will in all
probability also receive the NIS Reference Guide containing the
same report.. Because of the time gap between issuance of the
latest Quarterly Report (30 June 1957) and the issuance of the
NIS Reference Guide (about mid-August) some duplication in
dissemination of the NIS Quarterly Report is unavoidable. However,
members ware requested to re-examine their requirements for the
next NIS Quarterly Report which will be issued on 30 September 1957,
and advise of any changes that may be necessary.

The Navy member reported that his agency had re-
examined its requirements for the NIS Reference Guide and that
Navy will need 600 copies rather than I, 200 as previously
requested (refer to Item 5 NCM 245).
