What is PPE? sdigre@sandia.gov

• Polyphenylene Ether (PPE) a.k.a. Polyphenylene Oxide (PPO)
• Widely used, high-performance engineering thermoplastic
• Outstanding thermal, mechanical and electrical properties
• Usually blended with polystyrene (PS) to improve processability
• Commercially availably PPE/PS blends:
 • Noryl™ from SABIC Innovative Plastics
 • XYRON™ from Asahi Kasei

Summary of polymers used in PV connectors sdigre@sandia.gov

  • PV connector housings primarily use one of three materials:
 • Polycarbonate
 • Polyphenylene Ether + Polystyrene (PPE/PS)
 • Polyamide
  • PPE/PS is one of the most common materials
 • Best: dielectric strength, thermal expansion, and water absorption
 • Worst: elongation, brittleness, and cost

State of the emerging cracking problem sdigre@sandia.gov

• This year, Sandia has been contacted by three independent developers
  reporting a similar cracking issue
  • Problem affects one model (PPE-based, module-to-module)
  • Cracks appear just months after installation
  • Site-wide cracking
  • Remediation involves complete connector replacements at the cost of millions
  • Sandia is unsure how many sites are affected

Chemical differences between pristine and aged specimens sdigre@sandia.gov

 Pristine Aged and Cracked

  • Aged and cracked connectors tend to have additional peaks near 3300
 and 1700, not seen in pristine connectors
  • Peak at 3300 could be related to water uptake
  • Peak at 1700 could be related to oxidation
  • Baseline shift likely unrelated to chemical differences (penetration depth)
 29

Summary sdigre@sandia.gov

• Polymer cracks are not a new problem in the PV industry
 • However, the suddenness and severity of the cracking connector model is new
 and concerning
• Problem recap:
 • Some sites are experiencing wide-spread cracking of a specific connector model

• Preliminary investigation results:
 • Potential clearance issues with the O-ring, leading to high mechanical stress
 • Potential use of lubrication → polymer embrittlement
 • Could not demonstrate clear mechanical or chemical differences between the affected
 model and other PPE-based models using FTIR and tensile testing.
