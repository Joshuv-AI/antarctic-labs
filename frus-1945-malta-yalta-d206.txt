Marshal Stalin to President Roosevelt (10 December 1944, d206)

Translation[1]

Personal and Secret from Premier J. V. Stalin to President Franklin Roosevelt.

Thank you for your reply[2] on the French question. Together with General De-Gaulle we came to a decision that the conclusion of the Franco-Soviet Pact of mutual assistance will be beneficial to the cause of the French-Soviet relations as well as for the European security in general. Today the Franco-Soviet Pact was signed.[3]

As to the post-war border of France, the consideration of this question, as I have already written to you,[4] has been postponed.

December 10, 1944.

Source: Roosevelt Papers
