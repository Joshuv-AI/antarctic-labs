Public Law 119–11: Disapproving the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Defining Larger Participants of a Market for General-Use Digital Consumer Payment Applications”.

Joint Resolution Disapproving the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Defining Larger Participants of a Market for General-Use Digital Consumer Payment Applications”.May 9, 2025[S.J. Res. 28]

That Congress disapproves the final rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Defining Larger Participants of a Market for General-Use Digital Consumer Payment Applications” (89 Fed. Reg. 99582 (December 10, 2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

Approved May 9, 2025.
