None to report.

B. OTHER ACTIVITIES

1. Three Part-Time Basic (RSW) classes in Romance languages
started this week. One class in Spanish with six students, two
in French with six and five students respectively. One Romanian
Basic Reading class also started with four students. This brings
the teaching load in the Romance section to 94 hours weekly, a
maximum with the present staff.

2. This has otherwise been a routine week, with everyone
concentrating on the classes which have just begun.
