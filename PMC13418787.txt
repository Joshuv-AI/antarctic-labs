Retraction Note: MiR-20a-5p represses multi-drug resistance in osteosarcoma by targeting the KIF26B gene
Authors: Youguang Pu, Qiyi Yi, Fangfang Zhao, Haiyan Wang, Wenjing Cai, Shanbao Cai
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Cancer Cell Int (2016) 16:64

10.1186/s12935-016-0340-3

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this article. After publications concerns were raised about an image similarity across articles from shared authors. Specifically, there appear to be high similarity between Fig. 5B, NC in this article and Fig. 5, 5PM in [1].

Additional concerns were also raised about animal experiments conducted in this research. In particular, Fig. 7B appears to show that the welfare of the animals used in this study does not adhere to ethical guidelines.

Upon request, the Authors acknowledged the image similarity but didn’t provide any explanation to the concerns raised.

Due to the lack of sufficient explanation and indication of breaches of the Journal’s editorial policies for research involving animals, the Editors-in-Chief no longer has confidence in the results and conclusions of this article.

None of the authors responded to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction notice.
