The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President (8 February 1945, d397)

[Yalta, February 8, 1945.][1]

Mr. President:—The Joint Chiefs of Staff told Welles[2] that it would be difficult for us to defend their coasts against Japan. That is Welles excuse.

Harry

Source: Hopkins Papers

Notes
[1] This note is undated but is assumed to have been passed to the President at the plenary session on February 8 in connection with the President’s reference to Welles and the reasons why certain South American states had not declared war. See ante, p. 773.
[2] The Department of Defense states that there is no evidence in the files of the Joint Chiefs of Staff to substantiate this statement.
