Introductory Note to Final Technical Report – Phase 1 Performance Report
Narrative (PRN)

September 24, 2025
Pursuant to guidance provided by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations
(OCED) on June 5, 2025, The American Cast Iron Pipe Company (AMERICAN) is submitting its last quarterly report
– the Phase 1 Performance Report Narrative (PRN) to comply with the requirement of submitting a Final Technical
Report, in lieu of producing an entirely new report.

The report following this introductory note covers the period from November 20, 2020, through the end of the first
quarter of calendar year 2025 (March 31, 2025) and was submitted on April 29, 2025. This was the final quarterly
report submitted prior to DOE’s termination of Cooperative Agreement DE-CD0000084 on May 29, 2025.

Due to the termination of the Cooperative Agreement during Budget Period / Phase 1, the report does not contain the
scientific findings typical of other Final Technical Reports. Instead, it provides an update on AMERICAN’s progress in
completing all Budget Period / Phase 1 Deliverables as of March 31, 2025.

Given the unique nature of this document and how it will be presented for public consumption, additional clarifications
should be provided:

 1. Redactions have been made to protect AMERICAN’s strategic business information that would not normally
 be included in a technical report aimed at sharing scientific knowledge.

 2. All deliverables referenced in this report that were submitted as a part of the Cooperative Agreement were
 submitted under a Limited Rights Data designation. Due to the elapsed time between March 31, 2025, and
 the termination of the Cooperative Agreement on May 29, 2025, many of the deliverables described as “Not
 Yet Submitted,” were in fact submitted prior to the termination. These too were submitted with a Limited
 Rights Designation.

 1 Overall Progress Summary
 1.1 Project Context

 In November 2024, AMERICAN entered into a Cooperative Agreement with the Office of Clean
 Energy Demonstrations (OCED) regarding the Industrial Decarbonization and Emissions
 Reduction Demonstration-To-Deployment Funding Opportunity for the “Right Way” Next
 Generation Melt Project (“Project”). This report encompasses initial planning and analysis
 activities to ensure that the project is viable.

 1.2 Use of Terminology

 This report uses ‘Phase’ or ‘Budget Period’ to refer to the period of performance outlined in the
 Cooperative Agreement for the current phase, Phase (Budget Period) 1, from November 20,
 2024 – May 31, 2025, and ‘Reporting Period’ to refer to the period from November 20, 2024 –
 March 31, 2025. Future performance reports will typically limit reporting periods to three-month
 intervals that align with federal reporting quarters (January-March, April-June, July-September,
 and October-December). However, because OCED waived the reporting requirements for the
 fourth quarter of calendar year 2024, this Reporting Period starts at the project start date and
 extends through the end of March 2025.

 1.3 Overall Project Progress

 AMERICAN has made significant progress toward achieving the vision and objectives of the
 project, with several key milestones and deliverables successfully completed during this
 reporting period. These achievements have advanced the project closer to its overarching
 goals, demonstrating effective implementation of planned strategies. The key accomplishments,
 based on the Award-Specific Terms and Conditions of the Cooperative Agreement (Term 3
 Table 3-1), during this reporting period include:

 • Continued to advance the design of the “Right Way” Next Generation Melt project,

 • Developed a list of production requirements based on a review of historical production
 rates (hourly, daily, yearly), potential market growth, ladle delivery timing, chemical and
 temperature limits, and other provisions.
 • Finalized specifications for the furnace, major equipment, and facility layout.
 • Completed and received approval for the Air Quality Impacts Quantification Plan.
 • Submitted for review the Environmental Information Volume (EIV) Ch. 1-3 for Phase 3-4
 activities and updated Environmental Considerations Summary (ECS) for Phase 2.
 • Finalized and submitted for review a Permitting Plan, including an initial list of all state,
 local, and federal approvals and permits required before construction begins.
 • Developed the site safety plan. This deliverable is under review as of the end of this
 reporting period.
 • Defined individual project requirements to finalize the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
 • Refined and updated the Integrated Project Schedule (IPS).
 • Received OCED’s confirmation to proceed with the procurement of two long lead
 equipment purchase orders for the furnaces and the cranes
 .
 • Refined the project budget for Phase 2 to a +/- 25% accuracy level.

 • Confirmed the project funding requirements and sources.
 • Conducted a second risk workshop and updated the Risk Register.
 • Developed initial workforce and training plans for operations and maintenance
 personnel.
 • Submitted for review the Continuation Application to advance to Budget Period 2.

 1.4 Areas of Risk or Concern

 AMERICAN has not encountered any major challenges during this reporting period that would
 significantly impact the project’s progress. The project vision and objectives remain unchanged
 and are aligned with the original plan. Similarly, the approach and strategies for project
 execution have not required significant adjustments, as the current methods have proven
 effective in achieving the desired outcomes.

 AMERICAN has approached the progress and work during this reporting quarter with flexibility
 and collaboration. Dedicated working groups have been established for each workstream to
 draft deliverables, report on progress, and collaborate effectively through weekly meetings.
 These efforts have been complemented by weekly steering committee meetings with leadership
 and semi-monthly coordination meetings with OCED.

 These regular touchpoints have allowed AMERICAN to proactively address risks, issues, and
 barriers through ongoing risk management and mitigation strategies. In tandem, AMERICAN’s
 risk workshop and updates to the Risk Register have been instrumental in identifying emerging
 risks early and managing them effectively.

 8 Technology Maturation Update (if applicable)

 8.1 Technology Maturation Update Risks and Concerns

 AMERICAN currently does not expect any additional risks or issues concerned with technology
 maturation outside of what was mentioned in the Technology Maturation Plan. The foundation of
 this project’s technology implementation builds upon well-established induction melting
 applications in the foundry industry. Through site visits to facilities
 AMERICAN has documented successful
 implementation of medium-frequency coreless induction furnaces at various scales.

 There are two key technological advances stemming from this project:
 • The scale of operations will be greater than that of the largest domestic induction-melting
 foundry
 •

 AMERICAN’s approach to risk management recognizes the unique challenges of both scale-up
 operations and process control. Scale-up risks will be addressed through systematic capacity
 validation that includes emissions testing. Process control risks,
 will be mitigated through robust control protocols and rapid
 response procedures for any deviations. Integration risks between systems will be managed
 through comprehensive interface planning, testing, and validation of material handling system
 compatibility.
