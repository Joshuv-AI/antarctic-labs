The Ambassador in Turkey (Steinhardt) to the President, the Secretary of State, and the Under Secretary of State (Stettinius)[1] (14 November 1943, d127)

Ankara, November 14, 1943—5 p.m.

1869. Most secret for the President, the Secretary and the Under Secretary.

In expressing his keen pleasure with President Roosevelt’s letter of October 26 to President Inönü,[2] the Minister for Foreign Affairs[3] remarked to me last night that Inönü would be delighted to meet our President and that if President Roosevelt could not be persuaded to visit Turkey, he would be more than pleased to go to meet him should he visit any other part of the Near East.

Steinhardt

Source: 811.001 Roosevelt, F. D./9453½: Telegram

Notes
[1] On November 17, 1943, the White House Map Room telegraphed a paraphrase of this message to Greer, “to be held for delivery to the President upon arrival at Oran.”
[2] Ante, p. 43.
[3] Numan Menemencioğlu.
