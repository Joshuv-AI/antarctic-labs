No. 771 Proposal by the Soviet Delegation[1]

[Translation]

[Babelsberg,] July 30, 1945.

Regarding Reparations From Italy and Austria Soviet Proposal

Italy and Austria must pay reparations in compensation of those losses which they caused the United Nations by their participation in the war on the side of Germany.

Reparations shall be paid in the form of immediate removals of equipment from those branches of their economy which are the most important elements of their military potential. Such removals should be concluded during a period of one year.

… The Allied Reparations Commission shall define the amount of reparations from Austria.

… Austria [shall pay reparations] to the Soviet Union and Yugoslavia.

Source: 740.00119 (Potsdam)/7–3045
