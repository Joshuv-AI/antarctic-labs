No. 1085 The Acting Secretary of State to the Secretary of State

[Washington,] July 31 [1945.]

167. Department is instructing Embassies Moscow and London to inform FonOffs of our recommendation of appointment of tripartite commission in accordance with urtel Victory 392, July 28,[1] and to solicit indication by FonOffs of agreement of Soviet and British Governments to collaborate in this matter.

[Grew]
