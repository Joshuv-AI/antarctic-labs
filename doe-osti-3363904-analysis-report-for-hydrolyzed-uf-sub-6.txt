 1. PURPOSE

Under the auspices of the US Department of Energy/National Nuclear Security Agency’s Nuclear
Reference Material Program (NRMP), the Material Signatures and Isotopic Standards (MSIS) group of
Oak Ridge National Laboratory was tasked with analyzing two UF6 filled hoke tubes for uranium isotopic
composition. This report documents the results of the measurements performed by the MSIS group’s
International Organization for Standardization/International Electrotechnical Commission 17025:2017
accredited operating procedure CSD-AM-CIMS-IN20, Determination of Uranium and Plutonium
Isotopic Composition using Thermal Ionization Mass Spectrometry [1], and in accordance with the
quality assurance plan as described in QAP-X-96-CSD/RML-001, Nuclear Analytical Chemistry
Laboratory Section Quality Assurance Plan [2].

 2. PREPARATION Of SAMPLES

Samples were received in P-10 tubes, as shown in Figure 1.

 Figure 1. P-10 tubes containing UF6 material before hydrolyzation.

The hydrolyzation process was completed using ASTM C1346-19. The as-received P-10 tubes were
thoroughly cleaned and allowed to dry in a laminar flow hood, and then the clean tubes were weighed.
The P-10 tubes were subsequently submerged in liquid N2 for 10 min. The hardware was then quickly
removed, and the P-10 tubes were submerged in 18.2 MΩ·cm H2O and gently swirled on a hot plate at
60°C until all material was dissolved from the tube. The vessel was thoroughly washed with water to
remove any remaining material, and the solution was quantitatively transferred to a tared low-density
polyethylene bottle, yielding a master solution containing the species UO2F2 in dilute HF. Because this
matrix is not commonly analyzed, aliquots of the master solution were matrix-converted by drying down
the solution on a hot plate to near dryness and then bringing the solution back up to volume with 30%
HNO3. This dry-down and reconstitution process was completed three times for each aliquot to ensure
conversion of uranium to the nitrate form.

Aliquots of the matrix-converted materials were sampled and prepared for thermal ionization mass
spectrometry.

 3. ISOTOPIC MEASUREMENTS

Isotopic measurements were performed according to the modified total evaporation (MTE) method, using
NRMP certified reference material (CRM) U030-A as the comparator standard and NRMP CRM U200 as
the quality control (QC) standard. An additional QC, Institute for Reference Materials and Measurements
(IRMM) 024, was included as a process QC.

The MTE analytical methodology followed the double filament configuration described in ASTM
C1832-23, Determination of Uranium Isotopic Composition by Modified Total Evaporation (MTE)
Method Using Thermal Ionization Mass Spectrometer [4].
