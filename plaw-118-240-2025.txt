Public Law 118–240: To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 135 West Spring Street in Titusville, Pennsylvania, as the “Edwin L. Drake Post Office Building”.

An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 135 West Spring Street in Titusville, Pennsylvania, as the “Edwin L. Drake Post Office Building”.Jan. 4, 2025[H.R. 7417]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. — EDWIN L. DRAKE POST OFFICE BUILDING.

SUBSECTION (a) — Designation

The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 135 West Spring Street in Titusville, Pennsylvania, shall be known and designated as the “Edwin L. Drake Post Office Building”.

SUBSECTION (b) — References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Edwin L. Drake Post Office Building”.

Approved January 4, 2025.
