 Executive Summary
The aqueous waste from the Salt Waste Processing Facility is sampled semiannually for transfers to the Saltstone Disposal
Facility. Salt solution is treated at the Saltstone Production Facility and disposed of in the Saltstone Disposal Facility. Per
request of customer, X-TTR-Z-00027, Revision 0, one Saltstone Processing Facility Decontaminated Salt Solution and Z-
area premix material for the October 2025 semiannual Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure sample. The sample
contained 60:40 (by weight) of slag and fly ash (referred to as the “Cement-Free grout sample”). Results from the technical
report support Task 2: ‘Grout Leaching Analyses’ of the Task Technical Request prepared by Savannah River Mission
Completion. At 51 days cured, the Saltstone sample was collected and shipped to a certified laboratory for analysis using
the Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure. The October 2025 Semiannual Cement-Free grout sample met 61-
79.268.48 requirements for a non-hazardous waste form with respect to the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act
metals and Underlying Hazardous Constituents and met the Saltstone Production Facility Waste Acceptance Criteria that
was in effect at the time of the sample collection at the Salt Waste Processing Facility.

1.0 Introduction
A composite sample from the Salt Waste Processing Facility (SWPF) Decontaminated Salt Solution (DSS) was used
to prepare one Saltstone Disposal Facility (SDF) waste form sample, referred to as a Cement-Free grout sample1
per request of SWPF & End Stream Delivery (ESD) Engineering (S&E-E). The Cement-Free sample represents 6
months of saltstone processing and is the sample used to determine that saltstone produced by the Saltstone
Production Facility (SPF) is a non-hazardous waste form. The October 2025 semiannual Cement-Free saltstone
sample was prepared on January 27, 2026. The sample cured for 51 days, it was crushed, sieved, packaged, and
deemed “collected.”2 The sample was then shipped to Southwest Research Institute® (SwRI®) to analyze for
toxicity per the Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure (TCLP) method.1,3 The Cement-free saltstone sample
determined whether the non-hazardous nature of the grout meets the requirements of the South Carolina Code
of Regulations 61-79.261.244 for Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) metals and 61-79.268.485 for
inorganic/organic Underlying Hazardous Constituents (UHCs) (for informational purposes only).1

2.0 Experimental Procedure
2.1 Technical
The October 2025 Semiannual sample of the SWPF DSS is a composite from one month of SWPF processing during
the Third Quarter Fiscal Year 2025 (3QFY2025) and two months of SWPF processing during the Fourth Quarter
Fiscal Year 2025 (4QFY2025).6 Saltstone preparation was performed at Savannah River National Laboratory (SRNL)
S&E-E provided SRNL with the saltstone grout recipe as well as the premix components.1 Table 2-1 shows the
premix components obtained for the April 2025 semiannual TCLP sample and October 2025. Savannah River
Mission Completion (SRMC) provided SRNL personnel with the premix formulation for the October 2025
semiannual TCLP sample.
 Table 2-1. Premix Components for October 2025 Semiannual TCLP Sample
 Premix Component Date SRNL Received LOT # TCLP Sample
           Lehigh Slag            7/22/2025             2025-IR-05-1393 April 2025/October 2025
           SE Fly Ash             7/22/2025             2025-IR-05-1392 April 2025/October 2025

The saltstone sample was prepared with a water-to-premix ratio of 0.59 using the mixing method outlined in SRNL
work instructions7 and the October 2025 semiannual premix components in Table 2-1. The sample contained
60:40 (by weight) of slag and fly ash only referred to as the “Cement-Free grout sample.” The sample cured in a
sealed plastic storage bag for 51 days. After curing, the sample was crushed and sieved using the method outlined
in SRNL work instructions.8 Material that passed through the 3/8-inch (0.375”) sieve (9.252 mm) was subsequently
screened through a No. 4 sieve (4.76 mm). The material retained on the No. 4 sieve was placed in a 250 mL High
Density Polyethylene bottle. The sample was shipped on the same day that it was prepared to SwRI® by the SRNL
shipping group.

Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) for mixed radioactive and hazardous waste. For the October 2025
semiannual saltstone sample, the TCLP extraction method was performed on the Cement-Free sample using
extraction fluid #2. The final extract pH value resulting from the TCLP extraction fluid using fluid #2 was 6.38 for
the Cement-Free Sample.12

4.0 Conclusions
Analyses of the SDF Cement-Free waste form prepared from the October 2025 semiannual SWPF DSS sample and
premix material resulted in the following findings.
 • The RCRA metal TCLP result concentrations met the South Carolina Code of Regulations 61-79.261.24
 requirements for a nonhazardous waste form.4
 • The measured concentrations of the TCLP RCRA metals and additional inorganic/organic UHCs met the
 South Carolina Code of Regulations 61-79.268.48 non-wastewater standards.5
 • The measured concentrations of the TCLP RCRA metals met the SPF WAC.13
