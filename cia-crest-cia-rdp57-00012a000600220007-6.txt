This evaluation is based on a course of weeks duration in which there were students. In inter-

preting evaluations, the amount of opportunity for observation of individual students should be considered.

furnish information on how well the student learned the subject matter or skills taught. Secondarily,
incidental observations of interest are reported. These have significance only as they are related to other
information. Unless otherwise stated, performance is evaluated in terms of standards set by the instructors
or in relationship to performance of those who have or who are taking the course. The evaluation is without
