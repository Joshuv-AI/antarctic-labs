discussion ;

The Chairman observed that this proposal does not envision
the entry of EDIC into the operational field, hut represents an
attempt to call appropriate attention to the need for developing
more systematic and comprehensive procedures for utilizing the
knowledge of scientific and technical personnel in the identification
process.

The OSD member enumerated some of the Inherent difficulties
of conducting such a project within the Department of Defense. He
suggested, however, that coordination of the effort in Defense might
he carried out through the Psychological Warfare Panel (under the
Assistant Secretary of Defense), which directs research and develop-
ment activities in various fields.

Hr, Reiss of Commerce emphasized the importance to the
trade-control program of a systematic review of commodities by
technical personnel.

to corrections accepted by the Committee. (See EDIC Case 17, 13 February
1956, for corrected version) It w&b agreed to submit this paper to the
