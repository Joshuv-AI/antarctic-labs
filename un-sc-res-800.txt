'''Adopted by the Security Council at its 3157th meeting, on 8 January 1993''' [1]

''The Security Council,''

''Having examined'' the application of the Slovak Republic for admission to the United Nations, [2]

''Recommends'' to the General Assembly that the Slovak Republic be admitted to membership in the United Nations.


[1] Adopted without vote

[2] S/25046.
