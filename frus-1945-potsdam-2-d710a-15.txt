Editorial Note (Potsdam 1945 Vol. II, Unnumbered document following Document 710 (#15))

—While Truman was having luncheon with Churchill,[1] Byrnes was a luncheon guest at Eden’s quarters in Babelsberg.[2] No record of the substance of the conversation at this meeting has been found.

Notes
[1] See ante, p. 79.
[2] London Times, July 19, 1945, p. 4.
