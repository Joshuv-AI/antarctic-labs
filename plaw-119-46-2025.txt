Public Law 119–46: Requesting the Secretary of the Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements for displays on the National Mall and the Washington Monument during the period beginning on December 31, 2025, and ending on January 5, 2026.

Joint Resolution Requesting the Secretary of the Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements for displays on the National Mall and the Washington Monument during the period beginning on December 31, 2025, and ending on January 5, 2026.Dec. 2, 2025[H.J. Res. 133]

That Congress—

PARAGRAPH (1)

requests the Secretary of the Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements for the display of United States semiquincentennial materials and other significant artifacts, digital content, film footage, and associated audio and imagery in and around the vicinity of the National Mall, including projected onto the surface of the Washington Monument for 5 nights of public display, during the period beginning on December 31, 2025, and ending on January 5, 2026; and

PARAGRAPH (2)

SUBPARAGRAPH (A)

the Secretary of the Interior; and

SUBPARAGRAPH (B)

the Chair of the United States Semiquincentennial Commission.

Approved December 2, 2025.
