Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (21 March 1944, d3)

most secret

London, 21 March 1944.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt. Number 629 most secret and personal. Your 506.[2]

I am indeed grieved and trust rest will do you good.

I do not think the staff meeting worthwhile without our being there.

I hope you are still planning your visit here for the great event[3] after you are fully recovered.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the United States Military Attaché, London, via Army channels.
[2] Supra.
[3] The Allied invasion of Normandy (Overlord).
