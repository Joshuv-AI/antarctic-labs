Dear Mr. Houston:

Today I read with deep interest and benefit the
papers you so thoughtfully gave me last evening. They
are most helpful in giving not only an understanding of
your own program but of problems basic to the whole of
the loyalty-security system. The proposed regulation is
an unusually interesting effort to maintain the needed
secrecy and at the same time to give the employee all
practicable Information. The discussion last night and
your comments have confirmed my belief that the particular
method of selecting hearing board members under Executive
Order 10h50 is unfortunate. It does not secure members
who are independent and who will not be fearful of the
practical consequences on them and their positions. Nor
does it provide any useful kind of expertness, since the
members are not experienced in handling such cases nor
are they conversant with the problems of the particular
agency involved.

The comments on the defects in the espionage
laws dealt with a matter which we have not so far consi-
dered. Perhaps our Committee will be led at least to
point out the relation of criminal sanctions and the
practicability of their uses, with the personnel security
programs.

ELLIOTT e. CHEATHAM, Director
