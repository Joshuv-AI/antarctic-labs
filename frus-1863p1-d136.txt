Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 532.]

Department of State, Washington, March 28, 1863.

Sir: I enclose for your information the copy of a note of the 25th instant, from Lord Lyons, with a copy of the papers which accompanied it, relative to the reception of the Oreto or Florida at Nassau.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
