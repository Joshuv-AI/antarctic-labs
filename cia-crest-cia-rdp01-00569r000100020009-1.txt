ATTACHMENT B

CONTENT DESCRIPTION OF FOLDERS
(MISC-21)

MISC-21.1 In strument of Gift (from National Archives)

Copies of the Instruments of Gift that provided for the
Transfer of personal papers of Presidents Hoover, Truman,
Eisenhower, Kennedy and Johnson to the United States
Government. There is no Instrument of Gift as such covering
the transfer of Roosevelt's papers.

A copy of the Presidential Libraries Act, 69 STAT. Public
Law 373-Aug 12, 19.35 is also in this folder.

MISC-21. 2 C IA stipulations for Archives ^ Presidential Libraries

CIA memos outlining document control procedures, recommended
by the Agency, for access to classified CIA documents in
Presidential Libraries.

Also noted in a 12 Sep 68 memo ...'"an assurance on 11 Sep
1968 from Mr. Walker the officer in charge of Presidential
Libraries in the National Archives and Records Service that
classified material will not become available to the public
for many years - "we are thinking in terms of 75-100 years,"

. . . "and that CIA will remain master of the disposition and
use of its own donations to the National Archives, of which
the Presidential Libraries are a part."'

A copy of the procedures at National Archives covering the
acceptance and maintenance of material for deposit, restrictions
on availability and use, study, research, fees, etc., at all
Presidential Libraries.

MISC-21. 3 Contributions from CIA to LBJ Library and Library Annex!

Lists of contributions from all Directorates for the LBJ
Library. Notations are made throughout this file that some
material went directly to Austin, while the remainder was
being retained in either various vaulted areas in Headquarters
or in the CIA Annex awaiting instructions - -due to their
classification and sensitivity.

A September 1968 memo states that everything in the White
House vault was scheduled to be shipped to Austin. (This
same procedure was followed in assembling the Truman,

Eisenhower, and Kennedy Libraries.)

MI SC -2 1.4 Receipts

Copies of all receipts for documents sent to Austin and
Records Center.

ATTACHMENT B

MI SC -2 1.5 News clippings

Small collection of newspaper clippings on the Johnson Library
in Austin Texas,

MI SC -2 1.6 Coordinators and Contacts

List of people working on the CIA Johnson Library Project,
(inside Agency and outside)

MI SC -2 1.7 Planning of Contribtuions for the Project

Fairly large folder with questions, planning papers, meetings
and proposals for discussion and submission of contributions
from all Agency components to the LBJ Library and the LBJ
Library Annex.

MI SC -2 1.8 Duplicate copies of Memos

MI SC -2 1.9 Kennedy Presidential Library

A separate folder was made for the Kennedy Library material.

(See MISC-21.10 for all other Presidents.)

The Kennedy folder has many memos relating to the planning
phases attached to designating and collecting the appropriate
documents for the Library.

A complete list of CIA documents that have been transferred
to the JFK Library is included, as well as an index to material
that was retained by CIA because of the sensitivity of the
matters included in them. Any reference to this latter category
of reports must be made with specific permission of the Agency.

MISC-21.10 "OTH E R" Presidential Libraries, ie: Hoover, Roosevelt,
Truman, Eisenhower, and Nixon

This folder designates specific files in the Agency that con-*'"
tain information on the presidents named above; this informa-
tion could be useful to a person in writing a history on the
presidential eras.
