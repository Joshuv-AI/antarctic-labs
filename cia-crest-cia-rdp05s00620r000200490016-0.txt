Dear John:-

The main point here is to say thanks a million for your
outstanding lead-off presentation at our RIAC-12 Naval Reserve
Intelligence Seminar in Columbus on Saturday, 29 September.

It was a super way to start the day.

1 ^®ve received a great deal of feedback from the people

Best regards,
