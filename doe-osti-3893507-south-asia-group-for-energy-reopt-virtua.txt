 Solution: REopt Optimizes Integrated Energy Systems

• NLR’s REopt platform optimizes energy generation and storage decisions for more
  affordable and reliable site energy solutions.

• REopt integrates electric, heating, cooling, and hydrogen technologies to identify the
  optimal technology mix, sizing, and operations to achieve a site’s energy goals.

• It transforms complex decisions into actionable results for building owners, utilities,
  developers, industry, and academia via a user-friendly web tool and open-source software.

 What REopt Is and Is Not

  REopt is intended to be used for: REopt is NOT intended to be used for:

✓ Pre-feasibility planning to meet site ❌ Final system design

  energy goals ❌ Assessing distribution system impacts

✓ Initial estimates of technology sizing ❌ Additional electrical equipment or
  and economics upgrade costs.
✓ Evaluating multiple scenarios and
  performing sensitivity analyses.

 Engineering analysis and more detailed financial assessments are
 typically performed after, and can be informed by, a REopt analysis.

Example Use Cases

Merefa Community Microgrid Conceptual Design
 • After a solar photovoltaic system in Merefa,
 Ukraine, was damaged by a Russian air
 strike, NLR researchers used the REopt
 model to evaluate possible upgrades to a
 resilient community microgrid.
 • REopt was used to identify the least-cost
 system design and probability of meeting
 critical loads during loss of grid power.
Photo from Andriy Pavlov, Monolith

For more details, see Olis, et. al., 2024. “Merefa Community
Microgrid: Conceptual Design and Sequence of Operations.”
https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy24osti/89527.pdf.

 Try This At Home

• Before the next session, complete your own example REopt run:
 ◦ Choose technologies of interest
 ◦ Enter your location
 ◦ Enter the cost of energy* (Choose “Use custom electricity rate”)
