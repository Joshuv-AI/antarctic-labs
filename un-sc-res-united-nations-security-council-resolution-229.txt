Adopted by the Security Council at its 1329th meeting, (private meeting) on 2 December 1966

The Security Council,

Conscious of the proven qualities and high sense of duty of U Thant, and believing that his reappointment would be most conductive to the larger interests and purposes of the Organization,

Recommends the appointment of U Thant for another term of office as Secretary-General of the United Nations.

1966
