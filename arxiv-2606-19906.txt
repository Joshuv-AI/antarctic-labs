New Avenues of Heavy Neutral Lepton at Muon Collider
Fa-Xin Yang 1, Feng-Lan Shao 1, Zhi-Long Han 2, Honglei Li 2

Abstract
With initial state radiation, the multi-TeV muon collider can be regarded as an electroweak boson collider. The dominant production mode of the certain process becomes the vector boson fusion channel, because the corresponding cross section typically increases logarithmically at high energies. This also holds true for new physics beyond the standard model. Within the U ⁡ ( 1 ) U(1) gauged extension of seesaw models, the heavy neutral lepton has additional interactions with the new gauge boson Z ′ Z^{\prime} and heavy Higgs H H . In this paper, we investigate the production of heavy neutral lepton N N via the new vector boson fusion processes Z ′ ​ Z ′ → H → N ​ N Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to H\to NN with and Z ′ ​ Z ′ → N ​ N Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to NN without heavy Higgs at the multi-TeV muon collider. Different from the canonical vector boson fusion processes W ​ W / Z ​ Z → H → N ​ N WW/ZZ\to H\to NN , the new process Z ′ ​ Z ′ → H → N ​ N Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to H\to NN is not suppressed by the small mixing angle α \alpha between the Higgs bosons. Meanwhile, the pair production process Z ′ ​ Z ′ → N ​ N Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to NN is also viable even for heavy Higgs m H > s m_{H}>\sqrt{s} . Therefore, these new avenues provide alternative pathways to probe the intrinsic feature of the heavy neutral lepton. We then perform a detailed analysis of the lepton number violation signals via the new vector boson fusion with heavy Higgs μ + ​ μ − → μ + ​ μ − ​ H → μ + ​ μ − ​ N ​ N \mu^{+}\mu^{-}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}H\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}NN and without heavy Higgs μ + ​ μ − → μ + ​ μ − ​ N ​ N \mu^{+}\mu^{-}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}NN , followed by N → μ ± ​ j ​ j N\to\mu^{\pm}jj , where the two jets from W W boson decay are treated as one fat-jet J J .

II
The Model

In this paper, we focus on the
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
gauge extension of the Type-I seesaw model. Three generations of heavy neutral lepton
(N_{e},N_{\mu},N_{\tau})
are introduced to generate tiny neutrino masses, which have
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
charge
(0,1,-1)
. The gauged
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L\tau}
symmetry is broken spontaneously by a scalar singlet
S
with
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
charge
+1
. Neglecting the kinetic mixing term for simplicity
Hapitas:2021ilr
, the relevant kinetic terms of the new fields are

\mathcal{L}\supset-\frac{1}{4}F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}F^{\prime\mu\nu}+|D_{\mu}S|^{2},

(1)

where
F^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
is the field strength of the
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
symmetry, and
D_{\mu}=\partial_{\mu}-ig^{\prime}Z^{\prime}_{\mu}
is the covariant derivative with
g^{\prime}
being the
L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}
gauge coupling constant. After
S
develops nonzero vacuum expectation value
v_{S}
, the mass of new gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
is derived as
Nomura:2020vnk

m_{Z^{\prime}}=g^{\prime}v_{S}.

(2)

The new gauge interactions of the relevant fermions are

\mathcal{L}\supset g^{\prime}(\bar{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}\mu-\bar{\tau}\gamma^{\mu}\tau+\bar{\nu}_{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\nu_{\mu}-\bar{\nu}_{\tau}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\nu_{\tau}+\bar{N}_{\mu}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}N_{\mu}-\bar{N}_{\tau}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}N_{\tau})Z^{\prime}_{\mu}.

(3)

Currently, there are various constraints on the new gauge boson
Z^{\prime}

m_{Z^{\prime}}>100
GeV. The relevant constraint is from the neutrino trident production at the CCFR experiment

GeV

.

Under the
L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}
symmetry, the Yukawa interactions and mass terms are given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{L}

\displaystyle\supset

\displaystyle-y_{e}\bar{L}_{e}\tilde{\Phi}N_{e}-y_{\mu}\bar{L}_{\mu}\tilde{\Phi}N_{\mu}-y_{\tau}\bar{L}_{\tau}\tilde{\Phi}N_{\tau}-\frac{1}{2}M_{ee}\overline{N^{c}_{e}}N_{e}

(4)

\displaystyle-y_{e\mu}S^{*}\overline{N^{c}_{e}}N_{\mu}-y_{e\tau}S\overline{N^{c}_{e}}N_{\tau}-M_{\mu\tau}\overline{N^{c}_{\mu}}N_{\tau}+\text{h.c.},

where
L_{e},L_{\mu},L_{\tau}
are the lepton doublets, and
\tilde{\Phi}=i\tau_{2}\Phi^{*}
. After the spontaneous symmetry breaking, the Dirac neutrino mass matrix and heavy neutral lepton mass matrix are

\displaystyle M_{D}=\left(\begin{array}[]{c c c}\frac{y_{e}v_{0}}{\sqrt{2}}&0&0\\

\frac{y_{e\tau}v_{s}}{\sqrt{2}}&M_{\mu\tau}&0\end{array}\right).

Light neutrino masses are generated via the type-I seesaw mechanism

(11)

The scalar potential

\displaystyle V(\Phi,S)=m_{1}^{2}H\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi+m_{2}^{2}S^{\dagger}S+\lambda_{1}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)^{2}+\lambda_{2}(S^{\dagger}S)^{2}+\lambda_{3}(\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi)(S^{\dagger}S),

After the symmetry breaking, the vacuum expectation values of the scalars are denoted as

\displaystyle m_{H}^{2}=\lambda_{1}v_{0}^{2}+\lambda_{2}v_{s}^{2}+\sqrt{(\lambda_{1}v_{0}^{2}-\lambda_{2}v_{s}^{2})^{2}+(\lambda_{3}v_{0}v_{s})^{2}},

III
Decay Properties

In this Section, we consider the decay properties of the heavy Higgs
H
. Since we have set
\sin\alpha=0
to forbid the mixing between the neutral scalars, the heavy Higgs
H
will not directly couple to the SM particles. There are still two decay modes of the heavy Higgs. One is

H\to Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}
through the new gauge interaction.
The corresponding partial decay widths are

\displaystyle\Gamma(H\rightarrow NN)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m_{N}^{2}m_{H}}{4\pi v_{s}^{2}}\left(1-\frac{4m_{N}^{2}}{m_{H}^{2}}\right)^{3/2},

\displaystyle\Gamma(H\rightarrow Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{m_{H}^{3}}{32\pi v_{s}^{2}}\left(1-\frac{4m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}{m_{H}^{2}}+\frac{12m_{Z^{\prime}}^{4}}{m_{H}^{4}}\right)\left(1-\frac{4m_{Z^{\prime}}^{2}}{m_{H}^{2}}\right)^{1/2}.

(21)

Further decays of the new gauge boson and heavy Higgs could generate various interesting signatures. For instance, a novel same-sign tetralepton signature originates from the heavy Higgs-strahlung processes as
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\to Z^{\prime}H\to NN+NN\to 4\mu^{\pm}+4J

. In this paper, we focus on the
Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}
-fusion process
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\to\mu^{+}\mu^{-}H
followed by
H\to NN

|V_{\mu N}|\neq 0

|V_{eN}|=|V_{\tau N}|=0

\mu^{\pm}jj+\mu^{\pm}jj

or
\mu^{-}

\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\rightarrow\mu^{+}\mu^{-}H\rightarrow\mu^{+}\mu^{-}NN\rightarrow\mu^{+}\mu^{-}+\mu^{\pm}jj+\mu^{\pm}jj.

(26)

At the TeV scale muon collider, the dijets from the heavy neutral lepton decays could be highly boosted and could merge into one fat-jet
J

\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\mu^{\pm}\mu^{\pm}JJ
. The benchmark collision energies of the muon collider are regarded as
\sqrt{s}=
3 TeV, 10 TeV, 30 TeV with an integrated luminosity of 1 ab
-1
, 10 ab
-1
, 90 ab
-1

In the simulation, we use the

VI
Conclusion

The gauged
U(1)_{L\mu-L\tau}
extension of the type-I seesaw mechanism is well motivated by various evidence of new physics, but it is still less constrained by current limits. This model introduces three generations of heavy neutral leptons
(N_{e},N_{\mu},N_{\tau})
to explain the origin of tiny neutrino mass. The scalar singlet
S
with
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
charge
+1
breaks the
U(1)_{L_{\mu}-L_{\tau}}
symmetry spontaneously, which then results in the new gauge boson
Z^{\prime}
and heavy Higgs boson
H
.

Since the multi-TeV muon collider is effectively a gauge boson collider, we investigate the new vector boson fusion processes
Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to H\to NN
with and
Z^{\prime}Z^{\prime}\to NN
without the heavy Higgs at the multi-TeV muon collider. Focus on the visible decay channel of heavy neutral lepton
N\to\mu^{\pm}jj
with the dijets regarded as one fat-jet
J
, both processes could generate the lepton number violation signature
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\mu^{\pm}\mu^{\pm}JJ
. We report that the number of detected final state muons in the main detector is highly dependent on the parameters, such as
m_{Z^{\prime}}
and
m_{N}
. For instance, the
2\mu^{\pm}
channel is the dominant one for relatively light
m_{N}
or
m_{Z^{\prime}}
. On the other hand, the
3\mu^{\pm}+\mu^{\mp}
channel becomes the dominant one for relatively heavy
m_{N}
or
m_{Z^{\prime}}
. In this paper, we perform a comprehensive analysis of three different signals, i.e.,
3\mu^{\pm}+\mu^{\mp}
,
3\mu^{\pm}
, and
2\mu^{\pm}
channels.

For the signature
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\mu^{\pm}\mu^{\pm}JJ
with heavy Higgs
H
, we find that all three channels are promising at the muon collider. For the benchmark point with
m_{N}=200~\rm{GeV}
,
m_{H}=3\times m_{N}
,
m_{Z^{\prime}}=500~\rm{GeV}
and
g^{\prime}=0.6
, the
3\mu^{\pm}+\mu^{\mp}
channel is the dominant one at the 3 TeV muon collider. At the 10 TeV stage, all three channels have similar cross section after the selection cuts. While the
2\mu^{\pm}
channel becomes the dominant one at the 30 TeV stage. With an integrated luminosity of
90~\text{ab}^{-1}
, the 30 TeV muon collider could discover the largest parameter space. For example, the area of
m_{N}<8.4~\text{TeV}
and
m_{Z^{\prime}}<23~\text{TeV}
with
g^{\prime}=0.6
can be discovered. When fixing
m_{Z^{\prime}}=500
GeV and the relation
m_{H}=3m_{N}
, the 30 TeV stage could probe
g^{\prime}\gtrsim 0.12
and
m_{N}<9
TeV.

Without the
s
-channel enhancement, the signature
\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\mu^{\pm}\mu^{\pm}JJ
without heavy Higgs
H
is less promising at the muon collider. For the benchmark point with
m_{N}=200~\rm{GeV}
,
m_{Z^{\prime}}=500~\rm{GeV}
and
g^{\prime}=0.6
, only the
3\mu^{\pm}+\mu^{\mp}
channel can be discovered at the 3 TeV muon collider. At the 10 TeV muon collider, the
3\mu^{\pm}+\mu^{\mp}
and
3\mu^{\pm}
channel could be tested with
\mathcal{O}(10^{2})
fb data, while the
2\mu^{\pm}
channel requires
\mathcal{O}(10^{3})
fb data. At the 30 TeV stage, the
2\mu^{\pm}
channel has the largest signal cross section, but the corresponding background is also the largest. Therefore, the
3\mu^{\pm}
channel is the most promising one at the 30 TeV muon collider. With
90~\text{ab}^{-1}
data, the 30 TeV muon collider could discover the parameter space of
m_{Z^{\prime}}<5.1~\text{TeV}
and
m_{N}<13~\text{TeV}
with
g^{\prime}=0.6
. Provided
m_{Z^{\prime}}=500
GeV, the parameter space of
g^{\prime}>0.22
and
m_{N}<13
TeV could be probed at the 30 TeV muon collider.
