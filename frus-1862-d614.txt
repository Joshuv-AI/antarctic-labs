Mr. Seward to Mr. Wood.

No. 24.]

Department of State, Washington, February 10, 1862.

Sir: Your despatch of January 7 (No. 43) has been received and read with the interest which it was calculated to excite.

The improved condition of affairs at home will probably, in due time, modify the adverse opinions existing abroad.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Bradford R. Wood, Esq., &c., &c., Copenhagen.
