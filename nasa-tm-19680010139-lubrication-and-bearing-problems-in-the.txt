 FRICTION AND WEAR IN VACUUM
 As previously mentioned, one of the most important adverse effects
of the low pressures in space is the removal of surface films by evapor-

ation. If the surfaces become sufficiently clean, severe adhesion and

welding can occur between sliding surfaces. Since materials under such

conditions will have a tendency to rub together in their "virgin" states,
it would be desirable to avoid this condition where possible by providing
a contaminating film with lubricants of various types.
