No. 491 The Ambassador in Italy (Kirk) to the Secretary of State ad interim

secret

Caserta, June 28, 1945—noon.

2795. Broad showed us a tel he has just recd from ForOff instructing him if SAC[1] perceived no objection to proceeding Polish GHQs in Italy and explain to Gen Anders in as favorable a manner as possible why Poles abroad should welcome formation of new Pole Govt. ForOff was [has] authorized Broad to give to Anders story of Moscow negotiations more or less as outlined in Depcirtels on this subject[2] and to point out obvious advantages accruing to Poland by constitution of new Govt. …

Sent Dept, rptd Moscow 188.

Kirk

Source: 860c.01/6–2845: Telegram

Notes
[1] Field Marshal Sir Harold Alexander.
[2] Not printed.
