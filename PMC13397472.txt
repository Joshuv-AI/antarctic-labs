Safety of the feed additive consisting of chromium propionate (KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT) for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction (Kemin Europa N.V.)
Authors: EFSA Panel on Additives and Products or Substances used in Animal Feed (FEEDAP), Roberto Edoardo Villa, Giovanna Azimonti, Eleftherios Bonos, Henrik Christensen, Mojca Durjava, Birgit Dusemund, Ronette Gehring, Boet Glandorf, Maryline Kouba, Marta López‐Alonso, Francesca Marcon, Giovanna Martelli, Carlo Nebbia, Alena Pechová, Miguel Prieto‐Maradona, Katerina Theodoridou, Montserrat Anguita, Jaume Galobart, Matteo L. Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractAn assessment of the safety and efficacy of a feed additive consisting of chromium propionate (KemTRACE™ Chromium) was issued on 18 March 2021. Due to difficulties in implementing the proposed method for the official control of added organic chromium in premixtures and feed, the additive needed incorporating a tracer in the composition of a premix. The European Commission asked EFSA to issue a new opinion on the safety and efficacy of Cr propionate (KemTRACE™ Chromium) as a feed additive for all growing poultry species, including the newly submitted information by the applicant that involves the use of the microtracer. In the FEEDAP Panel opinion on the safety of the microtracer issued on 15 January 2025, no conclusion could be made on the environmental safety of the microtracer for the groundwater compartment. Following the request from the applicant and its acceptance by the European Commission, as indicated in its letter to EFSA of 12 January 2026, the scope of the current assessment has been modified to consider a change in the additive under assessment and a reduction of the target species. Therefore, EFSA delivers an assessment of the safety and efficacy of the additive consisting of Cr propionate (KemTRACE chromium 0.4% MT) for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction. The use of KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT (containing a microtracer) at the proposed conditions of use is safe for the target species, consumers and the environment. The additive is considered irritant to the eyes and a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users by any route is considered a risk. KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT has the potential to be efficacious as a zootechnical additive in chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction at the supplementation level of 0.4 mg Cr/kg feed.

In view of the above and in accordance with Article 29(1)(a) of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, the European Commission requests EFSA to deliver a new scientific opinion on a feed additive consisting of Chromium propionate as a zootechnical additive for all growing poultry and on the microtracer to be included in the composition of the premix, under the conditions of Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003, based on the supplementary information and data submitted by the applicant.

On 24 November 2025, Kemin Europa N.V. requested in writing the partial withdrawal of their application as regards minor growing poultry species, asking the Commission to proceed with the authorisation of the additive for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction. The Commission accepted this request.

On 15 December 2025, Kemin Europa N.V. informed the Commission in writing of their intention to modify their application, making KemTRACE Chromium 0.4%, a solid preparation including the microtracer, as the additive instead of the liquid preparation (KemTRACE™ Chromium).

On 12 January 2026, the European Commission requested EFSA to take these adjustments into consideration in the context of the present assessment.

Interpretation of the Terms of Reference

The original mandate from the European Commission aimed at requesting EFSA to assess the new data to support the safety of the additive for all growing poultry species and the safety of the microtracer for the groundwater compartment.

Following the request from the applicant and its acceptance by the European Commission, as indicated in its letter to EFSA of 12 January 2026, the scope of the current assessment has been modified to consider a change in the additive under assessment and a reduction of the target species. Therefore, EFSA will deliver an assessment of the safety and efficacy of the additive consisting of chromium propionate (KemTRACE chromium 0.4% MT) for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction.

Additional information

The additive KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT is a preparation containing chromium propionate and is currently not authorised as a feed additive in the European Union.

The FEEDAP Panel assessed the safety and efficacy of chromium propionate as a zootechnical additive for all growing poultry species (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2021, 2025a, 2025b), and the safety of an associated microtracer (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2025c).

ASSESSMENT

The additive chromium propionate (trade name: KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT) is intended to be used as a zootechnical feed additive (functional group: other zootechnical additives; improvement of growth parameters and carcass traits) for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction. Unless otherwise indicated, chromium in the opinion refers to chromium (III).

In the original application, the applicant requested the authorisation of a liquid feed additive containing chromium propionate 29%–32%, equivalent to ≥ 7% organic chromium (trade name: KemTRACE™ Chromium). The FEEDAP Panel fully characterised the product in its opinion in 2021. Subsequently, due to some limitations of the method of analysis of the liquid preparation in compound feed, the applicant proposed the use of the additive in a solid premixture in which a microtracer was added to allow for the quantification of organic Cr in premixtures of feed. This premixture, identified as KemTRACE Chromium 0.4%, was the subject of two opinions of the Panel (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2025a, 2025b). Overall, in those three opinions, the Panel concluded that the additive KemTRACE™ Chromium is safe for chickens for fattening and chickens reared for laying/breeding, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction, for the consumer and the environment, but could not conclude on the safety for other minor poultry species for fattening and reared for laying/breeding. The Panel also concluded that the use of the microtracer in the premixture KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% was safe for the same target species, consumers and raised some concerns for the users. Regarding the environment, the use of the microtracer was considered safe for the terrestrial compartment, but the Panel could not conclude on the safety of the colourants for the groundwater.

In the present submission, the applicant withdrew the application for minor poultry species and changed the subject of the request of the authorisation by considering the previously so‐called solid premixture KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% as the additive under assessment, with the trade name KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT, and modifying the composition of the microtracer (reduction of the concentration of the dyes).

The applicant provided data on the characterisation of the additive, of the new microtracer and on the safety of the microtracer for the environment.

Compliance with the specifications of the new microtracer was confirmed by analytical data from three batches, in which shellac ranged 1.4%–1.6%, Brilliant Blue pure dye ranged 0.14%–0.15% and Tartrazine pure dye ranged 0.14%–0.16%.

No other information was provided with regard to the physical and technological properties of the additive other than a study on the homogeneous distribution in feed. The Panel considers that the data on stability assessed in 2021 would also apply to the current application.

To support the capacity of the newly proposed microtracer to homogeneously distribute in feed, three samples of the additive, each of them prepared with a different batch of the microtracer, were mixed in a feed for chickens for fattening made of wheat, soybean meal and maize, subsequently pelleted. 15 A total of 10 subsamples were analysed for each feed form. The particles were counted (colour spots) by naked eye and by a computer‐assisted system. Results of mixing uniformity were expressed as probability (p) of the Poisson distribution. p values > 25% were considered indicative of an excellent mixing uniformity. In all cases, p values were > 25%, except one sample of pelleted feed, that had a p value of 23% and was considered still a good mix.

Conditions of use

The additive is intended to be used in feed for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction, to provide a minimum of 0.2 mg and a maximum of 0.4 mg organic Cr/kg complete feed. The supplementation level of 0.2 and 0.4 mg Cr/kg feed would correspond to 0.5 and 1 mg microtracer per kg feed, respectively.

Safety

The FEEDAP Panel assessed the safety of the liquid preparation of chromium propionate (KemTRACE Chromium) in previous opinions and concluded that its use in feed for chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and reared for reproduction is safe up to the maximum use level of 0.4 mg Cr/kg complete feed (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2021, 2025a). In the same opinions, the Panel concluded that the use of the additive KemTRACE Chromium is safe for the consumers and the environment and raised concerns for user safety due to the corrosiveness for the eyes.

The safety of the microtracer FS‐Green Lake used to formulate the premixture KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% was also assessed in a previous opinion (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2025c). In that opinion, the Panel concluded that the use of the microtracer was safe for the target species, the consumer and the terrestrial compartment of the environment but raised concerns for user safety (dermal and respiratory sensitiser) and, for the colouring dyes, the groundwater compartment.

Taking into account that the additive under assessment KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT corresponds to the premixture KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% described in the 2025 opinions, with the only difference being the reduced amount of dyes in the microtracer, the Panel considers that the previous conclusions regarding target species, consumer, user and the terrestrial compartment of the environment apply to the present assessment.

Therefore, the Panel concludes that the use of additive KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT at the proposed conditions of use is safe for the target species, the consumer and the terrestrial compartment of the environment. The additive should be considered irritant to the eyes, and a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users by any route is considered a risk.

The safety of the microtracer for the groundwater compartment is considered below.

Safety of the microtracer for the groundwater

Turkeys for fattening are considered the worst‐case scenario for growing poultry. The PECgw for the dyes of the microtracer when the additive is used in turkeys for fattening were calculated to be 0.05 and 0.06 μg/L for Brilliant Blue FCF and Tartrazine, respectively. These values are below the trigger value of 0.1 μg/L for groundwater. Therefore, no substantial safety concerns are expected for the groundwater from the use of the microtracer according to the conditions of use.

Efficacy of KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT

In its opinion of 2021, the FEEDAP Panel concluded that KemTRACE™ Chromium has the potential to be efficacious as a zootechnical additive in poultry species for fattening and reared for laying/breeding at the supplementation level of 0.4 mg Cr/kg feed.

The additive under assessment (KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT) consists of ■■■■■ of the liquid KemTRACE™ Chromium with a microtracer and a carrier. As the composition in terms of active substance and the conditions of use have not changed, the FEEDAP Panel considers that the previous conclusions on the efficacy of the liquid KemTRACE™ Chromium are applicable to the additive under assessment.

CONCLUSIONS

The use of the additive KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT (containing a microtracer) at the proposed conditions of use is safe for the target species, consumers and the environment. The additive is considered an irritant to the eyes and a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users by any route is considered a risk.

KemTRACE Chromium 0.4% MT has the potential to be efficacious as a zootechnical additive in chickens for fattening, chickens reared for laying/reproduction, turkeys for fattening and turkeys reared for reproduction at the supplementation level of 0.4 mg Cr/kg feed.
