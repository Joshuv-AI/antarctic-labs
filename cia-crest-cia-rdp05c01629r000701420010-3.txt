Dear Admiral Inman:

Recent discussions at meetings of the Coordinating Committee
on Technology Transfer, chaired by Gus E. Weiss, Jr., National
Security Council, have led to a decision to reactivate the
Interagency Working Group on Export Control Enforcement, which
was formed in April, 1980, under the National Security Council,
to improve interagency coordination in the enforcement of the
export control laws. As you know, this Administration and your
Department have emphasized the importance of improving the
enforcement of the export control laws; the activation of a
working level enforcement group is designed to assist in attain-
ing tha-ir objective.

Tbe working group will be the primary vehicle to ensure
that the Administration's export control enforcement programs
are effectively coordinated and vigorously pursued. The group
will serve as a forum for the exchange of information on enforce-
ment programs and on specific investigations which each of our
agencies is pursuing. The group will also function as a forum
to channel information developed by the investigative agencies
and the intelligence community, to fashion joint investigative
strategies, and to marshal enforcement resources. During the
working group meetings., problem areas in interagency enforcement
coordination and cooperation will be identified and resolved.

I would, like to emphasize that the principal focus of this
working group is improved enforcement of our export control
laws and increased investigation and prosecution of violations.

As chairman of the Interagency Working Group, I anticipate
that through our close cooperation the working group can become
an effective tool in our common effort to improve the enforcement
of oxir export control laws. During 'the course of the group's
