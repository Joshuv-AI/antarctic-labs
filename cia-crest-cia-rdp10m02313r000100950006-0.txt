MEMORANDUM FOR THOMAS CORMACK

Executive Secretary
Central Intelligence Agency

SUBJECT: NSDD-97 Steering Group (U)

Pursuant to Mr. McMahon's conversation with Robert Helm of the
NSC staff, this is to confirm the relationship of government
agencies with the NSDD-97 Steering Group. (U)

The National Communications System (NCS) , of which the CIA is a
principal member, will function as the interagency forum where
national security telecommunication initiatives in support of
NSDD-97 are developed. To the extent feasible, the NCS
membership will also address implementation and funding issues. (C)

NCS proposed initiatives and implementation recommendations will
be forwarded to the Steering Group for consideration. The
Steering Group will address and approve final implementation and
funding plans or, as required, refer issues to the President for
decision. (C)

Agencies will be invited to participate in Steering Group
meetings to discuss implementation and funding for national
security telecommunication initiatives directly related to their
mission and responsibilities. Agencies may also request to
participate in or observe Steering Group meetings at which issues
they feel bear upon their mission and responsibilities will be
discussed. (C)

This procedure is specifically designed to ensure that all
agencies will be represented at NSDD-97 Steering Group meetings
where implementation and funding of national security
telecommunication issues directly related to their missions and
budgets are discussed. (C)

Robert M. Kimmitt
Executive Secretary

11 July 1983

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD
SUBJECT: NSDD-97 Steering Group

1. As a result of my request to Judge Clark and concerns
expressed over the absence of CIA presence on the NSDD-97 Steering
Group, Clark had Bob Helm, NSC, contact me on 8 July.

2. Helm assured me that the Steering Group would have member
organizations of the National Communications Service in attendance
whenever such matters involving their programs or their responsi-
bility were discussed. In other words, if intelligence flow or
diplomatic communications were to be discussed then CIA would be
invited. I indicated that was not clear in any of the paperwork

I had seen. He assured me that that indeed was the intent and he
guaranteed CIA would be invited on any issue that would impact on
us.
