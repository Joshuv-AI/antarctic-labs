The Secretary of State to the Acting Secretary of State (Stettinius)[1]

us urgent

Moscow, October 31, 1943—9 p.m.

1798. For the Acting Secretary from the Secretary.

At the request of the Ambassador and because of the increase in work here as a result (DelAm 47[2]) of the Conference, I have decided to leave Bohlen here until the end of year. This would also make him available for the contemplated meeting. I therefore request that the necessary instructions be issued to him for assignment on a temporary basis for two months.[3]

Source: 740.0011 Moscow/97: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent by the Ambassador, Moscow, in the numerical series of the Embassy’s telegrams to the Department of State.
[2] Numeration of messages sent to the Department of State by the American Delegation to the Moscow Conference.
[3] On November 3, 1943, Bohlen was designated First Secretary of the Embassy at Moscow, for temporary duty, effective upon the termination of the Moscow Conference.
