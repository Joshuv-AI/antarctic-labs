No. 369 The Acting Secretary of State to the Secretary of State

secret

Washington, July 7, 1945.

3. The agreed recommendations submitted for your concurrence in Pauley’s 2441, July 6,[1] are in full conformity with the principles formulated in the instructions to Pauley prepared by the Informal Policy Committee on Germany, cleared with JCS and approved by the President. Pauley’s success in obtaining agreement of his Russian and British colleagues represents a considerable achievement. It is recommended that you communicate to Pauley your concurrence and gratification.

Grew
