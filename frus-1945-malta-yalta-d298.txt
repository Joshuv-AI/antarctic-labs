Editorial Note (Malta-Yalta 1945, Document 298)

The only record of the substance of this meeting that has been found is a summary of conversation between Stettinius and Churchill in Stettinius, pp. 60–62. The meeting was “a large formal dinner at Government House” given by the Governor and Commander in Chief of Malta, Lieutenant General Schreiber, in honor of the American and British Delegations. The information given here with respect to the meeting and the participants is taken from Stettinius, pp. 56, 60, 62.
