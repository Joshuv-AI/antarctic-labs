Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt [1] (27 November 1944, d199)

[Excerpts][2]

top secret

London, 27 November 1944.

Prime Minister to President Roosevelt Personal and Top Secret Number 834.

. . . . . . . . . . . . . .

3. Your last paragraph.[3] We have not got to this point yet and I agree with you we should collect a good many arms from the Germans. Still, I think when American divisions begin to return home there would be a strong case for their leaving some of their heavy weapons and equipment behind for the French to take over the job.

Prime
