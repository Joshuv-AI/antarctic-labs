 ABSTRACT

The following report consists of measurements on passive and active photonic components fabricated by
AIM Photonics as part of their QFlex platform, which at the time of fabrication was in its beta form
(version 0.6). This report is broken down into electrical and optical testing that was done on the chips.

 1. Test Results

1.1 Electrical measurements

Current-Voltage measurements were performed on active elements that include doped heaters and
photodiodes. These devices were tested using DC electric probes and a Qontrol Q8iv source
measurement unit which has a voltage and current compliance of 12 V and 24 mA, respectively. Results

1.2 Optical measurements

Optical measurements performed on chips were done with a tunable laser from Keysight (N7778C) with a
tuning range of 1450-1650 nm. A Keysight power meter (N77745C) was used for the optical readout.
Spectral sweeps were performed on passive devices like loopbacks and ring resonators. Figure 2 depicts
the spectral sweep of a silicon ring resonator with double bus configuration measured only in the thru-
port. Resonance peaks were fit using a Lorentzian model and their quality factors calculated using λ/Δλ
as determined by the fit. Also worth noting is the insertion loss (fiber-to-chip-to-fiber). Approximately 0
dBm of power was injected into the bus waveguide. Other loopback structures that tried to isolate edge
coupler loss for measurement revealed 2.1 +/- 0.54 dB (30 loopbacks from various chips were measured).

 2. Conclusion

The passive components from this design run showed a significant improvement from previous MPW
runs. Examples include lower propagation losses exhibited by the higher quality factors as well as the
fiber-to-chip coupling. Unfortunately, the active elements from this run turned out to be defective. I
suspect one of the primary failures was in the doping process.
