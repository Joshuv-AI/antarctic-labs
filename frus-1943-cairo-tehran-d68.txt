Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (29 October 1943, d68)

secret

London, 29 October 1943.

Former Naval Person to President Roosevelt. Personal and most secret. Nr. 477.

Earnestly hope you are making good progress.

It is very awkward waiting about for an answer from Uncle Joe. It is urgent to get dates settled and preparations made. Anyhow, I hope you and I can meet at Anfa between 15th and 20th November. I have a great wish and need to see you. All our troubles and toils are so much easier to face when we are side by side.

Propose code name see my immediately following msg.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Apparently sent via military channels.
