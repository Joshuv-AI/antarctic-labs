No. 1328 The Ambassador in Iran (Murray) to the Acting Secretary of State

restricted

Tehran, July 20, 1945—1 p.m.

511. Speaking in Majlis yesterday Deputy Ardalan urged early withdrawal British and Soviet troops in accordance Article 5 Tripartite Treaty.[1] Prime Minister Sadr replied that govt agreed and was studying problem.

Murray

Source: 891.00/7–2045: Telegram

Notes
[1] i. e., the Anglo-Soviet-Iranian treaty of January 29, 1942. Text in Department of State Bulletin, vol. vi, p. 249.
