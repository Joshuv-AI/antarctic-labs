The SUSY 2025 Conference
The 32nd International Conference on Supersymmetry and the Unification of Fundamen-
tal Interactions (SUSY 2025) was hosted by the Santa Cruz Institute for Particle Physics
(SCIPP) at the University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC) from August 18-23, 2025. The
local organizing committee was co-chaired by Wolfgang Altmannshofer and Howard Haber
and additionally included SCIPP faculty members Marco Battaglia, Stefania Gori, Michael
Hance, Tesla Jeltema, Jason Nielsen, Stefano Profumo, Steven Ritz, and Edgar Shaghoulian.
 For more than thirty years, the SUSY conference series has been one of the premier
international conferences in high-energy physics. While originally focused on theoretical
and experimental developments related to supersymmetry, the scope of the conference has
evolved to encompass a broader range of theoretical frameworks and experimental approaches
associated with both supersymmetric and non-supersymemtric phenomena that lie beyond
the Standard Model of particle physics and the standard mathematical framework of modern
cosmology.

 The primary goal of the conference was to bring together theorists and experimental-
ists working in particle physics, astroparticle physics, cosmology, mathematical physics,
and string theory to exchange ideas on recent theoretical developments and experimental
progress. SUSY 2025 hosted 175 registered participants from universities and research insti-
tutions worldwide. The scientific program included 40 plenary presentations covering major
developments and open questions in the field. In addition, parallel sessions were organized
in seven specialized areas:

 • SUSY phenomenology and experiment,
 • Higgs theory and experiment,
 • Formal SUSY, strings, and field theory,
 • Dark matter, astroparticle physics, and gravitational waves,
 • Flavor physics and neutrinos,
 • Non-SUSY extensions of the Standard Model,
 • Cosmology and the early Universe.

These parallel sessions were held over three afternoons, contained 128 talks, and provided
ample opportunities for detailed scientific discussion and collaboration.
 The conference webpage is available at:

 https://indico.cern.ch/event/SUSY2025

The pre-SUSY School
The SUSY 2025 conference was preceded by the week-long pre-SUSY school, held from Au-
gust 11-15, 2025. The school was designed primarily for graduate students and early-career
postdoctoral researchers and provided pedagogical lectures on advanced foundational topics
in particle physics, astroparticle physics, and cosmology. The school hosted 49 students and
early-career researchers.
 The pre-SUSY school served an important workforce-development role by helping to train
young scientists entering the field and by preparing participants for the scientific discussions
and presentations that took place at the SUSY 2025 conference which followed. Participants
of the pre-SUSY school were automatically admitted to attend the SUSY 2025 conference.
 The pre-SUSY school webpage is available at:

 https://indico.cern.ch/event/preSUSY2025

 2

Use of DOE Support
The DOE support of $10,000 was used to increase accessibility and participation in both the
pre-SUSY school and the SUSY 2025 conference, particularly for students and early-career
researchers. Specifically, the awarded funds covered classroom media access costs, the full
meeting support fees charged by UCSC Conference Services for the pre-SUSY school, and
part of the meeting support fees for the SUSY 2025 conference. As a result, the pre-SUSY
school was offered free of registration fees, and substantially reduced registration fees were
made available for student participants attending the SUSY 2025 conference.

Concluding Remarks
The SUSY 2025 conference and school supported the mission of the DOE Office of High
Energy Physics by fostering scientific exchange and collaboration across multiple areas of
particle physics, mathematical physics, astroparticle physics, and cosmology, while also sup-
porting the training and professional development of graduate students and early-career
postdoctoral researchers.
 Overall, both the SUSY 2025 conference and the pre-SUSY school were highly successful.
Feedback from participants attending the pre-SUSY school was very positive, with students
particularly valuing the pedagogical lectures and the opportunity to engage directly with
leading researchers in the field.

Appendices include the summer school and conference schedules, and the responses to the
