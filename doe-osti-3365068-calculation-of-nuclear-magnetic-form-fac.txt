 Calculation of nuclear magnetic form factors

 The knowledge of how electron and neutrinos interact in nuclei is critical to an
 understanding of finite nucle and of astrophysical environments like neutron stars and
 supernovae. Within this project we are studying nuclear magnetic form factors that
 reflect how electrons interact with nuclei at low momenta. We implemented realistic
 treatments of many-nucleon correlations and currents to enable high-precision studies
 of electron physics.
