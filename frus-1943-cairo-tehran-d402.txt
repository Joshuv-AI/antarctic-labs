Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt (1 December 1943, d402)

Tehran, 1 December 1943.

My Dear Franklin, I was indeed touched by your kind present.[1] It is a beautiful bowl, and I shall always treasure it as a reminder of our sunlit days in Tehran and of the most memorable of my Birthdays.

I cannot thank you enough for all your friendship and support in the years in which we have worked together, and I am glad of this occasion to send you a message of sincere affection and gratitude.

Yours always,

Winston S. Churchill

Source: Roosevelt Papers

Notes
[1] See ante, p. 469. According to Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, The Tehran Conference, p. 176, Roosevelt had sent the following signed message to accompany the bowl: “my affection, may we be together for many years”.
