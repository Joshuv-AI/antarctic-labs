 Liquid Abrasive Cutter Attachments Modernization
 Ilya Golosker
 NEST Mechanical Engineer

                                                                                               LLNL-TR-2009951
This document details the proposed plan to modernize the existing set of cutting attachments in use with the
Liquid Abrasive Cutter (LAC). This effort will leverage the replacement of the LAC power generating unit
(PGU), which is to be completed by the end of FY25. The PGU modernization will transition away from a diesel
direct drive of a high pressure pump to the use of a variable frequency drive, which will be powered by
electricity from the existing generator in use by our DOD customers1. The existing PGU and a model of the
planned replacement is shown below in Figure 1.
The current LAC and support equipment has reliability issues encountered as it nears the end of its service life.
The current nozzles and attachments were custom developed by LLNL and the lack of commercial-off-the-shelf
(COTS) parts has made replacement of consumable and damage prone components costly and time-
consuming.

 Figure 1: A diagram of the existing LAC PGU (left), powered by a diesel engine, and the proposed design by KMT, Inc., utilizing a
 variable frequency drive and powered by electricity.

This work will replace the custom nozzle and cutting attachments with COTS components to improve reliability
and lower operational costs. The project is expected to take one year to complete.
