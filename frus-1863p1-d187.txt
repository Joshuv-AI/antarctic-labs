Mr. Seward to Mr. Adams.

No. 581.]

Department of State, Washington, May 5, 1863.

Sir: Your despatch of the 16th of April (No. 374) transmitting a copy of the continuation of your correspondence with Earl Russell relative to the case of the Georgiana, has been received, and is approved.

I am, sir, your obedient servant,

WILLIAM H. SEWARD.

Charles Francis Adams, Esq., &c., &c., &c.
