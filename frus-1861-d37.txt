Mr. Corwin to Mr. Seward.

No. 1.]

Legation of the United States, City of Mexico, May 29, 1861.

Sir: * * * * * * * *

I deem it of the very first importance that our consuls at every port on the Gulf of Mexico should be at their respective posts, with careful and specific instructions as to their treatment of vessels sailing under the flag of the Confederate States, or having papers from ports within those States, made out by officers under their authority.

Should the relations now existing, or which may hereafter exist, between the United States and the seceding States be such as to require of me any specific act in relation to such state of things, I beg to be advised of it by the department as early as possible.

The present government of Mexico is well affected towards us in our present difficulties, but, for obvious reasons, will be unwilling to enter into any engagement which might produce war with the south, unless protected by promise of aid from the United States.

* * * * * * * *

I am, &c.,

THOMAS CORWIN.

Hon. W. H. Seward, &c., &c., &c.
