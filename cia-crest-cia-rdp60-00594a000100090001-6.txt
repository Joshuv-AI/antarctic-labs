The fog index is os© of the simpler readability formulas now being used 0
It takes account of two elements in style. The first is the number of idea s
the reader must deal with in one unit, and the second is the difficulty of the
ideas. Wa obtain the number of ideas per sentence by counting the number of
words psr sen-tones , and the difficulty by counting the number of words more
than 3 syllables long, since long words are usually difficult words s
You can derive a fog index as follows *

Give each word that can be pronounced in one or two sounds a value of 1 D
2 „ Give each word that is pronounced in more than two sounds a value of 3 0
3o vount the number of elements for each sentence separately,. Take any
complete statement of thought as a sentence j consider a semi-colon or a colon
as a period when making a fog count.

4<» Count only 1 element for proper names, place names, dates, numbers and
abbreviations svon though they are pronounced in more than 3 sounds 0
5 C Compute the average fog count psr sentence 0

6 ? , If the average fog count is 20 or more, divide by 2 to get the fog
