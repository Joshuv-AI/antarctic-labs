Direct determination of the structure functions F L F_{L} , F S F_{S} and G G from F 2 F_{2} and d ​ F 2 / d ​ Q 2 dF_{2}/dQ^{2} to O ⁡ ( α s 2 ) O(\alpha_{s}^{2})
G. R. Boroun, Loyal Durand, Phuoc Ha

Abstract
We extend the results of Lappi et al. , Eur. Phys. J. C 84 , 84 (2024), to show that it is possible to obtain expressions for the longitudinal, singlet and gluon structure functions F L F_{L} , F S F_{S} and G G in deep inelastic scattering directly in terms of the measured functions F 2 F_{2} and d ​ F 2 / ln ⁡ ( Q 2 ) dF_{2}/\ln(Q^{2}) modulo non-singlet corrections expected to be small at very small x x . The latter can be treated at low x x using existing quark distributions. Our results are presented consistently to O ⁡ ( α s 2 ) O(\alpha_{s}^{2}) , correcting and extending the mixed-order results of Lappi et al. .

Summary

We have presented a systematic development of the approach proposed by Lappi
