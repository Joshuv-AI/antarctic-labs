M3.4 Document Maps of Potential Seaweed productivity for NOAA Aquaculture Opportunity
 Areas
 Percentage Completion: 75% - We have been improving our MACMODS simulations in the Gulf of
 Mexico region (overlapping with the NOAA Aquaculture Opportunity Area) by including new
 nitrogen inputs, incorporating salinity in our seaweed growth model, increasing the time stepping
 resolution to 3 hours, and incorporating new uptake parameters derived from tank experiment work
 with Ocean Era for tropical seaweed species. We have produced new maps of seaweed biomass
 production in the Gulf and compared this to marine spatial planning information from NOAA (Fig 2).
 This analysis will be included in a manuscript to be submitted for peer-review.

Task 4 Milestones
M4.1 Perform MACMODS simulations to assess farm design for three seaweed farms
 Completed June 2024 – MACMODS simulations have been performed for the ORI kelp farm in
 Santa Barbara to examine farm line spacing, depth, plant-out timing, harvesting scenarios, etc and
 results are detailed in papers discussed below. Further simulations have been completed for the
 Tropical Seaweed Project site in Puerto Rico to help site farms and to predict growth rates for
 observed oceanographic conditions (detailed in previous quarterly reports and in reports sent to
 Loretta Roberson, MBARI). MACMODS simulations have also been carried out to inform deep
 water upwelling plume release on the Ocean Era farm offshore of Kona, Hawaii (see previous
 quarterly reports). While we have completed the proposed work, we continue to perform
 MACMODS simulations to predict farm performance at the active Ocean Rainforest farm.

M4.2 Publish Model-informed farm design paper
 Completed June 2024 – Publications from the model-informed farm design include:
 1. Yan, C., McWilliams, J. C., & Chamecki, M. (2021). Generation of attached Langmuir
 circulations by a suspended macroalgal farm. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 915, A76.
 2. Bo, T., McWilliams, J. C., Yan, C., & Chamecki, M. (2024). Langmuir turbulence in suspended
 kelp farms. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 985, A11.
 3. Bo, T., McWilliams, J. C., Frieder, C. A., Davis, K. A., & Chamecki, M. (2024). Nutrient
 replenishment by turbulent mixing in suspended macroalgal farms. Geophysical Research
      Letters, 51(14), e2024GL109128.
