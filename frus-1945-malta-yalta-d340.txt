The President’s Special Assistant (Hopkins) to the President [1] (5 February 1945, d340)

[Yalta, February 5, 1945.]

Why not agree to a French zone and consider later the question of putting them on control commission for Germany? or not—

Could you not add that French participation on Control Commission might be considered later?
