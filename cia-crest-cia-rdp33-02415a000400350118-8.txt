B. Specific Medical Personnel Requirements

1. On attempting to estimate personnel requirements for the
project, I feel compelled to point out that no one, at this particular
moment in the life of the project, can predict with finality the future medi-
cal personnel needs, either qualitatively or quantitatively. I believe we can
come fairly close to estimating future needs but any major change in opera-
tional concept may well disrupt your personnel requirements. Therefore, I
recommend that no final, fixed committments be made in regards total
personnel required, with the Surgeon General, until the final operational
plan is drawn up and approved.

importance to the over-all Air Force effort. Thus far we have attempted
