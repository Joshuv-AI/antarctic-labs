Prime Minister Churchill to President Roosevelt[1] (21 November 1943, d153)

secret

[Cairo?] 21 November 1943.[2]

Former Naval Person to President Roosevelt personal and most secret. No. 506.

My arrival in Egypt is bound to be known as I shall pass through to see Catroux and others: moreover British Parliament meets on 23rd and my absence must be explained. Unless I hear from you to the contrary I shall allow it to be stated on 22nd that I am in Cairo.

This publicity will be unsupported cover for your movement which I think should not be announced for a few days.

You will be receiving a telegram about military precautions[3] which are excellent.

Source: Roosevelt Papers: Telegram

Notes
[1] Sent to London; forwarded to Washington, apparently via United States military channels; and forwarded further by the White House Map Room to Roosevelt in North Africa.
[2] According to Alanbrooke (p. 74), Churchill arrived at Cairo at 2:30 p.m. by plane from Alexandria.
[3] Telegram 508 of November 21, 1943, post, p. 105.
