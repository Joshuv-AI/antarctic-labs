 Front End
 Structural Details

 Purpose and Objective:
 The purpose of the enclosed calculations and details is to document seismic restraint of the XRM
 scanner, generator unit, and a helium tank stand.

 Assumptions and Input:
 Information was provided by Xradia, the equipment manufacturer.

 Analytical Methods and Computations:
 Standard methods using Excel spreadsheets for code checks and stress analysis.

 Computer Programs:
 Microsoft Excel

 Results:
 The seismic restraint clips and anchorage using post-installed concrete anchors as shown herein
 provide the required seismic restraint.

 Conclusions:
 The enclosed structural calculations and details provide documentation of seismic restraint in
 compliance with DOE-STD-1020-2002 for a PC-1 SSC.

 References:
            DOE-STD-1020-2002
 2010 California Building Code
 ASCE 7-05
 ICC-ES ESR-1917 (Hilti Kwik Bolt TZ expansion anchors)
 ICC-ES ESR-2896 (Hilti HDI Concrete expansion anchors)
